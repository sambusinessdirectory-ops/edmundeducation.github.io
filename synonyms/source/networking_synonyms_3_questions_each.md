# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Networking

**本課集中在 `networking = 建立及發展人際／專業聯繫`，不是電腦網絡的 technical networking。它可以指初次結識、建立人脈網絡、發展專業關係、長期經營聯絡，或把不同人士互相連結。**

核心替代表達：

`relationship-building / making connections / making contacts / building a network / building professional relationships / cultivating contacts / connecting with others / linking people`

> **核心原則**：先判斷句子重點是 **forming a first connection, creating a wider network, developing professional relationships, maintaining contacts, general social connection, or connecting other people with one another**。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Relationship-building

**建立關係；關係建立**

和 `networking` 很接近，強調建立並維持有用、互信的關係，而不只是交換聯絡資料。可用於商業、社區、外交和專業場合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The conference focuses on relationship-building between local businesses.

會議重點是促進本地企業之間建立關係。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **developing useful, ongoing relationships**

**REGISTER · 語域**

商業、專業、正式

---

## 02 Making connections

**建立聯繫；結識人脈**

很自然、較口語的改寫，強調和新的人建立聯繫。比 `networking` 少一點商業術語感。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Industry events are useful for making connections.

業界活動很適合建立聯繫。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **forming new personal or professional connections**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、職涯、商業

---

## 03 Making contacts

**建立聯絡；結識業界人士**

特別強調認識新的人並取得聯絡方式，常見於求職、商業和專業活動。它比 relationship-building 更偏『擴大聯絡圈』。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She attended the trade fair to make contacts in the industry.

她參加商貿展以結識業界人士。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **meeting people who may become useful professional contacts**

**REGISTER · 語域**

商業、職涯、一般

---

## 04 Building a network

**建立人脈網絡；擴展關係網**

非常接近 `networking` 的核心意思，強調逐步建立一群可互相聯絡、交換資訊或提供機會的人。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Graduates should start building a network before they begin job hunting.

畢業生應在開始求職前建立人脈網絡。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **gradually creating a useful network of contacts**

**REGISTER · 語域**

職涯、商業、一般

---

## 05 Building professional relationships

**建立專業關係**

比一般 `relationship-building` 更明確限定於工作、商業或專業領域，適合描述長期合作、人脈和信任。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The internship helped her build professional relationships.

這份實習幫助她建立專業關係。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **developing lasting relationships in a professional context**

**REGISTER · 語域**

職涯、商業、正式

---

## 06 Cultivating contacts

**經營人脈；培養聯絡關係**

較正式，強調不只是認識人，而是持續維持、發展和經營聯絡關係。比 `making contacts` 更有長期性。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He spent years cultivating contacts across the industry.

他多年來一直在業界經營人脈。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **deliberately developing and maintaining useful contacts**

**REGISTER · 語域**

商業、專業、較正式

---

## 07 Connecting with others

**與他人建立聯繫**

廣泛、自然，強調和其他人建立人際或專業連結。它可以是 networking，但也可用於社交、社區或一般人際互動。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Volunteering is a good way of connecting with others.

做義工是與他人建立聯繫的好方法。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **forming interpersonal links with other people**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、社區、職涯

---

## 08 Linking people

**連結人群；把人連繫起來**

當 `networking` 的重點不是自己結識人，而是促成不同人士互相認識、合作或分享資源時，這個表達最合適。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The programme is designed to link people with similar professional interests.

這個計劃旨在把有相近專業興趣的人連繫起來。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **bringing people into contact with one another**

**REGISTER · 語域**

社區、商業、項目

# 02 False synonyms

## Networking / Relationship-building
兩者很接近；`relationship-building` 更強調互信和關係的持續發展，不只是認識新的人。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Networking can begin with a short introduction at a conference.  
Relationship-building continues after that first meeting and develops trust over time.

## Networking / Making connections
`making connections` 較口語，常指和新的人建立聯繫。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The event gives graduates time for networking.  
Many attend simply to make connections with people in the industry.

## Networking / Making contacts
`making contacts` 偏第一次認識有用的聯絡人，常見於求職和商業。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Networking may include maintaining existing relationships.  
Making contacts focuses more on meeting new people who may be useful contacts.

## Networking / Building a network
`building a network` 更明確表示逐步形成一整個人脈網絡。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A single conversation can be networking.  
Building a network usually happens through many connections over time.

## Networking / Building professional relationships
後者更正式、更長期，而且明確限定在專業／工作關係。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Networking at an event may last only a few minutes.  
Building professional relationships often requires repeated contact and trust.

## Making contacts / Cultivating contacts
前者偏初次建立聯絡；後者偏長期維持和經營。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
She made several new contacts at the conference.  
She later cultivated those contacts through regular communication.

## Networking / Connecting with others
`connecting with others` 更廣，可以是社區或社交連結，不一定有職涯／商業目的。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Networking often has a professional purpose.  
Connecting with others may simply reduce social isolation.

## Networking / Linking people
`linking people` 的主語常是平台、項目或中介者，重點是促成其他人互相認識。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
She networks to expand her own contacts.  
The programme links people who have similar professional interests.

| C | Building professional relationships |

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**A. Relationship-building · 建立關係；關係建立**

> The event is mainly about relationship-building between suppliers and retailers.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. Relationship-building ✓**  
句子線索「重點是建立持續合作關係」強調信任、合作和持續關係，而不只是第一次認識；`relationship-building` 最準確。

**B. Making connections**  
`making connections`：較口語，重點是和新的人建立聯繫。這句線索是「重點是建立持續合作關係」；若句子強調長期維繫、正式專業關係或主動把別人互相介紹，焦點不同。

**C. Building professional relationships**  
`building professional relationships`：明確是工作／專業領域的長期關係。這句線索是「重點是建立持續合作關係」；若只是一般社區社交或初次交換聯絡，會過於正式和長期化。

**D. Building a network**  
`building a network`：強調逐步形成一整個人脈網絡。這句線索是「重點是建立持續合作關係」；若只談單一關係、一次介紹或一般社交，這個表達範圍太大。

**E. Small talk**  
`Small talk`：small talk = 閒談；可以是 networking 的一部分，但不等於建立關係本身。 本句線索是「重點是建立持續合作關係」，所以它不是人際／專業 networking 的同一活動。

**F. Advertising**  
`Advertising`：advertising = 廣告宣傳；不是建立人際或專業網絡。 本句線索是「重點是建立持續合作關係」，所以它不是人際／專業 networking 的同一活動。

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Networking vs Relationship-building** — networking 可很短暫；relationship-building 強調持續和信任。
- **Making connections vs Making contacts** — connections 稍廣；contacts 更像可聯絡的具體人士。
- **Making contacts vs Cultivating contacts** — making = 初次建立；cultivating = 長期經營。
- **Networking vs Building a network** — networking 是活動；building a network 強調累積成一個關係網。
- **Relationship-building vs Building professional relationships** — 後者明確限定職場／專業語境。
- **Networking vs Connecting with others** — 後者可以純社交／社區，不一定有專業目的。
- **Networking vs Linking people** — 前者多數是建立自己的人脈；後者是促成別人互相連結。
- **Building a network vs Linking people** — 前者結果是自己的 network；後者重點是中介／牽線。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Network / networking

> build a network  
> do some networking  
> a networking event

`networking` 在這個意思通常是不可數活動名詞：

> Networking is important in some industries.

## Make connections / contacts

> make connections **with** people  
> make contacts **in** an industry  
> make contact **with** someone

注意 `make contact with` 常用單數 `contact`，但 `make contacts` 表示結識多個聯絡人。

## Relationship-building

作前置複合詞時常用連字號：

> relationship-building skills  
> relationship-building activities

## Cultivate

直接接受詞：

> cultivate contacts  
> cultivate professional relationships

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 重點是建立互信和持續關係？ → **relationship-building**
2. 想較口語說「建立聯繫」？ → **making connections**
3. 是結識新專業聯絡人？ → **making contacts**
4. 是逐步建立整個人脈網絡？ → **building a network**
5. 明確是長期職場關係？ → **building professional relationships**
6. 是長期維持、經營已有聯絡？ → **cultivating contacts**
7. 只是一般人際／社區連結？ → **connecting with others**
8. 是把其他人互相介紹／牽線？ → **linking people**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`relationship-building / making connections / making contacts / building a network / building professional relationships / cultivating contacts / connecting with others / linking people`

最重要的 near-miss：

`socialising / small talk / recruitment / collaboration / internet networking / data networking`

尤其要記住：

> **making contacts** = 初次結識聯絡人  
> **cultivating contacts** = 長期經營人脈  
> **relationship-building** = 深化互信  
> **building a network** = 累積整個人脈網絡  
> **linking people** = 促成別人互相連結
