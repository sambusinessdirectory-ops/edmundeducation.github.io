# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Be responsible for

**負責、主管、處理、監督、承擔問責——究竟是哪一種 be responsible for？**

本課主要聚焦：

**be responsible for = to have a duty to manage, deal with, supervise, care for, or be accountable for someone or something**

例如：

- be responsible for a team
- be responsible for a project
- be responsible for customer enquiries
- be responsible for the budget
- be responsible for the final result

這個詞組可以表達幾種不同的「負責」：

1. **management / control** — 掌管、管理  
2. **task ownership** — 被分派負責某項工作  
3. **handling / care** — 負責處理或照料  
4. **accountability** — 對結果或決定承擔責任、作出交代  
5. **entrusted responsibility** — 因信任而受託掌管

> **Excluded sense:** `be responsible for` 也可以表示 **cause / lead to（導致）**，例如 `Smoking is responsible for many preventable illnesses.`  
> 本課核心不把這個「導致」義和職責義混在同一 synonym pool。

核心近義與近義表達：

**be in charge of; be accountable for; oversee; manage; handle; take care of; be tasked with; have responsibility for; be answerable for; be entrusted with; look after**

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Be in charge of

**負責；主管；掌管**

和 `be responsible for` 最接近的日常替代表達之一。強調某人對一項工作、部門、人員或活動具有主要管理責任或控制權。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Maya is in charge of the customer support team.

Maya 負責管理客戶支援團隊。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- be in charge of a team
- be in charge of operations
- be in charge of a project
- put someone in charge of

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否強調某人是主要負責人，並具有管理或決策權？

---

## 02 Be accountable for

**對……負責；須為……承擔責任**

較正式，強調需要對結果、決定、資源或行為作出交代，尤其適合管理、法律、治理和專業語境。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Managers are accountable for the decisions made by their teams.

管理人員須為其團隊所作的決定承擔責任。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- be accountable for results
- be accountable for spending
- be held accountable for
- accountable to the board

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否重點在『需要交代／承擔後果』，而不只是日常分工？

---

## 03 Oversee

**監督；統籌；負責監管**

表示從較高層面監督一項工作、團隊、程序或項目。和 `be responsible for` 很接近，但更強調監察、統籌和確保工作正常進行。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

She oversees all recruitment and training activities.

她監督所有招聘和培訓工作。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- oversee operations
- oversee a project
- oversee staff
- oversee implementation

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否強調從上層監察和統籌，而不是親自完成每項工作？

---

## 04 Manage

**管理；負責處理**

表示負責日常運作、人員、資源或工作流程。和 `be responsible for` 在工作分工語境中高度接近。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

He manages the company's online store.

他負責管理公司的網上商店。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- manage a team
- manage a department
- manage operations
- manage a budget
- manage a project

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否不只是名義上負責，而是實際管理日常工作和資源？

---

## 05 Handle

**負責處理；應付**

較日常，表示某人負責處理某類任務、問題、查詢、文件或情況。比 `manage` 更偏具體事項。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Lena handles all customer complaints.

Lena 負責處理所有客戶投訴。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- handle enquiries
- handle complaints
- handle paperwork
- handle a case
- handle requests

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是在說某人負責處理某一類具體事情？

---

## 06 Take care of

**負責照料／處理；搞定**

常見口語表達，可表示負責照顧人，也可表示處理某項工作、安排或問題。語氣比 `be responsible for` 輕鬆自然。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

I'll take care of the bookings.

我會負責處理預訂安排。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- take care of the arrangements
- take care of customers
- take care of paperwork
- take care of the problem

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否適合用較口語的『這件事我來處理／照料』？

---

## 07 Be tasked with

**被委派負責；被指派執行**

較正式，表示某人或團隊被明確指派一項任務或責任。重點在『任務是被交付的』。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The committee was tasked with reviewing the policy.

委員會獲委派負責檢討該政策。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- be tasked with reviewing
- be tasked with developing
- be tasked with managing
- task someone with

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否強調責任是由上級／機構正式委派的？

---

## 08 Have responsibility for

**對……負有責任；負責**

和 `be responsible for` 幾乎是名詞化改寫。正式、中性，尤其適合職位說明、政策、組織架構和工作分工。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The finance director has responsibility for financial reporting.

財務總監負責財務報告工作。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- have responsibility for staff
- have overall responsibility for
- assume responsibility for
- share responsibility for

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否需要較正式地把 responsible 改寫成 responsibility 名詞結構？

---

## 09 Be answerable for

**須為……負責；須就……作交代**

較正式，和 `be accountable for` 很接近，表示如果出現問題或結果，需要向上級、公眾或其他權力機構作出解釋。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Senior officials are answerable for the department's performance.

高級官員須為部門表現作出交代。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- be answerable for decisions
- be answerable for conduct
- answerable to the public
- ultimately answerable for

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否強調『出了問題要向某人交代』？

---

## 10 Be entrusted with

**獲託付負責；受委託掌管**

表示某項重要工作、資產、資訊或責任因信任而交給某人負責。比 `be responsible for` 多了『被信任、受託』的語感。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

She was entrusted with managing the confidential records.

她獲託付負責管理機密紀錄。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- be entrusted with a task
- be entrusted with funds
- be entrusted with responsibility
- entrust someone with

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否強調因信任而把重要責任／財物／資訊交給某人？

---

## 11 Look after

**照顧；照料；負責照看**

較口語，尤其用於照顧人、動物、場所、設備或日常事務。在這些語境可自然替代 `be responsible for`。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Who looks after the equipment when the technician is away?

技術員不在時，誰負責照看設備？

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- look after children
- look after customers
- look after equipment
- look after the house

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是日常『照顧／照看』型責任？

# 02 False synonyms

看起來與 **be responsible for** 有關，但可能只表示參與、許可、因果或某一種較窄的責任。

## 01 Be involved in

**參與不等於負責**

`be involved in` 只表示參與某事；參與者未必承擔主要責任。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She is **involved in** the project, but David is **responsible for** it.

---

## 02 Be associated with

**有關聯不等於負責**

`associated with` 表示有關係或聯繫，不代表工作職責。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The brand is **associated with** luxury.

---

## 03 Cause

**造成不等於職責上的負責**

`be responsible for` 也可有『導致』義，但這是另一個 sense；在職務語境不能直接用 `cause`。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Heavy rain **caused** the flooding.

---

## 04 Be liable for

**法律責任比一般 responsible 更窄**

`liable for` 多指法律／財務上的責任或賠償義務，不是一般工作分工。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The company may be **liable for** the damage.

---

## 05 Be authorised to

**獲授權不等於負責**

`authorised to` 表示有權做某事；有權不代表必須負責。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She is **authorised to** sign the contract.

---

## 06 Be assigned to

**被分派到某處不等於負責某事**

`assigned to` 可表示被分派到一個部門、地點或案件，但不一定是主要負責人。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

He was **assigned to** the London office.

---

## 07 Supervise

**監督只是 responsibility 的一種**

`supervise` 很接近某些管理責任，但如果責任是親自處理、承擔結果或照顧，未必適合。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She **supervises** six trainees.

---

## 08 Own

**商業用語中的 ownership 不等於一般 responsible**

工作場合有時說 `own a task/process` 表示全權負責，但屬較新的商業用語，不能在所有語境自然替換。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Kim **owns** the onboarding process.

# Be in charge of

## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** Be in charge of · 負責；主管；掌管

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

Daniel is responsible for the evening shift.

### 中文翻譯

### OPTIONS


- **A. is in charge of**

- **B. is familiar with**

- **C. is opposed to**

- **D. is dependent on**

- **E. is absent from**

- **F. is interested in**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**is in charge of · 負責；主管；掌管**

Daniel **is in charge of** the evening shift.

- **B. is familiar with** — `is familiar with` = 熟悉，表示知道或了解，不表示負責。

- **C. is opposed to** — `is opposed to` = 反對。

- **D. is dependent on** — `is dependent on` = 依賴。

- **E. is absent from** — `is absent from` = 缺席。

|---|---:|---|
| **be in charge of** | 非常高 | 掌管、具有主要管理責任 |
| **be accountable for** | 非常高 | 對結果／決定須作交代 |
| **oversee** | 很高 | 從較高層面監督、統籌 |
| **manage** | 非常高 | 日常管理人員、資源、流程 |

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Be responsible for vs Be in charge of

**be in charge of** 更強調管理權和主要負責人身份；`be responsible for` 可以只是承擔某一職責。

---

## Be responsible for vs Be accountable for

**accountable for** 更強調需要就結果作交代或承擔後果。

---

## Oversee vs Manage

**oversee** 偏高層監督和統籌；**manage** 偏日常管理和資源運作。

---

## Manage vs Handle

**manage** 適合持續管理團隊／部門／預算；**handle** 更適合具體任務、查詢、投訴或個案。

---

## Take care of vs Look after

兩者都較口語；**look after** 特別自然於照顧人／動物／場所，**take care of** 也常表示把工作『處理妥當』。

---

## Be tasked with vs Be entrusted with

**tasked with** 強調正式分派；**entrusted with** 強調基於信任而託付。

---

## Be accountable for vs Be answerable for

兩者很接近；**answerable for** 尤其帶『須向某個權力／公眾交代』的意味。

---

## Have responsibility for vs Be responsible for

語義幾乎相同；前者是名詞化、較正式的結構。

---

# 06 Common expressions / idiomatic equivalents


## Take ownership of

**主動承擔責任；全權負責**

常見職場表達：`She took ownership of the problem and resolved it.`

---

## Own the process

**全權負責整個流程**

商業／項目管理用語：`The operations team owns the process.`

---

## Have something under one's care

**負責照看／保管**

較正式或描述性：`The curator has several valuable works under her care.`

---

## Carry the responsibility for

**承擔……責任**

比一般 `be responsible for` 更強調責任份量：`He carries responsibility for the final decision.`

---

## The buck stops with someone

**最終責任由某人承擔**

慣用語，表示最終問責落在某人身上：`The buck stops with the chief executive.`

---

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Be responsible for + noun / -ing

常見結構：

- `be responsible for the budget`
- `be responsible for staff training`
- `be responsible for organising the event`

`for` 後面如接動詞，使用 **-ing**，不能直接接原形動詞。

---

## 02 In charge of also takes noun / -ing

- `in charge of the department`
- `in charge of organising the conference`

它通常比 `responsible for` 更強調主要主管身份。

---

## 03 Tasked with / entrusted with + noun / -ing

- `tasked with reviewing the policy`
- `entrusted with managing the funds`

`with` 後面同樣常接名詞或 `-ing`。

---

## 04 Responsible to vs responsible for

這兩個結構不要混淆：

- **responsible for something** = 對某項工作／結果負責  
- **responsible to someone** = 向某人負責／向某人匯報

Example:

`The finance manager is responsible **for** the budget and responsible **to** the chief executive.`

---

## 05 Accountability is stronger than ordinary task responsibility

Compare:

- `She is responsible for preparing the report.`  
  → 她的工作分工。

- `She is accountable for the accuracy of the report.`  
  → 如果報告出錯，她需要承擔後果／作出交代。

---

## 06 Responsible for can also mean "cause"

另外一個重要 sense：

- `Poor maintenance was responsible for the failure.`
- `Heavy rainfall was responsible for the flooding.`

這裡不是「工作職責」，而是：

- cause
- lead to
- result in
- account for

為避免語義混亂，本課核心 synonym pool 沒有把兩個 sense 混合。
