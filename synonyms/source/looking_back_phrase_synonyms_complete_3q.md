# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Looking back · phrase

「回想起來／事後回顧／回頭反思」，究竟是哪一種 `looking back`？

`looking back` 可以只是**回想過去**，也可以表示**事後重新評價**。使用者提供的 **in retrospect** 和 **thinking about the past** 都保留：前者偏 retrospective judgement，後者偏單純回憶。另加入 **on reflection**（經思考後重新判斷）、**with hindsight**（知道後來結果後回看）、**reflecting on**（深入反思某段經歷）與 **when I think back**（較口語、個人化的回憶）。

> 本課主要處理 `looking back = thinking about a past event or period from the present, sometimes simply recalling it and sometimes reassessing it with later knowledge`。
>
> 同義詞數量**不固定**；本課按回憶、重新評估、審慎反思、後見之明、深入反省與個人敘事選用 **6 個**替代表達。
>
> Format: **6 curated alternatives + 8 false-synonym contrasts + 3 exercises per target synonym (18 questions total)**.

---

# 01 同義詞指南

### 01 In retrospect
**回想起來；事後回顧**

使用者提供。`in retrospect` 是 `looking back` 最核心、最自然的正式近義表達之一，表示從現在回頭評估過去事件，通常帶有後來才看清某事的意味。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> In retrospect, we should have tested the system more thoroughly.

**中文翻譯**

事後回想起來，我們本來應該更徹底地測試系統。

**USAGE CLUE**

- evaluating a past event from the present with later knowledge

---

### 02 Thinking about the past
**回想過去；想起從前**

使用者提供。這是最透明、最一般的解釋性改寫，單純表示把思緒轉向過去，不一定包含後見之明、判斷或懊悔。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Thinking about the past, she remembered how uncertain she had felt.

**中文翻譯**

回想過去，她記得自己當時有多麼不安。

**USAGE CLUE**

- simply recalling or mentally revisiting earlier experiences

---

### 03 On reflection
**經過反思；仔細想過之後**

`on reflection` 表示經過一番思考後重新評估某件事。它可以回顧過去，但比一般 `looking back` 更強調 deliberation / reconsideration。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> On reflection, I think the original plan was too ambitious.

**中文翻譯**

經過反思，我認為原來的計劃太進取。

**USAGE CLUE**

- a conclusion reached after deliberate reconsideration

---

### 04 With hindsight
**事後看來；有後見之明地**

`with hindsight` 特別強調在知道後來發生了什麼之後，回頭看某個決定、風險或錯誤。它比 `in retrospect` 更鮮明地帶有「事後才看得清」的後見之明。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> With hindsight, the decision was riskier than we realised.

**中文翻譯**

事後看來，那個決定比我們當時意識到的更冒險。

**USAGE CLUE**

- judging the past with knowledge of what happened afterwards

---

### 05 Reflecting on
**反思；回顧並思考**

`reflecting on + 名詞/-ing` 表示較深入地思考過去的經歷、決定或學到的東西。它比 `thinking about the past` 更有 reflective / analytical 色彩。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Reflecting on the experience, she realised how much she had learned.

**中文翻譯**

反思這段經歷，她意識到自己學到了很多。

**USAGE CLUE**

- thoughtful consideration of a past experience or decision

---

### 06 When I think back
**當我回想起來；回想起……時**

較口語、個人化。`when I think back` / `when we think back` 常用於敘述個人回憶、生活經歷或帶感情色彩的回顧。它比 `in retrospect` 更自然於個人故事。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> When I think back, I remember how nervous I was on my first day.

**中文翻譯**

當我回想起來，我記得第一天時有多緊張。

**USAGE CLUE**

- personal, conversational recollection of an earlier experience

# 02 False synonyms

## 01 looking back / in retrospect

兩者高度接近；`in retrospect` 更偏事後評價，而 `looking back` 也可以只是回憶。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> In retrospect, the plan was too ambitious.  
> Looking back, I remember the old classroom.

---

## 02 looking back / thinking about the past

`thinking about the past` 只需回想過去；`looking back` 有時還會包含後見之明或重新評估。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Thinking about the past, she remembered her first job.  
> Looking back, she realised the decision had been risky.

---

## 03 looking back / with hindsight

`with hindsight` 明確要求知道後來發生了什麼；普通 looking back 不一定有這一層。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> With hindsight, the warning signs were obvious.  
> Looking back, I remember how excited we were.

---

## 04 looking back / on reflection

`on reflection` 強調經過思考後重新得出結論；looking back 可以只是敘述回憶。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> On reflection, I think we chose the wrong route.  
> Looking back, I remember the journey clearly.

---

## 05 looking back / remembering

`remembering` 是記得某個資訊／事件；`looking back` 更像主動回顧一段過去。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> I remember his name.  
> Looking back on that year, I learned a lot.

---

## 06 looking back / looking behind

`looking behind` 是實際把視線轉到身後；`looking back` 本課是比喻性的回顧過去。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> She looked behind her.  
> Looking back, the decision seems obvious.

---

## 07 looking back / nostalgia

`nostalgia` 是對過去的懷念情緒；looking back 不一定帶懷舊或溫暖感情。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> He felt nostalgia for his childhood.  
> Looking back, the project clearly had problems.

---

## 08 looking back / reflection

`looking back` 是片語；`reflection` 是名詞，可指反思、倒影或沉思，不能直接替換句法。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> After reflection, she changed her mind.  
> Looking back, she understood the mistake.

## 練習 01.1

**Target word/phrase: in retrospect · 回想起來；事後回顧**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> Looking back, I realise that we moved too quickly.

**中文翻譯**

回想起來，我意識到我們當時行動得太快。

A. in retrospect
B. on reflection
C. with hindsight
D. reflecting on
E. thinking about the past
F. when I think back

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**in retrospect · 回想起來；事後回顧**

> In retrospect, I realise that we moved too quickly.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. in retrospect ✓**  
本題的決定性線索是 **a present reassessment of a past decision**；這裡需要的是「從現在重新評估過去」，所以 **in retrospect** 最精確。

**B. on reflection**  
強調經過思考後重新考慮並得出判斷。 本題的決定性線索是 **a present reassessment of a past decision**；這裡需要的是「從現在重新評估過去」。因此 **on reflection** 雖與 `looking back` 有關，但語義焦點並不最吻合。

**C. with hindsight**  
明確表示因知道後來結果而看清過去，常與 should have / obvious now 等搭配。 本題的決定性線索是 **a present reassessment of a past decision**；這裡需要的是「從現在重新評估過去」。因此 **with hindsight** 雖與 `looking back` 有關，但語義焦點並不最吻合。

**D. reflecting on**  
強調較深入、較有分析性的回顧，常帶 learning / lessons / understanding。 本題的決定性線索是 **a present reassessment of a past decision**；這裡需要的是「從現在重新評估過去」。因此 **reflecting on** 雖與 `looking back` 有關，但語義焦點並不最吻合。

**E. thinking about the past**  
只表示回想以前，未必有判斷、後見之明或結論。 本題的決定性線索是 **a present reassessment of a past decision**；這裡需要的是「從現在重新評估過去」。因此 **thinking about the past** 雖與 `looking back` 有關，但語義焦點並不最吻合。

**F. when I think back**  
口語、個人化，常與 remember, laugh, first day, childhood 等回憶搭配。 本題的決定性線索是 **a present reassessment of a past decision**；這裡需要的是「從現在重新評估過去」。因此 **when I think back** 雖與 `looking back` 有關，但語義焦點並不最吻合。

# 05 高混淆組

## in retrospect vs with hindsight
- **in retrospect**：一般事後回顧／重新評估。
- **with hindsight**：特別強調知道後來結果後的後見之明。

## thinking about the past vs reflecting on
- **thinking about the past**：單純回想。
- **reflecting on**：更深入、更分析性。

## on reflection vs in retrospect
- **on reflection**：經過思考後得出結論。
- **in retrospect**：從現在回看過去整體。

## when I think back vs in retrospect
- **when I think back**：個人、口語、回憶敘事。
- **in retrospect**：較中性／正式的評估。

## looking back vs remembering
- **looking back**：主動回顧一段過去。
- **remembering**：單純記得某事。

## looking back vs nostalgia
- **looking back**：可中性、正面或負面。
- **nostalgia**：帶懷舊情緒。

## looking back vs looking behind
- **looking back**：本課是比喻回顧。
- **looking behind**：字面向後看。

## looking back vs reflection
- **looking back**：片語。
- **reflection**：名詞。

# 08 生成／評估注意事項

1. **同義詞數量不是固定值。** 本課按回憶、重新評估、審慎反思、後見之明、深入反省與個人敘事選 **6 個**。
2. 因此本課有 **3 exercises per target = 18 exercises**。
3. 使用者提供的 `in retrospect` 與 `thinking about the past` 全部保留。
4. `in retrospect` 需要 present-day reassessment，不只是在記憶。
5. `thinking about the past` 只在 simple recollection 是焦點時使用。
6. `on reflection` 需要 deliberate reconsideration / revised judgement。
7. `with hindsight` 需要 later knowledge / outcome-based clarity，特別常與 `should have`, `obvious now` 類線索搭配。
8. `reflecting on` 需要 deeper analysis / lessons / understanding。
9. `when I think back` 主要保留給 first-person conversational recollection。
10. False synonyms 特別測試 remembering / looking behind / nostalgia / reflection，避免把記憶、字面視線、懷舊情緒與詞性混在一起。
11. Distractors 全部使用真實自然英文片語，不使用人工標籤。
12. 每題以 decisive clue 建立唯一答案，例如 `later outcome`, `should have recognised`, `learned`, `I remember`。
13. 原始 `looking back` 句子保持自然；正解需要改變句首結構或主語時採用自然英文。
14. 本課處理的是比喻性的 **looking back = 回顧過去**，不混入字面「回頭看」。
