核心近義與近義表達：

**self-improvement; self-development; personal growth; individual growth; self-growth; self-betterment; personal advancement; growth and development; self-cultivation**

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Self-improvement

**自我提升；自我改善**

主動改善能力、習慣、知識、態度或生活表現。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Reading can play an important role in self-improvement.

閱讀可以在自我提升中發揮重要作用。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- self-improvement goals

- self-improvement plan

- focus on self-improvement

- personal self-improvement

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Professional development

**專業發展只是其中一部分**

它集中於工作技能、專業知識和職場能力；personal development 更廣。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Professional development improves work-related capability; personal development is broader.

---

## 02 Career development

**職涯發展不等於整體個人發展**

它聚焦職業道路、職位與工作能力。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A person may pursue personal development outside their career.

---

## 03 Self-promotion

**自我宣傳不等於自我提升**

它是展示自己、提高能見度或形象。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Self-promotion changes how others see you; self-improvement changes your skills or habits.

---

## 04 Personal achievement

**成就不等於發展過程**

achievement 是成果；development 是持續成長的過程。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Winning an award is an achievement; developing confidence is personal development.

---

## 05 Education

**教育是其中一種途徑**

education 可促進 personal development，但兩者不等同。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Education can support personal development without being identical to it.

---

## 06 Maturity

**成熟是可能結果**

maturity 描述成熟程度；personal development 包含通往成熟的多種變化。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Personal development can lead to greater maturity.

---

## 07 Self-help

**自助內容／方法不等於發展本身**

self-help 常指書籍、方法或自主解決問題的做法。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A self-help book may support personal development.

---

## 08 Personality development

**人格發展只是部分重疊**

它特別聚焦人格特質、社交風格或個性塑造。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Personal development can include skills and habits beyond personality.

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Personal development is usually uncountable
自然：
- personal development
- support personal development
- opportunities for personal development

## 02 Self-improvement vs self-development
- self-improvement = 更強調改善現有不足／習慣／技能
- self-development = 更強調長期發展潛能和能力

## 03 Personal growth
通常比 self-improvement 更偏：
- maturity
- self-understanding
- life experience
- emotional growth

## 04 Professional development is narrower
只限：
- work skills
- professional knowledge
- career capability

## 05 Self-cultivation
較適合：
- character
- discipline
- reflection
- intellectual / moral development

## 06 Development vs advancement
- development = 成長過程
- advancement = 向前進步／提升位置
