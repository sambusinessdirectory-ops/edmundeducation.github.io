# FAILURE · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Failure

**「失敗／挫折／敗北／失靈」，要先分清是暫時受挫、競爭落敗、目標未達、系統故障，還是整個計劃徹底垮掉。**

本單元集中處理 **failure = lack of success, inability to achieve an intended result, or failure of a person, plan, system, or device to perform as intended**。你提供的 **setback; defeat; lack of success** 分別代表 **暫時受挫、競爭敗北、中性未成功** 三個核心方向。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且高實用度的詞／表達，並按最新 inspection 標準區分 **breakdown / collapse / disappointment / flop / fiasco / abortive attempt / non-performance / malfunction / non-achievement / things not working out**。每個目標設 **3 道題**；所有較強、較口語或技術性表達均明確標示使用限制。

## Tier 1 — Core setback / defeat / system-failure equivalents · 核心挫折、敗北與失靈近義詞

### 01 Setback

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：挫折；倒退；阻礙  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：a problem or event that delays progress or makes success harder without necessarily ending the effort  
**用法提示**：和 failure 最核心的近義詞之一，尤其當事情沒有完全失敗，而只是暫時受挫、進度倒退時。  
**常見搭配**：major setback; temporary setback; suffer a setback; setback to progress; financial setback.  
**常見搭配對象**：project, recovery, career, plan, progress.  
**與 failure 的辨析**：Setback 通常不是最終失敗；failure 更可能表示整件事沒有成功。  
**使用限制**：若事情已正式結束而沒有達成目標，failure / defeat 更直接。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The funding cut was a serious setback for the project.  
削減撥款對項目是一個嚴重挫折。

### 02 Defeat

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：失敗；敗北；被擊敗  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：failure to win in a contest, conflict, election, battle, or competitive situation  
**用法提示**：當 failure 是在比賽、競爭、戰爭、投票或對抗中沒有勝出時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：suffer defeat; election defeat; military defeat; crushing defeat; accept defeat.  
**常見搭配對象**：team, candidate, army, side, campaign.  
**與 failure 的辨析**：Defeat 需要競爭／對抗背景；failure 的使用範圍更廣。  
**使用限制**：機器故障、考試不及格、計劃失敗通常不用 defeat。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The team suffered a narrow defeat in the final.  
球隊在決賽中以些微差距落敗。

### 03 Lack of success

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：未能成功；缺乏成果  
**語域**：neutral / explanatory  
**核心語義**：the condition of not achieving the intended result or desired level of success  
**用法提示**：這是 failure 最透明的解釋性改寫之一，特別適合避免過強的負面語氣。  
**常見搭配**：lack of success; relative lack of success; continued lack of success; explain the lack of success; lack of success in.  
**常見搭配對象**：project, policy, attempt, programme, campaign.  
**與 failure 的辨析**：Lack of success 比 failure 語氣較中性，也較少帶徹底失敗感。  
**使用限制**：明顯災難性失敗時，這個說法可能過於淡化。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The review examined the reasons for the programme's lack of success.  
檢討研究該計劃未能成功的原因。

### 04 Breakdown

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：故障；崩潰；失靈  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：a failure of a machine, system, process, relationship, or communication to continue functioning properly  
**用法提示**：當 failure 是系統、機器、溝通、談判或關係失靈時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：system breakdown; communication breakdown; mechanical breakdown; breakdown in negotiations; breakdown of trust.  
**常見搭配對象**：system, machine, communication, negotiation, relationship.  
**與 failure 的辨析**：Breakdown 強調運作停止或關係崩解；failure 更廣。  
**使用限制**：單純比賽落敗不叫 breakdown。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
A communication breakdown caused confusion between the two teams.  
溝通失靈導致兩個團隊之間出現混亂。

### 05 Collapse

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：崩潰；垮掉；失敗  
**語域**：neutral / strong  
**核心語義**：a sudden or complete failure of a system, organisation, plan, structure, or market  
**用法提示**：當 failure 是快速、嚴重、近乎完全的垮掉時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：economic collapse; collapse of negotiations; market collapse; organisational collapse; plan collapsed.  
**常見搭配對象**：economy, system, negotiation, market, organisation.  
**與 failure 的辨析**：Collapse 比 failure 更強，通常帶突然、嚴重、整體崩解感。  
**使用限制**：小型或局部失敗不宜用 collapse。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The negotiations ended in complete collapse.  
談判最終完全破裂。

## Tier 2 — Outcome / public / attempt variants · 結果、公開失敗與未成功嘗試變體

### 06 Disappointment

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：令人失望的結果；失望  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：a result that fails to meet hopes or expectations and causes dissatisfaction  
**用法提示**：當 failure 的重點是結果不如預期、令人失望，而不一定完全沒有成果時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：major disappointment; bitter disappointment; disappointing result; end in disappointment; source of disappointment.  
**常見搭配對象**：result, performance, launch, season, outcome.  
**與 failure 的辨析**：Disappointment 聚焦期望落空；failure 聚焦目標未達。  
**使用限制**：技術故障或明確競賽敗北通常用 failure / breakdown / defeat。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The product launch was a disappointment despite strong publicity.  
儘管宣傳強勁，產品發布仍令人失望。

### 07 Flop

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：徹底失敗；不成功之作  
**語域**：informal  
**核心語義**：a conspicuous failure, especially a film, product, event, performance, or commercial venture  
**用法提示**：當 failure 是明顯、公開而且效果很差的商業／娛樂失敗時很地道。  
**常見搭配**：box-office flop; commercial flop; complete flop; product flop; event was a flop.  
**常見搭配對象**：film, product, show, launch, event.  
**與 failure 的辨析**：Flop 比 failure 更口語，也常指作品／產品／活動。  
**使用限制**：正式報告、事故分析或嚴重人命事件中不合適。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The film was a commercial flop despite its large budget.  
儘管製作預算龐大，這部電影仍然商業失敗。

### 08 Fiasco

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：慘敗；一團糟；災難性失敗  
**語域**：neutral / expressive  
**核心語義**：a complete and embarrassing failure, often involving confusion, poor planning, or public humiliation  
**用法提示**：當 failure 不只是沒成功，而且過程混亂、尷尬或公開出醜時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：complete fiasco; political fiasco; organisational fiasco; turn into a fiasco; public-relations fiasco.  
**常見搭配對象**：launch, event, operation, campaign, meeting.  
**與 failure 的辨析**：Fiasco 比 failure 更強，加入混亂和尷尬。  
**使用限制**：只是一個安靜、普通的失敗不宜用 fiasco。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The poorly organised launch turned into a fiasco.  
這場組織不善的發布活動變成一場慘敗。

### 09 Abortive attempt

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：未成功的嘗試；無功而返的嘗試  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：an attempt that fails before achieving its intended objective  
**用法提示**：當 failure 指某次具體嘗試沒有達成目的時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：abortive attempt; failed attempt; abortive effort; unsuccessful attempt; attempt to.  
**常見搭配對象**：reform, escape, negotiation, launch, rescue.  
**與 failure 的辨析**：Abortive attempt 只適合『嘗試』這個 sense；failure 更廣。  
**使用限制**：人物、機器、整個制度本身不能一概改成 abortive attempt。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The first reform effort was an abortive attempt to simplify the system.  
第一次改革是一次未能成功簡化制度的嘗試。

### 10 Non-performance

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：未履行；未達成；未能運作  
**語域**：formal / legal / business  
**核心語義**：failure to carry out an obligation, duty, contract, or expected function  
**用法提示**：當 failure 是未履行責任、未交付、未完成合約義務時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：non-performance of contract; non-performance of duties; contractual non-performance; failure to perform; non-performance penalty.  
**常見搭配對象**：contract, obligation, duty, service, requirement.  
**與 failure 的辨析**：Non-performance 是責任／合約型 failure；一般失敗不用。  
**使用限制**：日常語言較少用，主要是正式商業／法律語境。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The contract allows compensation for non-performance of key obligations.  
合約容許就未履行主要義務作出賠償。

## Tier 3 — Technical / administrative / conversational variants · 技術、行政與口語變體

### 11 Malfunction

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：故障；失靈  
**語域**：technical / neutral  
**核心語義**：failure of a machine, device, system, or component to operate correctly  
**用法提示**：當 failure 明確是技術設備或系統沒有正常運作時最精準。  
**常見搭配**：technical malfunction; equipment malfunction; system malfunction; malfunction warning; device malfunction.  
**常見搭配對象**：machine, device, sensor, system, component.  
**與 failure 的辨析**：Malfunction 是技術性故障；failure 可指完全失效或更廣泛結果。  
**使用限制**：計劃、考試、比賽失敗不能用 malfunction。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The shutdown was caused by a sensor malfunction.  
停機是由感應器故障造成。

### 12 Non-achievement

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：未達成；未實現  
**語域**：formal / administrative  
**核心語義**：failure to achieve a target, objective, standard, or intended outcome  
**用法提示**：當 failure 被寫在績效、目標、評估或行政文件中，重點是某個目標未達成時可用。  
**常見搭配**：non-achievement of targets; non-achievement of objectives; target non-achievement; reasons for non-achievement; performance target.  
**常見搭配對象**：target, objective, goal, standard, outcome.  
**與 failure 的辨析**：Non-achievement 非常正式、行政化；failure 更自然。  
**使用限制**：一般英語不必用這麼官樣的詞。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The report analysed the reasons for non-achievement of the annual target.  
報告分析年度目標未達成的原因。

### 13 Things not working out

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：事情沒有成功；事情不順利  
**語域**：informal / conversational  
**核心語義**：a situation in which plans, arrangements, or efforts do not succeed as hoped  
**用法提示**：當 failure 需要用較柔和、口語、非正式方式表達時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：things didn't work out; if things don't work out; plans not working out; relationship didn't work out; it didn't work out.  
**常見搭配對象**：plan, arrangement, relationship, attempt, situation.  
**與 failure 的辨析**：這是口語型柔化表達，比 failure 少責備感。  
**使用限制**：正式技術或法律文件不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The partnership ended after things did not work out as expected.  
合作關係因事情未如預期發展而結束。

## 04 False synonyms / failure traps · 容易誤選的詞與失敗陷阱

### 01 Mistake

**錯誤不等於 failure**  
mistake 是一個錯誤判斷或行動；一個 mistake 可以導致 failure，但本身不是整體失敗。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A mistake can cause failure, but it is not the same as failure.

### 02 Error

**錯誤／誤差不等於 failure**  
error 通常是錯誤、計算錯或系統錯誤；failure 是未能達成目標或停止正常運作。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
An error is a wrong result or action; failure is broader.

### 03 Problem

**問題不等於 failure**  
problem 是需要處理的困難；它未必令整件事失敗。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A problem may be solved before it causes failure.

### 04 Loss

**損失／落敗不等於所有 failure**  
loss 可以指金錢損失、死亡或比賽落敗；failure 的範圍和語義不同。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Loss and failure overlap only in some contexts.

### 05 Weakness

**弱點不等於 failure**  
weakness 是不足之處；可以有 weakness 但仍成功。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A weakness is a limitation, not necessarily a failure.

### 06 Defeat

**defeat 只在競爭語境接近 failure**  
defeat 需要對手或對抗；一般計劃、機器或制度失敗不能用 defeat。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Defeat is competitive; failure is broader.

### 07 Setback

**setback 通常比 failure 輕**  
setback 是暫時受挫，通常仍可繼續；failure 更可能代表未成功。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A setback delays progress; a failure may end the attempt.

### 08 Fail / failure

**詞性陷阱**  
fail 是動詞；failure 是名詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Fail is a verb; failure is a noun.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Setback · 挫折；倒退；阻礙

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The funding cut was a serious failure for the project, but work continued.

**中文翻譯**  
削減撥款對項目是一個嚴重挫折，但工作仍然繼續。

- A. setback
- B. defeat
- C. mistake
- D. error
- E. collapse
- F. loss

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**setback** · 挫折；倒退；阻礙

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The funding cut was a serious setback for the project, but work continued.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. setback ✓ — but work continued 明確表示不是最終失敗，而是進度受挫。**
- **B. defeat — defeat 需要競爭／對手。**
- **C. mistake — mistake 是錯誤行動，不是外部資金問題。**
- **D. error — error 是具體錯誤，不是整體進度倒退。**
- **E. collapse — collapse 會表示整個項目近乎垮掉，和 continued 矛盾。**
- **F. loss — loss 可指資金損失，但不能完整表達進度受阻。**

|---|---|---|

| 系統／溝通失靈 | **breakdown** | 是否是 communication / machine / negotiations？ |
| 徹底崩潰 | **collapse** | 是否 sudden / complete / system-wide？ |
| 期望落空 | **disappointment** | 是否主要是結果令人失望？ |
| 商業／娛樂失敗 | **flop** | 是否是 film / product / show / launch？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **failure vs setback**: setback 通常只是暫時受挫；failure 更可能表示最終未成功。
- **failure vs defeat**: defeat 需要競爭／對抗背景。
- **failure vs breakdown**: breakdown 強調運作或關係停止正常 functioning。
- **failure vs collapse**: collapse 更強、更突然、更全面。
- **failure vs disappointment**: disappointment 聚焦期望落空和感受。
- **failure vs flop**: flop 偏口語，常形容產品、電影、表演或活動。
- **failure vs fiasco**: fiasco 包含混亂、尷尬和公開失敗。
- **failure vs malfunction**: malfunction 是技術設備／系統失靈。
- **failure vs mistake**: mistake 是一個錯誤；不等於整體失敗。
- **failure vs problem**: problem 是困難，可以解決而不導致 failure。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & failure traps · 文法、搭配與失敗程度陷阱

- **failure** 可數：a failure / several failures；亦可作不可數概念：fear of failure。
- 動詞是 **fail**：The plan failed / She failed to qualify。
- 常見結構：**failure to + verb**：failure to comply / failure to deliver。
- **setback** 常和 suffer / experience 搭配：suffer a setback。
- **defeat** 常和 suffer / accept 搭配：suffer defeat / accept defeat。
- **breakdown in + process/relationship**：breakdown in communication / negotiations。
- **collapse of + system/market**：collapse of the market；動詞也可直接說 negotiations collapsed。
- **flop** 是較口語：The film was a flop。
- **fiasco** 是可數：a complete fiasco；語氣很強。
- **non-performance of + obligation/contract**：正式法律／商業用語。
- **malfunction** 可數：a sensor malfunction；動詞也可說 the device malfunctioned。
- **non-achievement of + target/objective** 很行政化，一般英語可改寫為 failure to meet the target。
- **failure ≠ mistake / error**：一個錯誤可能造成失敗，但不是同一概念。
