# DISCRIMINATION · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Discrimination

**「歧視／不平等待遇／不公平對待」，要先分清是內在偏見、決策偏差、實際差別待遇，還是長期排斥和邊緣化。**

本單元集中處理 **discrimination = unfair or unjust differential treatment of a person or group, often linked to a personal or group characteristic**。你提供的 **prejudice; unfair treatment** 都很重要，但層次不同：**prejudice** 主要是態度／成見；**unfair treatment** 則更接近實際行為與待遇。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且具教學價值的詞／表達，並把 **bias / unequal treatment / exclusion / marginalisation / adverse treatment / disparate treatment / denial of equal opportunity** 分開。較法律化的術語會清楚標記，避免把所有不公平都自動視為法律上的 discrimination。每個目標設 **3 道題**。

## Tier 1 — Core prejudice / unfair-treatment equivalents · 核心偏見與不公平待遇近義表達

### 01 Prejudice

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：偏見；成見  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：an unfair or unreasonable opinion or attitude about a person or group, often formed before knowing the facts  
**用法提示**：當 discrimination 的重點是內在態度／成見時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：racial prejudice; prejudice against; overcome prejudice; social prejudice; prejudice and discrimination.  
**常見搭配對象**：group, minority, community, person, identity.  
**與 discrimination 的辨析**：Prejudice 是內在態度；discrimination 更常是外在行為／待遇。  
**使用限制**：若已明確講招聘、服務、薪酬等實際待遇，unfair treatment / unequal treatment 更精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The campaign aims to reduce prejudice against people with disabilities.  
這項運動旨在減少對殘疾人士的偏見。

### 02 Unfair treatment

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：不公平待遇；不公正對待  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：treatment that is unjust, unreasonable, or not based on fair standards  
**用法提示**：和 discrimination 最透明、最實用的解釋性近義表達之一。  
**常見搭配**：unfair treatment at work; experience unfair treatment; complain about unfair treatment; protect against unfair treatment; unfair treatment of staff.  
**常見搭配對象**：employee, customer, student, applicant, group.  
**與 discrimination 的辨析**：Unfair treatment 很廣；discrimination 通常還涉及某個特徵／群體基礎上的差別對待。  
**使用限制**：所有不公平都不一定屬 discrimination。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Employees can report unfair treatment through the complaints procedure.  
員工可透過投訴程序舉報不公平待遇。

### 03 Unequal treatment

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：不平等待遇；差別待遇  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：treating comparable people or groups differently in ways that create unequal outcomes or opportunities  
**用法提示**：當 discrimination 的核心是同類情況的人受到不同待遇時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：unequal treatment of workers; unequal treatment under the rules; challenge unequal treatment; evidence of unequal treatment; unequal treatment between groups.  
**常見搭配對象**：workers, applicants, students, customers, groups.  
**與 discrimination 的辨析**：Unequal treatment 描述待遇差異；discrimination 通常還涉及不當理由或受保護特徵。  
**使用限制**：合理而有正當原因的不同處理，不一定是 discrimination。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The review found evidence of unequal treatment between applicants.  
檢討發現申請人之間存在不平等待遇。

### 04 Bias

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：偏見；偏向；偏差  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：a tendency or preference that unfairly influences judgment, decisions, or treatment  
**用法提示**：當 discrimination 源於決策、制度、評審或個人判斷中的偏向時很常用。  
**常見搭配**：unconscious bias; racial bias; bias in hiring; show bias; reduce bias.  
**常見搭配對象**：hiring, assessment, decision, algorithm, manager.  
**與 discrimination 的辨析**：Bias 是偏向／傾向，可以存在而尚未造成具體 discrimination。  
**使用限制**：若已有明確不公平待遇結果，只說 bias 可能過於籠統。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The organisation introduced training to reduce bias in recruitment.  
機構推出培訓，以減少招聘中的偏見。

### 05 Discriminatory treatment

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：歧視性待遇  
**語域**：formal / legal / professional  
**核心語義**：treatment that disadvantages someone because of a relevant personal or group characteristic  
**用法提示**：當需要把 discrimination 展開成歧視性待遇時最精準。  
**常見搭配**：discriminatory treatment; discriminatory practice; discriminatory policy; discriminatory conduct; prohibit discriminatory treatment.  
**常見搭配對象**：employee, applicant, tenant, customer, student.  
**與 discrimination 的辨析**：這是 discrimination 的直接行為型改寫。  
**使用限制**：若只談偏見想法而沒有待遇行為，prejudice / bias 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The policy prohibits discriminatory treatment in recruitment.  
政策禁止招聘過程中的歧視性待遇。

## Tier 2 — Differential / exclusion / adverse-treatment variants · 差別待遇、排斥與不利待遇變體

### 06 Differential treatment

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：差別待遇；不同處理  
**語域**：formal / analytical  
**核心語義**：treating people or groups differently from one another  
**用法提示**：在研究、政策、法律分析中可先中性描述不同待遇，再判斷是否合理或構成歧視。  
**常見搭配**：differential treatment of groups; differential treatment based on; evidence of differential treatment; justify differential treatment; differential treatment in practice.  
**常見搭配對象**：group, applicant, customer, employee, category.  
**與 discrimination 的辨析**：Differential treatment 本身可以中性；discrimination 通常已帶不公平／不當意味。  
**使用限制**：不要把所有 differential treatment 自動稱為 discrimination。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The study examined whether there was differential treatment of applicants by age.  
研究檢視申請人是否因年齡而受到不同待遇。

### 07 Inequitable treatment

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：不公正待遇；不公平處理  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：treatment that is unfair because benefits, burdens, opportunities, or standards are distributed unjustly  
**用法提示**：當 discrimination 被放在公平、公正、資源分配或制度結果框架下討論時很合適。  
**常見搭配**：inequitable treatment; inequitable access; inequitable outcomes; inequitable practice; address inequity.  
**常見搭配對象**：employee, patient, student, community, group.  
**與 discrimination 的辨析**：Inequitable 強調公平分配；discrimination 更常聚焦差別對待的基礎。  
**使用限制**：一般口語中 unfair treatment 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The report identified inequitable treatment in access to services.  
報告指出服務取得方面存在不公平待遇。

### 08 Exclusion

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：排斥；排除；拒於門外  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：the act or process of preventing someone from participating, entering, benefiting, or belonging  
**用法提示**：當 discrimination 的具體形式是把某人／群體排除在工作、活動、服務或機會之外時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：social exclusion; exclusion from employment; exclusion from services; systematic exclusion; prevent exclusion.  
**常見搭配對象**：employment, service, activity, group, opportunity.  
**與 discrimination 的辨析**：Exclusion 是 discrimination 的一種可能結果／形式；不是所有 discrimination 都是排除。  
**使用限制**：薪酬差異、侮辱性待遇等歧視不一定涉及 exclusion。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The rules led to the exclusion of some disabled applicants.  
這些規則導致部分殘疾申請人被排除在外。

### 09 Marginalisation

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：邊緣化；排斥至社會邊緣  
**語域**：formal / social sciences  
**核心語義**：the process of pushing a person or group to the edge of social, economic, or institutional participation  
**用法提示**：當 discrimination 長期、制度性地令某群體失去聲音、資源或參與機會時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：social marginalisation; marginalisation of minorities; economic marginalisation; risk of marginalisation; marginalised communities.  
**常見搭配對象**：minority, community, migrant group, disabled people, low-income group.  
**與 discrimination 的辨析**：Marginalisation 是較長期的社會結果；discrimination 可以是一宗具體行為。  
**使用限制**：單一次不公平決定未必足以稱為 marginalisation。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Repeated discrimination can contribute to the marginalisation of minority groups.  
反覆的歧視可令少數群體逐漸被邊緣化。

### 10 Adverse treatment

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：不利待遇；負面對待  
**語域**：formal / legal / HR  
**核心語義**：treatment that places someone in a worse position, often because of a particular action, status, or characteristic  
**用法提示**：法律、人力資源和投訴文本中常用來描述某人受到較差待遇。  
**常見搭配**：adverse treatment at work; suffer adverse treatment; adverse action; subject someone to adverse treatment; less favourable treatment.  
**常見搭配對象**：employee, complainant, applicant, worker, customer.  
**與 discrimination 的辨析**：Adverse treatment 強調待遇變差；是否構成 discrimination 要看原因和法律框架。  
**使用限制**：一般日常說法可用 unfair treatment；adverse treatment 較正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Staff should not face adverse treatment after making a complaint.  
員工作出投訴後不應受到不利待遇。

## Tier 3 — Legal / opportunity / justice variants · 法律、平等機會與公正變體

### 11 Disparate treatment

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：差別性不利待遇  
**語域**：legal / technical  
**核心語義**：intentional unequal treatment of comparable individuals or groups, especially in discrimination law  
**用法提示**：較技術性的法律表達，特別常見於美國反歧視法分析。  
**常見搭配**：disparate treatment claim; evidence of disparate treatment; intentional disparate treatment; disparate treatment analysis; similarly situated individuals.  
**常見搭配對象**：employee, applicant, claimant, comparator, protected group.  
**與 discrimination 的辨析**：比一般 discrimination 更技術化，通常涉及有意識的差別待遇和比較對象。  
**使用限制**：一般英語和香港日常政策寫作不必優先使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The complaint alleged disparate treatment in promotion decisions.  
投訴指稱晉升決定中存在差別性不利待遇。

### 12 Denial of equal opportunity

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：剝奪平等機會；拒絕給予平等機會  
**語域**：formal / policy  
**核心語義**：preventing someone from having the same fair chance to participate, compete, work, study, or access a benefit  
**用法提示**：當 discrimination 的核心結果是某人失去與他人相同的機會時非常清楚。  
**常見搭配**：denial of equal opportunity; equal opportunity in employment; equal access; deny someone an opportunity; barriers to opportunity.  
**常見搭配對象**：employment, promotion, education, housing, service.  
**與 discrimination 的辨析**：這是結果型改寫，聚焦機會被剝奪，而不是偏見態度本身。  
**使用限制**：侮辱、騷擾或單純偏見未必涉及 opportunity。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The rule resulted in a denial of equal opportunity for some applicants.  
這項規則令部分申請人失去平等機會。

### 13 Unjust treatment

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：不公正待遇；不義對待  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：treatment that violates fairness or justice  
**用法提示**：當 discrimination 需要用較一般的公平／正義語言重述時可用。  
**常見搭配**：unjust treatment; suffer unjust treatment; challenge unjust treatment; unjust practice; unjustly treated.  
**常見搭配對象**：worker, student, customer, group, individual.  
**與 discrimination 的辨析**：Unjust treatment 是廣義不公；discrimination 通常還需要特定差別基礎。  
**使用限制**：不是所有 unjust treatment 都屬 discrimination。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The complaint centred on unjust treatment of temporary staff.  
投訴主要涉及對臨時員工的不公正待遇。

## 04 False synonyms / discrimination traps · 容易誤選的詞與歧視陷阱

### 01 Harassment

**騷擾不等於所有 discrimination**  
harassment 是令人受辱、受威嚇或處於敵意環境的行為；它可屬歧視的一種形式，但範圍不同。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Harassment can be discriminatory, but it is a specific form of harmful conduct.

### 02 Stereotyping

**刻板印象不等於 discrimination**  
stereotyping 是把固定特徵套在某群體；它可導致歧視，但不等於實際差別待遇。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Stereotyping is a belief pattern; discrimination is treatment or conduct.

### 03 Racism

**種族主義不等於所有 discrimination**  
racism 只涉及種族／族裔層面；discrimination 可以基於性別、年齡、殘疾等多種因素。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Racism is narrower than discrimination.

### 04 Sexism

**性別歧視／性別偏見不等於所有 discrimination**  
sexism 聚焦性別；discrimination 的範圍更廣。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Sexism is one possible basis of discrimination, not a general synonym.

### 05 Bullying

**欺凌不等於 discrimination**  
bullying 是持續欺壓或傷害；可以沒有任何群體／身份基礎。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Bullying may be unfair and harmful without being discriminatory.

### 06 Favouritism

**偏袒不等於所有 discrimination**  
favouritism 是偏愛某人；它可能造成不公平，但不一定涉及受保護特徵或群體。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Favouritism can create unfairness without amounting to discrimination.

### 07 Inequality

**不平等不等於 discrimination**  
inequality 是結果或狀態；discrimination 是可能造成不平等的行為／制度機制。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Inequality is an outcome or condition; discrimination is a possible cause.

### 08 Discriminate / discrimination

**詞性陷阱**  
discriminate 是動詞；discrimination 是名詞。句法位置不同。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Discriminate is a verb; discrimination is a noun.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Prejudice · 偏見；成見

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The campaign aims to reduce discrimination in attitudes toward disabled people.

**中文翻譯**  
這項運動旨在減少對殘疾人士的偏見。

- A. prejudice
- B. harassment
- C. bullying
- D. inequality
- E. favouritism
- F. exclusion

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**prejudice** · 偏見；成見

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The campaign aims to reduce prejudice against people with disabilities.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. prejudice ✓ — attitudes toward a group 指內在成見／偏見，prejudice 最精準。**
- **B. harassment — harassment 是具體騷擾行為，不是單純態度。**
- **C. bullying — bullying 涉及欺壓行為，原句沒有持續欺負線索。**
- **D. inequality — inequality 是不平等狀態，不是人的態度。**
- **E. favouritism — favouritism 是偏愛某人，不是對殘疾人士的負面成見。**
- **F. exclusion — exclusion 是被排除在參與／機會之外，這句只談 attitudes。**

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Discrimination 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 負面成見／態度 | **prejudice** | 是在講 beliefs / attitudes 嗎？ |
| 廣義不公平待遇 | **unfair treatment** | 是否只是實際受到不公平對待？ |
| 同類人受到不同待遇 | **unequal treatment** | 是否有 comparable people treated differently？ |
| 決策／制度中的偏向 | **bias** | 是否是 hiring / rating / algorithm 的偏差？ |
| 正式歧視性待遇 | **discriminatory treatment** | 是否是 policy / HR / complaint formal context？ |
| 中性描述不同處理 | **differential treatment** | 是否尚未判斷差異是否合理？ |
| 公平分配失衡 | **inequitable treatment** | 是否是 access / distribution / equity 問題？ |
| 被拒參與／被排除 | **exclusion** | 是否是 kept out / prevented from participating？ |
| 長期被推向社會邊緣 | **marginalisation** | 是否是 repeated / structural social effect？ |
| 受到較差待遇 | **adverse treatment** | 是否是 employee/complainant 被置於較差位置？ |
| 技術法律差別待遇 | **disparate treatment** | 是否是 intentional + comparator legal analysis？ |
| 失去同等機會 | **denial of equal opportunity** | 是否是 employment / promotion / education access？ |
| 一般不公正待遇 | **unjust treatment** | 是否要用公平／正義語言概括？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **discrimination vs prejudice**: prejudice 是態度；discrimination 更常是實際行為／待遇。
- **discrimination vs bias**: bias 是偏向，可存在而尚未形成明確歧視待遇。
- **discrimination vs unequal treatment**: unequal treatment 描述差異；discrimination 通常還暗示差異不當。
- **discrimination vs differential treatment**: differential treatment 可保持中性，之後才判斷是否合理。
- **discrimination vs harassment**: harassment 是具體敵意／侮辱行為，只是歧視的一種可能形式。
- **discrimination vs stereotyping**: stereotyping 是固定印象，不等於待遇行為。
- **discrimination vs racism**: racism 是更窄的種族層面。
- **discrimination vs inequality**: inequality 是結果／狀態；discrimination 是可能成因。
- **discrimination vs exclusion**: exclusion 是被排除，是歧視可能採取的一種形式。
- **discrimination vs marginalisation**: marginalisation 是長期社會結果，比單一歧視事件更宏觀。

## 08 Grammar, terminology & discrimination traps · 文法、術語與歧視陷阱

- **discrimination against + person/group**：discrimination against disabled people。
- **discrimination based on + characteristic**：discrimination based on age / sex / disability。
- **discrimination in + area/process**：discrimination in recruitment / housing / education。
- 動詞是 **discriminate**：discriminate against someone；名詞是 **discrimination**。
- **prejudice against + group/person**：prejudice against migrants。
- **bias in + process**：bias in recruitment / assessment；亦可說 bias against / in favour of。
- **unequal / differential treatment of + group**：unequal treatment of workers。
- **exclusion from + opportunity/activity**：exclusion from employment / training。
- **marginalisation of + group**：the marginalisation of minority communities。
- **adverse treatment** 和 **disparate treatment** 都較正式；後者尤其偏法律技術語境。
- **inequality ≠ discrimination**：統計上有差距，不足以單靠字面證明有歧視行為。
- **harassment / bullying / stereotyping** 各自有獨立意思，不應機械當成 discrimination 的同義詞。
- 在法律語境中，何種行為構成 unlawful discrimination 取決於具體司法管轄區和法律定義；本課聚焦英語語義辨析。
