# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Courtesy classes

**禮儀課、禮貌訓練、禮貌課、社交行為課——究竟是哪一種 courtesy classes？**

本課聚焦：

**courtesy classes = lessons or training designed to teach polite behaviour, good manners, etiquette, respectful interaction, and appropriate social conduct**

例如：

- courtesy classes for children
- courtesy training for staff
- etiquette lessons before formal events
- manners classes at school

核心近義與近義表達：

**etiquette lessons; politeness training; manners classes; courtesy training; social etiquette training; good-manners lessons; civility training; courtesy and manners training; social conduct lessons; etiquette training**

> **Precision rule:**  
> `etiquette lessons / etiquette training` 最接近正式禮儀。  
> `politeness training / manners classes` 更偏日常禮貌。  
> `civility training` 更偏正式、職場尊重。  
> `social conduct lessons` 稍廣，包含得體行為規範。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Etiquette lessons

**禮儀課；禮節課程**

和 `courtesy classes` 最直接的近義表達之一。重點是教授社交禮儀、正式場合規矩、待人接物方式等。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The school offers etiquette lessons for younger students.

學校為年幼學生提供禮儀課。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- etiquette lessons
- social etiquette lessons
- formal etiquette training
- etiquette class

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是在教授社交場合的禮節與規矩？

---

## 02 Politeness training

**禮貌訓練；有禮行為訓練**

當 `courtesy classes` 主要教授說話、回應、請求、道謝、道歉等有禮行為時，`politeness training` 很貼切。比 etiquette 更聚焦日常禮貌。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Politeness training helped students communicate more respectfully.

禮貌訓練幫助學生更尊重地溝通。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- politeness training
- polite behaviour training
- communication and politeness training
- train in politeness

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否主要是訓練學生如何有禮貌地說話和行事？

---

## 03 Manners classes

**禮貌課；禮儀課**

非常自然、日常的 near-synonym，尤其適合兒童與學校語境。`manners` 比 etiquette 更生活化，常包括說話禮貌、餐桌禮儀和尊重他人。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The children took manners classes during the summer programme.

孩子們在暑期課程中上禮貌課。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- manners classes
- good-manners class
- table-manners class
- manners training

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是較日常、兒童導向地教授好禮貌？

---

## 04 Courtesy training

**禮貌訓練；禮儀訓練**

直接把 `classes` 改成 `training`，保留 courtesy 的核心意思。比 `courtesy classes` 稍偏技能訓練，可用於學校、服務業和職場。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

New staff receive courtesy training before meeting customers.

新員工在接觸顧客前會接受禮貌訓練。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- courtesy training
- customer courtesy training
- staff courtesy training
- professional courtesy training

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是把禮貌視為需要訓練和實踐的技能？

---

## 05 Social etiquette training

**社交禮儀訓練**

比一般 etiquette lessons 更明確聚焦社交互動，例如介紹自己、打招呼、餐桌禮節、正式場合應對等。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The programme includes social etiquette training for formal events.

該計劃包括正式場合的社交禮儀訓練。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- social etiquette training
- formal social etiquette
- etiquette and protocol training
- social-skills etiquette

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是正式社交場合的禮儀與互動規範？

---

## 06 Good-manners lessons

**良好禮貌課；好習慣禮儀課**

很自然的解釋性 paraphrase，尤其適合兒童教育。重點是建立良好日常行為與待人態度。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Good-manners lessons are built into the primary-school programme.

小學課程中加入了良好禮貌課。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- good-manners lessons
- good-manners training
- teach good manners
- good-manners programme

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是兒童／基礎教育中教授日常好禮貌？

---

## 07 Civility training

**文明禮貌訓練；得體交往訓練**

較正式，強調尊重、克制、禮貌和文明互動。比 `manners classes` 更適合職場、公共服務或成人專業語境。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The organisation introduced civility training after several workplace complaints.

機構在收到多宗職場投訴後推出文明禮貌訓練。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- workplace civility training
- civility programme
- professional civility
- civility and respect training

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是較正式地訓練尊重和文明互動，尤其在職場？

---

## 08 Courtesy and manners training

**禮貌與禮儀訓練**

透明、範圍較完整的 paraphrase，同時涵蓋 polite behaviour 和 social manners。適合課程名稱、機構培訓或 machine-oriented 標籤。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The centre runs courtesy and manners training for children.

中心為兒童提供禮貌與禮儀訓練。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- courtesy and manners training
- manners and etiquette training
- politeness and manners programme
- courtesy-skills training

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否需要同時涵蓋禮貌態度與具體禮儀規範？

---

## 09 Social conduct lessons

**社交行為課；得體行為課**

較正式、較制度化的 near-synonym，重點是教授在公共或社交場合應如何表現得體。比 etiquette 稍廣，可包含尊重、行為規範和互動方式。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Social conduct lessons formed part of the orientation programme.

社交行為課是迎新課程的一部分。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- social conduct lessons
- standards of conduct
- appropriate social conduct
- conduct training

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是教授公共／社交場合的得體行為規範？

---

## 10 Etiquette training

**禮儀訓練；禮節培訓**

比 `etiquette lessons` 更偏技能培訓，可用於兒童、成人、服務業、外交或商務場合。屬非常高價值的廣泛近義表達。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Staff received etiquette training before the international conference.

員工在國際會議前接受禮儀培訓。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- etiquette training
- business etiquette training
- dining etiquette training
- professional etiquette training

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是較正式或實務導向的禮儀技能培訓？

# 02 False synonyms

以下詞語和禮貌／行為教育有關，但在目的和範圍上並不等同 **courtesy classes**。

## 01 Discipline classes

**紀律課不等於禮貌課**

`discipline` 偏守規則、服從和行為管理；courtesy classes 主要教禮貌和禮儀。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Discipline addresses rule-breaking; courtesy classes teach polite behaviour.

---

## 02 Social skills training

**社交技巧範圍更廣**

`social skills` 可包括對話、眼神接觸、合作、衝突處理等，範圍比 courtesy/etiquette 更廣。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Etiquette is one part of broader social-skills training.

---

## 03 Customer-service training

**客服培訓不等於禮儀課**

`customer-service training` 可包含禮貌，但還涉及產品知識、流程、投訴處理等。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Courtesy is one component of customer-service training.

---

## 04 Character education

**品格教育範圍更廣**

`character education` 涵蓋責任、誠信、同理心、公民意識等，不只 manners。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Good manners may be taught within a broader character-education programme.

---

## 05 Behaviour management

**行為管理偏控制／矯正**

`behaviour management` 是管理或改變行為的方法，不是教授禮儀本身。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Behaviour management may enforce rules; etiquette lessons teach conventions.

---

## 06 Protocol training

**禮賓／官方程序更專門**

`protocol` 特別適合外交、官方、正式儀式程序，範圍比一般 courtesy 更窄更正式。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Diplomatic protocol goes beyond everyday politeness.

---

## 07 Public-speaking training

**演說訓練目的不同**

它主要教授演講、聲線、結構和台風，不是禮貌規範。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A polite speaker may still need separate public-speaking training.

---

## 08 Life-skills classes

**生活技能課範圍太廣**

`life skills` 可包括理財、溝通、自理、解難等，courtesy 只是其中可能的一小部分。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Manners may appear in life-skills classes, but the terms are not equivalent.

---

# 03 練習與解釋

> **每個核心近義表達 3 題，共 30 題。**
> 每題盡量保留原句句法，只在目標表達需要不同結構時作必要改寫。
> Distractors 優先使用 discipline / social skills / customer service / behaviour / public speaking 等 plausible near-misses。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Courtesy vs politeness

- **courtesy** = 有禮、尊重他人的行為，稍正式
- **politeness** = 日常禮貌語言／行為

---

## 02 Courtesy vs etiquette

- **courtesy** = 態度與待人方式
- **etiquette** = 特定場合的禮儀規範

例如：
- saying thank you = courtesy/politeness
- knowing formal dining rules = etiquette

---

## 03 Manners

常見：
- good manners
- bad manners
- table manners
- mind your manners

`manners classes` 很適合兒童和學校語境。

---

## 04 Civility

`civility` 比 politeness 更正式，常見於：

- workplace civility
- respectful workplace conduct
- public discourse
- professional behaviour

---

## 05 Lessons vs training

- **lessons/classes** = 課堂式教學
- **training** = 技能型、實務型、職場型

所以：
- etiquette lessons
- etiquette training

都可，但語域略有不同。

---

## 06 Courtesy classes is not discipline

Courtesy classes 教：
- please / thank you
- greetings
- respectful language
- table manners
- introductions
- appropriate social behaviour

Discipline programmes 管：
- rule-breaking
- sanctions
- compliance
- consequences
