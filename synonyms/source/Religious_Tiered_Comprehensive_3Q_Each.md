# RELIGIOUS · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Religious

**「宗教的／與宗教有關的」看似簡單，其實要分清 institutional religion、personal spirituality、worship/devotion、sacred status、denominational affiliation 等不同語境。**

本單元集中處理 **religious = connected with religion, religious belief, worship, religious institutions, or religious identity**。你提供的 **faith-based; spiritual; relating to religion** 分別代表 **信仰導向機構、靈性／內在信仰、透明一般改寫** 三個核心方向。

本次保留 **10 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達。會特別標明哪些詞只適合機構、宗派、教會制度、敬拜或神聖性，避免把 spiritual / sacred / faith-based 當作所有 religious 的全面同義詞。共 **30 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Religious 的 sense | 核心問題 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|---|
| 一般與宗教有關 | 是否只是 broadly connected with religion？ | relating to religion, faith-related |
| 宗教背景機構／服務 | 是否 organisation/school/charity rooted in faith？ | faith-based, religiously affiliated |
| 個人靈性／內在信仰 | 是否 prayer/reflection/inner life？ | spiritual |
| 敬拜／虔誠活動 | 是否 prayer/worship/reading/music？ | devotional |
| 神聖地位 | 是否 holy/revered object/place/text？ | sacred |
| 宗派／教會制度 | 是否 denomination / church law / church authority？ | denominational, ecclesiastical |

## Tier 1 — Core faith / spirituality / general-religion equivalents · 核心信仰、靈性與一般宗教近義詞

### 01 Faith-based

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：以宗教信仰為基礎的；信仰導向的  
**語域**：neutral / public-service / organisational  
**核心語義**：connected with or organised around a particular religious faith or belief system  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，尤其適合 organisation, school, charity, service, initiative 等制度／機構語境。  
**常見搭配**：faith-based organisation; faith-based school; faith-based charity; faith-based service; faith-based initiative.  
**常見搭配對象**：organisation, school, charity, service, programme.  
**與 religious 的辨析**：Faith-based 比 religious 更常修飾機構、服務或計劃，強調它以某種信仰為基礎。  
**使用限制**：個人的祈禱、儀式或教義不一定自然用 faith-based。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The charity works with several faith-based organisations.  
這間慈善機構與多個宗教信仰團體合作。

### 02 Spiritual

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：靈性的；精神上的；與信仰體驗有關的  
**語域**：neutral / broad  
**核心語義**：connected with the human spirit, inner life, or a sense of the sacred, sometimes but not always within organised religion  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，當 religious 實際在說祈禱、內在信仰、神聖感或精神生活時可以成立。  
**常見搭配**：spiritual life; spiritual practice; spiritual experience; spiritual guidance; spiritual wellbeing.  
**常見搭配對象**：life, practice, experience, guidance, wellbeing.  
**與 religious 的辨析**：Spiritual 比 religious 更廣，不一定屬於正式宗教制度。  
**使用限制**：宗教法律、教派、正式儀式等通常不能一律改成 spiritual。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Meditation can be part of a person's spiritual practice.  
冥想可以是個人靈性修習的一部分。

### 03 Relating to religion

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：與宗教有關的  
**語域**：neutral / explanatory  
**核心語義**：connected with religion, religious belief, worship, institutions, or practice  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是最透明、最安全的描述性改寫，特別適合教學或需要避免詞義歧義時。  
**常見搭配**：matters relating to religion; issues relating to religion; practices relating to religion; laws relating to religion.  
**常見搭配對象**：issue, practice, institution, law, belief.  
**與 religious 的辨析**：語義非常接近 religious，但更冗長、解釋性更強。  
**使用限制**：自然句子中通常優先直接用 religious。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The course examines laws relating to religion and public life.  
課程探討與宗教及公共生活有關的法律。

### 04 Faith-related

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：與信仰有關的  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：connected with religious faith, belief, worship, or faith communities  
**用法提示**：適合政策、社區、教育或研究語境，用作中性 descriptive compound。  
**常見搭配**：faith-related issue; faith-related activity; faith-related identity; faith-related service; faith-related concern.  
**常見搭配對象**：issue, identity, service, activity, community.  
**與 religious 的辨析**：Faith-related 比 religious 更像描述性分類標籤，語氣較中性。  
**使用限制**：正式教義、經典或宗教儀式常直接用 religious 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The survey included questions about faith-related identity.  
調查包括有關宗教信仰身份的問題。

### 05 Devotional

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：敬拜的；祈禱性的；宗教修持的  
**語域**：religious / formal  
**核心語義**：connected with prayer, worship, or acts expressing religious devotion  
**用法提示**：當 religious 的核心是 prayer, worship, hymn, reading 等虔敬活動時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：devotional practice; devotional reading; devotional music; devotional text; devotional exercise.  
**常見搭配對象**：prayer, reading, music, practice, text.  
**與 religious 的辨析**：Devotional 是 religious 的窄義詞，專指敬拜／虔誠修持。  
**使用限制**：宗教制度、法律、身份或建築不能全面用 devotional。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The group meets weekly for devotional reading and prayer.  
小組每週聚會進行宗教讀經和祈禱。

## Tier 2 — Sacred / church / denomination / affiliation variants · 神聖、教會、宗派與隸屬變體

### 06 Sacred

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：神聖的；宗教上受尊崇的  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：regarded as holy, spiritually important, or deserving special religious respect  
**用法提示**：當 religious 表示『神聖／受敬奉』的場所、文本、物件或儀式時可用。  
**常見搭配**：sacred text; sacred place; sacred site; sacred object; sacred ritual.  
**常見搭配對象**：text, place, site, object, ritual.  
**與 religious 的辨析**：Sacred 強調 holiness / special religious status，而不是一般『與宗教有關』。  
**使用限制**：宗教組織、宗教政策等不能用 sacred。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Pilgrims travelled to the sacred site during the festival.  
朝聖者在節日期間前往該神聖地點。

### 07 Ecclesiastical

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：教會的；教會制度的  
**語域**：formal / Christian / institutional  
**核心語義**：connected with the Christian Church, especially its organisation, offices, law, or administration  
**用法提示**：當 religious 實際指教會制度、職位、建築管理或教會法律時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：ecclesiastical law; ecclesiastical authority; ecclesiastical office; ecclesiastical court; ecclesiastical history.  
**常見搭配對象**：law, authority, office, court, institution.  
**與 religious 的辨析**：Ecclesiastical 比 religious 更窄，而且主要屬基督宗教教會制度語境。  
**使用限制**：不能用於所有宗教傳統或一般個人信仰。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The dispute was referred to an ecclesiastical court.  
這宗爭議被轉交教會法庭處理。

### 08 Denominational

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：宗派的；教派的  
**語域**：formal / religious / education  
**核心語義**：connected with a particular religious denomination or branch  
**用法提示**：當 religious 指某一宗派／教派辦學、身份、隸屬或制度時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：denominational school; denominational affiliation; denominational identity; denominational differences; denominational education.  
**常見搭配對象**：school, affiliation, identity, institution, group.  
**與 religious 的辨析**：Denominational 強調 particular branch/tradition，而不是一般宗教性。  
**使用限制**：不涉及宗派分類時不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The area has several denominational schools.  
該區有數間宗派學校。

### 09 Religiously affiliated

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：有宗教隸屬的；與宗教團體有關聯的  
**語域**：formal / institutional  
**核心語義**：formally connected or affiliated with a religious body, tradition, or institution  
**用法提示**：適合學校、醫院、慈善機構、大學等有正式宗教背景或隸屬關係的機構。  
**常見搭配**：religiously affiliated school; affiliated institution; religious affiliation; faith affiliation.  
**常見搭配對象**：school, hospital, university, charity, institution.  
**與 religious 的辨析**：比 religious 更精確表示 institutional affiliation，而非單純有宗教特色。  
**使用限制**：個人信仰、儀式或神聖物件不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The hospital is religiously affiliated but serves patients of all backgrounds.  
這間醫院有宗教隸屬背景，但服務不同背景的病人。

## Tier 3 — Formal descriptive paraphrase · 正式描述性改寫

### 10 Of a religious nature

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：具有宗教性質的；宗教性的  
**語域**：formal / legal / descriptive  
**核心語義**：having a religious character, purpose, or content  
**用法提示**：當需要正式、法律或行政上描述 activity/content/event 的性質時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：activity of a religious nature; event of a religious nature; content of a religious nature; ceremony of a religious nature.  
**常見搭配對象**：activity, event, ceremony, content, expression.  
**與 religious 的辨析**：這是 formal paraphrase，不是日常單字同義詞。  
**使用限制**：普通口語中顯得冗長。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The policy covers activities of a religious nature held on school premises.  
政策涵蓋在校園內舉行的宗教性質活動。

## 04 False synonyms / religious traps · 容易誤選的宗教、文化與靈性陷阱

### 01 Moral

**道德的 ≠ religious**  
moral 涉及對錯與倫理判斷，不一定與宗教有關。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Moral and religious are separate concepts.

### 02 Cultural

**文化的 ≠ religious**  
宗教可以是文化的一部分，但 cultural 的範圍更廣。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Cultural is broader and not necessarily religious.

### 03 Traditional

**傳統的 ≠ religious**  
傳統習俗可能是世俗的，不一定源自宗教。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Traditional does not imply religious.

### 04 Spiritual

**只在靈性／內在信仰 sense 接近**  
spiritual 可不屬任何正式宗教。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Spiritual is broader than organised religion.

### 05 Sacred

**只在神聖 sense 接近**  
sacred 表示受宗教尊崇，不等於所有 religious。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Sacred is holiness-focused.

### 06 Ceremonial

**儀式性的 ≠ religious**  
ceremonial 可完全世俗，例如畢業典禮。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Ceremonial does not necessarily mean religious.

### 07 Faith-based

**只在機構／服務／計劃 sense 特別自然**  
faith-based 不適合所有 religious 搭配。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Faith-based is organisation/programme oriented.

### 08 Religion / religious

**詞性陷阱**  
religion 是名詞；religious 是形容詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check word class before replacing.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Faith-based · 以宗教信仰為基礎的；信仰導向的

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The council works with several religious charities that provide food support.

**中文翻譯**  
市議會與數間提供食物支援的宗教慈善機構合作。

- A. Faith-based
- B. Religiously affiliated
- C. Faith-related
- D. Cultural
- E. Traditional
- F. Ceremonial

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Faith-based** · 以宗教信仰為基礎的；信仰導向的

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The council works with several faith-based charities that provide food support.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Faith-based ✓** — means organised around or rooted in a religious faith; it is especially natural for organisations, schools, charities and services. In this sentence, charities organised around a religious faith are naturally described as faith-based. That makes **Faith-based** the most precise replacement for this particular sense of *religious*.
- **B.** **Religiously affiliated** — means formally connected with a religious body or tradition at the institutional level. It is related to belief, culture, ritual or institutional religion, but it misses the decisive clue here: charities organised around a religious faith are naturally described as faith-based.
- **C.** **Faith-related** — is a neutral descriptive label for matters connected with faith, often in policy, research or service contexts. It is related to belief, culture, ritual or institutional religion, but it misses the decisive clue here: charities organised around a religious faith are naturally described as faith-based.
- **D.** **Cultural** — means connected with culture and can include many non-religious practices. It is related to belief, culture, ritual or institutional religion, but it misses the decisive clue here: charities organised around a religious faith are naturally described as faith-based.
- **E.** **Traditional** — means established by custom or long use and does not imply religion. It is related to belief, culture, ritual or institutional religion, but it misses the decisive clue here: charities organised around a religious faith are naturally described as faith-based.
- **F.** **Ceremonial** — means relating to formal ceremony and can be entirely secular. It is related to belief, culture, ritual or institutional religion, but it misses the decisive clue here: charities organised around a religious faith are naturally described as faith-based.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **religious vs faith-based**: faith-based 特別自然於機構、服務、學校和計劃。
- **religious vs spiritual**: spiritual 更廣，可以不屬於正式宗教制度。
- **religious vs devotional**: devotional 只限虔誠修持／敬拜相關。
- **religious vs sacred**: sacred 強調神聖／受敬奉地位。
- **religious vs ecclesiastical**: ecclesiastical 主要是基督宗教教會制度用語。
- **religious vs denominational**: denominational 強調某一宗派／教派。
- **religious vs religiously affiliated**: 後者強調機構上的正式隸屬。
- **religious vs cultural**: 文化可以包含宗教，但 cultural 不等於 religious。
- **religious vs moral**: moral 關於倫理對錯，不必有宗教成分。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & polysemy traps · 文法、搭配與『宗教的』陷阱

- **religious + noun**：religious belief / practice / organisation / service。
- **faith-based + organisation/school/charity/programme**：機構與服務語境特別自然。
- **spiritual + life/practice/wellbeing/guidance**：不一定等於 organised religion。
- **devotional + reading/music/practice**：祈禱、敬拜和虔誠修持。
- **sacred + text/site/object/ritual**：神聖地位。
- **ecclesiastical + law/authority/office/court**：教會制度。
- **denominational + school/affiliation/identity**：宗派歸屬。
- **religiously affiliated + institution**：正式宗教隸屬。
- **of a religious nature**：法律／行政正式分類。
- **religion** 是名詞；**religious** 是形容詞；**religiously** 是副詞。
- 不要把 **moral, cultural, traditional, ceremonial** 當作 religious 的全面同義詞。
- 英式／香港拼寫保持 **organisation, programme**。
