# INEQUALITY · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Inequality

**「不平等／差距／失衡」，要先分清是在描述可量度的差距、比例失衡、不平等待遇、資源分配不均，還是帶明確不公平判斷的 inequity。**

本單元集中處理 **inequality = a state in which people, groups, regions, or outcomes are not equal in resources, opportunities, treatment, power, or results**。你提供的 **disparity; imbalance; unequal treatment; unequal distribution** 分別代表 **差距、失衡、待遇不平等、分配不均** 四個核心方向。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且高實用度的詞／表達，共 **39 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。同時清楚區分 **inequality / disparity / inequity / unequal access / social divide / structural inequality / unequal outcomes**，避免把所有差異都當成同一種不平等。

## Tier 1 — Core disparity / imbalance / unequal-treatment equivalents · 核心差距、失衡與不平等待遇近義詞

### 01 Disparity

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：差距；不均等  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：a noticeable difference or gap between people, groups, regions, or outcomes, especially when the difference seems unfair or significant  
**用法提示**：和 inequality 最核心的近義詞之一，特別適合收入、健康、教育、地區和社會結果差距。  
**常見搭配**：income disparity; health disparity; regional disparity; gender disparity; wide disparity.  
**常見搭配對象**：income, health, region, gender, outcome.  
**與 inequality 的辨析**：Disparity 強調『可觀察到的差距』；inequality 更明確帶不平等／不均等概念。  
**使用限制**：若是待遇不公或歧視行為，unequal treatment / discrimination 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The report highlights large regional disparities in access to healthcare.  
報告指出不同地區在醫療服務使用方面存在巨大差距。

### 02 Imbalance

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：不平衡；失衡  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：a lack of balance between two or more parts, groups, quantities, or forces  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很自然，當 inequality 表現為比例、資源、權力或供求失衡時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：power imbalance; regional imbalance; gender imbalance; trade imbalance; resource imbalance.  
**常見搭配對象**：power, representation, resources, trade, population.  
**與 inequality 的辨析**：Imbalance 強調比例／分配不均；inequality 更常帶社會公平含義。  
**使用限制**：如果重點是法律上的不平等待遇，imbalance 會太抽象。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The policy aims to reduce the imbalance between urban and rural services.  
政策旨在減少城鄉服務之間的不平衡。

### 03 Unequal treatment

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：不平等待遇；差別對待  
**語域**：neutral / legal / social  
**核心語義**：treating people or groups differently in ways that are unfair or disadvantageous  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，當 inequality 直接體現在人受到不同待遇時最精準。  
**常見搭配**：unequal treatment; unfair treatment; unequal treatment at work; unequal treatment under the law; challenge unequal treatment.  
**常見搭配對象**：employee, group, citizen, applicant, patient.  
**與 inequality 的辨析**：Unequal treatment 是行為／制度做法；inequality 可以只是結果或分布上的不平等。  
**使用限制**：純收入差距未必涉及直接待遇不同。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The complaint concerned unequal treatment of part-time staff.  
投訴涉及對兼職員工的不平等待遇。

### 04 Unequal distribution

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：不均等分配；分配不均  
**語域**：neutral / economics / social science  
**核心語義**：a situation in which resources, income, wealth, opportunities, or benefits are distributed unevenly  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很適合財富、收入、資源、機會、服務等分配語境。  
**常見搭配**：unequal distribution of wealth; unequal distribution of resources; unequal distribution of income; uneven distribution; distributional inequality.  
**常見搭配對象**：wealth, income, resource, opportunity, benefit.  
**與 inequality 的辨析**：Unequal distribution 強調資源如何被分配；inequality 可更廣，包括待遇、權力、機會和結果。  
**使用限制**：不涉及分配的身份或法律不平等不宜用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The debate focuses on the unequal distribution of wealth.  
這場討論集中於財富分配不均。

### 05 Inequity

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：不公平；不公正的不平等  
**語域**：formal / social policy / health  
**核心語義**：an unfair and avoidable difference in treatment, opportunity, resources, or outcomes  
**用法提示**：當 inequality 不只是有差距，而且明確被判斷為不公平、可避免時很重要。  
**常見搭配**：health inequity; social inequity; racial inequity; structural inequity; address inequities.  
**常見搭配對象**：health, education, opportunity, treatment, access.  
**與 inequality 的辨析**：Inequity 帶明確『不公平』判斷；inequality 本身可以只是描述差異。  
**使用限制**：技術統計中若只描述差距而不作公平判斷，disparity / inequality 更中性。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The programme seeks to reduce inequities in access to care.  
該計劃旨在減少醫療服務使用方面的不公平差異。

## Tier 2 — Gap / access / divide variants · 差距、機會與社會分化變體

### 06 Gap

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：差距；落差  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：a measurable or noticeable difference between two groups, levels, outcomes, or conditions  
**用法提示**：當 inequality 要用更直接、較口語的方式表示差距時很常見。  
**常見搭配**：income gap; gender gap; wealth gap; achievement gap; pay gap.  
**常見搭配對象**：income, pay, wealth, achievement, access.  
**與 inequality 的辨析**：Gap 是簡潔描述差異的詞；inequality 更抽象、制度化。  
**使用限制**：若涉及複雜制度性不公，gap 可能過於簡化。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The gender pay gap has narrowed but has not disappeared.  
性別薪酬差距已收窄，但尚未消失。

### 07 Unevenness

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：不均；不平均  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：the state of being uneven, inconsistent, or irregular across places, groups, or outcomes  
**用法提示**：當 inequality 表現為服務、發展、質素、資源在不同地方不平均時可用。  
**常見搭配**：unevenness in development; unevenness of provision; regional unevenness; uneven distribution; uneven outcomes.  
**常見搭配對象**：development, provision, quality, access, growth.  
**與 inequality 的辨析**：Unevenness 比 inequality 更描述性，也較少帶公平判斷。  
**使用限制**：對人作不公平待遇時不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The study found considerable unevenness in service provision.  
研究發現服務供應存在相當程度的不均。

### 08 Disproportion

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：比例失衡；不成比例  
**語域**：formal / analytical  
**核心語義**：a lack of proportion between different groups, quantities, effects, or shares  
**用法提示**：當 inequality 是某組人承受／獲得的比例明顯過高或過低時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：disproportion in representation; disproportionate share; demographic disproportion; disproportion between; disproportion of.  
**常見搭配對象**：representation, burden, share, population, outcome.  
**與 inequality 的辨析**：Disproportion 聚焦比例；inequality 更廣。  
**使用限制**：若沒有可比較比例，這個詞未必適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
There is a clear disproportion between population size and political representation.  
人口規模與政治代表性之間存在明顯比例失衡。

### 09 Unequal access

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：不平等的獲取機會；使用機會不均  
**語域**：formal / policy  
**核心語義**：a situation in which different people or groups do not have the same opportunity to obtain services, resources, or opportunities  
**用法提示**：當 inequality 具體體現在教育、醫療、網絡、住房或公共服務取得機會時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：unequal access to healthcare; unequal access to education; unequal access to technology; unequal access to opportunities; access inequality.  
**常見搭配對象**：healthcare, education, technology, housing, opportunity.  
**與 inequality 的辨析**：Unequal access 是 inequality 的一個具體機會面向。  
**使用限制**：收入差距本身未必可直接叫 unequal access。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Rural communities still face unequal access to specialist healthcare.  
鄉郊社群仍面對專科醫療服務使用機會不均。

### 10 Social divide

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：社會分化；社會鴻溝  
**語域**：neutral / media / social science  
**核心語義**：a broad social separation between groups based on wealth, opportunity, class, geography, or other factors  
**用法提示**：當 inequality 已形成群體間明顯社會分隔時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：social divide; wealth divide; digital divide; class divide; widening divide.  
**常見搭配對象**：class, wealth, region, generation, digital access.  
**與 inequality 的辨析**：Social divide 強調群體分隔與斷層；inequality 可以只是差距。  
**使用限制**：小型統計差異不必說成 divide。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Rising housing costs have widened the social divide.  
房屋成本上升擴大了社會分化。

## Tier 3 — Socioeconomic / structural / outcome variants · 社會經濟、結構與結果型不平等

### 11 Socioeconomic inequality

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：社會經濟不平等  
**語域**：academic / policy  
**核心語義**：inequality associated with income, wealth, education, occupation, living conditions, and social position  
**用法提示**：當 inequality 涉及多個社會經濟維度而非單一收入指標時很重要。  
**常見搭配**：socioeconomic inequality; socioeconomic disparities; reduce socioeconomic inequality; socioeconomic status; structural inequality.  
**常見搭配對象**：income, education, health, occupation, living condition.  
**與 inequality 的辨析**：這是 inequality 的特定學術類型。  
**使用限制**：純法律程序上的不平等待遇未必屬 socioeconomic inequality。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The research examines how socioeconomic inequality affects health outcomes.  
研究探討社會經濟不平等如何影響健康結果。

### 12 Structural inequality

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：結構性不平等  
**語域**：academic / social policy  
**核心語義**：persistent inequality produced or maintained by social, economic, political, or institutional structures  
**用法提示**：當 inequality 不是單一事件，而是制度和結構長期造成時非常精準。  
**常見搭配**：structural inequality; structural disadvantage; institutional inequality; structural barriers; address structural inequality.  
**常見搭配對象**：institution, labour market, education, housing, society.  
**與 inequality 的辨析**：Structural inequality 強調制度性和持續性來源。  
**使用限制**：單次不公平事件不一定足以稱 structural inequality。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The report argues that structural inequality shapes access to housing.  
報告指出結構性不平等影響住房取得機會。

### 13 Unequal outcomes

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：不平等結果；結果差異  
**語域**：formal / policy / research  
**核心語義**：different outcomes experienced by groups, especially where one group consistently fares worse  
**用法提示**：當 inequality 具體體現在教育、健康、收入、就業等最後結果上時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：unequal outcomes; unequal health outcomes; unequal educational outcomes; outcome disparities; persistent outcomes.  
**常見搭配對象**：health, education, employment, income, justice.  
**與 inequality 的辨析**：Unequal outcomes 聚焦最終結果；inequality 亦可存在於機會、待遇和資源分配。  
**使用限制**：如果問題在起點／機會而非結果，unequal access / opportunity 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The policy has not yet eliminated unequal outcomes between regions.  
該政策尚未消除不同地區之間的不平等結果。

## 04 False synonyms / inequality traps · 容易誤選的詞與不平等概念陷阱

### 01 Difference

**差異不等於 inequality**  
difference 只表示不同，未必涉及資源、機會或待遇不均。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A difference is neutral; inequality is more specific.

### 02 Diversity

**多樣性不等於 inequality**  
diversity 是差異與多元，本身不表示不公平。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Diversity is not inequality.

### 03 Poverty

**貧窮不等於 inequality**  
poverty 指資源不足；社會可以貧窮但相對平等，也可以富裕但高度不平等。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Poverty and inequality are distinct concepts.

### 04 Discrimination

**歧視不等於所有 inequality**  
discrimination 是一種不平等待遇／行為機制；inequality 也可由其他因素造成。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Discrimination can cause inequality but is not the same thing.

### 05 Unfairness

**不公平不等於所有 inequality**  
unfairness 是價值判斷；inequality 可以只是描述可觀察差距。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Not every inequality is automatically labelled unfair.

### 06 Disparity

**disparity 與 inequality 接近但更偏『差距』**  
disparity 描述可觀察差異，未必明示不公平。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Disparity is descriptive and often narrower.

### 07 Inequity

**inequity 比 inequality 更明確帶不公平判斷**  
inequity 常指不公平且可避免的差異。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Inequity is explicitly normative.

### 08 Inequality / unequal

**詞性陷阱**  
inequality 是名詞；unequal 是形容詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Inequality is a noun; unequal is an adjective.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Disparity · 差距；不均等

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The report highlights regional inequality in access to healthcare.

**中文翻譯**  
報告指出不同地區在醫療服務使用方面存在差距。

- A. disparity
- B. diversity
- C. poverty
- D. discrimination necessarily
- E. unfairness only
- F. difference only

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**disparity** · 差距；不均等

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The report highlights regional disparities in access to healthcare.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. disparity ✓ — regional gaps in access are naturally described as disparities。**
- **B. diversity — diversity means variety, not unequal outcomes。**
- **C. poverty — poverty is resource deprivation, not the regional gap itself。**
- **D. discrimination necessarily — discrimination necessarily would require evidence of unequal treatment causing the gap。**
- **E. unfairness only — unfairness only is a moral judgement rather than a measured difference。**
- **F. difference only — difference only is too neutral and unspecific.**

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Inequality 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 可量度差距 | **disparity** | 是否是 income / health / regional gap？ |
| 比例／資源失衡 | **imbalance** | 是否是 power / representation / resources？ |
| 待遇不平等 | **unequal treatment** | 是否明確涉及 how people are treated？ |
| 分配不均 | **unequal distribution** | 是否是 wealth / resources / benefits distribution？ |
| 不公平且可避免 | **inequity** | 是否 explicitly unfair / unjust / avoidable？ |
| 簡潔差距 | **gap** | 是否是 pay / wealth / achievement gap？ |
| 空間／供應不均 | **unevenness** | 是否是 service / development uneven across places？ |
| 比例失衡 | **disproportion** | 是否 one group's share is out of proportion？ |
| 獲取機會不均 | **unequal access** | 是否 healthcare / education / technology access？ |
| 群體社會鴻溝 | **social divide** | 是否 groups are becoming separated socially/economically？ |
| 多維社會經濟不平等 | **socioeconomic inequality** | 是否 income + education + occupation 等一起出現？ |
| 制度長期造成 | **structural inequality** | 是否 persistent and embedded in institutions？ |
| 最終結果不平等 | **unequal outcomes** | 是否 survival / graduation / employment results？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **inequality vs disparity**: disparity 更偏描述可觀察差距；inequality 更抽象、更制度化。
- **inequality vs inequity**: inequity 明確表示差異是不公平、可避免的。
- **inequality vs imbalance**: imbalance 聚焦比例或構成失衡。
- **inequality vs unequal treatment**: unequal treatment 是行為／制度做法，不只是結果。
- **inequality vs unequal distribution**: unequal distribution 聚焦資源如何分配。
- **inequality vs gap**: gap 是最簡潔、較口語的差距說法。
- **inequality vs poverty**: poverty 是資源不足；inequality 是相對差距。
- **inequality vs discrimination**: discrimination 是可能造成 inequality 的機制之一。
- **inequality vs unequal access**: unequal access 聚焦機會和取得服務。
- **inequality vs unequal outcomes**: unequal outcomes 聚焦最後結果。

## 08 Grammar, terminology & inequality traps · 文法、搭配與不平等概念陷阱

- **inequality** 可數／不可數皆可：income inequality / inequalities in health。
- 常見搭配：**income inequality, wealth inequality, social inequality, gender inequality, educational inequality**。
- **disparity** 通常可數：a disparity / regional disparities。
- **gap** 常見固定搭配：gender pay gap / wealth gap / achievement gap。
- **inequity** 在公共健康、政策和社會公義語境很重要，通常帶明確不公平判斷。
- **unequal treatment / unequal access / unequal outcomes** 分別對應待遇、機會、結果三個不同層面。
- **structural inequality** 強調制度與結構長期造成的不平等。
- **socioeconomic inequality** 通常涵蓋收入、教育、職業、住房和生活條件。
- **discrimination ≠ inequality**：歧視可能導致不平等，但兩者不是同一概念。
- **poverty ≠ inequality**：社會可相對平等但普遍貧窮，也可富裕但差距很大。
- **difference / diversity ≠ inequality**：單純不同或多樣性本身不等於不平等。
- 形容詞是 **unequal**：unequal pay / unequal access；名詞是 **inequality**。
