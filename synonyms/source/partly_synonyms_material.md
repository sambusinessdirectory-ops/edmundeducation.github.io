# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Partly

**部分地、在某程度上、有點、某些方面、部分原因、並非完全——究竟是哪一種 partly？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**in part; partially; to some extent; somewhat; to a degree; incomplete; in some respects; partially because; not entirely**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **in part; partially; to some extent** 全部保留。  
> 本課特別區分 `partly` 的三種高價值功能：**部分完成／部分受影響、程度有限、某原因只是多個原因之一**。

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 In Part

**部分地；在某程度上**

最直接、最中性的近義表達之一。當 `partly` 表示某件事只有一部分成立、發生或造成時很自然，語氣較書面。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The delay was caused in part by bad weather.

延誤部分是由惡劣天氣造成。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- in part because

- due in part to

- caused in part by

- explain in part

## 02 Partially

**部分地；不完全地**

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The road was partially blocked after the accident.

意外後道路部分受阻。

## 03 To Some Extent

**在某程度上**

當 `partly` 修飾意見、判斷、原因或抽象性質，而不是具體物理部分時很自然。它強調『有一定程度，但不是完全』。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

I agree to some extent with your argument.

我在某程度上同意你的論點。

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 In part

**最直接、較書面**

常用於原因、責任、影響只有一部分。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The delay was caused in part by weather.

---

## 02 Partially

**較正式、技術**

特別自然於 blocked / healed / operational / completed。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The road was partially blocked.

---

## 03 To some extent

**抽象程度**

最自然於 agreement, justification, effectiveness 等抽象評價。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

I agree to some extent.

---

## 04 Somewhat

**程度副詞**

`somewhat` = 有點，不等於『其中一部分』。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The result was somewhat surprising.

---

## 05 In some respects

**方面 vs 程度**

表示『某些方面』，不是『某種程度』。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The models are similar in some respects.

---

## 06 Not entirely

**否定式改寫**

可把 `partly correct` 改成 `not entirely correct`，語氣常較委婉。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The claim is not entirely correct.

---

## 07 Partly / partial

**詞性陷阱**

`partly` 是副詞；`partial` 是形容詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The road is partly blocked; there is a partial blockage.

---

## 08 Partly / partially

**語體差異**

兩者很多時候可互換，但 `partially` 通常較正式／技術，`partly` 更日常。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The system is partially operational; I partly agree.

# 03 練習與解釋

> **每個核心近義表達 3 題，共 27 題。**
> 每題均以『部分性、程度、方面、完成度、因果範圍、否定整體』作辨析。


## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** in part · 部分地；在某程度上

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

The delay was partly caused by bad weather.

### 中文翻譯

延誤部分是由惡劣天氣造成。

### OPTIONS


- **A. in part**

- **B. partially**

- **C. to some extent**

- **D. solely**

- **E. entirely**

- **F. completely**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**in part · 部分地；在某程度上**

The delay was caused **in part** by bad weather.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. in part ✓** — `in part` = 部分地；一部分原因／效果。 **本句為何最合適：** bad weather is one contributing factor rather than the sole cause。

- **B. partially** — `partially` = 部分地；未完全地，較正式。 較正式，尤其自然於 blocked / operational / healed 等可量化狀態；口語立場表達可較生硬。 **本句線索：** bad weather is one contributing factor rather than the sole cause。

- **C. to some extent** — `to some extent` = 在某程度上，常用於抽象評價。 最適合 agreement / justified / effective 等抽象程度；物理上一半堵塞通常不用它。 **本句線索：** bad weather is one contributing factor rather than the sole cause。

- **D. solely** — `solely` = 僅僅；唯一地。 表示唯一，和 partly 的部分性相反。 **本句線索：** bad weather is one contributing factor rather than the sole cause。

- **E. entirely** — `entirely` = 完全地。 完全地，和 partly 相反。 **本句線索：** bad weather is one contributing factor rather than the sole cause。

- **F. completely** — `completely` = 完全地。 完全地，和 partly 相反。 **本句線索：** bad weather is one contributing factor rather than the sole cause。

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Partly vs In part
`in part` 最直接，常見於：
- due in part to
- caused in part by

## Partly vs Partially
`partially` 較正式、技術：
- partially blocked
- partially healed
- partially operational

## Partly vs To some extent
最適合抽象程度：
- agree to some extent
- justified to some extent

## Partly vs Somewhat
`somewhat` = 有點，不等於物理上的一部分。

## Partly vs In some respects
- to some extent = 程度
- in some respects = 方面

## Partly vs Not entirely
- partly correct
- not entirely correct

語義相近，但後者是委婉的否定式改寫。

## Partly because
用於多因一果：
- partly because of cost
- partly because demand fell

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Partly vs partial
- partly = adverb
- partial = adjective

例如：
- The road is partly blocked.
- There is a partial blockage.

## 02 Partly vs partially
很多語境可互換，但：
- **partly** 更日常
- **partially** 較正式／技術

## 03 Partly because
- partly because of cost
- partly because demand increased

表示其中一個原因，不是唯一原因。

## 04 To some extent
常見於：
- opinions
- judgements
- explanations
- effectiveness

## 05 Not entirely
常用來柔化批評：
- not entirely correct
- not entirely clear
- not entirely convincing
