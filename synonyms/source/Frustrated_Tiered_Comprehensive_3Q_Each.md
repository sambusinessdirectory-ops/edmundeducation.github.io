# FRUSTRATED · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Frustrated

**「挫敗的／惱火的／不耐煩的」，要先分清是因目標受阻、反覆問題而氣惱、等待太久、期望落空，還是已經灰心。**

本單元集中處理 **frustrated = feeling upset, annoyed, or discouraged because progress is blocked, a goal cannot be achieved, or a problem keeps recurring**。你提供的 **exasperated; irritated** 分別代表 **強烈不耐與氣惱**、以及 **較一般的被惹惱**。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且高實用度的詞／表達，共 **39 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。另外把『人的情緒』和『事情被阻礙』兩類 sense 清楚分開。

## Tier 1 — Core annoyance / frustration equivalents · 核心挫敗與惱怒近義詞

### 01 Exasperated

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：氣惱至極；惱怒不耐  
**語域**：neutral / strong  
**核心語義**：extremely annoyed and impatient because a problem has continued or repeated  
**用法提示**：反覆出錯、拖延或同一問題一再出現時，比 frustrated 更強。  
**常見搭配**：exasperated by delays; exasperated sigh; increasingly exasperated; exasperated response.  
**常見搭配對象**：delay, repeated mistake, bureaucracy, behaviour.  
**與 frustrated 的辨析**：比 frustrated 更強，帶失去耐性的感覺。  
**使用限制**：若只是受阻而未明顯惱怒，frustrated 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She became exasperated by the repeated delays.  
她因一再延誤而感到十分氣惱。

### 02 Irritated

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：惱火的；被惹惱的  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：slightly to moderately annoyed, especially by something unpleasant or repetitive  
**用法提示**：當 frustrated 較輕，偏被噪音、打擾或小麻煩惹煩時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：irritated by noise; visibly irritated; irritated tone; become irritated.  
**常見搭配對象**：noise, interruption, delay, comment.  
**與 frustrated 的辨析**：更像『被煩到』，不一定涉及目標受阻。  
**使用限制**：若重點是努力卻做不到，frustrated 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He was irritated by the constant interruptions.  
他被不斷的打斷弄得很惱火。

### 03 Annoyed

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：生氣的；煩躁的；惱怒的  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：slightly angry or bothered by something unpleasant  
**用法提示**：最常見的日常近義詞之一，語氣通常比 exasperated 輕。  
**常見搭配**：annoyed with someone; annoyed about something; annoyed by delays; get annoyed.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, delay, mistake, inconvenience.  
**與 frustrated 的辨析**：一般惱怒；frustrated 更常涉及受阻或沒有進展。  
**使用限制**：若強調無力感或困局，annoyed 可能太表面。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She was annoyed about the last-minute change.  
她對臨時更改感到惱火。

### 04 Fed up

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：受夠了；厭煩透了  
**語域**：informal / very common  
**核心語義**：very tired of an unpleasant situation because it has continued for too long  
**用法提示**：長期重複問題累積到『受夠了』時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：fed up with delays; completely fed up; fed up with waiting; get fed up.  
**常見搭配對象**：delay, waiting, problem, behaviour.  
**與 frustrated 的辨析**：強調長時間累積的不耐煩和厭倦。  
**使用限制**：正式寫作通常用 frustrated / exasperated / dissatisfied。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
They were fed up with waiting for a decision.  
他們已經受夠了等待決定。

### 05 Disappointed

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：失望的；感到落空的  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：unhappy because something did not happen or was not as good as expected  
**用法提示**：當 frustration 的核心其實是期望落空，而非被阻礙或被惹怒時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：deeply disappointed; disappointed with the result; disappointed by; feel disappointed.  
**常見搭配對象**：result, performance, decision, outcome.  
**與 frustrated 的辨析**：強調期望落空；frustrated 強調想做但做不到。  
**使用限制**：若問題仍在進行且令人束手無策，frustrated 往往更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The team was disappointed with the final result.  
團隊對最終結果感到失望。

## Tier 2 — Impatience / discouragement / dissatisfaction variants · 不耐、氣餒與不滿變體

### 06 Aggravated

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：被激怒的；更惱火的  
**語域**：neutral / especially AmE  
**核心語義**：made more annoyed or angry by a worsening or repeated problem  
**用法提示**：當 frustrated 進一步變成惱怒，尤其問題再次發生或惡化時可用。  
**常見搭配**：aggravated by delays; increasingly aggravated; feel aggravated.  
**常見搭配對象**：delay, traffic, repeated error, inconvenience.  
**與 frustrated 的辨析**：更偏怒氣被加劇；frustrated 更偏受阻。  
**使用限制**：英式英語中 irritated / exasperated 往往更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He became aggravated when the system failed again.  
系統再次失靈時，他變得更惱火。

### 07 Impatient

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：不耐煩的；急躁的  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：annoyed because something is taking too long or not happening quickly enough  
**用法提示**：當 frustrated 主要因等待、進度慢或程序拖延而來時最精準。  
**常見搭配**：grow impatient; impatient with delays; impatient for results; become impatient.  
**常見搭配對象**：delay, waiting, process, response.  
**與 frustrated 的辨析**：聚焦等待和速度；frustrated 可以源自更多障礙。  
**使用限制**：若不是時間／進度問題，impatient 未必合適。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Customers grew impatient as the queue barely moved.  
隊伍幾乎沒有移動，顧客愈來愈不耐煩。

### 08 Discouraged

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：氣餒的；灰心的  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：less confident or hopeful because of difficulty, failure, or lack of progress  
**用法提示**：當 frustrated 開始變成失去信心或想放棄時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：feel discouraged; discouraged by failure; become discouraged; deeply discouraged.  
**常見搭配對象**：failure, slow progress, rejection, setback.  
**與 frustrated 的辨析**：更偏信心下降，而非單純惱怒。  
**使用限制**：若人仍積極但只是被卡住，frustrated 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She felt discouraged after several unsuccessful attempts.  
幾次嘗試失敗後，她感到氣餒。

### 09 Dissatisfied

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：不滿的；不滿意的  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：not pleased because something fails to meet expectations or standards  
**用法提示**：對服務、結果、工作或安排不滿時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：dissatisfied with service; dissatisfied customers; dissatisfied with the result.  
**常見搭配對象**：service, result, product, policy.  
**與 frustrated 的辨析**：聚焦評價『不滿意』；frustrated 更偏受阻和情緒。  
**使用限制**：無法解題而感到挫敗時通常不用 dissatisfied。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Many customers were dissatisfied with the slow response.  
很多顧客對緩慢回應感到不滿。

### 10 At the end of one's tether

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：忍耐到了極限；快受不了了  
**語域**：British / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：so frustrated, worried, or exhausted that one feels unable to cope any longer  
**用法提示**：很適合英式／香港英語，表示 frustration 已累積到幾乎無法再忍。  
**常見搭配**：be at the end of one's tether; reach the end of one's tether.  
**常見搭配對象**：ongoing problem, delay, stress, repeated failure.  
**與 frustrated 的辨析**：比 frustrated 強很多，帶幾乎無法再應付的感覺。  
**使用限制**：正式報告中不宜使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
After weeks of delays, she was at the end of her tether.  
延誤數星期後，她已經忍耐到極限。

## Tier 3 — Blocked-goal / deeper emotional variants · 受阻與較深情緒變體

### 11 Stymied

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：受阻的；被卡住的  
**語域**：formal / neutral  
**核心語義**：prevented from making progress by a difficult obstacle  
**用法提示**：當 frustrated 的重點在『事情被卡住、進不了』時可用。  
**常見搭配**：stymied by bureaucracy; stymied by lack of funds; progress was stymied.  
**常見搭配對象**：effort, plan, progress, team.  
**與 frustrated 的辨析**：描述受阻狀態，不一定描述情緒。  
**使用限制**：若要表達人的感受，要用 frustrated / exasperated。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The team was stymied by a lack of reliable data.  
團隊因缺乏可靠資料而受阻。

### 12 Thwarted

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：受挫的；被阻止的  
**語域**：formal / neutral  
**核心語義**：prevented from achieving an intended aim  
**用法提示**：當 frustration 來自明確目標、計劃或嘗試被阻止時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：thwarted attempt; thwarted plan; efforts were thwarted; thwarted ambition.  
**常見搭配對象**：goal, attempt, plan, ambition.  
**與 frustrated 的辨析**：聚焦目標被阻止；frustrated 聚焦人的情緒。  
**使用限制**：不適合單純因噪音或等待而惱火。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Their plans were thwarted by a sudden change in the rules.  
規則突然改變，使他們的計劃受挫。

### 13 Disheartened

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：心灰意冷的；氣餒的  
**語域**：neutral / literary  
**核心語義**：having lost confidence, hope, or enthusiasm because of difficulty or disappointment  
**用法提示**：當 frustration 變成較深的灰心、士氣低落時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：feel disheartened; disheartened by setbacks; deeply disheartened.  
**常見搭配對象**：setback, rejection, failure, criticism.  
**與 frustrated 的辨析**：比 discouraged 更情緒化，也比 frustrated 更偏失去希望。  
**使用限制**：小型短暫煩惱通常太強。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The volunteers were disheartened by the lack of progress.  
志願者因進展不足而感到心灰意冷。

## 04 False synonyms / frustration traps · 容易誤選的詞與「挫敗」陷阱

### 01 Angry

**生氣不等於 frustrated**  
angry 強調怒氣；frustrated 的核心常是受阻、無法進展。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Anger and frustration overlap, but frustration is obstacle-based.

### 02 Sad

**傷心不等於 frustrated**  
sad 是悲傷；frustrated 通常帶受阻、不耐煩或無力感。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Sadness is different from frustration.

### 03 Stressed

**有壓力不等於 frustrated**  
stressed 是承受壓力；frustrated 是事情卡住或無法達成目標。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Stress can cause frustration, but the states differ.

### 04 Confused

**困惑不等於 frustrated**  
confused 是不明白；可能因困惑而 frustrated，但不是同義。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Confusion may lead to frustration.

### 05 Disappointed

**失望只在部分語境接近 frustrated**  
disappointed 是期望落空；frustrated 更常是進展被阻。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Disappointment is expectation-based; frustration is obstacle-based.

### 06 Annoyed

**annoyed 通常比 frustrated 更表面**  
annoyed 可因小事惱火；frustrated 往往涉及受阻或無法解決。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Annoyance is often lighter and less goal-focused.

### 07 Exasperated

**exasperated 通常比 frustrated 更強**  
exasperated 常是反覆問題令耐性耗盡。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Exasperation is a stronger, more impatient form of frustration.

### 08 Frustrate / frustrated

**詞性陷阱**  
frustrate 是動詞；frustrated 是形容詞／過去分詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Frustrate is a verb; frustrated describes the resulting state.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Exasperated · 氣惱至極；惱怒不耐

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

She was frustrated by the repeated delays.

**中文翻譯**  
她因一再延誤而感到十分氣惱。

- A. Exasperated
- B. angry
- C. sad
- D. stressed
- E. confused
- F. disappointed

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**Exasperated** · 氣惱至極；惱怒不耐

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
She was exasperated by the repeated delays.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. Exasperated ✓ — repeated delays signal accumulated loss of patience。**
- **B. angry — angry 表示怒氣本身，沒有特別指出受阻或反覆問題。**
- **C. sad — sad 表示悲傷，和受阻造成的挫敗感不同。**
- **D. stressed — stressed 表示承受壓力，但不一定有受阻或惱怒。**
- **E. confused — confused 表示不明白／困惑，並非這句的主要情緒。**
- **F. disappointed — disappointed 聚焦期望落空，這句更重視另一種情緒線索。**

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Frustrated 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 反覆問題令耐性耗盡 | **exasperated** | 是否 repeated problem / same mistake again？ |
| 被小事或噪音惹惱 | **irritated** | 是否只是被打擾／煩到？ |
| 一般惱火 | **annoyed** | 是否是 everyday annoyance？ |
| 長期累積到受夠 | **fed up** | 是否是 fed up with waiting / delays？ |
| 期望落空 | **disappointed** | 是否 expectations were not met？ |
| 惱怒被加劇 | **aggravated** | 是否 problem happened again / got worse？ |
| 等待太久 | **impatient** | 是否 delay / queue / waiting 是核心？ |
| 信心下降、氣餒 | **discouraged** | 是否 less hopeful / less confident？ |
| 對服務／結果不滿 | **dissatisfied** | 是否 evaluation of quality / needs not met？ |
| 幾乎忍無可忍 | **at the end of one's tether** | 是否 feel unable to cope much longer？ |
| 事情進展被卡住 | **stymied** | 是否 obstacle prevents progress？ |
| 目標／計劃被阻止 | **thwarted** | 是否 aim / plan / attempt was prevented？ |
| 心灰意冷 | **disheartened** | 是否 loss of hope / enthusiasm？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **frustrated vs exasperated**: exasperated 更強，常因反覆問題而失去耐性。
- **frustrated vs irritated**: irritated 更偏被惹煩，不一定有目標受阻。
- **frustrated vs annoyed**: annoyed 是一般惱怒，通常更表面。
- **frustrated vs fed up**: fed up 強調長期累積到『受夠了』。
- **frustrated vs disappointed**: disappointed 強調期望落空。
- **frustrated vs impatient**: impatient 主要與等待和速度有關。
- **frustrated vs discouraged**: discouraged 強調信心下降。
- **frustrated vs dissatisfied**: dissatisfied 是對結果／服務不滿意。
- **frustrated vs stymied**: stymied 描述進展被阻，不一定是情緒。
- **frustrated vs thwarted**: thwarted 描述計劃或目標被阻止。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & frustration traps · 文法、搭配與情緒程度陷阱

- **frustrated with/by/at + noun**：frustrated with the system / by delays / at the lack of progress。
- **frustrated that + clause**：She was frustrated that nothing had changed。
- 動詞是 **frustrate**；名詞是 **frustration**。
- **exasperated by/with** 常帶反覆、耐性耗盡的感覺。
- **irritated by/with** 常見於噪音、打斷、小麻煩。
- **fed up with + noun/-ing**：fed up with waiting。
- **impatient with + person/process**；**impatient for + result**。
- **discouraged by + setback/failure**；**disheartened by** 語氣更深。
- **dissatisfied with + service/result/product** 很常見於客戶／正式語境。
- **at the end of one's tether** 是英式慣用語，語氣很強。
- **stymied by / thwarted by** 更常描述受阻的人、計劃或努力，而不是單純情緒。
- **angry / sad / stressed / confused ≠ frustrated**：它們可能同時出現，但不是同一情緒核心。
