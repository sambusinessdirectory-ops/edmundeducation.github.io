# FITNESS · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Fitness

**「體能／體適能／身體狀況」，要先分清是在談整體身體狀態、健康、耐力、肌力、心肺能力，還是運動員的競技準備狀態。**

本單元集中處理 **fitness = the state of being physically capable, healthy, and able to perform bodily activity effectively**。你提供的 **physical condition; health; stamina** 分別代表 **整體身體狀態、廣義健康、耐力** 三個核心方向。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且高實用度的詞／表達，並按最新 inspection 標準區分 **physical fitness / conditioning / endurance / strength / cardiovascular fitness / exercise capacity / functional capacity / physical readiness / in good shape**。每個目標設 **3 道題**；所有只屬 fitness 某一部分的詞都明確標記為子項，而不當成 universal synonym。

## Tier 1 — Core condition / health / stamina equivalents · 核心身體狀況、健康與體能近義詞

### 01 Physical condition

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：身體狀況；體能狀態  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：the general state of a person's body, especially in relation to strength, health, and ability to perform physical activity  
**用法提示**：和 fitness 最直接的近義表達之一，尤其當重點是整體身體狀況，而不是某一項運動能力。  
**常見搭配**：good physical condition; improve physical condition; poor physical condition; overall physical condition; maintain condition.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, athlete, patient, worker, participant.  
**與 fitness 的辨析**：Physical condition 比 fitness 更描述性、更廣；fitness 常帶可活動、可應付體能要求的意味。  
**使用限制**：若特別談心肺耐力、肌力或運動表現，stamina / conditioning 更精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Regular exercise can improve overall physical condition.  
定期運動可以改善整體身體狀況。

### 02 Health

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：健康；健康狀況  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：the general condition of the body and mind, especially whether a person is free from illness and functioning well  
**用法提示**：當 fitness 是廣義健康狀態的一部分時很常見。  
**常見搭配**：good health; improve health; physical health; health benefits; maintain health.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, population, lifestyle, body, wellbeing.  
**與 fitness 的辨析**：Health 比 fitness 更廣，包含疾病、心理和整體身心狀況。  
**使用限制**：體育測試中的 fitness 不應一律改成 health。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Walking regularly can improve both fitness and general health.  
定期步行可以改善體能和整體健康。

### 03 Stamina

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：耐力；持久力  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：the physical or mental ability to continue an activity for a long time without becoming exhausted  
**用法提示**：當 fitness 的核心是『能持續活動多久』時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：build stamina; improve stamina; physical stamina; stamina for running; endurance and stamina.  
**常見搭配對象**：runner, athlete, worker, activity, exercise.  
**與 fitness 的辨析**：Stamina 只涵蓋 fitness 的耐力面向。  
**使用限制**：不能代表整體健康、肌力或靈活度。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Long-distance running can gradually build stamina.  
長跑可以逐步提升耐力。

### 04 Physical fitness

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：體適能；身體適能  
**語域**：neutral / health / sports  
**核心語義**：the ability of the body to perform physical activity effectively, often involving endurance, strength, flexibility, and body composition  
**用法提示**：當 fitness 需要更完整、正式地表示身體適能時最直接。  
**常見搭配**：physical fitness; improve physical fitness; fitness level; fitness assessment; physical-fitness programme.  
**常見搭配對象**：student, athlete, adult, test, programme.  
**與 fitness 的辨析**：Physical fitness 是 fitness 的完整形式，語義通常幾乎相同。  
**使用限制**：若 fitness 指『適合某用途』的非身體 sense，physical fitness 不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The school introduced a programme to improve students' physical fitness.  
學校推出計劃提升學生的體適能。

### 05 Conditioning

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：體能；體能訓練狀態  
**語域**：sports / fitness  
**核心語義**：the state or process of developing strength, stamina, and readiness for physical performance  
**用法提示**：當 fitness 和運動訓練、賽季準備、體能狀態有關時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：physical conditioning; strength and conditioning; conditioning level; conditioning programme; improve conditioning.  
**常見搭配對象**：athlete, team, player, programme, performance.  
**與 fitness 的辨析**：Conditioning 更偏運動訓練與比賽準備；fitness 更一般。  
**使用限制**：一般健康調查不一定用 conditioning。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The players returned early to improve their conditioning before the season.  
球員提早歸隊，以在賽季前改善體能狀態。

## Tier 2 — Endurance / strength / exercise-capacity variants · 耐力、肌力與運動能力變體

### 06 Endurance

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：耐久力；耐力  
**語域**：neutral / sports  
**核心語義**：the ability to sustain prolonged physical effort or resist fatigue  
**用法提示**：當 fitness 特別指長時間運動能力時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：aerobic endurance; muscular endurance; build endurance; endurance training; endurance capacity.  
**常見搭配對象**：runner, cyclist, athlete, exercise, muscle.  
**與 fitness 的辨析**：Endurance 和 stamina 很接近，但更常見於運動科學與訓練。  
**使用限制**：整體 fitness 不應只用 endurance 概括。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Cycling is useful for building cardiovascular endurance.  
踏單車有助提升心肺耐力。

### 07 Strength

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：力量；肌力  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：the physical ability of muscles to exert force  
**用法提示**：當 fitness 的重點是肌肉力量、阻力訓練或負重能力時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：muscular strength; build strength; strength training; upper-body strength; core strength.  
**常見搭配對象**：muscle, athlete, body, training, exercise.  
**與 fitness 的辨析**：Strength 只是 fitness 的一個組成部分。  
**使用限制**：不涵蓋耐力、心肺、平衡和靈活度。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Resistance training can improve muscular strength.  
阻力訓練可以提升肌力。

### 08 Cardiovascular fitness

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：心肺適能；心血管體適能  
**語域**：health / sports science  
**核心語義**：the ability of the heart, lungs, and circulatory system to supply oxygen during sustained physical activity  
**用法提示**：當 fitness 特別涉及跑步、步行、踏單車、心率和有氧能力時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：cardiovascular fitness; improve cardiovascular fitness; aerobic fitness; fitness test; cardiovascular capacity.  
**常見搭配對象**：heart, lungs, running, cycling, endurance.  
**與 fitness 的辨析**：這是 physical fitness 的一個重要子類。  
**使用限制**：肌力或柔軟度不能單靠 cardiovascular fitness 表達。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Brisk walking can improve cardiovascular fitness.  
快步行可以改善心肺適能。

### 09 Exercise capacity

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：運動能力；活動耐受力  
**語域**：medical / health  
**核心語義**：the amount or intensity of physical activity a person can perform, often measured clinically  
**用法提示**：當 fitness 出現在病人評估、復康、心肺測試或醫學語境中很有用。  
**常見搭配**：exercise capacity; reduced exercise capacity; improve exercise capacity; exercise test; functional capacity.  
**常見搭配對象**：patient, rehabilitation, heart disease, lung disease, test.  
**與 fitness 的辨析**：Exercise capacity 偏醫學可測量能力；fitness 更日常和廣泛。  
**使用限制**：普通健身討論中可能顯得過於臨床。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The rehabilitation programme improved patients' exercise capacity.  
復康計劃提升了病人的運動能力。

### 10 Athletic condition

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：運動狀態；競技體能狀態  
**語域**：sports / descriptive  
**核心語義**：a person's physical state in relation to athletic performance and readiness  
**用法提示**：當 fitness 是賽前、賽季中或恢復後的競技狀態時可用。  
**常見搭配**：good athletic condition; peak condition; return to condition; competitive condition; playing condition.  
**常見搭配對象**：player, athlete, runner, team, competitor.  
**與 fitness 的辨析**：Athletic condition 比 fitness 更限定於運動表現。  
**使用限制**：一般大眾健康不需要這麼運動化。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The athlete returned to good athletic condition after rehabilitation.  
運動員在復康後恢復良好競技狀態。

## Tier 3 — Functional / readiness / informal variants · 功能能力、準備度與口語變體

### 11 Functional capacity

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：功能能力；身體活動能力  
**語域**：medical / formal  
**核心語義**：the ability to perform physical tasks and activities required in daily life  
**用法提示**：當 fitness 被放在復康、老年健康或臨床功能評估中時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：functional capacity; assess functional capacity; improve functional capacity; reduced capacity; physical function.  
**常見搭配對象**：patient, older adult, rehabilitation, daily activity, assessment.  
**與 fitness 的辨析**：Functional capacity 聚焦能否完成日常活動；fitness 更偏體能狀態。  
**使用限制**：競技運動表現不一定適合這個臨床詞。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Strength training can help older adults maintain functional capacity.  
肌力訓練可以幫助長者維持日常活動能力。

### 12 Physical readiness

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：身體準備狀態；體能準備度  
**語域**：formal / sports / occupational  
**核心語義**：the degree to which a person is physically prepared to perform a demanding task or activity  
**用法提示**：當 fitness 指執行工作、軍事、比賽或高強度任務前的身體準備程度時可用。  
**常見搭配**：physical readiness; readiness test; operational readiness; physical-readiness programme; readiness for duty.  
**常見搭配對象**：athlete, worker, recruit, task, duty.  
**與 fitness 的辨析**：Physical readiness 強調『是否準備好做某事』；fitness 更一般。  
**使用限制**：日常健身習慣不必用 readiness。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The assessment checks physical readiness for demanding field work.  
評估檢查人員是否具備從事高強度戶外工作的身體準備狀態。

### 13 In good shape

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：身體狀態良好；很有體能  
**語域**：informal / very common  
**核心語義**：physically fit and in generally good bodily condition  
**用法提示**：當 fitness 需要用自然口語表達『身體很fit／狀態好』時非常實用。  
**常見搭配**：be in good shape; get in shape; stay in shape; keep in shape; get back into shape.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, athlete, worker, body, lifestyle.  
**與 fitness 的辨析**：In good shape 比 fitness 更口語，是狀態描述而不是名詞概念。  
**使用限制**：正式健康報告和研究通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She walks every day to stay in good shape.  
她每天步行以保持良好體能狀態。

## 04 False synonyms / fitness traps · 容易誤選的詞與體能陷阱

### 01 Health

**健康不等於所有 fitness**  
health 是更廣的身心狀況；一個人可沒有疾病但體能很差。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Health is broader than physical fitness.

### 02 Strength

**力量不等於 fitness**  
strength 只是體適能的一個組成部分。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Strength is one component of fitness.

### 03 Stamina

**耐力不等於整體 fitness**  
stamina 只強調持久力。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Stamina covers endurance, not total fitness.

### 04 Weight

**體重不等於 fitness**  
weight 是身體重量；較輕或較重都不能單獨證明體能好壞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Body weight alone does not measure fitness.

### 05 Slimness

**瘦不等於 fitness**  
身形纖瘦不代表心肺、肌力或耐力良好。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Being slim does not necessarily mean being fit.

### 06 Muscle

**肌肉不等於 fitness**  
肌肉量只是身體組成或力量相關因素，不能代表整體體適能。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Muscle mass is not the same as overall fitness.

### 07 Exercise

**運動不等於 fitness**  
exercise 是活動／行為；fitness 是身體能力或狀態。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Exercise can improve fitness, but it is not fitness itself.

### 08 Fit / fitness

**詞性陷阱**  
fit 是形容詞；fitness 是名詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Fit is an adjective; fitness is a noun.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Physical condition · 身體狀況；體能狀態

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

Regular exercise can improve overall fitness.

**中文翻譯**  
定期運動可以改善整體身體狀況。

- A. physical condition
- B. weight
- C. slimness
- D. muscle only
- E. exercise itself
- F. health only

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**physical condition** · 身體狀況；體能狀態

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
Regular exercise can improve overall physical condition.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. physical condition ✓ — overall bodily state is exactly what physical condition describes。**
- **B. weight — weight is only one measurement and does not show overall ability。**
- **C. slimness — slimness is appearance/body size, not physical performance。**
- **D. muscle only — muscle only is one component。**
- **E. exercise itself — exercise is the activity that improves fitness, not the condition itself。**
- **F. health only — health only is broader and does not specifically describe physical capability.**

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **fitness vs physical condition**: physical condition 更描述性；fitness 更聚焦身體能力和適能。
- **fitness vs health**: health 更廣，可包含疾病、心理和整體身心狀況。
- **fitness vs stamina**: stamina 只涵蓋耐力。
- **fitness vs endurance**: endurance 偏持續運動能力。
- **fitness vs strength**: strength 只是體適能的一個組成部分。
- **fitness vs conditioning**: conditioning 更偏運動員訓練和賽前體能。
- **fitness vs cardiovascular fitness**: 後者只聚焦心肺有氧能力。
- **fitness vs exercise capacity**: exercise capacity 偏醫療可測量運動能力。
- **fitness vs functional capacity**: functional capacity 聚焦日常活動能力。
- **fitness vs exercise**: exercise 是活動；fitness 是能力／狀態。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & fitness traps · 文法、搭配與體能陷阱

- **fitness** 通常不可數：improve fitness / physical fitness / fitness levels。
- 形容詞是 **fit**：be fit / get fit / physically fit。
- **physical condition** 常見：in good physical condition / improve physical condition。
- **stamina / endurance** 多作不可數：build stamina / improve endurance。
- **strength** 可作不可數概念：build strength；也可指某項優勢。
- **conditioning** 在運動語境多作不可數：pre-season conditioning。
- **cardiovascular fitness** 和 **aerobic fitness** 常高度接近。
- **exercise capacity**、**functional capacity** 多見醫學／復康語境。
- **in good shape / get in shape / stay in shape** 是非常常見口語。
- **health ≠ fitness**：健康良好的人仍可能體能差。
- **weight / slimness ≠ fitness**：身形或體重不能單獨判斷體適能。
- **exercise ≠ fitness**：運動是提升體能的方法之一。
