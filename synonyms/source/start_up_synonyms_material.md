# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Start-up

**新企業、新成立公司、新創事業、早期公司、正在冒起的企業——究竟是哪一種 start-up？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**new business; newly founded company; new venture; fledgling company; early-stage company; young company; emerging company; entrepreneurial venture; new enterprise**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **new business; newly founded company** 全部保留。

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 new business

**新企業；新生意**

剛成立或剛開始營運的企業；最廣泛、最自然的替換之一。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

She left her job to launch a start-up selling sustainable products.

她辭去工作，創辦一家銷售可持續產品的新企業。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- launch a new business

- run a new business

- new business owner

- new business venture


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 重點是剛成立、仍在早期發展、尚未成熟、正在冒起，還是創業者承擔新事業風險？

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Small business

**小企業不等於 start-up**

small business 強調規模小；start-up 強調發展階段。小企業可以經營數十年。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A family shop may be a long-established small business rather than a start-up.

---


## 02 New business

**很接近但更廣**

任何剛開業的 business 都可叫 new business；start-up 往往還帶尋找商業模式、創新或成長導向。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A newly opened local shop is a new business but may not be described as a start-up.

---


## 03 Entrepreneur

**創業者不是公司**

entrepreneur 指創辦／經營事業的人。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

An entrepreneur may found a start-up.

---


## 04 Venture

**新事業不一定是獨立公司**

venture 可以是既有企業內部的新項目。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A corporation can launch a new venture without creating a separate start-up.

---


## 05 Scale-up

**成長階段不同**

scale-up 通常指已進入快速擴張階段的企業，通常比 start-up 更後期。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A start-up may become a scale-up after achieving repeatable growth.

---


## 06 Spin-off

**來源關係不同**

spin-off 指從大學、公司或既有組織分拆出來的企業；並非所有 start-ups 都是 spin-offs。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A university spin-off can also be an early-stage start-up.

---


## 07 Start-up / startup

**拼寫變體**

`start-up` 和 `startup` 都很常見，可視為拼寫變體。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Both 'start-up' and 'startup' are widely used.

---


## 08 Start up / start-up

**詞性陷阱**

`start up` 通常是動詞片語；`start-up` / `startup` 是名詞／定語形式。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

They plan to start up a business; they founded a start-up.
