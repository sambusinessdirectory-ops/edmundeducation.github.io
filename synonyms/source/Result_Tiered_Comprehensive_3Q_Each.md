# RESULT · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Result

**Result 是高度多義名詞。** 它可以指事情的最終結局、某行動造成的後果／效果、研究發現、成功成果、考試分數，甚至系統或生產過程的輸出。因此最重要的是先判斷『這是哪一種 result』。

你提供的 **outcome; consequence; effect; findings; achievements** 正好覆蓋五個主要方向：**final outcome、causal consequence、effect、research findings、successful achievements**。

本次保留 **10 個**自然且高實用度的近義／情境表達，不把 cause、reason、process、evidence 等相鄰概念誤當同義詞。共 **30 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Result 的 sense | 核心問題 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|---|
| 最終結局／最終狀態 | 最後發生了甚麼？ | outcome, end result |
| 因果後果／效果 | 某事造成甚麼改變？ | consequence, effect, impact |
| 研究／調查結果 | 研究發現了甚麼？ | findings |
| 成功成果／回報 | 努力後取得甚麼成功？ | achievements, payoff |
| 數值結果 | 測驗／比賽得多少？ | score |
| 技術／生產產出 | 系統／工廠產生甚麼？ | output |

## Tier 1 — Core outcome / consequence / effect / findings equivalents · 核心結局、後果、效果與研究結果近義詞

### 01 Outcome

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：結果；結局  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：the final result of a process, event, decision, competition, or course of action  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **result** 最核心、最廣泛的近義詞之一，尤其當重點是事情最後『變成怎樣』。  
**常見搭配**：final outcome; likely outcome; positive outcome; outcome of the meeting; improve outcomes.  
**常見搭配對象**：decision, treatment, election, meeting, process.  
**與 result 的辨析**：Outcome 比 result 更聚焦 final state/end point；result 也可以是數據、分數、研究結果或因果效果。  
**使用限制**：研究數據的 findings、測驗分數的 score 等更窄語境不宜一律改成 outcome。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The negotiations produced a better outcome than either side had expected.  
談判取得了比雙方預期更好的結果。

### 02 Consequence

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：後果；結果  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：a result that follows from an action, decision, or event, often with an emphasis on cause and effect  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，當 result 表示某行動帶來的後果時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：serious consequence; unintended consequence; consequence of; face the consequences; long-term consequences.  
**常見搭配對象**：decision, action, policy, mistake, change.  
**與 result 的辨析**：Consequence 強調『因某事而來』，而且常帶負面或需要承擔的意味。  
**使用限制**：比賽結果、研究 findings、考試分數通常不用 consequence。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
One consequence of the delay was a significant increase in costs.  
延誤的一個後果是成本大幅增加。

### 03 Effect

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：效果；影響；結果  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：a change or result produced by a particular cause, action, treatment, or influence  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 result 在因果語境中的核心替代詞。  
**常見搭配**：effect on; positive effect; side effect; long-term effect; have an effect.  
**常見搭配對象**：policy, treatment, change, behaviour, environment.  
**與 result 的辨析**：Effect 強調 cause → change；result 更廣，可只是最終結果而不特別標示因果。  
**使用限制**：若只是比賽勝負或研究所發現的內容，effect 不合適。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The new timetable had a positive effect on punctuality.  
新時間表對準時程度產生了正面效果。

### 04 Findings

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：研究結果；調查發現  
**語域**：academic / research / formal  
**核心語義**：the information or conclusions discovered through research, investigation, analysis, or an inquiry  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，當 results 指 study/survey/research/investigation 的結果時，**findings** 往往比 general result 更準。  
**常見搭配**：research findings; survey findings; key findings; report the findings; preliminary findings.  
**常見搭配對象**：study, survey, research, investigation, analysis.  
**與 result 的辨析**：Findings 是『研究／調查發現了甚麼』；result 可以是任何結果。  
**使用限制**：一般生活事件或比賽結局不能用 findings。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The findings suggest that regular exercise improves sleep quality.  
研究結果顯示，定期運動可改善睡眠質素。

### 05 Achievements

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：成就；成果  
**語域**：neutral / positive  
**核心語義**：successful results reached through effort, skill, work, or persistence  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 適合 result = positive accomplishments，尤其談個人、團隊、學校、機構或計劃的成果。  
**常見搭配**：major achievements; academic achievements; notable achievements; celebrate achievements; list achievements.  
**常見搭配對象**：student, team, career, project, organisation.  
**與 result 的辨析**：Achievements 必須帶成功／完成感；result 本身可以好、壞或中性。  
**使用限制**：失敗結果、負面後果或純數據不能叫 achievements。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The annual report highlights the organisation's main achievements.  
年報重點介紹機構的主要成果。

## Tier 2 — Impact / final-result / payoff / score variants · 影響、最終成果、回報與分數變體

### 06 Impact

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：影響；成效  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：a strong or noticeable effect that an action, policy, event, or change has on something  
**用法提示**：當 result 的核心不是『最後發生甚麼』，而是『造成了多大影響』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：impact on; positive impact; measurable impact; long-term impact; assess the impact.  
**常見搭配對象**：policy, programme, community, environment, performance.  
**與 result 的辨析**：Impact 通常比 effect 更強調 significance/magnitude；result 則未必表示影響程度。  
**使用限制**：考試結果、比賽結果、調查 findings 不可直接改成 impact。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The mentoring programme had a measurable impact on student attendance.  
師友計劃對學生出席率產生了可量度的成效。

### 07 End result

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：最終結果；最後成果  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：the final outcome after all stages, influences, or changes have taken place  
**用法提示**：當 result 需要突出『最後』而不是中間過程時可用。  
**常見搭配**：end result; final result; desired end result; achieve the end result; the end result of.  
**常見搭配對象**：process, negotiation, change, work, decision.  
**與 result 的辨析**：End result 比 result 更強調終點，語義上略有重複但在自然英語中很常見。  
**使用限制**：若句子本身已有 final，通常不需要再說 final end result。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The end result was a simpler and more reliable system.  
最終結果是一套更簡單、更可靠的系統。

### 08 Payoff

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：回報；成果；最終好處  
**語域**：informal / business  
**核心語義**：a beneficial result or reward obtained after effort, investment, patience, or risk  
**用法提示**：當 result 明確是努力後得到的好處／回報時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：big payoff; long-term payoff; payoff from investment; worth the effort; eventual payoff.  
**常見搭配對象**：investment, effort, training, patience, risk.  
**與 result 的辨析**：Payoff 帶 positive return / reward 色彩，比 neutral result 更窄。  
**使用限制**：負面結果、純研究數據或法律後果不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The extra training produced a clear payoff in higher productivity.  
額外培訓帶來明顯回報，生產力有所提高。

### 09 Score

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：分數；得分結果  
**語域**：neutral / education / sport  
**核心語義**：a numerical result in a test, assessment, game, or competition  
**用法提示**：當 result 指測驗、考試或比賽中的數字結果時，score 是最自然的專門詞。  
**常見搭配**：test score; exam score; final score; high score; score of 80.  
**常見搭配對象**：test, exam, match, game, assessment.  
**與 result 的辨析**：Score 只涵蓋 numerical performance result；result 的範圍遠更廣。  
**使用限制**：一般事件結局、研究 findings 或政策效果不能叫 score。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Her final test score was higher than she had expected.  
她的期末測驗分數比預期高。

## Tier 3 — Technical output variant · 技術／生產輸出變體

### 10 Output

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：產出；輸出結果  
**語域**：technical / business / computing  
**核心語義**：the amount, product, information, or material produced by a person, process, machine, system, or economy  
**用法提示**：當 result 是 process/system 所產生的具體產出時可用，特別是技術、經濟、生產和電腦語境。  
**常見搭配**：system output; production output; economic output; output data; increase output.  
**常見搭配對象**：system, machine, process, factory, economy.  
**與 result 的辨析**：Output 強調 produced product/data/quantity，而不是任何類型的 result。  
**使用限制**：人的生活結果、法律後果、研究 findings 不能一律改成 output。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The software converts the raw data into a visual output.  
軟件把原始資料轉換成視覺化輸出結果。

## 04 False synonyms / result traps · 容易誤選的原因、證據與結果陷阱

### 01 Cause

**原因 ≠ result**  
cause 是令事情發生的因素；result 是其後發生的結果。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Cause precedes a result.

### 02 Reason

**理由／原因 ≠ result**  
reason 解釋為甚麼；result 說明最後發生甚麼。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Reason and result answer different questions.

### 03 Process

**過程 ≠ result**  
process 是通往結果的一連串步驟。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A process produces or leads to a result.

### 04 Evidence

**證據 ≠ result**  
evidence 可支持結論，但不等於研究結果本身。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Evidence supports findings.

### 05 Conclusion

**結論 ≠ 所有 result**  
conclusion 是分析後作出的判斷；findings/result 可以包括未經總結的數據或觀察。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A conclusion is an interpretation of results.

### 06 Impact

**只在顯著影響 sense 接近**  
impact 需要明顯影響，不等於所有 result。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Impact is effect-focused.

### 07 Achievement

**只在成功成果 sense 接近**  
achievement 帶正面成功意味。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Achievement is positive and effort-based.

### 08 Result / resulting

**詞形陷阱**  
result 可作名詞／動詞；resulting 通常是現在分詞或形容詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check word class and sentence structure.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Outcome · 結果；結局

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The result of the meeting was a decision to postpone the launch.

**中文翻譯**  
會議結果是決定延遲推出日期。

- A. Outcome
- B. Consequence
- C. Effect
- D. Cause
- E. Process
- F. Conclusion

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Outcome** · 結果；結局

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The outcome of the meeting was a decision to postpone the launch.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Outcome ✓** — means the final state or end result of an event, process or decision. The clue is that the sentence asks what the meeting finally produced, not what caused it. In this collocation, **Outcome** captures exactly the kind of result being described.
- **B.** **Consequence** — means a result that follows from an action or event, often with a causal and sometimes negative nuance. This would make the sentence sound more explicitly causal and often more negative than intended. Here, the decisive clue is that the sentence asks what the meeting finally produced, not what caused it.
- **C.** **Effect** — means a change produced by a cause, treatment, action or influence. This works only when the emphasis is a change produced by a cause; it is not the best label for every final result. Here, the decisive clue is that the sentence asks what the meeting finally produced, not what caused it.
- **D.** **Cause** — means the factor that produces a result, so it points in the opposite causal direction. This reverses the logic: a cause produces the result rather than naming the result itself. Here, the decisive clue is that the sentence asks what the meeting finally produced, not what caused it.
- **E.** **Process** — means the sequence of steps leading to a result. This names the steps leading to the result, not what those steps finally produce. Here, the decisive clue is that the sentence asks what the meeting finally produced, not what caused it.
- **F.** **Conclusion** — is an interpretation or judgement drawn after considering evidence or findings. This is a judgement drawn from evidence; it is not automatically the observed result or outcome. Here, the decisive clue is that the sentence asks what the meeting finally produced, not what caused it.

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Result 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 事情最後的結局 | **outcome** | 是否在問 final state/end point？ |
| 某行動帶來的後果 | **consequence** | 是否強調 action → downstream result，常帶負面意味？ |
| 某因素造成的改變 | **effect** | 是否 cause → measurable change？ |
| 研究／調查發現 | **findings** | 是否 study/survey/investigation discovers something？ |
| 成功成果 | **achievements** | 是否 positive accomplishment through effort？ |
| 顯著影響／成效 | **impact** | 是否 noticeable effect on something？ |
| 經過多階段後的最後成果 | **end result** | 是否要特別突出 final result？ |
| 努力／投資後的回報 | **payoff** | 是否 benefit after cost, effort or patience？ |
| 測驗／比賽分數 | **score** | 是否 numerical performance result？ |
| 系統／機器／工廠產出 | **output** | 是否 produced data/product/quantity？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **result vs outcome**: outcome 更強調最後結局／最終狀態。
- **result vs consequence**: consequence 強調某行動造成的後果，常帶負面色彩。
- **result vs effect**: effect 是某原因產生的改變。
- **result vs findings**: findings 專用於研究、調查或分析發現。
- **result vs achievements**: achievements 必須是正面的成功成果。
- **result vs impact**: impact 強調影響程度或成效。
- **result vs payoff**: payoff 是努力／成本／投資後得到的好處。
- **result vs score**: score 是測驗、評估或比賽的數值結果。
- **result vs output**: output 是系統、機器、生產或經濟活動的產出。
- **result vs cause**: cause 在前，result 在後；不要把因果方向倒轉。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & polysemy traps · 文法、搭配與『結果』多義陷阱

- **result of + noun**：the result of the meeting / experiment。
- **result in + noun/-ing**：The delay resulted in higher costs。
- **result from + noun**：The problem resulted from poor planning。
- **outcome of + event/process/treatment**：the outcome of the election。
- **consequence of + action/event**：an unintended consequence of the policy。
- **effect of X on Y**：the effect of sleep on concentration。
- **findings of/from + study/survey**：the findings of the survey。
- **achievement in + field / achievement of + goal**：academic achievements / achievement of the target。
- **impact on + noun**：the impact on small businesses。
- **payoff from + effort/investment**：the payoff from training。
- **score in/on + test/game**：a score of 85 / her score on the test。
- **output of/from + system/process**：the output of the model。
- **result** 可作名詞或動詞；**resulting** 常作分詞／形容詞。
- 不要把 **cause, reason, process, evidence, conclusion** 自動當作 result 的同義詞。
