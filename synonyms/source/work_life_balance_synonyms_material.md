**工作與個人生活平衡、健康的工作休閒搭配、均衡生活方式、工作生活和諧、可持續工作模式——究竟是哪一種 work-life balance？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**balance between work and personal life; healthy work and leisure mix; work-personal life balance; healthy work-life mix; balanced lifestyle; work-life harmony; balance between professional and personal life; healthy division of time between work and life; sustainable work-life pattern**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **balance between work and personal life; healthy work and leisure mix** 全部保留。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Remote work can improve the balance between work and personal life for some employees.

遙距工作可以改善部分員工的工作與個人生活平衡。

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Balanced lifestyle

**範圍更廣**

balanced lifestyle 不只包括工作與私人生活，還可包括飲食、運動、睡眠、社交等。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Work-life balance is one part of a balanced lifestyle.

---

## 02 Work-life harmony

**重點是共存而非分割**

work-life harmony 強調工作與生活能互相協調，不一定追求對半分配。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Harmony can mean integration rather than strict separation.

---

## 03 Time management

**方法不等於結果**

time management 是管理時間的方法；work-life balance 是較廣泛的生活／工作狀態。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Good time management can improve work-life balance.

---

## 04 Flexible working

**措施不等於 balance**

flexible working 是安排方式，可能幫助 balance，但本身不等於 balance。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Flexible hours may support work-life balance.

---

## 05 Wellbeing

**結果／狀態更廣**

wellbeing 涵蓋身心健康與生活質素，work-life balance 只是其中一部分。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Good work-life balance can support wellbeing.

---

## 06 Work-life integration

**整合概念不同**

integration 強調工作與私人生活交織，而 balance 常帶較明顯界線或比例概念。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Integration may blur boundaries between work and personal life.

---

## 07 Work-life / work life

**連字符辨析**

作複合定語通常寫 `work-life`，如 `work-life balance`。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Work-life balance is a common compound expression.

---

## 08 Balance / balanced

**詞性陷阱**

`balance` 是名詞／動詞；`balanced` 是形容詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She wants better balance; she wants a more balanced lifestyle.

| **work-life harmony** | 強調協調共存，不必嚴格分割 |
| **balance between professional and personal life** | 正式／書面版本 |
| **healthy division of time between work and life** | 特別聚焦時間分配 |
| **sustainable work-life pattern** | 強調長期可維持、避免 burnout |

---

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Work-life balance vs Balance between work and personal life
最接近、最透明，幾乎不增加額外語義。

## Work-life balance vs Healthy work and leisure mix
後者較窄，因 personal life 還包括 family / care / social responsibilities。

## Work-life balance vs Balanced lifestyle
後者範圍更廣，不只處理工作。

## Work-life balance vs Work-life harmony
`harmony` 更強調共存與協調，而不是比例或界線。

## Work-life balance vs Healthy division of time
只聚焦時間，忽略角色衝突、壓力、界線與心理恢復。

## Work-life balance vs Sustainable work-life pattern
後者特別適合 burnout / workload / long-term wellbeing 語境。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Work-life
作複合定語時通常加連字符：
- work-life balance
- work-life policy
- work-life conflict

## 02 Balance between A and B
- balance between work and personal life
- balance between professional and personal responsibilities

## 03 Work-life balance is broader than leisure
personal life 可包括：
- family
- caregiving
- health
- rest
- social life
- personal development

## 04 Work-life harmony
不是完全等同：
- balance = 比例／界線
- harmony = 協調／共存

## 05 Related but different
- time management = 方法
- flexible working = 工作安排
- wellbeing = 更廣的身心狀態
- work-life integration = 工作與私人生活交織
