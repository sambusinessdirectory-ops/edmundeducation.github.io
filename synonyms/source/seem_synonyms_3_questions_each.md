# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Seem

**`seem` 可以表示『似乎、看起來、聽起來、感覺上、給人某種印象』。不同替代表達的核心差別，是判斷來自視覺、聽覺、主觀感受、社交印象，還是較正式的表面推斷。**

核心替代表達：

`appear / look / sound / feel / give the impression / come across as / strike someone as / give the appearance of`

> **核心原則**：先判斷 seem 指的是 **formal appearance, visual impression, auditory impression, subjective feeling, communicated impression, social impression, personal reaction, or outward appearance that may differ from reality**。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Appear

**似乎；看來**

和 `seem` 最接近、最通用的正式替代表達之一。可接形容詞、不定式或 `that` 子句，表示根據表面資訊作出判斷。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The plan appears reasonable at first glance.

這個計劃乍看之下似乎合理。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **seeming to be true or to have a particular quality**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、正式、書面

---

## 02 Look

**看起來；顯得**

當 `seem` 的判斷主要來自外觀、表情或可見跡象時，`look` 很自然。它通常接形容詞，如 `look tired / look expensive`。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> You look tired after the long journey.

長途旅程後你看起來很疲倦。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **seeming a particular way based mainly on appearance**

**REGISTER · 語域**

口語、一般

---

## 03 Sound

**聽起來；似乎**

當 `seem` 的判斷主要來自聽到的資訊、說法、語氣或描述時，`sound` 很自然。常見於 `sound good / sound difficult / sound like...`。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> That sounds like a sensible solution.

那聽起來像是一個合理的解決方法。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **seeming a particular way based on what is heard or described**

**REGISTER · 語域**

口語、一般

---

## 04 Feel

**感覺似乎；給人……感覺**

當 `seem` 的判斷來自主觀感受、氣氛、觸感或整體體驗時，`feel` 很合適。它不一定是視覺判斷。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The room feels much larger after the renovation.

翻新後這個房間感覺大得多。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **seeming a particular way through personal or sensory experience**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、口語

---

## 05 Give the impression

**給人……印象；讓人覺得**

當 `seem` 強調某人或某事向別人傳達某種印象時，`give the impression` 很自然。常接 `that` 子句或 `of + noun`。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> His answer gave the impression that he was uncertain.

他的回答給人一種他並不確定的印象。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **causing other people to form a particular impression**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、正式

---

## 06 Come across as

**給人感覺是；顯得**

較口語，特別適合形容一個人在交流、面試、演講或社交中給別人的整體印象。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She comes across as confident and well prepared.

她給人的感覺是自信而且準備充分。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **seeming to others to have a particular personality or quality**

**REGISTER · 語域**

口語、工作、社交

---

## 07 Strike someone as

**使某人覺得；給某人……感覺**

表示某人對人、事、想法產生即時主觀印象。通常用 `strike + person + as + adjective/noun`。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The proposal struck me as unnecessarily complicated.

這個建議令我覺得不必要地複雜。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **causing someone to form a particular immediate impression**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、書面、評論

---

## 08 Give the appearance of

**呈現……的樣子；看似**

較正式，強調外在呈現出某種樣子，但未必代表實際情況就是如此。比一般 `seem` 更偏『表面看來』。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The changes give the appearance of progress without solving the main problem.

這些改變表面上看似有進展，但沒有解決主要問題。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **creating an outward appearance that may or may not reflect reality**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、評論、書面

# 02 False synonyms

## Seem / Appear
兩者非常接近；`appear` 稍正式、書面。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The plan seems reasonable.  
The plan appears reasonable.

## Seem / Look
`look` 主要根據外觀判斷。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The situation seems difficult.  
You look tired.

## Seem / Sound
`sound` 主要根據聽到的說法、描述或語氣判斷。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The idea seems practical.  
The idea sounds practical from your explanation.

## Seem / Feel
`feel` 主要根據主觀體驗、氣氛或感受。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The room seems larger.  
The room feels larger after the renovation.

## Seem / Give the impression
後者更明確地表示某人／某事向別人傳達了某種印象。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
He seemed uncertain.  
He gave the impression that he was uncertain.

## Seem / Come across as
`come across as` 特別適合社交、面試、演講或書面溝通中給人的人格／風格印象。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
She seems confident.  
She comes across as confident in meetings.

## Seem / Strike someone as
`strike someone as` 帶明顯主觀反應，常有 `me/us/him/her`。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The plan seems complicated.  
The plan strikes me as complicated.

## Seem / Give the appearance of
後者特別適合「表面如此，但可能不是事實」的語境。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The changes seem substantial.  
The changes give the appearance of being substantial, but they are mostly cosmetic.

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Seem vs Appear** — 意思最接近；appear 稍正式。
- **Look vs Seem** — look 偏視覺外觀。
- **Sound vs Seem** — sound 偏聽到的資訊。
- **Feel vs Seem** — feel 偏主觀體驗。
- **Give the impression vs Come across as** — 前者較廣；後者特別常形容人的整體印象。
- **Strike someone as vs Seem** — 前者強調『對某人來說』的主觀反應。
- **Give the appearance of vs Appear** — 前者更容易帶『只是表面』意味。
- **Look like vs Look + adjective** — `look like + noun`; `look + adjective`。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Appear
> appear + adjective  
> appear to + verb  
> it appears that...

## Look
> look + adjective  
> look like + noun

> looks expensive ✓  
> looks like expensive ✗

## Sound
> sound + adjective  
> sound like + noun

> sounds difficult ✓  
> sounds like a difficult job ✓

## Feel
> feel + adjective

> feels easier ✓  
> feels like easier ✗

## Give the impression
> give the impression **that**...  
> give the impression **of** being...

## Come across
固定：

> come across **as** confident

## Strike
詞序：

> strike **me** as unusual  
> strike **us** as vague

## Give the appearance
固定：

> give the appearance **of** being...

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 想正式寫「似乎」？ → **appear**
2. 主要根據外表？ → **look**
3. 主要根據聽到的內容？ → **sound**
4. 主要根據體驗／感受？ → **feel**
5. 強調別人形成了某種印象？ → **give the impression**
6. 形容一個人在交流中給人的感覺？ → **come across as**
7. 想明確說「令我／我們覺得」？ → **strike someone as**
8. 想表達「只是表面看似如此」？ → **give the appearance of**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`appear / look / sound / feel / give the impression / come across as / strike someone as / give the appearance of`

尤其要記住：

> **look** = 視覺印象  
> **sound** = 聽覺／描述印象  
> **feel** = 主觀體驗  
> **come across as** = 社交印象  
> **strike me as** = 個人即時判斷  
> **give the appearance of** = 表面看似

最重要的一點：

> **seem** 很廣，但 `look / sound / feel` 各自暗示不同的資訊來源。
