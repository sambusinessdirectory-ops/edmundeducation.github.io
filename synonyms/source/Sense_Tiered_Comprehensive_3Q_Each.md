# SENSE · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Sense

**Sense 是高度多義詞。** 本課集中處理 **feeling / awareness / perception / impression / intuition / understanding** 這一組抽象義項，不處理 **five senses（五官感覺）**、**sense of direction（方向感）**、**make sense（有道理）** 等其他義項。

你提供的 **feeling; awareness; perception** 正好代表三個核心方向：**主觀感覺、察覺／意識、感知／看法**。

本次保留 **10 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達。會特別區分 vague feeling、conscious awareness、interpretive perception、intuition、recognition、understanding 等層次。共 **30 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Sense 的 sense | 核心問題 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|---|
| 主觀感覺／印象 | 是否只是 vague subjective impression？ | feeling, impression, notion |
| 察覺／意識 | 是否知道某事存在？ | awareness, consciousness |
| 感知／看法 | 是否涉及 how something is perceived/interpreted？ | perception |
| 不經分析的直覺 | 是否 immediate judgement without reasoning？ | intuition |
| 對細微變化的感受力 | 是否 notice subtle signals/changes？ | sensitivity |
| 識別／認清 | 是否 identify a sign/pattern/need？ | recognition |
| 理解 | 是否有較深入 comprehension？ | understanding |

## Tier 1 — Core feeling / awareness / perception equivalents · 核心感覺、察覺與感知近義詞

### 01 Feeling

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：感覺；感受  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：an impression, intuition, or emotional response about a situation, person, or possibility  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **sense** 在『一種感覺／直覺』sense 最核心的近義詞之一。  
**常見搭配**：have a feeling; strong feeling; feeling that; gut feeling; uneasy feeling.  
**常見搭配對象**：situation, person, possibility, danger, change.  
**與 sense 的辨析**：Feeling 比 sense 更日常，也可更情緒化；sense 常較模糊地表示 an impression or intuition。  
**使用限制**：如果是對環境或問題的認知程度，awareness 更準；如果是感官知覺，perception 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
I had a feeling that something was wrong.  
我有一種感覺，覺得有些事情不對勁。

### 02 Awareness

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：意識；察覺；認知  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：knowledge or consciousness of a situation, fact, risk, or condition  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，當 sense = being conscious of something / recognising its presence 時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：awareness of; raise awareness; public awareness; self-awareness; awareness of risk.  
**常見搭配對象**：risk, problem, situation, environment, need.  
**與 sense 的辨析**：Awareness 強調知道／察覺某事存在；sense 可以更直覺、更模糊。  
**使用限制**：如果是『我有種感覺他會來』，awareness 不自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
There is growing awareness of the risks of excessive noise.  
人們愈來愈意識到過度噪音的風險。

### 03 Perception

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：感知；看法；認知方式  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：the way something is noticed, understood, or interpreted through the senses or the mind  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 適合 sense = perception of something, especially visual/social/interpretive contexts。  
**常見搭配**：perception of risk; public perception; visual perception; perception of quality; change perceptions.  
**常見搭配對象**：risk, quality, image, colour, situation.  
**與 sense 的辨析**：Perception 強調 how something is perceived/interpreted；sense 更可表示直覺、方向感或模糊印象。  
**使用限制**：若只表示 awareness of existence，不一定要用 perception。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Lighting can affect our perception of colour.  
光線會影響我們對顏色的感知。

### 04 Impression

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：印象；感覺  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：an idea or feeling formed about someone or something, often from limited information  
**用法提示**：當 sense 表示『整體給人的感覺／印象』時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：give the impression; first impression; impression of; create an impression; overall impression.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, place, situation, meeting, quality.  
**與 sense 的辨析**：Impression 常由接觸或觀察形成；sense 可以更像內在直覺。  
**使用限制**：正式 awareness 或 sensory perception 不一定適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The room gives a sense of calm.  
這個房間給人一種平靜的感覺。

### 05 Intuition

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：直覺；直觀感受  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：an immediate understanding or feeling about something without conscious reasoning  
**用法提示**：當 sense 表示 instinctive understanding / gut feeling 時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：strong intuition; intuitive sense; rely on intuition; intuition tells me; professional intuition.  
**常見搭配對象**：decision, danger, person, outcome, situation.  
**與 sense 的辨析**：Intuition 比 sense 更明確表示不經分析而得出的判斷。  
**使用限制**：一般 awareness 或 physical sense 不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Her intuition told her that the figures needed checking.  
她的直覺告訴她那些數字需要再核對。

## Tier 2 — Consciousness / sensitivity / recognition / understanding variants · 意識、敏感度、識別與理解變體

### 06 Consciousness

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：意識；知覺狀態  
**語域**：formal / abstract  
**核心語義**：the state of being aware of oneself, one's surroundings, or a broader issue  
**用法提示**：當 sense 比較抽象，表示 broad awareness or consciousness of an issue 時可用。  
**常見搭配**：social consciousness; environmental consciousness; consciousness of; public consciousness; state of consciousness.  
**常見搭配對象**：self, society, environment, issue, surroundings.  
**與 sense 的辨析**：Consciousness 比 awareness 更抽象，也可指清醒／意識狀態。  
**使用限制**：日常『感覺某事會發生』通常不用 consciousness。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The campaign helped build environmental consciousness among students.  
這項活動有助提升學生的環境意識。

### 07 Sensitivity

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：敏感度；感受力  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：the ability to notice, respond to, or understand subtle changes, feelings, signals, or conditions  
**用法提示**：當 sense 表示『對細微變化的感受能力』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：sensitivity to; cultural sensitivity; sensitivity to light; emotional sensitivity; heightened sensitivity.  
**常見搭配對象**：light, sound, mood, culture, change.  
**與 sense 的辨析**：Sensitivity 強調 responsiveness to subtle stimuli；sense 是更一般的感知／直覺。  
**使用限制**：如果只是知道某問題存在，awareness 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Good teachers develop sensitivity to changes in students' mood.  
優秀教師會培養對學生情緒變化的敏感度。

### 08 Recognition

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：辨認；認知；察覺  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：the act of identifying, acknowledging, or becoming aware of something as familiar, important, or true  
**用法提示**：當 sense 表示『辨認／認清某情況』，尤其 pattern, symptom, need, problem 時可用。  
**常見搭配**：recognition of; symptom recognition; recognition of need; pattern recognition; public recognition.  
**常見搭配對象**：symptom, pattern, problem, need, person.  
**與 sense 的辨析**：Recognition 比 awareness 更有『識別出』的動作感。  
**使用限制**：模糊的 gut sense 或情緒 feeling 不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Early recognition of the symptoms can improve treatment outcomes.  
及早識別症狀可以改善治療結果。

### 09 Understanding

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：理解；認識  
**語域**：neutral / broad  
**核心語義**：knowledge of the meaning, nature, or significance of something  
**用法提示**：當 sense 不只是察覺，而是帶一定理解程度時可用。  
**常見搭配**：understanding of; deepen understanding; clear understanding; public understanding; shared understanding.  
**常見搭配對象**：issue, process, risk, concept, situation.  
**與 sense 的辨析**：Understanding 比 sense 更深入、更理性；sense 可以只是模糊感覺。  
**使用限制**：純感官知覺或 instinct 不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The discussion gave students a better understanding of the issue.  
討論令學生更深入理解這個議題。

## Tier 3 — Vague-idea variant · 模糊概念型變體

### 10 Notion

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：概念；想法；模糊印象  
**語域**：neutral / slightly informal  
**核心語義**：an idea, belief, or vague impression about something  
**用法提示**：當 sense 表示 a vague idea / impression rather than solid knowledge 時可用。  
**常見搭配**：have a notion; vague notion; notion that; general notion; preconceived notion.  
**常見搭配對象**：idea, possibility, situation, belief, expectation.  
**與 sense 的辨析**：Notion 比 sense 更偏『想法／概念』，未必帶感官或直覺成分。  
**使用限制**：awareness、sensory perception、direction sense 等不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He had only a vague notion of what the job involved.  
他對這份工作涉及甚麼只有模糊概念。

## 04 False synonyms / sense traps · 容易誤選的感覺、認知與直覺陷阱

### 01 Emotion

**情緒 ≠ sense**  
emotion 是較明確的情感狀態；sense 可以只是印象、直覺或認知。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Emotion is more affective and specific.

### 02 Knowledge

**知識 ≠ sense**  
knowledge 通常更確定、更有內容；sense 可以模糊或直覺。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Knowledge is stronger and more explicit.

### 03 Opinion

**意見 ≠ sense**  
opinion 是較明確的看法或判斷；sense 可只是感覺。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Opinion is a formed view.

### 04 Instinct

**本能 ≠ 所有 sense**  
instinct 只適合直覺／本能 sense，不適合 awareness/perception 全部義項。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Instinct is intuition-specific.

### 05 Feeling

**只在主觀感受 sense 接近**  
feeling 不等於 awareness or perception 的所有用法。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Feeling is subjective.

### 06 Perception

**只在感知／看法 sense 接近**  
perception 比 sense 更聚焦如何理解或感知事物。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Perception is interpretation-focused.

### 07 Awareness

**只在察覺 sense 接近**  
awareness 需要知道某事存在，不等於 intuition。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Awareness is consciousness-focused.

### 08 Sense / sensible

**詞形陷阱**  
sense 是名詞／動詞；sensible 是形容詞，表示理智的／合理的。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check word class and meaning.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Feeling · 感覺；感受

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

I had a sense that something was wrong.

**中文翻譯**  
我有一種感覺，覺得有些事情不對勁。

- A. Feeling
- B. Awareness
- C. Impression
- D. Emotion
- E. Opinion
- F. Knowledge

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Feeling** · 感覺；感受

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
I had a feeling that something was wrong.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Feeling ✓** — means a subjective impression, intuition or emotional response about something. Here, the sentence expresses a subjective impression rather than explicit knowledge. That makes **Feeling** the most precise replacement for this particular sense of *sense*.
- **B.** **Awareness** — means conscious knowledge or recognition that a fact, risk, condition or issue exists. This requires conscious recognition that something exists; it is less suitable for intuition, impression or deeper understanding. The decisive clue is that the sentence expresses a subjective impression rather than explicit knowledge.
- **C.** **Impression** — means an idea or feeling formed from observation, often from limited information. This is an idea formed from observation; it does not always carry the awareness, sensory, or intuitive nuance needed here. The decisive clue is that the sentence expresses a subjective impression rather than explicit knowledge.
- **D.** **Emotion** — means a specific affective state such as fear, anger or joy. This is too affective and specific; the sentence is not naming a clear emotional state. The decisive clue is that the sentence expresses a subjective impression rather than explicit knowledge.
- **E.** **Opinion** — means a formed view or judgement. An opinion is a formed view or judgement, stronger and more explicit than a vague sense. The decisive clue is that the sentence expresses a subjective impression rather than explicit knowledge.
- **F.** **Knowledge** — means explicit information or understanding that is generally more definite than a vague sense. Knowledge is more definite and content-rich than the softer or more subjective meaning tested here. The decisive clue is that the sentence expresses a subjective impression rather than explicit knowledge.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **sense vs feeling**: feeling 更主觀、日常；sense 可以更模糊或更廣。
- **sense vs awareness**: awareness 強調知道某事存在。
- **sense vs perception**: perception 強調如何感知／理解某事。
- **sense vs impression**: impression 常由觀察形成。
- **sense vs intuition**: intuition 明確表示不經推理的直覺。
- **sense vs consciousness**: consciousness 更抽象，常指整體或社會意識。
- **sense vs sensitivity**: sensitivity 是對細微刺激或變化的感受能力。
- **sense vs recognition**: recognition 是識別出某具體事物／模式／需要。
- **sense vs understanding**: understanding 更深入、更理性。
- **sense vs notion**: notion 是較模糊的想法／概念。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & polysemy traps · 文法、搭配與『sense』多義陷阱

- **a sense that + clause**：I had a sense that something was wrong。
- **a sense of + noun**：a sense of danger / calm / responsibility。
- **awareness of + noun**：awareness of risk / need。
- **perception of + noun**：perception of quality / distance。
- **impression of + noun / impression that + clause**。
- **intuition that + clause / intuition about + noun**。
- **sensitivity to + noun**：sensitivity to light / mood changes。
- **recognition of + noun**：recognition of symptoms / need。
- **understanding of + noun**：understanding of the process。
- **notion of / notion that**：a vague notion of what happened。
- 本課不處理其他常見義項：**sense of smell, sense of direction, common sense, make sense**。
- **sense** 可作名詞或動詞；**sensible** 是形容詞，意思是『理智的／合理的』，不是同義詞。
