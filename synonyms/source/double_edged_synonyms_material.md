# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Double-edged

**有利有弊、具有兩面性、福禍參半、有幫助但有風險、有利但有代價、涉及取捨——究竟是哪一種 double-edged？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**having both benefits and drawbacks; two-sided; a mixed blessing; having pros and cons; both beneficial and harmful; helpful but risky; advantageous but costly; a trade-off**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **8 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **having both benefits and drawbacks** 完整保留。  
> 本課聚焦 `double-edged` 的**比喻義**：同一事物同時有正面與負面效果；字面「雙刃的」sense 另行區分。

---

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Having Both Benefits And Drawbacks

**既有好處亦有缺點的**

最直接、最透明的描述性改寫。表示同一事物同時帶來正面效果和負面代價，幾乎完整保留 `double-edged` 的核心語義。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Remote work can be something having both benefits and drawbacks for employees.

遙距工作對員工而言可能有利有弊。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- benefits and drawbacks

- both benefits and drawbacks

- advantages and disadvantages

- positive and negative effects


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡只是一般『有利有弊』、有兩個相反面向、福禍參半、同時有益有害、帶風險、帶明確成本，還是得到一項好處就要犧牲另一項？

---

## 02 Two-Sided

**具有兩面性的；有正反兩面的**

表示事情有兩個不同面向；在合適語境中可指一面有利、一面不利。不過單獨使用時不一定自動表示『好壞並存』，因此句子需有清楚線索。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The impact of tourism is two-sided: it creates jobs but can damage local culture.

旅遊業的影響具有兩面性：它創造職位，但亦可能損害本地文化。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- two-sided effect

- two-sided impact

- two-sided issue

- two-sided outcome


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

---

## 03 A Mixed Blessing

**有利有弊的事；福禍參半**

非常自然的成語式等義表達，表示某事帶來真正好處，但同時也造成明顯問題或代價。雖然詞性由形容詞轉為名詞片語，語義高度吻合。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Living in the city centre is a mixed blessing: everything is nearby, but it is noisy.

住在市中心福禍參半：一切都很方便，但環境很嘈吵。

## 04 Having Pros And Cons

**有利亦有弊的**

### EXAMPLE · 例句

## 07 Advantageous But Costly

**有利但代價高的**

當好處伴隨金錢、時間、資源、社會或其他明確成本時適合。這是 contextual paraphrase，不是所有 double-edged 情況都涉及 cost。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

對小型公司而言，聘請更多專才有利，但成本高。

## 08 A Trade-Off

**取捨；利弊交換**

當得到某項好處必須犧牲另一項好處、承受成本或接受缺點時非常自然。它不是純形容詞同義詞，而是高價值概念等義表達。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

There is a trade-off between speed and accuracy: improving one may reduce the other.

速度與準確度之間存在取捨：改善其中一項可能削弱另一項。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- trade-off between A and B

- involve a trade-off

- accept a trade-off

- cost-benefit trade-off

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Two-sided

**兩面不一定等於一利一弊**

two-sided 只說有兩個面向；只有語境清楚顯示正面與負面時才接近 double-edged。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The issue is two-sided; one side may be economic and the other social.

---

## 02 Ambiguous

**模糊不等於雙刃**

ambiguous 表示意思或結果不明確；double-edged 是正負效果都真實存在。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

An ambiguous message is unclear; a double-edged policy has benefits and drawbacks.

---

## 03 Mixed

**混合不一定有利有弊**

mixed 可表示由不同成分組成，或評價好壞參半；需要搭配 context 才能等於 double-edged。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The reviews were mixed, but the policy itself may be double-edged.

---

## 04 Risky

**有風險不等於有好處**

risky 只標示危險／不確定性，沒有說明正面作用。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A risky strategy may have no benefit at all.

---

## 05 Trade-off

**相關但結構不同**

trade-off 強調得到一項就犧牲另一項；double-edged 只要求同一事物有好壞兩面，不必是直接交換。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Remote work can be double-edged without every benefit requiring a matching sacrifice.

---

## 06 Mixed blessing

**高度接近但詞性不同**

`a mixed blessing` 是名詞片語；`double-edged` 是形容詞，句型通常要改寫。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The promotion was double-edged; the promotion was a mixed blessing.

---

## 07 Double-edged / double-sided

**不要機械互換**

double-sided 常表示物件有兩面或兩面都可用；未必表示利弊並存。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Double-sided printing uses both sides of the paper.

---

## 08 Double-edged

**另有字面義**

double-edged 也可字面形容刀刃兩邊都有刃。本課聚焦比喻的利弊並存義。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A double-edged sword can be literal or metaphorical.

## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** having both benefits and drawbacks · 既有好處亦有缺點的

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

Remote work can be double-edged for employees.

### 中文翻譯

### OPTIONS


- **A. having both benefits and drawbacks**

- **B. two-sided**

- **C. having pros and cons**

- **D. a mixed blessing**

- **E. purely beneficial**

- **F. entirely harmful**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**having both benefits and drawbacks · 既有好處亦有缺點的**

Remote work can be **something having both benefits and drawbacks** for employees.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. having both benefits and drawbacks ✓** — `having both benefits and drawbacks` = 同一事物同時帶來好處和缺點。 **本句為何最合適：** the statement is deliberately general: the same arrangement has positive and negative effects。

- **C. having pros and cons** — `having pros and cons` = 同時有優點和缺點。 偏一般評估語言；若一面是實際 harm、risk 或明確 cost，專門表達可更準。 **本句線索：** the statement is deliberately general: the same arrangement has positive and negative effects。

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Double-edged vs Having both benefits and drawbacks
最直接、最透明，幾乎不增加額外語義。

## Double-edged vs Two-sided
`two-sided` 只表示有兩個面向；必須由 context 告訴我們那兩面是一利一弊。

## Double-edged vs A mixed blessing
兩者語義非常接近，但詞性不同：
- The promotion was double-edged.
- The promotion was a mixed blessing.

## Double-edged vs Having pros and cons
`pros and cons` 最適合一般選項／制度／安排評估，語氣較日常。

## Double-edged vs Both beneficial and harmful
後者更強，要求負面效果真正構成 harm。

## Double-edged vs Helpful but risky
只有 downside 明確是 risk 時才最精確。

## Double-edged vs Advantageous but costly
只有 downside 明確是 monetary/time/resource cost 時才最精確。

## Double-edged vs A trade-off
`trade-off` 不只是好壞並存，而是**取得一項好處要接受某種犧牲**。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Double-edged
作形容詞：
- a double-edged policy
- a double-edged effect
- the technology is double-edged

## 02 A double-edged sword
最常見的成語式搭配：
- Social media is a double-edged sword.

## 03 Mixed blessing
需要冠詞：
- It is **a** mixed blessing.

## 04 Trade-off
常見結構：
- a trade-off between speed and accuracy
- involve a trade-off
- accept the trade-off

## 05 Double-sided is not the same
`double-sided` 很常是字面：
- double-sided tape
- double-sided printing

它不自動表示利弊並存。

## 06 Literal sense separated
`a double-edged blade/sword` 也可字面表示兩邊都有刃。  
本課的核心 synonym pool 不混入這個物理 sense。
