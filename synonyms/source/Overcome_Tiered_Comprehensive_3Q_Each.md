# OVERCOME · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Overcome

**「克服／戰勝／熬過」，要先分清是戰勝恐懼與成癮、跨過一道障礙、正式克服重大困難、逐步處理情緒，還是突破阻力。**

本單元集中處理 **overcome = to succeed in dealing with, defeating, moving beyond, or gaining control over a difficulty, obstacle, fear, problem, or adverse situation**。你提供的 **conquer; get past; surmount** 分別代表 **強勢戰勝、口語跨過、正式克服** 三個核心方向。

這個動詞有一組語義清楚而實用的替代表達，因此本次保留 **12 個**自然且具教學價值的詞／表達，不追求固定數量。共 **36 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**；正確答案位置在 A–F 間平均分布。

## Tier 1 — Core conquer / get-past / surmount equivalents · 核心戰勝、跨過與克服近義詞

### 01 Conquer

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：克服；戰勝  
**語域**：neutral / strong  
**核心語義**：to successfully defeat or gain control over a difficult problem, fear, weakness, or challenge  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 overcome 的強勢近義詞，尤其適合 fear、addiction、difficulty 等需要『戰勝』的語境。  
**常見搭配**：conquer a fear; conquer difficulties; conquer addiction; conquer self-doubt; conquer a challenge.  
**常見搭配對象**：fear, addiction, weakness, difficulty, self-doubt.  
**與 overcome 的辨析**：Conquer 比 overcome 更強，帶『戰勝／征服』意味。  
**使用限制**：普通小困難若用 conquer 可能顯得過重。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She gradually conquered her fear of public speaking.  
她逐漸克服了公開演講的恐懼。

### 02 Get past

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：熬過；跨過；擺脫  
**語域**：informal / very common  
**核心語義**：to move beyond a problem, difficulty, emotional barrier, or stage so that it no longer prevents progress  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很自然，特別適合口語表示『終於過了這一關』。  
**常見搭配**：get past a problem; get past a setback; get past fear; get past the first stage; get past an obstacle.  
**常見搭配對象**：problem, setback, fear, stage, obstacle.  
**與 overcome 的辨析**：Get past 比 overcome 更口語，也較強調『跨過之後繼續前進』。  
**使用限制**：正式寫作通常用 overcome / surmount。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Once we get past this problem, the rest should be easier.  
一旦我們克服這個問題，其餘部分應會容易得多。

### 03 Surmount

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：克服；越過  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：to overcome a serious difficulty, obstacle, or problem successfully  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，正式書面語中非常接近 overcome。  
**常見搭配**：surmount an obstacle; surmount difficulties; surmount a problem; surmount barriers; successfully surmount.  
**常見搭配對象**：obstacle, difficulty, barrier, problem, challenge.  
**與 overcome 的辨析**：Surmount 比 overcome 更正式，常與 obstacle / difficulty 搭配。  
**使用限制**：日常口語較少使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The team surmounted several technical obstacles.  
團隊克服了數個技術障礙。

### 04 Beat

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：戰勝；克服  
**語域**：informal / common  
**核心語義**：to defeat or overcome a problem, illness, fear, habit, or difficult situation  
**用法提示**：當 overcome 帶『打敗某個問題／疾病／壞習慣』意味時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：beat an illness; beat addiction; beat fear; beat the odds; beat a problem.  
**常見搭配對象**：illness, addiction, fear, odds, habit.  
**與 overcome 的辨析**：Beat 比 overcome 更口語、更有對抗感。  
**使用限制**：正式政策／學術語境通常用 overcome。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He worked hard to beat his addiction.  
他努力克服自己的成癮問題。

### 05 Rise above

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：超越；克服並不受影響  
**語域**：neutral / figurative  
**核心語義**：to overcome a difficult situation, negative emotion, criticism, or background and not be controlled by it  
**用法提示**：當 overcome 不只是解決問題，而是『不讓逆境／負面因素拖住自己』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：rise above adversity; rise above criticism; rise above difficulties; rise above prejudice; rise above circumstances.  
**常見搭配對象**：adversity, criticism, prejudice, hardship, circumstance.  
**與 overcome 的辨析**：Rise above 強調不被負面環境控制。  
**使用限制**：純技術問題通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She rose above years of criticism and continued her work.  
她超越多年的批評，繼續自己的工作。

## Tier 2 — Problem-solving / persistence / gradual-process variants · 問題處理、堅持與逐步克服變體

### 06 Deal with successfully

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：成功處理；妥善應對  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：to handle a problem or difficulty effectively and reach a satisfactory result  
**用法提示**：當 overcome 需要透明改寫成『成功應付／處理』時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：deal with successfully; deal with a problem; deal effectively with; successfully address; handle successfully.  
**常見搭配對象**：problem, issue, challenge, difficulty, conflict.  
**與 overcome 的辨析**：較描述性、語義透明，但沒有 overcome 的『跨越阻礙』意象。  
**使用限制**：若是情緒／恐懼，不一定最自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The team dealt successfully with the unexpected problem.  
團隊成功處理了突發問題。

### 07 Resolve

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：解決；克服問題  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to solve a problem, difficulty, dispute, or issue  
**用法提示**：當 overcome 的結果是『問題被解決』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：resolve a problem; resolve difficulties; resolve a dispute; resolve an issue; resolve obstacles.  
**常見搭配對象**：problem, issue, dispute, conflict, difficulty.  
**與 overcome 的辨析**：Resolve 強調 problem solved；overcome 可表示成功應對，即使問題仍存在。  
**使用限制**：不要把所有 ongoing difficulty 都改成 resolve。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The engineers resolved the technical problem before launch.  
工程師在推出前解決了技術問題。

### 08 Push through

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：撐過；堅持克服  
**語域**：informal / common  
**核心語義**：to continue despite difficulty, tiredness, resistance, or discomfort until something is completed  
**用法提示**：當 overcome 強調『咬緊牙關繼續做到最後』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：push through difficulty; push through pain; push through resistance; push through the final stage; push through setbacks.  
**常見搭配對象**：pain, fatigue, resistance, setback, challenge.  
**與 overcome 的辨析**：Push through 強調持續努力和韌性。  
**使用限制**：若是一次性解決問題而無持續奮鬥，不一定合適。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
They pushed through the final difficulties and finished on time.  
他們撐過最後的困難，按時完成。

### 09 Work through

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：逐步處理並克服  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：to deal with a problem, emotion, or difficulty gradually and systematically  
**用法提示**：當 overcome 是逐步處理、理解和改善，而不是一次突破時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：work through a problem; work through grief; work through difficulties; work through issues; work through conflict.  
**常見搭配對象**：problem, emotion, grief, conflict, difficulty.  
**與 overcome 的辨析**：Work through 比 overcome 更強調 gradual process。  
**使用限制**：若是瞬間突破或單一障礙，surmount / get past 更直接。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She worked through her anxiety with professional support.  
她在專業支援下逐步克服焦慮。

### 10 Break through

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：突破；克服阻力  
**語域**：neutral / figurative  
**核心語義**：to overcome a barrier, resistance, or limitation and make significant progress  
**用法提示**：當 overcome 表示『突破瓶頸／阻力／限制』時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：break through a barrier; break through resistance; breakthrough moment; break through limitations; break through a deadlock.  
**常見搭配對象**：barrier, resistance, limitation, deadlock, obstacle.  
**與 overcome 的辨析**：Break through 強調突破阻隔並進入新階段。  
**使用限制**：一般情緒困難不一定自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The researchers finally broke through the technical barrier.  
研究人員終於突破了技術障礙。

## Tier 3 — Strong victory / formal prevailing variants · 強勢勝利與正式勝過變體

### 11 Triumph over

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：戰勝；成功克服  
**語域**：formal / literary  
**核心語義**：to achieve a clear and often impressive victory over a difficulty, adversity, or negative force  
**用法提示**：當 overcome 帶強烈成功感、逆境後勝出時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：triumph over adversity; triumph over fear; triumph over hardship; triumph over opposition; triumph over illness.  
**常見搭配對象**：adversity, fear, hardship, opposition, illness.  
**與 overcome 的辨析**：比 overcome 更強、更有戲劇性和成功色彩。  
**使用限制**：普通日常小問題不宜。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The community triumphed over years of hardship.  
社區戰勝了多年的困境。

### 12 Prevail over

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：勝過；克服  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：to succeed against a difficulty, opponent, pressure, or negative influence  
**用法提示**：當 overcome 帶『最終勝過某種阻力／壓力』意味時可用。  
**常見搭配**：prevail over difficulties; prevail over opposition; prevail over adversity; prevail over pressure; prevail over resistance.  
**常見搭配對象**：difficulty, opposition, adversity, pressure, resistance.  
**與 overcome 的辨析**：Prevail over 比 overcome 更正式，也更強調最終勝出。  
**使用限制**：主語常是人、力量或因素；一般物件問題未必適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Persistence eventually prevailed over doubt and hesitation.  
堅持最終戰勝了懷疑和猶豫。

## 04 False synonyms / overcome traps · 容易誤選的詞與『克服』概念陷阱

### 01 Avoid

**避開不等於 overcome**  
avoid 是繞開問題；overcome 是面對並成功克服。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Avoiding and overcoming are opposite strategies.

### 02 Ignore

**忽略不等於 overcome**  
ignore 只是沒有理會，問題可能仍然存在。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Ignoring does not solve a difficulty.

### 03 Endure

**忍受不等於 overcome**  
endure 表示承受困難，不一定成功克服。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Endurance is not necessarily victory.

### 04 Accept

**接受不等於 overcome**  
accept 可以和問題共存，而不是消除或跨越它。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Acceptance is different from overcoming.

### 05 Escape

**逃離不等於 overcome**  
escape 表示離開危險／困境，不一定真正處理問題。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Escape avoids or leaves the difficulty.

### 06 Resolve

**只在問題被真正解決 sense 接近**  
overcome 可以成功應對一個仍然存在的困難。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Resolve is solution-focused.

### 07 Conquer

**較強的近義詞**  
conquer 帶戰勝／征服感，強度通常高於 overcome。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Conquer is stronger and more combative.

### 08 Overcome / overcame / overcome

**詞形陷阱**  
現在式 overcome；過去式 overcame；過去分詞 overcome。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The past tense is overcame; the past participle is overcome.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **overcome vs conquer**: conquer 更強、更有戰勝／征服色彩。
- **overcome vs get past**: get past 更口語，強調跨過之後繼續前進。
- **overcome vs surmount**: surmount 更正式，常與 obstacles / difficulties 搭配。
- **overcome vs beat**: beat 更口語，也更有對抗感。
- **overcome vs endure**: endure 只是承受，不保證成功克服。
- **overcome vs resolve**: resolve 強調問題已解決；overcome 可只是成功應對。
- **overcome vs push through**: push through 強調持續努力直至完成。
- **overcome vs work through**: work through 強調逐步處理。
- **overcome vs break through**: break through 強調突破障礙或阻力。
- **overcome vs rise above**: rise above 強調不被逆境或批評控制。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & overcome traps · 文法、搭配與『克服』詞彙陷阱

- **overcome + noun**：overcome a problem / fear / obstacle / difficulty。
- 過去式是 **overcame**；過去分詞是 **overcome**：She overcame it / She has overcome it。
- **conquer + fear/addiction/difficulty**：語氣較強。
- **get past + problem/setback/stage**：口語高頻。
- **surmount + obstacle/difficulty/barrier**：正式書面語。
- **beat + illness/addiction/fear/the odds**：較口語。
- **rise above + adversity/criticism/prejudice**：比喻用法。
- **resolve + problem/issue/dispute**：表示真正解決。
- **push through + pain/resistance/difficulty**：表示撐過去。
- **work through + anxiety/grief/conflict/problem**：表示逐步處理。
- **break through + barrier/resistance/deadlock**：表示突破。
- **triumph over / prevail over + adversity/opposition**：較正式／強勢。
- **avoid / ignore / endure ≠ overcome**：分別是避開、不理、忍受，都不保證克服。
