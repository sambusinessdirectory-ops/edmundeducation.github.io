# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Employee

**僱員、員工、職員、工作者、受薪人士——究竟是哪一種 employee？**

本課聚焦：

**employee = a person who is employed by an organisation or employer, usually in return for wages or a salary**

核心近義與近義表達：

**worker; staff member; member of staff; member of personnel; staffer; team member; member of the workforce; salaried worker; wage earner; company employee; workforce member**

> **Precision rule:**  
> `staff member / member of staff` 最直接。  
> `worker` 範圍較廣。  
> `team member` 強調團隊角色。  
> `member of personnel / workforce member` 較正式。  
> `salaried worker / wage earner` 強調薪酬形式或收入身份。  
> `contractor / freelancer / volunteer` 不應和 employee 混為一談。

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Worker

**工人；工作者；僱員**

和 `employee` 很接近，但範圍更廣。`worker` 可指受僱人士，也可包括某類勞動者或臨時工作者；不一定強調正式僱傭關係。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Every worker must complete the safety training.

每名僱員都必須完成安全培訓。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- factory worker
- office worker
- skilled worker
- worker safety

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否要表達正式僱傭身份、一般工作者身份、團隊角色，還是薪酬／人力統計角度？

# 02 False synonyms

以下詞語都和工作場所相關，但有些代表僱主、外判人士、義工、職位、同事或集合名詞，不能直接當作單數 employee。

## 01 Employer

**僱主不等於僱員**

`employer` 是僱用別人的人／機構；`employee` 是受僱的人。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The employer pays wages; the employee receives them.

---

## 02 Contractor

**承包商不一定是僱員**

`contractor` 常以合約／服務提供者身份工作，法律及福利身份可能與 employee 不同。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A contractor may work for a company without being its employee.

---

## 03 Freelancer

**自由工作者通常不是僱員**

`freelancer` 為多個客戶提供服務，通常不是單一公司的 employee。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A freelancer may serve a client without joining its staff.

---

## 04 Volunteer

**義工通常不屬受薪僱員**

`volunteer` 主要以自願形式工作，通常沒有一般僱員薪酬關係。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A volunteer can work alongside employees without being one.

---

## 05 Manager

**經理只是職位**

manager 可能是 employee，但 employee 不等於 manager。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A manager is one kind of employee in many organisations.

---

## 06 Colleague

**同事不等於僱員身份**

`colleague` 表示一起工作的人，可能屬另一機構、專案團隊或合作單位。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A colleague may work with you without sharing your employer.

---

## 07 Personnel

**集合名詞，不通常指單一個人**

`personnel` 通常指某機構的人員整體；若指個人，較自然說 `member of personnel`。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Personnel are being briefed; one individual is a member of personnel.

---

## 08 Staff

**通常是集合名詞**

`staff` 多數表示全體／一組職員；若指一個人用 `staff member` / `member of staff`。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The staff are helpful; one person is a staff member.

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Employee vs employer
- employee = 受僱的人
- employer = 僱用人的人／機構

## 02 Staff is usually collective
自然：
- The staff are helpful.
- a staff member
- a member of staff

不建議在一般英語把 `a staff` 當作一名員工。

## 03 Worker
`worker` 可以包括：
- employees
- temporary workers
- agency workers
- some contract workers

所以它比 employee 更廣。

## 04 Personnel
`personnel` 通常是集合名詞：
- company personnel
- military personnel
- authorised personnel

單一個人可說：
- a member of personnel

## 05 Staffer
較常見：
- political staffer
- campaign staffer
- government staffer
- senior staffer

不一定適合所有正式 HR 文件。

## 06 Salaried worker vs wage earner
- salaried worker = 固定月薪／年薪
- wage earner = 靠工資收入的人，社經語境較多

## 07 Contractor / freelancer
兩者可能為公司工作，但法律／稅務／福利上通常和 employee 分開處理。

## 08 Be employed by
最直接描述僱傭關係：
`She is employed by a local bank.`
