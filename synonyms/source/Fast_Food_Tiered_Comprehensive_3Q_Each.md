# FAST FOOD · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Fast food

**「快餐／快速供應食品」，要先分清是在談快餐店的服務模式、預製即食餐、外帶食品、方便食品，還是營養上不健康的 junk food。**

本單元集中處理 **fast food = food prepared and served quickly, especially through quick-service restaurants, but sometimes used more loosely for rapidly available meals**。你提供的 **quick-service food; ready-made meals** 分別代表 **餐飲服務模式** 和 **預先製作、快速食用** 兩個重要方向。

這次保留 **13 個**高實用度詞／表達，並按最新 inspection 標準區分 **convenience food / takeaway food / grab-and-go food / prepared food / food to go / quick meal / junk food / highly processed food / quick-service meal**。其中 **junk food**、**highly processed food** 會明確標為相關分類而非真正 universal synonyms。

## Tier 1 — Core quick-service / ready-made equivalents · 核心快餐與即食近義表達

### 01 Quick-service food

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：快速服務食品；快餐  
**語域**：formal / industry  
**核心語義**：food sold by quick-service restaurants, where ordering and serving are designed to be fast  
**用法提示**：和 fast food 最接近的正式／行業說法之一，尤其適合餐飲業、市場分析和商業語境。  
**常見搭配**：quick-service food; quick-service restaurant; quick-service sector; quick-service chain; quick-service meal.  
**常見搭配對象**：restaurant, chain, sector, outlet, meal.  
**與 fast food 的辨析**：Quick-service food 強調服務模式快；fast food 也常暗示特定食物類型。  
**使用限制**：日常對話通常直接說 fast food。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The report compares quick-service food with full-service dining.  
報告比較快速服務食品與全服務餐飲。

### 02 Ready-made meals

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：即食餐；預製餐  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：meals prepared in advance and requiring little or no cooking before eating  
**用法提示**：當 fast food 的重點是『已預先製作、可快速食用』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：ready-made meal; ready meal; pre-prepared meal; ready-made food; heat a ready-made meal.  
**常見搭配對象**：supermarket, fridge, freezer, microwave, convenience store.  
**與 fast food 的辨析**：Ready-made meals 可以來自超市，不一定是 fast-food restaurant。  
**使用限制**：漢堡店現點現做的食品未必是 ready-made meal。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Many commuters buy ready-made meals on the way home.  
很多通勤人士回家途中會購買即食餐。

### 03 Convenience food

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：方便食品；便利食品  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：food prepared or packaged to reduce preparation time and effort  
**用法提示**：當 fast food 放在生活便利、節省煮食時間的語境中非常實用。  
**常見搭配**：convenience food; convenience-food market; packaged convenience food; frozen convenience food; rely on convenience food.  
**常見搭配對象**：consumer, supermarket, household, meal, product.  
**與 fast food 的辨析**：Convenience food 比 fast food 更廣，可包括冷凍食品、即食餐和包裝食品。  
**使用限制**：不是所有 convenience food 都由快餐店出售。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Busy households often rely on convenience food during the week.  
忙碌家庭平日經常依賴方便食品。

### 04 Takeaway food

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：外賣食品；外帶餐  
**語域**：British / common  
**核心語義**：food prepared by a restaurant or food outlet to be eaten elsewhere  
**用法提示**：在香港／英式英語中，當 fast food 的重點是買走食用而非堂食時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：takeaway food; takeaway meal; order a takeaway; takeaway shop; takeaway service.  
**常見搭配對象**：restaurant, shop, meal, order, delivery.  
**與 fast food 的辨析**：Takeaway food 說的是食用方式；很多 takeaway food 並不屬 fast food。  
**使用限制**：正式健康研究中不要把 takeaway 和 fast food 完全等同。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
We ordered takeaway food after working late.  
我們加班後叫了外賣。

### 05 Fast-service meals

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：快速供應餐點  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：meals designed to be prepared and served quickly  
**用法提示**：當需要避免重複 fast food，而又強調快速供應時可作透明改寫。  
**常見搭配**：fast-service meal; quickly served meal; rapid-service food; fast-service outlet; quick meal service.  
**常見搭配對象**：meal, restaurant, outlet, service, customer.  
**與 fast food 的辨析**：這是描述性 near-synonym，不是固定行業術語。  
**使用限制**：自然度不及 quick-service food / fast food，適合解釋性文本。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The station has several outlets selling fast-service meals.  
車站有數個售賣快速供應餐點的店舖。

## Tier 2 — Retail / takeaway / convenience variants · 零售、外帶與便利食品變體

### 06 Grab-and-go food

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：即買即走食品；便攜快食  
**語域**：neutral / retail  
**核心語義**：food packaged and ready for immediate purchase and consumption with minimal waiting  
**用法提示**：當 fast food 指便利店、咖啡店、車站等可直接拿走食用的食品時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：grab-and-go food; grab-and-go meal; grab-and-go section; grab-and-go lunch; grab-and-go options.  
**常見搭配對象**：sandwich, salad, snack, lunch, convenience store.  
**與 fast food 的辨析**：Grab-and-go 強調立即購買與便攜性；fast food 更常與餐飲連鎖和熱食相關。  
**使用限制**：需要坐下等候製作的餐點不算典型 grab-and-go。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The café offers sandwiches and salads in its grab-and-go section.  
咖啡店在即買即走區提供三文治和沙律。

### 07 Pre-prepared food

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：預先製作食品；預製食品  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：food prepared partly or fully before the point of sale or consumption  
**用法提示**：當 fast food 的製作特徵是大量預先準備、到售賣時快速完成時可用。  
**常見搭配**：pre-prepared food; pre-prepared ingredients; pre-prepared meal; commercially prepared food; prepared in advance.  
**常見搭配對象**：meal, ingredient, product, kitchen, outlet.  
**與 fast food 的辨析**：Pre-prepared 描述製作方式，不等於快餐類型。  
**使用限制**：現場新鮮快速烹調的 fast food 不一定 pre-prepared。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The outlet uses pre-prepared food to reduce waiting times.  
店舖使用預製食品以縮短等候時間。

### 08 Prepared food

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：熟食；已製作食品  
**語域**：neutral / retail  
**核心語義**：food that has already been cooked, assembled, or otherwise made ready for eating  
**用法提示**：超市、熟食櫃位和零售語境中常見，可涵蓋很多快速食用食品。  
**常見搭配**：prepared food; prepared-food section; prepared meals; freshly prepared food; prepared-food counter.  
**常見搭配對象**：supermarket, counter, meal, dish, retailer.  
**與 fast food 的辨析**：Prepared food 比 fast food 更廣，可包括較健康或較高檔熟食。  
**使用限制**：不要把所有 prepared food 都視為 fast food。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The supermarket expanded its prepared-food section.  
超市擴充了熟食區。

### 09 Food to go

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：外帶即食食品；便攜食品  
**語域**：British / retail  
**核心語義**：food intended to be bought and eaten away from the place of purchase, often immediately  
**用法提示**：英式零售、車站、咖啡店和便利店語境很自然。  
**常見搭配**：food to go; food-to-go market; food-to-go section; lunch to go; on-the-go food.  
**常見搭配對象**：sandwich, snack, lunch, commuter, retailer.  
**與 fast food 的辨析**：Food to go 強調便攜和外帶；fast food 更偏食物／餐飲類型。  
**使用限制**：堂食快餐不一定叫 food to go。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Demand for food to go is high around major stations.  
大型車站附近對外帶即食食品的需求很高。

### 10 Quick meal

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：快速餐；簡便的一餐  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：a meal that can be prepared, bought, or eaten in a short time  
**用法提示**：當 fast food 只是泛指『快速解決的一餐』，而不需要快餐店含義時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：quick meal; grab a quick meal; quick lunch; quick dinner; prepare a quick meal.  
**常見搭配對象**：lunch, dinner, breakfast, worker, traveller.  
**與 fast food 的辨析**：Quick meal 不帶特定食品類型含義；可是一碗麵或自製三文治。  
**使用限制**：若要討論 fast-food industry，不應用 quick meal 代替。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
We grabbed a quick meal before the train left.  
火車開出前，我們快速吃了一餐。

## Tier 3 — Nutrition / processing / industry-specific variants · 營養、加工與行業專門變體

### 11 Junk food

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：垃圾食品；高糖高脂低營養食品  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：food considered unhealthy because it is high in fat, sugar, salt, or calories and low in nutritional value  
**用法提示**：當 fast food 的重點是營養品質差時常被聯想到，但兩者並非同義。  
**常見搭配**：junk food; junk-food diet; cut down on junk food; junk-food advertising; highly processed junk food.  
**常見搭配對象**：snack, burger, sweets, crisps, sugary drink.  
**與 fast food 的辨析**：Junk food 是營養評價；fast food 是供應／餐飲形式。  
**使用限制**：有些 fast food 可以較健康，有些 junk food 也不是快餐店食品。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Health campaigns often encourage people to cut down on junk food.  
健康宣傳經常鼓勵人們減少進食垃圾食品。

### 12 Highly processed food

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：高度加工食品  
**語域**：health / formal  
**核心語義**：food that has undergone substantial industrial processing and often contains multiple additives or refined ingredients  
**用法提示**：在健康、營養和食品政策語境中，fast food 有時與 highly processed food 重疊。  
**常見搭配**：highly processed food; processed-food intake; highly processed meal; processed food product; reduce processed foods.  
**常見搭配對象**：snack, meal, product, diet, intake.  
**與 fast food 的辨析**：這是加工程度分類，不是 fast food 的真正同義詞。  
**使用限制**：現場新鮮製作的快餐未必高度加工。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Some fast-food items are also highly processed foods.  
部分快餐食品同時屬高度加工食品。

### 13 Quick-service meal

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：快速服務餐；快餐店餐點  
**語域**：formal / industry  
**核心語義**：a meal served through a quick-service restaurant model  
**用法提示**：當要精準指 QSR（quick-service restaurant）提供的一餐時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：quick-service meal; quick-service outlet; quick-service restaurant; quick-service chain; quick-service dining.  
**常見搭配對象**：meal, restaurant, outlet, customer, chain.  
**與 fast food 的辨析**：比 quick-service food 更聚焦一整餐，而不是食品類別。  
**使用限制**：超市即食餐不一定屬 quick-service meal。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The survey compared the price of quick-service meals across several chains.  
調查比較數個連鎖品牌的快餐價格。

## 04 False synonyms / fast-food traps · 容易誤選的詞與快餐陷阱

### 01 Snack

**小食不等於 fast food**  
snack 是餐與餐之間或少量食物；快餐通常可以構成完整一餐。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A snack is small; fast food can be a full meal.

### 02 Takeaway

**外賣不等於 fast food**  
takeaway 描述購買後帶走的方式；外賣可以是任何菜系和價位。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Takeaway describes service mode, not food type.

### 03 Ready meal

**即食餐不等於 fast food**  
ready meal 多指超市／零售預製餐；fast food 常指快餐店快速供應的餐點。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Ready meals and fast food overlap but are not identical.

### 04 Junk food

**垃圾食品不等於 fast food**  
junk food 是營養評價；fast food 是供應形式或餐飲類別。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Junk food concerns nutritional quality, not service speed.

### 05 Street food

**街頭食品不等於 fast food**  
street food 由街頭攤販出售；可以快速，也可以現場慢煮。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Street food is defined by venue, not speed.

### 06 Convenience food

**方便食品比 fast food 更廣**  
便利食品包括冷凍餐、罐頭、即食包裝食品等。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Convenience food includes many products not sold by fast-food outlets.

### 07 Processed food

**加工食品不等於 fast food**  
processed food 是加工程度分類，範圍非常廣。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Processing level and restaurant service format are different categories.

### 08 Fast / fast food

**詞性與結構陷阱**  
fast 是形容詞／副詞；fast food 是固定名詞片語。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Fast describes speed; fast food names a food/service category.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Quick-service food · 快速服務食品；快餐

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The report compares fast food with full-service dining.

**中文翻譯**  
報告比較快餐與全服務餐飲。

- A. quick-service food
- B. snacks
- C. takeaway only
- D. ready meals
- E. street food
- F. processed food

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**quick-service food** · 快速服務食品；快餐

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The report compares quick-service food with full-service dining.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. quick-service food ✓ — full-service dining is an industry contrast, so quick-service food is the precise formal term。**
- **B. snacks — snacks describe portion/meal size, not service model。**
- **C. takeaway only — takeaway only describes where food is eaten。**
- **D. ready meals — ready meals are retail-prepared products, not necessarily restaurant food。**
- **E. street food — street food is defined by selling location。**
- **F. processed food — processed food is a manufacturing category.**

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **fast food vs quick-service food**: quick-service food 更正式、更偏餐飲行業分類。
- **fast food vs ready-made meal**: ready-made meal 通常是超市／零售預製餐。
- **fast food vs convenience food**: convenience food 更廣，重點是節省準備時間。
- **fast food vs takeaway food**: takeaway 描述帶走食用，不是食物類型。
- **fast food vs grab-and-go food**: grab-and-go 強調可立即取走、幾乎不用等。
- **fast food vs food to go**: food to go 偏英式零售和便攜市場語境。
- **fast food vs quick meal**: quick meal 只表示快速吃／準備的一餐。
- **fast food vs junk food**: junk food 是營養評價，兩者不是同義。
- **fast food vs highly processed food**: 高度加工是製作程度分類，不等於快餐。
- **fast food vs street food**: street food 由售賣地點定義，而不是服務速度。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & fast-food traps · 文法、搭配與快餐陷阱

- **fast food** 通常作不可數集合概念：eat fast food / the fast-food industry。
- 作前置修飾語通常加連字號：**fast-food restaurant / fast-food chain**。
- **quick-service restaurant (QSR)** 是餐飲業常見正式術語；其食品可稱 quick-service food / meals。
- **ready-made meal / ready meal** 是可數：a ready-made meal / two ready meals。
- **convenience food** 多作不可數集合概念。
- **takeaway food** 是英式／香港常見；美式常用 takeout food。
- **grab-and-go** 作前置修飾語通常加連字號：grab-and-go lunch。
- **food to go** 在英式零售業很常見；作前置複合詞可見 food-to-go market。
- **junk food ≠ fast food**：不要因快餐常被批評不健康就把兩者完全等同。
- **processed food ≠ fast food**：加工食品包含麵包、罐頭等大量非快餐產品。
- **street food ≠ fast food**：街頭食品可以現做、慢煮，也可以很健康。
- **fast** 本身只是表示速度；**fast food** 是固定名詞片語。
