# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Tired

**疲倦、筋疲力盡、正式疲勞、想睡、累壞、精力耗盡、力氣用盡、昏昏欲睡、長期疲憊不振——究竟是哪一種 tired？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**weary; exhausted; fatigued; sleepy; worn out; drained; spent; drowsy; run-down**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **weary; exhausted; fatigued** 全部保留。  
> 本課特別區分 **疲勞強度、是否想睡、身體 vs 情緒耗損、正式語域、短期 vs 長期不振**。

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Weary

**疲倦的；疲憊的**

較書面、較成熟的近義詞。常形容因長時間工作、旅程、壓力或反覆經歷而感到身心疲憊，語氣比 `tired` 更重、也更持久。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

After walking for hours, the hikers were weary and slow.

走了幾個小時後，遠足者疲憊不堪，腳步也慢了。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- weary traveller

- weary workers

- grow weary

- weary of something


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 tired 是長時間消耗後的 weary、極度 exhausted、正式 fatigued、單純 sleepy、口語 worn out、情緒／精神 drained、能量用盡 spent、藥物／駕駛語境的 drowsy，還是長期 run-down？

## 02 Exhausted

**筋疲力盡的；極度疲倦的**

### EXAMPLE · 例句

I was exhausted after running the half-marathon.

跑完半馬後，我筋疲力盡。

- mentally exhausted

## 09 Run-Down

**疲憊不振的；身體虛弱的**

當 `tired` 不只是當下疲倦，而是因長期壓力、缺睡或輕微生病而整體狀態差、沒有精神時很自然。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Weary

**較持久／較書面**

常見於長途旅程、長時間工作、反覆應付同一問題。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The weary travellers finally arrived.

---

## 02 Exhausted

**比 tired 強很多**

表示能量幾乎耗盡，不應用於輕微疲倦。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

I was exhausted after the marathon.

---

## 03 Fatigued

**正式／醫療／安全語體**

常見於 fatigue management, driver fatigue, clinical contexts。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Fatigued workers are more likely to make mistakes.

---

## 04 Sleepy

**想睡不等於全身疲累**

sleepy 的核心是睡意。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

I feel sleepy because I stayed up late.

---

## 05 Drowsy

**更正式的 sleepiness**

常見於藥物副作用、駕駛安全警告。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

This medicine may make you drowsy.

---

## 06 Drained

**精神／情緒耗盡**

談艱難對話、照顧壓力、社交消耗時特別自然。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The conversation left me drained.

---

## 07 Tired / tiring

**詞性與主客體區別**

`tired` 形容感到疲倦的人；`tiring` 形容令人疲倦的事物。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

I am tired; the journey was tiring.

---

## 08 Tired

**其他搭配**

`tired of` 可表示『厭倦』，不只是身體疲勞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

I'm tired of hearing the same excuse.

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Tired vs Weary
`weary` 更持久，也更常見於：
- long journeys
- repetitive problems
- long working days

## Tired vs Exhausted
`exhausted` 強度遠高於 tired。

## Tired vs Fatigued
`fatigued` 更正式：
- medical
- workplace safety
- aviation
- driving

## Tired vs Sleepy
- tired = 疲累
- sleepy = 想睡

## Tired vs Worn out
`worn out` 是很自然的口語強化。

## Tired vs Drained
`drained` 特別適合：
- emotional stress
- difficult conversations
- caregiving
- social/mental overload

## Tired vs Spent
`spent` 常指劇烈活動後能量全用光。

## Tired vs Drowsy
`drowsy` 常見於：
- medicine side effects
- driving warnings
- reduced alertness

## Tired vs Run-down
`run-down` 是長期、整體性的疲憊不振。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Tired vs tiring
- tired = 感到疲倦
- tiring = 令人疲倦

例如：
- I am tired.
- The journey was tiring.

## 02 Tired of
`be tired of` = 厭倦：
- I'm tired of waiting.
- She's tired of the same excuses.

這個 sense 不等於 physically tired。

## 03 Exhausted
很強，不要用於輕微疲倦。

## 04 Sleepy / drowsy
兩者都偏睡意：
- sleepy = 日常
- drowsy = 較正式、醫療／安全

## 05 Run-down
常和一段時間搭配：
- feel run-down for weeks
- look run-down after a stressful month
