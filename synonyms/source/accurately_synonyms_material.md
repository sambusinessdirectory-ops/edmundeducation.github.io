# Accurately

**正確地、精確地、完全準確地、準確無誤地、如實地、精準地——究竟是哪一種 accurately？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**correctly; precisely; exactly; without error; faithfully; with precision; rightly; factually**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **8 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **correctly; precisely; without error** 全部保留。  
> 本課特別區分一般 correctness、測量／技術 precision、完全一致、零錯誤、忠實重現、正確判斷，以及事實層面的準確性。

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Correctly

**正確地**

最直接、最通用的近義詞之一，表示答案、資訊、操作、判斷或結果是對的，沒有出錯。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

She answered all the questions correctly.

她準確地回答了所有問題。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- answer correctly

- identify correctly

- enter correctly

- calculate correctly


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Precisely

**精密不等於所有 accurately**

precisely 特別適合數值、位置、時間、技術操作；一般『回答正確』通常 correctly 更自然。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She answered correctly; the instrument measured precisely.

---

## 02 Exactly

**通常比 accurately 更強**

exactly 常暗示完全一致，零偏差；accurately 可容許正常測量誤差範圍。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

An accurate measurement may still be rounded; an exact value implies no discrepancy.

---

## 03 Carefully

**小心不保證準確**

carefully 描述做事方式謹慎；結果仍可能出錯。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

He measured carefully but not accurately.

---

## 04 Faithfully

**忠實重現較窄**

faithfully 適合翻譯、肖像、記錄、轉述；不適合一般計算。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The translation faithfully preserves the original meaning.

---

## 05 Objectively

**客觀不等於準確**

objective 描述立場／方法不受偏見影響；資料仍可能不準確。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A report can be objective but factually inaccurate.

---

## 06 Approximately

**方向相反的高價值對比**

approximately 表示大約；accurately / precisely 則強調接近真實或精確值。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The distance is approximately 10 km; it was measured accurately.

---

## 07 Accurate / accurately

**詞性陷阱**

`accurate` 是形容詞；`accurately` 是副詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The result is accurate; the result was recorded accurately.

---

## 08 Accuracy / accurately

**詞性陷阱**

`accuracy` 是名詞；`accurately` 是副詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Accuracy matters; measurements must be taken accurately.

## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** Correctly · 正確地

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

### 中文翻譯

### OPTIONS


- **A. correctly**

- **B. precisely**

- **C. exactly**

- **D. faithfully**

- **E. incorrectly**

- **F. approximately**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**correctly · 正確地**

She answered all the questions **correctly**.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. correctly ✓** — `correctly` = 以正確方式／得到正確結果。 **本句為何最合適：** answers are right rather than merely detailed。

- **B. precisely** — `precisely` = 以高精度、精密程度完成或描述。 強調精密度；單純『答案答對』不一定需要 precisely。 **本句判斷：** answers are right rather than merely detailed。

- **C. exactly** — `exactly` = 與事實、數值、位置或原物完全一致。 強度較高，暗示完全一致；若只需一般正確，可能過強。 **本句判斷：** answers are right rather than merely detailed。

- **D. faithfully** — `faithfully` = 忠實、如實地重現原內容或原物。 要求忠實重現來源；計算、定位、機器操作不適合。 **本句判斷：** answers are right rather than merely detailed。

- **E. incorrectly** — `incorrectly` = 錯誤地。 方向相反。 **本句判斷：** answers are right rather than merely detailed。

- **F. approximately** — `approximately` = 大約地，不是精確地。 只表示大約，和 precisely/exactly 相反。 **本句判斷：** answers are right rather than merely detailed。

# 04 核心對比表

| 表達 | 核心差異 |
|---|---|

| **precisely** | 測量、位置、時間、技術精度高 |

| **without error** | 明確：零錯誤 |
| **faithfully** | 忠實重現、轉述、翻譯 |
| **with precision** | 正式：以高度精準方式操作 |
| **rightly** | 判斷、識別、結論正確 |
| **factually** | 事實內容正確 |

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Accurately vs Correctly
`correctly` 最通用，特別適合 answers / procedures / entries / calculations。

## Accurately vs Precisely
`precisely` 更偏 measurement / positioning / numerical detail。

## Accurately vs Exactly
`exactly` 通常更強，表示完全一致。

## Accurately vs Without error
後者直接把「沒有錯誤」說出來，語義透明但較描述性。

## Accurately vs Faithfully
`faithfully` 適合 translation / representation / reproduction / recording。

## Accurately vs With precision
後者偏工程、外科、技術操作。

## Accurately vs Rightly
`rightly` 適合 judgement / recognition / conclusion。

## Accurately vs Factually
`factually` 只處理 facts，不涵蓋測量或動作精度。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Accurate vs accurately
- accurate = adjective
- accurately = adverb

例如：
- The measurement is accurate.
- The device measures accurately.

## 02 Accuracy
名詞：
- measurement accuracy
- improve accuracy
- high accuracy

## 03 Precisely vs exactly
兩者都可表示非常準確：
- precisely measured
- exactly 10.0 cm

但 `exactly` 更常表「完全一致／確切數值」，`precisely` 更常表「高精度方式」。

## 04 Faithfully
自然搭配：
- faithfully reproduce
- faithfully represent
- faithfully translate

## 05 Factually
適合：
- factually correct
- factually accurate
- factually misleading / inaccurate
