# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Homeschoolers

**本課集中在 `homeschoolers = 主要在家接受教育、而非以一般學校作主要教育場所的學生／學童` 這個意思。**

核心替代表達：

`home-educated students / homeschooled students / home-educated pupils / pupils taught at home / students taught at home / students educated at home / children educated at home / home learners`

> **核心原則**：主要差異在英式／美式用法、students/pupils/children 的年齡感，以及正式教育政策語域 vs 社群內較非正式稱呼。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Home-educated students

**在家接受教育的學生**

正式、中性，英式教育和政策語境尤其自然。

---

## 02 Homeschooled students

**在家自學／接受家庭教育的學生**

美式英語尤其常見，和 homeschoolers 很接近。

---

## 03 Home-educated pupils

**在家接受教育的學童**

偏學齡兒童，英式教育語境自然。

---

## 04 Pupils taught at home

**在家受教的學童**

描述性說法，直接說明學童在家接受教學。

---

## 05 Students taught at home

**在家受教的學生**

較廣，可包括較年長學生。

---

## 06 Students educated at home

**在家接受教育的學生**

較正式，研究、政策語境自然。

---

## 07 Children educated at home

**在家接受教育的兒童**

明確把範圍限制在 children。

---

## 08 Home learners

**在家學習者**

較非正式、社群化，也可能泛指在家學習的人。

---

# 02 False synonyms

- **Homeschoolers / Home-educated students** — 前者較一般；後者較正式，英式教育語境尤其自然。
- **Homeschoolers / Homeschooled students** — 美式英語尤其自然，兩者非常接近。
- **Homeschoolers / Home-educated pupils** — pupils 更偏學齡兒童。
- **Homeschoolers / Distance learners** — 遙距學習不等於家庭教育。
- **Homeschoolers / Online students** — 網上上課只是學習模式之一。
- **Homeschoolers / Independent candidates** — 只表示考試身分，不代表整體教育是在家進行。
- **Homeschoolers / Children at home** — 只表示孩子在家，沒有教育制度含義。

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. Home-educated students ✓**

**C. Students educated at home**  
`Students educated at home`：較正式，研究、政策語境自然。 本句線索是「正式教育政策語境」，但它所強調的年齡層、正式程度、英美用法或社群色彩與本句不完全相同。

**D. Online students**  
`Online students`：網上學生；不一定是 homeschoolers。 本句線索是「正式教育政策語境」，所以不是最精準的 homeschoolers 替代表達。

**E. School pupils**  
`School pupils`：在校學童；通常和 homeschoolers 相對。 本句線索是「正式教育政策語境」，所以不是最精準的 homeschoolers 替代表達。

**F. Private students**

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Home-educated students vs Homeschooled students** — 前者英式正式語域更常見；後者美式日常更自然。
- **Students vs Pupils** — pupils 更偏學齡兒童。
- **Home-educated pupils vs Pupils taught at home** — 前者較像標準稱呼；後者更描述性。
- **Students taught at home vs Students educated at home** — educated at home 更正式、更像整體教育安排。
- **Children educated at home vs Home-educated students** — children 明確限制年齡。
- **Homeschoolers vs Home learners** — home learners 較非正式，而且語義可能更廣。
- **Homeschoolers vs Distance learners** — distance learning 是模式，不等於家庭教育。
- **Homeschoolers vs Online students** — online learning 可以出現在任何教育制度中。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

- `a home-educated student`
- `a homeschooled child`
- `children educated at home`
- `students taught at home`

作前置形容詞時通常用連字號：

> a **home-educated** student

但：

> The student is **educated at home**.

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 正式英式教育語境？ → **home-educated students**
2. 一般美式用法？ → **homeschooled students**
3. 明確是學齡兒童？ → **home-educated pupils**
4. 想直接描述「在家受教」？ → **pupils taught at home**
5. 年齡層較廣？ → **students taught at home**
6. 研究／政策正式描述？ → **students educated at home**
7. 明確指兒童？ → **children educated at home**
8. 社群內較非正式稱呼？ → **home learners**

# 10 Final note

核心替代表達：

`home-educated students / homeschooled students / home-educated pupils / pupils taught at home / students taught at home / students educated at home / children educated at home / home learners`

最重要的 near-miss：

`distance learners / online students / independent candidates / school pupils`

尤其要記住：

> **homeschooler** = 主要接受家庭教育的人  
> **distance learner** = 遙距學習者，未必是 homeschooler  
> **online student** = 網上學習者，也未必是 homeschooler
