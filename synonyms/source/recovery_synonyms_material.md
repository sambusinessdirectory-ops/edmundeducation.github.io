# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Recovery

**康復、癒合、休養、好轉、復康、恢復健康——究竟是哪一種 recovery？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**healing; recuperation; convalescence; improvement; return to health; regaining health; rehabilitation; getting better; restoration of health**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。
> 本課聚焦 health / medical sense，不處理 economic recovery / data recovery 等其他 sense。

---

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 healing

**康復；癒合；復原**

強調傷口、組織、身體或心理創傷逐步修復。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Her healing from the burns took several months.

她的燒傷癒合和康復花了數個月。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- wound healing

- healing process

- promote healing

- healing after surgery


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 是傷口／組織癒合、病後休養、康復期、病情好轉、重新恢復健康，還是透過訓練恢復功能？

---

## 02 recuperation

**休養康復；恢復體力**

較正式，強調病後、術後或疲勞後透過休息恢復健康和體力。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

He spent two weeks at home during his recuperation from the operation.

他手術後在家休養了兩星期。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- period of recuperation

- rest and recuperation

- post-operative recuperation

- time for recuperation


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 是傷口／組織癒合、病後休養、康復期、病情好轉、重新恢復健康，還是透過訓練恢復功能？

---

## 03 convalescence

**康復期；休養期**

較正式、醫療語域較強，指重病、手術或受傷後逐漸恢復的一段時期。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The patient remained under medical supervision during convalescence.

病人在康復期內仍接受醫療監察。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- period of convalescence

- during convalescence

- post-surgical convalescence

- convalescence after illness

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Treatment

**治療不等於 recovery**

treatment 是醫療干預／方法；recovery 是恢復過程／結果。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Treatment may support recovery, but treatment is not the recovery itself.

---

## 02 Rehabilitation

**復康只是特定 recovery 類型**

rehabilitation 通常包括物理、職業、語言或功能訓練，不是所有疾病後康復都需要。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A patient may recover from a cold without rehabilitation.

---

## 03 Improvement

**好轉不等於完全 recovery**

improvement 只表示比之前好；病人仍可能未恢復健康。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Symptoms can improve before full recovery.

---

## 04 Healing

**癒合不等於所有 recovery**

healing 特別常用於傷口、組織、骨折或創傷修復。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A wound heals; a patient recovers.

---

## 05 Remission

**緩解不等於 recovery**

remission 表示疾病症狀／徵象減輕或消失，但不一定等於治癒。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A disease can be in remission without complete recovery.

---

## 06 Cure

**治癒不等於 recovery process**

cure 表示疾病被治好／治療方法；recovery 是患者恢復的過程。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A cure removes the disease; recovery describes the patient's return to health.

---

## 07 Relapse

**復發是反方向概念**

relapse 表示改善後病情再次惡化。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A relapse interrupts or reverses recovery.

---

## 08 Recover / recovery

**詞性陷阱**

`recover` 是動詞；`recovery` 是名詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She recovered quickly; her recovery was quick.

**Target expression:** healing · 康復；癒合；復原

### 中文翻譯

- **D. rehabilitation**

- **E. injury**

- **F. infection**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. healing ✓** — `healing` = 傷口、組織或創傷逐步修復。 **句中線索：** burns 指組織受損，重點是身體逐步癒合。

- **D. rehabilitation** — `rehabilitation` = 透過治療和訓練恢復功能／能力。 需要治療／功能訓練語境；一般感冒後休息恢復不一定稱 rehabilitation。 **本句判斷：** burns 指組織受損，重點是身體逐步癒合。

- **E. injury** — `injury` = 受傷本身。 是受傷本身，不是康復。 **本句判斷：** burns 指組織受損，重點是身體逐步癒合。

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Recovery vs Healing
`healing` 偏組織、傷口、身體或心理創傷修復。

## Recovery vs Recuperation
`recuperation` 偏病後／術後休養和恢復體力。

## Recovery vs Convalescence
`convalescence` 特別指一段康復期。

## Recovery vs Improvement
`improvement` 只表示好轉，未必完全康復。

## Recovery vs Return to health
後者直接表示回到健康狀態。

## Recovery vs Regaining health
後者強調逐步重新取得健康。

## Recovery vs Rehabilitation
後者強調治療、訓練和功能恢復。

## Recovery vs Getting better
後者是非常自然的日常口語表達。

## Recovery vs Restoration of health
後者較正式／書面。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## Recovery from
- recovery from surgery
- recovery from illness
- recovery from an injury

## Recovery after
- recovery after treatment
- recovery after surgery

## Recovery vs improvement
`improvement` 可以只是部分好轉；`recovery` 通常包含更廣的恢復過程。

## Recovery vs rehabilitation
`rehabilitation` 通常需要有結構的治療／訓練，例如 physiotherapy、speech therapy、occupational therapy。

## Recovery vs convalescence
`convalescence` 特別指一段休養和逐步恢復的時期。

## Other senses excluded
- economic recovery
- data recovery
- debt recovery
- recovery of lost property
