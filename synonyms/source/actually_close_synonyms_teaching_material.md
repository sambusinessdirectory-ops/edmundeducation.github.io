# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Actually

**「其實／事實上」，有哪些真正接近、幾乎可以互換的說法？**

> 本課只保留和 **actually** 在「其實／事實上／實際上」這個 discourse-marker 意思上**非常接近或常可互換**的表達。  
> 不再為了湊固定數量而加入只有部分相關的詞。

---

## 01 Close synonyms

### 01 In fact

**事實上；其實**

和 `actually` 最接近、最常互換的表達之一。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

I thought the shop was closed, but in fact it was still open.

我原以為商店關門了，但其實它仍然營業。

**COMPARE**

I thought the shop was closed, but **actually** it was still open.  
I thought the shop was closed, but **in fact** it was still open.

兩句都很自然。

---

### 02 Really

**其實；真正地；真的**

在口語中，`really` 常可表達 `actually` 的「真正情況／真正想法」。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

I don't really want to leave yet.

其實我還不想離開。

**COMPARE**

What I **actually** need is more time.  
What I **really** need is more time.

兩者非常接近；`really` 更口語。

---

### 03 In reality

**實際上；現實中**

當表面印象、理論或想像與真實情況不同時，非常接近 `actually`。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

The job looked easy, but in reality it was extremely demanding.

這份工作看起來很容易，但實際上要求非常高。

**COMPARE**

It looked easy, but **actually** it was difficult.  
It looked easy, but **in reality** it was difficult.

`in reality` 更強調「表面 vs 真實」。

---

### 04 As a matter of fact

**事實上；其實**

非常接近 `in fact`，也常可代替 `actually` 來補充或糾正事實。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

I don't dislike the idea. As a matter of fact, I think it's quite good.

我並不討厭這個想法。其實，我覺得它相當不錯。

**COMPARE**

Actually, I think it's quite good.  
As a matter of fact, I think it's quite good.

後者稍長，也較有「事實上」的語氣。

---

### 05 Indeed

**的確；確實；事實上**

在加強或確認前面說法時，和 `actually / in fact` 很接近。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

The task was difficult. Indeed, several experienced staff members struggled with it.

這項任務很困難。事實上，幾名有經驗的員工也應付得很吃力。

**COMPARE**

The task was difficult. **In fact**, several experts struggled.  
The task was difficult. **Indeed**, several experts struggled.

`indeed` 更正式，也更偏向「加強／證實」。

---

### 06 In actual fact

**實際上；事實上**

比 `in fact` 稍正式、稍強調真實情況。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

The building looks ancient, but in actual fact it is only twenty years old.

這棟建築看起來很古老，但實際上只有二十年歷史。

**COMPARE**

Actually, it is only twenty years old.  
In actual fact, it is only twenty years old.

意思非常接近。

---

### 07 In truth

**事實上；真正而言**

較正式或文學化，但在揭示真正情況時與 `actually` 很接近。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

He appeared confident, but in truth he was very nervous.

他看起來很自信，但其實非常緊張。

**COMPARE**

He looked confident, but **actually** he was nervous.  
He looked confident, but **in truth** he was nervous.

`in truth` 更書面。

---

### 08 In point of fact

**事實上；確切而言**

非常正式，常見於辯論、法律或正式評論。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

The article suggests that costs increased. In point of fact, they remained almost unchanged.

文章似乎表示成本上升；事實上，成本幾乎沒有變化。

**COMPARE**

Actually, costs remained unchanged.  
In point of fact, costs remained unchanged.

語義非常接近，但正式程度差很多。

---

### 09 As it happens

**其實；碰巧事實是**

當要補充一個意外或相關的事實時，和 `actually` 很接近。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

As it happens, I already know the person you're talking about.

其實，我已經認識你所說的那個人。

**COMPARE**

Actually, I already know her.  
As it happens, I already know her.

`as it happens` 多一點「碰巧／原來」的感覺。

---

### 10 The fact is

**事實是；其實真正情況是**

當要直接指出真實情況時，可和句首 `actually` 很接近。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

The fact is, we don't have enough time to finish today.

事實是，我們今天沒有足夠時間完成。

**COMPARE**

Actually, we don't have enough time.  
The fact is, we don't have enough time.

`the fact is` 更強、更直接。

---

## 02 最接近程度

### 幾乎可直接互換

- **in fact**
- **as a matter of fact**
- **in actual fact**

### 很接近，但語氣／位置略有不同

- **really**
- **in reality**
- **indeed**
- **in truth**
- **in point of fact**

### 只在某些語境很接近

- **as it happens**
- **the fact is**

---

## 03 False friends / Not close enough

以下詞和 `actually` 有時會出現在相似語境，但不應視為近乎可互換的 synonym。

### Apparently

`apparently` = 看來／據說。  
它通常表示**不完全確定**，而 `actually` 表示真實情況。

> Apparently, he left.  
> Actually, he stayed.

---

### Currently

`currently` = 目前。  
和 `actually` 完全不同。

> Currently, she works in London.  
> Actually, she works in finance.

---

### Basically

`basically` = 基本上／簡單來說。  
它用來概括，不是揭示真實情況。

---

### Obviously

`obviously` = 顯然。  
它表示事情容易看出，不是「其實」。

---

### Literally

`literally` = 按字面地。  
不是一般性的「事實上」。

## 04 練習與解釋

> 這一組練習只在真正接近的表達之間做辨析。

### 練習 01

I thought the meeting was at nine, but ___ it starts at ten.

A. actually  
B. in fact  
C. in reality  
D. as a matter of fact

**Best answer:** **actually**

**Explanation:**  
四個都可能成立，但 `actually` 最自然地用來直接修正原先錯誤認知。

---

### 練習 02

The task was difficult. ___, even the most experienced staff struggled.

A. really  
B. indeed  
C. actually  
D. as it happens

**Best answer:** **indeed**

**Explanation:**  
後句是在進一步證實前句，而不是更正它，所以 `indeed` 最精確。

---

### 練習 03

The plan looks simple on paper, but ___ it is extremely complicated.

A. in reality  
B. indeed  
C. as it happens  
D. the fact is

**Best answer:** **in reality**

**Explanation:**  
這裡是典型的「表面／理論 vs 真實情況」。

---

### 練習 04

I don't dislike the proposal. ___, I think it's one of the better options.

A. in point of fact  
B. as a matter of fact  
C. in reality  
D. really

**Best answer:** **as a matter of fact**

**Explanation:**  
第二句補充一個比第一句更強的事實：不只是「不討厭」，而是「覺得很好」。

---

### 練習 05

What I ___ need is a few more days.

A. indeed  
B. really  
C. in fact  
D. in point of fact

**Best answer:** **really**

**Explanation:**  
`really` 在句中自然表示「真正需要」。

---

### 練習 06

He seemed relaxed, but ___ he was extremely worried.

A. in truth  
B. indeed  
C. as a matter of fact  
D. the fact is

**Best answer:** **in truth**

**Explanation:**  
較正式地揭示外表背後的真實情況。

---

### 練習 07

The report says output fell sharply. ___, it remained almost unchanged.

A. as it happens  
B. in point of fact  
C. really  
D. indeed

**Best answer:** **in point of fact**

**Explanation:**  
正式、精確地糾正前面的說法。

---

### 練習 08

___, I already know the person you're talking about.

A. As it happens  
B. Indeed  
C. In reality  
D. In point of fact

**Best answer:** **As it happens**

**Explanation:**  
有「碰巧原來如此」的意味。

---

### 練習 09

___, we don't have enough money to continue.

A. The fact is  
B. Really  
C. As it happens  
D. Indeed

**Best answer:** **The fact is**

**Explanation:**  
直接引出核心現實問題。

---

### 練習 10

The building looks old, but ___ it was completed only ten years ago.

A. in actual fact  
B. indeed  
C. really  
D. as it happens

**Best answer:** **in actual fact**

**Explanation:**  
明確修正外觀造成的錯誤印象。

---

## 05 核心對比速查

| Expression | Closest meaning | Main nuance |
|---|---|---|
| in fact | 事實上 | 最接近、最通用 |
| really | 其實／真正 | 口語、內心真實 |
| in reality | 實際上 | 表面 vs 現實 |
| as a matter of fact | 事實上 | 補充更強事實 |
| indeed | 的確 | 加強／證實 |
| in actual fact | 實際上 | 更強調真實情況 |
| in truth | 真正而言 | 正式／書面 |
| in point of fact | 確切而言 | 非常正式 |
| as it happens | 其實碰巧 | 意外／碰巧事實 |
| the fact is | 事實是 | 直接指出核心真相 |

---

## 06 教學核心

如果只保留真正接近的說法，可以把 **actually** 理解為幾個主要方向：

- 一般「其實／事實上」→ **in fact**
- 口語「真正／其實」→ **really**
- 「現實其實是」→ **in reality**
- 補充更強事實 → **as a matter of fact**
- 加強前句 → **indeed**
- 正式強調真實情況 → **in actual fact / in truth / in point of fact**
- 意外發現／碰巧 → **as it happens**
- 直接揭示核心真相 → **the fact is**

核心不是把所有 discourse markers 都塞進 synonym list，而是保留那些在實際句子中**真的很容易互換**的表達。
