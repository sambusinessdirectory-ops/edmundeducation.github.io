# PRIMARY SCHOOL · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Primary school

**「小學」是一個教育制度詞，真正的近義詞高度受地區和學制影響。最重要的是分清英式／香港的 primary school、北美的 elementary school，以及美式較口語的 grade school。**

本單元集中處理 **primary school = a school providing the first main stage of formal education for children**。你提供的 **elementary school; grade school** 分別代表 **北美標準稱呼** 和 **美式較口語稱呼**。

由於不同國家的年級劃分不完全相同，本次只保留 **8 個**有實際教學價值的近義／情境表達，而且會把 system-specific 或 descriptive paraphrases 清楚標示。共 **24 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**；不會暗示所有詞在每個國家都完全等值。

## Tier 1 — Core regional / formal equivalents · 核心地區與正式對應詞

### 01 Elementary school

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：小學；初等學校  
**語域**：North American English / very common  
**核心語義**：a school providing the first years of formal education for children, broadly equivalent to a primary school  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **primary school** 最直接的北美英語對應詞。美國及加拿大常用 elementary school，而英式／香港英語通常用 primary school。  
**常見搭配**：elementary school student; elementary school teacher; elementary education; attend elementary school; local elementary school.  
**常見搭配對象**：student, teacher, child, education, district.  
**與 primary school 的辨析**：和 primary school 在教育階段上高度接近，主要差別是地區用語。  
**使用限制**：實際涵蓋年級會因教育制度而異，因此不能假設所有國家的年級完全相同。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She teaches science at a local elementary school.  
她在一間本地小學教授科學。

### 02 Grade school

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：小學；年級學校  
**語域**：American English / informal-common  
**核心語義**：an American term for a school covering the early grades of compulsory education  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 在美式英語中很自然，尤其口語中可指 elementary school。  
**常見搭配**：grade school student; grade school teacher; attend grade school; grade-school years; grade-school children.  
**常見搭配對象**：child, teacher, student, years, education.  
**與 primary school 的辨析**：Grade school 通常比 elementary school 更口語；兩者在美國語境中常高度重疊。  
**使用限制**：英式／香港正式寫作通常不用 grade school。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
They became friends when they were in grade school.  
他們讀小學時成為朋友。

### 03 Primary-level school

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：小學階段學校；初等教育學校  
**語域**：formal / international / descriptive  
**核心語義**：a school that provides education at the primary level  
**用法提示**：在國際教育、比較教育或跨國文件中，如果需要避免某一地區專用詞，可以用這種透明描述。  
**常見搭配**：primary-level school; primary-level education; school at primary level; primary-level pupils; primary-level provision.  
**常見搭配對象**：school, pupil, provision, curriculum, education.  
**與 primary school 的辨析**：語義與 primary school 很接近，但更像描述性術語，而不是最自然的日常職稱。  
**使用限制**：日常英語通常直接說 primary school / elementary school。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The project supports primary-level schools in rural areas.  
這個項目支援鄉郊地區的小學。

### 04 Primary education school

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：初等教育學校；小學  
**語域**：formal / descriptive  
**核心語義**：a school whose main function is to provide primary education  
**用法提示**：適合較正式、解釋性或國際教育語境，用來說明學校所處的教育階段。  
**常見搭配**：primary education school; primary education sector; primary education provision; primary school system.  
**常見搭配對象**：education, sector, provision, curriculum, institution.  
**與 primary school 的辨析**：這是功能描述，不如 primary school / elementary school 地道簡潔。  
**使用限制**：不宜在自然口語中大量使用；更常見的是 primary school。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The charity helped rebuild a primary education school after the storm.  
慈善機構協助重建一間在風暴中受損的小學。

## Tier 2 — Age-based / research / system-specific variants · 年齡、研究與制度特定變體

### 05 School for primary-age children

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：為小學年齡兒童而設的學校  
**語域**：neutral / explanatory  
**核心語義**：a school serving children in the age range normally associated with primary education  
**用法提示**：當讀者來自不同教育制度，或需要避開年級差異時，這種解釋性改寫很清楚。  
**常見搭配**：school for primary-age children; primary-age pupils; children of primary-school age; primary-age education.  
**常見搭配對象**：child, pupil, school, age group, education.  
**與 primary school 的辨析**：它從學生年齡來解釋 primary school，而不是使用固定學校名稱。  
**使用限制**：較冗長，只適合說明或跨制度比較。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The programme provides books to schools for primary-age children.  
這個計劃向為小學年齡兒童而設的學校提供書籍。

### 06 Primary education setting

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：小學教育環境；初等教育場所  
**語域**：formal / education / research  
**核心語義**：an educational setting in which primary-level teaching and learning take place  
**用法提示**：研究、教育政策或教師培訓語境中，可用來泛指 primary school 這類教育環境。  
**常見搭配**：primary education setting; classroom setting; educational setting; primary setting; learning environment.  
**常見搭配對象**：teacher, classroom, pupil, curriculum, intervention.  
**與 primary school 的辨析**：它比 primary school 更廣，可指學校內的教育環境，而不一定是一間具體學校。  
**使用限制**：不是嚴格機構同義詞；屬研究／政策語境的 contextual variant。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The teaching strategy was tested in several primary education settings.  
這種教學策略在多個小學教育環境中進行測試。

### 07 Junior school

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：初級小學；小學高年級學校  
**語域**：British English / system-specific  
**核心語義**：a school or department for older primary-age children, traditionally around ages seven to eleven in parts of the UK  
**用法提示**：在英國某些制度中，junior school 屬 primary education 的一部分，因此在特定語境中與 primary school 有重疊。  
**常見搭配**：junior school pupil; junior school teacher; infant and junior school; junior department; junior years.  
**常見搭配對象**：pupil, teacher, school, department, age group.  
**與 primary school 的辨析**：Junior school 不是完整 primary school 的全面同義詞；它通常只涵蓋較高的小學年級。  
**使用限制**：只可在英國特定制度／年齡分段語境使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He moved from the infant school to the junior school at age seven.  
他七歲時由幼兒學校升讀初級小學。

## Tier 3 — Cross-system descriptive variant · 跨制度描述性變體

### 08 Elementary-level school

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：初等程度學校；小學階段學校  
**語域**：formal / descriptive  
**核心語義**：a school operating at the elementary or first stage of formal schooling  
**用法提示**：可在跨國報告、統計或教育比較中，作較中性的階段型描述。  
**常見搭配**：elementary-level school; elementary-level education; elementary-level pupils; school-level comparison.  
**常見搭配對象**：school, student, education, curriculum, system.  
**與 primary school 的辨析**：是描述性 paraphrase，不是日常固定用語；可幫助避免地區詞彙差異。  
**使用限制**：自然口語中優先 elementary school / primary school。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The survey included both secondary and elementary-level schools.  
調查包括中學和小學階段學校。

## 04 False synonyms / primary-school traps · 容易誤選的教育階段與制度陷阱

### 01 Secondary school

**中學 ≠ primary school**  
secondary school 是小學之後的教育階段。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Secondary school belongs to a later stage of education.

### 02 Middle school

**初中／中間學校 ≠ primary school**  
middle school 通常介乎 elementary 與 high school 之間。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Middle school is a separate stage in many systems.

### 03 Kindergarten

**幼稚園／學前班 ≠ primary school**  
kindergarten 屬幼兒／學前或小學前段，制度差異很大。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Kindergarten is not a general synonym for primary school.

### 04 Nursery school

**幼兒園 ≠ primary school**  
nursery school 面向學前幼兒。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Nursery school is pre-primary.

### 05 Preschool

**學前教育 ≠ primary school**  
preschool 明確指正式小學前的教育。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Preschool comes before primary education.

### 06 Prep school

**prep school ≠ 一般 primary school**  
在英國可指私立預備學校，在美國則常指 college-preparatory school；含義高度地區化。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Prep school is system- and region-specific.

### 07 Junior school

**只在英國特定小學高年級 sense 接近**  
junior school 常只是 primary education 的一部分。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Junior school is not a full synonym in every system.

### 08 Primary / primarily

**詞性陷阱**  
primary 可作形容詞；primarily 是副詞，意思是『主要地』。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Primary and primarily have different grammatical roles.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **primary school vs elementary school**: 教育階段高度接近；主要差別是地區慣例。
- **primary school vs grade school**: grade school 是美式較口語的說法。
- **primary school vs junior school**: junior school 通常只涵蓋較高的小學年級，並非完整同義詞。
- **primary school vs kindergarten**: kindergarten 屬更早階段或小學最初一年，制度差異大。
- **primary school vs nursery school**: nursery school 是學前幼兒教育。
- **primary school vs middle school**: middle school 屬更後的中間教育階段。
- **primary school vs secondary school**: secondary school 是小學之後的階段。
- **primary school vs prep school**: prep school 地區差異極大，不能直接當作一般小學同義詞。

## 08 Grammar, regional usage & school-system traps · 文法、地區用法與學制陷阱

- 英式／香港英語通常用 **primary school**；北美通常用 **elementary school**。
- 美式 **grade school** 較口語，尤其常見於回憶童年學校生活。
- **primary school pupil** 是英式／香港自然搭配；北美更常說 **elementary school student**。
- **primary education** 是教育階段／領域，不等於一間具體學校。
- **junior school** 在英國部分制度中只指較高的小學年級。
- **kindergarten / preschool / nursery school** 都不能一概視為 primary school。
- 不同國家的入學年齡、年級名稱和 primary/elementary 覆蓋範圍可能不同，因此跨國寫作應避免假設完全等值。
- **primary** 可作形容詞：primary education / primary school；**primarily** 是副詞，意思是『主要地』。
- 英式／香港拼寫保持 **programme, organisation** 等形式。
