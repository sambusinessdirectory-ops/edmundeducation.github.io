# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Become

**「變成／成為」，究竟是在說突然轉變、逐步成長、狀態改變，還是最後成為某種身份？**

`become` 是一個極廣泛的變化動詞，可以表示：

- **直接變成另一種狀態／形式** → `turn into`, `change into`
- **逐步成長／發展成** → `grow into`, `develop into`, `evolve into`, `mature into`
- **口語上的狀態改變** → `get`
- **明顯變得某種狀態** → `turn`, `grow`
- **經時間後逐漸成為／被視為** → `come to be`, `come to become`
- **深刻改造成另一種形式** → `transform into`
- **最後結果成為** → `end up as`
- **逐漸建立某種地位** → `emerge as`

> **核心原則**
>
> 這一組按照「close synonyms → broadly usable near-synonyms → common expressions / stronger variants」整理，但不顯示 tier。
>
> 真正需要分辨的是：
>
> **Is the change sudden, gradual, developmental, final, recognized by others, or simply a change of state?**

# 01 同義詞指南

## Become 的 14 個近義／近乎可互換說法

## 01 Turn into

**變成；轉變成**

`become` 最直接、最常見的近義表達之一。強調某人或某物由一種狀態、形式或身份轉變成另一種。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The small workshop turned into a successful company.

這間小工場後來變成一間成功的公司。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- turn into a problem
- turn into reality
- turn into a habit
- turn into something else

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **turn into = change from one state, form, or identity into another**

**REGISTER · 語域**

非常常用；日常、一般寫作

---

## 02 Grow into

**逐漸成為；長成**

強調隨時間逐步發展，最後成為某種人物、角色、組織或狀態。常用於人、企業、能力或關係。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She grew into a confident leader.

她逐漸成長為一位有自信的領袖。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- grow into a role
- grow into a leader
- grow into a business
- grow into adulthood

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **grow into = gradually develop until becoming something**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性；人物成長、商業、一般寫作

---

## 03 Develop into

**發展成；逐步變成**

和 `grow into` 很接近，但更廣泛、更正式，可用於疾病、問題、關係、組織、想法或情況。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The disagreement developed into a serious conflict.

這場分歧逐漸發展成嚴重衝突。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- develop into a problem
- develop into a crisis
- develop into a relationship
- develop into a major industry

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **develop into = gradually change or progress until becoming something else**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性至正式；新聞、學術、一般寫作

---

## 04 Come to be

**後來成為；逐漸被視為**

較書面、敘事性的表達，強調經過一段時間後『成為』某種狀態、身份或被人看待的方式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The building came to be regarded as a symbol of the city.

這座建築後來被視為城市的象徵。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- come to be known as
- come to be regarded as
- come to be seen as
- come to be accepted as

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **come to be = eventually become, often over time or through changing perception**

**REGISTER · 語域**

書面、敘事、正式

---

## 05 Get

**變得；成為**

當 `become` 後接形容詞，尤其描述日常狀態改變時，口語常直接用 `get`。例如 `get tired`, `get angry`, `get cold`。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> It is getting colder outside.

外面正變得愈來愈冷。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- get tired
- get angry
- get better
- get worse
- get ready

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **get = become, especially before adjectives in everyday English**

**REGISTER · 語域**

非常常用；口語、非正式至中性

---

## 06 Turn

**變得；變成**

當 `become` 後接顏色、情緒、狀態或某些形容詞時，`turn` 很自然，尤其是明顯的狀態改變。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The sky turned dark within minutes.

天空幾分鐘內變暗了。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- turn red
- turn cold
- turn sour
- turn serious
- turn violent

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **turn = become, especially when a state changes noticeably**

**REGISTER · 語域**

非常常用；一般寫作、敘事

---

## 07 Grow

**漸漸變得**

可直接接形容詞，表示某種狀態逐漸變強或變明顯，常見於 `grow tired`, `grow concerned`, `grow impatient`。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Residents grew increasingly concerned about the delays.

居民對延誤愈來愈擔心。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- grow tired
- grow concerned
- grow impatient
- grow stronger
- grow increasingly

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **grow = gradually become more of a particular quality or state**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性至書面；新聞、敘事、正式寫作

---

## 08 Change into

**變成；轉化成**

和 `turn into` 很接近，直接表示從一種形式、物質、身份或狀態轉換成另一種。比 `become` 更強調『改變』本身。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Water changes into ice when it freezes.

水結冰時會變成冰。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- change into a different form
- change into ice
- change into something else
- change into a new state

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **change into = transform from one form or state into another**

**REGISTER · 語域**

非常常用；一般、科學、教育

---

## 09 Evolve into

**演變成；逐步發展成**

強調長時間、逐步、通常較複雜的變化。常用於制度、技術、物種、想法、企業或社會現象。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The website evolved into a global platform.

這個網站逐步演變成一個全球平台。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- evolve into a system
- evolve into a platform
- evolve into a new form
- gradually evolve into

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **evolve into = gradually develop through a series of changes into something new**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性至正式；科技、商業、學術

---

## 10 Mature into

**成熟成；發展成**

當 `become` 強調經過成長、學習或時間後變得成熟、完整或成功時，`mature into` 很貼切。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The young musician matured into a highly accomplished performer.

這位年輕音樂家逐漸成熟，成為一位非常出色的表演者。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- mature into adulthood
- mature into a leader
- mature into a successful business
- mature into a stable system

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **mature into = develop over time into a more complete, capable, or established form**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性至正式；人物、商業、發展

---

## 11 Transform into

**轉化成；徹底變成**

比 `become` 更強，通常表示明顯、深刻或徹底的轉變。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The old factory was transformed into a cultural centre.

舊工廠被改造成文化中心。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- transform into a new space
- transform into a major industry
- transform into something useful
- be transformed into

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **transform into = change dramatically into a different form, function, or identity**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性至正式；設計、商業、敘事

---

## 12 End up as

**最後成為；結果變成**

強調經過一連串事件後，最後得到某個身份、結果或狀態，常帶有『未必一開始預計如此』的意味。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The temporary solution ended up as a permanent system.

這個臨時方案最後變成永久制度。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- end up as a manager
- end up as a problem
- end up as the winner
- end up as part of

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **end up as = eventually become something as the final result**

**REGISTER · 語域**

日常至中性

---

## 13 Emerge as

**逐漸成為；嶄露頭角成為**

當 `become` 指一個人、組織、國家或事物經過發展後，逐漸被認為是某種重要角色或地位時，`emerge as` 很自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The company emerged as a leader in battery technology.

這間公司逐漸成為電池技術領域的領導者。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- emerge as a leader
- emerge as a major force
- emerge as the winner
- emerge as a key player

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **emerge as = gradually become recognized or established as something**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性至正式；新聞、商業、分析

---

## 14 Come to become

**逐漸成為；後來成為**

較少見但自然的敘事性表達，強調隨時間推移逐步成為某種人物、角色或狀態。通常比單純 `become` 更有過程感。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He came to become one of the most trusted figures in the organization.

他後來逐漸成為組織中最受信任的人物之一。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- come to become known as
- come to become a leader
- come to become part of

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **come to become = gradually or eventually become over time**

**REGISTER · 語域**

較書面、敘事；頻率較低

# 02 False synonyms

## 01 Become / Be

### 狀態變化不等於單純存在

`be` 只描述某個狀態。  
`become` 表示由原本狀態轉變到新狀態。

> He is tired.  
> He became tired.

---

## 02 Become / Get

### 很接近，但 get 更口語

`get` 最常用於：

> get tired  
> get angry  
> get better  
> get worse

但不能把所有 `become + noun` 都改成 `get`。

---

## 03 Become / Turn into

### 直接轉變不一定等於所有 become

`turn into` 很適合 A → B 的明顯轉換。  
但像 `become aware`, `become necessary` 等通常不會直接改成 `turn into`。

---

## 04 Become / Grow into

### 成長過程不等於普通轉變

`grow into` 強調逐步發展：

> grow into a leader

如果只是突然變成另一種狀態，通常不適合。

---

## 05 Become / Develop into

`develop into` 強調發展過程。  
特別常見於：

- problem
- crisis
- relationship
- illness
- industry

---

## 06 Become / Transform into

`transform into` 比 `become` 強烈得多。  
它通常表示明顯、深刻或徹底改變。

---

## 07 Become / End up as

`end up as` 強調最後結果。  
常暗示這個結果不是一開始計劃好的。

---

## 08 Become / Emerge as

`emerge as` 不只是「成為」，還常有「逐漸被看見／公認為」某種角色的意味。

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**Turn into · 變成；轉變成**

> The small workshop turned into a successful company within ten years.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. Turn into ✓**  
題目描述的是由一種身份、形式或狀態直接轉變為另一種。`turn into` 是 `become` 最自然、最常見的替代表達之一。

**B. remain**  
`remain` = 保持、仍然是。它表示狀態沒有改變，正好和 `become` 的『發生變化』相反。

**C. avoid**  
`avoid` = 避免。它描述刻意不做某事或避開某情況，和『變成／成為』沒有同義關係。

**D. contain**  
`contain` = 包含。它描述一個整體內含某些內容，不表示狀態或身份發生改變。

**E. cause**  
`cause` = 導致。它表示某件事引起另一結果，但本身不等於『變成』。`X caused Y` 和 `X became Y` 是不同結構。

**F. return**  
`return` = 返回、恢復。它可以表示回到原本狀態，但 `become` 只是進入新狀態，未必是返回。

| 經成長後成熟成 | **mature into** |
| 深刻／徹底轉化成 | **transform into** |
| 最後結果成為 | **end up as** |

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

## Become vs Turn into

**become** 最廣泛。  
**turn into** 更有 A → B 的直接轉變感。

> The problem became serious.  
> The discussion turned into an argument.

---

## Grow into vs Develop into

兩者都強調逐步變化。

**grow into** 更常用於人、角色、企業的成長。  
**develop into** 更廣，可用於問題、疾病、關係、制度。

---

## Get vs Become

**get** 更口語：

> get tired  
> get worse

**become** 更中性／正式：

> become increasingly difficult  
> become aware of

---

## Turn vs Grow

兩者都可接形容詞。

**turn** = 較明顯、較突然的狀態改變。  
**grow** = 較漸進。

> turn red  
> grow impatient

---

## Change into vs Transform into

**change into** = 一般轉變。  
**transform into** = 明顯、深刻、徹底的轉變。

---

## Evolve into vs Develop into

兩者都可表示逐步發展。

**evolve into** 更常暗示經過多個階段、較長時間、較複雜的演變。  
**develop into** 更通用。

---

## Mature into vs Grow into

**grow into** = 廣泛的逐步成長。  
**mature into** = 特別強調成熟、完善、能力提升。

---

## Become vs End up as

**become** 只描述轉變。  
**end up as** 強調最終結果，有時帶意外意味。

---

## Become vs Emerge as

**emerge as** 常見於：

- leader
- winner
- major force
- key player
- centre

它通常帶「逐漸被公認／顯現為」的意味。

---

# 06 Idioms / common expressions · 常用表達

## Come into one's own

**開始充分展現能力；真正成熟起來**

> She came into her own as a manager during the crisis.

不是普通 `become` 同義詞，但常表示逐漸成為更成熟、更成功的角色。

---

## Come of age

**成年；成熟**

> The industry came of age in the 1990s.

既可字面指成年，也可比喻某行業變得成熟。

---

## Take shape

**逐漸成形**

> The new plan is beginning to take shape.

很適合表達某事逐步「成為有明確形態的東西」。

---

## Turn over a new leaf

**重新做人；改過自新**

不是普通 become 的同義詞，但表示人變成較好的狀態／開始新階段。

---

## Go from A to B

**由 A 變成 B**

> The company went from a small start-up to a global brand.

非常自然的描述性變化結構。

---

## Make the transition to

**轉型成；過渡到**

> The business made the transition from print to digital.

比 `become` 更強調過渡過程。

---

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Become + adjective

> become tired  
> become difficult  
> become popular

口語常可改為：

> get tired  
> get difficult  
> get popular

但不是所有 adjective 都同樣自然。

---

## Become + noun

> become a doctor  
> become a leader  
> become a problem

這時不能簡單改成：

> get a doctor ✗

---

## Turn into + noun

> turn into a problem  
> turn into a company

---

## Turn + adjective

> turn red  
> turn cold  
> turn serious

通常不用：

> turn into red ✗

---

## Grow + adjective

> grow tired  
> grow impatient  
> grow stronger

---

## Grow into + noun

> grow into a leader  
> grow into the role

---

## Emerge as + noun

> emerge as a leader  
> emerge as a major force

通常不是：

> emerge into a leader ✗

---

# 09 核心決策問題

1. **只是一般由 A 變 B 嗎？** → `turn into`
2. **是逐步成長為某角色／人物嗎？** → `grow into`
3. **是逐步發展成問題／情況／組織嗎？** → `develop into`
4. **是後來被視為／稱為某種身份嗎？** → `come to be`
5. **口語的「變得 + adjective」嗎？** → `get`
6. **是明顯變成某種顏色／狀態嗎？** → `turn`
7. **是逐漸變得更擔心／疲倦／強大嗎？** → `grow`
8. **是形式／物質直接轉換嗎？** → `change into`
9. **是長時間、多階段演變嗎？** → `evolve into`
10. **是成長後變得成熟、完整嗎？** → `mature into`
11. **是巨大、徹底轉變嗎？** → `transform into`
12. **強調最後結果嗎？** → `end up as`
13. **是逐漸被公認為某種角色嗎？** → `emerge as`
14. **想用較敘事性的『後來逐漸成為』嗎？** → `come to become`

---

# 10 Final note for system use

最有用的替代表達：

`turn into / grow into / develop into / come to be / get / turn / grow / change into / evolve into / mature into / transform into / end up as / emerge as / come to become`

常用 expressions：

`come into one's own / come of age / take shape / turn over a new leaf / go from A to B / make the transition to`

不要把以下詞當成 `become` 的一般同義詞：

`remain / return / cause / make / create`

它們分別表示 **保持、返回、導致、使某人變得、創造**，和主語自身「變成」某種狀態的語法角色不同。

真正的選詞依據是：

> **Is the change sudden, gradual, developmental, transformative, final, or a change in recognized status?**
