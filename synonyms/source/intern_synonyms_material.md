# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Intern

**實習生、受訓人員、工作實習學生、學徒、畢業培訓生——究竟是哪一種 intern？**

本課聚焦：

**intern = a person, often a student or recent graduate, who works temporarily in an organisation primarily to gain practical experience, training, or professional exposure**

核心近義與近義表達：

**trainee; work-placement student; apprentice; internship student; placement student; graduate trainee; trainee employee; junior trainee; work-experience student; student trainee; placement trainee**

> **Precision rule:**  
> `trainee` = 最廣，強調接受培訓。  
> `work-placement / placement student` = 學校／大學安排實習。  
> `apprentice` = 長期制度化技能培訓。  
> `graduate trainee` = 畢業後正式企業培訓計劃。  
> `work-experience student` = 短期職場接觸。  
> `trainee employee` = 正式受僱 + 培訓。

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Trainee

**受訓人員；實習培訓人員**

強調正在接受工作或專業培訓；不一定是學生，也不一定短期。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The company hired three trainees for the summer.

公司暑期聘用了三名受訓人員。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- trainee programme

- trainee position

- management trainee

- trainee role


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡重點是學生實習、一般培訓、正式受僱、畢業生計劃，還是長期職業學徒制度？

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Employee

**員工不等於 intern**

employee 只表示受僱；intern 通常帶學習、訓練或短期職場經驗目的。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

An intern may be paid, but not every employee is an intern.

---

## 02 Apprentice

**學徒只對部分 intern 情況**

apprenticeship 通常較長期、制度化、職業技能導向。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

An apprentice electrician usually follows a structured trade-training route.

---

## 03 Trainee

**很接近但範圍更廣**

trainee 可以是正式員工、畢業生培訓生或職業培訓者，不一定是 internship。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

All interns may be trainees in a broad sense, but not all trainees are interns.

---

## 04 Volunteer

**義工不等於實習生**

volunteer 核心是自願服務；intern 核心是工作學習／培訓安排。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A volunteer helps an organisation; an intern usually follows a learning or placement programme.

---

## 05 Assistant

**助理不等於實習生**

assistant 是職務／職級名稱，可是正式長期工作。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A research assistant may be a regular employee, while an intern is typically in training.

---

## 06 Work-experience student

**只對短期學生體驗 sense**

它常比 intern 更短期、更觀察導向。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A two-day school work-experience visit is not the same as a three-month internship.

---

## 07 Graduate trainee

**只對畢業生培訓計劃**

通常是正式招募後的 structured programme，和學生 internship 不同。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A graduate trainee may already be a full employee.

---

## 08 Probationer

**試用期員工不是 intern**

probationer 是已受僱、處於試用期的人，核心是僱傭評估而非實習學習。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A probationer is being assessed in a job; an intern is usually there to gain experience.

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Intern vs trainee
`trainee` 的範圍更廣，可包括 management trainee, graduate trainee, trainee technician 等。

## 02 Intern vs apprentice
- intern = 通常短期、取得經驗／職業 exposure
- apprentice = 通常長期、有系統地學習職業技能

## 03 Placement student
英式高等教育常見：
- placement year
- industrial placement
- sandwich course

## 04 Graduate trainee
通常已畢業，並正式獲公司招聘進入 structured training / rotation programme。

## 05 Work-experience student
常見於 secondary school、短期 placement、observation、career exploration，通常比 internship 更短、更淺。

## 06 Paid vs unpaid intern
`intern` 本身不告訴你有沒有薪酬：
- paid intern
- unpaid intern

## 07 Internship vs intern
- internship = 實習安排／實習期
- intern = 參加實習的人
