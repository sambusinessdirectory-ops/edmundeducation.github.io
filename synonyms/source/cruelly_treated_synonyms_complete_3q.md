# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Cruelly treated

「受到殘酷對待」，究竟是哪一種 `cruelly treated`？

被不人道對待、受虐待、受殘酷對待、遭惡劣對待、被忽視、被粗暴對待、受到不必要痛苦——英文會按**是否涉及身體或心理虐待、是否強調不人道、是否是惡劣待遇、是否包含疏忽照顧、是否是制度性不當對待，以及語氣是法律、動物福利、一般敘述還是正式報告**選用不同的字和常見表達。

> Each exercise includes context, Chinese translation, six options, correct rewrite, and detailed A–F feedback.

# 01 同義詞指南

## Cruelly treated 的 14 種更精準說法

### 01 Treated inhumanely
**被不人道對待**

最直接、最正式的近義表達之一，強調待遇缺乏基本人道標準，常見於法律、人權、動物福利和正式報告。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The animals had been treated inhumanely before they were rescued.

這些動物在獲救前一直受到不人道對待。

**USAGE CLUE**

- treated inhumanely
- formal / legal / welfare
- strong and precise

---

### 02 Abused
**受虐待；被濫用**

非常常見，表示受到身體、心理或其他形式的不當傷害。對人和動物都可用，但語義比 `cruelly treated` 更直接、更強。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The rescued dogs had been abused and neglected.

獲救的狗曾遭受虐待和疏忽照顧。

**USAGE CLUE**

- abused children / animals
- physical or psychological mistreatment
- stronger and more specific

---

### 03 Mistreated
**被虐待；受到不當對待**

比 `abused` 稍廣、稍中性，表示受到不公平、惡劣或不適當的對待。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The workers said they had been mistreated by their employer.

工人表示他們曾受到僱主不當對待。

**USAGE CLUE**

- people / animals / workers
- broad mistreatment
- less specific than abused

---

### 04 Ill-treated
**受到惡劣對待；被虐待**

英式英語常見，表示被不善、粗暴或殘酷地對待。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The charity cares for abandoned and ill-treated animals.

該慈善機構照顧被遺棄和受虐待的動物。

**USAGE CLUE**

- British English
- ill-treated animals / prisoners
- formal-neutral

---

### 05 Subjected to cruel treatment
**遭受殘酷待遇**

正式、明確，適合報告、法律、調查或歷史敘述。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Several detainees were subjected to cruel treatment.

數名被拘留者遭受殘酷待遇。

**USAGE CLUE**

- subjected to + treatment
- formal / legal
- explicitly names cruelty

---

### 06 Subjected to inhumane treatment
**遭受不人道待遇**

比 `treated inhumanely` 更名詞化、更正式，常見於政策、調查和人權文件。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> No person should be subjected to inhumane treatment.

任何人都不應遭受不人道待遇。

**USAGE CLUE**

- formal/legal
- inhumane treatment
- institutional wording

---

### 07 Badly treated
**受到很差的對待；被惡劣對待**

較一般、較口語，表示待遇很差，但不一定已達虐待或法律上的殘酷程度。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The animals had been badly treated by their previous owner.

這些動物曾被前主人惡劣對待。

**USAGE CLUE**

- plain English
- broad and less technical
- weaker than abused / inhumanely treated

---

### 08 Harshly treated
**受到嚴厲／苛刻對待**

表示待遇嚴厲、苛刻或缺乏同情，但未必一定構成虐待。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The prisoners were harshly treated during the winter.

囚犯在冬季受到苛刻對待。

**USAGE CLUE**

- harsh treatment
- severe but not always abusive
- context determines strength

---

### 09 Brutally treated
**受到殘暴對待**

表示待遇極其粗暴、殘酷，語氣比 `cruelly treated` 更強。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Witnesses said the captives had been brutally treated.

目擊者表示俘虜曾受到殘暴對待。

**USAGE CLUE**

- very strong
- physical cruelty often implied
- use only when context supports severity

---

### 10 Neglected
**被忽視照顧；遭疏忽**

表示沒有得到應有的食物、醫療、照顧或保護。它可以是 cruel treatment 的一部分，但不是所有 neglect 都等於主動虐待。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The horse had been neglected for months.

這匹馬數月來一直遭到疏忽照顧。

**USAGE CLUE**

- lack of care
- animal welfare / child welfare
- omission rather than active cruelty

---

### 11 Subjected to abuse
**遭受虐待**

正式而廣泛，表示某人或動物成為虐待行為的對象。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The investigation found that several residents had been subjected to abuse.

調查發現數名住客曾遭受虐待。

**USAGE CLUE**

- formal investigation wording
- broad abuse category
- stronger than badly treated

---

### 12 Treated with cruelty
**受到殘酷對待**

結構直接、正式，保留 `cruelty` 本身，適合敘述、報告和教育材料。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Animals should never be treated with cruelty.

動物絕不應受到殘酷對待。

**USAGE CLUE**

- direct paraphrase
- formal-neutral
- useful in ethical/welfare contexts

---

### 13 Subjected to unnecessary suffering
**遭受不必要痛苦**

常見於動物福利、倫理和法律語境，重點是待遇造成可避免的痛苦，而不一定只描述「態度殘酷」。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The law prohibits animals from being subjected to unnecessary suffering.

法律禁止讓動物遭受不必要痛苦。

**USAGE CLUE**

- animal welfare / law
- suffering-focused
- narrower legal/ethical sense

---

### 14 Treated without compassion
**被缺乏同情地對待；受到冷酷對待**

較描述性，強調待遇缺乏同情、關懷或人情味；語氣比 abuse 稍弱。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Vulnerable people should not be treated without compassion.

弱勢人士不應受到缺乏同情的對待。

**USAGE CLUE**

- compassion-focused
- descriptive
- weaker than brutally treated

# 02 False synonyms

## 01 cruelly treated / abused

### abused 更直接指虐待

`abused` 往往比 `cruelly treated` 更具體，可涉及身體、心理或其他形式的傷害。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The animals were cruelly treated.  
> The animals were abused over a long period.

---

## 02 cruelly treated / neglected

### neglect 是疏忽，不一定是主動傷害

`neglected` 指應有照顧沒有提供；`cruelly treated` 可以包括主動傷害、苛待或其他殘酷待遇。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The dog was neglected and left without proper care.  
> The dog was cruelly treated by its owner.

---

## 03 cruelly treated / harshly treated

### harsh 不一定等於 cruel

`harshly treated` 表示待遇嚴厲，但有時仍可能被認為是合法或制度化的；`cruelly treated` 帶更明確的道德否定。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The trainees were harshly treated by a very strict instructor.  
> The prisoners were cruelly treated in conditions that caused unnecessary suffering.

---

## 04 cruelly treated / unfairly treated

### 不公平不一定殘酷

`unfairly treated` 指不公正；`cruelly treated` 強調痛苦、傷害或冷酷。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> She was unfairly treated in the promotion process.  
> The animals were cruelly treated and denied proper care.

---

## 05 cruelly treated / badly treated

### badly treated 範圍較廣、力度較弱

`badly treated` 可以只是服務差、態度差或不公平；`cruelly treated` 更強調殘酷。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Customers complained that they were badly treated.  
> The rescued animals had been cruelly treated.

---

## 06 cruelly treated / punished

### punishment 不等於虐待

`punished` 只表示受到懲罰；懲罰可能合理，也可能殘酷，需看方式。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The student was punished for cheating.  
> The detainee was cruelly treated during punishment.

---

## 07 cruelly treated / tortured

### tortured 比 cruelly treated 強很多

`tortured` 指有意施加極端痛苦以懲罰、逼供或恐嚇，不能泛化成所有殘酷待遇。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The prisoners were cruelly treated.  
> Some detainees were tortured to obtain information.

---

## 08 cruelly treated / cruelty

### 詞性陷阱

`cruelly treated` 是被動動詞片語；`cruelty` 是名詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The animals were cruelly treated.  
> The investigation documented acts of cruelty.

# 03 練習與解釋

## 練習 01.1

**Target phrase: Treated inhumanely · 被不人道對待**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> Investigators found that the animals had been cruelly treated in conditions that failed basic welfare standards.

**中文翻譯**

調查人員發現這些動物曾在不符合基本福利標準的環境下受到不人道對待。

A. treated inhumanely  
B. treated unfairly  
C. treated strictly  
D. neglected only  
E. punished  
F. cruelty

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**treated inhumanely · 被不人道對待**

> Investigators found that the animals had been treated inhumanely.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. treated inhumanely ✓**  
`failed basic welfare standards` 是關鍵線索，表示待遇違反基本人道標準，最適合 inhumanely。

**B. treated unfairly**  
`unfairly` 重點是不公正，不能充分表達福利和痛苦問題。

**C. treated strictly**  
嚴格管理本身不等於不人道。

**D. neglected only**  
neglect 只涵蓋疏忽照顧；原句更廣泛地說待遇不人道。

**E. punished**  
受到懲罰未必等於殘酷對待。

**F. cruelty**  
這是名詞，不能直接取代被動動詞片語。

# 05 高混淆組

## cruelly treated vs treated inhumanely
- **cruelly treated**：一般描述殘酷待遇。
- **treated inhumanely**：更正式，強調違反基本人道標準。

## abused vs mistreated
- **abused**：更強、更具體，常涉及身體／心理傷害。
- **mistreated**：範圍更廣，可只是惡劣或不當對待。

## ill-treated vs badly treated
- **ill-treated**：較正式、英式。
- **badly treated**：較日常、力度可較弱。

## harshly treated vs brutally treated
- **harshly treated**：苛刻、嚴厲。
- **brutally treated**：殘暴、通常有很強暴力或極端粗暴意味。

## abused vs neglected
- **abused**：可包括主動傷害。
- **neglected**：缺乏應有照顧。

## cruel treatment vs inhumane treatment
- **cruel treatment**：強調殘酷。
- **inhumane treatment**：強調不符合基本人道／尊嚴標準。

## mistreated vs treated without compassion
- **mistreated**：廣義不當對待。
- **treated without compassion**：特別強調缺乏同情和關懷。

## cruelly treated vs subjected to unnecessary suffering
- **cruelly treated**：描述方式較廣。
- **subjected to unnecessary suffering**：聚焦可避免的痛苦，常見於動物福利法律。

# 08 生成／評估注意事項

1. 固定為 **14 alternatives + 3 questions each = 42 exercises**。
2. 每題包含：
   - Target word / phrase + Chinese gloss
   - READ THE CONTEXT
   - 中文翻譯
   - 六個選項
   - `CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案`
   - English target + Chinese gloss
   - 正確改寫
   - A–F 個別解釋
3. A–F feedback 採用：**詞義 → 正常搭配／語域 → 本題為何適合或不適合**。
4. 每組 False synonyms 都包含 **ENGLISH COMPARISON**。
5. Distractors 優先使用真正容易混淆的待遇詞：
   - mistreated
   - badly treated
   - harshly treated
   - abused
   - neglected
   - punished
   而不是大量無關詞。
6. 使用者提供的 `treated inhumanely`, `abused` 是核心：
   - treated inhumanely = 不人道
   - abused = 明確虐待
7. `mistreated` 是很重要的中間層次詞，比 abused 廣、比 badly treated 更正式。
8. `ill-treated` 是英式英語高價值替代，特別適合 animal welfare。
9. `subjected to cruel treatment` / `subjected to inhumane treatment` 適合法律、調查、正式報告。
10. `badly treated` 故意保留較弱語義，不應在證據不足時升格成 abused。
11. `harshly treated` 只表示苛刻，不一定構成 abuse。
12. `brutally treated` 很強，只有出現 extreme violence / severe brutality 等證據時才使用。
13. `neglected` 是「疏忽照顧」，重點是 omission，而不是主動傷害。
14. `subjected to unnecessary suffering` 是動物福利與法律語境的重要高精度替代。
15. `treated without compassion` 是描述性近義，適合缺乏同理／關懷但未必構成 abuse 的情境。
16. 真正進階題目應直接比較：
    - cruelly treated / treated inhumanely
    - abused / mistreated / ill-treated
    - badly treated / harshly treated / brutally treated
    - abused / neglected
    - cruel treatment / inhumane treatment
    - mistreated / treated without compassion
    - cruelly treated / subjected to unnecessary suffering
17. `cruelly treated` 本身仍然是最廣泛的核心表達；其他替代語主要增加「不人道程度、是否構成虐待、是否屬疏忽、待遇嚴厲程度、法律正式度與福利痛苦」上的精度。
