# SIMILAR · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Similar

**Similar 是一個很廣的比較詞，但並不等於 same。** 替換時要先判斷：是在說 **外觀相像、可比較、功能／結構類比、性質接近、數值很接近、發展模式平行，還是實際上幾乎一樣**。

你提供的 **alike; comparable; resembling** 正好代表三個核心方向：**彼此相像、可合理比較、像某個具體對象**。

本次保留 **10 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達。會特別區分 similar 與 **same / identical / equivalent** 的強度差異，避免把『相似』誤寫成『相同』。共 **30 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Similar 的語境 | 核心問題 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|---|
| 外觀／一般相像 | 是否 two things look/seem alike？ | alike, resembling |
| 可比較的程度／品質 | 是否 level/value/performance can be compared？ | comparable |
| 功能／結構上的類比 | 是否 different things work in similar ways？ | analogous |
| 性質上的近似 | 是否 similar in nature/character？ | akin, similar in nature |
| 程度非常接近 | 是否 only a small difference remains？ | close |
| 發展／模式平行 | 是否 similar paths/trends over time？ | parallel |
| 功能／價值等同 | 是否 actually corresponding in value/function？ | equivalent |
| 口語：大致一樣 | 是否 very similar with minor differences？ | much the same |

## Tier 1 — Core resemblance / comparison / analogy equivalents · 核心相像、比較與類比近義詞

### 01 Alike

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：相像的；相似的  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：similar to each other in appearance, character, behaviour, or other qualities  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **similar** 最核心的近義詞之一，特別常用於 **look alike / be alike**。  
**常見搭配**：look alike; very much alike; alike in many ways; seem alike; sound alike.  
**常見搭配對象**：people, objects, ideas, situations, twins.  
**與 similar 的辨析**：Alike 通常作表語，不常直接放在名詞前；similar 可作 **similar ideas / similar problems**。  
**使用限制**：不要說 *an alike problem*；應說 **a similar problem**。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The two sisters look remarkably alike.  
兩姊妹看起來非常相像。

### 02 Comparable

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：可相比的；相近的  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：similar enough in quality, size, value, or type to be meaningfully compared  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 適合數據、薪酬、表現、規模、標準等可比較語境。  
**常見搭配**：comparable to; comparable with; comparable results; comparable size; comparable quality.  
**常見搭配對象**：result, salary, performance, size, standard.  
**與 similar 的辨析**：Comparable 不只表示像，還表示『可以合理比較』。  
**使用限制**：外貌相像或日常簡單相似通常用 similar / alike 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The two groups achieved comparable results.  
兩組取得相若的結果。

### 03 Resembling

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：像……的；類似……的  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：having an appearance, form, or quality that is similar to something else  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很適合描述外觀、形狀、聲音、風格等『像某物』的關係。  
**常見搭配**：closely resembling; resembling a circle; resembling the original; bear a resemblance.  
**常見搭配對象**：shape, design, face, sound, pattern.  
**與 similar 的辨析**：Resembling 強調 A looks/is like B，通常需要一個被比較對象。  
**使用限制**：抽象統計比較時 comparable / analogous 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The building has a roof resembling a folded sheet of paper.  
這座建築的屋頂像一張摺起的紙。

### 04 Analogous

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：相似的；類比相近的  
**語域**：formal / academic  
**核心語義**：similar in function, structure, or relationship despite being different in other respects  
**用法提示**：適合學術、科學、政策、制度比較，尤其是『功能／關係上相似』。  
**常見搭配**：analogous to; analogous situation; analogous process; analogous structure; closely analogous.  
**常見搭配對象**：process, structure, situation, system, relationship.  
**與 similar 的辨析**：Analogous 比 similar 更抽象，通常不是外表相似，而是功能或關係相似。  
**使用限制**：普通日常物件外貌相似時通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The two systems are analogous in the way they process information.  
兩個系統處理資訊的方式相似。

### 05 Akin

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：相似的；近似的；有共同性質的  
**語域**：formal / literary  
**核心語義**：similar in nature, character, or quality  
**用法提示**：當 similar 指『性質上接近』而非外觀完全相像時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：akin to; closely akin to; something akin to; feelings akin to fear.  
**常見搭配對象**：feeling, concept, process, experience, relationship.  
**與 similar 的辨析**：Akin 幾乎總是用 **akin to**，語氣比 similar 更書面。  
**使用限制**：不常直接作名詞前修飾語。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The experience was akin to starting again from scratch.  
這種經歷近似從頭開始。

## Tier 2 — Close / parallel / equivalence variants · 接近、平行與相當型變體

### 06 Close

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：接近的；相似的  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：very similar in degree, value, relationship, or resemblance  
**用法提示**：當 similar 帶『非常接近』而非一般相似時可用。  
**常見搭配**：close match; close resemblance; close to; closely related; close parallel.  
**常見搭配對象**：match, result, estimate, relationship, resemblance.  
**與 similar 的辨析**：Close 比 similar 更強，表示差距很小。  
**使用限制**：並非所有 similar 都足夠 close。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The second estimate was very close to the first.  
第二個估算與第一個非常接近。

### 07 Parallel

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：相似而平行的；相應的  
**語域**：formal / analytical  
**核心語義**：similar in development, structure, pattern, or sequence  
**用法提示**：當兩件事呈現相似發展路徑、趨勢或結構時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：parallel development; parallel trend; parallel case; draw a parallel; run parallel to.  
**常見搭配對象**：trend, development, case, process, history.  
**與 similar 的辨析**：Parallel 強調兩者『沿著相似路線發展』，不只是一般相似。  
**使用限制**：外觀、顏色、單一特徵相似不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The two industries followed parallel patterns of growth.  
兩個行業呈現相似的增長模式。

### 08 Equivalent

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：等同的；相等的；功能相當的  
**語域**：formal / technical  
**核心語義**：equal or corresponding in value, function, meaning, or effect  
**用法提示**：當 similar 已接近『功能／價值上等同』時才適合。  
**常見搭配**：equivalent to; equivalent amount; equivalent qualification; functional equivalent; equivalent value.  
**常見搭配對象**：value, qualification, amount, function, expression.  
**與 similar 的辨析**：Equivalent 比 similar 強得多，表示相當或等同，不只是像。  
**使用限制**：如果只是有些相似而不等同，不要用 equivalent。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The overseas qualification is considered equivalent to a local degree.  
該海外資歷被視為相當於本地學位。

### 09 Much the same

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：大致相同；十分相似  
**語域**：informal / common  
**核心語義**：very similar, with only small or unimportant differences  
**用法提示**：適合口語和一般寫作，表示兩件事『基本上一樣』。  
**常見搭配**：much the same as; look much the same; remain much the same; work much the same way.  
**常見搭配對象**：method, result, experience, situation, appearance.  
**與 similar 的辨析**：比 similar 更口語，也更接近『差不多一樣』。  
**使用限制**：正式學術寫作通常用 similar/comparable/analogous。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The two versions work in much the same way.  
兩個版本的運作方式大致相同。

## Tier 3 — Nature-focused descriptive paraphrase · 性質相近的描述性改寫

### 10 Similar in nature

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：性質相近的；本質相似的  
**語域**：formal / descriptive  
**核心語義**：similar in essential character or type rather than necessarily in appearance  
**用法提示**：這是 contextual paraphrase，當要強調 nature/type rather than surface resemblance 時很清楚。  
**常見搭配**：similar in nature; broadly similar in nature; problems similar in nature; cases similar in nature.  
**常見搭配對象**：problem, case, issue, process, experience.  
**與 similar 的辨析**：比 general similar 更明確指出『本質／性質』是比較基礎。  
**使用限制**：日常簡單比較會顯得冗長。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The two complaints were similar in nature but involved different departments.  
兩宗投訴性質相近，但涉及不同部門。

## 04 False synonyms / similar traps · 容易誤選的相似、相同與相關陷阱

### 01 Same

**相同 ≠ similar**  
same 表示相同；similar 允許存在差異。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Same is stronger than similar.

### 02 Identical

**完全相同 ≠ similar**  
identical 表示完全一致，強度遠高於 similar。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Identical allows essentially no difference.

### 03 Related

**相關 ≠ similar**  
兩件事可以相關但並不相似。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Related means connected, not necessarily alike.

### 04 Equal

**相等 ≠ similar**  
equal 通常指數值、地位或程度相等，不表示外觀／性質相似。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Equal is equivalence-focused.

### 05 Comparable

**只在可比較 sense 接近**  
comparable 強調可以合理比較，不代表外觀一定相似。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Comparable is comparison-focused.

### 06 Equivalent

**只在相當／等同 sense 接近**  
equivalent 強度高於 similar。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Equivalent is stronger and more exact.

### 07 Resembling

**只在『像某物』sense 接近**  
resembling 常需要明確被比較對象。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Resembling is directional.

### 08 Similar / similarly

**詞性陷阱**  
similar 是形容詞；similarly 是副詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check word class before replacing.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Alike · 相像的；相似的

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The two children are very similar in appearance.

**中文翻譯**  
兩個孩子外貌非常相似。

- A. Alike
- B. Comparable
- C. Resembling
- D. Same
- E. Identical
- F. Related

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Alike** · 相像的；相似的

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The two children look very much alike.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Alike ✓** — means similar to each other and is especially common after linking verbs such as look, seem or be. Here, people looking similar to each other naturally take alike. That makes **Alike** the most precise replacement for this particular sense of *similar*.
- **B.** **Comparable** — means similar enough in quality, value, size or type to be meaningfully compared. This is best when meaningful comparison of level, quality or value is central; it does not simply mean 'looks alike'. The decisive clue is that people looking similar to each other naturally take alike.
- **C.** **Resembling** — means having an appearance, form or quality like a particular comparison object. This usually points from one thing to a specific comparison object and is especially natural for appearance or form. The decisive clue is that people looking similar to each other naturally take alike.
- **D.** **Same** — means not different in identity, type or value and is stronger than similar. This removes the difference that *similar* allows and therefore overstates the match. The decisive clue is that people looking similar to each other naturally take alike.
- **E.** **Identical** — means exactly the same and is much stronger than similar. This is even stronger than *same*: it implies virtually no difference at all. The decisive clue is that people looking similar to each other naturally take alike.
- **F.** **Related** — means connected or associated and does not require similarity. Things can be connected without being similar, so this changes the semantic relationship. The decisive clue is that people looking similar to each other naturally take alike.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **similar vs alike**: alike 多作表語：They look alike；similar 可放名詞前：similar ideas。
- **similar vs comparable**: comparable 強調可以合理比較。
- **similar vs resembling**: resembling 常是 A resembles B 的方向性比較。
- **similar vs analogous**: analogous 更抽象，偏功能／結構／關係相似。
- **similar vs akin**: akin to 偏性質、本質近似，較書面。
- **similar vs close**: close 表示差異非常小。
- **similar vs parallel**: parallel 強調趨勢、發展或結構沿相似路線。
- **similar vs equivalent**: equivalent 比 similar 強，接近相當／等同。
- **similar vs same**: same 表示相同；similar 仍容許差異。
- **similar vs identical**: identical 幾乎不容許任何差異。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & comparison traps · 文法、搭配與比較陷阱

- **similar to + noun**：similar to the original；不要用 *similar with* 作一般比較。
- **similar in + aspect**：similar in size / design / character。
- **alike** 常作表語：The two models are alike / look alike；不說 *an alike model*。
- **comparable to/with**：comparable results / comparable to last year's figure。
- **resemble + object** 不用 **to**：The building resembles a ship。
- **analogous to + noun**：analogous to another process。
- **akin to + noun**：akin to a partnership。
- **close to + noun/value**：close to the original estimate。
- **parallel to / parallel with**：a trend parallel to another trend。
- **equivalent to + noun**：equivalent to a local qualification。
- **much the same as + noun**：much the same as before。
- **same / identical / equivalent** 的強度通常高於 similar；不要因『有點像』就使用。
- **similar** 是形容詞；**similarly** 是副詞；**similarity** 是名詞。
