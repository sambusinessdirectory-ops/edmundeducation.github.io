# SPREAD · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Spread

**Spread 是高度多義動詞。** 它可以指範圍擴大、文件／消息流傳、知識廣泛傳播、內容分享、信號／錯誤擴散、思想逐步擴散、廣播，以及疾病／資料傳送。因此替換前要先判斷 **what is spreading, through what medium, and whether the spread is deliberate, gradual, technical, or interpersonal**。

你提供的 **expand; circulate; disseminate; share** 正好代表四個核心方向：**範圍擴張、群體內流通、正式廣泛傳播、個人／群體分享**。

本次保留 **9 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達，共 **27 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Spread 的語境 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|
| 範圍／規模擴展 | expand |
| 文件／消息流通 | circulate |
| 正式廣泛傳播資訊 | disseminate |
| 直接分享內容 | share |
| 技術性擴散 | propagate |
| 逐步擴散 | diffuse |
| 一對多發布 | broadcast |
| A → B 傳送 | transmit |
| 實體資料廣泛分發 | distribute widely |

## Tier 1 — Core equivalents

### 01 Expand

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：擴展；擴大；延伸  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：become larger in area, scope, reach, or influence  
**用法提示**：強調範圍／規模變大；資訊、疾病等傳播則常用 circulate/disseminate/transmit。  
**常見搭配**：expand rapidly; expand into new areas; expand coverage  
**常見搭配對象**：area, network, service, market, influence  
**與 spread 的辨析**：強調範圍／規模變大；資訊、疾病等傳播則常用 circulate/disseminate/transmit。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The service expanded into several neighbouring districts.  
這項服務擴展至數個鄰近地區。

### 02 Circulate

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：流傳；傳閱；流通  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：move or be passed around among people, places, or systems  
**用法提示**：強調群體內流通／傳閱；不等於所有空間或生物擴散。  
**常見搭配**：circulate information; circulate a memo; circulate rumours  
**常見搭配對象**：document, memo, rumour, message, information  
**與 spread 的辨析**：強調群體內流通／傳閱；不等於所有空間或生物擴散。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The memo was circulated to all staff yesterday.  
備忘錄昨天已傳閱給所有員工。

### 03 Disseminate

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：傳播；散布；廣泛發布  
**語域**：formal / academic / institutional  
**核心語義**：distribute information, knowledge, or findings widely and systematically  
**用法提示**：比 spread 更正式，帶 deliberate/systematic distribution 意味。  
**常見搭配**：disseminate information; disseminate findings; disseminate guidance  
**常見搭配對象**：information, findings, knowledge, guidance, message  
**與 spread 的辨析**：比 spread 更正式，帶 deliberate/systematic distribution 意味。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The team disseminated the research findings through workshops and reports.  
團隊透過工作坊和報告傳播研究結果。

### 04 Share

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：分享；傳給  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：give information, content, resources, or access to other people  
**用法提示**：偏個人／群體間傳遞；不適合疾病、火勢、地理擴散。  
**常見搭配**：share information; share a post; share a link  
**常見搭配對象**：information, post, link, file, knowledge  
**與 spread 的辨析**：偏個人／群體間傳遞；不適合疾病、火勢、地理擴散。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Please share the updated guidance with your team.  
請把更新後的指引分享給你的團隊。

### 05 Propagate

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：傳播；擴散；繁衍  
**語域**：formal / technical  
**核心語義**：spread or cause signals, errors, information, beliefs, or biological material to spread  
**用法提示**：更 technical/formal，適合信號、錯誤、觀念、植物繁殖。  
**常見搭配**：propagate a signal; propagate an error; propagate information  
**常見搭配對象**：signal, error, information, wave, plant  
**與 spread 的辨析**：更 technical/formal，適合信號、錯誤、觀念、植物繁殖。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The error propagated through the entire system.  
這個錯誤擴散至整個系統。

## Tier 2 — Diffusion / broadcast / transmission variants

### 06 Diffuse

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：擴散；散開  
**語域**：formal / scientific  
**核心語義**：spread gradually through an area, medium, population, or system  
**用法提示**：強調 gradual dispersal。  
**常見搭配**：diffuse through; diffuse across; diffusion of ideas  
**常見搭配對象**：gas, heat, idea, innovation, influence  
**與 spread 的辨析**：強調 gradual dispersal。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The new practice gradually diffused across the organisation.  
這項新做法逐漸在整個機構擴散。

### 07 Broadcast

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：廣播；廣泛發布  
**語域**：neutral / media / digital  
**核心語義**：send information or content to a large audience at the same time  
**用法提示**：one-to-many distribution；不等於人傳人擴散。  
**常見搭配**：broadcast a message; broadcast live; broadcast an announcement  
**常見搭配對象**：message, programme, announcement, news, signal  
**與 spread 的辨析**：one-to-many distribution；不等於人傳人擴散。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The warning was broadcast across local radio stations.  
警告透過本地電台廣泛播出。

### 08 Transmit

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：傳送；傳播  
**語域**：formal / technical  
**核心語義**：send or pass a signal, disease, message, or data from one point/person to another  
**用法提示**：強調 A → B 傳送鏈；spread 更強調整體擴散。  
**常見搭配**：transmit a signal; transmit data; transmit disease  
**常見搭配對象**：signal, data, disease, infection, message  
**與 spread 的辨析**：強調 A → B 傳送鏈；spread 更強調整體擴散。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The virus can be transmitted through close contact.  
這種病毒可透過密切接觸傳播。

## Tier 3 — Plain distribution paraphrase

### 09 Distribute widely

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：廣泛分發；廣為傳送  
**語域**：neutral / explanatory  
**核心語義**：send or provide material to many people or places  
**用法提示**：透明改寫，重點是 distribution mechanics。  
**常見搭配**：distribute widely; distribute materials; distribute information  
**常見搭配對象**：leaflet, document, guidance, material, information  
**與 spread 的辨析**：透明改寫，重點是 distribution mechanics。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The guidance was distributed widely to schools and community centres.  
這份指引被廣泛分發至學校和社區中心。

## 04 False synonyms / spread traps

### 01 Increase

**增加 ≠ spread**  
increase 只表示數量／程度變大，不表示向外擴散。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Increase is quantity-focused.

### 02 Grow

**增長 ≠ spread**  
grow 可表示變大，但不一定向外傳播。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Growth and spread can overlap but differ.

### 03 Publish

**出版／發布 ≠ 所有 spread**  
publish 是使內容公開，不等於內容實際流傳。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Publication is one distribution step.

### 04 Send

**傳送 ≠ spread**  
send 只是從一點傳到另一點，不一定擴散到更大範圍。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Send is point-to-point.

### 05 Share

**只在人與人分享 sense 接近**  
share 不適合疾病、火勢、地理擴張。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Share is interpersonal/content-focused.

### 06 Circulate

**只在流通／傳閱 sense 接近**  
circulate 不等於所有空間或生物擴散。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Circulate is movement-within-group focused.

### 07 Disseminate

**只在資訊／知識正式傳播 sense 接近**  
disseminate 不適合所有自然擴散。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Disseminate is information-focused.

### 08 Spread / widespread

**詞形陷阱**  
spread 可作名詞／動詞；widespread 是形容詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check word class and meaning.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Expand · 擴展；擴大；延伸

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The organisation plans to spread its services into nearby districts.

**中文翻譯**  
機構計劃把服務擴展到鄰近地區。

- A. Expand
- B. Grow
- C. Circulate
- D. Increase
- E. Publish
- F. Share

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Expand** · 擴展；擴大；延伸

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The organisation plans to expand its services into nearby districts.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Expand ✓** — means become larger in area, scope, reach or influence. Here, growth in geographic/service reach. That makes **Expand** the most precise replacement for this particular sense of *spread*.
- **B.** **Grow** — means become larger or develop, but does not inherently mean transmission. It says something becomes larger but does not capture transmission or circulation. The decisive clue is that growth in geographic/service reach.
- **C.** **Circulate** — means move or be passed around among people, places or systems. It is best for movement within a group/system, not geographic expansion or technical transmission. The decisive clue is that growth in geographic/service reach.
- **D.** **Increase** — means become greater in amount or degree, not necessarily spread outward. It changes the meaning to quantity or degree. The decisive clue is that growth in geographic/service reach.
- **E.** **Publish** — means make content publicly available, not necessarily ensure its spread. It only makes content available; it does not guarantee wider spread. The decisive clue is that growth in geographic/service reach.
- **F.** **Share** — means give information, content or resources to other people. It is interpersonal/content-focused and too narrow for disease, signals or geographic expansion. The decisive clue is that growth in geographic/service reach.

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Spread 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 服務／市場／網絡範圍變大 | **expand** | 是否 scale/reach is increasing？ |
| 文件／謠言在人群中流通 | **circulate** | 是否 passed around within a defined group？ |
| 研究／知識正式廣泛傳播 | **disseminate** | 是否 systematic institutional distribution？ |
| 檔案／連結／知識分享 | **share** | 是否 direct sharing with people/groups？ |
| 信號／錯誤／模型效應擴散 | **propagate** | 是否 technical spread through a system/medium？ |
| 思想／做法逐步擴散 | **diffuse** | 是否 gradual spread over time/population？ |
| 電台／電視一對多播送 | **broadcast** | 是否 mass-audience media distribution？ |
| 疾病／資料／信號傳送 | **transmit** | 是否 transmission route is central？ |
| 單張／材料廣泛分發 | **distribute widely** | 是否 physical/material distribution to many places？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **spread vs expand**: expand 是範圍／規模增大；spread 更可表示傳播。
- **spread vs circulate**: circulate 強調在群體／系統內流動或傳閱。
- **spread vs disseminate**: disseminate 更正式，專指資訊／知識廣泛傳播。
- **spread vs share**: share 是直接與人分享，不表示一定廣泛擴散。
- **spread vs propagate**: propagate 更 technical，適合 signal/error/idea。
- **spread vs diffuse**: diffuse 強調逐步散開／滲透。
- **spread vs broadcast**: broadcast 是 one-to-many media distribution。
- **spread vs transmit**: transmit 強調 A → B 的傳送鏈。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & polysemy traps

- **spread to + place/group**：The problem spread to other regions。
- **spread through + medium/system**：The error spread through the network。
- **spread among + people/group**：Rumours spread among staff。
- **expand into + market/area**。
- **circulate a memo to staff**。
- **disseminate + information/findings/knowledge**。
- **share + content + with + people**。
- **propagate through + system/medium**。
- **diffuse across/through + population/organisation**。
- **broadcast + message/news/programme**。
- **transmit + data/signal/disease**。
- **spread** 的過去式和過去分詞仍是 **spread**。
