# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Career

**「職業／職業生涯」，究竟是在說職業種類、整段工作人生、發展路徑，還是帶使命感的志業？**

`career` 的範圍比 `job` 大。它可以表示：

- **所從事的職業類型** → `profession`, `occupation`, `line of work`
- **整段工作人生** → `working life`, `professional life`
- **職涯發展方向** → `career path`, `career trajectory`
- **個人的職業歷程** → `professional journey`
- **具使命感的職業** → `vocation`, `calling`
- **實務技職** → `trade`
- **謀生方式** → `livelihood`

> **核心原則**
>
> 這一組按照「close synonyms → broadly usable near-synonyms → context-specific / expressive variants」整理，但不顯示 tier。
>
> 最大陷阱是把 `career` 和 `job` 當成完全一樣：
>
> **job = 一份具體工作**  
> **career = 長期職業／職涯發展**
>
> 另外，`profession`, `occupation`, `trade`, `vocation`, `livelihood` 都只覆蓋 `career` 的某些語義。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## Career 的 12 個近義／近乎可互換說法

## 01 Profession

**職業；專業**

當 `career` 指一個人所從事的專業職業時，`profession` 是最直接的近義詞之一。它常暗示需要專門訓練、資格或專業知識。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She chose medicine as her profession.

她選擇醫學作為自己的職業。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- enter a profession
- choose a profession
- medical profession
- legal profession
- professional career

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **profession = a type of skilled or specialized career**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性至正式；教育、招聘、專業領域

---

## 02 Occupation

**職業；工作**

較中性、正式地表示一個人以甚麼工作為職業。比 `career` 更偏『目前從事的工作類型』，未必包含長期發展軌跡。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Please state your occupation on the form.

請在表格上填寫你的職業。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- current occupation
- main occupation
- occupation field
- state your occupation
- occupational history

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **occupation = a person's regular job or type of employment**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、中性；表格、統計、行政

---

## 03 Line of work

**行業；職業範疇；工作種類**

自然、常用的描述性近義詞。當 `career` 指一個人從事哪一類工作，而不是具體職位時，`line of work` 很貼切。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> What line of work are you in?

你從事哪一行？

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- line of work
- in this line of work
- choose a line of work
- different line of work

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **line of work = the general type of work or career someone does**

**REGISTER · 語域**

日常至中性；職場、一般寫作

---

## 04 Working life

**職業生涯；工作人生**

當 `career` 指一個人由開始工作到退休的整段職業人生時，`working life` 很自然。它強調整體時間跨度，而不是單一職業名稱。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She spent most of her working life in education.

她大部分職業生涯都在教育界度過。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- working life
- throughout one's working life
- early working life
- later working life

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **working life = the period of a person's life spent in employment**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性；職涯、政策、一般寫作

---

## 05 Professional life

**職業生涯；專業生活**

比 `working life` 稍正式，指一個人在工作／專業領域中的整體經歷，常與 personal life 對比。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The award was one of the proudest moments of her professional life.

這個獎項是她職業生涯中最自豪的時刻之一。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- professional life
- throughout one's professional life
- professional and personal life
- professional life began

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **professional life = the work-related side or span of a person's life**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性至正式；職涯、傳記、商業

---

## 06 Career path

**職業道路；職涯路徑**

當 `career` 強調一個人如何由一個職位、階段或專業走向下一步時，`career path` 很精準。它突出發展方向。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She followed an unusual career path from engineering into journalism.

她走了一條不尋常的職涯道路，由工程轉到新聞業。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- career path
- choose a career path
- clear career path
- career path into...
- alternative career path

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **career path = the sequence and direction of jobs or roles in a career**

**REGISTER · 語域**

非常常用；招聘、教育、職涯規劃

---

## 07 Career trajectory

**職涯軌跡；職業發展走向**

較正式，強調職業生涯隨時間的上升、轉變或發展趨勢。常見於商業、學術、人物分析。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The promotion changed the trajectory of his career.

這次升職改變了他的職涯發展軌跡。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- career trajectory
- upward career trajectory
- change one's career trajectory
- professional trajectory

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **career trajectory = the direction in which a person's career develops over time**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式；商業、學術、職涯分析

---

## 08 Professional journey

**職業旅程；職涯歷程**

較敘事、較人性化的 near-synonym，強調一個人職涯中的不同階段、轉折和成長。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The interview traces her professional journey from teacher to school principal.

訪問追溯她由教師到校長的職業旅程。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- professional journey
- career journey
- throughout one's professional journey
- begin a professional journey

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **professional journey = the evolving story of a person's career over time**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性；傳記、職涯、企業敘事

---

## 09 Vocation

**職業；志業**

當 `career` 不只是謀生工作，而是帶有強烈使命感、天職感或長期投入時，`vocation` 很接近。常見於教育、醫療、宗教、社會服務。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> For her, teaching was more than a job; it was a vocation.

對她來說，教學不只是一份工作，而是一種志業。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- choose a vocation
- sense of vocation
- teaching vocation
- vocation in life

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **vocation = a career felt to be especially suitable, meaningful, or purposeful**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性至正式；教育、宗教、社會服務

---

## 10 Calling

**天職；使命感強的職業**

比 `vocation` 更帶情感和使命感。當 `career` 被描述成『這就是我注定要做的工作』時，`calling` 很自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She discovered that nursing was her calling.

她發現護理是自己的天職。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- find one's calling
- true calling
- feel a calling
- calling in life

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **calling = a career or role someone feels deeply drawn or destined to pursue**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性、敘事、較有感情色彩

---

## 11 Trade

**行業；手藝職業**

當 `career` 指需要實務技能、學徒訓練或手藝的職業，例如木工、電工、水喉等，`trade` 很精準。它不是所有 career 的一般同義詞。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He learned the electrical trade from his father.

他跟父親學習電工這門手藝職業。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- skilled trade
- learn a trade
- enter the trade
- trade career
- construction trades

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **trade = a skilled manual or practical occupation, often learned through training or apprenticeship**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性；技職、建造、教育

---

## 12 Livelihood

**生計；謀生方式**

當 `career` 的重點不是身份或發展，而是『靠甚麼維生』時，`livelihood` 是重要的 near-synonym。它強調收入來源。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Fishing has provided his family with a livelihood for generations.

捕魚世代為他的家庭提供生計。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- earn a livelihood
- make a livelihood
- source of livelihood
- lose one's livelihood

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **livelihood = the work or means by which someone earns enough to live**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性至正式；經濟、社會、一般寫作

# 02 False synonyms

## 01 Career / Job

### 長期職涯不等於一份工作

`job` 通常是某一份具體職位。  
`career` 可以跨越很多 jobs。

> She has changed jobs three times during her career.

---

## 02 Career / Profession

### 很接近，但 profession 更像職業領域

`profession` 常指具專門技能／資格的職業，例如：
- medicine
- law
- teaching

`career` 更能描述一個人的長期發展歷程。

---

## 03 Career / Occupation

`occupation` 很適合表格、人口統計、行政分類。  
它通常回答：

> What work do you currently do?

而 `career` 可以跨越不同 occupations。

---

## 04 Career / Career path

`career path` 是職涯中不同階段和方向。  
它不是 career 本身的所有意思。

---

## 05 Career / Vocation

`vocation` 多了一層：
- suitability
- purpose
- calling
- personal meaning

不是每一份 career 都會被視為 vocation。

---

## 06 Career / Trade

`trade` 特別適合實務技能／技職：

> electrician  
> plumber  
> carpenter  
> welder

不能把所有 professional careers 都叫 trade。

---

## 07 Career / Livelihood

`livelihood` 強調『靠甚麼生活』。  
它關心收入和生計，而不是升遷、專業身份或成就。

---

## 08 Career / Employment

`employment` 表示受僱／就業狀態或工作。  
一個人可以暫時沒有 employment，但仍然擁有某個 career background。

# 03 練習與解釋

> 每個目標詞／表達三題，共 36 題。  
> **每題六個選項均按該句語境重新設計；正確答案位置會變化。**
>
> Distractors 會混合 `job / profession / occupation / career path / livelihood / vocation / position` 等真正容易混淆的職涯詞。

## 練習 01.1

**Target phrase: Profession · 職業；專業**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> She decided to make law her career.

**中文翻譯**

她決定以法律作為自己的職業。

| | 選項 |
|---|---|
| A | profession |
| B | occupation |
| C | livelihood |
| D | career path |
| E | job |
| F | hobby |

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**A. profession**

> She decided to make law her profession.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. profession ✓**  
法律需要專門訓練與專業資格，`profession` 最自然。

**B. occupation**  
也可泛指職業，但未突出法律的專業性。

**C. livelihood**  
只強調謀生來源。

**D. career path**  
表示發展路徑，不是職業本身。

**E. job**  
只表示一份工作，範圍較窄。

**F. hobby**  
嗜好，與職業相反。

---

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

## Career vs Job

**job** = 一份具體工作。  
**career** = 長期職業發展。

> first job  
> twenty-year career

---

## Career vs Profession

**profession** 更像一個職業領域。  
**career** 更像一個人在該領域中的長期歷程。

> the medical profession  
> a thirty-year medical career

---

## Profession vs Occupation

**profession** 常暗示專門訓練或資格。  
**occupation** 更中性、行政化。

> profession: lawyer  
> occupation: retail assistant

---

## Career vs Line of work

**line of work** 很自然、較口語，問的是：

> What kind of work do you do?

`career` 可更強調長期發展。

---

## Working life vs Professional life

兩者很接近。

**working life** 更強調由工作到退休的時間跨度。  
**professional life** 更強調職業世界／專業面向，常與 personal life 對比。

---

## Career path vs Career trajectory

**career path** = 經過哪些職位／階段。  
**career trajectory** = 整體往哪個方向發展。

> path: trainee → analyst → manager  
> trajectory: upward / stalled / changed

---

## Professional journey vs Career path

**professional journey** 更敘事、更人性化，包含轉折、錯誤、成長。  
**career path** 更實際、更規劃化。

---

## Vocation vs Calling

兩者都帶使命感。

**vocation** 稍正式，可指適合自己、有意義的職業。  
**calling** 更有『內在召喚／注定要做』的感覺。

---

## Trade vs Profession

**trade** 偏實務手藝、技職、學徒訓練。  
**profession** 偏專業教育、資格和知識。

---

## Career vs Livelihood

**career** 關心：
- development
- identity
- progression
- achievement

**livelihood** 關心：
- earning a living
- income
- economic survival

---

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Career

常見：

> a career in medicine  
> a career as a teacher  
> pursue a career  
> begin a career  
> career development

---

## Profession

> enter the profession  
> choose a profession  
> the legal profession

---

## Occupation

通常可數：

> What is your occupation?  
> occupations in the service sector

---

## Line of work

固定結構：

> What line of work are you in?  
> people in this line of work

---

## Career as / career in

> a career as an engineer = 身份／職位  
> a career in engineering = 領域

---

## Vocation / Calling

常見：

> a vocation in teaching  
> feel a calling to serve  
> find one's calling

---

## Livelihood

常見：

> earn a livelihood  
> make a livelihood  
> source of livelihood

通常不拿它來表示升職或職涯規劃。

# 09 核心決策問題

1. **是專門職業／專業領域嗎？** → `profession`
2. **是表格／統計上的職業分類嗎？** → `occupation`
3. **只是日常問『做哪一行』嗎？** → `line of work`
4. **是整段工作人生嗎？** → `working life`
5. **是在和私人生活對比嗎？** → `professional life`
6. **是由一個職位走向下一個職位的路線嗎？** → `career path`
7. **是職涯整體上升／轉變方向嗎？** → `career trajectory`
8. **想講一個人的職涯故事與轉折嗎？** → `professional journey`
9. **是有意義、有使命感的志業嗎？** → `vocation`
10. **是『這就是我注定要做的』天職嗎？** → `calling`
11. **是木工、電工、焊接等技職嗎？** → `trade`
12. **真正重點是靠甚麼維生嗎？** → `livelihood`

# 10 Final note for system use

最有用的替代表達：

`profession / occupation / line of work / working life / professional life / career path / career trajectory / professional journey / vocation / calling / trade / livelihood`

常用 expressions：

`climb the career ladder / move up the ladder / make a career out of / career change / change careers / get one's foot in the door / find one's calling / make a living`

最重要的 false-synonym / near-miss group：

`job / position / employment / profession / occupation / livelihood / vocation / trade`

它們都和工作有關，但分別表示 **單一工作、職位、就業狀態、專業職業、行政職業分類、生計、志業和技職**。

真正的選詞依據是：

> **Does “career” mean a profession, an occupation, a line of work, an entire working life, a development path, a personal professional journey, a vocation, or simply a livelihood?**
