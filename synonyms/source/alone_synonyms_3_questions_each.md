# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Alone

**「獨自／單獨／只有」，究竟是哪一種 `alone`？**

`alone` 有兩個非常常見而且彼此不同的核心意思：

1. **沒有其他人在身邊／沒有別人一起做**  
   → `by oneself`, `on one's own`, `solo`, `unaccompanied`
2. **只有這一個／單憑這一項／僅僅**  
   → `solely`, `only`, `just`, `by itself`

所以 `alone` 的近義詞選擇，首先要判斷它是在說 **「一個人」**，還是在說 **「只有這一項」**。

> **核心原則**
>
> 這一組只保留和 `alone` **較接近、在特定語境中可以近乎互換** 的詞、固定搭配或常用表達。
>
> 也會收錄一些常見 idiomatic expressions，例如：
>
> - `all by oneself`
> - `on one's own`
> - `single-handedly`
> - `in that year alone`
>
> 它們不是在所有句子中都能機械替換，但在對應義項中非常接近。

# 01 同義詞指南

## Alone 的 12 個近義／近乎可互換說法

---

## 01 By oneself

**獨自；自己一個人**

最直接的近義表達之一，表示沒有其他人陪伴或協助。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She travelled across Europe by herself.

她獨自遊歷歐洲。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- by myself
- by yourself
- by himself / herself
- by ourselves / themselves

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **by oneself = alone, without another person**

---

## 02 On one's own

**獨自；靠自己**

和 `by oneself` 很接近，但常多一點「自己處理／沒有別人幫助」的意味。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He learned to cook on his own.

他靠自己學會煮食。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- live on one's own
- travel on one's own
- do something on one's own
- manage on one's own

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **on one's own = alone or independently, without help**

---

## 03 All by oneself

**完全自己一個；全靠自己**

比 `by oneself` 更強調「真的沒有別人一起／沒有幫手」。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She built the model all by herself.

她完全靠自己把模型做了出來。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- all by myself
- all by yourself
- all by himself
- all by themselves

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **all by oneself = completely alone or without assistance**

---

## 04 Solo

**單獨的；獨自地**

常見於旅行、演出、工作、飛行、運動等語境，語氣簡潔自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He completed his first solo flight at the age of nineteen.

他十九歲時完成第一次單獨飛行。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- solo trip
- solo traveller
- solo performance
- fly solo
- go solo

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **solo = done by one person alone**

---

## 05 Unaccompanied

**無人陪同的；獨自的**

較正式，強調沒有其他人陪伴，常見於旅遊、航空、醫療或行政語境。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Children under twelve may not travel unaccompanied.

十二歲以下兒童不得在無人陪同下旅行。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- travel unaccompanied
- unaccompanied child
- unaccompanied passenger
- arrive unaccompanied

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **unaccompanied = without another person accompanying you**

---

## 06 Unaided

**無人幫助地；靠自己**

強調沒有協助，不一定表示附近完全沒有人。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He was able to walk unaided after several weeks of rehabilitation.

經過數星期復康後，他已能在無人協助下步行。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- walk unaided
- stand unaided
- work unaided
- complete something unaided

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **unaided = without help or assistance**

---

## 07 Independently

**獨立地；自行地**

當 `alone` 的意思是「不靠別人，獨立完成」時很接近。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Students are expected to work independently.

學生應自行完成工作。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- work independently
- live independently
- operate independently
- think independently

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **independently = without relying on others**

---

## 08 Single-handedly

**單槍匹馬地；一手包辦地**

常用於一個人完成通常需要多人完成的事情，語氣比普通 `alone` 更強。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She single-handedly organized the entire conference.

她單槍匹馬籌辦了整個會議。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- single-handedly complete
- single-handedly run
- single-handedly organize
- almost single-handedly

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **single-handedly = alone, especially when doing something difficult or substantial**

---

## 09 Solely

**純粹；僅僅；單憑**

當 `alone` 表示「只靠這一項／只有這個原因」時很接近，語氣較正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The decision was based solely on the available evidence.

這個決定純粹根據現有證據作出。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- solely responsible
- based solely on
- solely because
- solely for

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **solely = only; and nothing else**

---

## 10 Only

**只有；僅僅**

在 `alone` 表示「只有這個／這些」時，是最直接的替代詞。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> In that year alone, the company opened twelve new stores.

單在那一年，公司便開設了十二間新店。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- only one
- only this year
- in this month alone
- that alone

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **only = no more than / no one or nothing else**

---

## 11 Just

**只是；僅僅；單單**

口語中常可代替表示「僅僅」的 `alone`。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Just one mistake can cause the entire system to fail.

單單一個錯誤就可能令整個系統失效。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- just one
- just this once
- just that
- just by itself

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **just = only; simply**

---

## 12 By itself

**單獨地；本身就；僅靠它自己**

可表示某物沒有其他東西一起存在，也可表示「單憑這一項就足以」。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> That fact by itself is enough to justify further investigation.

單憑那個事實本身，就足以支持進一步調查。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- by itself
- by themselves
- sufficient by itself
- not enough by itself

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **by itself = alone as a thing, or without anything else contributing**

# 02 False synonyms

## 01 Alone / Lonely

### 獨自不等於孤單

`alone` 描述客觀狀態：沒有其他人在身邊。  
`lonely` 描述主觀感受：感到寂寞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> She lives alone but does not feel lonely.

---

## 02 Alone / Single

### 一個人不等於單身

`single` 常表示未婚／沒有伴侶，也可表示「一個」。  
`alone` 表示沒有人陪伴。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> He is single, but he does not live alone.

---

## 03 Alone / Isolated

### 獨自不一定等於與世隔絕

`isolated` 通常更強，表示與其他人、地方或系統隔離。

---

## 04 By oneself / Unaided

### 沒有人陪不一定等於沒有人幫

`by oneself` 強調沒有其他人一起。  
`unaided` 強調沒有幫助。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> She travelled by herself but received help along the way.  
> He stood up unaided while the nurse watched.

---

## 05 On one's own / Independently

### 很接近，但語氣不同

`on one's own` 更日常。  
`independently` 更正式，而且常突出自主能力。

---

## 06 Single-handedly / Alone

### 單槍匹馬比普通 alone 更強

`single-handedly` 通常表示一個人完成本來很大、很難或通常需要多人完成的工作。

---

## 07 Solely / Alone

### `solely` 只適合「僅僅」義

不能把：

> She lives alone.

改成：

> She lives solely. ✗

---

## 08 Alone / Only

### `only` 只適合部分語境

`alone` 可以表示沒有陪伴，也可以表示「單單」。  
`only` 主要對應第二種。

---

# 03 練習與解釋

> 每個目標表達三題。原句故意使用寬泛的 `alone`；請根據語境選出最自然、最接近的替代表達。

---

## 練習 01.1

**Target phrase: By oneself · 獨自**

> She travelled alone through Italy for three weeks.

**中文翻譯**

她獨自到意大利旅行了三星期。

| | 選項 |
|---|---|
| A | by herself |
| B | solely |
| C | only |
| D | isolated |
| E | lonely |
| F | single |

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**by herself**

> She travelled through Italy by herself for three weeks.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. by herself ✓**  
表示沒有其他人陪同旅行，最直接。

**B. solely**  
不能表示「一個人旅行」。

**C. only**  
不自然。

**D. isolated**  
語氣太強，表示隔絕。

**E. lonely**  
表示感到寂寞，不是客觀獨自。

**F. single**  
不表示旅行時沒有人陪。

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

## By oneself vs On one's own

兩者非常接近。

**by oneself** = 強調沒有其他人在一起。  
**on one's own** = 常多一點「靠自己處理」的意味。

> She travelled by herself.  
> She learned the language on her own.

---

## By oneself vs All by oneself

**all by oneself** 語氣更強。

> He made dinner by himself.  
> He cooked the entire wedding meal all by himself.

---

## Solo vs Alone

**solo** 特別常見於：

- travel
- performance
- flight
- sport
- career

> solo trip  
> solo performance

---

## Unaccompanied vs Alone

**unaccompanied** 較正式，而且只強調沒有人陪同。

> an unaccompanied minor

---

## Unaided vs Unaccompanied

**unaided** = 沒有幫助。  
**unaccompanied** = 沒有陪伴。

一個人可以：

> walk unaided while accompanied by a nurse.

---

## Independently vs On one's own

兩者都可表示「靠自己」。

**independently** = 更正式、更強調自主。  
**on one's own** = 更日常。

---

## Single-handedly vs Alone

**single-handedly** 更強調成就感或工作量。

> She did it alone.  
> She single-handedly rebuilt the entire department.

---

## Solely vs Only

兩者都可表示「僅僅」。

**solely** = 較正式。  
**only** = 最通用。

> based solely on cost  
> based only on cost

---

## Just vs Only

在「僅僅」義中非常接近。

> just one reason  
> only one reason

`just` 更口語。

---

## Alone vs By itself

當主語是物件、因素、證據、價格等：

> This fact alone...  
> This fact by itself...

兩者非常接近。

---

## Leave someone alone

**別打擾某人；讓某人靜一靜**

> Leave me alone for a few minutes.

這不是「獨自」的普通替換，而是一個固定表達。

---

## Let well enough alone

**事情已經可以，就不要再亂改**

常見諺語式表達，意思接近「別多事／別把本來沒問題的事情弄壞」。

---

公司決定不與別人合作，自己單獨發展。

**SEMANTIC CUE**

> **go it alone = act independently without partners or support**

---

**獨立存在；自成一體**

這個裝置毋須連接電腦也能獨立運作。

---

**別去動已經沒問題的東西**

和 `let well enough alone` 非常接近。

---

這是 `alone` 表示「單單、僅僅」的高頻句型。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Alone after a linking verb

自然：

> She is alone.  
> He lives alone.

`alone` 通常不直接放在名詞前：

> an alone child ✗

通常說：

> a child who is alone  
> an unaccompanied child

---

## By oneself

代詞會跟主語改變：

- I → by myself
- you → by yourself / yourselves
- he → by himself
- she → by herself
- we → by ourselves
- they → by themselves

---

## On one's own

同樣要配合代詞：

> on my own  
> on your own  
> on his own  
> on their own

---

## Alone = only

在這一義中，`alone` 常放在名詞／時間片語後：

> this year alone  
> price alone  
> that reason alone  
> the evidence alone

---

## Solely

通常放在所修飾部分前：

> solely responsible  
> based solely on cost  
> solely because of...

# 09 核心決策問題

1. **只是沒有其他人陪嗎？** → `by oneself`
2. **重點是靠自己處理嗎？** → `on one's own`
3. **要強調完全自己完成嗎？** → `all by oneself`
4. **是單人旅行／演出／飛行嗎？** → `solo`
5. **需要正式說「無人陪同」嗎？** → `unaccompanied`
6. **是沒有任何協助嗎？** → `unaided`
7. **是獨立自主地完成或運作嗎？** → `independently`
8. **是一個人完成很大或很難的工作嗎？** → `single-handedly`
9. **是「純粹／僅僅因為」嗎？** → `solely`
10. **是「只有這一個／這一段時間」嗎？** → `only`
11. **想用口語說「單單／只是」嗎？** → `just`
12. **是「單憑某物本身」嗎？** → `by itself`

---

# 10 Final note for system use

`alone` 有兩個主要語義群，不能混在一起：

### A. without other people / without help
`by oneself / on one's own / all by oneself / solo / unaccompanied / unaided / independently / single-handedly`

### B. only / solely / by itself
`solely / only / just / by itself`

另外，高頻常用表達包括：

`go it alone / stand alone / leave someone alone / in that year alone / let well enough alone`

真正的選詞依據是：

> **Does “alone” mean without other people, without help, independently, or simply “only this one thing”?**
