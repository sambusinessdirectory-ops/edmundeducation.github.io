# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Turn into

**`turn into` 可以表示單純變成、重大轉變、改變形式、逐步發展、長期演變、功能性轉換、成長為某種狀態，或由一個階段過渡到另一個。不同替代表達的核心差別，是『變化幅度』『是否漸進』『是否涉及功能／格式』『是否帶成長』『是否屬有計劃過渡』。**

核心替代表達：

`become / transform into / change into / develop into / evolve into / convert into / grow into / transition into`

> **核心原則**：先判斷 turn into 指的是 **simple becoming, major transformation, change of form, gradual development, evolution, conversion, growth, or transition**。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Become

**變成；成為**

和 `turn into` 最廣泛、最直接的替代之一。當重點只是某人或某物進入新的狀態、身份或角色，而不特別強調轉變過程時，`become` 很自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The small company became a major supplier within a few years.

這間小公司在數年間成為主要供應商。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **entering a new state, identity, or role**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 02 Transform into

**轉變成；徹底變成**

當 `turn into` 指明顯、重大甚至徹底的變化時，`transform into` 很精準。它比 `become` 更強調變化幅度和過程。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The old factory was transformed into a community arts centre.

這座舊工廠被改造成社區藝術中心。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **changing substantially into a different form or function**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、設計、城市

---

## 03 Change into

**變成；換成**

非常直接的近義表達，當某物由一種形式、狀態或外觀變成另一種時很自然。它比 `transform into` 中性、幅度也可大可小。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The liquid changes into a gas when heated.

液體受熱時會變成氣體。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **moving from one form or state to another**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 04 Develop into

**發展成；逐漸成為**

當 `turn into` 指經過一段時間逐步發展，最終成為另一種事物時，`develop into` 很自然。它強調 gradual development。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The small project developed into a nationwide programme.

這個小型項目逐步發展成全國性計劃。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **gradually becoming something more developed or complex**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、工作、正式

---

## 05 Evolve into

**演變成；逐步演化成**

和 `develop into` 接近，但更強調長時間、自然或連續的演變，常用於制度、技術、組織、語言和社會現象。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The service evolved into a fully digital platform.

這項服務逐步演變成全面數碼平台。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **gradually changing into a new form over time**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、科技、社會

---

## 06 Convert into

**轉換成；改造成**

當 `turn into` 涉及用途、格式、單位、能源或物料的轉換時，`convert into` 很精準。它比 `change into` 更技術化、功能化。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The system converts waste heat into electricity.

系統把廢熱轉換成電力。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **changing something into a different form, use, or system**

**REGISTER · 語域**

技術、工程、正式

---

## 07 Grow into

**成長為；逐漸成為**

當 `turn into` 指人、企業、社區或生物隨時間成長，最後成為更成熟或更大型的狀態時，`grow into` 很自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The family business grew into an international company.

這盤家族生意逐步成長為國際公司。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **becoming something larger or more mature through growth**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、商業

---

## 08 Transition into

**過渡到；轉型成**

當 `turn into` 指由一個階段、角色、制度或運作模式有計劃地轉到另一個時，`transition into` 很合適。它比 `become` 更強調過渡過程。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The organisation transitioned into a hybrid working model.

機構過渡到混合工作模式。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **moving from one state, role, or system into another**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、商業、政策

# 02 False synonyms

## Turn into / Become
`become` 最廣，通常只表示進入新狀態／身份，不強調具體轉變機制。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The discussion turned into an argument.  
The discussion became an argument.

## Turn into / Transform into
`transform into` 強調較大、較明顯的改變。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The warehouse turned into an arts centre.  
The warehouse was transformed into an arts centre.

## Turn into / Change into
`change into` 最中性，表示由一種形式或狀態變成另一種。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Water turns into ice.  
Water changes into ice.

## Turn into / Develop into
`develop into` 強調逐步發展。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The hobby turned into a business.  
The hobby developed into a business over several years.

## Turn into / Evolve into
`evolve into` 更強調長時間、連續而自然的演變。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The network turned into an association.  
Over time, it evolved into a formal professional body.

## Turn into / Convert into
`convert into` 特別適合用途、格式、單位、能源等轉換。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The building was turned into offices.  
The building was converted into offices.

## Turn into / Grow into
`grow into` 帶有成長、成熟或規模擴大的意味。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The start-up turned into a large company.  
The start-up grew into a large company.

## Turn into / Transition into
`transition into` 強調從一個階段／角色／制度平穩或有計劃地過渡到另一個。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The company turned into a subscription business.  
The company transitioned into a subscription-based model.

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Become vs Turn into** — become 最廣，而且通常不接 into。
- **Transform into vs Change into** — transform 更強調重大變化。
- **Develop into vs Evolve into** — develop 偏發展；evolve 更偏長期連續演變。
- **Convert into vs Transform into** — convert 常是功能／格式轉換；transform 偏整體性改變。
- **Grow into vs Develop into** — grow into 更有成長／成熟意味。
- **Transition into vs Become** — transition into 更強調過渡過程。
- **Turn into ≠ become into** — `become` 不接 `into`。
- **Turn into ≠ turn to** — `turn to` 常是求助／轉向，不等於變成。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Turn into
可不及物：

> The discussion turned into an argument.

也可及物：

> They turned the warehouse into offices.

## Become
不要加 `into`：

> become a manager ✓  
> become into a manager ✗

## Transform into
> transform A **into** B  
> be transformed **into** B

## Change into
> change **into** a different form

## Develop into
> develop **into** a larger organisation

## Evolve into
> evolve **into** a new model

## Convert into
> convert A **into** B

## Grow into
> grow **into** a role  
> grow **into** a large company

## Transition into
> transition **into** a new role/model

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 只想說「變成」？ → **become**
2. 是重大／徹底改造？ → **transform into**
3. 只是形式／狀態改變？ → **change into**
4. 是逐步發展而成？ → **develop into**
5. 是多年自然演變？ → **evolve into**
6. 是格式／用途／能源轉換？ → **convert into**
7. 有成長／成熟意味？ → **grow into**
8. 是從一個角色／制度平穩過渡？ → **transition into**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`become / transform into / change into / develop into / evolve into / convert into / grow into / transition into`

尤其要記住：

> **become** = 單純變成  
> **transform into** = 大幅轉變  
> **develop into** = 逐步發展  
> **evolve into** = 長期演變  
> **convert into** = 功能／格式轉換  
> **grow into** = 成長為  
> **transition into** = 過渡到

最重要的一點：

> **become ≠ become into**

`become` 直接接名詞／形容詞；其他很多 turn-into 近義表達則會保留 `into`。
