# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Entirely

**完全地、全然地、充分地、徹底地、全數地、由始至終、毫無例外、在各方面都——究竟是哪一種 entirely？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**completely; wholly; fully; totally; entirely so; in full; from beginning to end; without exception; in every respect**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **completely; wholly; fully** 全部保留。  
> 本課特別區分一般完整程度、正式抽象語境、固定搭配、口語強調、全數金額／文件、整個時間流程、零例外，以及所有方面都成立。

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Completely

**完全地；徹底地**

最直接、最通用的近義詞之一。表示某事在程度、範圍或狀態上達到完整程度，沒有部分例外。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The room was completely empty by midnight.

到午夜時，房間已完全空了。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- completely different

- completely empty

- completely wrong

- completely forgotten


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 entirely 是一般『完全』、正式全然、狀態充分、口語強調、全數付款／完整文件、由頭到尾、全體零例外，還是在所有方面都成立？

## 02 Wholly

**完全地；全然地**

### EXAMPLE · 例句

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- wholly dependent

- wholly unacceptable

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Completely

**最直接但不是所有搭配都最佳**

completely 很廣；`fully aware`, `wholly dependent`, `paid in full` 等固定搭配往往更自然。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The room was completely empty; she was fully aware of the risk.

---

## 02 Wholly

**較正式**

wholly 常見於正式評價、責任與依賴關係，口語中通常少於 completely/totally。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The organisation is wholly dependent on donations.

---

## 03 Fully

**常表示狀態達到完整程度**

fully 很自然於 aware, understand, operational, booked, equipped。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The system is fully operational.

---

## 04 Totally

**較口語、強調**

totally 很常見於 spoken English，但正式文件不一定適合。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

That explanation is totally wrong.

---

## 05 Absolutely

**強度高，但搭配受限制**

absolutely 常與 gradable/strong adjectives 有特定搭配，不是任何 entirely 都能直接換。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

I absolutely agree; the room was completely empty.

---

## 06 Partly / largely

**程度未達全部**

partly = 部分；largely = 大部分，兩者都保留未涵蓋部分。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The plan was largely successful, not entirely successful.

---

## 07 Entire / entirely

**詞性陷阱**

`entire` 是形容詞；`entirely` 是副詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The entire report; the report was entirely accurate.

---

## 08 Entirely

**語義範圍**

entirely 也可用於否定或修飾 judgement，例如 `not entirely clear`，表示『不完全』。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The answer is not entirely clear.

# 03 練習與解釋

> **每個核心近義表達 3 題，共 27 題。**
> 每題均以完整程度、搭配習慣、全數範圍、群體例外與時間覆蓋作辨析；六個選項均逐項解釋。


## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** completely · 完全地；徹底地

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

The room was entirely empty by midnight.

### 中文翻譯

到午夜時，房間已完全空了。

### OPTIONS


- **A. completely**

- **B. fully**

- **C. totally**

- **D. partly**

- **E. slightly**

- **F. approximately**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**completely · 完全地；徹底地**

The room was **completely** empty by midnight.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. completely ✓** — `completely` = 完全地，沒有部分剩餘或例外。 **本句為何最合適：** empty describes a state with no remaining occupants or objects。

- **B. fully** — `fully` = 充分／完全地達到某狀態。 常搭配 understand / aware / operational 等狀態；若要強調強烈口語語氣或全數金額，其他形式更自然。 **本句線索：** empty describes a state with no remaining occupants or objects。

- **C. totally** — `totally` = 完全地；較口語、語氣較強。 語氣較口語、強烈；正式法律、付款或機構文本通常不首選。 **本句線索：** empty describes a state with no remaining occupants or objects。

- **D. partly** — `partly` = 部分地。 只表示部分成立，和 entirely 相反。 **本句線索：** empty describes a state with no remaining occupants or objects。

- **E. slightly** — `slightly` = 稍微地。 只表示輕微程度。 **本句線索：** empty describes a state with no remaining occupants or objects。

- **F. approximately** — `approximately` = 大約地。 表示大約，不是完全。 **本句線索：** empty describes a state with no remaining occupants or objects。

| 表達 | 核心差異 |
|---|---|
| **completely** | 最廣、最通用的「完全地」 |
| **wholly** | 較正式，常見於責任、依賴、評價 |
| **fully** | 狀態／理解／運作達到完整程度 |
| **totally** | 較口語、強調程度很高 |

| **in full** | 全數金額／整份內容／完整履行 |
| **from beginning to end** | 由整個過程開端至結束 |
| **without exception** | 全體符合，沒有任何例外 |
| **in every respect** | 在所有相關方面都成立 |

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Entirely vs Completely
`completely` 最通用：
- completely empty
- completely different
- completely wrong

## Entirely vs Wholly
`wholly` 更正式：
- wholly responsible
- wholly dependent
- wholly unacceptable

## Entirely vs Fully
`fully` 有很多強固定搭配：
- fully aware
- fully understand
- fully operational

## Entirely vs Totally
`totally` 較口語、強調：
- totally wrong
- totally different
- totally surprised

## Entirely vs In full
`in full` 不是一般程度副詞，而是：
- pay in full
- refund in full
- read in full

## Entirely vs Without exception
後者專門處理「每一個成員都符合」。

## Entirely vs In every respect
後者表示多個相關維度全部成立。

常見於：
- fully understand
- fully aware
- fully operational
- fully developed

## 04 Wholly
較正式：
- wholly owned
- wholly dependent
- wholly unacceptable

## 05 In full
