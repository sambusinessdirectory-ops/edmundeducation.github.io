# COLLEAGUES · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Colleagues

**「同事／同業／工作伙伴」，要分清『同一工作場所』、『同一團隊』、『同級』和『同一專業』。**

本單元集中處理 **colleagues = people connected through the same workplace, organisation, team, profession, or professional activity**。你提供的 **co-workers; fellow employees; workmates** 都屬核心近義詞，但精確焦點不同：**co-workers** 最一般，**fellow employees** 強調同一僱主，**workmates** 更口語、尤其常見於英式英語。

這次保留 **13 個**真正有教學價值的詞／表達，而不是硬湊數量。同時延續最新 inspection 標準：每個干擾選項均解釋**其本身意思 + 為何在該句不合適**。

## Tier 1 — Core workplace equivalents · 核心職場近義詞

### 01 Co-workers

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：同事；一起工作的人  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：people who work with someone, usually in the same organisation or workplace  
**用法提示**：和 colleagues 最直接、最常見的替代之一，尤其一般職場語境。  
**常見搭配**：co-workers and managers; get along with co-workers; support co-workers; talk to co-workers; former co-workers.  
**常見搭配對象**：office, workplace, company, team, shift.  
**與 colleagues 的辨析**：Co-worker 強調一起工作；colleague 稍較正式，也可指同一專業領域但不一定同一僱主的人。  
**使用限制**：若只是同一專業界別、沒有一起工作，professional peer 可能更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She asked her co-workers for help with the report.  
她向同事請教如何處理報告。

### 02 Fellow employees

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：同僱員；同公司的員工  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：other employees who work for the same employer  
**用法提示**：當 colleagues 明確指同一僱主旗下的其他員工時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：fellow employees; support fellow employees; consult fellow employees; employee network; fellow staff.  
**常見搭配對象**：company, organisation, employer, workplace.  
**與 colleagues 的辨析**：Fellow employees 強調同一僱主；colleague 可更廣。  
**使用限制**：跨公司合作夥伴不應叫 fellow employees。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He shared the update with his fellow employees.  
他把最新消息告訴同公司的其他員工。

### 03 Workmates

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：同事；工作夥伴  
**語域**：informal / especially British  
**核心語義**：people who work together in the same workplace  
**用法提示**：英式英語中很自然的口語替代，語氣比 colleagues 更親切。  
**常見搭配**：old workmates; chat with workmates; workmates at the factory; meet workmates; friendly workmates.  
**常見搭配對象**：workplace, factory, shop, office, team.  
**與 colleagues 的辨析**：Workmate 更口語；colleague 更中性、專業。  
**使用限制**：正式報告、商業信件通常優先用 colleague / co-worker。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She went for lunch with her workmates.  
她和同事一起去吃午飯。

### 04 Fellow staff members

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：其他職員；同事  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：other members of the same staff or workforce  
**用法提示**：當 colleagues 指同一學校、醫院、機構或部門的職員時自然。  
**常見搭配**：fellow staff members; support staff members; staff meeting; consult fellow staff; staff team.  
**常見搭配對象**：school, hospital, office, organisation, department.  
**與 colleagues 的辨析**：Staff members 強調屬於同一 staff；colleague 可不限正式 staff 名冊。  
**使用限制**：外部顧問、合作機構人員通常不算 fellow staff members。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Teachers discussed the change with fellow staff members.  
教師和其他職員討論了這項改動。

### 05 Team members

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：團隊成員；同隊同事  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：people who belong to the same working team  
**用法提示**：當 colleagues 明確是同一項目組、部門小組或工作團隊時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：team members; fellow team members; support team members; team-member feedback; communicate with the team.  
**常見搭配對象**：project team, department, task force, work group.  
**與 colleagues 的辨析**：Team member 比 colleague 更窄，只涵蓋同一團隊。  
**使用限制**：同公司其他部門的 colleagues 未必是 team members。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She thanked her team members for meeting the deadline.  
她感謝團隊成員如期完成工作。

## Tier 2 — Professional / role-based equivalents · 專業與角色近義表達

### 06 Peers

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：同儕；同級人士  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：people of similar status, rank, age, or professional standing  
**用法提示**：當 colleagues 的重點是同級、同資歷或相若職位，而不是單純一起工作時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：professional peers; peer support; peer feedback; respected by peers; compare with peers.  
**常見搭配對象**：profession, workplace hierarchy, academic field, industry.  
**與 colleagues 的辨析**：Peer 強調相若地位；colleague 不要求職級相同。  
**使用限制**：上司和下屬都可以是 colleagues，但通常不是 peers。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Managers are encouraged to exchange ideas with their peers.  
鼓勵經理和同級同儕交流意見。

### 07 Professional peers

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：專業同儕；同業人士  
**語域**：formal / professional  
**核心語義**：people with comparable professional roles or expertise  
**用法提示**：當 colleagues 來自同一專業領域、甚至不同機構時非常適合。  
**常見搭配**：professional peers; peer network; consult professional peers; respected by peers; peer discussion.  
**常見搭配對象**：medicine, law, education, research, engineering.  
**與 colleagues 的辨析**：Professional peers 可跨機構；colleagues 常令人想到直接職場關係。  
**使用限制**：一般辦公室同事若沒有專業同儕意味，用 co-workers 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She discussed the case with professional peers from other hospitals.  
她和其他醫院的專業同儕討論該個案。

### 08 Associates

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：同事；業務夥伴；合作人士  
**語域**：formal / business  
**核心語義**：people connected through work, business, or professional activity  
**用法提示**：在商業、法律或專業語境中可指工作上的同事／合作人士。  
**常見搭配**：business associates; professional associates; close associates; associates at the firm; senior associates.  
**常見搭配對象**：firm, business, law, consultancy, professional network.  
**與 colleagues 的辨析**：Associate 比 colleague 更模糊，可指合作伙伴、同業或特定職級。  
**使用限制**：若需明確表示同一工作場所，co-worker / colleague 更清楚。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He discussed the proposal with several associates at the firm.  
他和公司幾名同事討論了這項建議。

### 09 Fellow professionals

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：同業專業人士；專業同儕  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：other people working in the same profession or professional field  
**用法提示**：當 colleagues 指同一專業界別的人，而不一定在同一機構工作時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：fellow professionals; network with professionals; advice from fellow professionals; professional community; professional conference.  
**常見搭配對象**：teacher, doctor, lawyer, engineer, designer.  
**與 colleagues 的辨析**：Fellow professionals 強調共同專業；colleagues 可只是同一辦公室的人。  
**使用限制**：非專業職位或一般團隊語境會顯得過正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The conference allowed designers to meet fellow professionals.  
會議讓設計師有機會認識同業專業人士。

### 10 Collaborators

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：合作伙伴；協作者  
**語域**：neutral / professional  
**核心語義**：people who work together on a particular task, project, or piece of work  
**用法提示**：當 colleagues 的關係主要建立在共同項目或共同產出上時很貼切。  
**常見搭配**：research collaborators; project collaborators; external collaborators; work closely with collaborators; international collaborators.  
**常見搭配對象**：research, project, publication, design, creative work.  
**與 colleagues 的辨析**：Collaborator 強調共同合作的項目；colleague 更像持續職場關係。  
**使用限制**：同公司但從未合作的人仍可叫 colleague，卻不一定是 collaborator。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The researchers met their international collaborators online.  
研究人員在網上與國際合作伙伴會面。

## Tier 3 — Context-specific work-relationship variants · 特定工作關係變體

### 11 Counterparts

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：對口同事；相應職位人士  
**語域**：formal / professional  
**核心語義**：people in another organisation, department, or country who hold equivalent roles  
**用法提示**：當 colleagues 指其他單位中職責相對應的人時非常精準。  
**常見搭配**：foreign counterparts; industry counterparts; meet counterparts; counterpart organisation; departmental counterparts.  
**常見搭配對象**：government, company, department, international organisation.  
**與 colleagues 的辨析**：Counterpart 必須有『相應角色』；普通 colleague 不需要。  
**使用限制**：同一團隊內的一般同事通常不叫 counterparts。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The finance team met its counterparts in the regional office.  
財務團隊與區域辦事處的對口同事會面。

### 12 People I work with

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：和我一起工作的人；同事  
**語域**：informal / explanatory  
**核心語義**：a plain-language phrase for the people someone works with  
**用法提示**：很自然的口語／解釋性改寫，適合避免重複 colleague。  
**常見搭配**：people I work with; people we work with; work closely with; people at work; people on my team.  
**常見搭配對象**：conversation, personal account, interview, informal writing.  
**與 colleagues 的辨析**：這是解釋性短語，語義清楚但不像 colleague 那麼簡潔。  
**使用限制**：正式履歷、報告或合約文字通常不宜。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
I often learn new techniques from the people I work with.  
我經常從和我一起工作的同事身上學到新技巧。

### 13 Office colleagues

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：辦公室同事  
**語域**：neutral / context-specific  
**核心語義**：colleagues who work in the same office environment  
**用法提示**：當需要把 colleagues 限定為辦公室中的同事，而不是整個機構的人時可用。  
**常見搭配**：office colleagues; chat with office colleagues; support colleagues in the office; office team; office staff.  
**常見搭配對象**：office, administrative workplace, corporate setting.  
**與 colleagues 的辨析**：Office colleagues 只是 colleagues 的具體子類。  
**使用限制**：工場、醫院病房、戶外工作或跨機構同業不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She discussed the new schedule with her office colleagues.  
她和辦公室同事討論新的時間表。

## 04 False synonyms / relationship traps · 容易誤選的詞與關係陷阱

### 01 Friends

**朋友不等於 colleagues**  
朋友是私人社交關係；同事可以成為朋友，但工作關係本身不代表友誼。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A colleague may be a friend, but the two relationships are not the same.

### 02 Classmates

**同學不等於同事**  
classmates 一起上課；colleagues 一起工作或屬同一專業環境。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Classmates share a class; colleagues share a workplace or professional context.

### 03 Employer

**僱主不等於 colleague**  
employer 是僱用員工的個人或機構；colleague 是工作上的同輩或其他工作人士。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
An employer employs workers; a colleague works alongside them.

### 04 Employee

**僱員不一定是自己的 colleague**  
employee 只說某人受僱；若不是和你一起工作，便不一定是你的 colleague。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
An employee is defined by employment status, not by a relationship to you.

### 05 Client

**客戶不等於 colleague**  
client 接受專業服務或購買服務；colleague 是工作關係中的同事／同業。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A client receives a service; a colleague works with or alongside you.

### 06 Supervisor

**上司可以是 colleague，但不是同義詞**  
supervisor 強調管理／監督職責；colleague 不指定上下級關係。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A supervisor may be your colleague, but the word identifies a management role.

### 07 Subordinate

**下屬不等於 colleague**  
subordinate 強調職級低於另一人；colleague 通常避免突出上下級。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Subordinate describes hierarchy; colleague describes a work relationship.

### 08 Acquaintance

**相識的人不等於 colleague**  
acquaintance 只表示認識但不熟的人，未必有任何工作關係。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
An acquaintance is someone you know; a colleague is connected through work.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Co-workers · 同事；一起工作的人

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

She asked her colleagues for help with the report.

**中文翻譯**  
她向同事請教如何處理報告。

- A. co-workers
- B. clients
- C. employers
- D. classmates
- E. acquaintances
- F. friends only

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**co-workers** · 同事；一起工作的人

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
She asked her co-workers for help with the report.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. co-workers ✓ — 這個選項保留原句的工作關係，而且最符合此句的具體職場線索。**
- **B. clients — 這個詞指接受／購買服務的人；原句談的是一起工作或同業關係。**
- **C. employers — 這個詞指僱用員工的一方；colleague 並不等於僱主。**
- **D. classmates — 這個詞屬教育／學習身份，不表示職場同事關係。**
- **E. acquaintances — 這只表示認識某人，沒有『一起工作』的必要含義。**
- **F. friends only — 朋友是私人社交關係；同事可以是朋友，但原句並沒有要求私人友誼。**

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **colleague vs co-worker**: 兩者高度接近；colleague 稍正式，也可指同業人士。
- **colleague vs fellow employee**: fellow employee 明確同一僱主，範圍較窄。
- **colleague vs workmate**: workmate 更口語，尤其英式英語自然。
- **colleague vs team member**: team member 必須同一團隊；colleague 可跨團隊。
- **colleague vs peer**: peer 強調相若職級／地位；colleague 不要求同級。
- **colleague vs professional peer**: professional peer 可來自另一機構，只要專業角色相近。
- **colleague vs collaborator**: collaborator 強調共同項目，而非單純職場關係。
- **colleague vs counterpart**: counterpart 是另一單位中職責相應的人。
- **colleague vs friend**: 工作關係和私人友誼可以重疊，但不是同一概念。
- **colleague vs client**: client 是服務接受者；colleague 是工作上的同事／同業。

## 08 Grammar, register & workplace traps · 文法、語域與職場關係陷阱

- **colleague** 是可數名詞：a colleague / several colleagues。
- **co-worker / coworker** 兩種拼法都可見；本課採用 **co-worker**。複數：co-workers。
- **workmate** 在英式英語尤其自然；美式英語通常更常說 co-worker。
- **staff** 常作集合名詞；若要清楚逐個計算，可說 **staff member / staff members**。
- **peer** 不等於任何同事：它通常要求地位、職級、年資或專業程度相近。
- **associate** 在不同公司可有特定職級含義，因此使用時要看機構制度。
- **counterpart** 常和 **in another office / organisation / country** 搭配，表示職責相應的人。
- **collaborator** 不代表同一僱主；跨大學、跨公司的人也可以是 collaborators。
- **colleague ≠ employee**：employee 是受僱身份；只有和某人形成工作關係時才是那人的 colleague。
- 正式商業／學術寫作通常優先用 **colleague / co-worker / professional peer**，不要用過度口語的 workmate，除非語氣合適。
