# POOR SERVICE · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Poor service

**「服務欠佳／差劣服務」要先分清問題究竟是：整體質素低、未達標準、服務不足、令人失望、客戶照顧差，還是職員對顧客態度惡劣。**

本單元集中處理 **poor service = customer-facing service that is low in quality, insufficient, disappointing, unhelpful, or disrespectful**。你提供的 **unsatisfactory customer service; bad treatment of customers** 分別代表 **正式的服務未達標** 和 **顧客被差劣對待** 兩個重要方向。

這個概念有多個自然近義／情境表達，但它們並不完全互換。本次保留 **10 個**高實用度詞／表達；其中 Tier 2–3 有些是較窄的 contextual variants。共 **30 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**；所有題目先以自然的 **poor service** 語境建立線索，再判斷最精確改寫。

## Tier 1 — Core poor-service equivalents · 核心差劣服務近義表達

### 01 Unsatisfactory customer service

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：令人不滿的顧客服務；未達標的客戶服務  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：customer service that does not meet an acceptable or expected standard  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **poor service** 最直接、最正式的改寫之一，尤其適合投訴、調查、報告和商業寫作。  
**常見搭配**：unsatisfactory customer service; receive unsatisfactory service; complaint about customer service; service standards; customer-service failure.  
**常見搭配對象**：shop, restaurant, bank, airline, hotel.  
**與 poor service 的辨析**：比 poor service 更正式，重點是服務未達預期或標準。  
**使用限制**：如果問題不是 customer-facing service，而是技術服務／公共服務，customer service 可能太窄。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Several passengers complained about unsatisfactory customer service at the airport desk.  
數名乘客投訴機場櫃位的顧客服務令人不滿。

### 02 Bad customer service

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：差劣顧客服務  
**語域**：informal / very common  
**核心語義**：customer service that is clearly poor in quality, helpfulness, responsiveness, or professionalism  
**用法提示**：很自然、很直接，適合一般口語、評論和日常投訴。  
**常見搭配**：bad customer service; experience bad service; complain about bad service; bad service experience; poor response.  
**常見搭配對象**：shop, restaurant, hotel, call centre, company.  
**與 poor service 的辨析**：和 poor service 非常接近，但語氣更口語、更直接。  
**使用限制**：正式報告通常 prefer unsatisfactory / substandard customer service。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The restaurant received several reviews mentioning bad customer service.  
餐廳收到數篇提及差劣顧客服務的評論。

### 03 Substandard service

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：低於標準的服務；不合標準的服務  
**語域**：formal / evaluative  
**核心語義**：service that falls below an expected, agreed, or professional standard  
**用法提示**：當 poor service 的核心是『未達標準』，而不只是令人不開心時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：substandard service; substandard care; below-standard service; service quality; service standard.  
**常見搭配對象**：hotel, contractor, provider, transport, support.  
**與 poor service 的辨析**：Substandard 比 poor 更有 benchmark / standard 的意味。  
**使用限制**：如果句子只是形容態度無禮，而不是整體質素未達標，poor treatment / rude service 更精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The regulator investigated repeated complaints about substandard service.  
監管機構調查多宗有關服務低於標準的投訴。

### 04 Inadequate service

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：不足的服務；不充分的服務  
**語域**：formal / neutral  
**核心語義**：service that is not sufficient to meet needs, requirements, or reasonable expectations  
**用法提示**：當 poor service 問題是人手不足、支援不足、回應不足或服務能力不夠時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：inadequate service; inadequate support; inadequate staffing; inadequate response; service provision.  
**常見搭配對象**：support, transport, healthcare, customer support, public service.  
**與 poor service 的辨析**：Inadequate 強調 not enough / insufficient；poor service 不一定是『不足』。  
**使用限制**：如果服務存在但態度差、粗魯，inadequate 未必抓到核心。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Customers complained that the helpline provided inadequate service during busy periods.  
顧客投訴熱線在繁忙時段提供的服務不足。

### 05 Disappointing service

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：令人失望的服務  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：service that fails to meet expectations and leaves customers dissatisfied  
**用法提示**：當 poor service 的重點是『與期望有落差』而非正式判定未達標時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：disappointing service; disappointing experience; disappointing response; customer disappointment; service experience.  
**常見搭配對象**：restaurant, hotel, retailer, airline, support team.  
**與 poor service 的辨析**：Disappointing 比 poor 主觀，重點是 expectation gap。  
**使用限制**：若需要客觀、正式判斷，substandard / unsatisfactory 更準確。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Guests described the hotel's response to the problem as disappointing service.  
住客形容酒店處理問題的服務令人失望。

## Tier 2 — Customer-care / treatment / experience variants · 客戶照顧、對待與體驗型變體

### 06 Poor customer care

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：差劣客戶照顧；欠佳顧客服務  
**語域**：neutral / service-industry  
**核心語義**：weak or ineffective care, attention, and support given to customers  
**用法提示**：當 poor service 特別涉及關心、跟進、協助和 customer handling 時很適合。  
**常見搭配**：poor customer care; customer-care standards; quality of care; customer support; after-sales care.  
**常見搭配對象**：complaint, follow-up, support, after-sales service, assistance.  
**與 poor service 的辨析**：Customer care 比 customer service 更強調 attentive support and treatment。  
**使用限制**：若問題只是速度慢或技術故障，不一定是 customer care 問題。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The company apologised for poor customer care after several complaints were ignored.  
公司因多宗投訴被忽略而就差劣客戶照顧道歉。

### 07 Bad treatment of customers

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：對顧客態度差；差劣對待顧客  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：rude, unfair, dismissive, or disrespectful behaviour towards customers  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，當 poor service 實際是『顧客被差劣對待』而不是流程／速度問題時最精準。  
**常見搭配**：bad treatment of customers; rude treatment; unfair treatment; disrespectful treatment; treat customers badly.  
**常見搭配對象**：customer, guest, passenger, client, visitor.  
**與 poor service 的辨析**：比 poor service 更窄，專指 treatment / behaviour。  
**使用限制**：等待時間過長、系統故障、供應不足未必屬 bad treatment。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The manager investigated reports of bad treatment of customers at the front desk.  
經理調查有關前台差劣對待顧客的報告。

### 08 Poor-quality service

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：低質素服務；服務質素欠佳  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：service whose overall quality is low  
**用法提示**：當 poor service 需要明確指 overall quality，而不是某單一失誤時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：poor-quality service; service quality; low-quality support; quality problem; quality of service.  
**常見搭配對象**：provider, contractor, support, transport, hospitality.  
**與 poor service 的辨析**：語義透明，特別適合強調 quality dimension。  
**使用限制**：若是某一次員工無禮，bad treatment / rude service 更精確。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Customers stopped using the provider because of consistently poor-quality service.  
由於服務質素長期欠佳，顧客停止使用該供應商。

### 09 Poor customer experience

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：差劣顧客體驗  
**語域**：business / customer-experience  
**核心語義**：a negative overall customer experience caused by service, processes, communication, or treatment  
**用法提示**：當 poor service 只是造成整體負面 customer journey 的其中一部分時可用。  
**常見搭配**：poor customer experience; customer journey; improve customer experience; negative experience; service experience.  
**常見搭配對象**：website, store, support process, booking, complaint journey.  
**與 poor service 的辨析**：比 poor service 更廣，可能包括網站、付款流程、等候時間等，不只人員服務。  
**使用限制**：如果只評價 staff service，本詞可能過廣。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Long queues and unclear information created a poor customer experience.  
長時間排隊和資訊不清造成差劣顧客體驗。

## Tier 3 — Attitude-specific variant · 態度型限定變體

### 10 Rude service

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：無禮的服務；態度惡劣的服務  
**語域**：informal / direct  
**核心語義**：service characterised by impolite, disrespectful, or unfriendly behaviour  
**用法提示**：當 poor service 的關鍵線索是 rude / dismissive staff 時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：rude service; rude staff; rude response; impolite treatment; unfriendly service.  
**常見搭配對象**：waiter, receptionist, cashier, support agent, staff.  
**與 poor service 的辨析**：Rude service 只捕捉 attitude / manners，不等於所有 poor service。  
**使用限制**：如果問題是慢、錯誤多、缺乏資源而非態度，rude 不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The customer left after receiving rude service from a member of staff.  
顧客因受到職員無禮對待而離開。

## 04 False synonyms / poor-service traps · 容易誤選的服務問題與詞義陷阱

### 01 Slow service

**服務慢 ≠ 所有 poor service**  
slow service 只描述速度；服務可以慢但仍有禮、準確、專業。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Slow service is one possible cause of poor service, not a full synonym.

### 02 Rude staff

**職員無禮 ≠ 所有 poor service**  
rude staff 描述人員態度，不涵蓋系統故障、等候時間或支援不足。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Rudeness is only one dimension of service quality.

### 03 Customer complaint

**顧客投訴 ≠ poor service**  
complaint 是顧客作出的反應／文件，不是服務本身。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A complaint reports a problem; it is not the service.

### 04 Service failure

**服務失誤 ≠ poor service**  
service failure 通常指一次具體失敗事件；poor service 可指整體或持續質素差。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A service failure is often a specific incident.

### 05 Unfriendly

**不友善 ≠ poor service**  
unfriendly 只描述態度，未必代表整體服務不合格。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Unfriendly is attitude-focused.

### 06 Cheap service

**便宜的服務 ≠ poor service**  
cheap 可指價格低；價格低不代表質素差。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Cheap refers to price unless context clearly means low quality.

### 07 Inadequate service

**只在服務『不足』sense 接近**  
inadequate 著重不足以滿足需要，不涵蓋所有服務差的情況。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Inadequate is insufficiency-focused.

### 08 Service / serve

**詞性陷阱**  
service 是名詞；serve 是動詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Service names the provision; serve is the action.

Several passengers complained about unsatisfactory customer service at the airline desk.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Unsatisfactory customer service ✓** — is a formal way to say that customer service did not meet an acceptable or expected standard; it fits complaints, reports and management language. In this sentence, a formal complaint context calls for service that failed to meet acceptable expectations. That makes **Unsatisfactory customer service** the most precise replacement rather than just a generally negative description.

It is plausible vocabulary in the same topic area, but it does not match the decisive context clue here: a formal complaint context calls for service that failed to meet acceptable expectations.
- **C.** **Substandard service** — means service falling below a defined or expected standard; it is especially natural in regulatory, contractual or audit contexts. It is plausible vocabulary in the same topic area, but it does not match the decisive context clue here: a formal complaint context calls for service that failed to meet acceptable expectations.
- **D.** **Slow service** — describes speed only; slow service can still be polite, accurate and otherwise competent. It is plausible vocabulary in the same topic area, but it does not match the decisive context clue here: a formal complaint context calls for service that failed to meet acceptable expectations.
- **E.** **Customer complaint** — is a customer's report or reaction to a problem, not the poor service itself. It is plausible vocabulary in the same topic area, but it does not match the decisive context clue here: a formal complaint context calls for service that failed to meet acceptable expectations.
- **F.** **Rude staff** — describes employees' behaviour, not the whole service system. It is plausible vocabulary in the same topic area, but it does not match the decisive context clue here: a formal complaint context calls for service that failed to meet acceptable expectations.

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The review criticised the shop for poor service from an unfriendly employee.

**中文翻譯**  
評論批評店舖職員態度無禮。

- A. Bad treatment of customers
- B. Bad customer service
- C. Poor customer care
- D. Slow service
- E. Service failure
- F. Rude service

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**F. Rude service** · 無禮的服務；態度惡劣的服務

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The review criticised the shop for rude service from an unfriendly employee.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Bad treatment of customers** — describes rude, unfair, dismissive or disrespectful behaviour towards customers; it is treatment-focused. It is plausible vocabulary in the same topic area, but it does not match the decisive context clue here: an unfriendly employee points specifically to manners and attitude.
- **B.** **Bad customer service** — is a direct, everyday phrase for clearly poor customer-facing service; it is common in reviews and personal complaints. It is plausible vocabulary in the same topic area, but it does not match the decisive context clue here: an unfriendly employee points specifically to manners and attitude.
- **C.** **Poor customer care** — focuses weak attention, help, follow-up and handling of customers rather than every aspect of service delivery. It is plausible vocabulary in the same topic area, but it does not match the decisive context clue here: an unfriendly employee points specifically to manners and attitude.
- **D.** **Slow service** — describes speed only; slow service can still be polite, accurate and otherwise competent. It is plausible vocabulary in the same topic area, but it does not match the decisive context clue here: an unfriendly employee points specifically to manners and attitude.
- **E.** **Service failure** — usually identifies a specific breakdown or incident rather than a general or continuing low standard. It is plausible vocabulary in the same topic area, but it does not match the decisive context clue here: an unfriendly employee points specifically to manners and attitude.
- **F.** **Rude service ✓** — specifically means impolite or disrespectful service; it is appropriate only when manners or attitude are the problem. In this sentence, an unfriendly employee points specifically to manners and attitude. That makes **Rude service** the most precise replacement rather than just a generally negative description.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **poor service vs unsatisfactory customer service**: 後者更正式，表示未達可接受或預期標準。
- **poor service vs substandard service**: substandard 更強調 below a defined/expected standard。
- **poor service vs inadequate service**: inadequate 強調服務量／支援不足。
- **poor service vs disappointing service**: disappointing 強調期望落差，較主觀。
- **poor service vs poor customer care**: customer care 強調協助、關心、跟進和投訴處理。
- **poor service vs bad treatment of customers**: 後者專指對顧客的態度／行為差。
- **poor service vs poor customer experience**: customer experience 更廣，包括流程、網站、等候等。
- **poor service vs rude service**: rude 只限無禮態度，不涵蓋速度、錯誤或資源不足。
- **poor service vs service failure**: service failure 常是一宗具體失誤；poor service 可是整體／持續問題。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & service-quality traps · 文法、搭配與服務質素陷阱

- **poor service** 可以指一次體驗，也可以指持續的服務質素問題。
- **customer service** 通常不可數：good customer service / poor customer service。
- **unsatisfactory customer service** 很適合正式投訴、報告和管理語境。
- **substandard service** 常暗示有一個可比較的標準。
- **inadequate service/support/provision** 常表示『不夠』而非『無禮』。
- **customer care** 常與 complaint handling / follow-up / assistance 搭配。
- **bad treatment of customers** 通常需要態度、尊重、公平等線索。
- **poor customer experience** 比 **poor service** 更廣，可包括 digital journey / queues / forms / information。
- **rude service** 是較窄的 attitude-specific 表達。
- **service** 是名詞；**serve** 是動詞；**service quality** 是常見名詞搭配。
- 英式／香港語境可用 **customer service, customer care, complaint handling, programme, organisation** 等自然搭配。
