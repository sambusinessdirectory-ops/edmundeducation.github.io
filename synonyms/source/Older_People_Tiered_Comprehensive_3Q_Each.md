# OLDER PEOPLE · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Older people

**「長者／年長人士」，現代英語最重要的是分清一般年齡稱呼、正式政策用語、退休身份，以及是否帶社區／公民身份。這個主題亦要留意尊重和包容語氣。**

本單元集中處理 **older people = people in the later stages of adulthood**。你提供的 **seniors; elderly people; senior citizens** 分別代表 **一般／服務型簡稱、較傳統年齡稱呼、公共／公民服務語境稱呼**。

這個概念真正可自然互換的詞不算很多，而且一些常見詞其實只適用於退休金、退休身份或代際比較。因此本次保留 **11 個**自然且具教學價值的詞／表達，而不是追求固定數量。共 **33 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**；正確答案位置在 A–F 間盡量平均分布。

## Tier 1 — Core age-group equivalents · 核心年齡群體近義表達

### 01 Seniors

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：長者；年長人士  
**語域**：neutral / common, especially North American  
**核心語義**：older adults considered as an age group  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很常見，尤其在北美、服務、折扣、社區活動和一般公共資訊中。  
**常見搭配**：senior services; senior discount; senior housing; services for seniors; senior community.  
**常見搭配對象**：resident, customer, service, housing, programme.  
**與 older people 的辨析**：Seniors 比 older people 更簡潔，但帶地區和服務語境色彩。  
**使用限制**：在正式英式／香港文本中，older people / older adults 往往更穩妥。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The centre offers free classes for seniors.  
中心為長者提供免費課程。

### 02 Elderly people

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：老年人；長者  
**語域**：neutral but increasingly dated in some contexts  
**核心語義**：people of advanced age  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 仍然可理解，也常見於較舊文本和部分正式語境，但現代寫作通常更偏好 older people / older adults。  
**常見搭配**：elderly people; elderly residents; elderly patients; care for elderly people; support for the elderly.  
**常見搭配對象**：resident, patient, person, care, service.  
**與 older people 的辨析**：語義接近 older people，但 elderly 有時會顯得把年齡當成脆弱／依賴的標籤。  
**使用限制**：不應假設所有年長人士都身體虛弱；現代包容語體常優先 older people。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The programme provides transport for elderly people who have difficulty travelling alone.  
計劃為獨自出行有困難的長者提供交通服務。

### 03 Senior citizens

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：長者；年長市民  
**語域**：neutral / public-service / somewhat formal  
**核心語義**：older members of the public, especially in civic, benefit, or service contexts  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 常見於政府服務、優惠、公共設施和較正式公告。  
**常見搭配**：senior citizens; benefits for senior citizens; senior-citizen discount; services for senior citizens.  
**常見搭配對象**：benefit, service, discount, citizen, programme.  
**與 older people 的辨析**：Senior citizens 比 older people 更偏公共／公民身份語境。  
**使用限制**：若不是公民身份或公共服務語境，older people 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The museum offers reduced admission for senior citizens.  
博物館為長者提供門票優惠。

### 04 Older adults

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：年長成年人；長者  
**語域**：neutral / modern / formal  
**核心語義**：adults in later life, especially in health, research, policy, and professional contexts  
**用法提示**：現代正式寫作中非常實用，尤其醫療、研究、社會政策和長者服務。  
**常見搭配**：older adults; health in older adults; services for older adults; active older adults; support older adults.  
**常見搭配對象**：health, research, service, exercise, policy.  
**與 older people 的辨析**：Older adults 比 elderly people 更中性、較少刻板印象。  
**使用限制**：日常口語中 older people 可能更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Regular activity can help older adults maintain strength and mobility.  
規律活動可以幫助長者維持力量和活動能力。

### 05 Older persons

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：年長人士；長者  
**語域**：formal / policy / legal  
**核心語義**：people of advanced age, especially in legal, policy, rights, and international-institution contexts  
**用法提示**：當 older people 出現在權利、政策、法規或正式文件中，可用 older persons。  
**常見搭配**：rights of older persons; protection of older persons; services for older persons; older-person policy.  
**常見搭配對象**：rights, policy, protection, service, legislation.  
**與 older people 的辨析**：比 older people 更正式、法律／政策化。  
**使用限制**：日常會話通常顯得過於正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The policy aims to protect the rights of older persons.  
政策旨在保障長者的權利。

## Tier 2 — Residence / life-stage / generation / retirement variants · 居民、人生階段、世代與退休身份變體

### 06 Older residents

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：年長居民；年長住戶  
**語域**：neutral / local-government / community  
**核心語義**：older people who live in a particular area, building, or community  
**用法提示**：當 older people 的重點是『某地區的年長居民』時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：older residents; support older residents; local older residents; older residents' needs; services for residents.  
**常見搭配對象**：district, estate, neighbourhood, community, building.  
**與 older people 的辨析**：只適用於有明確居住地／社區身份的語境。  
**使用限制**：若討論一般年長人口，不應縮窄成 residents。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The council consulted older residents about access to local services.  
市議會就本地服務的使用情況諮詢年長居民。

### 07 People in later life

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：晚年人士；處於人生後期的人  
**語域**：neutral / respectful / policy  
**核心語義**：people who are in the later stages of adulthood  
**用法提示**：較委婉、尊重，也常見於社會服務、公共政策和生活階段討論。  
**常見搭配**：people in later life; support in later life; wellbeing in later life; learning in later life; life in later years.  
**常見搭配對象**：wellbeing, housing, learning, work, support.  
**與 older people 的辨析**：比 older people 更強調 life stage，而不是單純年齡標籤。  
**使用限制**：若需要清楚統計年齡界線，這個表達可能太模糊。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The charity supports people in later life who want to remain independent.  
慈善機構支援希望保持獨立生活的晚年人士。

### 08 The older generation

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：較年長一代；上一代  
**語域**：neutral / comparative  
**核心語義**：older people considered collectively as a generation, often in contrast with younger people  
**用法提示**：當 older people 與 younger people 形成代際比較時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：older generation; younger generation; members of the older generation; attitudes of the older generation.  
**常見搭配對象**：attitude, technology, culture, family, society.  
**與 older people 的辨析**：這是 generation-based 集合概念，不等於所有個別 older people。  
**使用限制**：若沒有代際比較，不一定是最佳表達。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The survey compared media habits across the younger and older generations.  
調查比較較年輕和較年長世代的媒體習慣。

### 09 Retirees

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：退休人士  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：people who have retired from paid work  
**用法提示**：當 older people 的語境其實聚焦退休身份、退休生活或退休福利時可用。  
**常見搭配**：retirees; retired people; services for retirees; retirees' income; retiree community.  
**常見搭配對象**：pension, income, housing, travel, benefit.  
**與 older people 的辨析**：Retirees 是 employment-status 詞，不是年齡同義詞；較年輕的人也可以退休。  
**使用限制**：不能把所有 older people 都叫 retirees。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The scheme provides financial guidance for retirees.  
計劃為退休人士提供理財指導。

## Tier 3 — Pension / community-specific variants · 退休金與社區特定變體

### 10 Pensioners

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：退休金領取者；退休人士  
**語域**：British English / public-service  
**核心語義**：people who receive a pension, often older retired people  
**用法提示**：在英式英語、福利、公共交通和退休金語境中很常見。  
**常見搭配**：state pensioners; pensioner household; support for pensioners; pensioner discount; pension income.  
**常見搭配對象**：pension, benefit, household, transport, income.  
**與 older people 的辨析**：Pensioner 強調領取 pension 的身份，而不是年齡本身。  
**使用限制**：並非所有 older people 都領退休金；亦不宜在純年齡研究中取代 older people。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The council provides discounted transport for pensioners.  
市議會為退休金領取者提供交通優惠。

### 11 Older members of the community

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：社區中的年長成員；長者  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：older people considered as members of a particular community  
**用法提示**：當 older people 出現在社區參與、義工、公共服務或地方活動語境時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：older members of the community; support community members; community participation; older community members.  
**常見搭配對象**：community, volunteer, event, service, neighbourhood.  
**與 older people 的辨析**：是清楚而包容的描述性表達，強調 community membership。  
**使用限制**：句子沒有社區脈絡時會顯得冗長。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Older members of the community helped plan the new public space.  
社區中的年長成員協助規劃新的公共空間。

## 04 False synonyms / older-people traps · 容易誤選的詞與年齡稱呼陷阱

### 01 Retirees

**退休人士不等於所有 older people**  
退休是就業狀態，不是年齡定義；有人提早退休，也有長者繼續工作。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Retirement status and age are different.

### 02 Pensioners

**領退休金人士不等於所有 older people**  
是否領取退休金取決於制度與資格。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Pension status is not the same as age.

### 03 Grandparents

**祖父母不等於 older people**  
有人年長但不是祖父母，也有人較年輕已成為祖父母。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Family role is not an age category.

### 04 The elderly

**可理解但較容易把人變成一個標籤**  
現代包容語體通常更偏好 elderly people / older people，而非把 elderly 單獨名詞化。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Person-first wording is often preferable.

### 05 Senior

**senior 單獨使用有多義**  
可指年長人士，也可指職級較高的人。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Senior can refer to age or rank.

### 06 Adults

**成年人範圍太廣**  
adult 包括所有成年年齡層。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Adult is much broader.

### 07 Older generation

**只在代際比較 sense 接近**  
它把人作為一整代比較，而不是一般年齡群體。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Generation framing is context-specific.

### 08 Old / older

**語用陷阱**  
old 可直接表示年老，但形容人時可能較生硬；older people 通常更中性。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Older is generally more neutral for people.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **older people vs seniors**: seniors 更簡潔，較常見於北美和服務／優惠語境。
- **older people vs elderly people**: elderly people 可理解，但部分現代語境會覺得較舊式或帶脆弱假設。
- **older people vs older adults**: older adults 很適合研究、醫療和專業寫作。
- **older people vs senior citizens**: senior citizens 偏公共／公民服務語境。
- **older people vs retirees**: retiree 是退休狀態，不是年齡類別。
- **older people vs pensioners**: pensioner 是退休金資格／領取狀態。
- **older people vs older generation**: older generation 是世代比較框架。
- **older people vs older residents**: older residents 需要明確地區／住所脈絡。
- **older people vs people in later life**: 後者更委婉、life-stage-focused，但年齡界線較模糊。

## 08 Grammar, register & respectful-language traps · 文法、語域與尊重用語陷阱

- **older people** 是非常穩妥、中性的集合稱呼。
- **older adults** 常見於 health / research / policy。
- **older persons** 更正式，常見於法律、國際機構和權利文本。
- **seniors** 在北美公共服務／優惠語境很常見；英式／香港正式文本可優先用 older people。
- **elderly people** 仍可理解，但不宜自動把所有年長人士描述成 frail / dependent。
- 避免單獨把人群稱為 **the elderly**，在包容語體中通常 person-first wording 更自然。
- **retiree** 與 **pensioner** 都是 status-based，而非純年齡分類。
- **older generation** 只在代際比較中特別自然。
- **senior** 亦可表示職級高：a senior manager，因此需靠語境判斷。
- **old people** 文法沒錯，但可能顯得較生硬；**older people** 通常更中性。
- **older people's needs** / **services for older people** 是自然搭配。
- 不要假設所有 older people 都退休、領退休金、身體虛弱或需要照護。
