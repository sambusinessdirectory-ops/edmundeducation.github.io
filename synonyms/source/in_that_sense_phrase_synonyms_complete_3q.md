# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# In that sense · phrase

「從那個角度看／在那一方面／按照那種理解」，究竟是哪一種 `in that sense`？

`in that sense` 是一個**承接前文、重新限定判斷方式**的連接片語。它可以表示從某個觀點來看 **from that perspective**、在某一方面 **in that respect**、正式回指某一面向 **in that regard**、換一種方式看 **viewed that way**、從某個人的立場看 **from that point of view**、按照某一特定解讀 **on that interpretation**，或依剛才設定的標準／概念來說 **in those terms**。

> 本課主要處理 `in that sense = if understood, judged or considered in the particular way just mentioned`。
>
> 同義詞數量**不固定**；本課按觀點、具體方面、正式回指、重新框架、立場、解讀與評估標準選用 **7 個**替代表達。
>
> Format: **7 curated alternatives + 8 false-synonym contrasts + 3 exercises per target synonym (21 questions total)**.

---

# 01 同義詞指南


### 01 From That Perspective
**從那個角度看；從那種觀點來看**

使用者提供。`from that perspective` 是 `in that sense` 最自然的改寫之一，表示如果採用剛才提到的觀點、標準或理解方式來看，某個判斷就成立。它比 `in that respect` 更明確帶有 viewpoint 的意味。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> From that perspective, the policy was a success.

**中文翻譯**

從那個角度看，這項政策是成功的。

**USAGE CLUE**

- evaluating something from a particular viewpoint or conceptual angle

---


### 02 In That Respect
**在那一方面；就那一點而言**

使用者提供。`in that respect` 強調某個特定方面、特徵或比較點。它比 `from that perspective` 少一點 viewpoint，多一點 aspect / dimension。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The two systems are similar in that respect.

**中文翻譯**

在那一方面，兩個系統很相似。

**USAGE CLUE**

- referring to one particular aspect, feature or point of comparison

---


### 03 In That Regard
**在那方面；就那一點而言**

`in that regard` 與 `in that respect` 很接近，但在正式書面語中稍常見於承接前一句提到的問題、責任、政策或考慮因素。它常用於 reports, policy discussion, formal analysis。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The company has made progress in that regard.

**中文翻譯**

公司在那方面已有進展。

**USAGE CLUE**

- formal reference back to a previously mentioned issue, consideration or area

---


### 04 Viewed That Way
**那樣看來；如果那樣理解**

較口語、自然。`viewed that way` 表示如果採用剛才所說的理解方式來看某事，就會得到某個結論。它比 `from that perspective` 更直接、更接近日常分析語氣。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Viewed that way, the delay was not entirely negative.

**中文翻譯**

那樣看來，這次延誤並不完全是負面的。

**USAGE CLUE**

- reframing a situation by looking at it in the particular way just described

---


### 05 From That Point Of View
**從那個觀點看；從那個立場看**

這是較完整、稍傳統的 viewpoint 表達。它與 `from that perspective` 非常接近，但語氣稍更明確地指『從某個人的立場／觀點出發』。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> From that point of view, the compromise makes sense.

**中文翻譯**

從那個觀點看，這項妥協是合理的。

**USAGE CLUE**

- judging something according to a clearly defined standpoint or stakeholder viewpoint

---


### 06 On That Interpretation
**按照那種理解；依那種解讀**

較正式、分析性。`on that interpretation` 適合語義、法律、文本、規則或論證存在多種解讀時，表示『如果採用剛才那種解釋』。它比 `in that sense` 更明確聚焦 interpretation。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> On that interpretation, the rule would apply to both cases.

**中文翻譯**

按照那種解讀，這條規則會適用於兩種情況。

**USAGE CLUE**

- accepting one particular reading or interpretation among possible alternatives

---


### 07 In Those Terms
**以那種方式來說；按那套標準來看**

較正式。`in those terms` 表示如果採用剛才提出的概念、標準、分類或衡量方式來描述／判斷某事。它比 `in that respect` 更偏 conceptual framing。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> In those terms, the change can be described as an improvement.

**中文翻譯**

按那套標準來看，這個改變可以被形容為改善。

**USAGE CLUE**

- framing or evaluating something according to a previously stated set of concepts or criteria

---

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 in that sense / from that perspective

兩者高度接近；`from that perspective` 更明確表示 viewpoint，而 `in that sense` 也可只是限定某一方面或某種解讀。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> From that perspective, the delay was useful.  
> In that sense, the two cases are similar.

---


## 02 in that sense / in that respect

`in that respect` 明確指一個 aspect / feature；`in that sense` 可以更抽象地表示『如果那樣理解』。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The models are similar in that respect.  
> In that sense, the failure was useful.

---


## 03 in that sense / in that regard

`in that regard` 較正式，常回指某個已提及的 policy issue / concern；`in that sense` 更廣。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The company improved in that regard.  
> In that sense, the argument is persuasive.

---


## 04 in that sense / therefore

`therefore` 表示邏輯結果『因此』；`in that sense` 只限定評價角度，兩者不能互換。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Costs fell; therefore, profits rose.  
> In that sense, the reform succeeded.

---


## 05 in that sense / in other words

`in other words` 是重新表述同一內容；`in that sense` 是限定理解或評價方式。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> In other words, the rule applies to everyone.  
> In that sense, the rule is fair.

---


## 06 in that sense / to that extent

`to that extent` 表示『到那個程度』，較偏 degree；`in that sense` 偏 interpretation / respect。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> To that extent, the criticism is justified.  
> In that sense, the two ideas overlap.

---


## 07 in that sense / on that interpretation

`on that interpretation` 只在 wording/rules/texts 有特定解讀時最精確；一般觀點不一定叫 interpretation。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> On that interpretation, the clause applies.  
> In that sense, the project was successful.

---


## 08 in that sense / sense

`sense` 單獨是名詞，可指感官、意義、判斷力等；固定片語 `in that sense` 不能被單獨 `sense` 取代。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The word has several senses.  
> In that sense, the statement is correct.

---

# 05 高混淆組

## from that perspective vs from that point of view
- **from that perspective**：較廣，任何概念角度都可。
- **from that point of view**：更明確是某個人／群體的立場。

## in that respect vs in that regard
- **in that respect**：一個具體方面。
- **in that regard**：較正式地回指前文面向。

## viewed that way vs from that perspective
- **viewed that way**：更像重新詮釋同一事實。
- **from that perspective**：一般 viewpoint。

## on that interpretation vs in that sense
- **on that interpretation**：特定文字／規則解讀。
- **in that sense**：更廣的理解方式。

## in those terms vs in that respect
- **in those terms**：按一套概念／標準。
- **in that respect**：只需某一方面。

## in that sense vs therefore
- **in that sense**：限定角度。
- **therefore**：表示因果結果。

## in that sense vs in other words
- **in that sense**：換角度理解。
- **in other words**：換句話說。

## in that sense vs to that extent
- **in that sense**：sense / interpretation。
- **to that extent**：degree / extent。

# 08 生成／評估注意事項

1. **同義詞數量不是固定值。** 本課按觀點、具體方面、正式回指、重新框架、立場、解讀與評估標準選 **7 個**。
2. 因此本課有 **3 exercises per target = 21 exercises**。
3. 使用者提供的 `from that perspective` 與 `in that respect` 全部保留。
4. `from that perspective` 只在 viewpoint / angle 是焦點時使用。
5. `in that respect` 只在 one specific aspect / feature 是焦點時使用。
6. `in that regard` 明確標示為較正式的前文回指詞。
7. `viewed that way` 主要處理 reframing：同一事實換一種理解方式。
8. `from that point of view` 需要明確 stakeholder / person's standpoint。
9. `on that interpretation` 需要文字、條款、規則或概念存在可辨識的 interpretation。
10. `in those terms` 需要 previously stated criteria / conceptual framing。
11. False synonyms 特別測試 therefore / in other words / to that extent，避免把邏輯結果、重述和程度誤當成 sense-based framing。
12. Distractors 全部使用真實自然英文片語，不使用人工標籤。
13. 每題都用 decisive clue 建立唯一答案，例如 `one specific dimension`, `policy issue`, `stakeholder viewpoint`, `quoted term`, `defined criterion`。
14. 原始 `in that sense` 句子保持自然；正解需要逗號位置或句法調整時採自然英文。
15. 本課處理固定片語 **in that sense**，不混入名詞 `sense` 的其他義項。
