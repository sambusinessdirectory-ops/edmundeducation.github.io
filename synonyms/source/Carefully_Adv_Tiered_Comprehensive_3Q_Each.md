# CAREFULLY · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Carefully · adverb

**Carefully 的核心不是單一意思。** 它可以表示避免危險、專心留意、細緻處理、密切監察、全面檢查，或慎重作決定。因此，選近義詞時要先判斷句子究竟強調 **risk, attention, detail, observation, completeness, deliberation, or prudent judgement**。

你提供的 **cautiously; attentively; with care** 正好代表三個最重要方向：**避險、專注、一般小心處理**。

本次保留 **8 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達，共 **24 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。這次特別按照 inspection 標準避免為了替換而製造不自然的 *carefully* 原句。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Carefully 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 |
|---|---|
| 避免危險／風險 | **cautiously** |
| 專心聆聽／觀看 | **attentively** |
| 一般小心處理 | **with care** |
| 極細緻處理細節 | **meticulously** |
| 密切觀察／監察 | **closely** |
| 完整徹底檢查 | **thoroughly** |
| 慎重、不匆忙地行動 | **deliberately** |
| 財務／資源上明智審慎 | **prudently** |

## Tier 1 — Core caution / attention / detail equivalents · 核心謹慎、專注與細緻近義詞

### 01 Cautiously

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：謹慎地；小心地  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：in a way that avoids danger, risk, or possible problems  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **carefully** 在安全、風險、未知情況 sense 最核心的近義詞之一。  
**常見搭配**：move cautiously; proceed cautiously; approach cautiously; speak cautiously; invest cautiously.  
**常見搭配對象**：road, machinery, negotiation, risk, unfamiliar situation.  
**與 carefully 的辨析**：Cautiously 比 carefully 更強調 risk avoidance；carefully 亦可只是專心或細緻。  
**使用限制**：純粹閱讀、校對、量度等『仔細』語境未必有風險，不一定適合 cautiously。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The driver approached the flooded road cautiously.  
司機謹慎地駛近被水淹浸的道路。

### 02 Attentively

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：專心地；留心地  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：with close attention to what is being said, shown, or done  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很自然，尤其適合 listen, watch, observe, follow 等需要集中注意力的動作。  
**常見搭配**：listen attentively; watch attentively; observe attentively; follow attentively.  
**常見搭配對象**：speaker, instructions, demonstration, lesson, discussion.  
**與 carefully 的辨析**：Attentively 強調 attention；carefully 還可強調精確、安全或避免錯誤。  
**使用限制**：搬運易碎品、駕駛、操作危險設備通常 cautiously / with care 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The students listened attentively to the safety instructions.  
學生專心聆聽安全指示。

### 03 With care

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：小心地；仔細地  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：in a careful manner, with attention and an effort to avoid damage or error  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是最透明、最廣泛的片語改寫，特別適合 handle, pack, choose, prepare。  
**常見搭配**：handle with care; choose with care; prepare with care; treat with care.  
**常見搭配對象**：fragile item, document, equipment, ingredients, decision.  
**與 carefully 的辨析**：With care 幾乎保留 carefully 的一般語義，但句法位置通常不同。  
**使用限制**：不能所有句子直接一對一替換；有時需要把副詞改成介詞片語。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
These instruments must be handled with care.  
這些儀器必須小心處理。

### 04 Meticulously

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：一絲不苟地；極仔細地  
**語域**：formal / precise  
**核心語義**：with extremely close attention to small details  
**用法提示**：當 carefully 的重點是細節、精確度、完整檢查時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：meticulously check; meticulously record; meticulously plan; meticulously clean; meticulously document.  
**常見搭配對象**：records, data, plan, procedure, restoration, evidence.  
**與 carefully 的辨析**：Meticulously 比 carefully 強很多，暗示細節處理得非常周密。  
**使用限制**：普通日常小心動作如果沒有細節要求，會顯得過度。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The conservators meticulously documented every stage of the restoration.  
文物修復人員一絲不苟地記錄修復的每個階段。

### 05 Closely

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：密切地；仔細地  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：with close observation, examination, or monitoring  
**用法提示**：當 carefully 與 examine, watch, monitor, inspect, follow 等搭配，而重點是『密切留意』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：examine closely; watch closely; monitor closely; inspect closely; follow closely.  
**常見搭配對象**：data, patient, market, document, behaviour.  
**與 carefully 的辨析**：Closely 強調近距離／密切觀察；carefully 可包含更廣的安全、精確或審慎意味。  
**使用限制**：如 carefully drive / carefully carry，不能改成 closely。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Doctors monitored the patient closely throughout the night.  
醫生整晚密切監察病人。

## Tier 2 — Completeness / deliberation / prudence variants · 完整、慎重與審慎變體

### 06 Thoroughly

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：徹底地；全面仔細地  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：completely and carefully, covering all relevant parts  
**用法提示**：當 carefully 帶有『全面檢查／完整處理』的意味時可用。  
**常見搭配**：check thoroughly; investigate thoroughly; clean thoroughly; review thoroughly; test thoroughly.  
**常見搭配對象**：report, equipment, complaint, room, evidence.  
**與 carefully 的辨析**：Thoroughly 強調 completeness；carefully 不一定表示全部都涵蓋。  
**使用限制**：若重點是避免危險而不是完整度，cautiously 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Please check the form thoroughly before submitting it.  
提交表格前請徹底檢查。

### 07 Deliberately

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：慎重地；經過考慮地  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：in a slow, considered, intentional way rather than hastily  
**用法提示**：當 carefully 指『不匆忙、經過思考後行動』時可用，尤其 decisions, wording, steps。  
**常見搭配**：act deliberately; speak deliberately; choose deliberately; proceed deliberately.  
**常見搭配對象**：decision, wording, strategy, response, action.  
**與 carefully 的辨析**：Deliberately 也常表示『故意地』，因此只有在 considered/intentional sense 才接近 carefully。  
**使用限制**：容易產生『故意』歧義；若只是細心檢查，不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She chose her words deliberately before answering the question.  
她回答問題前慎重地選擇措辭。

### 08 Prudently

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：審慎地；明智謹慎地  
**語域**：formal / financial / decision-making  
**核心語義**：with sensible judgement designed to avoid unnecessary risk or loss  
**用法提示**：當 carefully 用於財務、政策、資源分配、長期決策，而且帶『明智避險』時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：spend prudently; invest prudently; manage prudently; act prudently; plan prudently.  
**常見搭配對象**：money, budget, investment, resources, policy.  
**與 carefully 的辨析**：Prudently 比 cautiously 多了 sensible judgement / good management 的意味。  
**使用限制**：一般閱讀、搬運、觀察等動作不用 prudently。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The organisation must manage its reserves prudently.  
機構必須審慎管理儲備。

## 04 False synonyms / carefully traps · 容易誤選的速度、聲量與精確度陷阱

### 01 Slowly

**慢慢地 ≠ carefully**  
慢不等於小心；有些動作可以慢但仍不仔細。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Speed and care are different.

### 02 Quietly

**安靜地 ≠ carefully**  
quietly 描述聲量，不描述注意力或準確度。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Quietness is not carefulness.

### 03 Exactly

**精確地 ≠ carefully**  
exactly 描述結果／吻合程度，不代表過程有小心處理。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Exactness and careful process differ.

### 04 Seriously

**認真地 ≠ carefully**  
seriously 偏態度或嚴肅程度，不是所有仔細動作。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Seriousness is attitude-focused.

### 05 Cautiously

**只在避險 sense 接近**  
沒有風險的閱讀／校對語境未必適合。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Cautiously is risk-focused.

### 06 Thoroughly

**只在完整／徹底 sense 接近**  
thoroughly 強調 coverage/completeness。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Thoroughly is completeness-focused.

### 07 Deliberately

**可能表示『故意地』**  
與 carefully 只在慎重、非匆忙 sense 重疊。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Deliberately is polysemous.

### 08 Careful / carefully / care

**詞性陷阱**  
careful 是形容詞；carefully 是副詞；care 是名詞／動詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check word class.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Cautiously · 謹慎地；小心地

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

Drive carefully on the mountain road after dark.

**中文翻譯**  
入黑後在山路駕駛要小心。

- A. Cautiously
- B. Attentively
- C. Slowly
- D. Quietly
- E. Exactly
- F. Seriously

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Cautiously** · 謹慎地；小心地

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
Drive cautiously on the mountain road after dark.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Cautiously ✓** — in a way that avoids danger, risk, or possible problems。這句的關鍵是 **driving involves clear physical risk**。因此 **Cautiously** 不只是『小心』，還準確表達這個語境需要的注意、風險控制或完整度。
- **B.** **Attentively** — with close attention to what is being said, shown, or done。適合聆聽、觀看、跟隨內容，強調專注；若句子是在避免危險或處理易損物品，就不夠準。
- **C.** **Slowly** — at a low speed。只表示速度慢。慢慢做一件事並不等於有仔細檢查或謹慎判斷。
- **D.** **Quietly** — with little noise。只表示聲音小；與安全、精確、觀察或審慎沒有必然關係。
- **E.** **Exactly** — precisely or completely accurately。表示精確吻合或結果準確，通常不描述做事過程是否小心。
- **F.** **Seriously** — in an earnest or grave manner。表示態度認真或事情嚴重，不能自然代替『仔細地』。

| Carefully 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **carefully vs cautiously**：cautiously 偏避險。
- **carefully vs attentively**：attentively 偏專注。
- **carefully vs with care**：with care 是最透明的一般改寫。
- **carefully vs meticulously**：meticulously 程度更強，強調細節。
- **carefully vs closely**：closely 常與 observe / watch / monitor / inspect 搭配。
- **carefully vs thoroughly**：thoroughly 強調完整徹底。
- **carefully vs deliberately**：deliberately 只在『慎重、經考慮地』sense 接近，而且也可表示『故意地』。
- **carefully vs prudently**：prudently 偏資源、財務與長期決策。
- **carefully vs slowly**：慢不等於小心。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & adverb traps

- **carefully** 是副詞：read carefully / drive carefully / check carefully。
- **careful** 是形容詞：a careful driver / be careful。
- **with care** 常需要改變句法：handle it carefully → handle it with care。
- **listen attentively / watch attentively**：專注感比 cautiously 強。
- **monitor closely / inspect closely**：固定搭配非常自然。
- **check thoroughly / investigate thoroughly**：強調完整度。
- **meticulously document / record / plan**：強調細節。
- **prudently manage / invest / spend**：偏正式決策語境。
- **deliberately** 有『故意地』這個高頻義項，必須靠語境辨認。
