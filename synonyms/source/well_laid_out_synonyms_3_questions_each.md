# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Well laid out

**`well laid out` 通常形容空間、文件、網站、介面或物件的布局安排得好。真正精準的替代表達取決於你是在讚賞整體設計、結構秩序、操作效率、資訊層次、易用性、空間感、整齊程度，還是符合使用直覺。**

核心替代表達：

`well-designed / well-organised / efficiently arranged / clearly structured / user-friendly / spaciously arranged / neatly arranged / intuitively arranged`

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Well-designed

**設計良好的；規劃完善的**

當 `well laid out` 指某個空間、文件、網站或系統在整體設計上清晰、實用、令人容易使用時，`well-designed` 是最自然的近義詞之一。它比 `well-organised` 更強調 design quality。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The office is well-designed, with clear routes between work areas.

辦公室設計良好，各工作區之間的動線清晰。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **overall design is thoughtful, functional, and easy to use**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 02 Well-organised

**組織良好的；井然有序的**

當 `well laid out` 指內容、空間或資訊安排有條理、容易理解或查找時，`well-organised` 很自然。它偏 structure and order。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The report is well-organised and easy to follow.

這份報告組織良好，很容易閱讀理解。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **structured clearly and logically**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 03 Efficiently arranged

**安排有效率的；配置合理的**

當 `well laid out` 強調物件、房間、工作站或設施的配置能節省時間、動作或空間時，`efficiently arranged` 很精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The kitchen is efficiently arranged for quick food preparation.

廚房配置合理，方便快速備餐。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **arranged to minimise wasted time, movement, or space**

**REGISTER · 語域**

工作、設計、一般

---

## 04 Clearly structured

**結構清晰的**

當 `well laid out` 指文章、簡報、網頁或資料的層次清楚、順序合理時，`clearly structured` 很合適。它比 well-designed 更聚焦 information architecture。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The guide is clearly structured into short sections.

這份指南分成短小章節，結構清晰。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **organised into an easy-to-follow structure**

**REGISTER · 語域**

教育、工作、正式

---

## 05 User-friendly

**易用的；方便使用的**

當 `well laid out` 指網站、介面、表格或系統因布局清楚而容易操作時，`user-friendly` 很自然。它強調 usability，而不只是外觀。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The booking page is user-friendly and easy to navigate.

預訂頁面易用，而且很容易瀏覽。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **easy and intuitive for people to use**

**REGISTER · 語域**

科技、工作、一般

---

## 06 Spaciously arranged

**佈置寬敞的；空間配置舒適的**

當 `well laid out` 指房間、商店、住宅或展覽空間安排得不擠迫、動線舒適時，`spaciously arranged` 是有用的情境性改寫。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The showroom is spaciously arranged, with wide aisles between displays.

陳列室佈置寬敞，各展示區之間有寬闊通道。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **arranged with enough space for comfortable movement**

**REGISTER · 語域**

室內設計、一般

---

## 07 Neatly arranged

**整齊排列的；佈置整潔的**

當 `well laid out` 指物件、圖表、產品或資料排列整齊、視覺上清楚時，`neatly arranged` 很自然。它偏 visual order。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The samples are neatly arranged in labelled trays.

樣本整齊排列在有標籤的托盤中。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **placed in a tidy, orderly visual arrangement**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 08 Intuitively arranged

**配置直觀的；安排合乎使用直覺的**

當 `well laid out` 指控制、功能、選單或資訊放在使用者自然會預期的位置時，`intuitively arranged` 很精準。它比 user-friendly 更聚焦 layout logic。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The controls are intuitively arranged around the main display.

控制鍵配置直觀，圍繞主要顯示屏排列。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **arranged in positions that feel natural and predictable to users**

**REGISTER · 語域**

科技、產品設計、正式

# 02 False synonyms

## Well laid out / Well-designed
`well-designed` 最廣，表示整體設計周到、實用、完成度高。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The apartment is well laid out.  
The apartment is well-designed despite its small size.

## Well laid out / Well-organised
`well-organised` 強調內容、物件或空間有清楚秩序，容易查找和理解。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The handbook is well laid out.  
The handbook is well-organised into clear sections.

## Well laid out / Efficiently arranged
`efficiently arranged` 強調布局能減少走動、時間浪費或操作步驟。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The kitchen is well laid out.  
The kitchen is efficiently arranged for quick food preparation.

## Well laid out / Clearly structured
`clearly structured` 特別適合文章、報告、簡報、課程和資訊架構。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The report is well laid out.  
The report is clearly structured into logical sections.

## Well laid out / User-friendly
`user-friendly` 強調因布局清楚而容易使用，不只是看起來整齊。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The booking page is well laid out.  
The booking page is user-friendly and easy to navigate.

## Well laid out / Spaciously arranged
`spaciously arranged` 只適合實體空間，強調不擠迫、留有足夠活動空間。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The showroom is well laid out.  
The showroom is spaciously arranged with wide aisles.

## Well laid out / Neatly arranged
`neatly arranged` 強調視覺上整齊、有序地擺放。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The samples are well laid out.  
The samples are neatly arranged in labelled trays.

## Well laid out / Intuitively arranged
`intuitively arranged` 特別適合介面、控制鍵、選單和產品布局，強調「東西放在使用者預期的位置」。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The controls are well laid out.  
The controls are intuitively arranged around the main display.

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Well-designed vs Well-organised** — well-designed 偏整體設計品質；well-organised 偏秩序與分類。
- **Efficiently arranged vs Spaciously arranged** — 前者偏節省動線／時間；後者偏留有空間、不擠迫。
- **Clearly structured vs Well-organised** — clearly structured 更常用於文章、簡報、課程等資訊結構。
- **User-friendly vs Intuitively arranged** — user-friendly 是整體易用；intuitively arranged 特別聚焦元素放置是否符合預期。
- **Neatly arranged vs Efficiently arranged** — neat 是看起來整齊；efficient 是操作起來有效率。
- **Well laid out ≠ attractive in every context** — 好布局不只代表好看，也可能是功能和動線好。
- **Well laid out ≠ spacious in every context** — 小空間也可以 well laid out。
- **Well laid out ≠ organised in every context** — 有秩序不一定代表整體設計或使用體驗良好。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

- **well laid out** 通常作表語：`The office is well laid out.`
- **well-laid-out** 作前置形容詞時通常加連字號：`a well-laid-out office`
- **well-designed / well-organised / user-friendly** 作前置形容詞通常保留連字號
- **clearly structured / efficiently arranged / neatly arranged / intuitively arranged** 通常不用額外連字號
- British/HK spelling: **organised**；US: **organized**
- **layout** = noun；**lay out** = verb；**laid out** = past participle
- 不要混淆 **lay / laid / laid** 與 **lie / lay / lain**

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 是整體設計周到、實用？ → **well-designed**
2. 是分類與秩序井然？ → **well-organised**
3. 是配置能節省時間／動作？ → **efficiently arranged**
4. 是文章／簡報／資訊層次清晰？ → **clearly structured**
5. 是網站／表格／系統容易操作？ → **user-friendly**
6. 是實體空間寬敞、不擠迫？ → **spaciously arranged**
7. 是物件／圖表整齊擺放？ → **neatly arranged**
8. 是功能放在使用者自然預期的位置？ → **intuitively arranged**
