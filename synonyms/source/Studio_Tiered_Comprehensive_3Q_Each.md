# STUDIO · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Studio

**本課集中處理 studio = professional design / creative business 的義項。** 不處理錄音室、攝影棚、藝術家個人工作空間或 studio flat。這個商業義項最重要的差異是：**純設計公司、較廣創意代理、專業 design practice、策略顧問、品牌專門公司、獨立小型公司**。

你提供的 **design firm; creative agency; design practice** 正好代表三個核心方向：**專業設計公司、較廣創意代理機構、英式／專業設計事務所**。

本次保留 **9 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達。共 **27 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Tier 1 — Core design / agency / practice equivalents · 核心設計公司、創意代理與事務所近義詞

### 01 Design firm

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：設計公司；設計事務所  
**語域**：neutral / professional  
**核心語義**：a company or professional business that provides design services  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 studio 在商業設計語境中最直接、最自然的替代表達之一。  
**常見搭配**：design firm; leading design firm; independent design firm.  
**常見搭配對象**：graphic design, product design, interior design, branding.  
**與 studio 的辨析**：比 studio 更明確表示商業設計機構。  
**使用限制**：藝術家個人工作室、錄音室或攝影棚不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She works for a design firm specialising in retail interiors.  
她在一家專門從事零售室內設計的設計公司工作。

### 02 Creative agency

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：創意代理公司；創意機構  
**語域**：neutral / marketing / commercial  
**核心語義**：a business providing creative services such as branding, advertising, content, design, or campaigns  
**用法提示**：當 studio 提供 branding, advertising, campaign, content 等多種創意服務時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：creative agency; full-service creative agency; independent creative agency.  
**常見搭配對象**：branding, advertising, campaign, content, digital.  
**與 studio 的辨析**：通常比 design studio 範圍更廣。  
**使用限制**：若只做純設計，design firm / design practice 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The brand hired a creative agency to develop the campaign.  
品牌聘請一家創意代理公司策劃宣傳活動。

### 03 Design practice

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：設計事務所；設計執業機構  
**語域**：British English / professional  
**核心語義**：a professional design business or practice, often associated with architecture, interiors, or specialist design disciplines  
**用法提示**：很適合英式／香港專業語境，尤其 architecture, interior, urban design。  
**常見搭配**：design practice; architectural practice; multidisciplinary practice.  
**常見搭配對象**：architecture, interiors, urban design, product design.  
**與 studio 的辨析**：比 studio 更專業化、執業導向。  
**使用限制**：純廣告代理或非常口語的創意團隊描述不一定適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The project was led by a London-based design practice.  
這個項目由一家設於倫敦的設計事務所主導。

### 04 Creative studio

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：創意工作室；創作工作室  
**語域**：neutral / creative industries  
**核心語義**：a small or specialist creative business producing design, visual, digital, or artistic work  
**用法提示**：需要明確是創意產業 studio，而不是錄音／攝影用途時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：creative studio; independent creative studio; multidisciplinary studio.  
**常見搭配對象**：design, illustration, motion, digital, branding.  
**與 studio 的辨析**：與 studio 很接近，只是更明確指出創意業務性質。  
**使用限制**：若需要突出正式企業／顧問身份，design firm / consultancy 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The illustrations were produced by a small creative studio.  
這些插畫由一家小型創意工作室製作。

### 05 Design consultancy

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：設計顧問公司；設計顧問機構  
**語域**：formal / business  
**核心語義**：a business that provides design expertise and strategic advice to clients  
**用法提示**：當 studio 同時做 strategy, research, UX, service design 等顧問服務時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：design consultancy; strategic design consultancy; consultancy project.  
**常見搭配對象**：strategy, service design, UX, product, research.  
**與 studio 的辨析**：更強調 advisory/strategic role。  
**使用限制**：純製作型小工作室不一定適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The company appointed a design consultancy to rethink the customer experience.  
公司委聘一家設計顧問公司重新設計顧客體驗。

## Tier 2 — Specialist consultancy / branding / ownership variants · 專門顧問、品牌與公司型態變體

### 06 Branding agency

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：品牌設計公司；品牌代理公司  
**語域**：neutral / commercial  
**核心語義**：an agency specialising in brand identity, positioning, naming, visual systems, and related design  
**用法提示**：當 studio 的主要工作明確是 branding / identity 時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：branding agency; brand identity agency; rebranding project.  
**常見搭配對象**：brand, identity, logo, naming, positioning.  
**與 studio 的辨析**：是 studio 的專門 subtype。  
**使用限制**：產品、室內、建築或一般數碼設計不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The company chose a branding agency to create its new visual identity.  
公司選了一家品牌設計公司建立新的視覺識別。

### 07 Creative consultancy

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：創意顧問公司；創意顧問機構  
**語域**：formal / business  
**核心語義**：a consultancy combining creative development with strategic advice  
**用法提示**：適合 creative direction, brand strategy, innovation, experience design 等高層次顧問工作。  
**常見搭配**：creative consultancy; creative strategy; innovation consultancy.  
**常見搭配對象**：brand, strategy, innovation, experience, campaign.  
**與 studio 的辨析**：比 creative agency 更 consultancy-driven。  
**使用限制**：純執行廣告製作或小型設計製作團隊未必合適。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The museum worked with a creative consultancy on its new visitor strategy.  
博物館與一家創意顧問機構合作制定新的訪客策略。

### 08 Independent design company

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：獨立設計公司  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：a privately run design business operating independently rather than as part of a larger group  
**用法提示**：當 studio 的關鍵特徵是小型、獨立、非大型集團時可用。  
**常見搭配**：independent design company; independent firm; small design company.  
**常見搭配對象**：branding, product, digital, interior, graphic design.  
**與 studio 的辨析**：重點是 ownership/independence。  
**使用限制**：若獨立性不是重點，直接 design firm 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
They founded an independent design company after leaving the agency.  
他們離開代理公司後創立了一家獨立設計公司。

## Tier 3 — Boutique contextual variant · 精品小型設計公司變體

### 09 Boutique design firm

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：精品設計公司；小型專門設計公司  
**語域**：business / descriptive  
**核心語義**：a small, specialised design firm offering tailored or high-touch services  
**用法提示**：當 studio 具有 small, specialist, bespoke, premium 的定位時可用。  
**常見搭配**：boutique design firm; boutique agency; bespoke design.  
**常見搭配對象**：branding, interiors, luxury, identity, hospitality.  
**與 studio 的辨析**：多了 small/specialist/premium positioning。  
**使用限制**：大型 multidisciplinary studio 不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The hotel commissioned a boutique design firm to redesign the lobby.  
酒店委聘一家精品設計公司重新設計大堂。

## 04 False synonyms / studio traps · 容易誤選的設計公司與創意機構陷阱

### 01 Workshop

**工作坊／工場 ≠ studio**  
workshop 可指實體工場或培訓活動，不等於專業設計公司。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Workshop is space/activity-focused.

### 02 Office

**辦公室 ≠ studio**  
office 是工作場所，不說明是否提供設計服務。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Office is location-focused.

### 03 Agency

**代理公司 ≠ 所有 studio**  
agency 很廣，可能是招聘、媒體、廣告等，不一定是設計。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Agency needs service context.

### 04 Freelancer

**自由工作者 ≠ studio**  
freelancer 是個人，不等於公司／團隊。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Freelancer is an individual.

### 05 Architecture firm

**建築公司 ≠ 所有 design studio**  
只適合建築專業語境。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Architecture firm is discipline-specific.

### 06 Production house

**製作公司 ≠ design studio**  
production house 偏影片／廣告／內容製作。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Production house is production-focused.

### 07 Creative agency

**只在較廣創意服務 sense 接近**  
creative agency 通常比純 design studio 範圍更廣。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Creative agency is broader.

### 08 Studio / studio space

**公司 ≠ 場地**  
studio 既可指創意公司，也可指工作空間／攝影棚／錄音室。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Sense must be identified from context.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Design firm · 設計公司；設計事務所

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The studio designed the interiors for several new restaurants.

**中文翻譯**  
這家設計工作室為數間新餐廳設計室內空間。

- A. Design firm
- B. Creative studio
- C. Design practice
- D. Office
- E. Freelancer
- F. Production house

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Design firm** · 設計公司；設計事務所

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The design firm designed the interiors for several new restaurants.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Design firm ✓** — means a professional business providing design services. Here, professional design services to clients. The service model and professional register make **Design firm** the most precise replacement.
- **B.** **Creative studio** — means a small or specialist creative business producing visual, digital or artistic work. This keeps the creative-business idea but misses the more specific firm, consultancy, branding or independence nuance. The decisive clue is that professional design services to clients.
- **C.** **Design practice** — is a British/professional term for a design business, especially in architecture/interiors. This is most natural in professional architecture/interiors and formal design-practice contexts. The decisive clue is that professional design services to clients.
- **D.** **Office** — means a workplace and says nothing about the professional service provided. This only identifies a workplace, not the organisation's professional service. The decisive clue is that professional design services to clients.
- **E.** **Freelancer** — means an individual self-employed professional, not a studio/company. This refers to one self-employed person rather than a studio team or company. The decisive clue is that professional design services to clients.
- **F.** **Production house** — means a company focused on media/content production. This is production-focused, especially film/video/content, and does not match general design services. The decisive clue is that professional design services to clients.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **studio vs design firm**: design firm 更明確表示專業商業機構。
- **studio vs creative agency**: creative agency 服務範圍通常更廣，可包括廣告、內容與 campaign。
- **studio vs design practice**: design practice 更專業執業化，英式／香港語境自然。
- **studio vs creative studio**: creative studio 更明確排除錄音／攝影 studio。
- **studio vs design consultancy**: consultancy 更強調研究、策略與顧問角色。
- **studio vs branding agency**: branding agency 只限品牌相關服務。
- **studio vs creative consultancy**: creative consultancy 更偏高層策略與創意方向。
- **studio vs freelancer**: freelancer 是個人，不是公司／團隊。
- **studio vs production house**: production house 偏影片／內容製作。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & business-model traps

- **design firm / design practice / design consultancy** 都是專業設計業常見名稱，但 service model 不同。
- **creative agency** 常與 **campaign, advertising, content, brand** 搭配。
- **design practice** 在英式／香港專業語境很自然，尤其 architecture / interiors。
- **branding agency** 專門處理 identity / naming / positioning / rebranding。
- **creative consultancy** 強調 strategy + creative direction。
- **boutique design firm** 暗示 small, specialist, tailored/premium service。
- **studio** 亦可指錄音室、攝影棚、藝術家工作空間；必須先辨別 sense。
- **office** 只是工作地點；**workshop** 是工場／工作坊；都不是設計公司的全面同義詞。
- 英式／香港拼寫保持 **organisation, programme, specialise**。
