# SURVEILLANCE · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Surveillance

**Surveillance 可以表示持續監察、觀察、保安監控、追蹤、密切審視、治理監督或秘密監察。** 替換前要判斷到底是在 **watch, track, review, supervise, or secretly monitor**。

你提供的 **monitoring; observation** 正好代表兩個最核心方向：**持續監察** 與 **仔細觀察**。

本次保留 **9 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達，共 **27 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Tier 1 — Core monitoring / observation / security equivalents

### 01 Monitoring

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：監察；監測  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：持續或反覆檢查人、活動、系統、狀況或資訊。  
**用法提示**：比 surveillance 更中性、更廣；可用於系統、健康、環境。  
**常見搭配**：continuous monitoring; security monitoring; system monitoring  
**常見搭配對象**：activity, system, behaviour, compliance  
**與 surveillance 的辨析**：比 surveillance 更中性、更廣；可用於系統、健康、環境。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The building uses continuous monitoring to detect unusual activity.  
大樓採用持續監察，以偵測異常活動。

### 02 Observation

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：觀察；監看  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：仔細觀看或研究某人／某事。  
**用法提示**：可較短期或研究式；surveillance 通常更持續、系統化。  
**常見搭配**：close observation; direct observation; under observation  
**常見搭配對象**：person, behaviour, event, patient  
**與 surveillance 的辨析**：可較短期或研究式；surveillance 通常更持續、系統化。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The patient's condition remained under close observation overnight.  
病人的情況整晚都受到密切觀察。

### 03 Watching

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：監看；看守；注視  
**語域**：informal / common  
**核心語義**：看着或留意某人／某事。  
**用法提示**：更口語、不技術，也未必有錄影或記錄。  
**常見搭配**：watching closely; watch a location; keep watch  
**常見搭配對象**：person, entrance, building, area  
**與 surveillance 的辨析**：更口語、不技術，也未必有錄影或記錄。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Security staff were watching the entrance throughout the event.  
保安人員在整個活動期間一直監看入口。

### 04 Security monitoring

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：保安監察；安全監控  
**語域**：formal / security  
**核心語義**：為保護人員、財產、系統或處所而進行的監察。  
**用法提示**：比 general surveillance 更明確指出保安目的。  
**常見搭配**：security monitoring system; continuous security monitoring  
**常見搭配對象**：building, premises, camera, network  
**與 surveillance 的辨析**：比 general surveillance 更明確指出保安目的。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The site has round-the-clock security monitoring.  
該地點設有二十四小時保安監控。

### 05 Scrutiny

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：密切審視；仔細監察  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：對行為、決定、紀錄或做法作非常仔細且常帶批判性的檢視。  
**用法提示**：強調 critical examination，不是視覺監控。  
**常見搭配**：under scrutiny; regulatory scrutiny; close scrutiny  
**常見搭配對象**：conduct, decision, record, organisation  
**與 surveillance 的辨析**：強調 critical examination，不是視覺監控。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The company's data practices came under regulatory scrutiny.  
公司的資料處理做法受到監管機構密切審視。

## Tier 2 — Tracking / oversight / supervision variants

### 06 Tracking

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：追蹤；跟進  
**語域**：neutral / technical  
**核心語義**：持續追蹤人、物件、裝置或活動的位置／狀態。  
**用法提示**：強調 movement/status over time。  
**常見搭配**：location tracking; vehicle tracking; device tracking  
**常見搭配對象**：person, vehicle, phone, device  
**與 surveillance 的辨析**：強調 movement/status over time。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The app can disable location tracking in its privacy settings.  
這個應用程式可在私隱設定中關閉位置追蹤。

### 07 Oversight

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：監督；監管  
**語域**：formal / governance  
**核心語義**：監督及檢視機構、流程或活動，以確保妥善運作。  
**用法提示**：治理／問責概念，不是對個人持續監視。  
**常見搭配**：regulatory oversight; independent oversight; oversight body  
**常見搭配對象**：organisation, process, authority, programme  
**與 surveillance 的辨析**：治理／問責概念，不是對個人持續監視。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The programme operates under independent oversight.  
這個計劃在獨立監督下運作。

### 08 Supervision

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：監督；看管  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：看管人員或活動，以確保安全、正確或合規。  
**用法提示**：通常帶管理／責任；surveillance 可只是觀察。  
**常見搭配**：close supervision; adult supervision; staff supervision  
**常見搭配對象**：staff, child, activity, trainee  
**與 surveillance 的辨析**：通常帶管理／責任；surveillance 可只是觀察。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The experiment was carried out under close supervision.  
這項實驗在密切監督下進行。

## Tier 3 — Covert-monitoring contextual variant

### 09 Covert monitoring

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：秘密監察；暗中監控  
**語域**：formal / security / investigative  
**核心語義**：在對象不知情下秘密進行的監察。  
**用法提示**：比 surveillance 更窄，明確加入 secret element。  
**常見搭配**：covert monitoring; covert surveillance; covert observation  
**常見搭配對象**：suspect, employee, location, communication  
**與 surveillance 的辨析**：比 surveillance 更窄，明確加入 secret element。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The investigation used covert monitoring after judicial approval.  
調查在取得司法批准後使用秘密監察。

## 04 False synonyms / surveillance traps

### 01 Supervision

**監督 ≠ surveillance**  
通常包含管理／指導責任，不等於秘密或技術監視。

### 02 Inspection

**檢查 ≠ surveillance**  
通常是一次或定期檢查，不是持續監控。

### 03 Audit

**審計 ≠ surveillance**  
正式檢查紀錄／系統，不是觀察人或活動。

### 04 Tracking

**只在位置／狀態追蹤 sense 接近**  
強調 movement/status。

### 05 Observation

**只在觀察 sense 接近**  
可短期、研究式，不一定持續監視。

### 06 Monitoring

**比 surveillance 更中性、更廣**  
可涵蓋機器、環境、健康，不一定是對人監視。

### 07 Watching

**口語且不一定系統化**  
只是看着某人／某物，未必涉及記錄或長期追蹤。

### 08 Surveillance / surveil

**詞形陷阱**  
surveillance 是名詞；surveil 是較少見、偏美式的動詞。

**A. Monitoring** · 監察；監測

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The company uses monitoring to detect unusual network activity.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Monitoring ✓** — continuous or repeated checking over time. Here, continuous checking rather than watching a person. This is the most precise replacement.
- **B.** **Observation** — carefully watching or studying someone or something. It may be short-term or research-based and need not imply continuous monitoring. The decisive clue is that continuous checking rather than watching a person.
- **C.** **Tracking** — following movement, location or status over time. It specifically follows location, movement or status and is too narrow elsewhere. The decisive clue is that continuous checking rather than watching a person.
- **D.** **Inspection** — an episodic examination rather than continuous surveillance. It is usually a discrete check rather than ongoing surveillance. The decisive clue is that continuous checking rather than watching a person.
- **E.** **Audit** — a formal examination of records, systems or processes. It focuses on records or systems, not watching activity. The decisive clue is that continuous checking rather than watching a person.
- **F.** **Watching** — looking at or keeping an eye on someone or something. It is informal and unsystematic compared with formal surveillance. The decisive clue is that continuous checking rather than watching a person.

- **C.** **Covert monitoring ✓** — secret monitoring carried out without the subject's knowledge. Here, concealed monitoring. This is the most precise replacement.
- **D.** **Observation** — carefully watching or studying someone or something. It may be short-term or research-based and need not imply continuous monitoring. The decisive clue is that concealed monitoring.
- **E.** **Watching** — looking at or keeping an eye on someone or something. It is informal and unsystematic compared with formal surveillance. The decisive clue is that concealed monitoring.
- **F.** **Supervision** — overseeing people or activities with responsibility for safety or correctness. It implies responsibility for people or activities rather than merely watching them. The decisive clue is that concealed monitoring.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **surveillance vs monitoring**: monitoring 更中性、更廣，不一定是對人監視。
- **surveillance vs observation**: observation 可短期或研究式。
- **surveillance vs watching**: watching 更口語、更不技術。
- **surveillance vs security monitoring**: 後者明確指出保安目的。
- **surveillance vs scrutiny**: scrutiny 是仔細／批判性審視。
- **surveillance vs tracking**: tracking 強調位置、移動或狀態。
- **surveillance vs oversight**: oversight 是治理／問責監督。
- **surveillance vs supervision**: supervision 帶管理、指導或安全責任。
- **surveillance vs covert monitoring**: covert monitoring 明確表示秘密進行。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & monitoring traps

- **under surveillance**：The building is under surveillance。
- **surveillance of + person/place/activity**。
- **monitoring of + system/activity**。
- **under observation**：medical/research context 常見。
- **under scrutiny**：policy, conduct, organisation, decision。
- **location / vehicle / device tracking**。
- **regulatory / independent oversight**。
- **under supervision**：有人負責看管／指導。
- **covert monitoring / covert surveillance**：秘密監察。
- **inspection / audit** 通常是一次或定期檢查，不等於持續 surveillance。
- **surveillance** 是名詞；動詞通常用 **monitor / watch / observe**。
