# EXERCISE · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Exercise

**「運動／鍛鍊／訓練／練習」，要先分清是廣義身體活動、一次 workout、長期 training、單一 movement，還是特定類型如 aerobic activity / strength training。**

本單元集中處理 **exercise = physical activity or planned bodily activity performed for health, fitness, skill, rehabilitation, or performance**。你提供的 **physical activity; workout; training** 正好代表 **廣義活動、一次鍛鍊、一段系統訓練** 三個核心方向。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且高實用度的詞／表達，並按最新 inspection 標準區分 **conditioning / drill / routine / practice / movement / aerobic activity / strength training / exercise regimen**。每個目標設 **3 道題**；特定運動類型會明確標記為子類，而不是假裝成 universal synonym。

## Tier 1 — Core activity / workout / training equivalents · 核心運動、鍛鍊與訓練近義詞

### 01 Physical activity

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：體能活動；身體活動  
**語域**：neutral / health / very common  
**核心語義**：any bodily movement that uses energy, including exercise, sport, walking, household activity, and active transport  
**用法提示**：當 exercise 是廣義『身體活動』時最自然，尤其公共健康、學校健康和生活方式語境。  
**常見搭配**：regular physical activity; increase physical activity; physical-activity guidelines; moderate physical activity; levels of physical activity.  
**常見搭配對象**：adult, child, population, health, lifestyle.  
**與 exercise 的辨析**：Physical activity 比 exercise 更廣；exercise 通常是有目的、有結構的活動。  
**使用限制**：若是特定健身課或訓練計劃，workout / training 更精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Regular physical activity can improve cardiovascular health.  
定期體能活動可以改善心血管健康。

### 02 Workout

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：鍛鍊；健身訓練；運動一節  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：a session of physical exercise, especially one intended to improve fitness, strength, or endurance  
**用法提示**：當 exercise 指一次完整健身或鍛鍊時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：do a workout; morning workout; strength workout; intense workout; full-body workout.  
**常見搭配對象**：gym, fitness, strength, endurance, routine.  
**與 exercise 的辨析**：Workout 通常指一次 session；exercise 可指單一動作、整體活動或不可數概念。  
**使用限制**：一個單獨動作如 squat / stretch 不一定叫 workout。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
I usually do a short workout before breakfast.  
我通常在早餐前做一節短時間鍛鍊。

### 03 Training

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：訓練；鍛鍊  
**語域**：neutral / sports / professional  
**核心語義**：systematic practice designed to improve physical ability, skill, endurance, or performance  
**用法提示**：當 exercise 有長期、有目標、重複訓練的意味時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：strength training; endurance training; sports training; training programme; training session.  
**常見搭配對象**：athlete, team, fitness, skill, performance.  
**與 exercise 的辨析**：Training 強調持續、系統化改善；exercise 可以是任何一次活動。  
**使用限制**：隨便散步或一次簡單伸展通常不叫 training。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The runners increased their training before the competition.  
跑手在比賽前增加訓練量。

### 04 Fitness activity

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：健身活動；體適能活動  
**語域**：neutral / fitness  
**核心語義**：an activity performed to improve or maintain fitness  
**用法提示**：當 exercise 的目的明確是改善體能、心肺、肌力或整體健身時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：fitness activity; fitness programme; fitness class; fitness routine; fitness level.  
**常見搭配對象**：gym, class, routine, programme, health.  
**與 exercise 的辨析**：Fitness activity 強調目的在體適能；exercise 更廣。  
**使用限制**：競技技術訓練或復康活動未必以 fitness 為主要目標。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Swimming is a popular fitness activity for people of all ages.  
游泳是各年齡人士都常做的健身活動。

### 05 Exercise session

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：運動時段；鍛鍊一節  
**語域**：neutral / health  
**核心語義**：a period of time set aside for physical exercise  
**用法提示**：當 exercise 指一段具體時間內進行的運動時很清楚。  
**常見搭配**：exercise session; supervised exercise session; short exercise session; group exercise session; exercise programme.  
**常見搭配對象**：class, clinic, gym, participant, patient.  
**與 exercise 的辨析**：Exercise session 強調一段時間；exercise 本身可以更抽象。  
**使用限制**：若要描述長期訓練系統，training programme 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Each exercise session lasts about thirty minutes.  
每次運動時段約三十分鐘。

## Tier 2 — Conditioning / drill / routine / movement variants · 體能、操練、程序與動作變體

### 06 Conditioning

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：體能訓練；身體機能訓練  
**語域**：sports / fitness  
**核心語義**：physical training designed to improve fitness, strength, stamina, or readiness for performance  
**用法提示**：當 exercise 側重體能準備、耐力和運動表現時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：physical conditioning; strength and conditioning; conditioning programme; aerobic conditioning; conditioning work.  
**常見搭配對象**：athlete, team, stamina, performance, sport.  
**與 exercise 的辨析**：Conditioning 偏運動表現和體能準備；exercise 更一般。  
**使用限制**：日常輕量活動不一定稱 conditioning。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The team added extra conditioning before the new season.  
球隊在新賽季前增加額外體能訓練。

### 07 Drill

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：操練；反覆練習  
**語域**：sports / training  
**核心語義**：a repeated exercise designed to practise a specific skill, movement, or response  
**用法提示**：當 exercise 是重複某個動作或技能以改善熟練度時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：training drill; passing drill; agility drill; practice drill; repeated drill.  
**常見搭配對象**：skill, movement, technique, team, athlete.  
**與 exercise 的辨析**：Drill 強調反覆練某一技能；exercise 可以是一般鍛鍊。  
**使用限制**：整體健身 session 不應全部叫 drill。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The coach used a passing drill to improve accuracy.  
教練用傳球操練提升準確度。

### 08 Routine

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：運動程序；固定鍛鍊組合  
**語域**：neutral / fitness  
**核心語義**：a regular sequence of exercises or movements performed in a set order  
**用法提示**：當 exercise 是一套固定動作流程時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：exercise routine; fitness routine; stretching routine; morning routine; workout routine.  
**常見搭配對象**：movement, sequence, gym, class, habit.  
**與 exercise 的辨析**：Routine 強調固定次序／習慣；exercise 可只是一個單獨活動。  
**使用限制**：一次性的運動不一定構成 routine。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She follows a simple stretching routine every morning.  
她每天早上都做一套簡單伸展運動。

### 09 Practice

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：練習；操練  
**語域**：neutral / sports / skill  
**核心語義**：repeated performance of an activity or skill to improve ability  
**用法提示**：當 exercise 更偏技能改善而不是單純體能時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：regular practice; practice session; practise a skill; sports practice; daily practice.  
**常見搭配對象**：skill, technique, sport, movement, performance.  
**與 exercise 的辨析**：Practice 強調技能熟練；exercise 可純粹為健康或體能。  
**使用限制**：純粹消耗卡路里的健身活動不一定叫 practice。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Regular practice helps improve balance and coordination.  
定期練習有助改善平衡和協調。

### 10 Movement

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：動作；身體活動  
**語域**：neutral / health / fitness  
**核心語義**：a bodily action or series of bodily actions  
**用法提示**：當 exercise 指一個具體身體動作，而不是整個運動 session 時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：movement pattern; controlled movement; repeated movement; body movement; functional movement.  
**常見搭配對象**：body, joint, muscle, pattern, technique.  
**與 exercise 的辨析**：Movement 是最基本的動作單位；exercise 通常有訓練目的。  
**使用限制**：整套 workout 不能只用 movement 概括。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The therapist demonstrated the movement slowly.  
治療師慢慢示範這個動作。

## Tier 3 — Aerobic / strength / regimen variants · 帶氧、肌力與長期方案變體

### 11 Aerobic activity

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：帶氧活動；有氧運動  
**語域**：health / fitness  
**核心語義**：physical activity that raises heart rate and breathing for sustained periods  
**用法提示**：當 exercise 明確指步行、跑步、游泳、踏單車等有氧活動時最精準。  
**常見搭配**：aerobic activity; moderate aerobic activity; vigorous aerobic activity; aerobic exercise; aerobic fitness.  
**常見搭配對象**：walking, running, cycling, swimming, cardio.  
**與 exercise 的辨析**：Aerobic activity 是 exercise 的一個類型。  
**使用限制**：重量訓練和伸展不屬 aerobic activity。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Adults should include regular aerobic activity in their weekly routine.  
成年人每週應定期進行帶氧活動。

### 12 Strength training

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：肌力訓練；重量訓練  
**語域**：fitness / health  
**核心語義**：exercise that makes muscles work against resistance to improve strength  
**用法提示**：當 exercise 的目標是增強肌力或以阻力訓練為主時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：strength training; resistance training; strength exercise; strength-training programme; strength session.  
**常見搭配對象**：muscle, weight, resistance, gym, athlete.  
**與 exercise 的辨析**：Strength training 是特定 exercise 類型，不是一般同義詞。  
**使用限制**：跑步、伸展或技能練習不能泛稱 strength training。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She does strength training twice a week.  
她每星期做兩次肌力訓練。

### 13 Exercise regimen

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：運動方案；鍛鍊規程  
**語域**：formal / health / fitness  
**核心語義**：a structured and regular programme of exercise followed over time  
**用法提示**：當 exercise 指長期、有規律、較正式的鍛鍊安排時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：exercise regimen; regular regimen; follow a regimen; fitness regimen; prescribed exercise regimen.  
**常見搭配對象**：patient, athlete, adult, programme, schedule.  
**與 exercise 的辨析**：Regimen 強調長期規律和結構；exercise 可以只是單次活動。  
**使用限制**：一次 workout 不叫 regimen。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The doctor recommended a gradual exercise regimen after recovery.  
醫生建議康復後採用循序漸進的運動方案。

## 04 False synonyms / exercise traps · 容易誤選的詞與運動陷阱

### 01 Sport

**運動項目不等於 exercise**  
sport 通常有規則、技巧、競賽或休閒形式；exercise 可以完全沒有競賽。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Sport can involve exercise, but the concepts are not identical.

### 02 Game

**遊戲／比賽不等於 exercise**  
game 是有規則的遊戲或競賽；它可能帶來運動量，但不是所有 exercise 都是 game。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A game may involve exercise, but exercise is broader.

### 03 Stretch

**伸展不等於所有 exercise**  
stretch 是一種動作或伸展活動，只是 exercise 的一部分。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Stretching is one type of exercise.

### 04 Workout

**workout 和 exercise 很接近，但範圍較窄**  
workout 通常是一整節鍛鍊；exercise 也可指單一動作或廣義運動。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A workout is usually a session of exercise.

### 05 Training

**training 和 exercise 很接近，但通常更系統化**  
training 通常有持續目標、技能或表現提升；一般 exercise 未必。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Training is systematic; exercise can be casual.

### 06 Practice

**練習不等於所有 exercise**  
practice 偏技能熟練；exercise 可以純粹為健康、體能或復康。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Practice targets skill; exercise may target fitness.

### 07 Movement

**動作不等於 exercise**  
movement 是身體動作；exercise 通常是有目的的活動或訓練。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Movement is a component; exercise is purposeful activity.

### 08 Exercise / exercising

**詞性陷阱**  
exercise 可作名詞或動詞；exercising 是動名詞／現在分詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Exercise can be a noun or verb; exercising is the -ing form.

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

Regular exercise can improve cardiovascular health.

- B. sport
- C. game
- D. stretching only
- E. workout session
- F. practice

**physical activity** · 體能活動；身體活動

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. physical activity ✓ — public-health context + regular movement supports the broad term physical activity。**
- **B. sport — sport is only one possible form of physical activity。**
- **C. game — game is narrower and usually rule-based。**
- **D. stretching only — stretching only covers one subtype。**
- **E. workout session — workout session is a structured bout, while the sentence is broader。**
- **F. practice — practice focuses on skill development.**

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **exercise vs physical activity**: physical activity 更廣，包含所有身體活動；exercise 通常較有目的。
- **exercise vs workout**: workout 通常是一整節鍛鍊。
- **exercise vs training**: training 更系統化，通常有技能／表現目標。
- **exercise vs drill**: drill 是反覆練某一特定技能。
- **exercise vs routine**: routine 是固定動作順序／習慣。
- **exercise vs practice**: practice 更偏技能熟練。
- **exercise vs movement**: movement 是單一動作；exercise 通常有訓練目的。
- **exercise vs aerobic activity**: aerobic activity 是 exercise 的一個類型。
- **exercise vs strength training**: strength training 是以阻力提升肌力的特定類型。
- **exercise vs sport**: sport 常有規則、技能或競賽結構，並非所有 exercise 都是 sport。

## 08 Grammar, countability & exercise traps · 文法、可數性與運動陷阱

- **exercise** 可作不可數：Exercise is good for health；亦可作可數：Do three exercises。
- 動詞 **exercise**：I exercise three times a week。
- **workout** 是可數：a workout / two workouts；動詞短語是 **work out**。
- **training** 多作不可數：strength training / training for a race。
- **physical activity** 多作不可數概念；具體活動可說 forms of physical activity。
- **exercise session** 是可數單位：three exercise sessions a week。
- **drill** 是可數：a passing drill / several drills。
- **routine** 是可數：a stretching routine。
- **practice** 多作不可數：regular practice；亦可作可數 session in some sports contexts。
- **movement** 是可數：repeat the movement ten times。
- **aerobic activity** 和 **strength training** 是 exercise 的子類，不是 universal synonyms。
- **exercise regimen** 表示長期、有結構的安排；不要用來指單一次 workout。
- **sport ≠ exercise**：sport 可以包含競賽與技巧；exercise 可以完全沒有競賽。
