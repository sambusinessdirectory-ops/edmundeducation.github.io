# HOME-COOKED · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Home-cooked

**「家煮的／自家製的」，要先分清是在談『真的在家烹煮』、『由基本食材自己製作』，還是只是『家常菜風格』。**

本單元集中處理 **home-cooked = cooked or prepared in a home kitchen rather than bought ready-made or eaten as restaurant-prepared food**。你提供的 **homemade; prepared at home** 分別代表 **自家製** 和 **透明描述的在家準備**。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且高實用度的詞／表達，共 **39 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。特別把 **homemade / home-prepared / freshly cooked / from-scratch / home-style / scratch-cooked** 的邊界拆清，避免把『在家煮』和『家常風格』混在一起。

## Tier 1 — Core homemade / home-prepared equivalents · 核心自家製與家中準備近義詞

### 01 Homemade

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：自家製的；家中製作的  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：made at home rather than bought ready-made or produced commercially  
**用法提示**：和 home-cooked 最常見、最直接的近義詞之一，尤其可形容食物、醬料、蛋糕、湯和甜品。  
**常見搭配**：homemade food; homemade soup; homemade cake; homemade sauce; homemade bread.  
**常見搭配對象**：meal, soup, cake, bread, sauce.  
**與 home-cooked 的辨析**：Homemade 強調『在家製作』；home-cooked 更特別指『在家烹煮的熟食／餐點』。  
**使用限制**：未經烹煮的自家製食品也可叫 homemade，但未必是 home-cooked。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
They served homemade soup and fresh bread.  
他們供應自家製湯和新鮮麵包。

### 02 Prepared at home

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：在家準備的；在家製作的  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：made or prepared in a domestic setting rather than bought ready-to-eat  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很透明，適合一般說明、健康、飲食和比較外食／在家吃的語境。  
**常見搭配**：prepared at home; meals prepared at home; food prepared at home; freshly prepared at home; home-prepared meals.  
**常見搭配對象**：meal, food, lunch, dinner, dish.  
**與 home-cooked 的辨析**：Prepared at home 是描述性表達，比 home-cooked 稍寬，可包括不用烹煮的食品。  
**使用限制**：如果重點是熱食、烹調過程，home-cooked 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Meals prepared at home often allow more control over ingredients.  
在家準備的餐點通常更容易控制食材。

### 03 Home-prepared

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：家中準備的；自家準備的  
**語域**：neutral / health / formal  
**核心語義**：prepared in the home rather than commercially or in a restaurant  
**用法提示**：健康研究、營養、家庭飲食和正式寫作中很實用。  
**常見搭配**：home-prepared meal; home-prepared food; home-prepared lunch; home-prepared dishes; home-prepared diet.  
**常見搭配對象**：meal, food, lunch, dish, diet.  
**與 home-cooked 的辨析**：Home-prepared 比 home-cooked 稍正式，也不一定要求實際烹煮。  
**使用限制**：日常口語通常直接說 homemade / home-cooked。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The study compared home-prepared meals with takeaway food.  
研究比較家中準備的餐點和外賣食品。

### 04 Cooked at home

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：在家烹煮的；家中煮的  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：cooked in a home kitchen rather than in a restaurant or factory  
**用法提示**：當 home-cooked 需要最直接透明地改寫時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：cooked at home; meals cooked at home; food cooked at home; freshly cooked at home; usually cooked at home.  
**常見搭配對象**：meal, dinner, lunch, food, dish.  
**與 home-cooked 的辨析**：和 home-cooked 幾乎直接對應，只是變成較長的描述性結構。  
**使用限制**：不適合需要精簡形容詞的位置。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Most of their weekday dinners are cooked at home.  
他們平日大部分晚餐都在家烹煮。

### 05 Home-made meal

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：自家製餐點；家庭自製的一餐  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：a meal made at home from ingredients rather than purchased as a complete ready-made meal  
**用法提示**：當 home-cooked 具體指一整餐時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：homemade meal; home-made dinner; homemade lunch; homemade family meal; fresh homemade meal.  
**常見搭配對象**：dinner, lunch, family, guest, household.  
**與 home-cooked 的辨析**：Homemade meal 強調整餐由家中製作；home-cooked 也可修飾單一道菜。  
**使用限制**：英式英語 modern style 多寫 homemade，不一定加連字號。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
After travelling, he missed a simple homemade meal.  
旅行後，他很想念一頓簡單的自家製飯菜。

## Tier 2 — Fresh / from-scratch / home-style variants · 新鮮烹煮、從零製作與家常風格變體

### 06 Freshly cooked

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：新鮮烹煮的；即煮的  
**語域**：neutral / food service  
**核心語義**：cooked recently rather than reheated, packaged, or kept for a long time  
**用法提示**：當 home-cooked 的重點是新鮮烹煮，而不特別強調地點時可用。  
**常見搭配**：freshly cooked meal; freshly cooked food; freshly cooked rice; freshly cooked breakfast; freshly prepared and cooked.  
**常見搭配對象**：meal, rice, breakfast, dish, food.  
**與 home-cooked 的辨析**：Freshly cooked 強調時間和新鮮度，不等於『在家煮』。  
**使用限制**：餐廳即煮食物也可以 freshly cooked，所以不是 universal synonym。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The café serves freshly cooked meals throughout the day.  
咖啡店全日供應新鮮烹煮的餐點。

### 07 From-scratch

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：由基本食材自行製作的；從零開始煮的  
**語域**：informal / culinary  
**核心語義**：made from basic ingredients rather than from a ready-made mix, sauce, or prepared component  
**用法提示**：當 home-cooked 強調不是使用即食／半製成品，而是由基本食材開始製作時很地道。  
**常見搭配**：from-scratch cooking; from-scratch meal; cook from scratch; made from scratch; from-scratch recipe.  
**常見搭配對象**：meal, cake, sauce, bread, dinner.  
**與 home-cooked 的辨析**：From-scratch 強調製作方式；home-cooked 強調在家烹煮。  
**使用限制**：使用現成醬料在家煮的餐點仍可 home-cooked，但不一定 from-scratch。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She prefers from-scratch cooking at weekends.  
她週末較喜歡由基本食材開始自己煮。

### 08 Family-made

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：家庭自製的；由家人製作的  
**語域**：informal / descriptive  
**核心語義**：made by members of a family, often in a domestic and personal context  
**用法提示**：當 home-cooked 帶家庭參與、家人親手製作的溫暖語氣時可用。  
**常見搭配**：family-made meal; family-made food; family recipe; made by the family; family cooking.  
**常見搭配對象**：meal, dish, recipe, food, celebration.  
**與 home-cooked 的辨析**：Family-made 強調製作者是家人；home-cooked 只要求在家烹調。  
**使用限制**：不是固定高頻術語，屬自然描述性表達。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The festival featured family-made dishes from local residents.  
節慶活動展示本地居民家庭自製的菜式。

### 09 Home-style

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：家常風味的；家庭式的  
**語域**：neutral / restaurant / culinary  
**核心語義**：made or presented in a simple, traditional style associated with home cooking  
**用法提示**：當 home-cooked 指『家常菜風格』而不一定真的在家煮時很重要。  
**常見搭配**：home-style cooking; home-style meal; home-style dishes; home-style food; home-style recipe.  
**常見搭配對象**：dish, cooking, restaurant, meal, cuisine.  
**與 home-cooked 的辨析**：Home-style 是風格；home-cooked 是實際烹煮地點／方式。  
**使用限制**：餐廳菜也可 home-style，所以不能把它當作真正同義詞。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The restaurant is known for simple home-style cooking.  
這間餐廳以簡單家常菜聞名。

### 10 Made in one's own kitchen

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：在自己廚房製作的  
**語域**：conversational / descriptive  
**核心語義**：prepared personally in a home kitchen rather than bought from outside  
**用法提示**：當需要很自然地強調『自己在家做』時可用。  
**常見搭配**：made in my own kitchen; prepared in her own kitchen; made at home; home kitchen; kitchen-made.  
**常見搭配對象**：meal, cake, sauce, bread, dish.  
**與 home-cooked 的辨析**：是透明描述性改寫，比 home-cooked 更口語化。  
**使用限制**：較長，不適合需要簡潔標籤或標題。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The sauce was made in her own kitchen using fresh tomatoes.  
這個醬汁是在她自己的廚房用新鮮番茄製作的。

## Tier 3 — Scratch-cooking / culinary-style / ingredient-based variants · 基本食材烹調與描述型變體

### 11 Scratch-cooked

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：由基本食材烹調的；從零開始煮的  
**語域**：culinary / institutional  
**核心語義**：cooked from basic ingredients rather than assembled from highly prepared components  
**用法提示**：食堂、學校餐飲、餐飲政策和廚藝語境中可用，尤其強調 actual cooking from ingredients。  
**常見搭配**：scratch-cooked meals; scratch cooking; scratch-cooked food; scratch kitchen; cook from scratch.  
**常見搭配對象**：meal, school lunch, kitchen, food service, dish.  
**與 home-cooked 的辨析**：Scratch-cooked 強調烹調深度；home-cooked 強調在家。  
**使用限制**：較專門，不是所有學習者日常都需要使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The school is increasing the number of scratch-cooked meals.  
學校正增加由基本食材烹調的餐點數量。

### 12 Home-cuisine style

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：家常菜風格；家庭料理風格  
**語域**：descriptive / culinary  
**核心語義**：prepared in a style associated with ordinary domestic cooking rather than elaborate restaurant cuisine  
**用法提示**：當 home-cooked 其實是『味道／風格像家裏煮』時可作描述性近義表達。  
**常見搭配**：home-cuisine style; home-style cuisine; domestic cooking style; family-style cooking; simple home cooking.  
**常見搭配對象**：dish, restaurant, menu, cooking, cuisine.  
**與 home-cooked 的辨析**：強調風格，不保證真的在家製作。  
**使用限制**：自然度不及 home-style，屬較描述性表達。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The menu focuses on simple dishes in a home-cuisine style.  
餐牌集中提供家常料理風格的簡單菜式。

### 13 Prepared from basic ingredients

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：以基本食材自行準備的  
**語域**：neutral / explanatory  
**核心語義**：made using basic raw or minimally prepared ingredients rather than ready-made components  
**用法提示**：當 home-cooked 需要避開 marketing 語氣，精確說明烹調方式時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：prepared from basic ingredients; made from basic ingredients; simple ingredients; fresh ingredients; minimally prepared ingredients.  
**常見搭配對象**：meal, dish, soup, sauce, bread.  
**與 home-cooked 的辨析**：只說製作方式，不一定表示地點在家。  
**使用限制**：若要強調 domestic setting，需加 at home / in the home kitchen。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Most meals are prepared from basic ingredients rather than ready-made mixes.  
大部分餐點由基本食材製作，而不是使用即食混合料。

## 04 False synonyms / home-cooked traps · 容易誤選的詞與「家煮」陷阱

### 01 Fresh

**新鮮不等於 home-cooked**  
fresh 表示新鮮；餐廳食物可以很新鮮但不是家煮。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Freshness and place of preparation are different.

### 02 Healthy

**健康不等於 home-cooked**  
家煮食物可以健康，也可以高糖高脂；home-cooked 不保證營養質素。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Home-cooked does not automatically mean healthy.

### 03 Organic

**有機不等於 home-cooked**  
organic 是食材／生產標準；與在哪裏烹調無關。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Organic describes production standards, not cooking location.

### 04 Traditional

**傳統不等於 home-cooked**  
traditional 指文化或歷史做法；家煮菜可以很現代。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Traditional and home-cooked overlap only sometimes.

### 05 Homestyle

**home-style 不等於一定 home-cooked**  
餐廳也可提供 home-style food；它描述風格，不是實際地點。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Home-style describes style, not where it was cooked.

### 06 Ready-made

**即食／預製通常與 home-cooked 對比**  
ready-made 指預先製作、可直接食用或簡單加熱。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Ready-made food is usually contrasted with home-cooked food.

### 07 Restaurant-made

**餐廳製作不等於 home-cooked**  
restaurant-made 指由餐廳製作，和家庭廚房來源不同。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Restaurant-made and home-cooked identify different preparation settings.

### 08 Home cooking / home-cooked

**詞性陷阱**  
home cooking 是名詞片語；home-cooked 是形容詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Home cooking is a noun phrase; home-cooked is an adjective.

- **C. healthy — healthy concerns nutrition, not preparation setting。**
- **D. organic — organic concerns ingredient-production standards。**
- **E. traditional — traditional concerns style/history。**
- **F. restaurant-made — restaurant-made identifies the opposite preparation setting.**

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **home-cooked vs homemade**: homemade 更廣；未烹煮的自家製食品也可 homemade。
- **home-cooked vs prepared at home**: prepared at home 更描述性，也可包含不用煮的食品。
- **home-cooked vs home-prepared**: home-prepared 稍正式，常見於健康／研究文本。
- **home-cooked vs freshly cooked**: freshly cooked 強調新鮮度，不保證在家。
- **home-cooked vs from-scratch**: from-scratch 強調由基本食材開始，不強調地點。
- **home-cooked vs home-style**: home-style 是風格；餐廳也可以 home-style。
- **home-cooked vs ready-made**: ready-made 通常是重要對比項。
- **home-cooked vs healthy**: 家煮不等於一定健康。
- **home-cooked vs organic**: 有機與烹煮地點是不同概念。
- **home-cooked vs traditional**: 傳統菜不一定家煮，家煮菜也可以很現代。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & home-cooked traps · 文法、搭配與家煮陷阱

- **home-cooked** 通常作前置形容詞：home-cooked meal / home-cooked food。
- **homemade** 現代英語通常寫成一個字：homemade bread / homemade soup。
- **home-prepared** 常見於正式、營養和研究語境。
- **prepared at home / cooked at home** 是最透明的描述性改寫。
- **made from scratch / from-scratch cooking** 強調不用現成混合料／半製成品。
- **home-style** 描述味道或風格，不保證真的在家烹煮。
- **scratch-cooked** 在學校餐飲、食堂和機構廚房語境較常見。
- **home cooking** 是名詞片語：I miss home cooking。
- **home-cooked** 是形容詞：a home-cooked dinner。
- **fresh / healthy / organic / traditional ≠ home-cooked**：這些只是其他食物屬性。
- **ready-made** 常和 home-cooked / from-scratch 構成重要對比。
- 香港／英式英語中 **home-cooked meal, homemade food, home-style cooking, ready-made meal** 都很自然。
