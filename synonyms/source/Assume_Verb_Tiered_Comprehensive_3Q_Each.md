# ASSUME · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Assume · verb

**Assume 有幾個重要義項：一般推測、根據線索推定、想當然地接受、把某事暫時當作分析前提、預期未來，以及從證據推斷。** 這些 sense 不能混為一談。最重要的是判斷 **certainty, evidence, purpose, and whether the assumption is temporary, unverified, or predictive**。

你提供的 **suppose; presume; take for granted** 正好代表三個核心方向：**一般推測、較有依據的推定、未經質疑便視為理所當然**。

本次保留 **9 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達。會特別區分 **know / guess / infer / expect / believe**，因為它們與 assume 在確定程度和證據基礎上不同。共 **27 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Assume 的語境 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|
| 一般不確定推測 | **suppose** |
| 有一些線索的推定 | **presume** |
| 想當然／不加質疑地接受 | **take for granted** |
| 模型／計算中的工作前提 | **assume as a working premise** |
| 對未來的預期 | **expect** |
| 一般相信某事為真 | **believe** |
| 由證據得出結論 | **infer** |
| 正式提出理論假設 | **posit** |
| 未查證便接受 | **accept without verification** |

## Tier 1 — Core supposition / presumption / working-assumption equivalents · 核心推測、推定與分析前提近義詞

### 01 Suppose

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：假設；猜想；認為  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to think that something is probably true, often without being certain  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **assume** 最核心的近義詞之一，尤其適合一般推測、暫時相信或在缺乏完整證據下作判斷。  
**常見搭配**：suppose that; I suppose; suppose for a moment; suppose this is true.  
**常見搭配對象**：fact, situation, explanation, possibility, outcome.  
**與 assume 的辨析**：Suppose 通常比 assume 更口語、更保留不確定性；assume 常更直接把某事當作前提。  
**使用限制**：正式研究中的『設定前提』常用 assume / posit，而不是 suppose。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
I suppose the train will be crowded at this time of day.  
我想這個時段的列車應該會很擠。

### 02 Presume

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：推定；假定；料想  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to believe something is true because it seems likely or because the available evidence points that way  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 與 assume 很接近，但通常比 assume 更有『根據現有線索推定』的意味。  
**常見搭配**：presume that; be presumed innocent; presume from; reasonably presume.  
**常見搭配對象**：identity, intention, innocence, cause, situation.  
**與 assume 的辨析**：Presume 往往比 assume 多一點 evidential basis；assume 可完全只是方便起見的前提。  
**使用限制**：如果完全沒有任何依據，只是設一個 hypothetical premise，assume/suppose 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
We can reasonably presume that the payment was received.  
我們可以合理推定款項已經收到。

### 03 Take for granted

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：想當然地認為；視為理所當然  
**語域**：neutral / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：to accept something as true, certain, or guaranteed without questioning it  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，當 assume 帶『沒有查證便當作理所當然』的意味時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：take it for granted that; take someone for granted; take access for granted.  
**常見搭配對象**：fact, support, access, relationship, outcome.  
**與 assume 的辨析**：Take for granted 通常比 assume 更帶批判意味，暗示沒有質疑或沒有珍惜。  
**使用限制**：正式數學／研究假設不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
We should not take it for granted that every household has internet access.  
我們不應想當然地認為每個家庭都有互聯網連線。

### 04 Assume as a working premise

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：作為工作前提假設  
**語域**：formal / analytical  
**核心語義**：to temporarily accept a proposition as true so that analysis, planning, or reasoning can proceed  
**用法提示**：當 assume 是研究、規劃、模型或論證中的 **working assumption** 時，這個展開式改寫非常精準。  
**常見搭配**：working assumption; assume as a premise; proceed on the assumption; analytical premise.  
**常見搭配對象**：model, forecast, calculation, scenario, analysis.  
**與 assume 的辨析**：比一般 suppose 更明確表示『先當作前提，以便繼續推理』。  
**使用限制**：日常口語會顯得太 formal。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
For the calculation, we assume as a working premise that demand remains constant.  
為了計算，我們暫且假設需求維持不變。

### 05 Expect

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：預期；料想  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：to believe that something is likely to happen or be the case  
**用法提示**：當 assume 實際帶有對未來結果的預期，而不是純粹設定前提時可用。  
**常見搭配**：expect that; expect to; reasonably expect; expect a result.  
**常見搭配對象**：result, change, delay, outcome, demand.  
**與 assume 的辨析**：Expect 強調 likelihood / future anticipation；assume 更像把某事當作已成立。  
**使用限制**：如果只是邏輯前提，不能直接改成 expect。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
We expect demand to increase during the holiday period.  
我們預期假期期間需求會上升。

## Tier 2 — Belief / inference / theoretical variants · 信念、推論與理論型變體

### 06 Believe

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：相信；認為  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：to accept something as true or hold it as an opinion  
**用法提示**：當 assume 只是表示『相信某事為真』，而且不強調推理前提或證據程度時可用。  
**常見搭配**：believe that; widely believed; believe something to be true.  
**常見搭配對象**：claim, explanation, person, idea, situation.  
**與 assume 的辨析**：Believe 更直接表達心理信念；assume 常有『未核實便採納』或『作為前提』意味。  
**使用限制**：如果需要表達 temporary premise，believe 不夠精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Many people believe the change will improve safety.  
很多人相信這項改變會提升安全。

### 07 Infer

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：推斷；推論  
**語域**：formal / analytical  
**核心語義**：to reach a conclusion from evidence, clues, or reasoning  
**用法提示**：當 assume 其實是根據證據得出判斷時，infer 比 assume 更精準。  
**常見搭配**：infer from evidence; infer that; reasonably infer; inference from data.  
**常見搭配對象**：evidence, data, pattern, behaviour, result.  
**與 assume 的辨析**：Infer 一定要求 evidence/reasoning；assume 可以沒有證據。  
**使用限制**：純假設或主觀預設不能用 infer。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
From the data, we can infer that demand fell after the price increase.  
從數據可推斷，加價後需求下降。

### 08 Posit

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：提出假設；假定  
**語域**：formal / academic  
**核心語義**：to put forward a proposition or assumption as a basis for argument, theory, or analysis  
**用法提示**：適合學術、理論、哲學或模型語境中的正式假設。  
**常見搭配**：posit that; posit a relationship; posit a model; theoretical proposition.  
**常見搭配對象**：theory, relationship, mechanism, model, explanation.  
**與 assume 的辨析**：Posit 更正式，也強調『提出一個命題／假設供分析』。  
**使用限制**：日常推測通常不用 posit。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The theory posits that social trust affects cooperation.  
這個理論提出假設，社會信任會影響合作。

## Tier 3 — Verification-focused explanatory paraphrase · 核實導向解釋性改寫

### 09 Accept without verification

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：未經核實便接受；未查證便假定  
**語域**：formal / explanatory  
**核心語義**：to treat something as true without checking whether it is actually correct  
**用法提示**：這是 **assume** 在『未查證便接受』sense 的透明改寫，特別適合批判性思考、審核或風險語境。  
**常見搭配**：accept without verification; verify assumptions; unchecked assumption; unverified claim.  
**常見搭配對象**：claim, figure, identity, report, information.  
**與 assume 的辨析**：比 assume 更明確指出缺乏 verification，常帶警示意味。  
**使用限制**：如果假設本來就是有意設定的模型前提，這個表達會顯得負面。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Analysts should not accept figures without verification.  
分析人員不應在未經核實下便接受數字。

## 04 False synonyms / assumption traps · 容易誤選的『知道、猜測、推論』陷阱

### 01 Know

**知道 ≠ assume**  
know 表示有把握地知道，assume 則往往缺乏確認。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Knowledge implies stronger certainty.

### 02 Guess

**猜測 ≠ assume**  
guess 通常比 assume 更缺乏依據，也更隨意。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Guessing is more speculative.

### 03 Infer

**只在有證據推論 sense 接近**  
infer 需要 evidence/reasoning。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Inference is evidence-based.

### 04 Expect

**只在預期未來 sense 接近**  
expect 偏未來或可能結果。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Expectation is future/likelihood-focused.

### 05 Believe

**只在信念 sense 接近**  
believe 不一定有『作為前提』意味。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Belief is mental acceptance.

### 06 Presume

**通常比 assume 多一些依據**  
presume 常暗示已有合理線索。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Presumption is often evidence-supported.

### 07 Take for granted

**只在『想當然』sense 接近**  
常帶批判意味，並可表示不珍惜。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
It is idiomatic and evaluative.

### 08 Assume / assumption

**詞形陷阱**  
assume 是動詞；assumption 是名詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check word class.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Suppose · 假設；猜想；認為

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

I assume the shop will still be open at six.

**中文翻譯**  
我想那間商店六時應該仍然開門。

- A. Suppose
- B. Presume
- C. Believe
- D. Know
- E. Infer
- F. Guess

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Suppose** · 假設；猜想；認為

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
I suppose the shop will still be open at six.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Suppose ✓** — think something is probably true without being certain. Here, a casual uncertain belief is intended. That makes **Suppose** the most precise replacement for this particular sense of *assume*.
- **B.** **Presume** — believe something is true because it seems likely or evidence points that way. This usually suggests some supporting reason or evidence and is less suitable for a purely hypothetical or casual assumption. The decisive clue is that a casual uncertain belief is intended.
- **C.** **Believe** — accept something as true or hold it as an opinion. This expresses mental acceptance but does not necessarily imply a temporary premise or unchecked assumption. The decisive clue is that a casual uncertain belief is intended.
- **D.** **Know** — be certain of a fact. This is too certain; assume explicitly leaves room for lack of confirmation. The decisive clue is that a casual uncertain belief is intended.
- **E.** **Infer** — reach a conclusion from evidence or reasoning. This requires evidence or reasoning, which is not present in every assumption. The decisive clue is that a casual uncertain belief is intended.
- **F.** **Guess** — form an answer or opinion with little evidence. This is more speculative and casual, often with even less basis than assume. The decisive clue is that a casual uncertain belief is intended.

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Assume 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 日常推測 | **suppose** | 是否 casual and uncertain belief？ |
| 有線索的推定 | **presume** | 是否 some evidence/support is present？ |
| 未經質疑便當真 | **take for granted** | 是否 unquestioned acceptance is criticised？ |
| 模型／計算前提 | **assume as a working premise** | 是否 temporary analytical premise？ |
| 未來預期 | **expect** | 是否 likelihood/future outcome is central？ |
| 一般信念 | **believe** | 是否 mental acceptance rather than premise-setting？ |
| 根據資料推論 | **infer** | 是否 evidence leads to the conclusion？ |
| 理論提出假設 | **posit** | 是否 formal theoretical proposition？ |
| 未查證接受 | **accept without verification** | 是否 lack of checking is the key concern？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **assume vs suppose**：suppose 更口語、更保留不確定性。
- **assume vs presume**：presume 通常多一些合理依據。
- **assume vs take for granted**：後者常帶『想當然』的批判意味。
- **assume vs expect**：expect 偏未來／可能性。
- **assume vs believe**：believe 表達信念；assume 可是暫時前提。
- **assume vs infer**：infer 必須根據證據或推理。
- **assume vs posit**：posit 更正式，適合理論／模型。
- **assume vs know**：know 的確定程度更高。
- **assume vs guess**：guess 更隨意、依據更少。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & certainty traps

- **assume that + clause**：We assume that demand will remain stable。
- **assume + noun**：assume responsibility / assume control 是另一義項，本課不處理。
- **suppose that + clause**：較口語。
- **presume that + clause**：常暗示有一些合理根據。
- **take it for granted that + clause**：固定搭配。
- **be presumed innocent**：法律固定搭配。
- **infer from + evidence/data**：infer from the results that...
- **posit that + clause**：學術理論常見。
- **assumption** 是名詞；**assume** 是動詞。
- 不要把 **assume responsibility / assume office / assume control** 與本課『假設／認為』義項混在一起。
