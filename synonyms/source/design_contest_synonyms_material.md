# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Design contest

**設計比賽、創意挑戰、概念競賽、公開徵件、設計提案競賽——究竟是哪一種 design contest？**

本課聚焦：

**design contest = a competition or challenge in which participants create and submit designs in response to a theme, brief, problem, or set of criteria**

例如：

- a logo design contest
- an architecture design contest
- submit an entry
- competition brief
- judging panel
- winning design

核心近義與近義表達：

**design competition; creative challenge; design challenge; creative competition; design award competition; open call for designs; design brief competition; concept competition; design pitch competition; design challenge event**

> **Precision rule:**  
> `design competition` 最直接。  
> `creative challenge / design challenge` 更強調創意解題。  
> `open call for designs` 更強調公開徵件。  
> `concept competition` 更偏早期概念。  
> `design pitch competition` 更偏簡報／提案。  
> `design award competition` 更強調獎項。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Design competition

**設計比賽；設計競賽**

和 `design contest` 最直接、最中性的近義表達。可用於平面設計、產品設計、建築設計、時裝、UX 等。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The university launched a design competition for a new campus logo.

大學舉辦了一項新校園標誌設計比賽。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- design competition
- international design competition
- student design competition
- open design competition

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否只是一般地指一項設計類比賽，而不需要更特定的創意／提案語氣？

---

## 02 Creative challenge

**創意挑戰；創作挑戰**

當 `design contest` 更強調創意思考、解難或開放式創作，而不只是正式競賽排名時，`creative challenge` 很適合。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The event is a creative challenge for young designers.

這項活動是為年輕設計師而設的創意挑戰。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- creative challenge
- design challenge
- creative problem-solving challenge
- open creative challenge

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否強調創意解題與發想，而非純粹競爭？

---

## 03 Design challenge

**設計挑戰；設計任務**

比 `design competition` 稍少競賽感，常指圍繞明確 brief / problem 的設計任務。可有獎項，也可以只是創意活動。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The design challenge focuses on reducing plastic waste.

這項設計挑戰聚焦減少塑膠廢物。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- design challenge
- student design challenge
- innovation design challenge
- open design challenge

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是以明確問題／brief 為核心的設計任務？

---

## 04 Creative competition

**創意比賽；創作競賽**

較廣泛的 near-synonym，可涵蓋設計、藝術、創作或創新類比賽。若上下文不只限純設計，這個表達更合適。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The festival includes a creative competition for students.

節日活動包括一項學生創意比賽。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- creative competition
- student creative competition
- international creative competition
- creative arts competition

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是一項較廣泛的創意競賽，而設計只是其中核心或主要形式？

---

## 05 Design award competition

**設計獎競賽；設計獎項比賽**

當 `design contest` 的核心是角逐獎項／獎座時可用。它比一般 design competition 更強調 award outcome。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The studio entered an international design award competition.

工作室參加了一項國際設計獎競賽。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- design award competition
- award-winning design
- design awards
- international design award

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否主要是為了評選和頒發設計獎項？

---

## 06 Open call for designs

**公開徵集設計；公開徵件**

當 `design contest` 的重點是向公眾／設計師公開徵集作品，而不一定強調競賽氣氛時，這個表達很實用。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The council issued an open call for designs for the new memorial.

市議會為新紀念碑公開徵集設計。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- open call for designs
- call for entries
- open submission
- design submission call

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否重點在公開徵集作品／方案，而非比賽本身？

---

## 07 Design brief competition

**設計任務書競賽；按設計簡報比賽**

較專業／描述性，指參賽者根據一份 formal design brief 提交方案。常見於建築、產品、品牌與學生設計比賽。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The students entered a design brief competition on sustainable housing.

學生參加了一項以可持續住宅為題的設計簡報競賽。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- design brief competition
- competition brief
- respond to a design brief
- brief-based competition

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否明確有一份 design brief，所有參賽者按同一要求提交方案？

---

## 08 Concept competition

**概念設計競賽；方案概念比賽**

當 `design contest` 主要評審早期概念、創意方向或 vision，而非完整技術設計時，`concept competition` 很合適。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The museum launched a concept competition for its future extension.

博物館為未來擴建舉辦概念設計競賽。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- concept competition
- design concept competition
- architectural concept competition
- concept proposal

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否主要評審初步概念／vision，而不是完成品？

---

## 09 Design pitch competition

**設計提案競賽；設計簡報比賽**

當參賽者不只提交作品，還要向評審 pitch / present 設計概念時，這個表達很合適。常見於創業、產品和學生創新活動。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Teams presented their ideas in a design pitch competition.

各隊在設計提案競賽中展示構思。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- design pitch competition
- pitch event
- design pitch
- presentation-based competition

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否要向評審口頭／視覺 presentation 設計方案？

---

## 10 Design challenge event

**設計挑戰活動；創意設計活動**

較寬泛的 contextual paraphrase，適合短期、活動型、工作坊式或團隊解難式的 contest。競賽性可以有，但活動感更強。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The weekend design challenge event brought together students from five schools.

週末設計挑戰活動集合了五間學校的學生。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- design challenge event
- weekend design challenge
- team design challenge
- innovation event

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是一場短期、活動型、以團隊設計解題為主的比賽？

# 02 False synonyms

以下詞語都和設計活動、評選或徵件有關，但在是否競賽、是否採購、是否展示、是否委約等方面不同。

## 01 Design award

**獎項不等於比賽本身**

`design award` 可以指獎項／獎項制度；contest 是參賽和評選過程。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The competition selects the winner; the award is what the winner receives.

---

## 02 Tender

**招標不等於一般設計比賽**

`tender` 通常與採購、合約、價格和正式投標程序有關。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A tender selects a supplier, while a design competition primarily evaluates design proposals.

---

## 03 Hackathon

**黑客松是特定活動形式**

`hackathon` 通常是短時間團隊開發／創新活動，常涉及科技原型，不等於所有 design contests。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A hackathon may include design, but its format is broader and more intensive.

---

## 04 Exhibition

**展覽不等於競賽**

`exhibition` 展示作品；可以沒有排名、評審或獎項。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The exhibition displayed the entries after the competition.

---

## 05 Portfolio review

**作品集評審不等於設計競賽**

`portfolio review` 是評閱個人作品集，常用於招生、招聘或專業反饋。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A portfolio review evaluates a body of work, not a single contest brief.

---

## 06 Commission

**委約不等於比賽**

`commission` 是直接委託某人完成設計，可完全沒有公開競賽。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The artist received a commission without entering a competition.

---

## 07 Design workshop

**工作坊重點是學習／共創**

`design workshop` 可以產生方案，但通常不是以排名或獎項為核心。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The workshop generated ideas; the competition selected a winner.

---

## 08 Call for proposals

**徵集提案未必是競賽**

`call for proposals` 可能只是收集／評估方案，未必有公開排名或獎項。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A call for proposals can lead to selection without being framed as a contest.

---

# 03 練習與解釋

> **每個核心近義表達 3 題，共 30 題。**
> 每題盡量保留原句句法，只在目標表達需要不同結構時作必要改寫。
> Distractors 優先使用 design award / tender / hackathon / exhibition / portfolio review / commission 等 plausible near-misses。


# Design competition

## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** Design competition · 設計比賽；設計競賽

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

The school is holding a design contest for a new logo.

### 中文翻譯

學校正舉辦新標誌設計比賽。

### OPTIONS


- **A. design competition**

- **B. creative challenge**

- **C. pitch competition**

- **D. design award**

- **E. hackathon**

- **F. exhibition**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**design competition · 設計比賽；設計競賽**

The school is holding a **design competition** for a new logo.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. design competition ✓** — `design competition` 是本題最貼切的改寫；它保留 design contest 的競賽／創意提交核心，並符合 **Design competition** 的特定活動形式。

- **B. creative challenge** — `creative challenge` = 創意挑戰，偏解題與創新，競賽意味可較弱。

- **C. pitch competition** — `pitch competition` = 提案比賽，重點在口頭／視覺簡報。

- **D. design award** — `design award` = 設計獎項或獎項制度，不是比賽過程本身。

- **E. hackathon** — `hackathon` = 短時間團隊創新／技術活動，是特定活動形式。

- **F. exhibition** — `exhibition` = 展覽，展示作品但不一定評獎。

## Design contest vs Creative challenge

---

## Design contest vs Design challenge

---

## Design contest vs Creative competition

後者範圍更廣，可包含設計以外的創作。

---

## Design contest vs Design award competition

後者更強調獎項和榮譽結果。

---

## Design contest vs Open call for designs

後者強調公開徵件，不一定突出競賽氣氛。

---

## Design contest vs Design brief competition

後者強調所有人回應同一 design brief。

---

## Design contest vs Concept competition

後者主要評審初步概念與 vision。

---

## Design contest vs Design pitch competition

後者要求 pitch / presentation。

---

## Design contest vs Design challenge event

後者活動感、團隊協作和短期挑戰感更強。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Contest vs competition

兩者都可：

- design contest
- design competition

`competition` 通常稍正式、國際設計／建築語境尤其常見；
`contest` 稍日常、活動化。

---

## 02 Design challenge

`design challenge` 不一定真的有 winner。

可以是：
- classroom task
- innovation exercise
- team challenge
- formal competition

所以要靠 prize / judging / entries 等線索判斷。

---

## 03 Open call for designs

常見結構：

- issue an open call for designs
- launch a call for entries
- submit a design
- submission deadline

它可以有競賽結果，也可以只是 selection process。

---

## 04 Competition brief

通常包括：

- design problem
- target users
- constraints
- dimensions
- submission format
- judging criteria
- deadline

---

## 05 Concept competition

Concept competition 常只要求：

- sketches
- concept boards
- early visualisations
- design narrative
- broad strategy

未必要求：
- technical drawings
- full costing
- buildable detailing

---

## 06 Design pitch competition

常見：

- pitch to judges
- five-minute presentation
- Q&A
- visual boards
- prototype demonstration

---

## 07 Tender is different

Tender 通常核心是：

- procurement
- supplier selection
- price
- compliance
- contract

設計競賽核心通常是：

- design quality
- originality
- response to brief
- jury evaluation
