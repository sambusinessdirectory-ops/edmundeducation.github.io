# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Solitary

**獨自、孤立、自己一個人、孤單、幽靜、孤零零、隱居、無人陪同、單個——究竟是哪一種 solitary？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**alone; isolated; by oneself; lonely; secluded; lone; reclusive; unaccompanied; single**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **alone; isolated; by oneself** 全部保留。  
> 本課特別區分 `solitary` 的 **純粹獨處、社會／地理隔絕、情緒孤單、地方幽靜、單個物件、長期隱居、正式『無人陪同』** 等不同 senses。

---

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Alone

**獨自的；單獨的**

最直接、最常見的近義詞之一。當 `solitary` 只是表示沒有其他人在身邊、自己一個人時最自然。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

He preferred to walk alone in the evening.

他喜歡晚上獨自散步。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- live alone

- walk alone

- travel alone

- be alone


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

## 02 Isolated

**孤立的；與外界分隔的**

當 `solitary` 不只是『一個人』，還帶有與他人、社會、地理位置或支援網絡分隔的意味時最精確。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The old man lived an isolated life with little contact with others.

那位老人過着孤立的生活，很少與人接觸。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- socially isolated

- geographically isolated

- isolated village

- feel isolated

### EXAMPLE · 例句

He likes eating meals by himself after work.

### EXAMPLE · 例句

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Alone

**最直接，但只描述狀態**

alone 不一定帶寂寞；可以是自願、愉快的獨處。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She enjoys being alone.

---

## 02 Lonely

**情緒詞，不等於 solitary**

lonely 強調寂寞感；一個人即使不 lonely，仍可是 solitary/alone。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

He lives alone but does not feel lonely.

---

## 03 Isolated

**隔絕程度更高**

isolated 常帶社會、地理或功能上的分隔。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The village is isolated in winter.

---

## 04 Secluded

**多用於地方／生活環境**

secluded cottage / beach / garden 很自然；它通常帶安靜、隱蔽感。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

They stayed in a secluded cottage.

---

## 05 Lone

**名詞前高頻**

lone 常見於 `lone traveller / lone figure / lone tree`。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A lone tree stood in the field.

---

## 06 Reclusive

**刻意避免社交**

比 solitary 更強，通常是長期生活方式。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The writer became reclusive.

---

## 07 Solitary / solitude

**詞形辨析**

`solitary` 是形容詞；`solitude` 是名詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

a solitary walk; enjoy solitude

---

## 08 Solitary

**其他專門搭配**

`solitary confinement` 是『單獨囚禁』的固定法律／監獄搭配，語義比一般 alone 強。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

He was held in solitary confinement.

## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** alone · 獨自的；單獨的

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

He preferred solitary walks in the evening.

### 中文翻譯

他喜歡晚上獨自散步。

### OPTIONS


- **A. alone**

- **B. by oneself**

- **C. lone**

- **D. lonely**

- **E. together**

- **F. accompanied**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**alone · 獨自的；單獨的**

He preferred to walk **alone** in the evening.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. alone ✓** — `alone` = 沒有同伴、自己一個人。 **本句為何最合適：** the sentence simply describes being without companions。

- **B. by oneself** — `by oneself` = 自己一個人做某事。 自然描述『自己一個人』做事；不能直接修飾所有名詞位置，需要改寫句型。 **本句線索：** the sentence simply describes being without companions。

- **C. lone** — `lone` = 只有一個、孤零零地存在。 常直接放在名詞前，強調單獨一個；不表情感。 **本句線索：** the sentence simply describes being without companions。

- **D. lonely** — `lonely` = 感到孤單、寂寞。 必須有主觀寂寞感；一個人可以 alone 但完全不 lonely。 **本句線索：** the sentence simply describes being without companions。

- **E. together** — `together` = 一起。 一起，和 alone 相反。 **本句線索：** the sentence simply describes being without companions。

- **F. accompanied** — `accompanied` = 有人陪同。 有人陪同，和 unaccompanied 相反。 **本句線索：** the sentence simply describes being without companions。

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Solitary vs Alone
`alone` 只描述沒有人在身邊，未必不開心。

## Solitary vs Lonely
- alone = 狀態
- lonely = 感受

## Solitary vs Isolated
`isolated` 通常更強：
- socially isolated
- geographically isolated

## Solitary vs Secluded
最自然於地方：
- secluded cottage
- secluded beach
- secluded garden

## Solitary vs Lone
`lone` 特別常放名詞前：
- lone traveller
- lone figure
- lone tree

## Solitary vs Reclusive
`reclusive` 表示主動、長期避開社交。

## Solitary vs Unaccompanied
偏正式：
- unaccompanied child
- unaccompanied traveller

## Solitary vs Single
`single` 只處理數量：
- a single light
- a single house

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Solitary vs solitude
- solitary = adjective
- solitude = noun

例如：
- a solitary walk
- enjoy solitude

## 02 Alone
通常不直接放在普通名詞前：
- a solitary traveller ✓
- an alone traveller ✗
- the traveller was alone ✓

## 03 Lone
最自然作前置形容詞：
- a lone tree
- a lone figure

## 04 By oneself
不是形容詞，因此需要改寫句型：
- She travelled by herself.
- He sat by himself.

## 05 Solitary confinement
固定搭配：
- solitary confinement = 單獨囚禁

這個 sense 比一般 alone 強得多。
