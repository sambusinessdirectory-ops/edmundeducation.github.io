# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Vocational training

「職業訓練／職涯導向教育／實務工作訓練／技能培訓」，究竟是哪一種 `vocational training`？

`vocational training` 是一個廣義概念，可以指為就業與職涯準備的 **career-focused education**、直接練習工作任務的 **practical job training**、針對明確技能的 **skills training**、正式政策語體中的 **occupational training**、較專門的 **technical training**、地區／產業層面的 **workforce training**、特定崗位的 **job-specific training**、技工職業的 **trade training**，以及結合工作與正式學習的 **apprenticeship training**。

> 本課主要處理 `vocational training = education or training designed to prepare people for particular occupations, practical job tasks, trades, technical roles or employment`。
>
> 同義詞數量**不固定**；本課按職涯導向、實務程度、技能粒度、政策語域、技術內容、勞動力層級、職位專屬性與學徒制模式選用 **9 個**替代表達。
>
> Format: **9 curated alternatives + 8 false-synonym contrasts + 3 exercises per target synonym (27 questions total)**.

# 01 同義詞指南


### 01 Career-Focused Education
**以職業為導向的教育；職涯導向教育**

使用者提供。`career-focused education` 強調教育內容直接為某類職業或職涯路徑作準備，比一般 academic education 更貼近 employment outcomes。它不一定只包含 hands-on skills，也可包括專業知識與就業準備。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The college offers career-focused education in healthcare, construction, and digital media.

**中文翻譯**

學院提供醫療、建築和數碼媒體方面的職業導向教育。

**USAGE CLUE**

- education designed around preparation for specific careers or employment pathways

---


### 02 Practical Job Training
**實務工作訓練；實際職能培訓**

使用者提供。`practical job training` 強調實際操作、工作技能與職場任務，特別適合 hands-on practice、equipment、procedures 和 workplace routines。它比 career-focused education 更偏 skills practice。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The course includes practical job training in workshop safety and equipment use.

**中文翻譯**

課程包括工作坊安全和設備使用方面的實務工作訓練。

**USAGE CLUE**

- hands-on training in practical tasks needed for a job

---


### 03 Skills Training
**技能培訓；職能訓練**

較廣。`skills training` 強調獲得特定可操作技能，可以是職業技能、技術技能或工作場所技能。它不一定構成完整 vocational education programme。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The scheme provides skills training in welding and electrical installation.

**中文翻譯**

該計劃提供焊接和電氣安裝方面的技能培訓。

**USAGE CLUE**

- training focused on acquiring specific practical or technical skills

---


### 04 Occupational Training
**職業訓練；職種培訓**

較正式。`occupational training` 強調為某個 occupation 提供專門訓練，常見於政策、勞工市場、公共就業服務和正式報告。它和 vocational training 高度接近，但語體更制度化。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The programme provides occupational training for people entering the care sector.

**中文翻譯**

該計劃為進入照護行業的人士提供職業訓練。

**USAGE CLUE**

- formal training for entry into or progression within a specific occupation

---


### 05 Technical Training
**技術培訓；技術訓練**

當 vocational training 聚焦 machinery、software、engineering procedures、maintenance 或 technical systems 時，可用 `technical training`。它不等於所有 vocational training，因為服務業與一般職場技能未必屬 technical。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Technicians receive technical training before working on the new system.

**中文翻譯**

技術人員在操作新系統前接受技術培訓。

**USAGE CLUE**

- training in technical systems, equipment, procedures or specialised technical knowledge

---


### 06 Workforce Training
**勞動力培訓；職場人才訓練**

較制度化。`workforce training` 著重提升現有或準勞動人口的就業能力、技能和生產力，常見於政府、企業、地區經濟與 workforce-development 語境。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The region is investing in workforce training for advanced manufacturing.

**中文翻譯**

該地區正投資先進製造業的勞動力培訓。

**USAGE CLUE**

- training intended to strengthen the skills and employability of the workforce

---


### 07 Job-Specific Training
**特定職位培訓；職務專項訓練**

較窄。`job-specific training` 指為某個具體職位、職務或崗位所需流程、工具和責任而設的訓練。它比 vocational training 範圍更窄。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> New employees receive job-specific training during their first week.

**中文翻譯**

新員工在第一星期接受特定職位培訓。

**USAGE CLUE**

- training tailored to the tasks and requirements of one particular job

---


### 08 Trade Training
**技工培訓；手藝職業訓練**

較窄。`trade training` 特別適合 plumbing、carpentry、electrical work、welding 等 skilled trades。它不能泛化到所有 vocational education，例如 healthcare administration 或 office work。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The college offers trade training in carpentry and electrical installation.

**中文翻譯**

學院提供木工和電氣安裝方面的技工培訓。

**USAGE CLUE**

- training for skilled manual or technical trades

---


### 09 Apprenticeship Training
**學徒培訓；學徒制訓練**

當 vocational training 透過正式 apprenticeship 模式進行，結合僱主工作場所學習與課堂／技術教學時，可用 `apprenticeship training`。它是 vocational training 的一種特定交付模式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Apprenticeship training combines paid work with structured learning.

**中文翻譯**

學徒培訓把有薪工作與有系統學習結合起來。

**USAGE CLUE**

- structured training delivered through an apprenticeship combining work and study

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 vocational training / career-focused education

`career-focused education` 強調教育與職涯結果的連結；`vocational training` 可以更直接、實務、短期或技能導向。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The college provides career-focused education in healthcare.  
> The centre provides short vocational training courses.

---


## 02 vocational training / skills training

`skills training` 只要求教授特定技能，未必構成完整職業教育路線。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Workers receive skills training on the new software.  
> The college offers a two-year vocational training programme.

---


## 03 vocational training / technical training

`technical training` 只在內容確實屬設備、技術系統、工程程序等 technical domain 時適合。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Engineers receive technical training.  
> Hospitality staff receive vocational training.

---


## 04 vocational training / apprenticeship

`apprenticeship` 是一種制度／職位安排；`apprenticeship training` 是其中的培訓部分。不是所有 vocational training 都透過 apprenticeship 提供。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> She started an apprenticeship with an electrician.  
> Her apprenticeship includes structured training.

---


## 05 vocational training / on-the-job training

`on-the-job training` 明確是在工作場所、工作期間學習；vocational training 也可以在學院、訓練中心或工作坊進行。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> He learned through on-the-job training.  
> She completed vocational training at college.

---


## 06 vocational training / professional development

`professional development` 通常針對已在職人士持續提升專業能力；vocational training 常包含初次進入職業所需的基礎訓練。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Teachers attend professional development.  
> Trainees complete vocational training before employment.

---


## 07 vocational training / academic education

兩者可互補，但概念不同。`academic education` 通常更偏理論、學科知識與學術資格；vocational training 更直接連結職業技能與工作準備。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The course includes academic study.  
> The programme also includes vocational training.

---


## 08 vocational training / work experience

`work experience` 是實際接觸工作環境；它可以沒有結構化教學，因此不等於 vocational training。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Students complete two weeks of work experience.  
> They receive structured vocational training before placement.

# 05 高混淆組

## career-focused education vs practical job training
- **career-focused education**：較廣，重點是職涯／就業導向。
- **practical job training**：更 hands-on，重點是實際工作任務。

## skills training vs job-specific training
- **skills training**：技能可跨多個職位使用。
- **job-specific training**：只針對某一職位。

## occupational training vs workforce training
- **occupational training**：特定 occupation。
- **workforce training**：整體 labour force / sector。

## technical training vs trade training
- **technical training**：任何 technical system/role。
- **trade training**：木工、電工、焊接等 skilled trades。

## trade training vs apprenticeship training
- **trade training**：看職業類型。
- **apprenticeship training**：看 training delivery model。

## vocational training vs on-the-job training
- **vocational training**：可在校內、訓練中心或職場。
- **on-the-job training**：一定在實際工作中學。

## vocational training vs professional development
- **vocational training**：常為進入職業或掌握工作技能。
- **professional development**：通常針對已在職專業人士持續提升。

## vocational training vs work experience
- **vocational training**：有結構化教學／訓練。
- **work experience**：重點是接觸真實工作環境，未必有正式教學。

# 08 生成／評估注意事項

1. **同義詞數量不是固定值。** 本課按職涯導向、實務程度、技能粒度、政策語域、技術內容、勞動力層級、職位專屬性與學徒制模式選 **9 個**。
2. 因此本課有 **3 exercises per target = 27 exercises**。
3. 使用者提供的 `career-focused education` 保留作較廣的 employment-oriented education。
4. 使用者提供的 `practical job training` 保留作 hands-on workplace skill training。
5. `skills training` 比 vocational training 更廣／更碎片化，可能只是一門短課。
6. `occupational training` 是較正式、制度化的 occupation-level 用語。
7. `technical training` 只有 technical systems/equipment/procedures 明確時使用。
8. `workforce training` 只在 labour-market / sector / regional workforce 層面使用。
9. `job-specific training` 比 vocational training 更窄，只針對一個具體職位。
10. `trade training` 只適合 skilled manual/technical trades。
11. `apprenticeship training` 是 delivery model，不代表所有 vocational training。
12. False synonyms 特別測試 apprenticeship、on-the-job training、professional development、academic education、work experience 等相鄰概念。
13. Distractors 全部是真實自然英文表達，不使用人工標籤或不存在詞形。
14. 每個 distractor feedback 都按具體 clue 判斷，例如 employer demand、equipment use、labour market、exact role procedures、paid employment + college study 等。
15. 原始 `vocational training` 句子本身保持自然；較窄或較廣替換只在上下文真正支援時使用。
16. British English 優先；`programme`、`labour market` 等拼法保持英式風格。
17. 品質標準不是湊固定數量，而是每個替代表達都有清楚、可從上下文辨認的獨立語義工作。
