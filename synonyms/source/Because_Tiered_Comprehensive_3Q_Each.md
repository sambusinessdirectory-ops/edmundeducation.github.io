# BECAUSE · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Because

**「因為／由於／既然」，從核心因果連接詞到正式理由表達。**

本單元集中處理 **because = introducing the reason why something happens, is true, or is done**。和名詞類 headword 不同，這一課最重要的是**句法**：有些替代詞可以直接接完整從句（since, as, given that），有些則本身只接名詞（because of, due to, owing to），若要接完整從句必須擴展成 **due to the fact that / owing to the fact that**。

每項均包含 **核心語義、語域、用法提示、常見搭配、搭配結構、與 because 的直接辨析、使用限制、例句及翻譯**；每個目標詞／表達設 **3 道題**，每題六個選項均有逐題、逐選項的情境化解釋。

## Tier 1 — Close causal conjunctions · 核心因果連接詞

### 01 Since

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：因為；既然  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：introduces a reason or explanation for the main clause  
**用法提示**：和 because 最常見的替代詞之一。當原因已知、較自然地作背景資訊時尤其常見。  
**常見搭配**：since it was raining; since you are here; since we already know; since the price is lower; since there was no time.  
**常見搭配結構／對象**：full clause + full clause.  
**與 because 的辨析**：Because 通常更直接回答『為甚麼』；since 常把原因當作已知或背景。  
**使用限制**：Since 也可表示『自從』，要靠語境判斷。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Since the roads were icy, the school opened late.  
因為路面結冰，學校延遲開放。

### 02 As

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：因為；由於  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：introduces a reason, often as background information  
**用法提示**：當原因不是句子最強調部分時很自然，尤其常見於書面語。  
**常見搭配**：as it was late; as the weather was poor; as we had no choice; as everyone agreed; as demand was low.  
**常見搭配結構／對象**：full clause + full clause.  
**與 because 的辨析**：As 的因果力度通常比 because 弱，容易把原因放在背景。  
**使用限制**：As 也可表示『當……時／像……一樣』，有時會產生歧義。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
As the weather was poor, the event was moved indoors.  
由於天氣欠佳，活動移到室內舉行。

### 03 Given that

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：鑑於；考慮到；既然  
**語域**：neutral to formal  
**核心語義**：introduces a fact that should be taken into account as a reason  
**用法提示**：適合把已知事實作為判斷、建議或結果的理由。  
**常見搭配**：given that prices have risen; given that we have limited time; given that demand is high; given that this is the first attempt; given that no evidence was found.  
**常見搭配結構／對象**：fact, constraint, known situation.  
**與 because 的辨析**：Given that 比 because 更有『考慮到這個事實』的推理色彩。  
**使用限制**：不適合非常簡單的日常原因時可能顯得過正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Given that time is limited, we should focus on the main issues.  
鑑於時間有限，我們應集中處理主要問題。

### 04 Owing to the fact that

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：由於……這個事實；因為  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：introduces a reason in a formal, explicit way  
**用法提示**：是 because 的正式長形式，常見於行政、報告或較正式書面語。  
**常見搭配**：owing to the fact that demand fell; owing to the fact that the road was closed; owing to the fact that costs increased; owing to the fact that no staff were available; owing to the fact that the data were incomplete.  
**常見搭配結構／對象**：formal explanation, report, administrative reason.  
**與 because 的辨析**：語義接近 because，但更長、更正式，也常可簡化。  
**使用限制**：日常口語通常過於笨重。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The event was postponed owing to the fact that the venue was unavailable.  
由於場地未能提供，活動延期。

### 05 Due to the fact that

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：由於……這個事實；因為  
**語域**：formal / common in writing  
**核心語義**：introduces a reason by explicitly referring to a fact  
**用法提示**：正式書面中常見，意思和 because 很接近。  
**常見搭配**：due to the fact that costs rose; due to the fact that the system failed; due to the fact that demand changed; due to the fact that the road was blocked; due to the fact that staff were absent.  
**常見搭配結構／對象**：formal explanation, report, official statement.  
**與 because 的辨析**：比 because 更長、更官樣；很多情況可直接改成 because。  
**使用限制**：不要混淆 **due to + noun** 和 **due to the fact that + clause**。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Production slowed due to the fact that several machines were offline.  
由於數部機器停機，生產速度放慢。

## Tier 2 — Broadly usable reason expressions · 廣泛可用的原因表達

### 06 Considering that

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：考慮到；鑑於  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：introduces a fact that influences a judgment or conclusion  
**用法提示**：當 because 的原因帶『考慮這個條件後得出判斷』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：considering that it was late; considering that this is new; considering that prices are high; considering that we had little time; considering that the task was difficult.  
**常見搭配結構／對象**：evaluation, judgment, comparison, recommendation.  
**與 because 的辨析**：Considering that 常把原因變成評價背景；because 更直接。  
**使用限制**：如果只是單純客觀原因，可能不如 because 自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The result was impressive considering that the team had little preparation time.  
考慮到團隊準備時間很少，這個結果很出色。

### 07 Seeing that

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：既然；因為  
**語域**：neutral / conversational  
**核心語義**：introduces an obvious or known reason  
**用法提示**：常用於講者認為原因很明顯、已知或可直接推導時。  
**常見搭配**：seeing that you're here; seeing that it's late; seeing that we agree; seeing that no one objected; seeing that the shop is closed.  
**常見搭配結構／對象**：obvious fact, shared knowledge, immediate situation.  
**與 because 的辨析**：Seeing that 比 because 更有『既然情況如此』的語氣。  
**使用限制**：正式學術寫作中較少使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Seeing that everyone agrees, we can move to the next item.  
既然大家都同意，我們可以進入下一項。

### 08 In view of the fact that

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：鑑於……這個事實  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：introduces a reason that is formally taken into consideration  
**用法提示**：適合法律、行政、政策或正式報告中的因果說明。  
**常見搭配**：in view of the fact that costs increased; in view of the fact that evidence is limited; in view of the fact that demand fell; in view of the fact that conditions changed; in view of the fact that no agreement was reached.  
**常見搭配結構／對象**：policy, legal reasoning, formal decision, official explanation.  
**與 because 的辨析**：比 because 更正式、更偏『考量後決定』。  
**使用限制**：一般會話過於正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
In view of the fact that demand has fallen, the company will revise its forecast.  
鑑於需求下降，公司將修訂預測。

### 09 On the grounds that

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：基於……理由；因為  
**語域**：formal / legal  
**核心語義**：introduces the stated reason or justification for a decision or claim  
**用法提示**：當 because 的原因是正式理由、申請理由、拒絕理由或法律依據時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：rejected on the grounds that; refused on the grounds that; challenged on the grounds that; dismissed on the grounds that; approved on the grounds that.  
**常見搭配結構／對象**：decision, refusal, legal claim, application, objection.  
**與 because 的辨析**：它表示『正式提出的理由／依據』，比 because 更制度化。  
**使用限制**：日常簡單原因不宜使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The application was rejected on the grounds that key documents were missing.  
申請因欠缺重要文件而被拒絕。

### 10 For

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：因為；由於  
**語域**：literary / formal  
**核心語義**：introduces an explanatory reason after an independent clause  
**用法提示**：較文學或正式，常見於『結果／陳述，for + 原因』結構。  
**常見搭配**：we left, for it was late; she stayed, for she was tired; he agreed, for the evidence was clear; they waited, for the road was blocked; I knew, for I had seen it.  
**常見搭配結構／對象**：independent clause + explanatory clause.  
**與 because 的辨析**：For 作因果連接詞通常放在主句後，語氣較書面；because 可放前或後。  
**使用限制**：不能像 because 一樣自由地放在所有句首原因從句中。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
We decided to leave, for the weather was getting worse.  
我們決定離開，因為天氣愈來愈差。

## Tier 3 — Common expressions / stylistic variants · 常用表達與風格變體

### 11 In light of the fact that

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：鑑於……這個事實  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：introduces a fact that changes or supports a decision or interpretation  
**用法提示**：適合報告、政策、正式分析，強調『在這個事實的考量下』。  
**常見搭配**：in light of the fact that circumstances changed; in light of the fact that evidence is incomplete; in light of the fact that demand rose; in light of the fact that costs increased; in light of the fact that the deadline changed.  
**常見搭配結構／對象**：decision, review, policy, interpretation, recommendation.  
**與 because 的辨析**：比 because 更正式，並帶『基於新資訊重新考慮』的味道。  
**使用限制**：日常原因陳述通常顯得冗長。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
In light of the fact that costs have risen, the budget will be reviewed.  
鑑於成本上升，預算將重新檢討。

### 12 Seeing as

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：既然；因為  
**語域**：informal / conversational  
**核心語義**：introduces an obvious or practical reason  
**用法提示**：和 seeing that 接近，但更口語，常見於英式日常英語。  
**常見搭配**：seeing as you're here; seeing as it's raining; seeing as we agree; seeing as nobody objected; seeing as the shop is closed.  
**常見搭配結構／對象**：immediate situation, obvious fact, practical reason.  
**與 because 的辨析**：比 because 更口語，也更有『既然如此』的推論感。  
**使用限制**：正式寫作不建議使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Seeing as it's raining, we may as well stay indoors.  
既然下雨，我們不如留在室內。

### 13 Especially since

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：尤其因為；更何況  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：introduces a reason that is particularly important or strengthens a previous point  
**用法提示**：當 because 的理由需要額外強調時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：especially since it's late; especially since costs are rising; especially since we already agreed; especially since the weather is poor; especially since no alternative exists.  
**常見搭配結構／對象**：supporting reason, strengthened justification.  
**與 because 的辨析**：它不是中性的 because，而是把原因標記為特別重要。  
**使用限制**：只有在要突出該理由時才適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
We should leave early, especially since the roads may be busy.  
我們應該早點離開，尤其因為道路可能繁忙。

### 14 The reason being that

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：原因是；理由在於  
**語域**：neutral to formal  
**核心語義**：explicitly states the reason for a preceding statement  
**用法提示**：常用於補充說明前一句或前一分句的理由，語氣比 because 更解釋性。  
**常見搭配**：the reason being that costs rose; the reason being that time was limited; the reason being that demand fell; the reason being that no one was available; the reason being that conditions changed.  
**常見搭配結構／對象**：explanation after a statement or decision.  
**與 because 的辨析**：Because 直接連接原因；the reason being that 通常作補充說明，句法更重。  
**使用限制**：不能在所有 because 位置直接機械替換。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The meeting was shortened, the reason being that several speakers were absent.  
會議縮短了，原因是幾名講者缺席。

## 04 False synonyms / grammar traps · 容易誤選的詞與句法陷阱

### 01 Because of

**because of 後接名詞，不是完整從句**  
because 後接完整子句；because of 後接名詞、代名詞或名詞片語。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Because it rained, we stayed inside. Because of the rain, we stayed inside.

### 02 Due to

**due to 通常後接名詞片語**  
due to 本身不是 because 的完全句法替代；若後接完整子句，通常要用 due to the fact that。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The delay was due to heavy traffic; we were late because traffic was heavy.

### 03 Owing to

**owing to 通常後接名詞片語**  
owing to + noun；若要接完整子句，可用 owing to the fact that。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The match was cancelled owing to rain.

### 04 Therefore

**因此是結果，不是原因從句連接詞**  
therefore 引出結果；because 引出原因，邏輯方向相反。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
It was raining; therefore, we stayed inside. We stayed inside because it was raining.

### 05 So

**所以引出結果，不是原因**  
so 通常把原因放前、結果放後；because 則引出原因。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
It was late, so we left. We left because it was late.

### 06 Although

**雖然表示讓步，不是因果**  
although 表示『儘管』；不能代替 because。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Although it was raining, we went out.

### 07 If

**如果表示條件，不是原因**  
if 引出條件；because 引出已成立的原因。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
If it rains, we will stay inside.

### 08 While

**while 通常表示同時或對比**  
while 可表示時間或對比；一般不能當作 because 的直接替代。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
While I understand your point, I disagree.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Since · 因為；既然

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

Because the roads were icy, the school opened late.

**中文翻譯**  
因為路面結冰，學校延遲開放。

- A. Since
- B. Therefore
- C. Although
- D. If
- E. So
- F. Because of

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**Since** · 因為；既然

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
Since the roads were icy, the school opened late.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. Since ✓ — 完整原因從句，且把 icy roads 當作背景原因，很自然。**
- **B. Therefore — 引出結果，不是原因從句。**
- **C. Although — 表示讓步，邏輯不同。**
- **D. If — 變成假設條件，而原句原因已成立。**
- **E. So — 通常接結果，不適合放在此句首原因位置。**
- **F. Because of — 後面應接名詞片語，如 because of the icy roads。**

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Because 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 一般背景原因 | **since** | 原因是否已知／只是背景？ |
| 較弱、書面背景原因 | **as** | 是否不想過分強調原因？ |
| 把事實納入考量 | **given that** | 是否帶『鑑於這個事實』的推理？ |
| 正式完整從句原因 | **owing to the fact that** | 是否需要正式行政式說明？ |
| 正式完整從句原因 | **due to the fact that** | 是否需要正式書面改寫？ |
| 評價時的背景條件 | **considering that** | 是否在判斷表現／結果？ |
| 顯而易見的既知理由 | **seeing that** | 是否有『既然如此』語氣？ |
| 正式考量後決定 | **in view of the fact that** | 是否是政策／報告語境？ |
| 正式理由／依據 | **on the grounds that** | 是否是 reject/refuse/dismiss 等正式理由？ |
| 句後文學式解釋 | **for** | 是否是『主句, for + 原因』？ |
| 基於新資訊重新考慮 | **in light of the fact that** | 是否因新情況而調整決定？ |
| 口語『既然』 | **seeing as** | 是否是非正式日常推論？ |
| 特別強調某個理由 | **especially since** | 是否要突出這個原因特別重要？ |
| 補充說明原因 | **the reason being that** | 是否在前句後明確補充『原因是……』？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **because vs since**: because 更直接回答 why；since 常把原因當背景／已知事實。
- **because vs as**: as 的因果力度通常更弱，而且容易與時間／方式義混淆。
- **because vs given that**: given that 帶『把這個事實納入考量』的推理色彩。
- **because vs considering that**: considering that 常出現在評價、判斷或比較語境。
- **because vs seeing that / seeing as**: 兩者更有『既然情況如此』的語氣；seeing as 更口語。
- **because vs for**: for 通常置於主句後，較文學／正式，句法自由度較低。
- **because vs on the grounds that**: 後者是正式理由／依據，尤其常見於法律、申請、拒絕。
- **because vs because of**: because + clause；because of + noun phrase。
- **because vs due to / owing to**: due to / owing to 通常接名詞；完整從句需加 the fact that。
- **because vs therefore / so**: because 引出原因；therefore / so 引出結果，邏輯方向相反。

## 08 Grammar & punctuation traps · 文法與標點陷阱

- **because + clause**：We stayed inside because it was raining。
- **because of + noun phrase**：We stayed inside because of the rain。
- **due to / owing to + noun phrase**：The delay was due to traffic / owing to traffic。
- **due to the fact that / owing to the fact that + clause**：語法正確但較冗長，正式寫作很多時可直接簡化成 because。
- **since** 也有『自從』的時間義：Since I moved here, ...；要靠語境判斷是時間還是原因。
- **as** 也可表示『當……時／像……一樣』，若因果關係重要而可能歧義，because 通常更清楚。
- **for** 作原因連接詞通常前面有逗號，並接一個可獨立成句的解釋：We left, for it was late。
- **therefore** 常用分號／句號連接：It was late; therefore, we left。不要把 therefore 當 because 的從屬連接詞。
- **so**：It was late, so we left；原因在前、結果在後。
- **on the grounds that** 常與 **reject / refuse / dismiss / challenge** 等正式動詞搭配。
- **the reason is because** 在日常英語很常見，但較正式寫作往往偏好 **the reason is that**。
