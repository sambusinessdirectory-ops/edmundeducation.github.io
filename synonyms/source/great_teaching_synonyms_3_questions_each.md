# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Great teaching

**`great teaching` 很自然但較概括。更精準的表達可以突出整體質素、學習成效、教師專業、示範價值或正式表揚。**

核心替代表達：

`excellent instruction / high-quality teaching / effective teaching / outstanding teaching / first-rate instruction / expert instruction / exemplary teaching / superb teaching / strong teaching / skilled instruction`

> **核心原則**：quality → excellent/high-quality/first-rate；results → effective；expertise → expert/skilled；formal praise → outstanding/exemplary；consistent strength → strong teaching.

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Excellent instruction

**優秀的教學；卓越指導**

最直接的替代表達之一，強調講解、指導和教學質素非常高。

---

## 02 High-quality teaching

**高質素教學**

中性而清楚，強調整體教學質素高，包括講解、課堂設計、回饋和支援。

---

## 03 Effective teaching

**有效教學**

強調教學真正改善理解、技能或學習成果，而不只是令人覺得『很好』。

---

## 04 Outstanding teaching

**傑出教學**

語氣很強，常見於獎項、評核和正式表揚。

---

## 05 First-rate instruction

**一流教學；一流指導**

表示教學達到非常高的標準，和 excellent instruction 很接近。

---

## 06 Expert instruction

**專業教學；專家指導**

強調教學者本身具有高度專業知識或技能。

---

## 07 Exemplary teaching

**典範教學**

表示教學好到可作榜樣，特別適合正式評核和教師培訓。

---

## 08 Superb teaching

**極佳教學；出色教學**

非常強的正面評價，但比 exemplary 少正式評核意味。

---

## 09 Strong teaching

**扎實教學；優良教學**

比 outstanding 稍保守，強調穩健、可靠和持續良好。

---

## 10 Skilled instruction

**熟練教學；有技巧的指導**

強調教師具備良好教學技巧，能清楚示範、講解和引導。

---

# 02 False synonyms

- **Great teaching / Excellent instruction** — 非常接近；instruction 稍偏實際講解和指導。
- **Great teaching / High-quality teaching** — 中性、適合學校、政策、評核和教育研究。
- **Great teaching / Effective teaching** — effective 強調學生真的學得更好。
- **Great teaching / Outstanding teaching** — outstanding 語氣更強，常見於獎項和表揚。
- **Great teaching / Expert instruction** — 重點在教師／導師的專業知識。
- **Great teaching / Exemplary teaching** — exemplary = 好到可以作榜樣。
- **Great teaching / Strong teaching** — strong 較保守，偏穩健、可靠。
- **Great teaching / Academic support** — academic support 是支援服務，不等於 teaching 本身。

## 練習 01.1

**Target: Excellent instruction · 優秀的教學；卓越指導**

> The students praised the great teaching they received in the laboratory.

**中文翻譯**

學生讚揚他們在實驗室接受的優秀教學。

|---|---|
| A | High-quality teaching |
| B | Effective teaching |
| C | Excellent instruction |
| D | Good teaching |
| E | Popular teaching |
| F | Academic support |

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**C. Excellent instruction**

> The students praised the excellent instruction they received in the laboratory.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. High-quality teaching**  
`High-quality teaching`：中性而清楚，強調整體教學質素高，包括講解、課堂設計、回饋和支援。 本句線索是「整體教學質素非常高」，但它所強調的整體質素、實際成效、專業來源、正式表揚或示範價值與本句不完全相同。

**B. Effective teaching**  
`Effective teaching`：強調教學真正改善理解、技能或學習成果，而不只是令人覺得『很好』。 本句線索是「整體教學質素非常高」，但它所強調的整體質素、實際成效、專業來源、正式表揚或示範價值與本句不完全相同。

**C. Excellent instruction ✓**  
本句線索是 **整體教學質素非常高**；`Excellent instruction` 正好對應這種教學質素、成效、專業程度或正式評價。

**D. Good teaching**  
`Good teaching`：良好教學；程度比 great / excellent 稍弱。 本句線索是「整體教學質素非常高」，因此不是最精準的 great teaching 替代表達。

**E. Popular teaching**  
`Popular teaching`：受歡迎的教學；受歡迎不等於質素高。 本句線索是「整體教學質素非常高」，因此不是最精準的 great teaching 替代表達。

**F. Academic support**  
`Academic support`：學術支援；可輔助教學，但不等於 teaching 本身。 本句線索是「整體教學質素非常高」，因此不是最精準的 great teaching 替代表達。

# 04 Target-phrase contrast map

| 重點 | 較精準表達 |
|---|---|
| 直接說優秀教學／指導 | **excellent instruction** |
| 整體教學質素高 | **high-quality teaching** |
| 實際改善學習成果 | **effective teaching** |
| 高度正式表揚 | **outstanding teaching** |
| 一流標準 | **first-rate instruction** |
| 由專業人士提供 | **expert instruction** |
| 好到可以作榜樣 | **exemplary teaching** |
| 非常出色、強烈讚賞 | **superb teaching** |
| 穩健、可靠、扎實 | **strong teaching** |
| 強調教學技巧 | **skilled instruction** |

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Excellent instruction vs High-quality teaching** — 前者稍偏實際指導；後者偏整體質素。
- **High-quality vs Effective teaching** — high-quality 是質素評價；effective 是結果導向。
- **Outstanding vs Superb teaching** — 都很強；outstanding 更常見於正式表揚。
- **Outstanding vs Exemplary teaching** — exemplary 特別有「可作模範」意味。
- **Expert vs Skilled instruction** — expert 偏專業知識；skilled 偏教學技巧。
- **Strong vs Outstanding teaching** — strong 較保守，偏穩健可靠。
- **First-rate vs Excellent instruction** — 很接近；first-rate 評價語氣更強。

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 直接說優秀教學？ → **excellent instruction**
2. 整體高質素？ → **high-quality teaching**
3. 強調學生真的學得更好？ → **effective teaching**
4. 正式高度表揚？ → **outstanding teaching**
5. 一流標準？ → **first-rate instruction**
6. 由專家提供？ → **expert instruction**
7. 可作榜樣？ → **exemplary teaching**
8. 強烈讚賞「非常出色」？ → **superb teaching**
9. 想說穩健扎實？ → **strong teaching**
10. 強調教學技巧熟練？ → **skilled instruction**

# 10 Final note

核心替代表達：

`excellent instruction / high-quality teaching / effective teaching / outstanding teaching / first-rate instruction / expert instruction / exemplary teaching / superb teaching / strong teaching / skilled instruction`

最重要的 near-miss：

`good teaching / popular teaching / interesting teaching / academic support`

尤其要記住：

> **great teaching** = 廣義高度正面評價  
> **effective teaching** = 強調學習結果  
> **expert instruction** = 強調教師專業  
> **exemplary teaching** = 好到可以作模範
