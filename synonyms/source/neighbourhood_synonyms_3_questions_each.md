# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Neighbourhood (noun)

**`neighbourhood` 可以指住所附近的一帶地方、城市內某個區、居住在當地的人、某地鄰近範圍、具特色的城區、住宅區、事件／故事的地點，或某地周邊環境。不同替代表達的核心差別，是『local area / district / community / vicinity / quarter / residential area / locale / surroundings』。**

核心替代表達：

`local area / district / community / vicinity / quarter / residential area / locale / surroundings`

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Local area

**本地區域；附近一帶**

當 `neighbourhood` 只是泛指住所或某地附近的一帶地方，而不特別強調行政邊界或居民群體時，`local area` 很自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> There are several cafés in the local area.

附近一帶有幾間咖啡店。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **the nearby area around a place**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 02 District

**地區；區**

當 `neighbourhood` 指城市內較明確、有名稱或行政／功能界線的一個區域時，`district` 很合適。它通常比 neighbourhood 範圍更正式、更大。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The museum is located in the historic district.

博物館位於歷史區。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a defined area within a town or city**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、城市規劃、一般

---

## 03 Community

**社區；社群**

當 `neighbourhood` 的重點不是地方本身，而是居住在當地的人及其互動關係時，`community` 很精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The community organised a weekend clean-up.

社區居民組織了週末清潔活動。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **the people living in an area and their social connections**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 04 Vicinity

**附近地區；鄰近一帶**

較正式。當 `neighbourhood` 指某個地點附近的一帶區域時，`vicinity` 很自然，尤其常見於 `in the vicinity of`。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Several hotels are located in the vicinity of the station.

車站附近一帶有幾間酒店。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **the area immediately surrounding a particular place**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、書面

---

## 05 Quarter

**城區；某類型地區**

當 `neighbourhood` 指城市中具有特定歷史、文化或功能特色的一區時，`quarter` 很自然，例如 French Quarter, financial quarter。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> They stayed in the old quarter of the city.

他們住在城市的舊城區。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a distinct part of a city, often with a particular character**

**REGISTER · 語域**

書面、旅遊、城市描述

---

## 06 Residential area

**住宅區**

當 `neighbourhood` 特別指以住宅為主、居民生活為核心的地區時，`residential area` 很精準。它是功能性描述，而不是所有 neighbourhood 都可這樣替換。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The school is in a quiet residential area.

學校位於一個安靜的住宅區。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **an area mainly containing homes**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域、城市規劃

---

## 07 Locale

**地點；地區**

較正式或書面。當 `neighbourhood` 指某件事發生的地方或某個具特定環境特色的地點時，`locale` 可作近義改寫。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The seaside town was chosen as the locale for the film.

這個海邊小鎮被選作電影拍攝地點。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a place or setting associated with an activity or event**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、書面

---

## 08 Surroundings

**周邊環境；附近一帶**

當 `neighbourhood` 的重點是某地周圍的環境、景物和整體氛圍時，`surroundings` 很合適。它通常用複數。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The hotel has peaceful surroundings.

酒店周邊環境寧靜。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **the area and environment around a particular place**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

# 02 False synonyms

## Neighbourhood / Local area
`local area` 最中性，泛指某地附近一帶，不強調行政界線或居民群體。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
There are several cafés in the neighbourhood.  
There are several cafés in the local area.

## Neighbourhood / District
`district` 通常較正式、範圍較明確，可有行政、歷史或功能特色。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The neighbourhood is known for historic buildings.  
The district is known for historic buildings.

## Neighbourhood / Community
`community` 重點是「人」，不是地方本身。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The neighbourhood organised a clean-up.  
The community organised a clean-up.

## Neighbourhood / Vicinity
`vicinity` 指某個具體地點附近的一帶，常見 `in the vicinity of`。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
There are hotels in the neighbourhood of the station.  
There are hotels in the vicinity of the station.

## Neighbourhood / Quarter
`quarter` 常指具有歷史、文化或功能特色的城市區域。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
They stayed in the old neighbourhood.  
They stayed in the old quarter of the city.

## Neighbourhood / Residential area
`residential area` 特別按土地用途描述住宅為主的地區。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The school is in a quiet neighbourhood.  
The school is in a quiet residential area.

## Neighbourhood / Locale
`locale` 較書面，常把地方視為事件、故事、拍攝或活動的 setting。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The neighbourhood was used for filming.  
The locale was chosen for the television drama.

## Neighbourhood / Surroundings
`surroundings` 重點是某地周圍的環境、景物和氣氛，而且通常用複數。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The hotel is in a pleasant neighbourhood.  
The hotel has pleasant surroundings.

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Local area vs District** — local area 最泛；district 更正式、有較明確邊界或功能。
- **Neighbourhood vs Community** — neighbourhood 可指地方；community 更強調居民。
- **Vicinity vs Local area** — vicinity 更緊貼某個具體地標／地點。
- **Quarter vs District** — quarter 更有歷史、文化或風格特色；district 更中性、行政化。
- **Residential area vs Neighbourhood** — residential area 特別按土地用途定義。
- **Locale vs Neighbourhood** — locale 把地方視為 setting，不一定是居民生活區。
- **Surroundings vs Neighbourhood** — surroundings 是周邊環境，通常用複數。
- **Neighbourhood ≠ suburb** — suburb 是市郊地區；neighbourhood 可以在市中心。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

- 英式／港式拼法：**neighbourhood**；美式：**neighborhood**
- **in the neighbourhood / in the local area / in the district**
- **the community** 可作單數集合名詞
- 固定搭配：**in the vicinity of + place**
- **quarter** 可指城區，但也有「四分之一」等其他意思
- **residential** = adjective；**residence/residency** 不等於 residential
- **locale** = 地點；不要混淆 **local** = 本地的
- **surroundings** 通常是複數：`The surroundings are pleasant.`

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 只是泛指附近一帶？ → **local area**
2. 是較明確的城市區域？ → **district**
3. 重點是居民／社群？ → **community**
4. 是某個地標附近一帶？ → **vicinity**
5. 是有歷史／文化特色的城區？ → **quarter**
6. 是住宅用途為主？ → **residential area**
7. 是活動／故事／拍攝的 setting？ → **locale**
8. 是周圍環境、景觀和氣氛？ → **surroundings**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`local area / district / community / vicinity / quarter / residential area / locale / surroundings`

最重要的一點：

> **neighbourhood ≠ community ≠ surroundings**

`neighbourhood` = 地方／附近一帶；  
`community` = 居民群體；  
`surroundings` = 周邊環境。
