# RECRUITING · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Recruiting

**「招聘／招募」在 HR 語境中不是單一步驟。** 要先分清：一般 hiring、完整 recruitment process、整體 staffing、長期 talent acquisition，以及只屬前段的 talent/candidate sourcing。

本單元集中處理 **recruiting = the process of attracting, finding, assessing, and bringing people into roles**。你提供的 **hiring; staffing; talent-sourcing** 分別代表 **一般招聘／僱用、整體人手配置、前段人才物色** 三個重要方向。

本次保留 **10 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達。會特別標明哪些是完整 recruitment 的近義詞，哪些只是子流程或更宏觀的 workforce concept，避免把 sourcing / staffing / onboarding 混成同一件事。共 **30 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Recruiting 的語境 | 核心問題 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|---|
| 一般招聘／僱用 | 是否在找人並把人聘入職位？ | hiring, recruitment, employee recruitment |
| 長期策略人才管理 | 是否涉及人才管道、僱主品牌、未來技能？ | talent acquisition |
| 整體人手配置 | 是否是 headcount / coverage / staffing levels？ | staffing |
| 前段尋找候選人 | 是否只是在找 potential candidates？ | talent sourcing, candidate sourcing |
| 特定招聘方式 | 是否針對資深人才或大規模 campaign？ | headhunting, recruitment drive |
| 宏觀 workforce 層面 | 是否 sector/organisation workforce planning？ | workforce recruitment |

## Tier 1 — Core hiring / recruitment equivalents · 核心招聘與僱用近義詞

### 01 Hiring

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：招聘；僱用  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：the process of finding and appointing people to jobs, especially the final act of taking them on as employees  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **recruiting** 最直接、最常見的近義詞之一，尤其適合公司一般招聘語境。  
**常見搭配**：hiring process; hiring manager; hiring decision; hiring plans; hiring new staff.  
**常見搭配對象**：employee, staff, worker, manager, candidate.  
**與 recruiting 的辨析**：Hiring 和 recruiting 高度重疊，但 hiring 更容易包含最終僱用決定；recruiting 更常涵蓋吸引、尋找和篩選候選人的整個前段流程。  
**使用限制**：如果只是尋找候選人、尚未進入正式錄用階段，talent sourcing / candidate sourcing 更精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The company is hiring additional engineers for its new office.  
公司正為新辦公室招聘更多工程師。

### 02 Recruitment

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：招聘；招募  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：the organised process of attracting, identifying, assessing, and appointing people to roles  
**用法提示**：這是 recruiting 的名詞形式，也是正式人力資源語境中最核心的對應詞。  
**常見搭配**：recruitment process; recruitment campaign; recruitment team; staff recruitment; recruitment strategy.  
**常見搭配對象**：candidate, employee, graduate, staff, role.  
**與 recruiting 的辨析**：Recruitment 是名詞，recruiting 可作動名詞／現在分詞；語義本身高度接近。  
**使用限制**：句法位置要留意：不能把 recruitment 直接放進需要現在分詞的所有位置。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The recruitment process includes interviews and reference checks.  
招聘流程包括面試和背景查核。

### 03 Employee recruitment

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：員工招聘；僱員招募  
**語域**：formal / HR  
**核心語義**：the process of recruiting people specifically to become employees of an organisation  
**用法提示**：當 recruiting 需要明確排除志願者、會員或學生招募，而只談僱員時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：employee recruitment; recruitment of employees; employee recruitment strategy; employee recruitment process.  
**常見搭配對象**：employee, company, organisation, vacancy, role.  
**與 recruiting 的辨析**：比 general recruiting 更窄，明確指 employment。  
**使用限制**：志願者、研究參加者、會員等招募不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Employee recruitment has become more difficult in specialised technical fields.  
在專門技術領域，員工招聘變得更困難。

### 04 Talent acquisition

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：人才招募；人才獲取  
**語域**：HR / corporate  
**核心語義**：a strategic, often long-term approach to identifying, attracting, and hiring people with needed skills  
**用法提示**：當 recruiting 不只是填補眼前空缺，而是長期建立人才渠道、僱主品牌和關鍵技能儲備時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：talent acquisition team; talent acquisition strategy; talent acquisition specialist; acquisition of talent.  
**常見搭配對象**：specialist, graduate, leadership role, critical skill, workforce.  
**與 recruiting 的辨析**：Talent acquisition 通常比 recruiting 更策略性、長期化。  
**使用限制**：小型一次性招聘或單純刊登職位未必需要這個術語。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The firm expanded its talent acquisition team to support long-term growth.  
公司擴大人才招募團隊，以支援長期增長。

## Tier 2 — Staffing / sourcing / specialist-recruitment variants · 人手配置、尋源與專門招聘變體

### 05 Staffing

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：人手配置；招聘補員  
**語域**：neutral / HR / operations  
**核心語義**：the process of providing an organisation, department, or service with the people needed to operate  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，但 **staffing** 比 recruiting 更廣：它可包括招聘、調配、人手編制和補缺。  
**常見搭配**：staffing levels; staffing needs; staffing agency; staffing plan; staffing shortages.  
**常見搭配對象**：department, service, shift, project, organisation.  
**與 recruiting 的辨析**：Recruiting 是找人；staffing 是確保有足夠合適人手。  
**使用限制**：如果句子只聚焦尋找候選人，不宜把所有 recruiting 都換成 staffing。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The hospital is reviewing staffing needs before opening the new ward.  
醫院在開設新病房前正檢討人手需要。

### 06 Talent sourcing

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：人才尋源；人才物色  
**語域**：HR / recruitment  
**核心語義**：the process of identifying and finding potential candidates, often before they formally apply  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 recruiting 的重要前段子流程，尤其適合主動搜尋合適人才。  
**常見搭配**：talent sourcing; sourcing strategy; sourcing candidates; talent pipeline; sourcing channels.  
**常見搭配對象**：candidate, specialist, engineer, executive, talent pool.  
**與 recruiting 的辨析**：Talent sourcing 只處理『找出潛在人才』，通常不包括完整面試、甄選和錄用。  
**使用限制**：不是整個 recruiting process 的全面同義詞。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The team uses professional networks for talent sourcing in specialist roles.  
團隊利用專業網絡物色專門職位的人才。

### 07 Candidate sourcing

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：候選人尋源；物色候選人  
**語域**：HR / recruitment  
**核心語義**：the process of finding potential job candidates through databases, networks, referrals, or direct search  
**用法提示**：當 recruiting 的語境明確聚焦搜尋候選人名單時，比 general recruiting 更精準。  
**常見搭配**：candidate sourcing; sourcing candidates; sourcing channel; candidate pool; candidate database.  
**常見搭配對象**：candidate, vacancy, database, referral, network.  
**與 recruiting 的辨析**：和 talent sourcing 很接近，但 candidate sourcing 更直接聚焦某個 vacancy 的候選人。  
**使用限制**：不涵蓋完整錄用流程。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Candidate sourcing began before the vacancy was formally advertised.  
職位正式刊登前，候選人物色工作已經開始。

### 08 Headhunting

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：獵頭招聘；主動挖角  
**語域**：neutral / business  
**核心語義**：the practice of approaching specific experienced or senior people directly for jobs  
**用法提示**：當 recruiting 是主動接觸已有職位、具專業經驗或高級人才時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：headhunting firm; executive headhunting; headhunt a candidate; headhunting service; senior appointment.  
**常見搭配對象**：executive, specialist, manager, candidate, competitor.  
**與 recruiting 的辨析**：Headhunting 是 recruiting 的特定策略，主動、針對性高，常見於 senior/specialist roles。  
**使用限制**：一般大量招聘、畢業生招聘或入門職位通常不叫 headhunting。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The company used headhunting to fill the senior leadership role.  
公司透過獵頭方式招聘高級領導職位。

## Tier 3 — Campaign / workforce-level variants · 招聘行動與宏觀人力層級變體

### 09 Recruitment drive

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：招聘行動；集中招聘活動  
**語域**：neutral / organisational  
**核心語義**：an organised campaign to recruit a relatively large number of people within a particular period  
**用法提示**：當 recruiting 是一段時間內集中、大規模招人時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：recruitment drive; launch a recruitment drive; nationwide recruitment drive; seasonal recruitment drive.  
**常見搭配對象**：staff, nurse, teacher, graduate, volunteer.  
**與 recruiting 的辨析**：Recruitment drive 是一次 campaign/event-like effort，不是日常持續 recruiting 的全面同義詞。  
**使用限制**：單一職位或零散招聘通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The airline launched a recruitment drive for cabin crew.  
航空公司展開空中服務員招聘行動。

### 10 Workforce recruitment

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：勞動力招聘；人手招募  
**語域**：formal / policy / workforce planning  
**核心語義**：recruitment considered at the level of an organisation's or sector's overall workforce needs  
**用法提示**：當 recruiting 出現在人力規劃、公共政策、行業缺工或大型組織層面時可用。  
**常見搭配**：workforce recruitment; workforce planning; recruitment needs; workforce shortage; workforce strategy.  
**常見搭配對象**：sector, organisation, workforce, profession, service.  
**與 recruiting 的辨析**：比 general recruiting 更宏觀，通常和 workforce planning / shortages 一起出現。  
**使用限制**：不適合描述單一候選人或單一職位的招聘。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Workforce recruitment remains a priority in the healthcare sector.  
勞動力招聘仍是醫療界的重要工作。

## 04 False synonyms / recruiting traps · 容易誤選的招聘流程與 HR 陷阱

### 01 Onboarding

**入職 ≠ recruiting**  
onboarding 是候選人接受職位後的入職與融入流程。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Onboarding happens after recruitment and hiring.

### 02 Selection

**甄選 ≠ recruiting**  
selection 是從候選人中挑選合適人選，只是招聘流程的一部分。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Selection is one stage of recruitment.

### 03 Interviewing

**面試 ≠ recruiting**  
interviewing 是評估候選人的其中一種方法。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Interviewing is only one recruitment activity.

### 04 Advertising

**刊登職位 ≠ recruiting**  
advertising vacancies 是吸引候選人的一個渠道，不等於整個 recruiting。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Job advertising is only one sourcing channel.

### 05 Staffing

**只在整體人手配置 sense 接近**  
staffing 包括補員、調配和人手編制，比 recruiting 更廣。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Staffing is broader than recruiting.

### 06 Talent sourcing

**只在尋找候選人階段接近**  
sourcing 通常發生在甄選與錄用之前。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Sourcing is an early recruitment stage.

### 07 Headhunting

**只在主動挖角 sense 接近**  
headhunting 是針對特定資深／專門人才的招聘策略。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Headhunting is a specialised recruitment method.

### 08 Recruit / recruitment / recruiting

**詞形陷阱**  
recruit 可作動詞／名詞；recruitment 是名詞；recruiting 是動名詞／現在分詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check word class and sentence structure.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Hiring · 招聘；僱用

- A. Hiring
- B. Recruitment
- C. Staffing
- D. Selection
- E. Onboarding
- F. Advertising

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Hiring** · 招聘；僱用

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Hiring ✓** — means finding and taking people on as employees; it often highlights the employment decision more than early candidate attraction. In this sentence, general employment recruitment including taking people on is naturally expressed as hiring. That makes **Hiring** the most precise replacement for this particular sense of *recruiting*.
- **B.** **Recruitment** — is the formal noun for the organised process of attracting, assessing and appointing people. It is genuine HR vocabulary, but it does not match the decisive clue here: general employment recruitment including taking people on is naturally expressed as hiring.
- **C.** **Staffing** — means ensuring an organisation has enough appropriate people and can include recruitment, allocation and headcount planning. It is genuine HR vocabulary, but it does not match the decisive clue here: general employment recruitment including taking people on is naturally expressed as hiring.
- **D.** **Selection** — is the stage of choosing among candidates rather than the whole recruitment process. It is genuine HR vocabulary, but it does not match the decisive clue here: general employment recruitment including taking people on is naturally expressed as hiring.
- **E.** **Onboarding** — is the post-hire process of introducing a new employee to the organisation. It is genuine HR vocabulary, but it does not match the decisive clue here: general employment recruitment including taking people on is naturally expressed as hiring.
- **F.** **Advertising** — means publicising vacancies and is only one recruitment channel. It is genuine HR vocabulary, but it does not match the decisive clue here: general employment recruitment including taking people on is naturally expressed as hiring.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **recruiting vs hiring**: hiring 更容易涵蓋最終僱用決定；recruiting 常包括前段吸引和篩選。
- **recruiting vs recruitment**: 語義高度接近，但詞性不同。
- **recruiting vs staffing**: staffing 更廣，包括人手編制、調配和招聘。
- **recruiting vs talent acquisition**: talent acquisition 更策略性、長期化。
- **recruiting vs talent sourcing**: sourcing 只處理前段尋找潛在人才。
- **recruiting vs candidate sourcing**: candidate sourcing 更針對某空缺建立候選人池。
- **recruiting vs headhunting**: headhunting 是主動接觸資深／專門人才的策略。
- **recruiting vs recruitment drive**: recruitment drive 是一段時間內集中、大規模招聘。
- **recruiting vs onboarding**: onboarding 發生在聘用之後，不是招聘。
- **recruiting vs selection**: selection 只是從候選人中挑選人選的一個階段。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & HR-process traps · 文法、搭配與招聘流程陷阱

- **recruiting** 可作動名詞／現在分詞：Recruiting engineers is difficult / We are recruiting engineers。
- **recruitment** 是名詞：the recruitment process / recruitment strategy。
- **hiring** 既可作動名詞，也常作名詞修飾語：hiring manager / hiring decision。
- **staffing** 常與 **levels / needs / shortages / plan** 搭配。
- **talent acquisition** 是策略性 HR 術語，通常比單次 hiring 範圍更廣。
- **talent sourcing / candidate sourcing** 都屬招聘前段，重點是找人，不是最終錄用。
- **headhunting** 常見於 senior / executive / specialist roles。
- **recruitment drive** 指 campaign-like concentrated hiring。
- **onboarding** 在 offer accepted / hire completed 之後。
- **selection / interviewing / screening / advertising** 都只是 recruiting 流程的一部分。
- 英式／香港拼寫保持 **organisation, programme**；HR 術語如 recruitment / staffing / sourcing 沒有英美拼寫差異。
