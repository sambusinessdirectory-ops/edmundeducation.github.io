# Challenging

**困難、要求高、考驗人、艱難、艱巨、費神、嚴格、複雜、令人望而生畏——究竟是哪一種 challenging？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**difficult; demanding; testing; tough; arduous; taxing; rigorous; complex; formidable**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **difficult; demanding; testing** 全部保留。  
> 本課特別分開 `challenging` 的 **一般困難、高投入要求、耐性／能力考驗、口語艱難、長時間艱苦、能量消耗、高標準、複雜性、令人望而生畏的難度**。

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Difficult

**困難的；難做的**

最直接、最廣泛的近義詞之一。當 `challenging` 只是表示某項工作、問題、課程或情況不容易完成或處理時，`difficult` 最安全。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The final question was difficult for most students.

最後一題對大部分學生來說很困難。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- difficult question

- difficult task

- difficult situation

- difficult decision

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Difficult

**最廣泛**

只表示難，不說明為何難。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The final question was difficult.

---

## 02 Demanding

**投入要求高**

常見於 job, course, role, schedule, caregiving。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The role is demanding but rewarding.

---

## 03 Testing

**英式高頻：考驗人的**

常見於 patience, resilience, ability, difficult periods。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

It was a testing week.

---

## 04 Taxing

**消耗很大**

可說 mentally taxing, physically taxing, emotionally taxing。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The exam was mentally taxing.

---

## 05 Rigorous

**嚴格不等於一般困難**

常見於 training, academic programme, testing, methodology。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The programme is rigorous.

---

## 06 Complex

**複雜不等於費力**

一件事可以 complex 但不一定 physically demanding。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The legal case is complex.

---

## 07 Challenging / challenged

**詞形辨析**

`challenging` 描述事情有挑戰；`challenged` 通常不是一般『感到有挑戰』的自然替換。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

a challenging task

---

## 08 Challenging

**可帶正面色彩**

challenging 常暗示雖難但值得投入；difficult 本身較中性。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The course is challenging but rewarding.

> **每個核心近義表達 3 題，共 27 題。**
> 每題均以 **困難來源、投入強度、是否考驗耐性／能力、是否屬長期艱苦、是否因複雜性、是否因標準嚴格** 作辨析。


## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** difficult · 困難的；難做的

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

### 中文翻譯

最後一題對大部分學生來說很困難。

### OPTIONS


- **A. difficult**

- **B. demanding**

- **C. complex**

- **D. tough**

- **E. easy**

- **F. straightforward**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**difficult · 困難的；難做的**

The final question was **difficult** for most students.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. difficult ✓** — `difficult` = 不容易完成、理解或處理。 **本句為何最合適：** the sentence simply indicates that the question was hard to answer。

- **B. demanding** — `demanding` = 需要大量時間、精力、技能或注意力。 強調需要大量投入；如果只是邏輯上複雜但不耗時耗力，complex 更合適。 **本句線索：** the sentence simply indicates that the question was hard to answer。

- **C. complex** — `complex` = 由多個部分／因素構成而難處理。 困難來源在多因素／多部分；高體力消耗的任務不一定 complex。 **本句線索：** the sentence simply indicates that the question was hard to answer。

- **D. tough** — `tough` = 口語：艱難、難對付。 自然口語且範圍廣；正式學術標準或長期艱苦過程可用更精確詞。 **本句線索：** the sentence simply indicates that the question was hard to answer。

- **E. easy** — `easy` = 容易。 和 challenging 相反。 **本句線索：** the sentence simply indicates that the question was hard to answer。

- **F. straightforward** — `straightforward` = 直接、容易理解。 表示簡單直接，和 challenging 相反。 **本句線索：** the sentence simply indicates that the question was hard to answer。

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Challenging vs Difficult
`difficult` 最廣，不說明困難來源。

## Challenging vs Demanding
`demanding` 特別自然於：
- job
- course
- schedule
- role
- caregiving

## Challenging vs Testing
特別適合：
- patience
- resilience
- judgement
- difficult periods

## Challenging vs Arduous
`arduous` 需要：
- long duration
- sustained effort
- physical or mental strain

## Challenging vs Taxing
核心是 **draining**：
- mentally taxing
- physically taxing
- emotionally taxing

## Challenging vs Rigorous
核心是：
- strict standards
- disciplined process
- intensive training/testing

## Challenging vs Complex
核心是：
- many parts
- interacting factors
- complicated structure

## Challenging vs Formidable
語氣更強：
- opponent
- obstacle
- large-scale task

- challenging yet enjoyable

常修飾：
- job

- course
- role

英式英語特別自然：
- a testing week
- testing conditions
- a testing experience

## 05 Challenging can be positive
`challenging` 常暗示：
**difficult, but worthwhile / stimulating**

而 `difficult` 本身較中性。
