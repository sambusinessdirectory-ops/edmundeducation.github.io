# MOST OF THE TIME · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Most of the time · phrase

**Most of the time = on most occasions / during most of a period / in the majority of ordinary situations。** 替換時要先分清句子是在談 **personal routine, general tendency, normal circumstances, typical pattern, ordinary procedure, established rule, majority outcome, or overall condition across a period**。

你提供的 **usually; generally** 正好代表兩個核心方向：**大多數時候** 與 **一般而言**。

本次保留 **8 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達，共 **24 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。特別區分 **often**：它只是『經常』，不一定強到『大多數時候』。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Most of the time 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 |
|---|---|
| 個人習慣／一般高頻 | **usually** |
| 整體趨勢／一般而言 | **generally** |
| 正常情況／正常程序 | **normally** |
| 典型模式／特徵 | **typically** |
| 一般情況下（較正式） | **ordinarily** |
| 按慣例／作為一般規則 | **as a rule** |
| 多數結果／多半如此 | **more often than not** |
| 整體一段時間大致如此 | **for the most part** |

## Tier 1 — Core frequency / normal-pattern equivalents · 核心高頻與常態近義表達

### 01 Usually

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：通常；大多數時候  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：on most occasions or in most situations  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **most of the time** 最直接、最自然的單字替代之一。  
**常見搭配**：usually happens; usually works; usually arrives; usually means; usually takes.  
**常見搭配對象**：routine, habit, event, outcome, behaviour.  
**與 most of the time 的辨析**：Usually 幾乎直接對應『大多數時候』，但語法位置通常放在主要動詞前、be 動詞後。  
**使用限制**：若要強調『作為一般規則』或『大致上』，as a rule / for the most part 可能更貼切。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
I usually take the MTR to work.  
我通常乘港鐵上班。

### 02 Generally

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：一般而言；通常  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：in most cases, as a general rule, or broadly speaking  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，特別適合概括一般情況、趨勢或常態。  
**常見搭配**：generally works; generally accepted; generally true; generally speaking.  
**常見搭配對象**：rule, pattern, tendency, statement, behaviour.  
**與 most of the time 的辨析**：Generally 比 usually 更有『概括整體』的意味，不只是單純頻率。  
**使用限制**：個人日常習慣用 usually 往往更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The system generally works well under normal conditions.  
在正常情況下，這個系統一般運作良好。

### 03 Normally

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：通常；正常情況下  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：in the usual or expected way, or under normal circumstances  
**用法提示**：當 **most of the time** 帶『平常／正常情況下』的意味時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：normally takes; normally works; normally open; normally expect.  
**常見搭配對象**：routine, schedule, operation, procedure, behaviour.  
**與 most of the time 的辨析**：Normally 多了一層『正常狀態／正常程序』，而 most of the time 只需表示頻率高。  
**使用限制**：若事情本身沒有 normal/abnormal 對比，usually 可能更中性。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The journey normally takes about forty minutes.  
這段路程通常需時約四十分鐘。

### 04 Typically

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：通常；典型地  
**語域**：neutral / analytical  
**核心語義**：in a way that is characteristic of what usually happens or is expected  
**用法提示**：當 **most of the time** 用來描述典型模式、產品行為、群體特徵或常見流程時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：typically occurs; typically involves; typically lasts; typically used.  
**常見搭配對象**：pattern, process, symptom, case, product.  
**與 most of the time 的辨析**：Typically 強調『典型模式』，不只是發生頻率。  
**使用限制**：純粹個人習慣如『我大多數時候步行回家』通常不首選 typically。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The course typically takes six months to complete.  
這個課程通常需要六個月完成。

### 05 Ordinarily

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：通常；在一般情況下  
**語域**：slightly formal  
**核心語義**：in the normal course of events or under ordinary circumstances  
**用法提示**：當 **most of the time** 可理解為『一般情況下』，而語氣較正式時可用。  
**常見搭配**：ordinarily would; ordinarily takes; ordinarily allowed; ordinarily expected.  
**常見搭配對象**：procedure, rule, schedule, circumstance, expectation.  
**與 most of the time 的辨析**：Ordinarily 比 normally 稍正式，也常與例外情況形成對比。  
**使用限制**：日常對話中 usually / normally 更常見。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Visitors are ordinarily asked to book in advance.  
一般情況下，訪客會被要求預先預約。

## Tier 2 — Rule / majority-outcome variants · 慣例與多數結果變體

### 06 As a rule

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：通常；一般來說；按慣例  
**語域**：neutral / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：in general, according to what normally happens or is usually done  
**用法提示**：當 **most of the time** 描述穩定習慣、慣例或一般規則時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：as a rule, we...; as a rule, people...; as a general rule.  
**常見搭配對象**：habit, convention, policy, repeated practice.  
**與 most of the time 的辨析**：As a rule 帶『慣例／一般原則』意味，比純頻率 most of the time 更規則化。  
**使用限制**：如果只是統計上常發生、沒有慣例感，usually / often 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
As a rule, we avoid scheduling meetings on Friday afternoons.  
一般來說，我們避免在星期五下午安排會議。

### 07 More often than not

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：往往；多半；大多數時候  
**語域**：neutral / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：on more occasions than not; in a majority of cases  
**用法提示**：當 **most of the time** 強調『超過一半、多數情況都如此』時，是很自然的慣用改寫。  
**常見搭配**：more often than not, ...; more often than not ends up; more often than not works.  
**常見搭配對象**：outcome, tendency, repeated choice, prediction.  
**與 most of the time 的辨析**：More often than not 比 usually 更帶『多半會這樣』的語氣，常用來總結反覆結果。  
**使用限制**：如果要表達高度固定的 routine，usually / normally 更直接。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
More often than not, the simplest solution is the best one.  
多數時候，最簡單的解決方法就是最好的。

## Tier 3 — Overall-period contextual variant · 整體時段概括變體

### 08 For the most part

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：大致上；多半；大部分情況下  
**語域**：neutral / broad  
**核心語義**：mostly; in general, though not completely or without exceptions  
**用法提示**：當 **most of the time** 的實際意思接近『大致上／整體而言』，而不是真正逐次計算頻率時可用。  
**常見搭配**：for the most part, ...; works for the most part; quiet for the most part.  
**常見搭配對象**：overall situation, period, behaviour, condition, assessment.  
**與 most of the time 的辨析**：For the most part 可以概括一段時間或整體狀況；不一定嚴格表示『多數次數』。  
**使用限制**：若要精確表達 recurring frequency，usually / more often than not 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The office is quiet for the most part, except around lunchtime.  
辦公室大部分時間都很安靜，午飯時間除外。

## 04 False synonyms / frequency traps · 容易誤選的頻率與概括詞

### 01 Always

**總是 ≠ most of the time**  
always 表示毫無例外，強度過高。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Always means on every occasion.

### 02 Often

**經常 ≠ necessarily most of the time**  
often 表示頻繁，但不一定達到『大多數時候』。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Often may be weaker than most of the time.

### 03 Sometimes

**有時 ≠ most of the time**  
sometimes 只表示部分時候，明顯較弱。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Sometimes is much weaker.

### 04 Occasionally

**偶爾 ≠ most of the time**  
occasionally 表示低頻率，幾乎相反。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Occasionally is low-frequency.

### 05 Generally

**只在概括 sense 高度接近**  
generally 偏『一般而言』，不一定是逐次頻率。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Generally is generalisation-focused.

### 06 Normally

**只在正常情況 sense 高度接近**  
normally 暗示 normal circumstances。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Normally is expectation-focused.

### 07 For the most part

**只在整體概括 sense 接近**  
它可以描述整體狀態，而非事件發生次數。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
For the most part is broad-overall.

### 08 Most / mostly / most of the time

**詞形與結構陷阱**  
most、mostly、most of the time 的語法功能不同。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check structure and word class.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Usually · 通常；大多數時候

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

Most of the time, I walk home after work.

**中文翻譯**  
大多數時候，我下班後步行回家。

- A. Usually
- B. Generally
- C. Often
- D. Always
- E. Sometimes
- F. Occasionally

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Usually** · 通常；大多數時候

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
I usually walk home after work.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Usually ✓** — on most occasions or in most situations。本句的關鍵是 **a personal recurring habit is central**；這正是 **Usually** 最自然的語義焦點。
- **B.** **Generally** — in most cases, as a general rule, or broadly speaking。**generally** 偏整體概括；若句子明確是個人 routine、正常程序或多數次數的結果，未必是最佳。
- **C.** **Often** — frequently, but not necessarily on most occasions。**often** 只表示頻繁，不必達到『大多數時候』，強度可能不足。
- **D.** **Always** — on every occasion without exception。**always** 表示每一次都如此，沒有例外，明顯比 most of the time 強。
- **E.** **Sometimes** — on some occasions。**sometimes** 只表示部分時候，頻率明顯較低。
- **F.** **Occasionally** — from time to time, but not often。**occasionally** 表示偶爾，與『大多數時候』相差很遠。

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Most of the time 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 個人固定習慣 | **usually** | 是否 simply means on most occasions？ |
| 概括一般趨勢 | **generally** | 是否 broad generalisation is central？ |
| 正常操作／正常情況 | **normally** | 是否 normal circumstances are implied？ |
| 典型程序／特徵 | **typically** | 是否 characteristic pattern is central？ |
| 正常程序但有例外 | **ordinarily** | 是否 ordinary course + exception contrast？ |
| 穩定慣例／一般規則 | **as a rule** | 是否 customary practice is central？ |
| 重複結果多半如此 | **more often than not** | 是否 majority outcome is central？ |
| 一段時間整體而言如此 | **for the most part** | 是否 broad overall condition is the focus？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **most of the time vs usually**：usually 是最直接的單字替代。
- **most of the time vs generally**：generally 偏整體概括。
- **most of the time vs normally**：normally 多了『正常情況下』的預期感。
- **most of the time vs typically**：typically 強調典型模式。
- **most of the time vs ordinarily**：ordinarily 較正式，常與例外形成對比。
- **most of the time vs as a rule**：as a rule 帶慣例／一般規則意味。
- **most of the time vs more often than not**：後者強調多數結果。
- **most of the time vs for the most part**：後者可概括整體狀態，不一定是逐次頻率。
- **most of the time vs often**：often 可能只有『經常』，未必達到『大多數時候』。

## 08 Grammar, position & frequency traps

- **usually / generally / normally / typically** 通常放在主要動詞前：She usually walks。
- 配 **be** 時通常放在 be 後：The office is usually quiet。
- **most of the time** 可放句首或句尾：Most of the time, ... / ... most of the time。
- **as a rule** 常作句首插入語：As a rule, we avoid...
- **more often than not** 常置句首，也可放句中。
- **for the most part** 常概括整體狀況：The project went smoothly for the most part。
- **always > usually/most of the time > often > sometimes > occasionally** 是實用但非數學化的頻率尺度。
- **most**、**mostly**、**most of the time** 的結構不同，不能機械互換。
