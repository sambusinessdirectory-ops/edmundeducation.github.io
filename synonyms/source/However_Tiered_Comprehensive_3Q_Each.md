# HOWEVER · 分層同義詞學習系統

## However

**「然而／不過／儘管如此」，要先分清是一般轉折、讓步、兩面比較，還是真正的反向關係。這類詞的最大難點不是意思，而是句法位置和標點。**

本單元集中處理 **however = a conjunctive adverb used to introduce contrast, qualification, or concession**。你提供的 **nevertheless; yet; nonetheless** 都是核心近義表達，但 **yet** 的句法尤其不同。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且高實用度的詞／表達，共 **39 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。同時把 **however / but / yet / although / nevertheless / by contrast / conversely / albeit** 的邏輯關係與標點清楚拆開。

## Tier 1 — Core contrast / concession equivalents · 核心轉折與讓步近義詞

### 01 Nevertheless

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：儘管如此；然而  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：used to introduce a statement that contrasts with what has just been said, while accepting that the earlier point is true  
**用法提示**：和 however 最直接的近義連接副詞之一，特別適合正式寫作和論證。  
**常見搭配／結構**：nevertheless,; but nevertheless; nevertheless important; nevertheless remained; nevertheless true.  
**常見功能**：sentence contrast, concession, argument, result, exception.  
**與 however 的辨析**：Nevertheless 比 however 更明確帶『即使如此，後件仍成立』的讓步意味。  
**使用限制**：不是所有 however 的『無論如何／不管怎樣』sense 都可用 nevertheless。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The task was difficult. Nevertheless, the team completed it on time.  
任務很困難。儘管如此，團隊仍按時完成。

### 02 Yet

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：然而；可是；但  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：used to introduce a contrast that is surprising or unexpected in relation to the previous statement  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，句中尤其自然，常比 however 更簡潔。  
**常見搭配／結構**：and yet; yet still; yet another; simple yet effective; difficult yet possible.  
**常見功能**：clause contrast, adjective contrast, unexpected result, concession, description.  
**與 however 的辨析**：Yet 常放在同一句連接兩個分句；however 更常作連接副詞並以逗號隔開。  
**使用限制**：標準寫作中不要把 yet 當作 however 的所有位置都機械互換。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The plan is simple, yet it could have a major effect.  
這個計劃很簡單，然而可能產生重大影響。

### 03 Nonetheless

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：儘管如此；然而  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：despite what has just been said; even so  
**用法提示**：和 nevertheless 幾乎等義，正式書面語中很自然。  
**常見搭配／結構**：nonetheless,; but nonetheless; nonetheless useful; nonetheless remained; nonetheless significant.  
**常見功能**：argument, concession, limitation, result, observation.  
**與 however 的辨析**：Nonetheless 和 nevertheless 非常接近；however 稍更中性、用途更廣。  
**使用限制**：口語中使用頻率通常低於 however / still。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The evidence is limited. Nonetheless, the finding is worth examining.  
證據有限。儘管如此，這項發現仍值得研究。

### 04 Still

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：然而；儘管如此；還是  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：used to say that something remains true despite a contrasting fact or difficulty  
**用法提示**：當 however 帶『即使如此，仍然……』的語氣時很自然，口語和書面語都常見。  
**常見搭配／結構**：still,; but still; still possible; still important; still worth.  
**常見功能**：concession, continuing truth, possibility, judgement, result.  
**與 however 的辨析**：Still 比 however 更口語，也更明顯帶『仍然』意味。  
**使用限制**：still 亦可表示『仍舊』時間義，需靠語境判斷。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The route is longer. Still, it may be safer.  
這條路較長。然而，它可能更安全。

### 05 Even so

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：即使如此；儘管如此  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：despite that fact or situation  
**用法提示**：當 however 需要很清楚地表達讓步關係時很自然。  
**常見搭配／結構**：even so,; but even so; even so it remains; even so, however; even so useful.  
**常見功能**：concession, difficulty, limitation, result, judgement.  
**與 however 的辨析**：Even so 比 however 更直接地回指前句整體。  
**使用限制**：若只是一般對比而非讓步，however / by contrast 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The cost is high. Even so, demand remains strong.  
成本很高。即使如此，需求仍然強勁。

## Tier 2 — Conversational / balancing / comparison variants · 口語、兩面權衡與比較變體

### 06 All the same

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：儘管如此；還是  
**語域**：informal / conversational  
**核心語義**：despite what has just been said; nevertheless  
**用法提示**：較口語，常用來表示前面的缺點或反對理由仍不足以改變後面的判斷。  
**常見搭配／結構**：all the same,; but all the same; all the same I think; all the same worth; all the same true.  
**常見功能**：opinion, concession, decision, judgement, result.  
**與 however 的辨析**：All the same 比 however 更口語、更帶說話者態度。  
**使用限制**：正式學術寫作通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
It is expensive. All the same, I think it is worth buying.  
它很貴。儘管如此，我仍認為值得買。

### 07 That said

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：話雖如此；不過  
**語域**：neutral / conversational / professional  
**核心語義**：used to introduce a qualification or contrasting point after making a previous statement  
**用法提示**：當 however 用來『補充保留／修正上一點』時很自然。  
**常見搭配／結構**：that said,; having said that; that said, however; that said, it remains; that said, we should.  
**常見功能**：qualification, caveat, recommendation, judgement, contrast.  
**與 however 的辨析**：That said 很適合『先肯定，再補充限制』；however 更中性。  
**使用限制**：非常正式的學術論文可優先用 however / nevertheless。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The software is easy to use. That said, it lacks some advanced features.  
這套軟件容易使用。不過，它欠缺部分進階功能。

### 08 On the other hand

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：另一方面；可是  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：used to introduce a different or opposing consideration  
**用法提示**：當 however 的作用不是讓步，而是提出另一面觀點時很實用。  
**常見搭配／結構**：on the other hand,; but on the other hand; one the one hand ... on the other hand; consider on the other hand.  
**常見功能**：alternative view, opposing consideration, comparison, argument, perspective.  
**與 however 的辨析**：On the other hand 強調『另一面』；however 不一定要求成對觀點。  
**使用限制**：如果只是『儘管如此』，nevertheless / even so 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The flat is small. On the other hand, it is very close to the station.  
這個單位很小。另一方面，它非常近車站。

### 09 By contrast

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：相比之下；然而  
**語域**：formal / academic  
**核心語義**：used to introduce a clear difference between two things, situations, or groups  
**用法提示**：當 however 其實是比較兩項事物的差異時很精準。  
**常見搭配／結構**：by contrast,; in contrast; by contrast with; sharply by contrast; by comparison.  
**常見功能**：group, result, trend, method, situation.  
**與 however 的辨析**：By contrast 是比較型轉折；however 更廣，可表讓步或一般對比。  
**使用限制**：沒有明確比較對象時不宜使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
City rents rose. By contrast, rents in rural areas remained stable.  
城市租金上升。相比之下，鄉郊租金保持穩定。

### 10 Conversely

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：相反地；反過來說  
**語域**：formal / academic  
**核心語義**：used to introduce a statement in which the relationship or situation is reversed  
**用法提示**：當 however 引入真正的反向關係時可用。  
**常見搭配／結構**：conversely,; conversely true; conversely associated; conversely if; conversely the opposite.  
**常見功能**：relationship, condition, trend, argument, logical reversal.  
**與 however 的辨析**：Conversely 不只是『但是』，而是明確的反向／鏡像關係。  
**使用限制**：普通讓步不能用 conversely。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
High demand can raise prices. Conversely, weak demand can push them down.  
需求高可推高價格。相反地，需求疲弱可令價格下跌。

## Tier 3 — Formal / literary / embedded concession variants · 正式、文學與內嵌讓步變體

### 11 Be that as it may

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：話雖如此；即便如此  
**語域**：formal / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：used to acknowledge a point while indicating that it does not change the main argument or conclusion  
**用法提示**：較正式、修辭性強，適合論述或較莊重語體。  
**常見搭配／結構**：be that as it may,; but be that as it may; be that as it may, the fact remains; be that as it may, we must.  
**常見功能**：argument, objection, concession, conclusion, judgement.  
**與 however 的辨析**：比 however 更正式、更有『承認前點但不改變結論』的語氣。  
**使用限制**：日常對話通常太正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The explanation may be reasonable. Be that as it may, the rule still applies.  
這個解釋可能合理。話雖如此，規則仍然適用。

### 12 For all that

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：儘管如此；雖然如此  
**語域**：literary / formal  
**核心語義**：despite everything just mentioned  
**用法提示**：可用於較文學或正式的讓步轉折，表示前面的因素仍不足以推翻後面的結論。  
**常見搭配／結構**：for all that,; and for all that; for all that effort; for all that, still; for all that, however.  
**常見功能**：effort, difficulty, criticism, limitation, concession.  
**與 however 的辨析**：For all that 比 however 更文學、更強調『即使前面種種如此』。  
**使用限制**：現代一般寫作中使用頻率較低。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The journey was exhausting. For all that, it was unforgettable.  
旅程令人筋疲力盡。儘管如此，它仍令人難忘。

### 13 Albeit

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：雖然；儘管  
**語域**：formal / written  
**核心語義**：although; used to introduce a concessive phrase, adjective, adverb, or shortened clause  
**用法提示**：當 however 的功能可改寫成『雖然……』的內嵌讓步結構時很有用。  
**常見搭配／結構**：albeit briefly; albeit difficult; albeit with limitations; albeit somewhat; albeit at a cost.  
**常見功能**：adjective phrase, adverb phrase, shortened clause, qualification, limitation.  
**與 however 的辨析**：Albeit 不是句首連接副詞；它通常把讓步內容嵌入同一句。  
**使用限制**：不能把 however 的標點和句法直接套到 albeit。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The proposal was accepted, albeit with several conditions.  
建議獲接納，雖然附帶數項條件。

## 04 False synonyms / however traps · 容易誤選的連接詞與轉折陷阱

### 01 But

**but 與 however 接近但句法不同**  
but 是並列連接詞，可直接連接兩個分句；however 是連接副詞，標點不同。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
But is a conjunction; however is a conjunctive adverb.

### 02 Although

**although 不等於 however 的句法**  
although 引導讓步從句，不能像 however 一樣獨立連接兩個完整句子。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Although introduces a subordinate clause.

### 03 Therefore

**因此不等於 however**  
therefore 表示因果結果；however 表示轉折／讓步。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Therefore signals result, not contrast.

### 04 Moreover

**此外不等於 however**  
moreover 表示補充同方向資訊，不是轉折。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Moreover adds information rather than contrasting it.

### 05 Otherwise

**否則不等於 however**  
otherwise 常表示『否則／在其他情況下』，邏輯關係不同。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Otherwise does not mark simple contrast.

### 06 Meanwhile

**同時不等於 however**  
meanwhile 主要表示同時發生或另一條敘事線。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Meanwhile is temporal or parallel, not concessive.

### 07 Yet

**yet 與 however 接近但常是連接詞**  
yet 常直接連接同一句中的兩個分句；however 的位置和標點更靈活。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Yet often functions as a coordinating conjunction.

### 08 However / however + adjective

**多義陷阱**  
however 亦可表示『無論多麼』：however difficult；這個 sense 不等於 nevertheless。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
However can also mean 'no matter how'.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Nevertheless · 儘管如此；然而

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The task was difficult. However, the team completed it on time.

**中文翻譯**  
任務很困難。然而，團隊仍按時完成。

- A. nevertheless
- B. therefore
- C. moreover
- D. meanwhile
- E. otherwise
- F. although

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**nevertheless** · 儘管如此；然而

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The task was difficult. Nevertheless, the team completed it on time.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. nevertheless ✓ — the second sentence remains true despite the difficulty, which is a classic nevertheless relation。**
- **B. therefore — therefore would make completion a result of difficulty, which reverses the logic。**
- **C. moreover — moreover adds supporting information rather than concession。**
- **D. meanwhile — meanwhile signals simultaneity。**
- **E. otherwise — otherwise means 'if not' or 'in other circumstances'。**
- **F. although — although requires a subordinate-clause structure rather than standing here as a sentence connector.**

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **however vs nevertheless**: nevertheless 讓步意味通常更強。
- **however vs nonetheless**: 兩者很接近；nonetheless 稍偏正式書面。
- **however vs yet**: yet 常是並列連接詞，直接連兩個分句；however 是連接副詞。
- **however vs still**: still 更口語，也帶『仍然』語感。
- **however vs even so**: even so 更明確表示『即使如此』。
- **however vs that said**: that said 常用於前面肯定後面的保留／修正。
- **however vs on the other hand**: 後者強調另一面觀點，而不一定是讓步。
- **however vs by contrast**: by contrast 要有清楚比較對象。
- **however vs conversely**: conversely 要有真正反向關係。
- **however vs although**: although 引導從句，句法不同。

## 08 Grammar, punctuation & however traps · 文法、標點與轉折陷阱

- **however** 作連接副詞時，不能只用逗號把兩個完整句子硬連在一起：✗ *It was late, however we stayed.*
- 可寫：**It was late. However, we stayed.**
- 亦可寫：**It was late; however, we stayed.**
- **yet / but** 是並列連接詞，可寫：**It was late, yet we stayed.**
- **although** 引導從句：**Although it was late, we stayed.**
- **nevertheless / nonetheless / still / even so** 可像 however 一樣作句際轉折。
- **on the other hand** 最適合兩面權衡，不應機械替換所有 however。
- **by contrast / in contrast** 需要清楚比較對象。
- **conversely** 表真正的反向／鏡像關係，不只是普通『但是』。
- **albeit** 通常接 adjective / adverb / phrase：**successful, albeit expensive**。
- **however + adjective/adverb** 可表示『無論多麼』：**However difficult the task is, ...**；這不是『然而』sense。
- **however** 可插入句中：**The plan, however, is expensive.** 兩邊通常用逗號隔開。
- 在正式寫作中，避免過度連續使用 however；可按邏輯改用 nevertheless / by contrast / that said 等。
