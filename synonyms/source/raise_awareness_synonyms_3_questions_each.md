# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Raise awareness

**`raise awareness` 可以表示讓更多人知道某議題、引起注意、加深理解、教育公眾，或提升更深層的社會／道德意識。不同替代表達的核心差別，是『知道』的深度和宣傳目的。**

核心替代表達：

`increase understanding / draw attention to / promote awareness of / increase awareness of / highlight / bring attention to / educate people about / raise consciousness of`

> **核心原則**：先判斷 raise awareness 是 **deeper understanding, simple attention, ongoing awareness promotion, measurable awareness increase, highlighting, directing attention, education, or deeper social consciousness**。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Increase understanding

**加深理解；提升認識**

當 `raise awareness` 不只是令更多人知道某議題，而是希望他們真正理解原因、影響或背景時，`increase understanding` 很合適。它比單純 awareness 更深入。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The workshops aim to increase understanding of mental health issues.

工作坊旨在加深公眾對心理健康議題的理解。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **helping people understand an issue more fully**

**REGISTER · 語域**

教育、公共政策、一般

---

## 02 Draw attention to

**引起對……的注意；提醒關注**

當 `raise awareness` 的核心是讓人留意某個問題、風險或被忽視的議題時，`draw attention to` 很自然。它強調 attention，不保證產生深入理解。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The campaign draws attention to the dangers of distracted driving.

該活動提醒公眾注意分心駕駛的危險。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **making people notice or focus on an issue**

**REGISTER · 語域**

新聞、宣傳、一般

---

## 03 Promote awareness of

**提升對……的認識；推廣認知**

非常接近 `raise awareness of`，較正式，常見於機構、公共衞生、教育和倡議語境。重點是持續推動認知。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The programme promotes awareness of online safety among teenagers.

該計劃提升青少年對網絡安全的認識。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **actively encouraging greater awareness of an issue**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、教育、公共衞生

---

## 04 Increase awareness of

**提升對……的認知；增加認識**

和 `raise awareness of` 幾乎直接互換，語氣中性、透明，尤其適合報告、政策目標和機構聲明。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The initiative seeks to increase awareness of recycling options.

該倡議旨在提升公眾對回收選項的認知。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **making more people aware of something**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、政策、一般

---

## 05 Highlight

**突顯；強調**

當 `raise awareness` 的做法是特別指出某個風險、問題或重要事實時，`highlight` 很精準。它更像『把重點凸顯出來』，未必包含長期教育。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The report highlights the risks faced by older residents.

報告突顯年長居民面對的風險。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **making an important issue or fact stand out**

**REGISTER · 語域**

報告、新聞、正式

---

## 06 Bring attention to

**令大家注意；把注意力帶到**

和 `draw attention to` 很接近，較口語、直接。常用於活動、媒體和倡議，希望把焦點帶到某議題。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The exhibition brings attention to the problem of food waste.

展覽令大家注意食物浪費問題。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **directing people's attention toward an issue**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、媒體、倡議

---

## 07 Educate people about

**教育公眾了解；向人講解**

當 `raise awareness` 包含提供知識、解釋原因、教導預防方法等教育內容時，這個表達更精準。它比 awareness 更具資訊深度。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The campaign educates people about how to recognise online scams.

該活動教育公眾如何識別網上騙局。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **giving people knowledge and practical understanding**

**REGISTER · 語域**

教育、公共衞生、宣傳

---

## 08 Raise consciousness of

**提高對……的意識；喚起意識**

較正式、較書面，常用於社會、政治、環境或權益議題，強調令公眾更深刻意識到某問題的存在和重要性。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The documentary raised consciousness of housing inequality.

該紀錄片提高了公眾對房屋不平等問題的意識。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **developing deeper social or moral awareness of an issue**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、社會議題、學術

# 02 False synonyms

## Raise awareness / Increase understanding
`increase understanding` 比 awareness 更深入；它要求人真正理解原因、機制或背景。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A poster may raise awareness of a problem.  
A workshop may increase understanding of why the problem occurs.

## Raise awareness / Draw attention to
`draw attention to` 只保證人會留意，不保證形成較深理解。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A headline draws attention to a problem.  
A public-education campaign may raise awareness over time.

## Raise awareness / Promote awareness of
兩者非常接近；`promote awareness of` 更正式、更有持續倡議意味。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The campaign raises awareness of vaccination.  
The programme promotes awareness of vaccination through schools and clinics.

## Raise awareness / Increase awareness of
幾乎可直接互換；`increase awareness of` 更中性、透明，常見於政策目標。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The campaign aims to raise awareness of recycling.  
The project seeks to increase awareness of recycling options.

## Raise awareness / Highlight
`highlight` 是「特別指出、突顯」某事，不等於完整的 awareness-building。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The report highlights a serious gap in services.  
The campaign raises awareness of the issue among the wider public.

## Draw attention to / Bring attention to
兩者高度接近；`draw attention to` 更固定、更常見，`bring attention to` 較直接口語。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The article draws attention to unsafe housing.  
The exhibition brings attention to food waste.

## Raise awareness / Educate people about
`educate people about` 更強調提供知識、解釋和實用方法。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A slogan may raise awareness of online scams.  
A workshop educates people about how to recognise and avoid them.

## Raise awareness / Raise consciousness of
`raise consciousness of` 更正式、更偏社會、權益、政治或道德覺醒。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A poster raises awareness of a local issue.  
A social movement may raise consciousness of structural inequality.

> 每個目標詞／表達三題，共 **24 題**。  
> 每題六個選項；正確答案位置在 **A–F 各出現四次**。  
> 每個選項均按 **本句的宣傳目的、認知深度、搭配、詞形和介詞** 作具體解釋。

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Increase understanding vs Increase awareness** — understanding 更深；awareness 只要求知道。
- **Draw attention to vs Raise awareness** — attention 可是一次性注意；awareness 常是較持續的認知建立。
- **Promote awareness vs Increase awareness** — promote 強調主動倡議；increase 偏結果。
- **Highlight vs Draw attention to** — highlight 更像文本／報告中突顯重點。
- **Bring attention to vs Draw attention to** — 意思近；draw attention to 更固定。
- **Educate people about vs Raise awareness** — educate 要提供內容和知識。
- **Raise consciousness of vs Raise awareness of** — consciousness 更深、更社會／政治化。
- **Awareness ≠ understanding** — 知道問題存在，不代表理解問題原因。

# 06 Common expressions · 常用表達

- **increase understanding of** — 加深對……的理解
- **draw attention to** — 引起對……的注意
- **promote awareness of** — 推動對……的認識
- **increase awareness of** — 提升對……的認知
- **highlight a problem** — 突顯問題
- **bring attention to** — 令大家注意
- **educate people about** — 教育公眾了解
- **raise consciousness of** — 提高對……的意識
- **public awareness campaign** — 公眾認知宣傳活動
- **awareness-raising campaign** — 提升認知的宣傳活動

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Awareness
通常作不可數名詞：

> raise awareness  
> increase awareness of something

不要寫：

> raise an awareness ✗（一般這個意思）

## Draw / bring attention
固定介詞：

> draw attention **to** something  
> bring attention **to** something

## Educate
常見：

> educate people **about** online safety  
> educate students **on** a topic

不要堆疊：

> educate people on about... ✗

## Conscious / consciousness
> conscious = 形容詞  
> consciousness = 名詞

所以：

> raise consciousness of ✓  
> raise conscious of ✗

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 想加深真正理解？ → **increase understanding**
2. 只是令大家留意？ → **draw attention to**
3. 機構持續倡議認知？ → **promote awareness of**
4. 中性地說認知程度上升？ → **increase awareness of**
5. 報告／文章要突顯某問題？ → **highlight**
6. 較直接說把焦點帶到某議題？ → **bring attention to**
7. 要教授具體知識／方法？ → **educate people about**
8. 要表達更深層社會／權益意識？ → **raise consciousness of**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`increase understanding / draw attention to / promote awareness of / increase awareness of / highlight / bring attention to / educate people about / raise consciousness of`

尤其要記住：

> **awareness** = 知道／留意  
> **understanding** = 真正理解  
> **attention** = 當下焦點  
> **education** = 提供知識  
> **consciousness** = 更深層社會／道德意識
