# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Medical reasons

**`medical reasons` 可以是日常的健康原因，也可以是正式申請／豁免的醫療理據、臨床治療判斷、決策中的醫療考慮，甚至更專門的『醫療必要性』。這些表達不能在所有語境機械互換。**

核心替代表達：

`health reasons / health grounds / medical grounds / clinical reasons / health-related reasons / medical considerations / health considerations / medical necessity`

> **核心原則**：先判斷句子需要的是 **general health reason, formal grounds, clinical judgment, broad health-related description, decision factor, or medical necessity**。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Health reasons

**健康原因；健康方面的理由**

和 `medical reasons` 最自然、最廣泛的近義表達之一。它比 `medical reasons` 稍日常，可涵蓋疾病、身體狀況、康復需要或一般健康考慮。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She reduced her working hours for health reasons.

她因健康原因減少工作時數。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a broad health-related reason affecting a decision or action**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、工作、教育、行政

---

## 02 Health grounds

**基於健康理由；健康方面的理據**

較正式，常見於申請、豁免、工作安排、法律或行政決定，表示某項要求是基於健康狀況提出。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He was granted leave on health grounds.

他基於健康理由獲批休假。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **formal justification based on a person's health**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、行政、法律、工作

---

## 03 Medical grounds

**基於醫療理由；基於健康狀況**

和 `health grounds` 很接近，但更直接帶醫療／醫生判斷色彩，常見於正式豁免、休假、延期或特殊安排。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The hearing was postponed on medical grounds.

聆訊基於醫療理由延期。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **formal justification supported by a medical condition or assessment**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、法律、行政、醫療

---

## 04 Clinical reasons

**臨床原因；臨床上的理由**

當原因來自臨床判斷、病人狀況、治療選擇或醫療風險時很精準。它比一般 `medical reasons` 更專業、更偏醫護決策。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The treatment was changed for clinical reasons.

治療方案基於臨床原因作出更改。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **reasons arising from clinical assessment or treatment needs**

**REGISTER · 語域**

醫療、臨床、專業

---

## 05 Health-related reasons

**與健康有關的原因**

透明、自然的描述性改寫，適合在不需要高度正式或專業語氣時使用。範圍可包括身體和一般健康因素。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Some participants withdrew for health-related reasons.

部分參加者因與健康有關的原因退出。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **reasons connected with health in a broad sense**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性、研究、一般

---

## 06 Medical considerations

**醫療考慮；醫療因素**

不是完全同義，但當 `medical reasons` 指決策時需要考慮的醫療因素、風險或限制，這個表達很自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Medical considerations affected the timing of the operation.

醫療考慮影響了手術時間安排。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **medical factors that influence a decision**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、醫療、行政

---

## 07 Health considerations

**健康考慮；健康因素**

比 `health reasons` 更偏『作決定時納入考慮的健康因素』，不一定是一個單一原因。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Health considerations led the school to modify the activity.

基於健康考慮，學校修改了活動安排。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **health factors taken into account when making a decision**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性、政策、教育、行政

---

## 08 Medical necessity

**醫療必要性；醫療需要**

較專門，只有當 `medical reasons` 真正表示某項治療、設備、休假或措施在醫療上屬必要時才適合。它不是一般 medical reasons 的通用替代。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The insurer asked for evidence of medical necessity.

保險公司要求提供醫療必要性的證明。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a medically necessary need rather than a general health reason**

**REGISTER · 語域**

醫療、保險、正式

# 02 False synonyms

## Medical reasons / Health reasons
兩者很接近；`health reasons` 稍廣、稍日常，未必涉及醫生或醫療程序。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
She changed her routine for health reasons.  
She postponed the procedure for medical reasons after speaking to her doctor.

## Medical reasons / Health grounds
`health grounds` 較正式，常見於申請、豁免、休假和行政決定。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
He stayed home for health reasons.  
He was formally excused from the requirement on health grounds.

## Health grounds / Medical grounds
兩者都正式；`medical grounds` 往往更直接暗示具體醫療狀況或醫療證明。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A flexible arrangement may be approved on health grounds.  
A hearing may be postponed on medical grounds supported by a doctor's letter.

## Medical reasons / Clinical reasons
`clinical reasons` 專指醫護人員根據病況、治療效果或風險作出的臨床判斷。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
An employee may take leave for medical reasons.  
A doctor may change a treatment for clinical reasons.

## Medical reasons / Health-related reasons
`health-related reasons` 是透明、中性的描述，但不如 medical grounds / clinical reasons 專門。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Participants may withdraw for health-related reasons.  
A treatment may be changed for clinical reasons.

## Medical reasons / Medical considerations
`reasons` 解釋「為甚麼」；`considerations` 是作決定時需要衡量的醫療因素。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The trip was cancelled for medical reasons.  
Medical considerations influenced when the patient could travel.

## Health reasons / Health considerations
`health reasons` 更像直接原因；`health considerations` 更像決策時納入考慮的因素。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
She stopped working for health reasons.  
Health considerations led the employer to adjust her duties.

## Medical reasons / Medical necessity
`medical necessity` 比 medical reasons 窄得多，只表示「醫療上確有必要」。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A person may miss work for medical reasons.  
An insurer may require proof of medical necessity before paying for treatment.

# 03 練習與解釋

> 每個目標詞／表達三題，共 **24 題**。  
> 每題六個選項；正確答案位置在 **A–F 各出現四次**。  
> 每個選項都按該句的正式程度、醫療專業程度和決策功能作個別解釋。

## 練習 01.1

**Target phrase: Health reasons · 健康原因；健康方面的理由**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> She stopped travelling for medical reasons.

**中文翻譯**

她因健康原因停止旅行。

| | 選項 |
|---|---|
| A | Health reasons |
| B | Health-related reasons |
| C | Health considerations |
| D | Medical grounds |
| E | Personal preference |
| F | Financial reasons |

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**A. Health reasons · 健康原因；健康方面的理由**

> She stopped travelling for health reasons.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. Health reasons ✓**  
句子線索「日常說明健康狀況影響決定」只是廣義說健康狀況影響決定，沒有特別要求臨床或法律語氣；`health reasons` 最自然。

**B. Health-related reasons**  
`health-related reasons`：中性、描述性地表示與健康有關的原因。這句線索是「日常說明健康狀況影響決定」；若句子要求正式 grounds、專業 clinical judgment 或必要性，精確度不足。

**C. Health considerations**  
`health considerations`：表示作決策時納入的健康因素。這句線索是「日常說明健康狀況影響決定」；若語境要求一項正式批准理由或臨床判斷，`considerations` 會太廣。

**D. Medical grounds**  
`medical grounds`：正式、通常有醫療狀況或醫生證明支持。這句線索是「日常說明健康狀況影響決定」；若只是廣義健康因素或臨床上某個治療選擇，未必最自然。

**E. Personal preference**  
`Personal preference`：personal preference = 個人喜好；不是健康或醫療原因。 本句線索是「日常說明健康狀況影響決定」，所以原因類型或決策基礎與 medical reasons 不一致。

**F. Financial reasons**  
`Financial reasons`：financial reasons = 財務原因；和健康無關。 本句線索是「日常說明健康狀況影響決定」，所以原因類型或決策基礎與 medical reasons 不一致。

# 04 Target-phrase contrast map

| `medical reasons` 真正重點 | 較精準替代表達 |
|---|---|
| 廣義、日常健康原因 | **health reasons** |
| 正式的健康理據 | **health grounds** |
| 有醫療狀況／醫療證明支持的正式理據 | **medical grounds** |
| 醫護人員的臨床判斷 | **clinical reasons** |
| 中性說「與健康有關」 | **health-related reasons** |
| 決策中要考慮的醫療因素 | **medical considerations** |
| 決策中要考慮的健康因素 | **health considerations** |
| 醫療上確有必要 | **medical necessity** |

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Medical reasons vs Health reasons** — health reasons 稍廣、稍日常。
- **Health grounds vs Medical grounds** — medical grounds 更直接帶醫療證明／狀況色彩。
- **Medical reasons vs Clinical reasons** — clinical reasons 只適合醫護專業判斷。
- **Health reasons vs Health-related reasons** — 後者更描述性，常用於分類／研究語境。
- **Medical reasons vs Medical considerations** — reasons 解釋原因；considerations 是決策因素。
- **Health grounds vs Health considerations** — grounds 是正式理據；considerations 是被納入權衡的因素。
- **Medical grounds vs Medical necessity** — grounds 只是正式理由；necessity 表示「非做不可／醫療上需要」。
- **Clinical reasons vs Medical necessity** — clinical reasons 可以是風險或治療選擇；necessity 要求達到「必要」程度。

# 06 Common expressions · 常用表達

- **for health reasons** — 因健康原因
- **on health grounds** — 基於健康理由
- **on medical grounds** — 基於醫療理由
- **for clinical reasons** — 基於臨床原因
- **for health-related reasons** — 因健康相關原因
- **medical considerations** — 醫療考慮
- **health considerations** — 健康考慮
- **medical necessity** — 醫療必要性
- **medical evidence** — 醫療證明
- **doctor's letter / medical certificate** — 醫生證明／醫療證明書

| **medical grounds** | 正式、法律、行政、醫療 |

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Reasons

> for medical reasons  
> for health reasons  
> for clinical reasons  
> for health-related reasons

## Grounds

最常見介詞是 `on`：

> on health grounds  
> on medical grounds

不要寫：

> for medical grounds ✗

## Considerations

常見：

> because of medical considerations  
> health considerations influenced the decision

`considerations` 通常不是直接接在 `for` 後面代替 reasons。

## Medical necessity

通常不可數：

> evidence of medical necessity  
> establish medical necessity

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 一般健康原因？ → **health reasons**
2. 正式申請／豁免的健康理據？ → **health grounds**
3. 有醫療證明或明確醫療狀況支持？ → **medical grounds**
4. 是醫護人員的治療／病況判斷？ → **clinical reasons**
5. 只想中性分類「與健康有關」？ → **health-related reasons**
6. 是作決定時要衡量的醫療因素？ → **medical considerations**
7. 是政策／安排中的健康因素？ → **health considerations**
8. 必須證明某項醫療措施屬必要？ → **medical necessity**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`health reasons / health grounds / medical grounds / clinical reasons / health-related reasons / medical considerations / health considerations / medical necessity`

最重要的 near-miss：

`personal reasons / financial reasons / convenience / patient preference / administrative reasons`

尤其要記住：

> **health reasons** = 最廣、最日常  
> **health grounds / medical grounds** = 正式理據  
> **clinical reasons** = 臨床專業判斷  
> **medical / health considerations** = 決策因素  
> **medical necessity** = 醫療上確有必要
