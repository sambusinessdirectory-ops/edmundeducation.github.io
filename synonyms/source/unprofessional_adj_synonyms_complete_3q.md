# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Unprofessional · adj.

「不專業／不合職場規範／失禮／不具商務作風」，究竟是哪一種 `unprofessional`？

本課只處理 **unprofessional 作形容詞**。使用者提供的 **inappropriate at work, discourteous, not businesslike** 全部保留：`inappropriate at work` 最廣，直接表示不合職場規範；`discourteous` 只聚焦失禮／缺乏尊重；`not businesslike` 則偏向做事方式不夠正式、有條理或具商務作風。另加入 **improper**（正式地指不當）、**unbusinesslike**（書面商務語域）、**unsuitable for the workplace**（不適合工作場所）與 **unbecoming**（有失職位／身份）。

> 本課主要處理 `unprofessional = not behaving, appearing or communicating in a way that meets expected workplace, occupational or business standards`。
>
> 同義詞數量**不固定**；本課按職場規範、禮貌、商務作風、程序適當性、商業處事、工作場所適宜性與角色尊嚴選用 **7 個**替代表達。
>
> Format: **7 curated alternatives + 8 false-synonym contrasts + 3 exercises per target synonym (21 questions total)**.

---

# 01 同義詞指南


### 01 Inappropriate At Work
**在工作場合不恰當的；不合職場規範的**

使用者提供。`inappropriate at work` 是 `unprofessional` 最透明的解釋性改寫，表示某行為、言語、衣著或做法在職場環境下不合宜。它比 `discourteous` 更廣，也比 `not businesslike` 更直接點出 workplace norms。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Making personal remarks about colleagues is inappropriate at work.

**中文翻譯**

對同事作私人評論在工作場合並不恰當。

**USAGE CLUE**

- behaviour that violates expected workplace norms without necessarily being rude or incompetent

---


### 02 Discourteous
**不禮貌的；失禮的；無禮的**

使用者提供。`discourteous` 是較正式的詞，強調缺乏禮貌、尊重或基本禮節。它可描述 `unprofessional` 的其中一個具體面向，但不涵蓋能力不足、衣著不當或不可靠等其他問題。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> It was discourteous to interrupt the client repeatedly.

**中文翻譯**

一再打斷客戶說話是很失禮的。

**USAGE CLUE**

- showing a lack of politeness, respect or courtesy toward others

---


### 03 Not Businesslike
**不具商務作風的；不夠專業俐落的**

使用者提供。`not businesslike` 表示做事方式、溝通、外觀或安排不夠有條理、務實、正式或符合商務場合期待。它比 `unprofessional` 更偏 style and manner，而不是道德／禮貌判斷。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The presentation was friendly but not businesslike.

**中文翻譯**

這場簡報很親切，但不夠具商務作風。

**USAGE CLUE**

- lacking the organised, efficient, formal manner expected in business contexts

---


### 04 Improper
**不恰當的；不得體的；不合規範的**

`improper` 比 `inappropriate at work` 更正式，表示行為、程序或關係不符合應有規範、禮節或標準。它可用於 workplace conduct，尤其當語氣偏正式或政策性。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The investigation found improper use of company resources.

**中文翻譯**

調查發現有人不當使用公司資源。

**USAGE CLUE**

- conduct that violates accepted rules, standards or propriety

---


### 05 Unbusinesslike
**不具商務作風的；不符合商業習慣的**

較正式、較少見。`unbusinesslike` 表示處事方式不夠有條理、有效率、務實或正式，尤其在商業通信、會議、辦公流程中。它比 `not businesslike` 更簡潔，但語氣更書面。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The letter was vague and unbusinesslike.

**中文翻譯**

那封信內容含糊，而且不具商務作風。

**USAGE CLUE**

- lacking the efficient, organised or formal manner associated with business practice

---


### 06 Unsuitable For The Workplace
**不適合工作場所的；不宜在職場出現的**

解釋性片語。`unsuitable for the workplace` 強調某言語、衣著、裝飾、內容或行為不適合出現在工作環境。它比 `inappropriate at work` 更像 suitability judgment，也特別適合 policy or HR guidance。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The image was considered unsuitable for the workplace.

**中文翻譯**

該圖片被認為不適合在工作場所出現。

**USAGE CLUE**

- content, appearance or behaviour judged unsuitable for a workplace environment

---


### 07 Unbecoming
**不得體的；有失身分的；不相稱的**

較正式、較傳統。`unbecoming` 表示某行為不符合某人的職位、身份、角色或應有尊嚴。它比一般 `unprofessional` 更有『有失身分』的色彩。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Such behaviour is unbecoming of a senior manager.

**中文翻譯**

這種行為有失高級經理的身分。

**USAGE CLUE**

- behaviour considered inconsistent with the dignity or standards of a person's role

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 unprofessional / inappropriate at work

`inappropriate at work` 是透明解釋；`unprofessional` 更自然、更精簡，也可涵蓋能力、可靠性與形象問題。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The joke was inappropriate at work.  
> His conduct was unprofessional.

---


## 02 unprofessional / discourteous

`discourteous` 只表示失禮／不尊重；一件事可以 unprofessional 而並不無禮，例如文件格式混亂。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The reply was discourteous.  
> The report looked unprofessional.

---


## 03 unprofessional / not businesslike

`not businesslike` 強調做事方式、外觀或流程不夠商務化；`unprofessional` 範圍更廣。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The letter was not businesslike.  
> Mocking a colleague was unprofessional.

---


## 04 unprofessional / improper

`improper` 更正式，也可涉及程序、道德或規則違反；`unprofessional` 更聚焦專業／職場標準。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The payment was improper.  
> His workplace conduct was unprofessional.

---


## 05 unprofessional / incompetent

`incompetent` 指能力不足；unprofessional 可以是態度、衣著、語氣或禮貌問題，未必代表能力差。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The technician was incompetent.  
> The technician's rude email was unprofessional.

---


## 06 unprofessional / casual

`casual` 只表示隨意，不一定負面；某些工作環境本來就允許 casual dress 或語氣。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The office has a casual dress code.  
> His behaviour was unprofessional.

---


## 07 unprofessional / unethical

`unethical` 表示違反道德原則，通常比 unprofessional 更嚴重；不專業未必不道德。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Falsifying records is unethical.  
> Arriving badly prepared was unprofessional.

---


## 08 unprofessional / professional

`professional` 是相反方向的正面形容詞；不是近義詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Her response was professional.  
> His response was unprofessional.

# 05 高混淆組

## unprofessional vs inappropriate at work
- **unprofessional**：更自然、更廣。
- **inappropriate at work**：透明解釋，直接標示職場適宜性。

## discourteous vs unprofessional
- **discourteous**：禮貌問題。
- **unprofessional**：可禮貌、流程、能力、形象等多方面。

## not businesslike vs unbusinesslike
- **not businesslike**：較透明口語。
- **unbusinesslike**：較書面、正式。

## improper vs unprofessional
- **improper**：規範／程序／適當性。
- **unprofessional**：專業或職場標準。

## unsuitable for the workplace vs inappropriate at work
- **unsuitable for the workplace**：偏 content/appearance suitability。
- **inappropriate at work**：更廣泛行為判斷。

## unbecoming vs improper
- **unbecoming**：有失身份／職位。
- **improper**：一般不當／不合規範。

## unprofessional vs incompetent
- **unprofessional**：不符合職場標準。
- **incompetent**：能力不足。

## unprofessional vs unethical
- **unprofessional**：不一定涉及道德。
- **unethical**：違反道德原則。

# 08 生成／評估注意事項

1. **同義詞數量不是固定值。** 本課按職場規範、禮貌、商務作風、程序適當性、商業處事、工作場所適宜性與角色尊嚴選 **7 個**。
2. 因此本課有 **3 exercises per target = 21 exercises**。
3. 使用者提供的 `inappropriate at work`, `discourteous`, `not businesslike` 全部保留。
4. `inappropriate at work` 只在 workplace norm 是核心時使用。
5. `discourteous` 需要 politeness/respect clue。
6. `not businesslike` 需要 presentation/organisation/business-manner clue。
7. `improper` 需要 formal norm/procedure/propriety clue。
8. `unbusinesslike` 需要 business process/document/efficiency clue。
9. `unsuitable for the workplace` 需要 content/appearance suitability clue。
10. `unbecoming` 需要 status/role/dignity clue。
11. False synonyms 特別測試 inappropriate at work/discourteous/not businesslike/improper/incompetent/casual/unethical/professional。
12. Distractors 全部使用真實自然英文詞／片語，不使用人工標籤。
13. 每題以 decisive clue 建立唯一答案，例如 `dismissive and rude`, `inconsistent formatting`, `confidential case`, `key details missing`, `the office he held`。
14. 原始 `unprofessional` 句子保持自然；較窄詞只在上下文真正支援時使用。
15. 本課只處理 **unprofessional 作形容詞**。
