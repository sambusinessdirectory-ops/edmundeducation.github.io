# UNWANTED · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Unwanted

**Unwanted 可以表示『不再想要、不需要、數量過多、不受歡迎、已棄置、功能多餘、已過時，或已進入處置流程』。** 這些原因不能混為一談。替換前要先判斷：**Is it unwanted because of preference, lack of need, excess quantity, redundancy, obsolescence, or disposal status?**

你提供的 **no longer desired; surplus; discarded** 正好代表三個核心方向：**不再想要、數量多餘、已被丟棄**。

本次保留 **9 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達。會特別區分 **unused / old / broken / unpopular**，避免把『未使用』『舊』『損壞』『不受歡迎』誤當 unwanted。共 **27 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Unwanted 的語境 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|
| 不再想要 | **no longer desired** |
| 數量超出需要 | **surplus / excess** |
| 已經被丟棄 | **discarded** |
| 功能上不需要 | **unneeded / redundant** |
| 不受歡迎／令人困擾 | **unwelcome** |
| 技術／設備過時 | **obsolete** |
| 正式資產處置 | **items to be disposed of** |

## Tier 1 — Core desire / excess / disposal equivalents · 核心需求、過量與棄置近義詞

### 01 No longer desired

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：不再需要的；不再想要的  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：something that was once wanted or useful but is not wanted any more  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **unwanted** 最直接、最透明的改寫之一，尤其當重點是需求或偏好已改變。  
**常見搭配**：no longer desired; no longer wanted; unwanted item; no longer needed.  
**常見搭配對象**：item, possession, product, clothing, furniture.  
**與 unwanted 的辨析**：No longer desired 強調『以前想要，現在不想要』；unwanted 本身不一定表示曾經想要。  
**使用限制**：如果物件從來都不受歡迎，unwanted 更簡潔。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The charity collects household items that are no longer desired.  
慈善機構收集已不再需要的家居用品。

### 02 Surplus

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：多餘的；剩餘的  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：more than is needed or required  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很自然，當 unwanted 的原因是數量過多、超出需要時最準確。  
**常見搭配**：surplus stock; surplus equipment; surplus materials; surplus goods.  
**常見搭配對象**：stock, equipment, material, food, property.  
**與 unwanted 的辨析**：Surplus 強調 excess quantity；unwanted 可以只是因為不喜歡或不合用。  
**使用限制**：如果只是某件物品不想要，但不是因為過量，surplus 不一定適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The company sold its surplus office equipment.  
公司出售多餘的辦公室設備。

### 03 Discarded

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：被丟棄的；被棄置的  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：thrown away or abandoned because it is no longer wanted or useful  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 適合描述已經被處理掉的 unwanted items。  
**常見搭配**：discarded clothing; discarded packaging; discarded materials; discarded items.  
**常見搭配對象**：clothing, packaging, waste, material, object.  
**與 unwanted 的辨析**：Discarded 表示『已經丟棄』；unwanted 只是狀態，不一定真的被扔掉。  
**使用限制**：仍然保留着但不想要的物品不能自然稱 discarded。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Discarded packaging was collected for recycling.  
被丟棄的包裝被收集作回收。

### 04 Unneeded

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：不需要的；多餘的  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：not required for a particular purpose  
**用法提示**：當 unwanted 的重點是『沒有需要』而非情感上不喜歡時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：unneeded equipment; unneeded files; unneeded purchases; unneeded items.  
**常見搭配對象**：equipment, file, feature, cost, item.  
**與 unwanted 的辨析**：Unneeded 強調 necessity；unwanted 強調 desire / acceptance。  
**使用限制**：一件東西可能 needed but unwanted，兩者不能永遠互換。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The update removes several unneeded files.  
更新會移除數個不需要的檔案。

### 05 Unwelcome

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：不受歡迎的；不想要的  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：not wanted, appreciated, or welcomed, especially when referring to events, attention, consequences, or visitors  
**用法提示**：當 unwanted 修飾 attention, intrusion, change, consequence 等抽象事物時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：unwelcome attention; unwelcome visitor; unwelcome change; unwelcome consequence.  
**常見搭配對象**：attention, visitor, change, consequence, interference.  
**與 unwanted 的辨析**：Unwelcome 常有『不受歡迎／令人困擾』語氣；unwanted 更中性。  
**使用限制**：對庫存、材料、設備等物品，surplus/unneeded 通常更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The announcement brought unwelcome attention to the family.  
這項公布令這個家庭受到不受歡迎的關注。

## Tier 2 — Redundancy / excess / obsolescence variants · 多餘、過量與過時變體

### 06 Redundant

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：多餘的；不再需要的  
**語域**：formal / technical / British English  
**核心語義**：no longer necessary or useful because something else already performs the function  
**用法提示**：當 unwanted 的重點是『功能上不再需要／重複』時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：redundant equipment; redundant feature; redundant system; become redundant.  
**常見搭配對象**：equipment, feature, process, system, role.  
**與 unwanted 的辨析**：Redundant 強調 unnecessary because duplicated or obsolete；unwanted 更廣。  
**使用限制**：英式英語中 redundant 亦可指職位被裁撤；需要注意語境。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The software update makes several old features redundant.  
軟件更新令數個舊功能變得多餘。

### 07 Excess

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：過多的；超額的  
**語域**：formal / technical  
**核心語義**：beyond the amount needed, allowed, or useful  
**用法提示**：當 unwanted 指『超過需要的部分』時適合，尤其材料、庫存、容量等。  
**常見搭配**：excess stock; excess material; excess capacity; excess inventory.  
**常見搭配對象**：stock, material, capacity, inventory, supply.  
**與 unwanted 的辨析**：Excess 比 surplus 更偏『超出正常／需要量』，常帶數量概念。  
**使用限制**：不能用來表示單純情感上的不想要。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The factory sold excess stock at a discount.  
工廠以折扣價出售過量庫存。

### 08 Obsolete

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：過時的；淘汰的  
**語域**：formal / technical  
**核心語義**：no longer useful or current because it has been replaced by something newer or better  
**用法提示**：當 unwanted 是因為科技、設備、做法已過時時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：obsolete equipment; obsolete technology; obsolete system; become obsolete.  
**常見搭配對象**：technology, device, equipment, software, method.  
**與 unwanted 的辨析**：Obsolete 強調 outdated / superseded；unwanted 不一定因過時。  
**使用限制**：仍然現代但不想要的物品不能叫 obsolete。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Many obsolete devices are sent for recycling.  
很多過時裝置會被送去回收。

## Tier 3 — Administrative disposal paraphrase · 行政處置型改寫

### 09 Items to be disposed of

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：待處置物品；準備棄置的物品  
**語域**：formal / administrative  
**核心語義**：items identified for removal, disposal, recycling, or other final handling  
**用法提示**：這是行政、資產管理、清理或回收語境中對 unwanted items 的精確情境改寫。  
**常見搭配**：items to be disposed of; disposal list; asset disposal; dispose of unwanted items.  
**常見搭配對象**：furniture, equipment, stock, asset, waste.  
**與 unwanted 的辨析**：比 unwanted 更程序化，表示已進入 disposal decision。  
**使用限制**：一般口語會顯得太正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
All items to be disposed of must be recorded before collection.  
所有待處置物品在收集前都必須登記。

## 04 False synonyms / unwanted traps · 容易誤選的『未使用、舊、損壞』陷阱

### 01 Unused

**未使用 ≠ unwanted**  
unused 只表示沒有使用，可能仍然想保留。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Unused does not mean unwanted.

### 02 Unpopular

**不受歡迎 ≠ unwanted**  
unpopular 主要指很多人不喜歡，不是某人不再需要。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Unpopular is preference/popularity-focused.

### 03 Broken

**損壞 ≠ unwanted**  
broken 物品可能仍然想修理或保留。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Broken describes condition, not desirability.

### 04 Old

**舊 ≠ unwanted**  
old 只是年齡／時間狀態，不等於不想要。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Old does not necessarily mean unwanted.

### 05 Discarded

**只在已丟棄 sense 接近**  
discarded 表示處置已發生。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Discarded is outcome-focused.

### 06 Surplus

**只在數量過多 sense 接近**  
surplus 是因為超出需要，不等於任何 unwanted item。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Surplus is quantity-focused.

### 07 Redundant

**只在功能多餘 sense 接近**  
redundant 常表示功能被取代或重複。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Redundant is function-focused.

### 08 Unwanted / unwantedly

**詞形陷阱**  
unwanted 是形容詞；unwantedly 極少用且不自然。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Use unwanted mainly as an adjective.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: No longer desired · 不再需要的；不再想要的

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The shop collects unwanted clothes for charity.

**中文翻譯**  
商店收集不再需要的衣物作慈善用途。

- A. No longer desired
- B. Unneeded
- C. Surplus
- D. Unused
- E. Old
- F. Broken

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. No longer desired** · 不再需要的；不再想要的

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The shop collects clothes that are no longer desired for charity.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **No longer desired ✓** — previously wanted or useful but not wanted any more. Here, owners no longer want the items. That makes **No longer desired** the most precise replacement for this particular sense of *unwanted*.
- **B.** **Unneeded** — not required for a particular purpose. This focuses on lack of necessity, not desire, excess quantity, disposal status, or obsolescence. The decisive clue is that owners no longer want the items.
- **C.** **Surplus** — more than is needed or required. This specifically means more than is needed and is too quantity-focused elsewhere. The decisive clue is that owners no longer want the items.
- **D.** **Unused** — not currently used. Something can be unused but still wanted or valuable. The decisive clue is that owners no longer want the items.
- **E.** **Old** — not new. Age alone does not mean an item is unwanted. The decisive clue is that owners no longer want the items.
- **F.** **Broken** — damaged and not functioning properly. Damage is a condition and does not tell us whether the item is wanted. The decisive clue is that owners no longer want the items.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **unwanted vs no longer desired**：後者明確表示以前可能想要，現在不再需要。
- **unwanted vs surplus**：surplus 是因為數量超出需要。
- **unwanted vs discarded**：discarded 表示已經被棄置。
- **unwanted vs unneeded**：unneeded 是功能／需要層面，不是情感偏好。
- **unwanted vs unwelcome**：unwelcome 常用於 attention, visitor, consequence 等。
- **unwanted vs redundant**：redundant 表示功能被取代或重複。
- **unwanted vs excess**：excess 是數量過多。
- **unwanted vs obsolete**：obsolete 是過時、被新技術取代。
- **unwanted vs unused**：unused 只表示未使用，不等於不想要。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & disposal traps

- **unwanted + noun**：unwanted items / attention / messages / effects。
- **no longer desired / no longer wanted**：透明改寫。
- **surplus + stock/equipment/materials**：數量超出需要。
- **discarded + clothing/packaging/materials**：處置已發生。
- **unneeded + files/features/steps**：功能上不需要。
- **unwelcome + attention/change/visitor/consequence**。
- **redundant + equipment/feature/system**：功能被取代／重複。
- **obsolete + technology/equipment/software/format**：過時。
- **dispose of + unwanted items**：處置不要的物品。
- **unused / old / broken** 都不能單獨證明某物是 unwanted。
