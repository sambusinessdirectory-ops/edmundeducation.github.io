# TEAMWORK · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Teamwork · noun

**Teamwork = people working together effectively towards a shared goal.** 它可以強調 **共同協作、合力完成任務、互相配合、整隊共同成果、角色協調、集體投入，或單純『一起工作』的過程**。

你提供的 **collaboration; joint effort** 正好代表兩個核心方向：**共同協作** 與 **大家一起出力**。

本次保留 **7 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達，共 **21 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。沒有把 **communication, leadership, friendship, partnership** 等相關但不同的概念當作全面同義詞。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Teamwork 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 |
|---|---|
| 不同專業／部門共同協作 | **collaboration** |
| 大家一起出力完成事情 | **joint effort** |
| 彼此願意配合 | **cooperation** |
| 整個團隊共同完成成果 | **team effort** |
| 多角色／多部門同步協調 | **coordinated effort** |
| 整個社群／大群體共同投入 | **collective effort** |
| 日常說『一起合作』 | **working together** |

## Tier 1 — Core collaboration / cooperation / team-effort equivalents · 核心協作、配合與團隊努力近義詞

### 01 Collaboration

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：協作；合作  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：the act of working with others to achieve a shared result, especially by contributing different skills or ideas  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **teamwork** 最核心、最自然的近義詞之一，特別適合 work, research, design, education, business 等語境。  
**常見搭配**：close collaboration; effective collaboration; collaboration between teams; collaborative work.  
**常見搭配對象**：team, department, researcher, designer, organisation.  
**與 teamwork 的辨析**：Collaboration 強調共同工作與整合不同貢獻；teamwork 更強調一個團隊作為整體有效合作。  
**使用限制**：如果只是彼此配合、避免衝突，而沒有共同創作／共同完成任務，cooperation 可能更精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The project succeeded because of close collaboration between the design and engineering teams.  
這個項目成功，有賴設計和工程團隊之間的緊密協作。

### 02 Joint effort

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：共同努力；合力  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：an effort made together by two or more people or groups  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，當 **teamwork** 的重點是『大家一起出力』而不是團隊運作方式時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：a joint effort; through joint effort; joint effort by; require a joint effort.  
**常見搭配對象**：team, department, community, organisation, group.  
**與 teamwork 的辨析**：Joint effort 聚焦共同投入和付出；teamwork 還包括溝通、分工、互相配合。  
**使用限制**：如果要描述長期團隊文化或合作方式，collaboration / cooperation 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Finishing the event on time was a joint effort by everyone involved.  
準時完成活動是所有參與者共同努力的成果。

### 03 Cooperation

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：合作；配合  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：the act of working together willingly and constructively towards a shared aim  
**用法提示**：當 **teamwork** 的關鍵是配合、互相支持、願意合作時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：close cooperation; full cooperation; cooperation between; improve cooperation.  
**常見搭配對象**：staff, department, agency, colleague, group.  
**與 teamwork 的辨析**：Cooperation 比 teamwork 更廣，可以發生在不同團隊、機構甚至對手之間，而不必形成一個 team。  
**使用限制**：需要強調共同創作、共同行動或明確團隊身份時，collaboration / team effort 更貼切。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The smooth handover depended on cooperation between the two departments.  
順利交接有賴兩個部門之間的合作。

### 04 Team effort

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：團隊努力；全隊合力  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：work or effort contributed by members of a team towards a shared goal  
**用法提示**：當 **teamwork** 直接指『這是整個團隊共同完成的成果』時，是非常自然的替代。  
**常見搭配**：a real team effort; complete team effort; result of a team effort; team effort from everyone.  
**常見搭配對象**：project, event, success, recovery, achievement.  
**與 teamwork 的辨析**：Team effort 聚焦共同完成某件事的成果／投入；teamwork 更像合作能力或運作方式。  
**使用限制**：跨機構合作、長期合作文化未必最適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The successful launch was a real team effort.  
這次成功推出產品是真正的團隊共同努力成果。

## Tier 2 — Coordinated / collective-effort variants · 協調與集體努力變體

### 05 Coordinated effort

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：協調一致的共同努力  
**語域**：formal / organisational  
**核心語義**：a shared effort in which different people or groups organise their actions so they work together effectively  
**用法提示**：當 **teamwork** 強調 coordination, timing, roles, logistics，尤其 emergency response, operations, large projects 時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：coordinated effort; coordinated response; carefully coordinated effort; require coordination.  
**常見搭配對象**：emergency team, department, agency, operation, project.  
**與 teamwork 的辨析**：Coordinated effort 比 teamwork 更強調分工、時序和行動同步。  
**使用限制**：簡單的小組合作若沒有明顯 coordination 線索，會太正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The rescue operation required a carefully coordinated effort from several teams.  
救援行動需要多個團隊作出仔細協調的共同努力。

### 06 Collective effort

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：集體努力；共同付出  
**語域**：formal / broad  
**核心語義**：effort made by a group as a whole rather than by one individual  
**用法提示**：當 **teamwork** 強調 community, organisation, class, workforce 等整體群體共同投入時自然。  
**常見搭配**：collective effort; through collective effort; collective effort of; require collective effort.  
**常見搭配對象**：community, staff, class, organisation, public.  
**與 teamwork 的辨析**：Collective effort 比 teamwork 更廣，可指很大的群體，未必是一個正式 team。  
**使用限制**：若句子講小型團隊中的互相協作，team effort / collaboration 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Reducing waste across the campus will require a collective effort.  
要在整個校園減少浪費，需要大家共同努力。

## Tier 3 — Conversational process paraphrase · 日常『一起合作』情境改寫

### 07 Working together

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：一起合作；共同工作  
**語域**：neutral / conversational  
**核心語義**：people combining their work or actions to achieve something together  
**用法提示**：這是 **teamwork** 最自然的日常動詞式改寫，適合一般口語和教學。  
**常見搭配**：work together effectively; by working together; keep working together; learn to work together.  
**常見搭配對象**：people, colleagues, students, teams, departments.  
**與 teamwork 的辨析**：Working together 很自然但較一般；它不像 teamwork 那樣是名詞概念。  
**使用限制**：需要名詞位置時通常要重構句子。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
By working together, the students finished the task much faster.  
學生一起合作，令任務快得多完成。

## 04 False synonyms / teamwork traps · 容易誤選的友誼、溝通、領導與合作關係詞

### 01 Friendship

**友誼 ≠ teamwork**  
關係友好不代表能有效合作完成任務。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Friendship is relationship-focused.

### 02 Communication

**溝通 ≠ teamwork**  
溝通是 teamwork 的一部分，但不是 teamwork 本身。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Communication is one component.

### 03 Leadership

**領導 ≠ teamwork**  
領導可以促進 teamwork，但不是團隊合作本身。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Leadership is role-focused.

### 04 Cooperation

**廣義近義詞但不完全相同**  
cooperation 可以跨團隊／跨機構，不必有 team identity。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Cooperation is broader.

### 05 Collaboration

**只在共同工作／共創 sense 高度接近**  
collaboration 強調共同產出與整合貢獻。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Collaboration is joint-work-focused.

### 06 Coordination

**協調 ≠ teamwork**  
coordination 只是把行動／時間安排配合好。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Coordination is organisation-focused.

### 07 Partnership

**夥伴關係 ≠ teamwork**  
partnership 是關係結構，不等於實際合作表現。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Partnership is relationship/structure-focused.

### 08 Team / teamwork

**詞性陷阱**  
team 是名詞／動詞；teamwork 是不可數名詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check word class.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Collaboration · 協作；合作

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

Good teamwork between the writers and designers improved the final product.

**中文翻譯**  
編寫團隊與設計團隊的良好合作改善了最終成品。

- A. Collaboration
- B. Cooperation
- C. Team effort
- D. Friendship
- E. Communication
- F. Leadership

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Collaboration** · 協作；合作

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
Close collaboration between the writers and designers improved the final product.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Collaboration ✓** — working with others to achieve a shared result, often by combining different skills or ideas。本句關鍵是 **different specialist contributions are integrated**；因此 **Collaboration** 最精準。
- **B.** **Cooperation** — working together willingly and constructively towards a shared aim。**cooperation** 強調願意配合，可跨團隊／跨機構；若句子突出共創、明確 team achievement 或同步分工，另有更精準詞。
- **C.** **Team effort** — work or effort contributed by members of a team towards a shared goal。**team effort** 聚焦整隊共同投入完成某項成果；若句子重點是跨機構合作或長期協作方式，會較窄。
- **D.** **Friendship** — a relationship of mutual affection。**friendship** 是人際關係；朋友關係好不代表一定有有效 teamwork。
- **E.** **Communication** — exchange of information or ideas。**communication** 是 teamwork 的重要部分，但只涵蓋訊息交換。
- **F.** **Leadership** — the act or ability of leading。**leadership** 是帶領團隊的角色／能力，不是團隊合作本身。

**C. Working together** · 一起合作；共同工作

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Collaboration** — working with others to achieve a shared result, often by combining different skills or ideas。**collaboration** 很接近 teamwork，但更強調共同工作和整合不同貢獻；若只講配合或單一成果，另有更精準詞。
- **B.** **Cooperation** — working together willingly and constructively towards a shared aim。**cooperation** 強調願意配合，可跨團隊／跨機構；若句子突出共創、明確 team achievement 或同步分工，另有更精準詞。
- **C.** **Working together ✓** — people combining their work or actions to achieve something together。本句關鍵是 **natural conversational restructuring is central**；因此 **Working together** 最精準。
- **D.** **Friendship** — a relationship of mutual affection。**friendship** 是人際關係；朋友關係好不代表一定有有效 teamwork。
- **E.** **Leadership** — the act or ability of leading。**leadership** 是帶領團隊的角色／能力，不是團隊合作本身。
- **F.** **Partnership** — a relationship in which people or organisations work together。**partnership** 是合作關係的結構，不等於實際 teamwork 品質。

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Teamwork 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 設計、研究、跨學科共同工作 | **collaboration** | 是否 different contributions are integrated？ |
| 所有人一起出力完成任務 | **joint effort** | 是否 shared effort itself is central？ |
| 部門／機構之間願意配合 | **cooperation** | 是否 constructive cooperation is central？ |
| 比賽／簡報／恢復服務是全隊成果 | **team effort** | 是否 the outcome belongs to the team as a whole？ |
| 救援／活動／營運需多角色同步 | **coordinated effort** | 是否 timing and roles must be coordinated？ |
| 社群／全校／大型群體共同投入 | **collective effort** | 是否 a broad group acts as a whole？ |
| 日常說『一起做／一起合作』 | **working together** | 是否 a natural verb-like reformulation is best？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **teamwork vs collaboration**：collaboration 更強調共同工作與整合不同技能／想法。
- **teamwork vs joint effort**：joint effort 強調共同付出，不一定描述合作方式。
- **teamwork vs cooperation**：cooperation 更廣，可跨團隊、跨機構。
- **teamwork vs team effort**：team effort 偏一次具體成果或共同投入。
- **teamwork vs coordinated effort**：後者更強調分工、時序、同步。
- **teamwork vs collective effort**：collective effort 可涵蓋更大的群體，不必是正式 team。
- **teamwork vs working together**：後者是日常動詞式改寫。
- **teamwork vs communication**：communication 只是 teamwork 的一部分。
- **teamwork vs leadership**：leadership 能促進 teamwork，但不是 teamwork 本身。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & teamwork traps

- **good/effective/strong teamwork**：teamwork 通常不可數。
- **teamwork between/among + people/groups**。
- **collaboration between + teams/departments**。
- **joint effort by + people/groups**。
- **cooperation between/among + groups**。
- **a team effort**：可數，常描述一次具體成果。
- **a coordinated effort by/from + groups**。
- **collective effort of/from + group**。
- **by working together**：自然重構 teamwork 句子。
- **team** 可作名詞／動詞；**teamwork** 是名詞。
