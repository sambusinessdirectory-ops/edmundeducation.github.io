# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Listing

**廣告、商品刊登、物品貼文、交易平台條目、目錄項目、商品頁——究竟是哪一種 listing？**

本課聚焦：

**listing = a public or structured entry that presents an item for sale, usually with information such as price, description, photographs, condition, and seller details**

核心近義與近義表達：

**advertisement; ad; product listing; item listing; item posting; marketplace listing; online listing; for-sale listing; classified ad; catalogue entry; product page**

> **Precision rule:**  
> `product listing / item listing` = 最直接的電商／平台用語。  
> `advertisement / ad` = 更偏宣傳。  
> `item posting` = 更偏平台貼文。  
> `marketplace / online listing` = 強調發佈媒介。  
> `for-sale listing` = 明確是出售。  
> `classified ad` = 分類廣告 subtype。  
> `catalogue entry` = 偏目錄／資料。  
> `product page` = 完整商品頁面。  
> `sales entry` **不是自然的直接同義詞**，通常較像內部銷售紀錄。

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Advertisement

**廣告；商品廣告**

當 `listing` 指公開展示、推廣或招徠買家的銷售資訊時很接近。比 listing 更偏『廣告功能』，不一定是平台上的結構化商品頁。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The seller updated the advertisement with new photos.

賣家用新照片更新了商品廣告。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- place an advertisement

- online advertisement

- sales advertisement

- advertisement for

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Sales entry

**不是自然的 marketplace listing 同義詞**

`sales entry` 更容易理解為銷售系統、帳簿或資料庫中的一筆銷售紀錄，而不是公開待售頁。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A listing advertises an item for sale; a sales entry normally records a sale or sales data.

---

## 02 Sales record

**已售紀錄不等於待售刊登**

sales record 通常記錄已發生的交易、金額或歷史銷售資料。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The listing exists before the sale; the sales record is created after or during the transaction.

---

## 03 Product page

**頁面不等於所有 listing**

product page 可以承載一個 listing，但某些 listing 只是搜尋結果條目、卡片或簡短貼文。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A product page may contain a listing, but a listing need not occupy a full page.

---

## 04 Advertisement

**廣告很接近，但更偏宣傳**

有些 listing 主要是結構化資訊，而不是廣告式文案。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A product listing may be factual and structured rather than promotional.

---

## 05 Catalogue entry

**目錄條目不一定在出售**

catalogue entry 可以只是記錄產品資訊，未必有價格或可購買狀態。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A museum catalogue entry is not a sales listing.

---

## 06 Post

**貼文範圍太廣**

post 可以是社交媒體內容、論壇帖文、公告等，不一定涉及商品。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

An item posting is a sales-related post; a post in general may have nothing to sell.

---

## 07 Offer

**出價／優惠不等於 listing**

offer 可能是買家的出價，也可能是促銷優惠；listing 是公開刊登本身。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A buyer can make an offer on a listing.

---

## 08 Inventory entry

**庫存條目不等於公開刊登**

inventory entry 是內部存貨記錄，可能從未公開給買家。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A store may have an inventory entry for an item that has no public listing.

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Listing is countable
自然：
- a listing
- several listings
- create a listing
- remove a listing

## 02 Listing vs advertisement
- listing = 結構化刊登／條目
- advertisement = 廣告，較強調宣傳與招徠

## 03 Product listing vs product page
- product listing = 商品刊登本身
- product page = 承載完整商品資訊的頁面

搜尋結果中的商品卡片可以是 listing，但未必是一個獨立 product page。

## 04 Item listing
特別自然於：
- resale platforms
- peer-to-peer marketplaces
- second-hand goods
- auction platforms

## 05 Classified ad
常見於：
- newspapers
- classifieds websites
- local sales boards
- short-form sales notices

## 06 Catalogue entry
偏資訊化：
- product code
- dimensions
- specifications
- catalogue classification

未必代表商品目前可售。

## 07 Sales entry
`sales entry` 通常不是 marketplace listing 的自然直接同義詞。

它較可能表示：
- accounting record
- CRM entry
- sales database row
- transaction record
