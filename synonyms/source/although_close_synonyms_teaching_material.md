# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Although

**「雖然／儘管」，有哪些真正接近、幾乎可以互換的說法？**

> 本課只保留和 **although = 雖然；儘管** 在讓步（concession）語境中**非常接近或常可互換**的表達。
> 也會加入自然常見的固定表達／慣用結構，只要它們在功能上和 `although` 很接近。
>
> 每個 target expression 配 **3 道練習題**。

---

## 01 Close synonyms and common expressions

### 01 Though

**雖然；儘管**

和 `although` 最接近的說法之一，通常可以直接互換。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

Though it was raining, they continued the match.

雖然下著雨，他們仍繼續比賽。

**USAGE · 用法提示**

`though` 比 `although` 稍口語。  
而且 `though` 還可以放在句尾：

> It was difficult. I enjoyed it, though.

這種句尾用法不能直接改成 `although`。

---

### 02 Even though

**即使；雖然**

比 `although` 更強，強調前後內容有明顯反差。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

Even though he was exhausted, he kept working.

即使他筋疲力盡，仍繼續工作。

**USAGE · 用法提示**

如果讓步意味較強，`even though` 比 `although` 更自然。

---

### 03 While

**雖然；儘管**

在正式或中性寫作中，可用來引入一個讓步或對比。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

While the plan has advantages, it also carries significant risks.

雖然這個計劃有優點，但也帶有重大風險。

**USAGE · 用法提示**

`while` 也可以表示「當……的時候」，所以要靠上下文判斷。

---

### 04 Whereas

**雖然／而；相比之下**

常用來表達兩個事實的強烈對比。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

Whereas the first option is cheaper, the second is more reliable.

雖然第一個選項較便宜，但第二個更可靠。

**USAGE · 用法提示**

和 `although` 不完全相同；`whereas` 更強調「A 與 B 的對比」。

---

### 05 Whilst

**雖然；儘管**

英式英語中可作 `while` 的較正式變體。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

Whilst the proposal is ambitious, it may be difficult to implement.

雖然這個提案很有抱負，但可能難以實施。

**USAGE · 用法提示**

英式英語較常見，美式英語較少使用。

---

### 06 Despite the fact that

**儘管……這個事實**

和 `although` 很接近，但結構更長、更正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

Despite the fact that the road was closed, some drivers tried to continue.

儘管道路已封閉，仍有一些司機試圖繼續前進。

**USAGE · 用法提示**

比 `although` 更重、更正式。

---

### 07 In spite of the fact that

**儘管……這個事實**

和 `despite the fact that` 幾乎相同。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

In spite of the fact that she was nervous, she gave an excellent presentation.

儘管她很緊張，她仍做了一場出色的簡報。

**USAGE · 用法提示**

完整句型可替代 `although + clause`。

---

### 08 Granted that

**即使承認；雖然**

表示先承認某一點成立，再提出另一個限制或反駁。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

Granted that the project is expensive, it could still save money in the long term.

即使承認這個項目很昂貴，長遠而言仍可能省錢。

**USAGE · 用法提示**

較正式，也帶「先讓步，再提出另一點」的論證感。

---

### 09 Admittedly

**誠然；固然**

常用在正式寫作中，先承認一個事實，再補充轉折。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

Admittedly, the system is expensive, but it is extremely reliable.

誠然，這個系統很昂貴，但它非常可靠。

**USAGE · 用法提示**

不是連接詞，而是句子副詞。  
功能上和 `although` 很接近，但句法不同。

---

### 10 Much as

**儘管很……；雖然非常……**

常用於較自然或稍文學化的表達，尤其表示「雖然我很……」。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

Much as I like the idea, I don't think we can afford it.

雖然我很喜歡這個想法，但我不認為我們負擔得起。

**USAGE · 用法提示**

常見結構：

> Much as I would like to..., ...

不能在所有 `although` 句子中直接替換。

---

### 11 For all that

**儘管如此；雖然如此**

較書面或稍舊式，表示「即使前面是真的，後面仍然成立」。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

For all that he had little experience, he performed remarkably well.

儘管他經驗不多，表現卻非常出色。

**USAGE · 用法提示**

較少見，但屬於自然的讓步表達。

---

### 12 Be that as it may

**即便如此；話雖如此**

常見固定表達，用來承認前面的觀點後，轉回自己的主要論點。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

The plan may be popular. Be that as it may, we still need to examine the cost.

這個計劃可能很受歡迎。即便如此，我們仍需要審視成本。

**USAGE · 用法提示**

這不是 `although + clause` 的直接文法替換，但在 discourse function 上非常接近。

---

### 13 That said

**話雖如此；不過**

常見自然表達，用來承認前一句後加入轉折。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

The software is easy to use. That said, it lacks several important features.

這個軟件很容易使用。話雖如此，它缺少幾項重要功能。

**USAGE · 用法提示**

現代英語非常常見，尤其商業、評論和一般寫作。

---

### 14 All the same

**儘管如此；仍然**

表示即使前面的情況成立，後面的結果仍不變。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

The weather was terrible. All the same, we had a good time.

天氣很差。儘管如此，我們仍玩得很開心。

**USAGE · 用法提示**

更像 `nevertheless`，但在讓步功能上和 `although` 緊密相關。

## 02 最接近程度

### 幾乎可直接互換

- **though**
- **even though**
- **while**（讓步義）
- **whilst**（英式）
- **despite the fact that**
- **in spite of the fact that**

### 很接近，但更偏論證／正式語氣

- **whereas**
- **granted that**
- **admittedly**
- **much as**
- **for all that**

### 固定表達／discourse equivalents

- **be that as it may**
- **that said**
- **all the same**

## 03 False friends / Not close enough

### Because

**因為不等於雖然**

`because` 表示原因；`although` 表示讓步。

> Because it was raining, we stayed inside.  
> Although it was raining, we went outside.

---

### However

**但是不完全等於 although**

`however` 是副詞／連接副詞，通常連接兩個獨立句子。

> Although it was expensive, we bought it.  
> It was expensive. However, we bought it.

功能接近，但句法不同。

---

### Despite

**儘管，但後面不能直接接完整子句**

> Despite the rain, we went out.  
> Although it was raining, we went out.

不能說：

> ❌ Despite it was raining

---

### In spite of

和 `despite` 一樣，後面通常接名詞或動名詞。

> In spite of being tired, she continued.

---

### Nevertheless

非常接近讓步轉折，但通常不是從屬連接詞。

> Although it was risky, they continued.  
> It was risky. Nevertheless, they continued.

## 06 高階辨析補充

### Although vs Though

幾乎完全相同：

> Although it was late, we stayed.  
> Though it was late, we stayed.

`though` 更口語，而且能放句尾。

---

### Although vs Even though

- **although**：普通讓步。
- **even though**：反差更強。

> Although he was tired, he continued.  
> Even though he was exhausted, he continued.

---

### Although vs While

`while` 在分析／正式寫作中很常見：

> While the method is useful, it has limitations.

但要注意 `while` 也可以表示「當……時」。

---

### Although vs Whereas

- **although**：承認某事但後面仍然成立。
- **whereas**：更偏 A 與 B 形成對照。

---

### Although vs Despite

句法不同：

> Although it was raining, we went out.  
> Despite the rain, we went out.  
> Despite being tired, she continued.

---

### Although vs Admittedly

- **although**：從屬連接詞。
- **admittedly**：句子副詞。

> Although it is expensive, it is reliable.  
> Admittedly, it is expensive, but it is reliable.

---

### Although vs That said

`that said` 是跨句轉折：

> The plan is expensive. That said, it may still be worthwhile.

非常適合評論、商業寫作和分析。

---

### Although vs Be that as it may

`be that as it may` 是更固定、更修辭化的「即便如此」。

> He may be right. Be that as it may, we still need evidence.

## 07 常見搭配與句型

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| although + clause | Although it was late, we stayed. |
| though + clause | Though it was late, we stayed. |
| even though + clause | Even though it was late, we stayed. |
| while + clause | While the idea is useful, it has limits. |
| whereas + clause | Whereas A is cheaper, B is faster. |
| despite the fact that + clause | Despite the fact that it was late, we stayed. |
| granted that + clause | Granted that it is costly, it may still help. |
| admittedly, ... but ... | Admittedly, it is costly, but it works. |
| much as + clause | Much as I like it, I can't buy it. |
| that said, ... | It is costly. That said, it works well. |
| be that as it may, ... | It may be costly. Be that as it may, we need it. |
| all the same, ... | It was difficult. All the same, she succeeded. |

## 08 教學核心

如果只保留真正接近的說法，**although** 可以分成幾個主要方向：

- 最直接近義 → **though**
- 更強的讓步 → **even though**
- 正式／分析式讓步 → **while / whilst**
- 明顯兩方對比 → **whereas**
- 更正式完整結構 → **despite the fact that / in spite of the fact that**
- 論證式讓步 → **granted that / admittedly**
- 自然固定句型 → **much as / for all that**
- 跨句固定表達 → **be that as it may / that said / all the same**

最重要的界線是：

> **although 是讓步；because 是原因；however / nevertheless / that said 則通常是跨句轉折。**
