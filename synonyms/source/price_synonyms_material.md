# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Price


**價錢、成本、收費、費用、費率、票價、售價、叫價、零售價、購入價——究竟是哪一種 price？**


核心近義與近義表達：

**cost; charge; fee; rate; fare; tariff; selling price; asking price; retail price; purchase price**


> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **10 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。

---

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Cost

**成本；價錢；費用**

從買方或整體支出的角度表示取得商品、服務或完成某事所需付出的金額。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The cost of replacing the roof was higher than expected.

更換屋頂的費用比預期高。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- cost of living

- cost of repairs

- total cost

- rising cost


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 誰在收錢？誰在付款？是否按單位計價？是否已成交？是商品、服務、交通，還是正式費率？

---

## 02 Charge

**收費；費用**

表示商戶、公司或服務提供者要求支付的金額，常見於服務、附加項目或帳單。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The hotel adds a small charge for late checkout.

酒店會就延遲退房收取一筆小額費用。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- service charge

- delivery charge

- extra charge

- charge for


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 誰在收錢？誰在付款？是否按單位計價？是否已成交？是商品、服務、交通，還是正式費率？

---

## 03 Fee

**費用；手續費；服務費**

表示為專業服務、申請、會員資格、入場或行政程序支付的款項。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The university charges an application fee of HK$300.

大學收取三百港元申請費。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- application fee

- membership fee

- legal fee

- administration fee


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 誰在收錢？誰在付款？是否按單位計價？是否已成交？是商品、服務、交通，還是正式費率？

---

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Value

**價值不等於 price**

`value` 表示某物值多少或有多大價值；實際 price 可以高於或低於 perceived value。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The price is what is charged; the value is what something is considered to be worth.

---

## 02 Cost

**高度相關但視角不同**

cost 常從買方／支出角度出發，也可指企業生產成本；price 常是市場要求或標示的金額。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A shop's selling price must normally exceed its cost if it is to make a profit.

---

## 03 Charge

**收費不是所有 price**

charge 常見於服務、附加項目和帳單。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A delivery charge can be added to the price of a product.

---

## 04 Fee

**服務／行政費用不是商品售價**

fee 常與 lawyer, application, membership, tuition 等搭配。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A lawyer charges a fee; a shop displays a price.

---

## 05 Fare

**交通票價只是特定 subtype**

fare 專指交通費用。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The train fare is the price paid for the journey.

---

## 06 Rate

**費率不等於一次性價格**

rate 通常按 hour / night / kilometre / unit 等計算。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

An hourly rate determines the cost for each hour of work.

---

## 07 Worth

**詞性／結構陷阱**

`worth` 常用於『值……』結構，不是一般名詞 price 的直接替代。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The watch is worth $500; its price is $500.

---

## 08 Priced / pricing

**詞形陷阱**

`priced` 是過去分詞／形容詞；`pricing` 是定價活動／策略。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The product is competitively priced; the company reviews its pricing strategy.

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Price is countable
- a price
- prices
- the price of something

## 02 Price vs cost
- price = 賣方標示／要求／交易價格
- cost = 買方支出或生產成本

## 03 Charge vs fee
- charge = 廣義服務收費
- fee = 專業／行政／會員／申請費

## 04 Rate
常見：
- per hour
- per night
- per unit
- per kilometre

## 05 Fare
只適合交通：
- bus fare
- train fare
- air fare
- taxi fare

## 06 Asking price vs purchase price
- asking price = 賣方開價
- purchase price = 最終買入／成交價

## 07 Retail vs wholesale
- retail price = 最終消費者價格
- wholesale price = 批發價

## 08 Pricing
`pricing` = 定價策略／定價活動，不是單一 price。
