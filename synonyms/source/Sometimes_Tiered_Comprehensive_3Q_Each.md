# SOMETIMES · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Sometimes

**Sometimes 表示『有時／間中』，但不同近義詞會改變頻率、語域和事件模式。** 有些表達偏低頻（occasionally, once in a while），有些強調隔一段時間再發生（from time to time, every so often），有些偏口語（now and then），而 **sporadically** 更進一步表示零星、無規律。

你提供的 **occasionally; at times; now and then** 分別代表 **較低頻的一般偶爾、某些時候的狀態、口語間中** 三個核心方向。

本次保留 **9 個**自然且高實用度的近義／情境表達。這類詞數量不宜過多，因為很多頻率副詞其實會明顯改變原意。共 **27 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Sometimes 的語境 | 核心問題 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|---|
| 一般低頻『偶爾』 | 是否 infrequent but not rare？ | occasionally |
| 某些時候呈現某狀態 | 是否 describing a state/quality on some occasions？ | at times |
| 口語間中 | 是否 casual conversational tone？ | now and then, every now and then |
| 長期中不時發生 | 是否 recurring over time but not often？ | from time to time |
| 隔一陣一次 | 是否 recurring at irregular intervals？ | every so often |
| 很低頻口語 | 是否 fairly infrequent leisure/personal event？ | once in a while |
| 正式『偶爾』 | 是否 report/professional register？ | on occasion |
| 零星無規律 | 是否 rare + irregular + scattered？ | sporadically |

## Tier 1 — Core occasional / state-based / conversational equivalents · 核心偶爾、狀態型與口語近義詞

### 01 Occasionally

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：偶爾；有時  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：happening from time to time, but not often  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **sometimes** 最直接、最自然的近義詞之一，語氣稍比 sometimes 正式。  
**常見搭配**：occasionally visit; occasionally happen; occasionally feel; occur occasionally; occasionally use.  
**常見搭配對象**：event, visit, problem, feeling, activity.  
**與 sometimes 的辨析**：Occasionally 通常暗示頻率較低；sometimes 的頻率範圍較寬。  
**使用限制**：如果事情其實頗常發生，occasionally 可能太弱。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
I occasionally work from home on Fridays.  
我偶爾會在星期五在家工作。

### 02 At times

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：有時；間中  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：on some occasions, especially when contrasting one state or period with another  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很自然，常見於描述情況有時如此、有時不如此。  
**常見搭配**：at times difficult; at times frustrating; at times seem; at times feel; at times become.  
**常見搭配對象**：situation, feeling, condition, behaviour, experience.  
**與 sometimes 的辨析**：At times 很適合句中評論性描述；不像 occasionally 那樣純粹是頻率副詞。  
**使用限制**：不太適合精確頻率統計或極正式技術文本。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The work can be stressful at times.  
這份工作有時會令人感到壓力。

### 03 Now and then

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：間中；偶爾  
**語域**：informal / common  
**核心語義**：on some occasions, separated by intervals  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是口語中很自然的 sometimes 替代詞。  
**常見搭配**：now and then visit; every now and then; now and then feel; now and then check.  
**常見搭配對象**：visit, thought, problem, activity, event.  
**與 sometimes 的辨析**：Now and then 比 sometimes 更口語，也常帶『隔一陣就一次』的感覺。  
**使用限制**：正式研究或政策寫作通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
We still meet for coffee now and then.  
我們現在仍會間中一起喝咖啡。

### 04 From time to time

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：不時；間中；偶爾  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：on occasional occasions over a period of time  
**用法提示**：適合表示某件事隔一段時間會發生一次，口語與書面語都自然。  
**常見搭配**：from time to time review; from time to time occur; visit from time to time; need from time to time.  
**常見搭配對象**：review, maintenance, visit, problem, update.  
**與 sometimes 的辨析**：比 sometimes 稍有『 לאורך時間反覆但不頻繁』的意味。  
**使用限制**：如果事件集中在短時間內反覆發生，未必最自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The policy is reviewed from time to time.  
這項政策會不時檢討。

### 05 Every so often

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：每隔一段時間；偶爾  
**語域**：informal / common  
**核心語義**：occasionally, at irregular but recurring intervals  
**用法提示**：適合日常口語，強調事情隔一段時間就出現一次。  
**常見搭配**：every so often check; every so often happen; every so often visit; every so often change.  
**常見搭配對象**：problem, visit, update, check, event.  
**與 sometimes 的辨析**：比 sometimes 更有『隔一陣一次』的節奏感。  
**使用限制**：正式寫作通常用 occasionally / from time to time。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Every so often, the system needs to be restarted.  
系統每隔一段時間便需要重新啟動。

## Tier 2 — Low-frequency / formal / idiomatic variants · 低頻、正式與慣用變體

### 06 Once in a while

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：間中；偶爾  
**語域**：informal / conversational  
**核心語義**：occasionally, but not very often  
**用法提示**：當 sometimes 表示低頻、輕鬆口語的『間中一次』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：once in a while go out; once in a while treat yourself; once in a while happen.  
**常見搭配對象**：meal, visit, break, event, activity.  
**與 sometimes 的辨析**：Once in a while 通常比 sometimes 更低頻、更口語。  
**使用限制**：正式、學術或統計語境不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
We eat out once in a while.  
我們間中會外出吃飯。

### 07 On occasion

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：有時；偶爾  
**語域**：formal / neutral  
**核心語義**：on particular occasions, though not regularly  
**用法提示**：當 sometimes 出現在較正式報告、評論或專業寫作時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：on occasion require; on occasion occur; on occasion experience; on occasion prove.  
**常見搭配對象**：problem, exception, delay, difficulty, event.  
**與 sometimes 的辨析**：On occasion 比 occasionally 更像固定書面片語，正式度稍高。  
**使用限制**：日常對話用 occasionally / sometimes 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The service may, on occasion, experience short delays.  
這項服務有時可能出現短暫延誤。

### 08 Every now and then

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：時不時；間中  
**語域**：informal / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：occasionally, usually with a sense of something recurring after intervals  
**用法提示**：與 now and then 很接近，但語氣稍更完整、口語。  
**常見搭配**：every now and then remember; every now and then happen; every now and then check.  
**常見搭配對象**：memory, problem, visit, thought, event.  
**與 sometimes 的辨析**：比 sometimes 更口語、更有節奏感。  
**使用限制**：正式寫作通常避免。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Every now and then, an old memory comes back to me.  
我時不時會想起一段舊回憶。

## Tier 3 — Irregular scattered-occurrence variant · 零星無規律變體

### 09 Sporadically

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：零星地；偶發地  
**語域**：formal / analytical  
**核心語義**：happening irregularly and infrequently, with no clear pattern  
**用法提示**：當 sometimes 實際表示事件零星、無規律地發生時可用。  
**常見搭配**：occur sporadically; appear sporadically; sporadically reported; sporadic incidents.  
**常見搭配對象**：incident, case, report, outage, protest.  
**與 sometimes 的辨析**：Sporadically 比 sometimes 更窄，強調 irregular + scattered。  
**使用限制**：一般日常『有時』不能隨便升級成 sporadically。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Minor outages occurred sporadically during the week.  
一星期內零星出現輕微服務中斷。

## 04 False synonyms / frequency traps · 容易誤選的頻率副詞陷阱

### 01 Often

**經常 ≠ sometimes**  
often 表示頻率明顯較高。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Often is more frequent than sometimes.

### 02 Usually

**通常 ≠ sometimes**  
usually 表示大多數情況如此。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Usually describes the normal pattern.

### 03 Rarely

**很少 ≠ sometimes**  
rarely 表示頻率更低。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Rarely is much less frequent.

### 04 Periodically

**定期 ≠ sometimes**  
periodically 常暗示有一定週期或規律。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Periodically is more regular.

### 05 Intermittently

**間歇地 ≠ 所有 sometimes**  
intermittently 強調中斷再恢復的間歇模式。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Intermittently is stop-start.

### 06 Sporadically

**只在零星無規律 sense 接近**  
sporadically 強調零散、無規律。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Sporadically is irregular and scattered.

### 07 Occasionally

**通常頻率較低**  
occasionally 與 sometimes 很接近，但一般比 sometimes 稍低頻。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Occasionally is often less frequent.

### 08 Sometimes / sometime

**詞形陷阱**  
sometimes = 有時；sometime = 某個未確定時間。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Do not confuse sometimes with sometime.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Occasionally · 偶爾；有時

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

I sometimes work from home on Fridays.

**中文翻譯**  
我有時會在星期五在家工作。

- A. Occasionally
- B. At times
- C. From time to time
- D. Often
- E. Usually
- F. Rarely

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Occasionally** · 偶爾；有時

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
I occasionally work from home on Fridays.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Occasionally ✓** — means from time to time but not often. Here, the event happens, but not often. The frequency, register and pattern all fit **Occasionally** better than the other choices.
- **B.** **At times** — means on some occasions, often when describing a state or condition. This works well for states or qualities that are true on some occasions, but it is less natural for the lower-frequency or interval-based pattern tested here. The decisive clue is that the event happens, but not often.
- **C.** **From time to time** — means occasionally over a period, often with repeated but infrequent occurrence. This suggests recurrence over a period and is less suitable when the sentence is purely conversational or describes a temporary state. The decisive clue is that the event happens, but not often.
- **D.** **Often** — means frequently and is stronger than sometimes. This raises the frequency too much; the sentence does not say the event is frequent. The decisive clue is that the event happens, but not often.
- **E.** **Usually** — means in most cases or as the normal pattern. This changes the meaning substantially because it says the event is the normal or majority pattern. The decisive clue is that the event happens, but not often.
- **F.** **Rarely** — means not often and is generally less frequent than sometimes. This lowers the frequency too far and suggests the event is exceptional. The decisive clue is that the event happens, but not often.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **sometimes vs occasionally**: occasionally 通常稍低頻。
- **sometimes vs at times**: at times 特別適合描述狀態或性質有時如此。
- **sometimes vs now and then**: now and then 更口語。
- **sometimes vs from time to time**: from time to time 更有長期反覆但不頻繁的意味。
- **sometimes vs every so often**: every so often 強調隔一陣一次，口語感較強。
- **sometimes vs once in a while**: once in a while 通常更低頻、更口語。
- **sometimes vs on occasion**: on occasion 更正式。
- **sometimes vs sporadically**: sporadically 更窄，表示零星、無規律。
- **sometimes vs often**: often 頻率更高。
- **sometimes vs usually**: usually 表示大多數／正常情況如此。

## 08 Grammar, position & frequency traps · 文法、位置與頻率陷阱

- **sometimes** 常放在主語後、主要動詞前：I sometimes work late。
- **sometimes** 也可句首：Sometimes we take the bus。
- **occasionally** 位置較靈活：We occasionally meet / Occasionally, we meet。
- **at times** 常放句尾或句首：The work is difficult at times。
- **now and then / every now and then** 多見於口語。
- **from time to time** 適合一段時間內偶爾重複發生。
- **once in a while** 偏低頻、口語。
- **on occasion** 較正式，可插入句中：The service may, on occasion, be delayed。
- **sporadically** 常與 occur / appear / be reported 搭配。
- **periodically** 往往暗示週期或規律，不等於一般 sometimes。
- **intermittently** 強調時斷時續，不是一般『有時』。
- **sometimes** = 有時；**sometime** = 某個未確定時間；**some time** = 一段時間。
