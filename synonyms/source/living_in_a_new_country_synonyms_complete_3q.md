# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Living in a new country

「在新國家生活／海外定居／在另一國居住」，究竟是哪一種？

單純海外生活、正式居住狀態、搬遷後安頓、開始長期居留、在新地方立足、建立職涯與社交生活、把新地方變成家、以 expatriate 身分生活、展開人生新篇章——英文會按**是在描述居住狀態、安頓過程、長期定居、正式居留、生活建設、歸屬感、身份標籤，還是重新開始**選用不同表達。

> 本課主要處理 `living in a new country = 搬到本國以外的另一個國家並在當地建立日常生活`。  
> `settling abroad`、`residing in another country` 是核心方向：
> - `settling abroad`：強調安頓與建立生活的過程；
> - `residing in another country`：正式、中性地描述居住狀態。
>
> Format: **14 curated alternatives + 8 false-synonym contrasts + 3 exercises per target synonym (42 questions total)**.

# 01 同義詞指南


### 01 Settling Abroad
**在海外定居；在外國安頓下來**

強調搬到另一個國家後逐步建立生活、住所、日常安排與社會連結；比單純 `living abroad` 更有「安頓下來」的過程感。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> They spent their first year settling abroad and learning how local systems worked.

**USAGE CLUE**

- settle abroad / settle into life abroad

---


### 02 Residing In Another Country
**居住在另一個國家**

較正式、中性，單純描述居住地在另一國家，不一定暗示永久定居。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She is currently residing in another country for work.

**USAGE CLUE**

- formal residence/status wording

---


### 03 Living Abroad
**居住海外；在國外生活**

最自然、最廣泛的日常近義表達，表示在本國以外生活一段時間。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He has been living abroad for five years.

**USAGE CLUE**

- live abroad / experience of living abroad

---


### 04 Residing Abroad
**居住海外**

比 `living abroad` 更正式，常見於行政、法律、調查或正式書面語境。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Citizens residing abroad may vote by post.

**USAGE CLUE**

- formal/legal/administrative

---


### 05 Settling In Another Country
**在另一個國家定居；安頓**

與 `settling abroad` 很接近，但更明確指出是在另一個國家建立新生活。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The family spent several months settling in another country.

**USAGE CLUE**

- relocation + adaptation

---


### 06 Living Overseas
**在海外生活**

與 `living abroad` 高度接近；`overseas` 在 British English 特別常見，通常表示在本國以外生活。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She spent much of her career living overseas.

**USAGE CLUE**

- British English common

---


### 07 Residing Overseas
**居住海外**

較正式、行政化的 `living overseas`，常用於官方文件、保險、稅務或調查。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The form asks whether you are currently residing overseas.

**USAGE CLUE**

- formal status wording

---


### 08 Taking Up Residence Abroad
**在海外定居；在海外建立住所**

正式表達，強調開始把另一國作為居住地，常見於法律、行政或移居敘述。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He took up residence abroad after retirement.

**USAGE CLUE**

- formal relocation/residence

---


### 09 Establishing Oneself Abroad
**在海外立足；建立生活**

強調在新國家建立工作、社交、住所與生活基礎，帶較強主動努力意味。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> It took time to establish herself abroad professionally and socially.

**USAGE CLUE**

- build a foothold / establish oneself

---


### 10 Making A Life Abroad
**在海外建立生活**

較自然、帶人情色彩，表示在另一國家逐步建立日常、工作、關係與長期生活。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> They decided to make a life abroad after university.

**USAGE CLUE**

- life-building / long-term personal

---


### 11 Building A Life Abroad
**在海外建立生活**

與 `making a life abroad` 接近，稍更強調持續建立職涯、家庭與社會連結的過程。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She spent years building a life abroad.

**USAGE CLUE**

- long-term life-building

---


### 12 Making A Home In Another Country
**在另一個國家安家**

強調把另一個國家變成自己的生活基地與「家」，比單純 residence 更有人情與歸屬感。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The couple made a home in another country and raised their children there.

**USAGE CLUE**

- home/belonging-focused

---


### 13 Living As An Expatriate
**以外派／海外居住者身分生活**

較具身份標籤，表示作為 expatriate 在海外生活；適合職涯、派駐或自我身份語境，但並非所有海外居住者都會自稱 expatriate。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He spent several years living as an expatriate in Singapore.

**USAGE CLUE**

- identity/status-specific

---


### 14 Starting A New Life Abroad
**在海外展開新生活**

強調人生階段轉換與重新開始，通常暗示搬遷後建立新的工作、社交或家庭生活。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She moved after graduation and started a new life abroad.

**USAGE CLUE**

- new chapter / relocation

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 living in a new country / moving abroad

`moving abroad` 是搬遷動作／過程；`living in a new country` 是搬到之後的持續生活狀態。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> They are moving abroad next month.  
> They have been living abroad for three years.

---


## 02 living in a new country / travelling abroad

`travelling abroad` 通常是短期旅行，不代表建立日常居住生活。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> She travelled abroad for two weeks.  
> She lived abroad for two years.

---


## 03 living in a new country / visiting another country

`visiting` 是暫時停留；living 通常表示以當地為生活基地。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> He is visiting another country for a conference.  
> He is living in another country for work.

---


## 04 living in a new country / settling abroad

`settling abroad` 多了一層「安頓、建立生活」的過程與長期感。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> She is living abroad for six months.  
> They are settling abroad and looking for a permanent home.

---


## 05 living abroad / residing abroad

兩者意思接近；`residing abroad` 更正式、行政或法律化。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> She is living abroad this year.  
> Citizens residing abroad must update their address.

---


## 06 living abroad / immigrating

`immigrating` 是移入另一國並建立居留的遷移過程；living abroad 不必涉及永久移民身份。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> They are immigrating to Canada.  
> They are living abroad on a two-year work assignment.

---


## 07 living abroad / expatriate

`expatriate` 指人或身份標籤，不是單純生活狀態；而且並非所有海外居民都使用這個標籤。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> She is an expatriate working in Dubai.  
> She has been living abroad for several years.

---


## 08 reside / resident

`reside` 是動詞；`resident` 是名詞／形容詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> She resides abroad.  
> She is a resident of the city.

## 練習 01.1

**Target word/phrase: settling abroad · 在海外定居；在外國安頓下來**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> The first few months of living in a new country were spent finding a flat, opening bank accounts, and learning local systems.

**中文翻譯**

在新國家生活的最初幾個月，他們忙於找住所、開銀行戶口及熟悉當地制度。


A. settling abroad

B. living abroad

C. residing abroad

D. moving abroad

E. travelling abroad

F. starting a new life abroad


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**settling abroad · 在海外定居；在外國安頓下來**

> The first few months were spent settling abroad, finding a flat, opening bank accounts, and learning local systems.

**A. settling abroad ✓**  
找住所、處理銀行與熟悉制度都是安頓生活的過程，因此 `settling abroad` 比單純 living abroad 更精確。


**B. living abroad**  
最自然、最廣泛的日常表達，只表示在本國以外生活。 本題的決定性線索指向另一種更精確的海外生活／居住關係，因此不如 **settling abroad** 合適。


**C. residing abroad**  
正式版的 living abroad，常見於行政、法律或官方語境。 本題的決定性線索指向另一種更精確的海外生活／居住關係，因此不如 **settling abroad** 合適。


**D. moving abroad**  
描述搬遷動作／過程，不等於已經在海外生活。 本題的決定性線索指向另一種更精確的海外生活／居住關係，因此不如 **settling abroad** 合適。


**E. travelling abroad**  
通常指旅行或短期外遊，不表示日常居住。 本題的決定性線索指向另一種更精確的海外生活／居住關係，因此不如 **settling abroad** 合適。


**F. starting a new life abroad**  
強調人生新階段與重新開始。 本題的決定性線索指向另一種更精確的海外生活／居住關係，因此不如 **settling abroad** 合適。

# 05 高混淆組

## living abroad vs settling abroad
- **living abroad**：描述狀態。
- **settling abroad**：描述安頓、適應、建立生活的過程。

## living abroad vs residing abroad
- **living abroad**：日常自然。
- **residing abroad**：正式、行政、法律化。

## living abroad vs living overseas
- 兩者高度接近。
- **living overseas** 在 British English 尤自然。

## settling abroad vs taking up residence abroad
- **settling abroad**：生活過程、適應。
- **taking up residence abroad**：正式開始把海外作為居住地。

## establishing oneself abroad vs building a life abroad
- **establishing oneself abroad**：立足，常有職業／社交／財務基礎。
- **building a life abroad**：更全面、長期的人生建設。

## making a life abroad vs making a home in another country
- **making a life abroad**：人生／生活結構。
- **making a home in another country**：家、歸屬、社區與根基。

## living abroad vs living as an expatriate
- **living abroad**：中性，不加身份標籤。
- **living as an expatriate**：明確採用 expatriate 身份框架，常見於派駐／專業社群。

## moving abroad vs living abroad
- **moving abroad**：搬遷動作。
- **living abroad**：搬遷後的持續生活狀態。

# 08 生成／評估注意事項

1. 本課主要處理 `living in a new country = 搬到本國以外另一國並在當地建立日常生活`。
2. 固定為 **14 alternatives + 3 questions each = 42 exercises**。
3. 每題包含 Target、自然原句、中文翻譯、六個真實英語詞／片語選項、雙語正確答案、改寫及 A–F 診斷式解釋。
4. Feedback 優先處理：**居住狀態／安頓過程／正式居留／生活建設 → 正常搭配 → 本題關鍵線索**。
5. 使用者提供的核心：
   - settling abroad = 在海外安頓／定居
   - residing in another country = 正式描述居住於另一國
6. `living abroad` 是最廣泛、最自然的日常總稱。
7. `residing abroad` / `residing overseas` 應有 official form / tax / insurance / voting / registered address 等正式線索。
8. `settling abroad` / `settling in another country` 要有 housing / local systems / schools / social network / stable routine 等安頓線索。
9. `taking up residence abroad` 偏正式，適合 retirement / residence permission / permanent home 等開始居留語境。
10. `establishing oneself abroad` 要有 work / contacts / finances / independence 等立足線索。
11. `making a life abroad` / `building a life abroad` 強調長期人生建設，不適合短期交換或旅遊。
12. `making a home in another country` 必須有 home / family / community / roots / belonging 等歸屬線索。
13. `living as an expatriate` 帶身份標籤，只在 corporate posting / international professional community 等語境下使用；不應把所有 overseas residents 都稱為 expatriates。
14. `starting a new life abroad` 應有 graduation / divorce / new job / begin again 等人生轉折線索。
15. False synonyms 特別測試 moving abroad、travelling abroad、visiting another country、settling abroad、residing abroad、immigrating、expatriate，以及 reside/resident 詞性差異。
16. Distractors 使用真實、可混淆的近鄰，不使用人工標籤或不存在的詞形。
17. 原句只在 `living in a new country` 自然時使用；若真正重點是搬遷、安頓、正式居留、長期人生建設、身份框架或重新開始，則由上下文支持更精確替代。
18. 真正進階辨析應直接比較：
   - living abroad / settling abroad
   - living abroad / residing abroad
   - living abroad / living overseas
   - settling abroad / taking up residence abroad
   - establishing oneself abroad / building a life abroad
   - making a life abroad / making a home in another country
   - living abroad / living as an expatriate
   - moving abroad / living abroad
19. `living abroad` 仍是最廣泛、最自然的核心表達；其他替代語主要增加「安頓過程、正式居住狀態、長期性、職涯立足、歸屬感、身份標籤與人生階段」上的精度。
