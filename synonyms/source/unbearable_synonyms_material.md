# Unbearable

**無法忍受、壓倒性、完全難以撐過、令人受不了、劇痛、煎熬、壓迫難耐、超出承受範圍——究竟是哪一種 unbearable？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**intolerable; overwhelming; impossible to endure; insufferable; unendurable; excruciating; agonising; oppressive; too much to bear**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **intolerable; overwhelming; impossible to endure** 全部保留。  
> 本課特別區分 **一般不可忍受、超出應付能力、身體劇痛、心理煎熬、令人受不了的人／行為、悶熱／壓迫氣氛、情感負擔**。

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Intolerable

**難以忍受的；無法容忍的**

最直接、最通用的近義詞之一。當 `unbearable` 指情況、噪音、痛楚、壓力、行為或條件嚴重到不能接受或忍受時，`intolerable` 很自然，語氣較正式。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The noise from the construction became intolerable after midnight.

午夜後，建築工程的噪音變得難以忍受。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- intolerable noise

- intolerable conditions

- intolerable pressure

- become intolerable


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 unbearable 是一般不能忍受、壓倒應付能力、完全無法撐過、令人受不了的人／行為、正式 unendurable、劇烈身體痛楚、心理煎熬、悶熱／壓迫，還是情緒／責任負擔已超出承受範圍？

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The pressure of caring for everyone became overwhelming.

照顧所有人的壓力變得令人難以承受。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The heat inside the vehicle became impossible to endure.

### EXAMPLE · 例句

His arrogant behaviour became insufferable during the trip.

## 07 Agonising

**極其痛苦的；煎熬的**

英式拼寫。可形容身體痛楚，也可形容漫長等待、艱難決定、情感煎熬。比 `excruciating` 更容易延伸到心理痛苦。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The wait for the test results was agonising.

等待檢查結果的過程非常煎熬。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

### EXAMPLE · 例句

擠迫單位裏的夏日酷熱悶得令人受不了。

## 09 Too Much To Bear

**難以承受；超出可承受範圍**

自然的慣用改寫，特別適合悲傷、壓力、責任、痛苦、消息或情感負擔。語氣具體而有情感，比正式的 `intolerable` 更口語、更人性化。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The loss of both parents in one year was too much to bear for him.

一年內失去雙親對他來說實在難以承受。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- too much to bear

- burden too much to bear

- grief too much to bear

- pain too much to bear

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Intolerable

**最直接、較正式**

適合 conditions, noise, behaviour, pressure, pain 等廣泛語境。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The noise became intolerable.

---

## 02 Overwhelming

**超出應付能力**

特別自然於 grief, pressure, workload, emotion；不一定等於完全不能忍受。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The pressure became overwhelming.

---

## 03 Excruciating

**主要是劇痛**

常修飾 pain, headache, toothache, injury。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

He was in excruciating pain.

---

## 04 Agonising

**身心都可煎熬**

比 excruciating 更常延伸到等待、選擇、緊張。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

It was an agonising wait.

---

## 05 Insufferable

**常針對人／行為**

特別常見於 arrogance, boasting, behaviour, personality。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

His boasting was insufferable.

---

## 06 Oppressive

**悶熱／壓迫氣氛**

不只是 loud/intense，而是令人窒息、被壓住的感覺。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The heat was oppressive.

---

## 07 Unbearable / unbearable pain

**搭配範圍**

unbearable 可修飾 pain, heat, noise, grief, pressure, behaviour 等，範圍比大多近義詞廣。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The pain was unbearable; the grief was unbearable.

---

## 08 Bearable / unbearable

**反義與詞形**

`bearable` = 可以忍受；`unbearable` = 無法忍受。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The delay was inconvenient but bearable.

# 03 練習與解釋

> **每個核心近義表達 3 題，共 27 題。**
> 每題均以 **不可忍受程度、痛苦類型、是否屬情緒負擔、是否針對人／行為、是否屬悶熱壓迫環境** 作辨析。


## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** intolerable · 難以忍受的；無法容忍的

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

The noise from the construction became unbearable after midnight.

### 中文翻譯

午夜後，建築工程的噪音變得難以忍受。

### OPTIONS


- **A. intolerable**

- **B. unendurable**

- **C. overwhelming**

- **D. oppressive**

- **E. acceptable**

- **F. manageable**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**intolerable · 難以忍受的；無法容忍的**

The noise from the construction became **intolerable** after midnight.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. intolerable ✓** — `intolerable` = 嚴重到不能接受或忍受。 **本句為何最合適：** persistent noise crosses the threshold of what people can reasonably tolerate。

- **B. unendurable** — `unendurable` = 正式：超出耐受極限。 正式書面，和 intolerable 很接近；日常口語可能顯得過重。 **本句線索：** persistent noise crosses the threshold of what people can reasonably tolerate。

- **C. overwhelming** — `overwhelming` = 強烈到超出應付能力。 重點是超出 coping capacity；它不一定表示物理條件本身在客觀上『不可忍受』。 **本句線索：** persistent noise crosses the threshold of what people can reasonably tolerate。

- **D. oppressive** — `oppressive` = 壓迫、悶熱、窒息般令人難受。 特別適合熱、濕度、氣氛、制度壓力；牙痛或腳傷不適合。 **本句線索：** persistent noise crosses the threshold of what people can reasonably tolerate。

- **E. acceptable** — `acceptable` = 可接受的。 表示可以接受，和 intolerable 相反。 **本句線索：** persistent noise crosses the threshold of what people can reasonably tolerate。

- **F. manageable** — `manageable` = 可應付的。 表示可以應付，和 overwhelming/unbearable 相反。 **本句線索：** persistent noise crosses the threshold of what people can reasonably tolerate。

| **impossible to endure** | 透明描述：完全無法承受 |
| **insufferable** | 人／態度／行為令人受不了 |
| **unendurable** | 正式／書面：超出耐受極限 |
| **excruciating** | 極度身體痛楚 |
| **agonising** | 身體或心理煎熬 |
| **oppressive** | 悶熱、沉重、壓迫得令人難受 |
| **too much to bear** | 情緒／責任／悲傷負擔超出承受範圍 |

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Unbearable vs Intolerable
`intolerable` 是最廣的正式替換：
- intolerable noise
- intolerable conditions
- intolerable behaviour

## Unbearable vs Overwhelming
`overwhelming` 重點是：
**the person cannot cope with the intensity**。

## Unbearable vs Excruciating
`excruciating` 最適合：
- back pain
- toothache
- headache
- injury

## Unbearable vs Agonising
`agonising` 可延伸至心理：
- agonising wait
- agonising decision
- agonising uncertainty

## Unbearable vs Insufferable
最自然於：
- arrogant person
- boasting
- behaviour
- personality

## Unbearable vs Oppressive
最自然於：
- heat
- humidity
- atmosphere
- stifling conditions

## Unbearable vs Too much to bear
特別自然於：
- grief
- responsibility
- emotional burden
- cumulative bad news

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Unbearable vs bearable
- unbearable = 無法忍受
- bearable = 可以忍受

## 02 Unbearably
副詞：
- unbearably hot
- unbearably painful
- unbearably loud

## 03 Excruciating
通常不拿來描述一般工作量／壓力，除非是比喻化、非常強烈。

## 04 Insufferable
特別常用於人和行為，帶明顯負面評價。

## 05 Agonising
英式拼寫：
- agonising

美式常見：
- agonizing
