# DISAGREE · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Disagree · verb

**Disagree 可以只是意見不同，也可以是明確反對、正式持異議、針對某個 claim 提出質疑，或人與人長期看法不一致。** 因此替換時要先判斷句子是在談 **neutral difference, explicit objection, dissent from a majority, challenge to a claim, active opposition, or interpersonal disagreement**。

你提供的 **differ; object; have a different opinion** 正好代表三個核心方向：**中性分歧、明確反對、柔和地表達不同看法**。

本次保留 **8 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達，共 **24 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。按照 inspection 標準，所有原句先確保 *disagree* 本身自然，再建立只有一個最佳答案的替換題。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Disagree 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 |
|---|---|
| 中性意見不同 | **differ** |
| 明確反對 proposal/action/rule | **object** |
| 柔和表示不同看法 | **have a different opinion** |
| 少數／正式立場反對多數 | **dissent** |
| 針對 claim/argument 提出異議 | **take issue with** |
| 強烈反對政策／行動 | **oppose** |
| 質疑 claim/figure/evidence | **dispute** |
| 口語：兩人意見不合 | **not see eye to eye** |

## Tier 1 — Core disagreement / objection / dissent equivalents · 核心分歧、異議與反對近義詞

### 01 Differ

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：意見不同；有分歧  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to hold a different opinion or position from another person or group  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **disagree** 最核心的正式近義詞之一，尤其適合 opinions, views, interpretations, estimates 等。  
**常見搭配**：differ on; differ over; differ about; differ from; opinions differ.  
**常見搭配對象**：opinion, view, interpretation, estimate, approach.  
**與 disagree 的辨析**：Differ 比 disagree 稍正式，也很常用於『觀點彼此不同』，未必帶直接爭辯語氣。  
**使用限制**：若是明確反對提案、正式提出異議，object / oppose 更精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The two economists differ on how quickly interest rates should fall.  
兩位經濟學家對利率應多快下調有不同意見。

### 02 Object

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：反對；提出異議  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to express disagreement or opposition, especially to a proposal, action, decision, or arrangement  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，當 disagree 不只是『看法不同』，而是明確表示反對某項做法時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：object to a proposal; object strongly; object on the grounds that; raise an objection.  
**常見搭配對象**：proposal, plan, decision, rule, change.  
**與 disagree 的辨析**：Object 比 disagree 更行動化，通常是明確提出反對。  
**使用限制**：不能說 *object with someone*；標準搭配是 **object to + noun/-ing**。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Several residents objected to the proposed road expansion.  
數名居民反對擬議的道路擴建。

### 03 Have a different opinion

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：持不同意見；有不同看法  
**語域**：neutral / explanatory  
**核心語義**：to hold a view that is not the same as someone else's  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **disagree** 最透明、最安全的解釋性改寫，尤其適合一般討論和教學。  
**常見搭配**：have a different opinion about; have different opinions on; hold a different view.  
**常見搭配對象**：policy, issue, method, interpretation, decision.  
**與 disagree 的辨析**：語氣比 disagree 柔和，也較少對抗感。  
**使用限制**：正式政治／法律異議或強烈反對時可能過弱。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
We have a different opinion about how the project should be funded.  
我們對項目應如何融資持不同意見。

### 04 Dissent

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：持異議；不同意  
**語域**：formal / academic / legal  
**核心語義**：to disagree with an accepted, official, or majority opinion or decision  
**用法提示**：當 disagree 涉及 majority view, official decision, court judgement, institutional position 時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：dissent from; dissenting opinion; dissent from the majority; publicly dissent.  
**常見搭配對象**：majority view, judgement, policy, decision, official position.  
**與 disagree 的辨析**：Dissent 比 disagree 更正式，常涉及少數意見對主流／正式立場提出異議。  
**使用限制**：日常朋友之間看法不同通常不用 dissent。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Two members dissented from the committee's final recommendation.  
兩名成員不同意委員會的最終建議。

### 05 Take issue with

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：對……有異議；不同意……  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to disagree with a statement, claim, argument, or interpretation and often challenge it directly  
**用法提示**：很適合學術評論、辯論、專業寫作中對某個 claim / assumption / interpretation 提出不同意見。  
**常見搭配**：take issue with a claim; take issue with an argument; take issue with the assumption that.  
**常見搭配對象**：claim, argument, statement, interpretation, assumption.  
**與 disagree 的辨析**：比 disagree 更精準地表示『針對某個具體說法提出異議』。  
**使用限制**：不適合單純描述兩人一般看法不同。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Several researchers take issue with the claim that the policy caused the decline.  
數名研究人員不同意『該政策導致下降』這個說法。

## Tier 2 — Strong opposition / claim-challenge variants · 強烈反對與質疑變體

### 06 Oppose

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：反對；抵制  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to be against a proposal, policy, action, person, or change and often try to prevent it  
**用法提示**：當 disagree 升級為實際立場上的反對，尤其政策、法律、發展計劃或改革，可用 oppose。  
**常見搭配**：oppose a plan; oppose legislation; strongly oppose; oppose the proposal.  
**常見搭配對象**：policy, law, proposal, development, reform.  
**與 disagree 的辨析**：Oppose 比 disagree 強，表示站在反對一方，而非只是有不同意見。  
**使用限制**：不能把所有 opinion disagreement 都改成 oppose。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Local groups oppose the plan to close the community centre.  
本地團體反對關閉社區中心的計劃。

### 07 Dispute

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：質疑；不認同；爭議  
**語域**：formal / analytical  
**核心語義**：to question the truth, accuracy, validity, or interpretation of a claim or fact  
**用法提示**：當 disagree 是對 fact, figure, claim, evidence, interpretation 的真確性／有效性提出質疑時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：dispute a claim; dispute the figures; dispute the findings; strongly dispute.  
**常見搭配對象**：claim, evidence, figure, interpretation, finding.  
**與 disagree 的辨析**：Dispute 不只是表達不同意見，而是挑戰某說法是否成立。  
**使用限制**：一般品味、偏好或主觀意見不同不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The company disputes the claim that it failed to meet the safety standard.  
公司不認同其未達安全標準的說法。

## Tier 3 — Conversational interpersonal variant · 口語人際分歧變體

### 08 Not see eye to eye

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：看法不一致；意見不合  
**語域**：informal / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：to fail to agree with someone, especially over an issue on which both sides have clear views  
**用法提示**：這是 **disagree** 很自然的口語／慣用表達，適合人與人或團體之間持續看法不一致。  
**常見搭配**：not see eye to eye on; rarely see eye to eye; don't always see eye to eye.  
**常見搭配對象**：budget, strategy, parenting, politics, priorities.  
**與 disagree 的辨析**：語氣較口語，也常表示雙方長期或反覆意見不合。  
**使用限制**：正式報告、法律文書和單一 factual claim 通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The two managers do not always see eye to eye on staffing priorities.  
兩名經理在招聘優先次序上並不總是意見一致。

## 04 False synonyms / disagree traps · 容易誤選的『爭論、拒絕、否認』陷阱

### 01 Argue

**爭論 ≠ disagree**  
argue 是爭辯／提出論證的行為；人可以 disagree 而不爭吵。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Argument is an interaction, not merely a difference of opinion.

### 02 Refuse

**拒絕 ≠ disagree**  
refuse 是不願做某事，不代表一定不同意其觀點。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Refusal is about action.

### 03 Deny

**否認 ≠ disagree**  
deny 是否認某事為真或拒絕承認，不等於一般不同意。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Denial is truth/acknowledgement-focused.

### 04 Oppose

**只在較強反對 sense 接近**  
oppose 通常比 disagree 強，並可涉及阻止政策或行動。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Oppose is stronger.

### 05 Object

**只在明確提出異議 sense 接近**  
object 常針對 proposal/action，並有正式反對意味。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Object is explicit opposition.

### 06 Dispute

**只在質疑真確性／有效性 sense 接近**  
dispute 常針對 claims, figures, findings。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Dispute is claim-validity-focused.

### 07 Differ

**通常較中性**  
differ 可只表示看法不同，不一定有對抗。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Differ is often milder.

### 08 Disagree / disagreement

**詞性陷阱**  
disagree 是動詞；disagreement 是名詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check word class.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Differ · 意見不同；有分歧

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The two experts disagree on how the data should be interpreted.

**中文翻譯**  
兩名專家對數據應如何解讀有不同意見。

- A. Differ
- B. Have a different opinion
- C. Oppose
- D. Argue
- E. Refuse
- F. Deny

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Differ** · 意見不同；有分歧

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The two experts differ on how the data should be interpreted.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Differ ✓** — hold a different opinion or position。這句的決定性線索是 **neutral difference in interpretation**。因此 **Differ** 最精準表達這種 *disagree*。
- **B.** **Have a different opinion** — hold a view that is not the same as someone else's。這個表達很柔和，適合一般看法不同；若句子有正式異議、反對政策或挑戰 claim，就不夠有力。
- **C.** **Oppose** — be against a proposal, policy, action, or change。**oppose** 比普通 disagree 強，常表示站在反對一方，甚至採取行動阻止。
- **D.** **Argue** — engage in disagreement or present reasons in debate。**argue** 描述爭論或提出論證的行為；兩人可以 disagree 而完全沒有爭吵。
- **E.** **Refuse** — say or show that one will not do something。**refuse** 是拒絕做某事，不等於持不同意見。
- **F.** **Deny** — state that something is not true or refuse to acknowledge it。**deny** 是否認某事為真／拒絕承認，與一般 opinion disagreement 不同。

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Disagree 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 專家／觀點中性分歧 | **differ** | 是否 simply holding different views？ |
| 對計劃／規則明確反對 | **object** | 是否 explicit objection to an action/proposal？ |
| 一般討論中的不同看法 | **have a different opinion** | 是否 a softer wording is appropriate？ |
| 法官／委員反對多數意見 | **dissent** | 是否 disagreement with an official/majority view？ |
| 針對某個 claim/assumption | **take issue with** | 是否 a specific statement is being challenged？ |
| 政策／法例／計劃強烈反對 | **oppose** | 是否 active or firm opposition？ |
| 質疑數字／證據／說法 | **dispute** | 是否 truth, accuracy, or validity is at issue？ |
| 兩人長期意見不合 | **not see eye to eye** | 是否 informal interpersonal disagreement？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **disagree vs differ**：differ 更中性、更正式。
- **disagree vs object**：object 是明確提出異議，常接 **to**。
- **disagree vs have a different opinion**：後者更柔和。
- **disagree vs dissent**：dissent 常指少數／正式立場不同意多數。
- **disagree vs take issue with**：後者針對具體 claim / argument / interpretation。
- **disagree vs oppose**：oppose 更強，常涉及政策或行動。
- **disagree vs dispute**：dispute 質疑真確性／有效性。
- **disagree vs argue**：argue 是爭論行為，不等於只是意見不同。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & disagreement traps

- **disagree with + person/view**：I disagree with him / that view。
- **disagree about/on/over + issue**：disagree on policy / over funding。
- **differ on/about/over + issue**；**differ from + person/thing**。
- **object to + noun/-ing**：object to the plan / object to paying the fee。
- **dissent from + decision/view**：dissent from the majority judgement。
- **take issue with + claim/argument/assumption**。
- **oppose + noun/-ing**：oppose the proposal / oppose closing the centre。
- **dispute + claim/figure/finding**：通常直接接賓語。
- **disagree** 是動詞；**disagreement** 是名詞。
