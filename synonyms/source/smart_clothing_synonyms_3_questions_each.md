# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Smart clothing

**`smart clothing` 指加入電子、感測、連網、加熱、互動或其他數碼功能的服裝。不同替代表達主要按『是否連網』『是否屬整體可穿戴科技』『是否聚焦材料』『是否內置感測器』『是否可互動』來區分。**

核心替代表達：

`connected garments / technology-enabled clothing / wearable technology / smart garments / electronic textiles / e-textiles / sensor-enabled clothing / interactive clothing`

> **核心原則**：先判斷 smart clothing 指的是 **connected garments, technology-enhanced clothing, the broader wearable-tech category, smart garments, electronic textile materials, e-textiles, sensor-enabled clothing, or interactive clothing**。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Connected garments

**連網服裝；互聯衣物**

和 `smart clothing` 很接近，強調衣物可連接手機、網絡或其他裝置。它的重點是 connectivity，不一定包含感測器或主動功能。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The company is developing connected garments that sync with a mobile app.

公司正在開發可與手機應用程式同步的連網服裝。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **clothing that can connect to digital devices or networks**

**REGISTER · 語域**

科技、產品、正式

---

## 02 Technology-enabled clothing

**科技增強服裝；科技支援服裝**

較廣泛的正式表達，指加入電子、感測、通訊、加熱、追蹤或其他技術功能的服裝。它比 `connected garments` 更廣。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Technology-enabled clothing can monitor movement and body temperature.

科技增強服裝可以監測動作和體溫。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **clothing enhanced with built-in technological functions**

**REGISTER · 語域**

科技、研究、正式

---

## 03 Wearable technology

**可穿戴科技**

比 `smart clothing` 更廣，除了服裝外還包括智能手錶、手環、眼鏡等。只有在語境容許較廣範圍時才可近似替代。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Wearable technology is increasingly used in health monitoring.

可穿戴科技愈來愈多用於健康監測。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **technology designed to be worn on the body**

**REGISTER · 語域**

科技、商業、研究

---

## 04 Smart garments

**智能服裝；智慧衣物**

和 `smart clothing` 幾乎直接同義，語氣稍技術化。通常指內置感測器、導電纖維、電子元件或其他智能功能的衣物。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Smart garments can collect biometric data during exercise.

智能服裝可在運動期間收集生物特徵數據。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **garments with built-in sensing, computing, or interactive functions**

**REGISTER · 語域**

科技、研究、產品

---

## 05 Electronic textiles

**電子紡織品；電子布料**

較技術、材料導向，指把電子功能整合進紡織物本身。它未必已製成完整服裝，因此比 `smart clothing` 更偏材料層面。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Electronic textiles can incorporate conductive fibres and sensors.

電子紡織品可整合導電纖維和感測器。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **textiles that integrate electronic or conductive components**

**REGISTER · 語域**

材料科學、研究、技術

---

## 06 E-textiles

**電子紡織品**

是 `electronic textiles` 的常用縮寫形式。常見於設計、研究、時裝科技和教育語境，通常偏材料或織物本身。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The course introduces students to e-textiles and wearable sensors.

課程向學生介紹電子紡織品和可穿戴感測器。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a common short form of electronic textiles**

**REGISTER · 語域**

研究、設計、教育

---

## 07 Sensor-enabled clothing

**感測器服裝；內置感測器衣物**

當 `smart clothing` 的核心功能是透過感測器監測動作、生理數據或環境時，這個表達很精準。它比一般 smart clothing 更窄。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Sensor-enabled clothing can track posture and breathing patterns.

感測器服裝可以追蹤姿勢和呼吸模式。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **clothing equipped with sensors to collect data**

**REGISTER · 語域**

健康科技、運動、研究

---

## 08 Interactive clothing

**互動服裝**

當 `smart clothing` 可因觸摸、動作、環境或數碼輸入而作出反應時，`interactive clothing` 很合適。它強調 response/interactivity。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Interactive clothing can change colour in response to movement.

互動服裝可以因應動作改變顏色。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **clothing designed to respond to the wearer or environment**

**REGISTER · 語域**

設計、時裝科技、研究

# 02 False synonyms

## Smart clothing / Connected garments
`connected garments` 特別強調連網／連接其他數碼裝置；smart clothing 不一定需要連網。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Smart clothing may contain sensors without connecting to the internet.  
Connected garments can transmit data to a phone or cloud platform.

## Smart clothing / Technology-enabled clothing
兩者很接近；`technology-enabled clothing` 更廣、更正式，可包括加熱、感測、追蹤、通訊等多種功能。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Smart clothing is a common consumer-facing term.  
Technology-enabled clothing is useful in technical or research descriptions.

## Smart clothing / Wearable technology
`wearable technology` 範圍更廣，還包括智能手錶、手環、眼鏡等。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Smart clothing is one form of wearable technology.  
Not all wearable technology is clothing.

## Smart clothing / Smart garments
兩者幾乎直接同義；`smart garments` 稍技術化、產品化。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Smart clothing can monitor exercise data.  
Smart garments can include embedded biometric sensors.

## Smart clothing / Electronic textiles
`electronic textiles` 更偏材料層面，不一定已製成完整服裝。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Electronic textiles integrate conductive fibres or circuitry into fabric.  
Smart clothing uses those materials in finished garments.

## Electronic textiles / E-textiles
兩者同義；`e-textiles` 是較短、較常見於研究和設計的寫法。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The paper studies electronic textiles.  
The workshop introduces beginners to e-textiles.

## Smart clothing / Sensor-enabled clothing
`sensor-enabled clothing` 更窄，只在核心功能是感測／監測時適合。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Smart clothing may heat, light up, connect, or sense.  
Sensor-enabled clothing specifically collects data through sensors.

## Smart clothing / Interactive clothing
`interactive clothing` 更強調對穿着者或環境作出反應。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Smart clothing may simply collect data.  
Interactive clothing may change colour when touched or moved.

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Connected garments vs Smart garments** — connected 強調連接；smart 更廣。
- **Technology-enabled clothing vs Smart clothing** — 前者更正式、更廣泛。
- **Wearable technology vs Smart clothing** — wearable technology 是上位類別。
- **Electronic textiles vs Smart garments** — textiles 是材料；garments 是成衣。
- **E-textiles vs Electronic textiles** — 意思相同，前者是簡寫。
- **Sensor-enabled clothing vs Interactive clothing** — sensor-enabled 偏資料收集；interactive 偏作出反應。
- **Smart clothing ≠ wearable technology** — 前者只是後者的一個子類。
- **Smart clothing ≠ e-textiles** — 成衣和材料層面不同。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Clothing
`clothing` 通常是不可數名詞：

> smart clothing ✓  
> technology-enabled clothing ✓

通常不說：

> clothings ✗

## Garment
可數：

> a smart garment  
> smart garments

## Technology-enabled
作前置複合形容詞時用連字號：

> technology-enabled clothing

## Sensor-enabled
同樣用連字號：

> sensor-enabled clothing

## E-textiles
常見拼法：

> e-textiles

## Wearable technology
通常把 `technology` 當整體概念：

> wearable technology

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 主要強調連手機／雲端？ → **connected garments**
2. 具多種科技功能？ → **technology-enabled clothing**
3. 連手錶、眼鏡等也包括？ → **wearable technology**
4. 想直接近義改寫 smart clothing？ → **smart garments**
5. 重點是導電纖維／布料材料？ → **electronic textiles**
6. 研究／設計語境用簡寫？ → **e-textiles**
7. 重點是監測生理／動作數據？ → **sensor-enabled clothing**
8. 重點是對輸入／環境作反應？ → **interactive clothing**
