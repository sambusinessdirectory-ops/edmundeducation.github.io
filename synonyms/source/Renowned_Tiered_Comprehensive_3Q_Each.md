# RENOWNED · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Renowned

**「著名而享有聲望的」不只是 famous。** Renowned 通常帶正面意味，常表示某人、機構、地方或作品因成就、品質或專業能力而廣為人知。因此要分清 **fame、admiration、professional distinction、critical acclaim、prestige、reliability**。

本單元集中處理 **renowned = widely known and highly regarded, especially for achievement, quality, or expertise**。你提供的 **famous; celebrated** 分別代表 **廣為人知** 和 **廣受讚譽** 兩個核心方向。

本次保留 **10 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達。部分詞只適合人物、機構、獎項或專業領域，會清楚標示，不會把所有『出名』詞當作完全同義。共 **30 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Renowned 的語境 | 核心問題 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|---|
| 單純廣為人知 | 是否只需要 fame/familiarity？ | famous, well-known |
| 廣受讚譽 | 是否有 awards/praise/critical approval？ | celebrated, acclaimed |
| 專業成就與地位 | 是否是 scholar/scientist/judge/professor？ | distinguished, eminent, prominent |
| 機構／獎項高聲望 | 是否 status/prestige 是重點？ | prestigious |
| 歷史性顯赫成就 | 是否 long record/history/career？ | illustrious |
| 良好可靠聲譽 | 是否 trust/reliability rather than fame？ | reputable |

## Tier 1 — Core fame / acclaim / professional-distinction equivalents · 核心知名、讚譽與專業聲望近義詞

### 01 Famous

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：著名的；出名的  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：known by many people  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **renowned** 最直接、最常見的近義詞之一，適合人物、地方、品牌、作品和機構。  
**常見搭配**：famous actor; famous landmark; famous brand; famous for; internationally famous.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, place, brand, work, institution.  
**與 renowned 的辨析**：Famous 只表示很多人知道；renowned 通常帶較明顯的正面聲望和專業成就。  
**使用限制**：如果某人只是廣為人知但聲譽不好，famous 仍可用，而 renowned 通常不自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The city is famous for its historic harbour.  
這座城市以歷史悠久的港口聞名。

### 02 Celebrated

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：著名而備受讚賞的；享負盛名的  
**語域**：formal / positive  
**核心語義**：widely admired and publicly praised for achievements, talent, or cultural importance  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，當 renowned 不只表示出名，而是『廣受讚譽』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：celebrated author; celebrated artist; celebrated scientist; celebrated work; internationally celebrated.  
**常見搭配對象**：author, artist, scientist, musician, work.  
**與 renowned 的辨析**：Celebrated 比 famous 更強調 praise and admiration，與 renowned 的正面聲望很接近。  
**使用限制**：普通品牌知名度或純粹曝光度不一定適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She is a celebrated novelist whose work has won several major awards.  
她是一位備受讚譽的小說家，作品曾獲多個重要獎項。

### 03 Distinguished

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：傑出的；卓越而有聲望的  
**語域**：formal / professional  
**核心語義**：respected and admired because of notable achievement, expertise, or service  
**用法提示**：當 renowned 指某人在專業領域成就卓越、地位受尊重時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：distinguished professor; distinguished scientist; distinguished career; distinguished guest; distinguished service.  
**常見搭配對象**：professor, scientist, scholar, career, guest.  
**與 renowned 的辨析**：Distinguished 更強調 professional achievement and status；renowned 更強調廣泛聲望。  
**使用限制**：一般旅遊景點、產品或流行品牌通常不用 distinguished。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The lecture was delivered by a distinguished historian.  
講座由一位傑出的歷史學家主講。

### 04 Eminent

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：卓越的；德高望重的；顯赫的  
**語域**：formal / academic  
**核心語義**：highly respected and prominent within a profession or field  
**用法提示**：適合學術、法律、醫學、科學等正式專業語境。  
**常見搭配**：eminent scholar; eminent scientist; eminent physician; eminent judge; eminent figure.  
**常見搭配對象**：scholar, scientist, doctor, judge, expert.  
**與 renowned 的辨析**：Eminent 比 renowned 更正式，也更集中於高地位專業人士。  
**使用限制**：餐廳、品牌、景點等一般不使用 eminent。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The panel included several eminent scientists.  
專家小組包括數名卓越的科學家。

### 05 Acclaimed

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：廣受好評的；備受讚譽的  
**語域**：neutral / media / arts  
**核心語義**：praised enthusiastically by critics, audiences, or the public  
**用法提示**：當 renowned 的聲譽主要來自作品、演出、電影、餐廳或專業成果獲得好評時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：critically acclaimed; acclaimed director; acclaimed novel; acclaimed restaurant; widely acclaimed.  
**常見搭配對象**：film, director, novel, performance, restaurant.  
**與 renowned 的辨析**：Acclaimed 強調 praise/reviews；renowned 更偏長期名聲。  
**使用限制**：單純知名但沒有明確好評語境時，不一定用 acclaimed。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The film was directed by an internationally acclaimed filmmaker.  
這部電影由一位享譽國際的導演執導。

## Tier 2 — Familiarity / prominence / prestige variants · 知名度、突出地位與機構聲望變體

### 06 Well-known

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：知名的；廣為人知的  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：known to many people  
**用法提示**：比 renowned 更中性，適合任何『很多人知道』的語境。  
**常見搭配**：well-known author; well-known brand; well-known fact; well-known landmark; well-known for.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, brand, place, fact, work.  
**與 renowned 的辨析**：Well-known 幾乎只表示 familiarity，不自動帶 admired / prestigious。  
**使用限制**：如果要表達卓越聲望，renowned / celebrated / distinguished 更精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The museum is a well-known landmark in the city.  
這間博物館是城中一個知名地標。

### 07 Prominent

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：著名而重要的；突出的  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：important, influential, or noticeable within a field, group, or public context  
**用法提示**：當 renowned 同時帶『有地位、具影響力、很突出』時可以用。  
**常見搭配**：prominent figure; prominent scientist; prominent role; prominent member; prominent position.  
**常見搭配對象**：figure, leader, member, scientist, role.  
**與 renowned 的辨析**：Prominent 強調 importance/visibility，不一定等於廣受讚譽。  
**使用限制**：純粹藝術作品或景點的『享負盛名』通常 celebrated / famous 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He became a prominent figure in public health research.  
他成為公共衞生研究領域的重要知名人物。

### 08 Prestigious

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：享有盛譽的；有聲望的  
**語域**：formal / positive  
**核心語義**：having high status and respect because of quality, history, or achievement  
**用法提示**：當 renowned 修飾 institution, award, university, company, venue 等『有聲望』的事物時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：prestigious university; prestigious award; prestigious institution; prestigious prize; prestigious firm.  
**常見搭配對象**：university, award, institution, company, school.  
**與 renowned 的辨析**：Prestigious 強調 status and esteem；renowned 強調 widely known for excellence。  
**使用限制**：通常不直接形容個人為 *a prestigious scientist*；對人用 renowned / distinguished / eminent。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She received a scholarship from a prestigious university.  
她獲一所享有盛譽的大學頒發獎學金。

## Tier 3 — Historical distinction / reliability variants · 歷史顯赫與可靠聲譽變體

### 09 Illustrious

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：赫赫有名的；功績卓著的  
**語域**：formal / literary  
**核心語義**：famous and highly respected because of a long record of outstanding achievement  
**用法提示**：當 renowned 帶歷史感、功績顯赫、長期聲望時可用。  
**常見搭配**：illustrious career; illustrious history; illustrious predecessor; illustrious family; illustrious scientist.  
**常見搭配對象**：career, history, predecessor, family, person.  
**與 renowned 的辨析**：Illustrious 比 renowned 更華麗、更書面，也常暗示長期或歷史性的成就。  
**使用限制**：日常產品、一般名人或普通知名度不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The award recognised her illustrious career in medicine.  
這個獎項表揚她在醫學界功績卓著的職業生涯。

### 10 Reputable

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：有良好聲譽的；可信賴的  
**語域**：neutral / business  
**核心語義**：having a good reputation for reliability, quality, or professional standards  
**用法提示**：當 renowned 的重點不是名氣，而是『聲譽良好、可靠』時可作情境替代。  
**常見搭配**：reputable company; reputable dealer; reputable university; reputable source; reputable firm.  
**常見搭配對象**：company, dealer, source, institution, provider.  
**與 renowned 的辨析**：Reputable 不要求非常出名；它只要求 good reputation。  
**使用限制**：不能當作 renowned 的全面同義詞，尤其在 fame/celebrity 語境中。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Choose a reputable supplier with a strong safety record.  
應選擇安全紀錄良好的可靠供應商。

## 04 False synonyms / renowned traps · 容易誤選的知名度與聲望陷阱

### 01 Popular

**受歡迎 ≠ renowned**  
popular 表示很多人喜歡；renowned 表示以聲望或成就聞名。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Popularity and renown are different.

### 02 Notorious

**臭名昭著 ≠ renowned**  
notorious 表示因負面原因出名。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Notorious is negatively famous.

### 03 Recognisable

**容易認出 ≠ renowned**  
recognisable 只表示容易識別，不代表有聲望。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Recognisability is not reputation.

### 04 Influential

**有影響力 ≠ renowned**  
influential 表示能影響別人；不一定廣為人知。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Influence and renown can overlap but differ.

### 05 Prestigious

**只在高聲望機構／獎項 sense 接近**  
prestigious 偏 status，不是所有 renowned 的直接替代。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Prestigious is status-focused.

### 06 Prominent

**只在重要／突出人物 sense 接近**  
prominent 可能重要但未必廣受讚譽。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Prominence is visibility/status-focused.

### 07 Famous

**知名 ≠ 必然有正面聲望**  
famous 可以因任何原因出名，renowned 通常是正面。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Famous is more neutral.

### 08 Renown / renowned

**詞形陷阱**  
renown 是名詞；renowned 是形容詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Renown and renowned have different grammatical roles.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Famous · 著名的；出名的

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The town is renowned for its medieval architecture.

**中文翻譯**  
這個小鎮以中世紀建築聞名。

- A. Famous
- B. Well-known
- C. Celebrated
- D. Popular
- E. Notorious
- F. Prominent

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Famous** · 著名的；出名的

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The town is famous for its medieval architecture.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Famous ✓** — means known by many people and is neutral about whether the reputation is positive. In this sentence, general public familiarity is enough; no specialist prestige is required. That combination of fame, esteem and context makes **Famous** the most precise replacement.
- **B.** **Well-known** — means familiar to many people and does not automatically imply prestige or admiration. It belongs to the same fame/reputation field, but it misses the decisive clue here: general public familiarity is enough; no specialist prestige is required.
- **C.** **Celebrated** — means widely admired and publicly praised for achievements or cultural importance. It belongs to the same fame/reputation field, but it misses the decisive clue here: general public familiarity is enough; no specialist prestige is required.
- **D.** **Popular** — means liked by many people and is not the same as being renowned. It belongs to the same fame/reputation field, but it misses the decisive clue here: general public familiarity is enough; no specialist prestige is required.
- **E.** **Notorious** — means famous for negative reasons. It belongs to the same fame/reputation field, but it misses the decisive clue here: general public familiarity is enough; no specialist prestige is required.
- **F.** **Prominent** — means important, influential or highly visible within a field or group. It belongs to the same fame/reputation field, but it misses the decisive clue here: general public familiarity is enough; no specialist prestige is required.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **renowned vs famous**: famous 只要求出名；renowned 通常帶正面聲望。
- **renowned vs celebrated**: celebrated 更強調讚譽、表揚和文化成就。
- **renowned vs distinguished**: distinguished 更強調專業成就和尊重。
- **renowned vs eminent**: eminent 更正式，主要用於高地位專業人士。
- **renowned vs acclaimed**: acclaimed 強調好評和評論界／公眾讚譽。
- **renowned vs well-known**: well-known 最中性，只表示廣為人知。
- **renowned vs prominent**: prominent 是重要、突出、具能見度，不一定獲廣泛讚譽。
- **renowned vs prestigious**: prestigious 特別適合大學、獎項、機構等高聲望事物。
- **renowned vs illustrious**: illustrious 更華麗，常帶長期／歷史功績。
- **renowned vs reputable**: reputable 強調可信賴與良好聲譽，不要求非常出名。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & reputation traps · 文法、搭配與聲望詞彙陷阱

- **renowned for + noun/-ing**：renowned for its research / renowned for producing fine wine。
- **renowned as + role/title**：renowned as a surgeon / scholar。
- **famous for** 是最常見日常搭配，但語氣較中性。
- **celebrated + artist/author/composer/work**：常帶公開讚譽。
- **distinguished + professor/career/service**：專業成就與地位。
- **eminent + scholar/scientist/judge/physician**：正式專業語境。
- **acclaimed + film/novel/director/restaurant**：常與 reviews/awards 相關。
- **prestigious + university/award/institution/prize**：高 status。
- **illustrious + career/history/predecessor**：長期顯赫成就。
- **reputable + company/source/provider/dealer**：可靠聲譽。
- **renown** 是名詞：achieve renown；**renowned** 是形容詞。
- 不要把 **popular, notorious, recognisable, influential** 自動當作 renowned 的全面同義詞。
