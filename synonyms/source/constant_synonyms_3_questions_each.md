# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Constant (adjective)

**`constant` 可以表示沒有中斷、持續不退、保持不變、維持穩定、一再發生、沒完沒了、完全不被打斷，或品質／表現一致。不同替代表達的核心差別，是『continuous vs continual』『persistent vs unchanging』『steady vs consistent』『neutral continuity vs annoying repetition』。**

核心替代表達：

`continuous / persistent / unchanging / steady / continual / incessant / uninterrupted / consistent`

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Continuous

**連續不斷的；持續的**

當 `constant` 指某件事沒有中斷、一直持續時，`continuous` 很自然。它強調 uninterrupted duration，而不只是頻繁出現。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The machine produces a continuous humming sound.

這部機器持續發出沒有間斷的嗡嗡聲。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **continuing without interruption**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、一般、技術

---

## 02 Persistent

**持續存在的；反覆不退的**

當 `constant` 指問題、症狀、壓力或聲音長時間不消失時，`persistent` 很合適。它強調『一直存在／很難擺脫』，不一定完全沒有間斷。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Persistent noise from the road disturbed her sleep.

道路持續的噪音影響她睡眠。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **continuing or recurring despite efforts to stop it**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 03 Unchanging

**不變的；始終如一的**

當 `constant` 指某個數值、狀態、態度或特徵保持不變時，`unchanging` 很精準。它不表示『不停發生』，而是表示 state remains the same。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The temperature remained unchanging throughout the test.

整個測試期間，溫度一直保持不變。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **remaining the same over time**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、正式

---

## 04 Steady

**穩定的；持續而有規律的**

當 `constant` 指速度、增長、供應、壓力或進展保持穩定，不突然上落時，`steady` 很自然。它重點是 consistency and stability。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The company reported steady growth over three years.

公司錄得連續三年的穩定增長。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **regular, stable, and not changing suddenly**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 05 Continual

**接連不斷的；一再發生的**

當 `constant` 指同類事情反覆出現、常常發生，但中間可以有間隔時，`continual` 很精準。它和 `continuous` 的核心差別就是是否有 interruptions。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Continual interruptions made it hard to concentrate.

接連不斷的打擾令人難以集中。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **happening repeatedly with possible breaks in between**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、一般

---

## 06 Incessant

**不停的；沒完沒了的**

當 `constant` 指某種噪音、抱怨、談話、雨勢等幾乎不停，而且令人厭煩時，`incessant` 很自然。它比 continuous 更有 negative tone。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The incessant barking kept the neighbours awake.

沒完沒了的狗吠令鄰居無法入睡。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **continuing without stopping, often annoyingly**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、書面、一般

---

## 07 Uninterrupted

**不間斷的；連續的**

當 `constant` 特別強調過程、服務、供應或一段時間完全沒有被中斷時，`uninterrupted` 很精準。它比 continuous 更直接突出 no break。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The hospital requires an uninterrupted power supply.

醫院需要不間斷的電力供應。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **continuing with no break or interruption**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、技術、一般

---

## 08 Consistent

**一致的；穩定的**

當 `constant` 指表現、品質、行為或結果一直保持相近水平時，`consistent` 很自然。它重點是 pattern stays reliably similar，而不是完全不變。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She has shown consistent performance throughout the year.

她全年表現一直穩定。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **remaining reliably similar in quality, behaviour, or results**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

# 02 False synonyms

## Constant / Continuous
`continuous` 強調完全沒有中斷。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
There was constant noise all day.  
A continuous tone played for thirty seconds without stopping.

## Constant / Persistent
`persistent` 強調問題、症狀或情況一直不退／很難擺脫。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
She has constant back pain.  
She has persistent pain that has not improved for weeks.

## Constant / Unchanging
`unchanging` 不表示「不停發生」，而表示某個狀態、數值或態度保持相同。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The machine makes a constant noise.  
The temperature remained unchanging throughout the test.

## Constant / Steady
`steady` 強調穩定、有規律、不突然波動。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The company experienced constant growth.  
The company experienced steady growth over five years.

## Constant / Continual
`continual` 表示同類事情反覆出現，但中間可以有停頓。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Continuous rain fell for two hours without stopping.  
Continual showers returned throughout the day.

## Constant / Incessant
`incessant` 常帶負面語氣：沒完沒了、令人厭煩。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The machine made a constant sound.  
The neighbours complained about the incessant barking.

## Constant / Uninterrupted
`uninterrupted` 特別直接強調完全沒有 break。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The hospital needs a constant power supply.  
The hospital needs an uninterrupted power supply.

## Constant / Consistent
`consistent` 強調品質、表現、行為或結果一直保持相近水平，不表示完全不變。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Her effort was constant.  
Her performance was consistent throughout the year.

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Continuous vs Continual** — continuous = 沒有中斷；continual = 反覆發生，中間可停。
- **Persistent vs Continuous** — persistent = 不消失／難擺脫；continuous = 不中斷。
- **Unchanging vs Steady** — unchanging = 完全不變；steady = 可以有小變化，但整體穩定。
- **Steady vs Consistent** — steady 常指速度、增長、壓力；consistent 常指品質、表現、結果。
- **Incessant vs Continuous** — incessant 額外帶負面、令人煩厭的語氣。
- **Uninterrupted vs Continuous** — 意思很近；uninterrupted 更直接突出 no break。
- **Constant ≠ frequent** — frequent = 經常發生，但不一定幾乎一直存在。
- **Constant ≠ chronic** — chronic 偏長期問題／疾病；constant 偏持續、頻繁或不變。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

- **continuous** = adjective；**continuously** = adverb；**continuity** = noun
- **persistent** = adjective；**persist** = verb；**persistence** = noun；**persistently** = adverb
- **unchanging** = adjective；不要把 `unchange` 當自然形容詞
- **steady** = adjective；**steadily** = adverb；**steadiness** = noun
- **continual** = adjective；**continually** = adverb
- **incessant** = adjective；**incessantly** = adverb
- **uninterrupted** = adjective；**uninterruptedly** = adverb（較少用）
- **consistent** = adjective；**consistently** = adverb；**consistency** = noun

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 完全沒有中斷？ → **continuous**
2. 問題／症狀一直不退？ → **persistent**
3. 狀態／數值完全不變？ → **unchanging**
4. 速度／增長／壓力保持穩定？ → **steady**
5. 同類事情反覆發生但有間隔？ → **continual**
6. 幾乎不停而且令人厭煩？ → **incessant**
7. 特別強調完全沒有 break？ → **uninterrupted**
8. 品質／表現／結果保持可靠一致？ → **consistent**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`continuous / persistent / unchanging / steady / continual / incessant / uninterrupted / consistent`

最重要的一點：

> **continuous ≠ continual ≠ consistent**

`continuous` = 完全不中斷；  
`continual` = 反覆發生但可有間隔；  
`consistent` = 品質／表現保持相近。
