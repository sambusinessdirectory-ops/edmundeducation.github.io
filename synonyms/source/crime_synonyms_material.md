# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Crime

**罪行、違法、非法活動、犯罪行為、犯罪現象——究竟是哪一種 crime？**

本課聚焦：

**crime = an act or activity that breaks the law, especially one dealt with by the criminal justice system**

例如：

- commit a crime
- serious crime
- violent crime
- crime in the area
- prevent crime
- organised crime

核心近義與近義表達：

**offence; lawbreaking; illegal activity; criminal activity; wrongdoing; criminal conduct; violation of the law; criminal offence; unlawful conduct; criminality; delinquency**

> **Precision rule:**  
> `offence / criminal offence` 適合具體罪行。  
> `lawbreaking / illegal activity / criminal activity` 適合行為／活動。  
> `criminality` 適合整體犯罪現象。  
> `wrongdoing / unlawful conduct` 範圍更廣，不一定等同刑事犯罪。  
> `delinquency` 特別偏青少年違法。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Offence

**罪行；違法行為**

和 `crime` 最直接、最常用的近義詞之一，尤其在英式英語、法律和警方語境中。`offence` 可以涵蓋較輕和較重的違法行為。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Driving without insurance is a criminal offence.

無保險駕駛是一項刑事罪行。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- criminal offence
- serious offence
- minor offence
- commit an offence

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是在法律／警方語境中指一項具體違法行為？

---

## 02 Lawbreaking

**違法；犯法行為**

透明而廣泛的 paraphrase，表示違反法律的行為。比 `crime` 更概念化，常用於描述現象、行為模式或一般違法。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The campaign aims to reduce lawbreaking among young people.

這項活動旨在減少年輕人的違法行為。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- reduce lawbreaking
- patterns of lawbreaking
- serious lawbreaking
- lawbreaking behaviour

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是在概括『違反法律』這種行為，而不是單一罪名？

---

## 03 Illegal activity

**非法活動；違法活動**

廣泛、透明的近義表達，適合描述一類違法行為或持續進行的活動。比 `crime` 更偏 activity，而不是特定罪名。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The investigation uncovered illegal activity at the company.

調查揭發公司內的非法活動。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- illegal activity
- suspected illegal activity
- criminal activity
- prevent illegal activity

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是在說一類持續／多次發生的非法活動？

---

## 04 Criminal activity

**犯罪活動**

比 `illegal activity` 更明確地指刑事犯罪行為。常見於警方、新聞、犯罪學和安全語境。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The area has seen an increase in criminal activity.

該區的犯罪活動有所增加。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- criminal activity
- organised criminal activity
- suspected criminal activity
- criminal activity in the area

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否要明確指出是刑事犯罪活動，而非一般違規？

---

## 05 Wrongdoing

**不法行為；不當行為**

可涵蓋違法或不道德行為。比 `crime` 更廣，因為 wrongdoing 不一定達到刑事罪行程度。適合企業、公共機構、調查與倫理語境。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The inquiry found evidence of serious wrongdoing.

調查發現嚴重不當行為的證據。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- serious wrongdoing
- alleged wrongdoing
- evidence of wrongdoing
- corporate wrongdoing

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是較廣義的不法／不當行為，而不一定已構成刑事罪行？

---

## 06 Criminal conduct

**犯罪行為；刑事行為**

較正式的法律／調查用語，強調某人的行為本身具有刑事性。比 `crime` 更行為導向。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The report found evidence of criminal conduct.

報告發現犯罪行為的證據。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- criminal conduct
- alleged criminal conduct
- evidence of criminal conduct
- investigate criminal conduct

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是正式地描述一組行為具有刑事性？

---

## 07 Violation of the law

**違反法律；違法行為**

正式、透明的 paraphrase，強調某行為違反法律規定。比 `crime` 稍解釋性，也可涵蓋未必屬重罪的違法。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The conduct may amount to a violation of the law.

這種行為可能構成違法。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- violation of the law
- serious legal violation
- violate the law
- legal violation

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否重點在『違反法律規定』這個法律關係？

---

## 08 Criminal offence

**刑事罪行；刑事犯罪**

比單獨 `offence` 更明確地限定刑事範圍。適合正式法律、警方、判刑和法規語境。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Fraud is a serious criminal offence.

詐騙是一項嚴重刑事罪行。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- serious criminal offence
- commit a criminal offence
- criminal offence punishable by
- convicted of a criminal offence

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否需要明確指出是一項刑事罪行，而不是行政／民事違規？

---

## 09 Unlawful conduct

**非法行為；不合法行為**

較正式、較廣泛的法律表達，表示行為不符合法律。它未必等同刑事罪行，亦可涵蓋民事或行政違法。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The regulator investigated allegations of unlawful conduct.

監管機構調查有關非法行為的指控。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- unlawful conduct
- alleged unlawful conduct
- engage in unlawful conduct
- unlawful behaviour

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否要正式表達『不合法的行為』，但不一定限定刑事罪？

---

## 10 Criminality

**犯罪行為；犯罪現象；犯罪性**

較抽象、正式，常指犯罪作為社會現象、傾向或一系列行為，而不是單一 crime。常見於犯罪學、新聞和政策分析。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The study explores links between poverty and criminality.

研究探討貧窮與犯罪現象之間的關係。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- organised criminality
- patterns of criminality
- serious criminality
- combat criminality

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是在談犯罪作為現象、模式或整體問題，而不是單一案件？

---

## 11 Delinquency

**違法行為；少年犯罪；失德行為**

常見於 `juvenile delinquency`，指青少年違法或反社會行為。它比一般 `crime` 更具年齡與社會行為語境，不適合所有犯罪。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The programme aims to reduce juvenile delinquency.

這個計劃旨在減少青少年違法行為。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- juvenile delinquency
- youth delinquency
- delinquent behaviour
- reduce delinquency

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否特別是青少年／未成年人違法或反社會行為？

# 02 False synonyms

以下詞語和 **crime** 關係密切，但可能只是違規、不當行為、民事侵權、公共秩序問題或道德概念。

## 01 Misconduct

**不當行為不一定犯法**

`misconduct` 可違反職業、學校或機構規則，但未必構成 crime。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Professional misconduct may lead to discipline without being a criminal offence.

---

## 02 Violation

**違規不一定是刑事犯罪**

`violation` 可以是違反規則、政策或法律；範圍比 crime 更廣。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A parking violation may be unlawful without being treated like a serious crime.

---

## 03 Breach

**違反義務／規則不等於犯罪**

`breach` 常見於 contract, duty, policy, confidentiality 等。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A breach of contract is generally not the same as a criminal offence.

---

## 04 Wrongdoing

**可包括不道德但合法的行為**

`wrongdoing` 比 crime 更廣，並不保證有刑事責任。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The inquiry found wrongdoing but no criminal offence.

---

## 05 Sin

**宗教／道德概念不等於法律犯罪**

`sin` 是宗教或道德上的過錯，法律未必禁止。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Something can be considered a sin without being a crime.

---

## 06 Disorder

**混亂／治安問題不等於具體犯罪**

`disorder` 可指公共秩序混亂，也可由多種犯罪或非犯罪行為造成。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Police dealt with disorder as well as individual offences.

---

## 07 Accident

**意外不等於犯罪**

`accident` 缺乏故意性，且未必涉及違法；某些事故可因疏忽導致刑責，但概念仍不同。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The collision was an accident, not a deliberate crime.

---

## 08 Civil wrong

**民事侵權不等於刑事罪行**

`civil wrong` 通常透過賠償等民事程序處理，而 crime 由刑事司法系統處理。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Negligence may be a civil wrong without being a crime.

---

## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** Offence · 罪行；違法行為

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

The police recorded the crime as a serious case.

### 中文翻譯

### OPTIONS


- **A. offence**

- **B. lawbreaking**

- **C. misconduct**

- **D. wrongdoing**

- **E. violation**

- **F. incident**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**offence · 罪行；違法行為**

The police recorded the **offence** as a serious case.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. offence ✓** — `offence` 是本題最貼切的改寫；它保留 crime 的違法／犯罪核心，並符合 **Offence** 的特定法律語境。

- **B. lawbreaking** — `lawbreaking` = 一般違反法律的行為，偏概括。

- **C. misconduct** — `misconduct` = 不當行為，可能只是職業／機構規則問題。

- **D. wrongdoing** — `wrongdoing` = 不法或不當行為，範圍比 crime 更廣。

- **E. violation** — `violation` = 違反規則／政策／法律，未必屬刑事犯罪。

- **F. incident** — `incident` = 事件，本身不表示違法。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Crime vs offence

英式法律語境：

- commit a crime
- commit an offence
- criminal offence
- serious offence

`offence` 很常用來指具體法律罪名／違法行為。

---

## 02 Crime as count and uncount noun

### Count
`He committed a crime.`  
= 一項罪行

### Uncount / general phenomenon
`Crime has fallen in the area.`  
= 犯罪現象／犯罪率

這個 distinction 很重要：
- single crime → offence / criminal offence
- crime generally → criminality / lawbreaking / criminal activity

---

## 03 Illegal vs criminal

- **illegal** = 不合法
- **criminal** = 屬刑事法範圍

所有 criminal conduct 都是 unlawful，
但不是所有 unlawful conduct 都是 criminal.

---

## 04 Wrongdoing

`wrongdoing` 可包括：
- unethical behaviour
- abuse of position
- policy violations
- unlawful behaviour
- criminal behaviour

所以它比 crime 更廣。

---

## 05 Crime vs violation

`violation` 可指：
- traffic violation
- policy violation
- human-rights violation
- legal violation

未必都是 criminal offence。

---

## 06 Criminality

抽象用法：

- organised criminality
- patterns of criminality
- serious criminality

適合犯罪學、政策和社會研究，不適合替代每一個單一 crime。

---

## 07 Delinquency

最常見：

- juvenile delinquency
- youth delinquency

比 `youth crime` 更偏社會學／犯罪學語域。

---

## 08 Common crime collocations

- violent crime
- property crime
- organised crime
- cybercrime
- hate crime
- financial crime
- crime prevention
- crime rate
- crime scene
