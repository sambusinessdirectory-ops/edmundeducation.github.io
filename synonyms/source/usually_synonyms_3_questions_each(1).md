# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Usually

**`usually` 可以表示在正常情況下、一般而言、典型地、常見地、按慣例、總體而言，或多數時候。不同替代表達的核心差別，是『正常情況』『整體概括』『典型模式』『普遍程度』『規則性』『語域』。**

核心替代表達：

`normally / generally / typically / commonly / ordinarily / as a rule / in general / more often than not`

> **核心原則**：先判斷 usually 指的是 **normal circumstances, broad generalisation, a typical pattern, common occurrence, ordinary conditions, a general rule, an overall statement, or a majority of cases**。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Normally

**通常；一般情況下**

和 `usually` 最直接、最接近的替代表達之一。當重點是某件事在正常情況下大多如此時，`normally` 很自然。它常帶有『若沒有特別情況』的意味。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The office normally closes at six.

辦公室通常在六時關門。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **what happens in normal circumstances**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 02 Generally

**一般而言；通常**

當 `usually` 是對一類情況作概括，而不是描述固定慣例時，`generally` 很自然。它比 `normally` 更偏 broad generalisation。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> People generally prefer clear instructions.

一般而言，人們較喜歡清楚的指示。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **what is true in most cases overall**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、正式、學術

---

## 03 Typically

**通常；典型地**

當 `usually` 指某類人、事物或情況具有一種典型模式時，`typically` 很精準。它不只表示頻率，也帶『符合典型模式』的意味。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The course typically lasts twelve weeks.

這個課程通常為期十二星期。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **what is typical or characteristic of a case or category**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、研究、正式

---

## 04 Commonly

**通常；常見地**

當 `usually` 指某種做法、名稱、現象或形式很常見時，`commonly` 很自然。它比 `normally` 更偏 prevalence。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> This expression is commonly used in informal English.

這個表達在非正式英語中很常用。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **what is frequently found, used, or observed**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、正式、教育

---

## 05 Ordinarily

**通常；平常**

較書面，意思和 `normally` 接近，表示在平常情況下通常會如此。它往往暗示現時情況可能有例外。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Ordinarily, the process takes two working days.

一般情況下，這個程序需時兩個工作天。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **what would normally happen under ordinary conditions**

**REGISTER · 語域**

書面、正式

---

## 06 As a rule

**通常；一般來說**

是一個常用片語，表示『一般來說／通常情況下』。它很適合描述慣例、常規或大多數情況，但不是百分之百。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> As a rule, visitors must register at reception.

一般來說，訪客必須在接待處登記。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a general rule or usual practice, allowing exceptions**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、正式

---

## 07 In general

**一般來說；總體而言**

當 `usually` 的意思更接近『整體上、大體而言』時，`in general` 很自然。它偏總結和概括，不適合所有頻率語境。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> In general, smaller classes allow more individual support.

一般來說，較小班別能提供更多個別支援。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a broad overall generalisation**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 08 More often than not

**多數時候；往往**

較口語，表示某事發生的次數多於不發生，意思接近 usually，但帶較強的 conversational tone。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> More often than not, the simplest solution works best.

多數時候，最簡單的解決方法最有效。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **happening in a majority of cases**

**REGISTER · 語域**

口語、一般

# 02 False synonyms

## Usually / Normally
`normally` 最接近 usually，但常多一層「在正常情況下」的意味。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The train usually arrives before nine.  
The train normally arrives before nine unless there is a delay.

## Usually / Generally
`generally` 更偏對整體趨勢或一類情況作概括。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
I usually walk to work.  
People generally prefer shorter waiting times.

## Usually / Typically
`typically` 不只表示常常如此，也暗示這是某類情況的典型模式。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The meeting usually lasts an hour.  
The course typically lasts twelve weeks.

## Usually / Commonly
`commonly` 強調某事常見、常被使用或常被觀察到。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
I usually use this expression.  
This expression is commonly used in informal English.

## Usually / Ordinarily
`ordinarily` 較書面，表示平常／一般情況下通常如此。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
We usually reply within two days.  
Ordinarily, we reply within two working days.

## Usually / As a rule
`as a rule` 表示一項通常遵守的慣例，但並非百分之百沒有例外。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
We usually check the data twice.  
As a rule, we check the data twice before publication.

## Usually / In general
`in general` 更偏「總體而言」，不是單純頻率副詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
I usually work from home on Fridays.  
In general, remote workers value flexibility.

## Usually / More often than not
`more often than not` 較口語，表示發生次數多於不發生。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
He usually remembers his notebook.  
More often than not, he remembers to bring it.

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Usually vs Normally** — normally 多一層「在正常情況下」意味。
- **Generally vs In general** — 意思接近；前者是副詞，後者是片語。
- **Typically vs Usually** — typically 強調典型模式，不只是頻率。
- **Commonly vs Usually** — commonly 更偏「常見／常被使用」。
- **Ordinarily vs Normally** — ordinarily 更書面。
- **As a rule vs Usually** — as a rule 更像規則／慣例。
- **More often than not vs Usually** — 前者更口語，也更明確表示「多數情況」。
- **Always ≠ Usually** — usually 容許例外，always 通常表示沒有例外。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Normally / Generally / Typically / Commonly
都是副詞：

> normally happens  
> generally true  
> typically lasts  
> commonly used

不要用形容詞：

> normal happens ✗  
> typical lasts ✗

## As a rule
固定片語：

> As a rule, ...

## In general
固定片語：

> In general, ...

不要寫：

> in generally ✗

## More often than not
固定：

> more often **than** not

不是：

> more often then not ✗

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 想說「在正常情況下」？ → **normally**
2. 想作整體概括？ → **generally**
3. 想說某類情況的典型模式？ → **typically**
4. 想說某種用法／現象很常見？ → **commonly**
5. 想用較書面的「平常情況下」？ → **ordinarily**
6. 想表達一般規則／慣例？ → **as a rule**
7. 想說「總體而言」？ → **in general**
8. 想口語地說「多數時候」？ → **more often than not**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`normally / generally / typically / commonly / ordinarily / as a rule / in general / more often than not`

尤其要記住：

> **normally** = 正常情況下  
> **generally** = 一般而言  
> **typically** = 典型地  
> **commonly** = 常見地  
> **as a rule** = 按慣例  
> **in general** = 總體而言  
> **more often than not** = 多數時候

最重要的一點：

> **usually ≠ typically ≠ generally**

`usually` 偏頻率；`typically` 偏典型模式；`generally` 偏整體概括。
