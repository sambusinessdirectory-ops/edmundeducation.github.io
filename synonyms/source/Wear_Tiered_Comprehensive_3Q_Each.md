# WEAR · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Wear

**本課集中處理 wear = clothing / footwear / accessories on the body 的義項。** 最重要的區別是 **state vs action**：**wear / have on / be wearing** 描述已經穿着的狀態；**put on / don** 描述『穿上／戴上』的動作。另一些表達則強調整體服裝風格或顯眼展示。

你提供的 **have on; be dressed in; put on; dress in** 正好代表 **穿着狀態、整體服裝、穿上動作、穿衣風格** 四個核心方向。

這個義項的高質量近義詞不宜過多，因此本次保留 **8 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達。共 **24 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Wear 的語境 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|
| 口語：身上穿／戴着某物 | **have on** |
| 描述整體服裝／顏色 | **be dressed in** |
| 穿上／戴上的動作 | **put on** |
| 以某風格／方式穿衣 | **dress in** |
| 特定時間正穿着 | **be wearing** |
| 正式：穿上制服／防護裝備 | **don** |
| 顯眼展示衣物／外觀 | **sport** |
| 正式／文學：身穿／裹着 | **be clad in** |

## Tier 1 — Core wearing / dressing equivalents · 核心穿着與穿衣近義詞

### 01 Have on

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：穿着；戴着  
**語域**：neutral / conversational  
**核心語義**：to be wearing a particular item of clothing, footwear, or accessory  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **wear** 在日常口語中非常直接的替代表達，重點是『身上正穿／戴着甚麼』。  
**常見搭配**：have a coat on; have glasses on; have shoes on; have a hat on.  
**常見搭配對象**：coat, shoes, glasses, hat, uniform.  
**與 wear 的辨析**：Have on 與 wear 都可描述穿着狀態，但 have on 較口語，而且常指當下身上已有某件物品。  
**使用限制**：正式寫作通常用 wear / be wearing；表示『穿上』的動作時應用 put on。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She had a dark blue coat on when she arrived.  
她到達時穿着一件深藍色外套。

### 02 Be dressed in

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：穿着……服裝；身穿  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：to be wearing a particular set, colour, style, or type of clothing  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很自然，尤其適合描述整體服裝、顏色或服飾風格。  
**常見搭配**：be dressed in black; be dressed in uniform; be dressed in formal clothes; smartly dressed.  
**常見搭配對象**：black, uniform, formal clothes, traditional dress, casual clothes.  
**與 wear 的辨析**：Be dressed in 通常描述整體穿着；wear 可以只指一件衣物或配飾。  
**使用限制**：眼鏡、戒指、手錶等配飾通常直接用 wear，不自然說 be dressed in glasses。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The performers were dressed in traditional costumes.  
表演者身穿傳統服飾。

### 03 Put on

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：穿上；戴上  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to place clothing, footwear, or an accessory on the body  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **wear** 最常見的相關動詞，但它表示『穿上』這個動作，而不是穿着狀態。  
**常見搭配**：put on a coat; put on shoes; put on glasses; put on a hat.  
**常見搭配對象**：coat, shoes, glasses, hat, gloves.  
**與 wear 的辨析**：Wear = 已穿着的狀態；put on = 開始穿／戴的動作。  
**使用限制**：不能把所有 wear 句子直接換成 put on；時態與事件結構常需要重寫。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Put on your jacket before you go outside.  
外出前穿上外套。

### 04 Dress in

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：穿着；以……服裝打扮  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：to wear a particular type, style, or colour of clothing, often as a general way of dressing  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 適合描述某人的整體衣着方式、制服、顏色或風格。  
**常見搭配**：dress in black; dress in uniform; dress in layers; dress in formal clothes.  
**常見搭配對象**：black, uniform, layers, formal clothes, casual clothes.  
**與 wear 的辨析**：Dress in 比 wear 更着重整體穿衣方式或風格。  
**使用限制**：單件配飾如 watch / ring / glasses 通常不用 dress in。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Staff are expected to dress in business attire for formal meetings.  
員工在正式會議中應穿商務服裝。

### 05 Be wearing

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：正穿着；正戴着  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to have clothing, footwear, jewellery, or another item on the body at a particular time  
**用法提示**：這是 **wear** 最直接的進行式改寫，尤其當句子描述某個具體時間點的穿着狀態。  
**常見搭配**：be wearing a suit; be wearing glasses; be wearing a uniform; be wearing a watch.  
**常見搭配對象**：clothing, glasses, jewellery, uniform, shoes.  
**與 wear 的辨析**：Be wearing 特別適合『當時正穿着』；simple wear 亦可表示習慣性穿着。  
**使用限制**：習慣性或一般事實通常直接用 wear 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He was wearing a grey suit at the ceremony.  
他在典禮上穿着灰色西裝。

## Tier 2 — Formal / display-oriented variants · 正式穿戴與顯眼展示變體

### 06 Don

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：穿上；戴上  
**語域**：formal / literary  
**核心語義**：to put on a piece of clothing, especially something formal, distinctive, or protective  
**用法提示**：當 wear 的實際意思是『穿上』某件制服、禮服、保護裝備時可用。  
**常見搭配**：don a uniform; don protective clothing; don a mask; don formal dress.  
**常見搭配對象**：uniform, mask, gown, protective clothing, helmet.  
**與 wear 的辨析**：Don 等於較正式的 put on，表示動作而非持續穿着狀態。  
**使用限制**：日常對話通常用 put on；不能直接替代所有 wear。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Visitors must don protective clothing before entering the laboratory.  
訪客進入實驗室前必須穿上防護衣物。

### 07 Sport

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：穿着／戴着並展示；以……示人  
**語域**：informal / journalistic  
**核心語義**：to wear or display a noticeable item, style, or appearance with some degree of visibility or pride  
**用法提示**：當 wear 的物品很顯眼，例如 beard, jacket, logo, hairstyle, glasses，可用 sport。  
**常見搭配**：sport a beard; sport a new jacket; sport dark glasses; sport a logo.  
**常見搭配對象**：beard, jacket, glasses, hairstyle, logo.  
**與 wear 的辨析**：Sport 不只是『穿着』，還有顯眼展示、讓人注意到的語氣。  
**使用限制**：普通、不顯眼的日常穿衣不一定適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He was sporting a bright red jacket at the event.  
他在活動上穿着一件非常搶眼的鮮紅色外套。

## Tier 3 — Literary clothing-description variant · 文學式服裝描寫變體

### 08 Be clad in

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：身穿；裹着  
**語域**：formal / literary  
**核心語義**：to be dressed or covered in a particular material or type of clothing  
**用法提示**：這是較正式／文學化的描述，常用於 uniforms, armour, heavy clothing, black, protective gear。  
**常見搭配**：be clad in black; clad in armour; clad in protective gear; warmly clad.  
**常見搭配對象**：black, armour, protective gear, heavy clothing, uniform.  
**與 wear 的辨析**：Be clad in 比 be dressed in 更書面、更有描寫感。  
**使用限制**：日常口語通常太正式；配飾類也不自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The guards were clad in heavy protective gear.  
守衛身穿厚重防護裝備。

## 04 False synonyms / wear traps · 容易誤選的穿着動作與狀態陷阱

### 01 Carry

**攜帶 ≠ wear**  
carry 表示拿着／帶着，不代表物件在身上穿戴。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Carry is about possession/transport.

### 02 Use

**使用 ≠ wear**  
use 太廣；穿戴是使用某物的一種方式，但不是同義。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Use is much broader.

### 03 Hold

**拿着 ≠ wear**  
hold 表示手持，不是穿戴。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Hold is hand/position-focused.

### 04 Put on

**只在『穿上』動作 sense 接近**  
put on 表示開始穿戴，不是穿着狀態。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Put on is eventive.

### 05 Dress

**穿衣／給……穿衣 ≠ 所有 wear**  
dress 可指穿衣這個行為或給某人穿衣，語法不同。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Dress has different grammar.

### 06 Sport

**只在顯眼展示 sense 接近**  
sport 有展示／顯眼語氣，不是所有 wear 都自然。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Sport is display-focused.

### 07 Clothe

**給……穿衣 ≠ wear**  
clothe 通常是給另一個人穿衣或提供衣物。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Clothe is causative.

### 08 Wear / wore / worn

**不規則變化陷阱**  
wear–wore–worn；不要把 worn 當作一般現在式。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check verb forms.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Have on · 穿着；戴着

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

She was wearing a long coat when I saw her.

**中文翻譯**  
我見到她時，她穿着一件長外套。

- A. Have on
- B. Be wearing
- C. Put on
- D. Carry
- E. Hold
- F. Use

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Have on** · 穿着；戴着

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
She had a long coat on when I saw her.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Have on ✓** — be wearing a particular item of clothing, footwear, or accessory. Here, the sentence describes what was already on her body at that moment. That makes **Have on** the most precise replacement for this particular sense of *wear*.
- **B.** **Be wearing** — have clothing, footwear, jewellery, or another item on the body at a particular time. This is good for a temporary wearing state, but it does not express the action of putting something on or an overall style of dress. The decisive clue is that the sentence describes what was already on her body at that moment.
- **C.** **Put on** — place clothing, footwear, or an accessory on the body. This is eventive: it means starting to wear something, so it cannot replace a sentence that only describes an existing wearing state. The decisive clue is that the sentence describes what was already on her body at that moment.
- **D.** **Carry** — have something with you or transport it. Carrying something does not mean it is worn on the body. The decisive clue is that the sentence describes what was already on her body at that moment.
- **E.** **Hold** — keep something in the hands or in a position. Holding something is a hand/position action, not wearing. The decisive clue is that the sentence describes what was already on her body at that moment.
- **F.** **Use** — employ something for a purpose. This is far too broad and does not specify clothing or accessories. The decisive clue is that the sentence describes what was already on her body at that moment.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **wear vs have on**：have on 較口語，表示身上已有某物。
- **wear vs be dressed in**：後者更常描述整體衣着或顏色。
- **wear vs put on**：wear 是狀態；put on 是穿上的動作。
- **wear vs dress in**：dress in 強調穿衣風格／方式。
- **wear vs be wearing**：be wearing 特別適合當下或某時間點。
- **wear vs don**：don 是較正式的『穿上』。
- **wear vs sport**：sport 有顯眼展示、讓人注意的意味。
- **wear vs be clad in**：be clad in 更書面／文學化。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & aspect traps

- **wear + clothing/accessory**：wear a coat / glasses / a watch。
- **have + item + on**：have a jacket on。
- **be dressed in + colour/style/clothing**：be dressed in black。
- **put on + item**：put on your shoes。
- **dress in + colour/style**：dress in layers。
- **be wearing + item**：She is wearing a uniform。
- **don + formal/protective item**：don a gown / mask / uniform。
- **sport + conspicuous item/appearance**：sport a beard / bright jacket。
- **wear–wore–worn** 是不規則變化。
- 典型錯誤：**wear** 不是『穿上』這個瞬間動作；如果句子有 **before / then / first** 等改變狀態的線索，通常要考慮 **put on / don**。
