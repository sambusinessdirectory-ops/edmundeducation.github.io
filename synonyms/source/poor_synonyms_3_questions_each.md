# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Poor

**本課集中在 `poor = lacking money/resources` 的意思，而不是 `poor quality / poor performance / poor health`。不同替代表達會按收入水平、物質匱乏程度、是否需要援助、社會機會、地區資源和極端貧困程度來區分。**

核心替代表達：

`impoverished / low-income / needy / disadvantaged / underprivileged / deprived / poverty-stricken / destitute`

> **核心原則**：先判斷 `poor` 是 **low income, material poverty, need for aid, social disadvantage, lack of normal opportunities, area deprivation, severe poverty, or destitution**。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Impoverished

**貧困的；生活困苦的**

比 `poor` 更正式，強調因缺乏金錢和資源而陷入貧困。常形容 communities, families, regions，語氣比 `low-income` 更強。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The charity supports impoverished families in remote areas.

慈善機構支援偏遠地區的貧困家庭。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **seriously lacking money and material resources**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、新聞、社會議題

---

## 02 Low-income

**低收入的**

較中性、政策化，重點是收入水平低，而不一定表示生活極度困苦。常見於 housing, households, families, workers。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The programme provides rent support for low-income households.

該計劃為低收入住戶提供租金支援。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **having a relatively low level of income**

**REGISTER · 語域**

政策、社會、行政

---

## 03 Needy

**有需要的；貧困的**

表示需要經濟或物質幫助，常用於 charity / aid 語境。它比 `poor` 更突出『需要援助』。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The fund provides meals for needy families.

該基金為有需要的家庭提供膳食。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **in need of financial or material assistance**

**REGISTER · 語域**

慈善、一般、較傳統

---

## 04 Disadvantaged

**弱勢的；處於不利條件的**

範圍比 `poor` 更廣，可因收入、教育、地區、社會背景或機會不足而處於不利位置。並不自動等於貧窮。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The scheme helps disadvantaged students access tutoring.

該計劃幫助弱勢學生獲得補習支援。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **having fewer social, educational, or economic advantages**

**REGISTER · 語域**

教育、政策、社會

---

## 05 Underprivileged

**資源不足的；弱勢的**

表示缺少大多數人可享有的資源、機會或服務。比 `low-income` 更廣，也帶社會不平等意味。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The centre offers free classes to underprivileged children.

中心為資源不足的兒童提供免費課程。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **lacking normal social or economic advantages**

**REGISTER · 語域**

社會、教育、慈善

---

## 06 Deprived

**貧困的；資源匱乏的**

常形容 areas, communities, children，強調缺少基本資源、服務或機會。英式英語和社會政策語境很常見。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The project focuses on deprived neighbourhoods with limited public services.

該項目集中支援公共服務有限的貧困社區。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **lacking basic resources, services, or opportunities**

**REGISTER · 語域**

英式、政策、社會

---

## 07 Poverty-stricken

**極度貧困的；窮困潦倒的**

語氣強烈，表示深陷貧困。比 `poor`、`low-income` 都強得多，通常不適合中性統計語境。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Aid workers reached several poverty-stricken villages after the floods.

洪災後，救援人員抵達數個極度貧困的村落。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **suffering from severe poverty**

**REGISTER · 語域**

新聞、描述性、較強烈

---

## 08 Destitute

**一貧如洗的；赤貧的**

語氣最強之一，表示幾乎沒有金錢、住所或基本生活資源。不能用來泛指一般低收入人士。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The shelter provides emergency beds for destitute people.

庇護中心為赤貧人士提供緊急床位。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **having virtually no money or basic means of support**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、社福、強烈

# 02 False synonyms

## Poor / Impoverished
`impoverished` 比 `poor` 更正式，也通常暗示較深的物質匱乏。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A poor family may have limited income.  
An impoverished family may lack basic healthcare, housing, or reliable utilities.

## Poor / Low-income
`low-income` 是中性收入分類，不等於「極度貧困」。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A low-income household may still have stable housing.  
An impoverished household may struggle to meet basic needs.

## Poor / Needy
`needy` 重點是「需要援助」，不是單純收入低。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A low-income family is classified by income.  
A needy family is one that requires financial or material assistance.

## Poor / Disadvantaged
`disadvantaged` 可指教育、社會、地區或機會上的不利，不必然等於貧窮。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A disadvantaged student may lack educational opportunities.  
A poor student specifically lacks financial resources.

## Disadvantaged / Underprivileged
兩者接近；`underprivileged` 更強調缺少一般社會成員通常享有的資源和機會。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A disadvantaged student may face one or more barriers.  
An underprivileged child often lacks access to normal social or educational resources.

## Poor / Deprived
`deprived` 特別常形容地區、社區和兒童，強調基本資源／服務不足。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A poor person lacks money.  
A deprived neighbourhood may lack parks, transport, healthcare, and jobs.

## Poor / Poverty-stricken
`poverty-stricken` 語氣強很多，表示深陷嚴重貧困。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A poor district may have low average incomes.  
A poverty-stricken village may lack safe water and sanitation.

## Poor / Destitute
`destitute` 是最強烈的近義詞之一，表示幾乎沒有任何生活資源。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A low-income worker may still have a home and regular wages.  
A destitute person may have no money, food, or place to live.

|---|---|

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Impoverished vs Low-income** — impoverished 更嚴重；low-income 更中性、政策化。
- **Low-income vs Needy** — 前者按收入分類；後者按是否需要援助。
- **Poor vs Disadvantaged** — disadvantaged 範圍更廣，不只經濟。
- **Disadvantaged vs Underprivileged** — underprivileged 更突出缺少一般社會資源和機會。
- **Deprived vs Low-income** — deprived 常說地區資源／公共服務不足，不只收入。
- **Impoverished vs Poverty-stricken** — 後者語氣更強。
- **Poverty-stricken vs Destitute** — destitute 往往表示已失去幾乎所有基本生活支援。
- **Poor vs Affluent / Wealthy** — 後兩者是反義，不是程度不同的近義詞。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Low-income
作前置複合形容詞通常要用連字號：

> a low-income household  
> low-income workers

但：

> the household has a low income

## Impoverished / impoverishment
> impoverished families = 形容詞  
> impoverishment = 名詞

## Disadvantaged / disadvantage
> disadvantaged students = 形容詞  
> a disadvantage = 名詞

## Deprived / deprivation
> deprived areas = 形容詞  
> social deprivation = 名詞

## Poverty-stricken
固定複合形容詞：

> poverty-stricken communities

## Destitute / destitution
> destitute families = 形容詞  
> destitution = 名詞

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 想正式說「貧困、資源很少」？ → **impoverished**
2. 只按收入水平分類？ → **low-income**
3. 重點是需要援助？ → **needy**
4. 教育／社會／經濟多方面處於不利？ → **disadvantaged**
5. 缺乏正常資源和機會？ → **underprivileged**
6. 社區／地區缺乏服務和設施？ → **deprived**
7. 想強調嚴重貧困？ → **poverty-stricken**
8. 幾乎沒有錢、住所或基本生活來源？ → **destitute**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`impoverished / low-income / needy / disadvantaged / underprivileged / deprived / poverty-stricken / destitute`

最重要的 near-miss：

`homeless / unemployed / vulnerable / working-class / deprived / disadvantaged`

注意：這些詞有時和 poverty 有關，但並不自動等於 `poor`。

尤其要記住：

> **low-income** = 中性收入分類  
> **needy** = 需要援助  
> **disadvantaged** = 廣義處於不利  
> **deprived** = 資源／服務不足  
> **poverty-stricken** = 嚴重貧困  
> **destitute** = 赤貧
