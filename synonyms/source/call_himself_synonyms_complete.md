# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Call himself

「稱自己為……」，究竟是在**怎樣稱呼／看待自己**？

描述自己、視自己為、認為自己是、自我認同為、自稱某個身份、把自己塑造成某種形象——英文會按**內在認同、公開稱呼、自我評價、對外形象、正式程度及語氣**選用不同的說法。

> This material follows the same pedagogical architecture as the previous synonym sets: a 14-item synonym guide, 8 false-synonym contrasts, and 28 six-option exercises with full feedback. The lexical content below is curated around the meaning of **call himself + noun/adjective/complement**.

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## Call himself 的 14 種更精準說法

### 01 Describe himself as
**形容自己是；把自己描述為**

表示一個人用某些詞語**描述自己的身份、性格、背景或狀態**。語氣中性，重點在「怎樣描述」。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He describes himself as a practical person who prefers simple solutions.

他形容自己是一個務實、偏好簡單解決方法的人。

**USAGE CLUE**

- describe himself **as** + noun/adjective
- 重點是自我描述，不一定代表深層認同
- 常見於訪問、自我介紹、人物描述

---

### 02 Regard himself as
**視自己為；把自己看作**

表示一個人**在觀念上把自己歸入某種身份或角色**。比 `see himself as` 稍正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He regards himself as a teacher first and a researcher second.

他首先視自己為教師，其次才是研究人員。

**USAGE CLUE**

- regard himself **as** + noun/adjective
- 偏正式
- 強調「怎樣看待自己的身份或角色」

---

### 03 Consider himself
**認為自己是；把自己視為**

表示一個人經過判斷後**認為自己具有某種身份、特質或地位**。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He considers himself an experienced negotiator.

他認為自己是一名有經驗的談判者。

**USAGE CLUE**

- consider himself + noun/adjective
- 也可說 `consider himself to be ...`
- 通常**不用** `consider himself as ...` 表達這個意思

---

### 04 See himself as
**視自己為；認為自己是**

非常常見而自然，表示一個人的**自我形象或自我認知**。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He sees himself as someone who can bring different groups together.

他視自己為一個能把不同群體凝聚起來的人。

**USAGE CLUE**

- see himself **as** + noun/adjective
- 比 `regard himself as` 更日常
- 常用於身份、角色、自我認知

---

### 05 Think of himself as
**把自己當作；認為自己是**

表示一個人在心中**把自己理解為某種類型的人**。語氣自然、帶主觀自我概念。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He thinks of himself as an artist rather than a businessman.

他把自己看作藝術家，而不是商人。

**USAGE CLUE**

- think of himself **as** + noun/adjective
- 很適合身份、職業、性格類別
- 比 `consider himself` 更偏「自我概念」

---

### 06 Identify as
**自我認同為**

表示一個人把某種身份視為自己的一部分。常見於**身份、文化、職業、群體或個人認同**語境。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He identifies as a writer, although most of his income comes from teaching.

他自我認同為作家，雖然大部分收入來自教學。

**USAGE CLUE**

- identify **as** + identity/category
- 現代英語常直接用 `identify as`
- 不宜用來表達所有普通特質，例如通常不說 `*identify as tired`

---

### 07 Refer to himself as
**自稱；提到自己時稱自己為**

重點是他**實際使用某個稱號、名稱或身份詞來稱呼自己**。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> In interviews, he often refers to himself as an independent researcher.

在訪問中，他經常自稱為獨立研究員。

**USAGE CLUE**

- refer to himself **as** + noun/title
- 強調使用某個名稱或稱號
- 不一定表示別人也承認這個身份

---

### 08 Present himself as
**把自己呈現為；對外塑造自己為**

表示一個人在別人面前**刻意展現某種身份、形象或特質**。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He presents himself as a calm and reliable leader.

他把自己塑造成一個冷靜而可靠的領袖形象。

**USAGE CLUE**

- present himself **as** + noun/adjective
- 重點是對外呈現
- 不一定等於他內心真的如此看待自己

---

### 09 Portray himself as
**把自己描繪成；塑造成**

表示透過說話、寫作或公開形象，**把自己描繪成某種類型的人**。通常比 `present himself as` 更強調形象建構。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> In his memoir, he portrays himself as an outsider who challenged the system.

在回憶錄中，他把自己描繪成一個挑戰體制的局外人。

**USAGE CLUE**

- portray himself **as** + noun/adjective
- 常見於媒體、傳記、評論、敘事
- 有時帶「這是他塑造出的形象」的距離感

---

### 10 Characterize himself as
**把自己界定／描述為**

較正式，表示用某種**概括性的特徵或分類**來描述自己。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He characterizes himself as cautious rather than pessimistic.

他把自己界定為謹慎，而不是悲觀。

**USAGE CLUE**

- characterize himself **as** + noun/adjective
- 正式、分析性較強
- 常見於訪談、論述、學術或新聞語體

---

### 11 Style himself as
**自封為；自稱為；把自己塑造成**

表示一個人**採用某個稱號、角色或形象來稱呼／塑造自己**。常帶較正式、文學或略帶批判的語氣。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He styles himself as a defender of traditional craftsmanship.

他自稱為傳統工藝的捍衛者。

**USAGE CLUE**

- style himself **as** + title/role
- 常帶「這是他自己給自己的定位」之意
- 有時隱含別人未必同意

---

### 12 Bill himself as
**把自己宣傳為；自我標榜為**

表示一個人為了吸引注意、建立品牌或推廣自己，**公開宣傳自己是某種類型的人**。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He bills himself as a specialist in helping small companies grow.

他把自己宣傳為協助小型企業成長的專家。

**USAGE CLUE**

- bill himself **as** + role/expert/type
- 常見於廣告、表演、商業、自我推廣
- 強調宣傳意味

---

### 13 Label himself as
**給自己貼上……標籤；把自己歸類為**

表示明確地**用一個標籤或類別來定義自己**。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He is reluctant to label himself as either conservative or progressive.

他不願把自己標籤為保守派或進步派任何一方。

**USAGE CLUE**

- label himself **as** + category
- `label` 強調分類／標籤
- 常用於不願被簡單歸類的語境

---

### 14 Declare himself to be
**公開宣稱自己是**

表示較正式、明確地**宣布或宣稱自己具有某個身份、立場或狀態**。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He publicly declared himself to be an independent candidate.

他公開宣稱自己是獨立候選人。

**USAGE CLUE**

- declare himself **to be** + noun/adjective
- 也可說 `declare himself + noun/adjective`
- 比 `call himself` 更正式、更明確，也更像公開聲明

# 02 False synonyms

看起來和「稱自己／認為自己」有關，但語意、句型或語氣不同。

---

## 01 name himself / call himself

### 「給自己取名」不等於「把自己視為」

`name himself` 通常與**名字或正式名稱**有關；`call himself` 可以是名字，也可以是身份或稱號。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> He **named himself** Leo after moving abroad.  
> He **calls himself** a consultant.

---

## 02 introduce himself as / consider himself

### 「自我介紹為」不等於「內心認為自己是」

`introduce himself as` 是特定社交場合中的自我介紹行為；`consider himself` 是自我判斷。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> He **introduced himself as** the project manager.  
> He **considers himself** a patient person.

---

## 03 believe himself / consider himself

### 句型和自然度不同

現代一般英語通常說 `believe himself to be ...`，而不是直接 `believe himself + noun`；`consider himself + noun/adjective` 更自然。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> He **considers himself** an expert.  
> He **believes himself to be** an expert.

---

## 04 represent himself as / present himself as

### 「聲稱／表現成」可能帶更強的真偽含意

`represent himself as` 常表示他**聲稱自己具有某身份或資格**，某些語境甚至暗示可能失實；`present himself as` 更中性地描述對外形象。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> He **presented himself as** confident and approachable.  
> He **represented himself as** a licensed adviser.

---

## 05 pretend to be / call himself

### 「假裝」不等於普通自稱

`pretend to be` 明確表示**實際上不是，卻假裝是**。`call himself` 本身沒有這個含意。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> He **calls himself** an artist.  
> He **pretended to be** a police officer.

---

## 06 become / regard himself as

### 「成為」不等於「視自己為」

客觀身份改變和主觀自我認知是兩件事。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> He **became** a manager last year.  
> He **regards himself as** a mentor to younger staff.

---

## 07 describe / describe himself as

### 有沒有反身代詞，意思不同

`describe` 後面需要清楚指出描述的是誰或甚麼；若是「描述自己」，要用 `himself`。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> He **described the job as** demanding.  
> He **described himself as** patient.

---

## 08 identity / identify as

### 名詞不等於動詞

`identity` 是「身份／認同」的名詞；`identify as` 才是「自我認同為」。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> His professional **identity** changed over time.  
> He **identifies as** a writer.

---

## 練習 01.1

**Target phrase: Describe himself as · 形容自己是**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> In the interview, he called himself a quiet person who prefers listening to speaking.

**中文翻譯**

在訪問中，他形容自己是一個安靜、較喜歡聆聽而不是說話的人。

A. described himself as  
B. declared himself to be  
C. pretended to be  
D. became  
E. named himself  
F. billed himself as

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**described himself as · 形容自己是**

> In the interview, he described himself as a quiet person who prefers listening to speaking.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK

**A. described himself as ✓**  
這裡只是中性地說明他如何描述自己的性格，`describe himself as` 最自然。

**B. declared himself to be**  
語氣太正式、太像公開宣告，不適合普通性格描述。

**C. pretended to be**  
表示他實際不是安靜的人而故意假裝，原句沒有這個意思。

**D. became**  
表示客觀地變成某種狀態，不是自我描述。

**E. named himself**  
通常表示給自己取名字，不適合性格特質。

**F. billed himself as**  
帶宣傳或自我推廣意味，不合普通訪問語境。

# 05 高混淆組

## describe himself as vs see himself as

- **describe himself as**：重點是他**怎樣用語言描述自己**。
- **see himself as**：重點是他**內心怎樣理解自己**。

> He describes himself as quiet.  
> He sees himself as a future leader.

兩者有時都可以成立，但焦點不同。

---

## regard himself as vs consider himself

- **regard himself as**：較正式，`as` 必須保留。
- **consider himself**：通常直接接名詞／形容詞，不需要 `as`。

> He regards himself as an expert.  
> He considers himself an expert.

不要把兩個句型混成：

> *He considers himself as an expert.*

---

## see himself as vs think of himself as

兩者都談自我概念。

- **see himself as**：非常廣泛，偏向「自我形象」。
- **think of himself as**：更有「在心中把自己當作某種類型」的感覺。

> He sees himself as a leader.  
> He thinks of himself as more of a teacher than a manager.

---

## identify as vs refer to himself as

- **identify as**：身份上的自我認同。
- **refer to himself as**：實際使用某個詞或稱號稱呼自己。

> He identifies as a writer.  
> He refers to himself as a "working writer."

前者是認同；後者是措辭。

---

## present himself as vs portray himself as

- **present himself as**：在人際／公開場合把自己呈現成某種形象。
- **portray himself as**：透過敘事、文章、媒體或故事把自己描繪成某種角色。

> He presents himself as calm in meetings.  
> In his memoir, he portrays himself as an outsider.

---

## style himself as vs bill himself as

- **style himself as**：自稱／自封某種角色，較正式或帶評論色彩。
- **bill himself as**：明顯帶宣傳、品牌或商業推廣意味。

> He styles himself as a defender of tradition.  
> He bills himself as a leadership expert.

---

## label himself as vs characterize himself as

- **label himself as**：把自己歸入某個標籤或類別。
- **characterize himself as**：用某個特徵較正式地描述自己。

> He refuses to label himself as left or right.  
> He characterizes himself as cautious.

---

## call himself vs declare himself to be

- **call himself**：非常廣泛，可以只是普通自稱。
- **declare himself to be**：更正式、更強烈，常有公開宣告意味。

> He calls himself a writer.  
> He declared himself to be the new leader.

---

# 06 語氣與「距離感」

這組詞的一個重要特點是：有些只是中性描述，有些會讓說話者與該人的自我說法保持距離。

## 較中性

- describe himself as
- regard himself as
- consider himself
- see himself as
- think of himself as
- identify as
- refer to himself as

這些通常只報告「他如何描述／理解自己」。

## 較強調形象建構

- present himself as
- portray himself as
- characterize himself as

這些更容易把焦點放在「他呈現出的形象」。

## 可能帶自我標榜／評論意味

- style himself as
- bill himself as
- label himself as

尤其 `style himself as` 和 `bill himself as`，常暗示這個稱號是**他自己採用或宣傳的**，並不自動代表說話者認同。

## 較正式／公開

- declare himself to be

通常比普通 `call himself` 更有公開聲明感。

---

# 07 句型地圖

## + as

以下通常使用 `as`：

- describe himself **as**
- regard himself **as**
- see himself **as**
- think of himself **as**
- identify **as**
- refer to himself **as**
- present himself **as**
- portray himself **as**
- characterize himself **as**
- style himself **as**
- bill himself **as**
- label himself **as**

## 不通常用 as

### consider himself

> He considers himself **an expert**.  
> He considers himself **fortunate**.

而不是教學上常見的錯誤：

> *He considers himself as an expert.*

## + to be

### declare himself to be

> He declared himself **to be** independent.

`to be` 有時可省略：

> He declared himself independent.

# 08 生成／評估注意事項

1. `call himself` 的替代詞主要不是「強弱」不同，而是區分：
   - **自我描述**
   - **內在自我認知**
   - **身份認同**
   - **實際使用的稱號**
   - **對外呈現**
   - **敘事中的形象塑造**
   - **自我宣傳**
   - **自我標籤**
   - **公開宣稱**

2. 生成題目時，必須利用語境讓目標詞具有明確線索：
   - interview / questionnaire → `describe / characterize himself as`
   - inner self-concept → `see / think of / regard himself as`
   - identity → `identify as`
   - repeated wording/title → `refer to himself as`
   - public-facing image → `present himself as`
   - memoir/media narrative → `portray himself as`
   - self-appointed title → `style himself as`
   - website/promotional material → `bill himself as`
   - categories/labels → `label himself as`
   - formal public announcement → `declare himself to be`

3. 不應把 `pretend to be` 當普通近義詞。它增加了**虛假／假裝**的含意。

4. 不應把 `become` 當作近義詞。`become` 描述客觀狀態改變；本組大多描述**一個人怎樣稱呼、理解或呈現自己**。

5. 注意句型：
   - `regard himself as`
   - `see himself as`
   - `think of himself as`
   - `consider himself` + noun/adjective
   - `declare himself to be`

6. `style himself as`, `bill himself as`, `portray himself as` 有時會產生語用上的「距離感」：作者只是在報告此人如何塑造自己，而不是替該身份背書。

7. `identify as` 應保留給真正的身份／角色認同語境，不宜機械地取代所有 `call himself + adjective`。

8. `call himself` 本身仍然非常自然，而且比很多替代詞更廣泛：
   - He calls himself a writer.
   - He calls himself lucky.
   - He calls himself "The Doctor."

   因此本材料的目的不是消滅 `call himself`，而是教導在更精確的語境中選擇更合適的表達。
