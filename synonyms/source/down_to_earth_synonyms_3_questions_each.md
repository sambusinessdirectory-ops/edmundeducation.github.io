# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Down-to-earth (adjective)

**`down-to-earth` 可以表示務實、腳踏實地、不矯飾、明智、切合現實、冷靜理智、謙遜，或說話平實直接。不同替代表達的核心差別，是『practicality / grounded personality / lack of pretension / sound judgement / realism / calm judgement / modesty / communication style』。**

核心替代表達：

`practical / grounded / unpretentious / sensible / realistic / level-headed / modest / matter-of-fact`

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Practical

**務實的；實際的**

當 `down-to-earth` 指某人著重可行方法、實際效果，而不是空談或不切實際的想法時，`practical` 很自然。它是最核心的近義詞之一。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She gave practical advice that we could use immediately.

她提供了可以立即採用的務實建議。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **focused on workable solutions and real-world results**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 02 Grounded

**腳踏實地的；穩重的**

當 `down-to-earth` 指一個人理性、穩重、不浮誇，而且對現實有清楚認識時，`grounded` 很貼切。它比 practical 更偏 personality and emotional stability。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Despite her success, she remains grounded and approachable.

儘管事業成功，她仍然腳踏實地、平易近人。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **sensible, realistic, and emotionally steady**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、工作、人物描述

---

## 03 Unpretentious

**不矯飾的；樸實的**

當 `down-to-earth` 重點是不炫耀、不裝腔作勢、不刻意顯得高級時，`unpretentious` 最精準。它可形容人、餐廳、設計、風格。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The restaurant is simple, friendly, and unpretentious.

這間餐廳簡單、親切而不矯飾。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **simple and not trying to impress people**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、評論、正式

---

## 04 Sensible

**明智的；實際合理的**

當 `down-to-earth` 指判斷合理、做法不誇張、能按現實情況作決定時，`sensible` 很自然。它更偏 good judgement。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He suggested a sensible way to reduce costs.

他提出一個合理務實的減省成本方法。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **showing practical and sound judgement**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 05 Realistic

**現實的；切合實際的**

當 `down-to-earth` 指某人的期望、計劃或看法符合現實，不過分理想化時，`realistic` 很合適。它比 grounded 更偏 expectations and assessment。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> We need a realistic timetable for the project.

我們需要一個切合實際的項目時間表。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **based on what is actually possible or likely**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 06 Level-headed

**冷靜理智的；頭腦清醒的**

當 `down-to-earth` 指一個人在壓力下仍冷靜、理性、不衝動時，`level-headed` 很自然。它較強調 calm judgement。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She stayed level-headed during the emergency.

她在緊急情況下仍保持冷靜理智。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **calm and sensible, especially under pressure**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、人物描述

---

## 07 Modest

**謙遜的；不張揚的**

當 `down-to-earth` 指某人即使有成就也不自誇、不擺架子時，`modest` 很自然。它和 unpretentious 接近，但更偏 self-presentation。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He is modest about his achievements.

他對自己的成就十分謙遜。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **not boastful or self-important**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 08 Matter-of-fact

**實事求是的；平實直接的**

當 `down-to-earth` 指說話方式直接、平實、不戲劇化時，`matter-of-fact` 很合適。它更偏 communication style，而不是整體性格。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She gave a matter-of-fact explanation of what had happened.

她以實事求是、平實直接的方式解釋事情經過。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **plain, practical, and unemotional in manner or expression**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、書面、工作

# 02 False synonyms

## Down-to-earth / Practical
`practical` 強調方法可行、有實際用途。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
She is down-to-earth.  
She gave practical advice we could use immediately.

## Down-to-earth / Grounded
`grounded` 更偏人物性格：穩重、有現實感、不因成功而失去分寸。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
He is down-to-earth.  
Despite his success, he remains grounded.

## Down-to-earth / Unpretentious
`unpretentious` 強調不裝腔作勢、不炫耀。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The restaurant feels down-to-earth.  
The restaurant is simple and unpretentious.

## Down-to-earth / Sensible
`sensible` 更偏明智判斷，而不是整體人物氣質。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
She is down-to-earth.  
She made a sensible decision to save first.

## Down-to-earth / Realistic
`realistic` 用於期望、計劃、預算、估算等「符合現實」的語境。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
He has a down-to-earth attitude.  
He has realistic expectations about the project.

## Down-to-earth / Level-headed
`level-headed` 特別適合壓力下保持冷靜理智。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
She is down-to-earth.  
She stayed level-headed during the emergency.

## Down-to-earth / Modest
`modest` 主要表示不自誇、不張揚。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
He is down-to-earth despite his success.  
He is modest about his achievements.

## Down-to-earth / Matter-of-fact
`matter-of-fact` 更偏說話／解釋方式平實直接、不戲劇化。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
She has a down-to-earth manner.  
She gave a matter-of-fact explanation.

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Practical vs Realistic** — practical = 可行／有用；realistic = 符合現實可能性。
- **Grounded vs Modest** — grounded = 穩重有現實感；modest = 不自誇。
- **Unpretentious vs Modest** — unpretentious 可形容人／場所／風格；modest 更偏人的自我表現。
- **Sensible vs Practical** — sensible 更偏判斷好；practical 更偏方法可行。
- **Level-headed vs Grounded** — level-headed 特別強調壓力下冷靜；grounded 是較持久性格。
- **Matter-of-fact vs Practical** — matter-of-fact 偏說話方式；practical 偏解決方法。
- **Down-to-earth ≠ simple-minded** — down-to-earth 是正面務實，不等於頭腦簡單。
- **Down-to-earth ≠ unsophisticated** — 樸實不等於缺乏知識或品味。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

- **down-to-earth** 作形容詞通常連字號
- **practical** = adjective；**practically** = adverb；**practicality** = noun
- **grounded** = adjective；**grounding** 不是此義直接替代
- **unpretentious** = adjective；**unpretentiously** = adverb；**unpretentiousness** = noun
- **sensible** = adjective；**sensibly** = adverb；**sensibility** = noun
- **realistic** = adjective；**realistically** = adverb；**realism** = noun
- **level-headed** 固定常用連字號；**level-headedness** = noun
- **modest** = adjective；**modestly** = adverb；**modesty** = noun
- **matter-of-fact** 作形容詞通常連字號；**matter-of-factly** = adverb

1. 強調可行、實用？ → **practical**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`practical / grounded / unpretentious / sensible / realistic / level-headed / modest / matter-of-fact`

最重要的一點：

> **practical ≠ grounded ≠ unpretentious**

`practical` = 方法務實；  
`grounded` = 性格腳踏實地；  
`unpretentious` = 不矯飾、不炫耀。
