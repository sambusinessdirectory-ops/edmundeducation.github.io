# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Job

**職業、職位、職務、就業、專業、事業、空缺——究竟是哪一種 job？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**occupation; position; role; employment; post; profession; career; line of work; appointment; work; vacancy**

> `occupation` = 職業種類  
> `position / post` = 具體職位  
> `role` = 職責／功能  
> `employment` = 就業狀態  
> `profession` = 專業職業  
> `career` = 長期職業生涯  
> `work` = 工作內容，通常不可數  
> `vacancy` = 尚未填補的空缺

---

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Occupation

**職業；工作**

較正式，指一個人從事的職業種類或謀生工作。比 job 更偏職業類別。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Please state your current occupation on the form.

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- occupation
- current / new occupation
- apply for / enter / take a occupation（按語義適用）
- professional / full-time / senior occupation（按語義適用）

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 job 是職業類別、具體職位、職責功能、就業狀態、長期職涯，還是招聘空缺？

---

## 02 Position

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Work

work 比 job 更廣，而且通常不可數。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

I have a job. / I have a lot of work.

---

## 02 Career

career 是長期職業生涯，通常包含多份 jobs。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She changed jobs twice during her career.

---

## 03 Profession

profession 只適合需專業訓練／資格的職業。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Medicine is a profession; a temporary retail job usually is not.

---

## 04 Occupation

occupation 回答『你從事甚麼職業』，不一定是某個具體職位。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Her occupation is teaching; her position is head teacher.

---

## 05 Role

role 強調職責／功能，不保證是 paid employment。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

He has a leadership role in the club but no paid job there.

---

## 06 Task

task 只是工作中的一項任務。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Writing the report is one task within the job.

---

## 07 Duty

duty 是一項職責，不是整份工作。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Answering calls is one duty of the receptionist's job.

---

## 08 Vacancy

vacancy 只表示尚未填補的職位。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She applied for a vacancy and later got the job.

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Job vs work
最重要區別：

- job = 可數：a job / two jobs
- work = 通常不可數：some work / a lot of work

自然：
`I have a job.`  
`I have a lot of work.`

## 02 Position / post / vacancy
- position = 具體職位
- post = 英式正式職位
- vacancy = 尚未有人擔任的空缺

## 03 Occupation
常見於 forms / surveys / official records。

## 04 Role
