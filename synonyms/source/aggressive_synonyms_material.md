# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Aggressive

**強勢、咄咄逼人、對抗、好鬥、敵意、挑釁、專橫、高壓進取、激烈——究竟是哪一種 aggressive？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**forceful; pushy; confrontational; combative; hostile; belligerent; overbearing; hard-driving; fierce**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **forceful; pushy; confrontational** 全部保留。  
> 本課特別區分 **中性強勢、過度施壓、直接對抗、爭辯好鬥、明確敵意、挑釁好戰、專橫控制、高壓結果導向、競爭／策略激烈**。

---

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Forceful

**強勢的；有力的**

最接近而又較中性的近義詞之一。當 `aggressive` 指說話、領導、談判或行動強而有力、積極推進，但未必敵意或無禮時很自然。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

She gave a forceful defence of the proposal without insulting anyone.

她有力地為建議辯護，但沒有侮辱任何人。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- forceful argument

- forceful presentation

- forceful leadership

- forceful response


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 aggressive 是有力但不敵對、過度催逼、直接對抗、好爭辯、明確敵意、挑釁好戰、專橫控制、強勢結果導向，還是競爭／行動非常激烈？

---

## 02 Pushy

**咄咄逼人的；過分進取的**

當 `aggressive` 指一個人過度催逼、強迫別人接受意見、購買產品或作出決定時最自然。語氣明顯負面，尤其常見於 salespeople, colleagues, parents。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The salesperson was too pushy and kept pressuring us to buy.

那名售貨員太咄咄逼人，不斷施壓要我們購買。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- pushy salesperson

- pushy behaviour

- too pushy

- come across as pushy


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 aggressive 是有力但不敵對、過度催逼、直接對抗、好爭辯、明確敵意、挑釁好戰、專橫控制、強勢結果導向，還是競爭／行動非常激烈？

---

## 03 Confrontational

**對抗性的；好起衝突的**

當 `aggressive` 指語氣、態度或行為直接挑戰他人、容易引發衝突時最精確。重點是 confrontation，而不一定是暴力。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

His confrontational tone turned a minor disagreement into an argument.

他的對抗語氣把小分歧變成了爭吵。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- confrontational tone

- confrontational language

- confrontational approach

- become confrontational

## 04 Combative

較正式。當 `aggressive` 指人、語氣或政治／法律辯論中積極迎戰、反駁、爭論時很自然。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The lawyer adopted a combative style during cross-examination.

律師盤問時採取了好鬥、強硬的風格。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

## 05 Hostile

**敵意的；不友善的**

當 `aggressive` 帶明確敵意、不友善、威脅性或對立態度時最自然。`hostile` 比 `forceful` 更偏情緒和關係上的敵對。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

群眾對作客球隊變得充滿敵意。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

## 06 Belligerent

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The man became belligerent and appeared ready to start a fight.

### EXAMPLE · 例句

## 08 Hard-Driving

**強勢進取的；要求極高的**

常形容 manager, executive, entrepreneur 等對結果要求極高、節奏快、強勢推進。它未必表示敵意，而是高壓、結果導向。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

她是一名強勢進取、要求團隊迅速交出成果的行政人員。

## 09 Fierce

### EXAMPLE · 例句

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Assertive

**重要近義但不是 aggressive**

assertive 是堅定表達自己同時尊重他人界線，通常比 aggressive 正面。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She was assertive without being aggressive.

---

## 02 Forceful

**強勢但可中性**

可形容 presentation, argument, leadership，不一定有敵意。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

He made a forceful argument.

---

## 03 Pushy

**施壓／逼人**

常用於 sales, recruitment, social behaviour。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The salesperson was too pushy.

---

## 04 Confrontational

**直接引發對抗**

重點是 challenge / conflict，而非單純強勢。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

His confrontational tone escalated the dispute.

---

## 05 Hostile

**明確敵意**

比 aggressive 更集中於 antagonism 和不友善。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The crowd became hostile.

---

## 06 Belligerent

**挑釁好鬥，強度更高**

常帶 shouting, threats, readiness to fight 或好戰政策。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

He became belligerent after the argument.

---

## 07 Aggressive / aggressively

**詞性辨析**

`aggressive` 是形容詞；`aggressively` 是副詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

an aggressive strategy; market aggressively

---

## 08 Aggressive

**商業／醫療等延伸義**

可指 aggressive growth, aggressive treatment, aggressive pricing，未必含人際敵意。本課主要集中在人際、管理和競爭語境。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The company adopted an aggressive pricing strategy.

# 03 練習與解釋

> **每個核心近義表達 3 題，共 27 題。**
> 每題均以 **敵意程度、是否施壓、是否直接挑戰、是否控制他人、是否高壓追求結果、是否只描述競爭強度** 作辨析。


## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** forceful · 強勢的；有力的

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

She gave an aggressive defence of the proposal without insulting anyone.

### 中文翻譯

她有力地為建議辯護，但沒有侮辱任何人。

### OPTIONS


- **A. forceful**

- **B. assertive**

- **C. pushy**

- **D. confrontational**

- **E. passive**

- **F. hesitant**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**forceful · 強勢的；有力的**

She gave a **forceful** defence of the proposal without insulting anyone.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. forceful ✓** — `forceful` = 有力、強勢、堅定地推進，不必然敵對。 **本句為何最合適：** the speaker is strong and persuasive, but the sentence explicitly rules out hostility。

- **B. assertive** — `assertive` = 堅定表達自己而不侵犯他人界線。 健康而堅定地表達立場，不等於 aggressive；通常少了侵略／施壓意味。 **本句線索：** the speaker is strong and persuasive, but the sentence explicitly rules out hostility。

- **C. pushy** — `pushy` = 過分催逼或把自己的意願強加於人。 重點是過分催逼別人；法律辯論或軍事威脅不一定叫 pushy。 **本句線索：** the speaker is strong and persuasive, but the sentence explicitly rules out hostility。

- **D. confrontational** — `confrontational` = 傾向直接對抗、挑戰並引發衝突。 重點是直接對抗；高壓追求業績但沒有衝突時不夠準。 **本句線索：** the speaker is strong and persuasive, but the sentence explicitly rules out hostility。

- **E. passive** — `passive` = 被動的。 與 aggressive/forceful 相反。 **本句線索：** the speaker is strong and persuasive, but the sentence explicitly rules out hostility。

- **F. hesitant** — `hesitant` = 猶豫的。 猶豫不決，與 forceful 相反。 **本句線索：** the speaker is strong and persuasive, but the sentence explicitly rules out hostility。

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Aggressive vs Assertive
- assertive = 堅定 + 尊重界線
- aggressive = 可能侵犯界線／施壓／敵對

## Aggressive vs Forceful
`forceful` 可以完全正面：
- forceful argument
- forceful presentation

## Aggressive vs Pushy
最自然於：
- salesperson
- recruiter
- colleague
- parent

## Aggressive vs Confrontational
`confrontational` 的核心是 **direct challenge and conflict**。

## Aggressive vs Combative
特別適合：
- debate
- politics
- law
- criticism

## Aggressive vs Hostile
`hostile` 比較集中於 **antagonism / ill will**。

## Aggressive vs Belligerent
強度更高：
- threats
- shouting
- readiness to fight
- military stance

## Aggressive vs Overbearing
核心是控制和壓住別人。

## Aggressive vs Hard-driving
常見於 managers / executives，未必有敵意。

## Aggressive vs Fierce
`fierce` 特別自然於：
- competition
- rivalry
- campaign
- response

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Aggressive vs aggressively
- aggressive = adjective
- aggressively = adverb

例如：
- an aggressive strategy
- market aggressively

## 02 Aggressive towards
- aggressive towards staff
- aggressive towards opponents

## 03 Aggressive in
- aggressive in negotiations
- aggressive in pursuing growth

## 04 Assertive is not the same
`assertive` 通常是正面溝通技巧；不應把它機械當作 aggressive。

## 05 Non-interpersonal senses
`aggressive` 亦可自然用於：
- aggressive pricing
- aggressive growth
- aggressive treatment
- aggressive investment strategy

這些語境不一定包含敵意。
