# INVESTIGATE · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Investigate

**「調查／查究／研究」，要先分清是在仔細審視證據、開放式探討問題、正式查案、實體檢查，還是以數據分析來找原因。**

本單元集中處理 **investigate = to examine, inquire into, or study a matter systematically in order to discover facts, causes, explanations, or responsibility**。你提供的 **examine; explore; look into** 分別代表 **仔細審視、開放式探討、日常調查** 三個核心方向。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且高實用度的詞／表達，共 **39 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。同時清楚區分 **investigate / examine / inspect / research / analyse / probe / scrutinise / inquire into**，避免把『搜索、分析、檢查、調查』混為一談。

## Tier 1 — Core examination / exploration / inquiry equivalents · 核心審視、探討與調查近義詞

### 01 Examine

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：檢查；審視；研究  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to look at something carefully in order to understand it, assess it, or discover facts about it  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，當 investigate 強調仔細查看證據、問題、資料或情況時最直接。  
**常見搭配**：examine evidence; examine a problem; examine the causes; examine data; examine the issue.  
**常見搭配對象**：evidence, problem, cause, data, issue.  
**與 investigate 的辨析**：Examine 強調仔細查看；investigate 更常暗示有問題、疑點或待查事實。  
**使用限制**：刑事、事故等正式查案語境中 investigate 通常更強。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The committee examined the evidence before reaching a conclusion.  
委員會在作出結論前仔細審視證據。

### 02 Explore

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：探索；探討；深入了解  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to investigate a subject, possibility, or idea in an open-ended way  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很自然，尤其當目標不是查出罪責，而是深入了解主題、可能性或原因時。  
**常見搭配**：explore an issue; explore possibilities; explore the causes; explore options; explore a question.  
**常見搭配對象**：issue, possibility, cause, option, question.  
**與 investigate 的辨析**：Explore 比 investigate 更開放式，未必預設存在問題或 wrongdoing。  
**使用限制**：正式查案、違規調查通常不用 explore 取代 investigate。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The study explores why some students leave school early.  
研究探討為何部分學生提早離校。

### 03 Look into

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：調查；查究；了解  
**語域**：neutral / conversational  
**核心語義**：to investigate a problem, complaint, or situation in order to find out what happened  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是非常常用的口語近義表達，特別適合投訴、錯誤、事故和服務問題。  
**常見搭配**：look into a complaint; look into the matter; look into the cause; look into allegations; look into the problem.  
**常見搭配對象**：complaint, matter, cause, allegation, problem.  
**與 investigate 的辨析**：Look into 比 investigate 更口語、更日常。  
**使用限制**：高度正式法律／科研文本通常用 investigate / examine。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The manager promised to look into the complaint.  
經理承諾調查這宗投訴。

### 04 Inquire into

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：查問；調查；查究  
**語域**：formal / official  
**核心語義**：to make a formal or systematic investigation into an event, allegation, or matter  
**用法提示**：當 investigate 出現在正式委員會、公開聆訊、政府或法律語境時很合適。  
**常見搭配**：inquire into allegations; inquire into the incident; inquiry into misconduct; formally inquire into; public inquiry.  
**常見搭配對象**：allegation, incident, misconduct, complaint, matter.  
**與 investigate 的辨析**：Inquire into 比 investigate 更正式，常帶官方調查意味。  
**使用限制**：日常小問題通常不用如此正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
A panel was appointed to inquire into the incident.  
當局委任小組調查該事件。

### 05 Research

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：研究；調查研究  
**語域**：academic / professional  
**核心語義**：to study a subject systematically in order to establish facts, develop knowledge, or answer questions  
**用法提示**：當 investigate 是學術、科學、政策或市場層面的系統研究時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：research a topic; research the causes; research a market; research an issue; conduct research.  
**常見搭配對象**：topic, cause, market, issue, phenomenon.  
**與 investigate 的辨析**：Research 強調建立知識；investigate 更容易帶查明事件、問題或疑點的意味。  
**使用限制**：刑事案件通常說 investigate，不說 research the crime。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The team is researching the long-term effects of air pollution.  
團隊正在研究空氣污染的長期影響。

## Tier 2 — Inspection / probing / analysis variants · 檢查、深入追查與分析變體

### 06 Inspect

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：檢查；視察  
**語域**：neutral / technical / official  
**核心語義**：to examine something physically and carefully, often to check condition, safety, or compliance  
**用法提示**：當 investigate 的對象是設備、建築、場所或實物，而且重點是檢查狀況時最精準。  
**常見搭配**：inspect a building; inspect equipment; inspect the site; inspect for damage; safety inspection.  
**常見搭配對象**：building, equipment, site, vehicle, machinery.  
**與 investigate 的辨析**：Inspect 是實體檢查；investigate 可以處理原因、責任與抽象問題。  
**使用限制**：理論、政策、動機等抽象對象不宜用 inspect。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Engineers inspected the bridge for signs of damage.  
工程師檢查橋樑是否有損壞跡象。

### 07 Probe

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：深入調查；追查  
**語域**：formal / journalistic  
**核心語義**：to investigate something deeply and persistently, especially a sensitive or suspicious matter  
**用法提示**：當 investigate 帶強烈追查、深入挖掘疑點的語氣時很地道。  
**常見搭配**：probe allegations; probe corruption; probe the cause; launch a probe; probe further.  
**常見搭配對象**：allegation, corruption, cause, scandal, failure.  
**與 investigate 的辨析**：Probe 比 investigate 更有『深挖』和敏感調查意味。  
**使用限制**：普通學術研究通常不用 probe 作首選。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The regulator is probing allegations of misconduct.  
監管機構正深入調查有關失當行為的指控。

### 08 Scrutinise

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：仔細審查；嚴格檢視  
**語域**：formal / British English  
**核心語義**：to examine something extremely carefully, often critically, for errors, weaknesses, or hidden details  
**用法提示**：當 investigate 的重點是嚴格審視文件、數據、決策、開支或證據時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：scrutinise evidence; scrutinise spending; scrutinise a proposal; scrutinise records; scrutinise data.  
**常見搭配對象**：evidence, spending, proposal, record, data.  
**與 investigate 的辨析**：Scrutinise 聚焦高度細緻審查；investigate 更強調查明整件事。  
**使用限制**：口語中較少用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Auditors scrutinised the company's financial records.  
核數師仔細審查公司的財務紀錄。

### 09 Analyse

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：分析；剖析  
**語域**：neutral / academic  
**核心語義**：to study information, data, or a situation by breaking it into parts and examining relationships or patterns  
**用法提示**：當 investigate 主要透過資料、證據或數據分析來理解問題時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：analyse data; analyse evidence; analyse trends; analyse the causes; analyse results.  
**常見搭配對象**：data, evidence, trend, cause, result.  
**與 investigate 的辨析**：Analyse 是研究方法；investigate 是整體查究過程。  
**使用限制**：單純問人、搜查現場等不屬 analyse。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Researchers analysed the data to identify possible causes.  
研究人員分析數據以找出可能原因。

### 10 Study

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：研究；仔細探討  
**語域**：neutral / academic  
**核心語義**：to examine a subject, situation, or phenomenon carefully over time in order to understand it  
**用法提示**：當 investigate 不帶查案意味，而是持續觀察和研究現象時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：study a phenomenon; study behaviour; study the effects; study a problem; study patterns.  
**常見搭配對象**：phenomenon, behaviour, effect, problem, pattern.  
**與 investigate 的辨析**：Study 比 investigate 更學術、更中性，也較少暗示疑點。  
**使用限制**：事故責任、犯罪指控等通常用 investigate。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Scientists are studying how sleep affects memory.  
科學家正在研究睡眠如何影響記憶。

## Tier 3 — Informal / idiomatic / official-inquiry variants · 口語、慣用語與正式調查變體

### 11 Check out

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：查看；查清；了解一下  
**語域**：informal  
**核心語義**：to examine or investigate something informally to find out more about it  
**用法提示**：當 investigate 在非常口語的日常語境中只是『去看看／查一下』時可用。  
**常見搭配**：check out a problem; check out a claim; check out a place; check out the details; check something out.  
**常見搭配對象**：claim, problem, place, detail, report.  
**與 investigate 的辨析**：Check out 比 investigate 輕鬆、非正式得多。  
**使用限制**：正式調查報告、法律或學術文本通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
I'll check out the problem and get back to you.  
我會查看這個問題，之後再回覆你。

### 12 Get to the bottom of

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：查個水落石出；查明真相  
**語域**：idiomatic / conversational  
**核心語義**：to investigate a confusing or suspicious situation until the true cause or explanation is discovered  
**用法提示**：當 investigate 強調『一定要查明真正原因／真相』時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：get to the bottom of the problem; get to the bottom of what happened; get to the bottom of the issue; get to the bottom of the mystery.  
**常見搭配對象**：problem, issue, incident, mystery, discrepancy.  
**與 investigate 的辨析**：這個表達比 investigate 更口語，也更強調查出最終真相。  
**使用限制**：正式論文或正式公文通常改用 determine / investigate。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
We need to get to the bottom of why the system keeps failing.  
我們需要查明系統為何不斷故障。

### 13 Conduct an inquiry into

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：就……展開調查；進行正式調查  
**語域**：formal / official  
**核心語義**：to carry out a structured and often official investigation into an event, practice, or allegation  
**用法提示**：適合政府、機構、紀律、事故和公共事件的正式調查。  
**常見搭配**：conduct an inquiry into; formal inquiry; public inquiry; independent inquiry; inquiry into the incident.  
**常見搭配對象**：incident, allegation, practice, failure, misconduct.  
**與 investigate 的辨析**：這是 investigate 的完整正式名詞化改寫。  
**使用限制**：日常小問題會顯得過度正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The authority will conduct an inquiry into the safety failure.  
當局將就這宗安全事故展開正式調查。

## 04 False synonyms / investigate traps · 容易誤選的詞與「調查」陷阱

### 01 Search

**搜尋不等於 investigate**  
search 通常是尋找某人、某物或資料；investigate 是查明原因、事實或責任。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Searching may be one step in an investigation.

### 02 Check

**檢查不等於完整調查**  
check 往往只是確認狀態或正確性；investigate 通常更深入。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A check is often narrower and quicker.

### 03 Research

**research 只在系統研究 sense 接近 investigate**  
學術研究和事件調查目的不同。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Research builds knowledge; investigation often establishes what happened.

### 04 Inspect

**inspect 只在實體檢查 sense 接近 investigate**  
inspect 常檢查物件、場所或設備狀況。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Inspection is physical or compliance-focused.

### 05 Analyse

**分析不等於完整 investigate**  
analyse 是研究資料的方法之一，但調查還可能包括訪問、搜證、查紀錄等。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Analysis is one investigative method.

### 06 Accuse

**指控不等於調查**  
accuse 是聲稱某人做錯事；investigate 是查證是否真的發生。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Accusation is not investigation.

### 07 Interrogate

**盤問不等於 investigate**  
interrogate 是強力問話，可能是調查的一部分。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Interrogation is one possible investigative technique.

### 08 Investigation / investigate

**詞性陷阱**  
investigate 是動詞；investigation 是名詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Investigate is a verb; investigation is a noun.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Examine · 檢查；審視；研究

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The committee investigated the evidence before reaching a conclusion.

**中文翻譯**  
委員會在作出結論前仔細審視證據。

- A. examine
- B. searched
- C. accused
- D. interrogated
- E. checked briefly
- F. researched generally

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**examine** · 檢查；審視；研究

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The committee examined the evidence before reaching a conclusion.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. examine ✓ — evidence being looked at carefully before a conclusion is reached fits examine。**
- **B. searched — searched would mean trying to locate something。**
- **C. accused — accused changes from fact-finding to blaming someone。**
- **D. interrogated — interrogated applies to questioning people。**
- **E. checked briefly — checked briefly is too shallow for careful evidence review。**
- **F. researched generally — researched generally is broader and less specific to the evidence in hand.**

|---|---|---|
| 仔細審視證據／問題 | **examine** | 是否是 careful examination of evidence / issue？ |
| 開放式探討 | **explore** | 是否是 possibilities / questions / causes without wrongdoing assumption？ |
| 日常調查問題 | **look into** | 是否是 complaint / error / service problem？ |
| 正式官方查究 | **inquire into** | 是否是 panel / commission / official inquiry？ |
| 學術／市場研究 | **research** | 是否是 systematic knowledge-building？ |
| 實體／安全檢查 | **inspect** | 是否是 building / equipment / vehicle / site？ |
| 深入追查敏感事項 | **probe** | 是否是 allegations / corruption / unexplained failure？ |
| 極仔細審查 | **scrutinise** | 是否是 records / spending / evidence line by line？ |
| 數據／證據分析 | **analyse** | 是否是 data / trends / results / patterns？ |
| 持續研究現象 | **study** | 是否是 scientific or academic observation over time？ |
| 口語查看 | **check out** | 是否 casual 'take a look / find out more'？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **investigate vs examine**: examine 聚焦仔細查看；investigate 聚焦查明整件事。
- **investigate vs explore**: explore 更開放式，不一定有疑點或問題。
- **investigate vs look into**: look into 是最自然的日常口語近義詞之一。
- **investigate vs inquire into**: inquire into 更正式、更有官方調查意味。
- **investigate vs research**: research 是建立知識；investigate 常查明事件、原因或責任。
- **investigate vs inspect**: inspect 是實體／安全／合規檢查。
- **investigate vs probe**: probe 更深、更強，常見敏感事件或指控。
- **investigate vs scrutinise**: scrutinise 是極仔細審視細節。
- **investigate vs analyse**: analyse 是調查可用的方法之一。
- **investigate vs search**: search 只表示尋找，不等於完整調查。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & investigate traps · 文法、搭配與調查陷阱

- **investigate + object**：investigate a complaint / incident / allegation / cause。
- 一般不說 **investigate into**；可說 **investigate the matter**。相反，**look into / inquire into** 要用 into。
- **examine + evidence/data/problem**：強調仔細查看。
- **explore + issue/possibility/question**：強調開放式探討。
- **research + topic/market/cause**：常見學術、政策、市場研究。
- **inspect + building/equipment/site**：多為實體或合規檢查。
- **probe + allegation/scandal/cause**：正式、新聞語氣較強。
- 英式英語常寫 **scrutinise**；美式常見 **scrutinize**。
- **analyse + data/results/trends**；英式拼法 analyse，美式 analyze。
- **study + phenomenon/effect/pattern**：中性學術研究。
- **check out** 很口語，可表示『查看／了解一下』。
- **get to the bottom of + problem/issue/what happened**：強調查明根本原因或真相。
- 名詞是 **investigation**：conduct / launch / carry out an investigation。
- **accuse / interrogate / search ≠ investigate**：它們可能出現在調查中，但不是調查本身。
