# COPE WITH · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Cope with

**「應付／處理／捱過／適應」，關鍵不是事情有沒有完全解決，而是人在困難、壓力或變化中能否繼續運作。**

本單元集中處理 **cope with = to deal successfully enough with a difficult situation, pressure, problem, change, or emotional experience**。你提供的 **manage; handle; get through** 正好代表三個核心方向：**manage = 設法應付**、**handle = 有能力處理**、**get through = 熬過一段難關**。

這次保留 **13 個**高實用度詞／表達，並把 **工作量／問題處理、環境適應、制度回應、情緒接受、長期承受、危機挺過** 分開。每個目標設 **3 道題**；所有 distractor 均完整說明其本身意思及為何在該句不合適。

## Tier 1 — Core coping equivalents · 核心「應付／處理」近義表達

### 01 Manage

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：應付；設法處理；撐得住  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to succeed in dealing with a difficult situation, task, pressure, or problem  
**用法提示**：和 cope with 最直接、最常用的替代之一，尤其當重點是『有辦法應付』而不一定完全解決。  
**常見搭配**：manage the pressure; manage a difficult situation; manage stress; manage the workload; manage on your own.  
**常見搭配對象**：pressure, workload, stress, difficulty, situation.  
**與 cope with 的辨析**：Manage 可指成功處理各類事情；cope with 更常帶困難、壓力或逆境意味。  
**使用限制**：若是技術性處理某物，handle 可能更自然；若是熬過一段難關，get through 更貼切。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She is learning to manage the pressure of her new role.  
她正學習應付新職位帶來的壓力。

### 02 Handle

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：處理；應付；應對  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to deal with a situation, problem, person, task, or pressure competently  
**用法提示**：當 cope with 的重點是冷靜、有能力地處理某個問題／工作量／局面時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：handle pressure; handle a problem; handle the situation; handle criticism; handle the workload.  
**常見搭配對象**：problem, pressure, workload, criticism, difficult situation.  
**與 cope with 的辨析**：Handle 更偏實際處理能力；cope with 更偏在壓力或困難下撐住。  
**使用限制**：若重點是情緒上捱過悲傷／危機，get through / come to terms with 可能更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He handled the difficult situation calmly.  
他冷靜地處理了這個困難局面。

### 03 Get through

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：熬過；度過；捱過  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：to survive or successfully endure a difficult period, experience, or challenge  
**用法提示**：當 cope with 指的是『捱過一段時間／難關』時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：get through a difficult period; get through the week; get through a crisis; get through an illness; get through a hard time.  
**常見搭配對象**：crisis, difficult period, week, illness, hard time.  
**與 cope with 的辨析**：Get through 強調把難關熬過去；cope with 可指持續中的應對。  
**使用限制**：處理單一技術問題通常不用 get through。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Support from friends helped her get through a difficult year.  
朋友的支持幫助她捱過艱難的一年。

### 04 Deal with

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：處理；應付；面對  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to take action in response to a problem, situation, person, or demand  
**用法提示**：和 cope with 高度接近，但範圍更廣，可用於一般問題、投訴、工作事項。  
**常見搭配**：deal with stress; deal with a problem; deal with complaints; deal with change; deal with pressure.  
**常見搭配對象**：problem, complaint, stress, change, demand.  
**與 cope with 的辨析**：Deal with 可以純粹表示處理；cope with 通常暗示事情具有一定難度或壓力。  
**使用限制**：如果只是行政例行事項，deal with 比 cope with 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The team must deal with several unexpected problems.  
團隊必須處理數個突發問題。

### 05 Face

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：面對；應對  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：to confront or experience a difficult situation, challenge, or problem  
**用法提示**：當 cope with 的第一步是『面對』問題或挑戰時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：face a challenge; face pressure; face difficulties; face uncertainty; face a crisis.  
**常見搭配對象**：challenge, difficulty, crisis, pressure, uncertainty.  
**與 cope with 的辨析**：Face 只保證『面對到』；cope with 還表示在應付它。  
**使用限制**：若要表達成功應付，不應只用 face。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Small businesses are facing rising costs.  
小型企業正面對不斷上升的成本。

## Tier 2 — Adaptation / response / problem-solving variants · 適應、回應與主動處理變體

### 06 Adapt to

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：適應；調整以應付  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：to change behaviour, methods, or expectations in order to deal successfully with new conditions  
**用法提示**：當 cope with 的方法是改變自己以適應新環境、制度或變化時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：adapt to change; adapt to new conditions; adapt to pressure; adapt to a new environment; adapt quickly.  
**常見搭配對象**：change, environment, technology, system, conditions.  
**與 cope with 的辨析**：Adapt to 強調改變自己；cope with 不一定需要改變。  
**使用限制**：一次性的危機或情緒衝擊未必適合用 adapt to。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Employees had to adapt to the new working arrangements.  
員工必須適應新的工作安排。

### 07 Respond to

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：回應；應對  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to react or take action in response to a challenge, event, problem, or demand  
**用法提示**：當 cope with 強調採取回應措施，而不是情緒上承受時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：respond to pressure; respond to a crisis; respond to change; respond to demand; respond effectively.  
**常見搭配對象**：crisis, demand, change, pressure, problem.  
**與 cope with 的辨析**：Respond to 強調反應／行動；cope with 更強調持續應付難度。  
**使用限制**：個人悲傷、壓力承受未必只用 respond to。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The organisation responded quickly to the sudden increase in demand.  
機構迅速應對需求突然增加。

### 08 Tackle

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：著手處理；應付；解決  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：to make a determined effort to deal with a difficult problem or task  
**用法提示**：當 cope with 帶『主動著手處理』難題時很有力。  
**常見搭配**：tackle a problem; tackle stress; tackle inequality; tackle a backlog; tackle challenges.  
**常見搭配對象**：problem, backlog, challenge, inequality, issue.  
**與 cope with 的辨析**：Tackle 更主動、有解決導向；cope with 可以只是設法撐住。  
**使用限制**：悲傷、哀痛等情緒經驗通常不說 tackle grief，除非語氣刻意務實。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The council is tackling the housing shortage.  
議會正著手處理房屋短缺問題。

### 09 Navigate

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：應對；周旋；設法處理複雜局面  
**語域**：neutral / metaphorical  
**核心語義**：to find a workable way through a complicated, changing, or uncertain situation  
**用法提示**：當 cope with 的難點是制度複雜、選擇繁多、不確定性高時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：navigate change; navigate uncertainty; navigate a crisis; navigate bureaucracy; navigate challenges.  
**常見搭配對象**：change, uncertainty, bureaucracy, crisis, transition.  
**與 cope with 的辨析**：Navigate 強調在複雜環境中找路；cope with 更一般。  
**使用限制**：非常簡單、單一問題用 navigate 可能過於修辭。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
New managers must learn to navigate organisational change.  
新任經理必須學會應對機構變革。

### 10 Come to terms with

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：接受並逐步適應；面對現實  
**語域**：neutral / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：to gradually accept a difficult or painful reality and learn to live with it  
**用法提示**：當 cope with 涉及失落、改變、壞消息或不可改變現實時非常重要。  
**常見搭配**：come to terms with loss; come to terms with change; come to terms with the diagnosis; come to terms with reality; slowly come to terms.  
**常見搭配對象**：loss, diagnosis, change, reality, disappointment.  
**與 cope with 的辨析**：Come to terms with 強調心理接受；cope with 可包括任何應付方式。  
**使用限制**：技術任務、工作量、行政問題不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He is still coming to terms with the loss of his job.  
他仍在逐步接受失去工作的現實。

## Tier 3 — Endurance / ongoing-control variants · 承受、挺過與持續掌握變體

### 11 Bear up under

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：在……下撐住；忍受  
**語域**：formal / less common  
**核心語義**：to remain emotionally or mentally strong under pressure, difficulty, or suffering  
**用法提示**：當 cope with 重點是承受沉重壓力並維持狀態時可用。  
**常見搭配**：bear up under pressure; bear up under strain; bear up well; bear up under difficulties; bear up under grief.  
**常見搭配對象**：pressure, strain, grief, difficulty, stress.  
**與 cope with 的辨析**：Bear up under 強調承受力；cope with 更常見、更中性。  
**使用限制**：較書面、較少見，不宜大量用於日常口語。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She bore up remarkably well under intense pressure.  
她在巨大壓力下仍然撐得相當好。

### 12 Weather

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：挺過；捱過（危機／困難）  
**語域**：neutral / figurative  
**核心語義**：to survive a difficult period, crisis, or adverse conditions without being destroyed or seriously damaged  
**用法提示**：當 cope with 指企業、機構或個人成功捱過危機／衝擊時很地道。  
**常見搭配**：weather the crisis; weather the storm; weather a recession; weather difficult conditions; weather the downturn.  
**常見搭配對象**：crisis, recession, storm, downturn, difficult period.  
**與 cope with 的辨析**：Weather 暗示成功挺過外部衝擊；cope with 可用於仍在進行中的應付。  
**使用限制**：單一小問題或日常任務通常不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The company managed to weather the recession.  
公司設法捱過了經濟衰退。

### 13 Keep on top of

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：掌握；應付得住；不讓事情失控  
**語域**：informal / especially British  
**核心語義**：to remain in control of ongoing tasks, demands, information, or workload  
**用法提示**：當 cope with 指持續管理工作量、電郵、帳務等，使之不累積失控時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：keep on top of emails; keep on top of the workload; keep on top of bills; keep on top of tasks; keep on top of changes.  
**常見搭配對象**：workload, emails, bills, tasks, updates.  
**與 cope with 的辨析**：Keep on top of 強調持續控制與跟進；cope with 偏一般應付。  
**使用限制**：嚴重悲傷、災難或一次性危機通常不合適。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She uses a planner to keep on top of her workload.  
她用記事簿來掌握工作量，避免失控。

## 04 False synonyms / coping traps · 容易誤選的詞與應對陷阱

### 01 Solve

**解決不等於 cope with**  
solve 表示把問題解決；cope with 可以只是在問題仍存在時設法應付。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
You can cope with a problem without solving it.

### 02 Ignore

**忽視不是應付**  
ignore 是不理會；cope with 表示實際面對並設法處理／承受。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Ignoring a difficulty is not the same as coping with it.

### 03 Avoid

**避開不等於 cope with**  
avoid 表示不接觸或避開問題；cope with 表示問題存在時仍要應付。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Avoiding prevents contact; coping deals with the situation when it exists.

### 04 Accept

**接受不等於所有 cope with**  
accept 可是應對的一部分，尤其不可改變的現實，但不表示完整管理能力。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Acceptance can support coping, but the two are not identical.

### 05 Survive

**生存／捱過不等於所有 cope with**  
survive 只表示沒有被擊倒或死亡；cope with 還包括日常管理、調節和功能維持。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Survival is an outcome; coping is a process.

### 06 Control

**控制不等於 cope with**  
control 表示掌控情況；很多事情即使不能控制，人仍可 cope with。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
control is stronger: people often cope with situations they cannot control.

### 07 Fix

**修好不等於 cope with**  
fix 偏把故障／問題修正；cope with 可以是暫時應付而沒有修好根源。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Fixing removes or repairs a problem; coping manages its effects.

### 08 Cope

**注意介詞**  
標準用法是 **cope with something**；一般不說 *cope something*。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Cope normally takes with before its object: cope with stress.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Manage · 應付；設法處理；撐得住

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

She is learning to cope with the pressure of her new role.

**中文翻譯**  
她正學習應付新職位帶來的壓力。

- A. manage
- B. solve
- C. ignore
- D. avoid
- E. fix
- F. control completely

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**manage** · 應付；設法處理；撐得住

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
She is learning to manage the pressure of her new role.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. manage ✓ — pressure 是持續存在的負荷，manage 表示設法應付而不必消除它。**
- **B. solve — 壓力不是一條可一次解決的題目；原句也沒說壓力會消失。**
- **C. ignore — 忽視壓力不等於實際應付。**
- **D. avoid — 她已身處新職位，不能單靠避開來表達 cope。**
- **E. fix — fix 更像修正故障／根本問題，不適合一般壓力。**
- **F. control completely — manage 不要求完全掌控，這個選項過強。**

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Cope with 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 一般設法應付 | **manage** | 是否只是要維持運作、處理得來？ |
| 有能力處理 | **handle** | 是否重點是 competence / calm handling？ |
| 熬過一段難關 | **get through** | 是否有明確 difficult period / crisis？ |
| 一般處理問題 | **deal with** | 是否需要採取實際行動處理？ |
| 面對挑戰 | **face** | 是否只是強調遭遇／面對？ |
| 適應變化 | **adapt to** | 是否需要改變自己／做法？ |
| 採取回應 | **respond to** | 是否有 event/demand/change 需要反應？ |
| 主動著手解決 | **tackle** | 是否重點是積極處理難題？ |
| 複雜環境中找路 | **navigate** | 是否有 uncertainty / bureaucracy / transition？ |
| 心理接受現實 | **come to terms with** | 是否是 loss / diagnosis / irreversible change？ |
| 在壓力下撐住 | **bear up under** | 是否強調精神承受力？ |
| 挺過危機／衰退 | **weather** | 是否是外部衝擊，需要生存下來？ |
| 持續掌握工作量 | **keep on top of** | 是否要避免 emails/tasks/bills 累積失控？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **cope with vs manage**: manage 更廣；cope with 更常帶壓力／困難意味。
- **cope with vs handle**: handle 強調實際處理能力；cope with 更偏承受和適應。
- **cope with vs get through**: get through 強調熬過一段時間或事件。
- **cope with vs deal with**: deal with 可用於一般問題；cope with 更常暗示負荷或難度。
- **cope with vs face**: face 只表示面對；cope with 表示還要設法應付。
- **cope with vs adapt to**: adapt to 是透過改變自己來適應。
- **cope with vs tackle**: tackle 更主動、更解決導向。
- **cope with vs come to terms with**: 後者專門指逐步接受艱難現實。
- **cope with vs weather**: weather 強調成功挺過外部危機／衝擊。
- **cope with vs solve**: solve 表示解決問題；cope with 不要求問題消失。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & coping traps · 文法、搭配與應對陷阱

- 標準結構是 **cope with + noun/pronoun**：cope with stress / cope with the situation / cope with it。
- 一般不說 **cope something**。若沒有受詞，可說 **I’m coping**。
- **manage + noun**：manage the workload / manage stress；也可說 manage to + verb，但意思變成『設法做到』。
- **handle + noun**：handle pressure / criticism / complaints。
- **get through + period/event**：get through a difficult week / crisis / exam period。
- **deal with + issue/person/task**：deal with complaints / problems / requests。
- **adapt to + noun/-ing**：adapt to change / adapt to working remotely。
- **respond to + event/demand**：respond to pressure / a crisis / increased demand。
- **tackle + problem/challenge**：tackle a backlog / housing shortage；比 cope with 更主動。
- **come to terms with + reality/loss/change**：重點是心理接受，通常不是技術問題。
- **bear up under + pressure/strain**：較書面；日常口語更常用 cope well / hold up。
- **weather + crisis/storm/recession**：通常是及物動詞，不說 *weather with a crisis*。
- **keep on top of + ongoing tasks**：keep on top of emails / bills / workload。
- **cope with ≠ solve/fix**：能應付不代表已把根本問題解決。
