# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Struggling with debt

**`struggling with debt` 可以表示難以還貸、背負沉重債務、面對廣泛財政困難、單純處於負債狀態、承受財務壓力，或已經過度負債。不同替代表達最重要的差別，是『是否只是有債』『是否已還款困難』『是否屬廣泛財政困境』『債務是否已超出償還能力』。**

核心替代表達：

`having difficulty repaying loans / burdened by debt / facing financial hardship / in debt / under financial strain / struggling to make repayments / debt-ridden / over-indebted`

> **核心原則**：先判斷 struggling with debt 指的是 **repayment difficulty, debt burden, broad financial hardship, simple indebtedness, financial strain, missed/strained repayments, severe debt burden, or technical over-indebtedness**。

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Having difficulty repaying loans

**難以償還貸款；還款有困難**

當 `struggling with debt` 的核心是每月還款、貸款供款或債務償還壓力時，這個描述性表達最精準。它直接指出 repayment difficulty，而不是泛泛說財政困難。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Many households are having difficulty repaying loans after interest rates rose.

利率上升後，很多家庭難以償還貸款。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **difficulty meeting loan repayments**

**REGISTER · 語域**

金融、政策、正式

---

## 02 Burdened by debt

**債務纏身；背負沉重債務**

和原句非常接近，強調債務本身形成沉重負擔。它比 `having difficulty repaying loans` 更廣，不只限貸款，也可包括信用卡、按揭或其他債務。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The business became burdened by debt after several years of losses.

公司連續虧損數年後變得債務纏身。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **carrying a heavy debt burden**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、財經、正式

---

## 03 Facing financial hardship

**面對財政困難；陷入經濟困境**

比 `struggling with debt` 更廣。它可包含債務問題，但也可能包括失業、收入下降、醫療開支或生活成本壓力，因此不是完全同義。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The charity supports families facing financial hardship.

慈善機構支援面對財政困難的家庭。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **experiencing serious difficulty managing finances**

**REGISTER · 語域**

社會、政策、正式

---

## 04 In debt

**負債；欠債**

最簡潔、最直接，表示一個人或機構有債務。它只描述負債狀態，不一定表示已經『 struggling 』或還款困難。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He was deeply in debt after the business failed.

生意失敗後，他負債累累。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **owing money to others**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 05 Under financial strain

**承受財政壓力；財務緊張**

當 `struggling with debt` 強調現金流、供款和日常開支造成持續壓力時很自然。它比 `facing financial hardship` 稍中性，也不一定已到嚴重困境。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Higher mortgage payments have left many families under financial strain.

按揭供款上升令很多家庭承受財政壓力。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **experiencing pressure because money is tight**

**REGISTER · 語域**

財經、社會、正式

---

## 06 Struggling to make repayments

**難以按時還款；供款吃力**

和原句非常接近，特別適合信用卡、按揭、貸款或分期供款。它比一般 `struggling with debt` 更聚焦實際 repayment。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Some borrowers are struggling to make repayments on time.

部分借款人難以按時還款。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **having trouble meeting scheduled repayments**

**REGISTER · 語域**

金融、一般、正式

---

## 07 Debt-ridden

**債務纏身的；負債累累的**

較強、較書面，表示負債很多，甚至債務已成為嚴重問題。語氣比 `in debt` 更重，常形容個人、公司或政府。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The debt-ridden company was forced to sell assets.

這間債務纏身的公司被迫出售資產。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **heavily burdened with debt**

**REGISTER · 語域**

新聞、財經、正式

---

## 08 Over-indebted

**過度負債的；債務過重的**

較技術、政策化，表示債務水平相對收入或還款能力過高。常見於個人金融、消費者保護和研究語境。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The study focuses on over-indebted households with high repayment burdens.

研究聚焦於還款負擔沉重的過度負債家庭。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **having more debt than can reasonably be managed**

**REGISTER · 語域**

金融、研究、政策

# 02 False synonyms

## Struggling with debt / Having difficulty repaying loans
後者最具體，直接說明問題在於貸款／供款難以償還。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A person can be in debt and still repay comfortably.  
Someone having difficulty repaying loans is experiencing actual repayment stress.

## Struggling with debt / Burdened by debt
`burdened by debt` 強調債務形成沉重負擔，但不一定逐筆描述供款。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The company is burdened by debt.  
It may also be struggling to make repayments on its loans.

## Struggling with debt / Facing financial hardship
`facing financial hardship` 範圍更廣，可由失業、低收入、醫療費或生活成本造成。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Debt may cause financial hardship.  
Financial hardship, however, does not always come from debt.

## Struggling with debt / In debt
`in debt` 只表示有欠款，不等於一定有困難。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A homeowner with a manageable mortgage is in debt.  
A homeowner missing payments is struggling with debt.

## Struggling with debt / Under financial strain
`under financial strain` 強調現金流和開支壓力，原因不一定只是債務。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Higher bills can put a household under financial strain.  
High loan repayments can make that strain debt-related.

## Struggling with debt / Struggling to make repayments
後者把問題直接聚焦到供款是否能按時支付。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
She is struggling with debt.  
More specifically, she is struggling to make repayments each month.

## Struggling with debt / Debt-ridden
`debt-ridden` 語氣更強，通常表示債務非常沉重。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A person may be in debt without serious difficulty.  
A debt-ridden business may be close to insolvency.

## Struggling with debt / Over-indebted
`over-indebted` 是較技術、政策化的概念，表示債務相對收入或償還能力過高。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Many households are in debt.  
Only some are over-indebted relative to their repayment capacity.

# 03 練習與解釋

> 每個目標詞／表達三題，共 **24 題**。  
> 每題六個選項；正確答案位置在 **A–F 各出現四次**。  
> 每個選項均按 **是否只是有債、是否難以供款、是否廣泛財困、是否現金流受壓、是否過度負債、語域、連字號和構詞** 作句子層面的解釋。

> Many households are struggling with debt because their monthly loan payments have risen.

**中文翻譯**

很多家庭因每月貸款供款上升而陷入債務困境。

**A. having difficulty repaying loans · 難以償還貸款；還款有困難**

> Many households are having difficulty repaying loans because their monthly loan payments have risen.

**C. under financial strain**  
`under financial strain`：承受財務壓力；不一定只由債務造成。 本句線索是「monthly loan payments have risen，直接是還款困難」；它把重點放在一般負債、廣泛財政困難、現金流壓力或更技術性的過度負債，因此不是本句最佳替代。

**D. in debt**  
`in debt`：有債務；不一定表示還款困難。 本句線索是「monthly loan payments have risen，直接是還款困難」；它把重點放在一般負債、廣泛財政困難、現金流壓力或更技術性的過度負債，因此不是本句最佳替代。

**F. financially secure**

# 04 Target-phrase contrast map

| `struggling with debt` 真正重點 | 較精準替代表達 |
|---|---|
| 難以償還貸款 | **having difficulty repaying loans** |
| 債務形成沉重負擔 | **burdened by debt** |
| 面對廣泛財政困難 | **facing financial hardship** |
| 單純處於負債狀態 | **in debt** |
| 現金流／日常開支受壓 | **under financial strain** |
| 難以按時供款 | **struggling to make repayments** |
| 負債非常沉重 | **debt-ridden** |
| 債務超出合理償還能力 | **over-indebted** |

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **In debt vs Struggling with debt** — in debt 只表示有債；struggling 表示債務已造成困難。
- **Having difficulty repaying loans vs Struggling to make repayments** — 意思很接近；前者較描述性，後者更自然口語。
- **Burdened by debt vs Debt-ridden** — debt-ridden 語氣更重。
- **Facing financial hardship vs Under financial strain** — hardship 較嚴重；strain 可是中度持續壓力。
- **Over-indebted vs In debt** — over-indebted 涉及債務相對還款能力過高。
- **Debt-ridden vs Over-indebted** — 前者偏描述性／新聞；後者偏金融／政策。
- **Debt burden ≠ repayment failure** — 債務重，不一定已經漏供。
- **Financial hardship ≠ debt only** — 財政困難可以由很多原因造成。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## In debt
固定：

> be **in debt**

通常不說：

> be in debts ✗

## Burdened by debt
> be burdened **by** debt

也可見：

> be burdened **with** debt

## Repay / repayment
> repay a loan  
> make repayments  
> repayment difficulty  
> repayment burden

## Debt-ridden
作前置複合形容詞通常有連字號：

> a debt-ridden company

## Over-indebted
標準寫法通常有連字號：

> over-indebted households

## Financial hardship / strain
> face financial hardship  
> be under financial strain

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 直接說貸款難還？ → **having difficulty repaying loans**
2. 強調債務負擔沉重？ → **burdened by debt**
3. 不只債務，整體都財困？ → **facing financial hardship**
4. 只想說有欠款？ → **in debt**
5. 主要是現金流／日常支出壓力？ → **under financial strain**
6. 已經供款吃力／漏供？ → **struggling to make repayments**
7. 債務非常沉重、語氣較強？ → **debt-ridden**
8. 債務相對收入／償還能力過高？ → **over-indebted**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`having difficulty repaying loans / burdened by debt / facing financial hardship / in debt / under financial strain / struggling to make repayments / debt-ridden / over-indebted`

尤其要記住：

> **in debt** = 有債  
> **struggling to make repayments** = 還款困難  
> **burdened by debt** = 債務負擔重  
> **financial hardship** = 整體財困  
> **over-indebted** = 債務超出合理償還能力

最重要的一點：

> **in debt ≠ struggling with debt ≠ over-indebted**

三者分別只是表示 **有債、債務已造成實際困難、債務已高到超出合理還款能力**。
