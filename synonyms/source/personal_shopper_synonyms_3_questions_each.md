# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Personal shopper

**`personal shopper` 可以指一對一提供購物建議的人、實際替客戶尋貨／購買的人，或在時裝、高端零售和酒店環境中提供更專門的造型／禮賓購物服務。不同替代表達的職責範圍並不完全相同。**

核心替代表達：

`personal shopping consultant / shopping consultant / buying adviser / shopping adviser / personal buyer / style consultant / wardrobe consultant / shopping concierge`

> **核心原則**：先判斷角色重點是 **personal advice, product comparison, buying decisions, purchasing on behalf of a client, fashion/style, wardrobe planning, or premium concierge coordination**。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Personal shopping consultant

**私人購物顧問**

和 `personal shopper` 最接近、最正式的替代表達之一。強調專業地按客戶需要、預算、風格或場合提供個人化購物建議。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The department store offers appointments with a personal shopping consultant.

百貨公司提供私人購物顧問預約服務。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a professional who gives personalised shopping advice**

**REGISTER · 語域**

零售、奢侈品、正式

---

## 02 Shopping consultant

**購物顧問**

較簡潔、自然，表示協助客戶選擇產品、比較選項和作購買決定的人。比 `personal shopper` 稍少強調代客購買。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> A shopping consultant helped her choose furniture for the new flat.

購物顧問協助她為新居選擇家具。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a consultant who advises a customer on purchases**

**REGISTER · 語域**

零售、一般、服務

---

## 03 Buying adviser

**購買顧問；採購建議員**

強調提供購買決策建議，例如比較品質、價格、需要和價值。它可以用於個人消費，但比 `personal shopper` 更偏 advice 而非陪同購物或代購。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The buying adviser compared several premium coffee machines for the client.

購買顧問為客戶比較了數款高級咖啡機。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **an adviser who helps someone decide what to buy**

**REGISTER · 語域**

消費、零售、較正式

---

## 04 Shopping adviser

**購物顧問；購物建議員**

直接、透明的描述性表達，強調提供購物建議。自然度不及 `shopping consultant`，但在服務介紹或一般語境可用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The shopping adviser suggested several options within her budget.

購物顧問建議了數個符合她預算的選項。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **someone who advises on shopping choices**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、零售、描述性

---

## 05 Personal buyer

**私人買手；代客購買者**

當 `personal shopper` 不只是提供建議，而是實際代表客戶尋找、挑選和購買商品時，`personal buyer` 很合適。注意它有時也可令人聯想到採購職位。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The client asked her personal buyer to source a limited-edition handbag.

客戶請私人買手尋找一款限量手袋。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **someone who selects and purchases goods on behalf of a client**

**REGISTER · 語域**

奢侈品、私人服務、一般

---

## 06 Style consultant

**造型顧問；風格顧問**

只在 `personal shopper` 主要協助服裝、造型、配搭和個人形象時適合。它比 personal shopper 更聚焦 style，而不是一般購物。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> A style consultant helped him choose clothes for a new job.

造型顧問協助他為新工作挑選衣服。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a consultant who advises on personal style and clothing choices**

**REGISTER · 語域**

時裝、形象、零售

---

## 07 Wardrobe consultant

**衣櫥顧問；服裝顧問**

比 `style consultant` 更聚焦衣櫥整理、服裝搭配和添置合適衣物。是 fashion-specific contextual near-synonym。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The wardrobe consultant reviewed which clothes the client already owned before shopping.

衣櫥顧問在購物前先檢視客戶現有的衣物。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a specialist who advises on clothing and wardrobe choices**

**REGISTER · 語域**

時裝、私人服務、專業

---

## 08 Shopping concierge

**購物禮賓；購物專員**

常見於高級商場、酒店或奢侈品服務，表示為客戶安排購物、尋找商品、預約品牌或提供高度個人化協助。比一般 personal shopper 更有 concierge service 色彩。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The mall's shopping concierge arranged private appointments at several boutiques.

商場的購物禮賓為客戶安排了數間精品店的私人預約。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a premium service specialist who coordinates personalised shopping**

**REGISTER · 語域**

高端零售、酒店、奢侈品

# 02 False synonyms

## Personal shopper / Personal shopping consultant
兩者非常接近；後者更正式、更像職銜。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The store offers a personal shopper service.  
Clients can book a personal shopping consultant for a private appointment.

## Personal shopper / Shopping consultant
`shopping consultant` 偏購買建議與產品比較，未必會實際代客購物。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A shopping consultant may compare several products with you.  
A personal shopper may also select or purchase items on your behalf.

## Personal shopper / Buying adviser
`buying adviser` 重點是「是否值得買／買哪一款」的決策建議。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A buying adviser may compare price, quality, and value.  
A personal shopper may additionally source the product and arrange the purchase.

## Shopping consultant / Shopping adviser
兩者意思接近；`shopping consultant` 通常更自然、更像正式服務名稱。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A retailer may advertise a shopping consultant service.  
Shopping adviser is a transparent but less established description.

## Personal shopper / Personal buyer
`personal buyer` 更強調真正代表客戶購買商品。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A personal shopper may accompany a client and give advice.  
A personal buyer may source and purchase an item while the client is elsewhere.

## Personal shopper / Style consultant
`style consultant` 只在衣着、造型和個人形象方面接近。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A personal shopper may help someone buy furniture or gifts.  
A style consultant focuses on clothing, appearance, and styling.

## Style consultant / Wardrobe consultant
`wardrobe consultant` 更專門處理現有衣櫥、服裝缺口和長期衣着規劃。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A style consultant may suggest a look for an event.  
A wardrobe consultant may review an entire wardrobe before recommending purchases.

## Personal shopper / Shopping concierge
`shopping concierge` 更偏高端服務，常包括預約、尋貨、送貨和行程安排。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A personal shopper may help choose products in a department store.  
A shopping concierge may arrange boutique appointments and same-day delivery across several brands.

# 03 練習與解釋

> 每個目標詞／表達三題，共 **24 題**。  
> 每題六個選項；正確答案位置在 **A–F 各出現四次**。  
> 每個選項都按「建議、比較、代購、時裝造型、衣櫥規劃或高端禮賓服務」作個別解釋。

## 練習 01.1

**Target phrase: Personal shopping consultant · 私人購物顧問**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> The store assigned a personal shopper to help her choose gifts within a fixed budget.

**中文翻譯**

商店安排私人購物顧問協助她在固定預算內挑選禮物。

| | 選項 |
|---|---|
| A | Personal shopping consultant |
| B | Shopping consultant |
| C | Shopping adviser |
| D | Personal buyer |
| E | Sales assistant |
| F | Cashier |

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**A. Personal shopping consultant · 私人購物顧問**

> The store assigned a personal shopping consultant to help her choose gifts within a fixed budget.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. Personal shopping consultant ✓**  
句子線索「正式個人化購物建議」是正式、一對一、按個人需要提供購物建議；`personal shopping consultant` 最完整精確。

**B. Shopping consultant**  
`shopping consultant`：重點是比較產品和提供購物建議。這句線索是「正式個人化購物建議」；若服務特別是一對一私人化、代購、造型或高端禮賓，這個詞較廣。

**C. Shopping adviser**  
`shopping adviser`：描述性地表示提供購物建議。這句線索是「正式個人化購物建議」；若需要正式職銜、代購或專門時裝服務，這個詞不夠具體。

**D. Personal buyer**  
`personal buyer`：重點是代表客戶搜尋並實際購買商品。這句線索是「正式個人化購物建議」；若只提供建議而不代購，`buyer` 會把角色說得太實際執行化。

**E. Sales assistant**  
`Sales assistant`：sales assistant = 店務員／銷售助理；通常代表店舖銷售商品，不等於為客戶提供跨產品的私人購物顧問服務。 本句線索是「正式個人化購物建議」，所以職責和私人購物服務不同。

**F. Cashier**  
`Cashier`：cashier = 收銀員；負責付款交易，不是購物顧問。 本句線索是「正式個人化購物建議」，所以職責和私人購物服務不同。

## 練習 03.1

**Target phrase: Buying adviser · 購買顧問；採購建議員**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> The personal shopper gave him detailed advice on which laptop offered the best value.

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Personal shopper vs Personal shopping consultant** — 幾乎同義；consultant 更正式。
- **Shopping consultant vs Buying adviser** — consultant 可提供較全面選購協助；adviser 更偏購買決策。
- **Personal shopper vs Personal buyer** — shopper 可陪同／建議；buyer 更明確代客購買。
- **Shopping consultant vs Shopping adviser** — 意思近；consultant 通常更自然作服務名稱。
- **Personal shopper vs Style consultant** — style consultant 只限時裝／形象。
- **Style consultant vs Wardrobe consultant** — wardrobe consultant 更偏衣櫥整體管理和缺口分析。
- **Personal shopper vs Shopping concierge** — concierge 服務層級更高、更重安排與協調。
- **Personal buyer vs Procurement buyer** — personal buyer 為私人客戶；procurement buyer 通常為企業採購。

- **personal shopper service** — 私人購物服務

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Shopper / buyer
兩者都可數：

> a personal shopper  
> a personal buyer

但 `buyer` 在商業上也可表示企業採購人員，因此需要上下文。

## Adviser / advisor
兩種拼法都可見；英式／香港正式英語常見 `adviser`。

## Consultant
常見：

> shopping consultant  
> style consultant  
> wardrobe consultant

## Concierge
`concierge` 可以指酒店禮賓，也可延伸至高端專屬服務：

> shopping concierge

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 最正式直接的私人購物顧問？ → **personal shopping consultant**
2. 一般產品比較和購物建議？ → **shopping consultant**
3. 重點是買哪款／值不值得？ → **buying adviser**
4. 只需描述一般購物建議角色？ → **shopping adviser**
5. 實際代表客戶尋貨、付款、購買？ → **personal buyer**
6. 重點是服裝造型和形象？ → **style consultant**
7. 重點是整理和規劃整個衣櫥？ → **wardrobe consultant**
8. 高端預約／尋貨／送貨／跨品牌安排？ → **shopping concierge**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`personal shopping consultant / shopping consultant / buying adviser / shopping adviser / personal buyer / style consultant / wardrobe consultant / shopping concierge`

最重要的 near-miss：

`sales assistant / buyer / procurement specialist / stylist / concierge / retail assistant`

尤其要記住：

> **consultant / adviser** = 主要提供建議  
> **personal buyer** = 實際代客購買  
> **style / wardrobe consultant** = 時裝專門化  
> **shopping concierge** = 高端協調服務
