# CERTIFICATIONS · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Certifications

**「認證／資格／正式資歷／證書」，必須分清『資格狀態』、『證明文件』和『專業認可制度』。**

本單元集中處理 **certifications = formally recognised evidence that a person, organisation, product, or programme has met specified requirements or standards**。你提供的 **formal credentials; qualifications; certificates** 都很有價值，但三者焦點不同：**credentials** 強調可驗證的正式資歷，**qualifications** 強調勝任／合資格條件，**certificates** 則更偏實際證明文件。

這次保留 **14 個**真正有教學價值的詞／表達；每項均包含 **核心語義、語域、用法提示、常見搭配、搭配對象、與 certifications 的直接辨析、使用限制、例句及翻譯**。每個目標詞／表達設 **3 道題**，每題六個選項均有完整、逐題的情境化解釋。

## Tier 1 — Core credential equivalents · 核心資格／證明近義表達

### 01 Formal credentials

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：正式資歷；正式資格證明  
**語域**：formal / very common  
**核心語義**：official evidence that a person has completed training, met standards, or gained recognised competence  
**用法提示**：和 certifications 很接近，尤其當重點是正式、可驗證的專業或教育資格。  
**常見搭配**：formal credentials; professional credentials; recognised credentials; verify credentials; credential requirements.  
**常見搭配對象**：job applicant, professional, specialist, technician, instructor.  
**與 certifications 的辨析**：Credentials 是較廣的上位詞，可包括學位、證書、牌照等；certifications 通常更具體指某項認證。  
**使用限制**：如果只談一張實體證書，certificate 會更精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Applicants must provide formal credentials in first aid and safety training.  
申請人必須提供急救和安全培訓的正式資歷證明。

### 02 Qualifications

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：資格；資歷  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：education, training, skills, or credentials that make someone suitable or eligible for a role  
**用法提示**：當 certifications 用來概括某人的專業能力證明或應徵資格時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：professional qualifications; academic qualifications; relevant qualifications; gain qualifications; qualification requirements.  
**常見搭配對象**：job, profession, course entry, promotion, specialist role.  
**與 certifications 的辨析**：Qualifications 範圍比 certifications 更廣，可包括學位、經驗或其他資格。  
**使用限制**：如果要求的是某個明確考試／機構認證，certification 更具體。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The position requires relevant qualifications in project management.  
這個職位要求具備項目管理相關資歷。

### 03 Certificates

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：證書；證明書  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：official documents showing completion, achievement, status, or certification  
**用法提示**：當 certifications 重點是取得的正式文件或證書本身時最接近。  
**常見搭配**：training certificate; completion certificate; professional certificate; certificate holder; issue a certificate.  
**常見搭配對象**：course, training, examination, programme, attendance.  
**與 certifications 的辨析**：Certificate 是文件；certification 可以是認證制度、資格狀態或取得認證的過程。  
**使用限制**：不能把所有 certification programmes 簡化成一張 certificate。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Participants receive certificates after completing the course.  
參加者完成課程後會獲發證書。

### 04 Professional credentials

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：專業資歷；專業資格證明  
**語域**：formal / professional  
**核心語義**：recognised qualifications or certifications demonstrating professional competence  
**用法提示**：當 certifications 用於就業、專業服務或職業能力語境時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：professional credentials; industry credentials; recognised professional credentials; maintain credentials; credentialed professional.  
**常見搭配對象**：consultant, engineer, trainer, accountant, healthcare worker.  
**與 certifications 的辨析**：Professional credentials 是更廣的職業資格集合；certifications 往往只是其中一部分。  
**使用限制**：學校一般課程完成證書未必可稱 professional credentials。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The employer checks candidates' professional credentials before appointment.  
僱主在任命前會核實候選人的專業資歷。

### 05 Professional qualifications

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：專業資格  
**語域**：formal / very common  
**核心語義**：officially recognised qualifications required or valued in a profession  
**用法提示**：當 certifications 用作職業入行、晉升或專業認可時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：professional qualification; gain a qualification; recognised qualification; professional qualification framework; qualification exam.  
**常見搭配對象**：accountancy, engineering, teaching, finance, management.  
**與 certifications 的辨析**：Professional qualification 偏整體專業資格；certification 可由特定機構針對某項技能頒發。  
**使用限制**：不是所有短期 certification 都算完整 professional qualification。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She completed several professional qualifications in finance.  
她完成了多項金融專業資格。

## Tier 2 — Recognised certification / status equivalents · 認可與資格狀態近義表達

### 06 Accreditations

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：認可資格；認證資格；認可狀態  
**語域**：formal / institutional  
**核心語義**：official recognition that a person, organisation, or programme meets specified standards  
**用法提示**：當 certifications 的核心是由權威機構給予正式認可時可近似。  
**常見搭配**：professional accreditation; industry accreditation; accreditation body; gain accreditation; maintain accreditation.  
**常見搭配對象**：programme, organisation, professional, training provider, course.  
**與 certifications 的辨析**：Accreditation 很多時認可的是機構或課程；certification 更常認可個人、產品或技能。  
**使用限制**：不能把所有個人 certificates 都稱 accreditation。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The training centre holds several industry accreditations.  
該培訓中心持有多項業界認可資格。

### 07 Recognised credentials

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：獲認可的資歷；認可資格證明  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：credentials accepted by a relevant employer, authority, or professional body  
**用法提示**：當 certifications 的價值在於『被業界／機構認可』時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：recognised credentials; internationally recognised credentials; industry-recognised credentials; recognised professional credential; credential recognition.  
**常見搭配對象**：employment, immigration, profession, industry, training.  
**與 certifications 的辨析**：Recognised credentials 強調認受性；certification 本身未必在所有地方都被承認。  
**使用限制**：若沒有明確 recognition，不能自動加上 recognised。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The programme leads to recognised credentials in cybersecurity.  
該課程可取得網絡安全方面獲認可的資歷。

### 08 Industry certifications

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：業界認證；行業資格認證  
**語域**：neutral / professional  
**核心語義**：certifications issued or recognised within a particular industry  
**用法提示**：當 certifications 明確屬某個行業技能或職業標準時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：industry certification; industry-recognised certification; technical certification; certification exam; certification provider.  
**常見搭配對象**：IT, cybersecurity, cloud computing, finance, project management.  
**與 certifications 的辨析**：這是 certifications 的具體子類，不是更廣泛的所有資歷。  
**使用限制**：學位、一般參與證書不一定屬 industry certification。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The course prepares students for several industry certifications.  
這個課程為學生準備多項業界認證。

### 09 Professional certifications

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：專業認證  
**語域**：neutral / professional  
**核心語義**：formal certifications demonstrating professional knowledge or skill  
**用法提示**：當 certifications 直接與專業技能、職業標準或持續專業發展相關時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：professional certification; earn a certification; renew a certification; certification programme; certification exam.  
**常見搭配對象**：project management, IT, finance, training, safety.  
**與 certifications 的辨析**：Professional certifications 是 certification 的具體職業類別；比 qualifications 更窄。  
**使用限制**：一般學校畢業證書不一定屬專業認證。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Employees can earn professional certifications through the company scheme.  
員工可透過公司計劃取得專業認證。

### 10 Certified status

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：認證資格；已獲認證的身份  
**語域**：formal / descriptive  
**核心語義**：the official status of having met certification requirements  
**用法提示**：當 certifications 重點不是證書文件，而是『已獲認證』的資格狀態時可改寫。  
**常見搭配**：certified status; maintain certified status; gain certified status; officially certified; certified professional.  
**常見搭配對象**：professional, technician, organisation, product, service.  
**與 certifications 的辨析**：Certified status 指狀態；certification 可指制度、過程或資格本身。  
**使用限制**：如果句子談的是多張證書，不應改成單一 status。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Technicians must maintain certified status through regular training.  
技術員必須透過定期培訓維持認證資格。

## Tier 3 — Document / achievement / modern credential variants · 文件、成就與現代資格變體

### 11 Certificates of completion

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：修業證書；完成證書  
**語域**：neutral / educational  
**核心語義**：documents confirming that a person has completed a course or programme  
**用法提示**：當 certifications 其實只證明完成培訓，而不代表專業能力認證時很重要。  
**常見搭配**：certificate of completion; course completion certificate; training completion certificate; receive a certificate; issue completion certificates.  
**常見搭配對象**：course, workshop, training programme, module.  
**與 certifications 的辨析**：完成證書只證明 completed；certification 往往意味通過標準、考核或能力要求。  
**使用限制**：不能把 attendance/completion certificate 誇大成 professional certification。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Everyone who finishes the workshop receives a certificate of completion.  
完成工作坊的所有人都會獲發修業證書。

### 12 Diplomas

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：文憑；畢業證書  
**語域**：neutral / educational  
**核心語義**：formal awards showing completion of a recognised programme of study  
**用法提示**：在某些教育語境，certifications 可能和 diplomas 同屬正式資歷，但兩者層級和制度不同。  
**常見搭配**：professional diploma; postgraduate diploma; diploma programme; earn a diploma; diploma qualification.  
**常見搭配對象**：college, vocational study, professional programme, education.  
**與 certifications 的辨析**：Diploma 通常代表較完整課程；certification 常更聚焦特定技能／標準。  
**使用限制**：不是直接可互換詞，只有在『正式資歷』的上位語境接近。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The college offers diplomas and professional certifications in digital media.  
學院提供數碼媒體文憑和專業認證。

### 13 Professional designations

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：專業稱號；專業資格稱號  
**語域**：formal / professional  
**核心語義**：official titles or letters awarded after meeting professional requirements  
**用法提示**：當 certification 最終給予某個正式稱號、縮寫或會員資格時可近似。  
**常見搭配**：professional designation; earn a designation; hold a designation; designatory letters; recognised designation.  
**常見搭配對象**：accountancy, finance, project management, professional bodies.  
**與 certifications 的辨析**：Designation 是取得認證後使用的稱號；certification 是取得資格的制度／證明。  
**使用限制**：沒有正式 title/letters 的 certification 不應稱 designation。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The qualification allows successful candidates to use a professional designation.  
該資格讓合格人士使用正式專業稱號。

### 14 Digital credentials

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：數碼資歷；電子資格證明  
**語域**：neutral / modern  
**核心語義**：digitally issued and verifiable evidence of skills, learning, or certification  
**用法提示**：當 certifications 以電子形式發出、可在線驗證或儲存在資格平台時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：digital credential; verifiable credential; digital certificate; credential platform; share digital credentials.  
**常見搭配對象**：online learning, professional development, skills platform, employment.  
**與 certifications 的辨析**：Digital credential 描述發放／驗證形式；內容可以是 certification、badge 或其他資格。  
**使用限制**：實體紙本 certification 不一定屬 digital credential。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Learners can add their digital credentials to their online profiles.  
學員可以把數碼資歷加入網上個人檔案。

## 04 False synonyms / scope traps · 容易誤選的詞與範圍陷阱

### 01 Licence / License

**牌照不等於 certification**  
licence（英式名詞）通常由政府或監管機構授權某人合法從事某活動；certification 多數證明能力或符合標準。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A licence grants legal permission; a certification usually confirms competence or compliance.

### 02 Degree

**學位不等於 certification**  
degree 是大學授予的學術資格，通常涵蓋完整學位課程；certification 往往更短、更專門。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A degree is an academic award; a certification is usually narrower and skill- or standard-focused.

### 03 Membership

**會員身份不等於 certification**  
加入專業組織不一定表示已通過專業認證。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Membership may accompany credentials, but being a member does not automatically mean being certified.

### 04 Award

**獎項不等於 certification**  
award 可以是比賽、表現或成就獎勵；certification 是依標準授予的資格證明。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
An award recognises achievement; a certification verifies a standard or competence.

### 05 Training

**培訓本身不等於 certification**  
training 是學習過程；certification 是完成要求或通過評核後取得的資格／認證。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Training can lead to certification, but it is not the certification itself.

### 06 Experience

**經驗不等於 certification**  
工作經驗可以支持資格，但沒有正式認證時，不能稱 certification。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Experience demonstrates practice; certification provides formal verification.

### 07 Certificate of attendance

**出席證明不等於專業認證**  
出席證明只證明參加過活動，未必有考核，也不證明能力。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Attendance confirms presence; certification usually requires meeting defined requirements.

### 08 Accreditation

**認可與 certification 的對象常不同**  
accreditation 很多時針對機構、課程或服務；certification 更常針對個人、產品或技能。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Accreditation and certification overlap, but they often apply to different subjects and processes.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Formal credentials · 正式資歷；正式資格證明

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

Applicants must provide relevant certifications before appointment.

**中文翻譯**  
申請人獲任命前必須提供相關正式資歷證明。

- A. formal credentials
- B. training
- C. experience
- D. membership
- E. awards
- F. attendance

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**formal credentials** · 正式資歷；正式資格證明

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
Applicants must provide relevant formal credentials before appointment.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. formal credentials ✓ — provide + before appointment 表示需要可核實的正式資格證明，最貼近 certifications。**
- **B. training — 只是學習過程，未必有正式資格結果。**
- **C. experience — 可以支持申請，但不等於正式認證。**
- **D. membership — 會員身份未必證明專業能力。**
- **E. awards — 獎項不一定和職位標準相關。**
- **F. attendance — 只說出席，沒有正式資格證明。**

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **certification vs certificate**: certification 可指資格、制度或過程；certificate 是證明文件。
- **certification vs qualification**: qualification 更廣，可包括 degree、training、experience 等合資格條件。
- **certification vs credential**: credential 是上位概念，可包括 certification、degree、licence 等。
- **certification vs accreditation**: certification 常認證個人／產品／技能；accreditation 很多時認可機構／課程。
- **certification vs licence**: licence 授予合法許可；certification 證明符合能力或標準。
- **certification vs degree**: degree 是完整學術獎項；certification 通常更專門、更短。
- **certification vs certificate of completion**: 後者只證明完成，不必然證明能力達標。
- **certification vs professional designation**: designation 是取得資格後可使用的正式稱號。
- **certification vs digital credential**: digital credential 描述電子發放／驗證形式，內容可能就是 certification。

## 08 Grammar, terminology & credential traps · 文法、術語與資格陷阱

- **certification** 可數／不可數皆可：a certification / several certifications；作『認證過程』時亦可作不可數概念。
- **certificate** 是具體文件：receive / issue / upload a certificate。
- **credential** 常用複數 **credentials** 表示一個人的整套資歷：check someone's credentials。
- **qualification** 在英式／香港英語很常用來泛指學歷和專業資格：academic / professional qualifications。
- **licence** 是英式名詞拼寫；美式名詞通常寫 **license**。它和 certification 不同，重點是法律許可。
- **accreditation** 不要機械改成 certification；先問被認可的是人、產品，還是機構／課程。
- **certificate of attendance / completion** 不應誇大成 professional certification，除非真的有考核和正式能力標準。
- **professional designation** 常和姓名後字母／正式 title 有關，例如某些專業組織授予的稱號。
- **digital credential** 是載體／驗證形式，不自動表示資格層級高低。
- 在招聘文件中，**certifications required / preferred** 通常應保留原本具體認證名稱，不要籠統改成 qualifications 而失去精確要求。
