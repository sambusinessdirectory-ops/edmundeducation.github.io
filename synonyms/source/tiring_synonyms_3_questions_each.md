# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Tiring (adjective)

**`tiring` 可表示活動令人疲倦，但更精準的替代表達取決於：是幾乎耗盡精力、慢慢耗神、因重複而疲乏、技術上造成疲勞、需要大量體力、非常費神、極其艱苦，還是漫長而艱鉅。**

核心替代表達：

`exhausting / draining / wearying / fatiguing / strenuous / taxing / gruelling / arduous`

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Exhausting

**令人精疲力盡的；極累人的**

當 `tiring` 指一項活動、一天或過程把人的體力或精神幾乎耗盡時，`exhausting` 是最直接而且更強烈的近義詞。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The twelve-hour shift was exhausting.

十二小時的輪班令人筋疲力盡。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **causing extreme physical or mental tiredness**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 02 Draining

**令人耗盡精力的；消耗精神的**

當 `tiring` 特別指某件事慢慢消耗人的精神、情緒或注意力時，`draining` 很自然。它常見於 meetings, conflict, emotional work, constant demands。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The repeated complaints were emotionally draining.

反覆的投訴令人精神上非常疲累。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **gradually using up emotional or mental energy**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 03 Wearying

**令人厭倦疲累的；使人疲乏的**

當 `tiring` 指事情因為持續太久、重複或單調而令人逐漸疲乏時，`wearying` 很精準。它比 exhausting 更偏 prolonged repetition。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The long, repetitive negotiations became wearying.

漫長而重複的談判變得令人疲乏。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **causing tiredness through repetition or duration**

**REGISTER · 語域**

書面、正式

---

## 04 Fatiguing

**令人疲勞的；使人疲乏的**

較正式。當 `tiring` 指某種活動、姿勢、工作或環境會造成身體或精神疲勞時，`fatiguing` 很合適，常見於醫療、人體工學及技術語境。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Holding the tool above shoulder height is fatiguing.

把工具長時間舉高過肩會令人疲勞。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **causing physical or mental fatigue**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、技術、醫療

---

## 05 Strenuous

**費力的；需要大量體力的**

當 `tiring` 的原因是活動本身需要很大體力、強度高時，`strenuous` 很自然。它更強調 effort required，而不是最終有多累。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The hike was strenuous but rewarding.

這次遠足很費力，但很值得。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **requiring considerable physical effort**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 06 Taxing

**費神的；吃力的；負擔很重的**

當 `tiring` 指工作、問題或責任對注意力、腦力或耐力要求很高時，`taxing` 很精準。它可形容 mental 或 physical demand。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The negotiations were mentally taxing.

談判在精神上很費神。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **placing heavy demands on someone's energy or concentration**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、工作、一般

---

## 07 Gruelling

**極其艱苦累人的；折磨人的**

英式拼法 `gruelling`。當 `tiring` 指訓練、旅程、比賽、工作日等極度艱苦、持續而令人筋疲力盡時，`gruelling` 很有力。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> They completed a gruelling ten-hour climb.

他們完成了一次極其艱苦的十小時攀登。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **extremely demanding and exhausting over time**

**REGISTER · 語域**

英式、新聞、一般

---

## 08 Arduous

**艱鉅費力的；漫長辛苦的**

較正式。當 `tiring` 指一項工作、旅程或過程既困難又需要長時間努力時，`arduous` 很合適。它比 strenuous 更偏 prolonged difficulty。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Restoring the old building was an arduous process.

修復舊建築是一個艱鉅而漫長的過程。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **requiring sustained effort because of difficulty or duration**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、書面

# 02 False synonyms

## Tiring / Exhausting
`exhausting` 比一般 tiring 強，表示活動幾乎把人的體力或精神耗盡。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The twelve-hour shift was tiring.  
The twelve-hour shift was exhausting.

## Tiring / Draining
`draining` 特別適合情緒、精神、社交或持續壓力慢慢消耗能量的情況。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The constant complaints were tiring.  
The constant complaints were emotionally draining.

## Tiring / Wearying
`wearying` 強調長時間、重複、單調所造成的逐漸疲乏。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The repeated delays were tiring.  
The repeated delays became wearying.

## Tiring / Fatiguing
`fatiguing` 較正式，常見於醫療、人體工學、工作環境或技術描述。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The posture is tiring over time.  
The posture is physically fatiguing over time.

## Tiring / Strenuous
`strenuous` 強調活動本身需要大量體力，不等於單純「最後很累」。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The climb was tiring.  
The climb was strenuous because of the steep ascent.

## Tiring / Taxing
`taxing` 強調對注意力、腦力、判斷或耐力要求很高。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The review process was tiring.  
The review process was mentally taxing.

## Tiring / Gruelling
英式拼法 `gruelling`（美式常見 `grueling`）表示極其艱苦、持續而令人筋疲力盡。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The training session was tiring.  
The six-hour training session was gruelling.

## Tiring / Arduous
`arduous` 較正式，強調一項工作或旅程既困難又需要長時間努力。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Restoring the archive was tiring.  
Restoring the archive was an arduous process.

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Exhausting vs Draining** — exhausting 偏總體精力耗盡；draining 常偏精神／情緒能量慢慢流失。
- **Wearying vs Draining** — wearying 更常由重複、單調、時間過長造成；draining 更偏心理消耗。
- **Fatiguing vs Strenuous** — fatiguing 描述造成疲勞的效果；strenuous 描述活動需要多少體力。
- **Taxing vs Strenuous** — taxing 可主要是腦力／注意力；strenuous 通常偏身體用力。
- **Gruelling vs Exhausting** — exhausting 是結果「很累」；gruelling 還帶過程極其艱苦、持續的意味。
- **Arduous vs Gruelling** — arduous 正式地強調困難與長期努力；gruelling 更強烈、更帶折磨感。
- **Tiring ≠ tired** — tiring 形容「令人累的東西」；tired 形容「感到累的人／狀態」。
- **Tiring ≠ tiresome in every context** — tiresome 常表示「煩人的／惹人厭的」，不只是令人疲累。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

- **tiring** = causing tiredness；**tired** = feeling tired
- **exhausting** = causing exhaustion；**exhausted** = feeling exhausted
- **draining** = causing energy loss；**drained** = feeling depleted
- **wearying** = causing weariness；**weary** = feeling tired
- **fatiguing** = causing fatigue；**fatigued** = feeling fatigue
- **strenuous exercise/activity**
- **taxing task/problem/negotiation**
- British/HK spelling: **gruelling**；US: **grueling**
- **arduous task/journey/process**

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 幾乎把人全部精力耗盡？ → **exhausting**
2. 主要是情緒／精神被慢慢消耗？ → **draining**
3. 是長時間重複、單調而令人疲乏？ → **wearying**
4. 是正式／技術地說某活動造成疲勞？ → **fatiguing**
5. 是活動本身需要大量體力？ → **strenuous**
6. 是對腦力、注意力或判斷很有要求？ → **taxing**
7. 是極其艱苦、長時間折磨人的過程？ → **gruelling**
8. 是艱鉅而需要持續努力的任務／旅程？ → **arduous**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`exhausting / draining / wearying / fatiguing / strenuous / taxing / gruelling / arduous`

最重要的一點：

> **tiring ≠ tired**

`tiring` = 令人疲累；  
`tired` = 自己感到疲累。

同樣：
`exhausting` = 令人筋疲力盡；  
`exhausted` = 感到筋疲力盡。
