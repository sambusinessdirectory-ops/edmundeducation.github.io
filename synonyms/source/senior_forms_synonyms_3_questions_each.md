# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Senior forms

**本課集中在香港／英式學校語境中的 `senior forms`：中學較高年級、接近畢業或公開考試的階段。不同替代表達主要按『是否香港制度用語』『是否使用 form / grade』『是否強調最後幾年』『是否為國際化描述』來區分。**

核心替代表達：

`upper secondary years / senior secondary years / upper forms / senior years / final school years / final years of secondary school / senior grades / upper-school years`

> **核心原則**：先判斷 senior forms 指的是 **formal upper-secondary stage, Hong Kong senior-secondary stage, British/HK upper forms, general senior years, final school years, explicit final years of secondary school, grade-based senior grades, or upper-school division years**。

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Upper secondary years

**高中年級；高中階段**

和 `senior forms` 很接近，特別適合教育政策、課程和學校制度語境。它強調中學教育較後期的年級，而不是整個中學階段。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Students in the upper secondary years choose more specialised subjects.

高中階段的學生會選擇較專門的科目。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **the later years of secondary education**

**REGISTER · 語域**

教育、正式、國際

---

## 02 Senior secondary years

**高中年級；高年級中學階段**

在香港教育語境特別自然，直接對應中學後段年級。比 `upper secondary years` 更貼近香港常用說法。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Students in the senior secondary years prepare for public examinations.

高中年級學生會為公開考試作準備。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **the later years of secondary school, especially in Hong Kong-style usage**

**REGISTER · 語域**

香港教育、正式

---

## 03 Upper forms

**高年級；中學高年級**

和 `senior forms` 非常接近，保留了 British/HK `form` 的學校年級概念。比 `upper secondary years` 更校內、更傳統。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The upper forms take part in a separate careers programme.

高年級學生參加另一套升學及就業輔導計劃。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **the higher forms or classes in a school**

**REGISTER · 語域**

英式、香港、校內

---

## 04 Senior years

**高年級；高年級階段**

較廣泛、自然，可指中學後期，也可在其他教育制度中指較高年級。需要上下文已清楚是 secondary school。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Students in the senior years have more independent study time.

高年級學生有更多自主學習時間。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **the later or higher years of a school programme**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、教育

---

## 05 Final school years

**最後幾個學年；學校最後階段**

當 `senior forms` 的重點是離校前最後幾年，而不是制度名稱時，`final school years` 很自然。它強調接近畢業。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Career planning becomes more important in the final school years.

在學校最後幾年，生涯規劃變得更重要。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **the last years before completing school**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、教育

---

## 06 Final years of secondary school

**中學最後幾年**

較完整、清晰的描述性表達，適合國際讀者，避免 `form` 在不同地區可能不熟悉的問題。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Many students feel greater academic pressure in the final years of secondary school.

很多學生在中學最後幾年感到更大的學業壓力。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **the last years before finishing secondary school**

**REGISTER · 語域**

教育、國際、正式

---

## 07 Senior grades

**高年級**

在使用 `grade` 而不是 `form` 的教育制度中可作對應說法。它比 `senior forms` 更偏北美／國際學校語境。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Students in the senior grades can choose advanced science courses.

高年級學生可以選修進階科學課程。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **the higher grade levels in a school**

**REGISTER · 語域**

國際、北美式、教育

---

## 08 Upper-school years

**高年級學段；高中階段**

較描述性，常見於把學校分為 lower school / upper school 的制度。它不是所有學校都使用的固定層級名稱，但在相關制度中很自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Leadership opportunities increase during the upper-school years.

在高年級學段，學生有更多領導機會。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **the years spent in the upper division of a school**

**REGISTER · 語域**

國際學校、教育、描述性

# 02 False synonyms

## Senior forms / Upper secondary years
`upper secondary years` 最適合國際化、政策化描述中學後段教育。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The school organises activities for the senior forms.  
The curriculum framework covers the upper secondary years.

## Senior forms / Senior secondary years
在香港語境，`senior secondary years` 非常自然，尤其涉及 HKDSE、選修科和高中課程。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Students in the senior forms prepare for public examinations.  
Students in the senior secondary years prepare for the HKDSE.

## Senior forms / Upper forms
兩者高度接近；`upper forms` 保留傳統 British/HK `form` 用語。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The senior forms use a separate common room.  
The upper forms take part in the leadership programme.

## Senior forms / Senior years
`senior years` 更廣，不一定專指 secondary school；需要上下文已清楚。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Students in the senior years have more responsibility.  
In a secondary-school context, this may correspond to the senior forms.

## Senior forms / Final school years
`final school years` 強調離校前最後幾年，而不是正式制度名稱。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The senior forms follow an advanced curriculum.  
Career planning becomes important in the final school years.

## Senior forms / Final years of secondary school
這是最清楚、最國際化的描述性改寫之一，但比 `senior forms` 長。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Senior forms is natural in Hong Kong/British-style school language.  
Final years of secondary school is clearer for an international audience.

## Senior forms / Senior grades
`senior grades` 適合使用 `grade` 而不是 `form` 的制度，例如不少國際／北美式學校。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A Hong Kong school may refer to senior forms.  
A grade-based school may refer to senior grades.

## Senior forms / Upper-school years
只在學校本身有 `upper school / lower school` 分區時最自然。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The senior forms prepare for examinations.  
Leadership training is offered during the upper-school years.

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Upper secondary years vs Senior secondary years** — 前者較國際化；後者在香港語境更自然。
- **Senior forms vs Upper forms** — 幾乎直接對應，都是 British/HK `form` 語彙。
- **Senior years vs Final school years** — senior years = 高年級；final school years = 最後幾年。
- **Final school years vs Final years of secondary school** — 後者更清楚指定 secondary school。
- **Senior grades vs Senior forms** — grades 適合 grade-based 制度；forms 適合 British/HK 制度。
- **Upper-school years vs Upper secondary years** — upper-school 取決於校內分部；upper secondary 是教育階段概念。
- **Senior forms ≠ sixth form** — sixth form 是特定英國教育階段，不可泛化成所有 senior forms。
- **Senior forms ≠ junior secondary** — 兩者層次相反。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Form
British/HK school usage:

> Form 4 / Form 5 / Form 6  
> the upper forms

## Senior secondary
通常作複合形容詞：

> senior secondary curriculum  
> senior secondary students

若指多個年級：

> the senior secondary years

## Upper secondary
> upper secondary education  
> upper secondary years

## Grade / form
不要混合制度詞：

> senior grades = grade-based system  
> senior forms = form-based system

## Final years
固定結構：

> the final years **of** secondary school

不要漏：

> final years secondary school ✗

## Upper-school
作前置複合修飾時可用連字號：

> upper-school years

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 想用國際化正式教育術語？ → **upper secondary years**
2. 香港高中／HKDSE 語境？ → **senior secondary years**
3. 想保留 form-based 校內用語？ → **upper forms**
4. 只想泛指高年級？ → **senior years**
5. 強調快畢業／離校前幾年？ → **final school years**
6. 想讓國際讀者完全清楚？ → **final years of secondary school**
7. 學校用 grade 而不是 form？ → **senior grades**
8. 學校本身分 upper/lower school？ → **upper-school years**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`upper secondary years / senior secondary years / upper forms / senior years / final school years / final years of secondary school / senior grades / upper-school years`

尤其要記住：

> **senior secondary years** = 香港高中階段  
> **upper forms** = British/HK 高年級  
> **senior grades** = grade-based 高年級  
> **final school years** = 接近畢業的最後幾年

最重要的一點：

> **senior forms** 帶明顯 British/HK 教育制度色彩；對國際讀者，`upper secondary years` 或 `final years of secondary school` 往往更清楚。
