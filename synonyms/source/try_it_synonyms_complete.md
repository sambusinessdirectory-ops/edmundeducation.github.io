# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Try it

**「試一試」，究竟是哪一種「試」？**

隨便試試、正式嘗試完成、第一次親手做、勇敢挑戰、測試效果、實驗不同方法、試用產品、試吃／試飲、驗證性能——英文會按「嘗試的目的、難度、正式程度和對象」選用不同的說法。

> **核心原則**
>
> `try it` 是非常廣泛的說法。下面的表達不是全部都能在所有句子中直接互換；它們是在特定語境中把「試一試」說得更精準。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## Try it 的 14 種更精準說法

---

## 01 Have a go

**試一試；動手試做**

很常見的英式口語。通常表示「不妨自己試一下」，語氣輕鬆，未必暗示很大壓力。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> I've shown you how the machine works. Now have a go yourself.

我已經示範了機器怎樣操作，現在你自己試一試吧。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- have a go
- have a go at something
- have a go at doing something
- let me have a go

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **have a go = casually try doing something yourself**

---

## 02 Give it a try

**試試看；給它一次機會**

非常通用、自然，語氣比 `attempt` 輕鬆。常用於鼓勵別人不要先否定，而是先試一次。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The recipe looks difficult, but I'm going to give it a try.

這個食譜看起來很難，但我打算試試看。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- give it a try
- give something a try
- worth giving a try
- why not give it a try?

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **give it a try = try once and see what happens**

---

## 03 Attempt it

**嘗試完成；試圖做到**

比 `try it` 正式，通常暗示事情有一定難度、風險或不確定性，而且重點在「努力完成」。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The climb is extremely difficult, and only experienced hikers should attempt it.

這段攀登非常困難，只有有經驗的遠足者才應嘗試。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- attempt a task
- attempt the climb
- attempt to do something
- make an attempt

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **attempt = make a serious effort to accomplish something difficult**

---

## 04 Try your hand at it

**親自試做；初次嘗試某種技能或活動**

常用於第一次或不太熟悉的技能、工作、興趣或活動，帶有「看看自己做不做得來」的意思。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She had never made pottery before, but she decided to try her hand at it.

她以前從未做過陶藝，但決定親自試試。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- try your hand at painting
- try your hand at cooking
- try one's hand at writing
- try your hand at it

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **try your hand at = try an unfamiliar skill or activity yourself**

---

## 05 Take a shot at it

**試著挑戰一下；放手一試**

較口語，尤其常見於美式英語。通常表示結果不確定，但願意嘗試解決問題、回答問題或完成挑戰。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> I don't know whether I can fix the printer, but I'll take a shot at it.

我不知道能否修好打印機，但我會試試看。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- take a shot at it
- take a shot at solving something
- let me take a shot
- take your best shot

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **take a shot at = make an uncertain but willing attempt**

---

## 06 Have a crack at it

**試著做做看；挑戰一下**

非正式口語，尤其常見於英式、澳式英語。和 `have a go` 相近，但常帶一點「挑戰看看」的感覺。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> No one has solved the puzzle yet, so I'll have a crack at it.

還沒有人解開這道謎題，所以我來試試看。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- have a crack at it
- have a crack at the problem
- give someone a crack at it

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **have a crack at = informally attempt a challenge**

---

## 07 Give it a whirl

**試玩／試做一下；看看效果**

很口語，語氣輕鬆甚至帶點玩味，適合新工具、新活動、新方法。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> I've never used this editing app before, but I'll give it a whirl.

我以前沒用過這個剪輯程式，不過我來試玩一下。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- give it a whirl
- give the new app a whirl
- why not give it a whirl?

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **give it a whirl = try something new in a light, informal way**

---

## 08 Test it out

**測試一下；試用看看是否正常**

重點不是「能不能做到」，而是檢查產品、設備、功能、方法是否有效或是否符合預期。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> We installed the new software and tested it out before giving staff access.

我們安裝新軟件後先測試一下，才讓員工使用。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- test it out
- test out a feature
- test out the equipment
- test something before launch

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **test it out = try something in order to check how well it works**

---

## 09 Experiment with it

**實驗看看；嘗試不同做法**

表示不是只試一次，而是有意探索不同方法、設定、材料或變化，看會產生甚麼結果。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The designers experimented with several layouts before choosing the final version.

設計師試驗了數種版面配置，才選定最終版本。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- experiment with something
- experiment with different methods
- experiment with settings
- experiment with ingredients

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **experiment with = explore possibilities by trying different variations**

---

## 10 Trial it

**試行；試用；進行試驗**

較正式，常見於商業、醫療、教育、技術或政策語境，表示在正式採用之前先有限度測試。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The company will trial the new four-day schedule in two departments first.

公司會先在兩個部門試行新的四天工作制。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- trial a system
- trial a programme
- trial a product
- trial the new approach

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **trial = test something on a limited basis before wider adoption**

---

## 11 Sample it

**試吃；試飲；試用少量看看**

表示只接觸一小部分，以判斷味道、質素、風格或是否喜歡。特別常見於食物、飲品和可體驗內容。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Customers can sample the cheese before deciding whether to buy it.

顧客可以先試吃芝士，再決定是否購買。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- sample the food
- sample a wine
- sample a product
- free sample

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **sample = try a small amount in order to evaluate it**

---

## 12 Tackle it

**著手處理；迎難而上**

不只是「試試」，而是主動面對一個問題、任務或困難，並努力解決它。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The report is complicated, but let's tackle it one section at a time.

這份報告很複雜，但我們逐部分著手處理吧。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- tackle a problem
- tackle a task
- tackle the issue
- tackle it together

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **tackle = actively take on a difficult task or problem**

---

## 13 See if it works

**試試看是否有效**

直接說明嘗試的目的：不是單純體驗，而是看看某個方法、修正或方案能不能產生預期結果。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Restart the router and see if it works.

重新啟動路由器，看看是否有效。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- see if it works
- try this and see if it works
- let's see whether it works
- see if that fixes it

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **see if it works = try something specifically to check whether it solves the problem**

---

## 14 Put it to the test

**正式檢驗；實際考驗性能或能力**

強調把某個說法、產品、技能、方法或能力放到實際條件下驗證。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The team put the new emergency plan to the test during a full-scale exercise.

團隊在大型演習中實際檢驗新的緊急應變計劃。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- put it to the test
- put a theory to the test
- put a system to the test
- put someone's skills to the test

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **put to the test = subject something to a real or demanding evaluation**

# 02 False synonyms

有些說法和 `try it` 出現在相似情境中，但實際上描述的是不同動作或意圖。

---

## 01 Try / Practice

### 嘗試一次不等於反覆練習

`try` 可以只做一次；`practice` 表示反覆進行，以提升技能。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> I tried the piano once.  
> I practice the piano every day.

---

## 02 Attempt / Succeed

### 嘗試不等於成功

`attempt` 只表示努力去做，不保證完成。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> She attempted the climb but did not reach the top.

---

## 03 Test / Use

### 測試不等於正常使用

`test` 的目的在檢查表現；`use` 只是把東西拿來實際使用。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> We tested the new system before using it in daily operations.

---

## 04 Experiment / Guess

### 實驗不等於隨便猜

`experiment` 是有意改變條件並觀察結果；`guess` 是在資訊不足時作判斷。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> We experimented with different settings.  
> We guessed which setting might work.

---

## 05 Sample / Buy

### 試用少量不等於購買

`sample` 表示先體驗一小部分；之後可能買，也可能不買。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Customers can sample the drink before buying a bottle.

---

## 06 Tackle / Finish

### 著手處理不等於完成

`tackle` 表示主動面對並開始處理；不保證最後完成。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> We tackled the problem immediately, but it took several days to solve.

---

## 07 Have a go / Attempt

### 都是「試」，但語域不同

`have a go` 比較口語、輕鬆；`attempt` 較正式，而且常暗示任務較難。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Have a go at the puzzle.  
> Only trained climbers should attempt the route.

---

## 08 Trial / Experiment

### 試行不等於自由探索

`trial` 通常有正式、有限度的測試安排；`experiment with` 更強調探索不同變化和可能性。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The school trialled the programme for one term.  
> The teachers experimented with different lesson formats.

---

# 03 練習與解釋

> 每個目標表達兩題。請根據語境判斷「try it」究竟是哪一種嘗試。

---

## 練習 01.1

**Target phrase: Have a go · 試一試；動手試做**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> I've shown you how to use the drill. Now try it yourself.

**中文翻譯**

我已經示範了怎樣使用電鑽，現在你自己試一試。

| | 選項 |
|---|---|
| A | have a go |
| B | conclude it |
| C | sample it |
| D | suspend it |
| E | attempt |
| F | practice |

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**have a go · 試一試**

> I've shown you how to use the drill. Now have a go yourself.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. have a go ✓**  
說話者剛示範完，現在邀請對方親自動手試，語氣輕鬆自然。

**B. conclude it**  
`conclude` = 結束，意思相反。

**C. sample it**  
`sample` 通常指試吃、試飲或體驗少量，不適合操作電鑽。

**D. suspend it**  
`suspend` = 暫停，與嘗試無關。

**E. attempt**  
語法上需要具體受詞或 `attempt to...`；而且語氣比此情境正式、沉重。

**F. practice**  
`practice` 表示反覆練習，不只是首次試一下。

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

## Have a go vs Give it a try

**have a go** = 很口語，尤其常見於英式英語，重點在親自動手。  
**give it a try** = 更通用，重點在「不妨先試一次」。

> Let me have a go.  
> It may help, so give it a try.

---

## Give it a try vs Attempt it

**give it a try** = 輕鬆、中性，不一定困難。  
**attempt it** = 較正式，常暗示有難度、風險或不確定性。

> Give the recipe a try.  
> Only experienced climbers should attempt the route.

---

## Try your hand at it vs Have a go

**try your hand at** 特別強調嘗試某種技能或活動。  
**have a go** 更廣泛，可以只是任何動手試做。

> try your hand at photography  
> have a go at opening the jar

---

## Take a shot at it vs Have a crack at it

兩者都很口語，都有「我來挑戰看看」的意思。

**take a shot at it** 在美式英語很常見。  
**have a crack at it** 在英式、澳式英語更常見。

它們通常不是正式寫作的首選。

---

## Give it a whirl vs Test it out

**give it a whirl** = 輕鬆體驗新東西。  
**test it out** = 有意檢查功能或效果。

> Give the new app a whirl.  
> Test the new app out for bugs.

---

## Test it out vs Put it to the test

**test it out** = 一般測試看看。  
**put it to the test** = 更強調在真實、嚴格或有要求的條件下驗證。

> We tested the microphone out.  
> We put the emergency system to the test during the drill.

---

## Experiment with it vs Trial it

**experiment with** = 探索不同變化，結果可能很開放。  
**trial** = 有較正式的測試計劃、範圍或期間。

> experiment with different layouts  
> trial a new timetable for one semester

---

## Sample it vs Give it a try

**sample** = 體驗一小部分，常見於食物、飲品、產品內容。  
**give it a try** = 更廣泛，可用於幾乎任何一般嘗試。

> sample the wine  
> give yoga a try

---

## Tackle it vs Attempt it

**attempt** = 努力嘗試完成。  
**tackle** = 主動著手處理問題或任務。

> attempt the climb  
> tackle the backlog

`attempt` 更關注「能不能完成」；`tackle` 更關注「開始積極處理」。

---

## See if it works vs Test it out

**see if it works** 常用於解決問題：試一個方法，看能不能解決。  
**test it out** 常用於產品、設備、功能或方案本身的測試。

> Restart it and see if it works.  
> Test the new feature out before launch.

# 07 Word-family & grammar traps

| 形式 | 用法 | 常見錯誤 |
|---|---|---|
| try | 動詞／名詞 | `make a try` 通常不如 `give it a try` 自然 |
| attempt | 名詞／動詞 | `attempt doing` 有時可見，但 `attempt to do` 最常見 |
| trial | 名詞／動詞 | `trial` 作動詞較正式 |
| test | 名詞／動詞 | `test out` 常見於產品／功能 |
| experiment | 名詞／動詞 | 動詞常接 `with` |
| sample | 名詞／動詞 | 常指一小部分，而非完整使用 |
| tackle | 動詞 | 通常直接接 problem/task/issue |
| practice | 名詞／動詞 | 英式拼字：名詞 `practice`，動詞 `practise`；美式兩者多用 `practice` |

### Important structures

- **have a go at + noun / -ing**
- **give + noun + a try**
- **attempt + noun**
- **attempt to + verb**
- **try one's hand at + noun / -ing**
- **take a shot at + noun / -ing**
- **have a crack at + noun / -ing**
- **experiment with + noun**
- **trial + noun**
- **sample + noun**
- **tackle + noun**
- **put + noun + to the test**

# 08 核心決策問題

當你想把 `try it` 換成更精準的說法時，先問：

1. **只是輕鬆地自己試一下嗎？** → `have a go`
2. **只是給它一次機會看看嗎？** → `give it a try`
3. **事情有難度，需要認真努力完成嗎？** → `attempt it`
4. **是在第一次試某種技能或活動嗎？** → `try your hand at it`
5. **不確定成功，但想挑戰看看嗎？** → `take a shot at it`
6. **是在非正式語境中挑戰難題嗎？** → `have a crack at it`
7. **只是輕鬆試玩一個新東西嗎？** → `give it a whirl`
8. **目的是檢查功能或性能嗎？** → `test it out`
9. **是在嘗試不同變化、方法或設定嗎？** → `experiment with it`
10. **是在正式採用之前有限度試行嗎？** → `trial it`
11. **只先體驗／品嚐一小部分嗎？** → `sample it`
12. **是在主動著手處理一個難題嗎？** → `tackle it`
13. **是在試一個方法，看能不能解決問題嗎？** → `see if it works`
14. **是在真實或嚴格條件下正式驗證嗎？** → `put it to the test`

# 09 Final note for system use

不要把以下表達當成可以機械替換 `try it` 的完全同義詞：

`have a go / give it a try / attempt it / try your hand at it / take a shot at it / have a crack at it / give it a whirl / test it out / experiment with it / trial it / sample it / tackle it / see if it works / put it to the test`

它們的共同點只是：

> **都可以在某些語境中表達一種更具體的「嘗試、試用、挑戰、測試或驗證」行為。**

真正的選詞依據是：

> **What exactly are you trying to do — experience it, accomplish it, explore it, or test it?**
