# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# For the first time

**第一次、首次、起初、首度嘗試、首次到訪、首次亮相——究竟是哪一種 for the first time？**

本課聚焦：

**for the first time = on the earliest occasion that something happens or someone experiences, does, sees, visits, or achieves something**

核心近義與近義表達：

**initially; on the initial occasion; on the first occasion; for the first time ever; for the very first time; at first; at the outset; on one's first attempt; on one's first visit; on debut; first-ever**

> **Precision rule:**  
> `for the first time ever / for the very first time` = 真正首次，強調更強。  
> `on the first/initial occasion` = 第一次事件／場合。  
> `on one's first attempt / visit` = 專門情境。  
> `on debut` = 首演／首秀／首次上陣。  
> `first-ever` = 形容詞，需要名詞改寫。  
> `initially / at first / at the outset` 核心是『起初』，不一定表示此前從未發生。

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Initially

**最初；起初**

當 `for the first time` 實際表示某件事在開始階段首次發生／呈現時，`initially` 可作 contextual near-synonym。不過 `initially` 核心是『起初』，不一定明確表示以前從未發生過。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The software was initially used in a small pilot project.

這款軟件最初在一個小型試驗項目中使用。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- initially planned
- initially used
- initially offered
- initially introduced

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡真正要表達的是此前從未發生、開始階段、第一次嘗試／到訪，還是首次公開亮相？

## 02 On the initial occasion

**在最初一次；在第一次場合**

正式、描述性的 paraphrase，明確表示某事在最初那一次發生。較少見於日常口語，但在報告、程序或比較多次事件時很清楚。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The machine failed on the initial occasion it was tested.

機器在最初一次測試時失靈。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- on the initial occasion
- initial occasion of testing
- initial occasion on which
- on that initial occasion

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡真正要表達的是此前從未發生、開始階段、第一次嘗試／到訪，還是首次公開亮相？

## 03 On the first occasion

# 02 False synonyms

以下表達都和『開始／第一次』有關，但很多只表示起初、以前、一次、最新或終於，不能直接當作 **for the first time**。

## 01 Initially

**起初不一定等於第一次**

`initially` 說的是開始階段；某件事可以 initially 發生某種情況，但此前同類事情可能已發生過。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Initially, the service was free does not mean this was the first service of its kind.

---

## 02 At first

**起初通常暗示後來改變**

`at first` 常建立『起初……後來……』對比，不一定表示人生／歷史首次。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

At first I disliked it, but later I changed my mind.

---

## 03 First

**詞性／結構不同**

`first` 可作序數詞、副詞、形容詞，但不能在所有位置直接替代 `for the first time`。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She first visited Paris in 2020 = She visited Paris for the first time in 2020.

---

## 04 Originally

**原先不等於首次**

`originally` 表示最初的狀態、計劃或來源，重點是後來有改變。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The building was originally a school; this does not describe a first-time experience.

---

## 05 Once

**一次不等於第一次**

`once` 可表示『一次』或『曾經』，不一定是第一次。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

I visited once last year does not mean it was my first visit.

---

## 06 First-ever

**形容詞，不是自由副詞替代**

`first-ever` 通常修飾名詞：first-ever win / visit / event。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

They won for the first time = They achieved their first-ever win.

---

## 07 Newly

**新近不等於首次**

`newly` 表示最近變成／剛剛完成，例如 newly opened。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A newly opened shop is new, but opening it is not the same grammatical meaning as for the first time.

---

## 08 At last

**終於不等於第一次**

`at last` 表示經過等待／延誤後終於發生，重點不是它是否首次。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

At last, the train arrived; it may have arrived many times before.

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 For the first time
最自然基本結構：

- do something for the first time
- see something for the first time
- visit somewhere for the first time
- for the first time in years
- for the first time since...

## 02 Initially is not a strict synonym
`initially` = at the beginning.

Example:
`The service was initially free.`

這只表示開始時免費，不表示世界／公司從未有過類似服務。

## 03 At first
`at first` 通常有：
**initial state → later change**

`At first I disliked the course, but later I enjoyed it.`

因此不要把所有 first-time experiences 都改成 `at first`。

## 04 On the first occasion
較正式，適合重複事件比較：

`On the first occasion, the machine failed; on the second, it worked.`

## 05 First-ever
是形容詞：

- first-ever win
- first-ever overseas trip
- first-ever event

不能直接：
`She travelled first-ever.` ✗

## 06 On debut
常見於：
- sport
- theatre
- music
- product launches

例如：
`He scored on debut.`

## 07 First attempt
自然：
- succeed on the first attempt
- pass on her first attempt

比 general `for the first time` 更精確地表示 attempt。

## 08 For the first time vs once
`once` = 一次／曾經。  
它不保證是第一次。
