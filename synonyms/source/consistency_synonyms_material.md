# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Consistency

**規律性、穩定性、均一性、恆常性、可靠性、均勻性、連續性、邏輯一致——究竟是哪一種 consistency？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**regularity; steadiness; uniformity; stability; constancy; reliability; dependability; evenness; continuity; coherence**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **10 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **regularity; steadiness; uniformity** 全部保留。  
> 本課特別區分『固定頻率』『不大幅波動』『不同部分相同』『狀態穩定』『長期不變』『結果可靠』『值得依靠』『物理均勻』『不中斷』『邏輯前後一致』。

---

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Regularity

**規律性；穩定出現**

當 `consistency` 指某件事以相近頻率、固定節奏或持續模式出現時最自然。常見於 attendance, practice, exercise, output, routines。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The regularity of her training attendance has improved her fitness.

她規律參與訓練，令體能有所提升。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- regularity of attendance

- regularity of practice

- with regularity

- regularity of output


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 consistency 是固定頻率、不波動、跨樣本一致、狀態穩定、長期不變、可靠、值得依靠、物理均勻、不中斷，還是邏輯前後一致？

---

## 02 Steadiness

**穩定性；平穩**

強調表現、速度、進度、手勢或情緒沒有大幅波動。比 `regularity` 更關注『不忽高忽低』。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The steadiness of the runner's pace helped her finish strongly.

跑手保持穩定步速，幫助她有力完成比賽。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- steadiness of pace

- steadiness under pressure

- hand steadiness

- steady performance

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The factory checks each batch for uniformity in colour and size.

工廠檢查每批產品的顏色和尺寸是否一致。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Regularity

**規律性不等於所有 consistency**

regularity 偏頻率與重複模式；結果每次相同則更接近 uniformity/reliability。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Regular practice is about frequency; uniform results are about sameness.

---

## 02 Steadiness

**強調不波動**

steadiness 適合 pace, hand movement, performance, mood；不一定表示每週固定出現。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Her pace was steady even though she trained irregularly.

---

## 03 Uniformity

**強調多個部分相同**

uniformity 常用於 batches, samples, colours, sizes, standards。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The factory checks uniformity across batches.

---

## 04 Reliability

**可靠性包含結果可依賴**

reliability 不只是看起來一致，而是多次使用時結果可信。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A reliable instrument gives dependable repeated results.

---

## 05 Continuity

**連續不等於一致**

一件事可以不中斷地進行，但質素仍可有很大波動。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Continuity of care means care continues; it does not guarantee uniform quality.

---

## 06 Coherence

**只適合邏輯／內容一致**

coherence 用於 reasoning, argument, policy, text；不能一般替代 performance consistency。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A coherent argument fits together logically.

---

## 07 Consistent / consistency

**詞性陷阱**

`consistent` 是形容詞；`consistency` 是名詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Her performance is consistent; her consistency is impressive.

---

## 08 Consistency

**多義詞**

consistency 也可指食物／液體的稀稠度，例如 sauce consistency；本課不混入該 sense。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Add water until the sauce reaches the right consistency.

# 03 練習與解釋

> **每個核心近義表達 3 題，共 30 題。**
> 每題均用句中可觀察的線索區分頻率、波動、均一、可靠性、連續性與邏輯一致性；六個選項均逐項解釋。


## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** regularity · 規律性；穩定出現

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

Her consistency in attending training has improved her fitness.

### 中文翻譯

她規律參與訓練，令體能有所提升。

### OPTIONS


- **A. regularity**

- **B. steadiness**

- **C. continuity**

- **D. uniformity**

- **E. irregularity**

- **F. randomness**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**regularity · 規律性；穩定出現**

The **regularity** of her training attendance has improved her fitness.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. regularity ✓** — `regularity` = 按固定頻率或模式重複出現。 **本句為何最合適：** attendance happens repeatedly on a dependable schedule。

- **B. steadiness** — `steadiness` = 保持平穩，不大幅波動。 重點是沒有大幅波動；若句子明確是每週／每日重複，`regularity` 更自然。 **本句線索：** attendance happens repeatedly on a dependable schedule。

- **C. continuity** — `continuity` = 持續不中斷、前後銜接。 重點是不中斷、前後銜接；即使不中斷，結果仍可能不 uniform。 **本句線索：** attendance happens repeatedly on a dependable schedule。

- **D. uniformity** — `uniformity` = 不同部分／單位彼此一致。 重點是不同樣本、批次或部分彼此相同；它不是在說某一表現長時間不波動。 **本句線索：** attendance happens repeatedly on a dependable schedule。

- **E. irregularity** — `irregularity` = 不規律。 和 regularity 相反。 **本句線索：** attendance happens repeatedly on a dependable schedule。

- **F. randomness** — `randomness` = 隨機性。 表示沒有固定模式，和 regularity 相反。 **本句線索：** attendance happens repeatedly on a dependable schedule。

| **dependability** | 人／服務／供應商值得依靠 |
| **evenness** | 顏色、厚度、表面、分布均勻 |
| **continuity** | 持續不中斷、前後銜接 |
| **coherence** | 想法／論證／政策內部一致連貫 |

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Consistency vs Regularity
`regularity` 問的是「多久一次、是否有固定模式」。

## Consistency vs Steadiness
`steadiness` 問的是「有沒有大幅波動」。

## Consistency vs Uniformity
`uniformity` 問的是「不同樣本／部分是否相同」。

## Consistency vs Stability
`stability` 問的是「整體狀態會不會突然改變」。

## Consistency vs Reliability
`reliability` 問的是「多次使用後結果是否仍可信」。

## Consistency vs Dependability
`dependability` 特別自然於人、供應商與服務能否一貫履行責任。

## Consistency vs Evenness
`evenness` 適合可觀察的物理均勻：
- colour
- coating
- thickness
- distribution

## Consistency vs Continuity
`continuity` 是不中斷；不中斷不代表每次質素完全相同。

## Consistency vs Coherence
`coherence` 只處理內容／邏輯上的前後一致。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Consistent vs consistency
- consistent = adjective
- consistency = noun

例如：
- Her performance is consistent.
- Her consistency is impressive.

## 02 Consistency in
- consistency in performance
- consistency in quality
- consistency in applying rules

## 03 Consistency across
- consistency across branches
- consistency across batches
- consistency across departments

## 04 Consistency of
- consistency of colour
- consistency of results
- consistency of service

## 05 Another important sense: texture
`consistency` 也可表示食物／液體的稀稠度或質感：
- a thick consistency
- a smooth consistency
- sauce consistency

本課不把這個 sense 混入核心近義詞。
