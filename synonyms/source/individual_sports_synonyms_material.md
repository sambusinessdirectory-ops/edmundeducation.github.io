# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Individual sports

**個人運動、單人競技、個人項目、非團隊運動——究竟是哪一種 individual sports？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**solo sports; individual events; individual disciplines; solo athletic activities; single-player sports; individual competition; solo competition; non-team sports; self-paced sports; individual athletic pursuits; one-person events**

> **Precision rule:**  
> `solo sports` = 最直接。  
> `individual events / disciplines` = 賽事／分類用語。  
> `individual competition / solo competition` = 強調競賽方式。  
> `non-team sports` = 與 team sports 對比。  
> `self-paced sports` = 只適用部分個人運動。  
> `individual athletic pursuits` = 正式、書面且較廣。

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Solo sports

**個人運動；單人運動**

最直接自然的近義表達，強調主要由一個人參與、比賽或完成。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Solo sports

**非常接近但不涵蓋所有語境**

某些 individual sports 也可能有雙打、接力或團體賽形式。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Tennis can be an individual sport, but doubles tennis is not purely solo.

---

## 02 Individual events

**項目不等於整類運動**

常指某項比賽中的個人項目，而 individual sports 可指整種運動。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The 100 metres is an individual event; athletics contains many events.

---

## 03 Self-paced sports

**只對部分個人運動**

拳擊、劍擊等個人運動並不是 self-paced。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Jogging may be self-paced; fencing is individual but opponent-paced.

---

## 04 Non-team sports

**只是一種對比式定義**

它說明『不是團隊運動』，但不直接描述競技形式。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The label is useful mainly when contrasting with team sports.

---

## 05 One-player games

**game 不等於 sport**

容易指電子遊戲、桌遊等。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A single-player video game is not an individual sport.

---

## 06 Individual exercise

**運動鍛鍊不等於競技體育**

exercise 強調健身，不一定有 sport 的規則或比賽。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Stretching alone is exercise, not necessarily a sport.

---

## 07 Personal training

**私人訓練不等於個人運動**

personal training 是一對一健身指導服務。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Personal training may prepare someone for an individual sport.

---

## 08 Independent activity

**獨立活動範圍太廣**

可指任何一個人完成的活動，不限體育。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Reading alone is an independent activity, not an individual sport.

---

- **F. spectator sports**

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Individual sports vs team sports
- individual sports = 個人為主要參賽單位
- team sports = 團隊共同參賽

## 02 Sport vs event
- sport = 整種運動，例如 athletics / swimming / tennis
- event = 比賽中的單一項目，例如 100 metres / singles final

## 03 Individual disciplines
常見於 Olympics / championships / multi-sport events / formal sport classification。

## 04 Solo sports
最自然日常替代之一，但某些 sport 也可有 doubles / relay / team formats，所以要看賽制。

## 05 Self-paced
適合 jogging / cycling / swimming for fitness；不適合 boxing / fencing / squash，因節奏高度受對手影響。

## 06 Single-player
在 sports 中可理解，但比 `solo sports` / `individual sports` 固定度低；在科技語境也常使人想到 single-player video games。
