# GET RID OF · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Get rid of

**「去掉／擺脫／丟掉／取消」，要先分清是在移除物件、徹底消除問題、廢除制度、處理廢物，還是逐步淘汰某種做法。**

本單元集中處理 **get rid of = to remove, discard, eliminate, abolish, dispose of, or free oneself from something unwanted**。你提供的 **remove; eliminate; do away with** 分別代表 **一般移除、徹底消除、取消制度／做法** 三個核心方向。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且高實用度的詞／表達，並按最新 inspection 標準區分 **discard / dispose of / clear out / throw away / abolish / phase out / root out / eradicate / purge / rid oneself of**。每個目標設 **3 道題**；尤其會分清『丟掉東西』和『消除抽象問題／制度』兩大類。

## Tier 1 — Core remove / eliminate / disposal equivalents · 核心移除、消除與處理近義詞

### 01 Remove

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：移除；去除；拿走  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to take something away from a place, position, system, or situation  
**用法提示**：和 get rid of 最直接、最通用的正式近義詞之一，適合物件、污漬、內容、障礙、限制等。  
**常見搭配**：remove a stain; remove an obstacle; remove content; remove a restriction; remove from.  
**常見搭配對象**：object, stain, barrier, file, restriction.  
**與 get rid of 的辨析**：Remove 通常只是『拿走／去除』；get rid of 常帶『不再想要、想擺脫』的語氣。  
**使用限制**：若強調完全消滅問題或風險，eliminate / eradicate 更強。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Please remove any unnecessary files before submitting the folder.  
提交資料夾前，請移除任何不必要的檔案。

### 02 Eliminate

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：消除；排除  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to completely remove something unwanted, unnecessary, or harmful  
**用法提示**：當 get rid of 強調『徹底消除問題、風險、錯誤或可能性』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：eliminate risk; eliminate errors; eliminate waste; eliminate a problem; eliminate the need for.  
**常見搭配對象**：risk, error, waste, problem, possibility.  
**與 get rid of 的辨析**：Eliminate 比 remove 更強，常暗示徹底消除。  
**使用限制**：日常丟掉一件舊衣服通常不用 eliminate。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The new process is designed to eliminate unnecessary delays.  
新流程旨在消除不必要的延誤。

### 03 Do away with

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：廢除；取消；擺脫  
**語域**：neutral / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：to abolish, remove, or stop using something considered unnecessary or undesirable  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很自然，尤其用於規則、制度、做法、限制、傳統等。  
**常見搭配**：do away with a rule; do away with paperwork; do away with restrictions; do away with the old system; do away with unnecessary steps.  
**常見搭配對象**：rule, system, practice, restriction, procedure.  
**與 get rid of 的辨析**：Do away with 很常用於制度／做法；get rid of 更廣，也可用於具體物件。  
**使用限制**：正式法律文本通常用 abolish / repeal / eliminate。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The company plans to do away with unnecessary paperwork.  
公司計劃取消不必要的文書程序。

### 04 Discard

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：丟棄；棄置  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to throw away or stop keeping something because it is no longer useful or wanted  
**用法提示**：當 get rid of 指把不要的物件、材料、想法或版本丟掉時很合適。  
**常見搭配**：discard waste; discard packaging; discard an old version; discard an idea; safely discard.  
**常見搭配對象**：waste, packaging, document, version, item.  
**與 get rid of 的辨析**：Discard 強調『不再保留』；get rid of 還可表示擺脫問題或人。  
**使用限制**：不適合表示正式廢除政策。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Discard any damaged packaging before use.  
使用前請丟棄任何受損包裝。

### 05 Dispose of

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：處理掉；棄置；丟掉  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：to get rid of something, especially waste or unwanted material, in an appropriate way  
**用法提示**：處理垃圾、舊設備、化學品、文件等時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：dispose of waste; dispose of equipment; safely dispose of; dispose of rubbish; dispose of documents.  
**常見搭配對象**：waste, rubbish, equipment, chemical, document.  
**與 get rid of 的辨析**：Dispose of 常強調妥善處置；get rid of 比較口語和泛用。  
**使用限制**：注意 dispose of 不能省略 of。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Old batteries must be disposed of safely.  
舊電池必須安全處理。

## Tier 2 — Clearing / abolition / phase-out variants · 清走、廢除與逐步淘汰變體

### 06 Clear out

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：清走；清空；大掃除式清理  
**語域**：informal / common  
**核心語義**：to remove unwanted things from a room, cupboard, building, or other space  
**用法提示**：當 get rid of 指把某個地方的大量雜物清走時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：clear out a cupboard; clear out a room; clear out old clothes; clear out clutter; have a clear-out.  
**常見搭配對象**：cupboard, room, garage, wardrobe, clutter.  
**與 get rid of 的辨析**：Clear out 強調清理空間；get rid of 可以只處理一件東西。  
**使用限制**：抽象問題通常不用 clear out。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
We spent the weekend clearing out the garage.  
我們週末把車房裏的雜物清走。

### 07 Throw away

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：扔掉；丟掉  
**語域**：informal / very common  
**核心語義**：to put something in the rubbish because it is no longer wanted or useful  
**用法提示**：最常見的日常物件型近義詞之一。  
**常見搭配**：throw away rubbish; throw away food; throw away old clothes; throw it away; throw away packaging.  
**常見搭配對象**：rubbish, food, clothes, packaging, paper.  
**與 get rid of 的辨析**：Throw away 很具體、口語；get rid of 可更廣，未必真的是扔進垃圾。  
**使用限制**：制度、問題、風險不應直接用 throw away。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Don't throw away the receipt until the return period ends.  
退貨期完結前不要把收據丟掉。

### 08 Abolish

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：廢除；取消  
**語域**：formal / institutional  
**核心語義**：to officially end a law, system, practice, or institution  
**用法提示**：當 get rid of 指正式廢除規則、制度、稅項、限制時最精準。  
**常見搭配**：abolish a law; abolish a tax; abolish a system; abolish restrictions; formally abolish.  
**常見搭配對象**：law, tax, rule, institution, system.  
**與 get rid of 的辨析**：Abolish 帶官方、制度性終止；get rid of 更口語和更廣。  
**使用限制**：不能用來表示丟掉普通物件。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The proposal would abolish the outdated requirement.  
該建議會廢除這項過時要求。

### 09 Phase out

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：逐步淘汰；逐步取消  
**語域**：neutral / policy / business  
**核心語義**：to gradually stop using, producing, or providing something  
**用法提示**：當 get rid of 不是立即，而是逐步淘汰產品、技術、制度或做法時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：phase out a product; phase out fossil fuels; phase out old equipment; gradually phase out; phase-out plan.  
**常見搭配對象**：technology, product, system, fuel, equipment.  
**與 get rid of 的辨析**：Phase out 強調漸進；get rid of 不指定速度。  
**使用限制**：立即丟掉某物不宜用 phase out。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The company will phase out the old software over the next year.  
公司會在未來一年逐步淘汰舊軟件。

### 10 Root out

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：根除；徹底清除  
**語域**：neutral / emphatic  
**核心語義**：to find and completely remove something harmful, hidden, or deeply established  
**用法提示**：當 get rid of 指根除腐敗、欺詐、偏見、隱患或長期問題時很有力。  
**常見搭配**：root out corruption; root out fraud; root out bias; root out bad practice; root out the cause.  
**常見搭配對象**：corruption, fraud, bias, abuse, bad practice.  
**與 get rid of 的辨析**：Root out 比 eliminate 更帶『連根拔起／找出並清除』的語感。  
**使用限制**：普通物件不用 root out。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The organisation introduced controls to root out fraud.  
機構引入監控措施以根除欺詐。

## Tier 3 — Eradication / purge / formal reflexive variants · 根除、徹底清除與正式反身結構

### 11 Eradicate

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：根除；消滅  
**語域**：formal / strong  
**核心語義**：to destroy or remove something completely, especially a disease, pest, harmful practice, or serious problem  
**用法提示**：當 get rid of 的對象是疾病、害蟲、貧困、腐敗等，而且強調完全消除時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：eradicate disease; eradicate poverty; eradicate corruption; eradicate pests; eradicate infection.  
**常見搭配對象**：disease, poverty, corruption, pest, infection.  
**與 get rid of 的辨析**：Eradicate 是非常強的正式詞，比 get rid of 更絕對。  
**使用限制**：不宜用於小事或日常雜物。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The programme aims to eradicate the disease in the region.  
該計劃旨在根除區內這種疾病。

### 12 Purge

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：清除；肅清；清洗  
**語域**：formal / strong  
**核心語義**：to remove people, elements, records, or material considered unwanted or harmful, often thoroughly  
**用法提示**：可用於系統資料、檔案、組織成員或有害內容，但語氣較強。  
**常見搭配**：purge data; purge records; purge the system; purge corruption; purge unwanted files.  
**常見搭配對象**：data, record, system, organisation, file.  
**與 get rid of 的辨析**：Purge 強調大量、徹底清除；有時帶政治／組織清洗的強烈聯想。  
**使用限制**：描述普通家居清理時通常太強。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The maintenance tool can purge outdated files from the system.  
維護工具可以清除系統中的過時檔案。

### 13 Rid oneself of

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：擺脫；使自己脫離  
**語域**：formal / literary  
**核心語義**：to free oneself from something unwanted, troublesome, or burdensome  
**用法提示**：是 get rid of 的較正式、書面改寫，尤其適合 habit、fear、burden、dependency。  
**常見搭配**：rid oneself of a habit; rid oneself of fear; rid oneself of debt; rid oneself of dependence; rid the body of.  
**常見搭配對象**：habit, fear, burden, debt, dependency.  
**與 get rid of 的辨析**：語義非常接近 get rid of，但結構更正式：rid oneself of。  
**使用限制**：日常口語通常直接說 get rid of。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He was determined to rid himself of the habit.  
他決心擺脫這個習慣。

## 04 False synonyms / get-rid-of traps · 容易誤選的詞與「去掉」陷阱

### 01 Delete

**刪除不等於所有 get rid of**  
delete 主要指文字、檔案、數據等從系統移除。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Delete is mainly for digital or written content.

### 02 Clean

**清潔不等於 get rid of**  
clean 是清潔；可以去除污垢，但不一定把東西本身處理掉。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Cleaning may remove dirt, not necessarily the object itself.

### 03 Destroy

**摧毀不等於 get rid of**  
destroy 強調破壞；get rid of 不要求把對象毀掉。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
You can get rid of something without destroying it.

### 04 Lose

**失去不等於 get rid of**  
lose 常是非自願失去；get rid of 通常帶主動想移除。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Losing something is often accidental; getting rid of it is deliberate.

### 05 Avoid

**避免不等於 get rid of**  
avoid 是不接觸／避開；問題仍可能存在。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Avoiding something does not remove it.

### 06 Replace

**替換不等於 get rid of**  
replace 是用另一樣東西取代；可能包含移走舊物，但核心是替代。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Replacement focuses on substitution.

### 07 Eliminate

**eliminate 通常比 get rid of 更徹底**  
eliminate 常表示完全消除問題、風險或可能性。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Eliminate is stronger and more complete.

### 08 Rid / get rid of

**結構陷阱**  
可說 get rid of something 或 rid oneself of something；不能說 *rid of something* 單獨作一般動詞結構。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Use get rid of, or rid oneself of.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Remove · 移除；去除；拿走

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

Please get rid of any unnecessary files before submitting the folder.

**中文翻譯**  
提交資料夾前，請移除任何不必要的檔案。

- A. remove
- B. avoid
- C. replace
- D. lose
- E. destroy
- F. clean

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**remove** · 移除；去除；拿走

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
Please remove any unnecessary files before submitting the folder.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. remove ✓ — files are simply being taken out of the folder, so remove is the neutral precise verb。**
- **B. avoid — avoid means not interacting with the files, not deleting or taking them away。**
- **C. replace — replace would require substitute files。**
- **D. lose — lose suggests accidental loss。**
- **E. destroy — destroy is much stronger than necessary。**
- **F. clean — clean the folder does not specifically mean taking files out.**

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Get rid of 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 一般移除 | **remove** | 是否只是 take something away？ |
| 徹底消除問題 | **eliminate** | 是否是 risk / error / delay / need？ |
| 取消制度／做法 | **do away with** | 是否是 rule / paperwork / unnecessary steps？ |
| 丟棄不用物 | **discard** | 是否 no longer wanted / useful？ |
| 妥善處理廢物 | **dispose of** | 是否是 waste / battery / confidential material？ |
| 清空空間 | **clear out** | 是否是 room / wardrobe / garage full of clutter？ |
| 日常扔掉 | **throw away** | 是否是 box / food / receipt？ |
| 正式廢除 | **abolish** | 是否 law / tax / requirement / institution？ |
| 逐步淘汰 | **phase out** | 是否 gradually / over time / in stages？ |
| 根除深層問題 | **root out** | 是否 fraud / bias / corruption / bad practice？ |
| 徹底根除 | **eradicate** | 是否 disease / pest / serious systemic problem？ |
| 大量徹底清除 | **purge** | 是否 system data / records / files？ |
| 正式：使自己擺脫 | **rid oneself of** | 是否 habit / fear / dependence / burden？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **get rid of vs remove**: remove 最中性；get rid of 更常帶『不想要』的態度。
- **get rid of vs eliminate**: eliminate 更強，常表示徹底消除。
- **get rid of vs do away with**: do away with 特別常用於制度、規則和做法。
- **get rid of vs discard**: discard 偏不再保留物件／想法。
- **get rid of vs dispose of**: dispose of 強調妥善處理、棄置。
- **get rid of vs throw away**: throw away 最口語、最物件化。
- **get rid of vs abolish**: abolish 是正式制度性廢除。
- **get rid of vs phase out**: phase out 是逐步淘汰，不是立即停止。
- **get rid of vs root out**: root out 帶找出並根除深層問題的意味。
- **get rid of vs eradicate**: eradicate 是最強烈的完全根除之一。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & get-rid-of traps · 文法、搭配與結構陷阱

- 固定片語是 **get rid of + noun**：get rid of clutter / an old habit / unnecessary rules。
- 不要說 **get rid something**；一定要有 **of**。
- **remove + object + from + place**：remove the file from the folder。
- **eliminate + object**：eliminate errors / eliminate the need for...
- **do away with + noun**：do away with a rule / old practice。
- **dispose of + noun**：dispose of waste；不能省略 of。
- **clear out + place**：clear out the garage；亦可說 clear out old clothes。
- **throw away + object**：throw away the box。
- **abolish + law/system/tax**：正式制度性搭配。
- **phase out + object**：phase out old equipment / fossil fuels。
- **root out + harmful practice**：root out fraud / corruption / bias。
- **eradicate + serious problem/disease**：eradicate disease / poverty。
- **rid oneself of + noun**：rid oneself of a habit；較正式書面。
- **delete / clean / destroy / lose / avoid / replace ≠ get rid of**：它們只在特定語境部分重疊。
