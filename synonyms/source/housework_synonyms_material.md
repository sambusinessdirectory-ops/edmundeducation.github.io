# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Housework

**家務、家庭雜務、家務職責、家居維護——究竟是哪一種 housework？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**domestic work; household chores; domestic chores; household tasks; home duties; domestic duties; household work; home maintenance; cleaning and tidying; domestic responsibilities; chores around the house**

> **Precision rule:**  
> `domestic work` = 最正式廣義。  
> `household chores / domestic chores` = 一項項日常雜務。  
> `household tasks` = 中性任務。  
> `home/domestic duties` = 強調責任。  
> `home maintenance` = 維護／保養更強。  
> `cleaning and tidying` = 只是一個子類。  
> `domestic responsibilities` = 比 housework 更廣，可含照顧責任。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Domestic work

**家務；家庭勞務**

最直接、正式的近義詞之一，概括清潔、整理、洗衣、煮食等家務。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

She spends two hours a day doing domestic work.

她每天花兩小時做家務。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- do domestic work
- unpaid domestic work
- share domestic work
- domestic workload

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是一般家務、日常雜務、家庭責任、清潔整理，還是家居維護？

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Homework

**功課不等於家務**

`homework` 是學校／課程指定的學習任務；`housework` 是家庭清潔、煮食、洗衣等。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Children may finish their homework before helping with housework.

---

## 02 Errands

**跑腿不等於 housework**

`errands` 常指外出購物、寄件、取物等辦事。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Buying groceries is an errand; washing dishes is housework.

---

## 03 Cleaning

**清潔只是 housework 的一部分**

housework 還包括洗衣、煮食、整理、倒垃圾等。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Cleaning is one type of housework.

---

## 04 Laundry

**洗衣只是其中一類**

`laundry` 不能代表全部家務。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Laundry is part of household chores.

---

## 05 Cooking

**煮食只是其中一類**

cooking 可屬 housework，但範圍更窄。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Cooking dinner may be one household chore.

---

## 06 Maintenance

**maintenance 可超出一般家務**

可包括修理、保養、檢查，通常比日常 housework 更技術。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Fixing a leaking tap is home maintenance.

---

## 07 Domestic responsibilities

**比 housework 更廣**

可包括 childcare、elder care、家庭管理等。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Housework is one part of domestic responsibilities.

---

## 08 Paid domestic work

**有薪家務勞動不是普通 household activity**

加入 paid 後通常指家務工作者提供的有薪勞務。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Paid domestic work is employment.

- **D. cleaning and tidying** — `cleaning and tidying` = 只涵蓋 housework 的清潔整理子類。 它和 `domestic work` 在是否屬家務、是否只是子類、是否偏責任或維護方面不同。

- **E. domestic responsibilities** — `domestic responsibilities` = 比 housework 更廣，可包含照顧與家庭管理。 它和 `domestic work` 在是否屬家務、是否只是子類、是否偏責任或維護方面不同。

- **F. office work** — `office work` = 辦公室工作，不是家務。 它和 `domestic work` 在是否屬家務、是否只是子類、是否偏責任或維護方面不同。

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Housework vs Domestic work
**domestic work** 最正式、最廣。

## Housework vs Household chores
**household chores** 最自然地表示一項項日常家務。

## Housework vs Household tasks
適合任務清單、分工、家庭管理。

## Housework vs Home / Domestic duties
兩者把重點放在「責任」而不是單純工作內容。

## Housework vs Home maintenance
後者更偏保養、維護和小修理。

## Housework vs Cleaning and tidying
後者只是一個子類，不能涵蓋所有 housework。

## Housework vs Domestic responsibilities
後者更廣，可包括 childcare / elder care / family management。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Housework is usually uncountable
自然：
- do housework
- a lot of housework
- too much housework

不自然：
- a housework ✗
- houseworks ✗

若要數：
- a household chore
- three household tasks

## 02 Housework vs homework
- housework = 家務
- homework = 功課

## 03 Chore is countable
- a chore
- household chores
- daily chores

## 04 Domestic responsibilities is broader
可包括：
- childcare
- elder care
- household management
- family scheduling

## 05 Home maintenance
只有當語境包括 upkeep / repairs / checking equipment / property care 時，才比一般 housework 更精確。
