# Dishonest

**不誠實、欺騙、不可信、誤導、欺詐、不擇手段——究竟是哪一種 dishonest？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**deceitful; untrustworthy; deceptive; fraudulent; mendacious; duplicitous; dishonourable; unscrupulous; crooked; false; misleading**

> `deceitful` = 故意欺騙；`untrustworthy` = 不值得信任；`deceptive/misleading` = 造成錯誤印象；`fraudulent` = 欺詐；`duplicitous` = 兩面手法；`unscrupulous` = 不擇手段；`false` = 內容不真實。

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Inaccurate

**不準確不一定是不誠實**

可能是無心錯誤，未必有欺騙意圖。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

An inaccurate statement can be accidental; a dishonest statement is knowingly misleading.

---

## 02 Secretive

**保密不等於不誠實**

不願透露資訊不等於說謊。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Being secretive does not automatically mean being dishonest.

---

## 03 Biased

**偏見不等於欺騙**

偏向某一方，但內容未必虛假。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A biased report may still contain factually true information.

---

## 04 Unreliable

**不可靠比 dishonest 更廣**

可能因健忘、能力差或不穩定而不可靠。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Someone can be unreliable without deliberately deceiving anyone.

---

## 05 Evasive

**閃避不等於說謊**

避免直接回答，不一定說假話。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

An evasive answer avoids the point; a dishonest answer contains deception.

---

## 06 Corrupt

**腐敗更專門**

常涉及濫權、賄賂或非法利益交換。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A corrupt official is dishonest, but not every dishonest person is corrupt.

---

## 07 Fraudulent

**只對應欺詐 sense**

通常要求欺騙以取得金錢、利益或權利。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A dishonest excuse may be a lie without being fraudulent.

---

## 08 Misleading

**可能無意造成**

描述造成錯誤印象的效果，未必證明主觀欺騙意圖。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Misleading information is not always deliberately dishonest.

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Dishonest person vs dishonest statement
對人可用 `dishonest / deceitful / untrustworthy`；對內容可更精確地用 `false / misleading / deceptive / mendacious`。

## 02 Deceitful vs deceptive
`deceitful` 常形容有意欺騙的人／行為；`deceptive` 常形容造成錯誤印象的做法／外觀／廣告。

## 03 False does not always mean dishonest
資料可以是 false，但說話者可能真誠相信它是真的。

## 04 Misleading does not always prove intent
`misleading` 描述效果，不一定能證明主觀欺騙意圖。

## 05 Fraudulent
典型：fraudulent claim / transaction / document / activity。

## 06 Mendacious
正式、書面；日常通常用 dishonest / lying / deceitful。

## 07 Unscrupulous
不只是說謊，而是為利益不顧道德、規則或他人。

## 08 Crooked
非正式：crooked businessman / official / deal / scheme。
