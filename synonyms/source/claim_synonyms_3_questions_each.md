# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Claim

**「聲稱／主張」，究竟是在斷言、維持原說法、提出論證、作未證實指控，還是只中性地陳述某事為真？**

`claim` 是非常廣的報道動詞。它可以表示：

- **明確斷言某事為真** → `assert`
- **即使受質疑仍維持說法** → `maintain`
- **用理由／證據提出主張** → `argue`
- **中性地陳述為真** → `state`
- **正式提出一個立場** → `contend`
- **提出未經證實的指控** → `allege`
- **正式／公開宣布** → `declare`
- **確認某事為真／有效** → `affirm`
- **面對反對仍強烈堅稱** → `insist`
- **聲稱具有某信念／知識／感受** → `profess`
- **學術上提出一個命題或假設** → `posit`
- **自稱為／聲稱做到，但常帶質疑** → `purport`

> **First seen: 2013 set 6.3.**
>
> **核心原則**
>
> `claim` 本身常帶一點「這是某人的說法，不代表作者替它背書」的距離感。  
> 所以選近義詞時，最重要的是判斷：
>
> **How strongly is the speaker committing to the truth of the statement, and is the statement disputed, reasoned, alleged, confirmed, or merely reported?**

# 01 同義詞指南

## Claim 的 12 個近義／近乎可互換說法

## 01 Assert

**斷言；堅稱**

和 `claim` 非常接近，表示自信、明確地說某事是真的。`assert` 通常比 `claim` 更直接、更有把握。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The company asserted that the data had been collected lawfully.

公司斷言有關數據是依法收集的。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- assert that
- assert one's innocence
- firmly assert
- assert a right

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **assert = state firmly and confidently that something is true**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性至正式；新聞、法律、學術

---

## 02 Maintain

**堅稱；一直主張**

當 `claim` 帶有『即使受到質疑，仍然維持原有說法』的意味時，`maintain` 很自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He maintained that he had never seen the document.

他一直堅稱自己從未看過該文件。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- maintain that
- continue to maintain
- maintain one's innocence
- maintain a position

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **maintain = continue to state or defend a position despite doubt or challenge**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性至正式；新聞、法律、辯論

---

## 03 Argue

**主張；論證**

當 `claim` 不是單純斷言，而是配合理由、證據或分析提出觀點時，`argue` 很精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The researchers argue that early intervention improves outcomes.

研究人員主張早期介入可以改善結果。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- argue that
- argue for
- argue against
- strongly argue

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **argue = present a position as true or preferable, usually with reasons or evidence**

**REGISTER · 語域**

非常常用；學術、評論、辯論

---

## 04 State

**陳述；聲明為真**

比 `claim` 更中性，只表示清楚說出某件事。當你只需要『state as true』而不想加入懷疑或爭議色彩時，`state` 很合適。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The report states that demand increased by 12 percent.

報告指出需求增加了12%。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- state that
- clearly state
- state as fact
- state a position

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **state = express something clearly in words, often as a fact or position**

**REGISTER · 語域**

非常常用；所有正式語域

---

## 05 Contend

**主張；聲稱**

較正式，常見於法律、學術、評論和辯論。和 `argue` 很接近，但更像『提出並堅持某一立場』。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The defence contends that the evidence is unreliable.

辯方主張有關證據不可靠。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- contend that
- contend with an argument
- the author contends
- lawyers contend

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **contend = formally claim or argue that a particular position is correct**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式；法律、學術、評論

---

## 06 Allege

**指稱；聲稱（未經證實）**

當 `claim` 涉及指控、違法行為或負面行為，而且內容尚未獲證實時，`allege` 是最重要的精準替代詞。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The complaint alleges that the company misled customers.

投訴指稱公司誤導顧客。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- allege that
- alleged misconduct
- allege wrongdoing
- complaint alleges

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **allege = claim that someone did something wrong, usually without proof yet being established**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式；法律、新聞

---

## 07 Declare

**宣布；公開聲稱**

當 `claim` 是正式、公開、明確地表達某立場或事實時，`declare` 很自然。它通常比 `claim` 更公開、更正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The candidate declared that the campaign had reached its goal.

候選人公開宣布競選活動已達到目標。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- declare that
- publicly declare
- declare one's position
- declare victory

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **declare = state something openly, formally, or publicly**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性至正式；新聞、政治、法律

---

## 08 Affirm

**確認；堅定表示**

當 `claim` 帶有確認、再次肯定某事為真或表明支持某原則的意味時，`affirm` 很貼切。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She affirmed that the figures were accurate.

她確認有關數字準確。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- affirm that
- affirm a belief
- affirm one's commitment
- affirm the truth of

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **affirm = state positively and with confidence that something is true or valid**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式；法律、正式聲明、價值立場

---

## 09 Insist

**堅稱；堅持說**

當 `claim` 表示說話者面對質疑、反對或不相信，仍強烈堅持某說法時，`insist` 很自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She insisted that she had followed the correct procedure.

她堅稱自己已遵循正確程序。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- insist that
- continue to insist
- insist on one's innocence
- strongly insist

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **insist = state something strongly and repeatedly, especially when others disagree**

**REGISTER · 語域**

非常常用；所有語域

---

## 10 Profess

**聲稱；自稱；宣稱信奉**

較正式，常用於聲稱擁有某種感受、信念、知識或身份，而且有時帶說話者是否真誠的疑問。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He professed to know nothing about the arrangement.

他聲稱對這項安排毫不知情。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- profess to know
- profess belief
- profess innocence
- profess support

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **profess = claim openly to have a belief, feeling, knowledge, or quality**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、書面；有時帶懷疑語氣

---

## 11 Posit

**提出；假定為真**

學術性較強。當 `claim` 是把一個命題、理論或假設提出作為分析基礎時，`posit` 很精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The theory posits that language shapes perception.

該理論提出語言會塑造知覺。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- posit that
- theory posits
- posit a relationship
- posit an explanation

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **posit = put forward a statement or idea as a basis for argument or analysis**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、學術

---

## 12 Purport

**聲稱；自稱（常帶質疑）**

當某人、文件或產品 `claims to be / claims to do` 某事，而說話者不完全接受其真實性時，`purport` 很有用。通常用 `purport to + verb`。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The document purports to show that the payment was authorized.

該文件聲稱顯示付款已獲授權。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- purport to be
- purport to show
- purport to prove
- purportedly

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **purport = claim or appear to be or do something, often with an implication of doubt**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式；法律、新聞、評論

# 02 False synonyms

## 01 Claim / State

### 聲稱不等於作者確認

`state` 很中性。  
`claim` 往往保留距離：

> The company claims that the product is safe.

只表示公司如此說，未必代表作者認同。

---

## 02 Claim / Assert

`assert` 比 `claim` 更強、更自信：

> assert that something is true

它不特別表示內容已被證明。

---

## 03 Claim / Maintain

`maintain` 的關鍵是持續性：

> He maintained that he was innocent despite the evidence.

不是任何一次 claim 都適合改成 maintain。

---

## 04 Claim / Argue

`argue` 通常表示：
- 有理由
- 有證據
- 有論點

如果只是沒有論證地說「這是真的」，`assert` 或 `state` 更自然。

---

## 05 Claim / Allege

`allege` 通常用於：
- wrongdoing
- misconduct
- fraud
- abuse
- illegal acts

而且內容尚未被正式證實。

---

## 06 Claim / Insist

`insist` 比 `claim` 更強，常表示：
- others disagree
- others doubt
- speaker repeats the point

---

## 07 Claim / Affirm

`affirm` 有正面確認／重申真實性或承諾的意味。

> affirm a principle  
> affirm a commitment  
> affirm that something is true

---

## 08 Claim / Purport

`purport` 通常是：

> purport to be  
> purport to show  
> purport to prove

它經常暗示說話者對該 claim 保持懷疑。

# 03 練習與解釋

> 每個目標詞／表達三題，共 36 題。  
> **每題六個選項均按該句重新設計；正確答案位置會變化。**
>
> Distractors 會混合真正容易混淆的 reporting verbs，如 `assert / maintain / argue / state / allege / insist / affirm / purport`。

## 練習 01.1

**Target word: Assert · 斷言；堅稱**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> The company claimed that the data had been collected lawfully.

**中文翻譯**

公司聲稱有關數據是依法收集的。

| | 選項 |
|---|---|
| A | asserted |
| B | alleged |
| C | wondered |
| D | implied |
| E | denied |
| F | asked |

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**A. asserted**

> The company asserted that the data had been collected lawfully.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. asserted ✓**  
公司明確而自信地說數據收集合法，最符合 assert 的斷言語氣。

**B. alleged**  
通常用於未證實的負面指控，不適合公司為自己合法性作正面聲明。

**C. wondered**  
表示疑問，和 claim 的肯定方向相反。

**D. implied**  
暗示而非明說。

**E. denied**  
否認，語義方向不同。

**F. asked**  
詢問，不是聲稱。

**Target word: Purport · 聲稱；自稱（常帶質疑）**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> The document claims to show that the payment was authorized.

**中文翻譯**

文件聲稱顯示付款已獲授權。

| | 選項 |
|---|---|
| A | purports |
| B | states |
| C | affirms |
| D | argues |
| E | admits |
| F | proves |

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**A. purports**

> The document purports to show that the payment was authorized.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. purports ✓**  
文件 `claims to show` 且真實性未必被接受，是 purport 的典型用法。

**B. states**  
若文件直接陳述事實可用，但不保留質疑。

**C. affirms**  
會顯得確認有效。

**D. argues**  
文件未必在論證。

**E. admits**  
承認。

**F. proves**  
過強，原句只是聲稱。

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

## Claim vs Assert

**claim** 往往保留報道距離。  
**assert** 更像說話者本身非常肯定。

---

## Claim vs State

**state** 是最中性的「說明」。  
**claim** 有時暗示：
- disputed
- not independently verified
- according to the speaker

---

## Maintain vs Insist

兩者都可表示堅稱。

**maintain** = 持續維持原說法。  
**insist** = 強烈堅持，尤其面對反對。

---

## Argue vs Contend

兩者都常見於學術／辯論。

**argue** 更強調理由與論證。  
**contend** 更正式，強調提出並支持某立場。

---

## Claim vs Allege

**claim** 適用範圍極廣。  
**allege** 特別指尚未證實的負面／法律指控。

> claim a product works  
> allege fraud

---

## Declare vs Assert

**declare** = 公開／正式宣布。  
**assert** = 自信斷言內容為真。

---

## Affirm vs Assert

**assert** 更像首次或強烈提出說法。  
**affirm** 常帶確認／重申意味。

---

## Profess vs Claim

**profess** 常搭：
- belief
- knowledge
- support
- innocence

而且有時帶：
> “This is what the person says, but is it really true?”

---

## Posit vs Argue

**posit** = 先提出一個理論命題／假設。  
**argue** = 用理由支持一個結論。

---

## Purport vs Claim

**purport** 幾乎自帶距離感：

> The report purports to prove...

暗示：
> It says it proves this, but that may be questionable.

---

# 06 Idioms / common expressions · 常用表達

## Make a claim

**提出聲稱／主張**

> The company made several claims about the product.

---

## Lay claim to

**聲稱擁有；宣稱有權得到**

> Two countries lay claim to the island.

這是 `claim = ownership/right` 的另一個義項，不是本單元主要的 reporting-verb 意思。

---

## Claim credit for

**聲稱某功勞屬於自己**

> Both teams claimed credit for the improvement.

---

## Stand by one's claim

**堅持自己的說法**

> She stood by her claim despite criticism.

---

## Back up a claim

**用證據支持一項主張**

> The report provides data to back up the claim.

---

## Make the case that

**論證／提出理由說明**

> The authors make the case that prevention is cheaper than treatment.

和 `argue that` 很接近。

---

## Go on record as saying

**正式公開記錄地表示**

> He went on record as saying the policy had failed.

---

## According to

**據……所稱／根據……**

> According to the company, no customer data was lost.

這是報道 claim 時常用的中立距離結構。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Claim

常見：

> claim that + clause  
> claim to + verb  
> claim to be + noun/adjective

---

## Assert / Maintain / Argue / State / Contend

最常見：

> assert that...  
> maintain that...  
> argue that...  
> state that...  
> contend that...

---

## Allege

> allege that someone did something  
> allege misconduct

注意：
> allegedly

表示「據稱」，不等於已證實。

---

## Insist

> insist that + clause  
> insist on + noun / -ing

兩者語法不同。

---

## Profess

常見：

> profess to know  
> profess to believe  
> profess support

---

## Posit

> posit that...  
> posit a relationship / explanation

---

## Purport

通常：

> purport to + verb  
> purport to be

不是一般用：
> purport that... △

---

# 09 核心決策問題

1. **是自信、明確斷言嗎？** → `assert`
2. **是受質疑後仍一直維持說法嗎？** → `maintain`
3. **是有理由／證據的論點嗎？** → `argue`
4. **只是中性陳述嗎？** → `state`
5. **是正式爭辯立場嗎？** → `contend`
6. **是未證實的不當行為指控嗎？** → `allege`
7. **是公開／正式宣布嗎？** → `declare`
8. **是確認／重申真實性或承諾嗎？** → `affirm`
9. **面對反對仍強烈堅稱嗎？** → `insist`
10. **是聲稱信奉／知道／支持某事嗎？** → `profess`
11. **是學術理論先提出命題嗎？** → `posit`
12. **是自稱某身份／功能，而且作者保持懷疑嗎？** → `purport`

---

# 10 Final note for system use

最有用的替代表達：

`assert / maintain / argue / state / contend / allege / declare / affirm / insist / profess / posit / purport`

常用 expressions：

`make a claim / lay claim to / claim credit for / stand by one's claim / back up a claim / make the case that / go on record as saying / according to`

最重要的 false-synonym / near-miss group：

`confirm / prove / admit / suggest / imply / speculate / report`

它們分別表示 **確認、證明、承認、提出可能性、暗示、推測、報道**，和 `claim` 對真實性的承諾程度不同。

真正的選詞依據是：

> **Is the speaker asserting, maintaining, arguing, neutrally stating, alleging, affirming, insisting, professing, positing—or merely purporting that something is true?**
