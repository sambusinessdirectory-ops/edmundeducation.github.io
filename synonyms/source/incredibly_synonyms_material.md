# Incredibly

**極其、顯著地、難以置信地、格外地、非同尋常地、驚人地、引人注目地、巨大程度地——究竟是哪一種 incredibly？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**extremely; remarkably; unbelievably; exceptionally; extraordinarily; amazingly; astonishingly; strikingly; immensely**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **extremely; remarkably; unbelievably** 全部保留。  
> 本課特別區分 `incredibly` 的兩大功能：**純高程度 intensifier**，以及 **帶驚訝／難以置信評價的 intensifier**。

---

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Extremely

**極其；非常**

最直接、最通用的近義詞之一。當 `incredibly` 只是把形容詞或副詞程度推到很高，而不特別強調『令人難以置信』時，`extremely` 最安全。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The test was extremely difficult.

這個測驗極其困難。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- extremely difficult

- extremely hot

- extremely important

- extremely slowly

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Extremely

**最直接但沒有驚訝色彩**

extremely 只把程度推高；incredibly 常額外帶「令人難以相信／驚訝」語氣。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The exam was extremely difficult; the speed was unbelievably fast.

---

## 02 Remarkably

**值得注意，不只是很**

remarkably 強調某特徵超出一般、值得注意；不是所有 extremely 都能換成 remarkably。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She remained remarkably calm during the crisis.

---

## 03 Unbelievably

**語氣更強、更口語**

unbelievably 很接近 incredibly，但通常 disbelief 感更明顯。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The hotel was unbelievably expensive.

---

## 04 Exceptionally

**需要高於常態**

exceptionally 常暗示相對平均或正常水平特別突出。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She is exceptionally talented.

---

## 05 Amazingly / astonishingly

**驚訝程度不同**

兩者都帶 surprise；astonishingly 通常比 amazingly 更強。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The device worked amazingly well; the error rate was astonishingly low.

---

## 06 Very

**常見但語義較弱**

very 是一般程度副詞，通常沒有 incredibly 的強度或驚訝感。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The room was very hot; it was incredibly hot.

---

## 07 Incredible / incredibly

**詞性陷阱**

`incredible` 是形容詞；`incredibly` 是副詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The result is incredible; the result was incredibly good.

---

## 08 Incredibly

**多義功能**

它既可單純表示 `extremely`，也可保留『難以置信地』的評價語氣。需要靠 context 判斷。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The task was incredibly hard; she recovered incredibly quickly.

### 中文翻譯

- **A. extremely**

- **B. exceptionally**

- **C. remarkably**

- **D. unbelievably**

- **E. slightly**

- **F. moderately**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Incredibly vs Extremely
`extremely` 最中性，只表示程度非常高。

## Incredibly vs Remarkably
`remarkably` = 不只是高，而且**值得注意、超出預期**。

## Incredibly vs Unbelievably
最接近原本的「難以置信」語氣：
- unbelievably expensive
- unbelievably fast

## Incredibly vs Exceptionally
`exceptionally` 要有 normal / average baseline：
- exceptionally talented
- exceptionally mild

## Incredibly vs Extraordinarily
較正式、書面：
- extraordinarily complex
- extraordinarily difficult

## Incredibly vs Amazingly / Astonishingly
兩者都有驚訝：
- amazingly = 驚訝但較日常
- astonishingly = 更強烈、更震撼

## Incredibly vs Strikingly
只在特徵／差異『非常顯眼』時自然：
- strikingly similar
- strikingly different

## Incredibly vs Immensely
特別自然於：
- immensely popular
- immensely beneficial
- enjoy immensely

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Incredible vs incredibly
- incredible = adjective
- incredibly = adverb

例如：
- The result is incredible.
- The result was incredibly good.

## 02 Incredibly as a degree intensifier
很多時候近似：
- extremely
- very

例如：
- incredibly difficult
- incredibly hot

## 03 Incredibly with evaluative surprise
有時保留原義「難以置信地」：
- incredibly fast
- incredibly cheap
- incredibly lucky

## 04 Remarkably / strikingly
不是一般 very 的替換：
- strikingly similar ✓
- strikingly hungry ✗

## 05 Immensely
搭配受限制，但非常自然於：
- immensely popular
- immensely important
- immensely beneficial
- enjoy immensely
