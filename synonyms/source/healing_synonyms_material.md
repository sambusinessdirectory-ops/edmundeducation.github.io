# Healing

**康復、修復、療養、復康、再生、情緒復原、和解——究竟是哪一種 healing？**

本課分開幾個高頻 sense：

1. **physical recovery** — 疾病、受傷、手術後恢復  
2. **tissue repair / regeneration** — 組織修復或再生  
3. **rehabilitation / convalescence** — 有計劃復康或療養期  
4. **emotional healing** — 情緒、悲傷、創傷後復原  
5. **relational / social healing** — 關係或群體衝突後修復

核心近義與近義表達：

**recovery; restoration; recuperation; rehabilitation; mending; repair; regeneration; renewal; convalescence; emotional recovery; reconciliation**

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Recovery

**康復；恢復**

和 `healing` 最接近的廣義近義詞之一，強調從受傷、疾病、創傷或困難狀態逐步恢復。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Her recovery after the operation took several months.

她手術後的康復花了幾個月。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- full recovery
- recovery from illness
- recovery process
- make a recovery

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 healing 是一般康復、休養、功能復康、組織再生、情緒復原，還是關係／社會和解？

---

## 02 Restoration

**修復；恢復原狀**

當 `healing` 指恢復完整性、功能、關係或原本狀態時很合適。比 recovery 更強調『回復到較完整／原有狀態』。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The restoration of the damaged ecosystem took years.

受損生態系統的修復花了多年。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- restoration of function
- ecological restoration
- restoration of trust
- full restoration

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 healing 是一般康復、休養、功能復康、組織再生、情緒復原，還是關係／社會和解？

## 03 Recuperation

**休養；康復**

較正式，常指生病、手術或過度勞累後的休養與體力恢復。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

He stayed home for recuperation after the illness.

他生病後留在家中休養。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- period of recuperation
- rest and recuperation
- time for recuperation
- recuperation after surgery

### EXAMPLE · 例句

- rehabilitation exercises

## 06 Repair

**修復；修補**

### EXAMPLE · 例句

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- repair process

## 07 Regeneration

**再生；復原**

當受損組織、細胞、生態或系統透過再生而恢復時很精確。比一般 healing 更技術性。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The regeneration of nerve tissue can be limited.

神經組織的再生能力可能有限。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- tissue regeneration
- cell regeneration
- forest regeneration
- natural regeneration

- during convalescence

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Cure

**治癒不等於 healing 過程**

`cure` 強調疾病被消除或治好；healing 更常指逐步恢復過程。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A cure may remove a disease; healing describes the process of recovery.

---

## 02 Treatment

**治療不等於康復**

`treatment` 是為改善健康而採取的措施；healing 是身體／心理恢復本身。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Treatment may support healing, but the two are not identical.

---

## 03 Relief

**緩解不等於痊癒**

`relief` 表示痛楚或不適減輕，未必代表受損狀態已恢復。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Pain relief can occur before healing is complete.

---

## 04 Rest

**休息只是其中一個因素**

休息可以幫助 healing，但不等於 healing 本身。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Rest may support recovery.

---

## 05 Rehabilitation

**只對功能恢復 sense**

`rehabilitation` 通常有訓練／治療計劃，不能替代所有自然 healing。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A small cut heals without rehabilitation.

---

## 06 Regeneration

**只對再生 sense**

`regeneration` 偏組織、細胞、生態等重新生長。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Tissue regeneration is one biological aspect of healing.

---

## 07 Reconciliation

**只對關係／衝突修復**

`reconciliation` 不適用於一般身體傷口。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Communities may need reconciliation; wounds need healing.

---

## 08 Recovery

**很接近但不是每個 sense 都相同**

`recovery` 很廣，通常強調回到較好狀態；healing 有時更強調修復過程。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Recovery from illness may involve a long healing process.

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Healing vs recovery
- healing = 修復／療癒過程
- recovery = 回到較健康、穩定或正常狀態

## 02 Recuperation vs convalescence
兩者都可指病後／手術後休養：
- recuperation = 一般正式
- convalescence = 更醫療、較書面，常指康復期本身

## 03 Rehabilitation
通常涉及有計劃的：
- exercises
- therapy
- retraining
- functional recovery

## 04 Repair vs regeneration
- repair = 修補損傷
- regeneration = 重新生長／再生

## 05 Emotional healing
自然近義：
- emotional recovery
- healing after loss
- recovery from trauma

## 06 Social / relational healing
常見：
- reconciliation
- restoration of trust
- mending relationships

## 07 Cure is different
`cure` 更偏疾病被消除；healing 可在沒有「完全治癒」的情況下持續發生。
