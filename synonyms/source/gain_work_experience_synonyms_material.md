# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Gain work experience

**獲得實務經驗、累積專業經驗、親身操作、接觸職場、取得行業經驗——究竟是哪一種 gain work experience？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**acquire practical experience; build professional experience; gain practical experience; develop workplace experience; gain hands-on experience; build up experience; accumulate work experience; get workplace exposure; gain industry experience; gain on-the-job experience; develop professional skills through work**

> **Precision rule:**  
> `practical experience` = 實務經驗。  
> `professional experience` = 職涯累積。  
> `hands-on experience` = 親自操作。  
> `workplace exposure` = 初步接觸職場。  
> `industry experience` = 特定行業經驗。  
> `on-the-job experience` = 真實崗位中邊做邊學。

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Acquire practical experience

**獲得實際經驗；累積實務經驗**

透過實際工作、實習、項目或現場操作取得真實經驗。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Students can acquire practical experience through summer placements.

### 中文翻譯

學生可透過暑期實習獲得工作／實務經驗。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- relevant experience
- practical experience
- professional experience
- experience in + field

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡重點是親自實作、長期累積、職場接觸、特定行業，還是在職學習？

# 02 False synonyms

以下詞語都和職業發展有關，但它們只是取得經驗的途徑、資格或學習方式，不等同 **gain work experience**。

## 01 Get a job
**找到工作不等於累積經驗**

得到職位只是開始；之後的實際工作才形成 experience。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON
Getting a job creates an opportunity to gain work experience.

---

## 02 Training
**培訓不等於工作經驗**

Training can prepare someone for work, but it may happen without real workplace responsibility.

### ENGLISH COMPARISON
Training prepares you for work; work experience comes from participation.

---

## 03 Qualification
**資格不等於經驗**

A degree or certificate records learning or competence, not workplace experience.

### ENGLISH COMPARISON
A person can have strong qualifications but little work experience.

---

## 04 Internship
**實習是途徑，不是經驗本身**

An internship is a placement through which experience may be gained.

### ENGLISH COMPARISON
An internship can provide practical work experience.

---

## 05 Volunteering
**義工是活動／途徑**

Volunteering can generate useful experience, but the activity itself is not identical to experience.

### ENGLISH COMPARISON
Volunteering can provide practical experience.

---

## 06 Work placement
**工作實習安排是載體**

A placement is the arrangement; work experience is what the learner gains from it.

### ENGLISH COMPARISON
A work placement gives students an opportunity to gain experience.

---

## 07 Job shadowing
**通常以觀察為主**

Job shadowing often gives workplace exposure rather than substantial hands-on experience.

### ENGLISH COMPARISON
Job shadowing provides exposure, but not always hands-on responsibility.

---

## 08 Employment
**就業是狀態**

Employment is being employed; experience is the knowledge and competence accumulated through work.

### ENGLISH COMPARISON
Employment can lead to work experience over time.

---

## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** Acquire practical experience · 獲得實際經驗；累積實務經驗

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

Students can gain work experience through summer placements.

### 中文翻譯

這句描述透過實際工作或職場參與取得經驗。

### OPTIONS


- **A. acquire practical experience**

- **B. attend training**

- **C. earn a qualification**

- **D. study theory**

- **E. job shadowing**

- **F. get a job**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**acquire practical experience · 獲得實際經驗；累積實務經驗**

Students can **acquire practical experience** through summer placements.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

- **A. acquire practical experience ✓** — `acquire practical experience` = 透過實際工作、實習、項目或現場操作取得真實經驗。 本句的實際參與程度與目標語義吻合。
- **B. attend training** — 接受培訓；可能有助能力發展，但不等於真實工作經驗。 因此不等同本題的工作經驗累積。
- **C. earn a qualification** — 取得資格／證書；是 credential，不是 experience。 因此是資格而非工作經驗。
- **D. study theory** — 學習理論；沒有真實職場參與。 因此缺少實際職場參與。
- **E. job shadowing** — 跟隨觀察；提供 exposure，但 hands-on responsibility 通常較少。 只有在本題重點是初步 workplace exposure 時才更合適。
- **F. get a job** — 取得職位；真正經驗要在之後的工作中累積。 因此它是進入工作的起點，而不是累積後的 experience。

- **F. get a job** — 取得職位；真正經驗要在之後的工作中累積。 因此它是進入工作的起點，而不是累積後的 experience。

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Practical experience vs Hands-on experience
**practical experience** 較廣；**hands-on experience** 明確要求親自操作。

## Professional experience vs Work experience
**professional experience** 更偏職涯與專業能力；**work experience** 可包括入門、短期、兼職或實習。

## Workplace exposure vs Work experience
**workplace exposure** 通常較淺，可只是觀察、短期接觸或 job shadowing。

## Industry experience
只在特定 sector 中使用，例如 finance, retail, media, construction。

## On-the-job experience
強調 **learning while actually doing the job**。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Gain experience
- gain experience
- gain work experience
- gain practical experience
- gain experience in marketing

## 02 Work experience
英式教育／求職語境常見：
- work experience placement
- relevant work experience
- previous work experience

## 03 Experience is usually uncountable
自然：
- gain experience
- gain work experience
- have extensive experience

`an experience` 通常表示一次經歷，而不是「工作經驗」這個能力資產。

## 04 Practical vs hands-on
- practical = 實務
- hands-on = 親自操作

## 05 Workplace exposure
通常比 full work experience 淺：
- short visit
- observation
- job shadowing
- brief placement

## 06 Build / accumulate
- build up experience = 較口語
- accumulate experience = 較正式
