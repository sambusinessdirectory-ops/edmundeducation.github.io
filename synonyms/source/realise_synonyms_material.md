# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Realise

**意識到、認識到、察覺到、明白、發現、醒悟——究竟是哪一種 realise？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**recognise; become aware; understand; see; notice; discover; come to understand; come to realise; wake up to**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 本課採用英式拼法 **realise**；美式 **realize** 是同一詞的拼寫變體，不另列 synonym。

---

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Recognise

**意識到；認識到**

認識到某件事是真實、重要或存在的。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

She recognised that the plan would cost more than expected.

她意識到這個計劃的成本會比預期高。

- recognise that

- recognise the need

- recognise the problem

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Know

**知道不等於 realise**

`know` 表示已經擁有某知識；`realise` 常表示由不知道轉變為意識到。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

I knew the rule already; I realised my mistake only later.

---

## 02 Remember

**記起不等於 realise**

remember 表示從記憶中想起；realise 是形成認知／理解。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

I remembered his name, but I realised why he had called.

---

## 03 Notice

**注意到只是其中一種路徑**

notice 偏感官／具體細節；realise 可以是抽象結論、重要性或後果。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

You notice a crack; you realise the repair will be expensive.

---

## 04 Understand

**理解高度相關但焦點不同**

understand 重 comprehension；realise 更常強調『現在才知道／才意識到』。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

I understood the rule, but I only later realised how important it was.

---

## 05 Recognise

**很接近但不完全相同**

recognise 可表示辨認人／物，也可表示承認／認識到；只有後一義和 realise 接近。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

I recognised her face; I realised she was the person I had met before.

---

## 06 Discover

**發現通常需要新事實**

discover 偏經查找、研究或偶然發現新資訊；realise 可純粹來自思考。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

They discovered the cause through testing and realised its importance later.

---

## 07 Actualise

**字形相似但意思不同**

actualise 表示使某事成為現實／實現，不等於 realise = 意識到。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

They actualised the plan; they realised the plan was risky.

---

## 08 Realize / realise

**拼寫變體，不是不同 synonym**

`realise` 是英式常見拼法；`realize` 是美式常見拼法。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

British: realise; American: realize.

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Realise vs Recognise
`recognise` 是最直接近義詞之一，尤其指認識到一個事實或重要性。

## Realise vs Become aware
後者最突出「由不知道變成知道」。

## Realise vs Understand
後者重點是理解原因、含義或後果。

## Realise vs See
`see` 是自然口語的「明白／看出」。

## Realise vs Notice
`notice` 偏感官察覺具體變化。

## Realise vs Discover
`discover` 常暗示查找／測試後發現新事實。

## Realise vs Come to understand
後者強調理解逐漸形成。

## Realise vs Come to realise
後者加入明確 gradual sense。

## Realise vs Wake up to
後者帶警醒／終於認清先前忽視問題的語氣。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## Realise + that-clause
- realise that the plan is risky
- realise that we are late

## Realise + noun phrase
- realise the importance
- realise the danger
- realise the consequences

## Realise vs notice
- notice = 看到／聽到具體細節
- realise = 從細節得出認知／結論

## British vs American spelling
- British: realise
- American: realize

## Other senses excluded
- realise a dream = 實現夢想
- realise an asset = 變現資產
