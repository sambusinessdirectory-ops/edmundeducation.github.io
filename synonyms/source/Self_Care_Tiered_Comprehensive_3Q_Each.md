# SELF-CARE · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Self-care

**Self-care 是一個很廣的概念。** 它可以指日常照顧自己、促進身心健康的習慣、慢性病自我管理、基本個人護理、健康生活規律，甚至保留休息和恢復的個人時間。因此替換前要先判斷是在說 **general wellbeing, structured health management, personal hygiene, daily routines, emotional nurturing,** 還是 **relaxation time**。

你提供的 **looking after oneself; personal well-being practices** 正好代表兩個核心方向：**廣義照顧自己** 與 **具體身心健康做法**。

本次保留 **9 個**自然且有教學價值的近義／情境表達。部分詞只屬 self-care 的 subtype，會清楚標示，不會暗示所有語境都可互換。共 **27 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Self-care 的 sense | 核心問題 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|---|
| 廣義照顧自己 | 是否 simply caring for one's own needs？ | looking after oneself, taking care of one's wellbeing |
| 身心健康習慣 | 是否 sleep/exercise/relaxation/wellbeing activities？ | personal well-being practices, wellness practices, healthy routines |
| 健康狀況自我管理 | 是否 medication/symptom/condition management？ | self-management |
| 基本日常護理 | 是否 washing/dressing/hygiene？ | personal care |
| 情緒滋養／善待自己 | 是否 self-kindness/emotional replenishment？ | self-nurturing |
| 放鬆的個人時間 | 是否 time deliberately set aside for oneself？ | me-time |

## Tier 1 — Core self-care / wellbeing / personal-care equivalents · 核心自我照顧與身心健康近義表達

### 01 Looking after oneself

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：照顧自己；照料自己的需要  
**語域**：neutral / plain English  
**核心語義**：taking practical steps to care for one's own physical, emotional, and everyday needs  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **self-care** 最直接、最自然的 plain-English 改寫，適合一般健康、生活和教育語境。  
**常見搭配**：look after oneself; learn to look after oneself; looking after your health; look after your own needs.  
**常見搭配對象**：health, wellbeing, needs, routine, recovery.  
**與 self-care 的辨析**：Looking after oneself 語義很接近 self-care，但更口語、更具體，不帶術語感。  
**使用限制**：正式研究或專業健康文本通常直接用 self-care / self-management。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
After a demanding week, she made more time for looking after herself.  
經過忙碌的一週後，她花更多時間照顧自己。

### 02 Personal well-being practices

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：個人身心健康習慣；促進身心健康的做法  
**語域**：formal / health / education  
**核心語義**：regular activities or habits intended to support a person's physical, mental, or emotional wellbeing  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很適合把 self-care 拆解成具體習慣，例如 sleep, exercise, rest, relaxation, boundaries。  
**常見搭配**：well-being practices; personal wellbeing; wellbeing routine; healthy practices; wellbeing activities.  
**常見搭配對象**：sleep, exercise, relaxation, rest, stress.  
**與 self-care 的辨析**：比 self-care 更明確指出『具體做法』，而不是整體概念。  
**使用限制**：醫療自我管理或照顧基本生活能力時，self-management / personal care 可能更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The workshop introduced several personal well-being practices for managing stress.  
工作坊介紹數種有助管理壓力的個人身心健康習慣。

### 03 Self-management

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：自我管理；自行管理健康／生活  
**語域**：formal / healthcare / education  
**核心語義**：managing one's own health, condition, behaviour, time, or responsibilities through informed actions  
**用法提示**：當 self-care 涉及 chronic illness, medication, symptoms, routines 或 behaviour change 時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：self-management skills; condition self-management; self-management programme; health self-management.  
**常見搭配對象**：condition, symptom, medication, behaviour, routine.  
**與 self-care 的辨析**：Self-management 比 self-care 更目標導向、系統化，常涉及監察與決策。  
**使用限制**：休息、泡澡、放鬆等一般 self-care 不一定叫 self-management。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The programme teaches self-management skills to people with long-term conditions.  
這個計劃教授長期病患者自我管理技巧。

### 04 Personal care

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：個人護理；日常自理  
**語域**：neutral / care services  
**核心語義**：care relating to personal hygiene, dressing, grooming, and other basic daily needs  
**用法提示**：當 self-care 指洗澡、更衣、梳洗、個人衛生等日常生活自理能力時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：personal care needs; personal care routine; assistance with personal care; personal hygiene.  
**常見搭配對象**：washing, dressing, grooming, hygiene, toileting.  
**與 self-care 的辨析**：Personal care 比 self-care 更窄，偏 basic daily living tasks。  
**使用限制**：心理放鬆、情緒界線、休息等較廣 self-care 不能一律叫 personal care。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The support worker helps with personal care when needed.  
有需要時，支援工作員會協助個人護理。

### 05 Wellness practices

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：健康促進習慣；養生做法  
**語域**：neutral / lifestyle  
**核心語義**：activities intended to promote overall physical, mental, or emotional wellness  
**用法提示**：當 self-care 偏 lifestyle、stress reduction、sleep、exercise、mindfulness 等語境時自然。  
**常見搭配**：wellness practice; daily wellness routine; wellness activity; wellness habits; workplace wellness.  
**常見搭配對象**：sleep, exercise, mindfulness, relaxation, nutrition.  
**與 self-care 的辨析**：Wellness practices 偏主動促進健康；self-care 範圍更廣，也可包括休息、求助或日常基本照顧。  
**使用限制**：醫療疾病管理或 basic personal care 不宜一律改成 wellness。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Regular exercise and good sleep are simple wellness practices.  
定期運動和良好睡眠都是簡單的健康促進習慣。

## Tier 2 — Routine / nurturing / explanatory variants · 健康規律、滋養與描述性變體

### 06 Healthy routines

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：健康日常習慣；健康生活規律  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：regular habits and routines that support physical and mental health  
**用法提示**：當 self-care 具體指規律睡眠、飲食、運動、休息等日常安排時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：healthy routine; daily routine; establish healthy routines; maintain a routine; healthy habits.  
**常見搭配對象**：sleep, meal, exercise, rest, work.  
**與 self-care 的辨析**：Healthy routines 更強調 repeated daily structure；self-care 可包含偶爾或情境性的行動。  
**使用限制**：一次性的求助或情緒界線不一定屬 routine。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Maintaining healthy routines can make busy periods easier to manage.  
保持健康的日常規律可令繁忙時期更容易應付。

### 07 Self-nurturing

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：自我滋養；善待自己  
**語域**：reflective / wellbeing  
**核心語義**：treating oneself with care, kindness, rest, and emotional support  
**用法提示**：當 self-care 帶 compassion, gentleness, emotional replenishment 意味時可用。  
**常見搭配**：self-nurturing activity; nurturing oneself; emotional nourishment; self-kindness.  
**常見搭配對象**：rest, comfort, emotion, recovery, wellbeing.  
**與 self-care 的辨析**：Self-nurturing 比 self-care 更情緒化、溫和，也較少用於醫療管理。  
**使用限制**：正式臨床、政策或基本生活護理語境不常用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Quiet time can be an important form of self-nurturing.  
安靜獨處可以是一種重要的自我滋養方式。

### 08 Taking care of one's wellbeing

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：照顧自己的身心健康  
**語域**：neutral / explanatory  
**核心語義**：actively protecting and supporting one's physical, mental, and emotional wellbeing  
**用法提示**：這是 self-care 的透明描述性改寫，適合教學、健康宣傳和一般說明。  
**常見搭配**：take care of wellbeing; protect wellbeing; support wellbeing; care for mental wellbeing.  
**常見搭配對象**：mental health, physical health, stress, balance, recovery.  
**與 self-care 的辨析**：語義接近但更長，並把重點放在 wellbeing outcome。  
**使用限制**：若要指 very specific personal hygiene tasks，personal care 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Taking care of your wellbeing includes getting enough rest and asking for help when needed.  
照顧自己的身心健康包括有足夠休息，並在需要時尋求協助。

## Tier 3 — Informal personal-time variant · 口語個人放鬆時間變體

### 09 Me-time

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：屬於自己的時間；獨處放鬆時間  
**語域**：informal / lifestyle  
**核心語義**：time deliberately set aside for oneself to rest, relax, or do enjoyable personal activities  
**用法提示**：當 self-care 特別指保留個人時間、休息、放鬆時可作口語情境變體。  
**常見搭配**：make time for me-time; need some me-time; enjoy me-time; quiet me-time.  
**常見搭配對象**：rest, relaxation, hobby, reading, break.  
**與 self-care 的辨析**：Me-time 只是 self-care 的一個 subtype，重點是時間與放鬆。  
**使用限制**：不能代表醫療自我管理、個人護理或所有 self-care。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She protects an hour of me-time at the end of each week.  
她每週末都會保留一小時屬於自己的放鬆時間。

## 04 False synonyms / self-care traps · 容易誤選的自我照顧、休閒與個人護理陷阱

### 01 Selfishness

**自私 ≠ self-care**  
self-care 是合理照顧自身需要；selfishness 是不顧他人需要。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Self-care and selfishness are not the same.

### 02 Pampering

**寵愛自己 ≠ 所有 self-care**  
pampering 只是享受型活動，self-care 可包括睡眠、求助、治療和設立界線。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Pampering is only one possible form.

### 03 Leisure

**休閒 ≠ self-care**  
休閒可以促進 wellbeing，但並非所有 leisure 都有自我照顧目的。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Leisure is broader and purpose-neutral.

### 04 Personal care

**只在日常自理 sense 接近**  
personal care 偏洗澡、穿衣、梳洗等基本生活照顧。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Personal care is narrower.

### 05 Self-management

**只在系統性管理 sense 接近**  
self-management 常涉及健康狀況、行為、藥物或生活管理。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Self-management is more structured.

### 06 Wellness

**健康狀態／概念 ≠ self-care action**  
wellness 是狀態或整體理念；self-care 是實際行動／做法。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Wellness is an outcome or broader concept.

### 07 Rest

**休息 ≠ 所有 self-care**  
rest 是 self-care 的一種做法，但範圍太窄。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Rest is one component.

### 08 Care / self-care

**詞義與結構陷阱**  
care 可指照顧他人；self-care 明確指照顧自己。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The reflexive meaning matters.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Looking after oneself · 照顧自己；照料自己的需要

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

She has become more deliberate about self-care during stressful periods.

**中文翻譯**  
在壓力大的時期，她更刻意照顧自己。

- A. Looking after oneself
- B. Personal well-being practices
- C. Healthy routines
- D. Selfishness
- E. Leisure
- F. Pampering

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Looking after oneself** · 照顧自己；照料自己的需要

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
She has become more deliberate about looking after herself during stressful periods.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Looking after oneself ✓** — means taking practical care of one's own physical, emotional and everyday needs. Here, the sentence simply means taking practical care of one's own needs. That makes **Looking after oneself** the most precise reformulation of this particular sense of *self-care*.
- **B.** **Personal well-being practices** — means concrete habits or activities intended to support physical, mental or emotional wellbeing. This points to concrete wellbeing activities and is less suitable when the sentence is about broad self-care, clinical self-management, or basic personal care. The decisive clue is that the sentence simply means taking practical care of one's own needs.
- **C.** **Healthy routines** — means regular repeated habits that support physical and mental health. This requires repeated daily habits; it is too narrow for one-off support, emotional self-kindness, or personal-care tasks. The decisive clue is that the sentence simply means taking practical care of one's own needs.
- **D.** **Selfishness** — means prioritising oneself without due regard for others and is not the same as self-care. This changes the meaning negatively: caring for one's needs does not imply disregarding other people. The decisive clue is that the sentence simply means taking practical care of one's own needs.
- **E.** **Leisure** — means free time or recreational activity and does not necessarily have a self-care purpose. Leisure may help wellbeing, but free time is not automatically purposeful self-care. The decisive clue is that the sentence simply means taking practical care of one's own needs.
- **F.** **Pampering** — means giving oneself pleasurable or indulgent treatment and is only one possible form of self-care. This suggests indulgence or treats and is much narrower than the broader health and wellbeing concept. The decisive clue is that the sentence simply means taking practical care of one's own needs.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **self-care vs looking after oneself**: 語義很接近；後者更日常、更透明。
- **self-care vs personal well-being practices**: 後者強調具體健康活動／習慣。
- **self-care vs self-management**: self-management 更有系統，常用於健康狀況與行為管理。
- **self-care vs personal care**: personal care 偏洗澡、更衣、梳洗等基本生活需要。
- **self-care vs wellness practices**: wellness practices 偏生活方式和健康促進。
- **self-care vs healthy routines**: healthy routines 強調反覆、規律的日常結構。
- **self-care vs self-nurturing**: self-nurturing 更偏情緒上的溫柔、自我支持和恢復。
- **self-care vs me-time**: me-time 只是 self-care 的一個口語 subtype。
- **self-care vs selfishness**: 照顧自身需要不等於不顧他人。
- **self-care vs leisure**: 休閒可有益 wellbeing，但並非所有 leisure 都是有目的的 self-care。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & concept traps · 文法、搭配與『自我照顧』概念陷阱

- **self-care** 通常作不可數概念名詞：practise self-care / make time for self-care。
- **self-care routine / self-care practice / self-care strategy** 都是常見搭配。
- **look after oneself**：plain-English 動詞改寫。
- **personal well-being practices**：可數 practice，適合列舉 sleep/exercise/relaxation。
- **self-management of + condition**：self-management of diabetes / symptoms。
- **personal care**：常見於照護服務，尤其 washing/dressing/grooming/hygiene。
- **wellness practices**：偏 lifestyle / prevention / general wellbeing。
- **healthy routines**：強調規律性與日常結構。
- **self-nurturing**：較 reflective，偏 self-kindness / emotional recovery。
- **me-time**：口語且較窄，不等於所有 self-care。
- 不要把 **selfishness, pampering, leisure, rest** 自動當作 self-care 的全面同義詞。
- 英式／香港拼寫可使用 **well-being / wellbeing**；現代英式機構文本亦常見 **wellbeing**。
