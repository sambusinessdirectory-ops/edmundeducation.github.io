# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Especially

**尤其、特別、值得注意地、最重要的是、主要地、格外地——究竟是哪一種 especially？**

`especially` 可用來：

1. **highlight one item** — 從一組中突出某一人／事物  
2. **emphasise degree** — 表示程度特別高  
3. **narrow a group** — 尤其對某類人／情況  
4. **prioritise** — 在某些語境近似「最重要的是」

核心近義與近義表達：

**particularly; notably; above all; in particular; specifically; chiefly; primarily; mainly; markedly; exceptionally; singularly; most of all**

> **Precision rule:**  
> `particularly / in particular` 最接近。  
> `notably` = 值得注意。  
> `above all / most of all` = 最重要／最突出。  
> `specifically` = 具體指出。  
> `chiefly / primarily / mainly` = 主要，而非單純「尤其」。  
> `markedly / exceptionally / singularly` = 程度特別高。

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Particularly

**尤其；特別**

和 `especially` 最直接、最常用的近義詞之一。兩者很多語境可互換；`particularly` 稍正式，常用來突出某一項最值得注意。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The course is useful, particularly for beginners.

這課程很有用，尤其適合初學者。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- particularly useful
- particularly important
- particularly difficult
- particularly for

## 02 Notably

**尤其；值得注意地**

當 `especially` 不只是表示『特別』，還帶『值得注意』意味時很合適。常見於正式、書面、分析語境。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Several cities improved, notably Hong Kong and Singapore.

多個城市有所改善，尤其香港和新加坡值得注意。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- most notably
- notably absent
- notably higher
- notably among

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是要突出某一項、表示主要成分、具體收窄範圍，還是表示程度格外高？

## 03 Above all

**最重要的是；尤其**

當 `especially` 強調在多個因素中某一項最重要、應優先考慮時可用。它比 particularly 更有優先級意味。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The job requires patience, flexibility, and above all good judgement.

這份工作需要耐性、靈活性，最重要的是良好判斷力。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- above all else
- and above all
- above all important
- value above all

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是要突出某一項、表示主要成分、具體收窄範圍，還是表示程度格外高？

## 04 In particular

**尤其；特別是**

非常自然的片語副詞，常用於從一組事物中突出某一項、某一類或某一例子。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Many subjects are difficult, in particular mathematics.

很多科目都很難，尤其是數學。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- in particular
- one group in particular
- especially/in particular
- focus in particular on

## 05 Specifically

**具體而言；特別是**

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The policy targets young people, specifically those under 18.

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- specifically mentioned
- specifically aimed at

## 06 Chiefly

**主要地；尤其**

較正式，表示主要、首要地因為某一因素。比 especially 更接近 `mainly`，只在重點因素語境適合。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The decline was caused chiefly by higher fuel costs.

下降主要是由燃料成本上升造成。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- chiefly because
- chiefly concerned with
- chiefly responsible
- chiefly among

## 07 Primarily

**主要地；首要地**

表示主要原因、主要用途或主要群體。它和 especially 有交集，但更強調『主要』而不是『特別』。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The service is used primarily by older people.

## 08 Mainly

**主要地；大多**

# 02 False synonyms

以下詞語和 **especially** 很容易混淆，但有些表示『主要』『只限』『一般』『通常』或『重要的是』，語義焦點不同。

## 01 Mainly

**主要不等於尤其**

`mainly` 表示大部分／主要是某項；`especially` 表示某項比其他項更值得突出。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The audience was mainly students. I liked the talk especially for its examples.

---

## 02 Primarily

**首要成分不等於特別突出**

`primarily` 聚焦主要原因、用途或群體。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The service is primarily for residents, especially older residents.

---

## 03 Specifically

**具體指出不等於尤其**

`specifically` 收窄範圍；especially 則突出某一項的重要性／程度。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The rule applies specifically to buses, especially older models.

---

## 04 Exceptionally

**程度異常不等於選中特定項目**

`exceptionally` 通常修飾程度，如 exceptionally difficult。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The exam was especially hard for beginners, and exceptionally hard this year.

---

## 05 Importantly

**重要地不等於 especially**

`importantly` 表示『重要的是』，常引出一個重要觀點，而不是突出同類中的一項。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Most importantly, the system is safe. It is especially useful for beginners.

---

## 06 Exclusively

**只限不等於尤其**

`exclusively` = only，排除其他選項；especially 不排除其他人／事物。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The offer is exclusively for members, but it is especially popular with students.

---

## 07 Generally

**一般而言方向相反**

`generally` 表示整體／通常；especially 是從整體中特別突出。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The region is generally warm, especially in summer.

---

## 08 Usually

**通常不等於尤其**

`usually` 表示頻率；especially 表示突出程度或選定項目。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

I usually travel by train, especially during winter.

## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** Particularly · 尤其；特別

### OPTIONS


- **A. particularly**

- **B. notably**

- **C. primarily**

- **D. mainly**

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Especially vs specially
- **especially** = 尤其、特別
- **specially** = 專門地、特地

Example:
`This guide is especially useful for beginners.`  
`This guide was specially written for beginners.`

---

## 02 Especially + adjective/adverb
常見：
- especially important
- especially useful
- especially difficult
- especially well

---

## 03 Especially for / among / when
- especially for children
- especially among younger users
- especially when prices are high

---

## 04 Particularly
和 especially 非常接近：
`The course is especially useful for beginners.`  
`The course is particularly useful for beginners.`

---

## 05 In particular
常放在突出具體名詞：
`I like science subjects, biology in particular.`

---

## 06 Mainly / primarily / chiefly
這三個重點是 **main proportion / main reason / main function**，
不能自動當作 especially。

---

## 07 Markedly / exceptionally
兩者主要表示程度：
- markedly higher
- exceptionally difficult

它們不適合一般列舉：
`I like fruit, especially mangoes.`  
不能自然改成 `markedly mangoes`。

---

## 08 Above all / most of all
兩者表示排序／優先：
- above all = 最重要的是
- most of all = 最喜歡／最重視的是

比 especially 更強。
