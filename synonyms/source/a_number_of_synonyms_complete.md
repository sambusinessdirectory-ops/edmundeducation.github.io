# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# A number of

**「一些／若干／多個」，究竟是哪一種數量？**

幾個、數個、多個、很多、一系列、大量、若干、各種——英文會按「數量大小、正式程度、是否強調種類、是否強調集合、是否可數」選用不同的表達。

> **核心原則**
>
> `a number of` 通常表示「一些、若干、多個」，後接 **可數名詞複數**。下面的表達不是全部都能在所有句子中直接互換；它們會因數量、語域、搭配及語義焦點而不同。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## A number of 的 14 種更精準說法

### 01 Several

**幾個；數個**

通常表示超過兩個，但不是很多。是最自然、最常見的替代之一。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Several students stayed behind after class to ask questions.

幾名學生下課後留下來問問題。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- several students
- several times
- several reasons
- several different options

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **several = more than two, but not a large number**

---

### 02 Multiple

**多個；多重的**

強調不只一個，常見於技術、商業、醫療和正式語境；有時比 `several` 更強調「多個獨立項目」。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The system experienced multiple failures during the test.

系統在測試期間出現多次故障。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- multiple options
- multiple causes
- multiple locations
- multiple users

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **multiple = more than one, often emphasizing separate instances or components**

---

### 03 Some

**一些；某些**

非常廣泛而自然，可表示數量不確定；比 `several` 更模糊。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Some employees chose to work from home.

一些員工選擇在家工作。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- some people
- some cases
- some problems
- some examples

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **some = an unspecified number or amount**

---

### 04 A few

**幾個；少量**

表示數量不多，但通常仍有正面含意：有一些，而不是完全沒有。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> A few customers reported problems with the new update.

有幾位顧客反映新更新出現問題。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- a few people
- a few days
- a few examples
- a few questions

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a few = a small number, but at least some**

---

### 05 Numerous

**眾多的；大量的**

比 `several` 強得多，表示數量很多，常見於正式或書面語。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The report identified numerous weaknesses in the current system.

報告指出現行系統有大量弱點。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- numerous examples
- numerous studies
- numerous problems
- numerous occasions

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **numerous = many; a relatively large number**

---

### 06 Many

**很多；許多**

最直接表示數量大的普通用語，比 `a number of` 更明確地強調「多」。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Many residents opposed the proposed development.

很多居民反對這項發展計劃。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- many people
- many reasons
- many years
- many different ways

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **many = a large number of countable things**

---

### 07 Various

**各種；不同的若干**

重點不只是數量，而是「種類不同」。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The issue has been discussed from various perspectives.

這個問題已從不同角度討論。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- various methods
- various reasons
- various countries
- various factors

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **various = several of different kinds**

---

### 08 A range of

**一系列；多種**

強調涵蓋一個範圍或多種選項，常見於產品、服務、因素、能力等。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The course covers a range of practical skills.

這課程涵蓋一系列實用技能。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- a range of options
- a range of services
- a range of issues
- a wide range of

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a range of = a set of different items spanning some variety**

---

### 09 A variety of

**各式各樣；多種**

強調多樣性，比 `a range of` 更直接突出「種類多」。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The market sells a variety of fresh vegetables.

市場售賣多種新鮮蔬菜。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- a variety of foods
- a variety of methods
- a variety of activities
- a wide variety of

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a variety of = many different kinds**

---

### 10 A series of

**一連串；一系列**

強調項目之間有順序、連續性或共同主題。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The company announced a series of changes to its hiring process.

公司宣布對招聘流程作出一系列改動。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- a series of events
- a series of meetings
- a series of changes
- a series of tests

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a series of = several related items or events in sequence**

---

### 11 A collection of

**一組；一批；集合**

強調多個項目被放在一起形成一個集合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The website contains a collection of historical photographs.

網站收錄了一批歷史照片。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- a collection of images
- a collection of essays
- a collection of objects
- a collection of resources

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a collection of = a group of items gathered together**

---

### 12 A set of

**一組；一套**

強調多個項目作為一個完整單位或彼此相關的一組。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Participants were given a set of instructions before the experiment.

參加者在實驗前獲發一套指示。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- a set of rules
- a set of instructions
- a set of tools
- a set of questions

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a set of = a defined group of related items**

---

### 13 A handful of

**少數幾個；寥寥數個**

比 `a few` 更帶有「真的不多」的感覺，較口語。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Only a handful of applicants had the required experience.

只有少數幾名申請人具備所需經驗。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- a handful of people
- a handful of cases
- a handful of examples
- only a handful of

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a handful of = a very small number**

---

### 14 A considerable number of

**相當多；為數不少**

比 `a number of` 更明確地表示數量相當大，較正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> A considerable number of residents attended the public meeting.

有相當多居民出席公眾會議。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- a considerable number of people
- a considerable number of cases
- a considerable number of studies
- a considerable number of responses

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a considerable number of = quite a large number**

# 02 False synonyms

## 01 Several / Many

### 幾個不等於很多

`several` 通常是少量到中等數量；`many` 明確表示數量大。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Several people asked questions.  
> Many people attended the event.

---

## 02 A few / Few

### 有幾個不等於幾乎沒有

`a few` 通常帶正面含意：「有一些」。  
`few` 常帶負面含意：「少得幾乎不夠」。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> A few students understood the task.  
> Few students understood the task.

---

## 03 Various / Several

### 多種類不等於單純數量

`several` 只說有幾個；`various` 強調這些項目彼此不同。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> We considered several options.  
> We considered various options with different costs and risks.

---

## 04 Multiple / Numerous

### 多個不等於大量

`multiple` 重點是「不只一個／多個獨立項目」；`numerous` 重點是數量很多。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The error had multiple causes.  
> The report listed numerous complaints.

---

## 05 A range of / A series of

### 範圍不等於順序

`a range of` 強調種類或涵蓋範圍；`a series of` 強調一連串相關項目或事件。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The shop offers a range of services.  
> The company held a series of meetings.

---

## 06 A variety of / A collection of

### 多樣性不等於集合

`a variety of` 強調不同種類；`a collection of` 強調項目被聚集在一起。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The restaurant serves a variety of dishes.  
> The museum owns a collection of paintings.

---

## 07 A handful of / A considerable number of

### 少數不等於相當多

> A handful of people complained.  
> A considerable number of people complained.

---

## 08 Number / Amount

### 可數名詞不等於不可數名詞

`a number of` 通常接可數名詞複數。  
`an amount of` 通常接不可數名詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> a number of problems  
> an amount of money

---

# 03 練習與解釋

> 每個目標表達兩題。請根據語境判斷 `a number of` 究竟表示幾個、多個、多種、一系列，還是大量。


## 練習 01.1

**Target phrase: Several · 幾個；數個**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> A number of students stayed behind after class to ask questions.

**中文翻譯**

幾名學生下課後留下來問問題。

| | 選項 |
|---|---|
| A | several |
| B | numerous |
| C | a series of |
| D | a collection of |
| E | amount |
| F | variety |

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**several · 幾個；數個**

> Several students stayed behind after class to ask questions.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. several ✓**  
表示不多不少的幾名學生，最自然。

**B. numerous**  
表示很多，語氣比原句強。

**C. a series of**  
學生不是按順序出現的一連串事件。

**D. a collection of**  
不自然地把學生視為「收藏／集合」。

**E. amount**  
通常用於不可數名詞。

**F. variety**  
強調種類，不是人數。

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

## Several vs Multiple

**several** = 幾個，數量焦點。  
**multiple** = 多個，常強調不只一個獨立實例、原因或組件。

> several meetings  
> multiple causes

## Several vs A few

**several** 通常比 `a few` 稍多或較中性。  
**a few** 更明顯表示「少量，但確實有一些」。

## Some vs Several

**some** = 數量不明，最模糊。  
**several** = 大致知道是幾個，而且通常超過兩個。

## Many vs Numerous

**many** = 最普通、最通用。  
**numerous** = 較正式、書面，常強調數量多。

## Various vs A variety of

**various** = 形容詞，直接修飾複數名詞。  
**a variety of** = 名詞片語，更明確突出多樣性。

## A range of vs A variety of

**a range of** = 強調涵蓋範圍或選擇跨度。  
**a variety of** = 強調種類多樣。

## A series of vs A set of

**a series of** = 有順序或連續性。  
**a set of** = 作為一組完整、相關的項目。

## A collection of vs A set of

**a collection of** = 被聚集在一起。  
**a set of** = 本身構成一套或完整組合。

## A handful of vs A few

**a handful of** 通常更強調「真的只有少數」。  
**a few** 較中性。

## Numerous vs A considerable number of

**numerous** 是形容詞，較簡潔。  
**a considerable number of** 是較正式的數量片語，明確表示為數不少。

---

# 06 Grammar & countability traps

## A number of + plural countable noun

正確：

> a number of students  
> a number of problems  
> a number of reasons

不自然／錯誤：

> a number of money ✗  
> a number of information ✗

對不可數名詞通常用：

- an amount of money
- a quantity of water
- some information
- a great deal of information

## Verb agreement

`a number of + plural noun` 通常接 **複數動詞**：

> A number of students **are** absent.

但：

`the number of + plural noun` 接 **單數動詞**：

> The number of students **is** increasing.

這是非常重要的文法區別。

| 表達 | 結構 | 注意 |
|---|---|---|

| some | some + plural noun | 也可接不可數名詞 |

| a collection of | a collection of + plural noun | 強調聚集 |

# 08 核心決策問題

1. **只是幾個，不算很多嗎？** → `several`
2. **重點是不只一個／有多個獨立項目嗎？** → `multiple`
3. **數量完全不需要具體嗎？** → `some`
4. **只是少量幾個嗎？** → `a few`
5. **數量很多，而且語氣較正式嗎？** → `numerous`
6. **只是普通地說很多嗎？** → `many`
7. **重點是種類不同嗎？** → `various`
8. **重點是涵蓋某個範圍嗎？** → `a range of`
9. **重點是多樣性嗎？** → `a variety of`
10. **這些項目是一連串相關事件嗎？** → `a series of`
11. **這些東西是被聚集在一起的一批嗎？** → `a collection of`
12. **這些東西構成一套完整組合嗎？** → `a set of`
13. **只有極少數嗎？** → `a handful of`
14. **數量相當多、為數不少嗎？** → `a considerable number of`

不要把以下表達當成可以機械替換 `a number of` 的完全同義詞：

`several / multiple / some / a few / numerous / many / various / a range of / a variety of / a series of / a collection of / a set of / a handful of / a considerable number of`

它們的共同點只是：

> **都可以在某些語境中表達「不止一個」或「若干數量／種類」。**

真正的選詞依據是：

> **Are you emphasizing quantity, smallness, largeness, variety, sequence, or grouping?**
