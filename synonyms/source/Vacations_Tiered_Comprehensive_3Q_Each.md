# VACATIONS · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Vacations

**Vacations 可以指度假、休假、短期休息、旅行、正式年假或旅遊統計中的 leisure trips。** 在英式／香港英語中，日常對應詞通常是 **holidays**；而職場制度語境更常用 **leave / time off**。

你提供的 **holidays; breaks; trips** 正好代表三個核心方向：**一般度假、休息期、旅行行程**。

本次保留 **9 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達。會特別區分 **public holidays / weekends / leave / trips / staycations**，避免把不同休假制度和旅行概念混為一談。共 **27 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Vacations 的語境 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|
| 英式一般度假／假期 | **holidays** |
| 短暫休息／學習間歇 | **breaks** |
| 實際旅行行程 | **trips** |
| 離開工作休息 | **time off** |
| 短期休閒度假 | **getaways** |
| 正式職場休假 | **leave** |
| 假期時段規劃 | **holiday periods** |
| 旅遊研究中的休閒旅行 | **leisure trips** |
| 抽象『離開日常一段時間』 | **periods away** |

## Tier 1 — Core holiday / break / trip equivalents · 核心假期、休息與旅行近義詞

### 01 Holidays

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：假期；度假  
**語域**：British English / very common  
**核心語義**：periods of time away from work or school, often used for leisure or travel  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **vacations** 在英式／香港英語中最直接、最自然的對應詞。  
**常見搭配**：go on holiday; summer holidays; holiday period; family holiday; holiday destination.  
**常見搭配對象**：family, school, work, travel, destination.  
**與 vacations 的辨析**：在英式英語中 holiday 常對應美式 vacation；vacation 在香港正式教育語境亦可見，但 holiday 更自然。  
**使用限制**：Public holiday 是法定／公眾假期，不等於所有 vacations。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Many families travel during the summer holidays.  
很多家庭會在暑假期間外遊。

### 02 Breaks

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：休假；休息期；短假  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：periods away from normal work, study, or routine for rest  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很自然，當 vacations 的重點是『暫停工作／學習休息』而不是旅遊時適合。  
**常見搭配**：take a break; school break; holiday break; short break; career break.  
**常見搭配對象**：work, school, study, routine, term.  
**與 vacations 的辨析**：Breaks 比 vacations 更廣，也可以很短，不一定涉及旅行或正式假期。  
**使用限制**：長期旅遊度假若重點是行程，trips/holidays 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Employees are encouraged to take regular breaks from work.  
公司鼓勵員工定期休假休息。

### 03 Trips

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：旅行；旅程  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：journeys away from home, often for leisure, business, or another purpose  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 適合當 vacations 的實際重點是『外出旅行』。  
**常見搭配**：holiday trip; family trip; overseas trip; weekend trip; take a trip.  
**常見搭配對象**：destination, family, weekend, overseas travel, journey.  
**與 vacations 的辨析**：Trip 強調 travel/journey；vacation 強調 time off。可以去旅行但不一定是度假。  
**使用限制**：留在家休假的 staycation 不宜改成 trip。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
They took several short trips during the school holidays.  
他們在學校假期期間去了幾次短途旅行。

### 04 Time off

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：休假時間；離開工作／學習的時間  
**語域**：neutral / workplace  
**核心語義**：time when someone is not required to work or carry out normal duties  
**用法提示**：當 vacations 指員工請假、休假安排，而不是旅遊活動時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：take time off; paid time off; time off work; book time off.  
**常見搭配對象**：employee, work, leave, schedule, manager.  
**與 vacations 的辨析**：Time off 強調不工作；vacation 可能包含旅遊和休閒。  
**使用限制**：學校長假或旅遊產品語境通常不用 time off。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She booked two weeks of time off in August.  
她在八月預訂了兩星期休假。

### 05 Getaways

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：短期度假；小旅行  
**語域**：informal / travel  
**核心語義**：short leisure trips taken to escape normal routine  
**用法提示**：當 vacations 是短途、放鬆型旅程時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：weekend getaway; romantic getaway; short getaway; beach getaway.  
**常見搭配對象**：weekend, couple, resort, beach, city.  
**與 vacations 的辨析**：Getaway 通常比 vacation 短，也更有『暫時逃離日常』語感。  
**使用限制**：長達數週的正式年假或學校假期不一定適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The hotel is popular for weekend getaways.  
這間酒店很受週末短假旅客歡迎。

## Tier 2 — Workplace / planning / travel-research variants · 職場、規劃與旅遊研究變體

### 06 Leave

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：假期；休假；請假  
**語域**：formal / workplace / administrative  
**核心語義**：officially authorised time away from work or duty  
**用法提示**：當 vacations 指員工制度內的正式休假時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：annual leave; paid leave; take leave; leave entitlement; leave request.  
**常見搭配對象**：employee, employer, workplace, entitlement, policy.  
**與 vacations 的辨析**：Leave 是行政／僱傭制度詞；vacation 更日常、更休閒。  
**使用限制**：英式／香港職場常用 annual leave；不要把 school holiday 一概叫 leave。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Employees receive twenty days of annual leave each year.  
員工每年可享有二十日年假。

### 07 Holiday periods

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：假期時段；假日期間  
**語域**：formal / planning / travel  
**核心語義**：defined periods during which people commonly take holidays or schools/workplaces are closed  
**用法提示**：適合交通、旅遊、零售、學校或行政規劃。  
**常見搭配**：holiday period; peak holiday period; school holiday period; during holiday periods.  
**常見搭配對象**：traffic, demand, school, travel, retail.  
**與 vacations 的辨析**：比 vacations 更 period-focused，不表示某人的個人旅程。  
**使用限制**：個人『去度假』不自然用 holiday period。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Traffic increases sharply during major holiday periods.  
主要假期時段的交通量會大幅上升。

### 08 Leisure trips

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：休閒旅行；度假旅程  
**語域**：formal / travel / research  
**核心語義**：trips taken primarily for recreation, relaxation, or pleasure  
**用法提示**：當 vacations 在旅遊研究、交通或市場數據中指 leisure travel 時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：leisure trip; leisure travel; domestic leisure trip; overnight leisure trip.  
**常見搭配對象**：traveller, tourism, destination, transport, accommodation.  
**與 vacations 的辨析**：Leisure trips 強調 trip purpose；vacations 還包括在家休假。  
**使用限制**：探親、商務或留在家中的假期不一定屬 leisure trip。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The survey records both business travel and leisure trips.  
調查同時記錄商務旅行和休閒旅行。

## Tier 3 — Abstract time-away paraphrase · 抽象離開日常的改寫

### 09 Periods away

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：離開日常環境的一段時間；休假期間  
**語域**：formal / explanatory  
**核心語義**：periods spent away from one's normal work, study, or home routine  
**用法提示**：這是 contextual paraphrase，適合抽象描述 vacations 對壓力、家庭生活或工作節奏的影響。  
**常見搭配**：periods away from work; time away; periods away from home; extended time away.  
**常見搭配對象**：work, home, routine, study, family.  
**與 vacations 的辨析**：範圍很廣，強調 absence from routine，而不是旅遊本身。  
**使用限制**：日常對話直接用 holidays / trips 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Regular periods away from work can help reduce stress.  
定期離開工作一段時間有助減輕壓力。

## 04 False synonyms / vacation traps · 容易誤選的休假與旅行陷阱

### 01 Public holidays

**公眾假期 ≠ vacations**  
public holidays 是法定／公眾假日，不等於個人度假或年假。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Public holidays are calendar-based.

### 02 Weekends

**週末 ≠ vacations**  
weekend 是固定每週休息日，不一定是 vacation。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Weekends are routine weekly time off.

### 03 Leave

**只在職場正式休假 sense 接近**  
leave 是制度性休假，不等於所有休閒旅行。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Leave is employment/administrative.

### 04 Trips

**只在旅行 sense 接近**  
trip 強調 journey，不等於在家休假。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Trips are travel-focused.

### 05 Breaks

**只在休息 sense 接近**  
break 可以很短，甚至只是幾分鐘。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Break is broader and often shorter.

### 06 Staycation

**在家度假 ≠ vacations 全部**  
staycation 是 vacation 的一個 subtype。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Staycation is a subtype.

### 07 Tourism

**旅遊業／旅遊活動 ≠ vacations**  
tourism 是整體產業或活動概念。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Tourism is broader.

### 08 Vacation / vocational

**詞形陷阱**  
vacation = 假期；vocational = 職業訓練相關。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Do not confuse vacation with vocational.

**A. Holidays** · 假期；度假

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
Families often take holidays during the school summer break.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Holidays ✓** — periods away from work or school for leisure, the usual British equivalent of vacations. Here, British/Hong Kong English favours holidays for leisure time away. That makes **Holidays** the most precise replacement for this particular sense of *vacations*.
- **B.** **Breaks** — periods away from normal work, study, or routine for rest. This is broader and can be very short; it does not necessarily involve a holiday or travel. The decisive clue is that British/Hong Kong English favours holidays for leisure time away.
- **C.** **Trips** — journeys away from home for leisure or another purpose. This focuses on journeys and does not include vacations spent at home. The decisive clue is that British/Hong Kong English favours holidays for leisure time away.
- **D.** **Public holidays** — official calendar holidays. These are official calendar holidays, not personal vacations. The decisive clue is that British/Hong Kong English favours holidays for leisure time away.
- **E.** **Weekends** — regular weekly days off. These are routine weekly days off rather than vacations. The decisive clue is that British/Hong Kong English favours holidays for leisure time away.
- **F.** **Leave** — officially authorised time away from work or duty. This is formal workplace/administrative language and too narrow outside employment contexts. The decisive clue is that British/Hong Kong English favours holidays for leisure time away.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **vacations vs holidays**：英式／香港一般最自然用 holidays。
- **vacations vs breaks**：break 可以很短，也不一定旅遊。
- **vacations vs trips**：trip 強調旅程；vacation 強調休假。
- **vacations vs time off**：time off 偏職場不工作。
- **vacations vs getaways**：getaway 通常是較短的休閒出走。
- **vacations vs leave**：leave 是正式行政／僱傭制度用語。
- **vacations vs public holidays**：public holidays 是法定／公眾假日。
- **vacations vs leisure trips**：leisure trips 是旅遊研究分類詞。
- **vacations vs weekends**：weekends 是每週固定休息日，不是 vacation。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & British-English traps

- 美式常說 **go on vacation / take a vacation**；英式／香港通常說 **go on holiday / take a holiday**。
- **holidays** 可指假期時段或實際度假：summer holidays / go on holiday。
- **annual leave**：英式／香港職場最自然的『年假』。
- **time off work**：較中性口語。
- **take a break**：可只是短休息，不一定是正式假期。
- **take a trip**：重點是旅行本身。
- **weekend getaway**：短期休閒旅行。
- **public holiday**：公眾／法定假期，不等於個人 annual leave。
- **staycation**：留在本地或家中度假的 subtype。
