# PUBLIC AWARENESS · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Public awareness

**「公眾認知／大眾意識」不是單一概念。** 有時只是知道議題存在，有時是理解內容，有時是識別症狀／標誌，有時是對某議題形成更廣泛的社會意識。因此替換前要先判斷 awareness 的深度和範圍。

本單元集中處理 **public awareness = the extent to which members of the public know about, recognise, understand, or are conscious of an issue**。你提供的 **general understanding; community awareness; public knowledge** 分別代表 **理解程度、社區範圍、事實知識** 三個核心方向。

本次保留 **10 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達。Tier 2–3 中部分詞是更窄的 measured/contextual variants，不會暗示它們可在任何句子中直接替換。共 **30 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Tier 1 — Core understanding / knowledge / recognition equivalents · 核心理解、知識與識別近義詞

### 01 General understanding

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：普遍理解；一般認識  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：the broad level of understanding that people in general have about an issue, idea, or subject  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 public awareness 的核心近義表達之一，尤其當重點是『大眾對某議題理解到甚麼程度』而不只是知道它存在。  
**常見搭配**：general understanding of; improve understanding; public understanding; level of understanding; broader understanding.  
**常見搭配對象**：issue, risk, policy, health, environment.  
**與 public awareness 的辨析**：General understanding 比 public awareness 更深入，包含一定程度的 comprehension，而不只是知道某事。  
**使用限制**：如果只是提高『知道某議題存在』，awareness 可能比 understanding 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The campaign improved general understanding of food-waste reduction.  
這項宣傳活動提升了大眾對減少食物浪費的普遍理解。

### 02 Community awareness

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：社區認知；社區意識  
**語域**：neutral / public-service  
**核心語義**：the level of awareness of an issue within a particular community  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，當 public awareness 實際指某個地區、社區或群體內的認知程度時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：raise community awareness; community awareness campaign; awareness among residents; local awareness; community education.  
**常見搭配對象**：resident, neighbourhood, health, safety, environment.  
**與 public awareness 的辨析**：Community awareness 比 public awareness 範圍更窄，限定於某個 community。  
**使用限制**：全國或廣泛公眾語境不宜縮窄成 community awareness。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The workshops raised community awareness of fire safety.  
這些工作坊提升了社區對消防安全的認知。

### 03 Public knowledge

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：公眾知識；大眾所知  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：the information or facts that are known by members of the public  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，當 public awareness 偏向『公眾知道哪些事實』時可用。  
**常見搭配**：public knowledge of; increase public knowledge; level of public knowledge; widely known; public information.  
**常見搭配對象**：fact, risk, symptom, rule, issue.  
**與 public awareness 的辨析**：Public knowledge 比 awareness 更 facts-based；awareness 可以只是知道議題存在或重要。  
**使用限制**：若重點是態度、關注程度或覺察，knowledge 未必準確。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Public knowledge of the warning signs remains limited.  
公眾對這些警告徵兆的知識仍然有限。

### 04 Public understanding

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：公眾理解；大眾認識  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：the extent to which the public understands a subject, issue, process, or policy  
**用法提示**：是 public awareness 很自然的近義詞，尤其適合科學、政策、健康和公共教育。  
**常見搭配**：public understanding of science; improve public understanding; level of public understanding; public understanding campaign.  
**常見搭配對象**：science, policy, health, process, risk.  
**與 public awareness 的辨析**：比 awareness 更強調理解內容、原因和意義。  
**使用限制**：如果只是能否識別／注意到某問題，awareness 更貼切。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The exhibition aims to improve public understanding of climate science.  
展覽旨在提升公眾對氣候科學的理解。

### 05 Public recognition

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：公眾認知；大眾識別  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：the extent to which members of the public recognise, identify, or acknowledge something  
**用法提示**：當 public awareness 的核心是『能否識別某症狀、標誌、問題或品牌』時可用。  
**常見搭配**：public recognition of; improve recognition; symptom recognition; widespread recognition; public recognition campaign.  
**常見搭配對象**：symptom, sign, issue, name, problem.  
**與 public awareness 的辨析**：Recognition 強調識別／認出，並不等於深入理解。  
**使用限制**：若要表達對複雜議題的理解，public understanding 更合適。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The campaign improved public recognition of stroke symptoms.  
這項宣傳活動提升了公眾對中風症狀的識別能力。

## Tier 2 — Familiarity / consciousness / measurement variants · 熟悉度、社會意識與量度型變體

### 06 Awareness among the public

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：公眾中的認知；大眾意識  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：the extent to which people in the public are aware of a particular issue  
**用法提示**：這是 public awareness 的透明改寫，適合需要避免名詞堆疊或調整句子節奏時。  
**常見搭配**：awareness among the public; awareness among residents; raise awareness; level of awareness; public campaign.  
**常見搭配對象**：issue, risk, disease, service, policy.  
**與 public awareness 的辨析**：語義幾乎等同 public awareness，只是 syntactic paraphrase。  
**使用限制**：較冗長，通常不是最精煉選擇。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Awareness among the public increased after the television campaign.  
電視宣傳後，公眾中的認知程度有所提升。

### 07 Public familiarity

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：公眾熟悉程度；大眾熟悉度  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：the degree to which the public is familiar with a topic, service, idea, or term  
**用法提示**：當 public awareness 表示『對某東西有沒有聽過、熟不熟』而不是深入理解時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：public familiarity with; increase familiarity; level of familiarity; familiar to the public; public familiarity survey.  
**常見搭配對象**：service, term, concept, technology, programme.  
**與 public awareness 的辨析**：Familiarity 比 awareness 更聚焦熟悉度，但不保證理解正確。  
**使用限制**：若議題涉及風險覺察或行動意識，familiarity 可能太弱。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Public familiarity with the new recycling labels is still low.  
公眾對新的回收標籤仍不太熟悉。

### 08 Public consciousness

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：公眾意識；社會意識  
**語域**：formal / abstract  
**核心語義**：a broad collective awareness or concern about a social, political, environmental, or ethical issue  
**用法提示**：當 public awareness 帶『社會整體開始重視／意識到』意味時可用。  
**常見搭配**：public consciousness of; raise public consciousness; environmental consciousness; social consciousness; collective consciousness.  
**常見搭配對象**：environment, inequality, health, rights, ethics.  
**與 public awareness 的辨析**：Public consciousness 比 awareness 更抽象，也常帶 collective concern / social salience。  
**使用限制**：具體事實知識或症狀識別不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The documentary helped raise public consciousness of plastic pollution.  
這部紀錄片有助提升社會對塑膠污染的意識。

### 09 Level of public awareness

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：公眾認知水平；大眾意識程度  
**語域**：formal / research / reporting  
**核心語義**：the measured degree to which the public is aware of a topic or issue  
**用法提示**：當 public awareness 出現在調查、研究、報告或 before/after comparison 中，這個表達非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：level of public awareness; measure awareness; awareness level; survey awareness; increase awareness levels.  
**常見搭配對象**：survey, campaign, risk, disease, policy.  
**與 public awareness 的辨析**：這是 measurement-focused contextual paraphrase，強調程度／水平。  
**使用限制**：一般日常句子直接用 public awareness 更簡潔。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The survey measured the level of public awareness before and after the campaign.  
調查量度宣傳活動前後的公眾認知水平。

## Tier 3 — Understanding-plus-value variant · 理解兼重視型變體

### 10 Public appreciation

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：公眾理解與重視；大眾體會  
**語域**：formal / contextual  
**核心語義**：the extent to which the public understands and values the importance or significance of something  
**用法提示**：當 public awareness 不只表示知道，而是『理解並重視其重要性』時可用。  
**常見搭配**：public appreciation of; greater appreciation; appreciation of importance; enhance appreciation; public value.  
**常見搭配對象**：heritage, culture, science, conservation, risk.  
**與 public awareness 的辨析**：Appreciation 比 awareness 多一層 understanding + valuing。  
**使用限制**：不能用於純粹識別某事存在或記住事實。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The exhibition increased public appreciation of local heritage.  
這個展覽提升了公眾對本地文化遺產的理解與重視。

## 04 False synonyms / public-awareness traps · 容易誤選的公眾認知概念陷阱

### 01 Public opinion

**公眾意見 ≠ public awareness**  
opinion 是人們的看法或立場；awareness 是是否知道／理解某議題。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Opinion and awareness measure different things.

### 02 Publicity

**宣傳 ≠ public awareness**  
publicity 是宣傳活動或曝光；它可以提高 awareness，但不是 awareness 本身。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Publicity may cause awareness.

### 03 Attention

**注意 ≠ awareness**  
attention 是當下注意力；awareness 可以持續存在。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Attention is momentary focus.

### 04 Knowledge

**知識不一定等於 awareness**  
knowledge 要求知道內容；awareness 可以只是知道問題存在。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Knowledge is usually deeper.

### 05 Recognition

**識別不等於完整 awareness**  
recognition 是認得／識別，未必理解。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Recognition is narrower than broad awareness.

### 06 Familiarity

**熟悉不等於理解**  
familiarity 可以只是聽過或見過，不代表理解。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Familiarity can be shallow.

### 07 Education

**教育 ≠ awareness**  
education 是提高 awareness/knowledge 的過程，不是結果本身。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Education is an intervention.

### 08 Aware / awareness

**詞性陷阱**  
aware 是形容詞；awareness 是名詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Aware and awareness have different grammatical roles.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: General understanding · 普遍理解；一般認識

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The campaign improved public awareness of how household energy use affects emissions.

- B. Public understanding

- E. Attention
- F. Publicity

**A. General understanding** · 普遍理解；一般認識

The campaign improved general understanding of how household energy use affects emissions.

In this sentence, the sentence involves comprehension of how something works, not merely noticing the issue. That makes **General understanding** the most precise replacement for this particular sense of *public awareness*.
- **B.** **Public understanding** — means the extent to which the public understands an issue, process or subject. It is related to what the public knows, notices or thinks, but it does not match the decisive clue here: the sentence involves comprehension of how something works, not merely noticing the issue.
- **C.** **Public knowledge** — means factual information known by the public; it is stronger and more content-based than simple awareness. It is related to what the public knows, notices or thinks, but it does not match the decisive clue here: the sentence involves comprehension of how something works, not merely noticing the issue.
- **D.** **Public opinion** — means what the public thinks or believes as an attitude or position, not whether they are aware. It is related to what the public knows, notices or thinks, but it does not match the decisive clue here: the sentence involves comprehension of how something works, not merely noticing the issue.
- **E.** **Attention** — means current focus and is more momentary than awareness. It is related to what the public knows, notices or thinks, but it does not match the decisive clue here: the sentence involves comprehension of how something works, not merely noticing the issue.
- **F.** **Publicity** — means promotional exposure or media attention and can increase awareness without being awareness itself. It is related to what the public knows, notices or thinks, but it does not match the decisive clue here: the sentence involves comprehension of how something works, not merely noticing the issue.

| 整體社會意識／重視 | **public consciousness** | 是否 broad collective social concern？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **public awareness vs public understanding**: understanding 比 awareness 更深入，要求理解內容／原因。
- **public awareness vs public knowledge**: knowledge 更 facts-based。
- **public awareness vs community awareness**: community awareness 範圍較窄，只限某社區。
- **public awareness vs public recognition**: recognition 只強調識別／認出。
- **public awareness vs public familiarity**: familiarity 可以只是聽過／見過，不保證理解。
- **public awareness vs public consciousness**: consciousness 更抽象，常指社會整體意識。
- **public awareness vs public opinion**: opinion 是立場／看法，不是是否知道。
- **public awareness vs publicity**: publicity 是宣傳手段，不是認知結果。
- **public awareness vs public appreciation**: appreciation 包含理解並重視其價值／重要性。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & awareness traps · 文法、搭配與『公眾認知』陷阱

- **public awareness of + noun**：public awareness of road safety。
- **raise / increase / improve public awareness** 都很常見。
- **public understanding of + complex subject**：適合 science / policy / process。
- **public knowledge of + facts/rules/symptoms**：偏事實知識。
- **public recognition of + sign/symptom/logo**：偏識別。
- **community awareness of + issue**：scope 要明確是某社區。
- **public familiarity with + service/term/label**：常用介詞 **with**。
- **public consciousness of + social issue**：較正式、抽象。
- **level of public awareness** 很適合 survey / report / before-and-after comparison。
- **aware** 是形容詞；**awareness** 是名詞；不要把 **attention, publicity, opinion** 當同義詞。
- 英式／香港拼寫保持 **programme, organisation**。
