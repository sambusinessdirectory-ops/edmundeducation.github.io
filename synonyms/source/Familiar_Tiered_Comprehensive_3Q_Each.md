# FAMILIAR · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Familiar

**「熟悉的／眼熟的／習慣的／廣為人知的」，要先分清是『很多人都知道』、『一眼認得』、『對某主題有知識』，還是『因反覆經歷而習慣』。**

本單元集中處理 **familiar = known from previous experience, easily recognised, or well understood because of prior contact**。你提供的 **well-known; recognisable; acquainted with** 分別代表 **廣為人知、能辨認、已有一定認識** 三個核心方向。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且高實用度的詞／表達，並按最新 inspection 標準區分 **known / accustomed to / conversant with / knowledgeable about / used to / no stranger to / versed in / at home with / familiar-looking**。每個目標設 **3 道題**；多義 sense 會清楚分層，避免把 famous、aware of、used to 等機械互換。

## Tier 1 — Core recognition / knowledge / habituation equivalents · 核心認知、熟悉與習慣近義詞

### 01 Well-known

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：廣為人知的；著名的  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：known by many people or widely recognised within a group, place, or field  
**用法提示**：當 familiar 的意思是『很多人都知道』而不是『我個人熟悉』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：well-known author; well-known landmark; well-known example; well-known fact; well-known brand.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, place, fact, example, brand.  
**與 familiar 的辨析**：Well-known 強調很多人知道；familiar 可以只是對某個人來說熟悉。  
**使用限制**：若只表示某人認得一張臉或聲音，recognisable / familiar 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The building is a well-known landmark in the city centre.  
這座建築是市中心廣為人知的地標。

### 02 Recognisable

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：可辨認的；容易認出的  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：able to be identified because its appearance, sound, form, or features are known  
**用法提示**：當 familiar 指某張臉、聲音、標誌、旋律或外形『一看／一聽便認得』時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：recognisable face; recognisable voice; instantly recognisable; recognisable logo; recognisable style.  
**常見搭配對象**：face, voice, logo, tune, style.  
**與 familiar 的辨析**：Recognisable 強調『認得出』；familiar 也可指知識、經驗或環境上的熟悉。  
**使用限制**：對某個學科很熟悉不能只說 recognisable。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Her voice is instantly recognisable.  
她的聲音一聽便能認出。

### 03 Known

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：已知的；為人所知的  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：recognised, understood, or already known to a person or group  
**用法提示**：當 familiar 只是表示『不是陌生的／已知的』時可用，尤其搭配 known to。  
**常見搭配**：known to staff; known to residents; previously known; widely known; known issue.  
**常見搭配對象**：fact, issue, person, place, procedure.  
**與 familiar 的辨析**：Known 是更一般的『已知』；familiar 常帶反覆接觸或認得的感覺。  
**使用限制**：不要把 known 一概當作 famous。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The problem is already known to the support team.  
支援團隊已知道這個問題。

### 04 Acquainted with

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：熟悉；略有認識；有所接觸  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：having some knowledge or experience of a person, subject, situation, or thing  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很自然，尤其用於『對某人／某主題已有一定認識』。  
**常見搭配**：acquainted with the facts; acquainted with the system; become acquainted with; well acquainted with; personally acquainted with.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, facts, system, procedure, subject.  
**與 familiar 的辨析**：Acquainted with 通常較正式，也常表示有一定但未必深入的認識。  
**使用限制**：日常簡單說 familiar with 往往更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
All new staff should become acquainted with the safety procedures.  
所有新員工都應熟悉安全程序。

### 05 Accustomed to

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：習慣於；熟習於  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：used to something because of repeated experience, so that it no longer feels unusual  
**用法提示**：當 familiar 的核心不是『認得』，而是因重複經歷而『習慣』時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：accustomed to working; accustomed to noise; become accustomed to; accustomed to the routine; fully accustomed to.  
**常見搭配對象**：routine, noise, weather, workload, environment.  
**與 familiar 的辨析**：Accustomed to 強調習慣；familiar with 強調認識／熟悉。  
**使用限制**：熟悉一個人的名字不等於 accustomed to。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She is accustomed to working under tight deadlines.  
她已習慣在緊迫期限下工作。

## Tier 2 — Professional knowledge / experience variants · 專業知識與經驗型變體

### 06 Conversant with

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：熟悉；通曉  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：having a good working knowledge of a subject, system, method, or set of rules  
**用法提示**：當 familiar 指對專業主題、規則、系統有實際掌握時很合適。  
**常見搭配**：conversant with the law; conversant with procedures; fully conversant with; conversant with software; conversant with regulations.  
**常見搭配對象**：law, procedure, software, regulation, system.  
**與 familiar 的辨析**：Conversant with 比 familiar with 更正式，也通常暗示實用程度較高。  
**使用限制**：一般認得某個地方或面孔時不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Applicants should be conversant with current safety regulations.  
申請人應熟悉現行安全規例。

### 07 Well acquainted with

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：十分熟悉；對……很了解  
**語域**：formal / neutral  
**核心語義**：having considerable knowledge or experience of someone or something  
**用法提示**：當 familiar 的程度較深，且要強調長期接觸或較充分認識時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：well acquainted with the facts; well acquainted with local conditions; well acquainted with the system; personally acquainted; thoroughly acquainted.  
**常見搭配對象**：facts, conditions, person, system, issue.  
**與 familiar 的辨析**：比 acquainted with 更強，通常表示已有相當程度的熟悉。  
**使用限制**：口語可直接說 very familiar with。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She is well acquainted with the challenges facing small businesses.  
她十分熟悉小型企業面對的挑戰。

### 08 Knowledgeable about

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：對……有知識；熟悉  
**語域**：neutral / professional  
**核心語義**：having substantial information, understanding, or expertise about a subject  
**用法提示**：當 familiar 涉及知識層面的了解，而不是單純見過／聽過時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：knowledgeable about technology; knowledgeable about local history; highly knowledgeable; knowledgeable staff; knowledgeable about policy.  
**常見搭配對象**：technology, history, policy, product, subject.  
**與 familiar 的辨析**：Knowledgeable about 強調知識量；familiar with 可以只是基本接觸。  
**使用限制**：對某個聲音『聽起來熟悉』不能用 knowledgeable about。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The guide was knowledgeable about local history.  
導遊對本地歷史十分熟悉。

### 09 Used to

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：習慣於；熟習於  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：accustomed to something through repeated experience  
**用法提示**：當 familiar 表示『已經習慣這種情況』時，used to 是最常見口語替代。  
**常見搭配**：used to the noise; used to working late; get used to; used to the routine; used to change.  
**常見搭配對象**：noise, routine, weather, schedule, situation.  
**與 familiar 的辨析**：Used to 強調習慣；familiar with 更偏知道／認識。  
**使用限制**：注意 **be used to + noun/-ing** 與過去習慣 **used to + verb** 不同。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He is used to working with international teams.  
他已習慣與國際團隊合作。

### 10 No stranger to

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：對……並不陌生；熟悉  
**語域**：idiomatic / neutral  
**核心語義**：having considerable previous experience of something, often something difficult or notable  
**用法提示**：當 familiar 是因為過往多次經歷而『並不陌生』時很地道。  
**常見搭配**：no stranger to pressure; no stranger to hard work; no stranger to controversy; no stranger to change; no stranger to criticism.  
**常見搭配對象**：pressure, challenge, change, criticism, controversy.  
**與 familiar 的辨析**：No stranger to 強調過往經驗；familiar with 不一定親身經歷多次。  
**使用限制**：通常不用於中性小知識，例如 *no stranger to this word* 會很奇怪。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The team is no stranger to working under pressure.  
這個團隊對在壓力下工作並不陌生。

## Tier 3 — Advanced / idiomatic / visual variants · 深度熟悉、慣用與視覺辨認變體

### 11 Versed in

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：精通；熟悉  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：having strong knowledge or skill in a subject, field, or practice  
**用法提示**：當 familiar with 接近『很熟悉／頗精通』時可用，尤見於正式專業語境。  
**常見搭配**：well versed in; versed in law; versed in technology; versed in procedure; versed in classical literature.  
**常見搭配對象**：law, technology, literature, procedure, field.  
**與 familiar 的辨析**：Versed in 比 familiar with 更強，接近有相當專業知識。  
**使用限制**：只略知一二時不宜用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She is well versed in employment law.  
她十分熟悉僱傭法。

### 12 At home with

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：對……十分自在／熟悉；駕輕就熟  
**語域**：idiomatic / neutral  
**核心語義**：comfortable and confident in dealing with a subject, tool, environment, or type of task  
**用法提示**：當 familiar 不只是知道，而且使用起來很自在、有把握時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：at home with technology; at home with numbers; at home with complex ideas; feel at home; equally at home with.  
**常見搭配對象**：technology, numbers, language, system, task.  
**與 familiar 的辨析**：At home with 加入『自在、有信心』的語氣。  
**使用限制**：正式法律文件通常不用這個慣用語。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She is equally at home with data and visual design.  
她對數據和視覺設計都駕輕就熟。

### 13 Familiar-looking

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：看起來眼熟的；似曾相識的  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：having an appearance that seems known or previously seen, even if the person cannot immediately identify it  
**用法提示**：當 familiar 專指外表『眼熟』而未必能立即認出身份時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：familiar-looking face; familiar-looking building; familiar-looking logo; vaguely familiar-looking; strangely familiar-looking.  
**常見搭配對象**：face, building, logo, object, street.  
**與 familiar 的辨析**：Familiar-looking 比 recognisable 更弱：可能眼熟，但未必能確定是誰。  
**使用限制**：知識、技能或制度上的熟悉不能這樣用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
A familiar-looking face appeared in the crowd.  
人群中出現了一張眼熟的臉。

## 04 False synonyms / familiarity traps · 容易誤選的詞與「熟悉」陷阱

### 01 Famous

**著名不等於 familiar**  
famous 表示很多人知道；familiar 可以只對某一個人熟悉。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Something can be familiar to you without being famous.

### 02 Popular

**受歡迎不等於 familiar**  
popular 表示很多人喜歡或使用；熟悉度和受歡迎程度不同。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Popularity is about approval or use, not recognition.

### 03 Friend

**朋友不等於 familiar**  
friend 是人際關係；你可以熟悉某人但並非朋友。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Familiarity does not imply friendship.

### 04 Aware of

**知道不等於熟悉**  
aware of 只表示知道某事存在；familiar with 通常暗示較多接觸或知識。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Awareness is weaker than familiarity.

### 05 Understand

**理解不等於 familiar**  
understand 表示明白意思或機制；你可以熟悉某事但未真正理解它。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Familiarity and understanding overlap but are not identical.

### 06 Identical

**相同不等於 familiar**  
identical 表示完全一樣；familiar 表示曾接觸或能認得。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Identical concerns sameness, not recognition.

### 07 Recognisable

**recognisable 只在外觀／聲音 sense 接近 familiar**  
recognisable 表示能認出；對規則、系統或主題熟悉時通常不用。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Recognisable is about identification; familiar is broader.

### 08 Familiarity / familiar

**詞性陷阱**  
familiar 是形容詞；familiarity 是名詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Familiar is an adjective; familiarity is a noun.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Well-known · 廣為人知的；著名的

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The building is a familiar landmark throughout the city.

**中文翻譯**  
這座建築是全市廣為人知的地標。

- A. well-known
- B. popular
- C. identical
- D. friendly
- E. aware
- F. understood

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**well-known** · 廣為人知的；著名的

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The building is a well-known landmark throughout the city.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. well-known ✓ — throughout the city means many people know the landmark, so well-known is precise。**
- **B. popular — popular means liked, not necessarily known by everyone。**
- **C. identical — identical means exactly the same as something else。**
- **D. friendly — friendly describes behaviour or atmosphere, not public recognition。**
- **E. aware — aware cannot naturally modify landmark this way。**
- **F. understood — understood refers to comprehension, not public familiarity.**

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **familiar vs well-known**: well-known 是很多人知道；familiar 可以只對某人熟悉。
- **familiar vs recognisable**: recognisable 強調能認出外觀／聲音。
- **familiar vs known**: known 是一般『已知』；familiar 常帶先前接觸感。
- **familiar vs acquainted with**: acquainted with 較正式，通常表示已有一些認識。
- **familiar vs accustomed to**: accustomed to 是習慣，不是知識。
- **familiar vs conversant with**: conversant with 更正式，也通常要求較實用的掌握。
- **familiar vs knowledgeable about**: knowledgeable about 強調知識量。
- **familiar vs used to**: used to 是口語的『已習慣』sense。
- **familiar vs aware of**: aware of 只表示知道存在，通常比 familiar 弱。
- **familiar vs famous**: famous 是公開名氣；familiar 不需要任何名氣。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & familiarity traps · 文法、搭配與熟悉程度陷阱

- **familiar with + thing/topic**：I am familiar with the system。
- **familiar to + person**：The voice sounds familiar to me。
- 這兩個結構不要混淆：**I am familiar with it** / **It is familiar to me**。
- **well-known for + reason** / **well-known as + role**：well-known for research / well-known as a writer。
- **recognisable by/from + feature**：recognisable by its logo。
- **acquainted with + person/topic**：acquainted with the facts。
- **accustomed to + noun/-ing**：accustomed to noise / working late。
- **conversant with + subject/system**：conversant with current regulations。
- **knowledgeable about + topic**：knowledgeable about local history。
- **be used to + noun/-ing** 表示『習慣於』；**used to + base verb** 表示過去習慣：I used to work nights。
- **no stranger to + noun/-ing**：no stranger to pressure / working late。
- **(well) versed in + field**：well versed in law。
- **familiarity** 是名詞：familiarity with the system。
- **famous / popular / aware / understand ≠ familiar**：各自只在特定語境相關，不能機械替換。
