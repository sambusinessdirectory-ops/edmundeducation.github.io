# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Extreme (adjective)

**`extreme` 可以表示非常嚴重、強度極高、影響深遠、大幅劇烈、過度、急性嚴重、深刻，或根本徹底。不同替代表達的核心差別，是『severity / intensity / reach / scale of change / excess / urgency / depth / fundamental change』。**

核心替代表達：

`severe / intense / far-reaching / drastic / excessive / acute / profound / radical`

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Severe

**嚴重的；劇烈的**

當 `extreme` 指程度很嚴重、後果很大或情況很惡劣時，`severe` 很自然。常見於 weather, pain, damage, shortage, punishment。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The region experienced severe flooding after days of rain.

連日大雨後，該地區出現嚴重水浸。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **very serious, harsh, or damaging in degree**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 02 Intense

**強烈的；劇烈的**

當 `extreme` 指感覺、熱力、壓力、競爭、活動或情緒強度很高時，`intense` 很精準。它偏 strength rather than damage。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The athletes trained under intense pressure.

運動員在極大壓力下訓練。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **very strong in degree, force, or concentration**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 03 Far-reaching

**影響深遠的；波及廣泛的**

當 `extreme` 的實際意思是影響範圍非常廣、後果延伸很遠時，`far-reaching` 很合適。它不是一般強度詞，而是 effect / consequence 詞。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The policy could have far-reaching consequences for public services.

這項政策可能對公共服務造成深遠影響。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **having effects that extend widely or for a long time**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、政策、新聞

---

## 04 Drastic

**劇烈的；大刀闊斧的**

當 `extreme` 指改變、措施、削減或行動幅度非常大，而且明顯超出一般程度時，`drastic` 很自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The company introduced drastic cost-cutting measures.

公司推出大幅削減成本的措施。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **very large, sudden, or forceful in effect**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、新聞、工作

---

## 05 Excessive

**過度的；過量的**

當 `extreme` 指某種數量、程度或行為超出合理／正常範圍時，`excessive` 最精準。它帶明確負面判斷。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Excessive screen time can affect sleep.

過度使用屏幕可能影響睡眠。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **more than is reasonable, necessary, or healthy**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、一般

---

## 06 Acute

**嚴重而急性的；劇烈的**

當 `extreme` 指醫療症狀、痛楚、短缺或危機非常嚴重而且往往突然／急迫時，`acute` 很合適。它常有專門語域。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The patient was admitted with acute chest pain.

病人因急性劇烈胸痛入院。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **very severe and often sudden or urgent**

**REGISTER · 語域**

醫療、正式

---

## 07 Profound

**深刻的；極大的**

當 `extreme` 指影響、變化、差異、情感或理解非常深刻，而不是表面程度大時，`profound` 很自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The experience had a profound effect on her outlook.

這段經歷對她的人生觀產生深刻影響。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **very deep, significant, or far-reaching in effect**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、學術、書面

---

## 08 Radical

**徹底的；根本性的**

當 `extreme` 指改革、改變、主張或方法從根本上偏離現狀，`radical` 很精準。它強調 fundamental change rather than mere intensity。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The proposal calls for radical reform of the system.

這項建議要求對制度作根本改革。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **fundamental and far from conventional or moderate approaches**

**REGISTER · 語域**

政策、學術、正式

# 02 False synonyms

## Extreme / Severe
`severe` 強調情況、傷害、短缺、天氣或症狀非常嚴重。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The area faced extreme flooding.  
The area experienced severe flooding after days of rain.

## Extreme / Intense
`intense` 強調強度，不一定表示危害或負面後果。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The heat was extreme.  
The athletes trained in intense heat.

## Extreme / Far-reaching
`far-reaching` 不表示「強度很高」，而表示影響延伸得很廣、很久。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The reform was extreme.  
The reform could have far-reaching consequences.

## Extreme / Drastic
`drastic` 常用於 cuts, changes, measures, action，表示幅度非常大。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The response was extreme.  
The company introduced drastic cost-cutting measures.

## Extreme / Excessive
`excessive` 明確表示「超過合理／必要程度」，帶負面判斷。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The noise was extreme.  
The machine produced excessive noise for a residential area.

## Extreme / Acute
`acute` 常見於 medical symptoms, shortages, crises，帶嚴重而急迫／突然的意味。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The pain was extreme.  
He was admitted with acute chest pain.

## Extreme / Profound
`profound` 強調深度，適合 effect, change, sadness, influence, understanding。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The experience had an extreme effect on her.  
The experience had a profound effect on her outlook.

## Extreme / Radical
`radical` 強調根本性、從根源改變，常見於 reform, solution, departure, proposal。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The proposal was extreme.  
The proposal calls for radical reform of the system.

# 04 Target-word / phrase contrast map

| `extreme` 真正重點 | 較精準替代表達 |
|---|---|
| 嚴重、危害程度高 | **severe** |
| 強度非常高 | **intense** |
| 影響延伸很廣／很久 | **far-reaching** |
| 改變／措施幅度非常大 | **drastic** |
| 超過合理／必要程度 | **excessive** |
| 嚴重而急迫／急性 | **acute** |
| 影響／情感非常深刻 | **profound** |
| 從根本改變現狀 | **radical** |

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Severe vs Intense** — severe 偏嚴重／危害；intense 偏強度。
- **Drastic vs Radical** — drastic = 幅度大；radical = 根本性改變。
- **Far-reaching vs Profound** — far-reaching = 影響範圍／時間；profound = 影響深度。
- **Excessive vs Intense** — excessive = 超出合理水平；intense = 很強但未必過度。
- **Acute vs Severe** — acute 常兼具急迫／突然／醫療色彩；severe 只表示程度嚴重。
- **Extreme ≠ maximum in every context** — extreme 是高程度／遠離常態，不一定是數學上的 maximum。
- **Extreme ≠ far-reaching in every context** — far-reaching 只適合 effects, consequences, implications 等。
- **Extreme ≠ radical in every context** — radical 特別指根本性改變或非傳統主張。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

- **severe** = adjective；**severity** = noun；**severely** = adverb
- **intense** = adjective；**intensity** = noun；**intensely** = adverb
- **far-reaching** 固定常用連字號
- **drastic** = adjective；**drastically** = adverb
- **excessive** = adjective；**excess** = noun；**excessively** = adverb
- **acute** = adjective；**acutely** = adverb
- **profound** = adjective；**profoundly** = adverb；**profundity** = noun
- **radical** = adjective；**radically** = adverb；**radicalism** = noun

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 重點是嚴重、危害大？ → **severe**
2. 是熱力／壓力／情緒／競爭強度高？ → **intense**
3. 是 consequences / effects / implications 延伸很廣？ → **far-reaching**
4. 是 cuts / changes / measures 幅度非常大？ → **drastic**
5. 是超出合理／健康程度？ → **excessive**
6. 是醫療／短缺／危機中的嚴重急迫狀態？ → **acute**
7. 是影響／情感／理解非常深？ → **profound**
8. 是根本改革／徹底偏離現狀？ → **radical**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`severe / intense / far-reaching / drastic / excessive / acute / profound / radical`

最重要的一點：

> **severe ≠ intense ≠ radical**

`severe` = 嚴重程度；  
`intense` = 強度；  
`radical` = 根本性改變。
