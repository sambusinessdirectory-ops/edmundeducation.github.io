# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Entire life

「一生／整個人生」，究竟是哪一種 `entire life`？

一生、終其一生、整個人生、從出生到現在、整個生命歷程、畢生——英文會按**是否強調完整人生長度、截至目前的人生、從出生至死亡的整個跨度、某人長期持續的經歷、還是帶較文學／正式語氣**選用不同的詞和常見表達。

> Format: **14 curated alternatives + 8 false-synonym contrasts + 3 exercises per target synonym (42 questions total)**.  
> 每題包含自然原句、中文翻譯、六個選項、雙語正確答案、改寫句及 A–F 詳細解釋。

# 01 同義詞指南

## Entire life 的 14 種更精準說法

### 01 Whole life
**一生；整個人生**

最直接、最自然的近義詞之一，表示一個人的全部人生。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She has lived in the same town her whole life.

她一生都住在同一個小鎮。

**USAGE CLUE**

- one's whole life
- very common
- neutral and natural

---

### 02 All of one's life
**一生；終其一生**

表示在整段人生中一直如此，語氣稍比 `whole life` 完整、書面。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He had wanted to be a teacher all of his life.

他一生都想成為老師。

**USAGE CLUE**

- all of his/her life
- lifelong duration
- often with perfect or past-perfect meaning

---

### 03 All one's life
**一輩子；一生**

非常自然、常用，與 `all of one's life` 幾乎相同，但語氣更簡潔。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She has loved music all her life.

她一生都熱愛音樂。

**USAGE CLUE**

- all my life / all her life
- idiomatic and high-frequency
- no “of” required

---

### 04 Lifelong
**終身的；一生的**

形容詞，表示某種興趣、習慣、關係或狀態持續一生。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He developed a lifelong interest in history.

他對歷史產生了終身興趣。

**USAGE CLUE**

- lifelong friend / interest / habit
- adjective
- not a noun phrase replacement in every sentence

---

### 05 Throughout one's life
**一生之中；終其一生**

較正式、書面，強調某事在整段人生不同時期持續或反覆出現。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She remained curious throughout her life.

她終其一生都保持好奇心。

**USAGE CLUE**

- formal-neutral
- repeated/continuous over life stages
- throughout + possessive + life

---

### 06 Over the course of one's life
**在人生歷程中；一生之中**

強調人生是一段逐步發展的過程，常用於變化、累積、經驗和學習。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> People accumulate many experiences over the course of their lives.

人們在人生歷程中累積許多經驗。

**USAGE CLUE**

- developmental / analytical
- change over time
- formal

---

### 07 For one's entire lifetime
**終其一生；整個生命期間**

正式而完整，明確表示從生命開始到生命結束的整段時間。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The condition affected him for his entire lifetime.

這個病況影響了他整整一生。

**USAGE CLUE**

- full duration
- formal
- lifetime-focused

---

### 08 For one's whole lifetime
**整個一生；一輩子**

與 `for one's entire lifetime` 接近，但稍自然口語一點。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She carried the memory with her for her whole lifetime.

她一生都帶着這段記憶。

**USAGE CLUE**

- lifetime duration
- slightly less formal than “entire lifetime”
- emphasizes full span

---

### 09 From birth to death
**從出生到死亡；一生**

最明確地表示整個生命跨度，語氣直接、較正式或描述性。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The biography follows him from birth to death.

這本傳記記錄他從出生到死亡的一生。

**USAGE CLUE**

- full chronological span
- biography / history
- explicit endpoints

---

### 10 From cradle to grave
**從搖籃到墳墓；一生**

慣用語，表示從出生到死亡，較形象、帶文學或修辭色彩。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The system was designed to support citizens from cradle to grave.

這套制度旨在從出生到死亡支援公民。

**USAGE CLUE**

- idiomatic
- figurative
- policy / rhetorical / literary

---

### 11 Lifetime
**一生；終身**

名詞，可表示一個人的整個生命期間。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He achieved more in one lifetime than most people could imagine.

他在一生中取得的成就超乎大多數人想像。

**USAGE CLUE**

- in a lifetime
- during one's lifetime
- noun, not adjective

---

### 12 Life span
**一生的跨度；生命期間**

較中性、分析性，表示某人的生命長度或某事在生命中的整體跨度。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The study examines changes across the human life span.

研究探討人類一生不同階段的變化。

**USAGE CLUE**

- developmental / scientific
- span-focused
- not a personal idiom like “all my life”

---

### 13 From childhood to old age
**從童年到老年；一生大部分階段**

描述性表達，強調人生多個階段的延續；不一定包括出生和死亡兩端。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The book follows her from childhood to old age.

這本書記錄她從童年到老年的一生歷程。

**USAGE CLUE**

- life stages
- narrative
- slightly narrower than birth-to-death

---

### 14 For as long as one can remember
**從有記憶以來一直；記憶中一直**

不是嚴格等於整個一生，而是從最早記憶起一直如此。只有在「記憶中的一生」語境才合適。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He has loved the sea for as long as he can remember.

從有記憶以來，他一直熱愛大海。

**USAGE CLUE**

- memory-based
- not literally birth-to-present
- contextual near-synonym

# 02 False synonyms

## 01 entire life / whole life

### 幾乎同義，但語感略有不同

`whole life` 更自然口語；`entire life` 稍強調「全部、完整」。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> She has lived here her whole life.  
> She devoted her entire life to the project.

---

## 02 entire life / lifetime

### lifetime 是名詞，不一定可直接替換

`lifetime` 常用於 `in a lifetime`、`during his lifetime` 等結構。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> He lived there his entire life.  
> He saw many changes during his lifetime.

---

## 03 entire life / lifelong

### lifelong 是形容詞

`lifelong` 必須修飾名詞，如 `lifelong friend`、`lifelong passion`。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> She loved music her entire life.  
> She had a lifelong love of music.

---

## 04 entire life / all day

### all day 只是一整天

時間跨度完全不同，不能因為都有「整段時間」就混用。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> He worked all day.  
> He worked in the same profession his whole life.

---

## 05 entire life / childhood

### childhood 只是人生其中一段

`childhood` 不能代替 entire life。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> She lived abroad during childhood.  
> She lived abroad throughout her life.

---

## 06 entire life / adulthood

### adulthood 也只是人生階段

它從成年開始，並不包含童年。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> He spent most of adulthood in London.  
> He spent his whole life in Britain.

---

## 07 entire life / life expectancy

### life expectancy 是統計預期壽命

它不是某個人實際的一生。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Life expectancy has increased in many countries.  
> He lived in the same village his entire life.

---

## 08 entire life / alive

### 詞性與概念不同

`alive` 是形容詞「活着的」，不是「一生」這個時間名詞短語。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> She was still alive at ninety.  
> She had lived in the house all her life.

# 03 練習與解釋

## 練習 01.1

**Target phrase: Whole life · 一生；整個人生**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> She has lived in the same village her entire life.

**中文翻譯**

她一生都住在同一個村莊。

A. whole life  
B. adulthood  
C. childhood  
D. lifetime expectancy  
E. lifelong  
F. alive

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**whole life · 一生；整個人生**

> She has lived in the same village her whole life.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. whole life ✓**  
`has lived ... her whole life` 是極自然的完成式搭配，表示從出生至今整段人生都如此。

**B. adulthood**  
只包括成年後，無法涵蓋童年。

**C. childhood**  
只包括童年。

**D. lifetime expectancy**  
標準詞是 `life expectancy`，且表示統計預期壽命，不是實際人生經歷。

**E. lifelong**  
是形容詞，不能直接說 `her lifelong` 作時間狀語。

**F. alive**  
形容詞「活着的」，詞性和語義都不合。

# 05 高混淆組

## entire life vs whole life
- **entire life**：稍強調「完整、全部」。
- **whole life**：最自然、日常。

## whole life vs all one's life
- **whole life**：名詞短語。
- **all one's life**：常作時間狀語。

## entire life vs lifelong
- **entire life**：名詞短語。
- **lifelong**：形容詞。

## throughout one's life vs over the course of one's life
- **throughout**：強調全程／不同階段皆如此。
- **over the course of**：強調隨時間逐步變化或累積。

## lifetime vs life span
- **lifetime**：個人一生的時間。
- **life span**：較分析性，強調生命跨度。

## from birth to death vs from childhood to old age
- **birth to death**：完整生命兩端。
- **childhood to old age**：主要人生階段，不一定包括出生和死亡。

## whole life vs for as long as one can remember
- **whole life**：字面整個人生。
- **as long as one can remember**：只從有記憶起。

## entire life vs life expectancy
- **entire life**：實際的人生。
- **life expectancy**：統計預期壽命。

# 08 生成／評估注意事項

1. 固定為 **14 alternatives + 3 questions each = 42 exercises**。
2. 每題包含：
   - Target word / phrase + Chinese gloss
   - READ THE CONTEXT
   - 中文翻譯
   - 六個選項
   - `CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案`
   - English target + Chinese gloss
   - 正確改寫
   - A–F 個別解釋
3. A–F feedback 優先採用：**詞義／詞性 → 時間跨度／正常搭配 → 本題為何成功或失敗**。
4. 使用者提供的核心：
   - whole life = 最直接自然
   - all of one's life = 完整人生持續
5. `all one's life` 是高頻自然變體，不能漏掉。
6. `lifelong` 是形容詞，應用作 morphology / syntax distinction，而不是直接當名詞時間短語。
7. `throughout one's life` 適合持續／反覆；`over the course of one's life` 適合變化／累積。
8. `lifetime` 是名詞；`life span` 更偏分析、研究、發展跨度。
9. `from birth to death` 只有在完整生命兩端確實成立時使用。
10. `from cradle to grave` 是慣用、修辭性表達，不能在所有正式技術寫作中機械替換。
11. `from childhood to old age` 比 birth-to-death 稍窄，適合敘事人生階段。
12. `for as long as one can remember` 是 memory-based near-synonym，不等於字面整個人生。
13. False synonyms 要區分：
    - entire life vs childhood/adulthood
    - lifetime vs life expectancy
    - lifelong adjective vs lifetime noun
    - whole life vs all day
14. 本組加入 morphology / structure traps：
    - life / lifetime / lifelong
    - lifespan / life expectancy
    - whole life / all one's life
    - lifelong adjective vs lifetime noun
15. Distractors 優先使用真正相近但時間跨度或詞性不同的表達。
16. 原句只在 `entire life` 自然時使用；涉及 developmental research、memory horizon、biography endpoints 時，靠上下文選更精確替代。
17. 真正進階辨析應直接比較：
    - entire life / whole life
    - whole life / all one's life
    - lifetime / lifelong
    - throughout one's life / over the course of one's life
    - lifetime / life span
    - from birth to death / from childhood to old age
    - whole life / for as long as one can remember
18. `entire life` 本身仍是很自然的核心表達；其他替代語主要增加「語法形式、完整生命跨度、人生變化、敘事端點、記憶範圍與正式程度」上的精度。
