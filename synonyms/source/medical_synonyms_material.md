# Medical

**健康相關、臨床、醫療保健、藥用、治療、診斷、生物醫學——究竟是哪一種 medical？**

本課聚焦：

**medical = relating to medicine, healthcare, diagnosis, treatment, clinical practice, or professional care of illness and injury**

核心近義與近義表達：

**health-related; clinical; relating to medicine; healthcare; medicinal; therapeutic; diagnostic; biomedical; physician-related; healthcare-related; medical-care**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 11 個真正有辨析價值的表達。

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Health-related

**與健康有關的；健康相關的**

和 `medical` 有明顯重疊，但範圍更廣，可包括健康教育、公共健康、生活方式、健康政策等，不一定涉及醫生、診斷或治療。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The survey asks about health-related problems caused by stress.

調查詢問由壓力引起的健康相關問題。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- health-related problem

- health-related risk

- health-related information

- health-related issue

## 02 Clinical

**臨床的；診療相關的**

當 `medical` 指病人照護、診斷、治療、臨床試驗、醫療環境或醫護實務時非常精確。比 `medical` 更集中於實際診療。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The hospital provides clinical training for nurses.

醫院為護士提供臨床培訓。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- clinical practice

- clinical training

- clinical data

- clinical trial

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Health-related

**健康相關比 medical 更廣**

health-related 可包括運動、飲食、睡眠、公共健康等，不一定涉及醫學診療。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Exercise is health-related, but it is not necessarily medical.

---

## 02 Clinical

**臨床只是 medical 的一部分**

clinical 通常涉及病人、診斷、治療或實際醫護。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Clinical training is medical training involving patient care.

---

## 03 Medicinal

**藥用不等於一般 medical**

medicinal 只適合藥物／草藥／治療性物質與用途。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A medicinal herb is used therapeutically; a medical record is not medicinal.

---

## 04 Therapeutic

**治療不等於所有 medical**

therapeutic 強調治療目的或效果，不包括診斷、行政、醫學教育等。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A therapeutic device is intended to treat; a diagnostic device identifies a condition.

---

## 05 Diagnostic

**診斷不等於所有 medical**

diagnostic 只表示用於判斷疾病或狀況。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A diagnostic test is medical, but medical care includes far more than diagnosis.

---

## 06 Healthcare

**醫療服務不等於醫學本身**

healthcare 更偏服務提供、制度、資源與照護。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Healthcare policy concerns service systems; medical research may be laboratory-based.

---

## 07 Biological

**生物學不等於醫學**

biological 可涉及所有生物現象，並不一定有人類疾病或治療焦點。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Biological research can study plants without being medical research.

---

## 08 Surgical

**外科只是 medical 的 subtype**

surgical 只涉及手術／外科。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Surgical treatment is medical, but many medical treatments are non-surgical.

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Medical is primarily an adjective
常見：
- medical care
- medical treatment
- medical condition
- medical record
- medical staff

## 02 Medical vs health-related
`health-related` 更廣：
- sleep
- diet
- exercise
- wellbeing
- public health

這些可以 health-related，但未必是 clinical / medical treatment。

## 03 Medical vs clinical
`clinical` 常見：
- clinical trial
- clinical practice
- clinical data
- clinical training
- clinical diagnosis

它更貼近實際病人照護與診療。

## 04 Medicinal
常見：
- medicinal plant
- medicinal properties
- medicinal use

不要說：
- medicinal record ✗
- medicinal hospital ✗

## 05 Therapeutic vs diagnostic
- therapeutic = 治療
- diagnostic = 診斷

例如：
- therapeutic device
- diagnostic device

## 06 Biomedical
偏科研／技術：
- biomedical science
- biomedical engineering
- biomedical research

## 07 Healthcare
作前置修飾很常見：
- healthcare system
- healthcare worker
- healthcare policy
- healthcare costs
