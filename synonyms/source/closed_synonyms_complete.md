# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Closed

**關閉，究竟是哪一種「關閉」？**

門沒有打開、上鎖、密封、道路被阻塞、地點不能進入、服務暫時不可用、系統離線、業務停運、產品停產、程序正式結束——英文會按「關閉的方式、原因和結果」選用不同的字。

> **核心原則**
>
> `closed` 是非常廣泛的詞。下面的詞不是全部都能在所有句子中直接取代 `closed`；它們是在特定語境中把「關閉」說得更精準。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## Closed 的 14 種更精準說法

---

## 01 Shut

**關上的；關閉的**

最接近一般 `closed` 的日常說法，常指門、窗、蓋子、商店或設施處於沒有開放的狀態。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The door was shut to keep the cold air outside.

門關著，以防冷空氣進來。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- keep the door shut
- shut the window
- a shop is shut
- tightly shut

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **shut = simply not open**

---

## 02 Locked

**上鎖的；被鎖住的**

不只是「關上」，而是使用鎖或其他安全機制防止打開或進入。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The storage room remained locked because it contained valuable equipment.

儲物室一直上鎖，因為裡面存放了貴重設備。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- locked door
- locked room
- locked cabinet
- remain locked
- locked account

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **locked = closed + secured against opening/access**

---

## 03 Sealed

**密封的；封閉的**

強調開口被完全封住，使空氣、液體、物件或人不能進出；也可指官方封條。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The evidence bag was sealed immediately after the sample was placed inside.

樣本放入後，證物袋立即被密封。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- sealed container
- sealed envelope
- hermetically sealed
- officially sealed

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **sealed = closed so nothing can pass through / tampering is prevented**

---

## 04 Blocked

**被堵塞的；被阻斷的**

通道本身可能存在，但有障礙物、事故、物件或其他因素阻止通行或流動。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The road was blocked by a fallen tree after the storm.

暴風雨後，一棵倒下的樹阻塞了道路。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- blocked road
- blocked entrance
- blocked drain
- access is blocked
- completely blocked

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **blocked = passage exists, but something prevents movement through it**

---

## 05 Inaccessible

**無法進入的；無法接觸的**

強調人實際無法到達、進入、取得或使用某個地點、資訊或資源。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The mountain road became inaccessible after heavy snowfall.

大雪後，通往山區的道路變得無法通行。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- inaccessible area
- inaccessible building
- inaccessible data
- become inaccessible
- temporarily inaccessible

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **inaccessible = cannot be reached, entered, or obtained**

---

## 06 Unavailable

**不可用的；暫時無法提供的**

常用於服務、資源、人員、產品、功能、預約或資料，表示目前不能取得或使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Online banking will be unavailable between midnight and 2 a.m. for maintenance.

網上銀行服務將於午夜至凌晨兩時因維護而暫停使用。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- temporarily unavailable
- service unavailable
- currently unavailable
- unavailable for use
- unavailable due to maintenance

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **unavailable = not currently obtainable or usable**

---

## 07 Offline

**離線的；停止連線運作的**

用於網站、伺服器、系統、設備或網絡服務，表示目前沒有連線或不能正常提供線上功能。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The payment system went offline after a network failure.

付款系統在網絡故障後離線。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- system is offline
- website went offline
- server offline
- take a system offline
- offline mode

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **offline = not connected or not operating online**

---

## 08 Off-limits

**禁止進入的；不准接近的**

某地方不是物理上不能進入，而是因安全、規則或權限而不允許進入。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The laboratory is off-limits to visitors without authorization.

未經授權的訪客不得進入實驗室。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- off-limits to visitors
- remain off-limits
- area is off-limits
- strictly off-limits

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **off-limits = entry is prohibited**

---

## 09 Suspended

**暫停的；暫時停止的**

表示活動、服務、權利、運作或程序暫時停止，但可能之後恢復。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Train services were suspended because of severe flooding.

由於嚴重水浸，列車服務暫停。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- services suspended
- operations suspended
- account suspended
- temporarily suspended
- suspend trading

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **suspended = stopped temporarily, with possible resumption**

---

## 10 Discontinued

**停止提供的；停產的**

產品、服務、型號、功能或項目不再繼續生產或提供，通常表示較長期或永久停止。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The manufacturer discontinued the older model after introducing a new version.

製造商推出新版本後停止生產舊型號。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- discontinued product
- discontinued service
- line discontinued
- production discontinued
- no longer available

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **discontinued = no longer produced or offered**

---

## 11 Shuttered

**停業的；關閉營運的**

常用於商店、餐廳、工廠、辦公室或其他實體場所，表示停止營業或關閉營運；新聞和商業語境很常見。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Several restaurants were shuttered after the building failed a safety inspection.

大樓未能通過安全檢查後，數間餐廳被勒令停業。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- shuttered store
- shuttered factory
- permanently shuttered
- temporarily shuttered
- business was shuttered

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **shuttered = a business or physical operation has stopped operating**

---

## 12 Terminated

**正式終止的；被結束的**

表示合約、帳戶、安排、服務或法律／行政程序被正式結束。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The contract was terminated after both parties agreed to end the arrangement.

雙方同意結束安排後，合約正式終止。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- contract terminated
- account terminated
- agreement terminated
- employment terminated
- terminate a service

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **terminated = formally brought to an end**

---

## 13 Concluded

**結束的；告一段落的**

表示會議、談判、調查、活動或事件已完成並到達終點，不一定帶有「被強制停止」的意思。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The meeting concluded after the final vote.

最後表決後，會議結束。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- meeting concluded
- talks concluded
- investigation concluded
- successfully concluded
- conclude proceedings

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **concluded = naturally or formally reached its end**

---

## 14 Restricted

**限制開放的；受限制的**

不是完全「關閉」，而是只允許某些人、時間、用途或條件下進入或使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Access to the archive is restricted to authorized researchers.

檔案庫只限獲授權的研究人員進入。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- restricted access
- restricted area
- access restricted to...
- restricted opening hours
- restricted use

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **restricted = not fully closed, but access is limited**

# 02 False synonyms

看起來與 `closed` 有關，但描述的狀態並不相同。這些字有時會在相似情境中出現，但不能隨意互換。

---

## 01 Shut / Locked

### 關上不等於上鎖

`shut` 只表示沒有打開；`locked` 表示有安全機制阻止打開。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> A door can be shut but unlocked.  
> A locked door is both closed and secured.

---

## 02 Locked / Sealed

### 上鎖不等於密封

`locked` 防止人打開；`sealed` 防止內容物、空氣、液體或其他物質通過，或防止被拆動。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> A suitcase may be locked.  
> A laboratory sample may be sealed.

---

## 03 Blocked / Closed

### 被堵住不等於正式關閉

道路可以因事故被 `blocked`，即使管理機構沒有正式宣布它 `closed`。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The road was blocked by debris.  
> The road was officially closed by the authorities.

---

## 04 Unavailable / Offline

### 不可用不一定等於離線

`unavailable` 是較廣泛狀態；`offline` 特別指網絡、系統或設備沒有連線／線上運作。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> A doctor may be unavailable.  
> A server may be offline.

---

## 05 Inaccessible / Off-limits

### 無法進入不等於禁止進入

`inaccessible` 表示實際無法到達或使用；`off-limits` 表示規則上不允許。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> A cave may be inaccessible because of flooding.  
> A laboratory may be off-limits because visitors are not authorized.

---

## 06 Suspended / Discontinued

### 暫停不等於停止提供

`suspended` 通常暗示之後可能恢復；`discontinued` 通常表示已停止生產或提供。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The service was suspended for two days.  
> The old service was discontinued permanently.

---

## 07 Shuttered / Terminated

### 場所停業不等於正式終止關係

`shuttered` 多形容店舖、工廠等停止營運；`terminated` 多用於合約、帳戶、聘用或服務關係正式結束。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The factory was shuttered.  
> The supply contract was terminated.

---

## 08 Concluded / Terminated

### 正常結束不等於被終止

`concluded` 常表示事情完成並自然到達終點；`terminated` 更常表示某方正式把它結束。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The meeting concluded at 5 p.m.  
> The contract was terminated before its original end date.

---

# 03 練習與解釋

> 每個目標詞兩題。原句故意使用寬泛的 `closed / not open / not available / ended` 概念；請根據語境選出更精準的詞。

---

## 練習 01.1

**Target word: Shut · 關上的；關閉的**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> The bedroom window was closed because it was raining heavily.

**中文翻譯**

因為下大雨，睡房的窗戶關著。

| | 選項 |
|---|---|
| A | shut |
| B | suspended |
| C | unavailable |
| D | concluded |
| E | locked |
| F | closure |

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**shut · 關上的**

> The bedroom window was shut because it was raining heavily.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. shut ✓**  
窗戶只是處於「沒有打開」的狀態，因此 `shut` 最直接。

**B. suspended**  
`suspended` 表示活動或服務暫停，不能形容普通窗戶。

**C. unavailable**  
窗戶不是一項目前不能取得的服務或資源。

**D. concluded**  
`concluded` 表示活動或程序結束。

**E. locked**  
窗戶可能同時上鎖，但原句只說因下雨而關上，沒有安全鎖定的資訊。

**F. closure**  
名詞「關閉」，詞性不合。

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

## Shut vs Locked

**shut** = 沒有打開。  
**locked** = 關上並以安全機制阻止打開。

> The door is shut, but it is not locked.

---

## Locked vs Sealed

**locked** = 防止人打開。  
**sealed** = 防止內容、空氣、液體或拆動通過。

> a locked cabinet  
> a sealed container

---

## Blocked vs Inaccessible

**blocked** = 有具體障礙阻止通行。  
**inaccessible** = 最終結果是無法到達／進入，原因可以有很多種。

> The entrance is blocked by debris.  
> The village is inaccessible after the bridge collapse.

---

## Unavailable vs Offline

**unavailable** = 廣泛的「目前不可用」。  
**offline** = 特別指沒有線上連線或線上運作。

> The booking service is unavailable.  
> The server is offline.

---

## Inaccessible vs Off-limits

**inaccessible** = 實際不能進去。  
**off-limits** = 規則不准進去。

> The trail is inaccessible because of flooding.  
> The military area is off-limits to civilians.

---

## Suspended vs Discontinued

**suspended** = 暫時停止，可能恢復。  
**discontinued** = 不再提供／生產，通常較長期或永久。

> Flights were suspended for six hours.  
> The old route was discontinued.

---

## Shuttered vs Shut

**shut** 很廣泛，可以只是今天沒有開門。  
**shuttered** 常帶有整個商業場所停止營運的意味，較常見於新聞和商業語境。

> The café is shut tonight.  
> The factory was shuttered after the inspection.

---

## Terminated vs Concluded

**terminated** = 被正式結束，常可帶「提前／由某方終止」意味。  
**concluded** = 過程完成後到達終點。

> The contract was terminated early.  
> The meeting concluded at noon.

---

## Restricted vs Closed

**closed** = 完全不能進入／使用。  
**restricted** = 只有部分人、部分時間或某些條件下能進入／使用。

> The archive is closed today.  
> Access to the archive is restricted to researchers.

---

| 名詞 | 形容詞 / 動詞 | 常見錯誤 |
|---|---|---|
| closure | close / closed | `the door is closure` ✗ |
| lock | lock / locked | `the door is lock` ✗ |
| seal | seal / sealed | `the package is seal` ✗ |
| blockage | block / blocked | `the road is blockage` ✗ |
| accessibility | accessible / inaccessible | `the file is inaccessibility` ✗ |
| availability | available / unavailable | `the service is unavailability` ✗ |
| outage | offline | `the website is outage` ✗ |
| restriction | restrict / restricted | `access is restriction` ✗ |
| suspension | suspend / suspended | `service is suspension` ✗ |
| discontinuation | discontinue / discontinued | `the product is discontinuation` ✗ |
| shutter | shutter / shuttered | `the shop is shutter` ✗ |

# 07 核心決策問題

當你想把 `closed` 換成更精準的字時，先問：

1. **只是沒有打開嗎？** → `shut`
2. **有鎖防止打開嗎？** → `locked`
3. **是密封、防止任何東西通過嗎？** → `sealed`
4. **是被障礙物堵住嗎？** → `blocked`
5. **是實際無法到達／進入／存取嗎？** → `inaccessible`
6. **是目前不能提供或使用嗎？** → `unavailable`
7. **是系統或網站失去線上連線嗎？** → `offline`
8. **是規則上禁止進入嗎？** → `off-limits`
9. **是暫時停止、之後可能恢復嗎？** → `suspended`
10. **是產品／服務不再提供嗎？** → `discontinued`
11. **是店舖／工廠等停止營運嗎？** → `shuttered`
12. **是合約／帳戶／安排被正式終止嗎？** → `terminated`
13. **是會議／調查／活動完成了嗎？** → `concluded`
14. **不是完全關閉，而是有限制嗎？** → `restricted`

---

# 08 Final note for system use

不要把以下詞當成可以機械替換 `closed` 的同義詞：

`shut / locked / sealed / blocked / inaccessible / unavailable / offline / off-limits / suspended / discontinued / shuttered / terminated / concluded / restricted`

它們的共同點只是：

> **都可以在某些語境中表達一種更具體的「不再開放／不能進入／不能使用／已經停止」狀態。**

真正的選詞依據是：

> **In what sense is it closed?**
