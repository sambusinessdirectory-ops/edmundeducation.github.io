# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# At that time · phrase

「當時／那時候／在那段期間／在那個階段」，究竟是哪一種 `at that time`？

使用者提供的 **then** 與 **during that period** 全部保留：`then` 是最精簡的時間回指；`during that period` 強調一段持續期間。另加入 **at the time**（按當時情況來看）、**back then**（口語回望過去）、**at that point**（時間線／過程中的具體節點）、**at that stage**（發展階段）與 **in those days**（過去年代的典型情況）。

> 本課主要處理 `at that time = at the time being referred to, either at a specific point or during a particular period`。
>
> 同義詞數量**不固定**；本課按一般回指、時段持續、當時情況、口語回憶、事件節點、發展階段與歷史／生活時期選用 **7 個**替代表達。
>
> Format: **7 curated alternatives + 8 false-synonym contrasts + 3 exercises per target synonym (21 questions total)**.

---

# 01 同義詞指南


### 01 Then
**那時；當時；然後（此處指時間）**

使用者提供。`then` 是 `at that time` 最核心、最精簡的近義詞，表示在剛提到的某個時間點或時期。它非常常見，既可指過去，也可指未來語境中的某個既定時間。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> I was living in Bristol then.

**中文翻譯**

我那時住在布里斯托。

**USAGE CLUE**

- a concise reference back to a previously mentioned time

---


### 02 During That Period
**在那段期間；在那一時期**

使用者提供。`during that period` 表示某情況持續發生於一段有起訖或可辨識的時期內。它比 `then` 更強調 duration，適合 months, years, phases, historical periods。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> During that period, the company expanded into three new markets.

**中文翻譯**

在那段期間，公司拓展至三個新市場。

**USAGE CLUE**

- something occurring throughout or within an extended, identifiable period

---


### 03 At The Time
**當時；在那個時候**

`at the time` 是非常自然的回指表達，表示『在所談論的那個時候』。它通常比 `at that time` 更口語、更慣用，尤其用來補充當時的知識、情況或判斷。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> At the time, nobody knew how serious the problem was.

**中文翻譯**

當時沒有人知道問題有多嚴重。

**USAGE CLUE**

- referring back to the relevant time while contrasting what was true or known then with later knowledge

---


### 04 Back Then
**那時候；當年；以前那陣子**

較口語。`back then` 專指過去某個時期，通常帶一點回憶或今昔對比。它不能自然用於未來時間，而且比 `at the time` 更 conversational。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Back then, very few people worked remotely.

**中文翻譯**

那時候，很少有人遙距工作。

**USAGE CLUE**

- an informal reference to an earlier time, often contrasting the past with the present

---


### 05 At That Point
**在那一刻；在那個階段點；當時**

`at that point` 表示時間線、事件或過程中的某個特定節點。它比 `at that time` 更強調 sequence / turning point，常見於 narratives, projects, decisions, processes。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> At that point, the team decided to change its approach.

**中文翻譯**

在那個節點，團隊決定改變做法。

**USAGE CLUE**

- a specific point in a sequence, process or developing situation

---


### 06 At That Stage
**在那個階段；當時進展到那一步**

`at that stage` 強調某個 process, project, career, investigation, development 已進展到的階段。它比 `at that point` 更有 phase-like meaning，通常不指單純鐘錶時間。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> At that stage, the design was still only a rough draft.

**中文翻譯**

在那個階段，設計仍然只是一份初稿。

**USAGE CLUE**

- the state of a project, process or development at a particular phase

---


### 07 In Those Days
**在那些日子；在那個年代；當年**

`in those days` 指較久以前的一段生活時期或年代，通常帶回憶、歷史背景或今昔對比。它比 `back then` 更有 period/era flavour，也不適合精確單一時間點。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> In those days, most records were kept on paper.

**中文翻譯**

在那個年代，大多數紀錄都是以紙本保存。

**USAGE CLUE**

- a past era or extended period remembered as having different habits, conditions or technology

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 at that time / then

`then` 是最精簡的回指，可指過去或未來；`at that time` 更完整、更明示時間。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> We lived in Bristol then.  
> At that time, I was still a student.

---


## 02 at that time / during that period

`during that period` 必須是一段時期；單一瞬間或決策點不適合。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> During that period, sales rose steadily.  
> At that time, the manager made a decision.

---


## 03 at that time / at the time

兩者高度接近；`at the time` 更慣用於『按當時所知／情況』來解釋過去決定。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> At the time, we did not know the cost.  
> At that time, the office was still in Leeds.

---


## 04 at that time / back then

`back then` 較口語，只指過去，常與 today/now 形成對比；不能正常指未來。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Back then, few people had smartphones.  
> We will meet again in June; at that time, we will decide.

---


## 05 at that time / at that point

`at that point` 強調流程或敘事中的具體節點；`at that time` 不一定有 sequence meaning。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> At that point, we changed direction.  
> At that time, I lived nearby.

---


## 06 at that time / at that stage

`at that stage` 強調 development/process phase；普通日曆時間不一定能用。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> At that stage, the design was incomplete.  
> At that time, she was living abroad.

---


## 07 at that time / in those days

`in those days` 指過去一段年代／生活時期，常描述習慣與普遍情況；不適合單一時間點。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> In those days, people wrote more letters.  
> At that time, the train arrived.

---


## 08 at that time / meanwhile

`meanwhile` 表示『與此同時』，連接兩件同期發生的事；它不是單純回指某個時間。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Meanwhile, the second team continued testing.  
> At that time, no decision had been made.

---

# 05 高混淆組

## then vs at the time
- **then**：最短、最一般。
- **at the time**：更常帶『按當時情況』的語感。

## during that period vs in those days
- **during that period**：明確、可界定的時段。
- **in those days**：較寬鬆的過去年代／生活時期。

## back then vs in those days
- **back then**：更口語、可只指一段過去。
- **in those days**：更像談那個年代的普遍情況。

## at that point vs at that stage
- **at that point**：具體節點／轉折點。
- **at that stage**：發展階段。

## then vs back then
- **then**：可過去也可未來。
- **back then**：只用於過去。

## at the time vs back then
- **at the time**：常解釋當時判斷。
- **back then**：常作今昔對比。

## during that period vs then
- **during that period**：強調 duration。
- **then**：可只是時間點。

## at that time vs meanwhile
- **at that time**：回指時間。
- **meanwhile**：表示另一事件同時發生。

# 08 生成／評估注意事項

1. **同義詞數量不是固定值。** 本課按一般回指、持續時段、當時判斷、口語回憶、事件節點、發展階段與過去年代選 **7 個**。
2. 因此本課有 **3 exercises per target = 21 exercises**。
3. 使用者提供的 `then` 與 `during that period` 全部保留。
4. `then` 可指 past or future referenced time；`back then` 只指過去。
5. `during that period` 需要 duration clue，不能用來代替單一瞬間。
6. `at the time` 特別適合 hindsight / what was known then。
7. `back then` 需要 informal retrospective or then-versus-now flavour。
8. `at that point` 需要 sequence / trigger / turning-point clue。
9. `at that stage` 需要 project/process/development phase clue。
10. `in those days` 需要 habits/conditions across a past era。
11. False synonyms 特別測試 then/during that period/at the time/back then/at that point/at that stage/in those days/meanwhile。
12. Distractors 全部使用真實自然英文片語，不使用人工標籤。
13. 每題以 decisive clue 建立唯一答案，例如 `for six months`, `now`, `unlike today`, `failed twice`, `early planning phase`。
14. 原始 `at that time` 句子保持自然；較窄詞只在上下文真正支援時使用。
