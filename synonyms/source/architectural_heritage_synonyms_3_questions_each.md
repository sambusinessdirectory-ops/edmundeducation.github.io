# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Architectural heritage

**「建築遺產」，究竟是在說歷史建築本身、整體建成環境，還是代代相傳的建築傳統？**

`architectural heritage` 可以指一個地方從過去承傳下來、具有歷史、文化、審美或社會價值的建築、建成環境、城市肌理和建築傳統。

因此，接近的表達大致會落在幾個方向：

- **直接指建築遺產本身** → `built heritage`, `architectural legacy`
- **強調歷史建成環境** → `historic built environment`, `historic urban fabric`
- **強調建築實體／存量** → `historic building stock`, `heritage buildings`
- **強調承傳下來的建築文化與做法** → `architectural inheritance`, `inherited building traditions`, `vernacular building traditions`
- **較正式／文化遺產語域** → `built cultural heritage`, `architectural patrimony`
- **較敘事／概括式說法** → `the built legacy of the past`

> **核心原則**
>
> 這一組不是機械式完全同義詞，而是按不同語境選出的 **close synonyms、廣泛可用 near-synonyms，以及常見正式／描述性表達**。
>
> 真正的選詞依據是：你是在說 **建築遺產整體、歷史建成環境、城市紋理、建築存量，還是承傳的建築傳統**？

# 01 同義詞指南

## Architectural heritage 的 16 個近義／近乎可互換說法

---

## 01 Built heritage

**建成遺產；建築遺產**

最直接、最常用的近義詞之一。範圍可以包括歷史建築、橋樑、街道、公共空間及其他人造環境。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The city has introduced new measures to protect its built heritage.

城市推出新措施保護其建成遺產。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- protect built heritage
- conserve built heritage
- built heritage assets
- built heritage conservation

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **built heritage = inherited buildings and other constructed environments of cultural value**

---

## 02 Architectural legacy

**建築遺產；建築留下的文化成果**

比 `architectural heritage` 稍帶敘事感，強調一個時代、民族、建築師或社會留下來的建築成果。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The old quarter reflects the city's rich architectural legacy.

舊城區展現這座城市豐富的建築遺產。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- rich architectural legacy
- preserve an architectural legacy
- architectural legacy of...
- leave an architectural legacy

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **architectural legacy = architecture inherited from the past and left to later generations**

---

## 03 Historic built environment

**歷史建成環境**

強調不是單棟建築，而是整體歷史街區、道路、公共空間、建築群和環境關係。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Planning rules aim to protect the historic built environment of the town centre.

規劃規則旨在保護市中心的歷史建成環境。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- historic built environment
- protect the built environment
- character of the historic built environment
- conservation of the historic built environment

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **historic built environment = the inherited physical environment formed by buildings, streets, and spaces**

---

## 04 Built cultural heritage

**建成文化遺產**

較正式，常見於政策、保育、聯合國／文化遺產、規劃及學術語境。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The programme supports the conservation of built cultural heritage.

該計劃支持建成文化遺產的保育。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- built cultural heritage
- conservation of built cultural heritage
- built cultural heritage assets
- safeguard built cultural heritage

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **built cultural heritage = culturally significant heritage embodied in buildings and constructed places**

---

## 05 Historic architecture

**歷史建築；歷史建築風貌**

當 `architectural heritage` 主要指歷史建築本身或一個地方的歷史建築特色時，非常接近。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The district is known for its well-preserved historic architecture.

這個地區以保存良好的歷史建築聞名。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- historic architecture
- preserve historic architecture
- examples of historic architecture
- historic architectural character

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **historic architecture = architecture from the past that has historical or cultural significance**

---

## 06 Heritage architecture

**遺產建築；具保育價值的建築**

常用於設計、規劃、旅遊或保育語境，指具有遺產價值的建築及其風格。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The redevelopment plan incorporates several examples of heritage architecture.

重建計劃保留並整合了數座遺產建築。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- heritage architecture
- heritage architecture conservation
- heritage architecture tourism
- examples of heritage architecture

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **heritage architecture = architecture recognized for heritage value**

---

## 07 Architectural inheritance

**建築傳承；承繼下來的建築遺產**

強調「從過去承繼而來」這個概念，比 `architectural heritage` 更突出代際傳承。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The city sees its architectural inheritance as a key part of local identity.

城市把其建築傳承視為地方身份的重要部分。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- architectural inheritance
- preserve architectural inheritance
- shared architectural inheritance
- inherited architectural character

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **architectural inheritance = architectural forms and traditions inherited from previous generations**

---

## 08 Inherited building traditions

**承傳下來的建築傳統**

當 `architectural heritage` 指的不只是建築物，而是建造方法、形式、材料和傳統做法時，非常貼切。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Local craftsmen continue to use inherited building traditions.

本地工匠繼續使用世代相傳的建築傳統。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- inherited building traditions

- traditional building methods
- inherited construction practices

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **inherited building traditions = building practices and forms passed down over generations**

---

## 09 Historic building stock

**歷史建築存量**

常見於城市規劃、房屋政策、建築保育及技術文件，強調某地現存的歷史建築整體。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Much of the city's historic building stock requires careful maintenance.

城市大量歷史建築存量需要仔細維護。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- historic building stock
- existing building stock

- older building stock

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **historic building stock = the total body of surviving historic buildings in an area**

---

## 10 Historic fabric

**歷史肌理；歷史建築實體**

保育領域非常常見，可指原有建築材料、構造、街道紋理或歷史環境的實體部分。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The renovation preserved much of the building's historic fabric.

翻新工程保留了建築物大量原有歷史肌理。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- historic fabric
- preserve historic fabric
- original historic fabric
- loss of historic fabric

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **historic fabric = surviving physical material and structure that carries historic character**

---

## 11 Historic urban fabric

**歷史城市肌理**

當 `architectural heritage` 指整個舊城、街道網絡、建築群和公共空間的歷史形態時，非常接近。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> New development should respect the historic urban fabric.

新發展應尊重歷史城市肌理。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- historic urban fabric
- preserve urban fabric
- traditional urban fabric
- integrate with the historic urban fabric

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **historic urban fabric = inherited pattern of streets, buildings, plots, and public spaces**

---

## 12 Architectural tradition

**建築傳統**

當 `architectural heritage` 偏向某地長期形成的建築形式、風格、做法或文化時，很自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The houses reflect a long regional architectural tradition.

這些房屋反映當地悠久的建築傳統。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- architectural tradition
- local architectural tradition
- regional architectural tradition

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **architectural tradition = established architectural forms and practices passed through time**

---

## 13 Vernacular building traditions

**地方傳統建築做法；鄉土建築傳統**

比 `architectural tradition` 更具體，強調地方性、傳統性、民間技術、材料和氣候適應方式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The project draws on local vernacular building traditions.

這個項目借鑑本地傳統建築做法。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- vernacular building traditions
- vernacular architecture
- local building traditions
- traditional construction techniques

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **vernacular building traditions = locally evolved building practices and forms**

---

## 14 Architectural patrimony

**建築遺產；建築文化財產**

非常正式，常見於歐洲大陸語境、文化政策、法律、學術或 heritage discourse。`patrimony` 帶有「由前代傳下來、應共同守護的文化財產」意味。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The law provides special protection for the nation's architectural patrimony.

法律為國家的建築遺產提供特別保護。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- architectural patrimony
- national patrimony
- protect architectural patrimony
- cultural patrimony

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **architectural patrimony = formally recognized architectural inheritance of a community or nation**

---

## 15 Heritage buildings

**歷史／遺產建築**

如果 `architectural heritage` 在上下文中具體指那些被保育、具文化價值的建築物本身，可用這個常見表達。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Several heritage buildings were restored as part of the regeneration project.

多座遺產建築在市區更新項目中獲得修復。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- heritage buildings
- listed heritage buildings
- protect heritage buildings
- restore heritage buildings

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **heritage buildings = individual buildings recognized for heritage significance**

---

## 16 The built legacy of the past

**過去留下的建築遺產**

較敘事、概括式的常用表達，適合演講、文化文章、旅遊及一般寫作。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The old town represents the built legacy of the past.

舊城代表了過去留下的建築遺產。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- built legacy of the past
- legacy of earlier generations
- inherited built legacy
- preserve the built legacy

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **the built legacy of the past = the buildings and places inherited from earlier generations**

# 02 False synonyms

## 01 Architectural heritage / Historic buildings

### 建築遺產不只是一棟棟歷史建築

`historic buildings` 只指建築物。  
`architectural heritage` 還可以包括街道、城市肌理、公共空間、建築傳統和技術。

---

## 02 Architectural heritage / Architecture

### 建築遺產不等於所有建築

`architecture` 是整個建築領域或建築作品。  
`architectural heritage` 特別指具有承傳和遺產價值的部分。

---

## 03 Built heritage / Cultural heritage

### 建成遺產只是文化遺產的一部分

`cultural heritage` 還包括語言、音樂、儀式、文物和非物質文化。

---

## 04 Historic architecture / Architectural tradition

### 建築實體不等於建築傳統

`historic architecture` 強調建築作品。  
`architectural tradition` 強調形式、方法和文化傳承。

---

## 05 Historic fabric / Historic built environment

### 實體材料不等於整體環境

`historic fabric` 常指原有材料、結構和物理肌理。  
`historic built environment` 範圍更廣。

---

## 06 Heritage buildings / Architectural heritage

### 個別建築不等於整體遺產

`heritage buildings` 是 architectural heritage 的一部分，不是每次都能直接互換。

---

## 07 Vernacular architecture / Architectural heritage

### 地方傳統建築只是建築遺產的一部分

`vernacular architecture` 特別指地方性、傳統性建築。

---

## 08 Architectural patrimony / Property

### Patrimony 不是普通財產

`architectural patrimony` 是正式的文化遺產概念，不等於一般房地產或私人財產。

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

## Architectural heritage vs Built heritage

兩者非常接近。

**architectural heritage** 更直接聚焦建築價值。  
**built heritage** 範圍可以稍廣，包括其他建成環境元素。

---

## Architectural heritage vs Architectural legacy

**heritage** 帶保育、文化價值意味較強。  
**legacy** 更強調「前人留下給後世」。

---

## Built heritage vs Historic built environment

**built heritage** = 遺產概念。  
**historic built environment** = 更強調整體物理環境。

---

## Historic architecture vs Heritage architecture

**historic architecture** 強調歷史年代與建築特色。  
**heritage architecture** 強調已被視為具有遺產價值。

---

## Architectural inheritance vs Architectural tradition

**architectural inheritance** = 我們承繼了甚麼。  
**architectural tradition** = 一套持續演變、傳承的建築形式和做法。

---

## Historic building stock vs Heritage buildings

**historic building stock** = 一個地區現存歷史建築的整體存量。  
**heritage buildings** = 具體個別建築。

---

## Historic fabric vs Historic urban fabric

**historic fabric** 可指單棟建築的原有材料／構造，也可較廣泛使用。  
**historic urban fabric** 專指城市尺度的街道、地塊、建築群關係。

---

## Architectural tradition vs Vernacular building traditions

**architectural tradition** 較廣。  
**vernacular building traditions** 更強調地方性、民間性、傳統材料與技術。

---

## Architectural patrimony vs Built cultural heritage

兩者都很正式。

**built cultural heritage** 是較透明、國際化的政策用語。  
**architectural patrimony** 更像法律、文化財產或歐洲 heritage discourse。

---

# 06 Idioms / common expressions · 常用表達

這個詞本身屬專業文化遺產語域，所以真正的 idiom 不多；更常見的是高頻固定表達。

## Preserve the character of a place

**保留地方特色**

> New development should preserve the character of the historic district.

---

## Respect the historic fabric

**尊重／保留歷史肌理**

> The extension was designed to respect the historic fabric of the building.

---

## Give old buildings a new lease of life

**讓舊建築重獲新生**

常用於活化再利用。

> The project gave several old buildings a new lease of life.

---

## Breathe new life into old buildings

**為舊建築注入新生命**

> Adaptive reuse can breathe new life into old buildings.

---

## Link the past with the present

**連結過去與現在**

常見於 heritage / architecture 敘事。

> The restored market links the past with the present.

---

## Handed down through generations

**世代相傳**

> These building techniques have been handed down through generations.

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Heritage

通常是不可數名詞：

> architectural heritage ✓  
> cultural heritage ✓

一般不說：

> an architectural heritage ✗

除非在非常特殊、可數化的學術語境。

---

## Built heritage

也是通常不可數：

> protect built heritage

---

## Heritage building

可數：

> a heritage building  
> several heritage buildings

---

## Historic / Historical

在建築保育語境中，`historic` 通常表示「具有歷史重要性」。

> historic building  
> historic district

`historical` 更常表示「與歷史有關」。

---

## Fabric

在 `historic fabric` 中不是「布料」，而是：

> 原有材料、結構、物理肌理

---

# 09 核心決策問題

1. **只是要最直接替換 architectural heritage 嗎？** → `built heritage`
2. **想強調前代留下的建築成果嗎？** → `architectural legacy`
3. **是在城市／街區尺度談整體環境嗎？** → `historic built environment`
4. **是在正式文化遺產政策語境嗎？** → `built cultural heritage`
5. **主要是談歷史建築本身嗎？** → `historic architecture`
6. **強調建築已具有 heritage value 嗎？** → `heritage architecture`
7. **想突出代際承傳嗎？** → `architectural inheritance`
8. **是在談傳統建造方法、材料和工藝嗎？** → `inherited building traditions`
9. **是在技術上談現存歷史建築整體嗎？** → `historic building stock`
10. **是在談原有材料／構造嗎？** → `historic fabric`
11. **是在談舊城街道與建築群關係嗎？** → `historic urban fabric`
12. **是在談一套長期形成的建築文化嗎？** → `architectural tradition`
13. **是地方性、民間性的建築傳統嗎？** → `vernacular building traditions`
14. **需要非常正式的文化財產用語嗎？** → `architectural patrimony`
15. **是具體一棟棟受保育建築嗎？** → `heritage buildings`
16. **想用較自然、敘事性的總結說法嗎？** → `the built legacy of the past`

# 10 Final note for system use

這一組按照「close → broad near-synonym → common/formal expression」的邏輯整理，但不把層級寫死。

最有用的替代表達包括：

`built heritage / architectural legacy / historic built environment / built cultural heritage / historic architecture / heritage architecture / architectural inheritance / inherited building traditions / historic building stock / historic fabric / historic urban fabric / architectural tradition / vernacular building traditions / architectural patrimony / heritage buildings / the built legacy of the past`

常見 heritage expressions：

`preserve the character of a place / respect the historic fabric / give old buildings a new lease of life / breathe new life into old buildings / link the past with the present / handed down through generations`

真正的選詞依據是：

> **Are you referring to heritage buildings themselves, the wider historic built environment, the physical historic fabric, or inherited architectural traditions?**
