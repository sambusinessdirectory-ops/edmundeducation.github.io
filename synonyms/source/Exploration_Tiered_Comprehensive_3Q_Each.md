# EXPLORATION · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Exploration

**「探索／探究／考察」，要先分清是有目的的調查、開放式求知、學術研究、深入追問，還是實地考察。**

本單元集中處理 **exploration = the act or process of investigating, examining, discovering, or learning about something unfamiliar or not yet fully understood**。你提供的 **investigation; discovery; journey of inquiry** 分別代表 **查明事實、找到新知、探究歷程** 三個重要方向。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且高實用度的詞／表達，並按最新 inspection 標準區分 **inquiry / research / examination / study / fact-finding / probing / search for answers / quest for knowledge / expedition**。每個目標設 **3 道題**；較比喻性或情境限定的表達都清楚標示使用限制。

## Tier 1 — Core inquiry / research equivalents · 核心調查、探究與研究近義詞

### 01 Investigation

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：調查；探究  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：a systematic attempt to discover facts, causes, or explanations  
**用法提示**：當 exploration 強調有目的地查明真相、原因或證據時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：conduct an investigation; detailed investigation; scientific investigation; investigation into; further investigation.  
**常見搭配對象**：cause, incident, evidence, problem, question.  
**與 exploration 的辨析**：Investigation 比 exploration 更聚焦於查明事實或原因。  
**使用限制**：若只是開放式、沒有明確問題的探索，exploration / inquiry 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The team began a detailed investigation into the cause of the failure.  
團隊開始詳細調查故障原因。

### 02 Inquiry

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：探究；查詢；調查  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：the process of asking questions and seeking information or understanding  
**用法提示**：當 exploration 偏向提出問題、逐步尋找理解時很適合。  
**常見搭配**：inquiry into; line of inquiry; scientific inquiry; open inquiry; inquiry-based learning.  
**常見搭配對象**：question, topic, issue, evidence, learning.  
**與 exploration 的辨析**：Inquiry 強調問問題和求理解；exploration 更廣，可包括實地或創意探索。  
**使用限制**：已進入正式事故調查時 investigation 往往更精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The course encourages inquiry into how cities change over time.  
課程鼓勵學生探究城市如何隨時間改變。

### 03 Research

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：研究；研究工作  
**語域**：academic / formal  
**核心語義**：systematic study intended to establish facts, develop knowledge, or reach conclusions  
**用法提示**：當 exploration 發生在學術、科學或資料蒐集語境中很自然。  
**常見搭配**：conduct research; research into; academic research; scientific research; research project.  
**常見搭配對象**：topic, data, theory, problem, field.  
**與 exploration 的辨析**：Research 通常方法更系統、證據要求更高；exploration 可以更初步。  
**使用限制**：純粹個人好奇或旅遊式探索不一定叫 research。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Her research explores how sleep affects memory.  
她的研究探討睡眠如何影響記憶。

### 04 Examination

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：檢視；審視；研究  
**語域**：formal / academic  
**核心語義**：a careful and detailed consideration of something  
**用法提示**：當 exploration 重點是仔細看某個主題、資料、問題或案例時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：careful examination; detailed examination; examination of evidence; closer examination; systematic examination.  
**常見搭配對象**：evidence, issue, text, data, case.  
**與 exploration 的辨析**：Examination 強調仔細審視；exploration 可更開放、更發散。  
**使用限制**：探索未知地區通常不用 examination。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The report provides a careful examination of the policy's effects.  
報告仔細檢視該政策的影響。

### 05 Study

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：研究；探討  
**語域**：neutral / academic  
**核心語義**：careful learning, analysis, or investigation of a subject  
**用法提示**：當 exploration 指對主題進行有系統的學習或分析時很常用。  
**常見搭配**：study of; detailed study; case study; comparative study; scientific study.  
**常見搭配對象**：subject, behaviour, society, language, data.  
**與 exploration 的辨析**：Study 比 exploration 更穩定、學術；exploration 可以只是初步接觸。  
**使用限制**：短暫試探新想法時不一定適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The book is a study of changing family life.  
這本書研究家庭生活的變化。

## Tier 2 — Discovery / fact-finding / probing variants · 發現、查證與深入探究變體

### 06 Discovery

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：發現；探索所得  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：the act or process of finding something previously unknown  
**用法提示**：當 exploration 的重點是『找到了新事物／新知識』時可以作結果型近義表達。  
**常見搭配**：scientific discovery; discovery of; process of discovery; new discovery; journey of discovery.  
**常見搭配對象**：fact, place, method, species, idea.  
**與 exploration 的辨析**：Discovery 是探索的結果／過程之一；exploration 不保證一定有發現。  
**使用限制**：若重點是查問過程而非找到新事物，inquiry / investigation 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The project became a process of discovery for the whole team.  
這個項目成為整個團隊的一段發現過程。

### 07 Fact-finding

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：事實查證；蒐集事實  
**語域**：formal / professional  
**核心語義**：the process of gathering facts in order to understand a situation or make a decision  
**用法提示**：當 exploration 是為了蒐集可靠資料、了解情況時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：fact-finding mission; fact-finding exercise; fact-finding process; fact-finding visit; initial fact-finding.  
**常見搭配對象**：case, dispute, situation, policy, incident.  
**與 exploration 的辨析**：Fact-finding 強調蒐集已存在的事實；exploration 可包括理論和創意可能性。  
**使用限制**：哲學或創意探索通常不用 fact-finding。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The visit was mainly a fact-finding exercise.  
這次訪問主要是一次事實查證工作。

### 08 Probing

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：深入探查；追問；探究  
**語域**：formal / investigative  
**核心語義**：close, persistent questioning or investigation intended to uncover deeper information  
**用法提示**：當 exploration 帶有深入追問、挖掘隱藏原因或細節時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：probing questions; probing inquiry; probing investigation; careful probing; further probing.  
**常見搭配對象**：question, motive, cause, issue, evidence.  
**與 exploration 的辨析**：Probing 比 exploration 更深入、更具追問感。  
**使用限制**：開放式學習或旅遊探索不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The interview involved careful probing of the witness's account.  
訪談仔細追問證人的敘述。

### 09 Search for answers

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：尋找答案；探索答案  
**語域**：neutral / explanatory  
**核心語義**：an effort to understand a question by looking for explanations or solutions  
**用法提示**：當 exploration 是圍繞一個問題逐步尋找答案時，是透明自然的改寫。  
**常見搭配**：search for answers; continue the search; search for explanations; search for solutions; ongoing search.  
**常見搭配對象**：question, problem, mystery, cause, explanation.  
**與 exploration 的辨析**：這是目的型改寫，聚焦『找答案』；exploration 更廣。  
**使用限制**：沒有明確問題時可能太窄。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The documentary follows scientists in their search for answers.  
紀錄片跟隨科學家尋找答案的過程。

### 10 Quest for knowledge

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：求知探索；知識追尋  
**語域**：formal / expressive  
**核心語義**：a sustained effort to gain knowledge or deeper understanding  
**用法提示**：當 exploration 帶理想、求知、長期追求意味時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：quest for knowledge; intellectual quest; lifelong quest; pursuit of knowledge; quest for understanding.  
**常見搭配對象**：knowledge, truth, understanding, science, learning.  
**與 exploration 的辨析**：Quest for knowledge 比 exploration 更有長期追求和理想色彩。  
**使用限制**：一般短期資料蒐集不必用這麼強的表達。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The programme presents science as a continuing quest for knowledge.  
課程把科學呈現為持續的求知探索。

## Tier 3 — Journey / expedition variants · 探究歷程與實地考察變體

### 11 Journey of inquiry

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：探究之旅；求知歷程  
**語域**：expressive / educational  
**核心語義**：a gradual process of asking questions, investigating, and developing understanding  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很適合教育、反思、學習歷程等語境。  
**常見搭配**：journey of inquiry; begin a journey of inquiry; learning journey; inquiry process; reflective inquiry.  
**常見搭配對象**：learner, student, research, learning, question.  
**與 exploration 的辨析**：Journey of inquiry 是比喻性、歷程型表達，不是技術同義詞。  
**使用限制**：正式事故報告通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The project took students on a journey of inquiry into local history.  
這個項目帶領學生展開探究本地歷史的學習歷程。

### 12 Journey of discovery

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：發現之旅；探索歷程  
**語域**：expressive / common  
**核心語義**：a process in which someone gradually discovers new facts, places, ideas, or aspects of themselves  
**用法提示**：當 exploration 強調探索中不斷有新發現時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：journey of discovery; voyage of discovery; personal discovery; journey of self-discovery; process of discovery.  
**常見搭配對象**：learner, traveller, scientist, artist, individual.  
**與 exploration 的辨析**：Journey of discovery 更強調結果與體驗感；exploration 更中性。  
**使用限制**：高度正式或技術文件中會顯得太修辭化。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Learning another language can become a journey of discovery.  
學習另一種語言可以成為一段發現之旅。

### 13 Expedition

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：探險；考察；遠征  
**語域**：neutral / geographical / scientific  
**核心語義**：an organised journey undertaken for exploration, research, or a particular purpose  
**用法提示**：當 exploration 是實地、地理、自然科學或未知地區考察時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：scientific expedition; exploration expedition; research expedition; polar expedition; expedition team.  
**常見搭配對象**：region, cave, ocean, mountain, polar area.  
**與 exploration 的辨析**：Expedition 是一次組織化旅程；exploration 可以是整個活動或概念。  
**使用限制**：純思想／資料探索不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The scientists joined an expedition to study the glacier.  
科學家參加考察隊研究冰川。

## 04 False synonyms / exploration traps · 容易誤選的詞與探索陷阱

### 01 Experiment

**實驗不等於 exploration**  
experiment 是在控制條件下測試假設；exploration 可以完全沒有實驗設計。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
An experiment tests a hypothesis; exploration is broader.

### 02 Search

**搜尋不等於所有 exploration**  
search 強調尋找某個已知或預期目標；exploration 可以開放式地了解未知領域。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Search has a target; exploration can be open-ended.

### 03 Inspection

**檢查不等於 exploration**  
inspection 是按標準檢查狀況、合規或缺陷；探索通常更開放。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Inspection checks condition or compliance.

### 04 Survey

**調查／測量不等於所有 exploration**  
survey 可蒐集資料或概覽範圍，但不等於所有深入探索。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A survey samples or maps; exploration may go deeper.

### 05 Adventure

**冒險不等於 exploration**  
adventure 強調刺激、經歷或風險；探索不一定冒險。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Adventure is experiential; exploration is investigative or discoverative.

### 06 Travel

**旅行不等於 exploration**  
travel 只是移動到其他地方；沒有求知或發現目的也可以旅行。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Travel can occur without exploration.

### 07 Discovery

**discovery 只在部分語境接近 exploration**  
discovery 是找到新事物的結果；exploration 是尋找和了解的過程。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Discovery is often an outcome of exploration.

### 08 Explore / exploration

**詞性陷阱**  
explore 是動詞；exploration 是名詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Explore is a verb; exploration is a noun.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Investigation · 調查；探究

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The team began an exploration of the cause of the failure.

**中文翻譯**  
團隊開始探究故障原因。

- A. investigation
- B. adventure
- C. travel
- D. inspection only
- E. experiment necessarily
- F. discovery

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**investigation** · 調查；探究

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The team began an investigation into the cause of the failure.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. investigation ✓ — cause of the failure implies a focused attempt to establish facts and causes。**
- **B. adventure — adventure has an experiential/risk meaning, not causal analysis。**
- **C. travel — travel is movement between places, unrelated here。**
- **D. inspection only — inspection only checks condition; it may form part of the investigation but is narrower。**
- **E. experiment necessarily — experiment necessarily assumes controlled testing, which the sentence does not state。**
- **F. discovery — discovery would be the result, not the investigative process.**

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| 提出問題求理解 | **inquiry** | 是否以 questions / understanding 為核心？ |
| 學術系統研究 | **research** | 是否是 university / scientific / data context？ |
| 仔細審視 | **examination** | 是否是 detailed / closer examination？ |
| 有系統學習分析 | **study** | 是否是 study of a subject / behaviour？ |
| 找到新事物 | **discovery** | 是否重點是 finding something new？ |
| 蒐集事實 | **fact-finding** | 是否為決策／了解情況而 gather facts？ |
| 深入追問 | **probing** | 是否要 uncover deeper information？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **exploration vs investigation**: investigation 更聚焦查明事實、原因或責任。
- **exploration vs inquiry**: inquiry 強調問問題和求理解。
- **exploration vs research**: research 更系統、學術和證據導向。
- **exploration vs examination**: examination 強調仔細審視既有材料。
- **exploration vs discovery**: discovery 常是探索的結果。
- **exploration vs search**: search 通常已有較明確目標；exploration 可以開放式。
- **exploration vs experiment**: experiment 是測試假設的方法，不是所有探索。
- **exploration vs inspection**: inspection 是按標準檢查狀況或合規。
- **exploration vs expedition**: expedition 是一次組織化實地旅程。
- **exploration vs adventure**: adventure 強調經歷和刺激，不等於求知探索。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & exploration traps · 文法、搭配與探索陷阱

- **exploration of + topic/place**：exploration of space / a topic / new ideas。
- 動詞是 **explore**：explore a question / explore a region / explore possibilities。
- **investigation into + issue/cause**：an investigation into the accident。
- **inquiry into + topic**：an inquiry into the causes；教育語境亦常用 inquiry-based learning。
- **research into/on + topic**：research into memory / research on climate。
- **examination of + evidence/data**：a detailed examination of the evidence。
- **study of + subject**：a study of language use。
- **fact-finding** 常作前置修飾語：a fact-finding mission / exercise。
- **probe / probing** 常和 deeper questions / motives / causes 搭配。
- **journey of inquiry / journey of discovery** 是比喻性表達，不是正式技術術語。
- **expedition** 通常是可數：an expedition / two expeditions。
- **discovery ≠ exploration**：discovery 是發現；exploration 是尋找、了解的過程。
- **search ≠ exploration**：search 通常有較明確目標。
