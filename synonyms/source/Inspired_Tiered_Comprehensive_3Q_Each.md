# INSPIRED · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Inspired

**「受到啟發／激勵／感動」，要先分清是產生行動動力、作品受到來源影響、想法被激發，還是情感上受到觸動。**

本單元集中處理 **inspired = emotionally, creatively, or motivationally affected in a way that produces ideas, admiration, enthusiasm, or action**。你提供的 **motivated; influenced; stimulated** 分別代表 **行動動力、來源影響、思想／興趣被激發** 三個核心方向。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且高實用度的詞／表達，共 **39 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。同時清楚區分 **inspired / motivated / influenced / moved / encouraged / galvanised / sparked / driven**，避免把情感、創意來源和行動動力混為一談。

## Tier 1 — Core motivation / influence / stimulation equivalents · 核心激勵、影響與激發近義詞

### 01 Motivated

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：受到激勵的；有動力的  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：given a reason, desire, or determination to act, work, learn, or achieve something  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，當 inspired 的重點是『因此更想行動／努力』時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：highly motivated; motivated to succeed; motivated by; feel motivated; motivated learner.  
**常見搭配對象**：student, employee, athlete, learner, team.  
**與 inspired 的辨析**：Motivated 強調行動動力；inspired 亦可只是產生創意、敬佩或新想法。  
**使用限制**：若重點是創意來源而非行動意願，influenced / sparked by 更合適。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The speech left the students motivated to work harder.  
這場演講令學生更有動力努力學習。

### 02 Influenced

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：受到影響的；受啟發而形成的  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：affected in ideas, style, behaviour, or development by another person, work, experience, or factor  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，尤其當 inspired by 表示作品、思想、風格受到某來源影響時。  
**常見搭配**：influenced by; heavily influenced; influenced the design; cultural influence; artistic influence.  
**常見搭配對象**：artist, design, style, decision, idea.  
**與 inspired 的辨析**：Influenced 比 inspired 更中性；inspired 往往帶正面、創意或激勵意味。  
**使用限制**：單純受到負面影響也可用 influenced，但通常不會說 inspired。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Her early paintings were strongly influenced by Japanese art.  
她早期畫作深受日本藝術影響。

### 03 Stimulated

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：受到刺激的；被激發的  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：encouraged or activated so that thought, interest, activity, or development increases  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 適合思想、興趣、創造力、討論、經濟活動等被『激發』的語境。  
**常見搭配**：stimulated by; stimulate interest; stimulate creativity; intellectually stimulated; stimulate discussion.  
**常見搭配對象**：interest, creativity, thought, discussion, activity.  
**與 inspired 的辨析**：Stimulated 偏『被觸發／被激活』；inspired 更常帶情感、理想或創意上的鼓舞。  
**使用限制**：形容深受某榜樣感動時，inspired / encouraged 通常更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The workshop stimulated new ideas among participants.  
工作坊激發了參加者的新想法。

### 04 Encouraged

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：受到鼓勵的；獲得信心的  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：given confidence, support, or hope to continue or attempt something  
**用法提示**：當 inspired 的效果是令人更有信心、更願意繼續時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：feel encouraged; encouraged by progress; encouraged to apply; greatly encouraged; encouraged by support.  
**常見搭配對象**：student, applicant, patient, team, learner.  
**與 inspired 的辨析**：Encouraged 強調信心與支持；inspired 通常更強、更有理想或創意感。  
**使用限制**：若是藝術作品提供創意來源，encouraged 不夠精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She felt encouraged by the positive feedback.  
正面回饋令她受到鼓勵。

### 05 Inspired by

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：受……啟發；靈感來自……  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：having an idea, design, action, or feeling generated by a particular source  
**用法提示**：這是 headword 最核心搭配，特別適合創作、設計、理念、故事和人物榜樣。  
**常見搭配**：inspired by nature; inspired by history; inspired by a true story; inspired by art; inspired by experience.  
**常見搭配對象**：design, story, artwork, project, idea.  
**與 inspired 的辨析**：Inspired by 往往表示來源帶來新想法或創意；influenced by 只表示受到影響。  
**使用限制**：不要把所有 influence 都寫成 inspiration，尤其負面或無意識影響。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The collection was inspired by the colours of the sea.  
這個系列的靈感來自海洋的色彩。

## Tier 2 — Energy / emotion / action-driving variants · 幹勁、感動與行動推動變體

### 06 Energised

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：受到鼓舞而充滿幹勁的；精神振奮的  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：made more energetic, enthusiastic, or ready to act  
**用法提示**：當 inspired 的效果是精神振奮、重新有幹勁時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：feel energised; energised by; newly energised; energised team; energised after.  
**常見搭配對象**：team, audience, employee, campaign, group.  
**與 inspired 的辨析**：Energised 強調活力和幹勁；inspired 可更深層地涉及理念或創造力。  
**使用限制**：創作來源通常不用 energised by 取代 inspired by。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The team felt energised after the successful launch.  
成功發布後，團隊感到精神振奮、幹勁十足。

### 07 Moved

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：受到感動的；深受觸動的  
**語域**：neutral / emotional  
**核心語義**：emotionally affected, especially in a way that produces sympathy, admiration, or strong feeling  
**用法提示**：當 inspired 是因故事、人物或行動而深受感動時可用。  
**常見搭配**：deeply moved; moved by a story; moved to tears; emotionally moved; profoundly moved.  
**常見搭配對象**：speech, story, act, performance, person.  
**與 inspired 的辨析**：Moved 強調情感觸動；inspired 通常還帶『因此產生行動／理想／創意』。  
**使用限制**：被感動不一定等於被激勵採取行動。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The audience was deeply moved by her story.  
觀眾被她的故事深深感動。

### 08 Fired up

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：被激起熱情的；幹勁十足的  
**語域**：informal  
**核心語義**：made highly enthusiastic, excited, or determined to act  
**用法提示**：當 inspired 很有激情、臨場鼓舞或團隊動員意味時很地道。  
**常見搭配**：get fired up; fired up about; fired up for the game; fired-up team; really fired up.  
**常見搭配對象**：athlete, team, supporter, campaign, audience.  
**與 inspired 的辨析**：Fired up 比 inspired 更口語、更激昂。  
**使用限制**：正式寫作通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The coach's speech got the players fired up for the match.  
教練的演說令球員為比賽熱血沸騰。

### 09 Galvanised

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：受到強烈激發而採取行動的；被動員的  
**語域**：formal / strong  
**核心語義**：shocked, inspired, or energised into taking action  
**用法提示**：當 inspired 不只是情緒，而是強烈推動一群人立即行動時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：galvanised into action; galvanised by events; galvanise support; galvanise public opinion; galvanised campaigners.  
**常見搭配對象**：public, campaigner, movement, team, community.  
**與 inspired 的辨析**：Galvanised 比 inspired 更強調被推動至實際行動。  
**使用限制**：個人輕微受到啟發時太強。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The report galvanised campaigners into action.  
該報告強烈激發倡議者採取行動。

### 10 Spurred on

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：受到推動的；被激勵繼續前進的  
**語域**：neutral / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：encouraged or driven to make greater effort, often by success, competition, praise, or difficulty  
**用法提示**：當 inspired 表示某因素令人在原有方向上更努力時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：spurred on by success; spurred on by competition; spurred on to improve; spurred into action; spurred on by praise.  
**常見搭配對象**：athlete, worker, team, student, competitor.  
**與 inspired 的辨析**：Spurred on 強調額外推力和持續努力；inspired 可在起點產生全新想法。  
**使用限制**：藝術設計的靈感來源不用 spurred on。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She was spurred on by her early success.  
早期成功推動她繼續努力。

## Tier 3 — Strong drive / idea-trigger / sustained motivation variants · 強烈驅動、靈感觸發與持續動力變體

### 11 Impelled

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：被驅使的；受到強烈推動的  
**語域**：formal / literary  
**核心語義**：driven by a strong feeling, belief, or force to take action  
**用法提示**：當 inspired 帶內在信念或強烈情感驅使人行動時可用。  
**常見搭配**：impelled by duty; impelled to act; impelled by compassion; morally impelled; impelled by conviction.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, activist, leader, witness, individual.  
**與 inspired 的辨析**：Impelled 比 inspired 更有『被力量推着去做』的意味。  
**使用限制**：一般日常語氣偏正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She was impelled by compassion to offer help.  
同情心驅使她提供協助。

### 12 Sparked

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：被觸發的；被激起的  
**語域**：neutral / figurative  
**核心語義**：caused to begin suddenly, especially an idea, interest, discussion, movement, or creative process  
**用法提示**：當 inspired 的意思是某事觸發了想法、興趣或創作起點時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：sparked an idea; sparked interest; sparked creativity; sparked a debate; sparked by.  
**常見搭配對象**：idea, interest, debate, movement, curiosity.  
**與 inspired 的辨析**：Sparked 強調起點／觸發；inspired 更常描述整體靈感來源。  
**使用限制**：描述持續動力時 motivated / spurred on 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
A childhood memory sparked the idea for the novel.  
一段童年回憶觸發了小說的構思。

### 13 Driven

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：受到驅動的；有強烈動力的  
**語域**：neutral / strong  
**核心語義**：strongly motivated by a goal, value, ambition, need, or belief  
**用法提示**：當 inspired 的效果是產生持續、強烈的內在動力時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：driven by ambition; driven by curiosity; highly driven; driven to succeed; purpose-driven.  
**常見搭配對象**：student, entrepreneur, athlete, researcher, activist.  
**與 inspired 的辨析**：Driven 更持續、更目標導向；inspired 可以是一刻的感動或靈感。  
**使用限制**：不適合描述單純受到藝術風格影響。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He was driven by a desire to improve public education.  
改善公共教育的願望驅動着他。

## 04 False synonyms / inspired traps · 容易誤選的詞與「受到啟發」陷阱

### 01 Excited

**興奮不等於 inspired**  
excited 是情緒高漲；inspired 常帶新想法、理想或行動動力。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Excitement may accompany inspiration but is not the same.

### 02 Interested

**有興趣不等於 inspired**  
interested 只表示注意／好奇；inspired 通常更強。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Interest is weaker than inspiration.

### 03 Impressed

**印象深刻不等於 inspired**  
impressed 是覺得出色；不一定因此產生行動或創意。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Admiration does not necessarily create inspiration.

### 04 Influenced

**influenced 只在『受到來源影響』sense 接近 inspired**  
influence 可以正面、負面或無意識；inspiration 通常更正面、有創意／激勵意味。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Influence is broader and more neutral.

### 05 Motivated

**motivated 只在『有行動動力』sense 接近 inspired**  
motivation 聚焦想做／努力做；inspiration 還可指創意來源。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Motivation is action-oriented.

### 06 Encouraged

**encouraged 通常比 inspired 輕**  
encouraged 強調得到信心或支持；inspired 往往更強、更理想化。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Encouragement is often milder.

### 07 Moved

**moved 是情感觸動，不保證產生行動或創意**  
一個故事可以令人感動，但未必令人受到啟發。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Being moved is emotional; being inspired may lead further.

### 08 Inspire / inspired

**詞性陷阱**  
inspire 是動詞；inspired 是形容詞／過去分詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Inspire is a verb; inspired describes the resulting state.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Motivated · 受到激勵的；有動力的

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The speech left the students inspired to work harder.

**中文翻譯**  
演講令學生受到激勵，更想努力。

- A. motivated
- B. excited
- C. interested
- D. impressed
- E. moved
- F. influenced

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**motivated** · 受到激勵的；有動力的

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The speech left the students motivated to work harder.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. motivated ✓ — to work harder shows action-oriented motivation, making motivated the best fit。**
- **B. excited — excited only describes emotional arousal。**
- **C. interested — interested is much weaker and does not imply effort。**
- **D. impressed — impressed means admiring the speech, not acting because of it。**
- **E. moved — moved is emotional rather than goal-directed。**
- **F. influenced — influenced is broader and does not specify motivation.**

| 有行動動力 | **motivated** | 是否 to work / apply / improve / succeed？ |
| 作品／思想受來源影響 | **influenced** | 是否 design / style / thinking shaped by a source？ |
| 思想／興趣被激發 | **stimulated** | 是否 ideas / interest / curiosity increased？ |
| 得到信心 | **encouraged** | 是否 support / feedback makes someone continue？ |

| 深受感動 | **moved** | 是否 emotional response / moved to tears？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **inspired vs motivated**: motivated 聚焦『想做／努力做』；inspired 更廣，可包含創意和理想。
- **inspired vs influenced**: influenced 更中性，可正面或負面；inspired 通常偏正面。
- **inspired vs stimulated**: stimulated 強調想法／興趣被激活。
- **inspired vs encouraged**: encouraged 比較輕，重點是信心和支持。
- **inspired vs moved**: moved 是情感觸動，不一定帶行動或創意。
- **inspired vs energised**: energised 是精神／幹勁提升。
- **inspired vs galvanised**: galvanised 明確強調被推動至行動。
- **inspired vs sparked**: sparked 強調某個想法／興趣的起點。
- **inspired vs driven**: driven 是長期、持續、目標導向的內在動力。
- **inspired vs impressed**: impressed 只是覺得出色，不等於受到啟發。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & inspired traps · 文法、搭配與語義陷阱

- **inspired by + source**：inspired by nature / a true story / a teacher。
- **inspired to + verb**：inspired to act / learn / create。
- **motivated to + verb**；**motivated by + reason**。
- **influenced by + source** 常見於藝術、設計、思想和決定。
- **stimulated by + source**；亦常說 stimulate interest / discussion / creativity。
- **encouraged by + progress/support**；**encouraged to + verb**。
- **moved by + story/person**；固定搭配 **moved to tears**。
- **fired up** 是口語：get fired up / be fired up for a match。
- **galvanise + person/group + into action** 是很強的行動推動結構。
- **spur someone on**：spurred on by success / competition。
- **impelled by + duty/compassion** 偏正式書面。
- **spark + idea/interest/debate**：重點是開始。
- **driven by + ambition/curiosity/purpose**：重點是持續內在動力。
- **excited / interested / impressed ≠ inspired**：它們可能伴隨 inspiration，但語義更弱或不同。
