# ROUTINE · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Routine

**Routine 可以是習慣、固定模式、慣常做法、標準程序、時間安排，甚至是帶儀式感的反覆行為。** 因此替換前要先問：是在說 **one repeated behaviour、a recurring pattern、an organisational practice、a formal procedure、a timed schedule，還是 a structured regimen**？

你提供的 **habit; regular pattern; usual practice** 正好代表三個核心方向：**個人反覆行為、規律模式、慣常做法**。

本次保留 **9 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達；不把 timetable、tradition 等相鄰詞硬當全面同義詞。共 **27 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Routine 的 sense | 核心問題 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|---|
| 個人反覆行為 | 是否一個常做、甚至自動化的行為？ | habit |
| 規律模式 | 是否重點是 predictable repetition？ | regular pattern, day-to-day pattern |
| 慣常做法／慣例 | 是否組織一向都這樣做？ | usual practice, customary practice |
| 標準步驟 | 是否正式、技術、可重複的 procedure？ | standard procedure |
| 健康／訓練固定安排 | 是否 structured exercise/treatment/training？ | regimen |
| 時間安排 | 是否重點在何時做甚麼？ | schedule |
| 固定而有儀式感的行為 | 是否帶 fixed form / personal significance？ | ritual |

## Tier 1 — Core habit / pattern / practice equivalents · 核心習慣、模式與慣常做法近義詞

### 01 Habit

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：習慣；慣常做法  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：a behaviour or action that someone does regularly, often automatically  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **routine** 在『個人反覆做的行為』sense 最核心的近義詞之一。  
**常見搭配**：daily habit; healthy habit; bad habit; develop a habit; break a habit.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, behaviour, exercise, sleep, study.  
**與 routine 的辨析**：Habit 更偏個人反覆行為，甚至帶自動化；routine 常是一連串有固定次序的活動。  
**使用限制**：一個完整晨早流程通常叫 routine，不只是單一 habit。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Checking her diary every morning has become a habit.  
每天早上查看日程表已成為她的習慣。

### 02 Regular pattern

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：固定模式；規律模式  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：a repeated and predictable way in which actions or events occur  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很自然，當 routine 的重點是『反覆出現的規律模式』而不是具體一套步驟時可用。  
**常見搭配**：regular pattern of activity; regular pattern of behaviour; establish a pattern; repeated pattern.  
**常見搭配對象**：activity, behaviour, sleep, work, movement.  
**與 routine 的辨析**：Regular pattern 偏描述規律性；routine 通常更像既定做法／流程。  
**使用限制**：若需要表示某機構正式做事程序，procedure / standard practice 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The team follows a regular pattern of meetings and reviews.  
團隊依循固定的會議與檢討模式。

### 03 Usual practice

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：通常做法；慣常做法  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：the normal way in which something is usually done  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，尤其適合 workplace, administration, service, organisation 等『一貫做法』。  
**常見搭配**：usual practice; normal practice; standard practice; follow usual practice; depart from usual practice.  
**常見搭配對象**：organisation, office, service, procedure, administration.  
**與 routine 的辨析**：Usual practice 強調 normal way of doing things；routine 可以是個人行程或一連串日常活動。  
**使用限制**：睡眠／健身等個人生活流程通常直接用 routine / habit 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
It is our usual practice to confirm appointments by email.  
我們通常會以電郵確認預約。

### 04 Customary practice

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：慣例；慣常做法  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：a practice that has become established through repeated use or tradition  
**用法提示**：當 routine 帶『長久形成的慣例』意味時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：customary practice; customary procedure; established practice; customary way; according to custom.  
**常見搭配對象**：institution, profession, community, organisation, ceremony.  
**與 routine 的辨析**：Customary practice 比 usual practice 更強調 established convention / tradition。  
**使用限制**：臨時形成的工作日程或個人晨間 routine 不一定稱 customary。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
It is customary practice to record all formal decisions in writing.  
把所有正式決定以書面記錄是慣常做法。

### 05 Standard procedure

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：標準程序；常規程序  
**語域**：formal / workplace / technical  
**核心語義**：an established sequence of steps normally followed in a particular situation  
**用法提示**：當 routine 指工作場所或技術情境中的『固定步驟』時非常精準。  
**常見搭配**：standard procedure; follow procedure; operating procedure; routine procedure; safety procedure.  
**常見搭配對象**：workplace, laboratory, hospital, office, system.  
**與 routine 的辨析**：Standard procedure 比 routine 更正式、更步驟化，也常有 compliance 意味。  
**使用限制**：個人生活習慣通常不用 procedure。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Checking the equipment before use is standard procedure.  
使用前檢查設備是標準程序。

## Tier 2 — Regimen / schedule / ritual variants · 訓練安排、時間表與儀式型變體

### 06 Regimen

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：固定養生／訓練安排；日常規律  
**語域**：formal / health / training  
**核心語義**：a structured and regular programme of diet, exercise, treatment, or training  
**用法提示**：當 routine 是健康、復康、健身、護膚、訓練等有規律安排時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：exercise regimen; training regimen; treatment regimen; skincare regimen; strict regimen.  
**常見搭配對象**：exercise, training, treatment, diet, skincare.  
**與 routine 的辨析**：Regimen 比 routine 更有 structured discipline / programme 感。  
**使用限制**：一般辦公室日常程序或家庭瑣事不宜用 regimen。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The athlete follows a strict training regimen.  
這名運動員依循嚴格的訓練安排。

### 07 Schedule

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：時間表；固定安排  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：a planned arrangement of activities or events according to times  
**用法提示**：當 routine 的核心是『何時做甚麼』而不是行為本身時可用。  
**常見搭配**：daily schedule; regular schedule; work schedule; follow a schedule; fixed schedule.  
**常見搭配對象**：work, class, meeting, exercise, appointment.  
**與 routine 的辨析**：Schedule 重點是 time arrangement；routine 重點是 repeated activity pattern。  
**使用限制**：如果沒有時間安排，只是習慣行為，schedule 不合適。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Her daily schedule includes two hours of study in the evening.  
她的每日時間表包括晚上兩小時溫習。

### 08 Ritual

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：固定儀式；例行習慣  
**語域**：neutral / figurative  
**核心語義**：a repeated action or sequence that has a fixed form and often personal, social, or symbolic significance  
**用法提示**：當 routine 帶固定、反覆、甚至有個人儀式感的意味時可用。  
**常見搭配**：morning ritual; bedtime ritual; daily ritual; pre-match ritual; family ritual.  
**常見搭配對象**：morning, bedtime, meal, sport, family.  
**與 routine 的辨析**：Ritual 比 routine 更有 fixed form / symbolic or emotional significance。  
**使用限制**：正式工作程序不應隨便改成 ritual。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Making tea before work is part of his morning ritual.  
上班前泡茶是他晨早固定儀式的一部分。

## Tier 3 — Broad everyday-pattern paraphrase · 整體日常模式改寫

### 09 Day-to-day pattern

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：日常模式；每天的固定模式  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：the recurring pattern of ordinary activities that make up everyday life or work  
**用法提示**：當 routine 是描述整體日常節奏，而不是單一行為或正式程序時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：day-to-day pattern; day-to-day routine; everyday pattern; daily rhythm; pattern of daily life.  
**常見搭配對象**：life, work, activity, behaviour, household.  
**與 routine 的辨析**：這是 descriptive paraphrase，偏整體 daily rhythm，而不是一個精準 lexical synonym。  
**使用限制**：正式 technical procedure 不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Remote work changed the family's day-to-day pattern considerably.  
遙距工作大幅改變了這個家庭的日常模式。

## 04 False synonyms / routine traps · 容易誤選的習慣、程序與時間安排陷阱

### 01 Schedule

**時間表 ≠ 所有 routine**  
schedule 強調何時做；routine 強調反覆做甚麼。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A schedule is time-focused.

### 02 Procedure

**程序 ≠ 所有 routine**  
procedure 是步驟化做法，通常較正式。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Procedure is step-focused.

### 03 Habit

**習慣 ≠ 完整 routine**  
habit 常是一個反覆行為；routine 可以是一整套行動。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A habit is often one behaviour.

### 04 Tradition

**傳統 ≠ routine**  
tradition 通常跨代或社群延續，並不等於日常例行活動。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Tradition is culturally or socially inherited.

### 05 Ritual

**儀式 ≠ 所有 routine**  
ritual 常有固定形式或象徵／情感意義。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Ritual carries form or significance.

### 06 Timetable

**時間表 ≠ routine**  
timetable 是正式時間安排，不代表活動模式本身。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Timetable is schedule-like.

### 07 Regimen

**只在健康／訓練 sense 接近**  
regimen 通常帶有結構化健康、治療或訓練安排。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Regimen is domain-specific.

### 08 Routine / routinely

**詞性陷阱**  
routine 可作名詞／形容詞；routinely 是副詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check word class before replacing.

Checking her diary every morning is part of her routine.

- B. Regular pattern

- D. Schedule
- E. Procedure
- F. Ritual

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Habit** · 習慣；慣常做法

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
Checking her diary every morning has become a habit.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Habit ✓** — means a behaviour someone repeats regularly, often automatically, and is often narrower than a whole routine. Here, one repeated personal behaviour is being described. The sentence therefore calls for **Habit**, not just any word meaning something repeated.
- **B.** **Regular pattern** — means a predictable repeated way in which activities or events occur. This describes repetition and predictability, but does not by itself express an established practice, fixed procedure or personal ritual. The decisive clue is that one repeated personal behaviour is being described.
- **C.** **Usual practice** — means the normal way an organisation or person usually does something. This is strongest for an organisation's normal way of doing something; it is less suitable for personal habits, timed schedules or formal step-by-step procedures. The decisive clue is that one repeated personal behaviour is being described.
- **D.** **Schedule** — means a planned arrangement of activities according to time. This is about timing and planned slots; it does not capture a habit, institutional practice or ritual unless time arrangement is central. The decisive clue is that one repeated personal behaviour is being described.
- **E.** **Procedure** — means an established sequence of steps and is usually more formal than a general routine. This is step-focused and formal, not a general synonym for everyday routine. The decisive clue is that one repeated personal behaviour is being described.
- **F.** **Ritual** — means a repeated action or sequence with fixed form and often personal or symbolic significance. This adds a fixed-form or personally meaningful quality that the context may not support. The decisive clue is that one repeated personal behaviour is being described.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **routine vs habit**: habit 常是一個行為；routine 常是一連串活動。
- **routine vs regular pattern**: pattern 描述規律性，不一定是刻意建立的流程。
- **routine vs usual practice**: usual practice 特別適合機構／工作場合的慣常做法。
- **routine vs customary practice**: customary practice 更正式，帶慣例／傳統意味。
- **routine vs standard procedure**: procedure 更步驟化、更正式，也常涉及規範。
- **routine vs regimen**: regimen 適合健康、訓練、治療等有紀律的安排。
- **routine vs schedule**: schedule 是時間安排；routine 是反覆做的活動模式。
- **routine vs ritual**: ritual 帶固定形式或個人／象徵意義。
- **routine vs timetable**: timetable 只是列出時間，不是行為模式本身。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & routine traps · 文法、搭配與『例行／慣常』陷阱

- **daily / morning / evening routine**：最常見個人生活搭配。
- **get into / settle into a routine**：形成固定生活／工作模式。
- **break / change a routine**：打破／改變日常模式。
- **habit of + -ing**：a habit of checking emails early。
- **usual/customary practice to + verb**：It is usual practice to confirm bookings。
- **standard procedure for + -ing / to + verb**：standard procedure for checking identity。
- **training / exercise / treatment regimen**：健康與訓練語境。
- **work / daily schedule**：時間安排。
- **morning / bedtime / pre-match ritual**：固定又有儀式感。
- **routine** 可作名詞及形容詞：a daily routine / a routine check。
- **routinely** 是副詞：The system is routinely checked。
- 不要把 **tradition, timetable, procedure, schedule** 全部當作 routine 的全面同義詞。
