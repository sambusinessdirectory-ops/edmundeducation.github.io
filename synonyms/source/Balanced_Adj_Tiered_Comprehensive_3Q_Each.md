# BALANCED · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Balanced · adjective

**Balanced 可以表示比例勻稱、分配均勻、程度適中、發展全面、相稱、公平、和諧或穩定。** 這些 sense 彼此相關，但不能全部互換。最重要的是判斷 **proportion, distribution, degree, breadth, fairness, harmony, or stability**。

你提供的 **well-proportioned; even; moderate** 正好代表三個核心方向：**比例協調、分布均勻、程度適中**。

本次保留 **9 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達。會特別區分 **equal / neutral / average / symmetrical / fair**，避免把『相等』『中立』『平均』『對稱』『公平』誤當所有 balanced。共 **27 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Balanced 的語境 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|
| 比例勻稱 | **well-proportioned** |
| 分布均勻 | **even** |
| 程度適中 | **moderate** |
| 多方面全面發展 | **well-rounded** |
| 相對比例相稱 | **proportionate** |
| 元素互相協調 | **harmonious** |
| 公平合理分配 | **equitable** |
| 狀態平穩 | **stable** |
| 兩方勢均力敵 | **evenly balanced** |

## Tier 1 — Core proportion / distribution / moderation equivalents · 核心比例、分布與適中近義詞

### 01 Well-proportioned

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：比例勻稱的；搭配均衡的  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：having parts that are in pleasing or appropriate proportion to one another  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **balanced** 在外形、設計、構圖、比例 sense 最直接的近義詞之一。  
**常見搭配**：well-proportioned design; well-proportioned room; well-proportioned figure; well-proportioned composition.  
**常見搭配對象**：design, room, body, composition, building.  
**與 balanced 的辨析**：Well-proportioned 強調比例關係；balanced 更廣，還可指觀點、分配、飲食或程度。  
**使用限制**：如果 balanced 指意見公平、數量相等或飲食營養，不能直接用 well-proportioned。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The room has a well-proportioned layout.  
這個房間的佈局比例勻稱。

### 02 Even

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：均勻的；平均的；平衡的  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：distributed, developed, or arranged fairly equally without large differences  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，當 balanced 的重點是分布均勻、程度接近或沒有明顯偏差時自然。  
**常見搭配**：even distribution; even balance; even spread; even growth; even surface.  
**常見搭配對象**：distribution, workload, growth, temperature, coverage.  
**與 balanced 的辨析**：Even 強調 equality/uniformity；balanced 不一定完全相等，只需各部分配合適當。  
**使用限制**：如果各部分比例不同但整體仍合理，balanced 可能比 even 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The workload is now more evenly distributed across the team.  
工作量現時在團隊之間分配得更平均。

### 03 Moderate

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：適中的；不過度的  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：neither too great nor too small; kept within reasonable limits  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 適合 balanced = neither excessive nor insufficient 的程度 sense。  
**常見搭配**：moderate amount; moderate exercise; moderate intake; moderate pace; moderate approach.  
**常見搭配對象**：exercise, consumption, pace, temperature, amount.  
**與 balanced 的辨析**：Moderate 強調程度適中；balanced 常還暗示不同元素之間的搭配。  
**使用限制**：如果重點是各部分互補、比例協調，而不是程度不極端，moderate 不夠準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
A moderate level of exercise is suitable for most beginners.  
適量運動適合大多數初學者。

### 04 Well-rounded

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：全面均衡的；多方面發展的  
**語域**：neutral / positive  
**核心語義**：including a good range of different elements, qualities, skills, or experiences  
**用法提示**：當 balanced 指一個人、課程、教育、技能組合等『各方面都有發展』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：well-rounded education; well-rounded person; well-rounded skill set; well-rounded programme.  
**常見搭配對象**：education, student, programme, skill set, experience.  
**與 balanced 的辨析**：Well-rounded 強調 breadth + variety；balanced 更一般，未必涉及多方面發展。  
**使用限制**：數量分配、物理平衡、飲食比例等不一定適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The school aims to provide a well-rounded education.  
學校旨在提供全面均衡的教育。

### 05 Proportionate

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：成比例的；相稱的；適度的  
**語域**：formal / analytical  
**核心語義**：appropriate in size, degree, or amount relative to something else  
**用法提示**：當 balanced 指各部分與需求、風險、規模或責任之間相稱時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：proportionate response; proportionate share; proportionate allocation; proportionate measure.  
**常見搭配對象**：response, share, cost, risk, burden.  
**與 balanced 的辨析**：Proportionate 強調 relative proportion；balanced 強調整體平衡。  
**使用限制**：如果沒有明確比較基準，proportionate 可能不自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The response should be proportionate to the level of risk.  
應對措施應與風險程度相稱。

## Tier 2 — Harmony / fairness / stability variants · 和諧、公平與穩定變體

### 06 Harmonious

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：協調的；和諧平衡的  
**語域**：neutral / aesthetic / formal  
**核心語義**：having elements that work together smoothly and pleasingly  
**用法提示**：當 balanced 描述顏色、設計、團隊關係、構圖或聲音彼此協調時可用。  
**常見搭配**：harmonious design; harmonious colours; harmonious relationship; harmonious composition.  
**常見搭配對象**：design, colour, relationship, composition, arrangement.  
**與 balanced 的辨析**：Harmonious 比 balanced 更強調 elements work well together，而不是數量或比例相等。  
**使用限制**：數學、統計、營養等技術平衡通常不用 harmonious。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The interior uses a harmonious combination of natural materials and soft colours.  
室內採用天然物料與柔和色彩的和諧搭配。

### 07 Equitable

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：公平合理的；分配均衡的  
**語域**：formal / policy / social  
**核心語義**：fair and reasonable in the way resources, opportunities, burdens, or treatment are distributed  
**用法提示**：當 balanced 的重點是公平分配，而不是純粹相等時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：equitable distribution; equitable access; equitable allocation; equitable treatment.  
**常見搭配對象**：resources, access, funding, workload, opportunity.  
**與 balanced 的辨析**：Equitable 強調 fairness；balanced 則未必涉及公平原則。  
**使用限制**：外觀、飲食、比例或中庸程度不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The policy aims for a more equitable distribution of resources.  
政策旨在更公平合理地分配資源。

### 08 Stable

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：穩定的；平穩的  
**語域**：neutral / technical  
**核心語義**：remaining steady without large fluctuations or sudden changes  
**用法提示**：當 balanced 指系統、狀態、財政、情緒或力量關係保持平穩時可用。  
**常見搭配**：stable system; stable balance; stable condition; stable economy; stable state.  
**常見搭配對象**：system, condition, economy, structure, mood.  
**與 balanced 的辨析**：Stable 強調持續不波動；balanced 強調各因素之間達成平衡。  
**使用限制**：單純公平、全面或比例良好時不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The redesigned structure remains stable under heavy loads.  
重新設計的結構在高負荷下仍保持穩定。

## Tier 3 — Explicit equality-of-sides paraphrase · 明確勢均力敵改寫

### 09 Evenly balanced

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：均衡的；勢均力敵的  
**語域**：neutral / explanatory  
**核心語義**：balanced so that neither side, factor, or group has a clear advantage or excess  
**用法提示**：這是 balanced 的透明強化改寫，適合比賽、爭論、分配或兩方力量相近的語境。  
**常見搭配**：evenly balanced contest; evenly balanced debate; evenly balanced teams; evenly balanced distribution.  
**常見搭配對象**：team, contest, argument, group, distribution.  
**與 balanced 的辨析**：比 balanced 更明確表示 two or more sides are close to equal。  
**使用限制**：若 balanced 指飲食、教育或中庸程度，這個 phrase 未必自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The two teams were evenly balanced throughout the match.  
兩隊在整場比賽中一直勢均力敵。

## 04 False synonyms / balanced traps · 容易誤選的『相等、中立、平均、對稱』陷阱

### 01 Equal

**相等 ≠ balanced**  
equal 要求相同；balanced 只需比例或配搭合理。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Equal is stricter than balanced.

### 02 Neutral

**中立 ≠ balanced**  
neutral 表示不偏向任何一方，不等於所有 balanced。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Neutral is stance-focused.

### 03 Average

**平均 ≠ balanced**  
average 是統計或一般程度概念，不代表分配或搭配合理。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Average is not balance.

### 04 Symmetrical

**對稱 ≠ balanced**  
symmetrical 是形狀／排列鏡像對稱；balanced 可不對稱。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Symmetry is geometric.

### 05 Moderate

**只在『程度適中』sense 接近**  
moderate 不代表各部分相互配合。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Moderate is degree-focused.

### 06 Stable

**只在『平穩』sense 接近**  
stable 可維持不變，但未必各因素均衡。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Stable is fluctuation-focused.

### 07 Fair

**公平 ≠ all balanced**  
fair 偏公正，不等於營養、構圖、比例等 balanced。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Fair is justice-focused.

### 08 Balanced / balance

**詞性陷阱**  
balanced 常作形容詞；balance 可作名詞或動詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check word class.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Well-proportioned · 比例勻稱的；搭配均衡的

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The building has a balanced façade with windows arranged in pleasing proportions.

**中文翻譯**  
這座建築的立面比例勻稱，窗戶排列協調。

- A. Well-proportioned
- B. Even
- C. Harmonious
- D. Equal
- E. Average
- F. Symmetrical

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Well-proportioned** · 比例勻稱的；搭配均衡的

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The building has a well-proportioned façade with windows arranged in pleasing proportions.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Well-proportioned ✓** — having parts in pleasing or appropriate proportion. Here, proportion and visual relationship are central. That makes **Well-proportioned** the most precise replacement for this particular sense of *balanced*.
- **B.** **Even** — distributed or developed fairly equally without large differences. This stresses equal or uniform distribution and may miss proportion, fairness, breadth, or stability. The decisive clue is that proportion and visual relationship are central.
- **C.** **Harmonious** — having elements that work together smoothly and pleasingly. This is aesthetic/relational and does not fit numerical, legal, or distributional balance. The decisive clue is that proportion and visual relationship are central.
- **D.** **Equal** — the same in amount, size, or value. Equal is stricter: balanced elements need not be exactly the same. The decisive clue is that proportion and visual relationship are central.
- **E.** **Average** — typical or mathematically mean. Average is a statistical or typical-value concept, not a balance concept. The decisive clue is that proportion and visual relationship are central.
- **F.** **Symmetrical** — having corresponding mirror-like parts. Symmetry concerns mirrored arrangement and is not required for balance. The decisive clue is that proportion and visual relationship are central.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **balanced vs well-proportioned**：後者強調比例。
- **balanced vs even**：even 強調均勻／接近相等。
- **balanced vs moderate**：moderate 強調程度不極端。
- **balanced vs well-rounded**：well-rounded 強調多方面發展。
- **balanced vs proportionate**：proportionate 要有相對比較基準。
- **balanced vs harmonious**：harmonious 偏協調與美感。
- **balanced vs equitable**：equitable 偏公平合理。
- **balanced vs stable**：stable 偏不波動。
- **balanced vs equal**：equal 要求相同；balanced 未必完全相等。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & balance traps

- **balanced diet / balanced view / balanced approach / balanced distribution**。
- **well-proportioned + design/room/figure**。
- **even + distribution/coverage/growth**。
- **moderate + amount/pace/intake/exercise**。
- **well-rounded + education/person/skill set**。
- **proportionate to + noun**：a response proportionate to the risk。
- **equitable distribution / equitable access**：公平合理分配。
- **stable system / condition / economy**：平穩不波動。
- **balanced** 是形容詞；**balance** 可作名詞或動詞。
- 不要把 **equal, average, neutral, symmetrical** 當作 balanced 的全面同義詞。
