# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Look for

**尋找、搜尋、尋求、四處找、主動找出、追查、物色、留意——究竟是哪一種 look for？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**search for; seek; try to find; hunt for; seek out; search out; track down; be on the lookout for; look around for; scout for; go in search of**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 11 個真正有教學價值、能代表不同搜尋方式或語域的表達。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Search for

**搜尋；尋找**

最直接、廣泛的近義表達之一；可用於人、物件、資訊、答案、工作、證據等。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The police are searching for the missing child.

警方正在尋找失蹤兒童。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- search for information

- search for a person

- search for evidence

- search for a solution


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是一般搜尋、正式尋求、費力搜尋、主動找出、追查、持續留意，還是物色合適人選／地點？

---

## 02 Seek

**尋求；尋找**

較正式，常用於 opportunities, help, advice, employment, solutions 等目標性名詞。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The company is seeking new investment opportunities.

公司正在尋求新的投資機會。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- seek advice

- seek help

- seek employment

- seek opportunities


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

---

## 03 Try to find

**試圖找到；設法尋找**

非常自然的描述性改寫，明確表示嘗試找到，不保證成功。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

I am trying to find a cheaper hotel.

我正試圖找一間較便宜的酒店。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- try to find a solution

- try to find a place

- try to find information

- try to find someone


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是一般搜尋、正式尋求、費力搜尋、主動找出、追查、持續留意，還是物色合適人選／地點？

---

## 04 Hunt for

**四處尋找；搜尋**

比普通 look for 更強，暗示花較多時間或精力到處找。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

They are hunting for a flat near the station.

他們正四處尋找車站附近的單位。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- hunt for bargains

- hunt for clues

- hunt for a flat

- hunt for information

## 05 Seek out

## 08 Be on the lookout for

### EXAMPLE · 例句

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Wait for

等待不等於尋找

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

I waited for the bus. / I looked for the bus stop.

---

## 02 Ask for

索取不等於尋找

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She asked for help. / She looked for help online.

---

## 03 Watch for

留意不完全等於搜尋

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Watch for traffic when crossing the road.

---

## 04 Expect

預期不等於尋找

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

We expect good results. / We are looking for a solution.

---

## 05 Find

找到不等於尋找

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

I looked for my keys and finally found them.

---

## 06 Look at

看某物不等於尋找

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Look at the map. / Look for the station on the map.

---

## 07 Look after

照顧不等於尋找

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She looks after the children.

---

## 08 Look up

查閱不等於一般尋找

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Look up the word in a dictionary.

## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** Search for · 搜尋；尋找


### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

The police are looking for the missing child.


### 中文翻譯

警方正在尋找失蹤兒童。


### OPTIONS


- **A. search for**

- **B. seek**

- **C. try to find**

- **D. track down**

- **E. look around for**

- **F. wait for**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**search for · 搜尋；尋找**

The police are **searching for** the missing child.


### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. search for ✓** — `search for` = 主動搜尋人、物、資訊或答案；廣泛中性。 這個表達和本句所需的搜尋方式、主動程度及語域完全吻合。

- **B. seek** — `seek` = 較正式地尋求目標、機會、幫助、資訊等。 它較正式，尤其自然於抽象目標、機會、幫助或工作；本題需要的搜尋方式不同。

- **C. try to find** — `try to find` = 嘗試找到，不保證成功。 它和 **Search for** 在搜尋強度、正式程度或主動性上不同。

- **D. track down** — `track down` = 依線索、多番嘗試後追查並找到。 它暗示目標較難找，通常需要線索或持續追查；普通搜尋未必有這層意思。

- **E. look around for** — `look around for` = 在某地四處看看尋找。 它強調在某個實體場所四處查看，範圍比一般搜尋更具體。

- **F. wait for** — `wait for` = 等待，不是主動尋找。 它表示被動等候，而本句需要主動尋找。

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Look for vs Search for
`search for` 最直接、廣泛，稍正式或更強調搜尋行為。

## Look for vs Seek
`seek` 較正式，特別自然於 help / advice / employment / opportunities / solutions。

## Look for vs Try to find
後者明確強調「嘗試找到」，不保證成功。

## Look for vs Hunt for
`hunt for` 強調較費力、到處找。

## Look for vs Seek out
`seek out` 強調主動、特意去找某個目標。

## Look for vs Search out
`search out` 更書面，強調搜尋並把目標找出。

## Look for vs Track down
`track down` 暗示目標難找，需要線索或持續追查。

## Look for vs Be on the lookout for
後者偏持續留意，而不是立即搜尋。

## Look for vs Look around for
後者強調在某地方四處看看。

## Look for vs Scout for
後者特別適合物色人才、地點、機會或資源。

## Look for vs Go in search of
後者較敘事／書面，常帶「出發去尋找」意味。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Look for vs find
- look for = 尋找過程
- find = 找到的結果

`I looked for my keys for an hour and finally found them.`

## 02 Seek
`seek` 通常直接接賓語，不加 `for`：
- seek advice ✓
- seek help ✓
- seek employment ✓
- seek for advice ✗

## 03 Search
- search for + target
- search + place/container

`We searched for the document.`  
`We searched the office.`

## 04 Track down
通常要求目標較難找，帶線索／追查意味。

## 05 Scout for
常見於：
- talent
- locations
- candidates
- opportunities

## 06 Look for 的其他 sense
`look for` 偶爾也可表示 **expect / anticipate**，例如：
`We look for stronger growth next year.`

這一義與本課主要的「搜尋／尋找」sense 分開處理。
