# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Often

**經常、頻繁、定期、反覆、屢次、習慣性地、例行地——究竟是哪一種 often？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**frequently; regularly; commonly; repeatedly; many times; on many occasions; time and again; again and again; habitually; routinely**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **10 個**真正自然、有辨析價值的核心表達。

---

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Frequently

**頻繁地；經常**

最直接、最廣泛的近義詞之一，適合一般及較正式書面語。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

She frequently travels abroad for work.

她因工作經常到外地出差。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- frequently asked questions

- frequently occur

- frequently used

- frequently visit

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Usually

**通常不等於 often**

usually 表示在大多數情況／大部分時候如此；often 只表示頻繁，不必達到『通常』的程度。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

I often walk to work does not necessarily mean I usually walk to work.

---

## 02 Sometimes

**有時不等於 often**

sometimes 是中等或不確定頻率，通常比 often 低。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Sometimes means on some occasions; often means on many occasions.

---

## 03 Occasionally

**偶爾不等於 often**

occasionally 明確表示較低頻率。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

We occasionally eat out is weaker than we often eat out.

---

## 04 Constantly

**不斷地通常比 often 強很多**

constantly 可表示幾乎一直或非常頻繁。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The phone constantly rings is stronger than the phone often rings.

---

## 05 Always

**總是不等於 often**

always 表示每次／一直，通常不容許例外。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Often allows exceptions; always normally does not.

---

## 06 Repeatedly

**反覆很接近但焦點不同**

repeatedly 強調同一事情一再重複；often 更廣泛地表示高頻率。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Repeatedly highlights repetition; often highlights frequency.

---

## 07 Commonly

**常見不完全等於 frequently**

commonly 常表示在很多人／情況中普遍存在。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

This word is commonly used refers to prevalence of use, not necessarily one person's repeated action.

---

## 08 Regularly

**規律不完全等於 often**

regularly 可強調固定時間或穩定模式；事情可以 often 發生但沒有固定規律。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

It rains often here, but not necessarily regularly.

## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** Frequently · 頻繁地；經常

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

She often travels abroad for work.

### 中文翻譯

她因工作經常到外地出差。

### OPTIONS


- **A. frequently**

- **B. regularly**

- **C. commonly**

- **D. repeatedly**

- **E. occasionally**

- **F. rarely**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**frequently · 頻繁地；經常**

She **frequently** travels abroad for work.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. frequently ✓** — `frequently` = 在很多時候發生；直接表示高頻率。 本句所需的頻率、規律性或重複方式與 **Frequently** 完全吻合，因此最準確。

- **B. regularly** — `regularly` = 有規律、定期或反覆發生。 它包含規律、固定模式；若句子只表達高頻率，會更具體。

- **C. commonly** — `commonly` = 在很多人、情況或個案中很常見。 它偏『在很多情況中很常見』；若句子談同一主體反覆做某事，通常不如 frequently / repeatedly。

- **D. repeatedly** — `repeatedly` = 同一事情反覆發生多次。 它強調同一事件一再重複，比普通 often 更強。

- **E. occasionally** — `occasionally` = 偶爾；頻率明顯低於 often。 它表示偶爾，頻率明顯低於 often。

- **F. rarely** — `rarely` = 很少；和 often 方向相反。 它表示很少發生，方向相反。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Often is an adverb of frequency
常見位置：
- before a main verb: `I often walk home.`
- after `be`: `She is often tired.`
- after the first auxiliary: `It has often been said...`

## 02 Often vs usually
- often = 很多時候
- usually = 大多數時候／通常

## 03 Often vs regularly
`regularly` 可帶固定時間或穩定模式；事情可以 `often` 發生但完全不規律。

## 04 Often vs commonly
`commonly` 偏「普遍常見」，未必表示同一人反覆做某事。

## 05 Often vs repeatedly
`repeatedly` 強調 repetition，通常比 `often` 更強。

## 06 Often vs habitually / routinely
- habitually = 個人習慣／長期行為模式
- routinely = 制度、工作或日常程序中的常規做法

## 07 How often
- How often do you exercise?
- How often is the system updated?
