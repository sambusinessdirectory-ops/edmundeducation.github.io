# SHOOT · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Shoot

**本課只處理 shoot = 用槍射擊／開火 的義項。** 不處理攝影（shoot a film）、足球射門、植物長出嫩芽等其他義項。這一組最重要的差異是：**只是開火、朝目標射擊、是否擊中、是否受傷、是否死亡，以及正式法律用語**。

你提供的 **fire at; gun down** 分別代表 **向目標開火** 與 **對人開槍並造成嚴重結果** 兩個重要方向。

本次保留 **9 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達。部分詞是結果型或正式法律用語，會清楚標示，不會把它們當作任何 shoot 語境都能直接互換。共 **27 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Shoot 的 firearm sense | 核心問題 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|---|
| 朝目標開火 | 是否只是 in the direction of a target？ | fire at, shoot at |
| 開始向某目標射擊 | 是否強調 gunfire begins？ | open fire on |
| 單純開槍 | 是否只表示 discharge a firearm？ | fire, discharge a firearm |
| 開槍打傷 | 是否確認 injury but not death？ | shoot and wound, wound by gunfire |
| 開槍殺死 | 是否確認死亡結果？ | shoot and kill, gun down |

## Tier 1 — Core firing / shooting equivalents · 核心開火與射擊近義詞

### 01 Fire at

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：向……開火；朝……射擊  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to discharge a firearm in the direction of a person, vehicle, object, or position  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **shoot** 在『朝某目標開槍』sense 最直接的片語之一。  
**常見搭配**：fire at a target; fire at a vehicle; fire at police; fire at a position; shots fired at.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, vehicle, target, building, position.  
**與 shoot 的辨析**：Fire at 強調朝某目標開火，但不一定表示擊中。  
**使用限制**：如果重點是成功擊中、打傷或打死，需要 hit / wound / gun down 等更具結果性的詞。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Witnesses said someone fired at the vehicle as it drove away.  
目擊者表示，有人在車輛駛離時向它開火。

### 02 Gun down

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：開槍擊倒；槍殺  
**語域**：neutral / news / strong  
**核心語義**：to shoot someone, typically causing them to fall, and often implying serious or fatal injury  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 屬較強結果性表達，常見於新聞報道。  
**常見搭配**：gun down a victim; be gunned down; gunman gunned down; shot and killed.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, victim, officer, civilian.  
**與 shoot 的辨析**：Gun down 比 shoot 強很多，通常暗示對人開槍並造成嚴重結果。  
**使用限制**：不能用於普通射擊練習、未擊中的開火或非人物目標。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The victim was gunned down outside the building.  
受害者在大樓外遭槍擊倒地。

### 03 Shoot at

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：向……射擊  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to shoot in the direction of a target, whether or not the shot hits  
**用法提示**：這是 shoot 本身最直接的目標結構改寫。  
**常見搭配**：shoot at a target; shoot at someone; shoot at a vehicle; shoot at a sign.  
**常見搭配對象**：target, person, vehicle, object.  
**與 shoot 的辨析**：Shoot at 和 fire at 很接近，但更一般、更日常。  
**使用限制**：如果沒有明確目標，可直接用 shoot / fire。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The suspect was accused of shooting at a parked car.  
疑犯被指向一輛停泊的汽車開槍。

### 04 Open fire on

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：向……開火  
**語域**：formal / news / military  
**核心語義**：to begin shooting at a person, group, vehicle, or position  
**用法提示**：當 shoot 表示『開始向某目標連續或明確開火』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：open fire on; open fire at; suddenly open fire; troops opened fire.  
**常見搭配對象**：group, vehicle, crowd, position, building.  
**與 shoot 的辨析**：Open fire on 強調開始射擊這個動作，常比單次 shoot 更有持續或突發感。  
**使用限制**：單發、非突發或射擊練習語境未必合適。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The attackers opened fire on the convoy.  
襲擊者向車隊開火。

### 05 Fire

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：開槍；射擊  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to discharge a firearm  
**用法提示**：當 shoot 只是表示『開槍』，而不需要說明是否擊中時，fire 很自然。  
**常見搭配**：fire a shot; fire a weapon; fire repeatedly; fire into the air; fire on.  
**常見搭配對象**：shot, weapon, air, target.  
**與 shoot 的辨析**：Fire 更聚焦 firearm discharge；shoot 的範圍更廣，也可指攝影、射門等其他義項。  
**使用限制**：如果要表達擊中某人，單用 fire 不足。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The officer fired once after issuing a warning.  
警員發出警告後開了一槍。

## Tier 2 — Injury / fatal-result variants · 傷害與致命結果型變體

### 06 Shoot and wound

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：開槍打傷  
**語域**：neutral / news  
**核心語義**：to shoot someone and cause a non-fatal injury  
**用法提示**：當 shoot 的結果明確是『打傷而非死亡』時，比單純 shoot 更精確。  
**常見搭配**：shoot and wound; wounded by gunfire; shot and injured; gunshot wound.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, victim, officer, civilian.  
**與 shoot 的辨析**：比 shoot 更結果導向，明確指出傷勢。  
**使用限制**：如果死傷情況不清楚，不應自行加上 wound。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
One person was shot and wounded during the incident.  
事件中有一人遭槍擊受傷。

### 07 Shoot and kill

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：開槍殺死；槍殺  
**語域**：neutral / news  
**核心語義**：to shoot someone and cause their death  
**用法提示**：當 shoot 的結果明確是致命時，這是最清楚、最中性的新聞式表達之一。  
**常見搭配**：shoot and kill; shot and killed; fatally shot; killed by gunfire.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, victim, officer, civilian.  
**與 shoot 的辨析**：比 gun down 更中性、更直接交代結果。  
**使用限制**：只有死亡結果已知時才使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The man was shot and killed during the attack.  
該男子在襲擊中遭槍擊身亡。

### 08 Wound by gunfire

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：以槍火擊傷；遭槍傷  
**語域**：formal / medical / news  
**核心語義**：to injure someone through gunfire  
**用法提示**：適合較正式地表達 shoot = cause injury with a firearm。  
**常見搭配**：wounded by gunfire; suffer gunshot wounds; gunfire injury; firearm injury.  
**常見搭配對象**：victim, civilian, officer, patient.  
**與 shoot 的辨析**：這是結果型被動／描述性表達，重點在傷勢而不是射擊者的動作。  
**使用限制**：不適合沒有傷勢結果的 shoot。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Two civilians were wounded by gunfire.  
兩名平民遭槍火擊傷。

## Tier 3 — Formal legal firearm-discharge variant · 正式法律槍械用語

### 09 Discharge a firearm

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：發射槍械；開槍  
**語域**：formal / legal  
**核心語義**：to fire a gun or other firearm  
**用法提示**：當 shoot 出現在法律、警務或正式紀錄語境，這是精確但較技術性的表達。  
**常見搭配**：discharge a firearm; unlawfully discharge; firearm discharge; discharge a weapon.  
**常見搭配對象**：firearm, weapon, gun.  
**與 shoot 的辨析**：Discharge a firearm 非常正式，只描述開槍本身，不表示是否擊中。  
**使用限制**：日常英語顯得過度正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He was charged with unlawfully discharging a firearm.  
他被控非法發射槍械。

## 04 False synonyms / shoot traps · 容易誤選的開火、命中與傷亡結果陷阱

### 01 Hit

**擊中 ≠ shoot**  
hit 表示打中目標；shoot 不一定命中。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A shot can miss; hit requires contact.

### 02 Kill

**殺死 ≠ shoot**  
kill 說明死亡結果，但不說明手段一定是槍擊。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Kill is result-focused, not method-specific.

### 03 Wound

**使受傷 ≠ shoot**  
wound 表示造成傷勢，但傷勢可由不同方式造成。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Wound is injury-focused.

### 04 Fire

**只在 firearm-discharge sense 接近**  
fire 不表示一定擊中或傷害。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Fire focuses on discharging the weapon.

### 05 Attack

**襲擊 ≠ shoot**  
attack 比 shoot 更廣，可以用其他方式進行。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Attack is broader.

### 06 Threaten

**威脅 ≠ shoot**  
威脅可以完全沒有開槍。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Threatening is not the act of shooting.

### 07 Gun down

**只在嚴重／致命人物射擊 sense 接近**  
gun down 強度高，不適合所有 shoot。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Gun down is much stronger.

### 08 Shoot / shot

**詞形陷阱**  
shoot 是原形；shot 是過去式／過去分詞，也可作名詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check tense and word class.

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The attackers fired at the vehicle as it left the area.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Fire at ✓** — means discharge a firearm in the direction of a target, whether or not it is hit. Here, the sentence describes shots directed towards a target without stating that they hit. That makes **Fire at** the most precise wording for this particular meaning of *shoot*.
- **B.** **Shoot at** — means shoot in the direction of a target without necessarily hitting it. This gives directional shooting but does not add the stronger result or formal nuance required here. The decisive clue is that the sentence describes shots directed towards a target without stating that they hit.
- **C.** **Fire** — means discharge a firearm and does not itself say whether anyone was hit. This only states that a firearm was discharged and does not tell us whether a person was hit, wounded or killed. The decisive clue is that the sentence describes shots directed towards a target without stating that they hit.
- **D.** **Hit** — means strike the target and therefore requires successful contact. This requires successful contact and does not itself say the method was gunfire. The decisive clue is that the sentence describes shots directed towards a target without stating that they hit.
- **E.** **Attack** — means act violently against someone or something and is much broader than shooting. This is too broad because an attack can involve many methods other than shooting. The decisive clue is that the sentence describes shots directed towards a target without stating that they hit.
- **F.** **Threaten** — means communicate or imply harm and does not require any shooting. A threat may involve no physical action at all, so it cannot replace an actual shooting event. The decisive clue is that the sentence describes shots directed towards a target without stating that they hit.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **shoot vs fire at**: fire at 強調朝目標開火，不表示命中。
- **shoot vs gun down**: gun down 更強，通常指人並暗示嚴重或致命後果。
- **shoot vs shoot at**: shoot at 是最直接的 directional structure。
- **shoot vs open fire on**: open fire on 強調開始射擊。
- **shoot vs fire**: fire 只表示開槍，不表示命中或傷亡。
- **shoot vs shoot and wound**: 後者明確指出傷害結果。
- **shoot vs shoot and kill**: 後者明確指出死亡結果。
- **shoot vs hit**: hit 表示打中；shoot 可以射失。
- **shoot vs discharge a firearm**: 後者非常正式，常用於法律／警務文本。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & result traps · 文法、搭配與射擊結果陷阱

- **shoot at + target**：shoot at a vehicle / target。
- **fire at + target**：與 shoot at 接近，但更 firearm-focused。
- **open fire on + target**：強調開始開火。
- **fire + shot(s)**：fire a shot / fire several shots。
- **shoot + person + in the body part**：shoot someone in the leg。
- **be shot and wounded / be shot and killed**：新聞報道常見結果型搭配。
- **be wounded by gunfire**：偏傷勢／醫療／正式報道。
- **discharge a firearm**：正式法律／警務用語。
- **shoot** 的過去式和過去分詞是 **shot**。
- 不要把 **hit, wound, kill, attack, threaten** 當作 shoot 的全面同義詞；它們分別描述命中、傷害、死亡、較廣襲擊或威脅。
- 本課只處理槍械義項，不處理 **shoot a film / shoot a goal / plant shoots** 等其他義項。
