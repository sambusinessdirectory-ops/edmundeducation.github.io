# COMPETE · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Compete

**「競爭／角逐／爭奪／抗衡」，要先分清是在跟誰競爭、爭甚麼，以及競爭是否激烈或已經勝出。**

本單元集中處理 **compete = to try to win, gain an advantage, obtain a limited opportunity, or perform better than others**。你提供的 **rival; contend; vie** 都是高價值近義詞：**rival** 強調對手關係，**contend** 常用於角逐頭銜／位置，**vie** 強調多人積極爭奪同一目標。

這次保留 **14 個**有實際教學價值的詞／表達。除了核心同義詞，也特別處理 **compete against / for / with / on** 的介詞差異，並加入 **go head-to-head, battle for, race for, be in contention, outcompete** 等常用情境變體。每個目標設 **3 道題**，每個 distractor 均有完整情境化解釋。

## Tier 1 — Core rivalry / contest equivalents · 核心競爭與角逐近義詞

### 01 Rival

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：與……競爭；和……匹敵  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：to compete directly with another person, team, company, or product, often for superiority or the same goal  
**用法提示**：和 compete 很接近，尤其當兩個對手彼此形成明顯競爭關係時。  
**常見搭配**：rival another company; rival a competitor; rival each other; rival teams; rival products.  
**常見搭配對象**：company, team, candidate, product, school, nation.  
**與 compete 的辨析**：Rival 常暗示明確對手關係；compete 可有很多參與者。  
**使用限制**：如果沒有特定對手，只是參加比賽，compete 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The two companies rival each other in the smartphone market.  
兩間公司在智能手機市場互相競爭。

### 02 Contend

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：競爭；角逐  
**語域**：formal / common  
**核心語義**：to compete for a prize, position, title, or advantage  
**用法提示**：當 compete 指為冠軍、職位、獎項或優勢而角逐時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：contend for the title; contend for first place; contend for a position; leading contenders; contend seriously.  
**常見搭配對象**：title, championship, prize, position, nomination.  
**與 compete 的辨析**：Contend 常和 for 搭配，突出具體目標。  
**使用限制**：一般市場競爭通常 compete/rival 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Five teams are contending for the championship.  
五支隊伍正在角逐冠軍。

### 03 Vie

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：爭奪；競逐  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to compete eagerly with others for something desirable  
**用法提示**：當 compete 有『多人爭一個位置／獎項／注意力』的意味時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：vie for attention; vie for first place; vie for a contract; vie for market share; vie with one another.  
**常見搭配對象**：attention, award, contract, position, market share.  
**與 compete 的辨析**：Vie 常帶積極爭取、多人競逐感。  
**使用限制**：不及 compete 通用，且通常需要 for。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Several firms are vying for the government contract.  
數間公司正在爭奪政府合約。

### 04 Compete against

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：與……競爭；對賽  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to take part in competition directly against another opponent  
**用法提示**：當需要明確指出對手時，是 compete 最基本而自然的展開。  
**常見搭配**：compete against another team; compete against rivals; compete against each other; compete internationally; compete successfully.  
**常見搭配對象**：team, athlete, company, school, candidate.  
**與 compete 的辨析**：Compete against 強調直接對手。  
**使用限制**：爭取某一獎項時常用 compete for。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Our team will compete against last year's champions.  
我們的隊伍將與去年的冠軍對賽。

### 05 Compete for

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：爭取；爭奪  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to try to win or obtain the same prize, position, resource, or opportunity as others  
**用法提示**：當 compete 重點是大家爭同一目標時最精準。  
**常見搭配**：compete for a prize; compete for jobs; compete for attention; compete for funding; compete for customers.  
**常見搭配對象**：prize, job, funding, customers, resources, place.  
**與 compete 的辨析**：Compete for 指競爭目標；compete against 指競爭對手。  
**使用限制**：兩者不要混淆介詞。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Small businesses compete for customers in the local market.  
小型企業在本地市場爭取顧客。

## Tier 2 — Stronger / contextual competition expressions · 較強烈與情境化競爭表達

### 06 Challenge

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：挑戰；向……競爭  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：to compete with someone, especially by attempting to defeat or displace them  
**用法提示**：當 compete 是向現有冠軍、領先者或權威發起挑戰時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：challenge the champion; challenge for the title; challenge a dominant firm; mount a challenge; serious challenger.  
**常見搭配對象**：champion, leader, incumbent, dominant company, title holder.  
**與 compete 的辨析**：Challenge 比 compete 更有『向強者發起挑戰』的意味。  
**使用限制**：普通平等競賽不一定需要 challenge。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The young player is ready to challenge the defending champion.  
這名年輕球員準備挑戰衛冕冠軍。

### 07 Go head-to-head

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：正面交鋒；直接競爭  
**語域**：informal / common  
**核心語義**：to compete directly and closely against one another  
**用法提示**：當兩個對手正面比較、直接對打或在市場上硬碰硬時很地道。  
**常見搭配**：go head-to-head with; head-to-head competition; face each other; direct contest; head-to-head battle.  
**常見搭配對象**：teams, candidates, brands, products, companies.  
**與 compete 的辨析**：比 compete 更強調一對一或直接正面競爭。  
**使用限制**：多人混戰式競賽不一定合適。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The two brands will go head-to-head in the budget phone market.  
兩個品牌將在平價手機市場正面競爭。

### 08 Battle for

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：爭奪；激烈競爭  
**語域**：neutral / vivid  
**核心語義**：to compete intensely for a prize, position, market, or advantage  
**用法提示**：當 compete 的競爭非常激烈、持久時可用。  
**常見搭配**：battle for first place; battle for market share; battle for control; battle for survival; battle for the lead.  
**常見搭配對象**：title, market share, lead, control, survival.  
**與 compete 的辨析**：Battle for 比 compete for 更強烈、更具動態感。  
**使用限制**：語氣較戲劇化，不適合所有中性文本。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The two retailers are battling for market share.  
兩間零售商正在激烈爭奪市場份額。

### 09 Race for

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：競逐；爭取  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：to compete quickly or urgently to achieve something before others  
**用法提示**：當 compete 有速度、先到先得或搶先取得成果的感覺時很適合。  
**常見搭配**：race for first place; race for a deal; race for talent; race for market leadership; race to develop.  
**常見搭配對象**：position, contract, talent, technology, leadership.  
**與 compete 的辨析**：Race for 加入速度與時機競爭。  
**使用限制**：沒有時間壓力時普通 compete 更中性。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Tech firms are racing for leadership in artificial intelligence.  
科技公司正競逐人工智能領導地位。

### 10 Strive against

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：奮力對抗；競爭  
**語域**：formal / less common  
**核心語義**：to make a strong effort in opposition to another force or competitor  
**用法提示**：當 compete 帶長期努力、奮力抗衡的語氣時可用。  
**常見搭配**：strive against competitors; strive to stay ahead; strive for advantage; strive in competition; strive to outperform.  
**常見搭配對象**：competitor, rival, pressure, disadvantage.  
**與 compete 的辨析**：Strive against 強調努力本身，而非正式比賽結構。  
**使用限制**：不是最直接的 compete 同義詞，屬情境性近義。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Small firms must strive against larger competitors.  
小型公司必須奮力抗衡大型競爭者。

## Tier 3 — Grammar / result / business variants · 介詞、結果與商業變體

### 11 Compete on

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：在……方面競爭  
**語域**：neutral / business  
**核心語義**：to compete by using a particular dimension such as price, quality, speed, or service  
**用法提示**：商業語境非常實用，表示企業靠某個因素競爭。  
**常見搭配**：compete on price; compete on quality; compete on service; compete on speed; compete on innovation.  
**常見搭配對象**：price, quality, service, innovation, convenience.  
**與 compete 的辨析**：Compete on 說明競爭維度，不是對手或目標。  
**使用限制**：介詞 on 不能和 for / against 隨意互換。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The company does not compete on price alone.  
這家公司並不單靠價格競爭。

### 12 Compete with

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：與……競爭；與……抗衡  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to be in competition with another person, organisation, product, or alternative  
**用法提示**：和 compete against 很接近，但在商業、產品、選擇比較中 compete with 特別自然。  
**常見搭配**：compete with larger firms; compete with imports; compete with alternatives; compete with rivals; compete effectively.  
**常見搭配對象**：company, product, rival, import, alternative.  
**與 compete 的辨析**：With 常見於長期競爭關係；against 稍更像直接對抗。  
**使用限制**：競爭目標仍要用 for。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Local shops struggle to compete with large online retailers.  
本地商店難以與大型網上零售商競爭。

### 13 Be in contention

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：有力競爭；仍有機會爭勝  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to remain among the people or teams with a realistic chance of winning  
**用法提示**：當 compete 的重點不是單純參加，而是『仍然有力爭勝』時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：be in contention for; remain in contention; strong contention; in title contention; still in contention.  
**常見搭配對象**：title, medal, championship, prize, place.  
**與 compete 的辨析**：Be in contention 表示具有實際爭勝機會；不是所有 competitors 都 in contention。  
**使用限制**：不能用來表示單純參賽。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Three teams remain in contention for the league title.  
仍有三支隊伍有力爭奪聯賽冠軍。

### 14 Outcompete

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：競爭勝過；擊敗競爭者  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to compete more successfully than another person, company, species, or product  
**用法提示**：當 compete 不只是競爭，而是結果上勝過對手時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：outcompete rivals; outcompete larger firms; outcompete competitors; outcompete on price; successfully outcompete.  
**常見搭配對象**：rival, company, product, species, competitor.  
**與 compete 的辨析**：Outcompete 包含『贏過』結果；compete 本身不表示誰勝。  
**使用限制**：若比賽結果未知，不能提前用 outcompete。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The smaller firm outcompeted larger rivals on customer service.  
這間較小公司靠客戶服務競爭勝過大型對手。

## 04 False synonyms / competition traps · 容易誤選的詞與競爭陷阱

### 01 Win

**勝出不等於 compete**  
win 是競爭結果；compete 只表示參與競爭，未必勝出。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
To compete is to take part; to win is to succeed.

### 02 Fight

**打鬥／爭鬥不等於所有 compete**  
fight 可指身體衝突或激烈鬥爭；正常商業或體育競爭不一定是 fight。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Competition can be peaceful and rule-based; fighting implies conflict.

### 03 Play

**參賽／玩不等於 compete**  
play 可指進行某項運動，但沒有必然『爭勝』含義。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
You can play without competing.

### 04 Participate

**參與不等於競爭**  
participate 只表示參加；活動可以完全沒有競爭性。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Participation does not necessarily involve rivalry.

### 05 Oppose

**反對不等於 compete**  
oppose 是反對某人／政策／提議；compete 是為同一目標或優勢而競爭。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Opposition is not necessarily competition.

### 06 Compare

**比較不等於 compete**  
compare 是找相同與不同；compete 是試圖勝過或取得有限資源。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Comparing evaluates differences; competing seeks advantage or victory.

### 07 Perform

**表現不等於 compete**  
perform 指表現或演出；有人可以表現很好卻沒有參與競賽。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Performance can occur without competition.

### 08 Contest

**contest 作動詞可表示『質疑／爭辯』，也可表示爭奪，語義要小心**  
contest a decision 常是質疑；contest a seat 才可表示競逐。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Contest has multiple senses, so its object determines whether it means compete.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Rival · 與……競爭；和……匹敵

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The two companies compete directly in the smartphone market.

**中文翻譯**  
兩間公司在智能手機市場直接競爭。

- A. rival each other
- B. participate together
- C. compare each other
- D. play
- E. win
- F. perform

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**rival each other** · 與……競爭；和……匹敵

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The two companies rival each other in the smartphone market.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. rival each other ✓ — two companies + same market 表示明確直接競爭關係。**
- **B. participate together — participate 只表示共同參與，不表示彼此爭市場。**
- **C. compare each other — compare 是比較，不表示爭取優勢。**
- **D. play — 不適合公司市場競爭。**
- **E. win — 是結果，不表示持續競爭過程。**
- **F. perform — 只表示表現，不表示對手關係。**

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Compete 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 明確對手關係 | **rival** | 是否兩個對手長期互相競爭？ |
| 角逐頭銜／職位 | **contend** | 是否在爭 championship / position / title？ |
| 多人爭一個目標 | **vie** | 是否多人積極爭 contract / attention / place？ |
| 指出直接對手 | **compete against** | against 後面是不是 opponent？ |
| 指出競爭目標 | **compete for** | for 後面是不是 prize / customers / funding？ |
| 挑戰現有領先者 | **challenge** | 是否向 champion / leader / incumbent 發起競爭？ |
| 正面直接交鋒 | **go head-to-head** | 是否兩方直接硬碰硬？ |
| 激烈爭奪 | **battle for** | 是否有 intensely / fiercely 的語感？ |
| 搶先競逐 | **race for / race to** | 是否有速度、先到先得意味？ |
| 奮力抗衡 | **strive against** | 是否重點是長期努力對抗較強對手？ |
| 競爭維度 | **compete on** | 是在 price / quality / service 上競爭嗎？ |
| 商業抗衡關係 | **compete with** | 是否和另一 company/product/alternative 長期競爭？ |
| 仍有實際爭勝機會 | **be in contention** | 是否不是單純參賽，而是仍有勝算？ |
| 結果上勝過對手 | **outcompete** | 是否已明確 outperform / beat competitors？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **compete vs rival**: rival 更強調特定對手關係；compete 可有很多參與者。
- **compete vs contend**: contend 常用於 title / position / prize 的角逐。
- **compete vs vie**: vie 常帶多人積極爭一個目標的語氣。
- **compete against vs compete for**: against 接對手；for 接目標／獎項／資源。
- **compete with vs compete against**: with 在商業和長期關係中很自然；against 稍更像直接對抗。
- **compete on vs compete for**: on 接競爭維度；for 接爭取目標。
- **compete vs challenge**: challenge 常是向現有冠軍／領先者發起挑戰。
- **compete vs battle for**: battle for 比普通 compete 更激烈。
- **compete vs be in contention**: 後者表示仍有現實勝算。
- **compete vs outcompete**: outcompete 已包含競爭勝過對手的結果。

## 08 Grammar, prepositions & competition traps · 文法、介詞與競爭陷阱

- **compete against + opponent**：compete against another team / rival。
- **compete with + competitor/alternative**：compete with larger firms / imported products。
- **compete for + goal/resource**：compete for a prize / customers / funding / places。
- **compete on + dimension**：compete on price / quality / service / innovation。
- **contend for + title/position/prize**：contend for the championship。
- **vie for + noun** 或 **vie to + verb**：vie for attention / vie to host the event。
- **rival + object** 可直接及物：This product rivals the market leader；也可說 rivals each other。
- **go head-to-head with + opponent**；若後面是競爭目標，常再接 **for**。
- **be in contention for + title/prize** 表示仍有實際勝算，不等於只是 participate。
- **outcompete + opponent** 是及物動詞：outcompete rivals；它已表示結果上競爭勝出。
- **win ≠ compete**：win 是結果；compete 是過程。
- **participate ≠ compete**：參加活動不一定有競爭性。
- **contest** 要看賓語：contest a decision = 質疑；contest a seat = 競逐席位。
- 商業寫作中不要把 compete 過度戲劇化成 fight/battle，除非原文確實強調激烈程度。
