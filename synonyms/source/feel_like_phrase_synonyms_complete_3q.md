# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Feel like · phrase

「想要／有心情／覺得好像」，究竟是哪一種 `feel like`？

`feel like` 是高度多義的片語，至少要先分成兩大核心：  
1. **feel like + 名詞/-ing = 想要／有心情做某事**  
2. **feel like + 子句 = 覺得好像／有……的感覺**

因此，這個 target 不能用一組完全通用的同義詞處理。`want to`、`be in the mood for`、`fancy`、`be inclined to` 屬於**意願／心情**；`have the impression that`、`feel as if`、`seem to oneself` 則屬於**印象／主觀感覺**。

> 本課主要處理 `feel like = want or be inclined to do/have something; or have the subjective impression that something is the case`。
>
> 同義詞數量**不固定**；本課按意願、心情、英式口語、審慎傾向、主觀感覺與個人判斷選用 **7 個**替代表達。
>
> Format: **7 curated alternatives + 8 false-synonym contrasts + 3 exercises per target synonym (21 questions total)**.

---

# 01 同義詞指南


### 01 Want To
**想要；想做**

使用者提供。當 `feel like + -ing` 表示當下想做某事時，最直接的改寫之一是 `want to + 動詞原形`。`feel like` 通常更口語、更像一時的心情；`want to` 範圍更廣，也可表示較明確的意願。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> I feel like going for a walk.

**中文翻譯**

我想去散步。

**USAGE CLUE**

- a present desire to do an activity

---


### 02 Have The Impression That
**有……的感覺；覺得；有……的印象**

使用者提供的 `have the impression of` 在實際句法中更常以 **have the impression that + 子句** 或 **have the impression of + 名詞/-ing** 出現。它適合 `feel like` 表示基於感覺或跡象而產生某種印象，而不是『想做某事』。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> I have the impression that something has changed.

**中文翻譯**

我覺得好像有些事情變了。

**USAGE CLUE**

- an intuitive impression or belief based on how something seems

---


### 03 Be In The Mood For
**有心情做；想要（某活動／食物）**

`be in the mood for + 名詞/-ing` 很接近 `feel like + 名詞/-ing`，尤其強調某人當下有沒有那個心情。它比 `want to` 更明確突出 mood。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> I'm in the mood for something spicy.

**中文翻譯**

我現在想吃點辣的。

**USAGE CLUE**

- having the present mood or inclination for an activity or thing

---


### 04 Fancy
**想要；想做；有興趣（英式口語）**

英式口語中，`fancy + 名詞/-ing` 可非常自然地改寫 `feel like` 的『想要／想做』義。它語氣隨意，尤其適合食物、飲料、外出活動或邀請。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Do you fancy going out for dinner?

**中文翻譯**

你想出去吃晚飯嗎？

**USAGE CLUE**

- informal British expression of wanting or being inclined to do/have something

---


### 05 Be Inclined To
**傾向於；有意想要**

較正式、較弱。`be inclined to + 動詞原形` 可表示某人當下或一般有做某事的傾向。它比 `feel like` 更正式，也往往比 `want to` 少一點直接欲望、多一點 inclination。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> I'm inclined to wait until tomorrow.

**中文翻譯**

我傾向等到明天再說。

**USAGE CLUE**

- a relatively mild or considered tendency towards doing something

---


### 06 Feel As If
**感覺好像；覺得彷彿**

當 `feel like + 子句` 表示『感覺好像……』時，`feel as if + 子句` 是非常自然的改寫。它強調主觀感覺或體驗，而不是單純形成一個理性印象。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> I feel as if I've forgotten something.

**中文翻譯**

我感覺好像忘了甚麼。

**USAGE CLUE**

- subjective sensation that something is or seems to be the case

---


### 07 Seem To Oneself
**自己覺得好像；在自己看來**

較正式、較少用，但可在敘述或分析語境中改寫 `feel like` 的『覺得好像』義。典型句法是 `it seems to me that...`；若主語保持一致，也可重組為 `X seems to oneself...`。實際使用時通常以 **it seems to me that** 最自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> It seems to me that the plan is becoming too complicated.

**中文翻譯**

我覺得這個計劃好像變得太複雜。

**USAGE CLUE**

- a considered personal judgement about how a situation appears

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 feel like / want to

`want to` 只處理意願義；`feel like` 還可表示『覺得好像』。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> I want to leave early.  
> I feel like something is wrong.

---


## 02 feel like / be in the mood for

`be in the mood for` 強調當下心情／興致，不能替代 `feel like + 子句` 的印象義。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> I'm in the mood for a film.  
> I feel like the room is moving.

---


## 03 feel like / fancy

`fancy` 是英式口語的『想要／想做』，不等於 `feel like` 的『覺得好像』。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Do you fancy a coffee?  
> I feel like I've forgotten something.

---


## 04 feel like / feel as if

`feel as if` 只處理主觀感覺義；若 `feel like` 表示『想做某事』，不能改成 `feel as if`。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> I feel as if I'm falling.  
> I feel like going home.

---


## 05 feel like / look like

`look like` 主要基於外觀；`feel like` 可基於內在感受、欲望或印象。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> It looks like rain.  
> I feel like staying indoors.

---


## 06 feel like / seem like

`seem like` 描述某事看來如何；`feel like` 還可表示說話者的欲望或心情。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> It seems like a good idea.  
> I feel like taking a break.

---


## 07 feel like / would like

`would like` 較禮貌、較中性地表達想要；`feel like` 更像當下興致或衝動。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> I'd like some tea, please.  
> I feel like having tea.

---


## 08 feel like / feeling

`feel like` 是片語動詞式結構；`feeling` 是名詞或現在分詞，不能直接互換。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> I feel like resting.  
> I have a strange feeling.

---

# 05 高混淆組

## want to vs be in the mood for
- **want to**：一般意願。
- **be in the mood for**：特別強調當下心情。

## want to vs fancy
- **want to**：中性、通用。
- **fancy**：英式口語，更隨意。

## be inclined to vs want to
- **be inclined to**：較正式、較猶豫／審慎。
- **want to**：直接意願。

## feel as if vs have the impression that
- **feel as if**：更主觀、更像體感或內在感覺。
- **have the impression that**：較像對情況形成印象。

## have the impression that vs seem to oneself
- **have the impression that**：主觀印象。
- **it seems to me that**：較分析式、判斷式。

## feel like + -ing vs feel like + clause
- **feel like doing**：想做。
- **feel like + clause**：覺得好像。

## feel like vs look like
- **feel like**：欲望／感覺。
- **look like**：視覺外觀。

## feel like vs would like
- **feel like**：當下興致。
- **would like**：較禮貌、較中性的想要。

# 08 生成／評估注意事項

1. **同義詞數量不是固定值。** 本課按意願、心情、英式口語、審慎傾向、主觀感覺與個人判斷選 **7 個**。
2. 因此本課有 **3 exercises per target = 21 exercises**。
3. 使用者提供的 `want to` 保留作最核心的 desire paraphrase。
4. 使用者提供的 `have the impression of` 經句法正規化為更常用的 **have the impression that + clause**；若後接名詞／-ing，才自然用 `have the impression of ...`。
5. `be in the mood for` 只在 mood/inclination 是焦點時使用。
6. `fancy` 明確標示為英式口語，符合本系列偏好的 British English。
7. `be inclined to` 只在較正式、經思考或較溫和的偏向時使用。
8. `feel as if` 只處理 subjective sensation/impression，不可替代 desire sense。
9. `it seems to me/her/us that` 比 `seem to oneself` 更自然，因此 exercise rewrite 採自然句式，不做機械替換。
10. False synonyms 特別測試 want to / in the mood / fancy / feel as if / look like / seem like / would like / feeling。
11. Distractors 全部是真實自然英文表達，不使用人工標籤或不存在詞形。
12. 每題都先判斷 `feel like` 究竟是 **desire** 還是 **impression**；這是本詞最重要的 answer-exclusivity gate。
13. 原始 `feel like` 句子本身保持自然；若正解需要改變補語結構，使用自然句法，例如 `feel like going` → `want to go`、`feel like + clause` → `feel as if + clause`。
14. 本課處理的是固定片語 **feel like**，不把單獨動詞 `feel` 的其他義項混進來。
