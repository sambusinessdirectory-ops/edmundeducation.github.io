# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Be part of

**「是……的一部分」，究竟是在說隸屬、被包括、構成、成員身份，還是被整合進去？**

`be part of` 是非常廣泛的關係表達，可以表示：

- **隸屬於某個整體／群體** → `belong to`
- **被列入、包括在內** → `be included in`
- **構成較大整體的一部分** → `form part of`, `constitute part of`
- **是功能性／概念性組成部分** → `be a component of`, `be an element of`
- **具有正式成員身份** → `be a member of`
- **共同組成某個總體** → `make up part of`
- **後來被納入／融合** → `be incorporated into`, `be integrated into`
- **包含在某範圍／內容中** → `be contained within`, `fall within`

> **核心原則**
>
> 這一組按照「close synonyms → broadly usable near-synonyms → common/formal variants」整理，但不顯示 tier。
>
> 真正要判斷的是：
>
> **Is something already a constituent, formally included, a member, a functional component, or something newly integrated into a larger whole?**

# 01 同義詞指南

## Be part of 的 12 個近義／近乎可互換說法

## 01 Belong to

**屬於；是……的一部分**

當 `be part of` 表示某人或某物隸屬於某個群體、組織、類別或整體時，`belong to` 是最自然的近義表達之一。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> This department belongs to the university's Faculty of Science.

這個部門屬於大學理學院。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- belong to a group
- belong to an organization
- belong to a category
- belong to a system

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **belong to = be a member, element, or constituent of a larger group or whole**

**REGISTER · 語域**

非常常用；所有語域

---

## 02 Be included in

**包括在……之內；被納入**

當 `be part of` 強調某人、項目、內容或費用被列入一個更大的集合、計劃、名單或總額時，`be included in` 很接近。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Breakfast is included in the room price.

早餐已包括在房價內。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- be included in the price
- be included in the list
- be included in the programme
- be included in the total

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **be included in = be contained or counted as part of a larger set**

**REGISTER · 語域**

非常常用；商業、行政、一般寫作

---

## 03 Form part of

**構成……的一部分；屬於……的一部分**

非常接近 `be part of`，但稍正式。常用於制度、計劃、結構、歷史、法律或較正式說明。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The museum forms part of a larger cultural complex.

這座博物館是較大型文化綜合設施的一部分。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- form part of a system
- form part of a programme
- form part of a wider strategy
- form part of the evidence

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **form part of = constitute one part of a larger whole**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性至正式；英式英語尤其常見

---

## 04 Constitute part of

**構成……的一部分**

比 `form part of` 更正式，強調某個元素實際構成整體的一部分。常見於法律、學術、政策及技術寫作。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> These records constitute part of the official archive.

這些紀錄構成官方檔案的一部分。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- constitute part of the evidence
- constitute part of the agreement
- constitute part of the system
- constitute part of the record

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **constitute part of = formally make up one portion of a whole**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式；法律、學術、政策、技術

---

## 05 Be a component of

**是……的組成部分**

當 `be part of` 指一個系統、機器、計劃或結構中的功能性組件時，`be a component of` 很精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The sensor is a component of the safety system.

感應器是安全系統的一個組成部分。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- component of a system
- key component of
- major component of
- essential component of

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **be a component of = be a functional constituent of a larger system or structure**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性至正式；技術、工程、商業、學術

---

## 06 Be an element of

**是……的一個元素；是一部分**

當 `be part of` 指一個較抽象的整體，例如策略、文化、設計、理論或計劃中的其中一項元素時，`be an element of` 很自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Trust is an important element of effective teamwork.

信任是有效團隊合作的重要元素。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- element of a strategy
- element of a design
- key element of
- essential element of

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **be an element of = be one constituent feature or factor within a larger whole**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性至正式；學術、商業、設計、一般寫作

---

## 07 Be a member of

**是……的成員；屬於**

當 `be part of` 的主語是人、國家、機構或組織，而重點是正式成員身份時，`be a member of` 比一般 `be part of` 更精確。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She is a member of the research team.

她是研究團隊的成員。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- member of a team
- member of an organization
- member of a committee
- member of a group

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **be a member of = belong to a group as an individual or organization**

**REGISTER · 語域**

非常常用；所有語域

---

## 08 Make up part of

**組成……的一部分**

較自然、口語化的表達，強調某些人或事物共同組成一個較大的整體。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Small businesses make up part of the local economy.

小型企業構成本地經濟的一部分。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- make up part of the total
- make up part of a group
- make up part of the workforce
- make up part of the population

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **make up part of = contribute to the composition of a larger whole**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性；一般寫作、商業、統計

---

## 09 Be incorporated into

**被納入；被併入**

當 `be part of` 帶有『原本分開，後來被納入更大整體』的過程意味時，`be incorporated into` 很貼切。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The new guidelines were incorporated into the training programme.

新指引被納入培訓計劃。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- be incorporated into a plan
- be incorporated into the design
- be incorporated into the system
- incorporated into policy

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **be incorporated into = be added and made part of a larger whole**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式；政策、設計、商業、技術

---

## 10 Be integrated into

**被整合到；成為……的一部分**

當 `be part of` 強調不只是被加入，而且和整體協調、連接、融合時，`be integrated into` 很精確。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The new software was integrated into the existing system.

新軟件被整合到現有系統中。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- integrated into a system
- integrated into the curriculum
- integrated into daily life
- fully integrated into

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **be integrated into = become part of a whole in a connected or coordinated way**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性至正式；科技、教育、政策、社會

---

## 11 Be contained within

**包含在……之內；存在於……之中**

當 `be part of` 強調某項內容、資料、區域或物件位於一個更大範圍之內時，可用 `be contained within`。它比 `be included in` 更偏空間或內容容納。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The supporting data is contained within the appendix.

支援數據包含在附錄內。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- contained within the document
- contained within the system
- contained within the area
- information contained within

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **be contained within = exist inside the limits or contents of a larger whole**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式；技術、法律、學術、說明文

---

## 12 Fall within

**屬於……範圍；歸入……之內**

當 `be part of` 表示某事屬於某個類別、範圍、責任、規則或定義之內時，`fall within` 很自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> This issue falls within the department's responsibilities.

這個問題屬於該部門的職責範圍。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- fall within a category
- fall within the scope
- fall within someone's responsibility
- fall within the definition

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **fall within = be included inside a category, scope, definition, or area of responsibility**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式；法律、行政、政策、商業

# 02 False synonyms

## 01 Be part of / Take part in

### 是一部分不等於參與

`be part of` = 屬於／構成其中一部分。  
`take part in` = 參加某項活動。

> She is part of the team.  
> She took part in the competition.

兩者不能機械互換。

---

## 02 Be part of / Participate in

`participate in` 表示參與活動、討論、研究等。  
它不表示某人永久或正式屬於該組織。

> He participated in the meeting.  
> He is a member of the committee.

---

## 03 Be part of / Belong to

兩者很接近，但 `belong to` 更突出歸屬。

`be part of` 還可表示一件事構成某個程序或整體，而不一定有 ownership / membership 感。

---

## 04 Be part of / Be included in

`be included in` 更突出「被列入」。  
`be part of` 可以是本來就存在的結構性關係。

---

## 05 Be part of / Be a component of

`component` 更適合系統、機器、結構、計劃中的功能性組件。  
普通群體成員未必適合叫 component。

---

## 06 Be part of / Be a member of

`member` 通常適合人、國家、組織。  
物件或抽象因素一般不叫 member。

---

## 07 Be part of / Be incorporated into

`be incorporated into` 帶有「被加入進去」的過程意味。  
`be part of` 本身不一定表示這個加入過程。

---

## 08 Be part of / Fall within

`fall within` 表示歸入某個範圍、定義或職責。  
它不是一般的物理「組成部分」。

## 練習 01.1

**Target phrase: Belong to · 屬於；是……的一部分**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> This department is part of the university's Faculty of Science.

**中文翻譯**

這個部門屬於大學理學院。

| | 選項 |
|---|---|
| A | Belong to |
| B | take part in |
| C | replace |
| D | separate from |
| E | depend on |
| F | lead to |

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**Belong to · 屬於；是……的一部分**

> This department belongs to the university's Faculty of Science.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. Belong to ✓**  
題目重點是隸屬關係：某個部門、檔案或地區屬於一個更大的組織、集合或行政整體。`belong to` 很自然地表達這種歸屬。

**B. take part in**  
`take part in` = 參加、參與。它描述某人參與一項活動，而不是表示某人／某物屬於或構成一個更大的整體。

**C. replace**  
`replace` = 取代。它表示一樣東西代替另一樣東西，和『成為其中一部分』的關係完全不同。

**D. separate from**  
`separate from` = 與……分開。這和 `be part of` 的方向相反，表示並不屬於同一整體。

**E. depend on**  
`depend on` = 依靠、取決於。某事可以依賴另一事但並不是它的一部分，因此兩者不是同義。

**F. lead to**  
`lead to` = 導致。它表示因果關係，不表示組成、隸屬或包含關係。

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

## Be part of vs Belong to

兩者非常接近。

**be part of** = 最廣泛。  
**belong to** = 更突出歸屬／隸屬。

> This unit is part of the department.  
> This unit belongs to the department.

---

## Be part of vs Be included in

**be part of** 可以表示原本的結構關係。  
**be included in** 更強調被列入、計入或納入。

> Tax is included in the price.

---

## Form part of vs Constitute part of

意思很接近。

**form part of** = 正式但仍相當自然。  
**constitute part of** = 更正式，常見於法律、證據、協議。

---

## Component vs Element

**component** = 功能性、結構性部分。  
**element** = 抽象因素、特徵、概念性部分。

> a component of the engine  
> an element of the strategy

---

## Be a member of vs Belong to

**be a member of** 明確表示正式 membership。  
**belong to** 範圍較廣，也可以用於物件、類別或制度。

---

## Make up part of vs Form part of

兩者都表示構成。

**make up part of** 更自然、較口語。  
**form part of** 更正式。

---

## Incorporated into vs Integrated into

**incorporated into** = 被加入並納入。  
**integrated into** = 加入後與整體協調、融合。

> feedback incorporated into the plan  
> software integrated into the system

---

## Be contained within vs Be included in

**contained within** 更有「存在於界限／內容之內」的感覺。  
**included in** 更一般地表示被列入。

---

## Fall within vs Belong to

**fall within** 特別適合：
- scope
- definition
- responsibility
- category
- jurisdiction

`belong to` 更一般。

---

非正式。

---

## Be in the fold

**成為某個群體／陣營的一員**

較比喻、較敘事，不是所有 `be part of` 都適用。

自然、口語。

比普通 `be part of` 更強，表示某事是某種經驗／制度中不可避免、不可分割的一部分。

不是直接同義詞，但常用來描述「某部分如何屬於整體」。

---

## Be woven into

**融入；交織成……的一部分**

常用於文化、歷史、身份等比喻。

> Music is woven into the culture of the community.

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Belong to

`belong` 不用被動：

> This belongs to the group. ✓  
> This is belonged to the group. ✗

---

## Be included in

被動結構：

> Breakfast is included in the price.

---

## Form part of

不需要冠詞 `a`：

> form part of the system ✓  
> form a part of the system ✓（也可，但語感略不同）

---

## Constitute

`constitute` 本身就是「構成」：

> These documents constitute part of the record.

---

## Member of

通常：

> a member of the team

而不是：

> a member in the team △

---

## Incorporated / integrated into

兩者都常用 `into`：

> incorporated into the plan  
> integrated into the system

---

## Fall within

常見：

> fall within the scope  
> fall within the definition  
> fall within someone's responsibilities

---

# 09 核心決策問題

1. **只是表示隸屬於某個整體嗎？** → `belong to`
2. **是被列入／計入嗎？** → `be included in`
3. **想正式地說『構成其中一部分』嗎？** → `form part of`
4. **是在法律／正式文件語境嗎？** → `constitute part of`
5. **是系統中的功能性組件嗎？** → `be a component of`
6. **是策略／設計／概念中的一個元素嗎？** → `be an element of`
7. **是人或組織的正式成員身份嗎？** → `be a member of`
8. **是多個部分共同組成總體嗎？** → `make up part of`
9. **是被加入到計劃／政策／設計嗎？** → `be incorporated into`
10. **是被融合到現有系統／流程嗎？** → `be integrated into`
11. **是內容／區域包含在另一範圍內嗎？** → `be contained within`
12. **是歸入某個 scope / definition / responsibility 嗎？** → `fall within`

---

# 10 Final note for system use

最有用的替代表達：

`belong to / be included in / form part of / constitute part of / be a component of / be an element of / be a member of / make up part of / be incorporated into / be integrated into / be contained within / fall within`

常用 idioms / expressions：

`be in the mix / be in the fold / be one of the team / be part and parcel of / fit into the bigger picture / be woven into`

不要把以下詞當成 `be part of` 的一般同義詞：

`take part in / participate in / replace / depend on / lead to`

其中最重要的陷阱是：

> **be part of = 是其中一部分**  
> **take part in / participate in = 參與某活動**

真正的選詞依據是：

> **Does “be part of” mean belonging, inclusion, membership, composition, integration, or falling within a defined scope?**
