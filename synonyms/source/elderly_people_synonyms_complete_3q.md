# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Elderly people

「長者／老年人」，究竟是哪一種 `elderly people`？

較年長人士、老年人、長者、退休人士、高齡人士、年長人口——英文會按**語氣是否尊重、是否偏醫療／人口統計、是否與退休身分有關、是否只表示年紀較大，還是特別指高齡群體**選用不同說法。

> Format: **14 curated alternatives + 8 false-synonym contrasts + 3 exercises per target synonym (42 questions total)**.  
> 每題包含自然原句、中文翻譯、六個選項、雙語正確答案、改寫句及 A–F 詳細解釋。
>
> 現代一般正式英文中，**older adults / older people** 往往比 `the elderly` 更中性、更以人為本。`senior citizens` 仍常見，但語域較制度化或傳統。

# 01 同義詞指南

## Elderly people 的 14 種更精準說法

### 01 Older adults
**較年長成年人；長者**

現代醫療、研究、公共政策中非常常見且中性的說法。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The programme helps older adults remain physically active.

這個計劃幫助較年長成年人保持身體活躍。

**USAGE CLUE**

- health / research / policy
- respectful and neutral
- often preferred in formal writing

---

### 02 Older people
**年長人士；長者**

非常自然、廣泛使用，語氣中性，不特別標示法律或退休身份。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Older people may need extra support during heatwaves.

熱浪期間，年長人士可能需要額外支援。

**USAGE CLUE**

- everyday + formal
- broad and respectful
- people-focused

---

### 03 Senior citizens
**長者；高齡公民**

常用於票價、福利、社區服務和公共設施等制度語境；語氣比 `older adults` 更傳統。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The centre offers discounted classes for senior citizens.

中心為長者提供優惠課程。

**USAGE CLUE**

- discounts / public services
- institutional
- traditional but still common

---

### 04 Seniors
**長者；年長人士**

較簡潔、北美特別常見；可見於服務、住房、社區活動和市場分類。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The library runs computer classes for seniors.

圖書館為長者開辦電腦課程。

**USAGE CLUE**

- common in North America
- concise
- service/community context

---

### 05 Elderly adults
**老年成年人；高齡成年人**

較正式、偏醫療或研究，但現代寫作中有時會偏好 `older adults`。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The study included elderly adults living independently.

研究包括獨立生活的高齡成年人。

**USAGE CLUE**

- clinical / descriptive
- more age-marking than older adults
- use with care

---

### 06 Elderly individuals
**老年人士；高齡人士**

正式、個體化，常見於研究、照護和專業寫作。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Elderly individuals may respond differently to some medications.

高齡人士對某些藥物的反應可能不同。

**USAGE CLUE**

- formal / clinical
- individual-level focus
- somewhat impersonal

---

### 07 Older individuals
**較年長人士**

比 `elderly individuals` 更中性，適合正式研究或個案描述。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Older individuals were more likely to use the service regularly.

較年長人士較常定期使用這項服務。

**USAGE CLUE**

- formal-neutral
- research / statistics
- less age-loaded than elderly

---

### 08 Older members of the population
**人口中的較年長成員；年長人口**

正式、人口統計式表達，強調群體中的年長部分。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Older members of the population are more vulnerable during extreme heat.

人口中的較年長成員在極端高溫下更脆弱。

**USAGE CLUE**

- demographics / public policy
- population-level
- long but precise

---

### 09 Older members of society
**社會中的年長人士；長者群體**

正式、社會政策語氣，強調長者作為社會成員。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The policy aims to reduce isolation among older members of society.

政策旨在減少年長社會成員的孤立。

**USAGE CLUE**

- social policy
- inclusion / participation
- respectful

---

### 10 People in later life
**人生後期人士；晚年人士**

較委婉、以人生階段為中心，常見於社福、健康和照護討論。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The service supports people in later life who wish to remain at home.

這項服務支援希望留在家中生活的晚年人士。

**USAGE CLUE**

- life-stage framing
- social care / wellbeing
- less direct about chronological age

---

### 11 Retired people
**退休人士**

只有在「退休身份」是核心時才適用；不是所有 elderly people 都已退休，也不是所有退休人士都屬高齡。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The scheme is popular among retired people with flexible schedules.

這項計劃很受時間較彈性的退休人士歡迎。

**USAGE CLUE**

- employment status
- not a true age synonym in all contexts
- narrower and contextual

---

### 12 Pensioners
**領退休金人士；退休人士**

英式英語常見，通常與退休金或退休身份有關，不等同所有老年人。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The discount is available to pensioners.

這項優惠適用於領退休金人士。

**USAGE CLUE**

- British English
- pension/benefit status
- institutional

---

### 13 People aged 65 and over
**65 歲或以上人士**

最精確的人口統計／研究表達之一，直接用明確年齡界線。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The survey included people aged 65 and over.

調查包括 65 歲或以上人士。

**USAGE CLUE**

- statistics / eligibility
- exact age threshold
- avoids vague age labels

---

### 14 The oldest age groups
**最高齡群體；最年長年齡組別**

適合人口研究、風險分層和統計比較，通常不指單一個人。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Vaccination rates were highest among the oldest age groups.

疫苗接種率在最高齡群體中最高。

**USAGE CLUE**

- demographic comparison
- group-level
- relative, not fixed-age

# 02 False synonyms

## 01 elderly people / older adults

### 很接近，但 older adults 通常更中性

現代正式寫作中，`older adults` 往往更以人為本；`elderly` 有時可能顯得更強調衰老或脆弱。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The clinic provides services for older adults.  
> The study also uses the term elderly adults in a clinical description.

---

## 02 elderly people / senior citizens

### senior citizens 帶制度／公共服務語氣

它常見於票價、福利、社區服務，不是所有一般描述都需要這種制度感。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Older people may experience social isolation.  
> Senior citizens receive a discount on admission.

---

## 03 elderly people / retired people

### 退休身分不等於年齡

有些退休人士仍不算高齡；有些高齡人士仍在工作。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The survey focuses on older adults aged 70 and above.  
> The travel club is open to retired people of different ages.

---

## 04 elderly people / pensioners

### pensioner 取決於退休金資格

`pensioner` 是制度身份，不是純年齡分類。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The service is designed for older people.  
> The payment is available to eligible pensioners.

---

## 05 elderly people / middle-aged people

### middle-aged 不是 elderly

`middle-aged` 通常指中年，不屬高齡群體。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The study compares middle-aged adults with older adults.  
> The two groups are not interchangeable.

---

## 06 elderly people / old people

### old people 可顯得過於直接

`old people` 並非語法錯誤，但正式或尊重語境常偏好 `older people`。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Older people is often the more neutral expression in formal writing.  
> Old people can sound more blunt in some contexts.

---

## 07 elderly people / elderly

### 是否作名詞使用要注意語氣

`the elderly` 可指一整個群體，但現代以人為本寫作常偏好 `older people`。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Older people may need accessible transport.  
> The elderly is a collective expression and can sound less person-centred.

---

## 08 elderly people / elder

### elder 不等於一般「老年人」

`elder` 常指家族／社群中的長者、長輩或具有地位的年長成員。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The programme supports older adults living alone.  
> Community elders met to discuss the issue.

---

# 03 練習與解釋

## 練習 01.1

**Target phrase: Older adults · 較年長成年人；長者**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> The clinic offers fall-prevention classes for elderly people living independently.

**中文翻譯**

診所為獨立生活的年長成年人提供防跌課程。

A. older adults  
B. pensioners  
C. retired people  
D. community elders  
E. middle-aged adults  
F. elderly

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**older adults · 較年長成年人；長者**

> The clinic offers fall-prevention classes for older adults living independently.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. older adults ✓**  
clinic + fall prevention 是健康服務語境，`older adults` 是現代醫療與研究中非常自然、尊重的表達。

**B. pensioners**  
`pensioners` 表示領退休金人士；跌倒風險是年齡／健康問題，不應把資格身分當成年齡同義詞。

**C. retired people**  
退休是就業狀態。仍工作的高齡人士也可能需要防跌服務，因此範圍不準。

**D. community elders**  
`elders` 常有長輩或社群地位意味，不是一般臨床年齡分類。

**E. middle-aged adults**  
中年群體不是原句所指的高齡人群。

**F. elderly**  
可作形容詞或 collective noun，但此處需要一個更自然的人本名詞短語。

# 05 高混淆組

## elderly people vs older adults
- **elderly people**：可理解，但有時較強調高齡／脆弱。
- **older adults**：現代醫療、研究中通常更中性。

## older adults vs older people
- **older adults**：較常見於專業、研究。
- **older people**：最廣、日常及正式皆自然。

## senior citizens vs seniors
- **senior citizens**：制度、公共服務感較強。
- **seniors**：較簡潔，北美尤其常見。

## older people vs retired people
- **older people**：年齡分類。
- **retired people**：就業狀態。

## retired people vs pensioners
- **retired people**：不再從事主要有薪工作。
- **pensioners**：領取退休金／符合退休金身份。

## elderly individuals vs older individuals
- **elderly individuals**：更明確標示高齡、偏臨床。
- **older individuals**：較中性。

## older members of the population vs older members of society
- **population**：人口統計。
- **society**：社會參與、包容與公共生活。

## people aged 65 and over vs oldest age groups
- **65 and over**：固定年齡界線。
- **oldest age groups**：相對於其他 age bands 的最高齡組。

# 08 生成／評估注意事項

1. 固定為 **14 alternatives + 3 questions each = 42 exercises**。
2. 每題包含：
   - Target word / phrase + Chinese gloss
   - READ THE CONTEXT
   - 中文翻譯
   - 六個選項
   - `CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案`
   - English target + Chinese gloss
   - 正確改寫
   - A–F 個別解釋
3. A–F feedback 優先採用：**詞義／身份條件 → 正常語域 → 本題為何成功或失敗**。
4. 使用者提供的核心：
   - older adults = 現代、專業、中性
   - senior citizens = 制度／公共服務常見
5. `older people` 是非常重要的自然一般替代，不能忽略。
6. `elderly adults` / `elderly individuals` 可用，但現代正式寫作經常更偏好 `older adults`；因此已明確標示語域。
7. `seniors` 在北美社區服務和市場語境常見，但不一定適合所有國際學術寫作。
8. `retired people` 與 `pensioners` 不能當成純年齡同義詞：
   - retired = employment status
   - pensioner = pension status
9. `people aged 65 and over` 在有明確 cut-off 時比任何模糊年齡標籤都更精確。
10. `the oldest age groups` 是 relative demographic category，不一定等於 65+。
11. `older members of the population` 適合人口統計；`older members of society` 更偏社會參與。
12. `people in later life` 是 life-stage framing，適合社福／wellbeing。
13. False synonyms 特別測試：
    - age vs retirement status
    - age vs pension status
    - senior = age vs senior = job rank
    - elder = older person vs community/family elder
14. 本組加入 morphology / structure traps：
    - elderly / elderly people
    - senior / seniors / seniority
    - pension / pensioner
    - retired / retirement
    - older / older people
15. Distractors 優先使用真正容易混淆的近鄰和身份詞，而非大量無關詞。
16. 原句只在 `elderly people` 自然可用時保留；改寫時則按 health / demographic / eligibility / retirement 等上下文選更精確說法。
17. 真正進階辨析應直接比較：
    - elderly people / older adults / older people
    - senior citizens / seniors
    - older people / retired people
    - retired people / pensioners
    - elderly individuals / older individuals
    - population / society framing
    - 65+ / oldest age groups
18. `elderly people` 本身仍可理解且常見，但現代中性教學應讓學生知道 **older adults / older people** 往往是更安全、更自然的首選。
