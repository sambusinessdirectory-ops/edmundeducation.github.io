# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# After-school hangouts

**放學後聚腳點、下課後見面地方——哪些說法真的接近 after-school hangouts？**

這份材料聚焦 **after-school hangouts = informal places where students or friends regularly gather, relax, talk, eat, or spend time after school/classes** 的意思，例如：

- cafés used as after-school hangouts
- popular after-school hangouts
- neighbourhood after-school hangouts
- places where students gather after class

這次沿用較嚴格的原則：**只保留與 after-school hangouts 高度接近、可在實際語境中幾乎互換的表達；不把單純的「社交場所」「青年中心」「咖啡店」等上位詞或具體地點類型硬當作同義詞。**

核心近義表達：

**places to gather after class; after-class meeting spots; after-school gathering spots; places to hang out after school; after-school meeting places; after-school social spots; after-class hangout spots**

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## After-school hangouts 的 7 種高度接近說法

---

## 01 Places to gather after class

**下課後聚集的地方**

最直接、描述性最強的近義表達。強調學生或朋友在下課後聚在一起。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The café became one of the most popular places to gather after class.

那間咖啡店成為最受歡迎的下課後聚集地之一。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- popular places to gather after class
- safe places to gather after class
- local places to gather after class
- places for students to gather after class

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否只是直接描述「下課後大家聚在一起的地方」？

---

## 02 After-class meeting spots

**下課後見面地點；聚腳點**

表示學生或朋友下課後經常碰面、集合或聊天的地點。比 *places to gather after class* 更簡潔。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The bakery is a favourite after-class meeting spot for local students.

那間麵包店是本地學生很喜歡的下課後見面地點。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- favourite after-class meeting spot
- regular after-class meeting spot
- popular after-class meeting spot
- informal meeting spot

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這是否主要是一個大家下課後約定碰面的固定地方？

---

## 03 After-school gathering spots

**放學後聚集地；聚腳點**

和 *after-school hangouts* 非常接近，但語氣稍微中性、描述性。強調「聚集」而不是「閒逛」。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The park has become a common after-school gathering spot for teenagers.

那個公園已成為青少年常見的放學後聚腳點。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- common after-school gathering spot
- popular after-school gathering spot
- neighbourhood gathering spot
- student gathering spot

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否想用較中性方式表示放學後大家聚集的地方？

---

## 04 Places to hang out after school

**放學後消磨時間的地方；聚腳地方**

和原詞意思最透明的改寫之一。強調放學後一起聊天、休息、吃東西或消磨時間。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Students are always looking for affordable places to hang out after school.

學生總是在找價格相宜、可以放學後聚在一起的地方。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- places to hang out after school
- good places to hang out
- safe places to hang out
- nearby places to hang out

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否要完整保留「hang out」那種輕鬆、非正式相處的感覺？

---

## 05 After-school meeting places

**放學後見面場所**

表示學生在放學後見面、集合和一起活動的地方。比 *hangouts* 稍正式、稍中性。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The shopping centre has long served as an after-school meeting place for teenagers.

那個商場長久以來一直是青少年的放學後見面場所。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- after-school meeting place
- regular meeting place
- local meeting place
- popular meeting place

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否重點在「見面場所」而不是一定強調長時間閒逛？

---

## 06 After-school social spots

**放學後社交地點；聚會地點**

表示學生放學後用來社交、聊天和與朋友相處的地方。比 *hangouts* 稍偏「社交功能」。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Small cafés often become after-school social spots for students.

小型咖啡店經常成為學生放學後的社交聚腳點。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- after-school social spot
- student social spot
- informal social spot
- popular social spot

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否主要強調學生在該地社交和與朋友相處？

---

## 07 After-class hangout spots

**下課後聚腳點；下課後閒聚地點**

非常接近原詞，保留 *hangout* 的非正式語氣，同時把時間改寫成 *after class*。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The noodle shop is one of the area's busiest after-class hangout spots.

那間麵店是區內最熱鬧的下課後聚腳點之一。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- after-class hangout spot
- favourite hangout spot
- local hangout spot
- student hangout spot

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否需要幾乎保留原來 *hangout* 的口語和非正式感？

# 02 False synonyms

以下詞語和 **after-school hangouts** 有關，但不應直接視為同義詞。

---

## 01 Youth centres

**青年中心不等於所有放學後聚腳點**

青年中心可以是 after-school hangout，但它是一種具體設施，不是所有 hangout 的同義詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The **youth centre** offers organised activities.  
The café is a popular **after-school hangout**.

---

## 02 Cafés

**咖啡店不等於聚腳點本身**

咖啡店可以成為 hangout，但很多 after-school hangouts 也可能是公園、商場、便利店附近等。

---

## 03 Social venues

**社交場所範圍太廣**

*Social venue* 可以包括酒吧、宴會廳、活動中心等，不一定和學生或放學後有關。

---

## 04 Meeting points

**集合點不等於閒聚場所**

*Meeting point* 可能只是短暫集合的位置，不一定會長時間逗留。

---

## 05 Recreation centres

**康樂中心不是一般近義詞**

它是設施類型，可能包含運動和活動，但不一定是非正式聚腳地。

---

## 06 Student spaces

**學生空間範圍更廣**

可以是圖書館、自修室、學生會空間，不一定用來 hang out。

---

## 07 After-school clubs

**課後興趣班／社團不等於聚腳地**

*After-school club* 是活動或組織，不是地方本身。

---

## 08 Community spaces

**社區空間範圍太廣**

可以供不同年齡和用途使用，不一定是學生放學後聚腳。

---

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## After-school hangouts vs Places to gather after class

**After-school hangouts**
- 更口語
- 帶輕鬆閒聚意味

**Places to gather after class**
- 更描述性
- 語氣更中性

---

## After-class meeting spots vs After-school gathering spots

**After-class meeting spots**
- 偏「在哪裡碰面」

**After-school gathering spots**
- 偏「在哪裡聚集、一起待著」

---

## Places to hang out after school vs After-school meeting places

**Places to hang out after school**
- 更口語
- 強調一起消磨時間

**After-school meeting places**
- 稍正式
- 可以只是固定會合地

---

## After-school social spots vs After-school gathering spots

**After-school social spots**
- 突出聊天、交朋友、社交

**After-school gathering spots**
- 只需要表示聚集，不一定強調社交目的

---

## After-class hangout spots vs After-class meeting spots

**After-class hangout spots**
- 更口語
- 強調逗留、聊天、放鬆

**After-class meeting spots**
- 重點是碰面地點
- 可以只是先集合再離開
