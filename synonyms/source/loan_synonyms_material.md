# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Loan

**借款、信貸、預支、融資、債務、貸款額度——究竟是哪一種 loan？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**borrowed money; credit; advance; financing; borrowing; debt; loan facility; personal loan; bank loan; cash advance; line of credit**

---

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Borrowed money

**借來的錢；借款**

最直接的描述性改寫，強調資金是借入、之後通常需要償還。


### EXAMPLE · 例句


She used borrowed money to pay for the course.


### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- borrowed money

- borrowed funds

- repay borrowed money

- use borrowed money


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 loan 是一次性借款、廣義信貸、預支、融資安排、債務結果，還是循環／額度型借貸？

---

## 02 Credit

**信貸；信用額度**

比 loan 更廣，可包括貸款、信用卡額度、賒帳或循環信貸。


### EXAMPLE · 例句


The shop offers credit to customers who cannot pay the full amount immediately.


### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- access to credit

- consumer credit

- extend credit

- credit facility


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 loan 是一次性借款、廣義信貸、預支、融資安排、債務結果，還是循環／額度型借貸？

---

## 03 Advance

**預支款；預付款**

指先行支付、之後扣回或償還的一筆錢，例如 salary advance / cash advance。


### EXAMPLE · 例句


She asked her employer for an advance before payday.


### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- salary advance

- cash advance

- royalty advance

- advance payment


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 loan 是一次性借款、廣義信貸、預支、融資安排、債務結果，還是循環／額度型借貸？

---

## 04 Financing

**融資；資金安排**

強調為購買、項目、企業或資產提供資金安排，範圍比單一 loan 更廣。


### EXAMPLE · 例句


The company needs financing to buy new equipment.


### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- arrange financing

- business financing

- project financing

- vehicle financing


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 loan 是一次性借款、廣義信貸、預支、融資安排、債務結果，還是循環／額度型借貸？

---

## 05 Borrowing

**借款；借貸**

較抽象，強調借入資金的行為或總體借款額，而不是一筆具體貸款。


### EXAMPLE · 例句


The government reduced its reliance on borrowing.


### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- household borrowing

- government borrowing

- borrowing costs

- reduce borrowing

---

## 06 Debt

## 10 Cash advance

以信用卡、薪金或其他安排先取得一筆現金，結構通常不同於標準 loan。


### EXAMPLE · 例句


He used his credit card to get a cash advance.

可在批准限額內按需要反覆提取，通常只對已使用額度計息。


### EXAMPLE · 例句


The company has a line of credit that it can use repeatedly up to an approved limit.

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Credit

比 loan 更廣

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A credit card provides credit, but each purchase is not a separate loan.

---

## 02 Debt

從負債角度看更廣

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A loan creates debt, but not all debt comes from a loan.

---

## 03 Advance

只對預支 sense

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A salary advance is usually repaid from future wages.

---

## 04 Mortgage

只是特定 loan subtype

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A mortgage is a loan, but a personal loan is not necessarily a mortgage.

---

## 05 Overdraft

透支不是普通一次性 loan

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

An overdraft can be used repeatedly rather than paid out once.

---

## 06 Grant

資助通常毋須償還

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A student grant is not a student loan.

---

## 07 Investment

投資不是借款

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Equity investment does not create the same repayment obligation as a loan.

---

## 08 Deposit

存款／按金方向相反

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A bank deposit is money you place with the bank; a bank loan is money you borrow.

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Loan vs Borrowed money
最直接、描述性，但不如 loan 正式精確。

## Loan vs Credit
`credit` 範圍更廣，可包括多種非一次性借貸安排。

## Loan vs Advance
`advance` 強調提前支付、之後扣回或償還。

## Loan vs Financing
`financing` 是較廣的資金安排概念，可包含 loan。

## Loan vs Borrowing
`borrowing` 強調借貸行為／總量，不是單筆可數貸款。

## Loan vs Debt
`debt` 從借款人的負債角度描述，來源更廣。

## Loan vs Loan facility
後者是正式、制度化的可借用額度／貸款安排。

## Loan vs Personal loan
後者只適合個人私人用途。

## Loan vs Bank loan
後者明確指出貸款來源是銀行。

## Loan vs Cash advance
後者是預支現金，費用與結構常不同於標準 loan。

## Loan vs Line of credit
後者可按需要循環提取，不是單一一次性放款。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Loan is countable
- a loan
- two loans
- take out a loan
- repay a loan

## 02 Borrowing is usually uncountable
- household borrowing
- government borrowing
- borrowing costs

## 03 Credit is broader
`credit` 可包括信用卡、賒帳、line of credit、loan 等多種借貸形式。

## 04 Debt
loan 通常形成 debt，但 debt 還可以來自：
- unpaid bills
- credit cards
- overdrafts
- other obligations

## 05 Advance
常見：
- salary advance
- cash advance
- royalty advance

## 06 Line of credit
和普通一次性 loan 不同：
- 可反覆提取
- 有 approved limit
- 常只對已使用金額計息

## 07 Loan as a verb
美式英語可說：
- The bank loaned the company the money.

英式英語中通常更自然：
- The bank lent the company the money.
