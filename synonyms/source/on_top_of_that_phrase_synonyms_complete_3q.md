# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# On top of that · phrase

「此外／而且／再加上／再說」，究竟是哪一種 `on top of that`？

`on top of that` 是一個**加法連接片語**，表示在前面已提到的事實、理由、優點或缺點之外，再補充另一點。使用者提供的 **furthermore** 和 **in addition** 全部保留：前者較正式、較論證性；後者最中性。另加入 **moreover**（進一步加強論點）、**additionally**（正式精簡補充）、**what's more**（較口語、帶強調）、**besides**（補充另一理由）與 **also**（最簡單的加法副詞）。

> 本課主要處理 `on top of that = in addition to what has already been mentioned, especially when adding another reason, fact, benefit or drawback`。
>
> 同義詞數量**不固定**；本課按正式程度、論證力度、口語色彩、理由補充與句法功能選用 **7 個**替代表達。
>
> Format: **7 curated alternatives + 8 false-synonym contrasts + 3 exercises per target synonym (21 questions total)**.

---

# 01 同義詞指南


### 01 Furthermore
**此外；而且；再者**

使用者提供。`furthermore` 是 `on top of that` 的正式書面近義詞，表示在已有理由、事實或論點之外再補充一點。它常見於 essays, reports, formal arguments。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The proposal is expensive; furthermore, it would take years to implement.

**中文翻譯**

這項提案成本高；此外，還需要多年才能實施。

**USAGE CLUE**

- formal addition of another supporting point or fact

---


### 02 In Addition
**此外；另外；加上**

使用者提供。`in addition` 是中性、非常通用的補充連接詞，可用於書面和口語。它比 `furthermore` 少一點論證感，也比 `what's more` 更中性。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The course includes lectures. In addition, students attend weekly workshops.

**中文翻譯**

課程包括講座。另外，學生每週也會參加工作坊。

**USAGE CLUE**

- neutral addition of another fact, feature or item

---


### 03 Moreover
**而且；此外；更何況**

`moreover` 較正式，常用來加入一個**進一步加強前面論點**的理由。它和 `furthermore` 很接近，但 often sounds slightly more argumentative or cumulative。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The scheme is costly; moreover, its benefits are uncertain.

**中文翻譯**

這項計劃成本高；而且，其效益也不確定。

**USAGE CLUE**

- formal addition of a point that strengthens an argument or conclusion

---


### 04 Additionally
**此外；另外**

`additionally` 是中性偏正式的副詞，常用於 reports, instructions, specifications, administrative writing。它功能接近 `in addition`，但句法更緊湊。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The software supports tablets. Additionally, it can run offline.

**中文翻譯**

這套軟件支援平板電腦。此外，它也能離線運作。

**USAGE CLUE**

- neutral or formal addition of another specification, requirement or fact

---


### 05 What'S More
**而且；更重要的是；再加上**

較口語。`what's more` 用於加入一個額外而往往更有力、令人注意的點。它比 `in addition` 更有 conversational emphasis。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The flat is close to work. What's more, the rent is reasonable.

**中文翻譯**

這間公寓離工作地點很近。而且，租金也合理。

**USAGE CLUE**

- conversational addition of an extra point that feels noteworthy or reinforcing

---


### 06 Besides
**此外；再說；況且**

`besides` 可作句子副詞，表示在前面理由之外再補充一個理由，常見於口語和較隨意書面語。它往往帶一點『再說……』的語氣，比 `in addition` 更有 justification flavour。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> I don't want to drive. Besides, parking will be difficult.

**中文翻譯**

我不想開車。再說，泊車也會很困難。

**USAGE CLUE**

- adding another reason, often to justify a decision or opinion

---


### 07 Also
**也；另外**

`also` 是最簡單、最中性的加法副詞。它可以改寫 `on top of that` 的基本 additive function，但語氣較弱，通常放在主語後、主要動詞前或助動詞後，而不是總以句首連接兩句。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The course is practical and also relatively inexpensive.

**中文翻譯**

這門課實用，而且價格也相對便宜。

**USAGE CLUE**

- simple, low-emphasis addition of another fact or property

---

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 on top of that / furthermore

`furthermore` 較正式，常見於論證與報告；`on top of that` 更口語，也常帶累積感。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Furthermore, the proposal is expensive.  
> On top of that, we'd need extra staff.

---


## 02 on top of that / in addition

`in addition` 最中性，可單純增加一項資訊；`on top of that` 往往更強調『再加上一點』。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> In addition, breakfast is included.  
> On top of that, parking is free.

---


## 03 on top of that / moreover

`moreover` 通常進一步強化前面論點；普通列舉資訊未必需要這種論證感。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Moreover, the evidence is weak.  
> In addition, the report includes an appendix.

---


## 04 on top of that / besides

`besides` 常有『再說／況且』的理由補充功能；`on top of that` 可以只是增加事實。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Besides, it would cost too much.  
> On top of that, the package includes breakfast.

---


## 05 on top of that / also

`also` 是句內加法副詞，語氣較弱；`on top of that` 更像獨立 discourse connector。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> She also teaches French.  
> On top of that, she runs the club.

---


## 06 on top of that / therefore

`therefore` 表示因果結果『因此』；`on top of that` 只是加一點，沒有必然因果。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The road was closed; therefore, we turned back.  
> The road was closed. On top of that, it was snowing.

---


## 07 on top of that / however

`however` 表示轉折；`on top of that` 表示累加，功能相反。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The room is small; however, it is bright.  
> The room is small. On top of that, it is noisy.

---


## 08 on top of that / to make matters worse

`to make matters worse` 只適合加入**更壞的負面情況**；`on top of that` 可以加優點、缺點或中性資訊。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The car broke down; to make matters worse, it began to rain.  
> The hotel is cheap; on top of that, breakfast is included.

---

# 05 高混淆組

## furthermore vs moreover
- **furthermore**：正式增加另一論點。
- **moreover**：更常有『而且這一點還進一步加強前文』的感覺。

## in addition vs additionally
- **in addition**：片語，書面口語都可。
- **additionally**：單字副詞，行政／技術語氣稍強。

## what's more vs besides
- **what's more**：強調額外一點本身很值得注意。
- **besides**：較常用來補充另一個支持理由。

## also vs in addition
- **also**：通常放句內，強調度低。
- **in addition**：常作句首連接語。

## on top of that vs to make matters worse
- **on top of that**：可正面、負面、中性。
- **to make matters worse**：只能是負面而且更糟。

## on top of that vs therefore
- **on top of that**：加法。
- **therefore**：因果結果。

## on top of that vs however
- **on top of that**：累加。
- **however**：轉折。

## on top of that vs besides
- **on top of that**：可只是添加資訊。
- **besides**：常有『再說／況且』的辯護或理由功能。

# 08 生成／評估注意事項

1. **同義詞數量不是固定值。** 本課按正式程度、論證力度、口語色彩、理由補充與句法功能選 **7 個**。
2. 因此本課有 **3 exercises per target = 21 exercises**。
3. 使用者提供的 `furthermore` 與 `in addition` 全部保留。
4. `furthermore` 只在 formal argumentative addition 明確時使用。
5. `in addition` 是最中性的 independent connector。
6. `moreover` 需要第二點能 reinforce the previous argument。
7. `additionally` 偏正式，尤其適合 requirements/specifications/administrative prose。
8. `what's more` 保留給較口語且帶 emphasis 的額外一點。
9. `besides` 需要『再補一個理由』的 justificatory flavour。
10. `also` 主要作低強度句內加法副詞，句法位置與 discourse connectors 不同。
11. False synonyms 特別測試 therefore/however/to make matters worse，避免把因果、轉折或純負面升級混成一般 additive meaning。
12. Distractors 全部使用真實自然英文連接詞／副詞，不使用人工標籤。
13. 每題以 decisive clue 建立唯一答案，例如 `formal analysis`, `neutral feature list`, `reinforces criticism`, `supports a choice`, `simple sentence-internal addition`。
14. 原始 `on top of that` 句子保持自然；正解若需調整標點或副詞位置，採用自然英文。
15. 本課只處理 **on top of that 作加法連接片語**。
