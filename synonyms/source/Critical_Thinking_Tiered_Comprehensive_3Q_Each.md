# CRITICAL THINKING · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Critical thinking

**「批判性思考／審辨思考」，核心不是『批評』，而是分析、檢查假設、評估證據、推理，以及作出有理據的判斷。**

本單元集中處理 **critical thinking = disciplined thinking used to analyse information, question assumptions, evaluate evidence, reason logically, and reach justified judgments**。你提供的 **analytical thinking; reasoned judgment; careful evaluation** 正好代表三個核心部分：**analysis**、**judgment**、**evaluation**。

這次保留 **13 個**真正有教學價值的詞／表達，並把 **分析、邏輯、反思、證據、獨立思考、客觀評估、審視、假設檢查** 分開處理。每個目標設 **3 道題**；所有 distractor 都完整說明其意思及為何在該句不合適。

## Tier 1 — Core analysis / reasoning equivalents · 核心分析、推理與判斷近義表達

### 01 Analytical thinking

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：分析思考；分析性思維  
**語域**：neutral / academic  
**核心語義**：the ability to break information or problems into parts, identify relationships, and examine how those parts work together  
**用法提示**：和 critical thinking 最核心的近義表達之一，特別適合分析資料、問題結構、因果關係和證據。  
**常見搭配**：analytical thinking skills; develop analytical thinking; analytical approach; analytical ability; analytical reasoning.  
**常見搭配對象**：problem, data, argument, evidence, situation.  
**與 critical thinking 的辨析**：Analytical thinking 偏『拆解和分析』；critical thinking 還包括質疑假設、評估證據和作判斷。  
**使用限制**：若重點是最終作出判斷，reasoned judgment / careful evaluation 可能更精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The task develops analytical thinking by asking students to compare several explanations.  
這項任務要求學生比較多個解釋，從而培養分析思考。

### 02 Reasoned judgment

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：有理據的判斷；理性判斷  
**語域**：formal / academic  
**核心語義**：a judgment reached through evidence, reasons, and deliberate consideration rather than impulse  
**用法提示**：當 critical thinking 的核心是根據證據和推理作出判斷時非常精準。  
**常見搭配**：exercise reasoned judgment; reach a reasoned judgment; sound judgment; informed judgment; reasoned conclusion.  
**常見搭配對象**：decision, conclusion, evidence, policy, argument.  
**與 critical thinking 的辨析**：Reasoned judgment 強調最終判斷；critical thinking 還包括此前的分析、質疑和比較過程。  
**使用限制**：若句子只是描述分析步驟而沒有作結論，analytical thinking 更貼切。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Students must use reasoned judgment when deciding which source is most reliable.  
學生在判斷哪個資料來源最可靠時，必須作出有理據的判斷。

### 03 Careful evaluation

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：仔細評估；審慎評價  
**語域**：neutral / academic  
**核心語義**：the deliberate assessment of evidence, claims, options, or information against relevant criteria  
**用法提示**：和你提供的 seed 一致，當 critical thinking 指仔細衡量證據、優缺點、可靠性或可信度時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：careful evaluation of evidence; evaluate sources; careful assessment; evaluate claims; evaluate alternatives.  
**常見搭配對象**：evidence, source, claim, option, argument.  
**與 critical thinking 的辨析**：Careful evaluation 偏『評估』；critical thinking 範圍更廣，可包含生成問題和推理。  
**使用限制**：若只是創意發想而非評估，不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The assignment requires careful evaluation of conflicting evidence.  
這份作業要求仔細評估互相矛盾的證據。

### 04 Critical analysis

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：批判性分析；審辨分析  
**語域**：formal / academic  
**核心語義**：the systematic examination of ideas, arguments, texts, or evidence to identify strengths, weaknesses, assumptions, and implications  
**用法提示**：學術寫作中和 critical thinking 非常接近，尤其分析文章、政策、研究、論證時。  
**常見搭配**：critical analysis; conduct a critical analysis; critical analysis of evidence; analytical essay; critically analyse.  
**常見搭配對象**：text, argument, policy, research, evidence.  
**與 critical thinking 的辨析**：Critical analysis 是把 critical thinking 應用於具體材料的分析活動。  
**使用限制**：日常決策語境中可能顯得較學術。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The essay requires critical analysis of the author's main argument.  
這篇文章要求對作者的主要論點作批判性分析。

### 05 Logical reasoning

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：邏輯推理；邏輯思考  
**語域**：neutral / academic  
**核心語義**：the process of reaching conclusions by applying valid logical relationships and consistent reasoning  
**用法提示**：當 critical thinking 重點是推理是否前後一致、結論是否由前提支持時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：logical reasoning skills; use logical reasoning; sound reasoning; reasoning process; logical conclusion.  
**常見搭配對象**：argument, conclusion, premise, problem, evidence.  
**與 critical thinking 的辨析**：Logical reasoning 是 critical thinking 的核心組成之一，但不包括所有證據評估或反思。  
**使用限制**：來源可信度、偏誤等問題不能只靠 formal logic 處理。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The puzzle tests logical reasoning rather than factual recall.  
這道題測試的是邏輯推理，而不是事實記憶。

## Tier 2 — Reflective / evaluative variants · 反思、評估與證據導向變體

### 06 Reflective thinking

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：反思思考；反思性思維  
**語域**：neutral / academic  
**核心語義**：the deliberate reconsideration of experiences, assumptions, decisions, or beliefs in order to improve understanding  
**用法提示**：當 critical thinking 涉及回顧自己的想法、偏見、決策或學習過程時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：reflective thinking; reflective practice; reflect on assumptions; reflective learning; reflective judgment.  
**常見搭配對象**：experience, assumption, decision, learning, practice.  
**與 critical thinking 的辨析**：Reflective thinking 強調向內檢視；critical thinking 也會分析外部證據。  
**使用限制**：純粹比較數據時 analytical thinking 更直接。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Reflective thinking helps learners recognise weaknesses in their own reasoning.  
反思思考有助學習者察覺自己推理中的弱點。

### 07 Evaluative thinking

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：評估性思維；評價性思考  
**語域**：formal / academic  
**核心語義**：the ability to judge quality, credibility, relevance, or value using explicit criteria  
**用法提示**：當 critical thinking 特別著重判斷來源、證據或方案的質素時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：evaluative thinking; evaluative judgment; evaluation criteria; assess credibility; evaluate quality.  
**常見搭配對象**：source, evidence, claim, programme, option.  
**與 critical thinking 的辨析**：Evaluative thinking 聚焦『判斷質素』；critical thinking 還包含分析與推論。  
**使用限制**：如果沒有評估標準，這個詞可能太技術化。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The exercise builds evaluative thinking by asking students to rank sources by reliability.  
這項練習要求學生按可靠程度評定資料來源，從而培養評估性思維。

### 08 Evidence-based reasoning

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：以證據為本的推理；證據導向推理  
**語域**：formal / academic  
**核心語義**：reasoning that bases conclusions primarily on relevant and credible evidence  
**用法提示**：當 critical thinking 的關鍵是用證據支持結論、避免無根據判斷時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：evidence-based reasoning; evidence-based conclusion; use evidence; weigh evidence; evidence-informed judgment.  
**常見搭配對象**：claim, conclusion, decision, research, policy.  
**與 critical thinking 的辨析**：Evidence-based reasoning 特別強調證據基礎；critical thinking 還包括問題設定和假設檢查。  
**使用限制**：不應誤解為所有證據都同樣可靠，仍需評估證據質素。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The debate encourages evidence-based reasoning rather than unsupported opinion.  
辯論鼓勵以證據為本的推理，而不是沒有根據的意見。

### 09 Independent thinking

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：獨立思考  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：the ability to form one's own judgments rather than simply accepting other people's views  
**用法提示**：當 critical thinking 強調不盲從、自己思考和形成判斷時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：independent thinking; think independently; independent judgment; independent thought; encourage independent thinking.  
**常見搭配對象**：opinion, decision, argument, learning, judgment.  
**與 critical thinking 的辨析**：Independent thinking 強調自主；critical thinking 還要求理據、證據和方法。  
**使用限制**：『自己想』不等於『想得好』；獨立思考仍可能缺乏證據。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The course encourages independent thinking instead of memorising model answers.  
課程鼓勵獨立思考，而不是背誦標準答案。

### 10 Objective assessment

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：客觀評估；客觀判斷  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：an assessment that aims to minimise personal bias and rely on relevant evidence and criteria  
**用法提示**：當 critical thinking 需要盡量公平、中立地評估資訊或選項時很適合。  
**常見搭配**：objective assessment; objective evaluation; assess objectively; objective criteria; impartial assessment.  
**常見搭配對象**：evidence, performance, proposal, claim, risk.  
**與 critical thinking 的辨析**：Objective assessment 強調減少偏見；critical thinking 還包含推論和反思。  
**使用限制**：完全客觀有時難以做到，正式語境常理解為『盡量按標準和證據評估』。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The panel made an objective assessment of the competing proposals.  
評審小組對各項競爭方案作出客觀評估。

## Tier 3 — Scrutiny / assumption-checking / judgment variants · 審視、假設檢查與判斷變體

### 11 Close scrutiny

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：仔細審視；嚴格檢視  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：very careful examination intended to identify weaknesses, inconsistencies, risks, or hidden assumptions  
**用法提示**：當 critical thinking 的語氣是『不要輕信，要仔細檢查』時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：close scrutiny; under scrutiny; scrutinise evidence; careful scrutiny; withstand scrutiny.  
**常見搭配對象**：claim, evidence, policy, argument, data.  
**與 critical thinking 的辨析**：Scrutiny 強調檢查強度；critical thinking 是更完整的思維能力。  
**使用限制**：若需要提出新方案或作綜合判斷，scrutiny 不足以涵蓋全程。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The claim does not withstand close scrutiny.  
這項說法經不起仔細審視。

### 12 Questioning assumptions

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：質疑假設；檢視前設  
**語域**：neutral / academic  
**核心語義**：the practice of identifying and examining beliefs or assumptions that have been taken for granted  
**用法提示**：這是 critical thinking 的代表性操作之一，尤其論證、研究和決策語境。  
**常見搭配**：question assumptions; challenge assumptions; examine assumptions; hidden assumptions; underlying assumptions.  
**常見搭配對象**：argument, model, policy, belief, decision.  
**與 critical thinking 的辨析**：這是 critical thinking 的一個核心步驟，而不是完整同義詞。  
**使用限制**：若只需要數值計算或資料整理，未必涉及 assumptions。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Good critical thinking involves questioning assumptions before accepting a conclusion.  
良好的批判性思考包括在接受結論前質疑當中的假設。

### 13 Sound judgment

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：健全判斷；可靠判斷  
**語域**：formal / common  
**核心語義**：good judgment based on relevant information, experience, logic, and proportionate consideration  
**用法提示**：當 critical thinking 的成果是成熟、合理、可信的判斷時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：exercise sound judgment; use sound judgment; sound decision-making; sound conclusion; professional judgment.  
**常見搭配對象**：decision, risk, evidence, policy, professional situation.  
**與 critical thinking 的辨析**：Sound judgment 是 critical thinking 的成果之一；不是分析過程本身。  
**使用限制**：如果重點是拆解論證而不是作決定，critical analysis 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The role requires sound judgment when information is incomplete.  
這個職位要求在資料不完整時作出健全判斷。

## 04 False synonyms / thinking traps · 容易誤選的詞與思考陷阱

### 01 Intelligence

**智力不等於 critical thinking**  
intelligence 是更廣的認知能力；critical thinking 是一套可練習的分析、評估和推理技能。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Intelligence is broad cognitive capacity; critical thinking is a disciplined way of reasoning.

### 02 Knowledge

**知識不等於 critical thinking**  
knowledge 是知道甚麼；critical thinking 是如何分析、評估和運用所知。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Knowledge supplies information; critical thinking evaluates and uses it.

### 03 Creativity

**創意不等於 critical thinking**  
creativity 著重產生新想法；critical thinking 著重檢驗想法、證據和推理。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Creativity generates possibilities; critical thinking evaluates them.

### 04 Problem-solving

**解難不等於所有 critical thinking**  
problem-solving 可包含 critical thinking，但也包括執行、技術操作和選擇方案。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Critical thinking supports problem-solving, but the two are not identical.

### 05 Opinion

**意見不等於 critical thinking**  
有意見並不代表有分析證據、檢查假設或推理。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
An opinion can be unsupported; critical thinking requires reasons and evaluation.

### 06 Scepticism

**懷疑不等於 critical thinking**  
scepticism 可以是起點，但批判思考不是凡事否定，而是按證據判斷。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Scepticism questions claims; critical thinking evaluates them fairly.

### 07 Memory

**記憶不等於 critical thinking**  
記得資料是 recall；critical thinking 要對資料作分析、比較和判斷。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Memory retrieves information; critical thinking reasons with it.

### 08 Criticism

**批評不等於 critical thinking**  
criticism 可指負面評價；critical thinking 的 critical 指審辨、分析，不等於挑錯或負面批評。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Critical thinking is evaluative reasoning, not simply being critical or negative.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Analytical thinking · 分析思考；分析性思維

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The exercise develops critical thinking by asking students to break the problem into parts.

**中文翻譯**  
這項練習要求學生把問題拆成幾部分，培養分析思考。

- A. analytical thinking
- B. memory
- C. creativity
- D. opinion
- E. criticism
- F. knowledge

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**analytical thinking** · 分析思考；分析性思維

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The exercise develops analytical thinking by asking students to break the problem into parts.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. analytical thinking ✓ — break the problem into parts 是分析思考的直接特徵。**
- **B. memory — 記憶只涉及提取資料，不需要拆解問題結構。**
- **C. creativity — 創意著重新想法，不是這裡的拆解分析。**
- **D. opinion — 表達個人看法不等於分析。**
- **E. criticism — 批評可以是負面評價，沒有系統分析含義。**
- **F. knowledge — 知識是內容基礎，不是分析過程。**

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Critical thinking 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 拆解問題／找關係 | **analytical thinking** | 是否要把問題拆開、找模式／因果？ |
| 按理據作判斷 | **reasoned judgment** | 是否要在多個選項中作合理結論？ |
| 仔細衡量證據 | **careful evaluation** | 是否要比較 evidence / claims / options？ |
| 分析論證／文本 | **critical analysis** | 是否在 academic text / policy / research 語境？ |
| 檢查推論是否成立 | **logical reasoning** | 是否重點是 premise → conclusion？ |
| 反思自己的思考 | **reflective thinking** | 是否在檢視 own assumptions / decisions？ |
| 判斷質素／可信度 | **evaluative thinking** | 是否有 explicit criteria？ |
| 以證據支持結論 | **evidence-based reasoning** | 是否要避免 unsupported opinion？ |
| 不盲從他人 | **independent thinking** | 是否重點是自己形成判斷？ |
| 減少偏見 | **objective assessment** | 是否要按同一標準、分開 evidence 與 preference？ |
| 嚴格仔細檢查 | **close scrutiny** | 是否要找出 weakness / inconsistency / risk？ |
| 檢查隱藏前設 | **questioning assumptions** | 是否在問 what has been taken for granted？ |
| 成熟合理決策 | **sound judgment** | 是否在不完整資訊下仍要作好判斷？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **critical thinking vs analytical thinking**: analytical thinking 強調拆解與分析；critical thinking 還包含評估與判斷。
- **critical thinking vs logical reasoning**: logical reasoning 聚焦推論有效性；critical thinking 範圍更廣。
- **critical thinking vs careful evaluation**: careful evaluation 是其中一個核心步驟。
- **critical thinking vs reflective thinking**: reflective thinking 主要檢視自己的經驗和思考。
- **critical thinking vs independent thinking**: 獨立思考是自主形成判斷，但仍需證據和推理才稱得上 critical thinking。
- **critical thinking vs creativity**: 創意產生新點子；critical thinking 檢驗點子。
- **critical thinking vs knowledge**: knowledge 提供內容；critical thinking 評估和運用內容。
- **critical thinking vs scepticism**: 懷疑只是起點；批判思考仍要公平檢驗證據。
- **critical thinking vs criticism**: critical 在此是『審辨』，不是『負面批評』。
- **critical thinking vs sound judgment**: sound judgment 是批判思考可能產生的成熟結果。

## 08 Grammar, terminology & reasoning traps · 文法、術語與推理陷阱

- **critical thinking** 通常作不可數能力概念：develop critical thinking / critical-thinking skills。
- 前置修飾語常加連字號：**critical-thinking skills / critical-thinking exercise**。
- **analytical thinking** 和 **logical reasoning** 通常也是不可數能力名稱。
- **reasoned judgment / sound judgment** 常和 **exercise / use / reach** 搭配。
- **evaluate + evidence/source/claim**；名詞可用 **evaluation of evidence**。
- **critical analysis of + object**：critical analysis of a text / policy / argument。
- **reflect on + experience/decision**；名詞是 **reflection / reflective thinking**。
- **evidence-based** 作前置形容詞通常加連字號：evidence-based reasoning / evidence-based decision-making。
- **objective assessment of + object**：objective assessment of risk / evidence / performance。
- **under scrutiny / close scrutiny**：The claim is under scrutiny; it does not withstand close scrutiny。
- **question assumptions / challenge assumptions / examine assumptions** 都是常見搭配。
- **critical ≠ negative**：critical analysis 可以同時指出 strengths and weaknesses。
- **critical thinking ≠ intelligence / knowledge / memory**：這些能力可支持批判思考，但不能互換。
