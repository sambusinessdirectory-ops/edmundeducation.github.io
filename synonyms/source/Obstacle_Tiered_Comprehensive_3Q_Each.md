# OBSTACLE · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Obstacle

**「障礙／阻礙／難關」，要先分清是阻止進入的 barrier、需要跨越的一關 hurdle、正式書面上的 impediment、流程瓶頸 bottleneck，還是進展中的 setback。**

本單元集中處理 **obstacle = something that blocks, delays, restricts, or makes progress, movement, access, or success more difficult**。你提供的 **barrier; hurdle; impediment** 分別代表 **阻隔、需要跨越的難關、正式的妨礙因素**。

這個詞的高質量近義與情境替代表達相當豐富，因此本次保留 **12 個**自然且具教學價值的詞／表達，不追求固定數量。共 **36 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**；正確答案位置在 A–F 間平均分布。

## Tier 1 — Core barrier / hurdle / impediment equivalents · 核心障礙、難關與阻礙近義詞

### 01 Barrier

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：障礙；屏障  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：something that blocks, prevents, or makes progress, access, movement, or communication more difficult  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 obstacle 最直接、最常用的近義詞之一，尤其適合制度、溝通、交通、教育和機會語境。  
**常見搭配**：barrier to entry; language barrier; physical barrier; remove a barrier; barrier to progress.  
**常見搭配對象**：access, communication, participation, movement, progress.  
**與 obstacle 的辨析**：Barrier 常帶『阻隔／阻止進入或前進』意味；obstacle 更廣。  
**使用限制**：若重點是需要跨越的一個具體挑戰，hurdle 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
High fees can be a barrier to further study.  
高昂費用可以成為繼續升學的障礙。

### 02 Hurdle

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：難關；障礙；需要跨越的關卡  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：a difficulty or problem that must be overcome before progress can continue  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，當 obstacle 是一個需要逐步克服的關卡或難題時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：clear a hurdle; overcome a hurdle; major hurdle; legal hurdle; final hurdle.  
**常見搭配對象**：approval, application, project, negotiation, reform.  
**與 obstacle 的辨析**：Hurdle 比 obstacle 更有『跨過一關』的進程感。  
**使用限制**：不適合形容永久性的物理屏障。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Securing approval is the final hurdle before construction can begin.  
取得批准是動工前的最後一道難關。

### 03 Impediment

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：阻礙；妨礙因素  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：something that slows, obstructs, or interferes with progress, movement, or development  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，較正式，常見於法律、政策、發展和書面分析。  
**常見搭配**：impediment to progress; serious impediment; legal impediment; remove an impediment; impediment to growth.  
**常見搭配對象**：growth, reform, movement, development, agreement.  
**與 obstacle 的辨析**：Impediment 比 obstacle 更正式，常強調拖慢或妨礙。  
**使用限制**：日常口語通常直接用 obstacle / barrier。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Poor infrastructure remains a serious impediment to economic growth.  
基建不足仍然是經濟增長的一大阻礙。

### 04 Obstruction

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：阻塞；阻礙物；妨礙  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：something that physically or procedurally blocks a route, process, or action  
**用法提示**：當 obstacle 帶『擋住通道／阻塞程序』意味時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：physical obstruction; obstruction of traffic; obstruction in the airway; remove an obstruction; procedural obstruction.  
**常見搭配對象**：road, airway, passage, process, investigation.  
**與 obstacle 的辨析**：Obstruction 比 obstacle 更強調實際阻塞或阻撓行為。  
**使用限制**：抽象生活困難不一定適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Workers removed the obstruction from the drainage channel.  
工人清除了排水渠中的阻塞物。

### 05 Difficulty

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：困難；難題  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：a problem or condition that makes something harder to do  
**用法提示**：當 obstacle 只是泛指『做某事時遇到的困難』，而沒有明確阻擋形象時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：face difficulties; practical difficulty; financial difficulty; technical difficulty; overcome difficulties.  
**常見搭配對象**：task, project, finance, study, implementation.  
**與 obstacle 的辨析**：Difficulty 比 obstacle 更廣，也較少『阻擋』意象。  
**使用限制**：若是明確阻止進入或前進，barrier / obstacle 更精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
A lack of reliable data created serious difficulties for the study.  
缺乏可靠資料為研究帶來嚴重困難。

## Tier 2 — Challenge / constraint / process variants · 挑戰、限制與流程型變體

### 06 Challenge

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：挑戰；難題  
**語域**：neutral / positive-to-neutral  
**核心語義**：a demanding problem or task that requires effort, skill, or persistence to overcome  
**用法提示**：當 obstacle 可被看成需要處理、克服的挑戰時很自然，語氣通常較建設性。  
**常見搭配**：major challenge; face a challenge; overcome a challenge; technical challenge; practical challenge.  
**常見搭配對象**：project, study, reform, career, implementation.  
**與 obstacle 的辨析**：Challenge 比 obstacle 更少負面阻塞感，較強調應對與能力。  
**使用限制**：若是完全阻止進展，barrier / impediment 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Staff shortages remain a major challenge for the service.  
人手短缺仍然是這項服務的一大挑戰。

### 07 Constraint

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：限制；制約因素  
**語域**：formal / academic  
**核心語義**：a limit or condition that restricts what can be done  
**用法提示**：當 obstacle 其實是資源、時間、法律或制度上的限制時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：budget constraint; time constraint; resource constraint; legal constraint; operate under constraints.  
**常見搭配對象**：budget, time, resources, policy, design.  
**與 obstacle 的辨析**：Constraint 是限制可行選項，而不一定完全阻止行動。  
**使用限制**：不能把所有 obstacle 都改成 constraint。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Budget constraints limited the scale of the project.  
預算限制了項目的規模。

### 08 Bottleneck

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：瓶頸；阻滯點  
**語域**：neutral / business / systems  
**核心語義**：a point in a process where progress is slowed because capacity is limited  
**用法提示**：當 obstacle 出現在流程、交通、供應鏈、審批或生產系統中，重點是某一環節拖慢整體時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：production bottleneck; traffic bottleneck; approval bottleneck; remove a bottleneck; bottleneck in the process.  
**常見搭配對象**：process, production, traffic, supply chain, approval.  
**與 obstacle 的辨析**：Bottleneck 強調某個狹窄環節限制整體流量／速度。  
**使用限制**：一般個人困難或法律禁令通常不用 bottleneck。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The approval stage has become a bottleneck in the process.  
審批階段已成為整個流程的瓶頸。

### 09 Setback

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：挫折；阻礙進展的事件  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：an event or problem that delays progress or makes a situation worse after some progress has been made  
**用法提示**：當 obstacle 不是固定障礙，而是突然令進展倒退或延誤的事件時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：major setback; temporary setback; suffer a setback; setback for the project; overcome a setback.  
**常見搭配對象**：project, recovery, reform, negotiation, research.  
**與 obstacle 的辨析**：Setback 強調『進展後被打回去／延誤』；obstacle 可以在開始前已存在。  
**使用限制**：永久性制度障礙不一定叫 setback。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The funding cut was a major setback for the project.  
削減撥款對項目是一大挫折。

## Tier 3 — Idiomatic / formal hindrance variants · 慣用與正式妨礙型變體

### 10 Stumbling block

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：絆腳石；主要障礙  
**語域**：neutral / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：a problem or issue that repeatedly prevents agreement or progress  
**用法提示**：當 obstacle 是一個反覆卡住談判、計劃或合作的關鍵問題時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：major stumbling block; stumbling block to agreement; remain a stumbling block; remove the stumbling block.  
**常見搭配對象**：agreement, negotiation, reform, cooperation, plan.  
**與 obstacle 的辨析**：比 obstacle 更具比喻性，常指一個明確而持續卡住進展的問題。  
**使用限制**：正式技術文本可能優先用 barrier / impediment。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Funding remains the main stumbling block to an agreement.  
資金問題仍然是達成協議的主要絆腳石。

### 11 Roadblock

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：路障；重大阻礙  
**語域**：informal / figurative  
**核心語義**：a serious obstacle that stops progress, often suddenly or completely  
**用法提示**：比喻用法常見於工作、計劃、談判，表示『進度被堵死』。  
**常見搭配**：hit a roadblock; major roadblock; roadblock to progress; remove a roadblock; regulatory roadblock.  
**常見搭配對象**：project, plan, negotiation, approval, reform.  
**與 obstacle 的辨析**：Roadblock 比 obstacle 更形象，也常暗示幾乎完全停下來。  
**使用限制**：正式學術寫作較少用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The team hit a roadblock when the permit was refused.  
許可證被拒後，團隊遇上重大阻礙。

### 12 Hindrance

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：妨礙；阻力  
**語域**：formal / slightly literary  
**核心語義**：something that makes progress or action more difficult without necessarily stopping it completely  
**用法提示**：當 obstacle 主要是拖慢、增加麻煩，而不是完全阻斷時可用。  
**常見搭配**：hindrance to progress; serious hindrance; prove a hindrance; without hindrance; remove a hindrance.  
**常見搭配對象**：progress, communication, movement, work, development.  
**與 obstacle 的辨析**：Hindrance 比 impediment 稍一般，但仍偏正式，通常表示妨礙而非完全阻止。  
**使用限制**：日常口語較少見。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Outdated equipment proved a hindrance to efficient production.  
過時設備妨礙了高效生產。

## 04 False synonyms / obstacle traps · 容易誤選的詞與『障礙』概念陷阱

### 01 Problem

**問題不等於 obstacle**  
problem 很廣；有些問題需要解決，但未必阻擋進展。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Problem is broader than obstacle.

### 02 Risk

**風險不等於 obstacle**  
risk 是可能發生的不利結果；obstacle 是已存在或實際遇到的阻礙。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Risk is potential; an obstacle is actual.

### 03 Delay

**延誤不等於 obstacle**  
delay 是時間上的遲延，可以由 obstacle 引起。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A delay can be caused by an obstacle.

### 04 Failure

**失敗不等於 obstacle**  
failure 是未能成功的結果；obstacle 是過程中需要克服的阻礙。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Failure is an outcome.

### 05 Challenge

**挑戰只在可克服難題 sense 接近**  
challenge 語氣較建設性，未必真正阻擋進展。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Challenge is effort-focused.

### 06 Constraint

**限制只在限制條件 sense 接近**  
constraint 限制選項或資源，但不一定阻止進行。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Constraint is limit-focused.

### 07 Barrier

**barrier 偏阻隔／進入限制**  
並非所有 obstacle 都有明確的『阻隔』結構。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Barrier is blocking/access-focused.

### 08 Obstacle / obstruct

**詞性陷阱**  
obstacle 是名詞；obstruct 是動詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Obstacle is a noun; obstruct is a verb.

- **C.** risk — is a possible future harm, not an existing obstacle; the sentence instead points to the sentence concerns access being blocked or made harder.
- **D.** delay — is a period of lateness and may result from an obstacle rather than being the obstacle itself; the sentence instead points to the sentence concerns access being blocked or made harder.
- **E.** failure — is an unsuccessful outcome rather than something to overcome during progress; the sentence instead points to the sentence concerns access being blocked or made harder.

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Obstacle 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 阻止進入／溝通／參與 | **barrier** | 是否 access / entry / communication is blocked？ |
| 需要逐關跨越 | **hurdle** | 是否 one stage must be cleared before proceeding？ |
| 正式：妨礙發展 | **impediment** | 是否 formal analysis of something slowing progress？ |
| 物理／程序阻塞 | **obstruction** | 是否 a route/process is actually blocked？ |
| 泛指困難 | **difficulty** | 是否 the issue simply makes something harder？ |
| 可克服的挑戰 | **challenge** | 是否 effort/skill is needed to deal with it？ |
| 資源／時間／法律限制 | **constraint** | 是否 a limit restricts available options？ |
| 流程瓶頸 | **bottleneck** | 是否 one stage slows the whole flow？ |
| 進展後出現挫折 | **setback** | 是否 progress was delayed or reversed after it began？ |
| 反覆卡住進展的問題 | **stumbling block** | 是否 one persistent issue prevents agreement/progress？ |
| 突然堵死進度 | **roadblock** | 是否 progress suddenly stops or nearly stops？ |
| 妨礙但未完全阻止 | **hindrance** | 是否 something makes progress harder without fully blocking it？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **obstacle vs barrier**: barrier 更強調阻隔、進入或接觸受阻。
- **obstacle vs hurdle**: hurdle 是需要逐關克服的一道難關。
- **obstacle vs impediment**: impediment 更正式，常見政策、法律、發展分析。
- **obstacle vs obstruction**: obstruction 偏實際阻塞通道或程序。
- **obstacle vs difficulty**: difficulty 更廣，未必真的阻擋進展。
- **obstacle vs challenge**: challenge 更建設性，強調需要努力應對。
- **obstacle vs constraint**: constraint 是限制條件，不一定完全阻止。
- **obstacle vs bottleneck**: bottleneck 是流程中限制整體速度的一個環節。
- **obstacle vs setback**: setback 是進展後發生的倒退／延誤事件。
- **obstacle vs roadblock**: roadblock 更形象，常暗示幾乎完全停下。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & obstacle traps · 文法、搭配與『障礙』詞彙陷阱

- **obstacle to + noun/-ing**：an obstacle to growth / to reaching agreement。
- **barrier to + noun/-ing**：a barrier to entry / to participation。
- **hurdle to/for** 可用，但更常見 **overcome/clear a hurdle**。
- **impediment to + noun**：an impediment to progress / development。
- **obstruction to/of** 依語境而定：obstruction to traffic / obstruction of justice。
- **difficulty with/in + noun/-ing**：difficulty with the system / difficulty in finding staff。
- **challenge for/to**：a challenge for the team / a challenge to existing policy。
- **constraint on + noun**：constraints on spending / design。
- **bottleneck in + process/system**：a bottleneck in production。
- **setback for/to**：a setback for the project / to recovery。
- **stumbling block to + noun**：a stumbling block to agreement。
- **hit a roadblock** 是常見慣用表達。
- **obstacle** 是名詞；**obstruct** 是動詞；**obstructive** 是形容詞。
