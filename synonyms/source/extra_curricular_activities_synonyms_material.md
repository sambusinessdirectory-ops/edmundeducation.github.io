# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Extra-curricular activities

**課後活動、課堂以外活動、增潤活動、聯課活動、學會及社團——究竟是哪一種 extra-curricular activities？**

本課聚焦：

**extra-curricular activities = optional or additional activities undertaken outside the formal taught curriculum, often for interests, skills, recreation, leadership, service, or personal development**

核心近義與近義表達：

**after-school activities; out-of-class activities; enrichment activities; co-curricular activities; extracurricular programmes; school clubs; student activities; enrichment programmes; non-academic activities; beyond-the-classroom activities; clubs and societies; student enrichment**

> **Precision rule:**  
> `after-school activities` = 時間限定在放學後。  
> `out-of-class activities` = 最廣泛的課堂外活動。  
> `enrichment activities / programmes` = 強調增潤和發展。  
> `co-curricular activities` = 與教育目標／課程較緊密。  
> `school clubs / clubs and societies` = 社團型子類。  
> `non-academic activities` 不能涵蓋所有學術型課外活動。  
> `student activities` 比 extra-curricular 更廣。

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 After-school activities

**課後活動**

和 `extra-curricular activities` 非常接近，特別適合在放學後舉行的體育、音樂、學會、補充學習或興趣活動。限制是：有些課外活動可能在午膳、週末或假期進行，因此不一定都是 after-school。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The school offers many after-school activities, including football and drama.

學校提供很多課後活動，包括足球和戲劇。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- after-school activity
- after-school club
- after-school programme
- join after-school activities

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡重點是活動時間、課堂之外、教育增潤、社團形式，還是整體學生發展？

---

## 02 Out-of-class activities

**課堂以外活動；課外活動**

非常直接的概括語，強調活動發生在正式課堂之外。比 after-school 更廣，可涵蓋午膳、週末、校外及假期活動。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The school encourages learning through out-of-class activities as well as lessons.

學校除課堂外，也鼓勵透過課外活動學習。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- out-of-class learning
- out-of-class activity
- out-of-class experience
- learning outside class

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡重點是活動時間、課堂之外、教育增潤、社團形式，還是整體學生發展？

## 03 Enrichment activities

**增潤活動；延伸學習活動**

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The school uses enrichment activities to broaden students' interests and skills.

學校透過增潤活動擴闊學生的興趣和技能。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- enrichment activity

## 04 Co-curricular activities

### EXAMPLE · 例句

# 02 False synonyms

以下詞語都可能發生在課堂以外或校園內，但在是否屬正式課程、是否為照顧服務、是否屬活動，以及是否只是其中一個子類方面不同。

## 01 Homework

**功課不等於課外活動**

`homework` 是正式課程指定的學習任務，即使在課堂外完成，也不是 extracurricular activity。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Homework is part of formal study; a chess club is extracurricular.

---

## 02 After-school care

**課後照顧不等於課後活動**

課後託管可能包含活動，但核心是照顧／監護服務。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

After-school care may include activities, but its primary purpose is supervision.

---

## 03 Tuition classes

**補習課不一定是 extracurricular**

補習通常延伸正式學科教學；extracurricular activities 多指非必修的發展／興趣活動。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Extra tuition supports academic study; a drama club is extracurricular.

---

## 04 School events

**學校活動比 extracurricular 更廣**

運動會、家長日、畢業禮等 school events 不一定是學生持續參與的課外活動。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A graduation ceremony is a school event, not usually an extracurricular activity.

---

## 05 Student services

**學生服務不等於活動**

輔導、住宿、行政、福利等都是 student services，但不是 activities。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Counselling is a student service; debating is a student activity.

---

## 06 Clubs

**學會只是其中一類**

很多 extracurricular activities 是 clubs，但也包括比賽、服務、演出、訓練、交流等。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A robotics club is extracurricular, but so is a one-off service project.

---

## 07 Recreation

**康樂只涵蓋休閒／活動面**

`recreation` 不一定與學校教育、學生發展或課程延伸有關。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Swimming can be recreation without being an extracurricular school activity.

---

## 08 Curriculum

**正式課程方向相反**

`curriculum` 是學校正式教授的課程；extracurricular 通常指課程之外。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Mathematics is in the curriculum; the chess club is extracurricular.

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Extra-curricular vs extracurricular

兩種拼寫都可見：

- **extracurricular**：現代英語非常常見，尤其美式及國際教育語境
- **extra-curricular**：英式傳統拼法仍常見

本課保留 headword 的 `extra-curricular`，但在 `extracurricular programmes` 中也展示閉合拼法。

## 02 After-school is narrower
不是所有 extracurricular activity 都是 after-school：

- lunchtime club
- weekend trip
- holiday programme
- Saturday competition

都可能屬 extracurricular，但不是 after-school。

## 03 Co-curricular
`co-curricular` 通常暗示活動：
- 支援學生發展
- 和教育目標有關
- 補充正式課程
- 但不屬一般 timetabled lesson

## 04 Enrichment
`enrichment` 強調：
- broaden knowledge
- develop skills
- extend interests
- challenge students
- provide wider experience

## 05 Clubs and societies
尤其適合：
- universities
- secondary schools
- student unions
- campus life

但不是所有 extracurricular 都是 club/society。

## 06 Non-academic
這個詞要小心：
- football = non-academic extracurricular
- debating = 可算 academic-related extracurricular
- coding olympiad = 明顯帶學術性

所以不能把 extracurricular 全部定義為 non-academic。

## 07 Student activities
比 extracurricular 更廣，可包括：
- orientation
- student union events
- social events
- campus campaigns
- clubs
- volunteering

## 08 Whole-person development
香港教育語境常把聯課／課外活動和：
- leadership
- communication
- service
- teamwork
- creativity
- resilience
- whole-person development

連繫起來。
