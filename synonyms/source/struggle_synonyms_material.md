# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Struggle

**有困難、覺得很難、遇到麻煩、努力應付、苦苦處理、長期抗爭——究竟是哪一種 struggle？**

本課聚焦 difficulty / effort sense：

**struggle = experience difficulty doing something, or make a strong effort to deal with a difficult situation**

核心近義與近義表達：

**have difficulty; find it hard; have trouble; have problems; strive; wrestle with; grapple with; battle with; labour**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **have difficulty; find it hard** 全部保留。  
> 本課把「單純覺得難」「實際遇到問題」「努力追求目標」「苦苦處理複雜問題」「長期抗爭」分開處理。

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Have difficulty

**有困難；感到困難**

最直接、最自然的近義表達之一，表示做某事不容易、需要付出額外努力。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Many students have difficulty understanding this concept.

很多學生理解這個概念時感到困難。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- have difficulty doing

- have difficulty with

- difficulty understanding

- difficulty accessing


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡只是覺得事情很難、出現具體問題、積極追求目標，還是在長時間處理複雜問題／情緒／疾病？

## 02 Find it hard

**覺得很難；感到難以做到**

非常自然的日常表達，強調主觀感受：某人覺得某件事很難。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- find it hard to concentrate

- find it hard to say no

- find it hard to cope

- find it hard to understand


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Try

**嘗試不等於 struggle**

try 只表示嘗試；struggle 還表示事情很難、需要很大努力。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She tried to open the door; she struggled to open it because it was stuck.

---

## 02 Strive

**努力不等於所有 struggle**

strive 多帶正面目標導向；struggle 可以只是因困難而吃力。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

They strive for excellence but struggle with limited resources.

---

## 03 Fight

**搏鬥／對抗比 struggle 更強**

fight 可以是實際衝突或積極對抗；struggle 範圍更廣。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

He fought the decision in court but struggled to understand the paperwork.

---

## 04 Suffer

**受苦不等於 struggle**

suffer 強調承受痛苦；struggle 更常表示努力應付／難以做到。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She suffered from pain and struggled to walk.

---

## 05 Fail

**失敗不是 struggle**

一個人可以 struggle 但最終成功。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

He struggled with the task but eventually completed it.

---

## 06 Difficulty

**詞性不同**

`struggle` 是動詞／名詞；`difficulty` 是名詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She struggled to focus; she had difficulty focusing.

---

## 07 Struggle / struggling

**詞形陷阱**

`struggling` 可作現在分詞／形容詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The business is struggling; it is a struggling business.

---

## 08 Struggle with / struggle to

**結構差異**

`struggle with + noun/-ing`；`struggle to + verb`。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She struggles with anxiety; she struggles to sleep.

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Struggle vs Have difficulty
`have difficulty` 是最直接的語義替換之一，偏中性。

## Struggle vs Find it hard
`find it hard` 更口語、主觀。

## Struggle vs Have trouble
`have trouble` 常見於具體操作、記憶、連接、理解等日常問題。

## Struggle vs Have problems
`have problems` 更像持續性／多項問題，不一定強調努力。

## Struggle vs Strive
`strive` 是正面目標導向的努力，不等於所有 struggle。

## Struggle vs Wrestle with
`wrestle with` 很適合決定、內疚、倫理問題等抽象難題。

## Struggle vs Grapple with
`grapple with` 更正式，適合政策、研究、城市問題、複雜限制。

## Struggle vs Battle with
`battle with` 強度更高，尤其適合長期疾病、痛苦、反覆故障。

## Struggle vs Labour
`labour` 重點是費力工作、長時間完成任務。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Struggle to + verb
- struggle to understand
- struggle to sleep
- struggle to cope

## 02 Struggle with + noun / -ing
- struggle with anxiety
- struggle with communication
- struggle with managing time

## 03 Have difficulty + -ing
- have difficulty understanding
- have difficulty accessing

通常不是：
- have difficulty to understand ✗

## 04 Find it hard to + verb
- find it hard to focus
- find it hard to say no

## 05 Wrestle / grapple / battle with
都用 `with`：
- wrestle with a decision
- grapple with a problem
- battle with illness

## 06 Other sense
`struggle` 還可表示實際扭打／搏鬥：
- struggle with an attacker

本課不把 physical-fight sense 作核心。
