# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Gradually

「逐漸／漸漸／一步一步」，究竟是哪一種 `gradually`？

慢慢發生、持續演變、逐步增加、一點一點累積、分階段推進——英文會按**變化速度、是否持續穩定、是否分成明確步驟、是否以小幅度增加、是否強調長期演變，以及語氣是日常、正式還是帶修辭色彩**選用不同的詞和表達。

> 本課主要處理 `gradually = 某種變化並非一次完成，而是在一段時間內逐步發生`。  
> `slowly`、`progressively`、`little by little` 很接近，但重點不同：`slowly` 側重速度慢；`progressively` 側重連續發展；`little by little` 側重每次改變都很小。
>
> Format: **14 curated alternatives + 8 false-synonym contrasts + 3 exercises per target synonym (42 questions total)**.  
> 每題包含自然原句、中文翻譯、六個選項、雙語正確答案、改寫句及 A–F 詳細解釋。

# 01 同義詞指南

## Gradually 的 14 種更精準說法

### 01 Slowly
**慢慢地；逐漸地**

最直接的近義詞之一，主要強調變化速度慢。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The pain slowly began to ease.

疼痛開始慢慢減輕。

**USAGE CLUE**

- speed-focused
- very common
- not always equivalent when gradual change is not especially slow

---

### 02 Progressively
**逐步地；漸進地；日益地**

強調某種變化持續向同一方向發展，常見於正式、醫療、技術或分析語境。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The condition became progressively more difficult to manage.

病況逐漸變得更難控制。

**USAGE CLUE**

- continuous development
- formal
- often with comparative forms: progressively more/less

---

### 03 Little by little
**一點一點地；逐漸地**

日常而自然，強調每次改變都不大，但累積後形成明顯結果。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Little by little, she became more confident.

她一點一點變得更有自信。

**USAGE CLUE**

- small repeated changes
- conversational
- cumulative effect

---

### 04 Steadily
**穩步地；持續地**

強調變化持續、規律且沒有太大中斷，未必表示速度很慢。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Sales have risen steadily for six months.

銷售額六個月來一直穩步上升。

**USAGE CLUE**

- continuous, regular trend
- data / performance / growth
- not necessarily slow

---

### 05 Incrementally
**逐步小幅地；按增量地**

正式、技術性較高，表示每次只增加或改變一小部分。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The software was improved incrementally over several releases.

軟件經數次版本逐步小幅改進。

**USAGE CLUE**

- small increments
- technical / business / policy
- precise and formal

---

### 06 Step by step
**一步一步地；逐步地**

強調按清楚步驟、次序或階段推進，而不只是「速度慢」。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The team rebuilt the process step by step.

團隊一步一步重建流程。

**USAGE CLUE**

- ordered sequence
- procedural
- each stage follows another

---

### 07 Bit by bit
**一點一點地；逐步地**

和 `little by little` 很接近，語氣較口語，常表示逐小部分完成或改變。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Bit by bit, the old building was restored.

舊建築一點一點被修復。

**USAGE CLUE**

- small portions
- informal-neutral
- physical or abstract progress

---

### 08 Over time
**隨着時間；逐漸地**

強調變化在一段時間內形成，而不特別說每次改變的大小。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Her views changed over time.

她的看法隨着時間逐漸改變。

**USAGE CLUE**

- long-term development
- neutral
- time-span focused

---

### 09 By degrees
**逐漸地；漸次地**

較正式或書面，表示變化以一連串小幅度逐步發生。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> By degrees, the tension in the room began to ease.

房間裡的緊張氣氛逐漸緩和。

**USAGE CLUE**

- formal/literary
- gradual small changes
- less common in conversation

---

### 10 In stages
**分階段地；逐步地**

表示整個變化或計劃不是一次完成，而是分成若干明確階段。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The new system will be introduced in stages.

新系統將分階段引入。

**USAGE CLUE**

- implementation / rollout
- distinct phases
- not simply slow change

---

### 11 In small increments
**以小幅度逐步地；逐次小量地**

解釋性、精確，表示每一次變化量都很小。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The dosage was increased in small increments.

劑量以小幅度逐步增加。

**USAGE CLUE**

- measurable small changes
- medicine / engineering / finance
- quantity-focused

---

### 12 Slowly but surely
**緩慢但穩定地；一步步終會完成地**

慣用語，表示雖然進展不快，但持續向成功或完成推進。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Slowly but surely, the team reduced the backlog.

團隊雖然進展緩慢，但穩步減少了積壓工作。

**USAGE CLUE**

- idiomatic
- slow + reliable progress
- implies confidence in eventual progress

---

### 13 As time went on
**隨着時間過去；漸漸地**

敘事性表達，表示情況隨時間推移逐漸改變。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> As time went on, he became less worried.

隨着時間過去，他漸漸沒那麼擔心。

**USAGE CLUE**

- narrative
- past-time development
- often with past tense

---

### 14 At a gradual pace
**以漸進的速度；逐步地**

較正式、解釋性，直接表示變化以不突然的速度發生。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The economy continued to recover at a gradual pace.

經濟繼續以漸進速度復甦。

**USAGE CLUE**

- formal
- pace-focused
- often in reports

# 02 False synonyms

## 01 gradually / slowly

### slowly 主要講速度，gradually 主要講變化方式

一件事可以 slowly 發生但不一定是逐步變化；`gradually` 更強調不是突然完成。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The train moved slowly.  
> The weather gradually became warmer.

---

## 02 gradually / steadily

### steadily 強調持續穩定

`steadily` 可以相當快，只要趨勢穩定；`gradually` 則更偏漸進。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Sales rose steadily throughout the year.  
> The colour gradually faded.

---

## 03 gradually / progressively

### progressively 強調持續朝同一方向發展

`progressively` 常見於 `progressively more/less`，比普通 `gradually` 更正式。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The symptoms gradually improved.  
> The task became progressively more difficult.

---

## 04 gradually / eventually

### eventually 是「最後」，不是「逐漸」

`eventually` 指最終結果何時到來；它不描述過程是否漸進。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The noise gradually faded.  
> The noise eventually stopped completely.

---

## 05 gradually / suddenly

### suddenly 是直接反義

`suddenly` 表示變化突然發生；`gradually` 表示變化分散在一段時間內。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The temperature gradually fell overnight.  
> The lights suddenly went out.

---

## 06 gradually / step by step

### step by step 要有明確步驟

所有 step-by-step progress 都是逐步的，但不是所有 gradual change 都有可辨認的階段。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> Her eyesight gradually worsened.  
> The technician repaired the system step by step.

---

## 07 gradually / increasingly

### increasingly 表示程度愈來愈高

`increasingly` 常修飾形容詞，如 `increasingly difficult`；它不是一般「逐漸」的自由替代。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The room gradually became darker.  
> The problem became increasingly difficult to ignore.

---

## 08 gradual / gradually

### 詞性陷阱

`gradual` 是形容詞；`gradually` 是副詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> There was a gradual improvement.  
> Conditions gradually improved.

# 03 練習與解釋

## 練習 01.1

**Target word: Slowly · 慢慢地；逐漸地**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> The door gradually opened as the old mechanism turned.

**中文翻譯**

舊機械裝置轉動時，門慢慢打開。

A. slowly  
B. progressively  
C. in stages  
D. eventually  
E. suddenly  
F. gradual

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**slowly · 慢慢地；逐漸地**

> The door slowly opened as the old mechanism turned.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. slowly ✓**  
句子描述的是門移動的速度，而不是多個發展階段；`slowly opened` 是最自然的速度副詞搭配。

**B. progressively**  
通常表示某種狀態持續向同一方向發展，如 progressively worse；對單次開門動作過於分析化。

**C. in stages**  
需要可辨認的階段，例如先開四分之一、再開一半；原句只說轉動很慢。

**D. eventually**  
表示「最後打開」，不描述打開過程的速度。

**E. suddenly**  
表示突然，與機械慢慢轉動的語境相反。

**F. gradual**  
是形容詞，不能直接修飾動詞 `opened`。

# 05 高混淆組

## gradually vs slowly
- **gradually**：重點是變化不是一次完成。
- **slowly**：重點是速度慢。

## gradually vs progressively
- **gradually**：一般逐漸變化。
- **progressively**：持續朝同一方向發展，常配 comparative。

## gradually vs steadily
- **gradually**：漸進。
- **steadily**：持續而規律，未必特別慢。

## little by little vs bit by bit
- **little by little**：常用於抽象狀態與累積改善。
- **bit by bit**：常突出逐小部分完成，但兩者高度重疊。

## gradually vs step by step
- **gradually**：不一定有清楚階段。
- **step by step**：強調明確先後次序。

## gradually vs in stages
- **gradually**：變化方式。
- **in stages**：實施被切成明確階段。

## incrementally vs in small increments
- **incrementally**：正式副詞。
- **in small increments**：更明確說每次變化量很小。

## gradually vs eventually
- **gradually**：描述過程。
- **eventually**：描述最終結果。

# 08 生成／評估注意事項

1. 本課主要處理 `gradually = 在一段時間內逐步變化，而不是一次完成`。
2. 固定為 **14 alternatives + 3 questions each = 42 exercises**。
3. 每題包含：
   - Target word / phrase + Chinese gloss
   - READ THE CONTEXT
   - 中文翻譯
   - 六個選項
   - `CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案`
   - English target + Chinese gloss
   - 正確改寫
   - A–F 個別解釋
4. A–F feedback 優先採用：**詞義／詞性 → 正常搭配／變化機制 → 本題為何成功或失敗**。
5. 使用者提供的核心：
   - slowly = 速度慢
   - progressively = 持續朝同一方向發展
   - little by little = 小幅累積
6. `slowly` 不能機械等同所有 gradually；例如某指標可 gradually 變化但速度未必「慢」。
7. `progressively` 特別適合 `progressively more/less + adjective`。
8. `steadily` 必須有 regular / continuous / no large interruption 等趨勢線索。
9. `incrementally` 與 `in small increments` 應有 measurable small changes。
10. `step by step` 必須有 ordered procedure / clear sequence。
11. `in stages` 必須有 distinct rollout/implementation phases。
12. `bit by bit` / `little by little` 都是小幅累積，但前者更常帶逐小部分完成感。
13. `over time` 強調時間跨度本身，不必指定每次變化大小。
14. `by degrees` 是較正式／書面表達，不適合所有日常語境。
15. `slowly but surely` 不只表示慢，還暗示穩定而可靠的進展，因此不能用於惡化或無方向波動的所有 gradual change。
16. `as time went on` 特別適合 past-tense narrative development。
17. `at a gradual pace` 偏報告／分析語體，常用於 recovery / growth / increase。
18. False synonyms 特別測試：
   - gradually vs slowly
   - gradually vs steadily
   - gradually vs progressively
   - gradually vs eventually
   - gradually vs suddenly
   - gradually vs increasingly
19. 本組加入 morphology / word-class traps：
   - gradual / gradually
   - slow / slowly / slowness
   - progressive / progressively / progression
   - incremental / incrementally / increment
   - steady / steadily / steadiness
20. Distractors 優先使用真正容易混淆的近鄰：
   - slowly
   - progressively
   - steadily
   - eventually
   - step by step
   - in stages
   - increasingly
21. 原句只在 `gradually` 自然時使用；若真正語義是慢速運動、穩定趨勢、明確階段或小幅定量增加，則由上下文引導更精確替代。
22. 真正進階辨析應直接比較：
   - gradually / slowly
   - gradually / progressively
   - gradually / steadily
   - little by little / bit by bit
   - gradually / step by step / in stages
   - incrementally / in small increments
   - gradually / eventually
23. `gradually` 本身仍是最廣泛、最自然的核心副詞；其他替代語主要增加「速度、連續性、增量大小、階段結構、時間跨度、規律程度與語體」上的精度。
