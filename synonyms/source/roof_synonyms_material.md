# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Roof

**頂部覆蓋物、上方結構、覆蓋層、頂篷、遮蔽、屋頂結構、屋面材料、頂部——究竟是哪一種 roof？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**top covering; overhead structure; covering; canopy; shelter; roof structure; roofing; top**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **8 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **top covering; overhead structure** 全部保留。  
> 本課聚焦 `roof = the upper covering/structure of a building, vehicle, shelter, or similar object`，並明確分開 **完整屋頂、承重結構、屋面材料、輕型遮篷、遮蔽功能、一般頂部**。

---

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Top Covering

**頂部覆蓋物；屋頂覆蓋層**

最直接、最透明的描述性改寫。當 `roof` 指建築物、車輛或其他結構最上方用來遮蔽和保護的部分時很合適。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The top covering protects the house from rain and direct sunlight.

屋頂保護房屋免受雨水和陽光直接照射。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- top covering of a building

- protective top covering

- waterproof top covering

- metal top covering

## 02 Overhead Structure

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The overhead structure is supported by steel beams.

屋頂結構由鋼樑支撐。

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Ceiling

**天花板不是屋頂**

ceiling 是室內看到的頂面；roof 是建築最外層上方結構。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Rain damaged the roof, but the ceiling inside also became stained.

---

## 02 Roofing

**材料不等於整個 roof**

roofing 指瓦片、金屬板、防水層等屋面材料／系統。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The roofing was replaced, but the roof structure remained.

---

## 03 Canopy

**只適合某些輕型／伸出遮篷**

canopy 常在入口、站台、露台等半開放空間。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A glass canopy covers the entrance.

---

## 04 Shelter

**功能詞，不是形體詞**

shelter 表示保護作用，可由 roof、tent、tree 等提供。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The roof provides shelter from rain.

---

## 05 Top

**範圍更廣**

車、箱、貨櫃可說 top；正式建築 roof 通常不能只用 top 取代。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The top of the container was covered in snow.

---

## 06 Roof structure

**工程語義更窄**

指樑架、承重、跨度等構造，而不是瓦片或屋面材料。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Engineers reinforced the roof structure.

---

## 07 Roof / rooftop

**高價值區別**

roof = 屋頂整體；rooftop = 屋頂表面／屋頂上的空間。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The roof needs repair; they held a party on the rooftop.

---

## 08 Roof

**其他 senses**

`roof of the mouth` 指上顎／口腔頂部；本課聚焦建築／物體頂部覆蓋 sense。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The roof of the mouth is a different anatomical sense.

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Roof vs Ceiling
- roof = 建築外部最上方結構
- ceiling = 室內天花板

## Roof vs Roofing
- roof = 整個屋頂
- roofing = 屋面材料／覆蓋層

## Roof vs Roof structure
後者特別涉及：
- beams
- load
- reinforcement
- span
- structural damage

## Roof vs Canopy
`canopy` 特別自然於：
- entrances
- bus stops
- platforms
- terraces
- outdoor seating

## Roof vs Shelter
`shelter` 說的是功能：
**保護人／物免受天氣影響**。

## Roof vs Top
`top` 最適合：
- car
- box
- container
- general object

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Roof plural
一般複數：
- roofs

## 02 Roof vs rooftop
- roof = 屋頂整體
- rooftop = 屋頂表面／屋頂上的地方

例如：
- repair the roof
- a rooftop garden

## 03 Roofing
通常是不可數／材料類概念：
- metal roofing
- roofing material

## 04 Canopy
不是完整 building roof 的一般替換；通常較輕、較局部。

## 05 Other sense excluded
- roof of the mouth = 口腔頂部／上顎

本課不混入解剖學 sense。
