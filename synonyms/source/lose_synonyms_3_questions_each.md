# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Lose

**`lose` 是高度多義詞。它可以表示不再擁有、放錯物件、因規則而喪失、主動放棄、被剝奪、失去興趣、減重／脫落，或在競賽中落敗。這些意思不能用同一組近義詞機械互換。**

核心替代表達：

`cease to have / cease to possess / misplace / forfeit / relinquish / surrender / let go of / give up / be deprived of / become less interested in / shed / be defeated`

> **核心原則**：先判斷 lose 屬於哪一個 sense：**possession, temporary location, penalty, voluntary surrender, external deprivation, declining interest, physical reduction, or defeat**。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Cease to have

**不再擁有；失去**

正式而直接，表示原本擁有某物、某權利、某能力或某特徵，但之後不再擁有。比 `lose` 更書面、較少情緒色彩。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The company may cease to have access to the data after the contract ends.

合約結束後，公司可能不再擁有存取該數據的權限。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a possession, right, ability, or state no longer remains**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、法律、行政

---

## 02 Cease to possess

**不再持有；失去所有權**

比 `cease to have` 更正式，特別適合財產、權利、資格或法律上的持有關係；日常英語較少用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The organisation ceased to possess the property after the transfer.

轉讓後，該機構不再持有該物業。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **formal loss of possession or ownership**

**REGISTER · 語域**

法律、正式、書面

---

## 03 Misplace

**放錯地方；一時找不到**

當 `lose` 指把物件放在某處後找不到，但東西未必真正失去時，`misplace` 最精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> I misplaced my keys and found them under the sofa later.

我把鎖匙放錯地方，之後在梳化底找到。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **temporarily losing track of an object's location**

**REGISTER · 語域**

日常、一般

---

## 04 Forfeit

**喪失；被取消；因違規或條件而失去**

表示因自己的行為、違規、失敗或未符合條件而失去權利、資格、按金、獎勵或機會。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Players who break the rules may forfeit their prize money.

違規的參賽者可能喪失獎金。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **losing a right, benefit, or possession as a penalty or consequence**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、法律、體育

---

## 05 Relinquish

**放棄；交出；不再保留**

較正式，通常表示自願或正式地放棄控制權、職位、權利、所有權或責任。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She relinquished control of the company to a new management team.

她把公司控制權交給新的管理團隊。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **voluntarily giving up possession, control, or a right**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、商業、法律

---

## 06 Surrender

**交出；放棄；喪失控制**

表示把某物、權利、控制權或地位交出去，常帶被迫、戰敗、壓力或正式交還的意味。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The tenant had to surrender the keys at the end of the lease.

租約結束時，租客必須交還鎖匙。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **giving up possession or control, often under pressure or obligation**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、法律、一般

---

## 07 Let go of

**放手；放下；不再抓住／執着**

較口語，可指物理上鬆手，也可指情緒上放下某段關係、想法或過去；比一般 `lose` 更有主動放開意味。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He finally let go of the anger he had carried for years.

他終於放下多年來一直懷有的憤怒。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **releasing physical or emotional hold**

**REGISTER · 語域**

口語、一般、心理

---

## 08 Give up

**放棄；不再繼續擁有／追求**

非常常用，表示主動停止追求、使用、保留或嘗試某事；和 `lose` 只有在『主動放棄而失去』的語境中重疊。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She gave up her seat so an older passenger could sit down.

她讓出座位給一名年長乘客。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **voluntarily stopping or surrendering something**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域；較口語

---

## 09 Be deprived of

**被剝奪；被奪去**

當 `lose` 是因外力、制度、懲罰或他人行動而失去權利、自由、資源或機會時非常精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The workers were deprived of access to basic safety equipment.

工人被剝奪使用基本安全設備的機會。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **losing something because it is taken away by an external force**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、法律、社會

---

## 10 Become less interested in

**變得較不感興趣；失去興趣**

專門對應 `lose interest in`。它不是一般『失去某物』的同義詞，而是描述興趣、熱情或注意力逐漸減弱。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Some students become less interested in the subject when lessons feel repetitive.

當課堂變得重複時，有些學生會對該科目變得較不感興趣。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **interest or enthusiasm gradually decreases**

**REGISTER · 語域**

中性、教育、一般

---

## 11 Shed

**減去；甩掉；失去**

當 `lose` 指減掉體重、脫落毛髮、葉子、皮層或擺脫某些負擔時很自然；它不是一般 possession 的同義詞。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He shed five kilograms after changing his diet.

他改變飲食後減掉五公斤。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **losing weight or naturally dropping an outer layer**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、健康、自然科學

---

## 12 Be defeated

**落敗；輸掉**

當 `lose` 指在比賽、選舉、戰役或競爭中輸給對手時，`be defeated` 是較正式的替代表達。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The team was defeated in the final.

球隊在決賽落敗。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **losing a contest, match, election, or battle**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、體育、新聞

# 02 False synonyms

## Lose / Misplace
`misplace` 只表示暫時不知道物件放在哪裡；東西通常仍然存在，而且之後可能找回。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
I lost my wallet and never found it.  
I misplaced my wallet, then found it under the seat.

## Lose / Forfeit
`forfeit` 有明確的規則／條件／懲罰原因。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
You can lose a ticket accidentally.  
You forfeit a deposit because you break the cancellation terms.

## Lose / Relinquish
`relinquish` 通常是較正式、主動地放棄權利、控制權或持有。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A director may relinquish control voluntarily.  
A person may lose control because circumstances change.

## Lose / Surrender
`surrender` 比 relinquish 更常帶被迫、義務、戰敗或正式交還意味。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
She relinquished her role voluntarily.  
He surrendered his passport when ordered by the court.

## Lose / Be deprived of
`be deprived of` 表示外力把某物奪走，主語不是主動放棄。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Workers may give up a benefit voluntarily.  
Workers are deprived of a benefit when someone else takes it away.

## Lose / Become less interested in
只適用於 `lose interest / enthusiasm / attention` 一類心理變化。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
He lost interest in the hobby.  
He became less interested in the hobby.

## Lose / Shed
`shed` 特別適合體重和自然脫落。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
She lost five kilograms.  
She shed five kilograms.

## Lose / Be defeated
`be defeated` 只表示競賽／選舉／戰役中輸掉。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The team lost the final.  
The team was defeated in the final.

## 練習 01.1

**Target phrase: Cease to have · 不再擁有；失去**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> After the licence expires, the company will lose the right to operate the service.

**中文翻譯**

牌照到期後，公司將不再擁有營運該服務的權利。

| | 選項 |
|---|---|
| A | Cease to have |
| B | Cease to possess |
| C | Be deprived of |
| D | Relinquish |
| E | Retain |
| F | Acquire |

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**A. Cease to have · 不再擁有；失去**

> After the licence expires, the company will cease to have the right to operate the service.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. Cease to have ✓**  
句子表示某項權利、存取權或正式狀態在某時點後不再存在；`cease to have` 是中性正式的精準改寫。

**B. Cease to possess**  
`cease to possess`：偏法律或正式的持有／所有權終止。這句線索是「權利到期後不再存在」；若不是正式 possession/ownership，而是興趣、比賽、情緒或暫時找不到物件，就不合。

**C. Be deprived of**  
`be deprived of`：表示某物由外力、制度或他人奪去。這句線索是「權利到期後不再存在」；若是自己主動放棄或只是興趣下降，就不是『被剝奪』。

**D. Relinquish**  
`relinquish`：正式、自願地交出控制權、權利或持有。這句線索是「權利到期後不再存在」；若是被迫交出、被制度剝奪或只是意外失去，不夠準確。

**E. Retain**  
`Retain`：retain = 保留；和失去／放棄相反。 本句線索是「權利到期後不再存在」，所以這個選項的方向、主動性、語域或語義類型與句子要求不符。

**F. Acquire**  
`Acquire`：acquire = 取得；方向和 cease to have/possess 相反。 本句線索是「權利到期後不再存在」，所以這個選項的方向、主動性、語域或語義類型與句子要求不符。

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Lose vs Misplace** — lose 可永久；misplace 通常只是暫時找不到。
- **Forfeit vs Be deprived of** — forfeit 常因自己的行為／條件；be deprived of 是外力奪去。
- **Relinquish vs Surrender** — relinquish 可自願；surrender 更常帶壓力、義務或戰敗。
- **Give up vs Relinquish** — give up 較日常；relinquish 較正式，常配 rights / control / claim。
- **Let go of vs Give up** — let go of 偏鬆手／放下；give up 偏停止保留或追求。
- **Cease to have vs Cease to possess** — possess 更正式、更偏法律持有。
- **Lose interest vs Become less interested in** — 後者是透明改寫，只適合 interest 這一 sense。
- **Lose weight vs Shed weight** — shed 很自然但帶較口語／媒體色彩。
- **Lose a match vs Be defeated** — be defeated 更正式，必須有競爭勝負關係。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Lose / lost / lost

> lose access  
> lost access  
> have lost access

## Misplace

> misplace your keys  
> misplaced the receipt

不要把 `misplace` 用於抽象權利或比賽：

> misplace your legal rights ✗

## Forfeit

直接接受詞：

> forfeit a deposit  
> forfeit the right to compete

## Relinquish / surrender

> relinquish control  
> surrender control  
> surrender a passport

通常不需要 `of`。

## Let go of

固定要有 `of`：

> let go of the rope  
> let go of anger

## Be deprived of

固定結構：

> be deprived of freedom  
> be deprived of access

## Interested

> become less interested **in** something

## Defeated

> be defeated **by** an opponent  
> be defeated **in** the final
