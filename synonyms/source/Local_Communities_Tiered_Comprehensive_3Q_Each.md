# LOCAL COMMUNITIES · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Local communities

**「本地社群／地區社區」，要先分清是在談附近居民整體、正式社區團體、居民協會，還是項目諮詢中的本地持份者。**

本單元集中處理 **local communities = people and social groups living in, belonging to, or organising within a particular local area**。你提供的 **nearby communities; neighbourhood groups** 分別代表 **地理上鄰近的社群** 和 **組織化的鄰里團體**。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且高實用度的詞／表達，共 **39 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。同時清楚區分 **local communities / local residents / community groups / residents' associations / local stakeholders / local population**，避免把『居民』、『組織』、『持份者』和『人口統計』混在一起。

## Tier 1 — Core nearby / resident / community-group equivalents · 核心鄰近社群、居民與社區團體近義詞

### 01 Nearby communities

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：附近社群；鄰近社區  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：communities located close to a particular place or project  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，當 local communities 強調地理上接近某地點時最直接。  
**常見搭配**：nearby communities; nearby residents; neighbouring communities; surrounding communities; communities nearby.  
**常見搭配對象**：village, town, project, facility, development.  
**與 local communities 的辨析**：Nearby communities 強調距離接近；local communities 可包括更廣泛的本地居民與群體。  
**使用限制**：若重點是正式社區組織，community groups 更精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The project will create jobs for nearby communities.  
該項目將為附近社群創造就業機會。

### 02 Neighbourhood groups

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：社區團體；鄰里組織  
**語域**：neutral / community  
**核心語義**：groups of residents or organisations active within a particular neighbourhood  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，當 local communities 實際指地方居民組織、街坊團體或鄰里組織時自然。  
**常見搭配**：neighbourhood groups; residents' groups; community groups; neighbourhood association; local group.  
**常見搭配對象**：resident, volunteer, district, neighbourhood, campaign.  
**與 local communities 的辨析**：Neighbourhood groups 指組織／團體；local communities 可指整個居民群體。  
**使用限制**：若不是正式或半正式團體，不宜把整個社區都稱為 groups。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Neighbourhood groups helped organise the clean-up campaign.  
社區團體協助組織清潔活動。

### 03 Community groups

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：社區團體；地區組織  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：organised groups of local people working around shared interests, needs, or activities  
**用法提示**：在政策、慈善、教育、社會服務和居民參與語境中很自然。  
**常見搭配**：community groups; local community groups; voluntary groups; community organisations; grassroots groups.  
**常見搭配對象**：resident, volunteer, charity, campaign, service.  
**與 local communities 的辨析**：Community groups 是 local communities 中的組織化部分。  
**使用限制**：不要用它代替沒有組織形式的居民整體。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Community groups were invited to comment on the proposal.  
社區團體獲邀就建議發表意見。

### 04 Local residents

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：本地居民；區內居民  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：people who live in the area being discussed  
**用法提示**：當 local communities 實際指『住在這裏的人』時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：local residents; nearby residents; residents of the area; local people; resident concerns.  
**常見搭配對象**：neighbourhood, district, village, development, consultation.  
**與 local communities 的辨析**：Local residents 明確指人；local communities 可包括機構、團體和共同體。  
**使用限制**：若要涵蓋商戶、學校、社團等，local stakeholders 可能更廣。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Local residents raised concerns about traffic noise.  
本地居民對交通噪音表示憂慮。

### 05 Local people

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：本地人；當地居民  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：people who live in or belong to a particular area  
**用法提示**：比 local residents 稍口語，適合一般敘述、旅遊、發展和社會議題。  
**常見搭配**：local people; support local people; involve local people; local people's views; benefit local people.  
**常見搭配對象**：village, town, district, region, project.  
**與 local communities 的辨析**：Local people 更口語、更廣泛；residents 稍正式。  
**使用限制**：正式規劃文件常用 local residents / communities。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The programme was designed with input from local people.  
該計劃在本地居民參與下設計。

## Tier 2 — Surrounding / organised / stakeholder variants · 周邊社群、組織化居民與持份者變體

### 06 Surrounding communities

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：周邊社群；周邊社區  
**語域**：neutral / planning / environmental  
**核心語義**：communities located around a particular site, facility, or development  
**用法提示**：當項目、設施、工廠、機場等對周邊地區產生影響時很常見。  
**常見搭配**：surrounding communities; surrounding area; neighbouring villages; surrounding neighbourhoods; community impact.  
**常見搭配對象**：factory, airport, project, site, development.  
**與 local communities 的辨析**：Surrounding communities 比 local communities 更強調『圍繞某中心點』。  
**使用限制**：如果沒有明確中心地點，local communities 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The factory works with surrounding communities on environmental issues.  
工廠與周邊社群合作處理環境議題。

### 07 Neighbouring communities

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：鄰近社群；相鄰社區  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：communities directly next to or close to one another  
**用法提示**：當兩個或多個相鄰地區之間需要比較、合作或共享資源時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：neighbouring communities; neighbouring villages; neighbouring districts; cross-community cooperation; shared services.  
**常見搭配對象**：village, district, town, region, border.  
**與 local communities 的辨析**：Neighbouring 強調彼此相鄰；nearby 只需距離近。  
**使用限制**：大型城市內較抽象的群體關係未必適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Neighbouring communities share the same health clinic.  
鄰近社群共用同一所健康診所。

### 08 Residents' associations

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：居民協會；業主／住戶組織  
**語域**：formal / civic  
**核心語義**：organised associations representing residents in a neighbourhood, building, or local area  
**用法提示**：當 local communities 具體指居民代表組織時非常精準。  
**常見搭配**：residents' association; local residents' group; tenants' association; neighbourhood association; residents' committee.  
**常見搭配對象**：estate, building, district, street, neighbourhood.  
**與 local communities 的辨析**：這是 formal organised body，不是整體社區。  
**使用限制**：沒有正式組織的居民群體不能一概稱 association。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The residents' association met the council to discuss parking.  
居民協會與市議會會面討論泊車問題。

### 09 Grassroots organisations

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：基層組織；草根組織  
**語域**：formal / civic / NGO  
**核心語義**：locally based organisations created and driven by ordinary members of a community  
**用法提示**：當 local communities 強調由下而上、居民主導的參與時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：grassroots organisation; grassroots movement; community-led group; local advocacy group; grassroots network.  
**常見搭配對象**：resident, volunteer, campaign, community, advocacy.  
**與 local communities 的辨析**：Grassroots organisations 比 community groups 更強調居民主導和由下而上。  
**使用限制**：政府部門或大型企業不能稱為 grassroots organisations。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Grassroots organisations played a major role in the relief effort.  
基層組織在救援工作中扮演重要角色。

### 10 Local stakeholders

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：本地持份者；地區相關人士／團體  
**語域**：formal / policy / planning  
**核心語義**：people and organisations in a local area who are affected by or have an interest in a decision or project  
**用法提示**：當 local communities 需要涵蓋居民、商戶、學校、社團、服務機構等多方時最實用。  
**常見搭配**：local stakeholders; stakeholder consultation; engage stakeholders; community stakeholders; local stakeholder groups.  
**常見搭配對象**：resident, business, school, NGO, authority.  
**與 local communities 的辨析**：Local stakeholders 比 local communities 更偏決策參與和利益關係。  
**使用限制**：若只是單純指居民，local residents 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The council consulted local stakeholders before approving the plan.  
市議會在批准計劃前諮詢本地持份者。

## Tier 3 — Statistical / individual-member / collective-community variants · 人口統計、個別成員與集合社區變體

### 11 Local population

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：本地人口；當地居民人口  
**語域**：formal / statistical  
**核心語義**：the people living in a particular area considered as a population  
**用法提示**：當 local communities 出現在統計、醫療、規劃和人口研究中可用。  
**常見搭配**：local population; population needs; serve the local population; population growth; population profile.  
**常見搭配對象**：district, town, health service, region, census.  
**與 local communities 的辨析**：Local population 是統計集合概念；communities 更強調社會關係與共同體。  
**使用限制**：社區參與和身份認同語境中會顯得過於統計化。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The clinic serves a local population of around 30,000.  
這間診所服務約三萬名本地人口。

### 12 Community members

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：社區成員；社群成員  
**語域**：neutral / civic  
**核心語義**：individual people who belong to or participate in a particular community  
**用法提示**：當 local communities 需要聚焦社區內個別成員時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：community members; involve community members; community participation; community voices; active members.  
**常見搭配對象**：resident, volunteer, participant, family, local group.  
**與 local communities 的辨析**：Community members 是個體；local communities 是集合體。  
**使用限制**：若談整體社區影響，不必逐一說 members。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Community members helped design the new public space.  
社區成員協助設計新的公共空間。

### 13 The local community

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：本地社區；當地社群  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：the people and social groups living in a particular local area considered collectively  
**用法提示**：這是 local communities 的單數集合表達，當只指一個地區時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：the local community; support the local community; community engagement; community needs; benefit the community.  
**常見搭配對象**：resident, school, business, charity, service.  
**與 local communities 的辨析**：單數 the local community 常指一個共同體；複數 local communities 可比較或涵蓋多個地區。  
**使用限制**：若實際涉及多個相鄰地區，應使用複數 communities。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The library plays an important role in the local community.  
圖書館在本地社區中扮演重要角色。

## 04 False synonyms / local-community traps · 容易誤選的詞與社區概念陷阱

### 01 Local area

**本地區域不等於 local communities**  
local area 是地理範圍；communities 是人和社會群體。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Area is a place; community is people.

### 02 Neighbourhood

**社區／街區不等於所有 communities**  
neighbourhood 可指地理街區，也可指社區，但語義更偏地點。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Neighbourhood is often place-focused.

### 03 Residents

**居民不等於所有 local communities**  
residents 只指住戶，不包括商戶、學校或組織。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Residents are one part of a community.

### 04 Stakeholders

**持份者不等於所有 community members**  
stakeholders 因某決策或項目而受影響／有利益關係。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Stakeholders are interest-based.

### 05 Population

**人口不等於 community**  
population 是統計集合；community 強調關係、身份和互動。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Population is demographic, community is social.

### 06 Community groups

**community groups 只是組織化的一部分**  
整個 local community 不一定全部屬於任何 group。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Groups are organised subsets.

### 07 Nearby communities

**nearby 只強調距離接近**  
local communities 可以是行政／社會上本地但不一定緊鄰。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Nearby is distance-focused.

### 08 Community / communal

**詞形陷阱**  
community 是名詞；communal 是形容詞，常表示共用／集體。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Community is a noun; communal is an adjective.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Nearby communities · 附近社群；鄰近社區

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The new hospital will serve local communities close to the site.

**中文翻譯**  
新醫院將服務場址附近的社群。

- A. nearby communities
- B. local area
- C. population only
- D. community groups only
- E. stakeholders
- F. neighbourhood

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**nearby communities** · 附近社群；鄰近社區

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The new hospital will serve nearby communities.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. nearby communities ✓ — close to the site directly signals nearby communities。**
- **B. local area — local area is a place, not people。**
- **C. population only — population only is statistical and less social。**
- **D. community groups only — community groups only excludes unorganised residents。**
- **E. stakeholders — stakeholders are defined by interest in a decision。**
- **F. neighbourhood — neighbourhood is usually a place or one local district.**

| Local communities 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 地理上附近的社群 | **nearby communities** | 是否 close to a site / facility / project？ |
| 鄰里組織 | **neighbourhood groups** | 是否 organised resident groups in one neighbourhood？ |
| 一般社區組織 | **community groups** | 是否 local organisations running activities/campaigns？ |
| 住在區內的人 | **local residents** | 是否 people who live there？ |
| 一般本地人 | **local people** | 是否 broad everyday reference to people from the area？ |
| 圍繞某中心地點 | **surrounding communities** | 是否 around a factory / airport / project？ |
| 彼此相鄰的社群 | **neighbouring communities** | 是否 directly next to one another？ |
| 正式居民代表組織 | **residents' associations** | 是否 elected/formal resident body？ |
| 由下而上的地方組織 | **grassroots organisations** | 是否 community-led / volunteer-led / bottom-up？ |
| 決策中的本地相關方 | **local stakeholders** | 是否 residents + businesses + schools + NGOs？ |
| 統計人口集合 | **local population** | 是否 numbers / age profile / demographic change？ |

| 單一集合社區 | **the local community** | 是否 one area's people considered collectively？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **local communities vs nearby communities**: nearby 更強調地理距離接近。
- **local communities vs local residents**: residents 只指居住者本人。
- **local communities vs community groups**: community groups 是組織化的子集合。
- **local communities vs residents' associations**: 後者是正式／半正式居民代表組織。
- **local communities vs local stakeholders**: stakeholders 由某項目／決策中的利益或影響關係定義。
- **local communities vs local population**: population 是人口統計集合；community 是社會共同體。
- **local communities vs neighbourhood**: neighbourhood 常偏地理街區。
- **local communities vs local people**: local people 更口語、更直接指人。
- **local communities vs surrounding communities**: surrounding 強調圍繞某一中心地點。
- **local communities vs community members**: members 是個體；communities 是集合。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & local-community traps · 文法、搭配與社區概念陷阱

- **community** 可數：a local community / several local communities。
- **the local community** 常用於單一地區作集合概念。
- **local residents** = 住在當地的人；不一定加入任何社區組織。
- **community groups / neighbourhood groups** 通常是有組織的居民團體。
- **residents' association** 是正式／半正式居民代表組織；注意複數所有格 residents'。
- **grassroots organisation** 強調由下而上、居民／義工主導。
- **local stakeholders** 常見於 planning / consultation / policy 文件。
- **local population** 常見於統計、醫療規劃和人口研究。
- **nearby / neighbouring / surrounding communities** 都與地理關係有關，但角度不同。
- **nearby** = 距離近；**neighbouring** = 彼此相鄰；**surrounding** = 圍繞某中心。
- **community member** 是個別人；**community group** 是組織；**community** 是整體共同體。
- **local area ≠ local community**：前者是地方，後者是人。
