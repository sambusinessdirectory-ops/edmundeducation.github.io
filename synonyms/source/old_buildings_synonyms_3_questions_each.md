# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Old buildings

**`old buildings` 很廣：可以只是樓齡較高，也可以是具有歷史意義、文化遺產價值、某時期建築特色、傳統風格，或因年齡而出現維修問題的樓宇。這些意思需要用不同替代表達。**

核心替代表達：

`historic structures / older properties / historic buildings / heritage buildings / period buildings / older structures / ageing buildings / traditional buildings`

> **核心原則**：先判斷 `old` 是在說 **age alone, historical significance, heritage value, period style, structural age, deterioration, or traditional architecture**。

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Historic structures

**歷史建築；歷史結構物**

當 `old buildings` 不只是年代久遠，而是具有歷史價值、與重要時期或事件有關時，`historic structures` 很自然。`structures` 比 `buildings` 稍廣，也可包括塔、橋、城牆等。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The city is restoring several historic structures in the old district.

城市正在修復舊區數座歷史建築。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **old structures valued for their historical significance**

**REGISTER · 語域**

保育、規劃、正式

---

## 02 Older properties

**較舊物業；舊樓物業**

當 `old buildings` 出現在房地產、維修、保險或物業管理語境時，`older properties` 很自然。它只表示樓齡較高，不一定有歷史或保育價值。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Older properties may require more maintenance.

較舊物業可能需要更多維修。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **properties that are older in age, especially in real-estate contexts**

**REGISTER · 語域**

房地產、保險、一般

---

## 03 Historic buildings

**歷史建築**

比 `historic structures` 更直接限定為建築物。通常表示具有歷史意義、文化價值或保育價值，而不只是『舊』。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The council protects several historic buildings in the town centre.

議會保護市中心數座歷史建築。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **buildings recognised for historical significance**

**REGISTER · 語域**

保育、建築、正式

---

## 04 Heritage buildings

**文物建築；具文化遺產價值的建築**

強調建築被視為文化遺產的一部分，常見於保育、城市規劃和政府文件。它比 `historic buildings` 更突出 heritage / conservation status。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The redevelopment plan preserves two heritage buildings.

重建計劃保留兩座文物建築。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **buildings valued as part of cultural or architectural heritage**

**REGISTER · 語域**

保育、政府、規劃

---

## 05 Period buildings

**具某時期特色的建築；年代建築**

常見於英式房地產和建築描述，強調建築保留某個歷史時期的典型設計或特色，例如 Victorian / Edwardian period buildings。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The street is lined with attractive period buildings.

街道兩旁排列着具年代特色的建築。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **buildings characteristic of a particular historical period**

**REGISTER · 語域**

房地產、建築、英式

---

## 06 Older structures

**較舊建築物；較舊結構**

中性、描述性，比 `historic structures` 少歷史價值意味。適合工程、檢查、維修或一般比較新舊建築時使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Engineers inspected several older structures for signs of corrosion.

工程師檢查數座較舊結構是否有腐蝕跡象。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **structures that are older, without implying heritage value**

**REGISTER · 語域**

工程、維修、一般

---

## 07 Ageing buildings

**老化中的建築；日漸老舊的樓宇**

強調建築因年齡增長而出現維修、結構、設備或安全問題。重點是『老化過程』，不是歷史價值。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The city is facing rising maintenance costs for ageing buildings.

城市正面對老化樓宇維修成本上升的問題。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **buildings becoming older and requiring more maintenance**

**REGISTER · 語域**

工程、政策、維修

---

## 08 Traditional buildings

**傳統建築**

當 `old buildings` 的重點是傳統建築風格、建造方法或文化特色時可用。它不一定真的很舊，新建築也可能採用 traditional style。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The village contains many traditional buildings made of stone.

村落有很多以石材建造的傳統建築。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **buildings associated with traditional architectural styles or methods**

**REGISTER · 語域**

文化、建築、旅遊

# 02 False synonyms

## Old buildings / Historic structures
`historic structures` 表示具有歷史意義；不是所有舊建築都 historic。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
An old warehouse may simply be old.  
A historic structure is valued because of its historical significance.

## Old buildings / Older properties
`older properties` 最適合房地產、保險、驗樓和物業管理語境。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Older properties may need more maintenance.  
Historic buildings may be protected because of their cultural significance.

## Historic structures / Historic buildings
兩者接近，但 `structures` 範圍較廣，可包括橋、塔、城牆等非一般 building 的構造物。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A historic house is a historic building.  
A historic city wall is a historic structure but not normally a building.

## Historic buildings / Heritage buildings
`heritage buildings` 更強調文化遺產／保育價值；`historic buildings` 更直接強調歷史意義。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A building may be historic because an important event happened there.  
A heritage building is valued as part of architectural or cultural heritage.

## Old buildings / Period buildings
`period buildings` 強調某個時代的典型風格和原有特色。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
An old apartment block may have no special architectural style.  
A Victorian period building has features characteristic of that era.

## Old buildings / Older structures
`older structures` 是中性工程／描述用語，不自動帶歷史價值。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Engineers inspect older structures for deterioration.  
Conservationists protect historic structures for their heritage value.

## Old buildings / Ageing buildings
`ageing buildings` 強調隨時間老化和維修問題。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
An old building may be perfectly well maintained.  
An ageing building may develop leaks, corrosion, or outdated systems.

## Old buildings / Traditional buildings
`traditional buildings` 強調傳統風格和建造方法，不一定真的年代很久。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A newly built house can use traditional architecture.  
An old building is defined by age, not necessarily by style.

# 03 練習與解釋

> 每個目標詞／表達三題，共 **24 題**。  
> 每題六個選項；正確答案位置在 **A–F 各出現四次**。  
> 每個選項都按「樓齡、歷史意義、文化遺產、時期風格、工程狀況或傳統建築特色」作個別解釋。

**Target phrase: Historic structures · 歷史建築；歷史結構物**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> The government has protected several old buildings because of their role in local history.

**中文翻譯**

政府因數座舊建築在本地歷史中的角色而加以保護。

| | 選項 |
|---|---|
| A | Historic structures |
| B | Historic buildings |
| C | Heritage buildings |
| D | Older structures |
| E | New developments |
| F | Temporary shelters |

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**A. Historic structures · 歷史建築；歷史結構物**

> The government has protected several historic structures because of their role in local history.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. Historic structures ✓**  
句子線索「因歷史意義而保護」明確加入歷史價值，而且語境可涵蓋比一般 building 更廣的結構；`historic structures` 最合適。

**B. Historic buildings**  
`historic buildings`：明確指具有歷史價值的建築物。這句線索是「因歷史意義而保護」；若只是在工程上較舊、正在老化或強調傳統風格而非歷史重要性，就不一定合適。

**C. Heritage buildings**  
`heritage buildings`：突出文化／建築遺產及保育價值。這句線索是「因歷史意義而保護」；若建築只是年代久遠而沒有 heritage status，這個詞太強。

**D. Older structures**  
`older structures`：中性說結構較舊，不暗示保育價值。這句線索是「因歷史意義而保護」；若句子特別談文化遺產、歷史意義或傳統建築風格，這個詞太一般。

**E. New developments**  
`New developments`：new developments = 新發展項目；和 old/historic structures 相反。 本句線索是「因歷史意義而保護」，所以它不是這裡所說的舊／歷史／傳統建築。

**F. Temporary shelters**  
`Temporary shelters`：temporary shelters = 臨時庇護設施；不等於具有歷史價值的結構。 本句線索是「因歷史意義而保護」，所以它不是這裡所說的舊／歷史／傳統建築。

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Historic structures vs Historic buildings** — structures 範圍更廣。
- **Historic buildings vs Heritage buildings** — historic 強調歷史意義；heritage 更強調文化／保育價值。
- **Older properties vs Older structures** — properties 偏房地產；structures 偏工程／技術。
- **Older properties vs Period buildings** — period buildings 要有明顯年代建築特色。
- **Old buildings vs Ageing buildings** — ageing 特別突出老化和維修問題。
- **Period buildings vs Traditional buildings** — period = 某歷史時期；traditional = 傳統風格／工法。
- **Historic vs Old** — old 只表示年代久；historic 表示具有歷史意義。
- **Heritage vs Traditional** — heritage 是文化遺產價值；traditional 是建築風格／方法。

# 06 Common expressions · 常用表達

- **historic structures** — 歷史建築／結構

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Historic / historical

> a historic building = 一座具有歷史重要性的建築  
> historical research = 歷史研究／與歷史有關

`historical building` 可見，但若要表示「具歷史重要性」，`historic building` 通常更直接。

## Older

比較級通常需要比較背景，但也常委婉表示「較舊」：

> older properties  
> older buildings

## Heritage

常作前置名詞修飾：

> heritage building  
> heritage site  
> heritage conservation

## Period

> a period building  
> period features  
> period property

## Ageing

> ageing buildings  
> ageing infrastructure

強調逐漸老化，而不是單純 old。

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 有歷史意義，而且可能包括塔／牆／橋？ → **historic structures**
2. 房地產／保險中只想說樓齡較高？ → **older properties**
3. 明確是一座具歷史價值的建築？ → **historic buildings**
4. 具文化遺產／保育價值？ → **heritage buildings**
5. 有 Victorian / Edwardian 等年代特色？ → **period buildings**
6. 工程語境中性說較舊結構？ → **older structures**
7. 重點是老化、維修和安全問題？ → **ageing buildings**
8. 重點是傳統風格、材料和工法？ → **traditional buildings**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`historic structures / older properties / historic buildings / heritage buildings / period buildings / older structures / ageing buildings / traditional buildings`

最重要的 near-miss：

`ancient ruins / historic sites / listed buildings / old-fashioned buildings / derelict buildings`

尤其要記住：

> **old** = 只表示年代久  
> **historic** = 有歷史意義  
> **heritage** = 有文化／保育價值  
> **period** = 有某歷史時期特色  
> **ageing** = 正在老化、出現維修問題  
> **traditional** = 傳統風格／工法
