# SUCCESS · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Success

**Success 可以指成就、完成一件事、理想結果、正面成效、達標、突破或勝利。** 因此替換前要先問：是在說 **what was achieved, what outcome occurred, whether a target was met, whether an obstacle was overcome, or whether there was a contest with a winner**？

你提供的 **achievement; accomplishment; favourable outcome** 正好代表三個核心方向：**成就、具體完成成果、理想結果**。

本次保留 **10 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達。會特別區分 **achievement / progress / result / victory / breakthrough**，避免把『有進展』或『有結果』誤當『已成功』。共 **30 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Success 的語境 | 核心問題 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|---|
| 具體成就／完成 | 是否完成了一項值得肯定的事情？ | achievement, accomplishment |
| 理想／正面結果 | 是否 process ended well？ | favourable outcome, positive result, successful outcome |
| 達到標準／目標 | 是否 achieved a defined level/target？ | attainment, goal attainment |
| 經困難後重大成功 | 是否 impressive success after adversity？ | triumph |
| 關鍵新進展 | 是否 major obstacle was overcome / new possibility opened？ | breakthrough |
| 競爭／選舉／法律勝利 | 是否 there is an opponent or contest？ | victory |

## Tier 1 — Core achievement / outcome / attainment equivalents · 核心成就、結果與達標近義詞

### 01 Achievement

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：成就；成果  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：something successfully completed or attained through effort, skill, or persistence  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **success** 最核心的近義詞之一，尤其適合個人、團隊、學業、事業和項目成果。  
**常見搭配**：major achievement; academic achievement; personal achievement; notable achievement; achieve success.  
**常見搭配對象**：career, study, project, team, student.  
**與 success 的辨析**：Achievement 強調『做成了一件事』；success 可指整體成功狀態、一次成功結果或某人的成功程度。  
**使用限制**：單純有利結果但未必來自努力時，favourable outcome 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Completing the degree was a major personal achievement.  
完成這個學位是一項重大的個人成就。

### 02 Accomplishment

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：成就；完成的成果  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：a task or goal successfully completed, often after effort  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 與 achievement 很接近，但更常突出完成了一項具體任務或目標。  
**常見搭配**：major accomplishment; significant accomplishment; personal accomplishment; professional accomplishment; sense of accomplishment.  
**常見搭配對象**：goal, project, career, task, milestone.  
**與 success 的辨析**：Accomplishment 比 success 更具體，常可數；success 可以是不可數概念。  
**使用限制**：若是公司整體市場成功或治療結果，不一定用 accomplishment。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Finishing the project ahead of schedule was a significant accomplishment.  
提早完成項目是一項重要成就。

### 03 Favourable outcome

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：有利結果；理想結果  
**語域**：formal / neutral  
**核心語義**：a result that is positive, beneficial, or desirable  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，當 success = good result，而不強調努力或成就感時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：favourable outcome; achieve a favourable outcome; likely outcome; treatment outcome; negotiation outcome.  
**常見搭配對象**：treatment, negotiation, case, decision, process.  
**與 success 的辨析**：Favourable outcome 強調結果好；achievement/accomplishment 強調成功完成。  
**使用限制**：人物職業生涯的『成功』通常不直接說 favourable outcome。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Early treatment increases the chance of a favourable outcome.  
及早治療可增加取得理想結果的機會。

### 04 Positive result

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：正面結果；良好成果  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：a result that is beneficial, desirable, or indicates improvement  
**用法提示**：當 success 只是表示『結果不錯／有效』時自然，適合教育、政策、試驗、工作表現等。  
**常見搭配**：positive result; produce positive results; see positive results; positive result from.  
**常見搭配對象**：programme, trial, policy, effort, intervention.  
**與 success 的辨析**：Positive result 比 success 更 outcome-focused，也較少包含 prestige or achievement。  
**使用限制**：如果 success 是『成功人士／成功企業』的廣義狀態，不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The new approach produced positive results within three months.  
新方法在三個月內取得正面結果。

### 05 Attainment

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：達成；成就；取得  
**語域**：formal / education / professional  
**核心語義**：the achievement of a goal, standard, level, or qualification  
**用法提示**：當 success 指達到某一具體標準、程度或資格時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：educational attainment; attainment of goals; high attainment; academic attainment; level of attainment.  
**常見搭配對象**：goal, standard, qualification, level, education.  
**與 success 的辨析**：Attainment 更像『達到某 level/target』；success 更廣。  
**使用限制**：日常口語較少用；沒有明確 target/standard 時不一定自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The programme aims to improve educational attainment among disadvantaged students.  
這個計劃旨在提升弱勢學生的教育成就。

## Tier 2 — Triumph / breakthrough / victory / outcome variants · 勝利、突破與結果型變體

### 06 Triumph

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：勝利；重大成功  
**語域**：neutral / emphatic  
**核心語義**：a great victory or highly impressive success, often after difficulty  
**用法提示**：當 success 是經過困難後的重大勝利時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：major triumph; personal triumph; political triumph; triumph over adversity; a triumph for.  
**常見搭配對象**：campaign, career, competition, adversity, project.  
**與 success 的辨析**：Triumph 比 success 強很多，帶 celebration/victory 色彩。  
**使用限制**：普通小成果或中性結果不要用 triumph。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Winning the championship was a personal triumph after years of setbacks.  
經歷多年挫折後贏得冠軍，是一次個人重大勝利。

### 07 Breakthrough

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：突破；重大進展  
**語域**：neutral / science / business  
**核心語義**：an important advance that overcomes a major difficulty or opens a new possibility  
**用法提示**：當 success 表示打破瓶頸、取得關鍵新進展時很適合。  
**常見搭配**：major breakthrough; scientific breakthrough; breakthrough result; achieve a breakthrough; breakthrough discovery.  
**常見搭配對象**：research, technology, negotiation, treatment, project.  
**與 success 的辨析**：Breakthrough 強調跨過障礙或進入新階段，不等於所有 success。  
**使用限制**：普通成功完成工作不能自動叫 breakthrough。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The trial produced a breakthrough in the treatment of the disease.  
這項試驗在治療該疾病方面取得重大突破。

### 08 Victory

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：勝利；成功  
**語域**：neutral / competition / politics  
**核心語義**：success in a contest, battle, election, campaign, or struggle  
**用法提示**：當 success 明確涉及競爭、選舉、運動或對抗時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：election victory; legal victory; decisive victory; victory over; claim victory.  
**常見搭配對象**：election, case, campaign, match, opponent.  
**與 success 的辨析**：Victory 是 success 的競爭型 subtype；沒有 opponent/contest 時通常不合適。  
**使用限制**：研究結果、學業成就、治療效果不應用 victory。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The court ruling was a significant legal victory for the group.  
這項法院裁決是該團體一次重要的法律勝利。

### 09 Successful outcome

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：成功結果；成功結局  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：an outcome in which the intended goal is achieved  
**用法提示**：適合 negotiation, treatment, operation, intervention, project 等有明確 objective 的語境。  
**常見搭配**：successful outcome; achieve a successful outcome; lead to a successful outcome.  
**常見搭配對象**：treatment, negotiation, operation, project, intervention.  
**與 success 的辨析**：比 success 更明確指 outcome 本身，而不是人的成就或整體成功狀態。  
**使用限制**：個人成就感、名望、職業成功不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Clear communication contributed to a successful outcome in the negotiations.  
清晰溝通促成談判取得成功結果。

## Tier 3 — Measurement-focused target-attainment variant · 量度型目標達成變體

### 10 Goal attainment

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：目標達成；達標  
**語域**：formal / management / research  
**核心語義**：the achievement of a defined goal or target  
**用法提示**：當 success 被 operationalised as whether a target was achieved 時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：goal attainment; target attainment; attainment score; measure goal attainment.  
**常見搭配對象**：goal, target, programme, treatment, performance.  
**與 success 的辨析**：這是 measurement-focused contextual paraphrase，比 success 更 technical。  
**使用限制**：日常英語顯得過度技術化。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The evaluation measured goal attainment at the end of the programme.  
評估在計劃結束時量度目標達成情況。

## 04 False synonyms / success traps · 容易誤選的努力、進展、結果與成功陷阱

### 01 Effort

**努力 ≠ success**  
effort 是投入過程，不保證成功。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Effort can exist without success.

### 02 Progress

**進展 ≠ success**  
progress 表示向目標前進，但尚未必達成目標。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Progress can occur before success.

### 03 Result

**結果 ≠ success**  
result 可以正面、負面或中性；success 是有利／達標的結果。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A result is not necessarily successful.

### 04 Attempt

**嘗試 ≠ success**  
attempt 只是試圖做到，可能失敗。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
An attempt can fail.

### 05 Popularity

**受歡迎 ≠ success**  
popular 不等於成功；某事可受歡迎但未達成目標。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Popularity and success differ.

### 06 Achievement

**只在完成／成就 sense 接近**  
achievement 比 success 更具體，常指完成的成果。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Achievement is accomplishment-focused.

### 07 Victory

**只在競爭／對抗 sense 接近**  
victory 只適合 contest/battle/election 等。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Victory is contest-focused.

### 08 Success / successful

**詞性陷阱**  
success 是名詞；successful 是形容詞；succeed 是動詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check word class before replacing.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Achievement · 成就；成果

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

Completing the degree was a major success for her.

**中文翻譯**  
完成學位對她而言是一項重大成功。

- A. Achievement
- B. Accomplishment
- C. Positive result
- D. Effort
- E. Progress
- F. Result

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Achievement** · 成就；成果

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
Completing the degree was a major achievement for her.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Achievement ✓** — means a concrete success reached through effort, skill or persistence. Here, the sentence names a concrete accomplishment reached through effort. That makes **Achievement** the most precise replacement for this particular sense of *success*.
- **B.** **Accomplishment** — means a task or goal successfully completed, often after effort. This is best for a concrete completed task; it is too specific for a general outcome or broader success state. The decisive clue is that the sentence names a concrete accomplishment reached through effort.
- **C.** **Positive result** — means a beneficial or improving result. This focuses on a beneficial result and does not carry the same accomplishment, victory, or target-attainment nuance. The decisive clue is that the sentence names a concrete accomplishment reached through effort.
- **D.** **Effort** — means work or energy put into trying and does not guarantee success. Effort describes what was put in, not whether the goal was achieved. The decisive clue is that the sentence names a concrete accomplishment reached through effort.
- **E.** **Progress** — means movement towards a goal without necessarily reaching it. Progress means movement towards success, not necessarily successful completion. The decisive clue is that the sentence names a concrete accomplishment reached through effort.
- **F.** **Result** — means an outcome that may be positive, negative or neutral. A result can be good, bad or neutral, so it does not guarantee success. The decisive clue is that the sentence names a concrete accomplishment reached through effort.

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Success 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 具體個人／團隊成就 | **achievement** | 是否 a concrete success through effort？ |
| 完成一項任務／目標 | **accomplishment** | 是否 completion itself is foregrounded？ |
| 治療／談判理想結果 | **favourable outcome** | 是否 outcome simply needs to be beneficial？ |
| 計劃／方法取得正面成效 | **positive result** | 是否 evidence of improvement is central？ |
| 教育／標準達成 | **attainment** | 是否 level/qualification/standard is measured？ |
| 克服困難後重大成功 | **triumph** | 是否 celebratory success after adversity？ |
| 關鍵突破 | **breakthrough** | 是否 obstacle overcome or new possibility opened？ |
| 競賽／選舉／法律勝利 | **victory** | 是否 there is a contest/opponent？ |
| 明確目標導向的成功結果 | **successful outcome** | 是否 a defined process reached its intended end？ |
| 量化目標達成 | **goal attainment** | 是否 target achievement is the measurement construct？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **success vs achievement**: achievement 更具體，常指某一項完成的成就。
- **success vs accomplishment**: accomplishment 更強調完成了具體任務／目標。
- **success vs favourable outcome**: 後者只強調結果有利。
- **success vs positive result**: positive result 偏成效／改善，不一定是重大成就。
- **success vs attainment**: attainment 偏達到 level/standard/qualification。
- **success vs triumph**: triumph 強度更高，帶勝利與慶祝意味。
- **success vs breakthrough**: breakthrough 是突破瓶頸的重大進展。
- **success vs victory**: victory 只適合競爭／對抗。
- **success vs progress**: progress 可有進展但尚未成功。
- **success vs result**: result 可以正面、負面或中性。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & outcome traps · 文法、搭配與『成功』陷阱

- **success in + noun/-ing**：success in business / in reducing costs。
- **success of + project/programme**：the success of the campaign。
- **achievement of + goal / achievement in + field**。
- **sense of accomplishment**：完成事情後的成就感。
- **favourable outcome for/of + treatment/case/process**。
- **positive result from + intervention / produce positive results**。
- **educational / academic attainment**：教育研究常見。
- **triumph over + adversity/opposition**。
- **breakthrough in + field / breakthrough in negotiations**。
- **victory over + opponent / election victory / legal victory**。
- **successful outcome** 常與 treatment, negotiation, operation, project 搭配。
- **goal attainment** 偏 management / research / evaluation。
- **success** 是名詞；**successful** 是形容詞；**succeed** 是動詞；**successfully** 是副詞。
- 不要把 **effort, progress, attempt, result, popularity** 當作 success 的全面同義詞。
