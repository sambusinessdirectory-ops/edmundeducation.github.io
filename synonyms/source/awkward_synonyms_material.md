# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Awkward

**不自在、動作笨拙、令人尷尬、拘謹、姿態不雅、處事笨拙、笨重難用、不方便、棘手——究竟是哪一種 awkward？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**uncomfortable; clumsy; embarrassing; self-conscious; ungainly; inept; cumbersome; inconvenient; tricky**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **uncomfortable; clumsy; embarrassing** 全部保留。  
> 本課特別分開 `awkward` 的 **社交不自在、身體動作笨拙、公開難堪、自我意識過強、姿態不雅、能力不足、物件／程序難用、安排不便、局面棘手**。

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Uncomfortable

**令人不自在的；尷尬不舒服的**

你提供的 seed，也是最廣泛的近義詞之一。當 `awkward` 指社交、沉默、談話、氣氛或姿勢讓人感到不自在時很自然。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

There was an uncomfortable silence after his question.

他問完問題後出現了一陣令人不自在的沉默。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- uncomfortable silence

- uncomfortable conversation

- uncomfortable situation

- feel uncomfortable

### EXAMPLE · 例句

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Uncomfortable

**最廣的社交不自在**

可用於 silence, conversation, situation，但未必有 embarrassment。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

There was an uncomfortable silence.

---

## 02 Clumsy

**動作／操作笨拙**

重點是 coordination，不是社交難堪。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

He made a clumsy movement.

---

## 03 Embarrassing

**事情令人尷尬**

注意和 `embarrassed` 區別：embarrassing thing / embarrassed person。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

It was an embarrassing mistake.

---

## 04 Self-conscious

**人因被注視而不自然**

常見於 public speaking, entering a room alone, being watched。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She felt self-conscious in front of the audience.

---

## 05 Inept

**能力不足**

適合 handling, response, attempt, management；不是純粹手腳不靈活。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

It was an inept response.

---

## 06 Cumbersome

**笨重／繁複**

可形容 equipment, process, form, procedure。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The old system is cumbersome to use.

---

## 07 Awkward / awkwardly

**詞性辨析**

`awkward` 是形容詞；`awkwardly` 是副詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

an awkward movement; move awkwardly

---

## 08 Awkward

**多義詞**

可表示 social discomfort, clumsy movement, embarrassment, inconvenience, difficulty；近義詞必須按語境選。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

an awkward silence; an awkward movement; an awkward time

# 03 練習與解釋

> **每個核心近義表達 3 題，共 27 題。**
> 每題均以 **社交 vs 動作、感受 vs 造成尷尬、能力 vs 協調性、物理笨重 vs 安排不便、一般困難 vs 棘手局面** 作辨析。


## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** uncomfortable · 令人不自在的；尷尬不舒服的

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

There was an awkward silence after his question.

### 中文翻譯

他問完問題後出現了一陣令人不自在的沉默。

### OPTIONS


- **A. uncomfortable**

- **B. embarrassing**

- **C. self-conscious**

- **D. tense**

- **E. comfortable**

- **F. relaxed**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**uncomfortable · 令人不自在的；尷尬不舒服的**

There was an **uncomfortable** silence after his question.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. uncomfortable ✓** — `uncomfortable` = 令人不自在、緊張或不舒服。 **本句為何最合適：** the silence creates social unease rather than physical clumsiness。

- **B. embarrassing** — `embarrassing` = 令人尷尬或難堪。 描述事情令人難堪；若主語是人『感到拘謹』，self-conscious 更準。 **本句線索：** the silence creates social unease rather than physical clumsiness。

- **C. self-conscious** — `self-conscious` = 因過度在意別人眼光而拘謹不自然。 描述人因別人注視而不自然；物件、時間、設備不會 self-conscious。 **本句線索：** the silence creates social unease rather than physical clumsiness。

- **D. tense** — `tense` = 緊張的。 表示緊張，但不必然帶 awkward 的社交不自在。 **本句線索：** the silence creates social unease rather than physical clumsiness。

- **E. comfortable** — `comfortable` = 舒服／自在。 和 uncomfortable 相反。 **本句線索：** the silence creates social unease rather than physical clumsiness。

- **F. relaxed** — `relaxed` = 放鬆。 和 uncomfortable/tense 相反。 **本句線索：** the silence creates social unease rather than physical clumsiness。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Awkward vs awkwardly
- awkward = adjective
- awkwardly = adverb

例如：
- an awkward movement
- move awkwardly

## 02 Embarrassing vs embarrassed
- embarrassing situation
- embarrassed person

## 03 Socially awkward
固定而常見：
- socially awkward person
- feel socially awkward

## 04 Awkward to + verb
- awkward to carry
- awkward to use
- awkward to explain

## 05 Polysemy
`awkward` 跨多個 senses，不能把所有句子都機械替換成 `uncomfortable` 或 `clumsy`。
