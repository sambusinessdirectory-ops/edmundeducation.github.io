# IN MANY CASES · 分層同義詞學習系統

## In many cases · phrase

**In many cases 可以表示『很多時候』『很多情況下』『大量個案中』『很多不同類型的案例中』。** 因此替換前要先判斷句子強調的是 **frequency, situations, individual instances, case count, prevalence, breadth of contexts, or majority tendency**。

你提供的 **often; in many situations** 正好代表兩個核心方向：**經常發生** 與 **在很多不同情況下**。

本次保留 **8 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達，共 **24 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。按照 inspection 標準，所有原句先確保 *in many cases* 本身自然，再建立只有一個最佳答案的替換題。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| In many cases 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 |
|---|---|
| 一般頻率：很多時候 | **often** |
| 很多不同情況 | **in many situations** |
| 正式：許多個別例子 | **in numerous instances** |
| 大量可數個案 | **in a large number of cases** |
| 較正式頻率 | **frequently** |
| 普遍／常見現象 | **commonly** |
| 廣泛不同類型案例 | **in a wide range of cases** |
| 多數時候 | **more often than not** |

## Tier 1 — Core frequency / situation / case-count equivalents · 核心頻率、情況與個案數量近義表達

### 01 Often

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：經常；很多時候  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：frequently; on many occasions  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **in many cases** 最簡潔、最常用的替代表達之一，尤其當句子談的是某種情況經常發生。  
**常見搭配**：often occurs; often happens; often leads to; often seen; often used.  
**常見搭配對象**：event, behaviour, outcome, pattern, situation.  
**與 in many cases 的辨析**：Often 強調 frequency；in many cases 強調『很多個案例／情況中』。兩者高度接近，但觀點不同。  
**使用限制**：若句子需要強調不同情境／個案而不是出現頻率，in many situations / in numerous instances 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
In many cases, early treatment improves recovery; often, early treatment improves recovery.  
很多情況下，及早治療有助改善康復。

### 02 In many situations

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：在很多情況下  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：across a large number of situations or circumstances  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很直接，當 **cases** 不是法律／研究個案，而是一般情境時尤其自然。  
**常見搭配**：in many situations; useful in many situations; applicable in many situations.  
**常見搭配對象**：work, school, travel, communication, daily life.  
**與 in many cases 的辨析**：比 often 更明確保留『不同情境』的框架。  
**使用限制**：若句子是統計個案／研究案例，in many instances / in a large proportion of cases 更精確。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
In many situations, a simple explanation is more effective than a technical one.  
在很多情況下，簡單解釋比技術性解釋更有效。

### 03 In numerous instances

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：在許多案例中；在不少情況下  
**語域**：formal / analytical  
**核心語義**：in a large number of individual examples or occurrences  
**用法提示**：當 **in many cases** 出現在正式報告、研究、分析或書面論述中，這是很自然的正式替換。  
**常見搭配**：in numerous instances; observed in numerous instances; documented in numerous instances.  
**常見搭配對象**：case, example, event, report, finding.  
**與 in many cases 的辨析**：比 in many cases 更正式，也更強調可數的個別案例。  
**使用限制**：日常口語會顯得偏正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
In numerous instances, the same error appeared in more than one document.  
在不少案例中，同一錯誤出現在多於一份文件。

### 04 In a large number of cases

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：在大量案例中；在很多個案中  
**語域**：formal / precise  
**核心語義**：in many individual cases within a set or group  
**用法提示**：這是 **in many cases** 最透明的正式擴展，特別適合研究、審核、醫療、法律或統計語境。  
**常見搭配**：in a large number of cases; observed in a large number of cases; true in a large number of cases.  
**常見搭配對象**：patient, claim, complaint, application, sample.  
**與 in many cases 的辨析**：比 often 更保留『個案數量多』這個語義。  
**使用限制**：句子若追求簡潔，often / frequently 可能更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
In a large number of cases, the application was delayed by missing documents.  
在大量個案中，申請因欠缺文件而延誤。

### 05 Frequently

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：頻繁地；經常  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：often; many times or on many occasions  
**用法提示**：當 **in many cases** 可直接理解為『經常』時，frequently 是較正式的單字替代。  
**常見搭配**：frequently occurs; frequently reported; frequently used; frequently observed.  
**常見搭配對象**：pattern, error, symptom, behaviour, event.  
**與 in many cases 的辨析**：Frequently 與 often 同樣聚焦 frequency，但語氣稍正式。  
**使用限制**：若要保留『多個不同案例中』的 case-based framing，這個詞會略為抽象化。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
This problem frequently occurs when the system is overloaded.  
系統負荷過重時，這個問題經常發生。

## Tier 2 — Prevalence / breadth variants · 普遍性與廣泛適用變體

### 06 Commonly

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：普遍地；常見地  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：in a way that is usual or widespread across many cases  
**用法提示**：當 **in many cases** 實際要表達某現象『很常見／普遍存在』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：commonly used; commonly seen; commonly found; commonly associated with.  
**常見搭配對象**：practice, symptom, method, feature, belief.  
**與 in many cases 的辨析**：Commonly 強調普遍性／常見程度，不只是事件發生次數。  
**使用限制**：若句子講的是個別事件頻繁發生，而不是一個普遍 pattern，often / frequently 更合適。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
This method is commonly used in introductory courses.  
這種方法在入門課程中很常見。

### 07 In a wide range of cases

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：在廣泛不同的案例中  
**語域**：formal / analytical  
**核心語義**：across many different kinds of cases rather than merely a high number of them  
**用法提示**：當 **in many cases** 不只表示數量多，還隱含涵蓋多種類型／背景時很適合。  
**常見搭配**：in a wide range of cases; effective across a wide range of cases; applicable in a wide range of cases.  
**常見搭配對象**：application, patient, scenario, dispute, design problem.  
**與 in many cases 的辨析**：它比 in many cases 多了 diversity/breadth 的意味。  
**使用限制**：如果只是數量多，不應硬加 wide range。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The approach proved effective in a wide range of cases.  
這種方法在廣泛不同的案例中都證明有效。

## Tier 3 — Majority-tendency idiomatic variant · 多數傾向慣用變體

### 08 More often than not

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：多數時候；往往  
**語域**：informal / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：in most cases or on most occasions, though not always  
**用法提示**：當 **in many cases** 的實際意思接近『大多數時候』，而不是單純『不少情況』時可用。  
**常見搭配**：more often than not; more often than not, ....  
**常見搭配對象**：general observation, repeated outcome, everyday pattern.  
**與 in many cases 的辨析**：比 in many cases 更強，通常接近『超過一半／多數情況』。  
**使用限制**：不能用於只是『很多但未必多數』的情況。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
More often than not, a short explanation is enough.  
多數時候，簡短解釋已經足夠。

## 04 False synonyms / frequency traps · 容易誤選的『總是、通常、有時、一般而言』陷阱

### 01 Always

**總是 ≠ in many cases**  
always 表示所有情況，強度過高。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Always means without exception.

### 02 Usually

**通常 ≠ in many cases**  
usually 常暗示大多數情況；in many cases 不一定達到『通常』。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Usually is stronger in frequency.

### 03 Sometimes

**有時 ≠ in many cases**  
sometimes 通常比 in many cases 弱得多。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Sometimes is weaker.

### 04 Generally

**一般而言 ≠ in many cases**  
generally 是整體概括，不一定指多個具體案例。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Generally is generalisation-focused.

### 05 Frequently

**只在頻率 sense 接近**  
frequently 不保留『多個案例』的框架。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Frequently is frequency-focused.

### 06 Commonly

**只在普遍性 sense 接近**  
commonly 偏 widespread/usual，而不是單純 case count。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Commonly is prevalence-focused.

### 07 In most cases

**多數情況 ≠ in many cases**  
in most cases 強度更高，明確表示 majority。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Most is stronger than many.

### 08 Case / cases

**數與詞義陷阱**  
case 可指情況、個案、案件；要按語境理解。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check countability and sense.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Often · 經常；很多時候

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

In many cases, a brief explanation is enough.

**中文翻譯**  
很多情況下，簡短解釋已經足夠。

- A. Often
- B. Frequently
- C. Usually
- D. Always
- E. Sometimes
- F. Generally

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Often** · 經常；很多時候

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
Often, a brief explanation is enough.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Often ✓** — frequently; on many occasions。原句只說「簡短說明在很多情況下已足夠」。**Often** 可自然作句首頻率副詞；**Frequently** 也可，原句沒有數字可把兩者分成唯一對錯。
- **B.** **Frequently** — often; many times or on many occasions。**frequently** 聚焦頻率；如果句子要保留『多個個案／情境』的框架，會少一層 case-based 意味。
- **C.** **Usually** — in most cases or on most occasions。**usually** 往往暗示『大多數情況』，強度通常比 in many cases 更高。
- **D.** **Always** — on every occasion without exception。**always** 表示毫無例外，明顯太強。
- **E.** **Sometimes** — on some occasions but not often。**sometimes** 只表示有時，通常比 in many cases 弱很多。
- **F.** **Generally** — in most respects or as a broad generalisation。**generally** 是整體概括，不一定表示有很多個具體案例。

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| In many cases 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 一般說『很多時候』 | **often** | 是否 frequency is the main point？ |
| 多種日常／實際情況 | **in many situations** | 是否 different circumstances are central？ |
| 正式報告多個例子 | **in numerous instances** | 是否 formal case-by-case examples？ |
| 行政／醫療／研究大量個案 | **in a large number of cases** | 是否 case count is explicit？ |
| 正式頻繁發生 | **frequently** | 是否 repeated occurrence is central？ |
| 某方法／特徵很普遍 | **commonly** | 是否 prevalence/usualness is central？ |
| 多種類型／背景都適用 | **in a wide range of cases** | 是否 breadth/diversity is central？ |
| 實際意思接近『大多數時候』 | **more often than not** | 是否 majority tendency is intended？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **in many cases vs often**：often 聚焦頻率；前者聚焦很多情況／個案。
- **in many cases vs in many situations**：後者更一般、更情境化。
- **in many cases vs in numerous instances**：後者更正式，強調個別例子。
- **in many cases vs frequently**：frequently 是正式頻率副詞。
- **in many cases vs commonly**：commonly 偏普遍／常見。
- **in many cases vs in a wide range of cases**：後者多了『類型廣泛』。
- **in many cases vs in most cases**：most 比 many 強，表示 majority。
- **in many cases vs more often than not**：後者通常接近『多數時候』。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & quantifier traps

- **many + plural count noun**：many cases / many situations / many examples。
- **in many cases** 常放句首或句中：In many cases, ... / This is, in many cases, unnecessary。
- **often / frequently / commonly** 是副詞，通常直接修飾動詞或整句。
- **in numerous instances** 偏正式報告。
- **in a large number of cases** 強調 case count。
- **in a wide range of cases** 強調 breadth，不只是數量。
- **more often than not** 是慣用語，意思接近『多數時候』。
- **always / usually / sometimes** 與 **many** 的強度不同，不能機械互換。
