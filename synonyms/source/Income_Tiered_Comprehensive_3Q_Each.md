# INCOME · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Income

**「收入／所得／薪酬／營業收入」，這是一個範圍很廣的詞。最重要的是分清個人工作收入、固定薪金、公司營業收入、交易所得，以及稅後可支配收入。**

本單元集中處理 **income = money received by a person, household, business, or organisation over a period**。你提供的 **earnings; wages; revenue; pay** 分別代表 **賺取所得、工資、企業營業收入、工作薪酬**。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且高實用度的詞／表達，共 **39 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。其中 **revenue / receipts / proceeds** 只在企業、政府或交易語境接近 income；**salary / wages / pay** 則只屬就業收入，不會假裝成 universal synonyms。

## Tier 1 — Core earnings / employment-income equivalents · 核心所得與就業收入近義詞

### 01 Earnings

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：收入；所得；賺得的金錢  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：money received from work, business activity, investments, or other sources, especially money that has been earned  
**用法提示**：和 income 最核心的近義詞之一，尤其適合個人工作所得、公司盈利報告和某段時間的賺取金額。  
**常見搭配**：annual earnings; average earnings; earnings growth; earnings from work; reported earnings.  
**常見搭配對象**：worker, employee, household, company, profession.  
**與 income 的辨析**：Earnings 強調『賺得』的錢；income 更廣，可包括租金、利息、福利等未必來自工作。  
**使用限制**：政府補助或被動收入未必自然稱 earnings。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Her annual earnings increased after the promotion.  
她升職後的年度收入增加了。

### 02 Wages

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：工資；薪金  
**語域**：neutral / employment  
**核心語義**：money paid to a worker, often calculated by the hour, day, or week  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，當 income 明確指受僱工作的工資時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：hourly wages; weekly wages; minimum wage; wage growth; earn wages.  
**常見搭配對象**：worker, employee, labourer, hourly worker, job.  
**與 income 的辨析**：Wages 是 employment income 的一部分；income 可包含更多來源。  
**使用限制**：高層按年領取的固定薪酬通常更常說 salary。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Many workers have seen their wages rise this year.  
很多工人今年的工資有所上升。

### 03 Pay

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：薪酬；工資；報酬  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：money received for doing a job or piece of work  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很自然，日常就業語境中比 remuneration 更常見。  
**常見搭配**：take-home pay; pay rise; equal pay; basic pay; rate of pay.  
**常見搭配對象**：employee, job, worker, salary package, role.  
**與 income 的辨析**：Pay 強調工作報酬；income 更廣。  
**使用限制**：租金、利息、投資所得通常不叫 pay。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The new role offers higher pay and better benefits.  
新職位提供較高薪酬和更佳福利。

### 04 Salary

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：薪金；月薪；年薪  
**語域**：neutral / employment  
**核心語義**：a fixed regular payment, typically expressed as an annual amount, for employment  
**用法提示**：當 income 明確指固定受僱薪金時最精準。  
**常見搭配**：annual salary; starting salary; salary increase; salary range; basic salary.  
**常見搭配對象**：employee, manager, teacher, professional, job.  
**與 income 的辨析**：Salary 是固定就業收入；income 可以包括工資以外的來源。  
**使用限制**：時薪、自由工作者按項目收費或企業營業收入不宜稱 salary。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The position comes with a competitive annual salary.  
該職位提供具競爭力的年薪。

### 05 Remuneration

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：薪酬；報酬  
**語域**：formal / employment / legal  
**核心語義**：money and other financial compensation received for work or services  
**用法提示**：正式合約、人力資源、董事酬金和法律文本中很常見。  
**常見搭配**：remuneration package; executive remuneration; remuneration committee; fair remuneration; total remuneration.  
**常見搭配對象**：employee, director, executive, consultant, service.  
**與 income 的辨析**：Remuneration 比 pay / salary 更正式，亦可包括津貼、獎金等。  
**使用限制**：一般日常對話通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The contract sets out the consultant's remuneration.  
合約列明顧問的報酬。

## Tier 2 — Business / transaction / net-pay variants · 企業、交易與實收薪金變體

### 06 Compensation

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：薪酬；報酬；補償  
**語域**：professional / especially AmE  
**核心語義**：payment and benefits given in return for work, or money paid to compensate for loss or injury  
**用法提示**：在人力資源、企業薪酬、尤其美式英語中，可作 employment income 的近義表達。  
**常見搭配**：compensation package; total compensation; executive compensation; employee compensation; compensation structure.  
**常見搭配對象**：employee, executive, role, package, benefit.  
**與 income 的辨析**：Compensation 可表示薪酬，也可表示賠償；需靠語境。  
**使用限制**：一般家庭總收入不宜直接叫 compensation。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The company reviewed its compensation package for senior staff.  
公司檢討高級員工的薪酬待遇。

### 07 Revenue

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：營業收入；收入；收益  
**語域**：business / finance  
**核心語義**：money received by a business or organisation from its normal activities before costs are deducted  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，但它只在公司／機構 income sense 接近。  
**常見搭配**：annual revenue; sales revenue; revenue growth; generate revenue; revenue stream.  
**常見搭配對象**：company, business, organisation, product, service.  
**與 income 的辨析**：Revenue 是企業總收入，通常在扣除成本前；personal income 不能隨便改成 revenue。  
**使用限制**：個人工資或家庭收入一般不用 revenue。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The company reported higher revenue from online sales.  
公司錄得較高的網上銷售收入。

### 08 Proceeds

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：所得款項；收益  
**語域**：formal / finance  
**核心語義**：money obtained from a particular sale, event, transaction, or activity  
**用法提示**：當 income 指某次出售、籌款、活動或交易所得時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：sale proceeds; proceeds from the event; net proceeds; proceeds of sale; donate the proceeds.  
**常見搭配對象**：sale, auction, event, transaction, fundraiser.  
**與 income 的辨析**：Proceeds 通常指特定活動／交易的所得；income 是較持續、廣義的收入。  
**使用限制**：固定月薪不叫 proceeds。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The proceeds from the charity sale will support local services.  
慈善義賣所得款項將用於支持本地服務。

### 09 Receipts

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：收入款項；進帳  
**語域**：formal / accounting / public finance  
**核心語義**：money received by a business, organisation, or government during a period  
**用法提示**：會計、財政、政府收入語境中可作較正式的收入表達。  
**常見搭配**：tax receipts; cash receipts; government receipts; total receipts; business receipts.  
**常見搭配對象**：government, business, tax authority, organisation, account.  
**與 income 的辨析**：Receipts 強調『收到的款項』；income 更一般。  
**使用限制**：個人薪金日常不會說 receipts。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Tax receipts were higher than expected.  
稅收收入高於預期。

### 10 Take-home pay

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：實收薪金；到手收入  
**語域**：neutral / employment  
**核心語義**：the amount of pay a worker receives after tax and other deductions  
**用法提示**：當 income 指扣除稅項、強積金／退休供款等後實際到手的薪酬時非常精準。  
**常見搭配**：monthly take-home pay; increase take-home pay; net pay; take-home income; after-tax pay.  
**常見搭配對象**：employee, worker, household budget, payslip, salary.  
**與 income 的辨析**：Take-home pay 是 net employment income；income 可指稅前總收入。  
**使用限制**：投資、租金等非薪酬收入不能直接叫 take-home pay。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Higher deductions reduced his monthly take-home pay.  
較高扣款令他每月實收薪金減少。

## Tier 3 — Economic / recurring-source / livelihood variants · 經濟、持續收入來源與生計變體

### 11 Disposable income

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：可支配收入  
**語域**：economics / finance  
**核心語義**：income available to spend or save after direct taxes have been deducted  
**用法提示**：當 income 出現在家庭財政、消費能力、宏觀經濟和生活水平語境中很重要。  
**常見搭配**：disposable income; household disposable income; real disposable income; disposable-income growth; higher disposable income.  
**常見搭配對象**：household, consumer, economy, family, spending.  
**與 income 的辨析**：Disposable income 是 income 的特定經濟概念。  
**使用限制**：不要把 gross income 直接叫 disposable income。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Lower taxes can increase household disposable income.  
較低稅項可以增加家庭可支配收入。

### 12 Income stream

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：收入來源；收入流  
**語域**：business / finance  
**核心語義**：a continuing source or flow of money received over time  
**用法提示**：當 income 強調『持續收入來源』而不是單一金額時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：steady income stream; multiple income streams; recurring income stream; create an income stream; diversify income streams.  
**常見搭配對象**：business, freelancer, investor, household, creator.  
**與 income 的辨析**：Income stream 強調來源／持續流入；income 可只是一個總額。  
**使用限制**：單次售賣所得不宜叫 income stream。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Freelancers often try to build several income streams.  
自由工作者經常嘗試建立多個收入來源。

### 13 Livelihood

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：生計；謀生收入來源  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：the means by which a person earns enough money to live  
**用法提示**：當 income 的重點是『賴以維生的收入來源／工作』時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：earn a livelihood; make a livelihood; secure a livelihood; livelihood depends on; source of livelihood.  
**常見搭配對象**：worker, farmer, family, community, occupation.  
**與 income 的辨析**：Livelihood 是『謀生方式／收入來源』，不是某一筆收入金額。  
**使用限制**：不能直接替換 annual income / monthly income。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Fishing provides a livelihood for many families in the village.  
捕魚為村內很多家庭提供生計。

## 04 False synonyms / income traps · 容易誤選的詞與收入概念陷阱

### 01 Profit

**利潤不等於 income / revenue**  
profit 是收入扣除成本和開支後的剩餘。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Profit is revenue minus costs, not revenue itself.

### 02 Wealth

**財富不等於 income**  
wealth 是累積資產；income 是某段時間流入的金錢。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Wealth is a stock; income is a flow.

### 03 Savings

**儲蓄不等於 income**  
savings 是未花掉並累積的金錢；income 是收入來源。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Savings come from income but are not income.

### 04 Turnover

**營業額不等於所有 income**  
英式商業語境中 turnover 常指銷售收入，但個人收入不用。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Turnover is business-specific.

### 05 Revenue

**revenue 只在企業／機構語境接近 income**  
revenue 是業務收入，通常在成本扣除前。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Revenue is business income, not personal pay.

### 06 Wages

**wages 只在就業收入 sense 接近 income**  
wages 是工作報酬的一種；income 還包括投資、租金、福利等。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Wages are one source of income.

### 07 Salary

**salary 只是一種固定就業收入**  
salary 是固定薪金，不等於所有 income。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Salary is a subset of income.

### 08 Income / incoming

**詞形陷阱**  
income 是名詞；incoming 通常表示『進來的／即將到任的』，不是收入的普通形容詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Income is a noun; incoming has different meanings.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Earnings · 收入；所得；賺得的金錢

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

Her annual income increased after the promotion.

**中文翻譯**  
她升職後的年度收入增加了。

- A. earnings
- B. profit
- C. wealth
- D. savings
- E. revenue
- F. turnover

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**earnings** · 收入；所得；賺得的金錢

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
Her annual earnings increased after the promotion.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. earnings ✓ — promotion + annual amount earned from work fits earnings。**
- **B. profit — profit is what remains after business costs。**
- **C. wealth — wealth is accumulated assets rather than yearly inflow。**
- **D. savings — savings are money retained after spending。**
- **E. revenue — revenue is business income, not an employee's pay。**
- **F. turnover — turnover is business sales revenue.**

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Income 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 一般賺取所得 | **earnings** | 是否是 money earned from work/business activity？ |
| 時薪／週薪工資 | **wages** | 是否是 hourly / weekly worker pay？ |
| 一般工作薪酬 | **pay** | 是否是 job + benefits / pay rise？ |
| 固定月薪／年薪 | **salary** | 是否 fixed regular employment payment？ |
| 正式薪酬總稱 | **remuneration** | 是否 contract / executive / HR context？ |
| 美式 HR 薪酬 | **compensation** | 是否 compensation package / executive pay？ |
| 公司營業收入 | **revenue** | 是否 business money before costs？ |
| 特定交易／活動所得 | **proceeds** | 是否 sale / auction / charity event？ |
| 政府／會計進帳 | **receipts** | 是否 tax / cash / government receipts？ |
| 扣款後實收薪金 | **take-home pay** | 是否 after tax/deductions？ |
| 稅後可支配收入 | **disposable income** | 是否 available to spend/save after tax？ |
| 持續收入來源 | **income stream** | 是否 recurring source over time？ |
| 謀生來源 | **livelihood** | 是否 means of earning enough to live？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **income vs earnings**: earnings 更強調賺得的錢；income 可包含被動收入和福利。
- **income vs wages**: wages 只是就業收入的一類，常見於時薪／週薪。
- **income vs salary**: salary 是固定就業薪金。
- **income vs pay**: pay 是工作報酬的通用日常詞。
- **income vs remuneration**: remuneration 更正式，常包括薪金、花紅、津貼等。
- **income vs revenue**: revenue 是企業／機構營業收入，不是個人薪酬。
- **income vs profit**: profit 是收入扣除成本後的剩餘。
- **income vs proceeds**: proceeds 常是某次出售／活動所得。
- **income vs wealth**: wealth 是累積資產；income 是一段時間的金錢流入。
- **income vs disposable income**: disposable income 是扣除直接稅後可支配的收入。

## 08 Grammar, terminology & income traps · 文法、搭配與收入概念陷阱

- **income** 可數性通常偏不可數：income rose / household income；但可說 **an income** 表示一份收入來源／收入水平。
- **earnings** 通常用複數形式：annual earnings / average earnings。
- **wages** 常用複數：weekly wages；**wage** 可作單數修飾：wage growth / minimum wage。
- **salary** 是可數：an annual salary / salary range。
- **pay** 多作不可數：higher pay / equal pay / take-home pay。
- **remuneration** 多作不可數正式名詞。
- **revenue** 多作不可數商業名詞：revenue increased / annual revenue。
- **profit ≠ revenue**：profit 通常是 revenue 減去成本與開支。
- **proceeds from + sale/event**：the proceeds from the auction。
- **receipts** 常見於 tax receipts / cash receipts / government receipts。
- **take-home pay** = net employment pay after deductions。
- **disposable income** = income available after direct taxes；不是單純 salary。
- **income stream** 是持續來源；**livelihood** 是謀生方式／來源，而不是一個收入數字。
