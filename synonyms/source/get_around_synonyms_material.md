# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Get around

**出行、四處走動、逐處移動、到處旅行、設法穿行、從一處到另一處、導航找路——究竟是哪一種 get around？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**travel; move from place to place; get about; move around; travel around; make one's way around; get from place to place; navigate; go from place to place**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **travel; move from place to place** 全部保留。  
> 本課只聚焦 `get around = travel / move between places` 的 **mobility sense**；`get around a rule / news gets around / get around someone` 等其他片語義另行分開。

---

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Travel

**出行；旅行；往來**

最直接、最廣泛的近義表達之一。當 `get around` 泛指從一個地方前往另一個地方時很自然，但它不一定強調同一地區內頻繁移動。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

It is easy to travel around the country by train.

乘火車在這個國家各地出行很方便。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- travel by train

- travel easily

- travel for work

- travel across the country

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Travel

**很接近但範圍較廣**

travel 可是長途、跨城市或跨國；get around 常更偏在某地內移動／出行。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

We travelled to France, then used the metro to get around Paris.

---

## 02 Move around

**偏局部移動**

move around 最自然於 room, museum, site, city area；不必然表示利用交通工具。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Visitors can move around the museum freely.

---

## 03 Get about

**英式近義**

get about 和 mobility-sense 的 get around 很接近，尤其談長者或病人的行動能力。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

After the operation, he found it hard to get about.

---

## 04 Navigate

**需要找路意味**

navigate 強調在道路、系統或建築中辨認路線；只是搭巴士出行不一定叫 navigate。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The map helps visitors navigate the old town.

---

## 05 Commute

**規律往返，不等於 get around**

commute 通常是家與工作／學校之間固定往返，範圍較窄。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She commutes to work by train.

---

## 06 Tour

**遊覽不等於一般出行**

tour 強調參觀多處景點／有遊覽目的；日常買餸、上班不是 tour。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

They toured the island for three days.

---

## 07 Get around

**其他片語義**

`get around` 亦可表示避開規則、消息傳開、說服／哄某人等。本課只聚焦 travel/mobility sense。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

They found a way to get around the rule uses a different sense.

---

## 08 Around

**結構辨析**

`get around + place` 可直接接地點；也可單獨用 `get around` 表示四處活動。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

It is easy to get around London; he has trouble getting around.

# 03 練習與解釋

- **E. stay put**

- **F. remain**

| **navigate** | 在複雜環境中找路並移動 |
| **go from place to place** | 依次前往不同地點／逐站拜訪 |

---

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Get around vs Travel
`travel` 範圍更廣；`get around` 很常是在某一城市／地區內移動。

## Get around vs Get about
英式英語中兩者非常接近，尤其談：
- older people
- illness / recovery
- walking aids
- independent mobility

## Get around vs Move around
`move around` 偏局部：
- a room
- a museum
- a site
- a playground

## Get around vs Travel around
後者更明確表示在一個地區內遊走多個地點。

## Get around vs Make one's way around
後者常暗示：
- crowded conditions
- snow
- unfamiliar streets
- some effort or route-finding

## Get around vs Get from place to place
特別適合談：
- cars
- metro systems
- wheelchairs
- transport access

## Get around vs Navigate
`navigate` 要有「找路」這一層意思。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Get around + place
- get around London
- get around the city
- get around the island

## 02 Get around by + transport
- get around by bus
- get around by bicycle
- get around by train

## 03 Get about
英式英語非常自然：
- He has trouble getting about.
- She still gets about independently.

## 04 Travel around
通常需要較大的地理範圍或多地點旅行：
- travel around Europe
- travel around the countryside

## 05 Other phrasal-verb senses excluded
本課沒有混入：
- get around a rule = 規避規則
- news gets around = 消息傳開
- get around someone = 哄／說服某人（依語境）
- get around to doing = 終於抽時間去做
