# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Honestly

**坦白地、如實地、真誠地、坦率地、不隱瞞地、真心地、老實說——究竟是哪一種 honestly？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**frankly; truthfully; sincerely; candidly; openly; genuinely; in all honesty; to be honest; with complete honesty**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **frankly; truthfully; sincerely** 全部保留。  
> 本課特別區分 `honestly` 的兩大核心：**方式副詞**（誠實／如實／真誠地）與 **話語標記**（老實說／坦白說）。

---

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Frankly

**坦白地；老實說**

當 `honestly` 用作句首／插入語，表示說話者直接表達真實看法、不兜圈子時最自然。常見於 `Frankly, ...`，語氣可較直接甚至尖銳。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Frankly, I don't think this plan will work.

坦白說，我認為這個計劃行不通。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- frankly speaking

- frankly admit

- frankly say

- quite frankly


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 honestly 是直接說真實看法、如實回答、真心道歉／感謝、正式坦率披露、不隱瞞地討論、真實感受，還是句首『老實說』？

## 02 Truthfully

**如實地；真實地**

當 `honestly` 指內容符合事實、沒有說謊時最精確。重點是 factual truth，而不是態度是否真誠。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

He answered the police questions truthfully.

他如實回答警方的問題。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- answer truthfully

- speak truthfully

- report truthfully

- respond truthfully

當 `honestly` 指情感、道歉、感謝、希望或承諾是真心的時最自然。它強調 genuineness，而不只是事實正確。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

## 07 In All Honesty

## 08 To Be Honest

**老實說；坦白說**

非常常見、口語自然，用於引出真實看法、承認或保留意見。與 discourse-marker sense 的 `honestly` 高度接近。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

## 09 With Complete Honesty

**完全坦誠地；毫不隱瞞地**

描述性較強，表示沒有隱瞞、沒有修飾地完整說出真實情況。適合正式或強調透明度的語境。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

She described the financial situation with complete honesty to the board.

她完全坦誠地向董事會交代財務狀況。

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Frankly

**坦白但可較尖銳**

frankly 常引出直接意見；它不一定表示 factual truth。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Frankly, I disagree is about candour, not verifying facts.

---

## 02 Truthfully

**重點是事實真實**

truthfully 適合 answers, reports, statements；不適合表達 heartfelt thanks。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She answered truthfully; she thanked them sincerely.

---

## 03 Sincerely

**真誠不等於如實**

sincerely 強調情感或意圖由衷；一個 sincere belief 仍可能在事實上錯誤。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

He sincerely believed it, but the belief was false.

---

## 04 Candidly

**較正式的坦率**

candidly 常見於 interviews, discussions, admissions, feedback。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The director spoke candidly about the failures.

---

## 05 Openly

**公開／不隱瞞，不一定直接**

openly 強調沒有藏起來；frankly 更強調說話直接。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

They discussed the problem openly.

---

## 06 Really

**強調不等於誠實**

really 可強調程度或真實性，但沒有 honesty 的道德／溝通含義。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

I was really surprised; I spoke honestly.

---

## 07 Honest / honestly

**詞性陷阱**

`honest` 是形容詞；`honestly` 是副詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She is honest; she answered honestly.

---

## 08 Honestly

**話語標記 vs 方式副詞**

句首 `Honestly, ...` 常等於 `to be honest / frankly`；句中 `answer honestly` 則較接近 truthfully。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Honestly, I disagree; please answer honestly.

# 03 練習與解釋

> 每題均以『事實真實、情感真誠、坦率程度、是否隱瞞、話語標記功能』作辨析；六個選項均逐項解釋。


## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** frankly · 坦白地；老實說

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

Honestly, I don't think this plan will work.

### 中文翻譯

坦白說，我認為這個計劃行不通。

### OPTIONS


- **A. frankly**

- **B. candidly**

- **C. to be honest**

- **D. truthfully**

- **E. evasively**

- **F. politely**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**frankly · 坦白地；老實說**

**Frankly**, I don't think this plan will work.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. frankly ✓** — `frankly` = 直接、坦白地表達真實看法。 **本句為何最合適：** the sentence introduces a direct personal opinion that may be unwelcome。

- **B. candidly** — `candidly` = 坦率、不隱瞞地說出真實意見。 較正式，強調坦率揭露；一般口語開場 `Honestly, ...` 可用 frankly/to be honest 更自然。 **本句線索：** the sentence introduces a direct personal opinion that may be unwelcome。

- **C. to be honest** — `to be honest` = 老實說；口語引出真實看法或承認。 口語話語標記；不適合句中直接修飾 `reported/answered` 等 factual verbs。 **本句線索：** the sentence introduces a direct personal opinion that may be unwelcome。

- **D. truthfully** — `truthfully` = 如實地，內容符合事實。 只保證內容真實，不表示情感是否由衷；道歉、感謝、希望通常用 sincerely。 **本句線索：** the sentence introduces a direct personal opinion that may be unwelcome。

- **E. evasively** — `evasively` = 閃爍其詞地。 表示避重就輕，和 frankly/candidly 相反。 **本句線索：** the sentence introduces a direct personal opinion that may be unwelcome。

- **F. politely** — `politely` = 有禮貌地。 表示有禮，與是否坦白無直接關係。 **本句線索：** the sentence introduces a direct personal opinion that may be unwelcome。

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Honestly vs Frankly
句首表達真實意見時高度接近：
- Honestly, I disagree.
- Frankly, I disagree.

`frankly` 通常更直接。

## Honestly vs Truthfully
`truthfully` 處理 factual truth：
- answer truthfully
- report truthfully

## Honestly vs Sincerely
`sincerely` 處理 heartfelt feeling：
- sincerely apologise
- sincerely thank
- sincerely hope

## Honestly vs Candidly
`candidly` 較正式，適合：
- interviews
- professional feedback
- admissions
- discussions of failure

## Honestly vs Openly
`openly` 的核心是「不隱瞞」，不一定像 frankly 那麼直接。

## Honestly vs Genuinely
`genuinely` 表示感受／反應是真的：
- genuinely surprised
- genuinely interested
- genuinely care

## Honestly vs To be honest / In all honesty
兩者都是 **discourse markers**，不能機械取代句中的 `answer honestly`。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Honest vs honestly
- honest = adjective
- honestly = adverb

例如：
- She is honest.
- She answered honestly.

## 02 Sentence-initial honestly
句首：
- Honestly, I don't agree.

通常是話語標記，接近：
- To be honest, ...
- Frankly, ...
- In all honesty, ...

## 03 Honestly after a verb
- answer honestly
- speak honestly
- report honestly

通常更接近：
- truthfully
- candidly
- openly

視語境而定。

## 04 Sincerely
不要把 factual truth 和 heartfelt sincerity 混為一談：
- truthful answer
- sincere apology

## 05 Genuinely
最常修飾真實情感／反應：
- genuinely surprised
- genuinely interested
- genuinely grateful
