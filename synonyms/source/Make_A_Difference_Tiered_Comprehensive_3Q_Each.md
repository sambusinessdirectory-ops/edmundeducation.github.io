# MAKE A DIFFERENCE · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Make a difference

**「產生影響／起作用／帶來改變」，要先分清是在說某事『很重要』、造成可觀察影響、真正帶來改變，還是成為左右最終結果的關鍵因素。**

本單元集中處理 **make a difference = to have a noticeable effect, matter to an outcome, improve a situation, or cause meaningful change**。你提供的 **have an impact; matter; bring about change; have an effect** 分別代表 **影響、重要性、促成改變、效果** 四個核心方向。

這個詞組語義層次清楚但不需要為湊數加入太多弱表達，因此本次保留 **12 個**自然且具教學價值的詞／表達，共 **36 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**；正確答案位置在 A–F 之間平均分布。

## Tier 1 — Core impact / importance / change equivalents · 核心影響、重要性與改變近義詞

### 01 Have an impact

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：產生影響；帶來影響  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to affect a person, situation, result, or process in a noticeable way  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，當 make a difference 強調『造成明顯影響』時最直接。  
**常見搭配**：have an impact on; significant impact; positive impact; lasting impact; impact outcomes.  
**常見搭配對象**：policy, person, community, result, environment.  
**與 make a difference 的辨析**：Have an impact 比 make a difference 更正式、更結果導向。  
**使用限制**：如果重點是『是否重要／是否值得』，matter 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Small changes can have a real impact on energy use.  
小改變可以對能源使用產生實際影響。

### 02 Matter

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：重要；有關係；有影響  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to be important or to affect the outcome of a situation  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，當 make a difference 的意思是『這件事是重要的／有作用的』時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：really matter; matter to someone; matter in the long term; what matters; it matters.  
**常見搭配對象**：decision, detail, action, person, outcome.  
**與 make a difference 的辨析**：Matter 強調重要性或是否會影響結果，而不一定描述具體改變。  
**使用限制**：不能機械替換所有 make a difference，尤其是明確『改變了某事』的句子。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Every vote matters in a close election.  
在勢均力敵的選舉中，每一票都很重要。

### 03 Bring about change

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：帶來改變；促成變化  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to cause a change to happen  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，當 make a difference 明確表示『造成實際改變』時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：bring about change; bring about reform; bring about improvement; bring about social change; bring about results.  
**常見搭配對象**：policy, campaign, reform, initiative, action.  
**與 make a difference 的辨析**：Bring about change 明確表示因果和變化發生。  
**使用限制**：如果只是產生影響而沒有明確變化，不一定適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The campaign helped bring about change in local housing policy.  
這場運動促成了本地住房政策的改變。

### 04 Have an effect

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：產生效果；造成影響  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to cause a change or result in something  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是非常直接的近義表達，適合科學、政策、教育和日常語境。  
**常見搭配**：have an effect on; positive effect; little effect; measurable effect; long-term effect.  
**常見搭配對象**：health, behaviour, result, performance, environment.  
**與 make a difference 的辨析**：Have an effect 比 make a difference 更中性，也較容易和可量度結果搭配。  
**使用限制**：若強調個人意義或重要性，matter 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Regular feedback can have a positive effect on learning.  
定期回饋可以對學習產生正面效果。

### 05 Make an impact

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：產生顯著影響；留下影響  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：to produce a noticeable or memorable effect  
**用法提示**：當 make a difference 帶較強的『造成明顯影響』意味時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：make an impact on; make a positive impact; make an immediate impact; make a lasting impact.  
**常見搭配對象**：audience, community, organisation, result, field.  
**與 make a difference 的辨析**：Make an impact 比 have an impact 更動態，較強調『造成』影響。  
**使用限制**：若只是陳述存在某種影響，have an impact 更中性。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The new teacher quickly made an impact on the class.  
新老師很快便對班級產生了明顯影響。

## Tier 2 — Conversational / positive-change / contribution variants · 口語、正面改善與貢獻變體

### 06 Change things

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：改變局面；令事情不同  
**語域**：informal / conversational  
**核心語義**：to alter a situation in a meaningful or noticeable way  
**用法提示**：當 make a difference 用於口語，表示某件事真的令情況有所不同時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：change things for the better; really change things; change things significantly; change things around.  
**常見搭配對象**：situation, team, household, service, community.  
**與 make a difference 的辨析**：Change things 比 make a difference 更直接、更口語。  
**使用限制**：正式寫作通常改用 bring about change / have an impact。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
A few practical reforms could really change things.  
幾項實際改革可能真的改變局面。

### 07 Improve the situation

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：改善情況；令局面變好  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：to make a difficult or unsatisfactory situation better  
**用法提示**：當 make a difference 明確表示『帶來正面改善』時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：improve the situation; improve conditions; improve outcomes; improve matters; improve things.  
**常見搭配對象**：service, condition, problem, outcome, community.  
**與 make a difference 的辨析**：這個表達只適用於正面差異；make a difference 本身可正可負。  
**使用限制**：若句子只說『有影響』而未說變好，不宜使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Better staffing could improve the situation considerably.  
增加人手可以大幅改善情況。

### 08 Contribute

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：作出貢獻；有助於  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to help cause or improve something as one of several factors  
**用法提示**：當 make a difference 是『雖非唯一原因，但有實際貢獻』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：contribute to change; contribute to success; contribute significantly; contribute to improvement; make a contribution.  
**常見搭配對象**：success, improvement, outcome, community, project.  
**與 make a difference 的辨析**：Contribute 強調部分作用，而不是單獨造成整個改變。  
**使用限制**：若某因素是主要或唯一原因，bring about / cause 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Even a small donation can contribute to the programme's success.  
即使小額捐款也能為計劃成功作出貢獻。

### 09 Count

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：有作用；算數；重要  
**語域**：neutral / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：to be important or to have value in determining an outcome  
**用法提示**：當 make a difference 的意思接近『每一點努力都有作用』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：every bit counts; every vote counts; effort counts; what counts; count for something.  
**常見搭配對象**：effort, vote, contribution, detail, action.  
**與 make a difference 的辨析**：Count 和 matter 很接近，但更常出現在『每一點都算數』這類語境。  
**使用限制**：不能用於所有 affect / impact 類句子。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
When resources are limited, every contribution counts.  
資源有限時，每一份貢獻都有作用。

## Tier 3 — Consequential / outcome-shifting / decisive variants · 深遠後果、結果改變與決定性變體

### 10 Be consequential

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：具有重大後果；影響深遠  
**語域**：formal / academic  
**核心語義**：to have important or lasting consequences  
**用法提示**：當 make a difference 指某決定或事件帶來重大、長期後果時可用。  
**常見搭配**：consequential decision; consequential change; highly consequential; consequential effects; consequential event.  
**常見搭配對象**：decision, policy, event, change, development.  
**與 make a difference 的辨析**：比 make a difference 更正式、更強調重大後果。  
**使用限制**：小幅或日常影響通常不用 consequential。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The court ruling proved consequential for later reforms.  
法院判決後來對改革產生了深遠影響。

### 11 Shift the outcome

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：改變結果；扭轉結果  
**語域**：formal / descriptive  
**核心語義**：to alter the final result of a process, decision, competition, or event  
**用法提示**：當 make a difference 具體表示『令最後結果不同』時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：shift the outcome; alter the outcome; change the result; influence the outcome; determine the outcome.  
**常見搭配對象**：election, experiment, case, negotiation, decision.  
**與 make a difference 的辨析**：聚焦最終 outcome；make a difference 範圍更廣。  
**使用限制**：如果沒有明確結果變化，不宜使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
A small change in turnout could shift the outcome.  
投票率的小幅變化也可能改變結果。

### 12 Tip the balance

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：成為關鍵因素；左右結果  
**語域**：idiomatic / neutral  
**核心語義**：to provide the small but decisive influence that causes one side or outcome to prevail  
**用法提示**：當 make a difference 表示『最後那一點影響決定結果』時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：tip the balance; tip the scales; decisive factor; make the difference; swing the result.  
**常見搭配對象**：decision, competition, negotiation, vote, choice.  
**與 make a difference 的辨析**：比一般 make a difference 更強，表示決定性差異。  
**使用限制**：若影響只是一般性的，不宜使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
One strong recommendation could tip the balance in her favour.  
一封有力推薦信可能成為讓結果對她有利的關鍵因素。

## 04 False synonyms / make-a-difference traps · 容易誤選的詞與語義陷阱

### 01 Change

**改變不等於 make a difference**  
change 只表示不同；未必重要、未必有影響力。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Change can be trivial or neutral.

### 02 Help

**幫助不等於一定 make a difference**  
help 表示提供協助，但未必足以改變結果。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Help may contribute without changing the outcome.

### 03 Matter

**matter 只在『重要／有作用』sense 接近**  
matter 不一定表示實際發生了改變。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Matter is importance-focused.

### 04 Have an impact

**很接近，但較正式、更結果導向**  
它通常強調可觀察影響。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Impact is effect-focused.

### 05 Cause

**造成不等於所有 make a difference**  
cause 強調直接因果，語氣可能比 make a difference 更強。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Cause is more direct and causal.

### 06 Improve

**改善只適用於正面差異**  
make a difference 可以是正面、負面或中性。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Improvement is positive by definition.

### 07 Influence

**影響不等於一定改變結果**  
influence 可以很輕微，也未必足以改變最終結果。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Influence can be partial or subtle.

### 08 Difference / different

**詞性陷阱**  
difference 是名詞；different 是形容詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Difference is a noun; different is an adjective.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Have an impact · 產生影響；帶來影響

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The new policy made a difference to waiting times.

**中文翻譯**  
新政策對輪候時間產生了影響。

- A. Have an impact
- B. change
- C. help
- D. matter
- E. cause
- F. be irrelevant

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Have an impact** · 產生影響；帶來影響

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The new policy had an impact on waiting times.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** Have an impact ✓ — waiting times changed in a noticeable way; this makes **Have an impact** the clearest replacement.
- **B.** change — means make something different, but the change may be trivial or unrelated to importance; the sentence instead points to waiting times changed in a noticeable way.
- **C.** help — means assist, but assistance may not be strong enough to alter the outcome; the sentence instead points to waiting times changed in a noticeable way.
- **D.** matter — focuses importance rather than an observable effect or change; the sentence instead points to waiting times changed in a noticeable way.
- **E.** cause — states direct causation and is stronger/more mechanical than the target nuance; the sentence instead points to waiting times changed in a noticeable way.
- **F.** be irrelevant — means have no importance or effect, the opposite of make a difference; the sentence instead points to waiting times changed in a noticeable way.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **make a difference vs have an impact**: have an impact 更正式、更結果導向。
- **make a difference vs matter**: matter 強調重要性，不一定有具體改變。
- **make a difference vs bring about change**: 後者明確表示真正促成改變。
- **make a difference vs have an effect**: have an effect 更中性、較常見於可量度因果。
- **make a difference vs improve**: improve 只表示正面變好；make a difference 可正可負。
- **make a difference vs contribute**: contribute 通常只是眾多原因中的一部分。
- **make a difference vs count**: count 常見於 every vote/effort counts 類語境。
- **make a difference vs be consequential**: consequential 更正式、更強調重大長期後果。
- **make a difference vs shift the outcome**: shift the outcome 只聚焦最終結果改變。
- **make a difference vs tip the balance**: tip the balance 表示最後一個決定性因素。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & make-a-difference traps · 文法、搭配與語義陷阱

- **make a difference to + noun**：make a difference to learning / people's lives。
- **make a difference in + area/situation**：make a difference in the community / in practice。
- **make the difference** 常表示『成為決定性差異』：One vote made the difference。
- **have an impact on + noun**：have an impact on health / performance。
- **matter to + person** 或單獨使用：It matters / Every vote matters。
- **bring about + change/result**：bring about change / reform / improvement。
- **have an effect on + noun**：have an effect on behaviour / outcomes。
- **make an impact on + noun**：較動態、較強調 noticeable effect。
- **contribute to + noun/-ing**：contribute to success / improving outcomes。
- **every bit / vote / contribution counts** 是常見固定模式。
- **consequential** 是形容詞：a consequential decision；**consequence** 是名詞。
- **shift / alter the outcome** 聚焦最終結果。
- **tip the balance / tip the scales** 表示在勢均力敵時成為決定性因素。
