# LAUNDROMAT · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Laundromat

**「自助洗衣店」，要先分清是在談對外營業的自助洗衣店、住宅／旅舍內的共用洗衣房，還是由店員代洗代摺的 laundry service。**

本單元集中處理 **laundromat = a public self-service laundry equipped with washing machines and dryers that customers operate themselves**。你提供的 **self-service laundry; laundry shop** 分別代表 **最直接的自助洗衣近義表達** 和 **範圍較廣的洗衣店總稱**。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且具教學價值的詞／表達，共 **39 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。特別加入英式／香港語境很重要的 **launderette**，並清楚區分 **laundromat / laundry room / dry cleaner's / wash-and-fold / coin laundry**。

## Tier 1 — Core self-service / shop / British equivalents · 核心自助洗衣、洗衣店與英式近義詞

### 01 Self-service laundry

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：自助洗衣店；自助洗衣服務  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：a place where customers use coin-, card-, or app-operated washing machines and dryers themselves  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 laundromat 最直接、最透明的近義表達之一。  
**常見搭配**：self-service laundry; self-service laundry shop; self-service washing; self-service machines; laundry facilities.  
**常見搭配對象**：customer, washing machine, dryer, neighbourhood, building.  
**與 laundromat 的辨析**：Self-service laundry 清楚說明營運方式；laundromat 是較固定的店名式名詞。  
**使用限制**：若店舖由職員代洗，不能只稱 self-service laundry。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
There is a self-service laundry near the station.  
車站附近有一間自助洗衣店。

### 02 Laundry shop

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：洗衣店  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：a shop that provides washing, drying, pressing, or related laundry services  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 範圍較廣，可涵蓋自助或由店員提供服務的洗衣店。  
**常見搭配**：laundry shop; local laundry shop; neighbourhood laundry; laundry service; laundry business.  
**常見搭配對象**：clothes, customer, neighbourhood, service, washing.  
**與 laundromat 的辨析**：Laundry shop 比 laundromat 更廣，不一定是自助式。  
**使用限制**：若要明確表示自助洗衣，應用 self-service laundry / laundromat。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She dropped off her clothes at the laundry shop.  
她把衣服拿到洗衣店送洗。

### 03 Launderette

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：自助洗衣店  
**語域**：British English / common  
**核心語義**：a self-service laundry where customers wash and dry clothes using machines  
**用法提示**：英式英語非常重要，香港／英式語境可優先教授；美式英語較常說 laundromat。  
**常見搭配**：local launderette; coin-operated launderette; use the launderette; neighbourhood launderette; launderette machines.  
**常見搭配對象**：washing machine, dryer, customer, neighbourhood, laundry.  
**與 laundromat 的辨析**：Launderette 幾乎等同英式 laundromat。  
**使用限制**：美式使用者可能覺得較英式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He takes his bedding to the launderette every Sunday.  
他每星期日都把床上用品拿到自助洗衣店清洗。

### 04 Coin laundry

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：投幣式洗衣店；投幣自助洗衣  
**語域**：neutral / retail  
**核心語義**：a self-service laundry where washing machines and dryers are traditionally operated with coins  
**用法提示**：當 laundromat 特別強調投幣式設備時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：coin laundry; coin-operated laundry; coin-operated washer; coin laundry business; local coin laundry.  
**常見搭配對象**：washer, dryer, coin, customer, neighbourhood.  
**與 laundromat 的辨析**：Coin laundry 是 laundromat 的一種，但現代 laundromat 也可能使用卡或手機付款。  
**使用限制**：若完全無投幣設備，coin laundry 可能不精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The building has a coin laundry on the ground floor.  
大樓地下有一間投幣式自助洗衣店。

### 05 Laundry centre

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：洗衣中心；洗衣服務中心  
**語域**：neutral / business  
**核心語義**：a place providing laundry facilities or multiple laundry-related services  
**用法提示**：當 laundromat 規模較大，兼有自助、代洗、烘乾、摺衣等服務時可用。  
**常見搭配**：laundry centre; self-service laundry centre; community laundry centre; laundry facilities; full-service laundry centre.  
**常見搭配對象**：washing, drying, folding, customer, facility.  
**與 laundromat 的辨析**：Laundry centre 比 laundromat 更寬，可包括多種服務。  
**使用限制**：小型純自助店通常直接說 laundromat / launderette 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The new laundry centre offers washing, drying, and folding services.  
新的洗衣中心提供洗衣、烘乾和摺衣服務。

## Tier 2 — Facility / regional / descriptive variants · 設施、地區與描述型變體

### 06 Coin-operated laundry

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：投幣式自助洗衣店  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：a laundry facility equipped with coin-operated washing machines and dryers  
**用法提示**：比 coin laundry 更完整、描述性更強，適合物業、住宿和設施說明。  
**常見搭配**：coin-operated laundry; coin-operated machines; shared laundry facility; laundry room; laundry service.  
**常見搭配對象**：apartment, hostel, residence, building, facility.  
**與 laundromat 的辨析**：這是功能性描述，不一定是一間對外營業的街舖。  
**使用限制**：若設備以卡／手機付款，需改成 self-service laundry。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Residents have access to a coin-operated laundry in the basement.  
住客可使用地庫的投幣式自助洗衣設施。

### 07 Laundry facility

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：洗衣設施；洗衣設備區  
**語域**：formal / property / hospitality  
**核心語義**：a place or set of equipment provided for washing and drying clothes  
**用法提示**：酒店、宿舍、住宅大樓、醫院等設施語境中特別實用。  
**常見搭配**：laundry facility; on-site laundry facilities; shared laundry facilities; resident laundry; laundry amenities.  
**常見搭配對象**：hotel, hostel, residence, apartment, campus.  
**與 laundromat 的辨析**：Laundry facility 強調設施，而非商店身份。  
**使用限制**：街邊商業 laundromat 不一定會稱 facility。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The hostel provides a shared laundry facility for guests.  
旅舍為住客提供共用洗衣設施。

### 08 Wash-and-dry shop

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：洗衣烘乾店；洗烘店  
**語域**：informal / descriptive  
**核心語義**：a shop where customers can wash and dry clothes, often using self-service machines  
**用法提示**：是透明的描述性表達，適合說明 laundromat 的核心功能。  
**常見搭配**：wash-and-dry shop; washing and drying; self-service wash and dry; wash-and-fold; laundry shop.  
**常見搭配對象**：clothes, bedding, customer, washer, dryer.  
**與 laundromat 的辨析**：比 laundromat 更描述性、較少固定詞彙感。  
**使用限制**：不是所有地區都把它當固定店舖名稱。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The wash-and-dry shop stays open late for shift workers.  
這間洗衣烘乾店營業至深夜，方便輪班工人。

### 09 Washateria

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：自助洗衣店  
**語域**：regional US / informal  
**核心語義**：a regional American term for a self-service laundry  
**用法提示**：主要見於美國部分地區，尤其南部／西南部，是 laundromat 的地區變體。  
**常見搭配**：local washateria; coin washateria; neighbourhood washateria; self-service washateria; laundry machines.  
**常見搭配對象**：customer, washer, dryer, neighbourhood, laundry.  
**與 laundromat 的辨析**：語義接近 laundromat，但地域色彩很強。  
**使用限制**：國際英語與英式／香港語境通常不優先使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
There is a small washateria beside the supermarket.  
超級市場旁邊有一間小型自助洗衣店。

### 10 Laundry room

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：洗衣房；共用洗衣間  
**語域**：neutral / residential  
**核心語義**：a room containing washing machines and sometimes dryers, typically within a building  
**用法提示**：當 laundromat-like function 位於住宅、宿舍、酒店或大樓內部時很重要。  
**常見搭配**：shared laundry room; apartment laundry room; laundry-room machines; communal laundry room; basement laundry room.  
**常見搭配對象**：apartment, hostel, dormitory, hotel, residence.  
**與 laundromat 的辨析**：Laundry room 是房間／設施，不是通常對外營業的 laundromat。  
**使用限制**：街舖自助洗衣店通常不叫 laundry room。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The apartment building has a shared laundry room.  
這座住宅大樓有共用洗衣房。

## Tier 3 — Staffed-service / commercial variants · 代洗服務與商業模式變體

### 11 Wash-and-fold service

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：洗衣摺衣服務；代洗代摺服務  
**語域**：neutral / commercial  
**核心語義**：a laundry service in which staff wash, dry, and fold customers' clothes  
**用法提示**：很多 laundromat 會附設此服務；當重點不是自助而是交給店員處理時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：wash-and-fold service; drop-off laundry; laundry drop-off; same-day wash and fold; laundry service.  
**常見搭配對象**：clothes, bedding, customer, service, drop-off.  
**與 laundromat 的辨析**：Wash-and-fold service 是 laundromat 可能提供的服務，而不是店舖本身的完整同義詞。  
**使用限制**：不要用它取代純自助式店名。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The laundromat also offers a same-day wash-and-fold service.  
這間自助洗衣店亦提供即日代洗代摺服務。

### 12 Drop-off laundry

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：送洗服務；代洗店  
**語域**：neutral / commercial  
**核心語義**：a laundry service where customers leave clothes for staff to wash, dry, and possibly fold  
**用法提示**：當 laundromat 同時提供代客服務，或語境本身是『送洗』時可用。  
**常見搭配**：drop-off laundry; drop-off service; laundry drop-off; same-day laundry; service wash.  
**常見搭配對象**：clothes, bedding, customer, staff, order.  
**與 laundromat 的辨析**：Drop-off laundry 強調由店員代洗；laundromat 典型上是自助。  
**使用限制**：純自助店不可直接改稱 drop-off laundry。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She uses a drop-off laundry when she has too much washing to do.  
衣物太多時，她會使用送洗服務。

### 13 Self-service wash

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：自助洗衣服務；自助洗滌  
**語域**：informal / commercial  
**核心語義**：a self-service washing arrangement in which customers operate the machines themselves  
**用法提示**：可用於招牌、設施說明或簡短宣傳語，強調 self-service。  
**常見搭配**：self-service wash; self-service washing; wash and dry; self-service machines; laundry wash.  
**常見搭配對象**：clothes, bedding, washer, dryer, customer.  
**與 laundromat 的辨析**：這是服務描述，不是最標準的場所名詞。  
**使用限制**：正式詞彙學習仍應優先 laundromat / launderette / self-service laundry。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The shop offers self-service wash and dry from early morning until midnight.  
店舖由清晨至午夜提供自助洗衣及烘乾服務。

## 04 False synonyms / laundromat traps · 容易誤選的詞與自助洗衣概念陷阱

### 01 Dry cleaner's

**乾洗店不等於 laundromat**  
dry cleaner's 主要提供乾洗及專業衣物護理，通常不是自助洗衣。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A dry cleaner's provides professional cleaning, not self-service washing.

### 02 Laundry room

**洗衣房不等於街舖 laundromat**  
laundry room 通常是大樓內共用空間。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A laundry room is a room/facility inside a building.

### 03 Washing machine

**洗衣機不等於洗衣店**  
washing machine 是設備本身。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A washing machine is one appliance inside a laundromat.

### 04 Laundry service

**洗衣服務不等於 laundromat**  
laundry service 可指代洗、收送、酒店洗衣等多種服務。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Laundry service is broader than a laundromat.

### 05 Launderette

**英式近義詞，並非語義陷阱但有地區差異**  
英式英語常用 launderette，美式英語更常用 laundromat。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Launderette is the common British equivalent.

### 06 Coin laundry

**投幣式 laundromat 只是其中一種**  
現代自助洗衣店亦可能以卡或手機付款。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Coin laundry is a subtype based on payment method.

### 07 Wash-and-fold

**代洗代摺不等於自助洗衣店**  
wash-and-fold 是服務模式，顧客通常把衣物交給店員處理。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Wash-and-fold is a staffed service.

### 08 Laundry / laundromat

**詞義範圍陷阱**  
laundry 可指待洗衣物、洗衣活動、洗衣房或洗衣業務；laundromat 特指自助洗衣場所。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Laundry is much broader than laundromat.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **laundromat vs launderette**: 兩者幾乎等義；launderette 是常見英式英語，laundromat 更偏美式。
- **laundromat vs self-service laundry**: self-service laundry 是最透明的描述性改寫。
- **laundromat vs laundry shop**: laundry shop 更廣，可包括由職員提供服務。
- **laundromat vs laundry room**: laundry room 通常位於住宅、宿舍或酒店內。
- **laundromat vs dry cleaner's**: dry cleaner's 主要是專業乾洗，不是自助洗衣。
- **laundromat vs coin laundry**: coin laundry 只適用於投幣式付款模式。
- **laundromat vs wash-and-fold**: wash-and-fold 是店員代洗代摺服務。
- **laundromat vs drop-off laundry**: drop-off laundry 強調送洗後稍後取回。
- **laundromat vs laundry facility**: facility 強調設備／設施，而不一定是街舖。
- **laundromat vs washing machine**: washing machine 是設備，而 laundromat 是場所。

## 08 Grammar, regional usage & laundromat traps · 文法、地區用法與洗衣詞彙陷阱

- **laundromat** 是可數名詞：a laundromat / two laundromats。
- 美式英語常用 **laundromat**；英式英語常用 **launderette**。
- 香港英語若採英式慣例，**launderette / self-service laundry** 都很自然。
- **laundry shop** 是較廣總稱，不保證自助。
- **laundry room** 通常是住宅、宿舍、酒店或大樓內的洗衣空間。
- **dry cleaner's** 通常提供乾洗、熨燙及專業衣物護理，不等於 laundromat。
- **coin laundry / coin-operated laundry** 只適用於以硬幣操作的設備。
- 現代 laundromat 亦可能接受八達通類電子支付、信用卡或手機付款；所以 payment method 不屬 laundromat 的核心定義。
- **wash-and-fold service** / **service wash** 通常由職員替顧客洗、乾、摺。
- **drop-off laundry** 強調把衣物留下，由店員處理後再取回。
- **laundry** 本身可指待洗衣物、洗衣活動、洗衣房或洗衣業務，語義比 laundromat 廣很多。
- **do the laundry** = 洗衣；**take clothes to the laundromat** = 把衣物拿到自助洗衣店。
