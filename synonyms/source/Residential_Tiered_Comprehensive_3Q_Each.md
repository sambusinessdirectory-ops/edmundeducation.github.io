# RESIDENTIAL · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Residential

**Residential 主要有三個常見方向：住宅／住房相關、供家庭使用、以及土地或建築的住宅用途。** 這些義項看似接近，但搭配差異很大；例如 **domestic energy**, **housing development**, **for residential use**, **household waste** 都不能任意互換。

本單元集中處理 **residential = connected with homes, housing, private household use, or land/buildings intended for people to live in**。你提供的 **housing-related; domestic; for homes** 分別代表 **住房議題、家居／家庭使用、plain-English 用途改寫** 三個核心方向。

這個概念相對集中，因此本次保留 **8 個**真正有教學價值的近義／情境表達，而不是為了數量加入生硬詞。共 **24 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Residential 的 sense | 核心問題 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|---|
| 住房政策／住房問題 | 是否談 affordability, supply, development, policy？ | housing-related, housing |
| 家庭／家居使用 | 是否 private household use rather than commercial use？ | domestic, home-use, household |
| 供住宅使用 | 是否產品／系統是為 homes 設計？ | for homes |
| 正式土地／建築用途 | 是否 zoning / permission / conversion？ | for residential use |
| 技術性住宅單位 | 是否 regulation / individual dwelling unit？ | dwelling-related |

## Tier 1 — Core housing / household-use equivalents · 核心住房與家居用途近義表達

### 01 Housing-related

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：與住房／住宅有關的  
**語域**：neutral / policy / planning  
**核心語義**：connected with housing, homes, accommodation, or the provision and condition of places where people live  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，尤其適合 policy, cost, issue, development, service 等『與住房有關』的抽象語境。  
**常見搭配**：housing-related costs; housing-related issues; housing-related policy; housing-related support; housing-related development.  
**常見搭配對象**：cost, issue, policy, support, problem.  
**與 residential 的辨析**：Housing-related 比 residential 更像描述『與住房議題有關』，而不是直接形容土地／建築作住宅用途。  
**使用限制**：若是 zoning / land use / building use，通常 residential-use / residential 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The council introduced new measures to address housing-related costs.  
市議會推出新措施處理與住房有關的成本問題。

### 02 Domestic

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：家居的；住宅內的；家庭使用的  
**語域**：neutral / British English  
**核心語義**：connected with the home, household, or private household use rather than industrial or commercial use  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，尤其適合 domestic energy, domestic waste, domestic appliance, domestic property 等英式用法。  
**常見搭配**：domestic use; domestic property; domestic energy; domestic waste; domestic appliance.  
**常見搭配對象**：property, energy, use, appliance, waste.  
**與 residential 的辨析**：Domestic 常強調家庭／家居使用；residential 常強調住宅區、住宅用途或住宅物業。  
**使用限制**：residential neighbourhood / residential zoning 通常不能自然改成 domestic。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The tariff applies to domestic electricity customers.  
這個收費適用於住宅用電顧客。

### 03 For homes

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：供住宅使用的；為家庭而設的  
**語域**：neutral / plain English  
**核心語義**：intended for use in homes or designed for household residents  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是最透明的 plain-English 改寫，適合把 residential products/services/equipment 解釋得更直接。  
**常見搭配**：designed for homes; suitable for homes; intended for homes; services for homes; products for homes.  
**常見搭配對象**：product, service, equipment, system, development.  
**與 residential 的辨析**：比 residential 更口語、解釋性強，通常需要重組句子。  
**使用限制**：正式 zoning / planning 術語不宜改成 for homes。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The system is designed for homes rather than factories.  
這套系統是為住宅而非工廠設計。

### 04 Housing

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：住宅的；住房方面的  
**語域**：neutral / policy / development  
**核心語義**：used attributively to describe matters connected with homes, accommodation, residential supply, or housing provision  
**用法提示**：在 planning / policy / development 語境，housing 可自然替代部分 residential 搭配，如 housing development / housing project / housing policy。  
**常見搭配**：housing development; housing project; housing policy; housing market; housing supply.  
**常見搭配對象**：development, project, policy, market, supply.  
**與 residential 的辨析**：Housing 更偏供應、政策、開發與住房系統；residential 更偏用途、區域或建築性質。  
**使用限制**：residential street / residential address 不宜改成 housing street / housing address。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The site has been approved for a large housing development.  
該地點已獲批准興建大型住宅發展項目。

## Tier 2 — Planning / product / technical variants · 規劃、產品與技術型變體

### 05 For residential use

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：供住宅用途；作居住用途  
**語域**：formal / planning / legal  
**核心語義**：intended, permitted, or designated to be used as homes rather than for commercial, industrial, or other purposes  
**用法提示**：當 residential 出現在 planning permission, zoning, property use, conversion 等正式用途分類時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：approved for residential use; zoned for residential use; converted for residential use; designated for residential use.  
**常見搭配對象**：land, building, site, property, floor.  
**與 residential 的辨析**：這是用途型 formal paraphrase，特別適合 land-use / legal classification。  
**使用限制**：一般描述『住宅區很安靜』不會說 an area for residential use。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The upper floors are approved for residential use.  
上層樓面已獲批准作住宅用途。

### 06 Home-use

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：家用的；家居使用的  
**語域**：neutral / product / technical  
**核心語義**：intended to be used in a private home rather than in a professional, industrial, or commercial setting  
**用法提示**：適合 residential equipment/device/system 的產品用途語境。  
**常見搭配**：home-use equipment; home-use device; home-use system; home-use product; home-use appliance.  
**常見搭配對象**：device, equipment, system, product, appliance.  
**與 residential 的辨析**：Home-use 比 residential 更明確表示產品的 intended user setting。  
**使用限制**：地區、街道、土地用途不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The company sells both commercial and home-use water filters.  
公司同時銷售商用和家用濾水器。

### 07 Dwelling-related

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：與住宅單位／居所相關的  
**語域**：formal / technical / legal  
**核心語義**：connected specifically with a dwelling, residential unit, or place used as a home  
**用法提示**：適合 building regulation, property law, technical reporting 等需要把『住宅單位』說得很精確的語境。  
**常見搭配**：dwelling-related requirement; dwelling-related work; dwelling-related provision; dwelling-related regulation.  
**常見搭配對象**：requirement, work, regulation, provision, standard.  
**與 residential 的辨析**：Dwelling-related 比 residential 更 technical，也更聚焦 individual dwellings rather than neighbourhoods.  
**使用限制**：日常英語較生硬，不宜過度使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The guidance explains several dwelling-related safety requirements.  
指引解釋數項與住宅單位有關的安全要求。

## Tier 3 — Household-level contextual variant · 住戶層面的情境變體

### 08 Household

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：家庭的；住戶的  
**語域**：neutral / contextual  
**核心語義**：connected with the people living together in a home, or with consumption and activities at household level  
**用法提示**：當 residential 的實際焦點是住戶層面的 consumption, waste, spending, energy use 時，household 往往比 residential 更自然。  
**常見搭配**：household energy use; household waste; household spending; household consumption; household service.  
**常見搭配對象**：energy, waste, spending, consumption, income.  
**與 residential 的辨析**：Household 指 residents/consumption unit，不等於 residential property/area。  
**使用限制**：不能用於 residential building / residential district / residential zoning。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The survey measured household energy use during winter.  
調查量度住戶在冬季的能源使用量。

## 04 False synonyms / residential traps · 容易誤選的住宅、地理與用途陷阱

### 01 Commercial

**商業的 ≠ residential**  
commercial 與 residential 在土地／物業用途上通常形成對比。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Commercial and residential are contrasting land-use categories.

### 02 Industrial

**工業的 ≠ residential**  
industrial 指工業用途，不是住宅用途。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Industrial is a different use category.

### 03 Urban

**城市的 ≠ residential**  
urban 只表示城市環境；城市地區可同時包含住宅、商業和工業用途。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Urban is location-based, not use-based.

### 04 Suburban

**郊區的 ≠ residential**  
suburban 描述地理／城市形態，並不等於住宅用途。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Suburban and residential often overlap but are not synonyms.

### 05 Domestic

**只在家居／家庭使用 sense 接近**  
domestic 不適合 residential district / zoning 等典型規劃搭配。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Domestic is household-use focused.

### 06 Household

**只在住戶／家庭層面接近**  
household 指人或消費單位，不是建築／土地用途。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Household is people/consumption focused.

### 07 Accommodation

**住宿／住房 ≠ residential adjective**  
accommodation 是名詞，不能直接代替形容詞 residential。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Word class and meaning differ.

### 08 Residence / residential

**詞形陷阱**  
residence 是名詞；residential 是形容詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check word class before replacing.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Housing-related · 與住房／住宅有關的

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The report focuses on residential costs faced by low-income families.

**中文翻譯**  
報告聚焦低收入家庭面對的住宅相關成本。

- A. Housing-related
- B. Housing
- C. Domestic
- D. Urban
- E. Commercial
- F. Accommodation

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Housing-related** · 與住房／住宅有關的

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The report focuses on housing-related costs faced by low-income families.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Housing-related ✓** — means connected with housing issues, provision, affordability, accommodation or places where people live. Here, the topic is a policy/cost issue connected with housing rather than the use classification of a building. The collocation and sense are both natural, so **Housing-related** is the best replacement.
- **B.** **Housing** — used attributively, refers to the provision, development, supply or policy of homes. This is strongest for housing supply, policy or development; it is not the best fit when the sentence is about household use or a technical dwelling unit. The decisive clue here is that the topic is a policy/cost issue connected with housing rather than the use classification of a building.
- **C.** **Domestic** — in British English often means connected with private household use rather than commercial or industrial use. This is natural for household use, utilities and appliances, but not for every planning or housing-policy meaning of residential. The decisive clue here is that the topic is a policy/cost issue connected with housing rather than the use classification of a building.
- **D.** **Urban** — means located in or characteristic of a city and does not specify land use. This only says 'city-related'; a city area can be residential, commercial or industrial. The decisive clue here is that the topic is a policy/cost issue connected with housing rather than the use classification of a building.
- **E.** **Commercial** — means connected with business use and usually contrasts with residential use. This normally contrasts with residential use, so it reverses the intended category. The decisive clue here is that the topic is a policy/cost issue connected with housing rather than the use classification of a building.
- **F.** **Accommodation** — is a noun meaning lodging or housing and does not grammatically replace the adjective residential. This is a noun, so it cannot simply occupy an adjective slot such as residential costs or residential systems. The decisive clue here is that the topic is a policy/cost issue connected with housing rather than the use classification of a building.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **residential vs housing-related**: housing-related 適合住房政策／問題；residential 更常直接修飾 property/area/use。
- **residential vs domestic**: domestic 偏家庭／家居使用，英式英語常見於 energy, appliance, waste。
- **residential vs for homes**: for homes 是透明解釋性改寫，通常需重組句子。
- **residential vs housing**: housing 適合 development, supply, policy, project。
- **residential vs for residential use**: 後者是規劃／法律上的用途分類。
- **residential vs home-use**: home-use 主要是產品和設備用途。
- **residential vs dwelling-related**: dwelling-related 更 technical，聚焦個別住宅單位。
- **residential vs household**: household 指住戶／家庭單位，不是建築或土地用途。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & land-use traps · 文法、搭配與住宅用途陷阱

- **residential area / district / street**：住宅區／住宅街道；不要機械改成 domestic area。
- **residential property / building**：住宅物業／建築。
- **housing development / housing supply / housing policy**：比 residential development/supply 在政策語境往往更自然。
- **domestic energy / domestic waste / domestic appliance**：英式英語常見家居用途搭配。
- **approved / zoned / converted for residential use**：規劃、法律、用途變更。
- **home-use device / equipment**：產品面向家用市場。
- **household energy use / household spending / household waste**：住戶作為消費／統計單位。
- **dwelling-related requirement**：技術規例用語，日常英語通常不需要。
- **urban / suburban** 是地理描述，不等於 residential。
- **commercial / industrial** 在 land-use 語境通常與 residential 對比。
- **residence** 是名詞；**residential** 是形容詞。
- 英式／香港用法可自然使用 **housing, domestic, organisation, programme** 等拼寫與搭配。
