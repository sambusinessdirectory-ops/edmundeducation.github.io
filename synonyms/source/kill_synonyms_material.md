# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Kill

**導致死亡、殺死、謀殺、暗殺、處決、屠殺、消滅——究竟是哪一種 kill？**

本課把 `kill` 分成幾個核心 sense：

1. **cause death** — 事故、疾病、災害等導致死亡  
2. **intentional killing** — 有意殺害  
3. **murder / assassination / execution** — 法律、政治、權力背景下的特定殺害  
4. **mass killing / slaughter** — 大量或殘酷殺害  
5. **figurative / non-human destruction** — 病原、物種、事業、計劃等被消滅／摧毀

核心近義與近義表達：

**cause the death of; slay; murder; assassinate; execute; slaughter; put to death; take a life; destroy; eliminate; wipe out**

> **Precision rule:**  
> `murder` ≠ 一般 kill：必須是非法、蓄意殺人。  
> `assassinate` = 重要人物的有計劃暗殺。  
> `execute` = 正式處決。  
> `slaughter` = 大量／殘酷殺害。  
> `destroy` = 主要是非人／比喻 sense。  
> `wipe out` = 徹底消滅整群／整類。

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Cause the death of

**導致……死亡；令……喪生**

和 `kill` 最中性、最客觀的改寫之一，特別適合事故、疾病、災害、毒物或其他沒有刻意施害者的語境。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The disease can cause the death of vulnerable patients.

這種疾病可導致脆弱病人死亡。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- cause the death of

- result in death

- lead to death

- fatal to

## 02 Slay

**殺死；殺戮**

較文學、歷史或戲劇化，常見於傳說、戰爭、史詩或新聞標題。日常中通常比 `kill` 更具修辭色彩。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The hero slew the dragon in the legend.

英雄在傳說中殺死了巨龍。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- slay a dragon

- slay an enemy

- be slain

- legendary slayer

**謀殺；蓄意殺害**

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The man was accused of murdering his neighbour deliberately.

該男子被控蓄意謀殺鄰居。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- murder a victim

- be murdered

- murder investigation

- premeditated murder

## 04 Assassinate

**暗殺**

指因政治、公共身份、權力或象徵地位而有計劃地殺害重要人物。不能泛指一般 murder。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The plot aimed to assassinate the head of state.

陰謀旨在暗殺國家元首。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- assassinate a leader

- political assassination

- assassination plot

- be assassinated

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The prisoner was executed after the court imposed the death penalty.

**屠殺；大量殺害**

### EXAMPLE · 例句

- be slaughtered

## 07 Put to death

**處死；使死亡**

較正式、委婉，常指依法、依命令或有意使某人死亡。比 `kill` 更書面。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

## 08 Take a life

**奪去生命；取人性命**

常見委婉／情感化表達，強調一條生命被奪去。可用於事故或蓄意行為，但語氣比 `kill` 更含蓄。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- take a life

- claim lives

## 09 Destroy

**摧毀；毀掉**

只在 `kill` 的非生物／比喻 sense 接近，例如 destroy a virus, destroy a career, destroy a plan。不能把一般『殺人』改成 destroy。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The treatment is designed to destroy harmful bacteria.

這種治療旨在消滅有害細菌。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- destroy bacteria

- destroy a career

- destroy a building

- destroy completely


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

## 10 Eliminate

**消滅；清除**

在正式、技術或委婉語境中可表示徹底除去威脅、病原或敵對力量；對人使用時常是委婉或軍事化表達，需依上下文判斷。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

新程序可消滅大部分有害微生物。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- eliminate a virus

- eliminate a threat

- eliminate risk

- eliminate completely


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

## 11 Wipe out

**消滅；徹底摧毀**

較口語、強烈，常指整個群體、物種、疾病或事物被完全消滅。通常比單純 `kill` 的範圍更大。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The disease nearly wiped out the entire population of the species.

疾病幾乎消滅整個物種族群。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- wipe out a population

- wipe out a disease

- wipe out crops

- nearly wipe out

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Murder

**murder 只對非法蓄意殺人**

事故、疾病、自然災害可以 kill，但不能 murder。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The crash killed two people; it did not murder them.

---

## 02 Execute

**execute 只對正式處決 sense**

需要權力、命令、判決或處決背景。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A court may order someone to be executed; an accident simply kills.

---

## 03 Assassinate

**assassinate 只對重要人物的有計劃暗殺**

通常和政治、公共身份、象徵地位有關。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A political leader may be assassinated; an ordinary accidental death is not assassination.

---

## 04 Slaughter

**通常更強烈／大量**

常帶殘酷、大量殺害或屠宰意味。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Killing one insect is not normally called slaughter.

---

## 05 Destroy

**對人不是一般直接同義詞**

destroy 適合物體、病原、制度、事業等，也可比喻。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The fire destroyed the building; it killed two occupants.

---

## 06 Die

**主客體方向不同**

`die` 是不及物動詞：某人自己死亡；`kill` 是及物動詞：某事使其死亡。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

He died in the crash. / The crash killed him.

---

## 07 Hurt

**傷害不等於殺死**

hurt 表示造成疼痛或傷害，未必死亡。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The fall hurt him but did not kill him.

---

## 08 Defeat

**擊敗不等於殺死**

在比賽、戰爭或競爭中 defeat 只表示勝過對方。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The team defeated its opponent; no one was killed.

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Kill vs die
最重要語法區別：

- kill = 及物：X kills Y
- die = 不及物：Y dies

Example:
`The crash killed two people.`  
`Two people died in the crash.`

## 02 Murder
只對人，而且核心是：
- intentional
- unlawful

所以：
`The storm murdered three people.` ✗

## 03 Assassinate
通常對：
- political leaders
- public figures
- officials
- symbolically important people

## 04 Execute
常見於：
- death penalty
- military orders
- state punishment
- historical regimes

## 05 Slay
常見於：
- legends
- fantasy
- historical narratives
- dramatic headlines

## 06 Destroy / eliminate / wipe out
這組詞最常用於：
- bacteria
- viruses
- pests
- species/populations
- systems
- plans
- careers
- threats

不應把它們當作一般「殺人」的中性直接同義詞。

## 07 Take a life
比 `kill` 更委婉、較具情感：
- an accident took two lives
- no one has the right to take a life
