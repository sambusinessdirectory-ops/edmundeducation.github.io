# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Actors

**演員、表演者、劇中演出人員——哪些詞真的接近 actors？**

這份材料聚焦 **actors = people who perform roles in films, television, theatre, or other dramatic productions** 的意思。

這次採用較嚴格的原則：**只保留與 actors 在實際語境中相當接近、可互換程度較高的詞或短語**。  
因此不刻意湊足 14 個，也不把「導演、藝人、明星、角色人物」等只屬相關概念的詞當作同義詞。

核心近義表達包括：

**performers, players, cast members, members of the cast, thespians, stage performers, screen performers, dramatic performers**

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## Actors 的 8 種高度相關近義說法

---

## 01 Performers

**表演者；演出人員**

最廣泛而自然的近義詞之一。可以指演員，也可以包括歌手、舞者、喜劇演員等，因此範圍比 *actors* 稍廣。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The performers received a standing ovation at the end of the play.

演出人員在話劇結束時獲得全場起立鼓掌。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- talented performers
- stage performers
- professional performers
- live performers

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否可以把演員視為「進行表演的人」，而不需要強調戲劇角色本身？

---

## 02 Players

**演員；演出者**

較傳統的說法，可指戲劇演員，尤其常見於劇團、舞台表演或較文學化語境。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The players returned to the stage for a final bow.

演員們回到舞台上作最後謝幕。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- the players
- principal players
- supporting players
- company of players

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這是否是較傳統、舞台化或文學化地稱呼演員？

---

## 03 Cast members

**演員陣容中的成員；劇組演員**

表示某一齣電影、電視劇、舞台劇或節目的演員之一。和 *actors* 很接近，但特別強調**屬於某個 cast**。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Several cast members attended the premiere.

幾位演員出席了首映禮。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- cast members
- main cast members
- original cast members
- supporting cast members

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是在談某一部作品的演員陣容成員？

---

## 04 Members of the cast

**演員陣容中的成員**

和 *cast members* 幾乎完全相同，只是形式更完整、更自然於正式敘述。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

All members of the cast were present for the final rehearsal.

所有演員都出席了最後一次綵排。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- members of the cast
- all members of the cast
- several members of the cast

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 是否想用較完整的方式表達「劇中演員成員」？

---

## 05 Thespians

**演員；戲劇演員**

較文學化、幽默或帶傳統戲劇色彩的詞。通常指舞台或戲劇演員，不如 *actors* 普通日常。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The festival brought together thespians from theatres across the country.

這個節慶匯聚了來自全國不同劇院的戲劇演員。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- veteran thespians
- young thespians
- celebrated thespians

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否適合較戲劇化、文學化或帶玩味地說「演員」？

---

## 06 Stage performers

**舞台表演者；舞台演員**

當 *actors* 明確指劇院、話劇、音樂劇等現場舞台演出者時，可以使用。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Stage performers must project their voices clearly without relying on microphones.

舞台演員必須清楚地投射聲音，不能完全依賴咪高峰。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- professional stage performers
- experienced stage performers
- live stage performers

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 actors 是否明確是在舞台上現場演出？

---

## 07 Screen performers

**影視演員；銀幕表演者**

表示在電影、電視或其他螢幕媒體中演出的演員。比 *actors* 更強調媒介。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Screen performers often work differently from stage actors.

影視演員的表演方式往往與舞台演員不同。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- screen performers
- film and television performers
- experienced screen performers

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否特別指電影或電視中的演員？

---

## 08 Dramatic performers

**戲劇表演者；戲劇演員**

較正式，指進行戲劇表演的人。語義很接近 *actors*，但日常使用頻率較低。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The programme trains dramatic performers in voice, movement, and character work.

這個課程訓練戲劇演員的聲線、動作和角色塑造。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- dramatic performers
- trained dramatic performers
- professional dramatic performers

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是在正式地談戲劇表演專業中的演員？

---

# 02 False synonyms

以下詞語和 **actors** 很相關，但不應直接當作同義詞。

---

## 01 Characters

**角色不等於演員**

*Character* 是故事中的人物；*actor* 是扮演該人物的人。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The **character** is a detective.  
The **actor** playing him is from Ireland.

---

## 02 Stars

**明星不等於所有演員**

*Star* 指特別知名、重要或主角級的演員；很多 actors 並不是 stars。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The film features several young **actors**.  
Its biggest **star** appears only briefly.

---

## 03 Entertainers

**藝人不等於演員**

*Entertainer* 範圍更廣，可以是歌手、主持人、魔術師、喜劇演員等。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Actors are one type of **performer**.  
An **entertainer** does not necessarily act in dramatic roles.

---

## 04 Artists

**藝術工作者不等於演員**

*Artist* 可以指畫家、音樂家、舞者、創作者等，範圍遠比 actor 廣。

---

## 05 Celebrities

**名人不等於演員**

*Celebrity* 的核心是知名度，而不是職業。名人可以是運動員、歌手、網紅或演員。

---

## 06 Crew members

**幕後工作人員不等於演員**

*Crew* 通常指導演、攝影、燈光、化妝、音響等幕後工作人員；*cast* 才是演員陣容。

---

## 07 Extras

**臨時演員／群眾演員不是所有 actors 的一般同義詞**

*Extras* 是沒有主要對白或角色的重要背景演員，只是 actor 的一個子類。

---

## 08 Leads

**主演不等於所有演員**

*Lead* 指主要角色的演員；只是整個 cast 中的一部分。

---

# 03 練習與解釋

> 每個核心近義詞兩題，共 16 題。  
> 題目集中於高互換度和語域差異。

---

## 練習 01.1

**Target word:** Performers

The actors received loud applause at the end of the show.

**Answer:** The **performers** received loud applause at the end of the show.

- performers ✓ — 自然泛指演出人員。
- characters — 是角色，不是演員。
- celebrities — 強調名氣。
- crew members — 幕後工作人員。
- leads — 只指主演。
- extras — 只指背景演員。

---

## 練習 01.2

**Target word:** Performers

The actors had to rehearse the dance sequence several times.

**Answer:** The **performers** had to rehearse the dance sequence several times.

- performers ✓ — 演員作為表演者很自然。
- stars — 不一定都是明星。
- artists — 範圍太廣。
- crew — 不是表演者。
- characters — 是劇中人物。
- extras — 範圍太窄。

---

## 練習 02.1

**Target word:** Players

The actors returned to the stage for another bow.

**Answer:** The **players** returned to the stage for another bow.

- players ✓ — 傳統舞台語境自然。
- performers — 也可，但較一般。
- stars — 不一定是明星。
- characters — 不合。
- crew — 不合。
- celebrities — 不合。

---

## 練習 02.2

**Target word:** Players

The theatre company employed twelve actors for the production.

**Answer:** The theatre company employed twelve **players** for the production.

- players ✓ — 劇團語境可用，略傳統。
- entertainers — 範圍較廣。
- leads — 只指主演。
- extras — 只指群演。
- characters — 不是人員職業。
- celebrities — 不表示戲劇職能。

---

## 練習 03.1

**Target word:** Cast members

Several actors attended the television drama's launch event.

**Answer:** Several **cast members** attended the television drama's launch event.

- cast members ✓ — 指這一部劇的演員成員。
- performers — 可用但沒有「屬於該劇 cast」的精確度。
- stars — 可能過度縮窄。
- crew members — 幕後。
- characters — 虛構人物。
- artists — 太廣。

---

## 練習 03.2

**Target word:** Cast members

The actors spent six months filming the final season.

**Answer:** The **cast members** spent six months filming the final season.

- cast members ✓ — 明確是該劇演員陣容。
- entertainers — 過廣。
- celebrities — 不一定知名。
- leads — 只包括主要演員。
- crew — 不同群體。
- characters — 不是演員本人。

---

## 練習 04.1

**Target word:** Members of the cast

**Answer:** All **members of the cast** attended the final rehearsal.

- members of the cast ✓ — 完整、自然。
- performers — 可用但較廣。
- crew members — 不等於演員。
- characters — 不合。
- stars — 只可能部分成立。
- extras — 只是一部分。

**Target word:** Members of the cast

**Answer:** Three **members of the cast** were absent because of illness.

- members of the cast ✓ — 清楚表示作品的演員。

The festival celebrates actors from independent theatre companies.

Several veteran actors shared stories about their early years on stage.

**Answer:** Unlike film actors, these **stage performers** perform in front of a live audience every night.

**Target word:** Screen performers

**Answer:** **Screen performers** often repeat the same scene from several camera angles.

- screen performers ✓ — 電影／鏡頭語境精確。
- stage performers — 媒介不同。
- players — 太傳統且不突出影視。
- entertainers — 太廣。
- celebrities — 強調知名度。

## 練習 07.2

**Target word:** Screen performers

Television actors may work on several episodes in the same week.

**Answer:** **Screen performers** may work on several episodes in the same week.

- screen performers ✓ — 電視演員屬影視表演者。
- thespians — 偏舞台／文學。
- stage performers — 不合。
- crew members — 幕後。
- leads — 只指主角。
- extras — 只指背景演員。

## 練習 08.1

**Target word:** Dramatic performers

The school trains actors in voice, movement, and character development.

**Answer:** The school trains **dramatic performers** in voice, movement, and character development.

- dramatic performers ✓ — 正式戲劇訓練語境。
- entertainers — 過廣。
- celebrities — 不合。
- characters — 不合。
- crew members — 不合。
- stars — 不合。

---

## 練習 08.2

**Target word:** Dramatic performers

Professional actors must often adapt their technique to different performance spaces.

**Answer:** Professional **dramatic performers** must often adapt their technique to different performance spaces.

- dramatic performers ✓ — 正式概括戲劇演員。
- screen performers — 過度限定影視。

# 04 核心對比表

| 詞語 | 與 actors 的接近程度 | 主要差異 |
|---|---:|---|
| **performers** | 很高 | 範圍稍廣，可包括歌手、舞者等 |
| **players** | 高 | 較傳統、舞台化 |
| **cast members** | 很高 | 強調屬於某一作品的演員陣容 |
| **members of the cast** | 很高 | 與 cast members 幾乎相同 |
| **thespians** | 高 | 文學化、戲劇化、較少日常使用 |
| **stage performers** | 高 | 只適用舞台演員 |
| **screen performers** | 高 | 只適用電影／電視演員 |
| **dramatic performers** | 高 | 較正式、頻率較低 |

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Actors vs Performers

**Actors**
- 專指扮演角色的人

**Performers**
- 範圍較廣
- 演員、歌手、舞者等都可以是 performers

---

## Actors vs Cast members

**Actors**
- 可以泛指演員這個職業群體

**Cast members**
- 必須屬於某一具體作品的 cast

---

## Actors vs Players

**Actors**
- 現代最普通說法

**Players**
- 傳統戲劇／劇團用法較常見
- 現代日常語境頻率較低

---

## Actors vs Thespians

**Actors**
- 中性、普通

**Thespians**
- 文學化、戲劇化、偶爾幽默
- 尤其容易讓人聯想到舞台戲劇

---

## Stage performers vs Screen performers

**Stage performers**
- 現場舞台
- theatre / musical / live drama

**Screen performers**
- 電影、電視、鏡頭媒體
