# TOUGHEN · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Toughen

**Toughen 可以表示『使更強、更耐受、更有韌性、更硬、更耐用，或使人適應艱苦環境』。** 這些 sense 不能完全互換。替換前要判斷：是要增加 **strength, resilience, hardness, structural support, protection, conditioning, durability,** 還是 **robustness**？

你提供的 **strengthen; make more resilient** 正好代表兩個最核心方向：**一般強化** 與 **提升韌性／復原力**。

本次保留 **9 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達，共 **27 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Toughen 的語境 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|
| 一般變強 | **strengthen** |
| 提升抗衝擊／復原力 | **make more resilient** |
| 使物料變硬 | **harden** |
| 加入支撐／加固 | **reinforce** |
| 加強防護 | **fortify** |
| 逐步增強能力／耐受 | **build up** |
| 訓練適應環境 | **condition** |
| 提升耐磨耐用 | **improve durability** |
| 提升系統穩健性 | **make more robust** |

## Tier 1 — Core strengthening / resilience / hardening equivalents

### 01 Strengthen

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：加強；強化；使更強韌  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：make something stronger, more effective, or better able to withstand pressure  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **toughen** 最直接、最廣泛的近義詞之一；strengthen 偏一般『變強』，不一定帶耐受衝擊／逆境的意味。  
**常見搭配**：strengthen muscles; strengthen a structure; strengthen resistance; strengthen skills  
**常見搭配對象**：muscles, material, structure, system, ability  
**與 toughen 的辨析**：你提供的 seed 是 **toughen** 最直接、最廣泛的近義詞之一；strengthen 偏一般『變強』，不一定帶耐受衝擊／逆境的意味。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Regular exercise can strengthen the muscles around the knee.  
規律運動可強化膝部周圍的肌肉。

### 02 Make more resilient

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：使更有韌性；提升復原力  
**語域**：neutral / explanatory  
**核心語義**：increase the ability to withstand difficulty, stress, damage, or disruption and recover  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很精準，當 toughen 強調 resilience / recovery rather than raw strength 時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：make systems more resilient; build resilience; resilient communities; resilient materials  
**常見搭配對象**：system, community, material, organisation, person  
**與 toughen 的辨析**：你提供的 seed 很精準，當 toughen 強調 resilience / recovery rather than raw strength 時最自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The upgrades are intended to make the network more resilient to outages.  
這些升級旨在提升網絡面對故障時的韌性。

### 03 Harden

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：使變硬；使更耐受  
**語域**：neutral / technical  
**核心語義**：make physically harder or less easily damaged, or figuratively less sensitive  
**用法提示**：Harden 更偏物理硬度、防護或抗攻擊能力；toughen 更廣，可指韌性、耐用性或心理適應。  
**常見搭配**：harden steel; harden a surface; hardened material; harden against attack  
**常見搭配對象**：steel, surface, coating, system, attitude  
**與 toughen 的辨析**：Harden 更偏物理硬度、防護或抗攻擊能力；toughen 更廣，可指韌性、耐用性或心理適應。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Heat treatment is used to harden the steel surface.  
熱處理用來使鋼材表面變硬。

### 04 Reinforce

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：加固；強化  
**語域**：neutral / technical  
**核心語義**：make something stronger by adding support, material, or emphasis  
**用法提示**：Reinforce 常暗示加入支撐／額外力量；toughen 可是不加新部件而提升耐受力。  
**常見搭配**：reinforce a wall; reinforce a structure; reinforce protection; reinforce training  
**常見搭配對象**：wall, structure, barrier, message, skill  
**與 toughen 的辨析**：Reinforce 常暗示加入支撐／額外力量；toughen 可是不加新部件而提升耐受力。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Engineers reinforced the bridge before reopening it to heavy traffic.  
工程師在重新開放橋樑予重型車輛前先進行加固。

### 05 Fortify

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：加強防護；鞏固  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：strengthen and protect something against attack, damage, pressure, or weakness  
**用法提示**：Fortify 比 toughen 更正式，也常帶防禦／保護意味。  
**常見搭配**：fortify defences; fortify a position; fortify a system; fortified structure  
**常見搭配對象**：defence, position, system, building, body  
**與 toughen 的辨析**：Fortify 比 toughen 更正式，也常帶防禦／保護意味。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The company fortified its cybersecurity after the attack.  
公司在遭受攻擊後加強了網絡保安防護。

## Tier 2 — Gradual / conditioning / durability variants

### 06 Build up

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：逐步增強；建立  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：increase strength, capacity, tolerance, or resilience gradually over time  
**用法提示**：Build up 強調 gradual development；toughen 可以更概括地表示變得更能承受壓力。  
**常見搭配**：build up strength; build up stamina; build up resistance; build up tolerance  
**常見搭配對象**：strength, stamina, resistance, tolerance, capacity  
**與 toughen 的辨析**：Build up 強調 gradual development；toughen 可以更概括地表示變得更能承受壓力。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

這個訓練計劃幫助跑手逐步增強耐力。

### 07 Condition

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：鍛鍊；使適應；調整至能承受  
**語域**：neutral / training / technical  
**核心語義**：train or adapt a person, body, material, or system so it performs better under particular conditions  
**用法提示**：Condition 強調訓練／適應特定環境或負荷；不是所有 toughen 都可換成 condition。  
**常見搭配**：condition muscles; condition the body; conditioned response; condition for heat  
**常見搭配對象**：body, muscle, athlete, material, system  
**與 toughen 的辨析**：Condition 強調訓練／適應特定環境或負荷；不是所有 toughen 都可換成 condition。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

這個計劃訓練新入職人員適應極端炎熱環境。

### 08 Improve durability

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：提升耐用性  
**語域**：formal / engineering / product  
**核心語義**：increase the ability of a material, product, or structure to resist wear, damage, or repeated use  
**用法提示**：這是 product/material context 的精確改寫；toughen 若指讓物料更耐磨耐損，常可如此表達。  
**常見搭配**：improve durability; increase durability; durable material; long-term durability  
**常見搭配對象**：material, product, coating, component, structure  
**與 toughen 的辨析**：這是 product/material context 的精確改寫；toughen 若指讓物料更耐磨耐損，常可如此表達。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

**Tier**：3

**語域**：formal / technical / explanatory  
**核心語義**：make something better able to withstand stress, failure, variation, or demanding use  
**用法提示**：這是 contextual paraphrase，特別適合 systems/design/process；比 toughen 更 technical。  
**常見搭配**：robust system; robust design; robust process; robust material  
**常見搭配對象**：system, design, process, structure, method  
**與 toughen 的辨析**：這是 contextual paraphrase，特別適合 systems/design/process；比 toughen 更 technical。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

## 04 False synonyms / toughen traps

### 01 Strengthen

**較廣，不一定等於 toughen**  
strengthen 只要求變強，不一定涉及耐衝擊、耐磨或復原力。

### 02 Harden

**只在變硬／抗攻擊 sense 接近**  
harden 可能增加硬度，但硬度與 toughness 並不是完全相同的材料性質。

### 03 Repair

**修復 ≠ toughen**  
repair 是把已損壞部分恢復，不等於提升未來耐受能力。

### 04 Protect

**保護 ≠ toughen**  
protect 可透過外部防護做到，不一定讓本體變得更強韌。

### 05 Train

**訓練 ≠ toughen**  
train 是過程，只有在提升耐受力的情境才可能相關。

### 06 Reinforce

**加固 ≠ 所有 toughen**  
reinforce 常加入支撐或額外材料。

### 07 Resilient

**形容詞 ≠ toughen**  
resilient 是形容詞；make more resilient 才能對應動詞 toughen。

### 08 Tough / toughen / toughness

**詞形陷阱**  
tough 是形容詞；toughen 是動詞；toughness 是名詞。

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Strengthen · 加強；強化；使更強韌

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The new exercises are designed to toughen the muscles around the ankle.

**中文翻譯**  
新運動旨在強化腳踝周圍的肌肉。

- A. Strengthen
- B. Reinforce
- C. Build up
- D. Repair
- E. Protect
- F. Weaken

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Strengthen** · 加強；強化；使更強韌

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The new exercises are designed to strengthen the muscles around the ankle.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Strengthen ✓** — make stronger or more effective. Here, general increase in physical strength is central. This makes **Strengthen** the most precise replacement for this sense of *toughen*.
- **B.** **Reinforce** — make stronger by adding support or extra strength. It usually implies added support or extra structural strength. The decisive clue is that general increase in physical strength is central.
- **C.** **Build up** — increase strength, tolerance, or capacity gradually. It specifically suggests gradual development over time. The decisive clue is that general increase in physical strength is central.
- **D.** **Repair** — restore something that is damaged. It restores existing damage rather than increasing future resistance. The decisive clue is that general increase in physical strength is central.
- **E.** **Protect** — keep safe from harm, often through external measures. It can keep something safe externally without making the thing itself tougher. The decisive clue is that general increase in physical strength is central.
- **F.** **Weaken** — make less strong. It is the opposite meaning. The decisive clue is that general increase in physical strength is central.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **toughen vs strengthen**：strengthen 更廣，只表示變強。
- **toughen vs make more resilient**：後者強調承受衝擊及恢復能力。
- **toughen vs harden**：harden 強調硬度，不等於所有 toughness。
- **toughen vs reinforce**：reinforce 常表示加入支撐。
- **toughen vs fortify**：fortify 偏防禦／保護。
- **toughen vs build up**：build up 強調逐步增強。
- **toughen vs condition**：condition 強調訓練和適應特定環境。
- **toughen vs improve durability**：後者偏產品／物料耐用性。
- **toughen vs make more robust**：後者偏系統、設計、流程的穩健性。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & toughness traps

- **toughen + noun**：toughen the material / system / rules。
- **toughen up**：較口語，可指使人變得更堅強或更能吃苦。
- **strengthen + noun**：strengthen muscles / protection / capacity。
- **make + noun + more resilient**：make the network more resilient。
- **harden + material/surface**：harden steel / a coating。
- **reinforce + structure/barrier**：reinforce a wall。
- **build up + strength/stamina/tolerance/resistance**。
- **condition + person/body + for + conditions**。
- **improve durability** 常見於 materials / products / engineering。
- **tough** 是形容詞；**toughen** 是動詞；**toughness** 是名詞。
