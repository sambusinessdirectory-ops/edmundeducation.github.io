# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Fuss (noun)

**`fuss` 可以表示騷動、麻煩、過度擔心、不必要的忙亂、繁瑣折騰、引起議論、沒完沒了的程序，或反應過大。不同替代表達的核心差別，是『commotion / inconvenience / excessive concern / needless ado / hassle / public stir / palaver / overreaction』。**

核心替代表達：

`commotion / bother / excessive concern / ado / hassle / stir / palaver / overreaction`

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Commotion

**騷動；混亂；喧鬧**

當 `fuss` 指一陣騷動、吵鬧或混亂，尤其很多人同時反應時，`commotion` 很自然。它比 fuss 更偏 outward disturbance。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The announcement caused a commotion in the hallway.

這項宣布在走廊引起一陣騷動。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a noisy disturbance involving several people or reactions**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、新聞、書面

---

## 02 Bother

**麻煩；費事；煩擾**

當 `fuss` 指一件事帶來麻煩、費事或令人煩惱時，`bother` 很自然。它比 commotion 更偏 inconvenience rather than noise。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> It was too much bother to move all the furniture again.

再次搬動所有家具實在太麻煩。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **trouble, inconvenience, or unnecessary effort**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、口語

---

## 03 Excessive concern

**過度擔心；過分在意**

當 `fuss` 指對小事過度擔心、過分關注或反應太大時，`excessive concern` 是非常清楚的正式改寫。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> There was excessive concern about a minor delay.

大家對一次輕微延誤過度擔心。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **more concern than the situation reasonably requires**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、描述性

---

## 04 Ado

**忙亂；大驚小怪；騷動**

較書面或略帶舊式色彩，尤其常見於 `without further ado`。當 `fuss` 指不必要的忙亂或大驚小怪時，`ado` 很貼切。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Without further ado, the chair began the meeting.

主席沒有再多作拖延，隨即開始會議。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **unnecessary activity, delay, or fuss**

**REGISTER · 語域**

書面、固定表達

---

## 05 Hassle

**麻煩；繁瑣；折騰**

當 `fuss` 指辦一件事很麻煩、程序繁瑣、令人厭煩時，`hassle` 很自然。它比 bother 更口語，也更強調 annoying inconvenience。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Returning the item was more hassle than I expected.

退貨比我預期中更麻煩。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **annoying difficulty or inconvenience**

**REGISTER · 語域**

口語、一般

---

## 06 Stir

**騷動；議論；轟動**

當 `fuss` 指某事引起議論、注意或一陣反應時，`stir` 很自然。它常見於 media, public reaction, controversy。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The decision caused quite a stir online.

這項決定在網上引起不少議論。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a noticeable reaction or public commotion**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、媒體、新聞

---

## 07 Palaver

**繁瑣折騰；沒完沒了的麻煩**

英式口語中，當 `fuss` 指一大堆不必要的程序、討論或麻煩時，`palaver` 很生動。它比 hassle 更有『折騰太多』意味。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Booking the tickets turned into a real palaver.

訂票變成一場真正的繁瑣折騰。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **unnecessary fuss, discussion, or complicated activity**

**REGISTER · 語域**

英式、口語

---

## 08 Overreaction

**過度反應；反應過大**

當 `fuss` 指人們對一件小事反應過大時，`overreaction` 很精準。它比 excessive concern 更偏 outward response，而不只是內在擔心。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The panic was an overreaction to a minor technical fault.

大家的恐慌是對一次輕微技術故障的過度反應。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a response that is stronger than the situation justifies**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、正式

# 02 False synonyms

## Fuss / Commotion
`commotion` 強調外在騷動、吵鬧或混亂。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
There was a fuss in the office.  
The announcement caused a commotion in the hallway.

## Fuss / Bother
`bother` 強調麻煩、費事、令人不便。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
It caused a lot of fuss.  
Moving everything again would be too much bother.

## Fuss / Excessive concern
`excessive concern` 強調對小事擔心過度，而不一定有外在騷動。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
There was too much fuss over the delay.  
There was excessive concern about a five-minute delay.

## Fuss / Ado
`ado` 較書面，常指不必要的忙亂、拖延或大驚小怪。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
There was a lot of fuss before the meeting.  
Without further ado, the meeting began.

## Fuss / Hassle
`hassle` 偏日常、實際上的繁瑣麻煩，語氣很口語。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Changing the booking was a fuss.  
Changing the booking was a real hassle.

## Fuss / Stir
`stir` 常指引起公眾、媒體或社交圈的議論／反應。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The comment caused a fuss.  
The comment caused quite a stir online.

## Fuss / Palaver
`palaver` 是英式口語，特別有「一大堆不必要的繁瑣折騰」意味。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The paperwork was a fuss.  
The paperwork turned into a real palaver.

## Fuss / Overreaction
`overreaction` 指反應比事情本身應有的程度更強。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
There was a lot of fuss over the small error.  
The panic was an overreaction to a minor error.

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Commotion vs Stir** — commotion 偏現場騷動；stir 偏引起議論／注意。
- **Bother vs Hassle** — bother 較廣；hassle 更口語、更偏繁瑣程序。
- **Excessive concern vs Overreaction** — 前者偏擔心程度；後者偏外在反應。
- **Ado vs Palaver** — ado 較書面；palaver 更英式口語、更有折騰感。
- **Fuss vs Trouble** — trouble 可更嚴重；fuss 常有『不必要／太多』意味。
- **Fuss ≠ panic** — panic 是強烈恐慌；fuss 可以只是忙亂或小題大做。
- **Fuss ≠ noise** — noise 只是聲音；fuss 涉及反應、麻煩或忙亂。
- **Fuss ≠ concern in every context** — fuss 也可表示騷動或繁瑣麻煩。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

- **commotion** 常見：`cause a commotion`
- **bother** 作名詞：`too much bother / no bother`
- **excessive concern about/over + noun**
- 固定表達：**without further ado**
- **hassle** 可數／不可數視語境：`a hassle / a lot of hassle`
- **stir** 常見：`cause/create quite a stir`
- **palaver** 多作不可數或單數：`a lot of palaver / a real palaver`
- **overreaction to + noun**
- **make a fuss about/over + noun**
- **fuss** ≠ **fussy**：`fussy` = 挑剔／難取悅的
