# VICTIM · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Victim

**Victim 可以指遭受實際傷害的人、被刻意針對的目標、法律上的受損害一方、事故傷亡者，或遭受不公平／虐待的人。** 因此替換時要先判斷：**Has the person been harmed, merely targeted, legally wronged, physically injured, or deliberately victimised?**

你提供的 **person harmed; target** 正好代表兩個核心方向：**實際遭受傷害** 與 **被人刻意鎖定／針對**。兩者不能完全互換。

這個概念較敏感，而且許多相鄰詞其實代表不同法律／事件角色，因此本次保留 **8 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達，而不為數量加入牽強詞。共 **24 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Victim 的語境 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|
| 實際遭受傷害 | **person harmed** |
| 被刻意鎖定 | **target** |
| 法律上的受損害一方 | **injured party** |
| 災害／行政事件受影響 | **affected person** |
| 事故／戰爭傷亡 | **casualty** |
| 申訴／行政上受屈 | **aggrieved party** |
| 遭欺凌／打壓／歧視 | **victimised person** |
| 避免身份標籤的正式表達 | **person subjected to harm** |

## Tier 1 — Core harm / targeting / legal / casualty equivalents · 核心受害、被針對與法律／事故近義詞

### 01 Person harmed

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：受害者；受到傷害的人  
**語域**：neutral / person-first  
**核心語義**：a person who has suffered physical, emotional, financial, or other harm  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **victim** 最直接而中性的 person-first 改寫之一，適合需要避免把人身份化為 victim 的語境。  
**常見搭配**：person harmed by; people harmed; harm suffered; person affected by harm.  
**常見搭配對象**：crime, abuse, accident, fraud, violence.  
**與 victim 的辨析**：Person harmed 直接描述遭受傷害這件事；victim 更簡潔，但帶較強身份標籤。  
**使用限制**：如果重點是被鎖定、但尚未真正受害，target 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Support is available for any person harmed by the fraud.  
任何因詐騙而受害的人都可獲得支援。

### 02 Target

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：目標；被針對的人  
**語域**：neutral / security / interpersonal  
**核心語義**：a person or entity deliberately selected for attack, abuse, fraud, harassment, or another harmful action  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，但只在 **victim = 被人針對的對象** 時接近。  
**常見搭配**：target of abuse; target of fraud; target of harassment; attack target; targeted individual.  
**常見搭配對象**：fraud, scam, bullying, harassment, attack.  
**與 victim 的辨析**：Target 強調『被選中／被針對』，不保證已經受到實際傷害。  
**使用限制**：事故、天災或非故意傷害的受害者通常不能稱 target。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Older adults are sometimes targeted by phone scams.  
長者有時會成為電話詐騙的目標。

### 03 Injured party

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：受損害一方；受害一方  
**語域**：formal / legal  
**核心語義**：a person or party that has suffered harm, loss, or legal injury because of another person's conduct  
**用法提示**：法律、合約、侵權或保險語境中可對應 victim，尤其重點是權益受損。  
**常見搭配**：injured party; compensate the injured party; loss suffered by the injured party.  
**常見搭配對象**：claim, damages, contract, negligence, liability.  
**與 victim 的辨析**：Injured party 偏法律權利／損失，不一定指身體受傷。  
**使用限制**：日常犯罪新聞或心理支援語境會顯得太 legalistic。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The court ordered compensation for the injured party.  
法院下令向受損害一方作出賠償。

### 04 Affected person

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：受影響的人；受害者  
**語域**：neutral / administrative / public-service  
**核心語義**：a person whose life, health, finances, or circumstances have been negatively affected by an event or action  
**用法提示**：當 victim 顯得過強，而重點是『受到影響』時很自然，常見於事故、政策、災害和支援服務。  
**常見搭配**：affected person; people affected; directly affected; support affected people.  
**常見搭配對象**：disaster, incident, policy, outage, contamination.  
**與 victim 的辨析**：Affected person 比 victim 更中性、更廣；影響可以嚴重，也可以較輕。  
**使用限制**：若需明確表示犯罪、暴力或虐待造成的傷害，person harmed / victim 更清楚。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Emergency housing was offered to people affected by the fire.  
當局向受火災影響的人提供緊急住宿。

### 05 Casualty

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：傷亡者；傷者  
**語域**：formal / emergency / military  
**核心語義**：a person injured or killed in an accident, disaster, conflict, or other serious incident  
**用法提示**：當 victim 指事故、災害或戰爭中的傷亡者時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：civilian casualty; accident casualty; casualty figures; casualties reported.  
**常見搭配對象**：accident, war, disaster, explosion, collision.  
**與 victim 的辨析**：Casualty 強調 injury/death in an incident；victim 可以包括詐騙、虐待、騷擾等非傷亡情況。  
**使用限制**：被騙錢、被網絡欺凌的人通常不叫 casualty。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The collision resulted in several casualties.  
碰撞事故造成數名傷亡者。

## Tier 2 — Grievance / victimisation variants · 申訴與受害化變體

### 06 Aggrieved party

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：受屈一方；權益受損的一方  
**語域**：formal / legal / administrative  
**核心語義**：a person or party that believes they have been wronged, unfairly treated, or legally disadvantaged  
**用法提示**：當 victim 出現在投訴、申訴、勞資、行政或法律爭議中可用。  
**常見搭配**：aggrieved party; complaint by the aggrieved party; remedy for an aggrieved party.  
**常見搭配對象**：complaint, dispute, decision, grievance, appeal.  
**與 victim 的辨析**：Aggrieved party 強調 wronged / unfairly treated，不一定有身體或直接實質傷害。  
**使用限制**：嚴重暴力、事故或自然災害受害者通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The appeal process allows the aggrieved party to seek a review.  
上訴程序容許受屈一方申請覆核。

### 07 Victimised person

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：被欺凌／迫害／不公平對待的人  
**語域**：formal / social / legal  
**核心語義**：a person subjected to deliberate mistreatment, abuse, discrimination, bullying, or exploitation  
**用法提示**：當 victim 的重點是 **victimisation** 而不是單次事故時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：victimised person; victimised employee; victimised group; experience victimisation.  
**常見搭配對象**：employee, student, minority, complainant, resident.  
**與 victim 的辨析**：比 victim 更明確指出有人對其作出不公平或傷害性行為。  
**使用限制**：純意外事故或天災中無施害者時通常不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The policy protects any employee who is victimised after making a complaint.  
政策保障任何在提出投訴後遭受打壓的員工。

## Tier 3 — Person-first explanatory paraphrase · 人本式解釋性改寫

### 08 Person subjected to harm

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：遭受傷害的人  
**語域**：formal / explanatory  
**核心語義**：a person who has been exposed to or experienced harmful treatment or consequences  
**用法提示**：這是較正式、避免標籤化的 contextual paraphrase，適合政策、研究、倫理或支援服務。  
**常見搭配**：person subjected to harm; subjected to abuse; subjected to violence; harm experienced.  
**常見搭配對象**：abuse, violence, coercion, exploitation, neglect.  
**與 victim 的辨析**：比 victim 更展開、更中性，重點放在遭遇而不是身份。  
**使用限制**：日常英語會顯得冗長；需要簡潔時直接用 victim/person harmed。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Services should be designed around the needs of the person subjected to harm.  
服務設計應以遭受傷害人士的需要為中心。

## 04 False synonyms / victim traps · 容易誤選的事件角色陷阱

### 01 Survivor

**倖存者 ≠ 所有 victim**  
survivor 強調經歷危險或傷害後仍存活／繼續生活，不能全面取代 victim。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Survivor is recovery/survival-focused.

### 02 Witness

**目擊者 ≠ victim**  
witness 看到事件，不代表自己受害。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A witness may not be harmed.

### 03 Complainant

**投訴人 ≠ victim**  
complainant 是提出投訴的人，投訴可能尚未證實。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Complainant is procedural.

### 04 Suspect

**疑犯 ≠ victim**  
suspect 是被懷疑涉案的人，角色相反。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Suspect is an accused/investigative role.

### 05 Target

**只在被針對 sense 接近**  
target 可以尚未受到實際傷害。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Target is selection-focused.

### 06 Casualty

**只在事故／災害／戰爭傷亡 sense 接近**  
casualty 不適合欺詐、騷擾等多數受害語境。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Casualty is incident-injury-focused.

### 07 Affected person

**較中性、更廣**  
affected person 可以只是受到影響，不一定稱得上受害。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Affected person is broader.

### 08 Victim / victimise / victimisation

**詞形陷阱**  
victim 是名詞；victimise 是動詞；victimisation 是名詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check word class and meaning.

## 05 練習與解釋 · Practice

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Person harmed · 受害者；受到傷害的人

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The charity offers counselling to victims of domestic abuse.

**中文翻譯**  
慈善機構為家暴受害者提供輔導。

- A. Person harmed
- B. Affected person
- C. Survivor
- D. Witness
- E. Complainant
- F. Suspect

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Person harmed** · 受害者；受到傷害的人

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The charity offers counselling to people harmed by domestic abuse.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Person harmed ✓** — a person who has suffered physical, emotional, financial, or other harm. Here, person-first wording foregrounds the harm rather than the identity label. That makes **Person harmed** the most precise replacement for this particular sense of *victim*.
- **B.** **Affected person** — a person negatively affected by an event, action, or circumstance. This is broader and can include people inconvenienced or indirectly affected without being victims of harm. The decisive clue is that person-first wording foregrounds the harm rather than the identity label.
- **C.** **Survivor** — a person who has lived through or continued after a dangerous or harmful experience. This foregrounds survival or recovery after harm and is not a general substitute for victim. The decisive clue is that person-first wording foregrounds the harm rather than the identity label.
- **D.** **Witness** — a person who saw an event. A witness may see the event without being harmed. The decisive clue is that person-first wording foregrounds the harm rather than the identity label.
- **E.** **Complainant** — a person who makes a formal complaint. This is a procedural role: someone who makes a complaint, whether or not victimisation is established. The decisive clue is that person-first wording foregrounds the harm rather than the identity label.
- **F.** **Suspect** — a person suspected of involvement in wrongdoing. This is an investigative role associated with possible wrongdoing, not victimhood. The decisive clue is that person-first wording foregrounds the harm rather than the identity label.

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Victim 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 實際受害 | **person harmed** | 是否已經 suffered actual harm？ |
| 被騙／被欺凌前被鎖定 | **target** | 是否 deliberate selection is central？ |
| 法律損失／賠償 | **injured party** | 是否 damages/liability/legal injury？ |
| 災害／停電／行政事件 | **affected person** | 是否 impact is broader than direct harm？ |
| 爆炸／事故／戰爭傷亡 | **casualty** | 是否 physical injury/death in a serious incident？ |
| 行政／申訴受屈 | **aggrieved party** | 是否 grievance/unfair decision is central？ |
| 欺凌／報復／歧視 | **victimised person** | 是否 deliberate mistreatment is explicit？ |
| 人本、避免標籤 | **person subjected to harm** | 是否 formal person-first wording is preferred？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **victim vs person harmed**：後者更 person-first、更中性。
- **victim vs target**：target 可尚未真正受害。
- **victim vs injured party**：injured party 偏法律權益／損失。
- **victim vs affected person**：affected person 更廣、更中性。
- **victim vs casualty**：casualty 只適合事故、災害、戰爭中的傷亡。
- **victim vs aggrieved party**：後者偏申訴／法律／行政上的『受屈』。
- **victim vs victimised person**：後者明確有 deliberate mistreatment。
- **victim vs survivor**：survivor 強調倖存／復原，不是所有 victim 都適合如此稱呼。
- **victim vs witness**：witness 只是目擊者。
- **victim vs complainant**：complainant 是程序角色，不代表受害已被證實。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & role traps

- **victim of + crime/event**：victim of fraud / abuse / violence。
- **target of + fraud/abuse/harassment**。
- **injured party** 常與 **damages, compensation, liability, claim** 搭配。
- **affected person / people affected by + event**：災害、停電、政策等。
- **casualty / casualties**：事故、戰爭、災害傷亡。
- **aggrieved party**：投訴、申訴、行政覆核、法律爭議。
- **victimise + person**；**victimisation** 是名詞。
- **survivor** 是否合適應視語境和當事人偏好；它與 victim 的焦點不同。
- 不要把 **witness, complainant, suspect** 當作 victim 的同義詞；它們是不同事件／法律角色。
