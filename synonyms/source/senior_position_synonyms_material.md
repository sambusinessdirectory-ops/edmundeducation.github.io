# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Senior (position)

**高級、高層、行政高層、管理層、主管級——究竟是哪一種 senior？**

本課聚焦職位／組織層級 sense：

**senior = relatively high in rank, responsibility, authority, or organisational level**

核心近義與近義表達：

**high-ranking; upper-level; executive; senior-level; top-level; higher-ranking; management-level; leadership-level; supervisory**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **high-ranking; upper-level; executive** 全部保留。  
> 本課不把 `experienced / veteran` 當作核心 synonym，因為資歷深並不必然等於職位高。

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 high-ranking

**高級的；高階的**

強調在正式組織、政府、軍隊或制度中的職級很高。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

She holds a high-ranking position in the civil service.

她在公務員體系中擔任高級職位。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- high-ranking official

- high-ranking officer

- high-ranking position

- high-ranking member


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是正式職級高、組織層級高、企業 executive、接近最高決策層、相對另一職位更高，還是具體管理／領導／督導責任？

---

**高層的；較高級別的**

強調位於組織層級的較上層，常見於管理與企業架構語境。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The company is recruiting for several upper-level management roles.

公司正招聘數個高層管理職位。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- upper-level management

- upper-level staff

- upper-level role

- upper-level position

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Experienced

**有經驗不等於 senior position**

experienced 強調經驗多；一個人可以很有經驗但職位不高。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

An experienced specialist may not hold a senior position.

---

## 02 Veteran

**資深人士不等於高職級**

veteran 強調長期經驗或年資，而不是組織 rank。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A veteran employee can still work in a non-management role.

---

## 03 Manager

**職銜不等於所有 senior**

manager 表示管理職；有些 senior roles 是專業職而不是管理職。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A senior engineer may outrank a junior manager in expertise without being a manager.

---

## 04 Executive

**只適合部分組織語境**

executive 很適合公司高層，但軍隊、公務員或學術職級未必用 executive。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A senior civil servant is high-ranking, not necessarily an executive.

---

## 05 Top-level

**通常比 senior 更強**

top-level 暗示最高或接近最高層，不能無條件替代所有 senior。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A senior manager may still report to top-level executives.

---

## 06 Supervisory

**主管職不一定高層**

supervisory 只要求監督他人，級別可能相對較低。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A shift supervisor is supervisory but not necessarily senior management.

---

## 07 Senior / elder

**詞義陷阱**

`senior` 在職位語境表示較高職級；`elder` 多指年齡較長的人。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A senior manager is high-ranking; an elder is an older person.

---

## 08 Senior / seniority

**詞性陷阱**

`senior` 可作形容詞／名詞；`seniority` 是名詞，指年資或職級優先地位。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She holds a senior post; promotion may depend on seniority.

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Senior vs High-ranking
`high-ranking` 最適合官員、軍官、公務員和正式組織職級。

## Senior vs Upper-level
`upper-level` 更強調組織層級位置，而不是正式 rank。

## Senior vs Executive
`executive` 特別適合企業／機構高級管理職位。

## Senior vs Senior-level
後者是招聘、履歷和職涯分類中的透明表達。

## Senior vs Top-level
`top-level` 通常比 senior 更強，暗示最上層或接近最上層。

## Senior vs Higher-ranking
`higher-ranking` 需要比較：A 比 B 職級更高。

## Senior vs Management-level
只有當職位真的包含管理責任時才精確。

## Senior vs Leadership-level
重點是領導與策略，而不只是職級。

## Senior vs Supervisory
`supervisory` 強調直接監督，並不保證屬於高層管理。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Senior + noun
常見：
- senior position
- senior role
- senior manager
- senior official
- senior executive

## 02 Senior to
英式／正式語境中可表示職級高於：
- She is senior to me.

不要說：
- senior than me ✗

## 03 Senior vs experienced
- senior = 職級／責任較高
- experienced = 經驗較多

兩者可以重疊，但不是同義。

## 04 Senior vs executive
不是所有 senior roles 都是 executive roles：
- senior engineer
- senior researcher
- senior teacher

## 05 Senior vs supervisory
supervisory 只表示監督責任；senior 往往還涉及較高決策權、責任或職級。
