# MOTIVATION · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Motivation

**「動機／動力／激勵」，最重要的是分清內在 drive、外在 incentive、來自他人或故事的 inspiration，以及 persistence-oriented determination。**

本單元集中處理 **motivation = the reason, desire, energy, or influence that causes someone to act, continue, or work towards a goal**。你提供的 **drive; incentive; inspiration** 分別代表 **內在推動力、外在誘因、鼓舞來源**。

這個詞的高質量近義與情境替代表達相當清楚，因此本次保留 **12 個**自然且具教學價值的詞／表達，不追求固定數量。共 **36 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**；正確答案位置在 A–F 間平均分布。

## Tier 1 — Core drive / incentive / inspiration equivalents · 核心動力、誘因與鼓舞近義詞

### 01 Drive

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：動力；幹勁；內在推動力  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：strong internal energy or determination that pushes someone to act or achieve  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 motivation 最核心的近義詞之一，尤其適合成就、工作、學習和競爭語境。  
**常見搭配**：personal drive; inner drive; drive to succeed; strong drive; competitive drive.  
**常見搭配對象**：success, career, study, achievement, performance.  
**與 motivation 的辨析**：Drive 比 motivation 更強調內在推動力和決心。  
**使用限制**：若動機來自外在獎賞或誘因，incentive 更精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Her drive to improve kept her practising every day.  
她想進步的動力令她每天持續練習。

### 02 Incentive

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：誘因；獎勵動機  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：something that encourages a person to act, especially a reward, benefit, or advantage  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，當 motivation 來自外在獎勵、利益或制度安排時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：financial incentive; incentive to work; provide an incentive; strong incentive; tax incentive.  
**常見搭配對象**：reward, employee, policy, payment, behaviour.  
**與 motivation 的辨析**：Incentive 是外在促使行動的因素；motivation 可來自內在或外在。  
**使用限制**：純粹熱情、理想或個人決心通常不用 incentive。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The bonus gives employees an incentive to reach the target.  
花紅為員工提供達標的誘因。

### 03 Inspiration

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：靈感；鼓舞；激勵來源  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：a person, idea, or experience that creates enthusiasm, creativity, or desire to act  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，當 motivation 來自某人、故事、理念或經歷的鼓舞時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：source of inspiration; inspiration for; draw inspiration from; provide inspiration; creative inspiration.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, story, idea, art, achievement.  
**與 motivation 的辨析**：Inspiration 強調被某事啟發或鼓舞；motivation 更廣，也可很務實。  
**使用限制**：金錢誘因、截止日期等不通常叫 inspiration。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Her teacher was a major source of inspiration.  
她的老師是重要的鼓舞來源。

### 04 Determination

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：決心；毅力  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：firm intention and persistence in achieving a goal despite difficulty  
**用法提示**：當 motivation 表現為『不放棄、堅持做下去』時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：strong determination; determination to succeed; show determination; sheer determination; determination and effort.  
**常見搭配對象**：goal, challenge, study, recovery, competition.  
**與 motivation 的辨析**：Determination 比 motivation 更偏堅持和意志力。  
**使用限制**：只表示『想做』但沒有堅持成分時，motivation 更廣。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Her determination helped her finish the course.  
她的決心幫助她完成課程。

### 05 Enthusiasm

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：熱情；熱忱  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：strong interest, excitement, and positive energy towards an activity or idea  
**用法提示**：當 motivation 帶『很有興趣、很有熱情去做』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：enthusiasm for learning; show enthusiasm; great enthusiasm; enthusiasm about; renewed enthusiasm.  
**常見搭配對象**：work, study, project, idea, activity.  
**與 motivation 的辨析**：Enthusiasm 是情緒性的熱情；motivation 還包括責任、獎賞、需要等。  
**使用限制**：有動機但不興奮時不一定有 enthusiasm。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The students showed great enthusiasm for the project.  
學生對這個項目表現出很大熱情。

## Tier 2 — Purpose / ambition / impetus / encouragement variants · 目標感、抱負、推動力與鼓勵變體

### 06 Purpose

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：目標感；目的；使命感  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：a meaningful reason for acting that gives direction to effort  
**用法提示**：當 motivation 來自『為甚麼要做』和方向感時很有用。  
**常見搭配**：sense of purpose; clear purpose; purpose in life; give someone purpose; purposeful action.  
**常見搭配對象**：work, life, study, service, goal.  
**與 motivation 的辨析**：Purpose 強調意義與方向；motivation 強調推動行動的力量。  
**使用限制**：短期獎勵或即時刺激通常不叫 purpose。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
A clear sense of purpose can sustain long-term effort.  
清晰的目標感可以支持長期努力。

### 07 Ambition

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：抱負；雄心  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：a strong desire to achieve success, status, or a particular long-term goal  
**用法提示**：當 motivation 和長期成就、職涯、地位或高目標有關時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：career ambition; political ambition; ambition to succeed; fulfil an ambition; personal ambition.  
**常見搭配對象**：career, success, position, achievement, goal.  
**與 motivation 的辨析**：Ambition 是特定成就導向的 motivation。  
**使用限制**：日常小事或利他動機未必適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
His ambition pushed him to apply for the leadership role.  
他的抱負促使他申請領導職位。

### 08 Impetus

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：推動力；促進因素  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：a force, event, or reason that makes a process or action begin or move forward  
**用法提示**：當 motivation 指某件事成為行動或改革的推動力時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：give impetus to; provide impetus; new impetus; impetus for change; political impetus.  
**常見搭配對象**：reform, project, campaign, change, development.  
**與 motivation 的辨析**：Impetus 比 motivation 更常指推動一個過程／行動的外在力量。  
**使用限制**：日常個人情緒語境較少用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The report gave fresh impetus to the reform campaign.  
這份報告為改革運動帶來新的推動力。

### 09 Encouragement

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：鼓勵；激勵  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：support, praise, or reassurance that increases someone's willingness or confidence to act  
**用法提示**：當 motivation 來自別人的支持、肯定或勸勉時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：words of encouragement; encouragement from; offer encouragement; encouragement to continue; positive encouragement.  
**常見搭配對象**：teacher, parent, coach, colleague, learner.  
**與 motivation 的辨析**：Encouragement 是外界給予的支持；motivation 是內在／外在推動力本身。  
**使用限制**：若沒有他人支持因素，不宜使用 encouragement。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
A little encouragement helped him continue.  
一點鼓勵幫助他繼續下去。

## Tier 3 — Descriptive motive / force variants · 描述型行動理由與推動力量變體

### 10 Reason to act

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：行動理由；行動動機  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：a reason that explains why someone chooses to do something  
**用法提示**：當 motivation 需要清楚改寫成『做這件事的理由』時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：reason to act; reason for doing; motivating reason; reason to continue; reason to change.  
**常見搭配對象**：decision, action, behaviour, choice, change.  
**與 motivation 的辨析**：是透明的描述性改寫，尤其適合解釋 motivation。  
**使用限制**：不一定包含能量、熱情或持續性。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Concern for her family gave her a strong reason to act.  
對家人的關心給了她一個強烈的行動理由。

### 11 Motivating force

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：激勵力量；推動因素  
**語域**：formal / descriptive  
**核心語義**：a force or influence that causes or sustains action  
**用法提示**：適合需要明確表達『推動某人／某事的力量』時。  
**常見搭配**：motivating force; powerful motivating force; force behind; main motivator; driving force.  
**常見搭配對象**：behaviour, decision, reform, effort, action.  
**與 motivation 的辨析**：比 motivation 更具解釋性，也可涵蓋正面或負面因素。  
**使用限制**：日常對話可能顯得較書面。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Fear of failure became a powerful motivating force.  
對失敗的恐懼成為強大的推動力量。

### 12 Driving force

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：主要推動力；驅動因素  
**語域**：neutral / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：the main person, idea, or factor that causes something to develop or continue  
**用法提示**：當 motivation 不只是個人心理，而是某個計劃、改革或行動背後的主要推動因素時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：driving force behind; main driving force; economic driving force; driving force for change; powerful force.  
**常見搭配對象**：change, project, movement, growth, behaviour.  
**與 motivation 的辨析**：Driving force 強調最主要的推動因素。  
**使用限制**：如果只是其中一個小因素，不宜用 driving force。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Community pressure was a driving force behind the reform.  
社區壓力是改革背後的主要推動力。

## 04 False synonyms / motivation traps · 容易誤選的詞與動機概念陷阱

### 01 Goal

**目標不等於 motivation**  
goal 是想達到的結果；motivation 是推動你去追求它的力量。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A goal is the destination; motivation is the force.

### 02 Reward

**獎勵不等於 motivation**  
reward 可以成為 incentive，但 reward 本身只是回報。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A reward can motivate, but it is not motivation itself.

### 03 Interest

**興趣不等於所有 motivation**  
interest 可以推動行動，但責任、需求、恐懼也可以。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Interest is only one possible source of motivation.

### 04 Discipline

**自律不等於 motivation**  
discipline 是即使沒有動機仍能持續行動的能力。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Discipline can operate without motivation.

### 05 Energy

**精力不等於 motivation**  
有精力不代表有行動理由或意願。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Energy is capacity, not motive.

### 06 Pressure

**壓力不等於 motivation**  
pressure 可以推動行動，但也可能降低 motivation。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Pressure is a possible external force.

### 07 Ambition

**抱負只在成就導向 sense 接近**  
ambition 通常與長期成就、地位或成功相關。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Ambition is achievement-focused.

### 08 Motivate / motivation

**詞性陷阱**  
motivate 是動詞；motivation 是名詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Motivate is the verb; motivation is the noun.

- A. Drive
- B. goal
- C. reward
- D. interest
- E. discipline
- F. energy

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Drive** · 動力；幹勁；內在推動力

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** Drive ✓ — strong internal determination and energy are the key idea; this makes **Drive** the clearest choice.
- **B.** goal — is the result someone wants to achieve, not the force that pushes them towards it; the sentence instead points to strong internal determination and energy are the key idea.
- **C.** reward — is a benefit received or expected and may act as an incentive, but it is not motivation itself; the sentence instead points to strong internal determination and energy are the key idea.
- **D.** interest — can motivate someone, but it is only one possible source of motivation; the sentence instead points to strong internal determination and energy are the key idea.
- **E.** discipline — is the ability to keep acting consistently, even when motivation is low; the sentence instead points to strong internal determination and energy are the key idea.
- **F.** energy — is physical or mental capacity and does not itself provide a reason to act; the sentence instead points to strong internal determination and energy are the key idea.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **motivation vs drive**: drive 更偏內在幹勁和決心。
- **motivation vs incentive**: incentive 通常是外在獎勵、利益或制度性誘因。
- **motivation vs inspiration**: inspiration 來自被某人、理念或故事鼓舞。
- **motivation vs determination**: determination 強調堅持不放棄。
- **motivation vs enthusiasm**: enthusiasm 是熱情和興奮，不涵蓋所有動機。
- **motivation vs purpose**: purpose 提供意義與方向。
- **motivation vs ambition**: ambition 是成就／地位導向的長期動機。
- **motivation vs impetus**: impetus 更常推動一個過程、改革或行動前進。
- **motivation vs encouragement**: encouragement 是由他人給予的支持。
- **motivation vs discipline**: discipline 可以在 motivation 很低時仍維持行動。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & motivation traps · 文法、搭配與動機詞彙陷阱

- **motivation to + verb**：motivation to study / motivation to continue。
- **motivation for + noun/-ing**：motivation for change / for learning。
- **drive to + verb**：drive to succeed / drive to improve。
- **incentive to + verb**：an incentive to invest / work harder。
- **inspiration for + noun** 或 **inspiration to + verb**。
- **determination to + verb**：determination to finish / recover。
- **enthusiasm for + noun/-ing**：enthusiasm for learning。
- **sense of purpose** 是固定高頻搭配。
- **ambition to + verb** 或 **ambition of + -ing**。
- **give / provide impetus to/for**：give impetus to reform。
- **encouragement from + person** / **encouragement to + verb**。
- **driving force behind + noun**：the driving force behind the project。
- **motivate** 是動詞；**motivation** 是名詞；**motivated** 是形容詞。
