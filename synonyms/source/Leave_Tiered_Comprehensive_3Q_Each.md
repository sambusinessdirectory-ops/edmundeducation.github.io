# LEAVE · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Leave

**「離開／出發／走開／退出」，這是一個非常多義的動詞。這個單元集中處理『離開某地／退出某情境』sense，而不是 leave something behind、leave a job 等其他義項。**

本單元集中處理 **leave = to go away from a place, person, situation, organisation, or activity**。你提供的 **depart; go away; exit** 分別代表 **正式出發、口語離開、跨出空間／系統** 三個核心方向。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且高實用度的詞／表達，共 **39 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。同時清楚區分 **leave / depart / exit / withdraw / walk out / move out / step out / vacate**，避免把普通離開、搬走、退出、撤離和突然離場混在一起。

## Tier 1 — Core departure / going-away equivalents · 核心離開與出發近義詞

### 01 Depart

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：離開；出發  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to leave a place, especially to begin a journey  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，當 leave 強調『從某地出發』時最自然，尤其見於交通、旅程和正式通知。  
**常見搭配**：depart from; depart at; scheduled departure; depart the station; depart for.  
**常見搭配對象**：station, airport, city, hotel, country.  
**與 leave 的辨析**：Depart 比 leave 更正式，也更常見於交通或旅程語境。  
**使用限制**：表示『留下某物』或『辭職』等 leave 的其他義項不能用 depart。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The train departs from Platform 4 at 8:30.  
火車於八時三十分從四號月台開出。

### 02 Go away

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：離開；走開  
**語域**：neutral / conversational  
**核心語義**：to leave a place or person and go somewhere else  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很日常，適合口語表示人離開某地、暫時離開或走遠。  
**常見搭配**：go away for the weekend; go away from; go away on holiday; tell someone to go away; go away for a while.  
**常見搭配對象**：home, person, town, holiday, weekend.  
**與 leave 的辨析**：Go away 比 leave 更口語，也常帶『離開一段時間』或『走開』意味。  
**使用限制**：正式交通時間表通常不用 go away。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
We're going away for a few days next week.  
我們下星期會離開幾天。

### 03 Exit

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：離開；退出；走出  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to go out of a place, system, vehicle, programme, or situation  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，當 leave 強調『從出口／系統／建築退出』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：exit the building; exit the vehicle; exit the programme; exit the motorway; emergency exit.  
**常見搭配對象**：building, room, vehicle, system, motorway.  
**與 leave 的辨析**：Exit 比 leave 更強調跨出邊界／離開某個封閉空間或系統。  
**使用限制**：口語中對人說『我要走了』通常不說 I will exit。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Please exit the building through the rear door.  
請從後門離開大樓。

### 04 Head off

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：出發；動身離開  
**語域**：informal / conversational  
**核心語義**：to leave and begin going somewhere, often with a clear destination or purpose  
**用法提示**：當 leave 表示開始一段短途旅程或準備動身時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：head off home; head off to work; head off early; head off for the airport; head off together.  
**常見搭配對象**：home, work, airport, trip, meeting.  
**與 leave 的辨析**：Head off 比 leave 更有『開始前往某處』的方向感。  
**使用限制**：離職、遺棄物件等非移動義項不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
We should head off now if we want to avoid the traffic.  
如果想避開交通，我們現在應該出發。

### 05 Set off

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：出發；動身  
**語域**：neutral / British English  
**核心語義**：to begin a journey  
**用法提示**：英式英語非常常見，當 leave 表示開始旅程時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：set off early; set off for; set off on a journey; set off from; set off at dawn.  
**常見搭配對象**：journey, trip, airport, home, route.  
**與 leave 的辨析**：Set off 強調旅程開始；leave 更廣。  
**使用限制**：美式英語更常見 head out / leave。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
We set off before sunrise to avoid the heat.  
我們在日出前出發，以避開炎熱天氣。

## Tier 2 — Withdrawal / temporary / relocation variants · 退出、短暫離開與搬離變體

### 06 Withdraw

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：離開；撤離；退出  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：to leave a place, activity, organisation, or situation, often deliberately or formally  
**用法提示**：當 leave 是『退出參與／撤離』時很適合。  
**常見搭配**：withdraw from the competition; withdraw from the area; withdraw troops; withdraw support; formally withdraw.  
**常見搭配對象**：competition, area, agreement, organisation, support.  
**與 leave 的辨析**：Withdraw 比 leave 更正式，帶主動退出或撤離意味。  
**使用限制**：普通離開房間不宜用 withdraw。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The team withdrew from the competition because of injury.  
球隊因傷退出比賽。

### 07 Walk out

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：走出去；突然離場  
**語域**：informal / strong  
**核心語義**：to leave suddenly, often because of anger, protest, or dissatisfaction  
**用法提示**：當 leave 帶情緒、抗議或突然離場意味時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：walk out of a meeting; walk out in protest; walk out on; walk out angrily; workers walk out.  
**常見搭配對象**：meeting, room, job, relationship, workplace.  
**與 leave 的辨析**：Walk out 比 leave 更突然、更有態度。  
**使用限制**：一般平靜離開不宜使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He walked out of the meeting after the argument.  
爭執後他離開了會議。

### 08 Move out

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：搬走；遷出  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：to leave a home or property in order to live somewhere else  
**用法提示**：當 leave 指搬離住所、宿舍或辦公地方時最精準。  
**常見搭配**：move out of; move out next month; move out permanently; move out of the flat; move-out date.  
**常見搭配對象**：flat, house, dormitory, office, property.  
**與 leave 的辨析**：Move out 是居住／使用地點的永久或較長期離開。  
**使用限制**：離開餐廳或車站不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
They plan to move out of the flat next month.  
他們計劃下月搬離這個單位。

### 09 Step out

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：出去一下；短暫離開  
**語域**：neutral / conversational  
**核心語義**：to leave a room, office, or building briefly  
**用法提示**：當 leave 是短暫離開、很快回來時很地道。  
**常見搭配**：step out for a moment; step out of the office; step outside; step out briefly; step out for lunch.  
**常見搭配對象**：office, room, building, meeting, desk.  
**與 leave 的辨析**：Step out 通常暗示短暫離開。  
**使用限制**：長期搬走或正式退出不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She's stepped out for a few minutes.  
她出去幾分鐘，很快回來。

### 10 Make one's way out

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：走出去；離開現場  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：to leave a place, especially by moving gradually towards the exit  
**用法提示**：當 leave 需要描寫離開過程、穿過人群或走向出口時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：make one's way out of; make for the exit; slowly make one's way out; make one's way outside; make one's way home.  
**常見搭配對象**：crowd, theatre, building, room, venue.  
**與 leave 的辨析**：比 leave 更具畫面感，強調離開的過程。  
**使用限制**：簡短指令或交通時刻表不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
After the concert, the audience slowly made their way out.  
演唱會後，觀眾慢慢離場。

## Tier 3 — Formal / forceful / property-related variants · 正式告辭、強烈離場與物業遷出變體

### 11 Take one's leave

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：告辭；離席  
**語域**：formal / literary  
**核心語義**：to say goodbye and leave a place or group of people  
**用法提示**：當 leave 帶禮貌告辭、宴會離席或較正式社交語氣時可用。  
**常見搭配**：take one's leave; take leave of; politely take one's leave; take my leave; take his leave.  
**常見搭配對象**：host, gathering, meeting, dinner, company.  
**與 leave 的辨析**：Take one's leave 比 leave 更正式、更有禮貌和社交意味。  
**使用限制**：日常口語偏書面。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
After thanking the host, she took her leave.  
她向主人道謝後便告辭離開。

### 12 Clear out

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：離開；搬走；清空離場  
**語域**：informal / strong  
**核心語義**：to leave a place, often quickly or completely, sometimes taking one's belongings  
**用法提示**：當 leave 有『趕快離開／全部撤走』意味時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：clear out of; clear out quickly; clear out the room; tell someone to clear out; clear out by Friday.  
**常見搭配對象**：room, flat, office, building, area.  
**與 leave 的辨析**：Clear out 比 leave 更強烈，常帶命令或徹底撤離語氣。  
**使用限制**：正式中性語境通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The landlord told them to clear out by Friday.  
業主叫他們星期五前搬走。

### 13 Vacate

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：騰出；撤離；搬離  
**語域**：formal / legal / property  
**核心語義**：to leave a place so that it becomes empty and available for others  
**用法提示**：當 leave 涉及物業、座位、房間、法院命令或正式通知時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：vacate the premises; vacate the room; vacate a property; vacate the seat; vacate by a deadline.  
**常見搭配對象**：premises, property, room, seat, office.  
**與 leave 的辨析**：Vacate 強調『離開後騰空』，常見法律／物業文本。  
**使用限制**：普通朋友聚會離開不會說 vacate。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Tenants must vacate the premises by the end of the month.  
租戶必須在月底前遷出該物業。

## 04 False synonyms / leave traps · 容易誤選的詞與「離開」陷阱

### 01 Abandon

**拋棄不等於普通 leave**  
abandon 帶放棄、遺棄、不再返回的強烈意味。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Abandon is much stronger than ordinary leave.

### 02 Escape

**逃走不等於 leave**  
escape 表示逃離危險、拘禁或不想要的情況。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Escape implies getting free from danger or control.

### 03 Quit

**quit 只在停止工作／活動 sense 接近 leave**  
quit a job 可以等於 leave a job，但不能泛指離開任何地方。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Quit is activity/job-focused.

### 04 Resign

**辭職不等於 leave**  
resign 只表示正式辭去職位。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Resign is a formal employment/office term.

### 05 Exit

**exit 只在離開空間／系統 sense 接近 leave**  
exit 偏跨出邊界，不等於所有 leave 義項。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Exit is boundary-focused.

### 06 Depart

**depart 只在出發／離開地點 sense 接近 leave**  
depart 較正式，且不涵蓋 leave behind 等義項。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Depart is movement-focused.

### 07 Go

**去不等於 leave**  
go 只表示移動；leave 強調離開起點。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Go focuses movement; leave focuses departure.

### 08 Leave / left

**詞形陷阱**  
leave 是原形；left 是過去式／過去分詞，也可作『左邊的』形容詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Left has multiple grammatical roles.

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The train leaves from Platform 4 at 8:30.

**中文翻譯**

- A. depart
- B. abandon
- C. escape
- D. quit
- E. resign
- F. go

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**depart** · 離開；出發

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. depart ✓ — scheduled transport leaving a station is a classic depart context。**
- **B. abandon — abandon implies desertion。**
- **C. escape — escape implies getting free from danger/control。**
- **D. quit — quit applies to jobs/activities。**
- **E. resign — resign applies to formal positions。**
- **F. go — go focuses movement but misses the scheduled departure nuance.**

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **leave vs depart**: depart 更正式，尤其適合交通與旅程。
- **leave vs go away**: go away 更口語，常帶暫時離開一段時間。
- **leave vs exit**: exit 強調離開封閉空間、車輛或系統。
- **leave vs withdraw**: withdraw 是正式退出活動、協議或撤離。
- **leave vs walk out**: walk out 帶突然、憤怒或抗議意味。
- **leave vs move out**: move out 指搬離住所／辦公地方。
- **leave vs step out**: step out 通常表示短暫出去，很快回來。
- **leave vs vacate**: vacate 是正式離開並騰空位置／物業。
- **leave vs abandon**: abandon 帶遺棄、放棄和不再返回的強烈語氣。
- **leave vs escape**: escape 強調逃離危險、拘禁或控制。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & leave traps · 文法、搭配與多義陷阱

- **leave + place**：leave the office / leave home / leave the country。
- **leave for + destination**：leave for London / leave for the airport。
- **depart from + place**：depart from Platform 4 / depart from the hotel。
- **head off / set off for + destination**：head off for home / set off for the airport。
- **withdraw from + activity/agreement/area**：withdraw from the competition。
- **walk out of + place/event**；亦可說 **walk out in protest**。
- **move out of + home/property**：move out of the flat。
- **step out of + room/office**：通常是短暫離開。
- **vacate + premises/seat/property**：正式法律／物業語氣。
- **take one's leave** 是固定表達：take my leave / take his leave。
- **leave** 還有其他重要義項：leave something behind（留下）、leave a job（離職）、leave someone alone（別打擾）。
- **quit / resign** 只適用於工作、職位或活動，不是所有離開。
- **left** 是 leave 的過去式／過去分詞，也可表示『左邊的』，需靠語境判斷。
