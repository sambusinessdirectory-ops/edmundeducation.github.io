# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Homework

**學校作業、帶回家完成的功課、課程作業、練習題、自主學習——究竟是哪一種 homework？**

本課聚焦：

**homework = school or course work assigned to a learner to be completed outside normal class time, often at home**

核心近義與近義表達：

**school assignment; take-home work; assignment; schoolwork; coursework; take-home assignment; practice work; exercises; written assignment; independent study work; at-home study**

> **Precision rule:**  
> `school assignment / assignment` = 正式指定作業。  
> `take-home work / take-home assignment` = 明確課外／家中完成。  
> `schoolwork` = 更廣。  
> `coursework` = 更正式、常計分。  
> `practice work / exercises` = 操練型。  
> `written assignment` = 書面型。  
> `independent study work` = 自主學習型。

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 School assignment

**學校作業；學校功課**

和 `homework` 很接近，特別適合正式地指由學校／老師指定、需要完成的學習任務。比 homework 更容易包括較大型或較正式的任務。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The teacher gave us a school assignment for the weekend.

老師給我們週末學校作業。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- complete a school assignment
- submit an assignment
- school assignment deadline
- assigned schoolwork

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 homework 是正式 assignment、在家完成的任務、課程評核、練習題，還是自主學習？

---

## 02 Take-home work

**帶回家完成的功課；課後作業**

直接強調工作要在課堂外／家中完成。它描述的是完成地點與安排，不一定是正式名稱。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The class was given take-home work to finish after school.

全班獲安排課後帶回家完成的功課。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- take-home work
- take work home
- complete work at home
- holiday take-home work

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 homework 是正式 assignment、在家完成的任務、課程評核、練習題，還是自主學習？

## 03 Assignment

**作業；指定任務**

教育語境中非常常見，尤其在中學、大專和大學。可指 homework，但範圍更廣，也包括 project、essay、report 等。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

I have a history assignment to complete tonight.

我今晚有很多歷史科作業。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- complete an assignment
- submit an assignment
- assignment deadline
- written assignment

## 04 Schoolwork

**學業；學校功課**

### EXAMPLE · 例句

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Classwork

**課堂作業不等於 homework**

`classwork` 通常在課堂內完成；homework 通常在課後／家中完成。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Students do classwork in class and homework afterwards.

---

## 02 Revision

**溫習不等於老師指定的功課**

revision 是複習內容，可自行進行，也可成為 homework 的一部分。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Revision may be homework, but the words are not identical.

---

## 03 Study

**學習比 homework 更廣**

`study` 可包括上課、閱讀、複習、研究，不一定是老師指定的任務。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Homework is one form of study.

---

## 04 Assignment

**很接近但範圍更廣**

assignment 可以是大型 project、essay、presentation 等，也可能在課堂內完成。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A homework task is an assignment, but not every assignment is homework.

---

## 05 Coursework

**課程作業通常更正式、更廣**

coursework 可跨數週／整學期，常計入評核。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Coursework may include several homework tasks and projects.

---

## 06 Exercise

**練習題只是其中一種 homework**

一份 homework 可以包含 exercises，但也可以是閱讀、報告、研究或項目。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Grammar exercises may form part of homework.

---

## 07 Project

**項目作業只是其中一類**

project 通常較大型、長期，不能泛指一般 homework。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A project can be assigned as homework, but most homework is not a project.

---

## 08 Worksheet

**工作紙只是載體／形式**

worksheet 是印刷／數碼工作紙；可在課堂或家中完成。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A worksheet can be used for homework or classwork.

---

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Homework is usually uncountable
自然：
- some homework
- a lot of homework
- too much homework

不自然：
- a homework ✗
- homeworks ✗

若要數：
- a homework assignment
- two homework tasks

## 02 Do homework
自然：
`do homework`

通常不用：
`make homework` ✗

## 03 Set vs assign homework
英式：
- set homework

美式／國際：
- assign homework

兩者都自然。

## 04 Homework vs assignment
Assignment 可：
- take several weeks
- be completed in class
- involve an essay/project/presentation

所以範圍比 homework 更廣。

## 05 Coursework
常見於：
- secondary education
- university
- continuous assessment
- graded projects/reports

## 06 Exercises / practice work
只在 homework 的目的明確是練習／鞏固時使用。

## 07 Schoolwork
Schoolwork 比 homework 更廣：
- classwork
- homework
- projects
- study tasks
