# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Deal with

**處理、應付、管理、著手解決、回應——究竟是哪一種 deal with？**

`deal with` 是高度多義的片語動詞。本課分開四個高頻核心 sense：

1. **handle / manage** — 一般處理或管理  
2. **address / tackle / resolve** — 面對問題並採取行動  
3. **cope with / respond to** — 應付壓力、困難或事件  
4. **be about / concern** — 內容涉及／討論某主題

核心近義與近義表達：

**handle; address; manage; tackle; resolve; cope with; attend to; process; respond to; take care of; see to; deal with**

> **Precision rule:**  
> `handle` 最廣。  
> `address` = 著手處理。  
> `tackle` = 積極攻克。  
> `resolve` = 真正解決。  
> `cope with` = 在困難下應付。  
> `process` = 程序化處理。  
> `take care of / see to` = 日常／英式負責辦妥。  
> `deal with` 本身還可表示「涉及／討論」。

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Handle

**處理；應付**

和 `deal with` 最直接、最常用的近義詞之一。可指處理問題、任務、情況、客戶或資訊，語氣中性而廣泛。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

She handled the complaint quickly and professionally.

她迅速而專業地處理了投訴。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- handle a problem
- handle a complaint
- handle a situation
- handle a request

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否只是一般地表示『處理／應付某事』，而沒有更特定的語義？

---

## 02 Address

**處理；著手解決；正視**

當 `deal with` 指正式地處理某個問題、議題、缺陷或風險時，`address` 很合適。它強調對問題採取行動，而不保證問題已完全解決。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The new policy aims to address staff shortages.

新政策旨在處理人手短缺問題。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- address a problem
- address an issue
- address concerns
- address a risk

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是在正式地著手處理某個問題／議題，而未必已經解決？

---

## 03 Manage

**管理；處理；應付**

當 `deal with` 涉及持續安排、控制資源、工作量、流程或複雜情況時，`manage` 很接近。它比 `handle` 更常帶持續管理意味。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The system helps staff manage large volumes of data.

這個系統幫助員工管理大量資料。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- manage a workload
- manage a team
- manage a situation
- manage resources

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是在持續地管理某個工作量、流程或複雜情況？

---

## 04 Tackle

**著手處理；應對；攻克**

比 `deal with` 更積極、有力度，常用於 difficult problem / challenge / issue。它暗示主動面對並努力改善。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The city is trying to tackle air pollution.

城市正嘗試應對空氣污染問題。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- tackle a problem
- tackle an issue
- tackle a challenge
- tackle inequality

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否需要表達主動、積極地面對一個棘手問題？

---

## 05 Resolve

**解決；處理妥當**

當 `deal with` 的語境明確表示把問題完全解決時，`resolve` 比 `handle` / `address` 更精確。它帶有完成結果。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The technician resolved the problem within an hour.

技術員在一小時內解決了問題。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- resolve a problem
- resolve an issue
- resolve a dispute
- resolve a complaint

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否不只是處理，而是把問題真正解決？

---

## 06 Cope with

**應付；設法處理**

當 `deal with` 表示在困難、壓力或不理想情況下設法應付時，`cope with` 很接近。重點是承受並應對，而不一定改善問題本身。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Many families are struggling to cope with higher living costs.

很多家庭正努力應付更高的生活成本。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- cope with stress
- cope with pressure
- cope with change
- cope with demand

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是在困難條件下設法應付，而不一定能解決問題？

---

## 07 Attend to

**處理；照料；着手辦理**

較正式，表示把注意力轉向某項工作、問題或人的需要並加以處理。常見於 service / administrative / care contexts。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

A member of staff will attend to your request shortly.

職員稍後會處理你的請求。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- attend to a matter
- attend to a request
- attend to a customer
- attend to a problem

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是較正式地表示『着手處理／照料』某項事務或需要？

---

## 08 Process

**處理；辦理；處理資料／申請**

當 `deal with` 指按程序處理資料、申請、付款、訂單或請求時，`process` 很精確。它偏行政／系統流程，而不是一般問題解決。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The office processes hundreds of applications each week.

辦公室每星期處理數百份申請。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- process an application
- process a payment
- process a request
- process data

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是按既定程序處理申請、資料、付款或訂單？

---

## 09 Respond to

**回應；應對**

當 `deal with` 重點是對問題、投訴、危機或需求作出反應時，`respond to` 很合適。它強調反應／回應，而不必表示已解決。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The company responded quickly to customer complaints.

公司迅速回應客戶投訴。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- respond to a complaint
- respond to a crisis
- respond to concerns
- respond to demand

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否重點在作出反應／回應，而不是完全解決？

---

## 10 Take care of

**處理；照顧；負責**

較口語、日常，表示負責處理某件事或照料某人。比 `deal with` 更親切，也可帶 care / responsibility 意味。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

Don't worry — I'll take care of the booking.

別擔心——我會處理預訂。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- take care of a task
- take care of arrangements
- take care of a customer
- take care of the problem

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是日常地表示『交給我處理／我來負責』？

---

## 11 See to

**處理；照料；負責辦妥**

英式英語中很自然，表示確保某事得到處理，或親自去照料某人／某事。比 `deal with` 稍口語且帶責任感。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

I'll see to the repairs tomorrow.

我明天會處理維修事宜。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- see to a matter
- see to the repairs
- see to a customer
- see to it that

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是否是英式口語中表示『我會負責處理好』？

---

## 12 Deal with

**處理；應付；涉及**

保留 headword 本身作為基準項，方便比較各 sense。它可以非常廣泛地表示處理問題、應付情況、負責事務，也可以表示『涉及／討論某主題』。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

This chapter deals with the causes of migration.

本章探討移民的成因。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- deal with a problem
- deal with a complaint
- deal with stress
- deal with a topic

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是哪一種 deal with：處理、應付、負責，還是『涉及／討論』？

# 02 False synonyms

以下詞語與 **deal with** 高度相關，但在是否真正解決、是否只是討論、是否屬程序化處理等方面不同。

## 01 Solve

**solve 通常要求找到答案**

`solve` 最適合 problem / puzzle / equation 等需要明確解法的事物；deal with 更廣。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

You can deal with a complaint without fully solving the underlying problem.

---

## 02 Resolve

**resolve 暗示問題已解決**

`resolve` 比 deal with 更結果導向。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The team dealt with the issue for days before finally resolving it.

---

## 03 Manage

**manage 偏持續管理**

`manage` 適合 workload / team / resources / ongoing situation，但未必適合所有單次問題。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She manages the workload but handles individual requests.

---

## 04 Address

**address 是著手處理，不保證完成**

`address` 常見於 issue / concern / risk。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The policy addresses the problem but may not resolve it completely.

---

## 05 Cope with

**cope with 偏困難下應付**

`cope with` 常見於 stress / pressure / loss / high demand，並不表示控制或解決。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

He can cope with pressure, but that does not mean he can remove it.

---

## 06 Process

**process 偏程序化處理**

`process` 適合 forms / payments / applications / data，而不是一般人際或抽象問題。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The office processes applications and handles unusual cases.

---

## 07 Discuss

**討論不等於處理**

`discuss` 只是談論；deal with 某問題通常還暗示採取行動。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The meeting discussed the issue, but nobody dealt with it.

---

## 08 Avoid

**avoid 與 deal with 方向相反**

`avoid` 是避開問題；deal with 是面對並處理。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Avoiding a problem is not the same as dealing with it.

---

> Distractors 優先使用 handle / address / manage / resolve / cope with / process 等 plausible near-misses。

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Deal with vs Handle

**handle** 是最廣泛、最直接的近義詞之一。

---

## Deal with vs Address

**address** 偏正式地著手處理 issue / concern / risk。

---

## Deal with vs Manage

**manage** 強調持續管理工作量、資源或情況。

---

## Deal with vs Tackle

**tackle** 更主動、有力度，常用於棘手問題。

---

## Deal with vs Resolve

**resolve** 表示成功解決，結果性更強。

---

## Deal with vs Cope with

**cope with** 強調在困難情況下應付，而非消除問題。

---

## Deal with vs Attend to

**attend to** 較正式，表示把注意力轉向某項事務並處理。

---

## Deal with vs Process

**process** 適合程序化處理資料、申請、付款和請求。

---

## Deal with vs Respond to

**respond to** 偏回應事件／關注，不一定解決。

---

## Deal with vs Take care of

後者較口語、親切，常有『交給我處理』意味。

---

## Deal with vs See to

**see to** 是英式自然口語，帶『確保辦妥』意味。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Deal with + noun

常見：

- deal with a problem
- deal with a complaint
- deal with stress
- deal with customers
- deal with a request

---

## 02 Deal with vs handle

兩者很多時可互換：

`She deals with complaints.`  
`She handles complaints.`

但 `handle` 通常更直接、更職務化。

---

## 03 Address vs resolve

- **address** = 開始處理／採取措施
- **resolve** = 已解決

Example:

`The policy addresses the issue, but it does not fully resolve it.`

---

## 04 Manage vs cope with

- **manage** = 有能力持續控制／安排
- **cope with** = 在困難下設法應付

Example:

`She manages a large team but struggles to cope with stress.`

---

## 05 Process

最適合：
- applications
- payments
- orders
- requests
- data
- claims

不適合：
- stress
- grief
- a difficult colleague
- a political issue

除非是其他特殊 sense。

---

## 06 Deal with = be about / concern

例如：

`This chapter deals with climate change.`

= 本章討論／涉及氣候變化。

這個 sense 更接近：
- concern
- cover
- discuss
- address

而不是 handle。

---

## 07 See to / take care of

日常責任表達：

- I'll take care of it.
- I'll see to it.
- Leave it with me.

都帶有「我會負責處理」意味。
