# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Pretend

**裝作、佯裝、假想遊戲、模擬、偽裝成、冒充、裝樣子、裝出反應、造假——究竟是哪一種 pretend？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**act as if; feign; make believe; simulate; masquerade as; pose as; play at; put on; fake**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **act as if; feign; make believe** 全部保留。  
> 本課把 `pretend` 明確分成：**一般假裝、正式佯裝、兒童想像／角色扮演、技術模擬、身份冒充、表面裝樣子、情緒／口音裝出、直接造假**。

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Act As If

**裝作；表現得好像**

最透明、最自然的改寫之一。當 `pretend` 指某人故意表現得像某種情況是真的，即使實際上並非如此時很適合。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

He acted as if he had not heard the question.

他裝作沒有聽到問題。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- act as if nothing happened

- act as if you know

- act as if it is true

- act as if unaffected

### EXAMPLE · 例句

He feigned illness so he could miss the meeting.

他假裝生病，好避開會議。

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Act as if

**最透明改寫**

只描述外在行為像某情況是真的，不一定很正式。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

He acted as if he had not heard.

---

## 02 Feign

**較正式、欺騙意味更明顯**

特別常見於 illness, surprise, ignorance, emotion。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She feigned surprise.

---

## 03 Make believe

**想像／遊戲 sense**

最自然於兒童、角色扮演和共享想像。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The children made believe the sofa was a ship.

---

## 04 Simulate

**技術模擬，不等於普通假裝**

用於訓練、科學、軟件或模型。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The exercise simulates a fire.

---

## 05 Pose as

**身份冒充**

常見於 scammer / suspect / investigator 冒充某身份。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

He posed as a delivery driver.

---

## 06 Masquerade as

**掩蓋真實身份／本質**

通常比 pose as 更書面，也可用於產品、網站、組織。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The website masqueraded as a bank.

---

## 07 Pretend / pretence

**詞形辨析**

`pretend` 是動詞；BrE 名詞通常 `pretence`，AmE 常 `pretense`。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

He pretended; it was a pretence/pretense.

---

## 08 Pretend / pretending

**語法結構**

`pretend to do`, `pretend (that) ...`, `pretend to be ...` 都很常見。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She pretended to sleep; he pretended that he knew.

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Pretend vs Act as if
最透明：
- act as if nothing happened
- act as if you know

## Pretend vs Feign
`feign` 較正式，也更常帶欺騙：
- feign illness
- feign surprise
- feign ignorance

## Pretend vs Make believe
最適合：
- children
- games
- imaginary worlds
- role-play

## Pretend vs Simulate
`simulate` 不是一般欺騙，而是有目的地模擬：
- emergency drill
- software
- scientific conditions

## Pretend vs Masquerade as
強調把真實身份／本質藏起來。

## Pretend vs Pose as
特別自然於人冒充：
- doctor
- customer
- employee
- journalist

## Pretend vs Play at
常有「只是裝樣子／沒有真正投入」的語氣。

## Pretend vs Put on
最自然於：
- put on an accent
- put on a smile
- put on a brave face

## Pretend vs Fake
`fake` 更直接、更口語，也更明確表示虛假。
