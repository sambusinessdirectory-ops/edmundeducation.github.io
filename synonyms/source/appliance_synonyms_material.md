# Appliance

**家用裝置、機器、電器、家電、家用電器、裝置、設備、小型實用裝置、廚房電器——究竟是哪一種 appliance？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**household device; machine; electrical appliance; home appliance; domestic appliance; device; piece of equipment; gadget; kitchen appliance**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **household device; machine** 全部保留。  
> 本課集中處理 `appliance = a device or machine, especially an electrical one used in the home`，並分開 **一般家用裝置、機械設備、電器、家電分類、英式正式家電、廣義 device、一般 equipment、小型 gadget、廚房電器**。

**家用裝置；家居設備**

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Household device

**透明而廣泛**

適合把 appliance 解釋為家中使用的功能裝置。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The household device saves time on cleaning.

---

## 02 Machine

**範圍更廣**

machine 可大可小、家用或工業；不是所有 machine 都是 appliance。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The machine uses a motor to rotate the drum.

---

## 03 Electrical appliance

**電力是必要線索**

如果句子提到 plug, socket, electricity, earthing，通常很自然。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The electrical appliance must be properly earthed.

---

## 04 Home appliance

**零售／產品分類高頻**

常見於 major home appliances, smart home appliances。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The retailer sells major home appliances.

---

## 05 Domestic appliance

**英式／正式**

常見於 regulations, repairs, energy efficiency。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The regulation covers domestic appliances.

---

## 06 Device

**最廣泛但資訊少**

phone, sensor, medical device 都是 device，但未必是 appliance。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The device switches itself off automatically.

---

## 07 Appliance / apparatus

**不是同一範圍**

apparatus 常指較正式、技術或科學設備，不宜機械替換 household appliance。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Laboratory apparatus is not the same category as household appliances.

---

## 08 Appliance

**其他固定用法**

`appliance` 通常是實體設備；不要把 software/app 當成 appliance。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A washing machine is an appliance; an app is software.

## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** household device · 家用裝置；家居設備

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

The appliance helps reduce the time needed for daily cleaning.

### 中文翻譯

這件家用裝置有助縮短日常清潔所需時間。

### OPTIONS


- **A. household device**

- **B. home appliance**

- **C. device**

- **D. piece of equipment**

- **E. industrial machine**

- **F. raw material**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**household device · 家用裝置；家居設備**

The **household device** helps reduce the time needed for daily cleaning.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. household device ✓** — `household device` = 家中用來完成某項實際工作的裝置。 **本句為何最合適：** the item is used in the home for a routine household task。

- **B. home appliance** — `home appliance` = 家居使用的家電／設備。 強調家居產品類別；實驗室或工業設備不適合。 **本句線索：** the item is used in the home for a routine household task。

- **C. device** — `device` = 具有特定功能的裝置，範圍很廣。 功能範圍最廣，但不保證是家用電器；如果零售分類就是『家電』，home appliance 更準。 **本句線索：** the item is used in the home for a routine household task。

- **D. piece of equipment** — `piece of equipment` = 完成某工作所需的一件設備。 強調工作／操作所需設備；小巧、消費型、裝飾性家電時不一定最自然。 **本句線索：** the item is used in the home for a routine household task。

- **E. industrial machine** — `industrial machine` = 工業機器。 工業用途且規模通常較大，和 household appliance 不同。 **本句線索：** the item is used in the home for a routine household task。

- **F. raw material** — `raw material` = 原材料。 是製造材料，不是裝置。 **本句線索：** the item is used in the home for a routine household task。

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Appliance vs Household device
最透明，但較描述性。

## Appliance vs Machine
`machine` 更廣：
- industrial machine
- factory machine
- vending machine

並非所有 machine 都是 appliance。

## Appliance vs Electrical appliance
當句子出現：
- plug
- socket
- power cord
- electricity
- earthing

通常 `electrical appliance` 更精確。

## Appliance vs Home appliance
最自然於：
- retail
- smart homes
- major appliances
- energy efficiency

## Appliance vs Domestic appliance
英式正式語境常見：
- regulation
- repair
- household safety
- energy standards

## Appliance vs Device
`device` 非常廣，不保證是家電。

## Appliance vs Gadget
`gadget` 通常較小：
- milk frother
- slicer
- compact travel device

大型雪櫃／洗衣機通常不稱 gadget。

## Appliance vs Kitchen appliance
只限廚房用途。
