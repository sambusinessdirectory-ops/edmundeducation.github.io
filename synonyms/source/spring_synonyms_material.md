# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Spring

**春天、冬季之後的季節、春季、較正式的 vernal season、春季月份、萬物更新季節、新生長季節——究竟是哪一種 spring？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**springtime; season after winter; spring season; vernal season; spring months; season of renewal; season of new growth**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **7 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **season after winter; springtime** 全部保留。  
> 本課只處理 `spring = the season after winter and before summer`，不混入泉水、彈簧、跳躍等其他 senses。

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Springtime

**春天；春季**

最直接、最自然的近義詞之一。`springtime` 比 `spring` 稍具描寫性，常用來談春季的天氣、景色、活動或感受。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The gardens are especially beautiful in springtime.

花園在春天特別美。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- in springtime

- springtime weather

- springtime flowers

- springtime sunshine

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Springtime

**最接近、較描寫性**

springtime 和 spring 高度接近，但前者更常用於談季節感受、景色和活動。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The gardens are beautiful in springtime.

---

## 02 Season after winter

**定義式，不是自然日常替換**

適合教學或解釋四季順序。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Spring is the season after winter.

---

## 03 Spring season

**正確但較冗長**

很多日常句子直接說 spring 更自然。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The festival takes place in spring.

---

## 04 Vernal

**正式／書面**

`vernal` 是形容詞，常見於 `vernal equinox / vernal season`。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The vernal equinox marks a seasonal point.

---

## 05 Spring months

**只處理時間範圍**

它表示春季內的幾個月份，不等於所有 spring senses。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Rainfall increased during the spring months.

---

## 06 Season of renewal

**象徵性，不是詞典式完全同義**

強調新生、希望、重啟。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Spring is often called a season of renewal.

---

## 07 Spring / springtime

**可數性與冠詞**

談季節通常不用冠詞：`in spring`；`in the spring` 也常見，尤其指某個特定春季。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

We travel in spring / in the spring.

---

## 08 Spring

**多義詞**

spring 也可指泉水、彈簧、跳躍等。本課只處理『春季』sense。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A natural spring uses a different noun sense.

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Spring vs Springtime
`sprintime` 最接近日常 spring，但稍具描寫性。

## Spring vs Season after winter
最適合：
- definitions
- school teaching
- explaining the annual cycle

## Spring vs Spring season
正確但較明確／較冗長，常見於 programme / climate / scheduling。

## Spring vs Vernal season
`vernal` 明顯較正式：
- ecology
- astronomy
- literary writing

## Spring vs Spring months
只適合當重點是：
- a multi-month period
- rainfall
- temperatures
- seasonal scheduling

## Spring vs Season of renewal
偏象徵：
- hope
- rebirth
- new beginnings

## Spring vs Season of new growth
偏自然／農業：
- shoots
- leaves
- crops
- flowering

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 In spring
最常見：
- Flowers bloom in spring.

## 02 In the spring
也非常自然，尤其指某個具體春季：
- We moved there in the spring of 2025.

## 03 Springtime
通常不可數／時間概念：
- in springtime
- during springtime

## 04 Vernal
`vernal` 是形容詞：
- vernal equinox
- vernal season

## 05 Other senses excluded
本課不混入：
- natural spring = 泉水
- metal spring = 彈簧
- spring = 跳躍／彈起
