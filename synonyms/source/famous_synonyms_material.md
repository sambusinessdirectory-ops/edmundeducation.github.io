# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Famous

**知名、享有盛名、備受讚譽、顯赫、傳奇、標誌性——究竟是哪一種 famous？**

本課聚焦：

**famous = known by many people, often because of achievement, position, reputation, cultural significance, or public visibility**

核心近義與近義表達：

**well-known; renowned; celebrated; prominent; noted; distinguished; acclaimed; eminent; illustrious; legendary; iconic**

> **Precision rule:**  
> `well-known` = 最中性。  
> `renowned` = 因卓越表現享有盛名。  
> `celebrated / acclaimed` = 著名而備受讚譽。  
> `prominent` = 地位突出。  
> `distinguished / eminent` = 專業成就與聲望。  
> `illustrious` = 長期、歷史性顯赫。  
> `legendary` = 傳奇級別。  
> `iconic` = 成為文化／品牌／時代象徵。

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Well-known

**知名的；眾所周知的**

和 `famous` 最直接、最中性的近義詞之一。可形容人物、地方、品牌、作品、事件等，通常強調很多人知道。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

She is a well-known television presenter.

她是一位知名電視主持。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- well-known person
- well-known brand
- well-known for
- widely well-known

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 famous 是單純很多人知道，還是因卓越、專業地位、讚譽、歷史聲望或象徵性而著名？

## 02 Renowned

**著名的；享有盛名的**

比 `well-known` 更正式，通常暗示因某方面的卓越表現而有良好聲譽。常見於專家、機構、餐廳、學府等。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The restaurant is renowned for its seafood.

這間餐廳以海鮮享有盛名。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- renowned expert
- renowned institution
- renowned for
- internationally renowned

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 famous 是單純很多人知道，還是因卓越、專業地位、讚譽、歷史聲望或象徵性而著名？

## 03 Celebrated

**著名的；備受讚譽的**

強調不只出名，而且受到讚賞、尊敬或慶賀。常見於藝術家、作家、作品、歷史人物。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The exhibition features works by a celebrated painter.

展覽展出一位備受讚譽畫家的作品。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

- celebrated author
- celebrated artist
- celebrated work
- widely celebrated

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡的 famous 是單純很多人知道，還是因卓越、專業地位、讚譽、歷史聲望或象徵性而著名？

## 04 Prominent

**著名的；重要的；顯赫的**

當 `famous` 強調在某領域中地位突出、具能見度或影響力時很合適。不是單純『很多人知道』。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

# 02 False synonyms

以下詞語都可能和名氣、成功、影響力或辨識度有關，但它們不一定表示『很多人知道』。

## 01 Popular

**受歡迎不等於著名**

`popular` 表示很多人喜歡／選擇；famous 表示很多人知道。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A restaurant can be popular locally without being famous nationally.

---

## 02 Successful

**成功不等於著名**

有成就的人或公司未必廣為人知。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A successful scientist may not be famous outside the field.

---

## 03 Influential

**有影響力不等於著名**

`influential` 強調改變他人想法／行為的能力；名氣不是必要條件。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

An influential adviser may work behind the scenes and remain little known.

---

## 04 Important

**重要不等於著名**

重要性指作用／地位／後果；不表示公眾知名度。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

An important discovery may be made by a scientist who is not famous.

---

## 05 Notorious

**惡名昭彰不是一般 famous**

`notorious` 是因負面原因而出名。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A notorious criminal is famous for something bad.

---

## 06 Recognisable

**能認出不等於著名**

`recognisable` 表示看得出／辨認得到；知名度範圍可很有限。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A logo can be recognisable to customers without being globally famous.

---

## 07 Accomplished

**有造詣不等於著名**

`accomplished` 指技能高、成就多，不要求廣為人知。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

An accomplished pianist may not be famous.

---

## 08 Celebrity

**名人是名詞，不是 famous 的直接形容詞同義詞**

`celebrity` 指名人身份；famous 是形容詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She is famous. She is a celebrity.

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Famous for
最常見：
- famous for its food
- famous for her novels
- famous for being...

## 02 Famous as
`famous as + role`

- famous as a singer
- famous as a scientist

`famous for` = 因什麼而聞名  
`famous as` = 以什麼身份聞名

## 03 Well-known
最安全、最中性的替代：
- well-known actor
- well-known brand
- well-known for

## 04 Renowned
通常帶正面聲望：
- renowned expert
- renowned university
- renowned restaurant

因此不適合一般負面 fame。

## 05 Prominent
不等於單純 famous：
- prominent politician
- prominent scientist
- prominent figure

可能主要表示地位高、能見度高。

## 06 Acclaimed
核心是 praise / positive reviews：
- critically acclaimed film
- acclaimed author

不是只要大家知道就可以用。

## 07 Eminent / distinguished
兩者常見於：
- academia
- medicine
- law
- science
- public service

比 famous 更正式、尊敬。

## 08 Legendary / iconic
- legendary = 傳奇、長期流傳
- iconic = 標誌性、成為象徵

兩者都比普通 famous 更強、更窄。
