# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Outdoor playground

**`outdoor playground` 可以指具體有遊具的戶外遊樂場，也可以較廣泛地指戶外供兒童玩耍的區域或空間。不同替代表達會強調露天位置、空間規劃、兒童用途或具體 playground 設施。**

核心替代表達：

`open-air play area / outdoor play area / outside play space / outdoor play space / open-air playground / children's play area / children's outdoor play area / playground`

> **核心原則**：先判斷句子重點是 **open-air location, general play area, spatial planning, children-specific use, or a concrete playground with equipment**。

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Open-air play area

**露天遊樂區；戶外遊戲區**

和 `outdoor playground` 很接近，強調遊樂區位於露天環境，而不是室內。它比 `playground` 稍描述性。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The housing estate includes an open-air play area for children.

屋苑設有一個供兒童使用的露天遊樂區。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a play area located in the open air**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、規劃、設施描述

---

## 02 Outdoor play area

**戶外遊樂區**

最直接、最自然的近義改寫之一。比 `outdoor playground` 稍廣，可包括有遊具的 playground，也可只是戶外活動空間。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The nursery has a secure outdoor play area.

幼兒園設有安全的戶外遊樂區。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **an outdoor area intended for children's play**

**REGISTER · 語域**

教育、設施、一般

---

## 03 Outside play space

**戶外遊戲空間**

自然、較描述性的說法，特別適合學校、幼兒教育和設施設計語境。`outside` 強調位置，`play space` 強調用途。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Children have access to a shaded outside play space.

兒童可以使用有遮蔭的戶外遊戲空間。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a designated space outside for play**

**REGISTER · 語域**

教育、一般、設計

---

## 04 Outdoor play space

**戶外遊戲空間**

和 `outside play space` 很接近，但 `outdoor` 作前置形容詞更常見於設計、教育和規劃文件。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The school expanded its outdoor play space last year.

學校去年擴建了戶外遊戲空間。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a purpose-designed outdoor space for play**

**REGISTER · 語域**

教育、規劃、一般

---

## 05 Open-air playground

**露天遊樂場**

和 `outdoor playground` 幾乎直接互換，強調遊樂場位於露天環境。語感較描述性，使用頻率通常低於 `outdoor playground`。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The park has a large open-air playground beside the café.

公園咖啡店旁有一個大型露天遊樂場。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a playground situated outdoors in the open air**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、旅遊、設施

---

## 06 Children's play area

**兒童遊樂區**

當上下文已經清楚是在戶外時，`children's play area` 可自然代替 `outdoor playground`。它突出使用者是兒童，不一定明示戶外。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> A children's play area is located next to the picnic lawn.

野餐草地旁設有兒童遊樂區。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **an area specifically intended for children to play**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、商場、公園、設施

---

## 07 Children's outdoor play area

**兒童戶外遊樂區**

比 `children's play area` 更精確，明確同時指出使用者是兒童和地點在戶外。常見於學校、屋苑和規劃語境。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The development includes a children's outdoor play area.

該發展項目包括一個兒童戶外遊樂區。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **an outdoor play area specifically for children**

**REGISTER · 語域**

規劃、教育、房地產

---

## 08 Playground

**遊樂場**

如果上下文已清楚是室外環境，單用 `playground` 通常已足夠。它比描述性短語更簡潔，也是最常見的基本詞。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The children spent an hour in the playground.

孩子們在遊樂場玩了一小時。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a place, usually outdoors, where children play**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

# 02 False synonyms

## Outdoor playground / Outdoor play area
兩者很接近；`outdoor play area` 稍廣，不一定有典型 playground 遊具。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The park has an outdoor playground with swings and climbing frames.  
The nursery has an outdoor play area for free play and games.

## Outdoor playground / Open-air play area
`open-air play area` 更描述性，特別突出「露天」。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
An outdoor playground may include fixed play equipment.  
An open-air play area simply emphasises that the play area is outside.

## Outside play space / Outdoor play space
兩者高度接近；`outdoor play space` 在教育、設計和規劃文件中通常更自然。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The nursery needs more outside play space.  
The planning report proposes additional outdoor play space.

## Outdoor play area / Outdoor play space
`area` 偏一個區域；`space` 更強調可用空間、面積和設計。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The park has a fenced outdoor play area.  
The school has limited outdoor play space because the site is small.

## Outdoor playground / Open-air playground
意思幾乎相同；`open-air playground` 較描述性、較少見。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The hotel has an outdoor playground.  
The brochure describes it as an open-air playground.

## Outdoor playground / Children's play area
`children's play area` 強調使用者是兒童，但不自動表示室外。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A shopping centre may have an indoor children's play area.  
A park may have an outdoor playground.

## Children's play area / Children's outdoor play area
後者明確補上「戶外」，資訊更完整。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The building contains a children's play area.  
The garden contains a children's outdoor play area.

## Outdoor playground / Playground
如果上下文已清楚是公園、校園等室外地方，單用 `playground` 通常已足夠。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The children ran to the playground in the park.  
The planning document specifies a new outdoor playground.

# 03 練習與解釋

> 每個目標詞／表達三題，共 **24 題**。

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**A. Open-air play area · 露天遊樂區；戶外遊戲區**

> The park includes an open-air play area beside the lake.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. Open-air play area ✓**  
句子線索「露天位置是重點」特別突出露天位置，而且不需要強調完整 playground 設施；`open-air play area` 最貼切。

**B. Outdoor play area**  
`outdoor play area`：最廣泛、自然，指戶外供遊戲的區域。這句線索是「露天位置是重點」；若需要突出露天、兒童專用、空間規劃或具體 playground 遊具，可用更具體詞。

**C. Outdoor play space**  
`outdoor play space`：偏教育／規劃，強調可供遊戲的戶外空間。這句線索是「露天位置是重點」；若是有鞦韆、攀爬架等明確 playground 設施，`playground` 更具體。

**D. Open-air playground**  
`open-air playground`：表示一個具體、露天的遊樂場。這句線索是「露天位置是重點」；若只是泛指戶外活動空間，這個詞可能過於具體。

**E. Indoor playroom**  
`Indoor playroom`：indoor playroom = 室內遊戲室；和 outdoor/open-air 的位置相反。 本句線索是「露天位置是重點」，所以地點、用途或設施類型不符合戶外遊樂場語境。

**F. Car park**  
`Car park`：car park = 停車場；不是遊樂區。 本句線索是「露天位置是重點」，所以地點、用途或設施類型不符合戶外遊樂場語境。

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Outdoor playground vs Outdoor play area** — playground 較具體；play area 較廣。
- **Open-air play area vs Outdoor play area** — 意思很近；open-air 更刻意強調露天。
- **Outside play space vs Outdoor play space** — outdoor 通常更自然於正式／設計文件。
- **Play area vs Play space** — area 偏界定區域；space 偏可用空間／面積。
- **Outdoor playground vs Open-air playground** — 幾乎同義，後者較描述性。
- **Children's play area vs Outdoor playground** — children's 強調使用者；outdoor 強調位置。
- **Children's play area vs Children's outdoor play area** — 後者把戶外條件說清楚。
- **Playground vs Play space** — playground 通常讓人想到具體遊樂設施；play space 可更開放、彈性。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Outdoor / outside

作前置形容詞時通常：

> outdoor playground  
> outdoor play area  
> outdoor play space

`outside` 也可作前置修飾語：

> outside play space

但在正式規劃／教育文本中，`outdoor play space` 往往更自然。

## Playground

可數：

> a playground  
> two playgrounds

## Play area / play space

> a play area  
> a play space

但泛指空間面積時 `play space` 也可較抽象：

> The school needs more outdoor play space.

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 強調露天位置？ → **open-air play area**
2. 最自然泛指戶外遊樂區？ → **outdoor play area**
3. 較描述性說室外遊戲空間？ → **outside play space**
4. 教育／規劃中強調可用空間？ → **outdoor play space**
5. 明確有 playground 設施且露天？ → **open-air playground**
6. 上下文已清楚戶外，只強調兒童？ → **children's play area**
7. 同時要說兒童＋戶外？ → **children's outdoor play area**
8. 上下文足夠清楚，想最簡潔？ → **playground**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`open-air play area / outdoor play area / outside play space / outdoor play space / open-air playground / children's play area / children's outdoor play area / playground`

最重要的 near-miss：

`recreation area / sports field / indoor playground / playroom / park`

尤其要記住：

> **playground** = 最具體、最常見  
> **play area** = 較廣  
> **play space** = 更偏空間／規劃概念  
> **children's play area** = 強調使用者  
> **open-air / outdoor** = 強調位置
