# LOSE INTEREST IN · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Lose interest in

**「對……失去興趣／興致減退」，要先分清是一般興趣下降、因重複而厭悶、失去熱情、失去投入，還是進一步變得漠不關心。**

本單元集中處理 **lose interest in = to stop finding someone or something as interesting, engaging, or appealing as before**。你提供的 **become less interested in; grow bored with** 分別代表 **一般興趣下降** 和 **因 boredom 而逐漸厭倦**。

這個詞組語義範圍相對集中，因此本次保留 **12 個**自然且真正有教學價值的詞／表達，而不是追求固定數量。共 **36 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**；正確答案位置在 A–F 之間平均分布。

## Tier 1 — Core declining-interest / boredom equivalents · 核心興趣下降與厭倦近義詞

### 01 Become less interested in

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：對……變得較沒興趣；興趣減退  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to have a lower level of interest in something than before  
**用法提示**：最直接、最透明的改寫；適合一般興趣逐漸下降。  
**常見搭配**：become less interested in a subject; become less interested in politics; gradually become less interested.  
**常見搭配對象**：subject, hobby, activity, topic.  
**與 lose interest in 的辨析**：最接近 lose interest in 的中性直述。  
**使用限制**：若原因是明確厭悶，grow bored with 更精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He became less interested in the course after the first few weeks.  
課程開始幾星期後，他對課程的興趣減退了。

### 02 Grow bored with

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：逐漸對……感到厭悶  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：to gradually become bored with something that was previously interesting  
**用法提示**：當失去興趣的原因是重複、單調或缺乏新鮮感時特別自然。  
**常見搭配**：grow bored with a routine; grow bored with a game; quickly grow bored.  
**常見搭配對象**：routine, game, task, activity.  
**與 lose interest in 的辨析**：明確指出 boredom 是興趣下降的原因。  
**使用限制**：若只是興趣自然轉移而沒有厭悶感，不宜強行使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She grew bored with the same exercise routine.  
她逐漸對同一套運動習慣感到厭悶。

### 03 Lose enthusiasm for

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：對……失去熱情；熱忱減退  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to stop feeling excited, energetic, or positive about an activity, plan, or idea  
**用法提示**：當原本很有熱情，但後來熱情與動力下降時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：lose enthusiasm for a project; lose enthusiasm for studying; gradually lose enthusiasm.  
**常見搭配對象**：project, study, plan, campaign.  
**與 lose interest in 的辨析**：強調熱情與動力下降，而非單純注意力減退。  
**使用限制**：原先並沒有明顯熱情時，語氣可能太強。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
After repeated delays, the team lost enthusiasm for the project.  
多次延誤後，團隊對這個項目失去熱情。

### 04 Tire of

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：厭倦；對……感到膩  
**語域**：neutral / concise  
**核心語義**：to become bored or annoyed with something after experiencing it repeatedly or for too long  
**用法提示**：適合重複活動、長期等待或一再發生的情況。  
**常見搭配**：tire of the routine; tire of waiting; tire of repetition; soon tire of.  
**常見搭配對象**：routine, waiting, repetition, behaviour.  
**與 lose interest in 的辨析**：常帶『做得太久／重複太多所以厭倦』意味。  
**使用限制**：若是中性興趣淡化，不一定適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He soon tired of doing the same task every day.  
他很快便厭倦每天做同一項工作。

### 05 Get bored with

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：對……感到無聊；厭倦  
**語域**：informal / very common  
**核心語義**：to start feeling bored by something and therefore stop finding it interesting  
**用法提示**：日常口語非常常見。  
**常見搭配**：get bored with a game; get bored with work; get bored easily.  
**常見搭配對象**：game, work, lesson, routine.  
**與 lose interest in 的辨析**：比 lose interest in 更直接、更口語地表達 boredom。  
**使用限制**：正式學術文體通常改用 become less interested / disengaged。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Children may get bored with the game if it lasts too long.  
如果遊戲持續太久，孩子可能會感到厭倦。

## Tier 2 — Disengagement / British / figurative variants · 失去投入、英式口語與比喻變體

### 06 Become disengaged from

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：對……失去投入；逐漸疏離  
**語域**：formal / education / workplace  
**核心語義**：to become less mentally, emotionally, or actively involved in something  
**用法提示**：適合教育、工作、培訓等參與度下降的語境。  
**常見搭配**：become disengaged from learning; disengaged from work; student disengagement.  
**常見搭配對象**：learning, work, school, project.  
**與 lose interest in 的辨析**：強調 attention / participation / involvement 下降。  
**使用限制**：普通娛樂喜好語境會顯得過於正式。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Some students become disengaged from lessons that feel repetitive.  
有些學生會對感覺重複的課堂逐漸失去投入。

### 07 Go off

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：不再喜歡；對……失去興趣  
**語域**：British English / informal  
**核心語義**：to stop liking or being interested in something or someone  
**用法提示**：英式英語很自然，可用於娛樂、食物、飲品、活動或人物。  
**常見搭配**：go off a TV series; go off coffee; go off someone.  
**常見搭配對象**：programme, food, drink, hobby, person.  
**與 lose interest in 的辨析**：比 lose interest in 更口語，也可帶『不再喜歡』。  
**使用限制**：正式寫作不宜；另有『食物變壞』等義項。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
I've gone off that series since the second season.  
第二季之後，我已經對那套劇集失去興趣。

### 08 Lose one's fascination with

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：對……失去着迷感；不再覺得吸引  
**語域**：neutral / expressive  
**核心語義**：to stop feeling strongly attracted to or captivated by something  
**用法提示**：當原本的興趣很強、接近着迷時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：lose fascination with fame; fascination faded; fascination with technology.  
**常見搭配對象**：technology, celebrity, subject, idea.  
**與 lose interest in 的辨析**：比一般 lose interest in 強，因 fascination 代表高度吸引。  
**使用限制**：原本只是普通興趣時不宜使用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Over time, she lost her fascination with celebrity culture.  
隨着時間過去，她不再對名人文化着迷。

### 09 Lose one's appetite for

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：對……失去興致；不再有意欲  
**語域**：figurative / neutral  
**核心語義**：to lose the desire or enthusiasm for an activity, risk, or course of action  
**用法提示**：常見於風險、投資、競爭、冒險等比喻語境。  
**常見搭配**：lose appetite for risk; appetite for investment; appetite for adventure.  
**常見搭配對象**：risk, investment, adventure, competition.  
**與 lose interest in 的辨析**：強調 desire / willingness 減退，而非 boredom。  
**使用限制**：普通學科興趣通常不用，除非刻意使用比喻。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
After the losses, investors lost their appetite for risky assets.  
遭受虧損後，投資者對高風險資產失去興致。

## Tier 3 — Indifference / weariness / gradual-drift variants · 冷淡、疲乏與逐漸淡出變體

### 10 Become indifferent to

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：對……變得漠不關心；不再在意  
**語域**：formal / neutral  
**核心語義**：to stop caring much about something or reacting strongly to it  
**用法提示**：當興趣下降進一步變成『不在乎／沒有反應』時可用。  
**常見搭配**：become indifferent to criticism; indifferent to politics; grow indifferent.  
**常見搭配對象**：politics, criticism, result, issue.  
**與 lose interest in 的辨析**：比 lose interest in 更冷淡、更接近不在乎。  
**使用限制**：若仍在乎，只是興趣稍降，這個詞太強。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He gradually became indifferent to the debate.  
他逐漸對這場辯論變得漠不關心。

### 11 Grow weary of

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：對……逐漸厭倦；感到疲乏  
**語域**：formal / literary  
**核心語義**：to become tired of something, especially because it has continued for too long  
**用法提示**：適合長期重複、延誤、藉口或耐性耗盡的語境。  
**常見搭配**：grow weary of delays; grow weary of arguments; weary of waiting.  
**常見搭配對象**：delay, argument, repetition, excuse.  
**與 lose interest in 的辨析**：比 tire of 稍正式，更有累積疲乏感。  
**使用限制**：普通輕微興趣下降時可能太重。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Residents grew weary of repeated promises with no action.  
居民逐漸厭倦一再承諾卻沒有行動。

### 12 Drift away from

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：逐漸疏遠；慢慢不再投入  
**語域**：neutral / figurative  
**核心語義**：to gradually become less involved in, attached to, or interested in something without a sudden break  
**用法提示**：適合興趣慢慢淡化、人生重點轉移、自然退出的語境。  
**常見搭配**：drift away from a hobby; drift away from old interests; gradually drift away.  
**常見搭配對象**：hobby, interest, group, activity.  
**與 lose interest in 的辨析**：強調漸進、自然淡出，不一定有 boredom。  
**使用限制**：若是明確厭倦或主動拒絕，tire of / go off 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
As his priorities changed, he drifted away from competitive gaming.  
隨着人生重點改變，他逐漸不再投入競技遊戲。

## 04 False synonyms / lose-interest traps · 容易誤選的詞與興趣下降陷阱

### 01 Dislike

**不喜歡不等於 lose interest in**  
dislike 表示負面喜惡；lose interest in 可以只是興趣淡化，未必討厭。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Loss of interest does not necessarily mean dislike.

### 02 Hate

**憎恨遠強於 lose interest in**  
hate 是強烈負面情緒。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Hate is much stronger than losing interest.

### 03 Ignore

**忽略不等於失去興趣**  
ignore 是不理會某事的行為；原因未必是興趣下降。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Ignoring is behaviour, not necessarily a change in interest.

### 04 Forget

**忘記不等於 lose interest in**  
forget 是記憶問題；lose interest in 是態度／注意力改變。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Forgetting and losing interest are different processes.

### 05 Give up

**放棄不等於一定失去興趣**  
give up 表示停止做某事；可能因時間、金錢或困難，而不是興趣下降。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Stopping an activity does not prove loss of interest.

### 06 Get bored with

**只在 boredom sense 接近**  
如果興趣因人生重點改變而淡化，不一定是 boredom。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Boredom is only one cause of lost interest.

### 07 Go off

**英式口語近義詞，但可帶『不再喜歡』**  
go off 比 lose interest in 更口語，也有其他義項。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Go off is informal and polysemous.

### 08 Interest / interested

**詞性陷阱**  
interest 可作名詞／動詞；interested 是形容詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Interest and interested have different grammatical roles.

- **C.** ignore — describes the behaviour of not paying attention; it does not itself explain a change in interest; the sentence instead points to after the first few weeks shows a simple decline in interest, without implying dislike or boredom.
- **D.** forget — is about memory, not about interest or motivation; the sentence instead points to after the first few weeks shows a simple decline in interest, without implying dislike or boredom.
- **E.** give up — means stopping an activity; people can give something up for reasons other than loss of interest; the sentence instead points to after the first few weeks shows a simple decline in interest, without implying dislike or boredom.
- **F.** become fascinated by — means interest becomes stronger, the opposite of the target meaning; the sentence instead points to after the first few weeks shows a simple decline in interest, without implying dislike or boredom.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **lose interest in vs become less interested in**: 後者是最透明、最中性的直接改寫。
- **lose interest in vs grow bored with**: grow bored with 明確指出 boredom 是原因。
- **lose interest in vs lose enthusiasm for**: lose enthusiasm for 強調熱情和動力下降。
- **lose interest in vs tire of**: tire of 常因長期重複或做得太久而厭倦。
- **lose interest in vs disengage**: disengage 更強調參與和投入下降。
- **lose interest in vs go off**: go off 是英式口語，亦可帶『不再喜歡』。
- **lose interest in vs indifferent**: indifferent 更強，表示已不太在乎。
- **lose interest in vs give up**: give up 是停止行動；原因未必是興趣下降。
- **lose interest in vs dislike**: dislike 是負面喜惡；失去興趣未必變成討厭。
- **lose interest in vs drift away from**: drift away from 特別強調逐漸、自然淡出。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & lose-interest traps · 文法、搭配與興趣下降陷阱

- **lose interest in + noun / -ing**：lose interest in politics / lose interest in reading。
- **become less interested in + noun / -ing** 是最直接改寫。
- **grow / get bored with + noun**；亦可見 bored of，但 with 很穩妥。
- **lose enthusiasm for + noun / -ing**：lose enthusiasm for the project / for studying。
- **tire of + noun / -ing**：tire of waiting / tire of the routine。
- **become disengaged from + noun** 常見於 education / workplace。
- 英式口語 **go off + noun/person**：go off a TV series / go off coffee。
- **lose one's fascination with + noun** 比一般 lose interest 強。
- **lose one's appetite for + noun** 常見於 risk / investment / competition。
- **become indifferent to + noun**：indifferent to criticism / politics。
- **grow weary of + noun / -ing**：weary of delays / waiting。
- **drift away from + noun/group/activity**：重點是 gradual decline。
- **dislike / hate / ignore / forget / give up ≠ lose interest in**：它們分別表示負面喜惡、強烈厭惡、不理會、忘記或停止行動。
