# Densely populated

「人口稠密」，究竟是哪一種 `densely populated`？

人口很多、居民密集、居住密度高、城市擁擠、人口集中、住戶密布——英文會按**是否強調人口密度本身、總人口很多、居民彼此住得很近、地方是否擁擠、是否屬城市規劃／人口統計語境，以及語氣是正式、描述性還是口語**選用不同的字和常見表達。

# 01 同義詞指南

## Densely populated 的 14 種更精準說法

### 01 Heavily populated
**人口眾多的；人口密集的**

非常接近 `densely populated`，表示某地有大量人口，常用於城市、地區、沿海帶或交通走廊。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The river passes through a heavily populated region.

這條河流經一個人口密集地區。

**USAGE CLUE**

- heavily populated area / region
- close synonym
- neutral-formal
- 常強調「很多人居住」

---

### 02 Crowded with residents
**住滿居民的；居民非常密集的**

描述性表達，強調某個地方住著很多人，空間感通常比 `densely populated` 更具體。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The district is crowded with residents and apartment blocks.

這個地區住滿居民和住宅大廈。

**USAGE CLUE**

- descriptive
- people visibly concentrated
- can sound more crowded than statistical

---

### 03 Populous
**人口眾多的**

較正式，表示某城市、地區或國家人口很多；重點偏向「總人口多」，不一定等於每平方公里密度高。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> It is one of the most populous cities in the region.

它是該區人口最多的城市之一。

**USAGE CLUE**

- populous city / country / region
- total population-focused
- formal
- not identical to high density

---

### 04 Highly populated
**人口很多的；人口高度集中的**

常見描述性說法，表示某地人口數量高；在自然英文中 `densely populated` / `heavily populated` 通常更地道地表達「密度高」。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The coastal zone is highly populated.

沿海地帶人口很多。

**USAGE CLUE**

- descriptive
- common but less precise than densely populated
- population level rather than technical density

---

### 05 Densely settled
**聚居稠密的；居民分布密集的**

表示人們在某地區居住得很密集，常見於地理、歷史和人口分布描述。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The valley has been densely settled for centuries.

這個山谷數百年來一直聚居稠密。

**USAGE CLUE**

- geography / history
- settlement pattern
- close to densely populated

---

### 06 Heavily settled
**聚居人口多的；居住密集的**

與 `densely settled` 接近，強調一個地區有大量居民定居。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The fertile plain is heavily settled.

這片肥沃平原聚居人口眾多。

**USAGE CLUE**

- settlement geography
- less common than densely settled
- region-focused

---

### 07 Densely inhabited
**居住人口稠密的**

正式描述，表示某地被大量人口居住。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The island's northern coast is densely inhabited.

這個島的北岸人口居住稠密。

**USAGE CLUE**

- formal / geographic
- inhabited = 有人居住
- density-focused

---

### 08 High-density
**高密度的；人口高密度的**

常見於城市規劃、住房和人口統計。需要由上下文說清楚是 `high-density population`, `high-density housing`, `high-density urban area`。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The city encourages high-density residential development near stations.

該市鼓勵在車站附近發展高密度住宅。

**USAGE CLUE**

- urban planning
- housing / development / population
- technical
- usually modifies another noun

---

### 09 High population density
**人口密度高**

最直接、技術性最強的改寫，適合數據、地理和人口統計。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The district has a very high population density.

這個地區人口密度非常高。

**USAGE CLUE**

- demographic / statistical
- measurable density
- noun phrase rather than adjective

---

### 10 Packed with people
**擠滿人；人非常多**

口語而生動，表示某地某時有很多人擠在一起。它可能只是暫時擁擠，不一定表示居民人口密度高。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The town centre was packed with people during the festival.

節日期間市中心擠滿了人。

**USAGE CLUE**

- informal
- visible crowding
- may be temporary
- not a demographic term

---

### 11 Crowded
**擁擠的；人多的**

表示某地有太多人，以致空間顯得擠迫。它可與人口稠密重疊，但 `crowded` 更偏當下空間感。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The neighbourhood has become increasingly crowded.

這個社區變得越來越擁擠。

**USAGE CLUE**

- crowded streets / housing / neighbourhood
- congestion feeling
- broader than population density

---

### 12 Teeming with people
**人潮湧動；到處都是人**

生動、較文學化，表示某地充滿大量人群。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The old city was teeming with people.

舊城區到處都是人。

**USAGE CLUE**

- vivid / descriptive
- temporary or general abundance
- not statistical

---

### 13 Densely occupied
**佔用密集的；居住密度高的**

較技術或描述性，可用於建築、土地或住房單元被大量居民佔用的情況；不如 `densely populated` 常見。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The inner-city blocks are densely occupied.

市中心街區居住密度很高。

**USAGE CLUE**

- built environment / housing
- occupancy-focused
- contextual, not universal synonym

---

### 14 Home to a large concentration of people
**聚居大量人口；人口高度集中**

較長但很自然的正式解釋性表達，特別適合地理、報告和教學。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The delta is home to a large concentration of people.

這個三角洲聚居著大量人口。

**USAGE CLUE**

- formal descriptive phrase
- concentration-focused
- useful when avoiding repeated “population density”

# 02 False synonyms

## 01 densely populated / populous

### populous 強調總人口，不一定強調密度

一個大國可以很 populous，但人口分布很分散；一個面積很小的城市可以人口總數不算全球最多，卻非常 densely populated。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> India is a highly populous country.  
> A small urban district may be densely populated even if its total population is much smaller.

---

## 02 densely populated / crowded

### crowded 更強調當下擠迫感

`crowded` 可以因遊客、活動或通勤人潮而暫時出現；`densely populated` 通常指居民人口分布。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The station is crowded during rush hour.  
> The surrounding district is densely populated throughout the year.

---

## 03 densely populated / urban

### 城市化不等於人口一定稠密

`urban` 只表示城市性質；有些城市區域人口密度並不高。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The area is urban and highly developed.  
> The central district is densely populated.

---

## 04 densely populated / overpopulated

### overpopulated 帶「人口過多」的負面判斷

`densely populated` 只是描述密度高；`overpopulated` 暗示人口超過資源、空間或環境承載能力。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The city is densely populated.  
> Critics argue that the island is overpopulated relative to its resources.

---

## 05 densely populated / congested

### congested 多指交通或空間阻塞

`congested` 常形容 roads、traffic、airspace，也可形容某區擁擠，但不是人口統計術語。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The highway is heavily congested.  
> The neighbourhood is densely populated.

---

## 06 densely populated / inhabited

### inhabited 只表示「有人居住」

`inhabited` 不代表人口很多；只要有人住就可以是 inhabited。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The island is inhabited by a small community.  
> The coastal plain is densely inhabited.

---

## 07 densely populated / high-density

### high-density 通常需要說明是哪種密度

`high-density` 常修飾 housing、development、population；單獨用時語義可能不完整。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The district is densely populated.  
> The council approved high-density housing near the station.

---

## 08 densely populated / population

### 詞性陷阱

`densely populated` 是形容詞片語；`population` 是名詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The area is densely populated.  
> The area has a large population.

# 03 練習與解釋

## 練習 01.1

**Target phrase: Heavily populated · 人口眾多的；人口密集的**

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

> The river flows through a densely populated region with several large cities.

**中文翻譯**

這條河流經一個人口密集、擁有多個大城市的地區。

A. heavily populated  
B. populous  
C. crowded  
D. inhabited  
E. urban  
F. population

### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**heavily populated · 人口眾多的；人口密集的**

> The river flows through a heavily populated region.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋

**A. heavily populated ✓**  
`region with several large cities` 表示大量人口集中居住，`heavily populated region` 是自然近義改寫。

**B. populous**  
也表示人口多，但更偏總人口數量，不如 heavily populated 強調該區人口分布密集。

**C. crowded**  
更偏「擁擠感」，不一定是人口統計描述。

**D. inhabited**  
只表示有人居住，力度太弱。

**E. urban**  
表示城市化，不必然人口密度高。

**F. population**  
是名詞，不能直接修飾 region 作表語替代。

# 05 高混淆組

## densely populated vs heavily populated
- **densely populated**：最標準地表達人口密度高。
- **heavily populated**：非常接近，更強調有大量人口。

## densely populated vs populous
- **densely populated**：人口相對於面積集中。
- **populous**：總人口多。

## densely populated vs crowded
- **densely populated**：常住人口密度。
- **crowded**：當下空間感，可能只是短時人潮。

## densely populated vs densely settled
- **densely populated**：人口分布。
- **densely settled**：聚落／定居格局。

## high-density vs high population density
- **high-density**：複合形容詞，常修飾 housing/development。
- **high population density**：人口統計名詞片語。

## crowded vs packed with people
- **crowded**：一般擁擠。
- **packed with people**：更口語、更強調擠滿人。

## packed with people vs teeming with people
- **packed with people**：強調沒有空間、很擠。
- **teeming with people**：強調人非常多、到處都是。

## inhabited vs densely inhabited
- **inhabited**：有人住。
- **densely inhabited**：人口居住密度高。

# 08 生成／評估注意事項

1. 固定為 **14 alternatives + 3 questions each = 42 exercises**。
2. 每題包含：
   - Target phrase + Chinese gloss
   - READ THE CONTEXT
   - 中文翻譯
   - 六個選項
   - `CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案`
   - English target + Chinese gloss
   - 正確改寫
   - A–F 個別解釋
3. A–F feedback 採用：**詞義 → 正常搭配／語域 → 本題為何適合或不適合**。
4. 每組 False synonyms 都包含 **ENGLISH COMPARISON**。
5. Distractors 優先使用真正容易混淆的人口／城市詞：
   - populous
   - crowded
   - inhabited
   - high-density
   - overpopulated
   - urban
6. 使用者提供的 `heavily populated`, `crowded with residents` 是核心：
   - heavily populated = 最接近 general synonym
   - crowded with residents = 更具空間感、描述性
7. `populous` 不能教成完全等同 densely populated：
   - populous = total population
   - densely populated = population per area / concentration
8. `highly populated` 可用，但自然度和精確度通常不如 heavily/densely populated。
9. `densely settled` / `heavily settled` 很適合歷史地理與聚落分布。
10. `densely inhabited` 是較正式地理描述，重點是居民密集居住。
11. `high-density` 是複合形容詞，通常需要修飾 housing / development / urban area。
12. `high population density` 是統計／人口學最精確表達。
13. `packed with people` / `teeming with people` 常描述暫時人潮，不能當成永久人口密度的直接同義詞。
14. `crowded` 強調擠迫感，不一定是居民人口密度。
15. `densely occupied` 只適合 occupancy / housing / built environment 等特定語境。
16. `home to a large concentration of people` 是高價值正式解釋性改寫。
17. 真正進階題目應直接比較：
    - densely populated / heavily populated
    - densely populated / populous
    - densely populated / crowded
    - densely populated / densely settled / densely inhabited
    - high-density / high population density
    - crowded / packed with people / teeming with people
    - inhabited / densely inhabited
    - densely populated / overpopulated
18. `densely populated` 本身仍然是最標準、最精確的核心表達；其他替代語主要增加「總人口、人口密度、聚落形式、住宅佔用、擠迫感、短時人潮與人口集中程度」上的精度。
