# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Appearance

**「外觀／樣子／形象」，究竟是哪一種 `appearance`？**

`appearance` 是一個很廣的詞，可以表示：

1. **人或物看起來的樣子**
   → `look`, `looks`, `visual appearance`, `outward form`
2. **外在呈現方式／視覺風格**
   → `visual style`, `presentation`, `form`, `aspect`
3. **對外建立的形象**
   → `image`
4. **只談表面、不談內在**
   → `outward appearance`

> **核心原則**
>
> 這一組只保留和 `appearance` **較接近、在特定語境中可以近乎互換** 的詞或常用表達。
>
> 不把 `beauty`, `fashion`, `face`, `design`, `reputation` 等列為同義詞，因為它們只和 `appearance` 有關，但不等於 `appearance`。

# 01 同義詞指南

## Appearance 的 10 個近義／近乎可互換說法

---

## 01 Look

**外觀；樣子**

最常見、最自然的近義詞之一。可指人、房間、產品、網站等「看起來的樣子」。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The new lighting changed the look of the room.

新的燈光改變了房間的外觀。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- the look of something
- change the look
- modern look
- professional look
- natural look

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **look = the way someone or something appears visually**

---

## 02 Looks

**外貌；相貌**

通常用複數，特別常指人的外表和相貌。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> People should not be judged only by their looks.

不應只根據外貌判斷一個人。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- good looks
- physical looks
- judge by looks
- youthful looks

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **looks = a person's physical appearance**

---

## 03 Image

**形象；外在形象**

當 `appearance` 指一個人、公司、品牌或組織給外界的整體印象時，`image` 很接近。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The company redesigned its stores to create a more modern image.

公司重新設計店舖，以建立更現代的形象。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- public image
- professional image
- brand image
- personal image
- improve one's image

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **image = the appearance or impression presented to others**

---

## 04 Outward appearance

**外表；外在樣子**

強調從外面看得到的樣子，不談內在情況。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The building's outward appearance has changed very little.

這座建築物的外觀幾乎沒有改變。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- outward appearance
- change in outward appearance
- judge by outward appearance
- outward appearance of...

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **outward appearance = what something looks like externally**

---

## 05 Outward form

**外形；外在形態**

較正式，強調物件或事物從外面看到的形狀、結構或形態。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The device has retained the outward form of the original model.

這部裝置保留了原型的外在形態。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- outward form
- external form
- retain its form
- change in form

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **outward form = external shape or visible structure**

---

## 06 Visual appearance

**視覺外觀**

較正式、技術性較強，常見於設計、工程、醫療、產品描述等。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The update changes the visual appearance of the interface.

這次更新改變了介面的視覺外觀。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- visual appearance
- overall visual appearance
- visual appearance of...
- improve visual appearance

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **visual appearance = how something looks to the eye**

---

## 07 Visual style

**視覺風格**

當 `appearance` 不只是外形，而是顏色、字體、版面、造型等整體風格時，`visual style` 很接近。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The website now has a cleaner visual style.

這個網站現在有更簡潔的視覺風格。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- visual style
- distinctive visual style
- modern visual style
- consistent visual style

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **visual style = the characteristic way something is visually presented**

---

## 08 Presentation

**呈現方式；外在呈現**

當 `appearance` 指某物如何被整理、展示或呈現給別人看時，`presentation` 很接近。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The food's presentation was simple but attractive.

食物的呈現方式簡單但吸引。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- visual presentation
- overall presentation
- presentation of the product
- improve presentation

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **presentation = the way something is arranged or shown outwardly**

---

## 09 Form

**形態；外形**

可指事物的形狀、結構或呈現形式。比 `appearance` 稍偏「形態」而不只是表面視覺印象。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The sculpture has a simple geometric form.

這件雕塑有簡單的幾何外形。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- physical form
- geometric form
- external form
- take a different form

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **form = visible shape or structural appearance**

---

## 10 Aspect

**外觀的一面；視覺面貌**

較正式，指某物呈現出來的一個面貌或特徵；在部分語境可接近 `appearance`。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The renovation improved the overall aspect of the building.

翻新改善了建築物的整體面貌。

**COMMON PATTERNS · 常見搭配**

- overall aspect
- visual aspect
- external aspect
- aspect of the design

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **aspect = one visible or noticeable side of something's appearance**

# 02 False synonyms

## 01 Appearance / Beauty

### 外觀不等於美貌

`appearance` 只是看起來的樣子。  
`beauty` 帶有正面審美判斷。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**

> The appearance changed.  
> Its beauty impressed everyone.

---

## 02 Appearance / Face

### 外貌不只是一張臉

`face` 只指臉。  
`appearance` 可以包括髮型、衣著、身形、整體造型。

---

## 03 Appearance / Image

### 外觀和形象高度重疊，但不完全相同

`image` 常包括「別人怎樣看你」的社會印象。  
`appearance` 可以只是純粹視覺外觀。

---

## 04 Appearance / Design

### 外觀不等於設計

`design` 包括功能、結構、規劃。  
`appearance` 只是其中可見的一面。

---

## 05 Look / Looks

### 單數和複數含義不同

`look` 常指物件或整體視覺效果。  
`looks` 常指人的外貌。

> the look of the room  
> her good looks

---

## 06 Visual appearance / Visual style

### 看起來怎樣不等於風格

`visual appearance` 更客觀。  
`visual style` 強調一致的審美特徵。

---

## 07 Presentation / Appearance

### 呈現方式不一定等於物件本身外觀

`presentation` 常包括擺放、包裝、展示方式。

---

## 08 Form / Appearance

### 形態比 appearance 更偏結構

`form` 常強調形狀和結構。  
`appearance` 更廣，包括顏色、表面、整體感覺。

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

## Appearance vs Look

**appearance** = 中性、範圍廣。  
**look** = 更日常、更自然。

> change the appearance  
> change the look

---

## Look vs Looks

**look** 常指人或物的整體視覺效果。  
**looks** 通常特別指人的外貌。

> the look of the room  
> her good looks

---

## Appearance vs Image

**appearance** = 看起來怎樣。  
**image** = 給外界的整體形象或印象。

---

## Outward appearance vs Visual appearance

**outward appearance** = 強調外表和內在的對比。  
**visual appearance** = 更客觀、更技術。

---

## Visual appearance vs Visual style

**visual appearance** = 客觀外觀。  
**visual style** = 有一致審美特色的外觀風格。

---

## Presentation vs Appearance

**presentation** = 怎樣展示／安排。  
**appearance** = 本身看起來怎樣。

---

## Form vs Appearance

**form** = 形狀、結構。  
**appearance** = 更廣，包括顏色、表面、風格。

---

## Aspect vs Appearance

**aspect** 常表示外觀的一個面向或較正式的「面貌」。  
`appearance` 更一般。

---

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

## Appearance

可數或不可數，視語境而定：

> physical appearance  
> a strange appearance

---

## Look / Looks

`look`：
> a modern look  
> the look of the room

`looks`：
> her looks  
> good looks

---

## Image

常見：

> public image  
> brand image  
> professional image

不要把 `image` 和 `appearance` 在所有物理外觀句子中機械互換。

---

## Form

常見：

> physical form  
> geometric form  
> external form

---

# 09 核心決策問題

1. **只是普通地說「外觀／樣子」嗎？** → `look`
2. **特別指人的外貌嗎？** → `looks`
3. **是品牌／人物的對外形象嗎？** → `image`
4. **要強調外表而不是內在嗎？** → `outward appearance`
5. **要強調外形、形態嗎？** → `outward form`
6. **是在技術／設計語境描述可見外觀嗎？** → `visual appearance`
7. **重點是整體審美風格嗎？** → `visual style`
8. **重點是怎樣被安排、展示或呈現嗎？** → `presentation`
9. **重點是形狀或結構嗎？** → `form`
10. **是在說外觀的一個面向或較正式的整體面貌嗎？** → `aspect`

---

# 10 Final note for system use

最接近的替代表達：

`look / looks / image / outward appearance / outward form / visual appearance / visual style / presentation / form / aspect`

常用 idioms / expressions：

`keep up appearances / appearances can be deceiving / judge a book by its cover / for appearance's sake / put on a good face`

不要把以下詞當成 `appearance` 的直接同義詞：

`beauty / face / fashion / design / reputation`

它們只是相關概念。

真正的選詞依據是：

> **Are you referring to someone's looks, an object's visible form, a brand image, a visual style, or the way something is presented?**
