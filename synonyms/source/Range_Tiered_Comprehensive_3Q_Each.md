# RANGE · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Range

**「範圍／種類／選擇／跨度」是一個高度多義的名詞。** 最重要的是先分清它是在說：多種類型、可供選擇的一組項目、兩端之間的跨度、連續光譜、涵蓋面，還是統計分布。

本單元集中處理 **range = a variety or selection of things, or the extent between limits, positions, values, or areas of coverage**。你提供的 **variety; span; selection** 正好代表 **多樣種類、跨度、可選項目** 三個核心方向。

由於 range 的義項很多，本次保留 **10 個**自然且高實用度的近義／情境表達，並明確區分 variety/selection 類、extent 類、coverage 類和 distribution 類。共 **30 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Range 的 sense | 核心問題 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|---|
| 多種類型／選擇 | 是否在說不同種類或可選項目？ | variety, selection, assortment, array |
| 兩端之間的跨度 | 是否有 lower-to-upper / from A to B？ | span, spectrum, spread, gamut |
| 涵蓋面／邊界 | 是否在說研究、課程、項目的覆蓋範圍？ | breadth, scope |

## Tier 1 — Core variety / span / selection equivalents · 核心種類、跨度與選擇近義詞

### 01 Variety

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：種類；多樣性  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：a number of different types or forms of something  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **range** 在『多種不同選擇／種類』sense 最核心的近義詞之一。  
**常見搭配**：a variety of options; wide variety; variety of products; variety of activities; variety of styles.  
**常見搭配對象**：option, product, activity, style, food.  
**與 range 的辨析**：Variety 強調 differences among types；range 可同時強調由一端到另一端的跨度。  
**使用限制**：若句子重點是 minimum-to-maximum extent，而不是多種類型，span / spread 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The shop sells a wide variety of local products.  
這間商店售賣多種本地產品。

### 02 Span

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：跨度；範圍  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：the full extent from one end, point, value, or period to another  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，當 range 表示時間、年齡、距離、數值或能力的『跨度』時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：age span; time span; span of years; broad span; full span.  
**常見搭配對象**：age, time, year, distance, value.  
**與 range 的辨析**：Span 強調兩端之間的 extent；variety 則強調不同種類。  
**使用限制**：產品種類或菜式選擇通常不用 span。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The participants covered an age span of more than forty years.  
參加者的年齡跨度超過四十年。

### 03 Selection

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：選擇；可供選擇的一系列項目  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：a group of items or options available to choose from  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 適合商店、餐單、課程、產品、款式等『可選項目』。  
**常見搭配**：wide selection; selection of products; good selection; selection of courses; selection available.  
**常見搭配對象**：product, course, item, dish, colour.  
**與 range 的辨析**：Selection 強調 available choices；range 更廣，可同時表示跨度、程度或種類。  
**使用限制**：年齡／溫度／時間範圍通常不能用 selection。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The library has a good selection of books for young readers.  
圖書館有很多適合年輕讀者選擇的書籍。

### 04 Assortment

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：各式各樣的一組；什錦選擇  
**語域**：neutral / retail  
**核心語義**：a collection of different types of items grouped or offered together  
**用法提示**：當 range 指一組不同款式、產品、甜點、工具等實體項目時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：assortment of goods; assorted products; wide assortment; assortment of cakes; product assortment.  
**常見搭配對象**：goods, cake, tool, product, item.  
**與 range 的辨析**：Assortment 比 selection 更像『一組各式各樣的東西』，常見於零售／實體物品。  
**使用限制**：抽象能力、意見、年齡跨度不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The café offers an assortment of cakes and pastries.  
這間咖啡店提供各式蛋糕和酥點。

### 05 Spectrum

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：光譜；範圍；連續系列  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：a broad continuous range of related positions, qualities, opinions, or possibilities  
**用法提示**：當 range 表示由一端到另一端的連續分布，例如政治立場、能力、症狀、顏色時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：broad spectrum; full spectrum; spectrum of opinion; spectrum of abilities; political spectrum.  
**常見搭配對象**：opinion, ability, symptom, colour, view.  
**與 range 的辨析**：Spectrum 通常暗示 continuum，而不是一堆離散選項。  
**使用限制**：單純商品選擇用 selection / assortment 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The survey included people from across the political spectrum.  
調查涵蓋不同政治立場的人士。

## Tier 2 — Coverage / abundance / distribution variants · 涵蓋面、豐富度與分布型變體

### 06 Array

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：一系列；大量多樣的選擇  
**語域**：neutral / slightly formal  
**核心語義**：a large and often impressive collection of different things  
**用法提示**：當 range 想強調『種類多而且數量豐富』時可用。  
**常見搭配**：wide array; array of services; array of options; impressive array; array of features.  
**常見搭配對象**：service, option, feature, product, tool.  
**與 range 的辨析**：Array 帶 abundance / impressive variety 色彩，比一般 range 更有『琳瑯滿目』感。  
**使用限制**：數值跨度或連續區間不用 array。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The centre provides a wide array of support services.  
中心提供多種支援服務。

### 07 Breadth

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：廣度；涵蓋面  
**語域**：formal / academic  
**核心語義**：the extent to which something covers many areas, subjects, experiences, or possibilities  
**用法提示**：當 range 的重點是涵蓋面廣，而不是列出具體項目時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：breadth of experience; breadth of knowledge; breadth of coverage; breadth of services; broad scope.  
**常見搭配對象**：experience, knowledge, coverage, service, curriculum.  
**與 range 的辨析**：Breadth 強調 coverage / width of scope，而不是具體可數選項。  
**使用限制**：實體商品 range 通常不用 breadth。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The breadth of the course allows students to explore several disciplines.  
課程涵蓋面廣，讓學生探索多個學科。

### 08 Scope

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：範圍；涵蓋界線  
**語域**：formal / very common  
**核心語義**：the extent or boundaries of what an activity, study, responsibility, or subject includes  
**用法提示**：當 range 指研究、工作、計劃、政策或責任的『涵蓋範圍』時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：scope of the study; project scope; scope of work; broad scope; within scope.  
**常見搭配對象**：study, project, work, policy, responsibility.  
**與 range 的辨析**：Scope 是 inclusion/boundary-focused；range 更可表示種類、數值跨度或選擇。  
**使用限制**：商品款式、顏色選擇等用 selection / variety 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The scope of the study was limited to urban schools.  
研究範圍只限於城市學校。

### 09 Spread

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：分布範圍；差距；跨度  
**語域**：neutral / statistical  
**核心語義**：the extent of distribution between lower and upper values or across different positions  
**用法提示**：當 range 指數據、價格、年齡、分數等『分布／差距』時可用。  
**常見搭配**：price spread; spread of scores; wide spread; spread of values; income spread.  
**常見搭配對象**：price, score, value, income, age.  
**與 range 的辨析**：Spread 比 span 更強調 distribution；span 更偏兩端之間的 extent。  
**使用限制**：產品種類或課程選項不用 spread。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
There was a wide spread of scores across the class.  
全班分數分布範圍很廣。

## Tier 3 — Full-spectrum literary variant · 全範圍書面型變體

### 10 Gamut

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：全部範圍；整個光譜  
**語域**：formal / literary  
**核心語義**：the complete or very broad range of possibilities, emotions, reactions, or types  
**用法提示**：常見固定搭配 **run the gamut from A to B**，表示涵蓋幾乎整個範圍。  
**常見搭配**：run the gamut; full gamut; gamut of emotions; gamut of responses; gamut from A to B.  
**常見搭配對象**：emotion, response, reaction, style, possibility.  
**與 range 的辨析**：Gamut 比 range 更強、更書面，常暗示 almost the whole spectrum。  
**使用限制**：日常簡單列表或數值區間通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The interviews covered the full gamut of opinions on the issue.  
訪談涵蓋了對這個議題幾乎全部的意見範圍。

## 04 False synonyms / range traps · 容易誤選的選擇、跨度與分類陷阱

### 01 Choice

**選擇 ≠ range**  
choice 可以是一個選擇行為或單一選項；range 通常指一組選擇或某種跨度。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Choice is singular/action-focused; range is broader.

### 02 List

**清單 ≠ range**  
list 是列出的項目；range 是項目的多樣性或涵蓋面。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A list is a format, not a semantic range.

### 03 Category

**類別 ≠ range**  
category 是一個分類；range 可以包含多個 categories。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A category is one grouping.

### 04 Level

**水平 ≠ range**  
level 是單一程度；range 通常包含多個程度或上下限。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A level is one point, not an interval.

### 05 Extent

**程度／範圍 ≠ 所有 range**  
extent 只適合範圍／程度 sense，不適合商品 selection sense。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Extent is scope-focused.

### 06 Series

**系列 ≠ range**  
series 強調順序或同類項目連續出現，不一定有多樣性或跨度。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Series is sequence-focused.

### 07 Spectrum

**只在 continuum sense 接近**  
spectrum 很適合連續分布，但不能全面代替 range。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Spectrum implies a continuum.

### 08 Range / ranging

**詞性陷阱**  
range 可作名詞或動詞；ranging 常是現在分詞／形容詞結構。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check grammar before replacing.

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

**中文翻譯**  
市場提供多種新鮮農產品。

- A. Variety
- B. Selection
- C. Assortment
- D. Choice
- E. List
- F. Category

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Variety** · 種類；多樣性

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The market offers a wide variety of fresh produce.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Variety ✓** — means different types or forms of something; it focuses on diversity rather than endpoints. In this sentence, different types of produce are the central idea. That makes **Variety** the most precise replacement for this particular sense of *range*.
- **B.** **Selection** — means a group of available items or options from which someone can choose. It is related to extent, diversity or choice, but it does not match the decisive clue here: different types of produce are the central idea.
- **C.** **Assortment** — means a mixed collection of different physical items, especially in retail or grouped sets. It is related to extent, diversity or choice, but it does not match the decisive clue here: different types of produce are the central idea.
- **D.** **Choice** — means the act or result of choosing and is not the same as the whole set of possibilities. It is related to extent, diversity or choice, but it does not match the decisive clue here: different types of produce are the central idea.
- **E.** **List** — is a written or spoken enumeration and says nothing by itself about diversity or extent. It is related to extent, diversity or choice, but it does not match the decisive clue here: different types of produce are the central idea.
- **F.** **Category** — is one class or grouping and can be only one part of a range. It is related to extent, diversity or choice, but it does not match the decisive clue here: different types of produce are the central idea.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **range vs variety**: variety 強調不同種類；range 也可以是跨度。
- **range vs span**: span 強調兩端之間的 extent。
- **range vs selection**: selection 強調可供選擇的項目。
- **range vs assortment**: assortment 常指一組各式實體物品。
- **range vs spectrum**: spectrum 通常暗示連續分布／光譜。
- **range vs array**: array 更有『大量、豐富』的感覺。
- **range vs breadth**: breadth 強調涵蓋面廣。
- **range vs scope**: scope 強調邊界與包括甚麼。
- **range vs spread**: spread 強調數值或群體的分布差距。
- **range vs gamut**: gamut 更書面，常表示幾乎完整的全部範圍。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & polysemy traps · 文法、搭配與多義陷阱

- **a range of + plural noun**：a range of products / activities / views。
- **range from A to B**：prices range from $10 to $50。
- **wide range / broad range / full range** 都常見，但不同受詞可更適合其他詞。
- **variety of + plural noun**：a variety of foods / methods。
- **selection of + plural noun**：a selection of courses / products。
- **span of + time/age**：a span of ten years / a wide age span。
- **spectrum of + views/abilities/symptoms**：通常帶 continuum 意味。
- **breadth of + experience/knowledge/coverage**：涵蓋面。
- **scope of + study/project/work**：邊界／包括內容。
- **spread of + scores/prices/values**：分布。
- 固定表達：**run the gamut from A to B**。
- **range** 可作名詞或動詞；不要把 **choice, category, level, list** 當作全面同義詞。
