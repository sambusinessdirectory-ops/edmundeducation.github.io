# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Pursue

**尋求、努力爭取、朝目標前進、追趕、追蹤、繼續——究竟是哪一種 pursue？**

本課把 `pursue` 分成主要語義：

1. **GOAL / OPPORTUNITY** — 追求目標、機會、理想  
2. **PATH / ACTIVITY** — 從事、沿某方向發展、繼續  
3. **CHASE / TRACK** — 追趕、追查、追蹤

核心近義與近義表達：

**seek; strive for; work toward; aim for; go after; follow; chase; track; continue with**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **9 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Seek

**尋求；追求**

`pursue` 最直接、最廣泛的近義詞之一，表示主動尋找、爭取或追求某個目標、機會、答案或結果。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

She decided to seek a career in medicine.

她決定追求醫療專業的職業道路。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- seek opportunities

- seek funding

- seek answers

- seek a solution


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 這裡是尋找／爭取目標、長期努力、沿路線發展、實際追趕、持續追蹤，還是把已開始的事情繼續下去？

---

## 02 Strive for

**努力爭取；力求**

強調持續努力去達成某個理想、標準或結果。比 `seek` 更突出 effort 和 determination。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The school continues to strive for excellence in teaching.

學校繼續力求教學卓越。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- strive for excellence

- strive for fairness

- strive for improvement

- strive for success


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

### EXAMPLE · 例句

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Achieve

**達成不等於 pursue**

`achieve` 表示已成功取得結果；`pursue` 表示朝結果努力的過程。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

You pursue a goal before you achieve it.

---

## 02 Obtain

**取得不等於追求**

obtain 表示獲得某物；pursue 強調尋求／爭取過程。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

You may pursue funding and later obtain it.

---

## 03 Follow

**高度相關但只適合部分 sense**

follow 很適合 career/path/course of action，但不能自然代替 pursue a suspect 或 pursue excellence。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

You can follow a career path, but police chase or pursue a suspect.

---

## 04 Chase

**追趕／追逐比 pursue 更強**

chase 常帶實際追跑或強烈追逐意味；正式目標追求語境未必適合。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Chasing fame sounds more intense than pursuing professional development.

---

## 05 Track

**追蹤不等於所有 pursue**

track 強調持續掌握位置、來源或線索。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Detectives may track a suspect electronically without physically chasing them.

---

## 06 Continue

**繼續只適合已開始的事情**

continue 不表示『尋求一個新目標』。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

You can continue your studies, but you seek a new opportunity.

---

## 07 Persue

**拼字陷阱**

`persue` 是常見拼錯；正確拼法是 `pursue`。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Correct: pursue / pursued / pursuing.

---

## 08 Pursuit

**詞性陷阱**

`pursue` 是動詞；`pursuit` 是名詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

They pursue knowledge; the pursuit of knowledge takes time.

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 Pursue is transitive
`pursue` 後面直接接賓語：
- pursue a goal
- pursue a career
- pursue an opportunity

不要說：
- pursue for a goal ✗

## 02 Pursue vs seek
- pursue = 朝着目標持續行動
- seek = 尋求／尋找

兩者高度重疊，但 `seek` 更容易和 answers / funding / help 搭配。

## 03 Pursue vs work toward
`work toward` 特別自然於長期目標：
- qualification
- independence
- carbon neutrality
- completion

## 04 Pursue vs chase / track
- chase = 實際追趕
- track = 持續掌握位置／線索
- pursue = 可涵蓋兩者，但較廣

## 05 Pursue studies / research
如果重點是「繼續」：
- continue with one's studies
- continue with the research

## 06 Word forms
- pursue
- pursued
- pursuing
- pursuit

注意不是 `persue`.
