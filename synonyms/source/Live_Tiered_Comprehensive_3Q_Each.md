# LIVE · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Live

**「生活／生存／過活」，是一個高度多義動詞。這個單元集中處理你提供的方向：在有限資源或困難情況下『活下去／應付生活／勉強過活』，而不是 live in Hong Kong 這類單純居住義。**

本單元集中處理 **live = to remain alive, continue one's life, or support oneself under particular conditions or with particular resources**。你提供的 **survive; manage; get by** 分別代表 **活下來、應付得到、勉強過活** 三個核心方向。

這次保留 **13 個**自然且高實用度的詞／表達，共 **39 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。同時清楚區分 **live / survive / manage / get by / subsist / cope / make ends meet / scrape by / eke out a living**，避免把『存在』、『居住』、『維生』和『財務上勉強過活』混在一起。

## Tier 1 — Core survival / coping / subsistence equivalents · 核心生存、應付與維生近義詞

### 01 Survive

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：生存；活下來  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to continue to live or exist despite danger, difficulty, illness, or hardship  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，當 live 強調『在困難、危險或匱乏中仍活下來』時最直接。  
**常見搭配**：survive an accident; survive on; survive the winter; survive financially; struggle to survive.  
**常見搭配對象**：accident, illness, winter, hardship, crisis.  
**與 live 的辨析**：Survive 比 live 更強調面對威脅或困難仍然活下來。  
**使用限制**：單純描述居住地點，例如 live in London，不能用 survive。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The family survived the winter with very little money.  
這個家庭靠很少的錢捱過了冬天。

### 02 Manage

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：勉強做到；應付着生活  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to succeed in doing something despite difficulty, including coping with everyday life  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，當 live 帶『雖然困難但仍應付得到』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：manage somehow; manage on a low income; manage alone; manage to cope; just manage.  
**常見搭配對象**：budget, low income, household, daily life, difficulty.  
**與 live 的辨析**：Manage 強調成功應付；live 本身不一定暗示困難。  
**使用限制**：若強調單純存在或居住，不用 manage。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
They manage on a very small monthly income.  
他們靠很少的每月收入勉強過活。

### 03 Get by

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：勉強過活；勉強應付  
**語域**：informal / very common  
**核心語義**：to manage to live or cope with only the resources, money, or ability available  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是非常自然的口語表達，尤其適合收入有限、能力有限但仍過得去的情況。  
**常見搭配**：get by on; just get by; get by with little; get by financially; manage to get by.  
**常見搭配對象**：income, savings, little money, basic English, limited resources.  
**與 live 的辨析**：Get by 比 live 更強調『夠用就算、勉強撐住』。  
**使用限制**：正式學術寫作通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She gets by on a modest pension.  
她靠一筆不高的退休金勉強過活。

### 04 Subsist

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：維生；勉強生存  
**語域**：formal  
**核心語義**：to stay alive by having only the minimum food, money, or resources necessary  
**用法提示**：當 live 強調靠很少資源維持基本生存時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：subsist on; subsist largely on; subsist by farming; subsistence living; subsist with little.  
**常見搭配對象**：food, income, pension, farming, aid.  
**與 live 的辨析**：Subsist 比 survive 更偏最低限度的日常維生。  
**使用限制**：普通舒適生活不宜用 subsist。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Some households subsist on irregular seasonal work.  
有些家庭靠不穩定的季節性工作維生。

### 05 Exist

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：存在；生存  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to be alive or present, without necessarily implying quality of life  
**用法提示**：當 live 只是表示『存在着／有生命』時可用，尤其哲學或抽象語境。  
**常見搭配**：continue to exist; barely exist; exist in nature; exist independently; exist under conditions.  
**常見搭配對象**：species, organism, institution, community, idea.  
**與 live 的辨析**：Exist 比 live 更抽象，也不帶日常生活或居住意味。  
**使用限制**：談日常生活、收入或居住地時通常不如 live 自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The species can exist only in a narrow range of habitats.  
這個物種只能在很有限的棲息地中生存。

## Tier 2 — Financial / emotional coping variants · 財務與生活壓力下的應付變體

### 06 Cope

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：應付；撐住  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：to deal successfully with a difficult situation or set of demands  
**用法提示**：當 live 的核心是『面對壓力或困境仍能撐住』時很實用。  
**常見搭配**：cope with stress; cope financially; cope alone; cope with change; struggle to cope.  
**常見搭配對象**：stress, workload, illness, money problems, change.  
**與 live 的辨析**：Cope 強調應對困難；live 更廣。  
**使用限制**：不能替換 live in / live with family 等居住義。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He is finding it hard to cope on his own.  
他發現自己一個人很難應付生活。

### 07 Make ends meet

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：收支平衡；勉強維持生活  
**語域**：neutral / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：to have just enough money to pay for basic living expenses  
**用法提示**：當 live 特別涉及財務上勉強過活時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：struggle to make ends meet; just make ends meet; hard to make ends meet; barely make ends meet; help make ends meet.  
**常見搭配對象**：rent, bills, wages, household expenses, low income.  
**與 live 的辨析**：這個表達只聚焦財務維生，不等於所有 live。  
**使用限制**：身體生存或居住地點不能用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Many families are struggling to make ends meet.  
很多家庭都在艱難維持收支平衡。

### 08 Scrape by

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：勉強過活；勉強撐過  
**語域**：informal  
**核心語義**：to manage to live or succeed with barely enough money, resources, or ability  
**用法提示**：比 get by 更強調非常勉強、幾乎不夠。  
**常見搭配**：scrape by on; barely scrape by; scrape by financially; scrape by each month; manage to scrape by.  
**常見搭配對象**：income, wages, savings, resources, monthly budget.  
**與 live 的辨析**：Scrape by 比 get by 更艱難、更接近最低線。  
**使用限制**：正式語體較少用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
They scrape by on temporary jobs and savings.  
他們靠臨時工作和積蓄勉強過活。

### 09 Carry on

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：繼續生活；繼續撐下去  
**語域**：neutral / conversational  
**核心語義**：to continue living, working, or functioning despite difficulty or disruption  
**用法提示**：當 live 帶『事情很難，但仍繼續過下去』時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：carry on despite; carry on as usual; carry on working; carry on with life; somehow carry on.  
**常見搭配對象**：life, work, routine, responsibility, hardship.  
**與 live 的辨析**：Carry on 強調持續而非單純生存。  
**使用限制**：不適合表示收入或居住地本身。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
After the loss, she somehow found the strength to carry on.  
經歷失去後，她仍設法找到力量繼續生活。

### 10 Keep going

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：繼續撐下去；堅持生活  
**語域**：informal / common  
**核心語義**：to continue despite tiredness, difficulty, discouragement, or lack of resources  
**用法提示**：當 live 表示『不要停下來，繼續撐住』時非常口語自然。  
**常見搭配**：keep going; somehow keep going; keep going through; keep going despite; enough to keep going.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, family, business, routine, recovery.  
**與 live 的辨析**：Keep going 強調持續運作／撐下去；live 更廣。  
**使用限制**：單純描述存在或居住不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The small business earned just enough to keep going.  
這間小企業賺的錢剛好足以繼續經營下去。

## Tier 3 — Livelihood / conversational / source-of-support variants · 謀生、口語應付與生活來源變體

### 11 Eke out a living

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：勉強謀生；艱苦維生  
**語域**：formal / literary  
**核心語義**：to earn just enough money to survive, often with difficulty and through limited work or resources  
**用法提示**：當 live 特別強調艱難謀生、收入微薄時非常精準。  
**常見搭配**：eke out a living; eke out a livelihood; eke out an existence; barely eke out; eke out a living from.  
**常見搭配對象**：farming, fishing, casual work, small trade, seasonal work.  
**與 live 的辨析**：Eke out a living 比 get by 更具艱苦謀生和收入有限的色彩。  
**使用限制**：舒適或一般生活語境不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
They eke out a living from a small farm.  
他們靠一個小農場勉強維生。

### 12 Get along

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：過得去；應付得到  
**語域**：informal / conversational  
**核心語義**：to manage reasonably well in a situation, especially with limited resources or support  
**用法提示**：當 live 指日常生活尚能應付、過得還可以時可用。  
**常見搭配**：get along fine; get along without; get along on; manage to get along; get along somehow.  
**常見搭配對象**：money, support, help, household, daily routine.  
**與 live 的辨析**：Get along 比 get by 稍少強調窘迫，也可表示『相處』，需靠語境判斷。  
**使用限制**：多義性高，若可能被理解為人際相處，要避免。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He gets along reasonably well on his pension.  
他靠退休金生活得還算可以。

### 13 Live on

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：靠……生活；以……維生  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to support oneself using a particular amount of money, source of income, or type of food  
**用法提示**：當 live 需要明確說明『靠甚麼過活』時非常實用。  
**常見搭配**：live on a pension; live on savings; live on very little; live on benefits; live on rice.  
**常見搭配對象**：pension, savings, income, benefits, food.  
**與 live 的辨析**：Live on 是 live 的固定結構，明確標示生活來源。  
**使用限制**：不是 headword 的替換詞，而是非常重要的搭配型變體。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
They live on a small pension and some savings.  
他們靠少量退休金和一些積蓄生活。

## 04 False synonyms / live traps · 容易誤選的詞與「生活／生存」陷阱

### 01 Stay

**留下／逗留不等於 live**  
stay 強調暫時停留；live 通常是居住、生活或生存。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Stay is usually temporary.

### 02 Reside

**居住不等於 survive/manage**  
reside 只在『居住於某處』sense 接近 live。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Reside is location-focused.

### 03 Exist

**exist 只在『存在』sense 接近 live**  
exist 不等於過日子、維生或居住。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Exist is abstract.

### 04 Survive

**survive 只在『面對困難仍活下來』sense 接近 live**  
survive 帶威脅／困難意味。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Survival implies adversity.

### 05 Manage

**manage 只在『應付得到』sense 接近 live**  
manage 不表示生命存在或居住地。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Manage is coping-focused.

### 06 Get by

**get by 只在『勉強過活』sense 接近 live**  
get by 帶資源有限或能力剛好足夠的意味。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Get by implies adequacy under limits.

### 07 Earn

**賺錢不等於 live**  
earn 只是取得收入；是否足夠維生是另一回事。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Earning money is not the same as living on it.

### 08 Live / alive

**詞性陷阱**  
live 可作動詞；alive 是形容詞，通常不放在名詞前作一般修飾。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Live is a verb; alive is an adjective.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Survive · 生存；活下來

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The family lived through the winter with very little money.

**中文翻譯**  
這個家庭靠很少的錢捱過了冬天。

- A. survive
- B. resided
- C. stayed
- D. earned
- E. managed comfortably
- F. existed abstractly

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**survive** · 生存；活下來

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The family survived the winter with very little money.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A. survive ✓ — winter + very little money indicates hardship overcome, so survive is precise。**
- **B. resided — resided/stayed concern location。**
- **C. stayed — earned concerns income generation, not living through hardship。**
- **D. earned — managed comfortably contradicts the difficulty。**
- **E. managed comfortably — existed abstractly is too detached from the hardship context.**

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Live 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 面對危險／困難仍活下來 | **survive** | 是否 danger / illness / winter / losses？ |
| 困難中仍應付得到 | **manage** | 是否 manage on little / manage alone？ |
| 資源有限但仍過得去 | **get by** | 是否 just enough money/ability/resources？ |
| 最低限度維生 | **subsist** | 是否 very little food/income/resources？ |
| 抽象存在 | **exist** | 是否 species / institution / tradition continues to be present？ |
| 應付壓力／生活需求 | **cope** | 是否 stress / illness / change / alone？ |
| 財務收支勉強平衡 | **make ends meet** | 是否 bills / wages / household expenses？ |
| 幾乎不夠地勉強撐住 | **scrape by** | 是否 barely enough each month？ |
| 困難後繼續生活 | **carry on** | 是否 continue life/work despite hardship？ |
| 繼續撐下去／運作 | **keep going** | 是否 continue despite exhaustion/lack of resources？ |
| 艱苦謀生 | **eke out a living** | 是否 small farm / casual work / low earnings？ |
| 生活得還可以 | **get along** | 是否 manage reasonably well with limited support/resources？ |
| 靠某來源生活 | **live on** | 是否 pension / savings / benefits / food source？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **live vs survive**: survive 帶危險、困難或威脅；live 不一定。
- **live vs manage**: manage 強調困難中仍應付得到。
- **live vs get by**: get by 表示資源有限但勉強夠用。
- **live vs subsist**: subsist 是最低限度維生，語氣更艱苦、更正式。
- **live vs exist**: exist 更抽象，只表示存在。
- **live vs cope**: cope 是處理壓力／困難，不是生命存在。
- **live vs make ends meet**: make ends meet 只指財務上能支付基本開支。
- **live vs scrape by**: scrape by 比 get by 更勉強、更接近最低線。
- **live vs eke out a living**: eke out a living 強調靠有限工作／資源艱苦謀生。
- **live vs reside**: reside 只處理居住地點，不處理過活／維生。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & live traps · 文法、搭配與多義陷阱

- **live** 可表示居住：live in Hong Kong / live with family；本單元不以這個 sense 為主。
- **live on + money/food/source**：live on a pension / live on savings / live on rice。
- **survive + event/period**：survive the winter / survive an accident。
- **survive on + resources**：survive on very little money。
- **manage on + amount/resources**：manage on a small income。
- **get by on + amount**；**get by with + limited resource/ability**。
- **subsist on + food/income**：subsist on basic food / a small pension。
- **cope with + stress/problem/change**；亦可說 cope alone / cope financially。
- **make ends meet** 是固定慣用語，常和 wages / bills / low income 搭配。
- **scrape by on + income/resources**：比 get by 更勉強。
- **carry on + -ing**：carry on living / working。
- **keep going** 可形容人、家庭、機構、企業在困難中繼續。
- **eke out a living** 是固定表達；亦可見 eke out an existence。
- **alive** 是形容詞：remain alive；不能直接當動詞取代 live。
