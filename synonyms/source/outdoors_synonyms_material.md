# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Outdoors

**露天、外面、開闊戶外、自然環境、戶外環境、開放空間、鄉郊、大自然——究竟是哪一種 outdoors？**

核心近義與近義表達：

**open air; outside; the open; natural environment; outdoor environment; open spaces; the countryside; nature**

> **核心詞數不固定。** 本課保留 **8 個**自然且辨析價值高的核心表達。  
> 你提供的 **open air; outside** 全部保留。  
> 本課聚焦名詞性概念 **the outdoors = places outside, especially natural/open environments**，並特別處理 `outside` 的詞性差異與 `nature / countryside / open spaces` 的範圍差異。

# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 Open Air

**戶外；露天**

最直接、最自然的名詞性近義表達之一。`the open air` 指建築物外的露天環境，特別常見於 `in the open air`、`exercise in the open air`。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The children spend most of the afternoon in the open air.

孩子們下午大部分時間都在戶外。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- in the open air

- exercise in the open air

- eat in the open air

- open-air activity

## 02 Outside

**外面；戶外**

非常常見，但詞性較靈活，常作副詞／名詞性位置使用。當 `outdoors` 指『在外面而不是室內』時很自然，語氣比 `the open air` 更日常。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

It was too warm indoors, so we went outside.

室內太熱，所以我們走到外面。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- go outside

- play outside

- wait outside

- move outside

---

## 03 The Open

**戶外空曠地方；露天**

較文學或稍書面，指沒有建築物遮蔽的戶外空間。常見於 `out in the open`。比一般 `outdoors` 更強調開闊、無遮蔽。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

After hours in the small cabin, they were glad to step out into the open.

在細小木屋內待了數小時後，他們很高興走到開闊的戶外。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- out in the open

- leave something in the open

- emerge into the open

- sleep in the open

## 04 Natural Environment

**自然環境**

當 `the outdoors` 強調自然世界而不只是『室外』時很自然。可包括森林、山野、河流、野外等。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The programme encourages children to spend more time in the natural environment and learn about wildlife.

課程鼓勵孩子多到自然環境活動並認識野生動物。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配

## 06 Open Spaces

**戶外開放空間；空曠地方**

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The city needs more access to open spaces for families.

## 07 The Countryside

**郊外；鄉郊**

當 `the outdoors` 指離開城市、到鄉郊或自然地區活動時可用。它比 `outdoors` 更具地理範圍，不涵蓋所有城市戶外空間。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

They leave the city every weekend to enjoy the countryside.

他們每逢週末離開城市，到郊外享受戶外生活。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- live in the countryside

- explore the countryside

- escape to the countryside

- walk in the countryside

## 08 Nature

**大自然；自然世界**

當 `the outdoors` 指親近自然、戶外體驗、野外景觀時非常自然。它強調自然本身，而不是純粹『在外面』。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

He loves nature and spends weekends hiking and camping.

他熱愛大自然，週末常遠足和露營。

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Open air

**最接近的名詞性改寫**

`the open air` 強調露天、沒有建築物包圍。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

We ate in the open air.

---

## 02 Outside

**詞性較靈活**

`outside` 很自然，但常作副詞：`go outside`。它不一定是名詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The children are playing outside.

---

## 03 The open

**更強調開闊／無遮蔽**

常見於 `out in the open`，比一般 outdoors 更有暴露感。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Do not leave the equipment out in the open.

---

## 04 Nature

**自然不等於所有戶外**

城市街道、停車場都屬戶外，但不一定是 nature。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

Hiking helps people connect with nature.

---

## 05 The countryside

**地理範圍更窄**

只指鄉郊；城市戶外空間不屬 countryside。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

They escaped to the countryside for the weekend.

---

## 06 Open spaces

**規劃／城市語境**

常指公園、綠地、廣場等可使用戶外空間。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The plan creates more open spaces.

---

## 07 Outdoors / outdoor

**詞性陷阱**

`outdoors` 常作副詞或名詞性概念；`outdoor` 是形容詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

We stayed outdoors; outdoor activities are popular.

---

## 08 The outdoors

**冠詞用法**

作名詞表示『戶外世界／大自然』時通常說 `the outdoors`。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

She loves the outdoors.

## 練習 01.1

**Target expression:** open air · 戶外；露天

### READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境

The children spend most of the afternoon in the outdoors.

### 中文翻譯

孩子們下午大部分時間都在戶外。

### OPTIONS


- **A. open air**

- **B. outside**

- **C. the open**

- **D. nature**

- **E. indoors**

- **F. enclosure**


### CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案

**open air · 戶外；露天**

The children spend most of the afternoon in the **open air**.

### SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的解釋


- **A. open air ✓** — `open air` = 露天／戶外空氣中的環境。 **本句為何最合適：** the focus is being outside in fresh, uncovered air rather than indoors。

- **B. outside** — `outside` = 外面；在室外。 最日常，但常作副詞；如果句子要求名詞性自然世界、鄉郊或開放空間，會太泛。 **本句線索：** the focus is being outside in fresh, uncovered air rather than indoors。

- **C. the open** — `the open` = 無遮蔽、開闊的露天地方。 特別強調無遮蔽、開闊；普通城市街道或一般自然環境未必需要這層意思。 **本句線索：** the focus is being outside in fresh, uncovered air rather than indoors。

- **D. nature** — `nature` = 大自然；自然世界。 強調大自然與自然體驗；露天但完全人工的環境不一定是 nature。 **本句線索：** the focus is being outside in fresh, uncovered air rather than indoors。

- **E. indoors** — `indoors` = 室內。 與 outdoors 相反。 **本句線索：** the focus is being outside in fresh, uncovered air rather than indoors。

- **F. enclosure** — `enclosure` = 封閉空間。 封閉空間，與 open air/the open 相反。 **本句線索：** the focus is being outside in fresh, uncovered air rather than indoors。

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

## Outdoors vs Open air
`the open air` 是最接近的名詞性改寫：
- eat in the open air
- exercise in the open air

## Outdoors vs Outside
`outside` 最口語：
- go outside
- play outside

但它通常不是純名詞替換。

## Outdoors vs The open
後者強調：
- exposed
- uncovered
- not sheltered

## Outdoors vs Natural environment
城市街道也可以是 outdoors，但未必是 natural environment。

## Outdoors vs Open spaces
偏城市規劃／公共空間：
- parks
- green spaces
- communal outdoor areas

## Outdoors vs The countryside
範圍更窄，只指鄉郊。

## Outdoors vs Nature
當語境是：
- hiking
- camping
- wildlife
- connection with nature

`nature` 通常比 outside 更自然。

# 07 Grammar and usage notes

## 01 The outdoors
作名詞表示「戶外世界／大自然」通常用：
- **the outdoors**

例如：
- She loves the outdoors.

## 02 Outdoors
亦非常常作副詞：
- We ate outdoors.
- The children played outdoors.

## 03 Outdoor
`outdoor` 是形容詞：
- outdoor activities
- outdoor education
- outdoor furniture

## 04 Outside
`outside` 可作副詞、介詞、形容詞或名詞性用法，語法比 `the outdoors` 更靈活。

## 05 Nature is narrower in one sense, broader in another
`nature` 強調自然世界；人工廣場雖在戶外，但不一定屬 nature。
