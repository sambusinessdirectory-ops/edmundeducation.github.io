# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# In general

**`in general` 可表示『一般來說／大致上／總體而言／通常』。真正要選哪個替代表達，關鍵在於你是在做 broad generalisation、概括大方向、描述頻率、作整體評估，還是說一項通常規律。**

核心替代表達：

`generally / broadly / usually / overall / on the whole / generally speaking / by and large / as a rule`

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Generally

**一般來說；通常而言**

和 `in general` 最直接、最常用的替代之一。用來概括普遍情況、趨勢或大多數人的情形，而不表示絕對。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Generally, people adapt quickly to small changes.

一般來說，人們很快便能適應小變化。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **a broad generalisation about what is commonly true**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 02 Broadly

**概括地說；大致上**

當 `in general` 表示從大方向、整體分類或概括層面來看時，`broadly` 很合適。它比 `generally` 更強調不談細節。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Broadly, the two proposals aim to solve the same problem.

概括地說，兩項建議都旨在解決同一問題。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **looking at the main pattern without fine detail**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、學術、一般

---

## 03 Usually

**通常；一般情況下**

當 `in general` 實際是在說某件事在大多數時候會發生時，`usually` 很自然。它偏頻率，不是純粹概括。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The train usually arrives before nine.

這班列車通常在九時前到達。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **happening in most cases or most of the time**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 04 Overall

**總體而言；整體來說**

當 `in general` 是對多項因素、整體表現或全局作總結時，`overall` 很自然。它常放句首或句尾。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Overall, the project was successful despite a few delays.

總體而言，儘管有少量延誤，這個項目仍然成功。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **considering everything together**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 05 On the whole

**總的來說；整體而言**

當 `in general` 用來在承認少量例外後作整體判斷時，`on the whole` 很自然。語氣比 `overall` 稍口語、較有平衡感。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> On the whole, residents are satisfied with the new service.

總的來說，居民對新服務感到滿意。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **considering the situation as a whole, despite exceptions**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、書面

---

## 06 Generally speaking

**一般而言；大體來說**

這是 `in general` 很自然的擴展表達，明確提示後面是一個概括性陳述。常用於口語、演講及說明。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> Generally speaking, smaller classes allow more individual attention.

一般而言，較小的班級可提供更多個別照顧。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **used to introduce a broad statement with possible exceptions**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 07 By and large

**大體上；總的來說**

較口語、帶少許慣用語色彩。當 `in general` 表示『整體上大致如此，雖然有例外』時很自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> By and large, the feedback from customers was positive.

大體上，顧客的回應都是正面的。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **mostly true when the whole situation is considered**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、口語

---

## 08 As a rule

**一般說來；通常**

當 `in general` 帶有『通常都是這樣』或『一般規律如此』的意味時，`as a rule` 很合適。它介乎 generalisation 和 habitual frequency 之間。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> As a rule, the office closes early on Fridays.

一般來說，辦公室逢星期五都會提早關門。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **normally or customarily, as a usual pattern**

**REGISTER · 語域**

一般、書面

# 02 False synonyms

## In general / Generally
`generally` 是最直接、最中性的替代，適合一般概括。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
In general, people adapt quickly.  
Generally, people adapt quickly.

## In general / Broadly
`broadly` 更強調從大方向概括，不談細節。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
In general, the plans are similar.  
Broadly, the plans follow the same approach.

## In general / Usually
`usually` 主要是頻率：大多數時候如此。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
In general, the shop closes at six.  
The shop usually closes at six.

## In general / Overall
`overall` 偏綜合多項因素後作總結。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
In general, the project was successful.  
Overall, the project was successful despite a few delays.

## In general / On the whole
`on the whole` 很適合「總體如此，但有少量例外」。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
In general, residents are satisfied.  
On the whole, residents are satisfied with the service.

## In general / Generally speaking
`generally speaking` 明確提示後面是概括性陳述。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
In general, smaller classes help.  
Generally speaking, smaller classes allow more individual attention.

## In general / By and large
`by and large` 較口語、慣用，表示大體上如此。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
In general, the feedback was positive.  
By and large, the feedback was positive.

## In general / As a rule
`as a rule` 偏通常規律、慣例或一般做法。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
In general, the office closes early on Fridays.  
As a rule, the office closes early on Fridays.

`rulefully`：不是標準英文。 本句關鍵是「the statement describes the usual rule while allowing possible exceptions」。它要麼把重點放在頻率、整體評估、概括程度或通常規律的另一層意思，要麼結構／詞性不合。

**E. there is never any such policy**  
`there is never any such policy`：從來沒有這項政策；相反。 本句關鍵是「the statement describes the usual rule while allowing possible exceptions」。它要麼把重點放在頻率、整體評估、概括程度或通常規律的另一層意思，要麼結構／詞性不合。

**F. as a rule ✓**  
`as a rule` = 一般說來／通常。線索「the statement describes the usual rule while allowing possible exceptions」指向慣例、規律或通常做法。

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Generally vs Usually** — generally = 一般概括；usually = 頻率。
- **Broadly vs Generally** — broadly 更強調 big picture / 不談細節。
- **Overall vs On the whole** — 都可作整體總結；on the whole 更自然地暗示有例外。
- **Generally speaking vs Generally** — 前者是完整 discourse marker，語氣更明顯。
- **By and large vs On the whole** — 意思很近；by and large 更口語、慣用。
- **As a rule vs Usually** — as a rule 更像規律／慣例；usually 更直接表示頻率。
- **In general ≠ always** — 一般如此不等於毫無例外。
- **In general ≠ overall in every context** — overall 最適合作綜合評估，不適合所有頻率句。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

- **generally / broadly / usually / overall** 可作副詞，但句中位置不同
- **usually** 常放在主要動詞前、be 動詞後：`She usually walks.` / `It is usually quiet.`
- **overall** 作句子副詞時常置句首：`Overall, ...`
- **on the whole / by and large / as a rule / generally speaking** 都是固定片語
- 不要寫 **on whole** ✗、**by large** ✗、**general speaking** ✗
- **broad** 是形容詞；**broadly** 才是副詞
- **general** 是形容詞；**generally** 才是副詞
- **in general** 不等於 **in particular**；兩者語義相反

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 只是一般概括？ → **generally**
2. 想強調大方向、不談細節？ → **broadly**
3. 是「大多數時候」？ → **usually**
4. 是把多方面合起來總結？ → **overall**
5. 是總體如此但承認例外？ → **on the whole**
6. 想明確引入一個 general statement？ → **generally speaking**
7. 想用較口語慣用語？ → **by and large**
8. 是通常規律／慣例？ → **as a rule**

# 10 Final note for system use

核心替代表達：

`generally / broadly / usually / overall / on the whole / generally speaking / by and large / as a rule`

最重要的一點：

> **generally ≠ usually ≠ overall**

`generally` = 一般概括；  
`usually` = 頻率；  
`overall` = 綜合後的整體判斷。
