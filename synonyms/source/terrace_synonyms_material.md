# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Patio

**通常偏地面層**

patio 常是住宅或餐廳旁鋪設的地面戶外空間；terrace 的位置和形式更廣。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A ground-floor paved area is often a patio; a rooftop space is more naturally a terrace.

---


## 02 Balcony

**樓上外挑空間**

balcony 通常較小、在樓上、由外牆突出並有欄杆。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A second-floor balcony is not the same as a ground-level terrace.

---


## 03 Deck

**結構／材質意味更強**

deck 常指木製或複合材料、架高的平台。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A wooden platform behind a house is often a deck.

---


## 04 Courtyard

**被建築包圍**

courtyard 的核心是四周或多面由牆／建築圍住。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A courtyard lies between buildings; a terrace may be open.

---


## 05 Veranda

**通常有上蓋**

veranda 常沿建築外側、有屋頂或簷篷。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A covered platform along a house is a veranda.

---


## 06 Porch

**入口空間較窄**

porch 通常位於入口前、有上蓋，常比 terrace 小。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A porch is normally attached to an entrance.

---


## 07 Terrace / terraced

**詞形差異**

terrace 是名詞；terraced 是形容詞，亦可指排屋式。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

We sat on the terrace; they live in a terraced house.

---


## 08 Terrace house

**多義辨析**

British English 的 terrace house / terraced house 指排屋，不是戶外平台。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A terrace house is a row house, not an outdoor seating area.
