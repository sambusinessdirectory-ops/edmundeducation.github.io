# PUT PRESSURE ON · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Put pressure on

**「向……施壓／令……承受壓力」其實有兩個主要語義家族：** 一是主動影響或迫使某人做某事；二是某種成本、工作量或情況令人／系統承受負擔。這兩類不能混為一談。

本單元集中處理 **put pressure on = to influence someone strongly, or to create demands, strain, or burden on a person, organisation, resource, or system**。你提供的 **pressurise; place demands on** 正好代表 **主動施壓** 與 **造成負擔** 兩個核心方向。

本次保留 **10 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達。Tier 2–3 中部分詞只適用於較窄語境，尤其 **coerce** 強度遠高於一般 pressure，不會被當作全面同義詞。共 **30 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| 核心 sense | 問題 | 常見替代表達 |
|---|---|---|
| 主動影響／迫使某人 | 是否有人刻意令另一方改變行動／決定？ | pressurise, pressure, lean on, push, apply pressure to |
| 造成負擔／吃緊 | 是否是成本、工作量、需求令資源或關係承受壓力？ | place demands on, bear down on, put a strain on |
| 強迫／脅迫 | 是否有威脅、恐嚇或幾乎沒有選擇？ | coerce |

## Tier 1 — Core interpersonal / demand equivalents · 核心施壓與負擔近義表達

### 01 Pressurise

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：施壓；向……施加壓力  
**語域**：British English / neutral  
**核心語義**：to try to make someone do something by applying psychological, social, or practical pressure  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **put pressure on** 最直接的英式單字近義詞之一，尤其適合人際、工作和談判語境。  
**常見搭配**：pressurise someone to do something; feel pressurised; pressurise staff; pressurise a witness; pressurise management.  
**常見搭配對象**：staff, employee, witness, customer, manager.  
**與 put pressure on 的辨析**：Pressurise 比 put pressure on 更緊湊，通常直接帶 **someone to do something**。  
**使用限制**：美式英語較常見 **pressure someone**；英式／香港英語可自然使用 **pressurise**。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Managers should not pressurise staff to work unpaid overtime.  
管理人員不應施壓要求員工無薪加班。

### 02 Place demands on

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：對……提出要求；令……承受負擔  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to require time, effort, resources, or performance from someone or something  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很重要，當 put pressure on 不是強迫某人，而是令某人／系統承受工作量、責任或資源要求時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：place demands on staff; place heavy demands on resources; demands on time; demands on services; demands on families.  
**常見搭配對象**：staff, time, resources, service, family.  
**與 put pressure on 的辨析**：Place demands on 強調 burden / requirements，不一定含 coercion。  
**使用限制**：如果是直接迫使某人改變決定，pressurise / pressure 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The new reporting rules place heavy demands on small organisations.  
新的匯報規則令小型機構承受很大負擔。

### 03 Pressure

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：向……施壓；迫使  
**語域**：neutral / common, especially American English  
**核心語義**：to try to influence or force someone into doing something  
**用法提示**：當 put pressure on 有明確人際／決策施壓意味時，**pressure someone to do something** 很自然。  
**常見搭配**：pressure someone into; pressure someone to; pressure staff; pressure a customer; pressure officials.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, employee, customer, official, witness.  
**與 put pressure on 的辨析**：作動詞時和 pressurise 很接近；美式英語尤其常見。  
**使用限制**：不要和名詞 pressure 混淆；正式英式文本可優先 pressurise。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The salesperson pressured the customer to sign immediately.  
售貨員施壓要求顧客立即簽署。

### 04 Lean on

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：向……施壓；逼迫  
**語域**：informal / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：to apply informal or personal pressure to persuade someone to act  
**用法提示**：當 put pressure on 帶私下、人際、權力關係或非正式游說意味時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：lean on someone; lean on officials; lean on a supplier; lean on management; lean on someone to act.  
**常見搭配對象**：official, colleague, supplier, manager, contact.  
**與 put pressure on 的辨析**：Lean on 比 pressure / pressurise 更口語，也常暗示利用關係或影響力。  
**使用限制**：正式報告、法律文件通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Senior figures leaned on the committee to change its decision.  
高層人物向委員會施壓，要求改變決定。

### 05 Push

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：催逼；推動；施壓  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：to urge someone strongly towards an action, decision, or higher level of effort  
**用法提示**：當 put pressure on 的語氣較輕，重點是『催促／推動』而不是明顯 coercion 時可用。  
**常見搭配**：push someone to improve; push for change; push staff too hard; push someone into; push a team.  
**常見搭配對象**：student, employee, team, organisation, decision-maker.  
**與 put pressure on 的辨析**：Push 常比 pressure 輕，既可正面也可負面，取決於語境。  
**使用限制**：若有威脅、強迫或不當壓力，coerce / pressure 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The coach pushed the team to maintain its concentration.  
教練催促球隊保持專注。

## Tier 2 — Formal / burden / intensifying-pressure variants · 正式、負擔與沉重壓力變體

### 06 Urge strongly

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：強烈敦促；力勸  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：to encourage or advise someone very strongly to take a particular action  
**用法提示**：當 put pressure on 主要是強烈勸說，而沒有真正強迫時可以改寫。  
**常見搭配**：strongly urge someone to; urge action; urge caution; urge the government; urge management.  
**常見搭配對象**：government, official, public, management, organisation.  
**與 put pressure on 的辨析**：Urge strongly 仍保留 choice；pressure 通常暗示更大的心理或實際壓力。  
**使用限制**：如果對方幾乎沒有拒絕空間，urge 太弱。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Campaigners strongly urged the council to reconsider the plan.  
倡議人士強烈敦促市議會重新考慮該計劃。

### 07 Apply pressure to

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：向……施加壓力  
**語域**：formal / neutral  
**核心語義**：to deliberately use influence, demands, or leverage to affect someone's behaviour or decision  
**用法提示**：這是 put pressure on 的正式近義改寫，適合政策、談判、商業和國際關係。  
**常見搭配**：apply pressure to; apply political pressure; apply financial pressure; apply pressure on; pressure campaign.  
**常見搭配對象**：government, company, negotiator, supplier, institution.  
**與 put pressure on 的辨析**：和 put pressure on 很接近，但語氣較書面。  
**使用限制**：日常口語通常直接用 put pressure on / pressure。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Investors applied pressure to the company to improve its governance.  
投資者向公司施壓，要求改善企業管治。

### 08 Bear down on

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：對……施加沉重壓力；逼近  
**語域**：formal / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：to exert strong and often growing pressure on a person, group, system, or situation  
**用法提示**：當 put pressure on 描述持續增強、沉重的外部壓力時可用。  
**常見搭配**：bear down on costs; bear down on a sector; pressure bearing down on; economic pressure; regulatory pressure.  
**常見搭配對象**：sector, budget, household, organisation, system.  
**與 put pressure on 的辨析**：Bear down on 更強調 pressure intensifying / weighing heavily。  
**使用限制**：直接叫某人做某事時不如 pressure / pressurise 自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Rising costs are bearing down on small businesses.  
不斷上升的成本正對小型企業造成沉重壓力。

### 09 Put a strain on

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：令……承受壓力；造成負擔  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：to create stress, difficulty, or excessive demand for a person, relationship, service, or resource  
**用法提示**：當 put pressure on 的核心是『造成負擔／令系統吃緊』，而非主動施壓某人時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：put a strain on services; put a strain on relationships; strain resources; financial strain; emotional strain.  
**常見搭配對象**：service, relationship, budget, resource, family.  
**與 put pressure on 的辨析**：Put a strain on 描述結果性的負擔，不表示有人刻意施壓。  
**使用限制**：談判、人際迫使某人同意時不適用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The sudden increase in demand put a strain on local services.  
需求突然增加令本地服務承受壓力。

## Tier 3 — Strong coercion variant · 強迫／脅迫型變體

### 10 Coerce

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：強迫；脅迫  
**語域**：formal / strong  
**核心語義**：to force someone to do something by using threats, intimidation, or serious pressure  
**用法提示**：當 put pressure on 已強到接近『強迫／脅迫』，而對方選擇空間極小時才適合。  
**常見搭配**：coerce someone into; coerce someone to; coercive pressure; coercive tactics; coercion.  
**常見搭配對象**：person, witness, employee, victim, participant.  
**與 put pressure on 的辨析**：Coerce 比 put pressure on 強很多，含明顯 force / intimidation。  
**使用限制**：普通勸說、職場催促或一般談判壓力不能隨便升級成 coerce。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
No employee should be coerced into signing a document they do not understand.  
任何員工都不應被強迫簽署自己不理解的文件。

## 04 False synonyms / pressure traps · 容易誤選的施壓、說服與負擔陷阱

### 01 Encourage

**鼓勵 ≠ put pressure on**  
encourage 通常是正面支持，並保留較大選擇空間。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Encouragement is weaker and more supportive.

### 02 Persuade

**說服 ≠ pressure**  
persuade 是透過理由令對方自願改變想法，不必有壓力。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Persuasion relies on reasons rather than pressure.

### 03 Demand

**要求 ≠ put pressure on**  
demand 是提出強硬要求；pressure 是影響對方接受要求的過程。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
A demand is a request/requirement, not the pressure itself.

### 04 Threaten

**威脅 ≠ 所有 pressure**  
threaten 是明確提出不利後果；很多 pressure 並沒有威脅。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Threatening is a stronger, narrower tactic.

### 05 Stress

**壓力狀態 ≠ 主動施壓**  
stress 可指心理壓力狀態，不等於某人向另一人施壓。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Stress is often a condition, not an influence tactic.

### 06 Burden

**負擔 ≠ 所有 pressure**  
burden 強調負重／責任，不一定有人主動影響決定。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Burden is load-focused.

### 07 Coerce

**只在強迫 sense 接近**  
coerce 比一般 put pressure on 強，通常包含威脅或幾乎沒有選擇。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Coerce implies serious force or intimidation.

### 08 Pressure / pressurise

**地區與詞性陷阱**  
pressure 可作名詞／動詞；pressurise 是英式常見動詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check grammar and regional usage.

- **D.** **Persuade** — means change someone's mind through reasons or argument rather than pressure. It is related to influence or burden, but it misses an important part of this context: direct interpersonal pressure to make staff act fits pressurise.
- **E.** **Encourage** — means support or motivate someone and is generally more positive and less forceful. It is related to influence or burden, but it misses an important part of this context: direct interpersonal pressure to make staff act fits pressurise.
- **F.** **Demand** — means state a strong requirement; it is the request itself rather than the influence used to secure compliance. It is related to influence or burden, but it misses an important part of this context: direct interpersonal pressure to make staff act fits pressurise.

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **put pressure on vs pressurise**: pressurise 是英式常見單字動詞，直接表示向某人施壓。
- **put pressure on vs place demands on**: 後者是增加要求／負擔，不一定有人刻意迫使對方。
- **put pressure on vs push**: push 通常較輕，可是建設性的催促。
- **put pressure on vs persuade**: persuade 靠理由說服，不必有壓力。
- **put pressure on vs urge**: urge 是強烈勸告，通常保留較完整的自由選擇。
- **put pressure on vs lean on**: lean on 更口語，也常暗示關係／權力影響。
- **put pressure on vs put a strain on**: strain 描述承受的負擔，不表示主動施壓。
- **put pressure on vs coerce**: coerce 強度高得多，涉及威脅、恐嚇或近乎強迫。
- **put pressure on vs threaten**: threaten 是一種具體強勢手段，不是所有 pressure 都包含威脅。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & pressure traps · 文法、搭配與『施壓』陷阱

- **put pressure on + person/organisation**：put pressure on management。
- **put pressure on someone to + verb**：put pressure on staff to work late。
- **pressurise someone to + verb**：英式／香港自然用法。
- **pressure someone into + -ing / to + verb**：pressure someone into signing / to sign。
- **place demands on + time/resources/services**：偏負擔，不偏 coercion。
- **lean on someone to + verb**：口語，常暗示私下影響。
- **push someone to + verb**：可表示催促改善，不一定負面。
- **apply pressure to/on**：正式商業／政策／談判語境。
- **put a strain on + relationship/service/budget**：描述承受壓力的結果。
- **coerce someone into + -ing** 是高強度強迫；不能因一般職場壓力就使用。
- **pressure** 可作名詞和動詞；**pressurise** 是動詞；**pressurised** 可作過去分詞／形容詞。
- 英式／香港拼寫用 **pressurise**；美式常見 **pressurize** 或直接動詞 **pressure**。
