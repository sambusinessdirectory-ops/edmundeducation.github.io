# WORKING CONDITIONS · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Working conditions

**Working conditions 是一個很廣的概念，可以涵蓋工時、薪酬、假期、安全、設備、工作量、管理文化、彈性和工作保障。** 因此替換前要先判斷是在談 **employment terms, physical workplace, social environment, personal job circumstances, or occupational research**。

你提供的 **employment conditions; job circumstances** 正好代表兩個核心方向：**正式僱傭條件** 與 **個人的實際工作處境**。

本次保留 **9 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達。會特別區分 **salary, benefits, workload, working hours, job satisfaction**，因為這些通常只是 working conditions 的構成因素或結果，而不是全面同義詞。共 **27 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Working conditions 的語境 | 典型替代表達 |
|---|---|
| 正式整體僱傭制度 | **employment conditions** |
| 個人實際工作處境 | **job circumstances** |
| 物理／現場工作條件 | **workplace conditions** |
| 整體社會／文化工作環境 | **work environment** |
| 正式合約條款 | **terms and conditions of employment** |
| 勞工政策／標準 | **conditions of work** |
| 某一職位的實際條件 | **job conditions** |
| 具體合約待遇條款 | **employment terms** |
| 職業健康／跨行業研究 | **occupational conditions** |

## Tier 1 — Core employment / workplace / contractual equivalents · 核心僱傭、工作場所與合約近義詞

### 01 Employment conditions

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：僱傭條件；就業條件  
**語域**：formal / employment / policy  
**核心語義**：the conditions under which a person is employed, including hours, pay, leave, security, and workplace arrangements  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **working conditions** 最核心、最正式的近義表達之一，尤其適合勞工政策、人力資源和研究語境。  
**常見搭配**：employment conditions; improve employment conditions; terms of employment; employment standards.  
**常見搭配對象**：pay, hours, leave, contract, job security.  
**與 working conditions 的辨析**：Employment conditions 比 working conditions 更容易涵蓋合約、薪酬、工時和福利；working conditions 亦可更偏日常工作環境。  
**使用限制**：若只談噪音、溫度、設備等物理環境，workplace conditions / work environment 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The union is negotiating for better employment conditions.  
工會正爭取更好的僱傭條件。

### 02 Job circumstances

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：工作情況；職務處境  
**語域**：neutral / descriptive  
**核心語義**：the practical circumstances surrounding a person's job, including workload, schedule, location, and pressures  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 適合較一般、描述性地談某人的工作處境。  
**常見搭配**：job circumstances; difficult job circumstances; changing job circumstances; personal work circumstances.  
**常見搭配對象**：schedule, workload, location, pressure, responsibility.  
**與 working conditions 的辨析**：Job circumstances 比 working conditions 更個人化、更情境化，未必涉及制度或正式待遇。  
**使用限制**：正式勞工法或合約語境通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Her job circumstances changed after the team was reorganised.  
團隊重組後，她的工作情況有所改變。

### 03 Workplace conditions

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：工作場所條件；工作環境條件  
**語域**：neutral / occupational  
**核心語義**：the physical, organisational, and practical conditions present in a workplace  
**用法提示**：當 working conditions 的重點是工作場所本身，包括安全、溫度、噪音、設備、空間和安排時很自然。  
**常見搭配**：workplace conditions; unsafe workplace conditions; improve workplace conditions; working environment.  
**常見搭配對象**：safety, noise, temperature, equipment, space.  
**與 working conditions 的辨析**：Workplace conditions 比 employment conditions 更偏現場環境；不一定涵蓋薪酬或合約。  
**使用限制**：若重點是工資、年假、解僱條款，employment conditions / employment terms 更準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The inspection found poor workplace conditions in the factory.  
檢查發現工廠的工作場所條件欠佳。

### 04 Work environment

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：工作環境  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：the physical, social, cultural, and organisational environment in which people work  
**用法提示**：當 working conditions 包括氣氛、文化、管理風格、設施和同事互動時非常自然。  
**常見搭配**：positive work environment; safe work environment; supportive work environment; working environment.  
**常見搭配對象**：culture, safety, management, colleagues, facilities.  
**與 working conditions 的辨析**：Work environment 比 working conditions 更偏整體環境與氛圍，較少直接指薪酬／工時。  
**使用限制**：正式合約待遇不宜只用 work environment。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
A supportive work environment can reduce staff turnover.  
具支持性的工作環境可降低員工流失率。

### 05 Terms and conditions of employment

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：僱傭條款及條件  
**語域**：formal / legal / HR  
**核心語義**：the formal contractual terms governing employment, such as pay, hours, leave, duties, and notice  
**用法提示**：當 working conditions 實際指正式合約條款時，這是最精確的法律／人力資源表達。  
**常見搭配**：terms and conditions of employment; contractual terms; employment contract; vary employment terms.  
**常見搭配對象**：pay, hours, leave, notice, duties.  
**與 working conditions 的辨析**：這比 working conditions 更法律化、更書面，重點是 contractually defined terms。  
**使用限制**：物理工作環境、管理文化或同事關係不屬此詞核心。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Any change to the terms and conditions of employment must be explained clearly.  
任何僱傭條款及條件的改動都必須清楚說明。

## Tier 2 — Labour-policy / job-specific / contractual variants · 勞工政策、職位與條款變體

### 06 Conditions of work

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：工作條件；勞動條件  
**語域**：formal / labour / policy  
**核心語義**：the conditions in which work is carried out, including hours, safety, workload, welfare, and employment arrangements  
**用法提示**：較正式、政策化的表達，常見於 labour standards、國際組織和法規語境。  
**常見搭配**：conditions of work; decent conditions of work; improve conditions of work; labour standards.  
**常見搭配對象**：hours, safety, welfare, pay, workload.  
**與 working conditions 的辨析**：與 working conditions 很接近，但語氣較正式、政策化。  
**使用限制**：日常職場對話較少用這個語序。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The report calls for safer conditions of work in high-risk industries.  
報告呼籲高風險行業提供更安全的工作條件。

### 07 Job conditions

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：工作條件；職位條件  
**語域**：neutral / concise  
**核心語義**：the practical conditions associated with a particular job  
**用法提示**：可作 working conditions 的簡潔改寫，尤其談一份工作本身的待遇、工時和要求。  
**常見搭配**：job conditions; poor job conditions; improve job conditions; job quality.  
**常見搭配對象**：hours, pay, workload, flexibility, security.  
**與 working conditions 的辨析**：比 working conditions 稍更 job-specific，常聚焦某職位而非整個 workplace。  
**使用限制**：極正式政策文件通常 employment conditions / conditions of work 更自然。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
Flexible hours have improved job conditions for many employees.  
彈性工時改善了很多員工的工作條件。

### 08 Employment terms

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：僱傭條款  
**語域**：formal / HR / legal  
**核心語義**：the specific contractual terms attached to a person's employment  
**用法提示**：當 working conditions 主要是 pay, hours, benefits, leave, notice 等明文條款時很精準。  
**常見搭配**：employment terms; revise employment terms; contractual employment terms; agree employment terms.  
**常見搭配對象**：salary, hours, leave, notice, benefit.  
**與 working conditions 的辨析**：Employment terms 比 working conditions 更窄，集中在合同條款。  
**使用限制**：不涵蓋所有非正式或物理工作環境因素。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The new employment terms include more flexible working hours.  
新的僱傭條款包括更彈性的工作時間。

## Tier 3 — Occupational-research contextual variant · 職業研究型情境變體

### 09 Occupational conditions

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：職業條件；職業工作條件  
**語域**：formal / research / occupational health  
**核心語義**：conditions associated with a particular occupation, especially in research on health, risk, workload, and job exposure  
**用法提示**：適合 occupational health、勞工研究或跨職業比較。  
**常見搭配**：occupational conditions; occupational risk; occupational exposure; occupational health.  
**常見搭配對象**：risk, exposure, workload, health, occupation.  
**與 working conditions 的辨析**：比 working conditions 更研究化、更 occupation-level，不是日常近義詞。  
**使用限制**：一般職場寫作會顯得過度 technical。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The study compares occupational conditions across several industries.  
研究比較數個行業的職業條件。

## 04 False synonyms / working-condition traps · 容易誤選的薪酬、工時與工作量陷阱

### 01 Salary

**薪酬 ≠ working conditions**  
薪酬只是工作條件的一部分。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Salary is one component, not the whole concept.

### 02 Benefits

**福利 ≠ working conditions**  
福利只涵蓋待遇的一部分。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Benefits are only one element.

### 03 Workload

**工作量 ≠ working conditions**  
工作量是條件之一，不代表整體工作環境。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Workload is one factor.

### 04 Job satisfaction

**工作滿意度 ≠ working conditions**  
滿意度是員工反應／感受，不是條件本身。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Satisfaction is an outcome/attitude.

### 05 Work environment

**只在環境／氛圍 sense 接近**  
不一定包括薪酬、合約和年假。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Work environment is environment-focused.

### 06 Employment terms

**只在合約條款 sense 接近**  
不涵蓋所有物理與社會工作環境。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Employment terms are contract-focused.

### 07 Working hours

**工時 ≠ working conditions**  
工時只是其中一個構成因素。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Hours are one component.

### 08 Working / workplace / employment conditions

**搭配陷阱**  
三者重點不同：working 最廣、workplace 偏現場環境、employment 偏僱傭制度。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Choose by scope.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Employment conditions · 僱傭條件；就業條件

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The union wants to improve working conditions for part-time staff.

**中文翻譯**  
工會希望改善兼職員工的僱傭條件。

- A. Employment conditions
- B. Job conditions
- C. Employment terms
- D. Salary
- E. Benefits
- F. Workload

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Employment conditions** · 僱傭條件；就業條件

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The union wants to improve employment conditions for part-time staff.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Employment conditions ✓** — the conditions under which someone is employed, including pay, hours, leave, and security. Here, pay, hours and employment arrangements are central. That makes **Employment conditions** the most precise replacement for this particular sense of *working conditions*.
- **B.** **Job conditions** — the practical conditions associated with a particular job. This is job-specific and less suitable when the context concerns broad employment systems or formal policy. The decisive clue is that pay, hours and employment arrangements are central.
- **C.** **Employment terms** — the specific contractual terms attached to employment. This is contract-focused and too narrow when the context includes physical or social workplace factors. The decisive clue is that pay, hours and employment arrangements are central.
- **D.** **Salary** — pay received for work. Salary is only one component of working conditions. The decisive clue is that pay, hours and employment arrangements are central.
- **E.** **Benefits** — non-wage employment advantages or entitlements. Benefits are only one component of working conditions. The decisive clue is that pay, hours and employment arrangements are central.
- **F.** **Workload** — the amount of work assigned. Workload is only one factor and cannot represent the whole concept. The decisive clue is that pay, hours and employment arrangements are central.

|---|---|---|

| 個人工作處境 | **job circumstances** | 是否 practical personal situation is central？ |
| 噪音／溫度／安全／設備 | **workplace conditions** | 是否 physical workplace factors are central？ |
| 文化／管理／同事氣氛 | **work environment** | 是否 social/organisational environment is central？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **working conditions vs employment conditions**：employment conditions 更偏僱傭制度與待遇。
- **working conditions vs job circumstances**：job circumstances 更個人化、更情境化。
- **working conditions vs workplace conditions**：workplace conditions 更偏現場物理／實際環境。
- **working conditions vs work environment**：work environment 更偏文化、氛圍和整體體驗。
- **working conditions vs terms and conditions of employment**：後者是正式法律／合約用語。
- **working conditions vs conditions of work**：後者更政策化、勞工標準化。
- **working conditions vs job conditions**：job conditions 更聚焦某職位。
- **working conditions vs employment terms**：employment terms 只聚焦具體合約條款。
- **working conditions vs job satisfaction**：job satisfaction 是員工感受／結果，不是條件本身。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & employment traps

- **working conditions** 通常用複數：improve working conditions。
- **employment conditions** 常與 **pay, hours, leave, security** 搭配。
- **workplace conditions** 常與 **safety, heat, noise, equipment** 搭配。
- **work environment** 常與 **supportive, positive, toxic, collaborative** 搭配。
- **terms and conditions of employment** 是正式固定搭配。
- **annual leave, pay, benefits, working hours, workload** 都只是 working conditions 的部分構成。
- **job satisfaction** 是對工作條件的反應，不是 working conditions 本身。
- 英式／香港職場語境自然使用 **annual leave, working hours, employment terms, organisation**。
