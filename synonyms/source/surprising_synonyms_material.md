# 01 同義詞指南


## 01 unexpected

**出乎意料的；意想不到的**

最中性、最直接，表示沒有被預料到。

### EXAMPLE · 例句

The sudden drop in prices was surprising.

價格突然下跌令人意外。

### COMMON COLLOCATIONS · 常見搭配


- unexpected result

- unexpected change

- unexpected victory

- unexpected decision


### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION · 判斷問題

> 只是沒有預料到，還是強烈驚訝、突然受驚、值得注意、正面讚嘆、顯眼、改變認知，或反直覺？

# 02 False synonyms


## 01 Unexpected

**最接近但只說未預期**

unexpected 不一定表示情緒很強。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The delay was unexpected; the size of the delay was surprising.

---


## 02 Astonishing

**通常比 surprising 強**

astonishing 表示非常驚人。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

An astonishing result is much stronger than a mildly surprising one.

---


## 03 Startling

**常帶突然衝擊感**

startling 往往使人立即受驚或提高警覺。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A startling noise may make you jump.

---


## 04 Shocking

**常帶負面震撼**

shocking 常涉及嚴重、令人反感或情緒衝擊強的內容。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A surprising gift is pleasant; a shocking discovery may be disturbing.

---


## 05 Remarkable

**值得注意不一定意外**

remarkable 可以完全在預期之內，只因表現突出而值得注意。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

A remarkable performance can still be expected.

---


## 06 Amazing

**常帶正面讚嘆**

amazing 在日常英語中常表示非常好／令人讚嘆。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The concert was amazing; the cancellation was surprising.

---


## 07 Surprised / surprising

**-ed / -ing 形容詞陷阱**

`surprising` 描述引起驚訝的事物；`surprised` 描述感到驚訝的人。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The result was surprising; we were surprised by it.

---


## 08 Surprisingly / surprising

**詞性陷阱**

`surprisingly` 是副詞；`surprising` 是形容詞。

### ENGLISH COMPARISON

The task was surprisingly easy; the result was surprising.

# 05 高價值近義詞對比

- **unexpected**：只要求「沒有預料到」。
- **astonishing**：比 surprising 強很多。
- **startling**：常帶突然衝擊或警示感。
- **remarkable**：值得注意，不必完全出乎意料。
- **amazing**：通常較正面、口語。
- **extraordinary**：強調遠超常態。
- **striking**：最適合 difference / similarity / contrast / increase。
- **eye-opening**：驚訝之外，還帶來新理解。
- **counterintuitive**：結果違反原先直覺。
