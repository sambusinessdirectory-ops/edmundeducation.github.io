# SYNONYMS · 同義詞學習系統

# Polished (adjective)

**`polished` 可以表示精緻成熟、呈現得宜、高雅 sophisticated、專業、視覺俐落、優雅、技巧熟練，或人物舉止從容得體。不同替代表達的核心差別，是『refinement / presentation / sophistication / professional standard / sleek design / elegance / accomplishment / poise』。**

核心替代表達：

`refined / well-presented / sophisticated / professional / sleek / elegant / accomplished / poised`

---

# 01 同義詞指南

## 01 Refined

**精緻的；優雅的；經過琢磨的**

當 `polished` 指風格、品味、語言或作品經過細緻改良，顯得成熟而精緻時，`refined` 很自然。它比 `professional` 更偏 taste and refinement。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The final design is simple, refined, and elegant.

最終設計簡潔、精緻而優雅。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **carefully improved and showing taste or sophistication**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、設計、評論

---

## 02 Well-presented

**呈現得宜的；外觀整潔得體的**

當 `polished` 指文件、簡報、作品或人的外觀整理得好、看起來專業而整齊時，`well-presented` 很貼切。它強調 presentation rather than depth。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The report is clear and well-presented.

這份報告清晰而且呈現得宜。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **neatly and attractively presented**

**REGISTER · 語域**

工作、教育、一般

---

## 03 Sophisticated

**成熟精緻的；高雅的**

當 `polished` 指設計、風格、表達或人的舉止成熟、有修養、有高度精緻感時，`sophisticated` 很合適。它比 refined 更常帶 modern / cultured feel。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The hotel has a sophisticated interior.

酒店有成熟精緻的室內設計。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **refined, cultured, and highly developed**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、一般、評論

---

## 04 Professional

**專業的；專業水準的**

當 `polished` 指簡報、文件、表現或外觀看起來具專業水準、完成度高時，`professional` 很自然。它強調 workplace standards and competence。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The presentation looked professional and well organised.

這份簡報看起來很專業，而且組織良好。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **meeting a high professional standard in appearance or performance**

**REGISTER · 語域**

工作、一般

---

## 05 Sleek

**俐落流線的；光滑精緻的**

當 `polished` 指產品、介面、車身或設計外觀流線、光潔、現代而俐落時，`sleek` 很精準。它偏 visual surface and design。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> The phone has a sleek aluminium body.

這部電話有俐落流線的鋁製機身。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **smooth, streamlined, and stylish in appearance**

**REGISTER · 語域**

設計、科技、一般

---

## 06 Elegant

**優雅的；高雅的**

當 `polished` 指設計、表達、外觀或方案簡潔而有品味、毫不笨重時，`elegant` 很自然。它比 sophisticated 更強調 graceful simplicity。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She gave an elegant and concise explanation.

她作出優雅而簡潔的解釋。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **graceful, tasteful, and pleasingly simple**

**REGISTER · 語域**

所有語域

---

## 07 Accomplished

**熟練的；有造詣的；完成度高的**

當 `polished` 指人的演出、技巧、寫作或表現熟練成熟、顯示大量訓練時，`accomplished` 很合適。它偏 skill rather than appearance。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> She gave an accomplished piano performance.

她作出了一場技巧成熟的鋼琴演奏。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **highly skilled and confidently executed**

**REGISTER · 語域**

正式、藝術、人物描述

---

## 08 Poised

**從容自信的；舉止得體的**

當 `polished` 指人的舉止、說話或公開表現從容、穩定、得體時，`poised` 很自然。它強調 self-possession and composure。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**

> He remained poised throughout the interview.

整個面試期間，他都保持從容自信。

**SEMANTIC CUE · 語義提示**

> **calm, confident, and self-controlled in manner**

**REGISTER · 語域**

人物描述、工作、正式

# 02 False synonyms

## Polished / Refined
`refined` 強調經過琢磨、品味成熟、細節克制。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The design looks polished.  
The design looks refined and understated.

## Polished / Well-presented
`well-presented` 強調外觀、格式、組織呈現得好，不一定代表內容很深。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The report is polished.  
The report is clear and well-presented.

## Polished / Sophisticated
`sophisticated` 強調成熟、高雅或高度發展，比 polished 更偏 complexity / refinement。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The system is polished.  
The system has become more sophisticated.

## Polished / Professional
`professional` 強調工作場合的可信度、完成度和專業標準。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The presentation looks polished.  
The presentation looks professional and well organised.

## Polished / Sleek
`sleek` 特別適合產品、介面、車身等流線、光滑、現代的外觀。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The laptop looks polished.  
The laptop has a sleek aluminium body.

## Polished / Elegant
`elegant` 強調優雅、簡潔、有品味，亦可形容 solution / explanation。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
The solution is polished.  
The solution is elegant and simple.

## Polished / Accomplished
`accomplished` 主要形容人的技能、演出、寫作達到成熟水準。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Her performance was polished.  
Her performance was accomplished and confident.

## Polished / Poised
`poised` 主要形容人的舉止／回應從容、自信、穩定。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
She looked polished in the interview.  
She looked poised and calm in the interview.

# 05 高價值近義詞辨析

- **Refined vs Sophisticated** — refined 偏細節／品味經過琢磨；sophisticated 偏成熟、高度發展。
- **Well-presented vs Professional** — well-presented 只保證呈現好；professional 更強調整體達到專業標準。
- **Sleek vs Elegant** — sleek 偏流線現代；elegant 偏優雅簡潔。
- **Accomplished vs Professional** — accomplished 更偏技能／藝術造詣；professional 偏職業水準。
- **Poised vs Polished** — poised 特別形容人的從容；polished 可形容人、作品、演出、文件。
- **Polished ≠ shiny in every context** — polished 作形容人／表現時不等於真的「有光澤」。
- **Polished ≠ perfect** — 完成度高不代表毫無缺點。
- **Polished ≠ sophisticated in every context** — 一個簡潔 presentation 可以 polished，但不一定 sophisticated。

# 08 Grammar & structure traps

- **polished** = adjective；**polish** = noun/verb
- **refined** = adjective；**refinement** = noun；**refine** = verb
- **well-presented** 作前置形容詞通常保留連字號
- **sophisticated** = adjective；**sophistication** = noun
- **professional** = adjective/noun；**professionally** = adverb
- **sleek** = adjective；**sleekness** = noun
- **elegant** = adjective；**elegance** = noun；**elegantly** = adverb
- **accomplished** = adjective；不要和 **accomplishment**（成就）混淆
- **poised** = adjective；**poise** = noun/verb

# 09 核心決策問題

1. 是經過細緻琢磨、品味成熟？ → **refined**
2. 是文件／作品看起來整理得很好？ → **well-presented**
3. 是成熟、高雅或技術上高度發展？ → **sophisticated**
4. 是達到工作場合專業標準？ → **professional**
5. 是產品／介面外觀流線現代？ → **sleek**
6. 是簡潔、優雅、有品味？ → **elegant**
7. 是演出／技能顯示多年訓練？ → **accomplished**
8. 是人在壓力下仍從容自信？ → **poised**
