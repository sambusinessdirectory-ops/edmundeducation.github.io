# SUDDENLY · 分層同義詞學習系統

## Suddenly · adverb

**Suddenly 可以表示某事發生得很突兀、沒有漸進、出乎意料、沒有預警，或在極短時間內一下子改變。** 因此替換前要分清 **sharp transition, surprise, simultaneity, absence of warning, complete unexpectedness, instant change,** 和 **rapid short-term change**。

你提供的 **abruptly; unexpectedly; all at once** 正好代表三個核心方向：**急促突變、出乎意料、一下子同時發生**。

本次保留 **7 個**自然且具教學價值的近義／情境表達，共 **21 道練習**，每個目標 **3 題**。沒有把 **immediately** 或 **quickly** 當作全面同義詞，因為它們分別表示『沒有延遲』和『速度快』，都不必然有突然感。

## Sense split · 義項分流

| Suddenly 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 |
|---|---|
| 急停／急轉／突然中斷 | **abruptly** |
| 結果／出現出乎意料 | **unexpectedly** |
| 一下子多人／多件事同時發生 | **all at once** |
| 完全沒有預警 | **without warning** |
| 口語：完全出乎意料 | **out of the blue** |
| 狀態在轉瞬間改變 | **in an instant** |
| 短時間內像一夜之間改變 | **overnight** |

## Tier 1 — Core abruptness / surprise / simultaneity equivalents · 核心突變、意外與同時發生近義詞

### 01 Abruptly

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：突然地；猛然地；出乎意料地中斷／改變  
**語域**：neutral / formal  
**核心語義**：suddenly and sharply, especially when something changes, stops, starts, or ends without a smooth transition  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 是 **suddenly** 最核心的近義詞之一，特別適合 stop, turn, end, change, leave 等帶明顯『急轉／中止』的動作。  
**常見搭配**：stop abruptly; end abruptly; turn abruptly; change abruptly; leave abruptly.  
**常見搭配對象**：movement, conversation, meeting, change, departure.  
**與 suddenly 的辨析**：Abruptly 比 suddenly 多一層『不平順、急促、沒有過渡』的感覺。  
**使用限制**：如果只是事情出乎意料地發生，而沒有 sharp change / interruption，unexpectedly 更精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The conversation ended abruptly when the fire alarm sounded.  
火警鐘響起時，談話突然中斷。

### 02 Unexpectedly

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：出乎意料地；意外地  
**語域**：neutral / very common  
**核心語義**：in a way that was not expected or predicted  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很核心，當 **suddenly** 的真正重點是『沒有預料到』而非速度或動作急促時最自然。  
**常見搭配**：arrive unexpectedly; unexpectedly resign; unexpectedly increase; unexpectedly appear.  
**常見搭配對象**：arrival, resignation, result, event, visitor.  
**與 suddenly 的辨析**：Unexpectedly 強調 surprise / lack of expectation；suddenly 強調事情發生得快、沒有漸進。  
**使用限制**：若句子明確描寫動作猛然停止／轉向，abruptly 更好。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
She unexpectedly announced that she was leaving the company.  
她出乎意料地宣布自己將離開公司。

### 03 All at once

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：突然一下子；一下子全部發生  
**語域**：neutral / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：suddenly, often with several things happening or becoming apparent at the same moment  
**用法提示**：你提供的 seed 很實用，特別適合 emotions, noises, lights, memories, people speaking 等『一下子』發生的語境。  
**常見搭配**：all at once, ...; happen all at once; start all at once; understand all at once.  
**常見搭配對象**：events, emotions, sounds, realisation, changes.  
**與 suddenly 的辨析**：All at once 常有『同一時間／一下子』意味；suddenly 不一定涉及 multiple/simultaneous effects。  
**使用限制**：正式技術寫作較少用；單一 sharp interruption 用 abruptly 更精準。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
All at once, everyone started talking.  
突然一下子，所有人都開始說話。

### 04 Without warning

**Tier**：1  
**中文**：毫無預警地；沒有先兆地  
**語域**：neutral / common  
**核心語義**：suddenly and with no advance sign or notice  
**用法提示**：當 **suddenly** 的關鍵是『沒有任何預兆／通知』，例如 power failure, collapse, attack, shutdown, rainstorm，這是很自然的改寫。  
**常見搭配**：happen without warning; stop without warning; collapse without warning; appear without warning.  
**常見搭配對象**：failure, shutdown, collapse, storm, attack.  
**與 suddenly 的辨析**：Without warning 強調 absence of prior signal；suddenly 只表示發生得快。  
**使用限制**：如果事件其實有先兆，只是發生得快，不能用 without warning。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The power went off without warning.  
電力毫無預警地中斷。

## Tier 2 — Idiomatic surprise / instant-change variants · 慣用意外與瞬間變化變體

### 05 Out of the blue

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：突如其來地；完全出乎意料地  
**語域**：informal / idiomatic  
**核心語義**：completely unexpectedly, with no obvious reason or warning  
**用法提示**：適合 phone call, message, offer, invitation, news, resignation 等『完全沒想到會發生』的口語語境。  
**常見搭配**：call out of the blue; appear out of the blue; receive an offer out of the blue.  
**常見搭配對象**：call, message, offer, invitation, news.  
**與 suddenly 的辨析**：比 unexpectedly 更口語、更強調『完全沒有心理準備』。  
**使用限制**：正式報告、事故技術描述通常不用。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
He called me out of the blue after ten years of silence.  
他沉寂十年後突然打電話給我，完全出乎意料。

### 06 In an instant

**Tier**：2  
**中文**：轉瞬間；一下子  
**語域**：neutral / vivid  
**核心語義**：within a very short moment, with the change happening almost immediately  
**用法提示**：當 **suddenly** 描述某個狀態／情況在極短時間內轉變時可用。  
**常見搭配**：change in an instant; disappear in an instant; mood changed in an instant.  
**常見搭配對象**：mood, scene, situation, expression, condition.  
**與 suddenly 的辨析**：In an instant 更強調速度極快；suddenly 更強調非漸進／突然發生。  
**使用限制**：如果重點是意外性，而不是速度，unexpectedly / out of the blue 更好。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The mood in the room changed in an instant.  
房間裏的氣氛轉瞬間改變了。

## Tier 3 — Rapid short-term change contextual variant · 短期快速變化情境變體

### 07 Overnight

**Tier**：3  
**中文**：一夜之間；突然在很短時間內  
**語域**：neutral / contextual  
**核心語義**：very quickly, especially when a change appears to happen from one day to the next  
**用法提示**：當 **suddenly** 用於 success, popularity, policy, prices, reputation 等『短時間內突然改變』的比喻語境時可用。  
**常見搭配**：become famous overnight; change overnight; disappear overnight; overnight success.  
**常見搭配對象**：fame, popularity, policy, reputation, market.  
**與 suddenly 的辨析**：Overnight 不是真的『瞬間』，而是強調短期內快速變化。  
**使用限制**：真正一秒內發生的事件、猛然轉身、突然停車等不適合。

**EXAMPLE · 例句**  
The small app became popular overnight.  
這個小型應用程式幾乎一夜之間變得流行。

## 04 False synonyms / suddenness traps · 容易誤選的立即、快速、逐漸與最終詞

### 01 Immediately

**立即 ≠ suddenly**  
immediately 強調沒有延遲；suddenly 強調發生得突兀／非漸進。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Immediately is delay-focused.

### 02 Quickly

**快速地 ≠ suddenly**  
quickly 表示速度快，但事情可以是預期且逐步進行。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Quickly is speed-focused.

### 03 Abruptly

**只在急促中斷／轉變 sense 高度接近**  
abruptly 多一層 sharp/no-transition 意味。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Abruptly is transition-focused.

### 04 Unexpectedly

**只在出乎意料 sense 高度接近**  
unexpectedly 強調 surprise，不一定發生得很快。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Unexpectedly is expectation-focused.

### 05 Instantly

**瞬間／立即 ≠ suddenly**  
instantly 強調幾乎沒有時間延遲，不一定出乎意料。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Instantly is immediacy-focused.

### 06 Eventually

**最終 ≠ suddenly**  
eventually 表示經過一段時間後，語義幾乎相反。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Eventually is delayed outcome-focused.

### 07 Gradually

**逐漸地 ≠ suddenly**  
gradually 表示慢慢變化，與 suddenly 相反。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Gradually is the opposite.

### 08 Sudden / suddenly

**詞性陷阱**  
sudden 是形容詞；suddenly 是副詞。

**ENGLISH COMPARISON**  
Check word class.

### 練習 01.1

**Tier 1 · Target word / phrase**: Abruptly · 突然地；猛然地；出乎意料地中斷／改變

**READ THE CONTEXT · 閱讀語境**

The car suddenly stopped in the middle of the road.

**中文翻譯**  
汽車突然在馬路中央停下。

- A. Abruptly
- B. Unexpectedly
- C. In an instant
- D. Immediately
- E. Quickly
- F. Gradually

**CORRECT ANSWER · 正確答案**

**A. Abruptly** · 突然地；猛然地；出乎意料地中斷／改變

**Correct rewrite · 正確改寫**  
The car stopped abruptly in the middle of the road.

**SIX-OPTION FEEDBACK · 六個選項的詳細解釋**

- **A.** **Abruptly ✓** — suddenly and sharply, without a smooth transition。本句關鍵是 **a sharp stop with no smooth transition is central**；因此 **Abruptly** 最精準。
- **B.** **Unexpectedly** — in a way that was not expected or predicted。**unexpectedly** 聚焦『沒想到』；若句子重點是急停、瞬間改變或同時發生，焦點不對。
- **C.** **In an instant** — within a very short moment, almost immediately。**in an instant** 聚焦速度極快；事情可以完全在預期之中，因此不等於 unexpectedly。
- **D.** **Immediately** — without delay。**immediately** 表示沒有延遲；它不表示事情是突如其來或沒有預警。
- **E.** **Quickly** — at a fast speed。**quickly** 只是動作速度快；可以是計劃中的、漸進的。
- **F.** **Gradually** — slowly, by small degrees。**gradually** 表示逐漸變化，與 suddenly 的核心語義相反。

- **E.** **Quickly** — at a fast speed。**quickly** 只是動作速度快；可以是計劃中的、漸進的。
- **F.** **Gradually** — slowly, by small degrees。**gradually** 表示逐漸變化，與 suddenly 的核心語義相反。

## 06 Semantic map · 語義總覽

| Suddenly 的語境 | 最接近的詞／表達 | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| 汽車急停／會議中斷／突然轉身 | **abruptly** | 是否 sharp change with no smooth transition？ |
| 辭職／結果／出現令人大感意外 | **unexpectedly** | 是否 lack of expectation is central？ |
| 所有人／多件事同時一下子發生 | **all at once** | 是否 simultaneity is central？ |
| 升降機／網站／樹木毫無預警出事 | **without warning** | 是否 there was no advance sign？ |
| 工作邀請／舊友訊息完全突如其來 | **out of the blue** | 是否 conversational complete surprise is intended？ |
| 氣氛／表情／場景轉瞬間改變 | **in an instant** | 是否 the state changes almost immediately？ |
| 成名／市場／輿論短時間巨變 | **overnight** | 是否 change happens over a very short period rather than literally instantly？ |

## 07 Direct contrasts · 核心辨析

- **suddenly vs abruptly**：abruptly 多一層急促、不平順、沒有過渡。
- **suddenly vs unexpectedly**：unexpectedly 聚焦『沒想到』。
- **suddenly vs all at once**：all at once 常有『同時／一下子全部』意味。
- **suddenly vs without warning**：後者明確表示沒有任何先兆。
- **suddenly vs out of the blue**：後者更口語，表示完全出乎意料。
- **suddenly vs in an instant**：後者更強調變化速度極快。
- **suddenly vs overnight**：overnight 是短時間內快速改變，不一定是瞬間。
- **suddenly vs immediately**：immediately = 沒有延遲，不等於突然。
- **suddenly vs quickly**：quickly = 速度快，不等於突如其來。

## 08 Grammar, collocation & suddenness traps

- **suddenly + verb**：suddenly stopped / suddenly appeared / suddenly changed。
- **abruptly + stop/end/turn/change/leave**。
- **unexpectedly + arrive/resign/increase/appear**。
- **all at once** 常放句首或句尾：All at once, everyone stood up。
- **without warning** 常放在動詞後：The system shut down without warning。
- **out of the blue** 常用於 call/message/offer/invitation 等口語語境。
- **in an instant** 常搭配 change/disappear/transform。
- **overnight** 常搭配 become famous/change/disappear。
- **sudden** 是形容詞；**suddenly** 是副詞。
