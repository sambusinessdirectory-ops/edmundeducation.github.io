The résumé version履歷版本

“Career politician with experience in naval strategy, coalition leadership, public speaking, and returning to a previous role after a six-year gap. Comfortable under pressure. Less comfortable with constructive feedback.”「職業政治家，熟悉海軍戰略、聯合政府領導及公開演說；曾離任六年後重返舊職。能在壓力下工作，對『建設性意見』則未必樂意接受。」

Churchill served as UK prime minister in 1940–45 and 1951–55. Before those appointments he had been a soldier, war correspondent, author, Liberal and Conservative MP, and minister across trade, the Home Office, the Admiralty, war supply, colonial policy and the Treasury. His books and speeches continued alongside politics; the 1953 Nobel Prize was for Literature.邱吉爾兩度出任英國首相，任期分別為1940至1945年及1951至1955年。此前他曾是軍人、戰地記者、作家、自由黨及保守黨國會議員，亦曾主管貿易、內政、海軍、軍需、殖民地及財政事務。他在從政期間持續寫作和演說；1953年獲頒的是諾貝爾文學獎。

Death逝世

Winston Leonard Spencer Churchill died on 24 January 1965, aged 90. The date was exactly seventy years after the death of his father, Lord Randolph Churchill. The Library of Congress’s funeral record notes that coincidence and the granting of a state funeral. 溫斯頓·倫納德·斯賓塞·邱吉爾於1965年1月24日逝世，享年90歲；當天恰是其父倫道夫·邱吉爾勳爵逝世70周年。美國國會圖書館的葬禮紀錄提及這個巧合及他獲安排國葬一事。[R001] [R122]

Funeral國葬

The state funeral took place on 30 January. It followed public lying-in-state and brought a lifetime of military, political and literary service into a national ceremonial setting. The order of service survives as a primary historical object, not merely as a later description of the occasion. 國葬於 1 月 30 日舉行。它遵循公開入葬儀式，將一生的軍事、政治和文學服務帶入國家儀式。服務順序作為主要的歷史物件而保留下來，而不僅僅是後來對場合的描述。[R001] [R122]

End of the timeline年表終點

This is the endpoint of the lifetime Experience record. Later biographies, memorials, films, archival publications and political arguments about Churchill are part of his reception and legacy, not activities he performed after death. 這是生命週期體驗記錄的端點。後來有關邱吉爾的傳記、紀念館、電影、檔案出版物和政治爭論是他的接受和遺產的一部分，而不是他死後所進行的活動。[R127]

Final profile assessment整體評估

The record contains resistance to Nazi Germany, major writing, social reform and institution-building, together with imperial coercion, strategic failures and policies with devastating human consequences. A useful historical profile does not reduce those to a single numerical approval rating. Its purpose is to let readers move from an accessible format to dated actions, evidence and informed judgement.該記錄包含對納粹德國的抵抗、主要著作、社會改革和機構建設，以及帝國的脅迫、戰略失敗和對人類造成毀滅性後果的政策。有用的歷史檔案不會將這些降低到單一的數位支援率。其目的是讓讀者從易於理解的格式轉向過時的行動、證據和明智的判斷。

Continuing elected service繼續擔任國會議員

Churchill retained Woodford after leaving the premiership, winning re-election in 1955 and 1959. His parliamentary tenure therefore outlasted his executive career. Age and declining health reduced his activity, but the seat remained his until the end of the 1959–64 Parliament. 邱吉爾在卸任首相後繼續留任伍德福德，並於 1955 年和 1959 年贏得連任。因此，他的議會任期比他的行政生涯更長久。年齡和健康狀況惡化減少了他的活動，但這個席位一直保留到 1959-64 年議會結束。[R011] [R001]

Father of the House下議院議齡最長議員

From 1959 he was Father of the House, a distinction based on seniority of continuous service among qualifying MPs, not simply on being the oldest person in the chamber. His earlier gap outside Parliament in 1922–24 matters to the calculation of continuous service. 1959年起，邱吉爾成為下議院議齡最長的議員。這項身分按符合條件議員的連續任職年資計算，並非單看年齡；他在1922至1924年未任議員的空檔，對年資計算有影響。[R001] [R133]

Final attendance and tribute最後出席下議院與致敬

He last attended the Commons on 27 July 1964. The House’s tributes and resolution were recorded on 28 July. These are adjacent but different dates; the tribute debate should not be used to move his final attendance forward a day. 邱吉爾最後一次出席下議院是1964年7月27日；下議院的致敬辯論與決議記於翌日。兩個日期相近卻不同，不能把致敬辯論日期當作其最後出席日期。[R001] [R133]

End of parliamentary career議會生涯結束

He did not contest the 1964 general election. The end of his membership belongs to the dissolution of that Parliament, not to a death in office in January 1965. By then he was already a former MP as well as a former Prime Minister. 他沒有參加 1964 年大選。他的任期結束是由於議會解散，而不是 1965 年 1 月在任期間去世。那時他已經是前議員和前首相。[R001]

Recognition榮譽授予

On 9 April 1963 President John F. Kennedy proclaimed Churchill an honorary citizen of the United States following congressional action. The JFK Library’s archival account records the process and ceremony. 1963 年 4 月 9 日，約翰·F·甘迺迪總統在國會採取行動後宣佈邱吉爾為美國榮譽公民。甘迺迪圖書館的檔案記述記錄了整個過程和儀式。[R132]

Attendance出席情況

Churchill was not physically present at the White House ceremony; members of his family represented him. The honour recognised a long connection with the United States, including his American mother and the wartime partnership, rather than an application for ordinary residence and naturalisation. 邱吉爾沒有親自出席白宮儀式；他的家人代表他。該榮譽認可了與美國的長期聯繫，包括他的美國母親和戰時夥伴關係，而不是申請普通居留和入籍。[R132]

Legal and profile distinction法律身分與檔案分類

The historically appropriate label is honorary US citizen. It should not be converted into an invented American government job, a claim that he had become President, or a statement that he moved permanently to the United States. 歷史上合適的標籤是美國榮譽公民。它不應該被轉化為虛構的美國政府職位、聲稱他已成為總統或他永久移居美國的聲明。[R132]

Career setting職涯背景

This recognition came eight years after he left the premiership, while he still held his parliamentary seat. It belongs to the final phase of a public life in which symbolic international recognition continued even as active governmental responsibility had ended. 這項認可是在他卸任首相八年後獲得的，當時他仍擔任議會席位。它屬於公共生活的最後階段，儘管積極的政府責任已經結束，但像徵性的國際認可仍在繼續。[R001] [R127]

The college project學院籌建

The plan for Churchill College became public in 1958. Its development sought to strengthen higher education, especially science and technology, as a memorial associated with Churchill. The institutional timeline records the stages from the project’s early conception to its opening. 邱吉爾學院的計劃於 1958 年公開。其發展旨在加強高等教育，特別是科學與技術，作為與邱吉爾相關的紀念館。機構時間表記錄了從專案早期構思到開業的各個階段。[R129]

Charter and students特許狀與學生

The Royal Charter was granted on 3 August 1960, and the first postgraduate students arrived that year. The college’s official opening followed in 1964. These are separate milestones: announcement, legal foundation, admission of students and opening should not be collapsed into one interchangeable foundation date. 皇家特許狀於 1960 年 8 月 3 日授予，第一批研究生於當年抵達。學院於 1964 年正式成立。這些是獨立的里程碑：公告、法律成立、學生入學和開學不應合併為一個可互換的成立日期。[R130] [R129]

American participation美國參與

The Winston Churchill Foundation of the United States was established in 1959 at Churchill’s request to encourage American participation in the college. This provides a specific institutional example of his continuing transatlantic interests outside government. 美國溫斯頓邱吉爾基金會於 1959 年應邱吉爾的要求成立，旨在鼓勵美國人參與該學院。這提供了他在政府之外持續關注跨大西洋利益的具體制度例子。[R131]

Role boundary職務界線

Churchill was a founding supporter and public sponsor, not the college’s day-to-day Master or an enrolled student. The institution continued developing after his death; the chronology should not credit him personally with every later academic achievement associated with his name. 邱吉爾是創始支持者和公共贊助者，而不是學院的日常院長或在校學生。他去世後，該機構繼續發展。年表不應將與他的名字相關的每一項後來的學術成就歸功於他個人。[R129]

Appointment任職

Churchill held the office of Lord Warden of the Cinque Ports from 1941 until his death. Walmer Castle was associated with the office, whose medieval history should not be confused with its largely ceremonial character in Churchill’s period. 邱吉爾自1941年出任五港總督，直至逝世。與該職相關的沃爾默城堡有悠久歷史；中世紀職權不能與邱吉爾任內主要屬禮儀性質的職務混為一談。[R014] [R001]

Distinction from executive office與行政職務的區別

This title did not make him the modern operational commander of all Channel shipping, nor was it a second Admiralty appointment. The practical authority of a medieval office and the ceremonial position held by a twentieth-century statesman are different things. 這個職銜沒有使他成為指揮英吉利海峽所有航運的現代作戰長官，也不是他第二次出任海軍大臣。中世紀職務原有的實權，與20世紀政治家所擔任的禮儀角色，是兩回事。[R014]

Beginning and practice繪畫起點與創作

Churchill took up painting in 1915 and continued it for many years. Churchill served with the Malakand Field Force on India’s north-west frontier in 1897, with an attachment to the 31st Punjab Infantry. The area is now in Pakistan. His dispatches and subsequent book drew on participation in the campaign, which involved British-Indian forces and local resistance to imperial power. 1897 年，邱吉爾在印度西北邊境的馬拉坎德野戰部隊服役，隸屬於第 31 旁遮普步兵團。該地區現在位於巴基斯坦。他的電報和隨後的書以參與這場戰役為基礎，其中涉及英印軍隊和當地對帝國權力的抵抗。[R001] [R020]

Publication delivered出版品已交付

The Story of the Malakand Field Force appeared in 1898. It was his first book, not his first writing of any kind. The narrative combines description of operations, judgements about commanders and broad arguments about frontier policy. It is valuable evidence of his developing prose and political outlook as well as of events he witnessed. 《馬拉坎德野戰部隊的故事》出版於 1898 年。這是他的第一本書，而不是他的第一部作品。敘述結合了對行動的描述、對指揮官的判斷以及對邊境政策的廣泛爭論。這是他不斷發展的散文和政治觀點以及他所目睹的事件的寶貴證據。[R020] [R021]

Evidence and accountability證據與問責

The book is a participant’s account written from an imperial perspective. Its labels for local people, explanations of resistance and justifications of punitive action should not be silently transferred into a modern factual narration. The campaign involved coercion and destruction, not merely adventurous travel. Reporting an author’s judgement requires identifying it as his judgement. 本書是參與者從帝國視角撰寫的記述。它對當地人的標籤、對抵抗的解釋以及懲罰行動的理由不應該被默默地轉變為現代的事實敘述。這場運動涉及強制和破壞，而不僅僅是冒險旅行。報道作者的判斷需要將其辨識為他的判斷。[R020]

The accurate role is attached officer and correspondent, with a publication outcome. It is not “Governor of the North-West Frontier” or “commander of the Malakand campaign.” This entry shows an important recurring pattern: Churchill both participated in events and helped shape how readers subsequently understood them. Those are two different forms of influence, and they need two separate lines on an honest résumé.準確的角色是附屬官員和通訊員，並發表成果。它不是“西北邊境總督”或“馬拉坎德戰役指揮官”。這篇文章展示了一個重要的反覆出現的模式：邱吉爾既參與了事件，又幫助塑造了讀者隨後如何理解這些事件。這是兩種不同形式的影響力，他們需要在誠實的履歷上分開兩條線。

Assignment任務

While on leave from the British Army, Churchill travelled with fellow officer Reginald Barnes to observe Spanish military operations against Cuban insurgents. He also supplied reports to the Daily Graphic. He was not a British commander in a British war against Cuba, nor an officer leading the Cuban independence movement. The distinction between observer, correspondent and combatant matters here. 在英國陸軍休假期間，邱吉爾與同事雷金納德‧巴恩斯一起前往觀察西班牙針對古巴叛亂分子的軍事行動。他還向《每日畫報》提供報告。他不是英國對古巴戰爭中的英國指揮官，也不是領導古巴獨立運動的軍官。觀察員、通訊員和戰鬥員之間的區別在這裡很重要。[R018]

Reporting and exposure to war報道和接觸戰爭

The visit gave him early experience of campaigning and of writing for a readership at home. It also placed him beside an imperial army attempting to suppress an independence struggle. His observations must consequently be read as those of a young British officer with particular assumptions and access, not as a comprehensive Cuban account of the conflict. 古巴之行讓他初次接觸軍事行動，也練習為英國讀者撰寫報道；同時，他站在一支鎮壓獨立運動的帝國軍隊一方。因此，閱讀其觀察應考慮這名年輕英國軍官的立場與資訊來源，不能把它當成對古巴衝突的全面記述。[R018] [R015]

The American connection與美國的聯繫

The journey included a visit to the United States. Churchill’s early transatlantic experiences preceded the wartime alliance by decades, but acquaintance with America in 1895 should not be confused with any diplomatic authority. At this stage he was an army subaltern building contacts and experience, not an official British envoy. 這次旅程包括訪問美國。邱吉爾早期的跨大西洋經驗比戰時聯盟早了幾十年，但 1895 年與美國的相識不應與任何外交權威混為一談。在這個階段，他是一名建立聯繫和累積經驗的陸軍下屬，而不是英國官方特使。[R015]

Churchill was commissioned as a second lieutenant in the 4th Hussars on 20 February 1895. This was a cavalry commission, not command of a regiment. Scope of work工作範圍

The regiment’s posting to India brought him to Bangalore in 1896. Departure from the regular regiment離開正規部隊

His letter to the Duke of Marlborough of 24 January 1899 says he had submitted his papers and expected to cease being a soldier in three months. The Library of Congress dates his resignation to 1899. The resignation was a calculated move towards writing and politics, not dismissal for incompetence. Gallipoli · 1915加里波利 · 1915

Churchill strongly promoted the Dardanelles naval plan. The attack failed to open the straits, and the land campaign did not meet its objectives. Other leaders approved and carried out the operations, but his advocacy is central to the story. 邱吉爾大力推動達達尼爾海峽海軍方案。進攻未能打通海峽，陸上戰役亦未達目標。其他領導人也批准及執行了行動，但邱吉爾的倡議是事件的重要一環。Study the campaign ↗了解這場戰役 ↗

Bengal famine · 1943–44孟加拉飢荒 · 1943–44

Millions died from hunger and disease amid wartime disruption, price pressures, unequal access to food and failures of relief. Churchill’s government bore responsibility for imperial shipping and supply decisions. His April 1944 appeal for American shipping shows a late request for help; it does not establish that relief was prompt or sufficient. 戰時供應受阻、物價上漲、食物分配不公及救濟不足，令數百萬人死於飢餓和疾病。邱吉爾政府須為帝國的船運與物資分配決策負責。他在1944年4月向美國請求船舶運送糧食，顯示求援甚遲；這不能證明先前的救濟及時或充足。Scholarly overview ↗學術研究概覽 ↗ · Contemporary message ↗當時的外交電文 ↗

Kenya emergency · from 1952肯尼亞緊急狀態·1952 年起

The emergency began during Churchill’s second premiership. The British government later acknowledged torture and ill-treatment by the colonial administration. Churchill’s political responsibility as prime minister should be distinguished from claims that he personally ordered each abuse. The emergency continued after he left office. 肯尼亞緊急狀態始於邱吉爾第二段首相任期。英國政府其後承認殖民當局曾施行酷刑與虐待。應區分邱吉爾作為首相的政治責任與「他親自下令每一宗虐待」的說法。他卸任後，緊急狀態仍然持續。UK Government statement ↗英國政府聲明 ↗

Iran · 1953伊朗 · 1953

His government supported Anglo-American efforts against Prime Minister Mohammad Mosaddegh. Diplomatic documents record Churchill’s message to the Shah. They do not justify saying that he directed every stage of the operation, particularly during his illness that summer. 邱吉爾政府支持英美兩國推翻伊朗首相穆罕默德·摩薩台的行動。外交文件記錄了他向伊朗國王傳達的訊息；但這不足以證明他親自指揮行動的每一步，尤其是考慮到他當年夏天患病。Diplomatic record ↗外交紀錄 ↗
