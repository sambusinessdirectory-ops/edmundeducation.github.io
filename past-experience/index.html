About

The résumé version

“Career politician with experience in naval strategy, coalition leadership, public speaking, and returning to a previous role after a six-year gap. Comfortable under pressure. Less comfortable with constructive feedback.”

Churchill served as UK prime minister in 1940–45 and 1951–55. Before that, his unusually long political career included the Board of Trade, Home Office, Admiralty, and Treasury. He also wrote extensively, winning the 1953 Nobel Prize in Literature.

Beyond the résumé

A career profile cannot be the whole history.

Churchill’s wartime leadership and rhetoric are central to his legacy. So are the failed Gallipoli campaign, his imperial politics, and the British government’s inadequate response to the 1943 Bengal famine. The famine affected millions; it should not be reduced to a workplace punchline.
