# Golden SOP — illustrated sentence journey realms

Version: 2026-09-12. Applies to additions and revisions of the sentence-structure learning map.

## 1. Lock the normal view first

Design the complete focal composition inside the original 1600-unit map width. Preserve original lesson and caption sizes. A 3200-unit painting may extend 800 units on either side, but those extensions contain quiet, basic scenery. Do not shrink the lessons or require zooming out to discover essential scene elements.

Render the actual application at standard zoom before polishing close-up details. Check the full moon, lamps, animal, house or castle, flags, moving objects and requested effects inside that display range. Compose for the map viewport rather than the browser's entire window. Keep enough margin for motion extrema and the soft realm boundary.

## 2. Establish a clear, spacious painting

Use the supplied reference for style, landmarks and atmosphere. Keep broad painted shapes and a restrained level of detail. Reject grainy microtextures, repeated tiny foliage, crowded borders and noisy generated artifacts. Use clouds where requested instead of merely whitening or blurring pillows.

Keep a zen garden recognisably planted, with grass/moss and stone paving around contained sand beds. Keep extended scenery visually quiet without turning the whole garden into sand.

For elements that will move, obtain a clean background with those elements absent. Use separate transparent artwork or code-native geometry. Never leave the original object painted underneath a moving duplicate. Inspect alpha edges against a contrasting background.

Save exact generation prompts, master dimensions, final asset paths and any authorized cleanup. Use the built-in image tool for raster creation/edits; use native geometry for suitable vector or three-dimensional work.

## 3. Lay out the learning journey

Platforms do not require a table or repeated rows. A winding trail may follow pillow slopes or other terrain when requested. Preserve lesson order and real IDs. Give captions, progress badges, companions and saved flags room to breathe.

Theme the platform surface to the realm: wood in autumn, stone in the garden, soft cushions in the dream. Use broad, soft contact shadows consistent with the painting. Avoid bright rigid coin rims and heavy extrusion.

Do not add connecting strips between realms. Realm clouds remain soft, blurred and penetrable. Existing terrain rules still govern water and bridges. An internal winding dream trail does not authorize roads in the other realms.

## 4. Make gentle motion visible

Judge motion at standard zoom. A changing transform in developer tools is not sufficient evidence that a person can see it.

Dream reference settings: hanging stars ±11 units, moon ±9°, flags with roughly 10 source pixels of total tip travel, and sparse 3–6 unit twinkles reaching full opacity. These are starting points; review actual screen displacement before accepting them. Stagger phases and periods so the whole scene does not move in sync.

Keep anchored subjects anchored. Move the rabbit's ears and eyelids, the cat's tail, and the bear's belly without drifting their bodies or faces. Give koi continuous swimming paths, local body flex and blinks. Alternate lotus rotation directions. Keep falling leaves sparse, light bloom restrained, and doorway cooking smoke visibly emerging from inside the door.

Use continuous deformation or real geometry. Do not crossfade complete poses, accumulate old frames, produce ghost trails, or flatten a vehicle as it turns. The train must be large enough to recognise at normal zoom, follow its rails continuously, and retain an upright body with wheels meeting the track.

Pause with the map, respect reduced motion, and release canvases/GPU resources during teardown. A static compatibility image is acceptable when a browser lacks the required graphics capability.

## 5. Verify in the real map

1. Capture standard-view, whole-realm and closer motion reviews. Inspect the default view before zoomed screenshots.
2. Check actual label/platform intersections, clipping, intended scenery margins and every moving object's extreme pose.
3. Measure visible displacement and brightness across multiple phases. Check changing cloud/belly pixels and stationary face/wall pixels. Inspect several train headings and the loop boundary.
4. Verify real lesson links, account isolation, progress ownership, companions, the single saved location, and travel across cloud boundaries. Run all route pairs when coordinates change.
5. Verify reduced motion and desktop, tablet and phone views. A smaller viewport may pan; it must not cause page overflow or make the essential lesson controls unusable.
6. Re-test affected behavior after meaningful changes. Preserve successful unrelated realms.

## 6. Publish the reviewed result

Review the scoped diff, retain asset provenance and dependency licenses, and update cache versions for changed modules/styles. Fetch the remote branch and publish by a normal fast-forward push. Do not overwrite other work.

Wait for repository Pages checks, then verify the canonical release commit and hashes of changed modules and assets. Report the live result with a usable preview and identify any remaining limitation. Do not report publication complete based only on a local screenshot or a successful push.
