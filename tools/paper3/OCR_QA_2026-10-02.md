# Paper 3 archive OCR and layout check — 2 October 2026

Scope: 14 imported B1/B2 readers, 208 displayed pages. This is a **triage audit**, not a claim that every word on every page has been manually proofread or that every page has been reconstructed to the visual standard of the 2025 B2 Wellness Month reader. The original scan remains visible alongside the selectable text.

## Method

- Enumerated all 208 pages and checked reader structure, text presence, source-page mapping, and translation files.
- Compared a second OCR pass against published text. Agreement is only a screening aid: two OCR passes can agree on the same error.
- Visually compared flagged pages with their original scans. Rebuilt confirmed tables, charts, calendars, and forms as structured HTML/CSS, and checked desktop and narrow-screen rendering.
- Corrected mark values only where the original scan could be read. Added grouped Chinese translations to reconstructed pages.

## Results

- **9 structured page reconstructions checked:** 2012 B1 leaf 8 (book jacket), 2015 B1 leaf 9 (iTunes table and charts), 2016 B1 leaf 8 (survey/progress tables), 2018 B1 leaf 6 (questionnaire charts), 2020 B1 leaves 9–10 (Wiki page and calendar), 2021 B1 leaf 9 (blog, schedule and chart), 2021 B2 leaf 3 (note sheet), and 2022 B1 leaf 8 (inventory). The 2015 charts reproduce categories and approximate visual proportions from the scan; they do not certify exact plotted values absent from the source labels.
- **9 manually corrected text pages:** the four other text corrections recorded in the earlier audit (2019 B1 leaf 13, 2021 B1 leaf 8, 2021 B2 leaves 2 and 4), plus five further QAB mark corrections: 2014 B1 leaf 13, 2014 B2 leaf 14, 2015 B1 leaf 12, and 2016 B1 leaves 10 and 12. The original scans show **18 marks**, where OCR had rendered `/8`. The 2021 B2 leaf 4 scan clips its right edge; a clearer source would be needed to guarantee every word.
- **1 partial reconstruction with missing source:** the supplied 2022 B1 Data File scan is blank at original page 3 (reader leaf 3). A separate text copy confirms its *Wish You Were Here* note-sheet headings, now displayed in a labeled partial layout. The scan area remains blank and the reader warns that full verification awaits the original page.
- The remaining pages passed structural checks and automated OCR comparison only. Smaller transcription, translation, and layout errors may still exist; the agreement score does not certify accuracy.
