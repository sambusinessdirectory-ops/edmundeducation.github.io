# Paper 3 archive OCR check — 2 October 2026

Scope: 14 newly imported B1/B2 readers, 208 displayed pages. This is a **triage audit**, not a claim that every page has been manually proofread or reconstructed to the visual standard of the 2025 B2 Wellness Month reader. The original scan remains visible alongside selectable text.

## Method

- Enumerated every displayed page and checked reader structure, text presence, source-page mapping and translation files.
- Ran a separate OCR pass on all 208 page images and compared tokens against published text. The agreement score in the CSV is a screening aid; two OCR passes can agree on the same mistake.
- Visually inspected the largest substantive discrepancies and several graphic-heavy pages against their scans. Corrected only text that could be read from the source scan.

## Findings

- **5 pages manually corrected/verified:** 2012 B1 Data File page 8 (book-jacket columns), 2021 B2 Data File pages 19 and 21 (reader leaves 2, 4), 2021 B1 Data File page 15 (leaf 8), and 2019 B1 QAB page 15 (leaf 13: 18 marks, not `/8`). The 2021 B2 page 19 correction was already deployed before this audit. On 2021 B2 page 21 the supplied scan clips its right edge; a clearer source is needed to guarantee every word.
- **8 confirmed layout defects remain:** 2015 B1 leaf 9 (iTunes table and charts); 2020 B1 leaf 9 (Wikipedia-style page); 2016 B1 leaf 8 (survey tables); 2018 B1 leaf 6 (charts); 2020 B1 leaf 10 (calendar); 2021 B1 leaf 9 (schedule and chart); 2021 B2 leaf 3 (note-taking form); 2022 B1 leaf 8 (inventory table). The 2012 B1 book-jacket text has also been corrected, but its visual layout still needs redrawing. The scans preserve these visual relationships, but the selectable text does not reproduce them faithfully.
- **1 unavailable source page:** 2022 B1 Data File original page 3 (reader leaf 3) is absent from the supplied PDF. The reader labels it as unavailable rather than inventing content.
- Other pages passed structural checks and received automated OCR comparison only; they still require human proofreading for smaller transcription/translation errors and layout fidelity.
