Services for newcomers

New to Hong Kong? We can provide the help you need. We offer the following services

新來香港？我們能提供你所需的協助，服務包括：

For Newcomers to Hong Kong
  • Written translation of formal documents from foreign country外國正式文件的書面翻譯
  • Interpreter service (e.g. for phone calls or for formal interviews)傳譯服務，例如電話或正式面試
  • Form filling service (e.g. forms from Education Bureau, HK taxes etc)協助填表，例如教育局或稅務表格
  • Child care services for working mothers為在職母親提供託兒服務
  • Introductions to local sports teams (adults and kids)介紹成人及兒童參加本地運動隊
  • Important contact numbers (e.g. for social services)重要聯絡電話，例如社會服務
  • Sightseeing guides for Kowloon and the Outlying Islands九龍及離島觀光指南
