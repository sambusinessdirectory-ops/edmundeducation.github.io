Subject: Jade Islands issues

To Whom it May Concern:

敬啟者：

I am writing on behalf of the Jade Islands Residents’ Concern Group. Let me start by saying how much we enjoy living on the Jade Islands. Dragon Peak Properties has done a fantastic job of providing an environmentally friendly place to live. The solar and wind technologies mean big savings on our electricity bills and the green roofs and balconies have been excellent for keeping us all cool in our increasingly hotter summers!

本人代表 Jade Islands 居民關注組來函。我們十分喜歡在此居住；DPP 提供環保環境，太陽能和風能技術節省電費，綠化屋頂與陽台亦使炎熱夏日更清涼。

However, not everything is perfect. We would like to draw your attention to two problems we feel very, very concerned about.

不過這裡並非十全十美。我們十分關注兩個問題。

First thing is the maintenance work on the underground rail link to the airport. My goodness, why does it have to be so noisy? AND it goes on ALL day and night and ALL through the weekend. It is driving us crazy! Please do something about it.

首先，通往機場的地下鐵路通道維修為何如此嘈吵？工程日夜不停，週末也繼續，令居民難以忍受，請採取措施。

As for the beaches — oh dear! Why oh why is there so much litter? Where is it all coming from?

至於海灘，為何有這麼多垃圾？垃圾究竟從何而來？

We all feel deeply embarrassed to show our friends and visitors our beaches. This is a terrible shame because the beaches are one of the best features of the Jade Islands. Come on DPP, you can do better than this!

我們都不敢帶親友參觀海灘，非常可惜，因為海灘是 Jade Islands 最好的特色之一。DPP 應做得更好。

We look forward to hearing your response and demand an explanation along with proposed solutions or a summary of action that you’ve taken on the issue.

我們期待收到回覆，並要求解釋、提出解決方案，或總結已採取的行動。

Yours

此致。

Mr Hannibal Chiu

Hannibal Chiu 先生
