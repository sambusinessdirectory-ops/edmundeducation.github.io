Museum Staff Manual

Section 4.5: Part-time Student Assistant Staff

4.5.1 Main Duties

Part-time assistants are permitted to do the following:

兼職助理可執行以下工作：

  • assist visitors
  • clean display items (after training)

協助訪客；接受培訓後清潔展品。

4.5.2 Working Hours

Assistants must not work more than 4 hours per day on any day.

助理每日工作不可超過四小時。
