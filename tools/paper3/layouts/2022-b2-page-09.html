Subject: Concerns over Fortune Phoenix Towers Lighting Display

To Whom It May Concern

致有關人士：

We are a group of concerned residents who live in Wan Yuen Estate. We are aware that Fortune Phoenix Towers intends to have your company install an outdoor lighting display measuring 50 metres by 300 metres. Plans that we have acquired also show a rotating cube on top of Fortune Phoenix Towers that will project images.

我們是 Wan Yuen Estate 的居民。Fortune Phoenix Towers 擬安裝由貴公司承辦的 50 米乘 300 米戶外燈光展示，天台並設有旋轉投影立方體。

As residents we hold two major concerns. The first is light pollution. Surely such a large display at night will make the night become day to any apartment that faces Fortune Phoenix Towers.

第一個憂慮是光污染：如此大型的夜間燈光或令面向大廈的住宅如同白晝。

Our second concern is about the safety of the rotating cube. We refer to the incident where a similar moving display on a building collapsed and destroyed a car in 2015. We have attached the relevant news story to jog your memory.

第二是旋轉立方體的安全；2015 年曾有類似裝置倒塌並壓毀汽車，附件為相關新聞。

We look forward to a prompt response to our very serious concerns.

期待你們盡快回應這些嚴重關注。

Yours faithfully,

此致

Mr Chan Kat Yi, Eric Chairperson, Wan Yuen Estate Residents’ Association
