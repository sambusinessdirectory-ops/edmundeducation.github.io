These are the three things that I’d like you to do in preparation for the Victoria Cup.

Special Arrangements Form

We need to complete the summary of the special arrangements for this year’s Victoria Cup to send to all players and agents. Please complete the attached Special Arrangements Form. Keep it brief.

我們需要完成今年 Victoria Cup 特別安排摘要，發給所有球員和經理人。請簡潔填妥附上的表格。

You can look at Dickson Tse’s WhatsApp message, the email exchange with Victor Laurent, the minutes from the Victoria Cup Committee meeting as well as your notes from the Zoom meeting.

你可參考 Dickson Tse 的 WhatsApp 訊息、與 Victor Laurent 的電郵往來、委員會會議紀錄及 Zoom 會議筆記。

Tennis Activity Day

Please write a short announcement about this year’s Tennis Activity Day. The announcement will be posted on our website so try to persuade the readers to apply. Please include the following information:

請為今年的 Tennis Activity Day 撰寫簡短公告，刊登在網站上，鼓勵讀者報名。請包括以下資料：

  • The purpose of the day
  • Where and when it will take place
  • Which tennis star will be there
  • What the main activities will be
  • How many participants will be selected
  • What people need to do to apply

活動目的、地點與時間、出席的網球明星、主要活動、入選人數及報名方法。

You can look at the email exchange with Victor Laurent, the feedback interview, the minutes from the Victoria Cup Committee meeting as well as your notes from the Zoom meeting.

你可參考與 Victor Laurent 的電郵、意見訪談、委員會會議紀錄及 Zoom 會議筆記。

Response to the Hong Kong Press & Journal article

The Hong Kong Press & Journal has published an article about the Victoria Cup. Please write a letter to the editor explaining how we will try to stop the littering and what TV options are available.

《Hong Kong Press & Journal》刊登了一篇關於 Victoria Cup 的文章。請致函編輯，解釋我們如何遏止亂拋垃圾，以及有哪些電視收看選擇。

You might also look at the job advertisement, our own blog website and the minutes of the committee meeting.

你亦可參考招聘廣告、我們的網誌和委員會會議紀錄。

Thanks again for your help.

再次感謝你的幫忙。
