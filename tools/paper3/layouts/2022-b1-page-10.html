Interview with Neon Master Rick Lai in Hong Kong Arts and Crafts Magazine

We spoke with Rick Lai, Hong Kong’s top lighting designer, who works for the award-winning Shiny Diamond Lighting Ltd.

我們訪問了香港頂尖燈光設計師 Rick Lai；他任職於屢獲殊榮的 Shiny Diamond Lighting Ltd。

Hong Kong Arts and Crafts:

What do you enjoy most about your work?

你最喜歡工作中的哪一部分？

Rick Lai:

There are many aspects to the job I enjoy but what I love most is taking a customer’s design and turning it into a neon sign. It’s quite a complicated process. Basically, in the planning stage, when we receive the design, we use a grid.

這份工作有許多我喜歡的部分，但我最喜歡把客人的設計變成霓虹燈牌。過程相當複雜；規劃時收到設計，我們會使用方格。

Hong Kong Arts and Crafts:

Why do you do that?

為甚麼？

Rick Lai:

We use it to decide how many glass tubes we need. That’s all before we start making the sign using a machine called the Glass Heater.

方格讓我們計算需要多少玻璃管，之後才用「玻璃加熱器」製作燈牌。

Hong Kong Arts and Crafts:

What does that do?

這機器有甚麼用？

Rick Lai:

We use it to bend the glass tubes. We then have another machine which cleans the glass tubes.

用來彎曲玻璃管；之後另一部機器會清潔玻璃管。

Hong Kong Arts and Crafts:

How does it get them clean?

它怎樣清潔？

Rick Lai:

It uses electricity. Shocking, right? Also, whenever we move the glass tubes, they can be broken very easily so we have to be very, very careful with them. That’s the boring part for me, because I like to work quickly! Finally, we are also very careful to test if the tubes are working before we move on to the display stage. One time, a customer returned a faulty sign. It was so embarrassing!

用電力清潔。很意外吧？玻璃管搬動時很容易破，所以必須非常小心。我喜歡做事快，這是我覺得無聊的部分。到展示階段前還要仔細測試玻璃管；有一次客人退回故障燈牌，真令人尷尬！

Hong Kong Arts and Crafts:

What is the future for lighting design in Hong Kong?

香港燈光設計的前景如何？

Rick Lai:

It’s very bright! We are looking for trainees at the moment, so if any of your readers are interested, please apply to be a trainee.

前景非常光明！我們正在招聘培訓生，有興趣的讀者可以申請。

Hong Kong Arts and Crafts:

How can they apply?

他們如何申請？

Rick Lai:

The instructions will be in recruitment emails we are sending out soon. One thing to pay attention to, though: applicants must send us 4 or 5 photos of their previous work. That way, we can really get a feel for their ability.

申請方法會寫在即將寄出的招聘電郵。請注意：申請者必須提交四至五張過往作品相片，讓我們了解其能力。
