先生：

I am writing about your request to film another cookery show at our private kitchen. The last time you filmed here your staff caused several problems. These problems must be addressed before you can film here again.

就貴方再次借用本店私人廚房拍攝烹飪節目一事，我必須指出上次拍攝造成多項問題；再次拍攝前須先處理。

  • Last time, you filled both our lifts with your big, heavy cameras. You took so many trips that people in the building had to either use the stairs or wait for a whole hour to use the lifts. This cannot happen again and I would like to know what you intend to do about it.

    上次大型攝影機佔用兩部升降機，其他人只好走樓梯或等候一小時。我想知道你們如何避免重演。

  • Also, your chef started a fire in the kitchen! When we called, your staff said not to worry as it was something minor. I’d really like to know what that was! It also seemed your staff members did not know what to do in case of a fire. This is unacceptable and I want to know how things will be better next time.

    你們的廚師曾在廚房引起火警，職員卻說只是小事，似乎也不知道如何應對。請解釋當時情況及下次如何改善。

Finally, this time we have decided to close the kitchen to customers on the day you film. We therefore ask for the additional sum of HK$10,000 to cover our costs.

這次拍攝當天我們決定暫停對顧客營業，因此要求額外一萬元補償成本。

We look forward to reading your reply.

期待回覆。

Ms Betty Ma
Golden Sun Tower Private Kitchen
