Email from Kerry Lam to Joey Wong

To: Joey Wong
From: Kerry Lam
Date: 12 April, 2014
Subject: Work to be done

Dear Joey,

Joey：

I’d like your help with a few things.

我有幾件事想請你幫忙。

We’re updating our website and we’ve decided to include an information page which has advice for young people or school leavers on becoming a veterinary nurse. Please can you write this page? You might want to include information about:

我們正在更新網站，計劃加入資訊頁，向年輕人或離校生介紹如何成為獸醫護士。請你撰寫，可涵蓋：

  • what veterinary nurses do and professional qualities they need
  • qualifications and future prospects
  • challenges

獸醫護士的職責和所需專業素質；資格與發展前景；工作挑戰。

Please give a title. You may want to start by checking your notes from the Pet Club podcast.

請加上標題。可先查看《Pet Club》播客的筆記。

Next month, we’re going to have an open day and I’m going to give a speech and I’d like you to write it for me. As we’re thinking about having a virtual pet page on our website, I’d like to talk about virtual pets. Please include a brief background on virtual pets and focus on how old people use virtual pets. I suggest that you start by looking at that ‘Inside the Virtual Jungle’ book.

下月我們將舉辦開放日；我會發表演說，請你代筆。網站也在考慮虛擬寵物專頁，因此我想談虛擬寵物。簡述背景，重點說明長者如何使用虛擬寵物；可參考《Inside the Virtual Jungle》。

Please write a letter to the editor of the Kowloon Herald in response to the letter from Mr. W. Squeers, which criticizes the Kowloon Exotic Pets Hospital. Make sure you mention all of the voluntary work we do for the community in Hong Kong. You might want to start by looking at Mr. Squeers’ letter in the Kowloon Herald.

請寫信給《Kowloon Herald》編輯，回應 W. Squeers 先生批評醫院的來信。務必提及我們為香港社區提供的所有義務服務。可先讀他的來信。

Thanks for your help.

謝謝幫忙。

Kerry
