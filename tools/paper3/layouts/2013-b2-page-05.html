Minutes from editorial meeting at Asia Life magazine

Editorial Meeting

Present
James Lee · Casey Wong · Mavis Lam · Vicki Swan
Date
14th March, 2013
Venue
meeting room, 6th Floor

1. Confirmation of minutes

3. Upcoming issue

Ideas for the next issue were discussed. Three topics were decided: unusual travel and tourism, buying a new car and the benefits of evening classes. The main focus would be on unusual travel and tourism.

會議決定下期雜誌的三個題目：非一般的旅遊、購買新車及夜間課程的好處；主題以非一般的旅遊為主。

4. Writers

JL agreed to contact Tony Kwok and Queenie Lau and ask them to contribute articles for publication. ML reminded the editorial team to follow the new 2013 guidelines for publication. CW reminded everyone that writers should be encouraged to submit 15-20 images per article, and that the editorial team would choose 5 for each article accepted for publication.

JL 將聯絡 Tony Kwok 和 Queenie Lau 邀稿。ML 提醒編輯組遵守 2013 年的新投稿指引。CW 提醒大家鼓勵作者每篇提交 15 至 20 張圖片，再由編輯組選出 5 張刊登。

5. Deadlines for articles

All agreed to set the deadline for 30th April.

各人同意截稿日期定為 4 月 30 日。

6. Payment

It was agreed that the payment to writers would be HKD 1000 per page. JL confirmed that this would include both text and pictures used.

稿酬每頁港幣 1,000 元，已包括採用的文字及圖片。

7. Next meeting

The next meeting date was decided as 6th May to give the team time to read through any submitted articles.

下次會議定於 5 月 6 日，讓編輯組有時間閱讀收到的稿件。

8. A. O. B.

VS reported that the word limit for articles in the next issue would be 900 words. All writers would be informed by email.

VS 說下期文章字數上限為 900 字；將以電郵通知所有作者。
