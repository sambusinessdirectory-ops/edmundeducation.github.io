Situation

You are Gehry Zhang and you work for a property developer called Dragon Peak Properties (DPP). Your boss is Norman Kwok, a publicity and marketing manager. DPP is currently working on two projects: the Jade Islands, which have recently been completed and the Golden Flower Island development, which is in the planning process.

你是 Gehry Zhang，在地產發展商 Dragon Peak Properties（DPP）工作。上司 Norman Kwok 負責宣傳及市場推廣。公司剛完成 Jade Islands，並在規劃 Golden Flower Island 項目。

You will now listen to a radio panel show called Business Matters with host Charlie Meadows. Appearing on the show are Norman Kwok and Ann Lam from the environmental group Green Treasure.

你將聆聽 Charlie Meadows 主持的《Business Matters》電台座談，嘉賓有 Norman Kwok 和環保組織 Green Treasure 的 Ann Lam。

Before the recording is played, you will have five minutes to study the Question-Answer Book and the Data File to familiarise yourself with the situation and the tasks.

錄音前有五分鐘閱讀問答冊與資料檔，熟悉情境及任務。

Complete the tasks by following the instructions in the Question-Answer Book and on the recording. You will find all the information you need in the Question-Answer Book, the Data File and on the recording. As you listen, you can make notes on page 3 of the Data File.

按問答冊及錄音指示完成任務。所需資料在問答冊、資料檔和錄音；聆聽時可在資料檔第 3 頁做筆記。

You now have five minutes to familiarise yourself with the Question-Answer Book and the Data File.

現在有五分鐘熟悉問答冊與資料檔。
