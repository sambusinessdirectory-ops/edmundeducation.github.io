Fact file

資料小檔案

Email to Plaza Hotel

給 Plaza Hotel 的電郵

Kim Ross at the Plaza Hotel has sent us an advertisement to put in the next issue, but there are some problems with it. Please email him and tell him what he needs to change in the advertisement. Look at the minutes from the last sales meeting and the copy of the advertisement — the sales team has made some notes on it. Remind him about the submission deadline and the first payment date.

Plaza Hotel 的 Kim Ross 寄來下期廣告，但有幾個問題。請參考上次銷售會議紀錄及廣告上的批註，電郵告訴他需要修改的地方，並提醒交稿及首期付款日期。

Short feature article

短篇專題文章

We want to include a short feature article to introduce unusual hotels and encourage people to try them.

我們想刊登短篇專題文章，介紹特色酒店並鼓勵讀者試住。

Please can you write the article and try to include the following information:

請在文章加入：

A suitable title for the article (don’t just call it ‘Unusual Hotels’); an interesting opening and closing; a description of three types of unusual hotels, where they are and why people like staying in them.

一個恰當而非單純「特色酒店」的標題；吸引人的開頭和結尾；描述三類特色酒店、所在地及旅客喜歡的原因。

You can look at the webpage for the Beacon Hotel, the travel bloggers webpage and I’ve also found some travel adverts which might help. Remember to take a look at your notes from the ‘Travel Report’ podcast too.

可參考 Beacon Hotel 網頁、旅遊網誌、旅遊廣告和《Travel Report》筆記。

Let me know when you’re done.
