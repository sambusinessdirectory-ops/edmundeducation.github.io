Email from Angelina Dai to Malcolm Parker

To: Malcolm Parker
From: Angelina Dai
Date: April 1 2015
Subject: Update

Hi Malcolm

Malcolm 你好：

Good to hear from you again. Hope the weather is better in London.

很高興再收到你的消息，希望倫敦天氣好一些。

Anyway, I’ve managed to find you a room for holding the interview (room 829). It’s excellent for conducting interviews and it will be available on 19th May (Tuesday) from 2:00pm to 5:00pm. I will personally make sure that no more than 4 people are allowed in to do the interview as you requested.

我找到適合訪問的 829 號房，5 月 19 日星期二下午 2 至 5 時可使用。我會按你的要求確保訪問者不超過四人。

Just to warn you that the hotel has some really tough rules about dress. Basically, the hotel will tell anyone who isn’t wearing smart clothes to leave. And sandals? Absolutely not!

酒店有嚴格衣着規定：衣着不整者會被請離，亦絕不能穿涼鞋。

Also, our helicopter will be ready at 5.30pm at Atlantic Plaza on 18th May (Monday). I am sure Bubblegum will enjoy the flight back to the hotel.

直升機將於 5 月 18 日星期一下午 5:30 在 Atlantic Plaza 準備好，接樂隊返回酒店。

Looking forward to another drink (or three!) with you soon.

期待很快再跟你喝幾杯。

Angelina
General Manager
Paradise Gardens Hotel

Letter from pupils at St. Godfrey’s Catholic School to Malcolm Parker

ST. GODFREY’S CATHOLIC SCHOOL

March 20, 2015

Dear Mr. Parker,

Parker 先生：

We are students at St. Godfrey’s Catholic School and we are really BIG fans of Bubblegum. We think their music is really great to listen to and their image is really cool and trendy.

我們是 St. Godfrey’s Catholic School 的學生，也是 Bubblegum 的忠實歌迷，很喜歡他們的音樂和形象。

We would be so honoured if they could do an interview with some of our senior students from our Media Club. What we would like to know is:

如果他們能接受媒體學會高年級學生的訪問，我們會非常榮幸。我們想知道：

  • The time, place and length of time for the interview. (Please note that due to school activities during the week Bubblegum are in Hong Kong, we will only be free after 4pm)

    訪問的時間、地點和長度。由於校內活動，我們只在下午 4 時後有空。

I know this is short notice and the band will be really busy in Hong Kong, but if you could find any time in their schedule to have an interview with us then that would be amazing.

我知道提出得很倉促，樂隊訪港也很忙，但若能安排任何訪問時間就太好了。

Please tell Bubblegum how much we love them!

請向 Bubblegum 轉達我們的喜愛！

Yours sincerely,
Bonnie Ng
Chairperson of the Media Club
St. Godfrey’s Catholic School
