Article from Hong Kong Real Estate Monthly

DRAGON PEAK MAKES DEVELOPERS GREEN - WITH ENVY

The results for the Greenest Development Award in the HKSAR were released by the Tai Shing Conservation Institute earlier this month (see below). Dragon Peak’s win should come as no surprise given the amount the company has spent on environmentally friendly building technologies over recent years (see chart).

本月 Tai Shing Conservation Institute 公布香港最環保發展項目獎；Dragon Peak 多年投資環保建築技術，因此獲獎並不意外。

This commitment to environmental protection is not only confined to Hong Kong. It was announced that the developer has recently donated a “substantial sum of money” to the Save the Great Barrier Reef in Australia and “will do all we can to preserve the coral in Australia for future generations to witness and to marvel at”.

公司也在香港以外支持環保，最近向澳洲大堡礁保育組織捐出可觀款項，表示會盡力保存珊瑚供後代欣賞。

Greenest Housing Development Awards 2016

  1. 1st Prize — Forest of Dreams Estate (Dragon Peak Properties)
  2. 2nd Prize — 21B Seashell Cove Avenue (Shek Gaan Developments)
  3. 3rd Prize — Enchanted Bay (Gault & Rand Land)
  4. 4th Prize — 88 Fecund Gardens Drive (Dark Matter Solutions)
