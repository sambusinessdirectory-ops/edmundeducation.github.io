Email from Malcolm Parker to Starsky Chung

To: Starsky Chung
From: Malcolm Parker
Date: 14 April 2015
Subject: Things to do

Dear Starsky

親愛的 Starsky：

I need you to do a few things before Bubblegum arrive in Hong Kong in May.

Bubblegum 五月來港前，我需要你完成幾件事。

Write an email to Ultimatum Products and tell them which products we’d like made. Make sure you ask them for a quote. I’ll send you the Ultimatum Products’ information page so you know what to include. You could have a look at the Bubblegum MyFace page that we set up and then select the items which are popular with the fans and follow their suggestions in the comments section. Don’t forget what was said in the Skype conference too.

請電郵 Ultimatum Products，告訴他們希望製作哪些產品，並索取報價。我會傳給你廠商的資料頁。你也可參考 Bubblegum 的 MyFace 頁面，根據粉絲投票及留言選擇受歡迎的產品；別忘了 Skype 會議內容。

Next, I read Ms. Ma’s response to your enquiry about filming the video on 19th May at The Peak Sky Deck. Can you email her to address her questions and concerns to help persuade her to grant our request?

我已讀過 Ma 女士回覆你有關 5 月 19 日在 The Peak Sky Deck 拍攝的查詢。請電郵回答她的疑問及顧慮，說服她批准。

We also have to deal with all the negative publicity surrounding Mark Nelson over that incident with the photographer from Gossip Week. Can you write a short article for the Hong Kong fans’ website? Start with a brief summary of the incident, then respond to what Gossip Week claims happened. Finish the article by briefly describing similar incidents that have happened to other celebrities. And, please don’t quote directly. We want this article to be factual, not “he said, she said”.

我們還需處理 Mark Nelson 與《Gossip Week》攝影師事件引起的負面宣傳。請為香港粉絲網站寫短文：先簡述事件，再回應雜誌的指稱，最後簡述其他名人遭遇的類似事件。不要直接引述；文章應以事實為主。

Malcolm
