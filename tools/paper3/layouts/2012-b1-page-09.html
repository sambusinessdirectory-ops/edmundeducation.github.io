An Immigrant’s Song

Lionel Chan

Publisher:
Kowloon University Press
Published:
2012
ISBN:
PB 0-916790-30-2

Comments from Reader Reviews

Loved it. Specially the stories about people from India, Colombia and Cameroon ;-).

很喜歡，尤其是印度、哥倫比亞和喀麥隆人物的故事。

Lionel sends his daughter to our local school in Tai Po. His wife is from Sri Lanka. He writes about immigration because he lives it.

Lionel 的女兒在大埔本地學校就讀，太太來自斯里蘭卡；他親身經歷移居生活，所以寫移民故事。

It’s a good book, we can learn about communication in Hong Kong from these stories! And did you know that he’s giving all the profits from the sales of this book to Cultural Commons? So buy it!

這本書讓我們了解香港的溝通。他會把全書收益捐給 Cultural Commons。

This is not as interesting as his last book, Talk to me. I give it a two:

不如他的前作《Talk to me》有趣；我給兩分。

I think it’s a good book – it’s only $100 HK. Read it.

我認為這是好書，只售港幣一百元，值得一讀。
