Instructors’ feedback from last year’s Planet Tarkov Problem-Solving Activity

Instructors’ feedbackSunita Chawla’s Comments
Instructor 1

“Please, please, please can we simplify the situation? Rather than all that complicated stuff about heading to a new colony on LV-426 and being hit by a comet, let’s just start by telling them they crashed on Tarkov. Also make the objective of the activity much much clearer. So, forget about the repairs. It would be much better, in my opinion, if they needed to survive for a year until they were rescued.”

簡化情境：直接說他們墜落 Tarkov；目標改成撐過一年等待救援。

These all seem sensible. I would agree to adopt all of these.

同意全部採納。

Instructor 2

“Fun to teach and most kids enjoyed it, but TBH there were a lot of kids not doing anything. We have to reduce the group size down from ten - I’d say to 5 - and give the children more than just 30 mins. to solve the problem. How about 2 hours overall - one hour to solve the problem, one hour for the groups to present to the other groups? I would also say that we should increase the amount of Physics. Make it like a real Science class so that the kids can learn something useful instead of just chatting.”

把每組十人減為五人，活動延長至兩小時；另建議加入更多物理知識。

Agree to adopt her suggestions regarding group size and time. Totally disagree with the point about Science. This is NOT school.

同意人數及時間調整，但不要把活動變成科學課。

Instructor 3

“Went well more or less. But could you make a 3D model of Tarkov and the spaceship so that kids can see it? I like the fact that they arrive on Tarkov in spring - this makes good sense, since it would give the crew time to actually do something before the weather turns extreme. I found a lot of interesting stuff on Tarkov and the science behind why the weather is so extreme on the NASA webpage. It really is fascinating. I think it would be good to add to the introduction.”

建議做 3D 模型；保留春天抵達；另加入更多科學背景。

The 3D model is a good idea but we have no budget. So no. And, again, no, I do not want to turn this into a Science class.

3D 模型無預算，亦不想變成科學課。

Instructor 4

“Need to get across just how extreme summer and winter on Tarkov are. Some kids don’t know how hot it is. One group even thought that all they would need is more shorts and T-shirts in Tarkov’s summer!!! Surely, we can add some exciting facts about the winter and summer.”

須讓學生明白 Tarkov 夏冬兩季有多極端，加入有趣事實。

Yes, agree. We can ask the astronomer Marvin Adams. He knows lots of amazing stuff about Tarkov.

同意，請天文學家 Marvin Adams 提供資料。
