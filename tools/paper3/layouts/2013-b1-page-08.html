Minutes from sales meeting at Asia Life magazine

Sales Meeting

Present:
James Lee · Casey Wong · Kai Lam · Rosie Tsang · Vicki Swan
Date:
3 April, 2013
Venue:
meeting room, 6th Floor

1. Confirmation of minutes

1. 確認會議紀錄

2. Matters arising

2. 跟進事項

3. Upcoming issue

3. 下期雜誌

JL reported that one of the main focuses for the next issue would be unusual travel and tourism.

JL 說下期的一個主要題材是特色旅遊。

4. Sales / advertising

4. 銷售／廣告

KL suggested contacting local travel agents and hotels to ask them if they wanted to advertise. CW asked him to remind advertisers to include the business address and phone number in the advertisement. KL reminded the sales team that all adverts needed the advertiser’s company registration number.

KL 建議聯絡本地旅行社和酒店招攬廣告。CW 要求提醒廣告客戶列出營業地址和電話；KL 指所有廣告也須列明公司註冊號碼。

5. Deadlines for advertisements

5. 廣告截稿日期

All agreed to set the deadline for 30th April.

同意截稿日期為 4 月 30 日。

6. Payment

6. 付款

It was agreed that the payment for all advertisements should be 50% one week before the issue was published and the remaining 50% afterward.

廣告費在出版前一週付一半，餘款在出版後支付。

7. Next meeting

7. 下次會議

The next meeting date was set for 10th May to give the team time to check submitted advertisements.

下次會議定於 5 月 10 日，讓團隊有時間核對廣告。

8. A.O.B.

8. 其他事項

VS reported that the word limit for half-page advertisements in the next issue would be 50–70 words. All advertisers would be informed by email.

VS 說下期半版廣告字數限 50 至 70 字，將以電郵通知所有客戶。
