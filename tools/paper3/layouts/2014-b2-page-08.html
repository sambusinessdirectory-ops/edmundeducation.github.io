Excerpt from the Elderly News magazine

Talking about the digital generation?

After all the fuss about Generation Y and the numerous reports in the media about the older generation not being able to do the simple things online, we asked you, our readers, to get in touch and tell us what you are doing online. To prove a point, and to provide a little challenge, we asked you to contact us — by SMS! Here are some samples from the hundreds of text messages we received. Congratulations to everyone who contacted us. Just shows that us oldies can still learn new tricks!

傳媒常說長者連簡單網上操作也做不到，因此我們請讀者透過短訊告訴大家如何使用網絡。以下選出數百則回覆中的幾則，證明長者也能學新事物。

I do everything online now — the shopping, talking to my grandchildren, my banking. I wish I’d had all of this when I was younger.

我現在上網購物、與孫兒聊天、處理銀行事務。真希望年輕時已有這些。

Eileen, 76, London

I’ve built up a pretty extensive breeding exchange programme of virtual birds online. Although I don’t trade them for money, I swap them for other birds I haven’t got.

我建立了龐大的網上虛擬雀鳥繁殖交換計劃。雖不賣錢，但會交換未擁有的雀鳥。

Kam, 80, Hong Kong

I’ve used the Internet to trace my family tree back four hundred years and found cousins I didn’t know I had on the other side of the world. We’re now in regular touch — all online!

我在網上追溯四百年家族史，找到身處世界另一端、從未知道的表親；如今定期網上聯絡。

Patrice, 66, France

Me and my friends have got about 6 online accounts each with different virtual pet sites and we breed virtual cats. We’re selling them to other online breeders. I tell you, we can make about 50 dollars a month each — good money if you’re retired.

我和朋友各有約六個不同虛擬寵物網站帳戶，繁殖虛擬貓再賣給其他玩家。每人每月約賺 50 美元，對退休人士不錯。

Bill, 69, Chicago

I keep virtual horses online with my husband. We’ve got a virtual ranch called Ponderosa. We’re the oldest virtual horse whisperers on the web!

我和丈夫養虛擬馬，還有一座名叫 Ponderosa 的虛擬牧場。我們大概是網上最年長的「馬語者」！

Winnie, 72, Kansas
