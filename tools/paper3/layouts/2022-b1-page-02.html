Situation

You are Nico Lin. You work for Shiny Diamond Lighting Ltd, a company that designs, makes and sells neon lighting products and services. Your work involves writing and editing texts for company use. Your boss, Floyd Mann, has asked you to do some tasks.

你是 Nico Lin，在設計、製造和銷售霓虹燈產品及服務的 Shiny Diamond Lighting Ltd 工作，負責撰寫和編輯公司文字。上司 Floyd Mann 指派你一些工作。

You will listen to a podcast of Wish You Were Here, an arts and culture radio programme on Hong Kong Radio. On the podcast you will hear Julia Din interview Pris Fong, owner of Shiny Diamond Lighting Ltd, and her chief designer and neon artist Rick Lai.

你將聆聽香港電台文化藝術節目《Wish You Were Here》的播客。主持 Julia Din 訪問公司東主 Pris Fong、首席設計師兼霓虹藝術家 Rick Lai。

Before the recording is played, you will have five minutes to study the Question-Answer Book and the Data File to familiarise yourself with the situation and the tasks.

錄音前有五分鐘閱讀問答冊和資料檔，熟悉情境與任務。

Complete the tasks by following the instructions in the Question-Answer Book and on the recording. You will find all the information you need in the Question-Answer Book, the Data File and on the recording. As you listen you can make notes on page 3 of the Data File.

請依問答冊及錄音指示完成任務。所有所需資料都在問答冊、資料檔和錄音中；可在資料檔第 3 頁記筆記。

You now have five minutes to familiarise yourself with the Question-Answer Book and the Data File.

現在有五分鐘熟悉問答冊和資料檔。
