親愛的 Nicky：

I've got three things for you to do.

有三件事想請你做。

We're going to carry out an online survey about barriers to communication in Hong Kong. Please write an introductory paragraph for the survey. Include:

我們將進行網上問卷，了解香港的溝通障礙。請為問卷寫引言，包括：

- what a barrier is - barriers related to the Hong Kong way of life - the effects of each barrier or how people feel as a result

甚麼是障礙；與香港生活方式相關的障礙；每種障礙的影響或當事人的感受。

Don't mention barriers related to body language as we did that in another survey. Have a look at that 'Living in Hong Kong' newsletter article as a starting point.

不要談身體語言障礙，因為另一份問卷已處理。先參考《Living in Hong Kong》通訊文章。

We want to start a new advice page on our website focusing on a different area of communication each month. This month's focus will be on body language. Use the website template to:

我們將在網站開設每月探討不同溝通範疇的建議頁，本月主題是身體語言。用網站範本：

- explain the problems associated with each aspect of body language shown - give advice about body language related to each

解釋所示每種身體語言帶來的問題，並分別提出建議。

Take a look at the extract from Isobel Tait's article 'A Review of Research into Body Language' as a starting point.

先參考 Isobel Tait 的《A Review of Research into Body Language》節錄。

Fairfax Secondary School has asked us to write a short article for their school newsletter. It's a special edition for their Multi-Cultural Awareness Week. They want us to write about the practical difficulties faced by people from different countries who come to live in Hong Kong and the help available from the community in Hong Kong.

Fairfax Secondary School 邀請我們為多元文化認識週特刊寫短文，介紹不同國籍人士來港後遇到的實際困難，以及香港社區提供的援助。

Please write the article. I found some statistics in one of Lionel Chan's research articles. Use that as your starting point. We've only been given space for 200 words so make sure it's about that length.

請撰文約二百字。我找到 Lionel Chan 研究文章中的一些統計數據，可先作參考。

Remember that your notes from the Bookmark podcast will be useful for all of these.

《Bookmark》播客的筆記也會對所有任務有用。

Thanks for your help. Best Manjula

謝謝你的幫忙。Manjula
