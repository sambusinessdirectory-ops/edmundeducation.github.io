How to: Film & Video Competitions

Are you organizing a film or video competition? It’s difficult isn’t it? Not to worry, just follow our tips and you will be guaranteed success!

你在籌辦電影或短片比賽嗎？雖然不容易，照以下提示做就能更順利。

Posters

  • The name of your organization or society must be really big so people know who you are. After all you want to promote yourself!

    組織或學會名稱應該非常顯眼，讓人知道主辦者是誰。

  • The title of your video competition should always be underlined.

    短片比賽的標題應加底線。

  • Make sure the website of your organization or society is on your poster. How will they find out all the details if you don’t?

    海報須列出組織或學會的網站，方便讀者查閱詳情。

  • Include any special requirements you have for your films or videos (e.g. how long it should be).

    列出影片的特別要求，例如片長。

  • Have you included what your first prize will be (if you have one)? State that clearly somewhere on the poster.

    如設有冠軍獎品，應在海報上清楚列明。

Rules for Youth by Youth Short Video Competition

The filmmaker should:

  • Attend school in Hong Kong

    在香港就讀

  • Be under 18

    未滿 18 歲

Length of videos:

  • Between 2 and 5 minutes

    片長為 2 至 5 分鐘

Content:

  • About young people, i.e. under 18 (NOT adults)

    內容關於 18 歲以下的年輕人，而非成年人

  • Can be in Cantonese, English or Putonghua

    可使用廣東話、英文或普通話

  • No swearing or foul language or other offensive content

    不得有粗言穢語或其他冒犯性內容

  • Music must be copyright free (i.e. Don’t use a song or music by a famous artist or pop band)

    音樂須無版權限制；不可使用著名歌手或流行樂隊的歌曲

First Prize:

  • Cubrix Video Camera model T-1000

    冠軍獎品：Cubrix T-1000 型號攝錄機
