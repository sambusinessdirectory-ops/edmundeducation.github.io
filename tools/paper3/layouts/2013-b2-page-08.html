Transcript excerpt of TV travel show See the World

[Ceci Yip video clip – extract 1]

We’ve all heard of student gap years, you know, where students take a year off and travel and that. Some go to places like South America and live with the natives – even for like up to a year. But did you know that there are more people going to live with native tribes in countries in Africa – but only for a few weeks? Welcome to a really new kind of culture tourism. I’m in Kenya and I’m going to a small village called Sauri, to find out what the impact might be on local people...

我們都聽過學生休學一年四處旅遊，有些人甚至到南美洲與原住民同住一年。如今也有人前往非洲部落居住數星期。我在肯尼亞的 Sauri 村，想了解這種新文化旅遊對當地人的影響。

[Ceci Yip video clip – extract 2]

I’ve got with me now Kemi – Kemi, you’re a teacher here in Sauri, what do you think about the effect of tourism on local people?

我現在和 Sauri 的教師 Kemi 一起。你認為旅遊對當地居民有甚麼影響？

Kemi

I think generally Ceci that it’s a good thing. I’d say positive for Sauri on the whole.

整體來說是好事；對 Sauri 大致有正面影響。

Ceci

Really? Well, that’s encouraging. How do you mean positive?

真的嗎？這很令人鼓舞。正面影響體現在哪些方面？

Kemi

Well, for example, in Sauri people are starting to share ideas with tourists.

例如 Sauri 的居民開始與旅客交流想法。

Ceci

Oh really? You mean sharing practical ideas?

你是說分享實用知識嗎？

Kemi

Yes, last year a young man from Turkey – Yusuf – came to the village. And helped us create a village webpage. So we have the Sauri village homepage on the Internet. It’s got a lot of information about the village that visitors can read.

對。去年一位來自土耳其的年輕人 Yusuf 來村裡，協助建立 Sauri 村網站，讓訪客在線上了解村莊。

Ceci

I heard that there was a writer who came to the village...

我聽說有位作家也來過村裡……

Kemi

Yes, yes, a very nice lady called Mrs. Jessica Glasser. We have a great tradition of story-telling and together with the local people Mrs. Glasser wrote down some of the stories and helped us to get those published. So that anybody who reads the book learns about our traditions.

對，Jessica Glasser 女士很友善。村裡有深厚的說故事傳統；她與居民一起把故事記錄並出版，讓讀者認識我們的傳統。

Ceci

So it really does sound like tourism is helping you keep your culture alive. In a real way.

聽起來旅遊確實有助你們以實際方式保存文化。
