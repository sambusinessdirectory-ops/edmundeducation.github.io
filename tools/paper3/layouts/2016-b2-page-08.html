Transcript of interview with Wang Chung Village resident

https://hkshm.org/researchproject14

Dr Jones

Wang Chung Village was such a famous village. I have also heard many things about its pearl rice. Can you tell me any more about this?

王涌村很有名，我也聽過珍珠米。你能多說一點嗎？

Mrs Wang

Well, I remember my father would have buyers for this rice from all over China. But it wasn’t just him. All the farmers in our village did this. Our rice was in huge demand then, you know. Pearl rice was not cheap. It was a luxury even for rich families.

我父親的珍珠米吸引全中國買家；村中其他農民也一樣。當年需求很大，連富裕家庭也覺得昂貴。

Dr Jones

Sadly, of course, we can’t see the village anymore, but can you describe what it was like?

可惜村莊已不在，你能描述它原來的樣子嗎？

Mrs Wang

Ah well, our village was so beautiful. Tourists from the city would come at the weekend to see the rice fields.

村莊很美，市區遊客週末會來看稻田。

Dr Jones

Yes, the “S”-shaped fields. I’ve some photos of them. Truly beautiful.

對，S 形的田。我有些照片，真美。

Mrs Wang

I remember the great flood of Wang Chung Village, you know.

我記得王涌村的大水災。

Dr Jones

Really? Typhoon Ellen in 1983?

是 1983 年的颱風愛倫嗎？

Mrs Wang

I think so. The two girls who survived on the kitchen cupboard? I knew them. Good little girls. Very pious. Their mother told me about what happened.

大概是。兩個靠廚櫃生還的女孩，我認識她們。她們的母親告訴我經過。

Dr Jones

Did she tell you about the markings on the cupboard door?

她有提到廚櫃門上的刻字嗎？

Mrs Wang

Yes. A prayer, a prayer to Guanyin.

有，是向觀音祈禱的字。

Dr Jones

Well, I’ll be. The Goddess of Mercy. They carved it on the right door of the cupboard?

她們刻在右門嗎？

Mrs Wang

No, she told me it was the left. They carved it when they were waiting to be rescued.

不是右門，是左門；她們等待救援時刻上的。

Dr Jones

Oh really? My mistake then. Now, after 1990 you moved to your present home in Fanling. Was that a big change?

原來如此。我記錯了。1990 年後你搬到粉嶺，變化很大嗎？

Mrs Wang

Well, of course there were some things from before that I missed a lot. Especially to begin with. The first few years I found it quite difficult to adapt to city life. But Fanling is a lovely place. There’s so much I can do here. I’m a member of the residents’ association on the estate. I am really involved in that and I like helping others on the estate. You might not believe it but I’m 79 this year. But there are others younger than me that need help so I do all I can. And in the end it’s family and friends that are important and I’m lucky. I have 14 grandchildren and one great granddaughter. I had family and friends in Wang Chung and still have them now I’m in Fanling.

初時很懷念從前，頭幾年也難以適應城市生活；但粉嶺很好，我參與屋苑居民會並幫助其他人。雖然今年已 79 歲，仍有較年輕的人需要幫忙。我有 14 個孫兒和一個曾孫女，親友最重要；在王涌村和粉嶺都有親友。

[Recording ends]
