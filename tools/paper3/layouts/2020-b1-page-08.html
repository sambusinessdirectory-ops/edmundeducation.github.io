Dear All

各位同事：

I thought I would send you all a quick email to share with you some exciting news.

寫封簡短電郵，與大家分享令人振奮的消息。

The Adventure Camps have been such a success that we are going to expand our operations. That means that we are going to expand from two camps a year to four. We are going to give them the names March, May, August and November Adventure Camps.

歷奇營大受歡迎，我們將擴充營運，由每年兩期增至四期，分別為三月、五月、八月和十一月營。

We will be continuing at our Sai Kung camp site for the foreseeable future, I’m afraid. As you know, we were hopeful that we would be able to move venues to Cheung Chau but that will now not be happening.

我們會繼續使用西貢營地，暫不會搬往長洲。

However, we are also going to increase our capacity for each camp from the present 40 children (8 in each of the 5 dormitories) to 50 children (10 in each dormitory). It’ll be a bit crowded but I think we can squeeze them in.

每期名額將由四十名（五間宿舍各八名）增至五十名（每間十名）。雖然較擠迫，但應能容納。

Ellen
