Ball Kids – Victoria Cup

Ball Kids – Victoria Cup (Period September 27 – October 3 2021)

Victoria Cup 義務拾球員（2021 年 9 月 27 日至 10 月 3 日）

Job description:

The main job of the ball kids is to retrieve tennis balls when they are out of play and to supply players with a towel and refreshments. They must perform these duties so that players and officials can concentrate without being distracted.

拾球員主要負責撿拾已停止比賽的網球，並向球員提供毛巾和茶點；須讓球員及工作人員免受干擾，集中精神。

Requirements:

We are seeking a dynamic team of 100 ball boys and/or ball girls for the Victoria Cup. Both spoken English and Cantonese required.

Victoria Cup 招募一百名有活力的拾球員，須能說英語和廣東話。

Deadline to express an interest: 25 April 2021

表達參與意向的截止日期：2021 年 4 月 25 日

If your charity would like to express an interest in taking part in the above, complete the Google Form on the Events Horizon website (www.events.horizon.com.hk).

Organisation Description (100 words max.)

R.S. is a Hong Kong-based trust that looks after forced migrant children mainly from Africa and South East Asia. We provide a safe and supportive living environment for these vulnerable members of society who have fled dangerous and life-threatening situations, often becoming orphaned in the process. Some of our children speak English but very few of them can speak Cantonese so we help them to learn the basics of the language and integrate into Hong Kong society. The refugee children stay in our centre up until the age of 10, after which they go on to one of our partner charities.

Refugee Sanctuary 是香港信託機構，照顧主要來自非洲和東南亞的被迫遷徙兒童。他們為逃離危險環境、部分已成孤兒的孩子提供安全支援。部分孩子會說英語，但很少會說廣東話；機構協助他們學習廣東話及融入香港社會。孩子留在中心至十歲，之後轉往合作慈善機構。

Organisation Description (100 words max.)

The Changing Young Lives Foundation is committed to identifying talented underprivileged youngsters in Hong Kong and changing their lives for the better. We operate a coeducational learning institute in Shek Kip Mei where the brightest young people between the ages of 11 and 21 who would otherwise not be able to afford adequate schooling can come free of charge. Our children develop all the essential academic and social skills, becoming proficient in both English and Cantonese, and often going on to study at prestigious universities.

Changing Young Lives 基金會發掘香港有才華的弱勢青年，幫助他們改善生活。基金會在石硤尾設有男女同校的學習機構，讓十一至二十一歲、原本負擔不起適當教育的青年免費就學。學生培養學術和社交能力，熟練英語及廣東話，不少人其後入讀知名大學。
