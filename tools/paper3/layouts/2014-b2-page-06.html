Interview excerpt from student podcast: A different kind of nurse!

Host:

We’re lucky enough today to be sitting with three new graduates from the School of Veterinary Medicine at King George University, Canada. They’ve all just completed their course in Veterinary Nursing. Please welcome Ian McBain, Randall Worthing and Candy Tse. Okay, let’s start with the easy question: What is a veterinary nurse? What do you do?

今天我們訪問加拿大 King George University 獸醫學院的三位獸醫護理畢業生：Ian McBain、Randall Worthing 和 Candy Tse。獸醫護士是做甚麼的？

Ian:

Well, most obviously we provide care and support for animals when they get sick.

我們在動物生病時提供護理與支持。

Host:

Okay. Great. So, you just finished your training. What do you think the best bit of being a veterinary nurse is going to be then?

你們剛完成培訓，最期待這份工作的哪部分？

Ian:

I think the clinical duties. In the training, we did so many different things. We even got to do minor surgical procedures. I did a cat that had been in a fight.

我期待臨床職責。培訓中接觸許多工作，甚至做過小型手術。我處理過一隻打架受傷的貓。

Randall:

Yeah, I had to do some dental cleaning!

我做過牙齒清潔！

Candy:

I’ve not done that. What animal was it?

我還沒做過。是哪種動物？

Randall:

A rabbit!

一隻兔子！

Host:

And from what you’ve seen in your internships, what were the challenges of being a trainee veterinary nurse?

你們實習時遇到甚麼挑戰？

Candy:

I think it was learning not to be nervous with animals.

我得學會在動物面前不緊張。

Host:

Was that hard?

難嗎？

Candy:

At first, but it got better — I asked the experienced nurses for tips and copied them!

起初難，但後來改善了。我向資深護士請教並學習他們的做法。

Ian:

For me it was the shift work, you know, I had to be on call at night. Make sure you go to bed on time. That’s my advice!

對我而言是輪班，晚上也要候命。我的建議是準時睡覺！

Host:

How about you, Randall?

Randall，你呢？

Randall:

The biggest challenge for me personally was when a sick animal died. I had to learn to develop a strategy to cope with that one.

最大的挑戰是病重動物死亡，我得學會應對。

Candy:

Yeah, that’s a tough one.

對，那很難受。

Host:

Okay, let’s move on — what about the future? What can you do now? Any plans?

接下來談談將來。你們有甚麼計劃？
