Minutes of Management Committee meeting

Date
30th March 2020, 3:00 PM
Venue
Meeting Room 101
In attendance
Ms Ellen Ochoa · Ms Sunita Chawla · Mr Scotty Wong · Mr Bowie Ma

1. Tarkov Problem-Solving Activity

Ms Chawla reminded the committee that it had been decided to replace the PowerPoint slides used to introduce the Planet Tarkov Problem-Solving Activity with a video. Ms Chawla drew the committee’s attention to the feedback provided by last year’s instructors, in which they made suggestions for improving the Planet Tarkov Problem-Solving Activity. Ms Chawla added that she herself had made comments on the suggestions.

Chawla 女士提醒委員會：去年用作介紹 Tarkov 活動的投影片將改為影片，並請大家參考導師的改善意見及她的批註。

Ms Ochoa agreed with Ms Chawla’s comments and said that she would tell Nico to write the script of the video and to make the appropriate changes based on the instructors’ feedback and Ms Chawla’s comments.

Ochoa 女士同意這些批註，會請 Nico 按意見修改影片稿。

2. M.A.T. Incident

Mr Wong presented the findings from his Multi-Axis Trainer (M.A.T.) Engineering Assessment. Ms Ochoa added that she would like to make one further recommendation: to cut back the branches of the trees above the M.A.T. building. She also asked Mr Wong to clarify how the roof would be sealed. Mr Wong agreed with Ms Ochoa’s additional recommendation and confirmed that a silicon sealant would be used.

Wong 先生報告多軸訓練器的工程檢查；Ochoa 女士另建議修剪建築物上方樹枝，並確認屋頂會用矽膠密封。

3. Request from Locae Foundation

Ms Ochoa referred the committee to the letter received from the Locae Foundation. Mr Wong presented his Space Capsule Transport Feasibility Assessment.

Ochoa 女士提及 Locae Foundation 的來信；Wong 先生介紹太空艙運送可行性評估。

Ms Ochoa agreed with the conclusions in Mr Wong’s assessment. Speaking confidentially, given our company’s dire financial situation, Ms Ochoa suggested that we needed to spend the money more wisely rather than on wasting money moving the capsule just to help a charity.

Ochoa 女士認同評估結論；她私下提到財政困難，認為應更明智用錢。

Ms Chawla said that in order not to seem like we are being cruel to a charity, they could be invited as VIPs to Star Pod’s Open Day. We should point out that it coincides with one of the days they requested. They could join the Open Day and meet the special guest at no extra expense to us. Ms Chawla said that as a special favour we could also give them ‘exclusive access’ to the Space Capsule and the M.A.T.

Chawla 女士建議邀請慈善機構成為開放日貴賓，與特別嘉賓午膳，並給予太空艙的專屬參觀機會。

Mr Wong noted that the M.A.T. would still be under repair and so not accessible. Ms Ochoa agreed that the Space Capsule only could be made available.

Wong 先生指出 M.A.T. 仍在維修，故只可開放太空艙。
