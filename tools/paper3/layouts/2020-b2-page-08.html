~Making young lives matter~

26 March 2020

Upper Wong Tai Sin Estate II

204 Ching Tak Street
Chuk Un

Dear Sir/Madam

敬啟者：

Since 1993, the Locae Foundation has been helping underprivileged children have life-building experiences that develop the confidence they need to fulfill their dreams and become productive members of our future society. Many of our children have gone on to become doctors, engineers and scientists. As one of our alumni, now a research chemist put it:

自 1993 年起，基金會協助弱勢兒童透過有益經歷建立信心，許多人成為醫生、工程師和科學家。

“The Locae Foundation gave me hope and the drive to work on my talents and achieve my ambitions.”

一名校友說基金會給他希望與發揮才能的動力。

As part of our programme, we bring children to our Stone Dragon Facilities where they engage in activities around an inspiring theme. This year, our theme is space exploration, a subject in which you at Star Pod are the leaders in Asia. That is why we are reaching out to you to make a special request.

今年活動主題是太空探索，故向亞洲領先的 Star Pod 提出請求。

I recently visited your website and was deeply impressed with your replica of the Space Capsule. We would most humbly request to borrow the capsule and in doing so give our children an experience they will treasure for the rest of their lives.

基金會希望借用太空艙複製品，讓孩子留下難忘回憶。

Were you to kindly accept our request, would the following details be acceptable to you?

如果同意，請考慮以下詳情。

  • We would like to borrow the capsule from 23rd to 27th of April
  • Children would play inside and outside the capsule under close supervision.

希望在 4 月 23 日至 27 日借用，讓孩子在監督下於艙內外活動。

Thank you for considering our request and we look forward to joining you in making the lives of our children richer and happier.

感謝考慮，希望攜手豐富孩子的生活。

Yours faithfully

此致

Denise Leung Director of Locae Foundation

Denise Leung，Locae Foundation 總監
