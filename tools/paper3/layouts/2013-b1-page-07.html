Press conference excerpt from the International Airport Committee

World’s Best Airport Announced

Good morning everyone, thanks to everyone for coming this morning. The International Airport Committee is pleased to announce the winners of the Best International Airport Award for 2012. The competition was close this year, with some airports showing a big improvement on the figures from 2011. Congratulations go to Hong Kong for winning first prize. We all know the airport at Chek Lap Kok is always a popular choice with world travellers. Moving up from third place last year to second place this year is Singapore, very well done to them. Finally, we award Seoul’s Incheon airport in South Korea, which claims third place. A special thank you must go to all the world travellers who voted in this year’s competition and our hearty congratulations to the winners. Now, are there any questions?

各位早晨，謝謝出席。國際機場委員會公布 2012 年最佳國際機場獎。競爭激烈，部分機場較 2011 年進步甚大。香港赤鱲角機場獲第一名；新加坡由去年的第三名升至第二名；韓國首爾仁川機場獲第三名。感謝所有參與投票的旅客，恭喜得獎者。現在歡迎提問。

We asked some of you to ‘blog in’ and tell us why you go on holiday and lots of you have been in touch. You also sent us some hotel stories — some of you have stayed in some pretty strange places. Here’s a few excerpts for you all. Enjoy reading them and remember to send us your travel blog links. Happy travels!

我們邀請讀者分享旅遊原因和酒店故事，收到許多有趣的投稿。以下是節錄，歡迎繼續寄來旅遊網誌連結。

From Ice Queen, Spain

Why do I travel? To meet new people of course! I stayed in an ice hotel in Norway, it was brilliant. You’d think it’d be really cold, but it wasn’t. The staff made sure we had lots of warm coats to wear. Give it a try! But don’t forget — only open in winter!

我旅遊是為了認識新朋友！我住過挪威冰酒店，十分精彩。原以為很冷，但職員提供了很多保暖外套。值得試試，不過只在冬天開放。

From Roberto, Brazil

Me and some friends stayed in a prison hotel — it was a great place to stay because the history of the prison was really interesting. But the downside was that the rooms were really small and we could only have one person per room. All the hotel guests had to cook dinner together in the prison kitchen. It was an unforgettable meal. I travel because I like trying new food and this was one of the best meals I’ve ever had.

我和朋友住過監獄酒店。監獄歷史很有趣，但房間細小，每房只能住一人。所有住客在監獄廚房一同煮晚餐，非常難忘。我旅遊是為了嘗試新食物，這是我吃過最好的餐之一。

From Klara, Denmark

@ Ice Queen, I stayed in an ice hotel and I didn’t like it too much to tell you the truth. I think I’m just someone who likes normal traditional hotels. Nothing unusual for me thank you!

回覆 Ice Queen：老實說，我住過冰酒店卻不太喜歡。我還是偏愛普通傳統酒店。

From Ferby, Hong Kong

I travel because I love seeing new places. I slept in a lighthouse hotel in Scotland last year. It was a great place to watch the bird life. It’s expensive though — about HKD 1200 per person, per night.

我旅遊是因為喜歡看新地方。去年住過蘇格蘭燈塔酒店，很適合賞鳥；但價格昂貴，每人每晚約港幣 1,200 元。
