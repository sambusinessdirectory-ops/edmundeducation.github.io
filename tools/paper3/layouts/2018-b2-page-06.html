What is the Acorn Grant?

甚麼是 Acorn Grant？

A helping hand for young entrepreneurs and their companies in Hong Kong.

But why an acorn?

Haven’t you heard of the saying, from a tiny acorn the mighty oak tree grows?

它從何時開始幫助年輕創業者？

Since 2010.

What are the chances of me winning?

HK$250,000. A one-time award. No strings attached. You win the grant, you get the money. No ifs or buts.

一次過獲得港幣 250,000 元，沒有附帶條件。
