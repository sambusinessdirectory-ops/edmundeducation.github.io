Interview notes from interview with Mei Cheng

Hong Kong Tourism: Past and Present

Interviewee: Mei Cheng　 Date of interview: 3rd March, 2013

Location: Dragon Hotel Hong Kong

Personal Background: Age = 97 Father – manager Dragon Hotel (DH) Mei grew up in DH

個人背景：Mei 現年 97 歲，父親曾任 Dragon Hotel 經理，她在酒店長大。

100th Anniversary of the DH DH opened in 1913 – 125 staff, 48 rooms Used by Japanese army in 2nd World War 100th Anniversary Ball planned for October 2013 – Mei to be guest of honour Opening of new spa facilities in December

酒店百週年：1913 年開業，有 125 名員工和 48 間客房；二戰時曾被日軍使用。2013 年 10 月將舉行百週年舞會，由 Mei 擔任嘉賓；12 月開設新水療設施。

Transport in 1920’s DH had stables for horses – like other hotels – not like now! People used to hire horse + carriage (now taxis – she doesn’t like taxis = pollution).

1920 年代的交通：酒店跟其他酒店一樣設馬廄，旅客租用馬車；現在使用的士，但她不喜歡其污染。

Types of people Interntnl tourists then – richer – more glamourous – DH had extra rooms for servants: when she was a girl always saw tourists travel with servants

當年的國際旅客較富有、形象更華麗；酒店有供僕人住的額外房間，她小時候常看到旅客帶僕人同行。

Tourists used to have so much luggage!! Before had different outfits for dinner etc. Now amount of luggage ↓

當年旅客帶很多行李，晚餐等場合會換不同服裝；現在行李較少。

Things tourists did Mei liked watching people dance after dinner – ladies = beautiful; wanted to be like them.

Mei 喜歡看晚餐後的舞會，覺得女士們很美，希望像她們一樣。

Letter printed from Kevin Hui in Asia Life magazine March issue

Dear Sir

尊敬的編輯：

I’m writing about the effects of tourism. I used to be a travel agent and I’ve travelled to many different places in the world because of my job and I have observed that the effects of tourism are increasingly negative.

我寫信談旅遊的影響。我曾任旅行社職員，因工作到過世界多地，觀察到旅遊帶來的負面影響日益增加。

Recently, the Brazilian government discovered a ‘lost tribe’ of people living deep inside the rainforest. Instead of using the tribe to generate more tourism as we have seen in so many other countries, they have decided to leave the tribes of people uncontacted. The government even has guards to stop everyone, especially tourists, going into those areas to contact the tribes.

巴西政府最近發現一個生活在雨林深處的「失落部落」。他們沒有藉此發展旅遊，反而決定讓部落不受外界接觸，並派守衛阻止遊客等人進入。

I couldn’t agree more with this decision. Now, these tribal groups can live immersed in their own traditions and without being negatively influenced by anyone from outside their own community or country.

我非常贊同這決定。這些部落群體便可繼續沉浸於自身傳統，不受外界社區或國家的人帶來負面影響。

Yours faithfully

此致

Kevin Hui Shatin

Kevin Hui，沙田
