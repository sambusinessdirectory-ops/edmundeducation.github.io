2013 Writers’ Guidelines for Publication

MEMO

To: Editorial team, Asia Life magazine

From: Casey Wong

Date: 1st February, 2013

Subject: 2013 Writers’ Guidelines for Publication

Please note the new submission guidelines with effect from 1st February, 2013:

以下新投稿指引由 2013 年 2 月 1 日起生效：

  1. All articles must be submitted on time to allow the editorial team time to read and suggest any revisions. Late articles will not be considered for publication.

    所有文章必須準時提交，以便編輯組閱讀及提出修改建議；逾期稿件不予考慮。

  2. Copyright: any materials used (e.g. photographs) must not breach copyright. Writers should state the source of images.

    版權：使用的材料（例如照片）不得侵權，作者須註明圖片來源。

  3. Images for articles: soft copy (high resolution JPEG file format).

    圖片須提交高解像度 JPEG 電子檔。

  4. Refer to the word limit set in editorial meetings as this may vary from issue to issue.

    須按編輯會議為該期訂定的字數上限撰稿；每期可能不同。

  5. Titles must not be more than 80 characters long.

    標題不得超過 80 個字元。

  6. Style of article to be decided by the writer, unless otherwise indicated by the editor.

    除非編輯另有指示，文章風格由作者決定。

  7. Asia Life magazine retains the rights to any articles published.

    《Asia Life》保留已刊登文章的權利。

  8. Once an article is accepted for publication, payment will be made.

    稿件獲接納刊登後才支付稿酬。
