Part B

Situation

You are Gehry Zhang and you work for a property developer called Dragon Peak Properties (DPP). Your boss is Norman Kwok, a publicity and marketing manager. DPP is currently working on two projects: the Jade Islands, which have recently been completed and the Golden Flower Island development, which is in the planning process.

You will now listen to a radio panel show called Business Matters with host Charlie Meadows. Appearing on the show are Norman Kwok and Ann Lam from the environmental group Green Treasure.

Before the recording is played, you will have five minutes to study the Question-Answer Book and the Data File to familiarise yourself with the situation and the tasks.

Complete the tasks by following the instructions in the Question-Answer Book and on the recording. You will find all the information you need in the Question-Answer Book, the Data File and on the recording. As you listen, you can make notes on page 3 of the Data File.

You now have five minutes to familiarise yourself with the Question-Answer Book and the Data File.

Extract from Flora and Fauna of South China by J.R. Hartley

Golden Dragon Tail Butterfly

This butterfly is quite common in Asia but exists in only a very few areas of South China. It is the only known species in South China that pollinates the extremely rare Splash of Gold Orchid. This rare and beautiful plant can now only be found on Golden Flower Island. Only half of this island has been designated by the HKSAR Government as a nature park due to it being an area of special scientific interest. Development is therefore only restricted in that part of the island. Trouble may be brewing, however, as the local press has reported that development plans are afoot for the island for 2017. Let’s hope common sense prevails and we can protect these beautiful plants and creatures.

Yes, I heard that a query had been raised on the Business Matters radio show. The solution at the Highland Adventure Centre was quite costly at the time and the centre was closed for six months. Minutes from Golden Flower Adventure Mall team meeting

Golden Flower Adventure Mall team meeting

Date:        8:00 AM, Fri. 7 April 2017
Venue:       The Eyrie, Dragon Peak Tower

Chairperson: Norman Kwok
Members:     Alison Lo
             Mari Nakamura
             Ripple Wu
Secretary:   Gehry Zhang

Opening Ceremony of Mall

Dr Lo presented a progress report on construction work of the Golden Flower Adventure Mall. She confirmed that the provisional completion date of 30 November 2018 was still in place. A discussion ensued as to the best date for the opening ceremony to take place. 1 January 2019 was suggested as was leaving it until Lunar New Year. Finally it was decided that 24 December would be best as it would mean that the mall could immediately benefit from the holiday period over Christmas.

Mr Kwok reported the results of the opening ceremony budget proposal meeting with the Finance Department as follows:

| Item                                  | Requested budget | Approved ✓ / Disapproved X | Reason                             |
|---------------------------------------|------------------|----------------------------|------------------------------------|
| 1. Guest of honour fees               | HK$100,000       | ✓                          | n/a                                |
| 2. Horse and carriage for ceremony    | HK$50,000        | X                          | Refused due to insurance concerns. |

Possible activities at the Opening Ceremony were discussed given the Finance Department’s refusal of Item 2. Dr Lo suggested that if the guest of honour swam then he or she could be the first person to swim in the new pool as a way of declaring it open. All agreed. Ms Nakamura presented a selection of possible celebrities to be the guest of honour at the Opening Ceremony. From these it was decided to approach Ms Mariana Tse. All agreed. Gehry to contact her.

Ms Wu pointed out that Ms Mariana Tse was in the media recently for refusing to accept fees for a similar event. Mr Kwok suggested using the fees budget to make a donation to the charity of Ms Tse’s choice. All agreed. Gehry to include this.

Meeting ended at 8:40 am.
