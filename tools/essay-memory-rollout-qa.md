# Model essay memorization rollout: local release and editorial QA

Status on 2026-11-02: **288 of 288 catalogue lessons have exact source bundles, authored teaching support, local manifest entries, and pass the release audit.** This covers 228 IELTS Writing Task 2 essays and 60 IELTS Writing Task 1 reports. The second editorial pass is complete for the assigned cohorts. The expanded release remains in the local worktree; it has not been published to the live site. Mechanical checks establish coverage and consistency; pedagogical judgment also required source-by-source review.

## Reference and scope

The two supplied *Model Essay Memorization Golden Manual* PDFs and the four earlier student lessons (Opinion 80, Opinion 3, Discuss Both Views 4, Cause / Solution 2) supply the system's reference behavior and visual style. The writing-practice catalogue is the canonical list of interactive essays. The request estimated 270 essays; the catalogue actually contains 288 unique interactive IELTS model essays, so all 288 are in scope. The attached manuals and mapping documents are references, not instructions to override the user's corrections.

The model essays and Traditional Chinese translations on writing-practice are the exact source for every lesson. Teaching paraphrases may simplify those sentences but may not silently change their stance, condition, negation, degree of certainty, data, or cause-and-effect relation. Task 1 questions retain their charts, tables, maps, or process images and their units. The 11-stage journey uses the same learning sequence as the reference lessons: meaning, easier wording, exact reconstruction, argument route, transfer, and sentence-pattern application, with the later paragraph/sentence reconstruction stages.

## User corrections carried into the system

The student journey includes a student-only login, larger text control, adjustable short-step audio speed, distinct colors for simple-word rebuilding and original-word reconstruction, a simple sentence displayed below the original, highlighted original vocabulary that is simplified, and a standalone recording collection separated from the speaking system. The hint in the medium-fill recording stage requires an unhinted retry before moving on. Sentence-order tiles leave the original tray when placed. Source text shown in sentence-order exercises avoids capitalization cues. The writing-practice catalogue links all 288 essays to their lessons, including the separate Task 1 key prefix.

Sentence-pattern cards show the exact original sentence and Traditional Chinese translation, segment the actual source wording, then explain meaning and a reusable grammatical/logical use at the correct level of abstraction. The `instead of` pattern compares alternative choices; a `so` clause expresses a result and is not limited to an audience benefit. Usage notes must not disguise a topic-specific observation as a general rule. Practice examples must contain fixed connector wording in the same order as the source frame; their distractors must be credible alternatives, not a length giveaway or a sentence from another grammatical pattern. Transfer exercises must identify source sentence indices and preserve the source reasoning when moving to another topic. Traditional Chinese choices are checked for one-to-three-character dangling lines at 320, 390, and 768 px and at three font scales.

## Why source matching alone was insufficient

The supplied Task 2 structure-mapping DOCX contains 4,477 authored rows across 238 PDF essays. All 228 interactive Task 2 essays match its catalogue, but PDF sentence positions can differ from the interactive source. A literal span match does not establish the correct function or usable practice example. The mapping also repeats a known misreading of `instead of`, so each source sentence was checked against the interactive essay before use. The release audit now includes pattern-specific checks for the known `instead of` and audience-bound `so` usage errors.

An early automatic teaching-support prototype failed editorial review: 2,430 of 3,723 alleged simpler sentences were unchanged, 2,058 structure rows used generic fallbacks, and 2,688 transfer tasks lacked source links. That prototype was removed. The current lesson files are individually authored and source-linked. The release audit rejects repeated English, source-span drift, missing feedback, wrong or missing links, and several previously rejected generic usage descriptions.

A broader heuristic scan found many wrong choices containing words such as `必然`, `永遠`, and `所有`. Such words can be legitimate when a lesson explicitly tests cautious language; they can also make a wrong answer too easy to spot. A manual sample found real weaknesses, so the reviewers replaced the conspicuous alternatives with plausible competing explanations and updated the corresponding feedback. The Advantage / Disadvantage cohort's final audit covered 28 lessons, 483 sentence patterns, and 280 transfer sets, with zero formula/slot mismatches, Chinese choice-length cues, or conspicuous absolute-word distractors. Opinion, Cause / Solution, Direct Question, Discuss Both Views, and Task 1 cohorts also received a second editorial pass. The heuristic itself is not an acceptance test; the final choice must require understanding the reasoning.
