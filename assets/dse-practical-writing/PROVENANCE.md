# DSE Practical Writing visual assets

## Ownership and permitted use

These three production assets were generated specifically for EdmundEducation
with OpenAI's built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-11-04. They are not crops, edits,
traces or derivatives of the supplied third-party game screenshots.

- `learning-route-v1.webp` — original academic learning-route desk.
- `writing-desk-v1.webp` — original practical-writing workspace.
- `briefing-folio-v1.webp` — original academic briefing folio.

The source screenshots were used only to identify abstract requirements such as
warm task lighting, tactile paper, environmental depth and a document-led
composition. They were not passed to ImageGen and are not included in these
files.

## Release audit

- No third-party names, logos or trademarks.
- No readable embedded text, menu labels, social icons or version numbers.
- No characters, weapons, military objects or occult symbols.
- No decorative controls that could be mistaken for working website controls.
- No recognisable reproduction of a supplied reference scene.
- All real labels and interactions remain HTML, keyboard-accessible and testable.

## Generation prompts

The three prompts required an entirely original contemporary education workspace,
warm editorial lighting, walnut and cream paper materials, restrained sage and
oxblood accents, large calm areas for HTML content, and explicit exclusion of
brands, game interfaces, military material, readable text and copyrighted art.
