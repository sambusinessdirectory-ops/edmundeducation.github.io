- **468 lessons** have a multi-row MCQ-ready source table.
- **63 lessons** lacked such a table. Their option descriptions were drafted from the PDFs’ numbered sense sections and should receive editorial review.
- **845 supplementary meanings** were drafted from PDF sections that were absent from their comparison table. Every heading and provisional wording is recorded in [MCQ-REVIEW.json](MCQ-REVIEW.json).
