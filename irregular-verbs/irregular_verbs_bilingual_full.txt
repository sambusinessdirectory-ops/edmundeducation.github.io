#Core irregular verbs


##1. arise（出現；產生）— arise / arose / arisen

1. Problems often arise when people avoid small issues.
【當人們迴避小問題時，問題常常會出現。】

2. A disagreement arose after the vote.
【投票後出現了一場分歧。】

3. Several new questions have arisen this week.
【本週已經出現了幾個新的問題。】



##2. awake（醒來；喚醒）— awake / awoke, awakened / awoken, awakened

1. I sometimes awake before dawn on busy days.
【忙碌的日子裡，我有時會在黎明前醒來。】

2. She awoke to the sound of heavy rain.
【她被大雨聲吵醒了。】

3. He had awakened before the alarm rang.
【他在鬧鐘響之前就醒了。】

Note: Variant past participle: awoken is also common.



##3. be（是；在；存在）— be / was, were / been

1. I want to be more patient with my students.
【我想對我的學生更有耐心。】

2. She was calm, and they were calm too.
【她很冷靜，而他們也很冷靜。】

3. We have been friends since childhood.
【我們從小就是朋友。】



##4. bear（承受；忍受；負擔；生育）— bear / bore / borne, born

1. I cannot bear that constant noise.
【我無法忍受那種持續不斷的噪音。】

2. She bore the pain in silence.
【她默默承受了那份痛苦。】

3. He has borne many hardships with courage.
【他勇敢地承受了許多艱難。】

Note: Birth sense: born. Example: She was born in Kaohsiung. 【她出生於高雄。】



##5. beat（打；擊敗；敲打；跳動）— beat / beat / beaten

1. Our team can still beat them tonight.
【我們隊今晚仍然可以擊敗他們。】

2. He beat the drum during the parade.
【他在遊行時敲打那面鼓。】

3. The other side has beaten us twice already.
【對方已經擊敗我們兩次了。】



##6. become（變成；變得）— become / became / become

2. The sky became dark in a few minutes.
【天空在幾分鐘內就變暗了。】

3. She has become much more confident this year.
【她今年變得自信多了。】



##7. begin（開始）— begin / began / begun

2. It began to rain as we left home.

3. The meeting has already begun.
【會議已經開始了。】



##8. bend（彎曲；彎腰）— bend / bent / bent

2. He bent down to pick up the coin.

3. The metal bar was bent in the crash.
【那根金屬條在撞擊中被弄彎了。】



##9. bet（打賭；下注）— bet / bet, betted / bet, betted

1. I would not bet all my money on one game.
【我不會把所有的錢都押在一場比賽上。】

2. He bet twenty dollars on the final.
【他在決賽上下注了二十美元。】

3. She has bet on that horse before.
【她以前曾經押過那匹馬。】

Note: Variant forms betted also exist, but bet is more common.



##10. bid（出價；投標；命令；祝願）— bid / bid, bade / bid, bidden

3. The company has bid for three public projects.

#Small but important notes

##bear / bore / borne, born
- borne usually means “carried” or “endured.”
  Example: He has borne great pain.
  【他承受了巨大的痛苦。】
- born is used for birth.
  Example: She was born in Tainan.
  【她出生於台南。】

##hang / hung / hung; hang / hanged / hanged
- hung is the normal form.
  Example: He hung the picture on the wall.
  【他把那幅畫掛在牆上。】
- hanged is traditionally used for execution.
  Example: The prisoner was hanged at dawn.
  【那名囚犯在黎明時被處以絞刑。】

##lie / lay / lain vs. lay / laid / laid
- lie means “to recline.”
  Example: I need to lie down.
  【我需要躺下。】
- lay means “to put or place.”
  Example: Please lay the book on the table.
  【請把書放在桌上。】

##read / read / read
- The spelling stays the same, but the pronunciation changes:
  - Present: read /riːd/
  - Past and past participle: read /rɛd/

##be
- The verb be is the most irregular verb in English:
  - am / is / are / was / were / been / being
