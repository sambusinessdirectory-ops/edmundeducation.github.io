# Archival media added to the 1944 and 1945 Churchill lessons

## Editorial boundary

The 1945 lesson contains the 8 May BBC broadcast (reader lines 1–56) followed by a separate House of Commons continuation (lines 57–86). The original broadcast is streamed from the [Churchill Project's VE Day source page](https://winstonchurchill.hillsdale.edu/victory-in-europe/). The downloaded source was checked locally as a playable 6:41 WAV, but it is **not** stored in this repository. No contemporary Commons audio is presented for lines 57–86.

The 1944 lesson contains Churchill's two House of Commons reports of 6 June. Parliamentary proceedings were not recorded at the time; the [Churchill Project discography](https://winstonchurchill.hillsdale.edu/churchill-recordings-speeches-memoirs/) distinguishes later Churchill recordings from delivery in the chamber. Its listed *D-Day Plus 20* record has not been verified as a complete, publicly playable match for this lesson, so the site does not present it as original full audio.

The photograph galleries identify whether an image shows the actual 1945 broadcast, another event on the same day, the preparations for D-Day, or the landings themselves. None of the 1944 images is described as showing the Commons speech.
