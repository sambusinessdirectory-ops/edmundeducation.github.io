Date: Thursday, March 27, 2025

日期：2025 年 3 月 27 日，星期四

Venue: Conference Room

地點：會議室

Present:

出席者：

Mr John Duncan (JD)Mr Timothy Siu (TS)Ms Melissa Henley (MH)Mr Manraj Singh (MS)
JD:

Hello everyone. Let’s get started. First on the agenda: ideas for events for the Asian Games Week. Would anyone like to share their ideas?

大家好，我們開始吧。第一項議程：亞洲運動會週的活動構思。有誰想分享意見？

TS:

How about a quiz? Maybe call it ‘The Asian Games Sports Quiz’?

舉辦問答比賽如何？或可稱為「亞洲運動會體育問答比賽」？

MH:

A quiz would be fun, but I think we should have questions about all aspects of the Asian Games, not just sports knowledge?

問答比賽會很有趣，但我認為題目應涵蓋亞運各方面，不應只有體育知識吧？

MS:

How about, then, ‘The Asian Games Quiz’? OK, good. We all agree. We can use the school IT system to play a presentation with information about the Asian Games to all students while they are in lesson one, then have the quiz. The quiz will be based on the information students get from the presentation. That way, everyone can take part at the same time and everyone gets the answers, if they’re listening!

那麼叫「亞洲運動會問答比賽」如何？好，大家都同意。我們可透過學校資訊科技系統，在第一節課向全體學生播放介紹亞運的簡報，然後舉行問答比賽。題目將根據簡報中的資訊。這樣所有人可同時參加，只要有留心聆聽，人人都會知道答案！

JD:

I like the idea of doing a school-wide quiz, and having the information in the presentation does make it fairer, but I don’t think the first lesson would work. Most of the students are still half asleep at that time in the morning!

我喜歡全校參與問答比賽的構思，在簡報中提供資訊也確實較公平，但我覺得第一節課不適合。大部分學生在那個早上時段仍未完全清醒！

MS:

How about during lesson six instead, just before lunch? Then the students can enjoy their lunch hour after having fun in the quiz.

改在午餐前的第六節課如何？學生享受問答比賽的樂趣後，就可以享用午餐。

JD:

OK, sounds good. Can we fit this in on Day One of the Asian Games Week?

好，聽來不錯。可以安排在亞洲運動會週的第一天嗎？

TS:

No, the Opening Event is on Day One. Day Two would be better.

不行，第一天有開幕活動。第二天會比較好。

JD:

Noted. Let’s go with that then. Melissa, you had the idea of an Athletics Jamboree. What’s that?

明白，那就這樣決定。Melissa，你提議田徑同樂日，那是甚麼？

MH:

For the Athletics Jamboree, students form teams and compete against each other in track and field events. I suggest we hold it on Day Four of the Asian Games Week.

田徑同樂日讓學生組隊，在田賽及徑賽項目中互相競賽。我建議在亞洲運動會週第四天舉行。

JD:

Ah, I think we said we wouldn’t have enough time for the track events.

啊，我記得我們說過沒有足夠時間進行徑賽。

MH:

Yes, sorry, that’s right. Only the field events. We can hold them at the Sports Ground.

對，不好意思，沒錯。只舉行田賽。我們可在運動場進行。

TS:

I think the students would enjoy that. But I think the morning of Day Five would be a better choice. The students can then be dismissed early and recover over the weekend.

我想學生會喜歡。但我認為第五天早上較合適。這樣學生可提早放學，並在週末休息恢復體力。

JD:

The teachers too! OK, I agree. Great, that’s two more events for the Asian Games Week.

老師也是！好，我同意。很好，亞洲運動會週又多了兩項活動。

Sports Asia interview transcriptSports Asia 訪問逐字稿

The following is a transcript of an interview by the Sports Asia podcast with star athlete Peter Wong.

以下是 Sports Asia 播客訪問明星運動員 Peter Wong 的逐字稿。

Interviewer:

Peter, you competed in the 2022 Asian Games in Hangzhou. Did you have a good time?

Peter，你參加了 2022 年杭州亞運。你玩得開心嗎？

Peter Wong:

Oh yes! The city was nice, and it was funny to see those small robots everywhere.

當然！那座城市很好，到處看到那些小機械人也很有趣。

Interviewer:

Robots?

機械人？

Peter Wong:

Well, they weren’t real robots. This was their mascot: three futuristic robot characters. For the 2026 Asian Games in Nagoya, I heard they’re using a tigerfish. That’s nice, because the Japanese word for tigerfish also means ‘flame’.

其實不是真機械人，而是吉祥物：三個充滿未來感的機械人角色。至於 2026 年名古屋亞運，我聽說他們會用虎魚。這很好，因為日文的虎魚一詞也有「火焰」的意思。

Interviewer:

How long have you been participating in the Asian Games?

你參與亞運多久了？

Peter Wong:

These games run in my family. My grandpa actually joined the first ever Asian Games in 1951. He competed in long-distance running, just like me. I’ve been lucky enough to compete in the cities of Incheon and Doha as well.

亞運是我們家的傳統。我祖父曾參加 1951 年首屆亞運，跟我一樣參加長跑。我也很幸運，曾在仁川及多哈參賽。

Interviewer:

Wow! The event must have changed a lot since it started, though?

嘩！不過，這項盛事自創辦以來一定改變了很多吧？

Peter Wong:

Yeah, pretty much. I mean, it’s still the same Asian Games, but they’ve added less physical sports such as chess and e-sports to attract a different audience.

對，變化很大。它仍是亞運，但新增了國際象棋及電子競技等對體能要求較低的運動，吸引不同觀眾。

Interviewer:

How does the Asian Games impact the world?

亞運對世界有甚麼影響？

Peter Wong:

The Asian Games always has a strong focus on raising awareness. In Incheon back in 2014, there was a heavy focus on environmental awareness. The organisers collected rainwater for us to use to lower water consumption. I read that the Asian Games in Nagoya will have a strong focus on health awareness.

亞運一直非常重視提升公眾意識。2014 年仁川亞運特別注重環保意識。主辦方收集雨水供我們使用，以降低用水量。我讀到名古屋亞運將十分重視健康意識。

Interviewer:

Is that the ‘walk for fun’ campaign?

就是「walk for fun」活動嗎？

Peter Wong:

Yes, to get the public walking more.

對，鼓勵公眾多步行。

Interviewer:

Any final thoughts, Peter?

Peter，最後有甚麼想分享？

Peter Wong:

Sure. Hong Kong may be small, but we have great athletes and a great attitude. Do you know how many medals we got at the last Asian Games?

有。香港雖小，但我們擁有出色的運動員及良好的態度。你知道我們上屆亞運拿到多少面獎牌嗎？

Interviewer:

How many?

多少面？

Peter Wong:

53! So, if you’re thinking of getting started with sports, go for it!

53 面！所以，如果你想開始參與運動，就去做吧！
