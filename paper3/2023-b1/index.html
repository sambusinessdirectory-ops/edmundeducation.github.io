Project Team memo

Project Team Memo

Reminders for the Live, Study, Cook Talent Competition

  1. What are the age limits for chefs on Live, Study, Cook? Chefs must be no younger than 14 and no older than 16 years old.

    參賽廚師須為十四至十六歲。

  2. Where should chefs get their recipe from? A recipe from the internet or a cookery book is fine, but the judges all prefer traditional family recipes. These are the most special.

    食譜可來自網絡或食譜書，但評判偏好傳統家庭食譜。

  3. What should chefs include in the video? As well as cooking their chosen recipe, chefs must clearly answer the following questions on the video: a) How much does each ingredient weigh? b) What alternative ingredients do you suggest?

    影片除示範烹調外，亦須清楚說明每種食材重量及可替代的食材。

  4. How much money should chefs spend on the ingredients? The limit is two hundred dollars for each recipe.

    每份食譜的食材開支上限為二百元。

  5. Is there anything that should not be used in the recipe? Yes - two types of products will result in disqualification: a) Pre-cooked ingredients, e.g. whole roast chicken, pie crusts. b) Alcoholic drinks, e.g. beer, wine.

    預先煮熟的食材和酒精飲品不可使用，否則會被取消資格。

Best Baked Potato Fries: William Puddle’s recipe

☆ William Puddle’s Best Baked Potato Fries Recipe ☆

This mouth-watering recipe is the ultimate comfort food. Serves 2 people.

這道令人垂涎的食譜是經典暖心美食，供兩人食用。

Ingredients

  • 3 large potatoes
  • Salt and pepper
  • Cooking oil – your choice!

食材：三個大馬鈴薯、鹽和胡椒，以及自選食用油。

Equipment

  • Sharp knife
  • Chopping board
  • Baking tray

用具：鋒利的刀、砧板和焗盤。

Instructions

Oven temperature

1.

Heat the oven to 220°C

預熱焗爐至 220°C。

Preparation

2.

Wash three large potatoes with cold water

用冷水清洗三個大馬鈴薯。

3.

Cut the potatoes into fries, about 1.5 cm thick

將馬鈴薯切成約 1.5 厘米厚的薯條。

4.

Put the fries on a baking tray

將薯條放在焗盤上。

5.

Pour over a little cooking oil

淋上少許食用油。

6.

Add salt and pepper

加入鹽和胡椒。

Cooking time

7.

Bake in the oven for 35 minutes

放入焗爐焗三十五分鐘。

Serving suggestion

8.

Serve with chili sauce

配辣椒醬食用。

What do you think about William’s recipe? Leave a comment below!

你覺得 William 的食譜怎樣？請在下方留言！

Our experts, Chef Christine and her team, will try your suggestions.

主廚 Christine 與團隊會試用你的建議。

The best suggestions will be included in the next update, so give it a try!

最佳建議會納入下一次更新，歡迎試試！
