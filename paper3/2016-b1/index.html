Email from Lara Gau to Indy Wong

To: Indy Wong
From: Lara Gau
Sent: Fri 8 April 2016 3:14 AM
Subject: Things to do

Hi Indy

Indy：

How are things in Hong Kong? Mexico is great! I’ll tell you all about it when I get back. While I am away, I’ve got three things I need you to do.

香港那邊如何？墨西哥很好！回來再告訴你。我不在期間，有三件事請你處理。

The first thing I want you to do is complete the 1980s Exhibition flyer. You might want to look at the Hong Kong Review and the minutes of the staff meeting as well as your notes from the podcast. Note that we got positive feedback in The Hong Kong Review so you should be able to find another quote from there to put at the top of the flyer (4 words).

首先完成 1980 年代展覽傳單。參考《香港評論》、員工會議紀錄和播客筆記。評論中有正面評語，請找出另一句四字引言放在傳單上方。

Secondly, you need to write the progress report for Dr Jones on the new museum café. Include the following information: the new name for the café, full details about the new location of the café, what work has been completed so far, including the children’s play area outside the café. I’ll forward you my emails with Mr Howard about this. You might want to look at the minutes of the last meeting as well as the Question Wizard results and Mr Lok’s interim report.

其次，為 Jones 博士撰寫新博物館咖啡室的進度報告，交代新名稱、新位置的完整資料，以及咖啡室和戶外兒童遊樂區的工程進度。可參考我和 Howard 先生的電郵、會議紀錄、問卷結果及 Lok 先生的中期報告。

Finally, can you write a letter to Ms Pollitti, the Careers Mistress at St Scritti’s School, and tell her about the part-time student assistant positions. We want to attract pupils from this excellent school. You will need to explain what a social history museum is as well as outline the details of the position. See also the minutes of the last meeting and the relevant page in the staff manual to help you. You should include positive feedback from previous student assistants to show why it will be a good experience.

最後請寫信給 St Scritti’s School 的就業輔導老師 Pollitti 女士，介紹兼職學生助理職位。解釋社會歷史博物館的性質及職位詳情，並引用過往助理的正面回饋。

Lara

Article from The Hong Kong Review

How we used to live in the 80s: The Hong Kong Social History Museum

Well, my immediate reaction when my editor told me to review a museum rather than my usual restaurant reviews was ‘Oh no! Not a boring old museum!’ But I had never been to the new Hong Kong Social History Museum, so I tried to keep an open mind....

編輯叫我評論博物館而非平常的餐廳時，我第一反應是「不要！無聊的舊博物館！」但我從未到訪新開的香港社會歷史博物館，於是嘗試保持開放心態。

The best part for me at the museum was the “Household of the 80s”. There are lots of rooms you can walk around and you get to try out different things. My favourite room was the “TV lounge”. I got to watch TV. Some old TV programmes I remembered watching when I was a kid. I also really enjoyed playing around on an old “Spectrum” computer and seeing if I could top my high scores on some old games.

最精彩的是「八十年代家居」展區。可以走進不同房間親身體驗。我最喜歡電視廳，看到童年看過的節目，也很享受玩舊式 Spectrum 電腦遊戲。

Another great thing about the Social History Museum is that it is really close to South Point MTR.

另一好處是博物館很靠近 South Point 港鐵站。

Overall, I have to say this was an excellent day out.

總括而言，這是很棒的一日遊。

Rating: ☀ ☀ ☀ ☀ ½

Minutes from museum staff meeting

Staff meeting of the Hong Kong Social History Museum

Date:
Tues. April 5, 2016
Venue:
Museum Seminar Room 2
Chairperson:
Dr Jack Jones, Director
In Attendance:
Limahl Howard, Building Management Director
Lara Gau, Public Relations and Retail
Xu Xinpeng, Facilities
Secretary:
Cindy Lau

Minutes of Meeting

  • Response to “How we used to live in the 1980s” Exhibition

    Dr Jones was pleased to announce that the exhibition has been very successful so far. The reviews in the newspapers have been very positive, especially about the household of the 1980s. Visitor feedback showed that the bathroom was the most popular room. People reported enjoying smelling the soaps and tasting the toothpaste as top highlights.

    Jones 博士表示展覽成績很好，報章尤其讚賞八十年代家居。訪客最喜歡浴室，特別是聞香皂和品嘗牙膏。

  • Update on the museum café

    Dr Jones was very concerned that Lara had not yet completed a progress report about the new café before leaving for Mexico. Lara said the final report would be completed by Indy.

    Jones 博士擔心 Lara 赴墨西哥前尚未完成新咖啡室進度報告；Lara 說由 Indy 完成。

    She informed the meeting that Minton’s had finished making the tables and chairs and that the expected delivery date was April 27. She added that Mr Lok, the builders, had promised to send an interim report later that day.

    Minton’s 已完成桌椅，預計 4 月 27 日送達；承建商 Lok 先生當天稍後會提交中期報告。

  • Part-time student assistants

    Dr Jones has agreed to the request to hire three part-time assistants this year. They will work four days a week from July to August.

    Jones 博士同意聘請三名兼職助理，於七至八月每週工作四天。

    Limahl enquired whether they would help on a daily basis in the café. The meeting rejected this idea but agreed for them to assist when it is very busy.

    會議否決讓他們每日在咖啡室工作的建議，但同意在繁忙時協助。

    It was agreed to increase the hourly rate to $38.

    時薪將增至 38 元。

  • Update on talk

    Dr Jones confirmed that he would give a talk at the museum. Exact date and time to be confirmed. He added that the talk would be about the changes in the traditional way of life on farms in the New Territories since the 1980s.

    Jones 博士會在館內演講，日期時間待定，主題是八十年代以來新界農場傳統生活方式的變化。
