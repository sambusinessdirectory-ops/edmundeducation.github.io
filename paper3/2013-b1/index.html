Part B · Situation

You are Marty Poon. You work for a magazine called Asia Life. You are an assistant to Casey Wong, the editor in charge of the magazine.

你是 Marty Poon，在《Asia Life》雜誌擔任編輯 Casey Wong 的助理。

You will listen to a recording of a podcast called Travel Report. In the podcast you will hear an interview with Adrian Lim and Kelly Johnson.

你將聽《Travel Report》播客訪問 Adrian Lim 和 Kelly Johnson。

Before the recording is played, you will have five minutes to study the Question-Answer Book and the Data File for Part B1 and the Question-Answer Book and the Data File for Part B2. Remember you must choose to do the tasks in either Part B1 or Part B2. Do NOT attempt both Parts B1 and B2.

播放錄音前有五分鐘閱讀 B1 和 B2 的問答冊與資料檔。乙部一與乙部二只可選一，不可兩部分都做。

Complete the tasks by following the instructions in the Question-Answer Book that you choose and on the recording. You will find all the information you need in the Question-Answer Book and Data File that you choose and on the recording. As you listen you can make notes on page 3 of the Data File.

按照選擇的問答冊及錄音指示完成任務。所需資料載於所選問答冊、資料檔和錄音；聆聽時可在資料檔第 3 頁記筆記。

You will have five minutes to familiarize yourself with the Part B Question-Answer Book and the Data File.

現在有五分鐘熟悉乙部問答冊與資料檔。

We’ve decided the topics for the next issue of the magazine and one of them is ‘Unusual Travel and Tourism’. There are three things I’d like you to do to help with the next issue.

我們已決定下期雜誌的主題，其中之一是「特色旅遊」。請協助完成以下三件事。

Fact file

資料小檔案

We’re going to post a Fun Holiday Fact File at the beginning of the next issue. Can you finish the rest of the information in the fact file? Look at your notes from the ‘Travel Report’ podcast, the statistics from the World Travel Organisation, the press conference excerpt from the International Airport Committee and the Travel Bloggers webpage. Remember to give each section a suitable heading.

我們會在下期開首刊出《Fun Holiday Fact File》。請補完內容：參考《Travel Report》筆記、世界旅遊組織統計、國際機場委員會記者會節錄和旅遊網誌；每節要加合適標題。

Email to Plaza Hotel

給 Plaza Hotel 的電郵

Kim Ross at the Plaza Hotel has sent us an advertisement to put in the next issue, but there are some problems with it. Please email him and tell him what he needs to change in the advertisement. Look at the minutes from the last sales meeting and the copy of the advertisement — the sales team has made some notes on it. Remind him about the submission deadline and the first payment date.

Plaza Hotel 的 Kim Ross 寄來下期廣告，但有幾個問題。請參考上次銷售會議紀錄及廣告上的批註，電郵告訴他需要修改的地方，並提醒交稿及首期付款日期。

Short feature article

短篇專題文章

We want to include a short feature article to introduce unusual hotels and encourage people to try them.

Please can you write the article and try to include the following information:

一個恰當而非單純「特色酒店」的標題；吸引人的開頭和結尾；描述三類特色酒店、所在地及旅客喜歡的原因。

You can look at the webpage for the Beacon Hotel, the travel bloggers webpage and I’ve also found some travel adverts which might help. Remember to take a look at your notes from the ‘Travel Report’ podcast too.

Let me know when you’re done.

Press conference excerpt from the International Airport Committee

World’s Best Airport Announced

Good morning everyone, thanks to everyone for coming this morning. The International Airport Committee is pleased to announce the winners of the Best International Airport Award for 2012. The competition was close this year, with some airports showing a big improvement on the figures from 2011. Congratulations go to Hong Kong for winning first prize. We all know the airport at Chek Lap Kok is always a popular choice with world travellers. Moving up from third place last year to second place this year is Singapore, very well done to them. Finally, we award Seoul’s Incheon airport in South Korea, which claims third place. A special thank you must go to all the world travellers who voted in this year’s competition and our hearty congratulations to the winners. Now, are there any questions?

各位早晨，謝謝出席。國際機場委員會公布 2012 年最佳國際機場獎。競爭激烈，部分機場較 2011 年進步甚大。香港赤鱲角機場獲第一名；新加坡由去年的第三名升至第二名；韓國首爾仁川機場獲第三名。感謝所有參與投票的旅客，恭喜得獎者。現在歡迎提問。

Travel bloggers webpage

File　Edit　View　Go　Bookmarks　Tools　Help

We asked some of you to ‘blog in’ and tell us why you go on holiday and lots of you have been in touch. You also sent us some hotel stories — some of you have stayed in some pretty strange places. Here’s a few excerpts for you all. Enjoy reading them and remember to send us your travel blog links. Happy travels!

我們邀請讀者分享旅遊原因和酒店故事，收到許多有趣的投稿。以下是節錄，歡迎繼續寄來旅遊網誌連結。

From Ice Queen, Spain

Why do I travel? To meet new people of course! I stayed in an ice hotel in Norway, it was brilliant. You’d think it’d be really cold, but it wasn’t. The staff made sure we had lots of warm coats to wear. Give it a try! But don’t forget — only open in winter!

我旅遊是為了認識新朋友！我住過挪威冰酒店，十分精彩。原以為很冷，但職員提供了很多保暖外套。值得試試，不過只在冬天開放。

From Roberto, Brazil

Me and some friends stayed in a prison hotel — it was a great place to stay because the history of the prison was really interesting. But the downside was that the rooms were really small and we could only have one person per room. All the hotel guests had to cook dinner together in the prison kitchen. It was an unforgettable meal. I travel because I like trying new food and this was one of the best meals I’ve ever had.

我和朋友住過監獄酒店。監獄歷史很有趣，但房間細小，每房只能住一人。所有住客在監獄廚房一同煮晚餐，非常難忘。我旅遊是為了嘗試新食物，這是我吃過最好的餐之一。

From Klara, Denmark

@ Ice Queen, I stayed in an ice hotel and I didn’t like it too much to tell you the truth. I think I’m just someone who likes normal traditional hotels. Nothing unusual for me thank you!

回覆 Ice Queen：老實說，我住過冰酒店卻不太喜歡。我還是偏愛普通傳統酒店。

From Ferby, Hong Kong

I travel because I love seeing new places. I slept in a lighthouse hotel in Scotland last year. It was a great place to watch the bird life. It’s expensive though — about HKD 1200 per person, per night.

我旅遊是因為喜歡看新地方。去年住過蘇格蘭燈塔酒店，很適合賞鳥；但價格昂貴，每人每晚約港幣 1,200 元。
