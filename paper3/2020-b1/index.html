Situation

You are Nico Lin. You work for Star Pod, an organisation that promotes space exploration and runs adventure camps for children. You are an assistant to the director of Star Pod, Ms Ellen Ochoa. Ms Ochoa has asked you to do some tasks.

你是 Nico Lin，在推廣太空探索並舉辦兒童歷奇營的 Star Pod 工作，是總監 Ellen Ochoa 女士的助理。她請你完成幾項工作。

You will listen to a recording of a Star Pod podcast in which Ellen Ochoa speaks to Captain Trillion Choi and Professor Marvin Adams. Take notes under the appropriate headings.

你將聆聽 Star Pod 的播客錄音，內容是 Ellen Ochoa 與 Trillion Choi 船長及 Marvin Adams 教授對談。請在適當標題下做筆記。

錄音播放前，你有五分鐘閱讀問答冊及資料檔，熟悉情境和任務。

Complete the tasks by following the instructions in the Question-Answer Book and on the recording. You will find all the information you need in the Question-Answer Book, the Data File and on the recording. As you listen you can make notes on Page 3 of the Data File.

按照問答冊及錄音中的指示完成任務。所需資料都在問答冊、資料檔及錄音內。聆聽時可在資料檔第 3 頁做筆記。

You now have five minutes to familiarise yourself with the Question-Answer Book and the Data File.

現在你有五分鐘熟悉問答冊及資料檔。

Letter from Mrs Sue Liu

28th March 2020

2020 年 3 月 28 日

To Whom it May Concern:

敬啟者：

I am writing to express my extreme disappointment with the Star Pod Adventure Camp programme my daughter Rey attended from 23rd to 27th March.

我的女兒 Rey 於 3 月 23 至 27 日參加 Star Pod 歷奇營，我對營地安排極度失望。

My concerns were first raised when my daughter told me she saw a dead dog in the swimming pool on her first day there. Can you imagine how disturbing and upsetting that is for an 11-year-old? Does no one clean the pool? How on earth could you miss a dead dog!

女兒說她第一天在泳池看到一隻死狗，令我非常擔憂。你能想像這對十一歲孩子有多麼可怕嗎？難道沒有人清理泳池？怎會看不見一隻死狗？

There was also the issue of the food. Rey said that she was given ‘Space Food’. How dare you feed children such terrible unhealthy junk food?

食物也有問題。Rey 說她吃了「太空食品」。你們怎可以給孩子吃這種不健康的垃圾食物？

Finally, can you please explain why my daughter was not allowed her turn in the Mars Experience Room? She is still upset about missing out on this experience.

最後，請解釋為何女兒未能輪到她進入火星體驗室；錯過體驗令她至今仍難過。

I would like you to answer all of the above as soon as possible.

請盡快答覆上述問題。

Yours, Sue Liu

此致，Sue Liu

Abstract of article in the Journal of Food and Health

Space Food: From NASA to popular high energy sports product

by Prof Kildare Au, Department of Food Health (University of Science and Technology, Hong Kong)

作者：香港科技大學食品健康學系 Kildare Au 教授

Abstract

In this article we review the popular food, Space Food. Space Food was originally designed by NASA to be eaten in space by astronauts. However, it is now sold to the public as a high energy food for sports activities such as running and hiking. After multiple tests it was found that Space Food is indeed a healthy food source if you are doing exercise.

本文回顧廣受歡迎的太空食品。它原由 NASA 設計，供太空人在太空食用，如今以高能量食品形式售予公眾，供跑步、遠足等運動使用。多次測試發現，做運動時食用它確實是健康的能量來源。

Minutes of Management Committee meeting

Date:
30th March, 2020
Venue:
Meeting Room 101
Chairperson:
Ms Ellen Ochoa — Director of Star Pod
In Attendance:
Ms Sunita Chawla — Senior Instructor and Course Organiser; Mr Bowie Ma — Manager of Star Pod Adventure Camp

Changes to Emergency Procedures

Ms Chawla informed the members that she had been contacted by the Lady Waglan Memorial Hospital. The hospital will be closed for renovations till August this year. Therefore, any emergency cases requiring immediate hospitalization should be taken to the Tsing Shan Hospital instead.

Chawla 女士表示，Lady Waglan Memorial Hospital 將因裝修關閉至今年八月，需即時住院的緊急個案應改送往 Tsing Shan Hospital。

Ms Chawla also confirmed the appointment of Dr Khatun and Mr Leung. This will mean that there will be a doctor and/or a nurse present at the adventure camp 24 hours a day for non-emergency situations.

Chawla 女士確認已聘用 Khatun 醫生及 Leung 先生，歷奇營將有醫生或護士全天候處理非緊急情況。

Food and Beverages

Mr Ma said that he was concerned about food allergies and that he had not seen it mentioned in the FAQ.

Ma 先生擔心食物過敏問題，並指出常見問題尚未提及。

Action to be taken: Inform parents in the FAQ that if a child has food allergies or requires a special diet we must be informed before camp begins.

待辦事項：在常見問題告知家長，如孩子有食物過敏或需特別飲食，必須在開營前通知。

Mars Experience Room

Ms Chawla announced some changes to the Mars Experience Room in May compared to in March. The length of time given to the children is to be extended to 15 minutes. The number of rock samples they need to find remains unchanged.

Chawla 女士宣布五月火星體驗室會較三月作出調整：每位孩子的體驗時間延長至十五分鐘，須尋找的岩石樣本數量不變。

A new feature in the Mars Experience Room is the dust storm (which we will do with lights and a big fan). The children will be told that if there is a dust storm they must evacuate the room immediately.

新增沙塵暴環節（以燈光和大風扇模擬）；孩子會獲告知一旦遇上沙塵暴，須立即離開房間。

Ms Chawla explained the reason why one child, Rey Liu, missed her turn in the Mars Experience Room. The girl, she explained, was hiding and playing on her phone. Staff found her after the Mars Experience Room activity had finished.

Chawla 女士解釋 Rey Liu 錯過輪候，是因她躲起來玩手機；活動結束後職員才找到她。

Action to be taken: Invite the girl to attend the Open Day (date to be confirmed). Tell her that she can use the Mars Experience Room then.

待辦事項：邀請 Rey 出席日期待定的開放日，屆時可體驗火星體驗室。

Dust Storms
