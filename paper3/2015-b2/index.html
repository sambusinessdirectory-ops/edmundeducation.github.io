Situation

You are Starsky Chung. You work for North Star Artists, an international agency that manages a range of pop music bands and singers.

你是 Starsky Chung，在管理流行樂隊與歌手的國際經理人公司 North Star Artists 工作。

Your boss is Malcolm Parker, who works in the London office of North Star Artists. He is sending a British pop band called Bubblegum to Hong Kong to do some promotions for their next CD. You need to look after them while they are here.

上司 Malcolm Parker 在倫敦辦公室工作，將派英國樂隊 Bubblegum 來港宣傳新唱片；你須照顧樂隊在港行程。

You will listen to a Skype conference call between Malcolm Parker, a music video director - Eduardo de Luca - and yourself.

你將聆聽 Malcolm Parker、音樂錄影帶導演 Eduardo de Luca 和你之間的 Skype 電話會議。

Before the recording is played, you will have five minutes to study the Question-Answer Book and the Data File to familiarize yourself with the situation and the tasks.

錄音前有五分鐘閱讀問答冊和資料檔，熟悉情境及任務。

Complete the tasks by following the instructions in the Question-Answer Book and on the recording. You will find all the information you need in the Question-Answer Book, the Data File and on the recording. As you listen you can make notes on page 3 of the Data File.

依照問答冊和錄音指示完成任務。資料見問答冊、資料檔和錄音；聆聽時可在資料檔第 3 頁記筆記。

You now have five minutes to familiarize yourself with the Question-Answer Book and the Data File.

現在有五分鐘熟悉問答冊與資料檔。

我已讀過 Ma 女士回覆你有關 5 月 19 日在 The Peak Sky Deck 拍攝的查詢。請電郵回答她的疑問及顧慮，說服她批准。

We also have to deal with all the negative publicity surrounding Mark Nelson over that incident with the photographer from Gossip Week. Can you write a short article for the Hong Kong fans’ website? Start with a brief summary of the incident, then respond to what Gossip Week claims happened. Finish the article by briefly describing similar incidents that have happened to other celebrities. And, please don’t quote directly. We want this article to be factual, not “he said, she said”.

我們還需處理 Mark Nelson 與《Gossip Week》攝影師事件引起的負面宣傳。請為香港粉絲網站寫短文：先簡述事件，再回應雜誌的指稱，最後簡述其他名人遭遇的類似事件。不要直接引述；文章應以事實為主。

Malcolm

Article from The Hong Kong Economic Times

Hong Kong’s landmarks still not on the tourist map

A survey recently conducted by the Hong Kong Tourism Working Group, in which international tourists transiting through Hong Kong’s airport were asked to identify world-famous landmarks, has shown that Hong Kong is still not on the map for many overseas tourists. The results below speak for themselves.

香港旅遊工作小組最近詢問途經香港機場的國際旅客能否認出世界知名地標。結果顯示，許多海外旅客仍不熟悉香港地標。

TABLE 1: Most recognized landmarks in major world cities
LandmarkCityCountry% of respondents who recognized itWhere did they recognize it from?
Statue of LibertyNew YorkUSA98%TV, movies and music videos
The Great Wall of ChinaBeijingChina94%TV, movies and music videos
Tsim Sha Tsui Clock TowerHong KongChina31%tourist brochures
The Peak Sky DeckHong KongChina27%tourist brochures

地標辨認率：自由女神像 98%、萬里長城 94%、尖沙咀鐘樓 31%、山頂觀景台 27%。前兩者主要從電視、電影和音樂錄影帶認識；香港兩個地標則主要從旅遊小冊子認識。

SOURCE: © The Hong Kong Tourism Working Group

Commenting on the results at a press conference held last week, spokesman for the Hong Kong Tourism Working Group, Harold Tam, gave his views on the findings of the survey.

小組發言人 Harold Tam 上週在記者會評論調查結果。

“The results are disappointing. It shows us that unlike New York, Hong Kong does not have its own equivalent of the Statue of Liberty. The question we need to ask ourselves is how can we make our landmarks known around the world?”

他指出結果令人失望，香港沒有像紐約自由女神像那樣舉世知名的地標，值得思考如何讓世界認識本港地標。

另一項訪問最近購買外國機票的歐美旅客的調查，問他們最喜歡外遊時哪些活動。

Article from Gossip Week magazine

Nelson Leaves His Mark on our Photographer

Mad Mark’s done it this time as he leaves one of our photographers in the gutter along with his reputation

By Bob Murdoch (London) 14th March 2015 8:35pm GMT

Mark Nelson of the hip new pop band Bubblegum will have something to chew on over the next few weeks.

新晉樂隊 Bubblegum 的 Mark Nelson，未來幾週恐怕有事要煩惱。

According to one of our Gossip Week photographers, Ruari King, Nelson went for him like a vicious animal yesterday.

《Gossip Week》攝影師 Ruari King 聲稱 Nelson 昨天像猛獸般向他撲來。

“I was down in Notting Hill taking some photos of the celebrities that live there. Then I saw that Mark from Bubblegum, so I went over to take his picture. He went crazy and pushed me over,” King said.

King 說他在倫敦 Notting Hill 拍名人照片，看到 Mark 後上前拍照，Mark 突然發狂把他推倒。

The latest victim of celebrity violence, King is now worried about the physical harm Nelson has done to him.

King 稱自己是名人暴力事件的最新受害者，擔心 Nelson 對他造成的傷害。

“He’s done me some real serious injuries. Violent people like him are a danger to honest hard working people like myself. My legs are so sore that I can’t walk.”

他說自己傷勢嚴重，雙腿痛得無法步行，並指控 Nelson 危害辛勤工作的普通人。

Extract of transcript of UK news programme A Closer Look at the Week’s News

REPORTER

Well, if you’ve been following the newspapers this week, one big story that everyone is talking about is the violent incidents involving celebrities and the paparazzi. TV actor Bernie Lee is still recovering from one incident he had with a photographer while he and his wife were trying to enjoy a quiet meal in a seafood restaurant in South London.

本週報章焦點之一，是名人與狗仔隊的暴力事件。演員 Bernie Lee 仍在康復：他和太太於倫敦南部餐廳用餐時與攝影師發生衝突。

BERNIE LEE

Well, urm, we had just finished our meal and erm in, in runs this photographer and starts to take pictures, click, click, click. When I, I tried... tried to make the man leave, urm, he... he pushed the camera into my face and... Ouch!... he cuts my nose with the camera. I wouldn’t mind but I had a photo shoot the next day I had to cancel.

我們剛吃完，攝影師衝進來不停拍照。我叫他離開，他把相機推到我臉前，割傷鼻子，令我翌日的拍攝工作也要取消。

POLICE SERGEANT ARNOLD WILLIAMS

We are becoming increasingly concerned about how photographers conduct themselves. Yes. Particularly around celebrities. Some feel vulnerable. The singer Donna Rae for example. The poor woman felt so threatened... so afraid. Especially when she was followed late at night. She has called us on several occasions.

我們越來越關注攝影師的行為，特別是他們對名人的做法。有些名人感到脆弱；歌手 Donna Rae 深夜被跟蹤後非常害怕，曾多次報警。

DONNA RAE

I had to call. Had to. These photographers, you know, they make me so angry, so angry. I called because I felt I needed protection. I’m frightened to leave the house. Ah, it’s an outrage.

我不得不報警。他們令我非常憤怒；我需要保護，現在害怕出門。

REPORTER

In a worrying development photographers seem to be deliberately making it look like celebrities are attacking them. As can be seen in the recent case of Mark Nelson from the pop band Bubblegum.

更令人擔憂的是，有攝影師似乎故意營造名人攻擊自己的假象，最近 Bubblegum 的 Mark Nelson 一案就是例子。

MARK NELSON

It was shockin’. I was just walkin’ near my home, havin’ a bit of a chat to my girlfriend on my mobile and, like, this photographer comes up right into my face with his big camera flashin’ away. Then before I know it - whhaammm! - he falls over. Total idiot. Wasn’t looking. Walkin’ backwards takin’ pictures like that - of course, he’s goin’ to trip over. So what do I do? I try to do the right thing, you know, give him a hand, like, to help him up. But then, then he starts shoutin’ at me, shoutin’ and sayin’ I pushed him. Before I know it, he’s back on his feet, runnin’ around and takin’ pictures of me again. Unbelievable!

我在家附近邊走邊和女友通電話，一名攝影師把相機貼到我面前不停閃光。他倒著走邊拍照，自己絆倒。我伸手幫他站起來，他卻大叫說我推他；轉眼又站起來四處拍我，真難以置信。
