Minutes of the Victoria Cup Committee meeting

Date and time:
Monday April 26th, 2021 at 2.00 pm
Venue:
Meeting Room 3b, Events Horizon Main Office
Present:
Tina Tong (TT); Sana Javed (SJ); Anthony Au (AA); Samuel Lo (SL)

1. Confirmation of minutes

2. Charity donations

TT gave a brief presentation outlining the characteristics of the two charities Refugee Sanctuary and Changing Young Lives. A lengthy discussion ensued regarding the relative merits of both.

TT 簡介 Refugee Sanctuary 和 Changing Young Lives 兩間慈善機構的特點，委員其後詳細討論兩者優劣。

SJ proposed we give both of them 50 complimentary tickets. She commented that it would show goodwill. All agreed that this would be a nice gesture.

SJ 建議各送五十張贈票，以示善意；眾人同意。

3. Volunteer Ball Kids

SJ informed the committee that having reflected on the safety issues and possible PR ramifications, the volunteer ball kids should be at least 11 years old. The committee agreed.

SJ 指出，考慮安全及公關影響，義務拾球員應至少十一歲；委員會同意。

4. Corporate sponsor tickets

SL brought to our attention public discontent with the number of corporate tickets last year for our sponsors and suggested restricting the number of such tickets this year.

SL 提及公眾不滿去年給贊助商的企業門票數量，建議今年限制張數。

AA opined that this would be very difficult to do because tickets had already been issued. He also pointed out that only 12% of tickets went to corporate sponsors.

AA 認為難以實行，因門票已派發；他亦指出只有百分之十二的門票給了企業贊助商。

SJ proposed a condition of punctuality. He specified that corporate sponsor ticketholders should lose their ticket if they had not arrived within 60 minutes of the start of play. All agreed that this measure would be harsh but fair.

SJ 建議加上準時條款：企業贊助商持票人在開賽後六十分鐘仍未到場便失去門票。眾人認為雖嚴格但公平。

5. Fanzone

SJ provided an update on the Fanzone preparations. She reported that she had contacted the All England Lawn Tennis Club regarding their use of the Fanzone and ticket system there. SJ proposed adopting the same system at the 2021 Victoria Cup so that even more real fans can attend. The meeting unanimously accepted the proposal.

SJ 匯報球迷區準備工作。她已聯絡全英草地網球俱樂部，了解其球迷區與票務制度，並建議 2021 Victoria Cup 採用相同做法，讓更多真正球迷到場；會議一致通過。

Next meeting to be held on Fri. 14 May at 2.00 pm.

下次會議定於 5 月 14 日星期五下午二時。

There being no other business, the meeting finished at 4.25 pm.

再無其他事項，會議於下午四時二十五分結束。

Letter to the Editor of the Hong Kong Press & Journal from Ms. Geeta Singh

24th April, 2021

Dear Sir/Madam,

敬啟者：

I am writing in regard to your article about the 2021 Victoria Cup which appeared in your newspaper on 21 April as I am keen for the organisers to avoid the mistakes they made last year regarding ticketing. First of all, last year, I was annoyed to find that the majority of the seats were reserved for corporate sponsors and their guests. As well as being unfair, I can also testify that a good number of these corporate sponsor seats are invariably left empty because they don’t show up.

我就貴報 4 月 21 日刊登的 2021 Victoria Cup 文章致函，希望主辦方避免重犯去年票務上的錯誤。去年大多數座位預留給企業贊助商及其賓客，令我不滿。這既不公平，而且不少贊助商座位因持票人沒有到場而空置。

Another issue last year was that us real fans could not get a hold of tickets whilst the people who actually got the tickets weren’t even real tennis fans. Ironically, it is exactly these people who end up talking through the matches and putting players off. I would like to know what Events Horizon intends to do about this type of spectator this year. We can’t go on like previous years when the stewards have been too scared to say anything.

另一問題是，真正球迷買不到票，持票者卻未必喜歡網球。諷刺的是，這些人往往在比賽期間不斷說話，令球員分心。我想知道 Events Horizon 今年會如何處理這類觀眾。不能再像往年一樣，讓工作人員怕得不敢出聲。

For the benefit of the Hong Kong public and for the health of the sport in general, I urge Events Horizon to address these issues.

為了香港市民及網球運動的長遠發展，我敦促 Events Horizon 正視這些問題。

Yours faithfully,

此致敬禮，

Geeta Singh

Transcript of phone conversation between Sana Javed and Leon Barnes

Date: Mon, April 12, 2021

Leon:

Hello. This is Leon Barnes from the All England Lawn Tennis Club at Wimbledon.

你好，我是溫布頓全英草地網球俱樂部的 Leon Barnes。

Sana:

Hi Leon, this is Sana Javed from Events Horizon. We’re organising the Victoria Cup.

你好 Leon，我是 Events Horizon 的 Sana Javed。我們正籌辦 Victoria Cup。

Leon:

Oh yes, hi. I was expecting your call.

噢，你好。我正等你的電話。

Sana:

Just so you know, I’m recording the call so that I can transcribe it to share the information with my colleagues. Is that ok?

先讓你知道，我會錄下通話並謄成逐字稿，與同事分享資料，可以嗎？

Leon:

Yes, no problem.

可以，沒問題。

Sana:

Ok, so, I’m calling to pick your brains about on-the-day ticketing. I wonder how you handle the demand from the public for the biggest matches at Wimbledon. In Hong Kong we have a Fanzone in Victoria Park, where fans can catch the action on the large screen, but to be honest it hasn’t been a success. Last year there were really barely any fans there.

我想請教你們如何處理當日門票，以及溫布頓熱門賽事的公眾需求。香港維多利亞公園有球迷區，球迷可看大螢幕直播，但坦白說成效不佳，去年幾乎沒有球迷。

Leon:

Right, well, at Wimbledon, we’ve found that the trick is to, you know, assure the fans that if they turn up on the day, they’ll have a chance to get a seat. You’d be surprised how many no-shows we get. Honestly, especially from the corporate sponsors. There’s no reason to waste a good seat, that’s what we say. So we give those tickets away to fans.

在溫布頓，我們會讓球迷知道：當日到場仍有機會入座。缺席者比你想像中多，尤其是企業贊助商。我們不想浪費好座位，便把門票給球迷。

Sana:

Wow. That sounds very good and everything but surely it causes a stampede!

聽起來很好，但這樣不會造成爭相搶票嗎？

Leon:

Not quite! I mean, as the fans enter, I suppose you could say what is the equivalent of your “Fanzone” at Wimbledon, they’re given a ticket number as they come in. Our staff keep track of any seats that are not claimed and release them to the fans that have the lowest ticket numbers once it’s confirmed that the seats won’t be occupied.

不會。球迷進入相當於你們球迷區的地方時會獲發號碼。工作人員追蹤未有人認領的座位，確認座位不會有人使用後，按號碼順序把票給球迷。

Sana:

Right, well thanks for the info. The use of the Fanzone’s an agenda item at our next committee meeting, so what you’ve just told will be useful for our discussion. I’m sure it’ll help.

謝謝你提供資料。下次委員會會議會討論球迷區的使用，你剛才說的對討論很有幫助。

Leon:

No problem. Good luck and do let me know how it goes.

不用客氣。祝順利，記得告訴我結果。

Bon Accord News article · 22 April 2021

Exclusive: Sponsor Joins Chorus of Criticism of Victoria Cup Advertising Campaign

Our reporter understands that Sportrite are considering withdrawing their corporate sponsorship of the Victoria Cup following a controversial poster campaign promoting the event. The poster in question featured an image of Suzie Gilmour, ranked number 80 in the world, posing according to one insider, “like an amateur model”. A spokesman for the company, Tim Lewis, revealed that the sponsors had made their expectations very clear to organisers Events Horizon:

本報得悉，Sportrite 正考慮因宣傳海報爭議而撤回對 Victoria Cup 的企業贊助。海報中世界排名第 80 的 Suzie Gilmour 擺出被一名知情者形容為「像業餘模特兒」的姿勢。公司發言人 Tim Lewis 說，贊助商已向主辦方 Events Horizon 清楚表明期望：

“We have made it clear to Events Horizon that this was certainly not who we expected to be on the poster. We expected the highest-ranking player.” Mr Lewis added, “We requested a photo of the player in action on the tennis court, not posing like some sort of high fashion supermodel. At present we certainly don’t think the poster is aligned with our company’s aim and vision.”

Lewis 先生表示，他們預期海報上的是排名最高的球員，並要求使用球員在網球場比賽中的照片，而非像時裝模特兒般擺姿勢。他們認為現有海報與公司的目標及理念不符。

Interview with singer Summer Li from Sonic Attack online magazine

Sonic Attack Reporter:

Summer, you have been known in the past for speaking up against what upsets you from the things you see. What’s upset you recently?

Summer，你以往會公開談論令你不滿的事。最近有甚麼令你不滿？

Summer:

A good question. Lots of things. Oh I know, a poster I just saw for the Victoria Cup.

好問題。很多事。噢，我知道了，就是剛看到的 Victoria Cup 海報。

Sonic Attack Reporter:

Really? I think I know which one you mean. What upset you most about it?

真的嗎？我想我知道是哪張。最令你不滿的是甚麼？

Summer:

What upset me even more than the image on the poster was the slogan.

比海報圖片更令我不滿的是口號。

Sonic Attack Reporter:

What was the slogan again? Something like “look like a woman, play like a man”?

口號是甚麼來着？好像是「look like a woman, play like a man」？

Summer:

Yes. I can barely repeat it. It makes me so angry.

對。我幾乎說不出口，實在令我生氣。

Sonic Attack Reporter:

Why? Why does it annoy you so much?

為甚麼？為何那麼令你反感？

Summer:

Well first of all, women shouldn’t feel that they have to look a certain way.

首先，女性不應覺得自己必須有某一種外貌。

Sonic Attack Reporter:

Yeah, totally.

對，完全同意。

Summer:

I mean, to fit this narrow mainstream perception of beauty. You know the usual one we’re bombarded with. Plus, there’s no reason in the world why women tennis players should aspire to play like men.

我的意思是，女性不必迎合狹隘的主流審美標準。而且，女子網球選手沒有理由要以「像男性般打球」為目標。

Sonic Attack Reporter:

Yeah I know, that’s such a lame thing to put.
