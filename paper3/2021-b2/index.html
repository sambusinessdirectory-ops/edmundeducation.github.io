Tam writing in regard to your article about the 2021 Victoria Cup which appeared in your newspaper on 21 April as I am keen for the organisers to avoid the mistakes they made last year Tegarding ticketing, First of all, last year, I was annoyed to find that the majority of the seats were Teserved for co rporate sponsors and their guests.
