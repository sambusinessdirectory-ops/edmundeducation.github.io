Foreword from the Exotic Pet Owner’s Guide

Foreword

Hello there! Thanks for buying my book. So what is an exotic pet then? Well, it’s any animal which is wild but is looked after by a person. I am often asked which exotic pet is the best to get. From my personal experience, I would say non-poisonous snakes. You can get them for your kids — a small snake is usually perfectly safe. My son got his first snake when he was six years old. He loved it. Another popular exotic pet is the tree frog. These can come in all the colours of the rainbow. Tree frogs are my particular passion as you will see!

Read on!

Scott ‘Rats’ Fink, 2012

Extract from an interview with Vicky Wong and Kit Poon

Interviewer: Vicky, you are the CEO of HKComputerPets.com. What is it exactly?

Vicky: Well, basically we run a website for virtual pets.

Interviewer: So, first things first, what is a virtual pet?

Vicky: Well, essentially it’s a pet that isn’t real.

Interviewer: Okay, and how can I get a virtual pet? Vicky: Oh, that’s a simple thing to do — you just go online to a virtual pet website and create an account.

Interviewer: Right. So what are the advantages of a virtual pet compared to a real one?

Vicky: Well, probably the most important is that it’s free. You don’t have to buy food or toys like you do for a real animal.

Interviewer: Now, Kit, you have a virtual pet, which is a tree frog. Is that right? Kit: Yes, I’ve had it for ten months.

Interviewer: And why do you have a virtual pet? Kit: My Dad said it was good for me to take responsibility. And I already have — like I need to remember to feed him and how to look after him. And my Dad said as well it was a good idea for me to have a virtual one before a real one. Interviewer: And?

Kit: Well, he says he’ll buy me a real one if I can look after my virtual tree frog for a year. Vicky: That’s great! We think of this as practice for having a real pet.

Interviewer: And Kit, what’s your favourite thing you can do on the website?

Kit: Well, I don’t know if it’s my favourite thing but I can send my pet or other pets on the site a birthday card! It’s really funny.
