Part B

Situation

You are Sammy Tsang. You work for a board game company called Griffin Workshop in Hong Kong. It is a small company set up by some friends. It is now working on its latest board game Escape to Hong Kong Island. Your boss, Angel Fu, has asked you to do some tasks.

你是 Sammy Tsang，在香港桌上遊戲公司 Griffin Workshop 工作。公司由一群朋友創辦，規模不大，現正開發最新桌遊《Escape to Hong Kong Island》。你的上司 Angel Fu 指派了幾項工作。

You will listen to a recording of the weekly podcast programme The Classical. In the podcast you will hear an interview with Angel Fu and Eric Park, a well-known board game designer. Take notes under the appropriate headings.

你將聆聽每週播出的播客節目《The Classical》，內容是 Angel Fu 與知名桌遊設計師 Eric Park 的訪問。請按標題記筆記。

Before the recording is played, you will have five minutes to study the Question-Answer Book and the Data File to familiarise yourself with the situation and the tasks.

錄音播放前，你有五分鐘閱讀問答冊和資料檔，熟悉情境與任務。

Complete the tasks by following the instructions in the Question-Answer Book and on the recording. You will find all the information you need in the Question-Answer Book, the Data File and on the recording. As you listen, you can make notes on page 3 of the Data File.

按照問答冊和錄音指示完成任務。所有所需資料均見於問答冊、資料檔和錄音；聆聽時可在資料檔第 3 頁記筆記。

You now have five minutes to familiarise yourself with the Question-Answer Book and the Data File.

現在有五分鐘熟悉問答冊和資料檔。

Dear Sammy

Sammy：

Could you please do the following three things for me?

請替我完成以下三件事：

  • We have received an invitation to MegaGame Conference, a games conference for board game designers. Please complete the application form for our company. You can look at the Skype chat between me and Mabel about this and the email exchange between me, Tommy and Mabel. You can also listen to me on The Classical podcast.

    我們收到桌遊設計師會議 MegaGame Conference 的邀請。請為公司填寫報名表。可參考我和 Mabel 的 Skype 對話、我與 Tommy 和 Mabel 的電郵，以及《The Classical》播客。

  • We want to ask Paul Achebe, a well-known game reviewer, to do a video review for our game Escape to Hong Kong Island for YouTube. He reviewed our other game last year. Write him an email. In the email, ask him if he wants to do the video review and briefly describe the game for him. Remember to mention the target audience of the game. You can look at the BoardGameFan forum and listen to the podcast to help you.

    我們想邀請知名遊戲評論員 Paul Achebe 為《Escape to Hong Kong Island》製作 YouTube 影片評論。他去年曾評論我們的另一款遊戲。請寫電郵邀請他，簡介遊戲並說明目標玩家。可參考 BoardGameFan 討論區及播客。

  • We tested Escape to Hong Kong Island with a group of people on Monday and gave them a questionnaire afterwards. Write a report for our company meeting next week. Summarize the results of the questionnaire and what the problems with the game were. Then report on what has been done and what follow-up action hasn’t been done yet. Look at the questionnaire results and the WhatsApp Escape to Hong Kong Island group messages to help you.

    我們星期一找了一組人試玩《Escape to Hong Kong Island》，之後請他們填問卷。請為下週公司會議寫報告，概述問卷結果和遊戲問題，再說明哪些跟進工作已完成、哪些尚未完成。可參考問卷和 WhatsApp 群組訊息。

Thanks
Angel

Questionnaire results from testing of Escape to Hong Kong Island

1. Overall do you like the game?

  • Yes — 83%
  • It’s OK — 12%
  • No — 5%

整體喜愛程度：喜歡 83%；還可以 12%；不喜歡 5%。

2. Is the game easy to learn?

  • No — 82%
  • Quite easy — 8%
  • Yes — 10%

是否容易學懂：不容易 82%；頗容易 8%；容易 10%。留言：「規則書需要更多例子。」

Comments
“Rulebook needs more examples.”

3. Do you like the artwork on the cards?

  • No — 79%
  • It’s OK — 14%
  • Yes — 7%

是否喜歡卡牌插畫：不喜歡 79%；還可以 14%；喜歡 7%。留言：「目標是青少年，不是小學生！」

Comments
“Meant to be for teenagers not primary children!”

4. Is the map clear?

  • Not clear — 91%
  • More or less — 5%
  • Very clear — 4%

地圖是否清晰：不清晰 91%；還可以 5%；非常清晰 4%。留言：「字體太小！」、「紅色文字看不清。」

Comments
“The font size is far too small!!!”
“You can’t see the red text.”
