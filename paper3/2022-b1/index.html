Interview with Neon Master Rick Lai in Hong Kong Arts and Crafts Magazine

We spoke with Rick Lai, Hong Kong’s top lighting designer, who works for the award- winning Shiny Diamond Lighting Ltd.

Hong Kong Arts and Crafts: What do you enjoy most about your work?

Rick Lai: There are many aspects to the job I enjoy but what I love most is taking a customer’s design and turning it into a neon sign. It’s quite a complicated process. Basically, in the planning stage, when we receive the design, we use a grid. Hong Kong Arts and Crafts: Why do you do that?

Rick Lai: We use it to decide how many glass tubes we need. That’s all before we start making the sign using a machine called the Glass Heater.

Hong Kong Arts and Crafts: What does that do?

Rick Lai: We use it to bend the glass tubes. We then have another machine which cleans the glass tubes.

Hong Kong Arts and Crafts: How does it get them clean?

Rick Lai: It uses electricity. Shocking, right? Also, whenever we move the glass tubes, they can be broken very easily so we have to be very, very careful with them. That’s the boring part for me, because I like to work quickly! Finally, we are also very careful to test if the tubes are working before we move on to the display stage. One time, a customer returned a faulty sign. It was so embarrassing!

Hong Kong Arts and Crafts: What is the future for lighting design in Hong Kong?

Rick Lai: It’s very bright! We are looking for trainees at the moment, so if any of your readers are interested, please apply to be a trainee. Hong Kong Arts and Crafts: How can they apply?

Rick Lai: The instructions will be in recruitment emails we are sending out soon. One thing to pay attention to, though: applicants must send us 4 or 5 photos of their previous work. That way, we can really get a feel for their ability.

香港工藝美術雜誌專訪霓虹燈大師賴克強
我們訪問了香港頂級燈光設計師Rick Lai，他任職於屢獲殊榮的閃亮鑽石照明有限公司。
香港工藝美術：您的工作最喜歡什麼？
Rick Lai：我喜歡這份工作的許多方面，但我最喜歡的是接受客戶的設計並將其變成霓虹燈標誌。這是一個相當複雜的過程。基本上，在規劃階段，當我們收到設計時，我們使用網格。香港工藝美術：為什麼要這麼做？
Rick Lai：我們用它來決定需要多少玻璃管。這就是我們開始使用稱為玻璃加熱器的機器製作標誌之前的全部內容。
香港工藝美術：那是做什麼用的？
Rick Lai：我們用它來彎曲玻璃管。然後我們有另一台機器來清潔玻璃管。
香港工藝品：如何讓它們變乾淨？
Rick Lai：它用電。令人震驚，對吧？另外，每當我們移動玻璃管時，它們很容易破裂，因此我們必須非常非常小心地對待它們。這對我來說是無聊的部分，因為我喜歡快速工作！最後，在進入展示階段之前，我們也非常仔細地測試電子管是否正常運作。有一次，一位顧客退回了一張有缺陷的標誌。太尷尬了！
香港工藝美術：香港燈飾設計的未來如何？
Rick Lai：非常亮！我們目前正在尋找實習生，所以如果您的讀者有興趣，請申請成為實習生。香港工藝美術：如何申請？
Rick Lai：相關說明將包含在我們即將發出的招募電子郵件中。但需要注意一件事：申請者必須向我們發送 4 或 5 張他們之前作品的照片。這樣，我們才能真正感受到他們的能力。
