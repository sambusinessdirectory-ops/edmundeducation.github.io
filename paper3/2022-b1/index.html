Part B

Situation

You are Nico Lin. You work for Shiny Diamond Lighting Ltd, a company that designs, makes and sells neon lighting products and services. Your work involves writing and editing texts for company use. Your boss, Floyd Mann, has asked you to do some tasks.

你是 Nico Lin，在設計、製造和銷售霓虹燈產品及服務的 Shiny Diamond Lighting Ltd 工作，負責撰寫和編輯公司文字。上司 Floyd Mann 指派你一些工作。

You will listen to a podcast of Wish You Were Here, an arts and culture radio programme on Hong Kong Radio. On the podcast you will hear Julia Din interview Pris Fong, owner of Shiny Diamond Lighting Ltd, and her chief designer and neon artist Rick Lai.

你將聆聽香港電台文化藝術節目《Wish You Were Here》的播客。主持 Julia Din 訪問公司東主 Pris Fong、首席設計師兼霓虹藝術家 Rick Lai。

Before the recording is played, you will have five minutes to study the Question-Answer Book and the Data File to familiarise yourself with the situation and the tasks.

錄音前有五分鐘閱讀問答冊和資料檔，熟悉情境與任務。

Complete the tasks by following the instructions in the Question-Answer Book and on the recording. You will find all the information you need in the Question-Answer Book, the Data File and on the recording. As you listen you can make notes on page 3 of the Data File.

請依問答冊及錄音指示完成任務。所有所需資料都在問答冊、資料檔和錄音中；可在資料檔第 3 頁記筆記。

You now have five minutes to familiarise yourself with the Question-Answer Book and the Data File.

To: Nico Lin

From: Floyd Mann
Subject: Things to do

Dear Nico,

Nico：

Busy week I’m afraid. As Public Relations Officer, can you apply your magic with words and get onto the following things ASAP?

恐怕這週很忙。作為公關主任，請發揮你的文字功力，盡快處理以下工作。

First, write an email to the students at the Hong Kong Design School to encourage them to apply to join Shiny Diamond Lighting Ltd as trainees. Write it so that it gets their interest and is clear about how they should apply. Listen to the Wish You Were Here podcast, and look at the company handbook and Rick Lai’s interview in the Hong Kong Arts and Crafts magazine for details.

先寫電郵給香港設計學校的學生，鼓勵他們申請成為公司的培訓生。內容要吸引他們，並清楚說明申請方法。可參考播客、公司手冊和《Hong Kong Arts and Crafts》對 Rick Lai 的訪問。

Next, Pris wants us to make a how-to leaflet to be given out to customers when they visit our neon light factory. I have put in the pictures, but you will need to finish the text. Use full sentences and don’t use bullet points! Take a look at the company handbook and Rick Lai’s interview in the Hong Kong Arts and Crafts magazine.

接着，Pris 想製作參觀霓虹燈工廠時派發給客人的製作指南。我已放入圖片，請你完成文字。要用完整句子，不要用項目符號！參考公司手冊和 Rick Lai 的訪問。

Finally, we need to update the webpage for our products. Look at the inventory from the previous stock take and the company handbook. Rick Lai and Pris Fong also discuss some product details on the Wish You Were Here podcast.

最後，我們需要更新產品網頁。參考上次盤點清單、公司手冊，以及播客中 Rick Lai 和 Pris Fong 談及的產品資料。

I’ve noticed customers discussing our products in the Customer Comments section of our website. That might help you with some of these tasks. Rick Lai and Pris Fong were also discussing all of these tasks on the Management WhatsApp group — I’ll forward the messages to you.

我注意到客人在網站留言區討論產品，或許對部分任務有幫助。Rick Lai 和 Pris Fong 也在管理層 WhatsApp 群組討論，我會轉發訊息給你。

Don’t blow a fuse!

別把保險絲燒掉了！

Floyd Mann
General Manager

Interview with Neon Master Rick Lai in Hong Kong Arts and Crafts Magazine

We spoke with Rick Lai, Hong Kong’s top lighting designer, who works for the award-winning Shiny Diamond Lighting Ltd.

我們訪問了香港頂尖燈光設計師 Rick Lai；他任職於屢獲殊榮的 Shiny Diamond Lighting Ltd。

Hong Kong Arts and Crafts:

What do you enjoy most about your work?

你最喜歡工作中的哪一部分？

Rick Lai:

There are many aspects to the job I enjoy but what I love most is taking a customer’s design and turning it into a neon sign. It’s quite a complicated process. Basically, in the planning stage, when we receive the design, we use a grid.

這份工作有許多我喜歡的部分，但我最喜歡把客人的設計變成霓虹燈牌。過程相當複雜；規劃時收到設計，我們會使用方格。

Hong Kong Arts and Crafts:

Why do you do that?

為甚麼？

Rick Lai:

We use it to decide how many glass tubes we need. That’s all before we start making the sign using a machine called the Glass Heater.

方格讓我們計算需要多少玻璃管，之後才用「玻璃加熱器」製作燈牌。

Hong Kong Arts and Crafts:

What does that do?

這機器有甚麼用？

Rick Lai:

We use it to bend the glass tubes. We then have another machine which cleans the glass tubes.

用來彎曲玻璃管；之後另一部機器會清潔玻璃管。

Hong Kong Arts and Crafts:

How does it get them clean?

它怎樣清潔？

Rick Lai:

It uses electricity. Shocking, right? Also, whenever we move the glass tubes, they can be broken very easily so we have to be very, very careful with them. That’s the boring part for me, because I like to work quickly! Finally, we are also very careful to test if the tubes are working before we move on to the display stage. One time, a customer returned a faulty sign. It was so embarrassing!

用電力清潔。很意外吧？玻璃管搬動時很容易破，所以必須非常小心。我喜歡做事快，這是我覺得無聊的部分。到展示階段前還要仔細測試玻璃管；有一次客人退回故障燈牌，真令人尷尬！

Hong Kong Arts and Crafts:

What is the future for lighting design in Hong Kong?

香港燈光設計的前景如何？

Rick Lai:

It’s very bright! We are looking for trainees at the moment, so if any of your readers are interested, please apply to be a trainee.

前景非常光明！我們正在招聘培訓生，有興趣的讀者可以申請。

Hong Kong Arts and Crafts:

How can they apply?

他們如何申請？

Rick Lai:

The instructions will be in recruitment emails we are sending out soon. One thing to pay attention to, though: applicants must send us 4 or 5 photos of their previous work. That way, we can really get a feel for their ability.

申請方法會寫在即將寄出的招聘電郵。請注意：申請者必須提交四至五張過往作品相片，讓我們了解其能力。
