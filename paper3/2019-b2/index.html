Minutes of the Meeting of the Inter-School Visual Media Society Committee

Meeting of the Inter-School Visual Media Society Committee

Date:
Thurs 28 March, 2019
Venue:
Multi-media room, Tai Kiu Secondary School
Chairperson:
Ms Mira Ray
In Attendance:
Mr Ringo Chow (Vice Chair)
Ms Alia Kapoor (Public Relations and Liaison)
Ms Maude Woo (Cinematic Consultant)
Mr Pierre Choi (Treasurer)
Secretary:
Nico Lin

Minutes of Meeting

Venue Requirements for Screening of ‘The Electronic Durian’

Ms Woo confirmed the following requirements for the venue for the screening of ‘The Electronic Durian’ and the post-screening discussion on Wed 19 June:

Woo 女士確認了 6 月 19 日星期三《The Electronic Durian》放映及映後討論場地的以下要求：

For the Post-Screening Discussion on Stage

  • Minimum size of stage for discussion: depth > 4 metres, length > 20 metres

    討論舞台的最低尺寸：深度超過 4 米，長度超過 20 米。

  • Wheelchair ramp access onto stage

    須有輪椅斜道通往舞台。

Venue Choice for Screening of ‘The Electronic Durian’

Of the three possible cinema venues, Mr Choi was of the very strong opinion that the Brighthouse Cinema in Sha Tak was the best option for the venue of the screening of ‘The Electronic Durian’ and the post-screening discussion as it is the cheapest of the three and within the Festival budget. Ms Woo and Ms Kapoor agreed.

在三個候選戲院中，Choi 先生強烈認為沙達的 Brighthouse Cinema 是《The Electronic Durian》放映及映後討論的最佳場地，因為它最便宜，而且符合電影節預算。Woo 女士和 Kapoor 女士表示同意。

Ms Ray and Mr Chow expressed very strong reservations about the suitability of the Brighthouse Cinema. Ms Ray told the committee that she had heard rumours that the Brighthouse Cinema failed a government inspection previously.

Ray 女士和 Chow 先生對 Brighthouse Cinema 是否合適表示強烈保留。Ray 女士告訴委員會，她聽聞該戲院之前沒有通過政府檢查。

Action to be taken:

Ms Alia Kapoor to conduct site visit of the Brighthouse Cinema in coming week.

Alia Kapoor 女士將於下週實地考察 Brighthouse Cinema。

Nico Lin to write report on Brighthouse Cinema for next committee meeting.

Nico Lin 將為下次委員會會議撰寫 Brighthouse Cinema 的報告。

Next meeting fixed for Tues. 9 April. Venue to be confirmed.

下次會議定於 4 月 9 日星期二舉行，地點待定。

Notes from site visit of Brighthouse Cinema

Brighthouse Cinema, Sha Tak

Date of visit: Fri. 5/4/2019

Site visit by: Alia Kapoor

Liaison: Mr. Kung (Manager of Brighthouse Cinema)

Building – 70s design. Wheelchair access into auditorium for audience.

建築：七十年代設計。觀眾可經輪椅通道進入放映廳。

Stage – Black and gold. Very stylish and classic cinema design. Little cherubs on ceiling. See Stage Plan below.

舞台：黑金配色，設計時尚而有經典戲院風格。天花板有小天使裝飾。見下方舞台圖。

Exit Signs and Emergency Exits – Green signs at all exits – in English only – all bright and working properly.

出口標誌和緊急出口：所有出口都有綠色標誌，但只有英文；燈光明亮，運作正常。

Outside food – Mr Kung said no outside food is allowed. So no noodles!

外來食物：Kung 先生說不准攜帶外來食物，所以也不能帶麵條。

General Environment – Aircon works, but not too cold. Temperature very comfortable. Smells of jasmine.

整體環境：冷氣正常，但不會太冷，溫度舒適，有茉莉花香。

Hygiene – One cleaner. Cleaner’s name = Doris. 1 cleaning per 2 days. Garbage taken away after every screening.

衛生：只有一名清潔員 Doris；每兩天清潔一次；每場放映後會清走垃圾。

Staff – 4 ushers. 1 ticket sales. All wear cinema uniform – black bottoms, red tops. Mr Kung said that there is no possibility of having more than 4 ushers.

員工：四名帶位員、一名售票員。制服是黑色下裝、紅色上衣。Kung 先生說不可能增加至超過四名帶位員。

Brighthouse failed govt. hygiene inspection in 2016 – lost licence for 12 months. Have had licence since.

Brighthouse 在 2016 年未能通過政府衛生檢查，因此被吊銷牌照 12 個月；此後一直持有牌照。

Interview from Plato’s Cave film magazine (extract)

This week Jodie Broch was delighted to meet up with Isabel Luna, SE Asia’s talented young filmmaker, at the Virtual Madness Convention in New Lossiemouth, USA to talk to her about her science fiction thriller, The Electronic Durian. Extract of transcript below:

本週，Jodie Broch 很高興在美國新洛西茅斯舉行的 Virtual Madness Convention 與東南亞才華洋溢的年輕電影人 Isabel Luna 見面，談談她的科幻驚悚片《The Electronic Durian》。以下為訪問節錄：

[…]

Broch: We loved the character of Sammi in the film. And great to see Rebecca Chin back in a leading role after taking time off to finish her degree. How did Rebecca cope with all of the rigours of the shoot?

Broch：我們很喜歡片中的 Sammi。Rebecca Chin 完成學位、暫別影壇後再度擔綱主演，也令人高興。她怎樣應付拍攝的種種辛苦？

Luna: Well, Rebecca had to wear special contact lenses for a lot of the filming. The trouble was that Rebecca is one of these people who just finds it really difficult to put in contact lenses in real life. Her eyes just close. So that was a challenge for her and her makeup people every morning.

Luna：Rebecca 有很多場戲要戴特殊隱形眼鏡。問題是她在現實生活中很難戴隱形眼鏡，眼睛會不自覺閉上。所以每天早上，她和化妝師都要克服這個難題。

Broch: Ah poor her! Speaking of lenses, when Rebecca’s character, Sammi takes them off it’s important for the plot right?

Broch：她真不容易！說到眼鏡，Rebecca 飾演的 Sammi 把它們摘下來時，對劇情很重要，對嗎？

Luna: Yes, that’s the turning point. That’s when she suddenly sees the real world. You know, not the one the corporation created.

Luna：對，那是轉折點。她突然看見真實世界，而不是那家公司創造的世界。

Broch: And that’s when her troubles really kick off, of course.

Broch：當然，她的麻煩也從那時真正開始。

Luna: Yeah, well that was the most exciting part to shoot. And fun too with all those noodles!

Luna：沒錯，那是拍起來最刺激的部分。還有那些麵條，也很有趣！

Broch: It’s all very different from the beginning of the film.

Broch：這跟電影開頭很不一樣。

Luna: Yes well I wanted to start the movie with the scene where it’s Sammi’s first day at her first job at the corporation. We can all remember how exciting that day is. I just wanted to show her having a normal life at the start.

Luna：是的，我想用 Sammi 在那家公司第一份工作的第一天作為電影開場。我們都記得那一天有多令人興奮。我只想先呈現她過着普通生活的樣子。

Broch: I also like the scene just after that when you show how the corporation in the film tricked its employees into wearing its lenses.

Broch：我也喜歡緊接的一幕：你呈現了片中的公司如何欺騙員工戴上它的眼鏡。

Luna: Right, thanks.

Luna：對，謝謝。

Broch: And from that moment, that’s it. They’re in this ideal world created by the corporation.

Broch：從那一刻起，他們便活在那家公司創造的理想世界裏。

Luna: Yeah supposedly ideal. But it’s not of course.

Luna：對，表面上是理想世界，當然實際上並不是。

Interview from Plato’s Cave film magazine (extract) (cont.)

Broch: OK, turning to another topic that I know is close to your heart, I understand it’s getting very difficult for filmmakers like yourself, or say like Ayu Putri in Hong Kong. You make these low-budget independent films and still get them shown in mainstream cinemas. Is it getting more difficult?

Broch：好，轉談一個我知道你很關心的話題。我聽說像你或香港的 Ayu Putri 這樣的電影人，製作低預算獨立電影並讓作品在主流戲院上映，越來越困難。情況真的越來越難嗎？

Luna: Well yes and no. It is difficult. I would never say otherwise. But you have to realise that the big studios need low-budget films to be a success. They need them to continue.

Luna：可以說是，也可以說不是。這確實困難，我不會否認。但你要明白，大型片廠需要低預算電影成功，也需要它們繼續存在。

Broch: But how do you mean? Surely the low-budget films are in direct competition with the big studios’ big blockbusters. The big studios want to beat these low-budget filmmakers.

Broch：你是甚麼意思？低預算電影不是直接與大片廠的賣座電影競爭嗎？大片廠應該想擊敗這些低預算電影人。

Luna: Well let me answer by asking a question: Where do the big studios get new talented directors from?

Luna：讓我反問你：大片廠從哪裏找到有才華的新導演？

Broch: Erm…the low-budget films I suppose.

Broch：嗯……我想是從低預算電影中吧。

Luna: So who are the directors who will be making the blockbusters in the future?

Luna：那麼，未來執導大片的會是哪些導演？

Broch: Erm…the same ones. The low-budget independent film directors of today.

Broch：嗯……就是同一批人，今天拍低預算獨立電影的導演。

Luna: Exactly. So the big studios need low-budget films to continue for that very reason.

Luna：正是如此。因此，大片廠需要低預算電影繼續存在。

Extract from Government Cross-departmental Regulations for Cinemas and Theatres

HKSAR Government · File　Edit　View　Go　Bookmarks　Tools　Help
https://www.cross-departmental.gov.hk/regulations

Cleaning

  • Cinemas must be cleaned at least once per day and must ensure that all rubbish is removed after each screening.

    戲院每天至少須清潔一次，並確保每場放映後清走所有垃圾。

  • Cleaning staff must be provided with facial masks in situations in which they are likely to be dealing with airborne dust particles.

    清潔員在可能接觸空氣中塵粒的情況下，必須獲提供口罩。

Escalator and Lift Maintenance

  • Escalators and lifts must have at least one inspection per year. The certificate of inspection must be available for viewing upon request by any government inspector.

    扶手電梯和升降機每年至少須接受一次檢查。政府督察要求查閱時，必須出示檢查證明書。

Restricted Films

  • Cinemas are liable if underage children are caught viewing Category III films.

    若發現未成年兒童觀看三級電影，戲院須承擔責任。

Safety and Evacuation

  • To ensure the safe and prompt evacuation of the cinema in case of emergency, all exits must be indicated by illuminated signs bearing the word “Exit” in English and Chinese.

    為確保緊急情況下能安全迅速疏散，所有出口必須設有以中英文標示「出口」的亮燈標誌。

  • All emergency exits must be free of obstructions to ensure orderly evacuation.

    所有緊急出口必須保持暢通，以確保有序疏散。
