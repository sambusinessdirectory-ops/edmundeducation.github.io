Part B

Situation

You are Nicky Leung. You work for Cultural Commons, which is a non-governmental organisation, an NGO, in Hong Kong. Cultural Commons helps people from different countries who come to live in Hong Kong. Your organisation is planning a Fun Day for a local community. Your boss, Manjula Pillai, has asked you to do some tasks.

你是 Nicky Leung，在香港非政府組織 Cultural Commons 工作，協助移居香港的不同國籍人士。機構正籌辦社區同樂日；上司 Manjula Pillai 請你完成數項任務。

You will listen to a recording of the weekly podcast programme Bookmark. In the podcast you will hear an interview with author Lionel Chan.

你將聆聽每週播客節目 Bookmark，當中訪問作家 Lionel Chan。

Before the recording is played, you will have five minutes to study the Question-Answer Book and the Data File for Part B1 and the Question-Answer Book and the Data File for Part B2. Remember you must choose to do the tasks in either Part B1 or Part B2. Do NOT attempt both Parts B1 and B2.

錄音播放前，你有五分鐘閱讀乙部一與乙部二的問答冊和資料檔。只可選答其中一部分，不可兩部分都作答。

Complete the tasks by following the instructions in the Question-Answer Book that you choose and on the recording. You will find all the information you need in the Question-Answer Book and Data File that you choose and on the recording. As you listen you can make notes on page 3 of the Data File.

按所選問答冊及錄音指示完成任務。所需資料載於問答冊、資料檔及錄音；聆聽時可在資料檔第 3 頁筆記。

You will have five minutes to familiarize yourself with the Part B Question-Answer Book and the Data File.

你有五分鐘熟悉乙部問答冊及資料檔。

Email from Manjula Pillai

To: Nicky Leung
From: Manjula Pillai
Sent: 31 March 2012
Subject: Things to do for Fun Day

Dear Nicky

Nicky：

Could you please do the following three things for me?

請幫我做以下三件事。

We want to hand out some leaflets before the Fun Day to encourage people to come. I’ve designed a basic template for a leaflet but I need you to add in the details. Include:

我們想在同樂日前派發傳單，鼓勵居民參加。我已設計傳單基本樣式，請補上細節，包括：

  • the place, date and time (in the top box)
  • the activities
  • the special guests (mention why they’re famous)
  • our organisation name and phone number (in the bottom box)

上方框內寫地點、日期及時間；活動；特別嘉賓及其知名原因；下方框內寫機構名稱和電話。

Look at the minutes of the last Fun Day Planning Committee meeting and your notes from the Bookmark podcast.

參考上次同樂日籌委會會議紀錄和 Bookmark 播客筆記。

I need the information about the Thai Ladies performance. Please send me an email with the information you’ve managed to find out, like:

我需要 Thai Ladies 表演的資料。請電郵告訴我查到的資訊，例如：

  • how much time the dancers need and how many people they’re bringing
  • what they need and if it’s available from the Hong Kong Parks Department
  • any costs involved
  • any possible problems

表演時間和人數、所需設備及公園署能否提供、費用和可能問題。

Finally, Lionel Chan is going to be one of our special guests at the Fun Day. Write a short descriptive paragraph about him to put on our website. Please include information about:

最後，Lionel Chan 是同樂日特別嘉賓之一。請為網站撰寫他的簡介，包括：

  • his background and family
  • his current and previous jobs
  • his new book

他的背景與家庭、現在及過往工作，以及新書。

You should be able to get information from the book jacket of his latest book, the Booklore website and your notes from the Bookmark podcast.

你可從新書封套、Booklore 網站及播客筆記找資料。

Thanks for your help.

謝謝你的幫忙。

Best
Manjula

Minutes of the 3rd meeting of the Fun Day Planning Committee

Date: 15th March, 2012

Present: Manjula Pillai (MP)
Nicky Leung (NL)
Elvis Chan (EC)
Davy Kulkarni (DK)

Agenda

1. Fun Day times

It was decided that the Fun Day should start at 2 pm. EC advised that the event should finish by 6.30 as it would get dark then. All agreed.

同樂日下午二時開始，傍晚六時半天黑前結束。

2. Venue

DK reported that the Fun Day would take place in the Chinese Garden in the park.

活動在公園內的中國花園舉行。

3. Cooking workshop and dance performance

DK reported that a local Indian cooking group had agreed to hold a cooking workshop.

本地印度烹飪團體同意舉辦烹飪工作坊。

NL reported that a local group of Thai women called Thai Ladies had agreed to do a dance performance.

Thai Ladies 泰國婦女團體同意跳舞表演。

Action agreed: NL to contact the Hong Kong Parks Department to ask about equipment available for the Fun Day and to ask the Thai Ladies what they will need on the day.

Nicky 將向公園署查詢設備，並向 Thai Ladies 詢問當天所需。

4. Other Fun Day activities

The committee discussed other activities and agreed to encourage people to come in national dress. It was decided that it could be made a competition.

鼓勵參加者穿民族服飾，並設比賽。

5. Special guests

DK reported that Duleep Singh had confirmed he would be a special guest at the Fun Day. MP agreed that having a famous film star for the guest was good news.

著名電影明星 Duleep Singh 已確認擔任特別嘉賓。

6. Any other business

No further points were raised.

沒有其他事項。

The next meeting was set for 10th April 2012.

下次會議定於 2012 年 4 月 10 日。

Email from the Thai Ladies group

To: Nicky Leung
From: Buppha@tladies.com
Sent: 27 March 2012
Subject: Fun day dance

Dear Nicky

Nicky：

Thanks for meeting me last week.

謝謝你上星期與我會面。

We’re delighted to be coming to the Fun Day. There will be 14 dancers and we’ll need about 25 – 30 minutes in total for the performance. We’ll also be bringing a few more people to help, probably three – four. So could you make sure we have enough space for all of us? Plus all of our dance costumes and bags?

我們很高興參加同樂日。十四位舞蹈員表演約需二十五至三十分鐘，另有約三至四人協助；請預留足夠空間放置人員、服裝和袋子。

We’ll need a stage so that people can see us and please make sure we have electricity available to play the music. We can provide a music system. It may be a good idea for you to provide some chairs for old people to sit down. I know that sometimes the Hong Kong Parks Department provide lights but we don’t need lights.

我們需要舞台和播放音樂的電力；音響由我們提供。建議為長者準備椅子。不需要燈光。

Regards
Buppha

Email from the Hong Kong Parks Department

To: Nicky Leung
From: hkparks@hkparks.hk
Sent: 28 March 2012
Subject: Re: Fun Day equipment enquiry

Dear Nicky

Nicky：

Thanks for your email about equipment. We can provide some different things free of charge for your Fun Day:

謝謝查詢同樂日設備。我們可免費提供：

  • A stage (capacity 10 people)
  • Tables
  • Microphones
  • Outdoor lights

舞台（最多十人）、桌子、麥克風和戶外燈光。

Please note that we can only provide one stage.

請注意，我們只能提供一個舞台。

If you need further equipment, we can provide things such as chairs and generators for electricity. Please note that we will need to charge you 500 dollars deposit for up to 100 chairs and 150 dollars deposit for each electricity generator. You would need one generator for a music system and one for lights.

如需椅子或發電機等其他設備，最多一百張椅子的按金是五百元，每部發電機按金是一百五十元。音響和燈光各需一部發電機。

Please let me know what you need as soon as possible.

請盡快告知所需設備。

Regards
Andrew Tse
Hong Kong Parks Department

Loved it. Specially the stories about people from India, Colombia and Cameroon ;-).

很喜歡，尤其是印度、哥倫比亞和喀麥隆人物的故事。

Lionel 的女兒在大埔本地學校就讀，太太來自斯里蘭卡；他親身經歷移居生活，所以寫移民故事。
