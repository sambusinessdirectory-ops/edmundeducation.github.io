Why I say Gamezstart ‘stinks’

Welcome back to my blog. This week I want to talk about getting funds for your board game.

歡迎重臨我的網誌。今週談談如何為你的桌遊籌集資金。

You’ve developed a board game and the next step is to sell it but you probably don’t have the cash to pay for a factory to produce it. So one way you can solve the problem is Gamezstart (www.gamezstart.com). And what’s so good about Gamezstart? Well, one huge plus without a doubt is that you get to advertise your game on their website. This attracts investors to invest in your game. The clever part is that you set the target amount of money you need to produce the game. It gives you ultimate flexibility! Sounds great eh? People invest and you get the money to continue developing your game. Problem solved! And when it’s finished you send one copy of the game to each investor. So everybody’s happy. It’s a win-win situation, right? Wrong!

開發好桌遊後要把它推出市場，但你可能沒有足夠現金支付工廠生產。Gamezstart 是一個方法：網站替遊戲宣傳，吸引投資者；你可自行設定生產所需的籌款目標，靈活運作。成功籌款後繼續開發，完成時送每位投資者一份遊戲。看似皆大歡喜，真的嗎？

Big companies who are already well-known, and who have already published a bunch of stuff, are now using Gamezstart to advertise their latest products. And if you have a Gamezstart project at the same time as another project, one from a big company, then your project is very likely gonna be ignored by investors. You’ll be a tiny fish competing with a great big shark.

不少知名大公司也在 Gamezstart 宣傳新產品。若你的項目與它們同時上架，投資者很可能忽略你；小魚難與巨鯊競爭。

And that’s why I say Gamezstart ‘stinks’ for non-professionals like you and me.

這就是我說 Gamezstart 對你我這種非專業人士「不行」的原因。
