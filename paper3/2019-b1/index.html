Youth by Youth Short Video Competition Poster

Ms Ray told the meeting that the Tyrell Coffee House can no longer be the sponsor as they have declared bankruptcy. Fortunately, Yutani Video Games has offered to become the new sponsor for the Youth by Youth Short Video Competition. This should be changed on the poster.

Ms Kapoor said she was unhappy with the image on the poster. She felt it should be replaced because the Saint Elgin’s School Video Club uses the same image. She suggested using an image of a video camera instead. The committee agreed.

Nico to give Ms Woo feedback explaining changes to be made on poster.

Prize Giving Ceremony for the Youth by Youth Short Video Competition

Mr Chow told the committee that the voting from all participating schools for the prize giving ceremony would end on 5 April. From the vote results, Nico should select the most popular choices for:

° the prize giving ceremony host e the activity for the ceremony

Ms Ray noted that due to budget restrictions, there will be one judge only. Nico should select the cheapest judge. Nico should refer to the tabled Fact File.

Mr Chow confirmed all attendees of the ceremony will be given free gift packs. Nico to include this information in the announcement for the event.

Next meeting fixed for Tues. 9 April. Venue to be confirmed.
